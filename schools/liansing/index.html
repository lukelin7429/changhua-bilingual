I.

Welcome to Liansing

歡迎來到聯興國小

A tranquil educational sanctuary surrounded by nature — where pineapple fields meet a nurturing campus.

自然環繞的寧靜教育環境——鳳梨田與溫暖校園相遇的地方。

Located in Niupu Li near the lush foothills of the Baguashan Plateau, Liansing Elementary stands amid an area renowned for its pineapple cultivation. Our small but vibrant campus is a place where natural beauty meets a nurturing learning environment, with butterflies adorning the surroundings and inspiring our students every day.

位於牛埔里，且鄰近八卦山台地山腳下，聯興國小坐落於一個以鳳梨栽培聞名的地區。我們的小型校園是自然美景與學習環境相融合的地方，學校周圍蝴蝶飛舞，每天啟發著我們的學生。

Embracing our rich agricultural heritage, we offer unique programs like Pineapple Expert and Happy Butterfly Expert — curricula that connect our students with the local ecosystem and culture, fostering an appreciation of our natural and community resources.

我們的課程以豐富的農業遺產為靈感，特設「鳳梨達人」和「快樂蝴蝶專家」等特色課程，讓學生與當地生態系統和文化相連結，培養他們對自然及社區資源的欣賞。

Since its establishment in 1964, Liansing has undergone a remarkable transformation — from humble beginnings serving the children of a nearby military village, to a campus with elegant buildings, a colorful running track, advanced e-learning facilities, and a passionate teaching team, a model of modern education in Changhua City.

1964 年創校之初，是為供眷村（影劇一村）子弟就讀而設立，校地窄小、一切因陋就簡。經過逾半世紀歷任校長、教職員工與家長會的努力，如今聯興國小已擁有優雅校舍、彩虹般的跑道、新穎的 e 化教學設備與充滿專業熱情的教學團隊，成為彰化市現代教育的典範，堪稱城市裡的桃花源。

Our dedication to reading education is unparalleled — in 2023, Liansing was the only school in Changhua County to receive the Ministry of Education's Rock of Reading Award, affirming our commitment to instilling a love for reading in every student.

我們在閱讀教育上的致力無與倫比——2023 年，聯興國小成為彰化縣唯一獲得教育部「閱讀磐石獎」的學校，證明了我們培養學生閱讀興趣的承諾。

Where pineapples grow and butterflies dance
鳳梨飄香，蝴蝶翩翩的校園

Our rainbow-colored running track circles a campus where local farming life becomes the classroom — through Pineapple Expert and Happy Butterfly Expert, children learn to see their own neighborhood as a place worth knowing deeply.

彩虹跑道環繞著校園，在地農村生活就是最好的課堂——透過「鳳梨達人」與「快樂蝴蝶專家」課程，孩子學會用心認識自己生長的這片土地。

II.

EAGLE · 「鷹」才施教

聯興課程意象——EAGLE 跨域識讀課程

Innovation · Friendliness · Well-being · Humanity — four words that shape every child's learning at Liansing.

創新、友善、樂活、人文——聯興國小的辦學願景，也是校訂課程「EAGLE 鷹才施教」的出發點。

Our Mascot · 吉祥物由來

Through a school-wide mascot vote, Liansing chose the eagle as the symbol of its school-based curriculum. EAGLE stands for five interwoven strands of learning that carry students from the classroom out into the world and back into their own community.

聯興透過全校吉祥物徵稿與投票，選出「鷹」作為校訂課程意象代表，並以 EAGLE 命名，內含五條相互交織的學習主軸，帶孩子從教室走向世界，也走回自己的社區。
