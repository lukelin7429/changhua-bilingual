I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話
"The way of education is none other than love and example." — Friedrich Fröbel
福祿貝爾：「教育之道無他，唯愛與榜樣而已。」

In August 2026, I begin a new chapter at Liansing Elementary — my fourth principalship in Changhua County. I carry with me the same conviction that first brought me to this profession: a deep love for education, expressed through supporting teachers' innovation and professional growth, and through leading by example.

2026 年 8 月，玟玟於聯興國小展開新的教育旅程——這是我在彰化縣服務的第四所學校。一路走來，我始終秉持著對教育的熱愛，將秉持這份熱忱推動教師教學革新、提昇教師專業發展與成長，並以身作則，服務教師全面落實教育工作，以提升學生學習成效。

A principal's core leadership task is to help teachers improve their craft. I think of the role as that of a lead teacher — bringing my own training in qualitative research and action research into a working dialog with classroom practice, hoping to model what proactive professional development can look like.

校長領導核心任務之一，即在協助教師改進教學，建構典範學習，提升教學成效，並以成為「首席教師」的角色自許。個人教學研究專長為「質性研究」，以教學實務與教學理論作結合，嘗試結合行動研究，藉此希冀提供良性的示範，帶動現場教師主動發展專業的可能性。

I have served as a principal since February 2012 — first at Jianxin Elementary (2012–2015), then Tongfang Elementary (2015–2022), then Cundong Elementary (2022–2026), and now at Liansing since August 2026. Across those years, our schools have completed major campus renovations, advanced reading, English, and international education, and built award-winning programs in environmental education, a Ten Drum Troupe, and a Unicycle Squad.

玟玟自 101 年 2 月起擔任國小校長，歷任建新國小（101/2–104/1）、東芳國小（104/2–111/7）、村東國小（111/8–115/7），現於 115 年 8 月起服務於聯興國小。這些年來，各校在校舍整建、閱讀英語國際教育推動，以及環境教育、十鼓隊、獨輪車隊等特色課程上，都締造了亮眼成果。

"Choose one task, devote a life, benefit all" remains my answer to what a life in education means. At Liansing, I hope to bring the same steady commitment — putting the school and its students first in every decision, and building on the work of the teachers, staff, and families who have cared for this campus long before I arrived.

「擇一事，做一生，惠眾生」是玟玟對教育志業一貫的註解。來到聯興，我期盼延續同樣的初心——為學校、為學生利益做最大的考量，並在教職同仁與社區家長長期耕耘的基礎上，繼續努力。感謝長官的提攜、同事的照顧，以及家人一路以來的支持與鼓勵。
