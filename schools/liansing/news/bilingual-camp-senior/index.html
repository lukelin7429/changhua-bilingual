Wild Words — Camp Day Three

部分領域雙語教學營隊 · 高年級

The senior-grade bilingual camp has now reached day three!

Learning by moving, speaking, and working together.

Beyond getting to know all kinds of mammals, students used English to speak, moved their bodies, and cooperated with each other to complete tasks — the learning space was full of laughter and energy!

From student-teacher interaction to peer teamwork, every scene showed just how engaged — and how much the children were growing. It's left behind a rich, unforgettable set of memories from the break.

There's still a middle-grade session to come, with more exciting activities on the way — stay tuned for updates!

中文原文 · Chinese

部分領域雙語教學營隊－高年級

目前營隊來到第三天～

孩子們在活動中除了認識各種動物，還用英語說、用身體動、彼此合作完成任務，學習現場充滿笑聲與活力！

從師生互動到同儕體驗，每一個畫面都看得到孩子的投入與成長，也為寒假留下豐富又難忘的回憶。

暑假期間還有中年級場次，精彩活動持續進行中，歡迎一起關注後續分享～
