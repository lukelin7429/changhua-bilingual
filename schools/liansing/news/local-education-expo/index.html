Where Mother Tongue Becomes a Game

本土教育發表會 · 母語闖關遊戲站

Today marked Changhua County's Local Education Expo, and Liansing set up its very own mother-tongue game booth — planned and run by Director Wang Miao-hsin.

Right next door was Dazhu Elementary's eye-catching Taiwanese opera booth, complete with costumes and stage props. The whole event was packed with stage performances and game stations, running all the way until one in the afternoon.

A whole afternoon of local language and local pride.

Families were warmly invited to bring their children out to join in the fun.

中文原文 · Chinese

今天是彰化縣本土教育發表會，本校設置母語闖關遊戲站，王妙心主任主辦設計執行，我們的旁邊是很有特色的大竹國小歌仔戲攤位，另外大會還有表演節目、闖關活動，非常精彩，一直到下午一點，歡迎家長帶孩子們來參加喔！
