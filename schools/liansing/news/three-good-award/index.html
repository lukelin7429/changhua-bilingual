An Honor Rooted in Kindness

榮獲第十五屆三好校園實踐學校獎

Congratulations are in order for Liansing Elementary! Our school has been honored as one of the winning schools in the 15th Three Good Deeds Practicing School Award, celebrated today at a special ceremony held at Huizhong Temple in Taichung City.

Do good deeds. Say good words. Keep good thoughts.

The Three Good Deeds award, promoted by Fo Guang Shan, recognizes schools that weave kindness into everyday campus life — encouraging students and staff alike to do good, speak kind words, and hold onto good thoughts. Representatives from award-winning schools across Taiwan gathered for the ceremony, taking the stage together to mark years of quiet, everyday effort.

For everyone at Liansing, this recognition is a moment of real gratitude — for the teachers who model kindness in the classroom, and for the students who carry it out onto the playground, and all the way home.
