Walking the Hills, Rooted in Community

淨山健行暨鄉土文化教學活動

This morning, Liansing held a special Children's Day activity — part environmental-education hike, part local-culture lesson — under clear blue skies that were perfect for walking.

Grades 3 through 6 headed to Dongfang Park, while grade 1 visited Guangfeng Temple and grade 2 visited Dongxing Temple. All along the trail, the children's energy never let up, full of songs and laughter.

A hike carried by the whole community.

Village Chief Liu of Taifong Village kindly opened the public restroom's water supply for the day, and on the way back, warm-hearted elders offered the children drinks and snacks — turning the hike into something carried by the whole community's care.

中文原文 · Chinese

115/4/2 今天學校舉辦115年慶祝兒童節環境教育淨山健行暨鄉土文化教學活動，一早天氣晴朗，適合戶外健行。

三~六年級去東方公園，一年級去廣鳳宮，二年級去東興宮，一路上小朋友活力十足，充滿歌聲與笑聲。

感謝台鳳里劉里長幫忙開公廁水源，也謝謝回程時熱心提供飲料餅乾的長輩，讓這趟健行充滿社區的愛與關懷!
