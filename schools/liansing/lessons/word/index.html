II.

Classroom Subjects

學科課堂

自然、數學、國語、遠距學習——聯興孩子每天的課表，都可以用英文說。

WORD 001
science classn.
自然科學課
The teacher is lecturing in science class.
老師正在自然科學課授課。
The students are learning how a jack lifts heavy things in science class.
在自然科學課，學生們正在學習千斤頂如何舉起重物。
WORD 002
math classn.
數學課
The students are in math class.
學生們正在上數學課。
The teacher teaches math in math class.
老師在數學課教數學。
WORD 003
Chinese classn.
國語課
The students are in Chinese class.
同學們正在上國語課。
They learn Chinese characters in Chinese class.
他們在國語課學習中文字。
WORD 004
distance learningn.
遠距學習
The students participate in a distance learning program.
學生參加遠距學習計畫。
In the distance learning program, the students focus on learning English.
在遠距學習計畫中，學生專注於學習英語。
III.

Arts & Music

藝術與音樂

直笛、弦樂器、彩繪、才藝表演——聯興的藝術課教室裡充滿創意與聲音。

WORD 005
colorn.
顏色
Some pieces of art aren't finished until the artist has added color.
有些藝術作品必須等藝術家上好顏色才算完成。
These students are using markers and colored pencils to add color to their drawings.
這些學生用馬克筆與色鉛筆來為他們的圖畫上顏色。
WORD 006
recordern.
直笛
The recorder is the first musical instrument that many students learn.
直笛是很多學生學的第一項樂器。
You can play many songs on a recorder.
你可以用直笛吹奏很多歌曲。
WORD 007
string instrumentn.
弦樂器
String instruments are any musical instruments that are played with a bow.
弦樂器是用弓演奏的任何樂器。
Some examples of string instruments are the cello and the violin.
弦樂器的一些例子是大提琴和小提琴。
WORD 008
talent shown.
才藝表演
The school holds a talent show.
學校舉辦才藝表演。
The students play different instruments at the talent show.
學生們在才藝表演中演奏不同的樂器。
IV.

Sports & Health

體育與健康

排球發球、勤洗手——聯興孩子在球場上動起來，也在生活裡照顧自己。

WORD 009
servev.
發球
The PE teacher shows her students how to serve the volleyball.
體育老師為學生示範排球的發球。
The students try to serve the volleyball to their classmates.
學生試著發球給同學。
WORD 010
wash your handsv.
洗手
It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
確定在吃東西之前要用香皂和水來洗手。
V.

Hands-On Learning

動手做學習

積木與組裝——聯興孩子動手做，數學和機器人課都不只是紙上談兵。

WORD 011
building blocksn.
積木
The students use square building blocks in math class.
學生在數學課上使用方形積木。
They use building blocks to do multiplication problems.
他們使用積木來解決乘法問題。
WORD 012
assemblev.
組裝
The students assemble their robots.
學生們組裝他們的機器人。
They follow the instructions to assemble their robots.
他們按照說明組裝機器人。
VI.

Library & Reading

圖書與閱讀

借書、閱讀——聯興的圖書室，是孩子認識世界的窗口。

WORD 013
check outv.
借書
The students check out books from the library at recess.
同學們利用下課到圖書室借書。
After the librarian checks out the students' books, they can take them home to read.
向圖書館員辦理借閱手續後，同學們可以將書帶回家看。
WORD 014
readv.
閱讀
The student chooses a book to read.
學生選擇一本書閱讀。
The student reads a book.
學生們在讀書。
VII.

Campus & School Life

校園生活

打掃校園、批改作業、老師進修——這些是聯興校園裡看得見的日常風景。

WORD 015
sweepv.
掃
Every day, students sweep away fallen leaves on school grounds.
每天學生打掃校園的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.
台灣的學生打掃校園，保持校園的乾淨整齊。
WORD 016
correctv.
改正
The teachers correct the students' work.
老師改同學的作業。
They use a red pen to correct the students' work.
他們使用紅筆來改學生的作業。
WORD 017
professional developmentn.
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都有教師專業訓練課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
在專業訓練課程中，老師可以學到新的教學技巧與策略。
