Yang Hsunfei楊巽斐 校長

A scientist-educator with three previous principalships in Changhua's smallest schools — Tuku, Lianxing, Xinmin — and a Ph.D. in Science Education from National Changhua University of Education. She returned to a small rural school not because it is small, but because "a small school is where every child is seen."
楊校長是科學教育出身的偏鄉小校經營者——博士學位來自彰化師大科教所，前三任校長職都在彰化縣的小校（土庫、聯興、新民），2022 年 8 月接任南州。她選擇繼續走小校這條路，不是因為「小」，而是因為「在小學校，每一個孩子都會被看見」。

II.

Education Philosophy

教育理念 · 科學的心、教育的眼、自然的腳
🧭 A Three-Point Compass · 三足羅盤
"Curious eyes, careful hands, kind hearts."
「好奇的眼，謹慎的手，溫柔的心。」
—— 楊巽斐校長 · A scientist-educator's compass for a small forest school

At Nanjhou, a small school under a hundred-year banyan, the principal's compass has three legs.
在這所百年老榕守護下的小校裡，校長的羅盤有三隻腳。

🔬
Curious Eyes
好奇的眼 · 科學家的眼
A science PhD's habit: ask why before what to do. Children copy what the school takes seriously — and at Nanjhou, we take questions seriously.
科教博士的習慣——先問「為什麼」，再決定「怎麼做」。孩子模仿的是「學校認真對待的事」，南州認真對待的是提問。
🌱
Careful Hands
謹慎的手 · 教育者的手
A plant takes years to root. So does a child. Three previous principalships in tiny Changhua schools taught us that the slow way is the only way.
一棵植物要花好幾年扎根；一個孩子也是。在三所偏鄉小校累積的校長經驗告訴我們：慢，是唯一的快。
🦜
Kind Hearts
溫柔的心 · 自然的心
As a standing supervisor of the Changhua Wild Bird Society, the principal teaches by example: be gentle with what's smaller than you. The banyan, the birds, the seventy-six children.
身為彰化縣野鳥學會常務監事，校長以身作則：對比自己小的，要輕——老榕、鳥群、與全校七十六個孩子，都是這條原則的對象。

IV.

Beyond the Office

校長辦公室以外 · 野鳥、科展、學術三線並進

A small-school principal serves a wider Changhua too. Principal Yang's after-hours work runs on three tracks — wild birds, science fairs, and academic writing — each one feeding back into what Nanjhou's classrooms can become. Her teacher-training work runs on a fourth, and gets a section of its own below.
小校校長的時間，往往也屬於整個彰化。楊校長的「下班後角色」分別在野鳥、科展、學術三條線上——每一條都會回饋到南州的教室。第四條線是師資培育，份量太重，另闢一節。

V.

Teaching the Teachers

師資培育 · 從縣內課後照顧，到全國夢N，再到大學講堂

One principal can reach seventy-six children. A teacher trainer reaches every classroom those teachers walk into. Principal Yang works at all three levels of Taiwan's science-teaching profession at once — and at each level the material is the same thing she is still testing at Nanjhou on a Tuesday morning.
一位校長能照顧七十六個孩子；一位師培講師，照顧的是這些老師往後走進的每一間教室。楊校長同時站在台灣自然科師資培育的三個層級上——而她在每一層講的內容，都是她週二早上還在南州課堂裡驗證的東西。

County · 縣級
After-school Care Trainer
課後照顧服務人員 培訓講師
The adults who take over when the bell rings need a professional education too. Principal Yang teaches on Changhua's after-school care training programme, so that the hours after three o'clock are taught — not merely supervised.
放學鐘響後接手孩子的大人，同樣需要專業訓練。楊校長在彰化縣課後照顧服務人員培訓授課，讓「三點以後」是被教學的時間，而不只是被看管的時間。
National · 全國
Convener, Elementary Science — Dream-N
夢的N次方（夢N） 國小自然 召集人
Dream-N is Taiwan's largest teacher-led professional-learning movement: built by teachers, for teachers, on their own weekends. Principal Yang convenes its elementary-science strand — assembling the instructor team and shaping the two-day programme for a regional session. The Yunlin session she convened at Douliu Junior High ran digital inquiry, bilingual science, solar motors and hydrogen fuel cells, and TPACK: the whole span of what a science classroom is currently becoming."Teachers Get Support, Kids Get Hope." — Dream-N夢的N次方是台灣規模最大的教師自主增能運動——由老師發起、為老師而辦，用的是自己的週末。楊校長擔任國小自然場次的召集人：組講師群、定兩天課程的走向。她召集的雲林場（斗六國中）涵蓋雲霧數位探究、雙語教學、太陽能馬達與氫氧燃料電池實作、TPACK——正好是一間自然教室正在長成的樣子。
National · 全國 · MOE
Instructor & Coach Teacher, MOE Science Inquiry Program
教育部國民中小學自然科學領域探究計畫 研習講師暨教練教師
A long-running appointment across three strands — inquiry teaching, motivation embedded in inquiry, and cross-disciplinary inquiry (STEM). A coach teacher is not a lecturer who leaves at four o'clock: the official citation credits her with staying with participants, actively and warmly, until their lesson plans were finished and submitted.
長期擔任教育部國民中小學自然科學領域的研習講師，主軸有三：探究教學、動機融入探究、跨領域探究（STEM）。「教練教師」和一般講師不同——官方名單上的評語是：積極且熱心指導學員完成教案。她不是講完就走，而是陪學員把作品寫完、送出。

🧪 Lesson-Plan Award · 教案設計競賽優選
Silver Cone Volcano — can a solution change its acidity?
〈銀錐火山－水溶液的酸鹼性可以改變嗎？〉
The title is the spectacle; the subtitle is the actual lesson. Children come for the volcano and stay for the question underneath it — if I change what is in the beaker, does the liquid become a different thing? Open with wonder, close with a variable: that is inquiry teaching in one sentence, and it is why this plan was selected in the MOE inquiry programme's lesson-plan competition. 標題是一場秀，副標才是這堂課真正的內容。孩子為了「火山」而來，卻留下來面對底下那個問題——如果我改變杯子裡的東西，這杯水會變成另一種東西嗎？以驚奇開場，以變因收尾——這就是探究教學的一句話定義，也是這份教案獲得教育部探究計畫教案設計競賽優選的原因。

Why she teaches teachers · 師培的初衷
She trains the teachers who are coming, and the teachers who are already there — because a good lesson only outlives a single classroom when the profession keeps growing.
她培訓未來的老師，也為現職自然科教師的專業而努力——因為唯有整個專業持續成長，一堂好課才不會只活在一間教室裡。
