I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Kuo Jia-wen (郭佳文) became the fifth principal of Changsing Junior High on August 1, 2025. He arrived from Beidou Junior High, where he had served as principal with a record that earned broad recognition from the Changhua County Department of Education.

郭佳文校長於 2025 年 8 月 1 日（民國 114 年 8 月）接任彰興國中第五任校長。他從北斗國中轉任，在北斗任職期間表現亮眼，獲彰化縣政府充分肯定。

His appointment to Changsing — the most enrollment-competitive junior high in Changhua City, where families relocate just to qualify — signals a shared confidence that the best chapter of Changsing's story is still ahead. He inherits a school shaped by Principal Wu Li-yue's outstanding academic legacy and takes it into its next era of bilingual excellence.

接掌彰興——這所連遷戶口都想擠進來的彰化市最熱門國中——意味著各界對他的信任與期待。他從前任吳麗月校長的亮眼升學基礎出發，帶領學校邁向雙語教育的全新篇章。
