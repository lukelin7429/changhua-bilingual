II.

Sports & Activities

運動與活動

球場上、操場邊，彰興學生用英文說運動。

WORD 001
basketballn.
籃球
Basketball is a fast, exciting sport played with a ball and a hoop.
籃球是一種節奏明快、刺激的運動，用球和籃框進行。
Changsing students play basketball every day at recess.
彰興的學生下課都會打籃球。
WORD 002
tee-balln.
樂樂棒球
Tee-ball is a beginner's version of baseball, hit off a stand instead of a pitch.
樂樂棒球是棒球的入門版，把球放在球座上打擊，不需要投手。
Our PE class practices tee-ball on the school field.
我們體育課會在操場上練習樂樂棒球。
WORD 003
jogv.
慢跑
To jog means to run at a slow, steady pace.
Jog（慢跑）是指用緩慢而穩定的速度跑步。
Students jog around the track before class starts.
上課前，學生會繞著操場慢跑。
III.

Nature & Environment

自然與環境

彰興校園有羊舍、禽園、蝴蝶園——每一角落都是自然課。

WORD 004
pondn.
池塘
A pond is a small body of still water, often home to fish and plants.
池塘是一小片靜止的水域，常常住著魚和水生植物。
Changsing's ecological garden has a small pond full of fish.
彰興的生態園裡有一座養著魚的小池塘。
WORD 005
water filtern.
濾水器
A water filter removes dirt and impurities so water is clean to drink.
濾水器可以濾除水中的髒污和雜質，讓水乾淨可以飲用。
Every classroom at Changsing has a water filter for students.
彰興每間教室都有提供學生使用的濾水器。
WORD 006
raken.
耙
A rake is a garden tool used to gather leaves and loosen soil.
耙是一種園藝工具，用來聚集落葉、鬆土。
Students use a rake to clean up fallen leaves in the garden.
學生用耙子清理花園裡的落葉。
WORD 007
butterflyn.
蝴蝶
A butterfly is an insect with large, colorful wings.
蝴蝶是一種有著大而色彩繽紛翅膀的昆蟲。
Changsing's ecological garden attracts many butterflies in spring.
彰興的生態園春天會吸引許多蝴蝶。
IV.

Science & Technology

科學與科技

天文台、機器人——彰興把好奇心當成一門學科。

WORD 008
experimentn.
實驗
An experiment is a test scientists do to check if an idea is true.
實驗是科學家用來驗證想法是否正確的一種測試。
Students do a science experiment in the lab every week.
學生每週都會在實驗室做一次科學實驗。
WORD 009
robotn.
機器人
A robot is a machine that can be programmed to do tasks automatically.
機器人是一種可以透過程式設計自動執行任務的機器。
Changsing's robotics club builds and programs robots for competitions.
彰興的機器人社團會組裝並寫程式操控機器人參加比賽。
V.

Music & Arts

音樂與藝術

口琴社是彰興的驕傲，副總統表揚的藝文社團——音樂從來不只是課外活動。

WORD 010
bandn.
樂隊
A band is a group of musicians who play instruments together.
樂隊是一群一起演奏樂器的音樂家組成的團體。
Changsing's band performs at every school assembly.
彰興的樂隊會在每次的全校集會上表演。
WORD 011
dancev.
跳舞
To dance means to move your body to the rhythm of music.
Dance（跳舞）是指隨著音樂的節奏擺動身體。
Students dance together during the school talent show.
學生會在學校才藝表演時一起跳舞。
WORD 012
thumb pianon.
拇指鋼琴
A thumb piano is a small instrument played by plucking metal keys with your thumbs.
拇指鋼琴是一種用拇指撥動金屬鍵演奏的小型樂器。
Music class lets students try playing the thumb piano.
音樂課會讓學生嘗試彈奏拇指鋼琴。
WORD 013
harmonican.
口琴
A harmonica is a small instrument you play by blowing and drawing air through it.
口琴是一種靠吹氣和吸氣來演奏的小型樂器。
Every Changsing student learns to play the harmonica in music class.
彰興每個學生都會在音樂課學吹口琴。
WORD 014
harmonica clubn.
口琴社
The harmonica club is a group of students who practice and perform harmonica music together.
口琴社是一群學生一起練習並演出口琴音樂的社團。
Changsing's harmonica club earned a Vice-President commendation for its performances.
彰興的口琴社曾以精彩表演獲得副總統的表揚。
WORD 015
decorationn.
裝飾
A decoration is something used to make a place look more beautiful.
裝飾是用來讓一個地方看起來更美觀的東西。
Students hang colorful decorations around the campus for festivals.
每逢節慶，學生會在校園裡掛上五顏六色的裝飾。
VI.

School Life

校園生活

家政課、童軍、藏書章——彰興校園裡藏著好多值得用英文說的事。

WORD 016
professional developmentn.
專業進修
Professional development means training that helps teachers improve their skills.
專業進修是指協助老師提升專業技能的培訓。
Changsing teachers attend professional development workshops every semester.
彰興的老師每學期都會參加專業進修工作坊。
WORD 017
home economics classn.
家政課
A home economics class teaches practical skills like cooking and sewing.
家政課教導像烹飪和縫紉這樣的生活技能。
Students learn to cook simple dishes in home economics class.
學生在家政課會學習烹煮簡單的料理。
WORD 018
bookplaten.
藏書章
A bookplate is a small label or stamp inside a book showing who it belongs to.
藏書章是貼或蓋在書本內頁、標示書本歸屬的小標籤。
Changsing's library gives students a bookplate to mark their favorite reads.
彰興的圖書館會給學生藏書章，標記他們喜愛的書。
WORD 019
scoutingn.
童軍活動
Scouting teaches young people outdoor skills, teamwork, and self-reliance.
童軍活動教導年輕人戶外技能、團隊合作與自立。
Changsing students join scouting activities to learn camping and first aid.
彰興的學生透過童軍活動學習露營和急救技能。
VII.

Everyday Words

日常詞彙

不一定在學校才會用到——但在彰興都有拍成影片。

WORD 020
chipn.
籌碼
A chip is a small token used to represent money in a game.
籌碼是遊戲中用來代表金錢的小型代幣。
Players use chips to place their bets in the game.
玩家用籌碼在遊戲中下注。
WORD 021
promotev.
推廣
To promote something means to help it become more popular or well known.
Promote（推廣）是指幫助某件事變得更受歡迎或更廣為人知。
Changsing's students help promote reading through campus events.
彰興的學生透過校園活動幫忙推廣閱讀風氣。
WORD 022
prayv.
祈福
To pray means to speak to a god or wish for something good to happen.
Pray（祈福）是指向神明祈求，或希望好事發生。
Students pray for good luck before their big exam.
學生在大考前會祈福，希望帶來好運。
WORD 023
puzzlen.
智力遊戲
A puzzle is a game or problem that tests your thinking skills.
智力遊戲是一種考驗思考能力的遊戲或問題。
Students solve puzzles together during break time.
學生下課時間會一起玩智力遊戲。
