Everyday School Life
校園日常 · 9 words
WORD 01
recess (n)
下課時間
After the bell rings for recess, most students go outside to play.
下課鈴聲響起後，大多數學生都在外面玩。
Recess is when kids can play outside, run around, and have fun.
下課時間是孩子們可以在外面玩耍、跑來跑去、並且玩得開心。
WORD 02
morning assembly
朝會
Morning assembly is a meeting that administrators, teachers, and students attend every morning at school.
朝會是行政人員、老師和學生每天早上要參加的聚會。
Morning assembly is a great time for staff and faculty members to share important announcements with the student body.
朝會是教職人員向學生宣布重要事情的好時機。
WORD 03
English class
英文課
The teacher is teaching the kids when to use 'a' and 'an' in English class.
老師在英文課教導學生何時使用a與an。
In English class, the students learn the rules of English.
在英文課中，學生們學習英文的規則。
WORD 04
EQ class
EQ 課程
The students attend EQ class.
同學們參加EQ的課程。
They learn about emotional intelligence in EQ class.
他們在EQ課程學習情緒商數的相關知識。
WORD 05
school lunch (n)
學校午餐
School lunch is eaten in the classroom in Taiwan.
台灣的學校午餐是在教室裡吃的。
School lunch is designed to be healthy.
學校午餐主旨在健康。
WORD 06
check-in
檢核
The students say English sentences for their check-in.
學生們說英語句子進行認證。
They get a stamp at the end of the check-in.
通過認證，他們會得一個戳印（蓋章）。
WORD 07
wash your hands
洗手
It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
確定在吃東西之前要用香皂和水來洗手。
WORD 08
professional development
專業進修
In Taiwan, all elementary schools have professional development on Wednesday afternoons.
台灣所有小學老師在週三下午有專業進修。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development.
老師在專業進修時學習新技能與教學策略。
WORD 09
kindergarten
幼稚園
Children play different games in kindergarten.
小朋友在幼兒園玩不同的遊戲。
In kindergarten, students learn basic skills.
學生在幼兒園學習基本的技能。
