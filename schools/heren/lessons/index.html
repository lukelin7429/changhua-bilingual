Ways to Learn

從校園生活學英文

Real school, real words.

每支影片、每份教材，都取材自和仁真實的校園日常。

45 Videos · 6 Themes
Course 01
Word of the Day
每日一字

45 bilingual videos filmed around campus, grouped into six themes — from recess and morning assembly to earthquake drills and the palm tree at the gate. Each word comes with two example sentences taken from the video.45 支雙語影片，全部在和仁校園實地拍攝，分為六大主題——從下課時間、朝會，到地震演練與校門口的棕櫚樹。每個單字都附兩句取自影片的例句。

External Resource Site
Course 02
Teacher Chris's Classroom
外師 Christopher Curry 資源站

The resource site of Christopher Curry — "Teacher Chris" — Heren's foreign English teacher, a University of Toronto graduate from Ontario, Canada. Teaching materials, an English Passport, and school activities from Halloween and baking to an AI-tools workshop and a class bike trip.和仁外師 Christopher Curry（Teacher Chris）的個人資源站——他來自加拿大安大略省，畢業於多倫多大學。站上有教學教材、English Passport，以及萬聖節、烘焙、AI 工具工作坊、單車之旅等校園活動紀錄。

Visit the Site ↗
Course 03
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers, taught by Sarah Thomas across 10 topics and 30 episodes — from greetings and roll call to exam instructions. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.給老師使用的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學，共 10 大主題、30 支影片——從問候點名到考試指令。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

Start Learning →
Course 04
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

"Broadcast English" — thirteen short episodes in which each office and section at Heren introduces, in English, the announcements it makes over the school PA system, from the curriculum section to the health office.「廣播英語」——13 支短片，由和仁各處室、各組用英語介紹平時透過校園廣播宣布的內容，從教務處教學組到學務處衛生組。
