Three Medals at the GreenMech World Finals

和仁勇奪世界機關王三項大獎
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Heren Elementary's "Sci-Tech and Creative Coding" team travelled to Bangkok, Thailand to represent Taiwan at the 2026 World GreenMech Contest — and came home with three medals, won with creativity, technology and teamwork on the international stage.

和仁國小「資科程創師生」臺灣代表隊遠赴泰國曼谷，參加 2026 世界機關王競賽，以創意、科技與團隊合作，在國際舞台上再創佳績，抱回三面獎牌。

In the R4M Basic division, the team "線在和力進擊隊" took first place in the world. In GMJR (programming), "Heren Phoenix" (和仁鳳王) finished second, and in the R4M Advanced division "特務King" took third — one gold, one silver and one bronze.

R4M 基礎組，「線在和力進擊隊」勇奪世界第一名；GMJR 程式小創客，「和仁鳳王（Heren Phoenix）」拿下世界第二名；R4M 進階組，「特務King」摘下世界第三名——一金、一銀、一銅。

These results came from the students' countless rounds of trying, fixing and trying again, and from the dedication of their coaching teachers: Su Kuo-Hsien, Hung Chuan-Fu, Ke Shih-Yu, Huang Tzu-Ying, Yang Shu-Fen and Cheng Kun-Hung.

這份榮耀，來自孩子們無數次的嘗試、修正與挑戰，也凝聚了指導老師群——蘇國賢、洪川富、柯詩彧、黃子瀅、楊淑芬、鄭昆泓老師——的專業與辛勤指導。

The school also thanks the county government, the township office, the parents' association and everyone whose support made the trip possible. From Heren to the world stage — congratulations to every student and teacher on the team. You make Heren proud!

更感謝縣府、鎮公所、家長會及各界一路以來的支持與鼓勵。從和仁出發，勇敢站上世界舞台——恭喜獲獎的師生們，你們是和仁的驕傲！
