A Safe, Happy Summer at Kindergarten

和仁幼兒園暑期托育，神隊友在這裡
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

When the long summer holiday arrives, Heren Elementary's affiliated kindergarten keeps its doors open. The summer care programme gives children a safe, happy summer full of chances to explore — with the teachers they already know, in the classrooms they already love.

暑假來了，彰化縣和美鎮和仁國小附設幼兒園的暑托課程也開始了。我們為孩子打造安全、快樂又充滿探索機會的暑假生活——由最熟悉的老師帶領，在最熟悉的環境裡學習。

The programme has four highlights. Life skills: children keep practising self-care and daily routines. Safe outdoor play: they run and burn off energy while staying safe. Indoor games for the body: activities that train hand-eye coordination and build the big muscles. Hands-on creativity: art, aesthetics and DIY making.

暑托課程有四大亮點。生活素養培育：自理能力與常規引導不間斷。戶外安全遊戲：盡情放電，同時兼顧安全。室內體能遊戲：訓練手眼協調、鍛鍊大肌肉。動手玩創意：美感藝術與手作 DIY。

Leaving your child with the teachers who know them best means a summer that stays regular, happy and full of learning.

把寶貝交給最熟悉的老師，讓孩子在規律的作息中，快樂地學習與成長。
