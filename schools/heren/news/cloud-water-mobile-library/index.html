The Cloud & Water Book Truck Comes to Heren

佛光山「雲水書坊」行動書車抵達和仁
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On 29 June, Fo Guang Shan's "Cloud and Water Mobile Library" book truck rolled into Heren Elementary, its shelves packed with books.

115 年 6 月 29 日，佛光山「雲水書坊」行動書車開進和仁國小，車上載著滿滿的圖書。

Children spent a sunny morning among the shelves, reading in the fresh air with the scent of books all around them.

和仁的孩子們在陽光滿溢、書香滿園的晨間時光裡，徜徉在一本本圖書之間。

Thank you to the Fo Guang Shan volunteers for reading alongside our students and starting the day with a full tank of reading energy.

感謝佛光山的志工群陪伴大家一起閱讀美好，為和仁啟動了滿滿閱讀能量的一天。
