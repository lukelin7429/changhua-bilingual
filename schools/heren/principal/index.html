I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Hu Su-Tzu trained in language education at National Taichung Teachers College and went on to earn a master's degree from the Department of Education at National Kaohsiung Normal University. She passed the Senior Civil Service Examination and completed the 151st elementary-school principal training programme at the National Academy for Educational Research.

胡素慈校長畢業於國立臺中師範學院語文教育學系，並取得國立高雄師範大學教育學系碩士學位；通過考試院公務人員高等考試，並結業於國家教育研究院第 151 期國民小學校長儲訓班。

Before returning to a school of her own, she spent years inside the Changhua County Education Department — as a staff officer, a school supervisor, and section head of both Social Education and Compulsory Education. That view of the whole county's schools now shapes how she leads Heren.

在接掌校務之前，胡校長長期服務於彰化縣政府教育處——歷任科員、督學，以及社會教育科、國民教育科科長。這份綜觀全縣教育的視野，如今融入她帶領和仁的每一個決定。

III.

Guiding Beliefs

辦學理念

One life moves another; one life changes another.

生命感動生命，生命改變生命。

有信心的孩子，方能有效學習；
有愛心的教師，方能發揮專業；
有願景的團隊，方能群眾齊力；
有支持的家長，方能併肩同行；
有活力的社區，方能共好共榮；
有接納、對話與尊重的學校，方能生命感動生命。
Principal Hu's educational creed, in six lines — each one a condition for the next. · 胡校長的教育信念，六個「有……方能……」，環環相扣。

"Education is a hope built drop by drop," she writes. Running a school takes the collective wisdom of many hands. Her promise is a simple one: to give it everything, to keep deepening the work, and to pursue excellence — seeing what each child needs to learn, uncovering what each child might become, and building the best environment for them to discover what is wonderful and attend to what is good, so they grow the ability to read and think, express and cooperate, create and make, and use technology well — with a healthy body and a feel for the arts to carry it.

「教育是點滴希望工程，」胡校長寫道，「學校經營需要『群人』發揮集體智慧。」她的承諾很簡單：全力以赴、持續深化、追求卓越——看見每個孩子的學習需要、發掘每個孩子的發展可能，營造最好的學習環境，讓每個孩子都能樂在學習：發現精彩（探究）、專注美好（表現），培養閱讀思考、表達合作、創造實作及善用科技的能力，並具備健康體能與藝術涵養，以培養未來競爭力為目標。
