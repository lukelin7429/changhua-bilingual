Welcome to Heren Elementary School · 歡迎來到和仁國小

Heren Elementary sits at the southern edge of Hemei Township. It opened its doors in September 2000, and from the 2015 school year has kept its enrolment deliberately capped — a calm, well-regarded neighbourhood school with the firm backing of the community around it.

和仁國小位於和美鎮南端，89 年 9 月設立招生，104 學年度起實施學生人數總量管制。校風優良穩健，深獲地方肯定與支持。

Our staff, our parents' association and our volunteer team work the same patch of ground together. We watch for what each child needs to learn, look for what each child might become, and try to build the best possible place for that to happen — a school where learning is a joy: discovering what is wonderful, and giving it your full attention.

全校師長、家長會、志工隊一起耕耘教育園地，發揮「群人」智慧，看見孩子的學習需要、發掘孩子的發展可能，營造最好的學習環境，讓學生樂在學習——發現精彩（探究）、專注美好（表現）。

Creative and cooperative, thoughtful and kind.
創意「和」群，閱讀「仁」人——培養優質的國際公民。
II.

A Campus That Greets You

每天迎接師生的校景

Two trees that everyone at Heren knows by heart.

兩種樹，和仁的每個人都記得。

Golden Blossom, Silver Palm
金黃黃槐，銀葉海棗

On both sides of the front gate, a stand of golden-shower trees flowers the whole year round — graceful, gold, and fluttering down to welcome students in each morning. At the roundabout in the front courtyard stands a Middle-Eastern date palm, planted with the help of the parents' association in the school's earliest years. Twenty-odd summers and winters later, it stands tall over the courtyard — a landmark view of Heren.

校門口兩側的黃槐樹群全年開花，樹姿優美、金黃翩翩，每日歡迎師生入校；前庭圓環處的中東海棗，是設校初年家長會協助種下的，陪伴師生二十年寒暑，如今已高拔挺立，是和仁重要的代表校景。

III.

Four Forces

課程地圖：四力整合

Reading, technology, the wider world, and working well together.

閱讀力、科創力、國際力、競合力——四力整合，一起前進。

Reading that Takes Flight
閱讀力 · 閱讀飛翔

We tie reading to real life — everyday language used to solve real problems, so that knowledge turns into ability. 推動閱讀材料與生活情境相聯結，透過生活化的語文學習解決問題，將知識轉化為能力。

Tech Know-How
科創力 · 資訊達人

Information technology, AI robotics and coding are a signature strength — a systematic path from first steps with a computer to AI and the Internet of Things. 以資訊科技、AI 機器人及程式教育為特色發展方向，由基本認識電腦到 AI 機器人物聯網課程，系統化規劃排課。

The Global Village
國際力 · 國際村

Our "Global Village" deepens multicultural and English education, with English readers and an English-rich learning environment for the whole school. 國際村扎根「國際力」，深化多元文化及英語教育，提供英語閱讀讀本及豐富的英語情境學習環境。

A Richer Heren
競合力 · 精采和仁

A friendly campus put into practice: character and life education, gender equality, block-building courses, food-and-farming and nutrition education, road safety, the environment, SH150 and off-campus learning. 落實友善校園、品格教育、生命教育、性別平等、積木課程、食農與營養教育、交通安全、環境教育、SH150 與校外教學。

IV.

Room to Learn, Room to Grow

豐富的教育量能

Spaces across campus, each built for a different kind of learning.

校園裡的每個空間，都為一種學習而準備。

A painted arcade and an aesthetics corridor. The Cook House food-and-aesthetics studio. The Book-思議 Library. Heren's own vegetable plot — "one mǔ of field." An AI English Resource Centre, a coding and computing base, a block-building classroom, and a central kitchen. Together they widen what this school is able to teach.

藝術彩繪川堂、美感廊道、Cook House 食育美學堂、Book 思議圖書館、和仁一畝田、AI 英資中心、資科程創基地、機冠王巢積木教室、中央廚房等設施，豐富學校的教育量能。

Between the Bells
下課時光

When the bell rings, Heren's children pour out onto the track, the courts and the shaded playground — a healthy body and an eye for beauty are part of the plan here, right alongside reading and coding.

下課鐘響，和仁的孩子們湧向跑道、球場與樹蔭下的遊戲場——健康體能與藝術涵養，和閱讀、程式一樣，都是課程的一部分。
