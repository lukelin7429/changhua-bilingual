Going Further進階學習 · 認識田徑

Have you ever heard of track and field? It's a sport that involves running, jumping, and throwing. It's a great way to stay active and healthy. Plus, it's a team sport, so you'll learn about teamwork. There are many different events in track and field. For example, there's the 100m Dash, where you run as fast as you can for 100m. There's also the long jump, where you jump as far as you can, and the shot put, where you throw a heavy ball as far as you can. Track and field is a fun and exciting sport for everyone to enjoy.

你有沒有聽過田徑？這是一項涵蓋跑步、跳躍和投擲的運動，也是保持活躍與健康的絕佳方式。此外，這也是一項團隊運動，因此你能從中學到團隊合作。田徑有許多不同的項目，例如 100 公尺衝刺、跳遠，以及鉛球。田徑是一項有趣又刺激的運動，適合每個人享受。
