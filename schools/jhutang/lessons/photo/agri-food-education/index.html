Agri-food Education

食農教育 · 走進稻田
← Photo Lessons

Our teachers and students visit a rice farm in Jhutang to harvest rice together — learning where our food comes from.

竹塘的師生一起走進稻田收割稻米，認識食物從何而來。竹塘是有名的「冠軍米故鄉」。

01

The teachers and students go to a rice farm.

老師和學生造訪稻田。

02

They work together to harvest the rice.

他們一起合作收割稻米。

03

The students learn about harvesting rice from teachers and farmers.

學生們從老師和農民那裡學習收割稻米的知識。

04

The teachers and students harvest the rice together.

老師和學生一起收割稻米。

05

The younger students observe the rice being harvested.

年幼的學生觀察稻米被收割的過程。

06

The teachers help the students harvest the rice.

老師幫助學生們收割稻米。

07

The combine harvester also harvests the rice.

聯合收割機也在收割稻米。

08

The students pay attention to a presentation about harvesting rice.

學生們聚精會神地聽一場關於收割稻米的介紹。
