Mushroom Rice Balls

菇米飯糰 · 食農動手做
← Photo Lessons

Students roll their own mushroom rice balls — and the mushrooms are grown right here in Jhutang.

學生們親手做香菇飯糰——而飯糰裡的香菇，正是竹塘在地生產的。

01

The students get together to prepare healthy food.

學生們聚在一起準備健康食物。

02

They lay rice, egg, and mushrooms on the seaweed paper.

他們把米飯、蛋和香菇鋪在海苔上。

03

They press it all together to make a mushroom rice ball.

壓緊之後，就成了一顆香菇飯糰。

04

The students pay attention as the teacher explains.

老師講解時，學生們專心聆聽。

05

They wrap each rice ball in plastic wrap.

他們用保鮮膜把每顆飯糰包起來。

06

The county magistrate gives a speech to the students.

縣長對學生們發表談話。

07

The mushrooms in the rice balls are grown right here in Jhutang.

飯糰裡的香菇，就是竹塘在地生產的。

08

Everyone makes the rice balls together.

大家一起動手做飯糰。

09

A student uses tongs to add the fillings.

學生用夾子夾取餡料。

10

The students work together in the kitchen.

學生們在廚房裡分工合作。

11

A guest gives a presentation about food education.

來賓進行一場食農教育簡報。

12

The teacher instructs the students step by step.

老師一步一步指導學生。

13

The teacher teaches the students all about rice.

老師教學生認識稻米的知識。

14

The rice balls are finished — time to enjoy!

飯糰完成了，開動囉！
