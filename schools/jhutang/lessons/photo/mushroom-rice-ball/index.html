Mushroom Rice Balls

菇米飯糰 · 食農動手做
← Photo Lessons

Students roll their own mushroom rice balls — and the mushrooms are grown right here in Jhutang.

學生們親手做香菇飯糰——而飯糰裡的香菇，正是竹塘在地生產的。

01

The students get together to prepare healthy food.

學生們聚在一起準備健康食物。

02

The students make rice balls with eggs and mushrooms as fillings.

學生們製作了以蛋和香菇為餡的飯糰。

03

They put rice, eggs, and mushrooms on seaweed paper.

他們將米飯、蛋和香菇放在海苔紙上。

04

The student uses tongs to put the ingredients onto the seaweed paper.

學生使用夾子將食材放到海苔紙上。

05

They fold the seaweed paper to make rice balls.

他們摺疊海苔紙做成飯糰。

06

The students work in the kitchen together to make the rice balls.

學生們一起在廚房裡合作製作飯糰。

07

The student pays attention to the presentation.

學生專心聽取介紹。

08

The county magistrate joins the teachers to make a presentation.

縣長加入老師一起做簡介。

09

The students wrap the rice balls in saran wrap.

學生們用保鮮膜包裹飯糰。

10

The teacher instructs the students on how to make rice balls.

老師指導學生如何製作飯糰。

11

The county magistrate makes a speech to the students and teachers.

縣長對學生和老師發表了演說。

12

The teacher teaches the students about rice.

老師教學生關於稻米的知識。

13

The mushrooms in the rice balls are produced in Jhutang.

飯糰裡的香菇是在竹塘生產的。

14

The students finish making the rice balls.

學生們完成了飯糰的製作。
