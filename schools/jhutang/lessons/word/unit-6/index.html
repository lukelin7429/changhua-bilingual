Word of the Day · 閱讀與圖書

📚 Reading & Library

每日一字 · 閱讀與圖書
← All Word of the Day

Word of the Day — Reading & Library. Watch each clip and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 閱讀與圖書。看影片，把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
reading
閱讀
Look at the students: they’re all reading quietly.
看看這群學生，他們閱讀時多麼安靜啊！
Reading often can help you improve your vocabulary and learn many new things.
閱讀經常可以讓你增加字彙並且學到很多新知。
Word 02
check out
借書
The students check out books from the library at recess.
同學們利用下課到圖書室借書。
After the librarian checks out the students' books, they can take them home to read.
向圖書館員辦理借閱手續後，同學們可以將書帶回家看。
Word 03
ebook (n)
電子書
The students use their tablets for ebooks in class.
學生們在課堂上用平板閱讀電子書。
It is very convenient for students to use ebooks in class.
上課中讓學生使用電子書閱讀非常便利。
Word 04
book (n)
書
The students take turns introducing a book in class.
同學們輪流在課堂上介紹一本書。
They have to tell the students about a book.
他們必須告訴其他學生一本書的內容。
Word 05
story (n)
故事
The students read a story in class.
學生們在課堂上閱讀一個故事。
They read the story out loud.
他們大聲唸出故事的內容。
Word 06
recite (v)
背誦
The students recite words and sentences in English.
同學們背誦英文單字與句子。
The teacher checks to make sure the students are reciting the words correctly.
老師檢查並確認學生們是否有正確背誦英文單字。
