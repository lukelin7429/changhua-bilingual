Word 01
recorder (n)
直笛
The recorder is the first musical instrument that many students learn.
直笛是很多學生學的第一項樂器。
You can play many songs on a recorder.
你可以用直笛吹奏很多歌曲。
Word 02
perform (v)
表演
The students wait in line to perform.
學生們正排隊等著要表演。
They play their drums and perform their dance in front of an audience.
他們在觀眾前表演打鼓與舞蹈。
Word 03
conductor (n)
指揮
The conductor guides the students in singing the national anthem.
指揮引導學生唱國歌。
The students follow the conductor to sing the national anthem.
同學們遵照指揮的引導唱國歌。
