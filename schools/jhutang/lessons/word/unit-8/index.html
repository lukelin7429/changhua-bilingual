Word of the Day — Science & Nature. Watch each clip and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 自然科學。看影片，把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
battery (n)
電池
Students learn about batteries in school.
同學們在學校學習電池的相關知識。
They make a closed circuit with a battery.
他們用電池做成閉合電路。
Word 02
dissolve (v)
溶解
The students dissolve a solute in a solvent.
同學們在溶劑中溶解溶質。
They dissolve a solute in a solvent to make a solution.
他們在溶劑中溶解溶質以便製作溶液。
Word 03
fish (n)
魚
The students make a fish in art class.
同學們在美術課製作魚的作品。
They use many different materials to make a fish.
他們使用不同的材料來製作魚的作品。
Word 04
height (n)
身高
The nurse is measuring the students' heights.
護理師正在量學生的身高。
The school measures students' heights to track their growth.
學校量學生的身高來追蹤他們的成長。
Word 05
measure (v)
測量
The students learn how to measure water in class.
學生們在上課中學習如何量水。
They use a measuring cup to measure water.
他們使用量杯來量水。
