Word of the Day · 整潔與健康

🧼 Clean & Healthy

每日一字 · 整潔與健康
← All Word of the Day

Word of the Day — Clean & Healthy. Watch each clip and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 整潔與健康。看影片，把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
wash your hands
洗手
It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
確定在吃東西之前要用香皂和水來洗手。
Word 02
wash (v)
洗
The students wash the kitchen utensils at the sink.
同學們在洗手槽洗廚房用具。
After lunch, the students work together to wash the dishes.
吃過午餐後，同學們一起洗碗。
Word 03
brush (v)
刷牙
The students brush their teeth after lunch.
同學們在吃過午餐後刷牙。
They brush their teeth in the classroom.
他們在教室刷牙。
Word 04
mouthwash (n)
漱口水
The teacher is giving her students mouthwash to gargle with.
這位老師給學生漱口水漱口。
Gargling with mouthwash helps keep your teeth healthy.
用漱口水漱口可以保持牙齒健康。
Word 05
wipe (v)
擦
The students wipe their desks clean.
學生們將他們的桌子擦乾淨。
They use a rag to wipe their desks.
他們用抹布來擦桌子。
Word 06
sweep (v)
掃
Every day, students sweep away fallen leaves on school grounds.
每天學生打掃校園的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.
台灣的學生打掃校園，保持校園的乾淨整齊。
Word 07
mop (v)
拖地
The students mop the classrooms and the bathrooms.
學生們在教室與廁所拖地。
Mopping the floors keeps the school clean.
拖地可以讓學校保持乾淨。
Word 08
water dispenser
飲水機
You can fill your water bottle at the water dispenser.
你可以在飲水機將水裝到水瓶。
The water dispenser has cold, warm, and hot water. "
飲水機有冷水、溫水與熱水。
Word 09
recycling (n)
回收
Recycling is a way to reuse materials like paper and plastic.
資源回收是將紙類和塑膠之類的材質再次回收使用。
Recycling can help take care of the planet.
資源回收有助於照顧我們的地球。
Word 10
nurse's office
保健室
The students go to the nurse's office to learn about health.
同學們到保健室學習與健康相關的知識。
The nurse gives the students a tour of the nurse's office.
學生們參觀保健室，護理師為他們做介紹。
Word 11
nap (v)
小睡
The students are all napping after lunch while the teacher works.
學生們在午餐吃飽後午休，老師則是在工作。
Napping after lunch will give you the energy you need to study during the afternoon.
午休可以讓你下午上課有足夠的精力。
Word 12
HIV/AIDS
愛滋病毒/愛滋病
Students go to the auditorium to learn about HIV/AIDS.
同學們到禮堂聽取有關HIV病毒與愛滋病的知識。
They learn about HIV/AIDS for health education.
學習HIV病毒與愛滋病相關知識是他們健康教育的一部份。
