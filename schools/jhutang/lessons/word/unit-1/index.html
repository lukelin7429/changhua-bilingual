Word of the Day · 運動體育

⚽ Sports & PE

每日一字 · 運動體育

Word of the Day — Sports & PE. Watch each clip and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 運動體育。看影片，把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
basketball (n)
籃球
Students love to play basketball during recess.
學生們喜歡在下課時打籃球。
A pick-up game is an on-the-spot game for basketball.
鬥牛就是現場組隊比賽的籃球。
Word 02
tee-ball (n)
樂樂棒球
In tee-ball, you hit the ball with a bat.
在玩樂樂棒時，您使用球棒擊球。
To score a point in tee-ball, the runner has to get to the home plate.
要在樂樂棒的比賽中得分，跑者必須到達本壘。
Word 03
softball (n)
壘球
The students play softball outside.
同學們在外面玩壘球。
The teacher shows the students how to play softball.
老師位同學們示範如何玩壘球。
Word 04
tchoukball (n)
巧固球
Tchoukball is a sport from Switzerland.
巧固球是一項來自瑞士的運動。
The students are practicing tchoukball.
學生們正在練習巧固球。
Word 05
relay race
接力賽
In the relay race, students pass their batons to the next runners.
在接力賽中，同學們把接力棒遞給下一棒。
The relay race is a very exciting event.
接力賽是一項非常刺激的比賽。
Word 06
shot put
鉛球
Shot put is a sport where people throw a heavy ball.
推鉛球是一種運動，選手必須將很重的球投擲出去。
The students are practicing shot put.
同學們正在練習推鉛球。
Word 07
jump rope
跳繩
Jumping rope is a great form of cardio.
跳繩是一項很好的有氧運動。
Once you get the hang of it, you can jump rope really fast!
一旦你掌握到跳繩的要領，你就可以跳得很快。
Word 08
Taekwondo (n)
跆拳道
The students are learning Taekwondo in PE.
學生們在體育課上學習跆拳道。
You learn to kick high in Taekwondo.
你學會在跆拳道踢得很高。
Word 09
skate (v)
溜冰
The students skate on the outdoor basketball court.
同學們在外面的籃球場上溜冰。
They practice skating by avoiding the cones.
他們練習溜冰時閃避三角錐。
Word 10
shoot (v)
投籃
The students are practicing shooting the basketball.
學生正在練習投籃。
They take turns shooting the ball.
他們輪流投籃。
Word 11
swing (v)
盪鞦韆
During recess, students like to swing.
下課時，學生們喜歡玩盪鞦韆。
You kick your legs up and down to swing.
盪鞦韆時，你的腳要上下踢動。
