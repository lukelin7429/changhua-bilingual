🎉 Festivals & Honors

Word of the Day — Festivals & Honors. Watch each clip and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 節慶與榮譽。看影片，把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
Christmas (n)
聖誕節
The students learn about Christmas in class.
同學們在課堂上學習有關聖誕節的知識。
They learn new Christmas vocabulary words.
他們學習聖誕節的相關詞彙。
Word 02
Christmas card
聖誕卡
The students make Christmas cards in class.
同學們在課堂上製作聖誕卡。
They decorate their Christmas cards with their crayons and markers.
他們用蠟筆與彩色筆來美化他們的聖誕卡。
Word 03
lantern (n)
燈籠
The students present their decorated lanterns to the class.
同學們對全班展示他們裝飾過的燈籠。
The lanterns have many different designs and colors.
他們的燈籠有很多不同的設計與顏色。
Word 04
celebrate (v)
慶祝
The students celebrate Christmas at their school.
同學們在學校慶祝聖誕節。
Students wear Santa hats to celebrate Christmas.
同學們戴聖誕老人的帽子來慶祝聖誕節。
Word 05
award (n)
獎
The students receive awards at the ceremony.
學生們在典禮時獲頒獎項。
They gather together to receive their awards.
他們聚在一起領獎。
Word 06
contest (n)
比賽
The students compete in a speaking contest at their school.
同學們參與學校辦的演說比賽。
Each student goes up and makes a speech for the contest.
比賽中，每位同學上台致詞。
