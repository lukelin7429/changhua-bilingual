Word of the Day · 午餐與在地食材

🍚 Lunch & Local Food

每日一字 · 午餐與在地食材
← All Word of the Day

Word of the Day — Lunch & Local Food. Watch each clip and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 午餐與在地食材。看影片，把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
school lunch
營養午餐
School lunch is eaten in the classroom in Taiwan.
學校午餐在教室享用。
School lunch is designed to be healthy.
學校午餐的菜色設計是符合身體健康的。
Word 02
serve (v)
打菜
The students serve lunch to their classmates.
學生為同學打菜。
The task of serving changes every week.
打菜的工作每週輪替一次。
Word 03
food cart
餐車
At lunch time, a food cart loaded with various dishes is brought to the classroom.
午餐時間，裝滿不同菜色的餐車被推到教室。
Some students are responsible for serving their classmates food from the food cart. ㄧ
些學生負責將餐車上的食物分配給同學。
Word 04
lunch (n)
午餐
The students eat lunch in the classroom.
同學們在教室吃午餐。
They always eat lunch at their desk.
他們總是在自己的書桌上吃午餐。
Word 05
rice (n)
米
The students learn about hanging rice upside down after harvest.
同學們學習在收割後將稻米倒吊的知識。
The teacher explains the benefits of hanging rice upside down after harvest.
老師為學生們解說收割後將稻米倒吊的好處有哪些。
Word 06
sticky rice ball
湯圓
The students make sticky rice balls for the winter solstice.
同學們為冬至搓湯圓。
Every year, sticky rice balls are made during the winter solstice.
每年大家都會在冬至的時候煮湯圓。
