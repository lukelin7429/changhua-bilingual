Word of the Day — Campus Life. Watch each clip and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 校園生活。看影片，把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
recess (n)
下課時間
After the bell rings for recess, most students go outside to play.
下課鈴聲響起後，大多數學生都在外面玩。
Recess is when kids can play outside, run around, and have fun.
下課時間是孩子們可以在外面玩耍、跑來跑去、玩得開心的時候。
Word 02
morning assembly
朝會
The students gather for the morning assembly.
學生們聚集參加朝會。
The flag is raised during the morning assembly.
在朝會時國旗會升上去。
Word 03
class duty
班級值日
The students have different class duties.
學生們擔任班級值日時有不同的工作職責。
They share these class duties to help maintain the school.
他們分擔不同的班級工作來協助學校運作。
Word 04
parking lot
停車場
Cars are parked in the school parking lot.
車子停在學校的停車場。
People park their cars in the school parking lot.
人們把他們的車子停在學校停車場。
Word 05
window (n)
窗戶
The students wipe the windows clean.
同學們把窗戶擦乾淨。
After cleaning the windows, they also clean the window guards.
擦完窗戶後，他們也會擦窗戶的護欄。
Word 06
drawer (n)
抽屜
The students clean out their desk drawers.
學生們清理他們書桌的抽屜。
After cleaning out their drawers, they organize their items.
清後抽屜後，他們把東西整理好。
Word 07
leave (v)
離開
The students gather to leave school.
同學們集合要離校了。
They line up to leave school.
他們排隊離校。
Word 08
watch (v)
看
The students watch videos in class.
學生們在課堂上看影片。
They watch videos to learn new vocabulary words.
他們看影片來學習新的英文字彙。
