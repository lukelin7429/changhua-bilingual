Word of the Day — Classroom & Subjects. Watch each clip and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 課堂與學科。看影片，把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
Chinese class
國語課
The students are in Chinese class.
同學們正在上國語課。
They learn Chinese idioms in Chinese class.
他們在國語課學習中文的成語。
Word 02
computer class
電腦課
The students are in the computer class.
學生們正在上電腦課。
They are studying Chinese in the computer class.
他們在電腦課中學習國語。
Word 03
exam (n)
考試
The students are taking an exam.
同學們正在考試。
They are working hard on their exam.
考試中他們很努力地作答。
Word 04
lecture (n)
演講
The students attend a lecture at their school.
學生們參加一場在學校舉辦的講座。
They are taking notes during the lecture.
他們在講座中寫筆記。
Word 05
color (v)
著色
The students color different images of Christmas.
同學們為聖誕節相關的圖畫著色。
They use different markers to color their drawings.
他們用不同顏色的麥克筆幫圖畫著色。
Word 06
type (v)
打字
The students type on the computer.
學生們在電腦上打字。
The students type using a keyboard.
學生們用鍵盤打字。
Word 07
eraser (n)
板擦
The students use erasers to erase the blackboards.
學生們用板擦擦黑板。
They hit the erasers with a stick to clean them.
他們用棍子打板擦來清板擦。
Word 08
professional development
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都有教師專業訓練課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
在專業訓練課程中，老師可以學到新的教學技巧與策略。
