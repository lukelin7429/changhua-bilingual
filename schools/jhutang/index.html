I.

Welcome

歡迎來到竹塘國小

A hundred-year school in the heart of Taiwan's "champion rice" township.

一所坐落於「冠軍米故鄉」的百年老校。

Founded in 1920, Jhutang Elementary has grown alongside the farming town of Zhutang for over a century. From rice fields to a central kitchen that has served the whole township for years, learning here is rooted in real life.

竹塘國小創校於 1920 年，與竹塘這座農業小鎮一起走過百年。從金黃稻田到供應全鄉多校的中央廚房，這裡的學習，扎根於真實的生活。

Under the leadership of Principal Zhou Xian-zheng, Jhutang weaves English into everyday school life — through a bilingual website, Word of the Day videos, photo lessons, and a reward system that makes learning fun.

在周憲徵校長的帶領下，竹塘把英語融入每天的校園生活——透過雙語網站、每日一字影片、看圖學英文，以及讓學習變有趣的獎勵制度。
