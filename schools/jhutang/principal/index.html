I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Today, we're talking about Principal Zhou, a dedicated teacher from Changhua. His parents taught him to keep trying no matter what. When Principal Zhou was young, he met a teacher, Mr. Liao, who changed his life and helped him a lot with his studies.

周憲徵校長是一位來自彰化的敬業教師。他的父母教導他無論如何都要堅持不懈；年輕時遇到改變他生命的廖老師，在學業上給了他許多幫助。

For many years, Principal Zhou worked hard in schools to improve the educational experience for kids. Now, as a principal, he aims to transform his school into a nurturing environment where every student can grow and learn effectively.

多年來，他在校園裡辛勤耕耘，只為改善孩子們的學習。如今身為校長，他期盼把學校打造成一個能讓每位學生全面成長、有效學習的溫暖環境。

"Keep trying, no matter what — and never stop learning together."
無論如何都要堅持不懈，並且持續一起學習。
