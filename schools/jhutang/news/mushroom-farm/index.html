Growing Space Mushrooms

食農教育 · 太空菇包種植與採收
← All News

Food Education · 食農 · Dec 12, 2024 · 2024 年 12 月

As part of a food-and-farming activity, fifth-graders used special 'space mushroom' bags to grow mushrooms at school. First they learned how mushrooms grow, spotting the white mycelium threads inside the bags.

在食農教育活動中，五年級學生用「太空菇包」在學校種菇。他們先認識菇類的生長原理，觀察菇包裡白色的菌絲。

Every day they checked the temperature and moisture and recorded the changes. After a few days, the mushrooms grew and the students harvested them by themselves — proud to grow their own food.

他們每天控制溫濕度、記錄生長變化；幾天後菇長成了，學生親手採收自己種的菇，滿是成就感。
