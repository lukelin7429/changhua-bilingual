Animal Protection Talk

動物保育生命教育講座
← All News

Life Education · 生命教育 · Jan 8, 2025 · 2025 年 1 月 8 日

Mr. Cha, an animal police officer, travelled to Jhutang to give a special talk about animal protection. He shared why caring for animals matters and what it really means to love them.

動物警察小查老師遠道而來，為竹塘的孩子帶來一場動物保育講座，分享為什麼要珍惜動物，以及愛動物的真正意義。

He reminded the students that loving animals means protecting them. The children learned how to care for animals and to be kind to all living things.

他提醒學生：保護動物，才是愛動物的表現。孩子們學會如何照顧動物、善待每一個生命。
