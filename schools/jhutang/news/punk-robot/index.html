DIY Punk Robots

二林高中木工手作 · 朋克機器人
← All News

Maker · 手作課程 · Oct 22, 2024 · 2024 年 10 月 22 日

Students went on a field trip to Erlin Senior High School and joined a woodcraft class to make punk robot keychains. The teacher first showed them some robot designs and the punk-robot style.

學生到二林高中校外教學，參加木工手作課，製作朋克機器人鑰匙圈。老師先介紹各種機器人設計與朋克風格。

With wood, screws, and metal parts, they used tools to cut and assemble their robots, then painted them and added cool decorations. At the end they proudly showed their creations to their friends.

他們用木板、螺絲與金屬零件，動手裁切、組裝，再上色加裝飾。完成後，孩子們驕傲地向同學分享自己的作品。
