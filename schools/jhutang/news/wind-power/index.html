Building Wind Turbines

綠能風力發電機模型製作
← All News

Science · 綠能 · Feb 17, 2025 · 2025 年 2 月 17 日

Students learned about wind energy and how it makes electricity. They were given parts like blades, motors, and stands, and then built their own wind turbines, making sure each structure was sturdy.

學生認識風力發電與電能轉換的原理，拿到葉片、馬達、支架等零件後，動手組裝出結構穩固的風力發電機。

After building, they used an electric fan to test the turbines and checked whether their models could really make power. Some students even painted their blades or wired up LED lights — turning wind into light.

組裝完成後，他們用電扇模擬風力測試，觀察模型能不能真的發電；部分學生還彩繪葉片、焊上 LED 燈，讓風力點亮燈光。
