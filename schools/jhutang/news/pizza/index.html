Mushroom Pizza Time

雙語課程 · 洋菇 Pizza 製作
← All News

Bilingual · 雙語課程 · Nov 25, 2024 · 2024 年 11 月 25 日

In a bilingual cooking class, students made mushroom pizza while learning English along the way. They learned the English names of ingredients like mushroom, cheese, and flour, then made the dough and talked about how it rises.

在雙語烹飪課，學生邊做洋菇 Pizza 邊學英文：先學會 mushroom、cheese、flour 等食材的英文，再動手揉麵、了解發酵過程。

They arranged the toppings, saying each name in English, watched the pizza bake, and finally tasted it — describing the flavor in English. A tasty, hands-on bilingual lesson.

他們一邊用英語說出配料名稱、一邊鋪料，看著 Pizza 在烤箱裡完成，最後品嚐並用英文描述口感——一堂美味又動手的雙語課。
