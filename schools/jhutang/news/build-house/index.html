Building a House

原斗國中小職業探索 · 蓋房子囉
← All News

Career · 職業探索 · Nov 5, 2024 · 2024 年 11 月 5 日

Students visited a job-exploration class run with Yuandou Junior High and Elementary, where they got to build a house. Wearing helmets, they learned how to mix and spread cement and discovered that every side of a brick has its own name.

學生參加與原斗國中小合辦的職業探索課程，體驗蓋房子。戴上安全帽，他們學習攪拌與塗抹水泥，還發現原來磚塊的每一面都有自己的名字。

They built their own small walls and tried different bricklaying methods. Even when the cement dropped a little, the children still felt happy — it was an exciting first experience, and they hope to do it again.

他們動手砌出自己的小牆、嘗試不同的砌法。即使水泥掉了一點，孩子們依然開心，因為這是新鮮又難忘的初體驗，希望下次還能再玩一次。
