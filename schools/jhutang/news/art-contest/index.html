Sharing Messages Through Art

政令宣導藝文比賽
← All News

Student Creative · 學生創作 · Nov 22, 2024 · 2024 年 11 月 22 日

Students joined an art contest in the activity center, combining creativity with social responsibility. The theme was about sharing good messages with the community.

學生在活動中心參加政令宣導藝文比賽，將藝術與社會責任結合，主題是把正向的訊息傳達給大家。

Some students entered calligraphy, writing beautiful Chinese characters; others created drawings full of color. The works were displayed, judged, and the winners were celebrated.

有人以書法揮毫、展現字體之美，有人用繪畫呈現繽紛創意。作品公開展示、由評審評分，並表揚得獎同學。
