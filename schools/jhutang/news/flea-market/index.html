School Flea Market

全校跳蚤市場
← All News

Community · 愛心環保 · Jan 16, 2025 · 2025 年 1 月 16 日

The whole school joined a flea market in the activity center. Students brought second-hand items from home, set up their own booths, made price tags, and decorated their tables.

全校在活動中心舉辦跳蚤市場。學生從家裡帶來二手物品，佈置自己的攤位、設計標價牌，把攤子裝飾得有模有樣。

During the market, students and teachers bought and sold things, practicing how to talk, trade, and give change. Afterwards, the children counted the money and donated it to people in need — learning, sharing, and doing good all at once.

市集開始後，師生一起買賣，練習溝通、交易與找零。活動結束後，孩子們統計所得，把義賣金額捐給弱勢團體——在學習中也學會分享與行善。
