Science · 綠能

Building Wind Turbines綠能風力發電機模型

Feb 17, 2025 · 2025 年 2 月

Students learned how wind makes electricity, then built their own turbines with blades, motors, and stands — and tested them with an electric fan.

學生認識風力如何發電，動手組裝葉片、馬達與支架做出風力發電機，再用電扇測試發電效果。

Community · 愛心環保

School Flea Market全校跳蚤市場

Jan 16, 2025 · 2025 年 1 月

Students brought second-hand items, set up their own booths, and practiced buying and selling. The money they raised was donated to people in need.

學生帶來二手物品擺攤，練習買賣與溝通，最後把義賣所得捐給需要幫助的人。

Life Education · 生命教育

Animal Protection Talk動物保育生命教育講座

Jan 8, 2025 · 2025 年 1 月 8 日

Mr. Cha, an animal police officer, visited Jhutang to share why loving animals means protecting them — and how to be kind to all living things.

動物警察小查老師到校宣導：愛動物，就是要保護動物，並學習善待每一個生命。

Festival · 冬至

Making Tangyuan for Winter Solstice冬至搓湯圓

Dec 19, 2024 · 2024 年 12 月 19 日

In the school hall, students learned how tangyuan stand for family and togetherness — then rolled their own with the principal for the Winter Solstice.

在活動中心禮堂，學生認識湯圓象徵團圓，並和校長一起搓湯圓，體驗冬至傳統。

Food Education · 食農

Christmas Cookie Time手作耶誕餅乾

Dec 18, 2024 · 2024 年 12 月

In the school kitchen, students mixed dough, shaped it with cookie cutters, decorated with icing, and baked their own Christmas cookies.

在學校廚房，學生和麵、用模具塑形、擠糖霜裝飾，烤出自己的耶誕餅乾。

Food Education · 食農

Growing Space Mushrooms太空菇包種植與採收

Dec 2024 · 2024 年 12 月

Using special ‘space mushroom’ bags, fifth-graders watched the white mycelium grow, checked temperature and moisture daily, and harvested mushrooms they grew themselves.

五年級用太空菇包種菇，觀察白色菌絲、每天記錄溫濕度，最後親手採收自己種的菇。

Achievement · 語文競賽

Language Contest Week校內語文競賽週

Dec 9-13, 2024 · 2024 年 12 月

A week of writing, Mandarin and Taiwanese reading, speeches, and word sound-and-shape — students showed off their language skills in the library.

為期一週的作文、國語與閩南語朗讀、演說、字音字形比賽，學生在圖書館大展語文實力。

Bilingual · 雙語課程

Mushroom Pizza Time雙語洋菇 Pizza 製作

Nov 25, 2024 · 2024 年 11 月 25 日

In a bilingual cooking class, students learned the English names of mushroom, cheese, and flour, made the dough, added toppings, and described the taste in English.

在雙語烹飪課，學生學會 mushroom、cheese、flour 等食材英文，動手揉麵、鋪料，再用英文描述口感。

Student Creative · 學生創作

Sharing Messages Through Art政令宣導藝文比賽

Nov 22, 2024 · 2024 年 11 月 22 日

Students entered a calligraphy and drawing contest, using beautiful Chinese characters and colorful pictures to share good messages with the school.

學生參加書法與繪畫比賽，用優美的字體與繽紛的圖畫，傳達政令宣導的好訊息。

Career · 職業探索

Building a House職業探索－蓋房子囉

Nov 5, 2024 · 2024 年 11 月 5 日

Wearing helmets, students mixed and spread cement, learned the names of brick sides, and built their own small walls — a hands-on taste of construction work.

戴上安全帽，學生學習攪拌與塗抹水泥、認識磚塊各面名稱，動手砌出自己的小牆，體驗營建工作。
