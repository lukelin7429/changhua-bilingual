Christmas Cookie Time

食農教育 · 手作耶誕餅乾
← All News

Food Education · 食農 · Dec 18, 2024 · 2024 年 12 月 18 日

In the school kitchen, students joined a fun food-and-farming class to make Christmas cookies. First they learned about the ingredients — flour and eggs — then mixed the dough and shaped it with their hands.

在學校廚房，學生參加食農教育課程做耶誕餅乾。先認識麵粉、雞蛋等食材，再學習和麵與塑形的基本技巧。

They used cookie cutters and decorated the cookies with icing before baking them in the oven. At the end, everyone tasted their own cookies and shared what they had made — a sweet and happy day.

他們用模具壓出造型、擠上糖霜，再送進烤箱。最後品嚐自己做的餅乾、交流心得，度過甜蜜又開心的一天。
