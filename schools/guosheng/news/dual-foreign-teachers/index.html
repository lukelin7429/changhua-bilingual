Two Foreign Teachers, One Global Classroom

雙外師接力上課，讓英語學習走向世界！

This week, foreign teacher Andrew joined Priya in the classroom, and the two teachers took turns leading lessons — giving the children an even richer range of English learning experiences.

這週的英語課程中，除了外師 Priya 持續陪伴孩子學習，外師 Andrew 也一同加入教學行列，透過兩位外師交替授課，帶給孩子更多元的英語學習經驗。

In class, Teacher Andrew took Japan as his theme — from the flag and geography to food and culture — guiding the children to discover a different country. They practiced not only vocabulary and sentence patterns, but also the courage to speak up through interaction, Q&A, and games.

課堂上，Andrew老師以日本為主題，從國旗、地理位置、飲食到文化特色，引導孩子認識不同國家。孩子們不只是學習英語單字與句型，也在互動、問答及遊戲中，練習勇敢開口表達。

Different teachers bring different accents, teaching styles, and cultural backgrounds. Learning from both in turn helps children grow comfortable with real, varied English communication, and broadens their international perspective — understanding that the world holds many different ways of life.

不同外師有不同的口音、教學方式與文化背景。透過交替學習，孩子能逐漸習慣真實而多元的英語溝通情境，也能拓展國際視野，理解世界上有許多不同的生活方式與文化特色。

"We hope English is more than a subject in a textbook — it's a key that lets children discover the world, and make friends with it."
我們希望英語不只是課本裡的科目，而是一把帶領孩子認識世界、與世界交朋友的鑰匙。

Thank you to foreign teachers Priya and Andrew for preparing these lessons with such care, and to our own teachers for their steady support — helping children build confidence and speak boldly in a relaxed, lively learning environment, so international education blends naturally into every lesson.

感謝外師 Priya 與 Andrew 用心準備課程，也感謝校內老師的協同陪伴，讓孩子在輕鬆、活潑的學習環境中，累積自信、勇敢開口，讓國際教育自然融入每一次的學習。
