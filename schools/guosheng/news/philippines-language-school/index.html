Arrived Safely in Clark, Philippines

順利抵達菲律賓克拉克～準備為期兩週的語言學校學習！

This year we've moved from Cebu to Clark — home to a former U.S. military base, the whole area feels like an oversized version of a quiet garden town. It doesn't have the bustle and convenience of a tourist city like Cebu, but what it does have is an environment that's genuinely built for studying.

今年從宿霧轉戰克拉克，這處美軍基地所在處，整體就是一處放大版的中興新村，沒有宿霧觀光城市那麼熱鬧方便，但有的是完全適合讀書的環境。

I think that after two weeks of language learning here, the children's English should improve quite a bit. Beyond that, I also hope they learn to take care of themselves and get along with others in a group living setting.

我想，小朋友在這裡經過2週的語言學習，除了英文能力應該可以精進不少，我也希望孩子是能學會生活自理、與人互動的團體生活。

And since this is a rare chance to travel abroad, I hope the children also learn how to navigate immigration and customs on their own. As always, I hope they keep an open mind — what you truly learn is what stays with you!

再者，難得出國一趟了，也希望孩子們能夠學到如何出境通關的能力，一樣，希望小朋友們放開心胸，學到的才是你的！
