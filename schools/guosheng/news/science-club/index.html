Play Your Way to Science

玩出科學力，探索無限可能！

This week Guosheng Elementary launched its ever-popular Science Play Club — through a series of hands-on, experience-first activities, letting children "learn by playing, learn by doing," and discover the wonders of natural science step by step.

本週國聖國小開辦了深受孩子喜愛的科學遊戲社團，透過一系列動手操作、親身體驗的活動，讓孩子在「玩中學、做中學」，一步步發現自然科學的奧妙。

From hand-launched gliders, air-and-pressure experiments, and balance-and-center-of-gravity challenges to playful chemistry, observation, and hands-on activities — every activity had children thinking, trying, and adjusting for themselves, then finding the answer through what they observed. In this classroom, it's not just about "knowing" — it's about learning to observe, question, experiment, and verify, building real problem-solving skills.

從手擲滑翔機飛行、空氣與壓力實驗、平衡與重心挑戰，到趣味化學、觀察與操作，每一項活動都讓孩子親自思考、嘗試、修正，再從現象中找出答案。課堂裡不只是「知道」，更重要的是學會觀察、提問、實驗與驗證，培養解決問題的能力。

Watching the children focus intently, discuss enthusiastically, and light up with excitement when an experiment finally works, we believe science isn't just knowledge from a textbook — it's a world waiting to be discovered, all around us in everyday life. Research also shows that weaving play and inquiry into science teaching helps boost students' motivation, engagement, and attitude toward science.

看著孩子們專注操作、熱烈討論，以及成功完成實驗時興奮的笑容，我們相信，科學並不是課本上的知識，而是生活中處處可見、等待探索的世界。研究也指出，將遊戲與探究活動融入科學教學，有助於提升學生的學習動機、參與度與科學態度。

"We hope every child holds onto this curiosity — exploring boldly, discovering joyfully, and growing into a little scientist of the future."
期待每一位孩子都能保持這份好奇心，勇敢探索、樂於發現，成為未來的小小科學家！

The club teacher carefully designed every activity to be both fun and grounded in real scientific principles, letting children build knowledge through laughter and confidence through hands-on making.

社團老師精心設計每一項有趣又富含科學原理的活動，讓孩子在歡笑中累積知識，在實作中建立自信。
