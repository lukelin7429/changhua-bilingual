I.

Welcome

歡迎來到國聖國小

A pillar of educational innovation, a sanctuary of learning in nature's embrace.

教育創新的重要據點，也是一處被自然環抱的學習淨土。

Located in the vibrant and culturally rich Changhua City, Guosheng Elementary stands as a pillar of educational innovation and a sanctuary of learning amidst nature's embrace. Our campus, a lush haven of diverse flora, offers an ideal environment for nurturing young minds.

國聖國小座落於文化底蘊深厚、充滿活力的彰化市，是教育創新的重要據點，也是一處被自然環抱的學習淨土。校園裡花木扶疏、綠意盎然，為孩子的成長提供了理想的環境。

At Guosheng, we are more than a school; we are a dynamic learning community where environmental consciousness, health, diverse skill development, and quality learning come together to shape the leaders of tomorrow.

在國聖，我們不只是一所學校，更是一個充滿活力的學習社群——環境意識、健康促進、多元能力發展與優質學習在這裡匯聚，共同型塑明日的領導者。

A campus in full bloom, built for childhood
花木扶疏、專為童年而生的校園

Golden trees line the running track, and a colourful playground waits under the shade — Guosheng's grounds are a lush haven of diverse flora, ideal for nurturing young minds.

黃花風鈴木沿著跑道盛開，色彩繽紛的遊戲場就在樹蔭下等著孩子——國聖的校園花木扶疏，是滋養孩子成長的理想天地。

II.

Our Vision

我們的願景：培育明日的創新者

Healthy and happy, brave and innovative, respectful and grateful.

健康快樂、勇敢創新、知禮感恩——國聖孩子的成長核心。

At Guosheng, our mission is to cultivate students who are not only 'healthy and happy' but also 'brave and innovative', and 'respectful and grateful'. We believe in fostering these core values to prepare our students for the challenges of the future.

在國聖，我們的使命是培養「健康快樂」、「勇敢創新」，同時也「知禮感恩」的孩子。我們深信唯有紮根這些核心價值，才能讓孩子做好面對未來挑戰的準備。

Our curriculum is designed with a keen focus on 'environmental education', empowering students to become conscientious global citizens. Supporting this, our sub-themes of 'food and agriculture education', 'reading education', and 'aesthetic education' ensure a well-rounded developmental journey.

學校課程以「環境教育」為核心主軸，培養學生成為具有責任感的世界公民；並以「食農教育」、「閱讀教育」、「美感教育」三大子主題相輔相成，確保孩子獲得均衡而全面的成長歷程。

Environmental Education
環境教育

The core of our curriculum — empowering students to become conscientious, responsible global citizens.

Food & Agriculture Education
食農教育

Hands-on learning that connects students to where their food comes from and the land that grows it.

Reading Education
閱讀教育

Building a lifelong love of books and inquiry, one story at a time.

Aesthetic Education
美感教育

Cultivating an eye and an ear for beauty, in art, in music, and in everyday life.

III.

A Record of Excellence

卓越的紀錄與成就

Health, character, and achievement — recognized near and far.

健康、品格與成就，獲得各界肯定。

Guosheng Elementary is synonymous with excellence. We have a rich history of accolades and achievements that stand testament to our commitment to quality education. Our efforts in health promotion have been internationally recognized with prestigious awards. Our initiatives in introducing foreign English teachers and deep engagement in character and life education set us apart. The diverse clubs and activities we offer provide ample opportunities for our students to explore and excel in various fields.

國聖國小向來與「卓越」二字劃上等號。我們累積了豐富的獲獎紀錄，見證學校對優質教育的長期投入。學校在健康促進方面的努力，屢獲國際級獎項肯定；引進外籍英語教師的計畫，以及深耕品格教育與生命教育，更是國聖的一大特色。多元的社團與活動，也為孩子提供了充分探索、發揮所長的舞台。

Our achievements are numerous: from topping national rankings in vision care to winning awards in music competitions and science exhibitions. These accolades not only highlight our academic prowess but also our dedication to fostering a well-rounded education.

國聖的成就不勝枚舉：從視力保健全國排名名列前茅，到音樂比賽與科展屢獲佳績——這些榮譽不僅展現了學校在學業上的實力，也彰顯了我們對全人教育的堅持。

"Our achievements highlight not only our academic prowess, but our dedication to a well-rounded education."
這些榮譽不僅展現學業實力，更彰顯我們對全人教育的堅持。
IV.

A Community of Excellence

卓越的社群

At Guosheng, education is a collaborative effort. Our success is built on the strong foundation of cooperation between parents, teachers, and students. We are a community that values and nurtures the individual strengths and talents of each student. Our teachers, recognized as some of the best in Changhua County, are dedicated to providing an enriching and supportive learning environment.

在國聖，教育是眾人齊心協力的成果。我們的成功，奠基於家長、教師與學生三方緊密合作的堅實基礎。這是一個珍視並用心栽培每個孩子獨特天賦的學習社群。學校的老師更是獲彰化縣肯定的優秀團隊，致力為孩子打造豐富且充滿支持的學習環境。

"Join Our Journey — at Guosheng, we are not just educating the next generation; we are shaping the future, one student at a time."
加入我們的旅程——在國聖，我們不只是在教育下一代，更是在一步一腳印地型塑未來。
