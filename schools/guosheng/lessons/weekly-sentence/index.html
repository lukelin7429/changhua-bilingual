II.

Daily English Conversation

Daily English Conversation · S.Y. 2025-2026 Second Semester
March
三月 · 校園清潔
Week 1
Put the trash in the bin.
把垃圾放進垃圾桶。
trash bin
Week 2
Let's throw away the trash.
我們把垃圾丟掉吧。
throw away trash
Week 3
Let's sort the trash.
我們來做垃圾分類吧。
sort trash
April
四月 · 教室需求
Week 1
Can I turn on the AC / fan?
我可以開冷氣或電風扇嗎？
turn on AC fan
Week 2
Can I open the window?
我可以開窗戶嗎？
open window
Week 3
Can I drink some water?
我可以喝一點水嗎？
drink water
May
五月 · 平板與求助
Week 1
Can I use the iPad?
我可以使用 iPad 嗎？
use iPad
Week 2
I can't log in.
我無法登入。
can't log in
Week 3
Can you help me with this?
你可以幫我看一下這個嗎？
help this
June
六月 · 暑假前後
Week 1
I'm excited for vacation.
我很期待放假。
excited vacation
Week 2
See you next school year.
下學年見。
see you school year
Week 3
I will help at home.
我會在家幫忙。
help home
Week 4
I will learn something new.
我會學新的東西。
learn something new
Listen once. Repeat twice. Try it with a friend.
先聽一次，跟讀兩次，再和同學互相練習。
