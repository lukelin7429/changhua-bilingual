II.

Arts & Performance

藝術與表演

跳舞、合唱、打鼓、太鼓、魔術、水彩——國聖孩子的表演與創作英文。

WORD 001
dancev.
跳舞
The students dance to the music.
學生們跟隨著音樂節奏跳舞。
The students dance together in class.
學生們在課堂上一起舞蹈。
WORD 002
choirn.
合唱團
The students practice singing in the choir.
合唱團的同學們練習唱歌。
The teacher plays the piano for the choir.
這位老師為合唱團彈鋼琴伴奏。
WORD 003
drumn.
鼓
The drum is a common instrument in Taiwan.
鼓是台灣常見的樂器。
The students are practicing playing the drums.
學生們正在練習打鼓。
WORD 004
Taiko drumn.
太鼓
The students practice the Taiko drum.
學生們練習打太鼓。
They play the Taiko drum in unison.
他們打太鼓時動作整齊劃一。
WORD 005
magicn.
魔術
The magician performs magic at the school.
魔術師在學校表演魔術。
He uses many different props to perform magic.
他使用很多道具來表演魔術。
WORD 006
watercolorn.
水彩
In art class, the students are painting with watercolor.
學生們在美術課用水彩畫畫。
To use watercolor, you need a brush, a palette and water.
用水彩繪畫時，你需要畫筆、調色盤還有水。
III.

PE, Nature & Safety

體育、自然與安全

踢足球、上游泳課、到戶外認識自然、地震演習——國聖孩子動起來、也保護自己的英文。

WORD 007
soccern.
足球
Soccer is a very popular sport.
足球是很受歡迎的運動。
The students are having a lot of fun playing soccer.
同學們正享受踢足球的樂趣。
WORD 008
swimming classn.
游泳課
The students learn how to swim in swimming class.
同學們在游泳課時學習如何游泳。
They learn the basics of swimming in swimming class.
他們在游泳課時學習游泳的基本動作。
WORD 009
naturen.
自然
The students go out and enjoy nature.
同學們到戶外享受大自然。
They learn about nature by going outdoors.
他們到戶外學習大自然的相關知識。
WORD 010
earthquake drilln.
地震演習
The students cover their heads during the earthquake drill.
在地震防災演練中，學生們用東西遮蓋頭部。
They go outside to learn more about an earthquake drill.
他們到教室外學習更多地震防災演練的知識。
IV.

Campus Life & Learning

校園生活與學習

圖書館、科丁社、自我評量、合作學習、掃地——國聖孩子每天的學習與生活英文。

WORD 011
libraryn.
圖書館
Students enjoy reading their favorite books in quiet library spots.
學生喜歡在圖書館找個安靜的地點閱讀他們最喜歡的書。
The students search the library bookshelves, looking for interesting books to read.
學生在圖書館的書櫃找有趣的書來閱讀。
WORD 012
coding clubn.
科丁社
The students participate in the coding club.
同學們參加科丁社的課程。
In the coding club, students learn how to code.
在科丁社當中，同學們可以學習編碼。
WORD 013
self-assessmentn.
自我評量
The students use tablets for self-assessment.
同學們用平板做自我評量。
The tablets help the students do their self-assessment.
平板讓學生可以做自我評量。
WORD 014
cooperative learningn.
合作學習
The students participate in cooperative learning activities.
同學們參與合作學習的活動。
They share ideas for one of their cooperative learning activities.
他們在合作學習的一個活動中分享自己的想法。
WORD 015
sweepv.
掃
The teachers sweep the school grounds.
老師們在掃校園。
They sweep to keep the school grounds clean.
他們打掃校園以保持地面乾淨。

School Events & Celebrations

學校活動與慶典

聖誕慶祝活動、團體照、頒獎典禮、集點戳章——國聖校園裡值得紀念的日子。

Christmas celebrationn.
聖誕慶祝活動
Santa Claus gives gifts to the students during the Christmas celebration.
在聖誕慶祝活動中，聖誕老人送禮物給學生們。
Santa Claus greets the students during the Christmas celebration.
在聖誕慶祝活動當中，聖誕老人向同學們問候。

group photon.
團體照
The students take group photos for memories.
同學們拍攝團體照留下回憶。

award ceremonyn.

同學們在頒獎典禮中獲頒獎項。
