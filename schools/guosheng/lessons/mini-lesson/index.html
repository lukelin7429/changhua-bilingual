介紹國聖國小
Vocabulary · 單字
nature (n) 自然・meet (v) 交會・dream (n) 夢想・leader (n) 領袖・important (adj) 重要的・value (n) 價值觀・focus on (v) 專注於・planet (n) 星球・appreciate (v) 欣賞・beauty (n) 美・win (v) 贏得・award (n) 獎項・healthy (adj) 健康的・smart (adj) 聰明的・club (n) 社團・discover (v) 發現・talent (n) 才能・each other (pron) 彼此・future (n) 未來・website (n) 網站

Welcome to Guosheng Elementary School in Changhua City. Our school is a special place where learning and nature meet. Our dream is to make students like you into tomorrow's leaders. We teach important values and focus on taking care of our planet, eating well, loving books, and appreciating beauty.

歡迎來到彰化市國聖國小。我們的學校是一個特別的地方，在這裡學習與自然相遇。我們的夢想是將像你這樣的學生培養成為明天的領袖。我們教授重要的價值觀，並專注於照顧我們的地球、健康飲食、喜愛閱讀、欣賞美的事物。

We're known for being great — our school has won many awards for being healthy and smart. We have lots of clubs for you to discover your talents. We work together; our school is a big family, where teachers, parents, and students all help each other.

我們學校以表現出色而聞名，因健康促進和學業上的優秀表現而獲得了許多獎項。我們有許多社團供你發掘你的才能。我們一起工作；我們的學校是一個大家庭，老師、家長和學生都互相幫助。

Come join us! We'd love for you to be part of our school, where we're not just learning — we're making the future better one student at a time. Check out our website to see all the cool things we do at Guosheng Elementary School. See you soon!

來加入我們吧！我們很希望你能成為我們學校的一部分，在這裡我們不僅僅是學習，更逐一幫助每個學生讓未來變得更好。查看我們的網站，看看我們在國聖國小都做了些什麼有趣的事，很快見！
