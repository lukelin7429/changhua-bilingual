I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

In August 2026, Principal Li Chin-Jen returned to Lu Ming Junior High School for a second term — reuniting with a campus he first led from 2011 to 2018. In between, he helped found the Lujiang International Experimental School as director of its preparatory office, and spent more than seven years as principal of Lukang Junior High School. In 2017, during his first term at Lu Ming, he received the Ministry of Education's Principal Leadership Excellence Award.

李錦仁校長於 2026 年 8 月起，二度掌舵彰化縣立鹿鳴國民中學——重回他 2011 年至 2018 年首度擔任校長的校園。在此期間，他曾兼任彰化縣立鹿江國際中小學籌備處主任，協助創設該校，隨後轉任彰化縣立鹿港國民中學校長逾七年。2017 年，任職鹿鳴期間，他榮獲教育部校長領導卓越獎。

"When a child wants to fly, we forge them strong wings. When a child wants to dream, we let them dream boldly. When a child stumbles, we support them as they heal. When a child is tired, we offer a warm harbor to hold and encourage them."
「孩子想飛，就鍛鍊他們強健的翅膀；孩子想夢，就讓他們勇敢做夢；孩子遭受挫折，就支持他們自我療傷；孩子累了，有一個溫暖的避風港給他們擁抱和打氣。」——李錦仁校長領導卓越名言

III.

Educational Philosophy

辦學理念
Belief I · 理念一
Children have limitless potential — give them a stage, and they'll perform beyond your imagination.
孩子潛力無窮，提供孩子舞台，他們會表現的比你想像的出色。
Belief II · 理念二
To lead students well, first run the school well — a principal needs Five Powers: coordination, talent recognition, cultivation, execution, and branding.
要帶好學生，首先要經營好學校，校長要有五力：協調力、識才力、育成力、執行力、品牌力。
Belief III · 理念三
A principal should serve with purpose — leading through vision, managing by goals, communicating closely, handling public relations thoroughly, and managing crises well, to mobilize collective strength for the school.
校長應以服務為宗旨，發揮正向影響力，帶動願景領導，掌握目標管理，緊密溝通協調，周延公共關係，妥善危機處理，糾集眾人之力，創造學校辦學綜效。
