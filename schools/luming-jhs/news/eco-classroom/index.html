SDGs Green Sustainability: A Forest Classroom × Moss Balls

SDGs綠色永續：大自然教室╳水苔球

On August 11, students from the summer camp visited the SDGs Nature Green Classroom at Da Yeh University. Guided by an instructor's in-depth explanation of forest ecology, followed by a hands-on moss ball workshop, the idea of sustainability stopped being just a lesson in a textbook and became something students could hold in their own hands.

Sustainability, once you can hold it in your hands.

This walk through the forest enriched students' diverse performance — and more importantly, deepened their sense of civic responsibility to protect the ecology around them.

中文原文 · Chinese

115-8-11營隊學生來到大葉大學SDGs大自然綠教室。透過講師深度的森林生態解說與水苔球實作體驗，SDGs永續概念不再只是課堂知識，而是化作具體行動。

這場林間漫步的探索之旅，不僅豐富了學生的「多元表現」，更深化其守護生態的公民素養！
