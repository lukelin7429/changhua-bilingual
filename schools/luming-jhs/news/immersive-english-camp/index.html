Voices and Hearts Together, Bilingual All the Way

聲情交會，雙語同行：沉浸式全英口語闖關與跨世代傳承紀實

To build students' international mobility and confidence in English communication, Lu Ming invited a professional team of foreign teachers to campus to lead a lively, multi-station "Immersive All-English Challenge." Through playful games and mission-based challenges, students opened their mouths to speak English in a natural, low-pressure atmosphere — turning abstract language learning into a practical, everyday skill.

Bilingual all the way, generation after generation.

What made the day especially memorable was that graduated alumni returned to campus as volunteers for the event. With warm, relatable stories from their own experience and patient guidance, these alumni led their younger schoolmates to bravely take on every station — a heartwarming example of cross-generational solidarity and mentorship!

中文原文 · Chinese

為提升本校學子的國際移動力與英語溝通自信，特別邀請專業外師團隊蒞校指導，規劃活潑多元的「沉浸式全英闖關活動」。透過寓教於樂的遊戲情境與任務挑戰，讓學生在自然無壓力的氛圍中開口說英語，將抽象的語言學習轉化為生活化的實踐能力。

特別值得一提的是，畢業的校友學長也返校擔任活動志工。學長們以親切的經驗分享與耐心的引導，帶領學弟妹勇於挑戰各項關卡，展現了跨世代共好與傳承的溫暖典範！
