Time-Traveling Through Lugang: New Students' Bilingual Cultural Walking Tour

穿梭古今走讀鹿港：新生雙語與在地文化采風

On July 27, incoming first-year students set out on a walking tour of Lugang, visiting the national heritage site Longshan Temple and the historic Lugang Old Street. Wandering through the antique red-brick lanes, students truly experienced what it means to take "one step, one heritage site" — feeling the weight of history and the beauty of local aesthetics with every step.

Life itself is the classroom, and the local is the international.

A special thank you to teacher Shih Shu-Ling for her expert and lively guided tour along the way, weaving together rich historical stories and cultural meaning.

We are also deeply grateful to the teams at Shih-Yi House and Shih-Yi Tea Tasting for generously offering a comfortable place to rest and refined local tea and snacks.

Through this cross-domain walking tour that combined a bilingual setting with cultural practice, students not only broadened their learning horizons, but also — through immersive experience — deepened their identification with and love for their hometown. Well done!

中文原文 · Chinese

115-07-27本次活動帶領新生走訪國定古蹟龍山寺與歷史悠久的鹿港老街，孩子們穿梭於古色古香的街巷紅磚間，真正體驗到「一步一古蹟」的歷史厚重感與在地美學。

特別感謝施淑鈴老師帶來專業且生動的沿途導覽，將豐富的歷史故事與文化意涵融會貫通。

也深深感謝「十宜樓」與「十宜甄茶」團隊熱忱提供舒適的參觀休憩空間與在地精緻茶點。

透過這場結合雙語情境與文化實踐的跨域走讀，孩子們不僅開闊了學習視野，更在沉浸式體驗中深化了對鄉土的認同與熱愛，落實「生活即課堂、在地即國際」的教育願景！讚！
