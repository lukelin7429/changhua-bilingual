I.

Welcome to Lu Ming

歡迎來到鹿鳴國中

Named after an ode of hospitality from the Book of Odes, Lu Ming Junior High has called the historic town of Lugang home since 1973.

取名自詩經《鹿鳴》篇，鹿鳴國中自民國 62 年設校以來，深耕鹿港這座歷史古鎮。

Lu Ming Junior High School was established in 1973 (Minguo 62), taking its name from the Lu Ming ode of the ancient Chinese Book of Odes (詩經) — a poem about deer calling to one another as they graze, welcoming honored guests to a shared feast. Through the work of generations of principals, teachers, and local patrons, the school has steadily grown into a well-rounded campus known for both academic strength and a rich school life. Each grade level maintains one music class, centered on Chinese orchestra, and one PE class, specializing in judo, track & field, and table tennis.

本校於民國 62 年，根據詩經鹿鳴篇，定名為「彰化縣立鹿鳴國民中學」。鹿鳴國中迭經歷任校長師長擘劃運籌，鄉里仕紳贊襄資助，學校規模漸次完備，不論在課業升學或軟硬體建設上，表現令人激賞，學子優秀傑出。本校各年級設有音樂班（以國樂為主）和體育班（柔道、田徑和桌球專長）各一班。

Lu Ming sits in the charming and historical town of Lugang, Changhua — a community rich in cultural heritage, from the famous Lugang Old Street to the town's temples and ancestral halls. Beyond the classroom, the school offers a wide range of extracurricular activities — music, sports, clubs, and organizations — to help every student discover and develop their own passions.

鹿鳴國中座落於彰化縣鹿港鎮，這座迷人且富有歷史底蘊的小鎮，擁有著名的鹿港老街、寺廟與宗祠等豐厚文化資產。除了課堂學習，學校也提供音樂、體育、社團等多元課外活動，幫助每位學生發展自己的興趣與熱情。

"Yo yo go the deer, feeding on the celery of the fields. I have here admirable guests; the lutes are struck, the organ blown."
「呦呦鹿鳴，食野之苹。我有嘉賓，鼓瑟吹笙。」——詩經《小雅・鹿鳴》，鹿鳴國中校名由來。

III.

The Four Powers of Lu Ming

鹿鳴四力

Rooted in the culture of Lugang, growing four strengths for every student.

善用鹿港在地豐富的文化語彙，發展「深耕、薪傳──在地文化」學校本位特色課程，透過課程提升學生的鹿鳴四力。

Lu Ming has built a systematic, module-based local-culture curriculum around Lugang's rich cultural heritage, working to raise the town's international recognition through the classroom — "Lugang's culture has its curriculum, and Lu Ming's curriculum has culture."

善用鹿港在地豐富的文化語彙發展「深耕、薪傳──在地文化」學校本位特色課程，設計出模組系統化的文化課程，透過課程提昇鹿港文化國際辨識度，具體實踐「鹿港文化有課程，鹿鳴課程有文化」。

Learning Power
學習力

Local-culture lessons spark curiosity and exploration, and through walking tours and cultural comparison, students learn to drive their own learning.

在地文化課程引導學生思考及探索，引發學生學習動機和熱忱，透過在地文化走讀和文化比較課程，讓學生學會自主學習。

Communication Power
溝通力

Through group collaboration and shared projects, students build consensus and complete cultural-action tasks — including communicating with international friends in English.

透過小組合作學習，以及團體共作，持續討論，凝聚共識，透過專題完成文化行動，尤其是面對國際友人，能用英語／雙語無障礙溝通。

Information Power
資訊力

Students produce projects and guided tours using digital media to pass on local culture, and use cloud platforms to share what they've learned.

專題製作及導覽建構，運用數位科技媒材，進行文化傳承，以及創新文化流通方式，善用網路雲端平台進行文化特色課程交流。

Character Power
品格力

Immersive cultural learning broadens students' everyday connection to the community, growing their willingness to participate and make a difference locally.

運用沉浸式文化學習，善用文化薰習改變認知，擴大在地文化生活體驗，進而關注在地文化，願意參與社區，發揮影響力。

IV.

Bilingual & Technology Education

雙語與科技教育 · 迎向 2030 雙語國家

Building a full bilingual learning environment while equipping every classroom for the digital age.

因應 2030 年雙語國家政策，除了推動國際教育，雙語和科技教育亦是本校重點發展課程。

Lu Ming has built a full bilingual teaching environment — rotating foreign teachers, ETAs (English Teaching Assistants), FETs (Foreign English Teachers), and the Teachers On The Go project bring native-speaking instruction into the classroom, alongside the Tutor JR online course, a structured English reading curriculum, and a summer English camp.

本校建置全方位雙語教學環境，透過外師巡迴、ETA、FET、Teachers On The Go 專案、Tutor JR 線上課程，規畫英語閱讀課程，辦理暑期英語營隊等，以厚實學生聽說讀寫外語能力。

V.

Music & PE Classes

音樂班與體育班
One Music Class, One PE Class — Every Grade
各年級音樂班（國樂）與體育班（柔道·田徑·桌球）並行
Every grade level at Lu Ming maintains a dedicated music class centered on Chinese orchestra, and a dedicated PE class specializing in judo, track & field, and table tennis — giving students a stage to grow both artistic and athletic strengths alongside their academic studies.

本校各年級設有音樂班（以國樂為主）和體育班（柔道、田徑和桌球專長）各一班，讓學生在課業之外，也能有舞台發展藝術與體育專長。
A campus rooted in Lugang, growing toward the world
扎根鹿港，放眼世界的校園

From the gold-lettered halls of Lu Ming Building to blossoming trees along the walkways, Lu Ming's campus carries the quiet pride of a historic town.

從鹿鳴樓的金字牌樓，到步道兩旁盛開的花木，鹿鳴國中的校園承載著鹿港古鎮的靜謐與驕傲。
