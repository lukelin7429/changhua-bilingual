Listening to the Voice of Seeds

蔡詠個展・傾聽種子的聲音
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

The Beidou Library was full of energy this March 6th. The community gathered for a very special opening tea party celebrating a solo art exhibition by a young and talented artist.

3月6日，北斗圖書館充滿了活力。社區居民齊聚一堂，參加一場十分特別的開幕茶會，慶祝一位年輕又有才華的藝術家舉辦個人美展。

Students from Dashin Elementary School kicked off the event with wonderful musical performances, and local leaders from Beidou came to show their support and encourage the children.

大新國小的學生以精彩的音樂表演為活動揭開序幕，北斗的地方首長也到場表達支持，為孩子們加油打氣。

The exhibition is beautifully titled Listening to the Voice of Seeds. It invites viewers to slow down and observe. The artwork captures different moments of growth and waiting, much like a seed before it sprouts. The exhibit ran until March 26th, taking visitors on a journey through the young artist's creative progress over the years.

展覽有個很美的名字——「傾聽種子的聲音」，邀請觀眾放慢腳步、細細觀察。作品捕捉了成長與等待的各種時刻，就像一顆尚未發芽的種子。展期至3月26日，帶領觀眾走過這位年輕藝術家多年來的創作歷程。

This event also highlights strong community bonds. The young artist's parents are both outstanding artists who have poured their hearts into helping Dashin Elementary School, and thanks to their dedication the school's art education is truly thriving. Principal Huang shared his vision of teamwork between family, school, and community — using art to build children's confidence and give them the resources to grow their imagination and creativity.

這場活動也展現了緊密的社區情誼。這位年輕藝術家的父母都是傑出的藝術工作者，長期投入心力協助大新國小，也因為他們的付出，學校的藝術教育才能蓬勃發展。黃校長分享了他的願景——家庭、學校與社區攜手合作，用藝術建立孩子的自信，給他們養分去長出想像力與創造力。
