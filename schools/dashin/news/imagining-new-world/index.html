Imagining a New World

美展・藝想新世界
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On October 3rd, 2025, Dashin Elementary School celebrated its 61st anniversary by opening a brand new art exhibition at the Beidou Township Library, titled Imagining a New World.

2025年10月3日，大新國小以在北斗鎮立圖書館開幕的全新美展慶祝61週年校慶，展覽名為「藝想新世界」。

The opening was a big success, visited by community members and leaders. Students performed the war drum and gave everyone a sneak peek at their English reading competition passage.

開幕十分成功，社區居民與地方首長都到場參觀。學生演出戰鼓，並搶先為大家呈現他們英語朗讀比賽的篇章。

After the performances by students and guests, everyone finally got to see the art projects that students and staff had worked hard to complete. The projects are themed around animals, continuing the school's interest in animals and nature. Following the theme of the school's beloved elephant slide mural, the artwork reflects the joy of animals and nature in the lives of Dashin's students and staff. The exhibit was open from October 3rd to October 30th.

在學生與來賓的表演之後，大家終於看到了學生與教職員努力完成的藝術作品。作品以動物為主題，延續學校對動物與自然的關注；呼應校園裡大家喜愛的大象溜滑梯彩繪，這些作品映照出動物與自然在大新師生生活中帶來的喜悅。展期自10月3日至10月30日。
