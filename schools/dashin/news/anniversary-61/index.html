Dashin's 61st Anniversary

61週年校慶
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On November 22nd, 2025, Dashin Elementary School celebrated its 61st anniversary with great energy and pride. The day began with wonderful welcome shows — the entire community came together, featuring an energetic dance and instrumental show and a beautiful violin performance. The community and the school truly made the celebration shine.

2025年11月22日，大新國小以充沛的活力與驕傲歡慶61週年校慶。這一天以精彩的歡迎表演揭開序幕——整個社區齊聚一堂，帶來活力四射的舞蹈與器樂演出，以及一段優美的小提琴演奏。社區與學校攜手，讓這場慶典熠熠生輝。

The students worked so hard all year. From the lively inline skating to the exciting dance show, they proudly showcased their talents, and the audience also saw amazing skills with Chinese yo-yo, karate, and unicycles during the talent shows.

學生們整整努力了一年。從活潑的直排輪到精彩的舞蹈演出，他們驕傲地展現自己的才藝；在才藝表演時段，觀眾還看到了扯鈴、空手道與獨輪車的精湛技巧。

The fun was not just for the students — it was for everyone. Families were invited to play together in the popular parent-child fun games, and everyone formed one big Dashin family. Everyone cheered loudly for the exciting races, and the sense of pride and cooperation was felt by all. Happy 61st anniversary!

樂趣不只屬於學生，而是屬於每一個人。家庭受邀一同參加大受歡迎的親子趣味競賽，所有人組成一個大大的大新家庭。大家為每一場精彩的賽跑高聲加油，那份驕傲與合作的氛圍，在場每個人都感受得到。61週年，生日快樂！
