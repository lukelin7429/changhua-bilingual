REPORT 01
Listening to the Voice of Seeds
蔡詠個展・傾聽種子的聲音

The Beidou Library filled with energy on March 6th for the opening tea party of a solo art exhibition by a young artist. Dashin students opened the event with musical performances, and local leaders came to encourage the children. The exhibition ran until March 26th.3月6日北斗圖書館舉行個展開幕茶會，大新學生以音樂表演揭開序幕，地方首長到場為孩子加油打氣。展期至3月26日。

REPORT 02
A Day of Cultural Exchange
國際交流・Dom Jones 來訪

On December 17th, Dashin welcomed Dom Jones — winner of the 2024 Young Leader of America Award — for a day of cultural exchange. Students gave a thunderous drum welcome, presented Taiwan's forms of transportation in English, and offered a commemorative poster signed by the whole school.12月17日，大新迎接2024年美國青年領袖獎得主 Dom Jones 蒞校進行文化交流。學生以震撼的戰鼓表演歡迎，用英語介紹台灣的交通工具，並致贈全校簽名的紀念海報。

REPORT 03
Dashin's 61st Anniversary
61週年校慶

On November 22nd, 2025, Dashin celebrated its 61st anniversary. The day opened with dance, an instrumental show, and a violin performance, then students showcased inline skating, Chinese yo-yo, karate, and unicycles. Families joined in the parent-child fun games and cheered for the races.2025年11月22日，大新歡慶61週年校慶。開場有舞蹈、器樂表演與小提琴演奏，學生接著展現直排輪、扯鈴、空手道與獨輪車。家庭一同參加親子趣味競賽，為每一場賽跑加油。

REPORT 04
Imagining a New World
美展・藝想新世界

To mark the 61st anniversary, Dashin opened the Imagining a New World exhibition at Beidou Township Library on October 3rd, 2025. Students performed the war drum and previewed their English reading competition passage. The animal-themed artwork echoes the school's beloved elephant slide mural.為慶祝61週年，大新於2025年10月3日在北斗鎮立圖書館舉辦「藝想新世界」美展。學生演出戰鼓，並搶先呈現英語朗讀比賽的篇章。以動物為主題的作品，呼應校園裡大家喜愛的大象溜滑梯彩繪。展期至10月30日。

REPORT 05
My Artistic World
大新美展・我的藝想世界

Dashin kicked off its 60th anniversary celebrations on October 8th, 2024 with the My Artistic World exhibition at Beidou Township Library. After a vibrant war drum performance, Principal Huang welcomed guests and encouraged everyone to explore the works — abstract, figurative, realistic, and sci-fi pieces created by parents, teachers, and students together, all built from cardboard boxes and small decorative items.2024年10月8日，大新以「我的藝想世界」美展揭開60週年校慶序幕，地點在北斗鎮立圖書館藝廊。在戰鼓隊熱力演出後，黃校長歡迎來賓並邀請大家細細欣賞作品。展品由家長、老師與學生共同創作，涵蓋抽象、具象、寫實與科幻風格，全都以紙箱與小裝飾品製成。展期至10月31日。

REPORT 06
Books for Taiwan
Books for Taiwan 贈書致謝

Principal Huang thanks Books for Taiwan and several American libraries for their generous donation of books, which will strengthen the school's English education and nurture a love of reading. Thanks also go to the principal of Sijhou Junior High School for distributing the books to schools across Changhua.黃校長感謝 Books for Taiwan 與多間美國圖書館慷慨捐贈書籍，這份心意將強化學校的英語教育、涵養孩子的閱讀熱情。同時也感謝溪州國中校長協助把書籍分送到彰化各校。

REPORT 07
Thanksgiving at Dashin
大新的感恩節

On November 24th, 2022, Teacher Amanda worked with the whole school to bring an authentic American Thanksgiving meal to Dashin. Traditional dishes were prepared by hand — caramelized sweet potatoes, green bean casserole, stuffing, corn, and apple pies. It was a first Thanksgiving in Taiwan for everyone at the table.2022年11月24日，Amanda 老師與全校同仁合作，為大新師生準備了一頓道地的美式感恩節大餐。傳統菜色全都手工製作——焦糖地瓜、青豆焗烤、填料、玉米與蘋果派。這是在場所有人在台灣的第一個感恩節。

REPORT 08
Beidou English Camp
北斗英語營隊

On Saturday, October 29th, 2022, four foreign English teachers and 55 elementary students from Beidou Township and nearby areas gathered for an English language camp. Activities included an interactive puppet game, a Halloween story, filming a video in a recording studio, and musical rhythm — with prizes, candy, and a copy of their own video to take home.2022年10月29日星期六，四位外籍英語老師與來自北斗鎮及鄰近地區的55位國小學生齊聚一堂參加英語營。活動包括互動偶戲、萬聖節故事、在錄音室拍攝影片與音樂律動，孩子們還帶回獎品、糖果和自己的影片。

II.

Words to Learn

單字學習

Ten words straight from the reports.

十個單字，全部取自上面的報導。點喇叭 🔊 聽發音。

exhibition/ˌɛksɪˈbɪʃən/展覽

The exhibition runs until October 30th.展覽持續到10月30日。

anniversary/ˌænɪˈvɜːrsəri/週年

Dashin celebrated its 61st anniversary.大新歡慶61週年校慶。

celebrate/ˈsɛləbreɪt/慶祝

The whole community came together to celebrate.整個社區齊聚一堂共同慶祝。

performance/pərˈfɔːrməns/表演

Students greeted the guest with a powerful drum performance.學生以震撼的戰鼓表演歡迎來賓。

community/kəˈmjuːnəti/社區

The community gathered for a special opening tea party.社區居民齊聚參加特別的開幕茶會。

creativity/ˌkriːeɪˈtɪvəti/創造力

Art helps children grow their imagination and creativity.藝術幫助孩子培養想像力與創造力。

donation/doʊˈneɪʃən/捐贈

Thank you for the generous donation of books to our school.感謝您慷慨捐贈書籍給我們學校。

grateful/ˈɡreɪtfəl/感激的

We are deeply grateful for your kindness.我們對您的善意深深感激。

traditional/trəˈdɪʃənəl/傳統的

Popular traditional foods were prepared by hand.受歡迎的傳統食物全都是手工製作的。

encourage/ɪnˈkɜːrɪdʒ/鼓勵

Local leaders came to encourage the children.地方首長到場鼓勵孩子們。

Sentence Patterns · 實用句型

Three patterns you can reuse
三個可以直接套用的句型

celebrate its ___ anniversary

The school celebrated its 61st anniversary with great energy and pride.學校以充沛的活力與驕傲慶祝61週年。

run until ___

The exhibit runs until March 26th.展覽持續到3月26日。

express my gratitude for ___

I'd like to express my gratitude for your generous donation.我想為您的慷慨捐贈表達由衷的感謝。
