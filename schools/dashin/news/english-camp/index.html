Beidou English Camp

北斗英語營隊
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On Saturday, October 29th, 2022, four foreign English teachers and 55 elementary students from Beidou Township and the surrounding area came together to participate in an English language camp.

2022年10月29日星期六，四位外籍英語老師與來自北斗鎮及鄰近地區的55位國小學生齊聚一堂，參加一場英語營隊。

The four groups of students met with each of the teachers for various activities that aimed to improve the students' English skills while experiencing cultural lessons through International Education.

四組學生輪流與每一位老師見面，進行各式各樣的活動，目標是在透過國際教育體驗文化課程的同時，提升學生的英語能力。

Activities included an interactive puppet game, listening to a Halloween story, filming a video in a recording studio, and demonstrating musical rhythm. Students received prizes, candy, a copy of their videos, and a day of fun — just in time for Halloween.

活動包括互動偶戲遊戲、聆聽萬聖節故事、在錄音室拍攝影片，以及展現音樂律動。學生們帶回了獎品、糖果、自己影片的檔案，還有一整天的歡樂時光——正好趕上萬聖節。
