My Artistic World

大新美展・我的藝想世界
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On October 8th, 2024, Dashin Elementary School kicked off its 60th anniversary celebrations with the opening of the My Artistic World art exhibition. The event was held at the art gallery in Beidou Township Library, featuring collaborative creations by parents, teachers, and students.

2024年10月8日，大新國小以「我的藝想世界」美展開幕，揭開60週年校慶的序幕。活動於北斗鎮立圖書館藝廊舉行，展出家長、老師與學生共同創作的作品。

The opening ceremony, attended by many members of the school community, began with a vibrant war drum performance by the school's drum team. Principal Huang welcomed guests, encouraging everyone to explore the creative works on display.

開幕典禮吸引許多學校社群成員參加，由學校戰鼓隊的熱力演出揭開序幕。黃校長歡迎來賓，並鼓勵大家細細探索展出的創作。

The exhibition showcases a variety of art styles including abstract, figurative, realistic, and sci-fi pieces, all created using cardboard boxes and small decorative items. Each teacher and student contributed, reflecting Dashin Elementary's commitment to fostering diverse talents and creativity. The principal emphasized that the exhibition is not just about art, but also about self-expression and the strengthening of bonds between parents, children, and the school. The exhibition ran until October 31st, 2024.

展覽呈現多元的藝術風格，包括抽象、具象、寫實與科幻作品，全都以紙箱與小裝飾品製作而成。每位老師與學生都有貢獻，展現大新國小培育多元才能與創造力的用心。校長強調，這場展覽不只關於藝術，更關於自我表達，以及家長、孩子與學校之間情感的凝聚。展期至2024年10月31日。
