Thank You, Books for Taiwan

Books for Taiwan 贈書致謝
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Principal Huang of Dashin Elementary School in Beidou, Taiwan expresses his gratitude for the generous donation of books to the school by the organization Books for Taiwan and several American libraries.

台灣北斗大新國小的黃校長，向 Books for Taiwan 這個組織以及多間美國圖書館表達由衷的感謝，感謝他們慷慨捐贈書籍給學校。

As an educator, he is aware of the critical role that reading plays in shaping the future of students. The donation will greatly enhance the school's efforts to promote English education and instill a love of learning in its students.

身為教育工作者，他深知閱讀在形塑學生未來上扮演的關鍵角色。這份捐贈將大大強化學校推動英語教育的力量，並在學生心中種下對學習的熱愛。

He also extends his thanks to the principal of Sijhou Junior High School for his efforts in distributing books to schools across Changhua. These collective contributions will inspire and educate students for years to come, and the school is deeply grateful.

他同時也感謝溪州國中校長費心將書籍分送到彰化各校。這些眾人齊心的付出，將在往後的歲月裡持續啟發並教育學生，學校對此深深感激。
