Thanksgiving at Dashin

大新的感恩節
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Thanksgiving came to Dashin Elementary School in Changhua County on November 24th, 2022. Thanksgiving is a holiday in the United States where family and friends come together to cook and share a meal.

2022年11月24日，感恩節來到了彰化縣的大新國小。感恩節是美國的節日，這一天家人與朋友會聚在一起下廚、共享一頓飯。

With this idea in mind, Teacher Amanda — with the cooperative efforts of administrators, teachers, and staff at Dashin Elementary School — worked hard to deliver the experience of an authentic Thanksgiving meal to the students.

帶著這樣的心意，Amanda 老師在大新國小行政人員、老師與職員的通力合作下，努力為學生端出一頓道地的感恩節大餐。

Popular traditional foods were prepared by hand, including caramelized sweet potatoes, green bean casserole, stuffing, corn, and apple pies. It was a memorable event for everyone, marking Teacher Amanda's first Thanksgiving in Taiwan and the first Thanksgiving for the students, teachers, staff, and administrators at Dashin Elementary School. Happy Thanksgiving!

受歡迎的傳統菜色全都手工製作，包括焦糖地瓜、青豆焗烤、填料、玉米與蘋果派。這對每個人來說都是難忘的一天——這是 Amanda 老師在台灣的第一個感恩節，也是大新國小學生、老師、職員與行政同仁的第一個感恩節。感恩節快樂！
