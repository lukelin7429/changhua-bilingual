I.

Welcome

歡迎來到大新國小

A small school with a big heart.

小小校園，滿滿用心。

Welcome to Dashin Elementary School! Nestled in the peaceful town of Beidou in Changhua County, Dashin is a close-knit small-school community — compact in size but full of energy, with a strong focus on helping every student grow a wide range of skills and interests.

歡迎來到彰化縣北斗鎮大新國小！我們座落於北斗的寧靜小鎮，是一所小巧而充滿活力的校園社區，儘管規模不大，卻致力於幫助每個孩子發展多元的技能與興趣。

Our students take part in a rich variety of extracurricular activities — lion dance, traditional battle drumming, unicycle riding, roller-skating, and Chinese yo-yo (kongzhu). These activities build coordination, teamwork, and self-confidence, all while having fun.

我們為學生規劃豐富多元的課外活動，包括舞獅、傳統鼓樂、獨輪車、直排輪與扯鈴。這些活動培養孩子的協調力、團隊合作與自信心，也讓學習充滿樂趣。

Bilingual education is also at the heart of what we do. Our students learn English directly from Teacher Amanda, a native English-speaking teacher on campus — building a strong foundation in both Mandarin and English that prepares them to connect with the wider world.

除了這些活動，我們也致力於推廣雙語教育——學生跟著校內的美籍教師Amanda老師學習英語，以穩固的中英文基礎，為與國際接軌做好準備。

We believe a strong sense of community lies at the heart of every successful school, and we welcome parents and neighbors to take part in every part of campus life. Come visit Dashin and see for yourself all that our small school has to offer.

我們深信凝聚的社區意識是成功學校的核心，誠摯歡迎家長與社區一同參與校園生活的每個角落。歡迎蒞臨大新國小，親自感受這所小校的用心與美好。

Every child deserves a beautiful childhood.
許孩子一個美好的童年——大新的老師，足感心。
Rooted in care, growing with heart
教育之美，美在用心

From the red-roofed clock tower to the sky-blue running track that curls around the field, Dashin's campus is small enough to know every face — and lively enough to fill every corner with color.

從紅頂鐘樓到環繞操場的天藍色PU跑道，大新的校園雖小，卻認得每一張臉孔；雖巧，卻讓每個角落都充滿色彩。

II.

Life at Dashin

大新的日常

Lion dance, drums, wheels, and words — a childhood in motion.

舞獅、鼓樂、輪子與雙語——動起來的大新童年。

Lion Dance & Battle Drums
舞獅與傳統鼓樂

Painted right on the school wall, Dashin's lion dance mural captures a tradition our students perform themselves — building rhythm, courage, and team spirit.

Unicycle & Roller-Skating
獨輪車與直排輪

The PU running track that curves around Dashin's field is where balance and speed come together — home to our unicycle and roller-skating clubs.

Chinese Yo-Yo & Clubs
扯鈴與社團

From kongzhu to basketball, Dashin's courts and clubs give every student a place to move, play, and belong.

Bilingual English
雙語美語

Inside Dashin's red-roofed halls, students learn English directly from Teacher Amanda — building bilingual confidence one lesson at a time.

III.

Our Philosophy

教育之美・八境

Give every child a beautiful childhood.

許孩子一個美好的童年。

教育之美，美在用心。
大新之美，美在貼心。
大新的老師，足感心。
We build with professionalism and passion, writing the beauty of education together. · 我們用專業與熱情，譜寫演譯教育之美。
I · Warmth · 溫馨
A Warm, Friendly Campus — We deliver life, gender-equity, character, human-rights, rule-of-law, and everyday-life education; caring for disadvantaged students through inclusive education, and designing a campus free of barriers in daily life, learning, and mental wellbeing.
落實生命教育、性平教育、品格教育、人權教育、法治教育、生活教育；關懷弱勢、實施融合教育，規劃生活、學習、心理層面的無障礙校園。
II · Growth · 成長
Multiple-Intelligence Teaching — We nurture students' linguistic, logical-mathematical, musical, spatial, bodily-kinesthetic, intrapersonal, interpersonal, and naturalistic intelligences, carrying forward the spirit of small-class teaching and adapting instruction to each student's own pace, path, and style.
開展學生語文、數理、音樂、空間、肢體、自省、人際、自然等八大智能；發揚小班教學精神，依學生「時間差、路徑差、風格差」適性教學。
III · Culture · 文化
Local Roots, Global Vision — We value mother-tongue instruction, English teaching, local heritage lessons, multicultural education, and cross-school exchange, building the school's own identity through a strategy of local-meets-global.
重視母語教學、英語教學、鄉土教學、多元文化教育、跨校城鄉交流；透過全球在地化、在地全球化策略，持續發展學校特色。
IV · Teamwork · 合作
Collaborative Teaching & Learning — We encourage teachers to form teaching groups for creative instruction, curriculum development, and action research, promoting group learning and a peer-tutor buddy system, and fielding teams for Hakka lion dance and English recitation competitions.
鼓勵成立教學群或教學團隊，進行創意教學、課程發展與行動研究；推動分組學習、愛心小老師制度，組隊參加客家獅及英語朗讀比賽。
V · Taste · 品味
Reading & the Arts — We build a learning organization, form reading circles, enrich our library, and guide students in reading strategies, enlivening curriculum and club activities to deepen every child's sense of music, art, and movement.
建構學習型組織、籌組讀書會、充實圖書設備，進行閱讀策略指導；活化課程、師資、社團活動，增進音樂、美術、肢體動作的美感體驗。
VI · Space · 空間
Art & a Green Campus — We pursue the vision of "school as art museum, school as park," designing arts corridors and inviting artists in residence, and building a sustainable, eco-friendly green campus that meets national green-building standards.
實現「學校即美術館」、「學校即公園」，規劃藝文走廊、延聘藝術家駐校；打造永續生態綠校園，符應綠建築九大指標。
VII · Fulfillment · 圓滿
Whole-Person Education — We pursue the whole-person goals of knowing, feeling, willing, and doing; body, mind, hand, and brain; virtue, wisdom, fitness, sociability, and beauty — through an integrated system of teaching, discipline, and counseling, with mentor pairing and one-on-one support.
踐履「知情意行」、「身心手腦」、「德智體群美」、「真善美聖」之教育目標；貫徹教訓輔三合一制度、認輔制度、小團體輔導與個別諮商。
VIII · Innovation · 創新
Technology & Reflection — We integrate digital learning, knowledge management, and knowledge leadership to build strong information literacy and digital ethics for teachers and students alike, continually refining curriculum and sharing a spirit of excellence and innovation.
整合數位學習、知識管理、知識領導，厚植師生資訊素養與資訊倫理；持續精進課程與教學，分享卓越創新的教育理念。
