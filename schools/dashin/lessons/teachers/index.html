II.

Classroom English

課室英語 · 給老師的口袋句庫（影片版）

The phrases teachers say between the lessons — taught on video.

老師在課堂裡每天會用上的英文句子——這次有人示範給你看。

A practical video library of the everyday English a Taiwanese teacher actually uses, taught by Sarah Thomas. The series runs to 30 numbered episodes across 10 topics, plus six short clips — each video isolates one situation so the phrases stick on first hearing.

這個影片教材庫涵蓋臺灣老師每天會用到的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學。系列共 10 大主題、30 支編號影片，另有 6 支短片。每支影片只處理一個情境，第一次聽就能記住。

III.

Bilingual Announcements

廣播英語 · 校園的英語之聲

The morning intercom, in two languages.

早上的校園廣播，雙語版本——依處室分集。

A 13-episode bilingual announcement series, organized by the four school offices: Academic Affairs, Student Affairs, General Affairs, and Counseling. Each section introduces what it does in both languages — turning the daily public-address routine into real bilingual exposure for the whole school.

這是一套 13 集的雙語廣播系列，依四個處室分組：教務處、學務處、總務處、輔導室，各組介紹自己的工作，雙語對照——把每天的廣播時間，變成全校學生在走廊上就能接收的英語浸潤。
