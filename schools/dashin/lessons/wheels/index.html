LESSON 01
Unicycle Vocabulary
獨輪車單字
A unicycle has a seat, pedals, and one wheel.
獨輪車有座墊、踏板和一個輪子。
You need balance to ride a unicycle.
騎獨輪車需要平衡感。
LESSON 02
Unicycle Sentences
獨輪車句子教學（進階）
A unicycle is a bike with one wheel, one seat, and two pedals.
獨輪車是一種只有一個輪子、一個座墊和兩個踏板的車。
It looks hard to ride, but if you have good balance, riding a unicycle is easy.
看起來很難騎，但只要平衡感好，騎獨輪車其實很簡單。
LESSON 03
Chinese Yo-Yo
扯鈴
The Chinese yo-yo is fun but hard to play — you must practice a lot.
扯鈴很好玩但不容易，需要多多練習。
Use the sticks to pull the string so your Chinese yo-yo can spin fast.
用兩根棒子拉扯繩子，讓扯鈴轉得又快又穩。
