LESSON 01
School Facilities
學校設施
At recess, students play games together on the playground.
下課時間，學生們在遊戲場一起玩遊戲。
You walk through the hallway to get to your classroom, and check the bulletin board for school news.
穿過走廊就能走到教室，公佈欄上可以看到學校的消息。
LESSON 02
School Offices
處室介紹
Attention: this is an announcement from the Academic Affairs Office.
請注意：這是教務處的廣播。
Please report to the Student Activity Section in the Student Affairs Office.
請到學務處訓育組報到。
LESSON 03
Virtual Class Equipment
視訊課設備
A virtual class lets learning take place when the teacher and students are not in the same place.
視訊課讓師生即使不在同一個地方也能上課。
A good internet connection, a computer, a microphone, and a webcam let everyone see and hear each other well.
穩定的網路、電腦、麥克風和視訊鏡頭，能讓大家看清楚也聽清楚。
LESSON 04
Virtual Class in Action
視訊課進行方式
The virtual class works as a live stream where everyone is interacting in real time.
視訊課就像直播，所有人都能即時互動。
Before class starts, everyone should do an equipment test to make sure cameras and microphones work.
上課前，大家都應該測試設備，確認鏡頭和麥克風正常運作。
