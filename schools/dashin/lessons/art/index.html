LESSON 01
Drawing & Sketching
畫畫與打草稿
Take out your pencils and start to draw.
拿出你的鉛筆，開始畫畫。
Sketch your picture first — don't push down hard with your pencil.
先輕輕打草稿，鉛筆不要用力壓。
LESSON 02
Painting & Color Mixing
水彩與調色
Pick your colors and put them in your palette.
選好顏色，放進調色盤裡。
Mix two colors together to make a new color.
把兩種顏色混合，調出新的顏色。
LESSON 03
Planning Your Day
綜合活動六年級・時間規劃
Plan a schedule for your day, and pick the most important thing as your priority.
規劃你一天的行程，並選出最重要的事情作為優先順序。
If you don't want to be late, start your task early and focus on what you are doing.
如果不想遲到，就提早開始並專注在正在做的事情上。
LESSON 04
Making a New Friend
綜合活動三上・認識新朋友
Let's meet a new friend — say hi, and ask what color they like.
來認識新朋友吧——打聲招呼，問問他喜歡什麼顏色。
Friends help each other — it's good to meet a new friend.
朋友要互相幫忙，認識新朋友是件好事。
