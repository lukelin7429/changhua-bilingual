LESSON 01
Dodgeball
躲避球
Two teams can play. You throw the ball and try to hit the people on the other team.
兩隊一起玩，你把球丟出去，設法打中對方隊伍的人。
Dodge out of the way so the ball does not hit you. If the ball hits you, you are out.
閃開躲掉球，別被打中；被球打到就出局了。
LESSON 02
Swimming Class
游泳課
You use your arms and kick your legs to swim.
游泳時要用手划水、用腳打水。
Your swimming cap will keep your hair dry, and your goggles help you see underwater.
泳帽可以保持頭髮乾爽，蛙鏡則幫助你在水下看得清楚。
LESSON 03
Swim Training
游泳訓練
You should stretch your body before swimming.
下水前要先伸展身體。
Your coach uses swim boards or pool noodles to train you during class.
教練會用浮板或浮條在課堂上訓練你。
LESSON 04
Warm-Up Exercises
體育課暖身運動
Time for gym class — let's do some warm-ups first so we can stretch our bodies.
體育課時間到了，我們先做暖身操，把身體伸展開來。
Roll your head in a circle to the left, then roll your head the other way to the right.
先把頭往左邊繞一圈，再往右邊繞回來。
LESSON 05
Rock, Paper, Scissors
剪刀石頭布
Say "one, two, three, go" and show a rock, scissors, or paper with your hands.
喊「一、二、三、開始」，然後用手比出石頭、剪刀或布。
Paper covers rock, scissors cuts paper, and rock crushes scissors.
布包石頭、剪刀剪布、石頭砸剪刀。
LESSON 06
Birthday Party
慶生會
It's your birthday — let's celebrate it at your school!
今天是你的生日，我們在學校一起慶祝吧！
Close your eyes, make a wish, and blow out the candles.
閉上眼睛許個願，然後把蠟燭吹熄。
LESSON 07
Field Trip
校外教學
A field trip is when students and teachers leave school for a day to go see a new place.
校外教學就是師生離開學校一天，去看看新的地方。
Field trips are a great way to learn outside of your classroom.
校外教學是走出教室學習的好方法。
LESSON 08
Halloween
萬聖節
Halloween is a holiday on October 31st. You can get candy when you wear a costume.
萬聖節是10月31日的節日，穿上變裝服就可以拿到糖果。
Your costume can be scary or cute.
你的裝扮可以很嚇人，也可以很可愛。
LESSON 09
Flu Shot
流感疫苗接種
A flu shot helps protect you from getting sick.
打流感疫苗可以保護你不生病。
You need to wait in line to see the nurse — don't be scared.
要排隊等著看護理師，不要害怕。
