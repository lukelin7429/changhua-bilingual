Halloween, Ready, Go! 🎃

西洋鬼仔節活動 Ready Go 🥳

Tap a photo to enlarge · 點照片可放大

English

From early morning, Jianxin’s campus was full of laughter and surprises! Students came to school in costumes they had put real creativity into — from adorable little witches and a dashing Sun Wukong to all kinds of playful little monsters — and the whole campus came alive.

A special thank-you goes to our English teachers, who designed three genuinely tough challenge stations for the day. None of them was easy, but the children didn’t back down. With plenty of fun, smarts and a never-give-up spirit, they used quick thinking and teamwork to clear every one.

Seeing the confident, shining smiles on the children’s faces as they cleared each challenge was truly moving. Thank you to the Jianxin teaching team for all their hard work — and a big thumbs-up to every brave child who took on the challenges today! 👍

中文原文

西洋鬼仔節活動Ready Go🥳
一早建新校園裡就充滿了歡笑與驚喜！學生們個個發揮創意、精心打扮到校，從超萌小魔女、帥氣孫悟空到各種搞怪小鬼怪，讓整個校園頓時熱鬧非凡。

特別感謝英文老師們的用心規劃，這次特地設計了三個超具挑戰性的闖關關卡。雖然關卡關關不容易，但孩子們絲毫不怯場，展現了滿滿的童趣、智慧與不服輸的精神，靠著臨場反應與團隊合作一一順利破關！

看著孩子們破關時閃閃發光的自信笑容，真的讓人無比感動。感謝建新教學團隊的辛苦付出，也給今天每一位勇敢闖關、表現優異的孩子們一個大大的讚！
