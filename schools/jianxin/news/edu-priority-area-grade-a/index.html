Educational Priority Area Program Rated “Grade A” 🏅

賀！本校「114 學年度教育優先區計畫」榮獲甲等！

本校「114 學年度教育優先區計畫」榮獲甲等 · Tap to enlarge 點圖可放大

English

🎉 Congratulations! Jianxin Elementary’s 114 school year (2025–26) Educational Priority Area Program has been rated Grade A!

The Educational Priority Area Program, run by the Ministry of Education’s K-12 Education Administration, works to narrow the education gap between cities and the countryside and to make schooling fairer for every child. It funds parent education, the development of each school’s own signature programs, and upgrades to basic teaching equipment.

Thanks to the combined efforts of school teams, teachers and parents, a number of schools earned a Grade A rating in the review of their 114 school year results — and Jianxin is proud to be one of them. 👏

中文原文

賀！本校「114學年度教育優先區計畫」，榮獲甲等！

教育部國民及學前教育署推動的「教育優先區計畫」，旨在平衡城鄉教育差距、落實社會正義。補助項目包含推展親職教育、發展學校教育特色、充實基本教學設備等。

在各校團隊、教師與家長的共同努力下，許多學校在 114 學年度的執行成果考評中獲得了甲等的高度肯定。
