A century of excellence in Lugang Township

Founded in 1920, Wenkai Elementary School has stood for over a century as a beacon of academic and cultural excellence in Lugang. Generations of committed principals and teachers have shaped a nurturing, joyful learning environment where every student is encouraged to thrive.

Our name carries the town's own literary heritage forward — Wenkai is proud to be part of Lugang's long tradition of scholarship, culture, and craftsmanship, welcomed by a community that has always placed learning at its center.

文開國小自 1920 年創校以來，超過一世紀的歷史，是鹿港鎮學術與文化卓越的代表。歷任校長與教師致力於創造充滿關懷與歡樂的學習環境，鼓勵每位學生茁壯成長。

校名承接鹿港深厚的文風傳統——文開與這座重視學問、文化與工藝的小鎮，一同寫下綿延百年的教育故事。

What We Cultivate

Read · Root · Grow

閱讀・扎根・成長 — 文開孩子的三種養成

A century-old school with a living curriculum — where children read widely, know their hometown deeply, and grow into well-rounded people.

📖

Read Reading & Language

Reading and language sit at the very center of learning at Wenkai. Class read-alouds, volunteer storytelling, and reading-and-writing initiatives spark imagination, while diverse English programs open the door to global opportunities.

文開深信閱讀是學習的基礎。班級共讀、志工媽媽說故事、讀寫計畫激發孩子的想像力，多元英語課程也為學生開啟走向世界的大門。

🏮

Root Local Heritage

Our signature local curriculum, "I Am a Lugang Tour Guide," leads students through the town's historic sites, traditional crafts, and folk customs — building deep appreciation for the community just outside our gate.

特色課程「我是鹿港小導遊」，帶著孩子走讀鹿港古蹟、體驗手工藝與民俗活動，深化對家鄉的認同與關懷。

🌱

Grow Whole-Child Character

Beyond the classroom, a wide range of clubs — from music and martial arts to environmental initiatives — along with life-skills and character education, help every child grow into a healthy, well-rounded person.

武術隊、管弦樂、圍棋、田徑等多元社團，加上生活教育與品格培養，讓每個孩子都能找到自己的舞台，全面發展。
