The 81st Graduation Ceremony第81屆畢業典禮

A joyful close to six years of learning.

Wenkai Elementary's 81st graduation ceremony came to a warm and successful close. With heartfelt guidance from teachers, loving support from parents, and sincere blessings from guests, our graduates carried gratitude and dreams as they wrote a beautiful final chapter of their elementary school life.

Six years of growing.

Six years of learning have built up a wealth of knowledge and countless precious memories. Now, our graduates step forward into a brand-new stage of learning, carrying confidence and courage into the next chapter of their lives.

Wishing every graduate well.

We wish all our graduates a heart full of ideals and a steady stride forward — may you keep growing, chasing your dreams bravely, and shining brightly on the road ahead.

中文原文 · Chinese

文開國小第81屆畢業典禮圓滿落幕。在師長的殷切叮嚀、家長的溫暖陪伴，以及來賓們的誠摯祝福下，畢業生們懷抱著感恩與夢想，為國小生活畫下美好的句點。

六年的學習時光，累積了豐富的知識與珍貴的回憶；如今，他們即將迎向嶄新的學習階段，帶著自信與勇氣，開啟人生新的篇章。

祝福所有畢業生心懷理想、步履堅定，在未來的道路上持續發光發熱，勇敢追夢、精彩成長！
