Word 01
dribble (v)
運球
The players dribble the basketball down the court.
球員在球場上運球前進。
She practices how to dribble with her left hand.
她練習用左手運球。
Word 02
fitness test (n)
體適能測驗
Every student takes a fitness test in PE class.
每位學生都要在體育課參加體適能測驗。
The fitness test checks strength, speed, and flexibility.
體適能測驗檢測力量、速度和柔軟度。
Word 03
jump ball (n)
跳跳球
The children love bouncing on the jump ball at recess.
下課時，孩子們喜歡在跳跳球上彈跳。
Hold onto the handles when you ride the jump ball.
騎跳跳球時要抓緊把手。
Word 04
Mother's Day (n)
母親節
Students make cards for Mother's Day.
學生們為母親節製作卡片。
We say thank you to our moms on Mother's Day.
我們在母親節向媽媽說謝謝。
Word 05
performance (n)
表演
The class practices for the year-end performance.
班上為期末表演做練習。
Everyone claps after the students finish their performance.
學生表演結束後，大家都鼓掌。
