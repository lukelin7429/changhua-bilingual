Word 01
desktop computer (n)
桌上型電腦
The computer room has ten desktop computers.
電腦教室裡有十台桌上型電腦。
Students use the desktop computer to practice typing.
學生使用桌上型電腦練習打字。
Word 02
tablet (n)
平板
Each student uses a tablet during the English lesson.
每位學生在英文課使用一台平板。
We play word games on the tablet.
我們在平板上玩單字遊戲。
Word 03
test (n)
考試
The students study hard for the English test.
學生們為了英文考試認真讀書。
Our teacher gives a short test every Friday.
我們老師每個星期五都會小考。
Word 04
correct (v)
改正
The teacher helps students correct their mistakes.
老師幫助學生改正錯誤。
Please correct your spelling before you turn in your homework.
交作業前請先改正拼字錯誤。
Word 05
test (n)
考試
Today the class has an oral test in English.
今天班上有一場英文口說考試。
Everyone feels a little nervous before the test begins.
考試開始前大家都有點緊張。
Word 06
go over
複習、檢討
The teacher goes over the answers after the quiz.
小考後，老師會檢討答案。
Let's go over today's new words one more time.
我們再複習一次今天的新單字。
