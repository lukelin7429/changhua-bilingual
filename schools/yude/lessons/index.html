II.

Word of the Day

每日一字 · 每段都是一支微影片

A whole school's worth of vocabulary — one short clip at a time.

全校一起累積的英文單字庫——每個字配一支短短的影片，讓孩子在五分鐘的零碎時間裡反覆練。

WOTD · 01
event(n.)
活動
Our school holds a big event every December.
我們學校每年十二月都會舉辦一個大活動。
Everyone at Yude looks forward to this event.
育德的每個人都很期待這個活動。
WOTD · 02
game(n.)
遊戲
The students play a fun game during recess.
學生們下課時間玩一個有趣的遊戲。
Everyone wants to win the game.
每個人都想贏得這個遊戲。
WOTD · 03
test(n.)
考試
The teacher gives a short test every Friday.
老師每個星期五都會有一個小考。
Students study hard before the test.
學生們在考試前認真讀書。
WOTD · 04
exam(n.)
考試（正式）
The final exam covers the whole semester.
期末考試涵蓋整個學期的內容。
Everyone feels nervous before a big exam.
大家在重要考試前都會感到緊張。
WOTD · 05
ukulele(n.)
烏克麗麗
Yude students learn to play the ukulele in music class.
育德的學生在音樂課學彈烏克麗麗。
The ukulele has only four strings.
烏克麗麗只有四條弦。
WOTD · 06
draw(v.)
畫畫
Students draw pictures of their families.
學生們畫他們家人的圖畫。
She loves to draw after school.
她放學後很喜歡畫畫。
WOTD · 07
exercise(n./v.)
運動、鍛鍊
The children do morning exercise together.
孩子們一起做早操。
Daily exercise keeps our bodies strong.
每天運動能讓我們的身體更強壯。
WOTD · 08
Chinese characters(n.)
國字
First graders practice writing Chinese characters every day.
一年級學生每天練習寫國字。
Each Chinese character tells its own little story.
每一個國字都有它自己的小故事。
WOTD · 09
go over(phr. v.)
複習
The teacher will go over the lesson before the test.
老師會在考試前複習課程內容。
It helps to go over new words every night.
每天晚上複習新單字會很有幫助。
WOTD · 10
professional development(n. phr.)
專業進修
Teachers attend professional development workshops every summer.
老師們每年暑假都會參加專業進修工作坊。
Professional development helps teachers keep learning too.
專業進修能幫助老師持續學習成長。
"Ten clips, one habit — and English starts to feel like something you can carry in your pocket."
「十支短片，一個習慣——英文開始像隨手帶在口袋裡的東西。」
III.

Four School-Based Curricula

四大校本課程 · 育德辦學的四根柱子

Four curricula, six years, sixty children — every child gets to every pillar.

四個課程，六年，六十個孩子——每個孩子都會走到這四根柱子前。

🎷
Soaring Arts
藝翔天開

A full-spectrum arts curriculum — ocarina, ukulele, keyboard, saxophone, drawing, calligraphy, dance. Our saxophone ensemble plays at township events; our biennial student exhibition fills the walls of the Erlin Township Library.

📖
Yude Reading Time
育德愛閱

From the Fo Guang Shan mobile-library truck that pulls into our courtyard, to daily reading rituals and community read-alongs — books are how Yude children meet a world larger than Outer Bamboo.

🌾
Exploring Outer Bamboo
走讀外竹

The village is the syllabus. Our students walk Outer Bamboo — its lanes, temples, fields, and elders — turning the place they live into the place they learn.

🥟
A Taste of Erlin
蕎見二林

Erlin grows more buckwheat than anywhere else in Taiwan. From the autumn buckwheat-flower fields to our winter dumpling festival with community-college partners, taste is how Yude children learn what it means to be from here.
