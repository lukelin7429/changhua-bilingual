I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話
林林志汀 校長

Principal Lin Chih-ting became the eleventh principal of Yude Elementary School in August 2024 — joining a small rural campus of six classrooms and roughly sixty children, in Outer Bamboo Village of Erlin Township. He inherits a school that has, against every demographic trend in the region, grown its student body by 23.5%.

林志汀校長於 2024 年 8 月就任，成為育德國小第十一任校長——接下一所位在二林鎮外竹里、6 班、約 60 個孩子的偏鄉小校。他承接的，是一所在彰化沿海少子化壓力下，學生數逆勢成長 23.5% 的學校。

His leadership rests on the school's four school-based curricula: Soaring Arts, Yude Reading Time, Exploring Outer Bamboo, and A Taste of Erlin. In a school this small, every child gets time on the saxophone, time on the page, time in the village, and time at the table — and the principal's job, as he sees it, is to make sure none of that is lost in the shuffle.

他的辦學軸線環繞學校四大校本課程——藝翔天開、育德愛閱、走讀外竹、蕎見二林。在這麼迷你的校園裡，每個孩子都有摸到薩克斯風的時間、翻開書頁的時間、走進社區的時間、與餐桌上的時間。校長的責任，是讓這四件事都不被擠掉。

Together with our resident foreign teacher Amanda Kalinowski — who joined Yude in September 2024 — Principal Lin is opening the classroom door wider: drama-rooted English, daily conversations, and a small-school confidence that comes from being heard by name.

他也與 2024 年 9 月起加入育德的駐校外師 Amanda 老師（Amanda Kalinowski）密切合作，把課堂的門開得更大——戲劇式英語、每日對話、與一種「被名字喊到」的小校自信。

"A small school doesn't mean a small ambition. It means more time for each child."
「迷你校不代表小志氣，而是給每個孩子更多時間。」——育德辦學精神。
