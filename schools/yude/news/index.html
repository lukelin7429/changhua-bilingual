本月焦點 · Dom Jones 訪校

A UN-SDGs ambassador walks into a sixty-child schoolyard.

一位聯合國永續發展目標大使，走進一所六十個孩子的校園。

Dom Jones visits Yude · 2026-05-25 · 育德國小校園新聞

On May 25, 2026, Ms. Dom Jones — Chairwoman of the Advocacy Board at My Culture Connect (人師教育協會) and a UN-SDGs ambassador — spent a day at Yude Elementary. She walked the courtyard with the children, sat in on English classes, and led a small conversation circle about what it means to "act locally, think globally" when your village has only one elementary school.

2026 年 5 月 25 日，Dom Jones 老師——人師教育協會董事兼聯合國永續發展目標 (SDGs) 倡議大使——蒞臨育德國小一整天。她與孩子們走過校園、走進英語課堂，並主持一場小小的對話圈：「當你的村子只有一所國小，『在地行動、全球思考』究竟意味著什麼？」

For a school whose four signature curricula already weave together the village, the table, the page, and the stage, Dom's visit landed as both encouragement and challenge — keep going, and keep widening the circle.

對一所四大校本課程早已把「社區、餐桌、書本、舞台」交織在一起的學校來說，Dom 的造訪同時是肯定，也是挑戰——繼續做，並把這個圈子畫得更大。

榮譽榜 · Yude students at their best

Eight stories. Eight ways our students stepped onto a stage larger than the village.

八則故事。八種育德孩子走上比村子更大的舞台的方式。

English · Group Recitation

Yude Grade-6 takes top honors in English group recitation六年甲班英語團體朗讀 D2 組優勝

2025-10-30 · Changhua County 114 English Group Recitation Contest

Our entire sixth-grade Class A stepped onto the stage at the 114 Changhua County English Group Recitation Contest and walked off with an Excellence Award in Division D2. For a six-class rural school, every grade-six child performing in unison is already a feat — to do it in English, and to take a county-level prize, is one of those moments that quietly redraws what "small school" can mean.

The recitation team practiced through breaks and after-school sessions, coached by Yude's English teacher with support from resident foreign teacher Amanda Kalinowski. Their English carried more than vocabulary — it carried a small, deliberate, collective pride.

六甲全班參加 彰化縣 114 年度國小英語團體朗讀比賽，在 D2 組榮獲 優勝。對 6 班規模的偏鄉小校來說，整個六年級齊聲上台已是一椿大事，能在英文上、在縣賽中拿獎，更悄悄改寫了「迷你校」的可能性。整支朗讀隊在下課與課後反覆練習，由英語科老師指導，並有外師 Amanda 老師協同。
Kendo · County Cup

Yude kendoists sweep top podium spots at the County Cup114 學年度縣長盃劍道錦標賽榮獲佳績

2025-10-21 · 114 Magistrate Cup Kendo Championship

At the 114 Changhua County Magistrate Cup Kendo Championship, Yude's young swordsmen and swordswomen brought home a clean run of first, second, and third place finishes across the lower-grade and middle-grade individual basic categories. Kendo is one of Yude's quietly distinctive co-curricular threads — discipline, posture, the long bow before a match.

For a school with only six classrooms, fielding multiple medalists in a single county-level event is the kind of result that reminds the community: small does not mean modest.

本校學生在 114 學年度縣長盃劍道錦標賽 個人基礎賽中，於低、中年級各組分別獲得 第一、第二、第三名 等成績。劍道是育德低調但鮮明的特色：紀律、姿勢、賽前那一個長長的鞠躬。6 班規模的學校在一場縣級活動裡同時跑出多位獎牌得主——「迷你」從不等於「平庸」。
Art · Lantern Painting

Erlin Creative Lantern Contest: four Yude entries take honors2026 二林鎮創意花燈彩繪比賽榮獲佳績

2026-01-13 · Erlin Township Creative Lantern Painting Contest

In the township's annual Creative Lantern Painting Contest, Yude students earned honorable mentions and final-round selections in both the lower-middle and middle-upper grade divisions. The lantern is one of Erlin's quiet civic traditions — paint your village onto paper, hold it up to the light, and walk it through the streets at festival time.

Each year, Yude's art-class curriculum (Soaring Arts) feeds directly into this contest. Children paint their own version of Outer Bamboo: red-brick gates, buckwheat blossoms, mobile-library trucks, and the rainbow arch above our schoolyard entrance.

在二林鎮一年一度的 創意花燈彩繪比賽 中，育德學生於中低年級組與中高年級組分別獲得 佳作 與 入選。花燈是二林安靜的市民傳統——把村子畫在紙上，舉起來對著光，在節慶夜晚走過街道。育德的「藝翔天開」校本課程，每年都把孩子的筆觸直接送進這場比賽。
Mother Tongue · Picture Book

Two fifth-graders win Excellence at Taiwanese mother-tongue picture-book contest五甲洪萱媃、尹苡蓁母語繪本製作優等

2026-04-07 · National Taiwanese Picture-Book Contest, Upper Elementary

Two of our fifth-graders — Hong Hsuan-jou and Yin Yi-chen — took home an Excellence Award in the upper-elementary Taiwanese-language division of the National Mother-Tongue Picture-Book Contest. The two of them wrote and illustrated the entire book themselves, in Taiwanese.

"Mother tongue" lands differently in a village school. The language Yude children grow up hearing from their grandparents is the same language they're now learning to write down and illustrate — turning the rhythms of home into something they can hand to someone else.

五甲洪萱媃、尹苡蓁參加 全國臺灣母語繪本製作比賽，於臺灣臺語國小高年級組榮獲 優等。「母語」在村校的意義不太一樣——孩子們從祖父母那裡聽來的語言，正是他們現在學著寫下來、畫出來的語言；把家裡的節奏，變成一份可以遞給別人的東西。
Mandarin Daily News

Fourth-grader Chen Yi-yun published twice in Mandarin Daily News四甲陳奕芸投稿國語日報兩度刊出

2026-01-29 · 2026-03-24 · Mandarin Daily News, Page 8

Chen Yi-yun, a fourth-grade student in Class A, has been published twice on Page 8 of the Mandarin Daily News — on January 23 and February 26 of 2026. The Mandarin Daily News is, for many Taiwanese children, the very first newspaper they learn to read; being published in it is also a quiet rite of passage for young writers.

Yi-yun's submissions came directly out of "Yude Reading Time" (育德愛閱), our daily reading curriculum. A child who reads carefully, the saying goes, eventually starts to write.

四甲陳奕芸於 《國語日報》 第八版 兩度刊出 個人投稿——分別於 115 年 1 月 23 日與 2 月 26 日。國語日報是許多台灣孩子第一份學會閱讀的報紙，被刊出也是寫作者的一個小小成年禮。奕芸的作品直接來自「育德愛閱」校本課程——讀得仔細的孩子，遲早會開始寫。
Drug Prevention · Poster

Yude artists win at the anti-drug poster contest防制毒品海報比賽甲等、佳作

2025-10-27 · Anti-Drug & Internet-Addiction Poster Painting Contest

Fifth-grader Yin Yi-chen earned a Merit Award, and second-grader Yen Ya-ju earned an Honorable Mention, at the Anti-Drug & Internet-Addiction Awareness Poster Contest. Both posters started life in our regular art class and grew into something public — a private painting becomes a piece of community education.

五甲尹苡蓁、二甲顏雅茹參加 防制新興毒品濫用及網路成癮海報繪畫比賽，分別獲得 甲等 與 佳作。兩張海報都從一般美術課裡長出來，最後走進公共領域——一張私人的畫，變成一則社區教育。
Art · County Contest

Third-grader Chen Po-han earns county-level honor in painting三甲陳柏瀚美術比賽繪畫組佳作

2025-10-17 · 114 Changhua County Student Art Competition

Chen Po-han, a third-grade student in Class A, took an Honorable Mention in the 114 Changhua County Student Art Competition — Painting Division, A2 Middle-Grade Elementary category. Po-han's award is the latest in a steady string of county-level recognitions for Yude's art programme.

三甲陳柏瀚於 114 學年度彰化縣學生美術比賽 繪畫組 A2 國小中年級組榮獲 佳作。柏瀚的獎項，是育德藝術校本長期累積、穩定發光的最新一筆紀錄。
Language Arts · Recitation

Fifth-grader Tsai Chia-en wins county-area Mandarin recitation五甲蔡嘉恩語文競賽國語朗讀優勝

2025-06-06 · 114 Changhua County Language Arts Preliminary, Area 4

Tsai Chia-en, a fifth-grader in Class A, won an Excellence Award in the 114 Changhua County Language Arts Competition — Mandarin Recitation, Elementary Group, Area 4 Preliminary. From "Yude Reading Time" on the page to standing in front of judges with a clear, steady voice — recitation is the bridge that many of our young readers cross.

五甲蔡嘉恩在 彰化縣 114 年度語文競賽初賽 國小組第四區國語朗讀項目榮獲 優勝。從「育德愛閱」翻開的書頁，到站在評審面前用清晰穩定的聲音朗讀——朗讀是許多育德小讀者一步步走過的橋。
