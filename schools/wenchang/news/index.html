Cool English Speaking Masters — Three Medals
酷英語口說達人競賽 · 三位同學獲獎
Three Wenchang students placed in this year's Cool English Speaking Masters Competition, a countywide English oral contest: Xu Chen-wen and Xie Yi-rong (both Grade 5A), along with Gong Jin-fei (Grade 4A). All three were coached by Teacher Wang Yu-tian. For a small village school, three placings in one round is an outstanding result.
Alumni · 校友升學 15 Jun 2026
🎓
Alumni Head to Top High School & University
校友升讀資優高中與清大資工
Two Wenchang alumni have gone on to selective programs: the school's 67th-cohort graduate Huang Shi-chi was admitted to Jingcheng Senior High School's gifted stream, and 61st-cohort graduate Zhou Yu-sheng was accepted into the Department of Computer Science at National Tsing Hua University. The watermelon fields of Fuxing grow more than fruit.

Go Competition — Bronze Medal

Cai Yi-shan (Grade 6A) took third place in the Go (圍棋) competition. Alongside taekwondo, English, and inline skating, Go is one of the four competition tracks Wenchang explicitly develops — a deliberate choice to give rural-village students a wide range of arenas to excel in.

Summer Kayaking Camp — Grades 3 to 6
暑期獨木舟營隊 · 三至六年級
From July 20 to 24, students in Grades 3 through 6 are invited to join a hands-on kayaking camp. The five-day program is designed to build water safety awareness, teamwork, and physical confidence — all outside the usual classroom setting. Sign-ups through the school office.
Upcoming · 即將登場 12–13 Aug 2026
🌿
Green Energy Summer Workshop
綠能科技暑期工作坊
A two-day green energy workshop on August 12–13 will introduce students to solar power, wind energy, and sustainable design. Part of the school's ongoing commitment to environmental literacy — Wenchang has previously run outdoor education at Chaoyang Green Energy Park, and this workshop extends that work into the summer.
International · 國際教育 15 Dec 2025
🌏
Online Cross-Cultural Exchange with Overseas Classrooms
國際教育線上文化交流
On December 15, Wenchang students connected live with classrooms abroad for a structured cross-cultural exchange session. Students introduced themselves, shared aspects of Taiwanese village life, and listened to their counterparts do the same. As a certified Setting Sail school under MOE's International Education 2.0 framework, these exchanges are a core part of how Wenchang goes beyond the textbook.
Outdoor · 戶外教育 24–25 Feb 2026
🚌
Two-Day Outdoor Education in Kaohsiung
高雄兩日戶外教育
Students traveled to Kaohsiung for a two-day outdoor education experience on February 24–25. The trip was designed around experiential learning beyond the classroom — combining cultural sites, teamwork activities, and exposure to urban life quite different from the watermelon fields of Fuxing Township.

115th Year Talent & Clubs Showcase
115 學年度才藝社團成果表演
Students from Wenchang's various clubs and extracurricular activities performed in the Changhua County Clubs Showcase for the 115th academic year. Cool English Self-Learning Recognition
酷英語自主學習表揚
Wenchang students participating in the Cool English self-directed learning platform were recognized for their consistent engagement outside class hours. The program rewards students who complete listening, speaking, and vocabulary activities independently — building the habit of learning English between the lessons, not only during them.
