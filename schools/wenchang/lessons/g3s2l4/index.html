Sentence Patterns 句型

Key Patterns
Pattern 1 · 陳述能力
I / He / She + can + verb
I can sing.
He can draw.
She can read.
You can write.
Pattern 2 · Yes/No 問句
Can you / he / she + verb?
A:Can you sing?
B:Yes, I can.
A:Can you dance?
B:No, I can't.
A:Can you draw?
B:No, I can't. But I can sing.

Grammar Focus 文法重點

can — No Changes Needed
can — No Changes Needed

Unlike be verbs (am / is / are), can NEVER changes regardless of the subject.

I can sing. ✅  (NOT I ams sing)
He can draw. ✅  (NOT He cans draw)
She can write. ✅

Always use the base form of the verb after "can": can sing, can draw, can write.

Subject 主詞 can Verb 動詞
I can sing
You can dance
He can draw
She can write
We / They can read

Mini Quiz · 小考

Select the best answer for each question. 選出每題最適合的答案。

Question 1 / 10
I sing. I love music!
Correct! I can sing. — "can" never changes. We never say "cans".
The answer is can. "can" never adds -s. We say "I can", not "I cans".
Question 2 / 10
A: Can you dance?  B: No, I .
Correct! No, I can't. — "can't" = "cannot". It's the negative short answer for "can".
The answer is can't. "can't" = cannot. The negative of "Can you...?" is "No, I can't."
Question 3 / 10
Which sentence is correct? 哪個句子正確？
Correct! He can draw. — "can" never changes, and the verb after "can" is always the base form (draw, not draws).
(A) is correct: He can draw. — "can" never adds -s, and use the base verb "draw" (not "draws") after "can".
Question 4 / 10
"can't" means ___
Correct! can't = cannot. It's the negative form of "can".
can't = cannot. "isn't" = is not, "don't" = do not — these are different negatives.
Question 5 / 10
A: Can she read?  B: Yes,
Correct! The short answer to "Can she…?" is Yes, she can.
The short answer is Yes, she can. — mirror the modal verb in the answer. "Yes, she is" uses the wrong verb.
Question 6 / 10
How do you say 跳舞 in English?
Correct! 跳舞 = dance. 💃
跳舞 = dance (D) 💃. Sing = 唱歌, draw = 畫圖, write = 寫字.
Question 7 / 10
Which sentence is WRONG? 哪個句子有錯誤？
Correct! (A) is wrong: "cans" is not a word. The correct form is She can write very well.
(A) is wrong: "cans" does not exist. "can" never changes. The correct form is: She can write very well.
Question 8 / 10
How do you say 畫圖 in English?
Correct! 畫圖 = draw. 🎨
畫圖 = draw (C) 🎨. Read = 閱讀, write = 寫字, sing = 唱歌.
Question 9 / 10
A: Can you write in English?  B: ___
Correct! Yes, I can. — this is the correct short answer for a "Can you…?" question.
The correct answer is Yes, I can. — always use "can / can't" to answer "Can you…?" questions.
Question 10 / 10
Which word goes in the blank?   We read and write in English.
Correct! We can read and write. — "can" is the same for all subjects: I / you / he / she / we / they.
The answer is can. "can" never changes no matter the subject — I can, he can, we can, they can.
