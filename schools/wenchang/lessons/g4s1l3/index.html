Sentence Patterns 句型

Key Patterns
Pattern 1 · 問在哪裡 (Where's / Where are)
Where's + person? → He's / She's in the ___
A:Where's grandpa?
B:He's in the kitchen.
A:Where are you?
B:I'm in the bedroom.
A:Where's your mom?
B:She's in the living room.
Pattern 2 · Review: What / Who / Where
What? → things  ·  Who? → people  ·  Where? → locations
Q:What's this?  (asking about things)
A:It's a pen.
Q:Who's she?  (asking about people)
A:She's my sister.
Q:Where's he?  (asking about location)
A:He's in the bathroom.

Grammar Focus 文法重點

Wh-Questions
Wh-Questions: What / Who / Where

每個疑問詞問的對象不同，記住這個表格就能開口問！

Question Word 疑問詞 Asks About 問的是 Example 例句
What Things / Objects 物品 What's this? → It's a pen.
Who People 人 Who's he? → He's my dad.
Where Locations 地點 Where's she? → She's in the kitchen.
Quick Tip 小提示

"Where's" = "Where is". 縮寫後意思一樣，口語常用縮寫。

For more than one person, use Where are: "Where are they?"
