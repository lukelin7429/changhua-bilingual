Sentence Patterns 句型

Key Patterns
Pattern 1 · Do you have…?
Do you have a ___? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
A:Do you have a dog?
B:Yes, I do. I have a dog.
A:Do you have a bird?
B:No, I don't. I have a fish.
Pattern 2 · Does he/she have…?
Does he/she have a ___? → Yes, he/she does. / No, he/she doesn't.
A:Does she have a cat?
B:Yes, she does.
A:Does he have a rabbit?
B:No, he doesn't. He has a turtle.

Grammar Focus 文法重點

Do vs. Does
Subject 主詞 Use 用法 Question 問句 Short Answer 簡答
I / You / We / They Do Do you have a pet? Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
He / She / It Does Does she have a cat? Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Common Mistake 常見錯誤

After Does, the main verb has NO -s:

Does she have a dog?  ✓

Does she has a dog?  ✗ — remove the -s from "has"

Mini Quiz · 小考

Select the best answer for each question. 選出每題最適合的答案。

Question 1 / 10
you have a cat?
Correct. With "you", use Do: Do you have a cat?
The answer is (B) Do. Use "Do" with I / You / We / They. "Does" is for He / She / It.
Question 2 / 10
A: Does he have a rabbit?   B: No, he . He has a fish.
Correct. Short answer for "Does he…?" → No, he doesn't.
The answer is (D) doesn't. Short answer: No, he doesn't. (Don't use "don't" for he/she/it.)
Question 3 / 10
Which sentence is correct? 哪個句子正確？
Correct. (A) Does she have a dog? — after "Does", the verb has no -s.
(A) is correct. After "Does", use the base verb: have (not "has"). (C) "she" needs "Does", not "Do".
Question 4 / 10
A: Do you have a bird?   B: Yes, .
Correct. Short answer for "Do you…?" → Yes, I do.
The answer is (C) I do. Short answer: Yes, I do. (Use "do" with I, not "does".)
Question 5 / 10
Which animal is a 魚 (fish)? 哪個是魚？
Correct. 魚 = fish.
The answer is (B) fish (魚). Turtle = 烏龜; rabbit = 兔子; bird = 鳥.
Question 6 / 10
"Don't" is the contraction of ___
Correct. don't = do not. "Doesn't" = does not.
The answer is (D) do not. Don't = do not. "Doesn't" = does not (for he/she/it).
