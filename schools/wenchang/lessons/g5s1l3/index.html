Sentence Patterns 句型

Key Patterns
Pattern 1 · 問天氣
How's the weather? → It's ___ (and ___)
A:How's the weather?
B:It's sunny and warm.
A:How's the weather in Taipei?
B:It's cloudy and cool.
Pattern 2 · Yes/No 問天氣
Is it ___? → Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. It's ___.
A:Is it cold?
B:No, it isn't. It's warm.
A:Is it windy?
B:Yes, it is.

Grammar Focus 文法重點

Intonation
Question Intonation: Wh-Questions vs. Yes/No Questions

The ending sound of a question tells listeners what kind of answer is expected.

Question Type Example Intonation
Wh-question (what / where / how) How's the weather? Goes DOWN ↘
Yes/No question Is it hot? Goes UP ↗

"How's" = "How is". "It's" = "It is". In Taiwan, the weather can be described using two adjectives: "It's sunny and hot."

Mini Quiz · 小考

Select the best answer for each question. 選出每題最適合的答案。

Question 1 / 10
A: the weather?   B: It's sunny.
Correct. We ask about weather with How's the weather?
The correct question word is How's. "How's the weather?" is the standard weather question.
Question 2 / 10
How's the weather? — cloudy and cool.
Correct. We always use It's for weather: It's cloudy and cool.
For weather, always use It's (= It is). "It's cloudy and cool."
Question 3 / 10
A: Is it windy?   B:
Correct. "Is it…?" → Yes, it is.
The correct short answer is Yes, it is. — match the be verb from the question.
Question 4 / 10
A: Is it rainy?   B: No, . It's sunny.
Correct. "Is it…?" → No, it isn't. (= it is not)
The negative short answer is it isn't. "isn't" = "is not". Use "it isn't" because the question uses "Is it".
Question 5 / 10
Which sentence correctly uses two weather words? 哪個句子正確使用了兩個天氣詞？
Correct. Use and to connect two weather adjectives: It's hot and sunny.
Use and between two adjectives: It's hot and sunny. Do not put them side by side without "and".
Question 6 / 10
"How's the weather?" — What does the intonation do at the end?
Correct. Wh-questions (How / What / Where) go down ↘ at the end.
Wh-questions go down ↘. Yes/No questions go up ↗. "How's" is a Wh-question word.
Question 7 / 10
"Is it cold?" — What does the intonation do at the end?
Correct. Yes/No questions go up ↗. "Is it cold?" expects a yes or no answer.
Yes/No questions (Is it…? Are you…?) go up ↗ at the end.
Question 8 / 10
Which weather word means 涼爽?
Correct. cool = 涼爽. It is milder than cold. warm = 溫暖, cold = 寒冷.
cool = 涼爽 (C). "cold" is colder than cool. "warm" = 溫暖.
Question 9 / 10
"How's" is the contraction of ___
Correct. How's = How is. "How's the weather?" = "How is the weather?"
How's = How is. The apostrophe + s replaces "is" in contractions.
Question 10 / 10
A: How's the weather in Kaohsiung?   B:
Correct. It's rainy and hot. — "How's…?" gets a statement answer with "It's ___".
"How's the weather?" needs a statement answer: It's rainy and hot. (D). Never start with Yes/No for a Wh-question.
