Sentence Patterns 句型

Key Patterns
Pattern 1 · 問過去在哪裡
Where was he / she + time? → He / She was at the ___ + time.
A:Where was she this morning?
B:She was at school this morning.
A:Where was Mike yesterday?
B:He was at the amusement park yesterday.
A:Where was Emma last Sunday?
B:She was at the museum last Sunday.
Pattern 2 · Yes / No 問過去位置
Was he / she at ___ + time? → Yes, he / she was. / No, he / she wasn't. He / She was at ___.
A:Was Ken at home last Sunday?
B:No, he wasn't. He was at the department store.
A:Was she at the restaurant this morning?
B:Yes, she was.

Grammar Focus 文法重點

Past Simple — was / were
Past Simple — Review was / were

Use was with I / He / She / It. Use were with You / We / They.

For past location questions, always use "at" with specific places: "at the museum", "at school", "at home".
(Exception: "at home" — no "the")

Subject 主詞 Past be verb Question form 問句 Short answer 簡答
I / He / She / It was Was he at…? Yes, he was. / No, he wasn't.
You / We / They were Were they at…? Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.

Mini Quiz · 小考

Select the best answer for each question. 選出每題最適合的答案。

Question 1 / 10
Which place means 遊樂園?
Correct. Amusement park = 遊樂園. "Museum" = 博物館, "department store" = 百貨公司.
The answer is (B) amusement park = 遊樂園. "Museum" = 博物館.
Question 2 / 10
A: Where she yesterday?  B: She was at school.
Correct. "She" is singular → past tense uses was. "Were" is for you / we / they.
"She" + past tense = was. "is / are" are present tense; "were" is past but for plural subjects.
Question 3 / 10
Which sentence is correct? 哪個句子正確？
Correct. (A) is right: "He was at the museum yesterday." Use "at" for locations and "the" before museum.
(A) is correct. (B) is missing "the". (C) uses "were" wrongly with "he". (D) should use "at", not "in", for locations.
Question 4 / 10
Was Ken at the restaurant last Sunday?  No, he .
Correct. "He" is singular past → wasn't (= was not). "Weren't" is for plural subjects.
"He" + past negative = wasn't. "weren't" goes with you / we / they; "isn't" is present tense.
Question 5 / 10
A: Was Emma at home this morning?  B: ___
Correct. Short answer for "Was she…?" → Yes, she was. Match the verb from the question.
The question uses "Was", so the answer must echo it: Yes, she was. "Were / is / did" are all wrong here.
Question 6 / 10
They at the department store an hour ago.
Correct. "They" + past tense = were. "Was" is only for I / he / she / it.
"They" is plural → past tense is were. "Was" is singular (I / he / she / it).
Question 7 / 10
Which time expression means 上星期日 (the Sunday before today)?
Correct. Last Sunday = 上星期日 (past). "This Sunday" = 這個星期日, "next Sunday" = 下星期日.
Last Sunday = 上星期日. "This Sunday" could be current/upcoming, "next Sunday" = future, "every Sunday" = habitual.
Question 8 / 10
A: you at home yesterday?  B: No, I wasn't.
Correct. "You" + past question = Were you…? "Was" is for he / she / it / I.
"You" needs Were in past questions. "Was" is for singular I / he / she / it. "Is / Are" are present tense.
Question 9 / 10
"wasn't" is the short form of ___
Correct. wasn't = was not. "Weren't = were not" is for plural subjects.
wasn't = was not (B). "weren't = were not" (plural); "isn't = is not" (present tense).
Question 10 / 10
Which sentence has an error? 哪個句子有錯誤？
Correct. (D) has an error: "she" is singular, so it should be Where was she yesterday? "Were" is for plural subjects.
The error is in (D). "She" is singular → Where was she yesterday? Use "were" only for you / we / they.
