Sentence Patterns 句型

Key Patterns
Pattern 1 · 問今天星期幾
What day is today? → It's ___.
A:What day is today?
B:It's Wednesday.
A:Is today Friday?
B:Yes, it is.
A:Is today Monday?
B:No, it isn't. It's Tuesday.
Pattern 2 · 縮寫 Abbreviations
Full name → Short form (abbr.)
Sunday → Sun.
Monday → Mon.
Wednesday → Wed.
Saturday → Sat.

Grammar Focus 文法重點

Using "It"
"It" for Day, Time, and Color

In English, "it" is used for day, time, and color questions — not a person or thing.

Question 問句 Answer 答句
What day is today? It's Friday.
What time is it? It's nine thirty.
What color is it? It's red.
Capitalization Rule 大寫規則

Days of the week ALWAYS start with a capital letter in English.

monday → Monday   friday → Friday

Question 3 / 10

Correct. No, it isn't. — since today is Sunday, not Saturday.
The correct answer is (A) No, it isn't. — "it" pairs with "isn't" for negatives.
Question 4 / 10
Saturday and Sunday are called the ___
Correct. Saturday and Sunday = the weekend. Monday to Friday = weekdays.
The correct answer is (C) weekend. Sat & Sun are the weekend; Mon–Fri are weekdays.
Question 5 / 10
Which is the correct abbreviation for Wednesday?
Correct. Wednesday → Wed. (with a period/full stop).
The standard abbreviation is (B) Wed. — note the period at the end.
Question 6 / 10

Correct. (D) It's Tuesday today. — days of the week must start with a capital letter.
The correct answer is (D) It's Tuesday today. — Monday, Friday, Thursday must all be capitalized.
Question 7 / 10
Monday to Friday are called ___
Correct. Monday through Friday = weekdays. Sat & Sun = the weekend.
Monday to Friday are called (A) weekdays. Saturday and Sunday are the weekend.
Question 8 / 10

The correct abbreviation for Saturday is (C) Sat. — "Sun." is for Sunday.
Question 9 / 10
