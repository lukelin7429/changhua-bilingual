Sentence Patterns 句型

Key Patterns
Pattern 1 · 問要去哪
Where are you / is he / she going? → I'm / He's / She's going to the ___.
A:Where are you going?
B:I'm going to the library.
A:Where is she going?
B:She's going to the bookstore.
Pattern 2 · 問位置
Where's the ___? → It's on ___ Road. / It's next to the ___.
A:Where's the post office?
B:It's on Green Road.
A:Where's the hospital?
B:It's next to the supermarket.
A:Where's the zoo?
B:It's across from the park.

Grammar Focus 文法重點

Present Continuous for Future Plans
Present Continuous for Future Plans

In English, we often use the present continuous (am / is / are + going) to talk about near-future plans.

Subject 主詞 Be verb Pattern Example 例句
I am I'm going to… I'm going to the park.
He / She is He's / She's going to… She's going to the zoo.
You / We / They are You're / We're / They're going to… They're going to the library.

Location Prepositions 位置介系詞

Where's the ___?
Preposition Meaning 意思 Example 例句
on ___ Road 在某條路上 It's on Blue Road.
next to 在...旁邊 It's next to the park.
across from 在...對面 It's across from the hospital.

Mini Quiz · 小考

Select the best answer for each question. 選出每題最適合的答案。

Question 1 / 10
A: Where she going?  B: She's going to the bookstore.
Correct. "She" is singular → Where is she going?
"She" is singular, so use is. "are" is for you / we / they.
Question 2 / 10
Where's the hospital?  It's the supermarket.
Correct. next to means 在...旁邊. "It's next to the supermarket."
Use next to (在...旁邊). "across from" means 在...對面, not used here.
Question 3 / 10
Which sentence is correct? 哪個句子正確？
Correct. I'm going to the library. — present continuous: am + going.
(A) is correct: I'm going to the library. — use am + going (not "go" or "goes").
Question 4 / 10
Where's the zoo?  It's the park.
Correct. across from means 在...對面. "It's across from the park."
Use across from (在...對面). "next to" means 在...旁邊.
Question 5 / 10
A: Where they going?  B: They're going to the zoo.
Correct. "They" is plural → Where are they going?
"They" needs are. "is" is for he / she / it.
Question 6 / 10
Where's the post office?  It's Green Road.
Correct. We say on a road: "It's on Green Road."
Use on for roads: "It's on Green Road." — not "at" or "in".
Question 7 / 10
Which place do you go to borrow books? 借書要去哪裡？
Correct. A library (圖書館) is where you borrow books.
You go to the library (圖書館) to borrow books.
Question 8 / 10
A: Where you going?  B: I'm going to the park.
Correct. "You" uses are → Where are you going?
"You" uses are: "Where are you going?" — not "am" (which is only for "I").
Question 9 / 10
Which place is a place to buy food and groceries? 哪個地方可以買食物和日用品？
Correct. A supermarket (超市) is where you buy food and groceries.
A supermarket (超市) is where you buy food and daily items.
Question 10 / 10
He going to the hospital.
Correct. "He" is singular → He is going (= He's going) to the hospital.
"He" needs is: "He is going to the hospital." — "is" goes with he / she / it.
