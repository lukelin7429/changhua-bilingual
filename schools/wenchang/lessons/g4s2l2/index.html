Sentence Patterns 句型

Key Patterns
Pattern 1 · 問單個物品位置
Where's the ___? → It's ___ the ___.
A:Where's the kite?
B:It's under the desk.
A:Where's the robot?
B:It's on the chair.
Pattern 2 · 問多個物品位置
Where are the ___s? → They're ___ the ___.
A:Where are the balls?
B:They're in the box.
A:Where are the dolls?
B:They're under the desk.

Grammar Focus 文法重點

Singular is vs. Plural are
Number 數量 Subject 主詞 Be Verb 動詞 Example 例句
1 (singular 單數) The doll is The doll is on the desk.
2+ (plural 複數) The dolls are The dolls are on the desk.
What vs. Where 比較

What = asking about the identity of something (問是什麼)

  → What's this? — It's a ball.

Where = asking about the location of something (問在哪裡)

  → Where's the ball? — It's on the desk.

Mini Quiz · 小考

Select the best answer for each question. 選出每題最適合的答案。

Question 1 / 10
A: Where's the kite? B: It's the desk.
Correct. "It's under the desk." — the kite is below the desk.
The pattern uses under here: "It's under the desk." "on" = top surface; "in" = inside.
Question 2 / 10
A: Where are the balls? B:
Correct. They're in the box. — plural subject uses "They're"; don't forget "the" before "box".
Plural "balls" → They're in the box. Use "They're", not "It's". Always use "the" before the location noun.
Question 3 / 10
Which preposition means "在…上面"?
Correct. on = 在…上面 (on the surface). "in" = inside; "under" = below.
on = 在…上面. "in" = 在…裡面. "under" = 在…下面.
Question 4 / 10
The robot on the chair.
Correct. "The robot" = singular → is. "The robots" (plural) would use "are".
Singular "The robot" uses is. "are" is for plural nouns (the robots, the balls).
Question 5 / 10
A: Where's the doll? B:
Correct. It's on the desk. — singular "the doll" → "It's". Always include "the" before the noun.
Singular "the doll" → It's on the desk. Use "It's" not "They're", and include "the" before "desk".
Question 6 / 10
The dolls under the desk.
Correct. "The dolls" = plural → are. "The doll" (singular) would use "is".
Plural "The dolls" → are. Singular nouns use "is"; plural nouns use "are".
Question 7 / 10
You want to ask about the location of a yo-yo. Which question is correct?
Correct. Where's the yo-yo? — "Where" asks about location; singular "yo-yo" uses "Where's" (= Where is).
Where's the yo-yo? — "Where" = location. "What" = identity. Singular uses "Where's" not "Where are".
Question 8 / 10
A: Where are the kites? B:
Correct. They're under the chair. — plural "kites" → "They're"; always use "the" before the location.
Plural "kites" → They're under the chair. Use "They're" not "It's", and include "the" before "chair".
Question 9 / 10
Which sentence is correct? 哪個句子正確？
Correct. The balls are on the desk. — plural subject "balls" pairs with "are".
(B) is correct: The balls are on the desk. Plural "balls" needs "are"; singular "ball" needs "is".
Question 10 / 10
"在…裡面" is expressed by the preposition ___
Correct. in = 在…裡面 (inside). "on" = top; "under" = below.
in = 在…裡面. "on" = 在…上面. "under" = 在…下面.
