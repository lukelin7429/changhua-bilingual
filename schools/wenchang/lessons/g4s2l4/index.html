Sentence Patterns 句型

Key Patterns
Pattern 1 · Do you like…?
Do you like ___? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
A:Do you like cake?
B:Yes, I do. I like cake.
A:Do you like milk?
B:No, I don't. I don't like milk.
Pattern 2 · Does he/she like…?
Does he/she like ___? → Yes, he/she does. / No, he/she doesn't.
A:Does she like pizza?
B:Yes, she does.
A:Does he like juice?
B:No, he doesn't. He likes water.

Grammar Focus 文法重點

Do / Does
Do / Does — Expressing Likes

Use Do with I / You / We / They.  Use Does with He / She / It.

Subject 主詞 Helper 助動詞 Verb 動詞 Negative 否定
I / You / We / They Do like don't like
He / She / It Does like doesn't like
Important: verb stays in base form after Does

After Does, the verb stays in base form (like, NOT likes):

✅ Does she like pizza?

❌ Does she likes pizza?

Mini Quiz · 小考

Select the best answer for each question. 選出每題最適合的答案。

Question 1 / 10
Which food is 蛋糕 in English?
Correct. 蛋糕 = cake.
蛋糕 = cake (B). Pizza = 披薩, juice = 果汁, milk = 牛奶.
Question 2 / 10
you like ice cream?  Yes, I do.
Correct. Use Do with "you" → Do you like ice cream?
Use Do (D) with I/You/We/They. "Does" is only for He/She/It.
Question 3 / 10
A: Do you like pizza?  B:
Correct. The short answer for "Do you like…?" is Yes, I do.
The correct short answer is Yes, I do. (A). "I does" is wrong — "does" is only for He/She/It.
Question 4 / 10
she like water?  Yes, she does.
Correct. Use Does with "she" → Does she like water?
Use Does (C) with He/She/It. "Do" is for I/You/We/They.
Question 5 / 10
Which sentence is correct? 哪個句子正確？
Correct. Does she like cake? — after "Does," the verb stays in base form (like, not likes).
(B) is correct: Does she like cake? After "Does," use base form. "Does she likes…" is a very common mistake — never add -s after Does.
Question 6 / 10
A: Does he like juice?  B: No, .
Correct. The negative short answer for "Does he…?" is No, he doesn't.
For "Does he…?" the negative answer is No, he doesn't. (D). "Don't" is for I/You/We/They.
Question 7 / 10
Which subject uses "Do" to ask a question?
Correct. We uses "Do" → Do we like pizza? She/He/It all use "Does."
"Do" goes with I/You/We/They (A). She, He, and It all use "Does."
Question 8 / 10
Which food is 冰淇淋 in English?
Correct. 冰淇淋 = ice cream.
冰淇淋 = ice cream (C). Water = 水, milk = 牛奶, juice = 果汁.
Question 9 / 10
A: Do they like cake?  B:
Correct. "They" uses "don't" → No, they don't.
For "Do they…?" the negative is No, they don't. (B). "Doesn't" is only for He/She/It.
Question 10 / 10
Which sentence correctly says that Tom likes pizza?
Correct. In a statement (not a question), He/She/It uses verb + -s → Tom likes pizza.
In a positive statement, He/She/It needs verb + -s → Tom likes pizza. (D). "Does likes" has two markers — that's a double error.
