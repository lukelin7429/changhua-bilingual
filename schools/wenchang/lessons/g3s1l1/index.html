Grammar Focus 文法重點

Writing Rules
Sentence Writing Rules 書寫規則

Always capitalize: (1) the first letter of a sentence, (2) the word "I", (3) proper names.

Wrong ❌ Correct ✅
my name is emma. My name is Emma.
what's your name? What's your name?
i'm alan. I'm Alan.

Mini Quiz · 小考

Select the best answer for each question. 選出每題最適合的答案。

Question 1 / 10
A: What's your name? B:
Correct! The answer is "My name is Alan." — subject first, then the verb.
The correct answer is (B) My name is Alan. — the order is: My name + is + name.
Question 2 / 10
Hello, Emma.
Correct! I'm = I am. The apostrophe replaces the letter "a". Always capitalize "I".
The correct answer is (D) I'm. "Im" has no apostrophe. "i'm" doesn't capitalize "I". "I'M" uses all caps incorrectly.
Question 3 / 10
Which sentence is written correctly? 哪個句子書寫正確？
Correct! (A) is right. The first word is capitalized, the apostrophe in "What's" is correct, and only the first word is capitalized.
(A) is correct. (B) is missing the apostrophe. (C) doesn't capitalize the first letter. (D) over-capitalizes every word.
Question 4 / 10
My is Wendy.
Correct! "My name is Wendy." is the standard sentence pattern for introducing your name.
The correct answer is (C) name. The pattern is: My name is ___.
Question 5 / 10
"What's" is a contraction. It is short for ___
Correct! What's = What is. The apostrophe + s replaces "i" from "is".
The correct answer is (B) What is. What's = What + is. The apostrophe replaces the "i" in "is".
Question 6 / 10
Which word is a contraction for "He is"? 哪個是「He is」的縮讀？
Correct! He's = He is. The apostrophe takes the place of the letter "i" in "is".
The correct answer is (D) He's. "Hes" has no apostrophe. "H'is" puts the apostrophe in the wrong place.
Question 7 / 10
Which sentence uses correct capitalization? 哪個句子的大寫用法正確？
Correct! (A) is right. "I" is always capitalized, and names (Alan) are always capitalized.
The correct answer is (A) I'm Alan. — the word "I" must always be capitalized, and proper names like "Alan" must also be capitalized.
Question 8 / 10
___ name is Wendy. (talking about a girl)
Correct! For a girl, use Her name is ___. For a boy, use His name is ___.
The correct answer is (C) Her name is Wendy. "Her" is used for girls. "His" is used for boys.
Question 9 / 10
"She's" is a contraction. It is short for ___
Correct! She's = She is. Same pattern as He's = He is, and I'm = I am.
The correct answer is (B) She is. She's = She + is. Compare: He's = He is, I'm = I am.
Question 10 / 10
Which sentence has a capitalization error? 哪個句子有大寫錯誤？
Correct! (D) has two errors: "her" should be "Her" (first letter of a sentence), and "wendy" should be "Wendy" (proper name).
The answer with errors is (D). "her" should be "Her" (start of sentence), and "wendy" should be "Wendy" (proper name). Correct: Her name is Wendy.
