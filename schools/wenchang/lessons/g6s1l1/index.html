Sentence Patterns 句型

Key Patterns
Pattern 1 · 問來自哪裡
Where is / are … from?
A:Where is she from?
B:She is from the USA.
A:Where are you from?
B:I'm from Taiwan.
Pattern 2 · Yes / No 問句
Is / Are … from …?
A:Is he from Japan?
B:Yes, he is.
A:Are they from Vietnam?
B:No, they aren't. They're from Korea.

Grammar Focus 文法重點

Be Verbs
常見錯誤 Common Mistake

Why can't we say Where are you come from?

come is a verb. One sentence cannot have two verbs together. Choose one:

Where are you from?  — remove "come"
Where do you come from?  — replace "are" with "do"

Subject 主詞 Statement 肯定句 Negative 否定句 Question 問句
I I am from Taiwan. I am not from Korea. Am I from …?
He / She / It / Name She is from France. She isn't from Japan. Is she from …?
We / You / They They are from the UK. They aren't from Vietnam. Are they from …?

Mini Quiz · 小考

Select the best answer for each question. 選出每題最適合的答案。

Question 1 / 10
A: she from France?  B: Yes, she is.
Correct. "She" is singular → Is she from France?
Not quite. "She" is singular, so use Is. "Are" is for plural subjects (they / we).
Question 2 / 10
A: Where they from?  B: They are from Korea.
Correct. "They" is plural → Where are they from?
"They" is plural, so use are. "is" is for he / she / it.
Question 3 / 10
Which sentence is correct? 哪個句子正確？
Correct. Mike is a name (singular) → Where is Mike from?
(A) is correct. (B) should use "are". (C) "she" needs "is". (D) is missing the question mark "?".
Question 4 / 10
Are Ken and Ben from Taiwan?  No, they . They are from Australia.
Correct. "They" is plural → No, they aren't.
"They" needs aren't (plural negative). "isn't" goes with he / she / it.
Question 5 / 10
"The UK" stands for ___
Correct. The UK = The United Kingdom. The USA = The United States of America.
The UK = The United Kingdom. The USA is The United States of America.
Question 6 / 10
"Does he from the USA?" contains an error. What is the correct form?
Correct. Use Is he from the USA? — we use be verbs with "from", not "does".
The correct form is (D) Is he from the USA? — use be verbs (am / is / are) with "from".
Question 7 / 10
A person from Taiwan is called ___
Correct. Taiwan → Taiwanese. Notice the pattern: Vietnam → Vietnamese, Japan → Japanese.
The correct nationality is Taiwanese. Pattern: Taiwan + ese = Taiwanese (like Japanese, Vietnamese).
Question 8 / 10
A: Is she from Vietnam?  B: ___
Correct. No, she isn't. — "she" uses "isn't" (= is not) for negative.
The correct answer is (B) No, she isn't. — "she" pairs with "is / isn't".
Question 9 / 10
Which sentence has an error? 哪個句子有錯誤？
Correct. (C) should be Is Wendy from the UK? — Wendy is one person (singular), so use "Is", not "Are".
The error is in (C). "Wendy" is singular → Is Wendy from the UK? (D) is correct: Emma and Mike = plural → "Are".
Question 10 / 10
A person from France is called ___
Correct. France → French. Same word is used for nationality and language.
The correct nationality for France is French (D). "Frenchman" means a French male person specifically.
