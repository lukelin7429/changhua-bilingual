Key Patterns

Sentence Patterns 句型練習

學會三種問法，掌握「來自哪個國家」的完整表達。

Pattern 1 · 問來自哪裡
Where is / are ... from?
⋯⋯來自哪裡？
A: Where is she from?
B: She is from the USA.
A: Where are you from?
B: I'm from Taiwan.
Pattern 2 · Yes / No 問句
Is / Are ... from ...?
⋯⋯來自⋯⋯嗎？
A: Is he from Japan?
B: Yes, he is.
A: Are they from Vietnam?
B: No, they aren't. They're from Korea.

Grammar Focus

Be Verb Rules 動詞規則

⚠️ Common Mistake — 常見錯誤

Why can't we say Where are you come from?

come is a verb. A sentence can only have ONE main verb — not both are and come.

✅ Two correct ways:
Where are you from?  (remove "come")
Where do you come from?  (use "do", remove "are")

Subject 主詞 Statement 肯定句 Negative 否定句 Question 問句
I I am from Taiwan. I am not from Korea. Am I from…?
He / She / It / Name She is from France. She isn't from Japan. Is she from…?
We / You / They They are from the UK. They aren't from Vietnam. Are they from…?

🎯 Mini Quiz — 小考時間

Select the best answer for each question. 選出每題最適合的答案。

Question 1 / 10
A:     she from France?  B: Yes, she is.
✅ Correct! "She" is singular, so use Is she from France?
❌ Not quite. "She" is singular → use Is. (C) "Are" is for plural subjects.
Question 2 / 10
A: Where     they from?  B: They are from Korea.
✅ Correct! "They" is plural → Where are they from?
❌ "They" is plural, so use are. "is" is for he/she/it.
Question 3 / 10
Which sentence is CORRECT? 哪個句子正確？
✅ Correct! Mike is a name (singular) → Where is Mike from? ✔
❌ The correct sentence is (A). (B) should use "are" not "do". (C) "she" needs "is". (D) missing the question mark "?".
Question 4 / 10
Are Ken and Ben from Taiwan? No, they    . They are from Australia.
✅ Correct! "They" is plural → No, they aren't. "isn't" is for he/she/it.
❌ "They" needs aren't (plural negative). "isn't" goes with he/she/it.
Question 5 / 10
"The UK" stands for ___
✅ Correct! The UK = The United Kingdom. The USA = The United States of America.
❌ The UK = The United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland). The USA is for the United States.
Question 6 / 10
The question "Does he from the USA?" has an error. What is the CORRECT form?
✅ Correct! Use Is he from the USA? — we use "be verb + from", not "does".
❌ The correct form is (D) Is he from the USA? — we use be verbs (am/is/are) with "from", never "does/do".
Question 7 / 10
A person from Taiwan is called ___
✅ Correct! Taiwan → Taiwanese. Also: Vietnam → Vietnamese, Japan → Japanese.
❌ The correct nationality is Taiwanese. Notice the pattern: Taiwan + ese = Taiwanese (like Japanese, Vietnamese).
Question 8 / 10
A: Is she from Vietnam?  B: ___
✅ Correct! No, she isn't. — "she" uses "isn't" (= is not) for negative.
❌ The correct answer is (B) No, she isn't. — "she" pairs with "is/isn't", never "do" or "are/aren't".
Question 9 / 10
Which sentence has an ERROR? 哪個句子有錯誤？
✅ Correct! (C) should be Is Wendy from the UK? — Wendy is one person (singular), so use "Is", not "Are".
❌ The error is in (C). "Wendy" is singular → Is Wendy from the UK? (D) is correct: Emma and Mike = plural → "Are".
Question 10 / 10
A person from France is called ___
✅ Correct! France → French. It's the same word used as nationality AND language. The UK → British (not Britishese!).
❌ The correct nationality for France is French (D). "Frenchman" means a French male person specifically — not used as a general nationality.
