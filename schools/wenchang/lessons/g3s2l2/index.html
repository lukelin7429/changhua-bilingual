Grammar Focus 文法重點

Writing Rules
Sentence Writing Rules — Review 寫句子的四個規則

1. Capitalize the first letter of every sentence. 每個句子的第一個字母要大寫。

2. The word "I" is always capitalized. "I" 永遠要大寫。

3. Leave a space between words. 單字與單字之間要有空格。

4. No space before punctuation (period, question mark). 標點符號前面不加空格。

Wrong ❌ Right ✅ Rule 規則
it's red. It's red. Capitalize the first letter.
what color is it ? What color is it? No space before "?".
