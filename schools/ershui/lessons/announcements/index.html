The Voice of the School

把廣播時間變成英語時間
The school offices, introduced in two languages.
用雙語認識學校的每一個處室。

A 13-episode bilingual series introducing the four administrative offices that keep a Taiwan elementary school running: Academic Affairs (教務處), Student Affairs (學務處), General Affairs (總務處), and the Counseling Office (輔導室). Each office explains what it does in both languages — turning the daily public-address routine into real-world bilingual exposure for the whole school.

這是一個 13 集的雙語影片系列，介紹維持一所台灣國小運作的四大處室：教務處、學務處、總務處、輔導室。各處室以雙語介紹自己的工作——把每天的廣播時間，變成全校都能接收到的英語浸潤。
