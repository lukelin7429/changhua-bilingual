Class Time & Subjects
上課與學科 · 10 words

The everyday English of lessons — Chinese class, social studies, quizzes, speeches, and the classics.

Word 01Chinese class(n.)國語課
The students are in Chinese class.同學們正在上國語課。
They learn Chinese idioms in Chinese class.他們在國語課學習中文的成語。
Word 02social studies(n.)社會科
The teacher is teaching social studies.這位老師正在教社會科。
The students pay attention to the teacher teaching social studies.老師上社會科時，學生們全神貫注。
Word 03study hall(n.)自修時間
The students are diligently studying in study hall.學生們在自修時間認真讀書。
In study hall the students have to work independently and quietly.在自修時間，學生們必須安靜、獨立地讀書。
Word 04quiz(n.)小考
The students are taking a quiz in Chinese class.同學們正在進行小考。
They listen to questions and write down their answers during the quiz.小考時，他們先聽題目，然後寫下答案。
Word 05correct(v.)改正
The teachers correct the students' work.老師批改同學的作業。
They use a red pen to correct the students' work.他們用紅筆來批改學生的作業。
Word 06prepare(v.)準備
The students prepare for a competition reciting texts in Chinese and Taiwanese.學生們準備一場比賽，用中文和台語背誦、朗讀文本。
The teachers coach the students to help them prepare for the big event.老師指導學生，幫助他們為這場重要比賽做準備。
Word 07speech(n.)演講
The teacher is giving a speech about health.老師正在演講有關健康的主題。
She is giving a speech in the auditorium.她正在禮堂裡演講。
Word 08slideshow(n.)投影片
A slideshow is a helpful teaching tool that teachers can use in class.投影片是老師可以在課堂上使用的好工具。
This teacher is using a slideshow with text and images to teach English.這位老師正用包含文字和圖片的投影片教英語。
Word 09traditional text(n.)傳統經典
The students learn a traditional Chinese text.同學們學習中國傳統經典。
The traditional Chinese text is about how to be a good student.這本中國傳統經典談的是如何當一個好學生。
Word 10professional development(n.)教師專業進修
In Taiwan, all elementary schools have professional development on Wednesday afternoons.在台灣，所有國小老師週三下午都有專業進修。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development.老師在專業進修時學習新技能與教學策略。
