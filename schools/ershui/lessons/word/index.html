Word of the Day

每日一字 · 43 字 · 五大主題

Real English, drawn straight from a day at Ershui.

真實、用得上的英文，全部取材自二水校園的一天。

Word of the Day is a video vocabulary library made with our partner My Culture Connect. Each word comes from something that really happens at school — a sport at recess, an instrument in music class, a job on cleaning duty, a festival on the calendar. Every clip is short, and every word comes with two bilingual example sentences so it sticks on first watch.

Word of the Day（每日一字）是與夥伴人師教育協會共同製作的影片單字庫。每個字都來自校園裡真實發生的事——下課時的運動、音樂課的樂器、打掃時的工作、行事曆上的節慶。每支影片都很短，每個字都配上兩句中英對照例句，讓你看一次就記住。

Sports & Play
Unit 01 · 運動與遊戲 · 13 words

From jump rope and badminton to tug-of-war and the relay race — the words of recess, P.E., and play.

Word 01jump rope(n.)跳繩
Jump rope is a good way to stay active and healthy.跳繩是保持活躍與健康的好方式。
The students enjoy playing jump rope at recess.同學們喜歡在下課時間跳繩。
Word 02badminton(n.)羽毛球
Badminton is a fun sport to play.羽毛球是一項有趣的運動。
The students are playing badminton in pairs.學生們兩人一組打羽毛球。
Word 03tennis(n.)網球
The students are playing tennis on the tennis court.學生們正在網球場上打網球。
Tennis is played with a ball and a racket.打網球要用球和球拍。
Word 04tee-ball(n.)樂樂棒球
In tee-ball, you hit the ball with a bat.在玩樂樂棒時，你用球棒擊球。
To score a point in tee-ball, the runner has to get to the home plate.要在樂樂棒比賽中得分，跑者必須到達本壘。
Word 05basketball court(n.)籃球場
The kids are playing on the basketball court.小朋友在籃球場上打籃球。
Most basketball courts have a hoop on each side.大部分的籃球場兩邊各有一個籃框。
Word 06rollerblade(n.)直排輪
The students are riding around on their rollerblades.學生們溜著直排輪四處穿梭。
They can go forwards and backwards on their rollerblades.他們可以溜著直排輪前進和後退。
Word 07snakeboard(n.)蛇板
The students are practicing riding their snakeboards.學生們正在練習滑蛇板。
The students ride their snakeboards together.學生們一起溜蛇板。
Word 08swing(v.)盪鞦韆
During recess, students like to swing.下課時，學生們喜歡玩盪鞦韆。
You kick your legs up and down to swing.盪鞦韆時，你的腳要上下踢動。
Word 09tug-of-war(n.)拔河
The students have a tug-of-war match.同學們進行一場拔河比賽。
They compete in a tug-of-war match.他們在拔河比賽中互相競爭。
Word 10relay race(n.)接力賽
In the relay race, students pass their batons to the next runners.在接力賽中，同學們把接力棒遞給下一棒。
The relay race is a very exciting event.接力賽是一項非常刺激的比賽。
Word 11team(n.)隊伍
There are two teams playing against each other.有兩支隊伍互相對抗。
The team on offense tries to score, while the team on defense tries to stop them.進攻的隊伍試圖得分，防守的隊伍則設法阻止他們。
Word 12contest(n.)比賽
The students compete in a speaking contest at their school.同學們參與學校辦的演說比賽。
Each student goes up and makes a speech for the contest.比賽中，每位同學上台致詞。
Word 13recess(n.)下課時間
After the bell rings for recess, most students go outside to play.下課鈴聲響起後，大多數學生都到外面玩。
Recess is when kids can play outside, run around, and have fun.下課時間是孩子們可以在外面玩耍、跑跳、開心一下的時候。

Music, Arts & Clubs
Unit 02 · 音樂・藝術・社團 · 8 words

Ershui's signature clubs — Taiko drums, violin, recorder, the Chinese yo-yo, calligraphy, and dance.

Word 01violin(n.)小提琴
Several students are playing the violin.有好幾位學生在演奏小提琴。
The violin is difficult to learn.小提琴很難學。
Word 02drum(n.)鼓
The students practice playing their drums.學生們練習打鼓。
Playing the drum is a popular activity in Taiwan.在台灣，打鼓是一種受歡迎的活動。
Word 03Taiko drum(n.)太鼓
The students practice the Taiko drum.學生們練習打太鼓。
They play the Taiko drum in unison.他們打太鼓時動作整齊劃一。
Word 04recorder(n.)直笛
The recorder is the first musical instrument that many students learn.直笛是很多學生學的第一項樂器。
You can play many songs on a recorder.你可以用直笛吹奏很多歌曲。
Word 05Chinese yo-yo(n.)扯鈴
The Chinese yo-yo is a popular toy many children enjoy.扯鈴是很受小孩子喜歡的玩具。
The students perform tricks with Chinese yo-yos.同學們用扯鈴表演特技。
Word 06calligraphy(n.)書法
The boys and girls are learning traditional Chinese calligraphy.這群男孩和女孩正在學習中國傳統的書法。
To get good at calligraphy, you have to have a lot of patience!書法要寫得好，必須要很有耐心！
Word 07dance(v.)跳舞；律動
Children celebrate Children's Day by dancing.孩子們用舞蹈慶祝兒童節。
Dancing is fun and it is good exercise.跳舞很有趣，也是很好的運動。
Word 08robot(n.)機器人
The students play with a robot in class.同學們在課堂上和一台機器人互動。
The robot plays different videos.這台機器人播放不同的影片。

Class Time & Subjects
Unit 03 · 上課與學科 · 10 words

The everyday English of lessons — Chinese class, social studies, quizzes, speeches, and the classics.

Word 01Chinese class(n.)國語課
The students are in Chinese class.同學們正在上國語課。
They learn Chinese idioms in Chinese class.他們在國語課學習中文的成語。
Word 02social studies(n.)社會科
The teacher is teaching social studies.這位老師正在教社會科。
The students pay attention to the teacher teaching social studies.老師上社會科時，學生們全神貫注。
Word 03study hall(n.)自修時間
The students are diligently studying in study hall.學生們在自修時間認真讀書。
In study hall the students have to work independently and quietly.在自修時間，學生們必須安靜、獨立地讀書。
Word 04quiz(n.)小考
The students are taking a quiz in Chinese class.同學們正在進行小考。
They listen to questions and write down their answers during the quiz.小考時，他們先聽題目，然後寫下答案。
Word 05correct(v.)改正
The teachers correct the students' work.老師批改同學的作業。
They use a red pen to correct the students' work.他們用紅筆來批改學生的作業。
Word 06prepare(v.)準備
The students prepare for a competition reciting texts in Chinese and Taiwanese.學生們準備一場比賽，用中文和台語背誦、朗讀文本。
The teachers coach the students to help them prepare for the big event.老師指導學生，幫助他們為這場重要比賽做準備。
Word 07speech(n.)演講
The teacher is giving a speech about health.老師正在演講有關健康的主題。
She is giving a speech in the auditorium.她正在禮堂裡演講。
Word 08slideshow(n.)投影片
A slideshow is a helpful teaching tool that teachers can use in class.投影片是老師可以在課堂上使用的好工具。
This teacher is using a slideshow with text and images to teach English.這位老師正用包含文字和圖片的投影片教英語。
Word 09traditional text(n.)傳統經典
The students learn a traditional Chinese text.同學們學習中國傳統經典。
The traditional Chinese text is about how to be a good student.這本中國傳統經典談的是如何當一個好學生。
Word 10professional development(n.)教師專業進修
In Taiwan, all elementary schools have professional development on Wednesday afternoons.在台灣，所有國小老師週三下午都有專業進修。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development.老師在專業進修時學習新技能與教學策略。

Around Campus, Safety & Roles
Unit 04 · 校園日常・安全・角色 · 8 words

The jobs and people that keep school running — cleaning, crossing guards, the nurse's office, and water safety.

Word 01sweep(v.)掃地
Every day, students sweep away the fallen leaves on the school grounds.每天學生打掃校園裡的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.台灣的學生打掃校園，保持環境乾淨整齊。
Word 02trash(n.)垃圾
The students are taking out the trash.學生們正要去倒垃圾。
The students have finished taking out the trash.學生們倒完了垃圾。
Word 03crossing guard(n.)導護
The crossing guards are helping the students cross the road.導護人員正在幫助學生過馬路。
The students walk across the street with the crossing guards.學生們在導護人員的陪同下過馬路。
Word 04mow(v.)割草
The man is mowing the grass.這個人正在割草。
You need to mow the grass to keep it short.你需要修剪草坪，讓草維持短短的。
Word 05station(n.)關卡
Students go to different stations around the gym.體育館內設立不同的關卡，讓同學一一造訪。
Each station has an activity to help students practice speaking English.每個關卡都有一項活動，幫助學生練習說英語。
Word 06kindergarteners(n.)幼兒園小朋友
The kindergarteners sing and dance to a song.這些幼兒園小朋友隨著歌曲又唱又跳。
The teacher leads the kindergarteners.老師帶領著這群幼兒園小朋友。
Word 07injury(n.)外傷
Lily goes to the nurse's office to treat her injury after a fall.Lily 跌倒後到保健室處理她的外傷。
The nurse cleans her injury and puts a band-aid on it.護理師清理她的外傷，並貼上 OK 繃。
Word 08water safety(n.)水上安全
The firefighter makes a presentation about water safety.這位消防員進行一場水上安全的宣導。
He explains the importance of water safety.他解釋水上安全的重要性。

Festivals, Votes & Culture
Unit 05 · 節慶・選舉・在地文化 · 4 words

Holidays and traditions, from an Easter egg hunt and spring couplets to class elections and the Taiwanese language.

Word 01egg hunt(n.)復活節找彩蛋
An egg hunt is a popular activity for kids on Easter Day.在復活節當天，找彩蛋是很受小朋友歡迎的活動。
After the egg hunt, students exchange their eggs for prizes.找彩蛋後，同學們用找到的蛋來換獎品。
Word 02spring couplet(n.)春聯
The students write a spring couplet in class.學生們在課堂上書寫春聯。
They use ink and brushes to write spring couplets.他們用毛筆和墨水來寫春聯。
Word 03Taiwanese(n.)台語
The teacher is teaching Taiwanese in the Taiwanese class.這位老師正在台語課裡教台語。
The students learn Taiwanese from a story book.學生們從故事書中學習台語。
Word 04ballot(n.)選票
First the students go into the voting booth to fill out their ballots.首先同學們進入投票亭填寫選票。
Once that's done, they vote by putting their ballots in the ballot box.完成後，他們將選票投入票匭來投票。
