Sports & Play
運動與遊戲 · 13 words

From jump rope and badminton to tug-of-war and the relay race — the words of recess, P.E., and play.

Word 01jump rope(n.)跳繩
Jump rope is a good way to stay active and healthy.跳繩是保持活躍與健康的好方式。
The students enjoy playing jump rope at recess.同學們喜歡在下課時間跳繩。
Word 02badminton(n.)羽毛球
Badminton is a fun sport to play.羽毛球是一項有趣的運動。
The students are playing badminton in pairs.學生們兩人一組打羽毛球。
Word 03tennis(n.)網球
The students are playing tennis on the tennis court.學生們正在網球場上打網球。
Tennis is played with a ball and a racket.打網球要用球和球拍。
Word 04tee-ball(n.)樂樂棒球
In tee-ball, you hit the ball with a bat.在玩樂樂棒時，你用球棒擊球。
To score a point in tee-ball, the runner has to get to the home plate.要在樂樂棒比賽中得分，跑者必須到達本壘。
Word 05basketball court(n.)籃球場
The kids are playing on the basketball court.小朋友在籃球場上打籃球。
Most basketball courts have a hoop on each side.大部分的籃球場兩邊各有一個籃框。
Word 06rollerblade(n.)直排輪
The students are riding around on their rollerblades.學生們溜著直排輪四處穿梭。
They can go forwards and backwards on their rollerblades.他們可以溜著直排輪前進和後退。
Word 07snakeboard(n.)蛇板
The students are practicing riding their snakeboards.學生們正在練習滑蛇板。
The students ride their snakeboards together.學生們一起溜蛇板。
Word 08swing(v.)盪鞦韆
During recess, students like to swing.下課時，學生們喜歡玩盪鞦韆。
You kick your legs up and down to swing.盪鞦韆時，你的腳要上下踢動。
Word 09tug-of-war(n.)拔河
The students have a tug-of-war match.同學們進行一場拔河比賽。
They compete in a tug-of-war match.他們在拔河比賽中互相競爭。
Word 10relay race(n.)接力賽
In the relay race, students pass their batons to the next runners.在接力賽中，同學們把接力棒遞給下一棒。
The relay race is a very exciting event.接力賽是一項非常刺激的比賽。
Word 11team(n.)隊伍
There are two teams playing against each other.有兩支隊伍互相對抗。
The team on offense tries to score, while the team on defense tries to stop them.進攻的隊伍試圖得分，防守的隊伍則設法阻止他們。
Word 12contest(n.)比賽
The students compete in a speaking contest at their school.同學們參與學校辦的演說比賽。
Each student goes up and makes a speech for the contest.比賽中，每位同學上台致詞。
Word 13recess(n.)下課時間
After the bell rings for recess, most students go outside to play.下課鈴聲響起後，大多數學生都到外面玩。
Recess is when kids can play outside, run around, and have fun.下課時間是孩子們可以在外面玩耍、跑跳、開心一下的時候。
