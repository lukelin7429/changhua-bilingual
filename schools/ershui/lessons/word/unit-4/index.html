Around Campus, Safety & Roles
校園日常・安全・角色 · 8 words

The jobs and people that keep school running — cleaning, crossing guards, the nurse's office, and water safety.

Word 01sweep(v.)掃地
Every day, students sweep away the fallen leaves on the school grounds.每天學生打掃校園裡的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.台灣的學生打掃校園，保持環境乾淨整齊。
Word 02trash(n.)垃圾
The students are taking out the trash.學生們正要去倒垃圾。
The students have finished taking out the trash.學生們倒完了垃圾。
Word 03crossing guard(n.)導護
The crossing guards are helping the students cross the road.導護人員正在幫助學生過馬路。
The students walk across the street with the crossing guards.學生們在導護人員的陪同下過馬路。
Word 04mow(v.)割草
The man is mowing the grass.這個人正在割草。
You need to mow the grass to keep it short.你需要修剪草坪，讓草維持短短的。
Word 05station(n.)關卡
Students go to different stations around the gym.體育館內設立不同的關卡，讓同學一一造訪。
Each station has an activity to help students practice speaking English.每個關卡都有一項活動，幫助學生練習說英語。
Word 06kindergarteners(n.)幼兒園小朋友
The kindergarteners sing and dance to a song.這些幼兒園小朋友隨著歌曲又唱又跳。
The teacher leads the kindergarteners.老師帶領著這群幼兒園小朋友。
Word 07injury(n.)外傷
Lily goes to the nurse's office to treat her injury after a fall.Lily 跌倒後到保健室處理她的外傷。
The nurse cleans her injury and puts a band-aid on it.護理師清理她的外傷，並貼上 OK 繃。
Word 08water safety(n.)水上安全
The firefighter makes a presentation about water safety.這位消防員進行一場水上安全的宣導。
He explains the importance of water safety.他解釋水上安全的重要性。
