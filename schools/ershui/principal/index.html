I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Welcome to Ershui Elementary School. Our principal, Chen Ching-te (陳靜德), believes that children are the hope of our future — and that education is the bridge that carries them toward their dreams. After many years as a classroom teacher, a department head, and a county curriculum supervisor, she came to Ershui to build a school full of love, hope, energy, and happiness.

歡迎來到二水國小。本校校長陳靜德相信，孩子是國家社會未來的希望，而教育正是引領孩子邁向理想與未來的橋樑。歷任教師、各處室主任與彰化縣課程督學多年後，她來到二水，要打造一所充滿愛與希望、幸福與活力的學校。

She often shares a line she loves: "Stand on your tiptoes and raise the lamp a little higher — and you can light the way for many more." For her, that is exactly what a principal should do: help every child learn to explore the world, care for people and the environment, and grow into the very best of who they can be.

她常分享一句最喜歡的話：「踮起腳尖把燈提高，可以照亮更多的人。」對她而言，校長就是那個把燈提高的人——幫助每個孩子學會探索世界、關懷人群與環境，適性揚才、成為最好的自己。

Principal Chen also led the whole Ershui family — from the principal to our youngest students — in a school-wide project where everyone stepped up to speak English on camera.

陳校長也帶領整個二水大家庭——從校長到最年幼的學生——一起完成了全校總動員的計畫，每個人都站到鏡頭前開口說英語。

"Stand on your tiptoes and raise the lamp a little higher — and you can light the way for many more."
「踮起腳尖把燈提高，可以照亮更多的人。」 — 陳靜德 校長
II.

Her Vision for Ershui

辦學理念 · 六個信念

Six beliefs that guide the school.

六個引領二水的辦學信念。

1
Every Child at the Center
以學生為主體，落實全人教育

Education serves each child's learning and growth. With multiple-intelligence teaching that is personalized and adaptive, Ershui develops character, creativity, and care for those who need it most — the whole child.

2
The Will to Learn
激發自發學習，培養自學能力

A lifelong learner is the best-prepared person. Reading builds the foundation; hands-on exploration lights the spark — so children learn to learn, for themselves and for life.

3
A Positive School Culture
營造正向文化，優質服務團隊

Open communication and positive leadership bring colleagues together into a warm, willing team — one whose whole purpose is to support good teaching, day after day.

4
Growing Great Teachers
提升教師專業，重建師道尊嚴

Listening to teachers, supporting their growth through workshops and shared lessons, and honoring their expertise — restoring the dignity and standing of the teaching profession.

5
A Safe, Warm, Lasting Campus
安全友善、永續的學習環境

Resources used wisely build a safe, beautiful, and sustainable campus — a warm, human place where every corner can teach and every child can learn happily.

6
School, Family & Community as One
學校、家長、社區生命共同體

A principal cannot teach behind closed doors. Parents, alumni, and the wider community are the school's strongest partners — together, one living community around every child.
