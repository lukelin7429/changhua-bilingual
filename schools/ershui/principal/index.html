I.

A Word of Welcome

校長的話
二二水國小 · 校長

Welcome to Ershui Elementary School. For more than a century, this small school at the foot of Bagua Mountain has been the heart of our township — a place where children of farming families learn, play, and grow up to carry the name of Ershui out into the world.

歡迎來到二水國小。一個多世紀以來，這所位於八卦山腳的小學一直是我們鄉鎮的中心——農家的孩子在這裡學習、玩耍、成長，長大後帶著「二水」之名走向世界。

Our principal believes that a country school should hold the same high horizons as any school in the city. That belief shows up in everything we do: a strong reading culture, music and arts clubs for every child, and — with our partner My Culture Connect — English woven into the ordinary moments of the school day.

本校校長相信，偏鄉學校應懷抱與都會學校一樣寬廣的視野。這份信念體現在我們所做的每一件事上：深耕的閱讀文化、讓每個孩子都能參與的音樂與藝術社團，以及——在夥伴人師教育協會的協助下——將英語融入校園日常的每一個平凡時刻。

In the short video below, our principal introduces our school in English — part of a school-wide project in which the whole Ershui family, from the principal to our youngest students, steps up to speak English on camera.

在下方的短片中，校長以英文親自介紹我們的學校——這是一項全校性的計畫，從校長到最年幼的學生，整個二水大家庭都站到鏡頭前開口說英語。
