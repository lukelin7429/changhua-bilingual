I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話
謝謝清森 校長

Principal Hsieh Ching-sen is a son of Pitou. He graduated from this very school as part of its 13th class — and years later, he came home to lead the campus that shaped him. Few principals can say they walked the same corridors as their students once did.

謝清森校長是道地的埤頭人，也是埤頭國中第 13 屆校友。多年後，他回到這所形塑了自己的母校擔任校長——能說「曾走過和學生一樣的走廊」的校長並不多。

At the school's 55th graduation, he urged students to "step forward and bravely face the challenges ahead," echoing the words of his own teachers. On the basketball court, in the classroom, and at every school event, he returns to one steady idea: character first, effort always.

在本校第 55 屆畢業典禮上，他勉勵畢業生「勇敢面對未來的挑戰」，將昔日恩師的叮嚀傳給下一代。無論在球場、教室或每一場校園活動，他始終回到同一個信念：先成人，再成材；盡力，便無憾。

He hopes every Pitou student will draw the very best from books and carry it for life — and that the school will keep growing as a warm place where teachers and students learn and grow together.

他期盼每位埤頭的孩子都能從書本中汲取精華、受用一生，也期盼學校持續成為一個溫暖的園地，讓親師生一起學習、一起成長。

"Win without arrogance; lose without despair."
勝不驕，敗不餒。 — 謝清森校長勉勵學生的話
