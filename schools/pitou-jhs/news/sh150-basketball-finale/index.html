One Last Battle on the Court114學年度健促SH150三對三鬥牛賽・九年級畢業生的最後一戰

The last piece of junior-high life, put in place by their own hands. On the courts of Pitou Junior High, this was more than a 3-on-3 basketball tournament — it was the ninth graders’ final school sports event before graduation, a “last battle” they fought for themselves.

The hottest kind of youth, running one more time across a familiar court.

Every sprint, every sharp pass, every clean shot was met by teammates and classmates cheering themselves hoarse from the sidelines. For three years, these students had studied together in the same classrooms and laughed together across the same campus — and today, on the court that knew them best, they wrote the final line of their junior-high story in sweat and effort.

Boys · 男子組
906 · Champion
冠軍 906・亞軍 903
Girls · 女子組
905 · Champion
冠軍 905・亞軍 901

Thank you to every opponent worth playing against, and to the teammates who had each other’s backs. Win or lose, the bond built shoulder to shoulder on this court is one of graduation’s finest gifts — and as Pitou’s own motto goes: win without arrogance, lose without despair. Let this fire keep burning long after graduation.

中文原文 · Chinese

114 學年度健促 SH150 三對三鬥牛賽
男子組：冠軍 906、亞軍 903
女子組：冠軍 905、亞軍 901

國中生涯的最後一塊拼圖，由我們自己拼上！今天在埤頭國中的球場上，正上演著一場屬於九年級畢業生的「終局之戰」——不僅僅是一場三對三籃球賽，更是大家在埤中留下的最後一場運動賽事。

看著場上奮力奔跑、汗水淋漓的身影，每一次精準的傳球、每一次帥氣的投籃，還有場邊破表、聲嘶力竭的加油聲——這就是我們最滾燙的青春！

過去的三個寒暑，我們一起在教室裡苦讀、在校園裡歡笑；而今天，我們要在最熟悉的球場上，用汗水與拼勁，為國中生涯畫下最完美的句點。

謝謝場上可敬的對手，也謝謝身旁最挺的隊友。無論今天的勝負如何，這份一起並肩作戰的感動、這段在埤中留下的精彩回憶，都將成為我們畢業禮物中最珍貴的一項。

畢業倒數，我們的熱血，永不熄滅！
