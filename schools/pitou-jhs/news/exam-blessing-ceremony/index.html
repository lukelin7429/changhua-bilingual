Blessings Before the Big Test謝校長偕師長赴合興宮・為九年級考生祈福

On an auspicious morning, Principal Hsieh Ching-sen and Community Association Chairman Hong led the school’s administrative staff and ninth-grade homeroom teachers to Hexing Temple, the guardian shrine of Pitou Township, to pray for every one of this year’s ninth-grade test-takers.

A quiet wish, carried skyward in temple smoke.

Standing before the incense burner, the group offered incense on behalf of students who couldn’t be there themselves — asking for calm hearts, clear heads, and the confidence to show what years of study had built.

With the Comprehensive Assessment Program for Junior High School Students (國中教育會考) just five days away — on May 16 and 17 — the temple visit carried one simple wish: that every Pitou ninth grader would walk into the exam hall steady, and walk out proud of what they’d given.

中文原文 · Chinese

115 年 05 月 11 日（一）吉日吉時，謝校長及洪會長帶領學校行政同仁及九年級導師，至本鄉合興宮為所有九年級考生拜拜祈福。

祈願所有九年級同學在 5 月 16、17 兩天的教育會考中，全力以赴、發揮實力，考取佳績！
