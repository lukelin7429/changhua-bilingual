International Bilingual Fun!

夏日樂學營隊－國際雙語樂學趣．精彩花絮

Wunde Elementary's Summer Learning Camp keeps getting better! Local and foreign teachers teamed up to design a series of fun, interactive international-education lessons in English — helping children pick up English naturally while having fun, and broadening their global view. ✨

文德國小夏日樂學營隊持續精彩！在中師與外師攜手合作下，精心設計一系列充滿趣味與互動的國際教育雙語課程，讓孩子在歡樂中自然學習英語，拓展國際視野。✨

The camp's lessons were rich and varied, including: 🥽 VR headset experiences — exploring the world through virtual reality for immersive learning. 🌏 International culture lessons — learning about the culture, life, and characteristics of different countries. 🏃 Bilingual physical activities — moving and speaking English at the same time. 🎭 Roleplay scenarios — building English expression through interactive dialogue. 🎨 Arts and crafts — combining creativity and culture, learning bilingually by hand.

營隊課程豐富又多元，包括：🥽 VR頭盔體驗－透過虛擬實境探索世界，開啟沉浸式學習。🌏 國際文化課程－認識不同國家的文化、生活與特色，培養國際觀。🏃‍♂️ 雙語體能活動－邊運動、邊說英語，讓語言融入生活。🎭 情境模擬－透過角色扮演與互動對話，提升英語表達與溝通能力。🎨 藝術手作－結合創意與文化元素，在動手做中學習雙語。

In this rich variety of lessons, the children bravely spoke up in English and happily experienced different cultures — making international education more than just knowledge, but a journey full of laughter and discovery. 🌈

孩子們在豐富多元的課程中，勇敢開口說英語、快樂體驗不同文化，也讓國際教育不只是知識，更是一段充滿歡笑與探索的學習旅程。🌈

Wunde continues to build a vibrant bilingual learning environment, letting children play to learn, do to learn, and connect with the world this summer!

文德持續打造充滿活力的雙語學習環境，讓孩子在暑假中玩中學、做中學、與世界接軌！
