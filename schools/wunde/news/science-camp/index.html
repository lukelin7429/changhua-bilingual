Hands-On Science, Full of Wonder

夏日樂學營隊－文德手作玩科學．精彩花絮

Summer isn't just for play — it's the perfect season to explore science! Wunde Elementary's "Hands-On Science" camp guided students to build and think through one hands-on natural science experiment after another, each one full of surprises.

暑假不只是玩樂，更是探索科學的好時機！文德國小「手作玩科學」營隊，在老師精心設計與引導下，帶著孩子們動手做、動腦想，完成一項項有趣又充滿驚喜的自然科學實驗。

From observing a phenomenon, to asking questions, to testing it with their own hands — again and again, students discovered: "Science is all around us!" 👀💡

從觀察現象、提出問題，到親手操作與驗證，孩子們在一次次實驗中發現：「原來科學就在我們身邊！」👀💡

Rather than simply telling children the answer, what matters more is letting them do it themselves — observe for themselves, and find the answer for themselves. Hands-on experience builds curiosity, observation, critical thinking, and problem-solving ability.

比起直接告訴孩子答案，更重要的是讓孩子自己動手、自己觀察、自己找答案。透過實作體驗，培養孩子的好奇心、觀察力、思考力與解決問題的能力。

Science isn't only textbook knowledge — you have to get your hands dirty to really learn something. This summer, Wunde's kids played hard, and learned just as much!

科學不只是課本上的知識，動手做，才能真正玩出學問！這個暑假，文德孩子玩得開心，也學得充實！
