Endless Creativity, Full of Color!

暑期美術營．創意無限，藝術滿點！

Wunde Elementary's Summer Art Camp was in full swing, with students freely using their imagination to complete one creative artwork after another under their teachers' guidance! ✨

文德國小暑期美術營熱鬧展開，孩子們盡情發揮想像力，在老師的帶領下完成一件件充滿創意的藝術作品！✨

The camp combined a variety of materials and rich themes — from color pairing, to 3D creations, to creative design — students turned their wildest ideas into one-of-a-kind works through making and thinking.

營隊結合多元媒材與豐富主題，從色彩搭配、立體創作到創意設計，孩子們透過動手做、動腦想，將天馬行空的想法化為獨一無二的作品。

Every piece of work showed the children's creativity, patience, and sense of beauty; every act of creating was a process of self-expression and growth. Seeing the children's proud smiles as they finished their pieces was the most beautiful scene of the whole summer!

每一件作品，都展現了孩子們的創意、耐心與美感；每一次創作，都是一次自我表達與成長的過程。看著孩子們完成作品時臉上充滿成就感的笑容，就是這個暑假最美的風景！

Thank you to the teachers for planning such wonderful lessons, letting the children explore through art and learn through creating — a summer as rich as it was colorful!

感謝老師用心規劃精彩課程，讓孩子們在藝術中探索、在創作中學習，度過充實又充滿色彩的暑假！
