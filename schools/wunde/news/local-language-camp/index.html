Local Language, Telling Our Hometown's Story

夏日樂學營隊，本土語言話家鄉．精彩花絮

Wunde Elementary's Summer Learning Camp kept the excitement going! This year's theme was "Summer Learning Together — Local Language, Telling Our Hometown's Story," using rich and fun lessons to help children get to know local culture with laughter, and feel the charm of Taiwanese. ✨

文德國小夏日樂學營隊精彩不間斷！今年以「暑假一同樂學趣－本土語言話家鄉」為主題，透過豐富又有趣的課程，讓孩子在歡笑中認識本土文化，感受臺語的魅力。✨

The camp's content was rich and varied, including: 🎵 Children's songs — singing the flavor of Taiwanese, feeling the beauty of traditional nursery rhymes. 🗨️ Taiwanese banter — experiencing the fun and wit of the Taiwanese language. 🪀 Traditional Taiwanese toys — revisiting classic games. 🎨 Hands-on crafts — combining creativity and culture. 📖 Reading short Taiwanese passages aloud — building local language ability and confidence.

營隊內容精彩多元，包括：🎵 囡仔歌－唱出臺語韻味，感受傳統童謠之美。🗨️ 答喙鼓－體驗臺語語言的趣味與智慧。🪀 臺灣童玩－重溫傳統遊戲，感受童年的純真樂趣。🎨 手作課程－結合創意與文化，動手做、快樂學。📖 台語短文唸讀－培養孩子的本土語文能力與表達自信。

On top of that, the children went out into the community for a hands-on experience of traditional glove puppetry — getting to know puppet-show culture and admiring the art of puppeteering, moving from the classroom into real life, and feeling the depth of Taiwan's traditional culture in a real setting. 👏

此外，孩子們更走進社區，實地體驗傳統布袋戲，認識布袋戲文化、欣賞操偶藝術，從課堂走向生活，在真實情境中感受臺灣傳統文化的深厚底蘊。👏

Thank you to the instructors, community partners, and everyone who helped make this happen — letting children learn happily this summer and get to know their hometown's culture more deeply, so the seeds of local language and culture keep sprouting and growing in their hearts!

感謝授課老師、社區夥伴及熱心人士的用心安排，讓孩子們在暑假中快樂學習、深入認識家鄉文化，讓本土語言與文化的種子在孩子心中持續萌芽、茁壯！
