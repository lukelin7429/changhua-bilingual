I.

Welcome

歡迎來到文德國小

Where nature soars, and learning takes flight.

大自然翱翔，學習展翅之處。

Wunde sits at "半線" of the sunrise — perched atop Bagua Mountain, it is the highest-elevation school in Changhua County. Grey-faced buzzard hawks trace wide arcs overhead, purple crow butterflies drift through the low garden shrubs, and beneath the shade of silver trumpet trees, a few dogs doze in the warm sun while cyclists pass by on quiet mountain roads.

文德在「半線」之東，太陽躍升之處，立足在卦山之巔，是彰化的最高學府。灰面鵟鷹展翅劃過穹頂，紫斑蝶在林間矮灌木中翩翩起舞，小葉欖仁樹下幾隻狗兒在暖陽中打盹，騎車的人們悠然經過。

The surrounding hillside community lives simply, largely by farming — growing pineapples and plums — and greets visitors with easy warmth. Nearby, the Tiaoshui Ancient Trail, Fengshan Temple, the Mother-and-Child Camphor Trees, and Monkey Exploration Well draw hikers year-round, while the campus itself stays lush and fragrant in every season, its grounds carefully tended by staff who treat ecology and English education as shared priorities.

學區居民多務農為生，生活簡樸而熱情，山區盛產鳳梨與梅子；鄰近挑水古道、鳳山寺、母子樟樹、猴探井等景點終年遊客不絕。校園本身四季花木扶疏、鳥語花香，同仁們也致力發展生態與英語課程，期盼帶給孩子更多元、更創新的學習樣貌。

In the Maker Workshop, students learn woodworking, laser cutting, and handcraft under expert guidance — turning an ordinary block of wood into something made with care by their own hands. Ecology lessons bring students face to face with the wild neighbours who share the campus: the hawks overhead and the free-roaming companion dogs who call Wunde home.

創客工坊中，學生在專業指導下學習木工、雷射切割與手作工藝，讓一塊平凡的木頭經過雙手變成充滿溫度的作品。生態課程則讓孩子親近與校園共處的鄰居——翱翔天際的鵟鷹，以及自由奔跑、與文德師生一同成長的陪伴犬。

English is treated as a bridge to the wider world here, brought to life through interactive dialogue, simulated international scenarios, and everyday cross-cultural practice. Music and dance fill every corner of campus too — from jazz drums and ocarina to ukulele — giving students a rhythm-filled outlet to find and express themselves.

英語在這裡不只是課程，更是通往世界的橋樑，透過互動對話、國際情境模擬與日常跨文化溝通融入生活。舞蹈與音樂的旋律則流淌校園每個角落，從爵士鼓、陶笛到烏克麗麗，讓孩子在節奏中找到自我表達的方式。

Welcome to Wunde Elementary School · 歡迎來到文德國小

Where every child finds their own path — not only to knowledge, but to a way of living and speaking with a wide, colourful world.
每個孩子都能在此找到屬於自己的道路——不僅學習知識，更學會如何與這個多姿多彩的世界對話。
Rooted at the mountain's summit, alive with green
立足山巔，綠意盎然的校園

Nearly every corner of Wunde's hillside campus opens onto shade trees, garden paths, and wide open sky — a setting built as much for wandering and watching hawks glide as for sitting in class.

文德依山而建，校園裡處處是林蔭步道與開闊天空——不只是教室，更是孩子觀察鵟鷹翱翔、探索自然的角落。

II.

Life at Wunde

文德的日常

Ecology, craft, reading, and play — four ways Wunde students grow.

生態、創作、閱讀與玩樂——文德孩子成長的四個方向。

Ecology Trail
生態步道

Quiet boardwalks thread through the hillside greenery, where students learn to observe and respect the wild neighbours who share their mountain campus.

Rooftop Lookout
屋頂瞭望台

From the school's rooftop lookout — the highest point of any campus in the county — students can watch hawks trace slow circles against the sky.

Reading Pavilion
閱讀迴廊

An open-air reading pavilion, its mosaic mural reading "Wunde's heart, Dazhu's spirit," invites students to slow down with a book among the trees.

Playground
遊戲天地

Slides, climbing frames, and open lawns give every student room to play hard between lessons — because for Principal Hsieh, play is a child's natural talent.
