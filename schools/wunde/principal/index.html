I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Hsieh Ting-Li leads Wunde Elementary School with a simple guiding image: education as a perfect circle, drawn out with goodness at its center and sincerity as its radius.

謝庭秝校長以一個簡單而深刻的意象帶領文德國小：把教育看作一個完美的圓，以良善為基點，真誠為半徑。

She sees the school community as three partners working together toward the same goal — parents, teachers, and students each playing an essential part in making Wunde a place children genuinely want to come to.

她將學校社群視為朝同一目標努力的三方夥伴——家長、教師與學生各自扮演關鍵角色，共同讓文德成為孩子真心嚮往的地方。

Based on goodness, with sincerity as the radius — using "love" to roll the perfect educational circle.
以良善為基點，真誠為半徑；用「愛」滾動完美教育圓。

II.

Vision & Goals

辦學理念與願景

Building a learning paradise children look forward to.

打造一所孩子嚮往的學習心樂園。

Parents · 家長
Parents as the school's best educational partners — sincere collaboration between family and teachers, working together to optimize every child's growth.
家長是教育最佳合夥人，親師真誠攜手，以優化學生成長為目標，成為學校最堅實的後盾。
Teachers · 教師
Teachers as the soul of instruction — building consensus and a sense of home among staff, because happy teachers raise happy students.
教師是教學靈魂，凝聚共識與向心力，營造學校為家的氛圍——有快樂的老師團隊，才有快樂的學生。
Students · 學生
Students at the center of it all — igniting hope in every child and letting their talents run free, with play as the key to future-ready learning.
學生是辦學核心，點燃每個孩子的希望，讓天賦自由；透過玩課程體驗，培養孩子適應未來的關鍵素養。
To cultivate future global citizens who enjoy learning, think innovatively, and display confidence — and to make Wunde a truly fun place to learn.
培養樂在學習、創新思考、展現自信的未來世界公民，讓文德國小成為一所好玩的學校。
