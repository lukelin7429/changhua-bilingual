I.

Bilingual at Fusing

福興雙語校園 · 一頁全覽
At Fusing, English isn't just a subject — it's the classroom we teach in, the broadcast we wake up to, and the language we use to talk with friends in Japan and Singapore.
在福興，英文不只是一個科目——它是我們上課的教室、早晨的廣播，以及與日本、新加坡朋友對話的共同語言。

This single page brings together everything bilingual at Fusing — our Classroom English video curriculum, the bilingual Campus Announcements, our international exchanges with partner schools abroad, and a direct link to the school's real-time bulletin system for parents and students.

本頁匯整福興所有雙語校園資源：課室英語影音、雙語校園廣播、與夥伴學校的國際交流，以及給家長與學生的即時公告入口——一頁全覽，免於跳轉。

III.

Bilingual Announcements

廣播英語 · 校園四處室自我介紹

Thirteen short episodes that introduce the school's four offices — Academic Affairs (5), Student Affairs (3), General Affairs, and Counselling — in both English and Chinese, hosted by Sarah Thomas and Susan Rose. Built for campus broadcasting, but equally useful as a quick orientation tour of any junior high.

13 集短片，由 Sarah Thomas 與 Susan Rose以英中雙語介紹學校四處室——教務處（5 集）、學務處（3 集）、總務處、輔導室。為校園廣播情境設計，但也是任何國中校園導覽的最佳起點。

IV.

English with the World

出走校園 · 與國際夥伴學校交流

Putting English to use the way it was meant to be used — speaking with someone from another culture. Through both online live sessions and in-person visits, Fusing students build real friendships with peers in Japan and Singapore.

讓英文回到它最該被使用的地方：與不同文化的人對話。透過線上同步課程與實體出訪雙軌，福興學生與日本、新加坡夥伴學校的學生建立真正的友誼。
