I.

Bilingual at Fusing

福興雙語校園 · 一頁全覽
At Fusing, English isn't just a subject — it's the classroom we teach in, the broadcast we wake up to, and the language we use to talk with friends in Japan and Singapore.
在福興，英文不只是一個科目——它是我們上課的教室、早晨的廣播，以及與日本、新加坡朋友對話的共同語言。

Below: 30 Classroom English video lessons organised into 10 teaching chapters (every video links directly to YouTube — pick the one you need), 13 bilingual campus announcements grouped by office, and our international exchange partners in Japan and Singapore.

IV.

English with the World

出走校園 · 與國際夥伴學校交流

Putting English to use the way it was meant to be used — speaking with someone from another culture. Through both online live sessions and in-person visits, Fusing students build real friendships with peers in Japan and Singapore.

讓英文回到它最該被使用的地方：與不同文化的人對話。透過線上同步課程與實體出訪雙軌，福興學生與日本、新加坡夥伴學校的學生建立真正的友誼。

🇯🇵
Japan Partner Schools
日本夥伴學校交流
Online live sessions and in-person school visits give Fusing students real conversation partners — and real reasons to keep speaking English after the lesson ends.
🇸🇬
Singapore Partner Schools
新加坡夥伴學校交流
A multicultural English-speaking neighbour — perfect for Fusing students to compare campus life, food, holidays, and study habits with peers their own age.
"A bilingual school is a school where English never has to be looked up."
「真正的雙語學校，是英文不需要被特地查找的那種。」
