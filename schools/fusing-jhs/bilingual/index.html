I.

Bilingual at Fusing

三條英語主軸合而為一
Three English threads run through every day at Fusing — the English we teach, the English we broadcast, and the bilingual bulletins that keep our community connected.
福興校園的每一天，由三條英語主軸交織而成——我們教的英語、我們播的英語，以及把社群串連起來的雙語公告。

This hub brings together three resources we treat as one: the Classroom English video series for teachers and students, the bilingual English Life announcements that ring through the campus, and the Announcements bulletin board that connects every family to school news in real time.

本頁匯整本校三條校園日常主軸：給師生的課室英語影音、響徹校園廣播的雙語英語生活，以及即時連結每位家長的校園公告系統——三者合而為一，呈現福興最真實的校園節奏。
