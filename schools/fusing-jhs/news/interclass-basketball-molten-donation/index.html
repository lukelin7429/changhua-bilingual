Buzzer-Beaters and a Generous Assist: Interclass Basketball Finals

班際籃球比賽 · 佐儀股份有限公司致贈籃球

February 13, 2026 · 115/2/13

Fusing's ninth graders spent last semester battling it out in an interclass basketball tournament. After two months of elimination rounds, the whole school celebrated together, tossing basketballs into the air in a joyful send-off to the finals.

Class 902 and Class 905 met in the championship game — a tight, nail-biting contest that came down to a free-throw shootout to decide the winner, with the whole gym packed and watching.

In the end, Class 902 took the boys' division title, while Class 903 claimed the girls' division championship. The celebration was made even sweeter by a generous donation: Molten distributor Zuoyi Co., Ltd. gave the school 40 new basketballs.

At the closing Championship Award Ceremony, alumni association chair Shih Chin-hsing presented two commemorative Slam Dunk basketballs to the winning classes — a fitting finish to a season built on teamwork and school spirit.

中文原文 · Chinese

九年級班級於上學期舉辦了班際籃球比賽，經過兩個月的層層比賽，全校師生在體育館歡欣鼓舞，將籃球拋向空中，為總決賽熱情送行。

最後由 902 和 905 兩個班級進行冠亞軍賽，緊張又刺激的比賽過程，兩班比分不相上下，最後採用投籃得分的方式決定冠亞軍，全場座無虛席。

最終由 902 班獲得班際籃球賽男生組冠軍，903 班則獲得女生組冠軍殊榮。這份喜悅還多了一份驚喜——佐儀股份有限公司（MOLTEN）慷慨捐贈學校 40 顆籃球。

在最後的頒獎典禮上，校友會理事長施進興學長捐贈兩顆灌籃高手紀念球給獲獎的班級，為這個充滿團隊精神與校園向心力的球季畫下完美句點。
