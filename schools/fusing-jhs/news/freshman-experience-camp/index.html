Fun and Learning: Fusing Hosts Future Students Camp

雙語與科學交織：福興國中舉辦國小新生體驗營

January 17, 2026 · 115/1/17

English

In December, Fusing Junior High School held a two-day "Freshman Experience Camp" for elementary school students. We invited foreign teachers to lead fun English classes. During the first week, the theme was "Sports and the Olympics," where students learned English words for different sports. In the second week, the theme was "Food." It was great to see the children introduce themselves confidently in English and enjoy the activities.

The camp also featured two exciting science workshops. Students used the Canva website to design their own patterns and made them into unique buttons. We also invited a teacher from Changhua Senior High School to show us how to build electric motors. Through this hands-on class, the children learned the secrets of how electricity and magnets work. Everyone went home with new knowledge, creative works, and big smiles!

中文

福興國中於十二月舉辦了為期兩週的「國小新生體驗營」，利用週六上午帶領孩子們提前探索國中校園生活。學校特別邀請外語講師全程以全英語授課，第一週以「奧運體育」為主題，讓孩子在律動中學習各種運動項目的英文單字；第二週則以「美食」為核心，學生們大方地使用英文自我介紹並積極融入互動，在歡樂的氛圍中提升口語表達的自信心。

除了充實的雙語課程，營隊更安排了兩場跨領域科學試驗。學生們首先結合數位科技，利用 Canva 網站設計獨具特色的個人圖樣，再親手壓製成專屬徽章。此外，學校也邀請到彰化高中的老師蒞臨指導，帶領孩子們製作電磁馬達，從實作中觀察並理解電磁運作的科學原理。兩天的營隊課程內容豐富、動靜皆宜，讓每位參與的學童不僅帶走精美的作品，更收穫了滿滿的知識與成就感。
