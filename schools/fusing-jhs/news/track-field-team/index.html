Run for Glory: Join the Fusing Track and Field Team

超越自我：福興國中田徑隊，在賽道上淬鍊品格與勇氣

March 26, 2026 · 115/3/26

English

Track and field is known as the "Mother of Sports." It combines running, jumping, and throwing to improve body coordination and mental strength. At Fusing Junior High School, our team focuses on professional training for all events. Sprints help students develop explosive power and focus, while long-distance running builds endurance and a strong will. Our athletes have won many awards in national competitions and built a solid foundation for their future sports careers.

We believe that character and teamwork are just as important as speed. Through relay races and daily practice, students learn how to cooperate, support each other, and stay humble. Our goal is to train students to be brave, calm, and respectful, both on and off the field. We welcome all students who are passionate about sports to join the Fusing Track and Field team. Let’s run together and become the best version of ourselves!

中文

田徑被譽為「運動之母」，是結合跑、跳、擲的基礎競技，不僅能全面鍛鍊身體協調性，更是淬鍊心智的最佳途徑。在福興國中田徑隊，我們針對不同專項進行專業訓練：短跑著重爆發力與專注力的突破，長跑則考驗心肺耐力與堅韌意志。透過紮實的校內常規訓練與寒暑假專項加強，學生不但在縣內及全國賽道上屢創佳績，更在國中階段奠定了深厚的體能基礎。

比起獎牌，我們更看重孩子「品格」與「團隊精神」的養成。田徑看似個人競技，實則透過接力賽的默契配合與隊友間的良性競爭，學會包容、合作與感恩。我們致力於培養勝不驕、敗不餒的運動家精神，讓孩子面對高壓賽事時依然冷靜沉著。無論是國小畢業生或想發掘潛能的國中生，都歡迎加入福興田徑隊，與我們一同挑戰自我，在田徑場上奔馳發光！
