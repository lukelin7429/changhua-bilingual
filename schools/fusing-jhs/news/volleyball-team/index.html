Legacy of Passion: Fusing Volleyball Team

熱血傳承：福興國中排球隊，接住每一個挑戰，永不放棄！

March 26, 2026 · 115/3/26

English

Volleyball is a dialog of trust and teamwork. Every pass, set, and spike is an extension of shared spirit. There are no individual heroes; only the bright light of a united team. The Fusing Junior High School Volleyball Team lives by the motto: "The ball never drops, and we never give up!" With every sweat and game, we train our bodies to be strong and our minds to stay calm and brave under pressure.

Now in its 13th generation, our team has a glorious history. The most touching part is our alumni. Whenever they have time, graduated players return to school to practice with and support their younger teammates. This powerful bond of "revolutionary friendship" is the secret to our success. We are also honored to perform at the anniversary of nearby Xishi Elementary School. We welcome all students passionate about volleyball to join the Fusing family. Let’s work together and shine on the court!

中文

排球，是一場關於信任與默契的對話。每一次傳球、舉球與扣殺，都是彼此心意的延伸；場上沒有個人英雄，只有同心協力的團隊光芒。福興國中排球隊秉持著「球不落地、永不放棄」的精神理念，在瞬息萬變的球場上保持冷靜，用汗水與堅持，將每一次跌倒磨練成面對挑戰的勇氣。

這支擁有輝煌歷史的隊伍已邁入第十三代。最感人的是，已畢業的學長學姊們總是在時間允許時回到母校，與學弟妹切磋球技，這種強大的「革命情感」正是福興排球隊屢創佳績的關鍵。鄰近的西勢國小校慶時，也常邀請排球隊前往表演，展現排球運動的魅力。我們相信只要堅持就能超越自我，歡迎每一位對排球有興趣的學生加入福興排球隊，和我們一起穩穩前進、發光發熱！
