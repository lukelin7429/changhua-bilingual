Space is Fun! When Snow White Meets Sun Wukong

太空科技變有趣！當白雪公主遇見孫悟空，開啟未來的「千里眼」

April 18, 2026 · 115/4/18

English

On April 18, 2026, our school hosted an exciting "Space Science Lecture"! To help elementary students explore the mysteries of the universe, we invited "Space Uncle" Mr. Hsu Han-Pin to share his knowledge about low-orbit satellites and AI technology.

Is space technology hard to understand? Mr. Hsu used fun metaphors: GIS mapping and weather apps are like Sun Wukong’s "thousand-mile eyes," and AI is like the "Magic Mirror" from Snow White. From Google Maps to weather forecasts, everything depends on satellites in space! He also talked about the Artemis II moon mission, encouraging students to study hard in math and science. He told us: "If you master space technology, you master the future!"

中文

115年4月18日，校園裡展開了一場別開生面的「太空科普講座」！為了帶領國小高年級的小朋友探索宇宙奧秘，特別邀請到人稱「太空叔叔」的許漢賓會長，以超生動的方式介紹低軌衛星、AI 與遙測應用。

誰說太空科技很遙遠？許會長把現代科技比喻成童話：手機裡的 GIS 遙測和氣象功能，就像孫悟空的「千里眼」與「順風耳」；而無所不知的 AI 搜尋，就像白雪公主裡的「魔鏡」。原來，我們生活中的地圖導航、氣象預報，背後都靠著太空中各司其職的衛星。許會長更分享了最新的阿提米絲二號（Artemis II）登月計畫，鼓勵孩子們學好英文、數理與科技，因為掌握了太空，就掌握了未來的鑰匙！
