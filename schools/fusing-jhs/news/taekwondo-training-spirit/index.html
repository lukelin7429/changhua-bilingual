More Than Kicks and Punches: What Taekwondo Really Teaches

手腳並用的修煉 · 跆拳道教會孩子的不只是自保

March 16, 2026 · 115/3/16

Taekwondo is a full-body sport that trains flexibility, coordination, and balance while building endurance, strength, speed, and sharp reflexes. Performing under pressure — whether in a demonstration or a real competition — teaches students to think on their feet, stay composed, and grow more confident. For children and for women especially, that means having real tools to protect themselves.

Fusing Junior High's Taekwondo Team has trained here for years, consistently placing at county and national competitions — including a 14-year winning streak as County Cup team champions, plus national titles and multiple individual national youth championships.

The program has sent several athletes on to represent Taiwan at the national and youth national team level, earning medals at competitions from Hualien to the county championships.

Behind every medal is hours of disciplined training — students practicing in full sparring gear, resting between rounds, and pushing each other to improve.

Beyond regular practice, the team travels for training camps during winter and summer breaks — sometimes to other schools, sometimes abroad — trading techniques with new training partners before setting off with the school flag held high.

Elementary and junior high students, including those outside the sports program, are welcome to join and learn how to protect themselves. The Fusing Taekwondo Team welcomes you.

中文原文 · Chinese

跆拳道是一種手腳並用的綜合性運動，鍛鍊身體四肢的靈活性、協調性、平衡性，增強耐力、力量、速度與靈敏反應。無論是演出或實戰，都能培養孩子隨機應變的能力，使孩子們不怯場，增強自信心。特別是對兒童及女性而言，即使孤身一人遭遇危險，也能擁有自保的能力。

福興國中跆拳道隊深耕多年，在縣內及全國賽中屢創佳績，締造縣長盃14 連霸團體冠軍的成績，更在全國中等學校運動會榮獲全國冠軍，青少年組也分別奪下第一名的佳績。

培育出多位少年、青少年國家代表隊選手，從花蓮到縣賽都屢屢奪牌，為縣為國家爭取最高榮譽。

每一面獎牌的背後，都是紀律嚴明的訓練時光——學生穿著完整護具對練、中場休息、互相砥礪進步。

跆拳道隊除了平常的校內練習之外，寒暑假期間更會安排移地訓練及出國訓練，讓學生在與他校交流中互相觀摩切磋，出發前總會高舉校旗、精神抖擻。

有興趣的國小同學及國中普通班學生都歡迎加入跆拳隊，學習如何保護自己，福興國中跆拳隊歡迎大家的加入。
