I.

Welcome

歡迎來到快官

A garden campus alive with century-old banyans, butterflies, and birdsong.

一所擁有百年榕樹、蝴蝶與鳥鳴的花園校園。

Kuaiguan Elementary is an ecology-rich school in Changhua City. Walk through the gate and you're greeted by a century-old banyan tree standing over a wide, grassy lawn. Bees, butterflies, dragonflies, squirrels, migratory birds, and even the shy black-crowned night heron all make their home here.

快官國小是一所位於彰化市、擁有多樣化生物的生態小學。進入校園映入眼簾的是百年榕樹矗立在草地上、大片綠草如茵的草原。校園內樹種豐富，也能發現蜜蜂、蝴蝶、蜻蜓、松鼠、候鳥，甚至害羞的黑冠麻鷺蹤跡。

Recognized with the Taiwan-America Eco-Partner Silver Award.
自然生態多元豐富，榮獲臺美生態夥伴學校銀質獎肯定。

Life on campus
快官的日常校園風景

From the shade of the old banyan to the bright colours of the playground, every corner of Kuaiguan invites kids outside to learn, play, and notice the wildlife around them.

從百年榕樹下的樹蔭，到色彩繽紛的遊戲場，快官的每個角落都邀請孩子走出教室，一邊玩耍，一邊留意身邊的自然生態。
