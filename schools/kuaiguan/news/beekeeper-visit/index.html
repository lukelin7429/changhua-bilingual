Meet the Beekeeper: Sweetness Straight from the Hive

蜂農達人蒞校分享

On June 25, Kuaiguan Elementary invited beekeeping expert Mr. Chang Yung-kun to visit the campus and share his knowledge with students, leading them into the world of bees — from how a hive works to how honey is actually made.

Mr. Chang explained the vital role bees play in nature, pollinating plants and keeping ecosystems healthy, while students gathered close to see real honeycomb frames pulled straight from a hive.

Behind every drop of honey is real, hard work — and now the children understand it firsthand.

Students got hands-on, too. Using a traditional shoulder pole, they took turns carrying heavy beehive boxes just as real beekeepers do, feeling for themselves how physically demanding the job can be.

The visit ended on a sweet note — literally — as students sipped ice-cold, fragrant honey water, a refreshing reward after a morning of learning. Between the expert's explanations and the hands-on experience, children left with a much deeper appreciation for the bees, and the beekeepers, that bring honey to our tables.

中文原文 · Chinese

今天學校特別邀請蜂農達人-張永坤先生蒞校分享，帶領孩子認識蜜蜂生態與蜂蜜製造過程，了解蜜蜂在自然環境中的重要角色。

活動中，學生親身體驗以扁擔挑蜂箱，感受蜂農工作的辛勞，並品嚐冰涼香甜的蜂蜜水。透過專業解說與趣味體驗，讓孩子在歡笑中學習，在實作中成長，收穫豐富，獲益良多！
