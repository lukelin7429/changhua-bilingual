The Principal Joins Morning Reading

校長入班晨讀，快官永續圖話室

On the morning of June 15, Class 1-A transformed into a "Sustainability Reading Room," as the principal personally joined the students for ten minutes of morning reading focused on disaster-preparedness education.

The class used "Little Sustainability Times," a newspaper for children from the Child Welfare League Foundation, reading together to build both sustainability awareness and disaster-safety knowledge among Kuaiguan's youngest learners.

A gentle morning voice, reading aloud together, is how a seed of sustainability begins to grow.

Together, students and their principal read through practical safety topics — from earthquake preparedness to keeping enough food and water on hand — turning ten quiet minutes into a lesson that could matter for a lifetime.

As the soft morning light filled the room and the sound of children reading aloud echoed through the class, a small seed of sustainability took root in every child's heart.

中文原文 · Chinese

今晨，一年甲班化身永續圖話室，由校長帶領一甲孩子晨讀，深入閱讀防災教育。

我們申請小兒福聯盟小永報，透過讀報培養快官孩子的永續素養與防災觀念。

美好的晨光伴隨著孩子們的琅琅閱讀聲，讓永續的種子在每個孩子心中萌芽！
