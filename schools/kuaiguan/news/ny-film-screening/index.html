When Kindness Walks Into Our Classroom

感謝有您，讓感動走進校園

On June 26, Kuaiguan Elementary warmly welcomed guests from the Changhua County Education Department's Volunteer Resource Integration Center, the Changhua Parent-Teacher Association, and the Landseed Sijing Charity Foundation, who came to host a special film screening of "I Come from New York" for our students.

Through the film's warm and moving story, children were guided to understand the importance of family love, respect, tolerance, and cultural diversity — walking away with real growth and inspiration from every scene.

A story can travel further than any lecture — it goes straight to the heart.

Students sat together in the school library, watching quietly and thoughtfully as the film unfolded, absorbing its lessons about kindness and understanding others.

We are sincerely grateful to every organization for their generous support and thoughtful planning, which gave our children a truly meaningful learning feast. We look forward to more collaborations that bring wonderful learning experiences like this one to Kuaiguan students in the future!

中文原文 · Chinese

誠摯感謝彰化縣教育處志工人力資源整合中心、彰化縣親師家長協會、秀傳錫卿文教基金會蒞臨本校辦理《我來自紐約》電影欣賞會，透過溫馨感人的劇情，引導孩子認識親情、尊重、包容與文化多元的重要價值，也從故事中獲得許多成長與啟發。

感謝各團體的熱心支持與精心安排，讓孩子擁有一場充滿教育意義的學習饗宴，期待未來攜手合作，帶給孩子更多美好的學習體驗！
