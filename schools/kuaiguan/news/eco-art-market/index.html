Brushes, Beats, and a Greener Future

暑假樂動樂學習

On July 7, Kuaiguan students joined Tianzhong Community's "Green Future Creative Art Market × Let's Paint for Earth" event, an afternoon built around caring for the planet and celebrating creativity.

In the outdoor painting contest, children picked up their brushes and captured the beauty of a green hometown — rooftops, rice fields, and trees rendered in bright, honest color, each canvas a small statement of environmental care.

Every brushstroke and every dance step was a way of saying: we love this earth.

Then it was time to perform. Wearing sky-blue shirts and waving glittering pom-poms, Kuaiguan's dance team took the outdoor stage under the highway overpass, their energetic routine drawing applause from the crowd and injecting the whole market with fresh enthusiasm.

Between paintbrushes and dance moves, students practiced two very different skills — but both carried the same message: protecting the environment can be joyful, expressive, and something every child can take part in. It was, in every sense, a rich and meaningful way to spend a summer afternoon.

中文原文 · Chinese

快官國小小朋友參與了田中社區主辦的「綠色未來創『藝』市集 × 來趣畫畫愛地球」活動。孩子們不僅在寫生比賽中用畫筆捕捉綠色家園的美好，展現純真創意，更帶來了活力四射的舞蹈表演，為現場注入滿滿的熱情與朝氣。

這場活動讓學生在實踐環境保護概念的同時，也透過藝術與才藝展現自我，體驗了一場充實、快樂又富有教育意義的暑期樂學習。
