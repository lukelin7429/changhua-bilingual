Word of the Day

每日一字 · 快官自製雙語影片
I.

32 Words, 32 Videos

32 個單字，32 支短片

Straight from Kuaiguan's own classrooms, courtyard, and clubs.

每一支都拍自快官自己的教室、校園與社團活動。

Every word below comes with a short bilingual video, plus two example sentences in English and Chinese. Watch, listen, and read along — then try saying it out loud.

下面每個單字都搭配一支雙語短片，以及兩句中英對照例句。看影片、聽發音、跟著讀——然後大聲說出來。

Word 01
professional development (n. phr.)
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都有教師專業訓練課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
在專業訓練課程中，老師可以學到新的教學技巧與策略。
Word 02
Chinese characters (n.)
國字
The students learn Chinese characters in class.
同學們在課堂上學習國字。
They read and write Chinese characters.
他們閱讀並書寫國字。
Word 03
online test (n. phr.)
線上測驗
The students take online tests in class.
學生在課堂上進行線上測驗。
They use tablets to take their online tests.
他們使用平板進行線上測試。
Word 04
write (v.)
寫
The students write in their workbooks.
同學們在習作本上寫字。
They are focused on writing neatly.
他們很專注把字寫工整。
Word 05
math class (n. phr.)
數學課
The students are in math class.
學生們正在上數學課。
The teacher teaches math in math class.
老師在數學課教數學。
Word 06
wash your hands (v. phr.)
洗手
It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
確定在吃東西之前要用香皂和水來洗手。
Word 07
sing (v.)
唱
The students sing the song in class.
學生們在課堂上唱這首歌。
The students sing along in class to the music.
學生們在課堂上跟著音樂唱歌。
Word 08
check out (v. phr.)
借書
The students check out books from the library at recess.
同學們利用下課到圖書室借書。
After the librarian checks out the students' books, they can take them home to read.
向圖書館員辦理借閱手續後，同學們可以將書帶回家看。
Word 09
read (v.)
閱讀
The student chooses a book to read.
學生選擇一本書閱讀。
The student reads a book.
學生們在讀書。
Word 10
storyteller (n.)
說故事的人
A good storyteller teaches a lesson and entertains you at the same time.
一個擅長講說故事的人可以教導大家道理，同時又兼具娛樂大眾。
The storytellers can use real or fictional information to tell a story.
說故事的人可能用真實或者是虛構的情節來說故事。
Word 11
basketball (n.)
籃球
The students play basketball outside.
同學們在外面打籃球。
They shoot the ball when playing basketball.
他們在打籃球的時候投籃。
Word 12
watercolor (n.)
水彩
In art class, the students are painting with watercolor.
學生們在美術課用水彩畫畫。
To use watercolor, you need a brush, a palette and water.
用水彩繪畫時，你需要畫筆、調色盤還有水。
Word 13
basketball practice (n. phr.)
籃球訓練
The students are outside for basketball practice.
同學們在外面做籃球訓練。
They work hard during basketball practice.
他們非常認真地做籃球訓練。
Word 14
swimming class (n. phr.)
游泳課
The students learn how to swim in swimming class.
學生在游泳課上學習如何游泳。
They learn the basics of swimming in swimming class.
他們在游泳課上學習游泳的基礎。
Word 15
ping pong (n.)
桌球
Ping pong, also known as table tennis, is a fun sport you can play indoors.
乒乓球又稱為桌球，這是一個有趣的室內運動。
Playing ping pong can help you improve your hand-eye coordination.
打桌球可以提升你的手眼協調性。
Word 16
Scratch (n.)
程式語言
Students are using computers to learn Scratch programming.
學生們正在使用電腦學習Scratch程式設計。
The teacher helps a student with a Scratch project.
老師幫助學生完成 Scratch 專案。
Word 17
aroma stone (n.)
擴香石
The students make aroma stones in class.
學生在課堂上製作芳香石。
They precisely measure everything to make the aroma stones.
他們精確測量製作芳香石的一切。
Word 18
puffed rice (n. phr.)
爆米香
The students make puffed rice in class.
學生在課堂上製作爆米花。
They use triangle-shaped molds to mold the puffed rice into triangles.
他們使用三角形模具將爆米花塑造成三角形。
Word 19
blender (n.)
攪拌機
The students learn how to use a blender in class.
學生在課堂上學習如何使用攪拌機。
They use a blender to blend nuts and puffed rice.
他們使用攪拌機攪拌堅果和米香。
Word 20
bitter orange tea (n. phr.)
酸柑茶
The students learn how to make traditional Hakka bitter orange tea.
學生學習如何製作傳統客家酸柑茶。
After the students learn how to make bitter orange tea, they gather together to drink the tea.
學生學習如何泡酸柑茶後，聚在一起喝茶。
Word 21
fried peanuts (n. phr.)
炒花生
The students learn how to make fried peanuts in class.
學生在課堂上學習如何製作油炸花生。
The students add salt at the end to make the fried peanuts.
學生最後加鹽來做炸花生米。
Word 22
recorder (n.)
直笛
The recorder is the first musical instrument that many students learn.
直笛是很多學生學的第一項樂器。
You can play many songs on a recorder.
你可以用直笛吹奏很多歌曲。
Word 23
draw (v.)
畫
The students draw the development of a seed's growth.
學生畫出種子生長的過程。
They use markers to draw plants.
他們用記號筆畫植物。
Word 24
oral hygiene (n. phr.)
口腔衛生
Students learn about oral hygiene in class.
學生在課堂上學習口腔衛生知識。
They practice brushing their teeth while they learn about oral hygiene.
他們在學習口腔衛生時練習刷牙。
Word 25
English (n.)
英語
The teacher is teaching English at the front of the classroom.
老師正在教室前面教英文。
The students are paying attention to the teacher as she teaches English.
老師講英語時，學生們都在專心聽講。
Word 26
group photo (n. phr.)
團體照
The photographer is taking group photos of all the graduates, teachers, and the principal.
攝影師正在為全體畢業生、老師、校長拍攝合照。
All the graduates, teachers, and the principal are having group photos taken at school.
全體畢業生、老師、校長在學校拍照。
Word 27
garden tools (n. phr.)
園藝工具
Garden tools, such as trowels, shovels, hoes, and picks, make gardening much easier.
園藝工具，如小鏟子、鏟子、鋤頭和尖鋤，使園藝變得更為容易。
Some garden tools are designed for loosening soil and digging into it.
有些園藝工具設計來鬆土與挖土。
Word 28
salad (n.)
沙拉
The students are gathering vegetables for a salad.
學生們正在採摘蔬菜做沙拉。
Many people enjoy eating different types of salad.
許多人喜歡吃不同類型的沙拉。
Word 29
space science (n. phr.)
太空科學
The students attend a fun talk about space science in the auditorium.
學生們在禮堂參加一場關於太空科學的有趣講座。
They learn interesting facts about space science and enjoy asking questions.
他們學習有關太空科學的有趣事實並喜歡提問。
Word 30
agriculture class (n. phr.)
食農課
In the agriculture class, students loosen the soil and mix in fertilizer.
在農業課上，學生鬆土、施肥。
They learn basic farming skills by working together outdoors during the agriculture class.
他們在農業課上一起在戶外工作，學習基本的農耕技能。
Word 31
online class (n. phr.)
線上課
Students join an online class as part of an international education program.
作為國際教育計畫的一部分，學生們參加了線上課程。
They interact with the teacher on screen and practice speaking English during this online class.
他們在線上課堂上與螢幕上的老師互動，並練習英語口語。
Word 32
ice rice dumpling (n. phr.)
冰粽
Students prepare to make ice rice dumplings with different fruits.
學生們準備用不同的水果來製作冰粽子。
Making and tasting ice rice dumplings are a fun way to celebrate summer and to learn about a traditional snack.
製作和品嚐冰粽子是慶祝夏天和了解傳統小吃的有趣方式。
