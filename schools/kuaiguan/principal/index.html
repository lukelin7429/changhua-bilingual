I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Huang views education as a "Century-long Tree Cultivation" project — a mission of hope "to educate people to become full adults." He believes the school exists for its students, and all actions must be student-centric.

黃校長認為教育是「百年樹人」的希望工程，學校因學生而存在，因此一切思考與作為必須以學生為主體。

He is committed to upholding the essence of education, dedicating his whole heart, effort, and wisdom to help students grow and achieve holistic education goals.

他承諾將秉持教育本質，用全心、全力、用智慧，幫助學生成長，達成全人教育目標。

Student-Centered. Instruction-Core. Team-Driven. Cooperation-Based. Sustainability-Principle.
以學生為中心、以教學為核心、以團隊為動力、以合作為基礎、以永續為原則。

IV.

Guiding Philosophy

辦學理念與方針

Five directives guide every decision at Kuaiguan.

五大辦學方針，指引快官國小的每一項決策。

01
Student-Centered: Implementing holistic education focused on students' overall development.
以學生為中心：落實全人教育，關注學生的整體發展。
02
Instruction-Core: Providing diverse learning opportunities and deepening curriculum content.
以教學為核心：提供多元學習機會，深化課程內容。
03
Team-Driven: Shaping a high-quality administrative culture to ensure administration supports instruction.
以團隊為動力：型塑優質行政文化，確保行政支援教學。
04
Cooperation-Based: Integrating multi-faceted resources and strengthening the partnership among parents, teachers, students, and the community.
以合作為基礎：整合多方資源，強化親師生及社區的夥伴關係。
05
Sustainability-Principle: Developing the school's unique characteristics based on sustainability for long-term excellence.
以永續為原則：發展學校特色，追求學校的長遠與卓越。
