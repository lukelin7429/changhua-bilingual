I.

Welcome

歡迎來到中正國小

A small village school carrying a quiet, seventy-year inheritance.

一所立足二林南境的鄉間小學，承載一段安靜的七十年傳承。

Our inheritance. Founded in 1955, Zhongzheng Elementary has carried the spirit of community education and the depth of humanistic learning for seven decades. Generation after generation, we have built our school on the belief in "whole-language elite education," deepening literacy and shaping character — and our students have brought home prizes from every kind of language-arts competition Changhua holds. The campus, warmed by a quiet humanistic atmosphere and a modern educational spirit, has watched generations of children grow and transform.

我們的傳承。本校創立於 1955 年，是一所承載地方教育精神與人文底蘊的學校。多年來，我們秉持「全語文菁英教育」理念，在語文教育與品格培養上深耕不輟，並於各項語文競賽中屢創佳績。校園融合溫暖的人文氛圍與現代教育精神，見證一代代學子的成長與蛻變。

Educating for the future. At Zhongzheng, we work to build a learning environment that holds character, innovation, and an international outlook side by side. Beyond solid language-arts foundations, we actively cultivate digital literacy and bilingual learning — nurturing independence, teamwork, and problem-solving. We believe every child carries a unique potential, and through multi-track curricula and adaptive growth, can become the kind of capable citizen our future asks for.

培育未來。我們致力於打造兼具品格、創新與國際視野的學習環境。除了扎實的語文教育基礎外，我們積極推動科技教育與雙語學習，培養學生自主探索、團隊合作與問題解決能力。我們相信，每位孩子都擁有獨特潛能，並透過多元課程與適性發展，成為能面對未來挑戰的優質公民。

II.

What Makes Zhongzheng

中正的四個特色
📜
Whole-Language Excellence
全語文菁英教育

Seven decades of investment in language arts. Our students bring home top honours every year from Changhua's county-wide language competitions — writing, calligraphy, recitation, and phonetics.

🌱
A Seed School
南境四校之母

Zhongzheng is the school three of Erlin's other elementary schools quietly grew out of — Guangxing, Xiangtian, and Yude all began as parts of our district before becoming campuses of their own.

🎺
Arts & Athletics
藝文與體育

Recorder ensemble, string orchestra, calligraphy, pottery, oil pastels, dance, tchoukball, basketball, soccer, table tennis, taekwondo, and inline skating — every child has a stage of their own.

💻
Makers & Bilingual Life
創客與雙語日常

Maker education, AI robotics, soccer-drones, computational thinking — and a daily bilingual layer through Classroom English and morning intercom announcements.

Where four campuses share one root
四校共一源 · 二林南境的學習家族

From the seed Erlin Elementary planted in 1955, three campuses bloomed in three villages. Together with Zhongzheng, they form a quiet network of elementary education across southern Erlin — four schools, one family.
