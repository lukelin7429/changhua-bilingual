I.

Welcome

歡迎來到中正國小

A small village school carrying a big history in southern Erlin.

一所立足二林南境的鄉間小學，承載著一段不小的歷史。

Zhongzheng Elementary sits in Nanguang Village, on the southern edge of Erlin Township, where the Changhua plain stretches toward the sea. In September 1955, Erlin Elementary carved out twenty of its classes and sent them south to serve five neighbouring villages — and that act of partition became, three months later, the school called Zhongzheng.

中正國小坐落於彰化縣二林鎮南光里，這裡是彰化平原往海岸延伸的南境。1955 年 9 月，二林國小劃出 20 個班級南遷，服務鄰近五個村庄；三個月後，這所新學校正式定名為「中正」。

Over the next seventy years, three of those original villages grew schools of their own — Guangxing, Xiangtian, and Yude all branched off from Zhongzheng's original district. We are still here in Nanguang, but our story is shared across four campuses in southern Erlin.

這七十年間，當年五里中的三個村莊各自長出了自己的學校——廣興、香田、育德都是從中正再分立而出。如今中正國小仍坐落在南光里，但她的故事，已經寫在二林南境的四所校園裡。

Under our tenth principal, Lin Jun-cheng, who took office in August 2024, we keep a steady, modest vision: a village school does not need to be loud, only steady — and that steadiness is earned one classroom, one child, one school day at a time.

在第十任林畯城校長於 2024 年 8 月接任後，我們持續守著一個低調而穩定的信念：一所鄉間的小學不必喧嘩，只需要穩——而那份穩，是要靠一間教室、一個孩子、一個校日一點一滴累積出來的。

II.

What Makes Zhongzheng

中正的特色
🌱
A Seed School
南境四校之母

Zhongzheng is the school three of Erlin's other elementary schools quietly grew out of — Guangxing, Xiangtian, and Yude all began as parts of our district before becoming campuses of their own.

💻
Digital-Hands Education
從手作到 AI 的科技教育

Maker education, computational thinking, one-tablet-per-student, Scratch, Microbit, Arduino, and AI literacy — city-grade learning tools in the hands of every village child.

📚
Quiet Doors to Books
無聲的閱讀工程

Free EIP access to the National Library of Public Information's e-books, the Reading Step Changhua programme, and Kang Hsuan academic contests — many quiet doors, always left open.

🌏
Two Languages, Not One
不只英語的雙語

Linked to the Changhua Bilingual Train, with Cool English on every desk and Hakka-language online classes — a real bilingual school remembers its grandparents' first language too.

Where four campuses share one root
四校共一源 · 二林南境的學習家族

From the seed Erlin Elementary planted in 1955, three campuses bloomed in three villages. Together with Zhongzheng, they form a quiet network of elementary education across southern Erlin — four schools, one family.
