I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Lin Jun-cheng walked through the gate of Zhongzheng Elementary in August 2024, becoming the tenth principal in the school's seventy-year history. He came to the role after decades inside Changhua County's elementary schools — first as a homeroom teacher, then through six different administrative chairs, and finally as a curriculum supervisor at the county government before being entrusted with this campus in southern Erlin.

2024 年 8 月，林畯城校長走進中正國小的校門，成為這所七十年校史中的第十任校長。他在彰化縣的小學系統服務多年——從級任老師起步，歷任六個不同處室的組長與主任，最後在縣府教育處擔任課程督學，才在 2024 年 8 月接下這所二林南境的校園。

His outlook is shaped by a long, steady career rather than a single dramatic event. He believes a village school's strength is not in being loud, but in being steady — in showing up, year after year, for every child who walks through the gate. Under his leadership, Zhongzheng continues the quiet work that has always defined the school: maker education, computational thinking, reading promotion, and a bilingual programme that includes the mother tongue.

他的視角來自一段長久而穩定的教育生涯，而非單一戲劇性的事件。他相信，鄉間小學的強項不在喧嘩，而在「穩」——日復一日，為每個走進校門的孩子站在那裡。在他帶領下，中正持續一向以來不喧嘩的工作：自造教育、運算思維、閱讀推廣、以及包含母語的雙語教育。

"A village school does not need to be loud. It needs to be steady — and that steadiness, you only earn by showing up for every child who walks through the gate."
一所鄉間的小學不必喧嘩。她需要的是穩——而那份穩，是要靠日復一日、為每個走進校門的孩子站在那裡，才能換來的。 — 林畯城校長
