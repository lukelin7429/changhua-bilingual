I.

What This Club Does

這個社團在做什麼

AI literacy through hands-on, not through theory.

親手玩 AI，不是只聽老師講理論。

Our AI Robotics club brings CANVA, ChatGPT and the Niwa platform together. Students learn to brief an AI, refine their prompts, generate visuals — and ground all of it in physical robotics they can build, test, and watch move.

中正 AI 機器人社團把 CANVA、ChatGPT、女媧平台結合在一起。孩子們學會怎麼跟 AI 對話、怎麼調整提示語、怎麼讓 AI 生出圖——然後把這些都連到真實的機器人上，組裝、測試、看它動起來。

The vocabulary below is the everyday English you'll hear in this club. Tap the 🔊 speaker on each card to hear how each word sounds.
