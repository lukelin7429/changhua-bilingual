I.

What These Clubs Do

這兩個社團在做什麼

Two of Zhongzheng's longest-running music clubs.

中正最資深的兩個音樂社團。

The recorder ensemble has represented the school across Changhua's music competitions for years. The ocarina club is the gentle, wooden-tone counterpoint that opens many of our school events. Both clubs teach the same quiet skills: how to read a note, how to breathe before you play, how to listen to the people next to you.

中正的直笛校隊長年代表學校參加彰化縣的音樂比賽。陶笛社則是溫潤、木質的另一面聲音，常在校園活動中打頭陣。兩個社團教的，其實是同一件靜靜的事：怎麼讀一個音符、吹之前怎麼呼吸、怎麼聽見旁邊的人。
