I.

What This Club Does

這個社團在做什麼

Drones, but for a team sport.

無人機，但變成一場團體運動。

In Soccer Drones, students fly small drones, coordinate plays, and experience real STEM concepts — physics, control, programming, teamwork — in the most fun applied form an elementary kid can ask for. Win or lose, you go home knowing how a propeller pushes air, why a battery runs out, and what it takes to land softly.

在足球無人機社團，孩子們駕駛小型無人機、配合戰術，把 STEM（科學、技術、工程、數學）真正用出來——物理、操控、程式、團隊合作。不管贏或輸，回家時你都會知道螺旋槳怎麼把空氣推開、電池為什麼會用完、降落要怎麼降得穩。
