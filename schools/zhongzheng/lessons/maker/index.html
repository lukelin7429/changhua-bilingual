I.

What This Club Does

這個社團在做什麼

The school's core base for competency-oriented education.

中正推動素養導向教育的重要基地。

Maker Club breaks the boundaries of traditional subjects. We build computational thinking through coding and logic, run cross-disciplinary hands-on projects, and train teamwork through shared problem-solving. Children observe a real need, combine science, technology and engineering, and make something with their own hands — work that has a temperature to it.

創客社團打破傳統學科的框架。我們透過程式設計和邏輯訓練運算思維，做跨領域的動手專題，並透過共同解決問題訓練團隊合作。孩子們觀察生活需求，把科學、技術、工程結合在一起，親手做出有溫度的作品。

These are the everyday words you'll hear in Maker Club. Tap 🔊 on any card to hear the word.
