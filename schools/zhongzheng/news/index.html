Incoming Class · 新生入學

Welcoming the Class of 2032歡迎 115 學年度小一新鮮人

Registration: April 11, 2026 (Sat) · 公告日期 2026-01-13

Zhongzheng's gate opens once again for a new generation of six-year-olds. The school has set Saturday, April 11, 2026 as the registration day for incoming first-graders in the 115th school year — children who, six years from now, will graduate as the Class of 2032. The school office invites families across Nanguang, Zhongxi and the surrounding villages of southern Erlin to walk through the gate, meet the principal and the first-grade teachers, and begin the long, quiet first chapter of their child's elementary years.

中正國小校門再次為新一代六歲的孩子敞開。學校訂於 2026 年 4 月 11 日（星期六）舉行 115 學年度小一新生報到——這群孩子六年後將以「2032 屆」自校門走出。歡迎南光、中西里以及二林南境鄰近村落的家庭走進校園，認識校長與一年級導師，與孩子一同開啟小學階段那段漫長而安靜的第一章。

Reading Promotion · 閱讀推動

Reading Step Changhua — Soaring Into the Cloud讀步彰化 · 飛越雲端

December 16, 2025 · 2025 年 12 月

Zhongzheng joined the county-wide "Reading Step Changhua — Soaring Into the Cloud" programme, an initiative to put more books — paper and digital — into more young hands. The campaign weaves together Zhongzheng's existing reading culture, the public library's e-book platform, and the school's daily reading time. For a village school whose own physical library is modest in size, programmes like this turn the whole county's collection into a Zhongzheng student's reading list.

中正國小參與彰化縣的「讀步彰化 · 飛越雲端」閱讀推動計畫——這是一項將更多紙本與數位書籍送進更多孩子手上的縣級行動。本計畫串連了校內既有的閱讀文化、公共圖書館的電子書平台，以及學校每日的閱讀時間。對一所實體館藏有限的鄉間小學來說，這類計畫等於把整個縣的藏書，轉化成中正孩子的書單。

Library Access · 圖書資源

A Library Card for Every Student全校 EIP 帳號免費登入國資圖電子書

August 27, 2025 · 2025 年 8 月

Every Zhongzheng student now carries — through their school EIP account — a free pass to the National Library of Public Information's e-book collection, available from any device with an internet connection. No paperwork, no extra registration, no separate library card. The school office made this quiet door available campus-wide in August 2025, and trusts that across a school year, more than a few children will walk through it and find a book that changes how they read.

透過學校 EIP 帳號，每位中正學生現在都擁有國立公共資訊圖書館電子書平台的免費登入權限——任何能上網的裝置都能使用。不需要額外申請、不需要再辦借閱證。學校自 2025 年 8 月起為全校開啟這扇無聲的門，相信在一整個學年裡，總會有一些孩子走進去，找到一本改變他們閱讀經驗的書。

Academic Contest · 學力競賽

Academic Excellence Tournament康軒雲學力爭鋒賽 · 中正國小場次

September 30, 2025 · 2025 年 9 月

Zhongzheng hosted its own round of the Kang Hsuan Cloud Academic Tournament, an online inter-school competition that lets country-school students go head to head with peers from across Taiwan on equal terms — same questions, same clock, same leaderboard. Prizes were generous, but the real prize for a village school is the chance to measure itself against the world beyond Erlin and discover what its own students can already do.

中正辦理了校內場次的康軒雲學力爭鋒賽——這是一項線上跨校競賽，讓鄉間孩子可以與全臺各地的同齡學生在相同題目、相同時間、相同排行榜上一較高下。獎品豐富，但對一所鄉間小學而言，真正的獎品，是讓自己跳出二林之外去丈量自己——並發現中正的孩子早已能做到些什麼。

Digital Citizenship · 數位素養

Teaching AI Wisely — New Ministry Guidelines教育部生成式 AI 注意事項 2.0 & 2.1

December 30, 2025 · 2025 年 12 月

As generative AI tools become part of children's everyday digital lives, the Ministry of Education has released versions 2.0 and 2.1 of its "Guidelines for Generative AI Use in Schools." Zhongzheng's IT and computer-class teachers have folded these guidelines into classroom conversations on responsible use — when AI helps, when it misleads, when to ask a teacher, and how to keep a child's own thinking at the centre of the learning. A small school facing a very new kind of textbook.

隨著生成式 AI 走進孩子的日常數位生活，教育部發布了「中小學使用生成式人工智慧注意事項」2.0 與 2.1 版本。中正的資訊組與電腦課教師將這些原則融入課堂對話——哪些情況下 AI 真的幫得上忙、哪些情況下它會誤導、什麼時候該問老師，以及如何讓孩子自己的思考始終是學習的核心。一所小校，面對一本很新的教科書。

Curriculum · 課程計畫

2025-26 Curriculum Plan Approved114 學年度學校課程計畫審核通過

Approved August 2025 · 公告 2025 年 8 月

Zhongzheng's full 114 (2025-26) school-year curriculum plan has been approved through the Changhua County online review process and entered into official record. Behind that single administrative line stands a year's worth of teacher planning, subject coordination, and quiet shaping of how the school's hours will be spent — from the first morning bell of August to the last summer afternoon. The blueprint is set; the year can begin.

中正國小 114 學年度（2025-26）的學校課程計畫，已通過彰化縣線上審核流程並完成備查。那一行行政紀錄背後，是教師整整一年的備課、領域協作、以及對「這所學校的時間如何使用」的細緻雕琢——從八月清晨的第一聲鐘響，到夏末的最後一個午後。藍圖就位，新的一年可以展開。
