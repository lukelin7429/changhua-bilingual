April 10, 2026 · 1150410 · Student Activity Centre

Hua Shan Foundation's Erlin Station director and volunteer grandmothers led our fourth-graders through a remarkable exercise: simulating an elder's body — unsteady walking, fading memory, hands that no longer respond. Safety · 防災演練

"Drop, Cover, Hold On"9/21 防災演練 · 趴下、掩護、穩住

September 20, 2025 · 114.9.20

At 9:21 sharp the alarm sounded, and our students dove under their desks in one fluid motion — gripping the legs, executing the three life-saving moves: Drop, Cover, Hold On. They stayed there until the commander (the principal) called the evacuation; then they crawled out, used their textbooks or seat cushions to shield head and neck, and followed teachers to the assembly point on the field. The students were practised and the teachers were calm. Our school's disaster preparedness is real.

921 地震防災演練：9:21 警報聲一響，小朋友立刻機警的躲至桌下，緊握桌腳，執行趴下、掩護、穩住，保命三動作，直到指揮官（校長）宣布進行疏散，小朋友才從桌底下爬出，手拿桌墊或書本遮蔽頭頸部，隨著老師指揮疏散到操場上的集合點。小朋友對於這些動作都很熟悉，教師也都指揮得很流暢——關於防災，本校師生都已有良好的知識與應變能力。

Athletics · 跳繩

Jump Rope, Whole Body Workout跳繩班際競賽 · 全身性運動的快樂

March 1–30, 2026 · 115.3.01-3.30 · Playground

A full month of inter-class jump rope competition for the lower grades. Jump rope is a simple but powerful whole-body exercise — a sustained jump-rope habit builds cardiovascular fitness and a healthy body. Athletics · 游泳教學

From Fear of Water to "Exhale, Inhale, Hold"游泳教學 · 從怕水到掌握水中節奏

March 10 – April 16, 2026 · 115.3.10-4.16 · Changnan Aquatic Centre

The annual swimming course — from "afraid of water" to learning how to breathe and float. Conquering fear comes first: when a student steps into water that surrounds them completely, the body tenses. By adapting to the temperature, practising face-immersion in the shallow end, and feeling the lift of the "jellyfish float," they learn the rhythm of swimming — exhale, inhale, hold — which makes the stroke easy and lasting. A core-and-cardio workout, and a life-saving skill in one.

一年一度的游泳教學，從怕水到學會換氣與漂浮——實用的求生技能更是體會到自我保護與水上自救的技能提升。克服對水的恐懼是讓學生進入完全被水包覆環境時的關鍵；透過學習適應水溫、淺水區練習臉部入水、簡單的「水母漂」感受浮力，掌握「吐、吸、憋」的循環節奏，游起來才會輕鬆持久——不僅鍛鍊全身肌肉與心肺，還能保護自我安全。

中正國小校門再次為新一代六歲的孩子敞開。學校訂於 2026 年 4 月 11 日（星期六）舉行 115 學年度小一新生報到——這群孩子六年後將以「2032 屆」自校門走出。歡迎南光、中西里以及二林南境鄰近村落的家庭走進校園。

Reading · 閱讀推動

Reading Step Changhua — Soaring Into the Cloud讀步彰化 · 飛越雲端

December 16, 2025

Zhongzheng joined the county-wide "Reading Step Changhua" programme — putting more books, paper and digital, into more young hands. For a village school whose own physical library is modest in size, the campaign turns the whole county's collection into a Zhongzheng student's reading list.

中正國小參與彰化縣的「讀步彰化 · 飛越雲端」閱讀推動計畫——將更多紙本與數位書籍送進更多孩子手上。對一所實體館藏有限的鄉間小學，這類計畫等於把整個縣的藏書，轉化成中正孩子的書單。

Library Access · 圖書資源

A Library Card for Every Student全校 EIP 帳號免費登入國資圖電子書

August 27, 2025

Every Zhongzheng student now carries — through their school EIP account — a free pass to the National Library of Public Information's e-book collection, available from any device with an internet connection. A quiet door, left open for the whole school.

透過學校 EIP 帳號，每位中正學生都擁有國立公共資訊圖書館電子書平台的免費登入權限——任何能上網的裝置都能使用。為全校開啟的一扇無聲的門。

Digital Citizenship · 數位素養

Teaching AI Wisely教育部生成式 AI 注意事項 2.0 & 2.1

December 30, 2025

As generative AI tools become part of children's everyday digital lives, the Ministry of Education has released versions 2.0 and 2.1 of its "Guidelines for Generative AI Use in Schools." Zhongzheng's IT and computer-class teachers have folded these into classroom conversations on responsible use — when AI helps, when it misleads, and how to keep a child's own thinking at the centre of learning.

隨著生成式 AI 走進孩子的日常數位生活，教育部發布了「中小學使用生成式人工智慧注意事項」2.0 與 2.1 版本。中正的資訊組與電腦課教師將這些原則融入課堂對話——哪些情況下 AI 真的幫得上忙、哪些情況下它會誤導，以及如何讓孩子自己的思考始終是學習的核心。

Curriculum · 課程計畫

2025-26 Curriculum Plan Approved114 學年度學校課程計畫審核通過

Approved August 2025

Zhongzheng's full 114 (2025-26) school-year curriculum plan has been approved through the Changhua County online review process. Behind that single administrative line stands a year's worth of teacher planning, subject coordination, and quiet shaping of how the school's hours will be spent.

中正國小 114 學年度（2025-26）的學校課程計畫，已通過彰化縣線上審核流程並完成備查。那一行行政紀錄背後，是教師整整一年的備課、領域協作、以及對「這所學校的時間如何使用」的細緻雕琢。

Anti-Fraud · 防詐宣導

Anti-Fraud Resources On the Homepage內政部警政署打詐儀錶板專區

November 27, 2025

As part of nationwide anti-fraud literacy efforts, the Ministry of the Interior's Anti-Fraud Dashboard is now linked from the school's official site — including a special section on protecting universal cash payments from scams. For families with children using digital devices, the resources are right there, two clicks away.

配合全民防詐意識推廣，內政部警政署打詐儀錶板已連結至學校官網首頁，含「普發現金防詐專區」。對於孩子已開始使用數位裝置的家庭，相關資源就在兩個點擊之內。
