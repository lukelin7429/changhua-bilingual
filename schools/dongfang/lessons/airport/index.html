1 Day Trip

This summer camp, we are going on a real airport field trip to Taoyuan International Airport. Each group will pick one area of the airport, learn the key English words, and film a short video to introduce it to the world.這次夏令營，我們要真的去桃園國際機場實地探索。每一組同學選定機場的一個區域，學習關鍵英語詞彙，並拍攝一段短片向大家介紹。

🕗 08:00 Depart from Dongfang · 東芳出發

🎬 Film your group's scene · 分組拍攝
🕓 16:00 Back at school · 返回學校
