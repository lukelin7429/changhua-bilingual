A complete little campus on the western edge of Changhua City.

Welcome to Dongfang Elementary School. Founded in 1947 and set in the western suburb of Changhua City — just 600 metres from the Changhua interchange — Dongfang is a warm neighbourhood school where leisure, learning, and activity spaces are laid out in tidy, thoughtful order on a compact 1.8-hectare campus.

東芳國小創校於民國 36 年（1947），座落於彰化市西郊、距彰化交流道約六百公尺。校地雖僅 1.80 公頃，但休閒區、教學區、活動區層次分明、規劃完整，是一所與社區同步成長的溫暖校園。

We are proud to be an ISA-accredited international school with a foreign English teacher on campus — and a school whose football teams have brought home national championships again and again.

我們以「國際學校獎 ISA 認證」與「外籍英語老師駐校」為傲，足球隊更屢獲全國冠軍——這裡的孩子，文武雙全、與世界接軌。

Meet Teacher NelNelson Dolim Sia · 東芳國小駐校外籍英語老師

Dongfang is proud to have a full-time foreign English teacher — Teacher Nel (Nelson Dolim Sia) — right here on campus. From everyday "Word of the Day" videos to animated lessons and English summer camps, Teacher Nel makes English something children hear, speak, and enjoy every single day.

東芳全校以擁有一位駐校外籍英語老師 Teacher Nel 為榮。從每日一字、動畫英語課到英語夏令營，外師讓英語成為孩子每天聽得到、說得出、玩得開心的語言。

⚽ Soccer · 足球

Five national titles — and counting少年盃 3 冠 · 學童盃 2 冠

Since the artificial-turf field opened on 20 January 2024, soccer has become Dongfang's heartbeat. Children from kindergarten through Grade 4 join after-school clubs and holiday camps, and our teams keep bringing home national championships.

2024 年 1 月 20 日人工草皮足球場啟用後，足球成為東芳的心跳。幼兒園到四年級都有課後社團與寒暑假育樂營，球隊屢屢奪下全國冠軍。
