About the Club

認識跆拳道社 · 運動與舞蹈

Step by step, even beginners learn simple self-defense and build confidence.

由淺入深，零基礎也能學會防身技巧、建立自信。

←All Clubs回到社團總覽

Our Taekwondo Club helps students learn step by step. Using our own activity space on campus, even complete beginners can get active, build real confidence, and learn simple self-defense together with friends!

本社以學生學習為核心，由淺入深教學。充分利用本校專屬活動空間與跨系共學氛圍，零基礎也能輕鬆掌握實用防身技巧、有效鍛鍊體能，在揮汗互動中收穫健康與自信！
