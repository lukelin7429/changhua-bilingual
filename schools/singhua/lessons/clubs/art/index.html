About the Club

認識繪畫社 · 藝文

Bright colors and big imagination — our young artists win prizes in town art contests.

揮灑繽紛色彩——社員屢獲鎮上繪畫比賽佳績。

←All Clubs回到社團總覽

Bring ideas to life with vibrant colors! Our Art Club encourages students to explore different mediums, composition skills, and creative self-expression. Through thoughtful practice, our young artists have won awards in local town art contests, filling our campus with color, creativity, and inspiring stories!

揮灑繽紛色彩，彩繪童年想像！繪畫社著重多元媒材探索與構圖引導，啟發孩子的觀察力與美感創造力。在自由揮灑與細心創作下，社員屢獲鎮上繪畫比賽佳績，用一支支畫筆記錄生活溫度，為校園增添豐富的藝術氣息！

paintv.
畫；上色
I like to paint birds.
我喜歡畫鳥。

paintbrushn.
畫筆
Wash your paintbrush in the water.
在水裡洗一洗你的畫筆。

What color is the parrot?
這隻鸚鵡是什麼顏色？

混合
Mix blue and yellow to make green.
把藍色和黃色混在一起變成綠色。
5

First, draw the fish with a pencil.
先用鉛筆畫出魚的樣子。
6

This is a picture of my family.
這是一張我家人的畫。
7

你是很棒的小畫家！
8

2Useful Sentences

我在畫一條大魚。

哇！你需要什麼顏色？

好啊，先在水裡洗一洗。
