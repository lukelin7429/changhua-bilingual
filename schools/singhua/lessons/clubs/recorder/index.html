Club 07 · Music

About the Club

認識直笛社 · 音樂

Building on music class — harmony, teamwork and the big stage at graduation.

延伸音樂課所學，多聲部合奏，是校慶與畢業典禮的亮點。

←All Clubs回到社團總覽

Building on music classes from grades 3 to 6, our Recorder Club helps students advance their playing skills. Through harmony practice, members build teamwork and ear training. The club performs at school anniversary celebrations and graduation ceremonies, helping students gain stage experience, share music, and build confidence!

延伸三至六年級音樂課所學，直笛社帶領孩子深化演奏技巧。透過多聲部合奏訓練聽力與團隊默契，更是校慶與畢業典禮上的亮眼焦點！孩子們在舞台展演中累積寶貴經驗，用悠揚笛聲傳遞祝福，收穫滿滿的自信與成就感。
