About the Club

認識小提琴社 · 音樂

From the right bow hold to playing together — patience you can hear.

從正確持弓到雙部合奏，用琴聲磨練耐心與音感。

←All Clubs回到社團總覽

Discover the graceful sounds of the violin! Our Violin Club guides students from proper posture and bow hold to scale fingerings and ear training. Through step-by-step guidance and ensemble practice, students build patience and fine musical sensitivity, creating lovely melodies and sharing their love for music with the school!

拉響優雅弦音，啟發音樂美感！小提琴社從正確夾琴持弓、音階指法與聽音對位循序引導。透過個別指引與雙部合奏練習，孩子們在細膩運弓中磨練耐心與敏銳音感，用純淨優美的旋律豐富校園生活，奏出專屬自己的音樂故事！
