About the Club

認識桌球社 · 運動與舞蹈

Quick feet, quick hands, and a lot of fun at the table.

敏捷揮拍，在高速來回中練出專注與反應。

←All Clubs回到社團總覽

Swing into action with quick reflexes! Our Table Tennis Club covers basic grips, footwork, and serving skills, focusing on hand-eye coordination and speed. Through regular multi-ball drills and friendly matches, students build sharp focus, quick thinking, and a true love for healthy sports!

敏捷揮拍，迎擊每一次挑戰！桌球社從握拍、步法到位與發球技巧循序切入，著重手眼協調與動態視力訓練。在多球練習與單雙打友誼賽中，孩子們在高速來回間培養專注度與臨場應變力，享受揮灑汗水與切磋球技的暢快！
