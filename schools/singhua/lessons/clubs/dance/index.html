Club 09 · Sport & Dance

About the Club

認識舞蹈社 · 運動與舞蹈

Stretch, count the beat, and shine on stages around town.

拉筋、數拍子，在社區舞台與鎮上慶典發光。

←All Clubs回到社團總覽

Dance to exciting K-POP beats! Our Dance Club focuses on stretching, flexibility, and rhythm through popular dance routines. From school club performances to lively community events and local town festivals, our members perform on many stages, lighting up the audience as the most vibrant stars!

跟著熱門 K-POP 節奏舞動！舞蹈社著重身體拉筋延伸與柔軟度養成，由淺入深帶領孩子掌握流行舞蹈律動。活躍於校內社團展演、社區舞台與鎮上慶典，孩子們在每一次登台演出中展現團隊默契，成為台上最耀眼的活力焦點！
