About the Club

認識陶笛社 · 音樂

Pure, earthy sounds from a little instrument made of clay.

吹響純淨泥土之聲，小小陶笛傳遞溫暖祝福。

←All Clubs回到社團總覽

Discover the pure, earthy sounds of music! Our Ocarina Club teaches simple breath control and basic fingerings, making this charming instrument easy to pick up. The club performs at school anniversary events and our heartfelt Mother’s Day gratitude performances, sharing harmony, joy, and warm blessings with the entire school!

吹響純淨泥土之聲，陶冶溫潤童心！陶笛社從氣息控制與基本指法切入，音色質樸清脆、輕巧好上手。活躍於校慶展演與溫馨的母親節感恩表演，孩子們在多部合奏中培養團隊默契，用純真樂聲為校園生活傳遞最溫暖的祝福！
