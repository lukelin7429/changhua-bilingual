Club 06 · Music

About the Club

認識烏克麗麗社 · 音樂

Easy chords and happy songs — on stage for the school anniversary and Mother’s Day.

輕快和弦、歡樂歌聲，在校慶與母親節舞台上傳遞感恩。

←All Clubs回到社團總覽

Strum happy tunes and sing along! Our Ukulele Club introduces students to easy chords and cheerful rhythms, making music learning fun and accessible. The club performs at school anniversary events and our heartfelt Mother’s Day gratitude performances, sharing warmth, joy, and appreciation with the whole school family!

指尖撥動琴弦，彈唱歡樂童年！烏克麗麗社以輕快節奏與基礎和弦入門，引導孩子在彈唱中感受音樂的樂趣。社團活躍於校慶展演與溫馨的母親節感恩表演，孩子們藉由悠揚琴聲表達感恩與喜悅，為校園增添滿滿溫暖！

1Key Words
關鍵單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
1
ukulelen.
烏克麗麗
The ukulele is small and easy to carry.
烏克麗麗小小的，很好帶著走。
2
strumv.
刷（弦）
Strum the strings with your finger.
用手指刷弦。
3
chordn.
和弦
C is an easy chord.
C 是一個簡單的和弦。
4
rhythmn.
節奏
Clap the rhythm with me.
跟我一起拍出節奏。
5
singv.
唱歌
We sing and play at the same time.
我們邊彈邊唱。
6
songn.
歌曲
This is my favorite song.
這是我最喜歡的歌。
7
stagen.
舞台
We played on stage at the school anniversary.
我們在校慶時上台表演。
8
performv.
表演
We perform for our moms on Mother’s Day.
母親節時我們為媽媽表演。
9
thankfuladj.
感恩的
We are thankful for our families.
我們感謝我們的家人。
