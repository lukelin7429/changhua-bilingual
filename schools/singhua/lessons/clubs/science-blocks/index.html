Club 02 · Science

About the Club

認識科學積木 · 科學

Build it, test it, fix it — science you can hold in your hands.

動手組裝、測試、修正——拿在手上的科學。

←All Clubs回到社團總覽

Science blocks combine hands-on construction with scientific exploration, so students learn by building, experimenting and solving problems. By putting together different structures and mechanisms, they discover scientific principles while growing their creativity, logical thinking and teamwork. Through trial and error, they learn to observe, think for themselves and work together to turn ideas into reality.

透過科學積木課程，引導學生從動手組裝、觀察與實驗中探索科學的奧妙。孩子們運用不同積木組合各式結構與機關，在實作過程中認識基礎科學原理，培養邏輯思考、創造力及問題解決能力。課程鼓勵孩子勇於嘗試、反覆修正，並與同學合作完成作品，將想像化為具體成果。
