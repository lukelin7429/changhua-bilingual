About the Club

認識書法社 · 藝文

Ink, brush and paper — and Spring Festival couplets every Lunar New Year.

筆墨生香——每逢農曆新年，還要親手寫春聯。

←All Clubs回到社團總覽

Discover the quiet charm of traditional ink and brush! Our Calligraphy Club guides students from proper posture to brushwork and character structure. Each Lunar New Year, students write their own Spring Festival couplets, learning to write neat characters while sharing festive blessings and rich cultural traditions!

筆墨生香，定靜生慧！書法社引導孩子從端正坐姿、執筆運筆與結構佈局循序練習，在墨香中沉澱心緒。每逢農曆新年，孩子們更親手揮毫書寫春聯，不僅寫出一手端正美觀的好字，更在落筆生花中傳遞年節祝福與傳統文化之美！

2Useful Sentences
