Course 01
Our Clubs
我們的社團

Go, calligraphy, violin, K-pop dance, table tennis and more — pick a club and learn its English: key words with example sentences, a dialogue you can listen to, a matching game and a quick quiz.圍棋、書法、小提琴、舞蹈、桌球……選一個社團，學它的英文：關鍵單字與例句、可聆聽的對話、配對遊戲和小考。

30 Episodes · For Teachers
Course 02
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers, taught by Sarah Thomas across 10 topics and 30 episodes — from greetings and roll call to exam instructions. A shared resource across Changhua’s bilingual schools.給老師使用的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學，共 10 大主題、30 支影片——從問候點名到考試指令。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

13 Episodes · By Office
Course 03
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

“Broadcast English” — thirteen short episodes in which each office and section introduces, in English, the announcements it makes over the school PA system, from the curriculum section to the counseling office. A shared resource across Changhua’s bilingual schools.「廣播英語」——13 支短片，由各處室、各組用英語介紹平時透過校園廣播宣布的內容，從教務處教學組到輔導室。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。
