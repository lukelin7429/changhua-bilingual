I.

Welcome to SingHua

歡迎來到興華國小

A countryside school where children learn with heart — and parents can rest easy.

在二林鎮郊，一所讓學生用心學習、家長放心的學校。

SingHua Elementary School sits on the outskirts of Erlin Township in Changhua County. Our school district covers neighborhoods 1–4 of Dongxing Village and Xinghua Village, and our campus spreads across 14,426 square meters of green lawns, shady trees and colorful corners.

本校位於彰化縣二林鎮市郊，主要學區為東興里 1–4 鄰及興華里，校地總面積 14,426 平方公尺。

Beyond regular classes, we put real care into remedial teaching, so no child is left behind. We also bring in resources from the wider community to run clubs and talent classes — looking after every part of a child’s growth, so that students learn with heart and parents can rest easy.

學校在正式課程外，落實補救教學，並引進民間資源，安排社團、才藝課程，提供全方位的照顧，讓學生用心學習、家長放心。

II.

LOVE at the Heart

以 LOVE 為核心的教育理念

Four values, one heart — just like the heart in our crest.

全心全力的「LOVE」——正如我們校徽中的那顆愛心。

The heart in our crest

Look closely at the SingHua crest and you will find a smiling figure with a heart right at its center. That heart stands for the idea behind everything we do: LOVE — giving our whole heart and our full effort to every child.

仔細看興華的校徽，中央是一個帶著笑臉的身影，胸前有一顆愛心。這顆心，代表我們全心全力的「LOVE」理念。

III.

Four Young Explorers

學校願景 · 四大學校特色

Four kinds of explorer we hope every SingHua child can become.

自然觀察家、生活應用家、藝文創作家、公民實踐家。

Vision 01
Nature Observers
自然觀察家

Exploring and caring for the natural world, and getting close to the land of our hometown. Children observe the environment around them and learn about local ecology — starting from the land they live on, they grow a habit of protecting nature and a love for where they come from.

探索愛護生態，親近家鄉土地。引導學生觀察自然環境、認識生態，並從家鄉土地出發，培養環境保護的觀念與在地認同。

Vision 02
Hands-on Makers
生活應用家

Building with structure blocks to spark logical thinking. Through hands-on projects, students practice reasoning step by step, solving problems, and the simple joy of making something themselves.

手做結構積木，啟發邏輯思維。透過結構積木與實作體驗，培養學生的邏輯思考、問題解決能力及動手做的精神。

Vision 03
Young Artists
藝文創作家

Shaping a sense of beauty and widening creative horizons. By enjoying art, making art and trying many kinds of activities, children grow their eye for beauty, their ability to express themselves, and their creative thinking.

型塑美感知能，開拓創作視野。透過藝文欣賞、創作及多元學習活動，培養學生的美感素養、表達能力與創意思考。

Vision 04
Active Citizens
公民實踐家

Building a global outlook and putting service into action. Children grow in international awareness, communication and teamwork, and are encouraged to use what they learn in everyday life — caring for others and working for the common good.

培養國際素養，實踐服務行動。培養學生的國際素養、溝通合作能力及服務精神，鼓勵孩子將所學落實於生活，實踐關懷與共好。

IV.

Robots, Coding & Making

科技與機器人教育 · 特色教學成果

A countryside school that turns high tech into everyday tools.

把看似高深、昂貴的科技裝備，變成生活中隨手可用的工具。

SingHua is a small school in Erlin Township, but its technology and robotics teaching stands out. Our students have taken first place in Changhua County technology competitions many times, and SingHua is one of the county’s model schools for AIoT (the Artificial Intelligence of Things) education.

興華國小雖然是一所位於二林鎮的偏鄉小學，但在科技教育與機器人教學上的表現亮眼，多次獲得彰化縣內科技比賽第一名，更是縣內推動 AIoT 智慧聯網教育的指標學校之一。

01 · Robots on Campus · 校園特色機器人與伴讀設計

A robot classmate. With support from an education charity fund, SingHua brought in an ASUS Zenbo robot as a “reading buddy,” so children could talk with artificial intelligence as part of daily school life — and learn subjects such as math through hands-on experience.

Zenbo 機器人新同學：學校曾運用教育公益基金引入 ASUS Zenbo 機器人作為學生的「伴讀生」，讓孩子們在日常生活中直接與人工智慧互動，融入數學等學科的體驗式學習。

A robot that fights germs. During the COVID-19 pandemic, our older students used LEGO science blocks, ultrasonic and infrared sensors and the Mindstorms EV3 programming platform to design and build an automatic hand-sanitizer robot. When a hand comes near, it sprays alcohol and announces “消毒完成” (“Disinfection complete!”). The project won warm praise from the Changhua County Magistrate.

科技防疫機器人：疫情期間，高年級學生利用樂高科學積木、超音波與紅外線感應器，搭配 Mindstorms EV3 程式控制開發平台，自行設計組裝出「自動噴酒精防疫機器人」與自動噴酒精系統，手一靠近就會自動按壓噴灑並發出「消毒完成」語音，當時受到彰化縣長的高度讚賞。

02 · Coding & Making · 資訊與程式設計課程

Braingo + Scratch. Middle and upper graders learn to use the sensors on a Braingo board and to write programs in Scratch, the block-based coding language.

Braingo 與 Scratch 結合：引導中高年級學童學習 Braingo 基板感測元件以及 Scratch 程式圖形化運作。

Bionic beasts and smart cars. Lessons bring code and real hardware together: students assemble bionic beasts and smart cars, learning by doing and doing by learning — and building logical thinking and problem-solving skills along the way.

仿生獸與智能車：課程將程式碼與實體硬體創意結合，讓學生動手組裝仿生獸及智能車，從「做中學、學中做」裡培養邏輯思考與解決問題的能力。

03 · Competitions · 國內外競賽常勝軍

SingHua has long trained students for technology competitions. Besides winning prizes in many county maker and computing contests, our teams have done well again and again at the national and international World GreenMech Contest (世界機關王大賽) — showing how a countryside school can turn things around with technology.

學校長期培育學生參與各大科技競賽，除了在縣內各項自造與資訊競賽獲獎外，也屢次在全國與國際級的世界機關王大賽（GreenMech）中取得優異表現，展現偏鄉學校科技翻轉的實力。

Technology that once seemed far away is now a tool in every child’s hands.
透過這些特色課程，興華國小將看似高深、昂貴的科技裝備，轉化為生活中隨手可應用的工具，有效翻轉了偏鄉的教育資源限制。
V.

Clubs & Talents

多元社團與才藝課程

After class, there’s a club for every kind of talent.

下課後，每一種才能都有屬於自己的社團。

With help from community resources, SingHua runs clubs in music, art, sport, strategy games and science. Our clubs perform at the school anniversary, graduation and Mother’s Day celebrations, and on stages around Erlin — so every child can find something they love, and a place to shine.

學校引進民間資源開設多元社團：音樂、美術、運動、棋藝、科學應有盡有。社團孩子們在校慶、畢業典禮、母親節感恩活動與鎮上的舞台上展演，讓每個孩子都找到自己喜歡的事、站上屬於自己的舞台。
