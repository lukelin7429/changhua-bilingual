Human Library: Alumna Chen Mei-he Comes Home to Share Life Lessons Through Drama and Plant Weaving

真人圖書館邀校友陳美合學姊返校分享　以戲劇與植物編織傳遞生命智慧
←Back to News回到新聞列表

For our Human Library, SingHua invited alumna Chen Mei-he back to school to share her life experience. Together with the storytelling studio 故事開門事務所, her whole family took the stage for a lively drama performance, turning moments from everyday life into touching stories. Full of courage, passion and feeling, the show invited the younger students to open the “book of life,” draw strength from real experiences, and explore their own paths of growth.

本校真人圖書館邀請校友陳美合學姊返校分享人生閱歷，並攜手故事開門事務所，由全家總動員帶來精彩生動的戲劇演出，將生活點滴化為舞台上的動人故事。透過充滿勇氣、熱情與感動的演出，引領學弟妹們翻開「人生之書」，從真實生命經驗中汲取力量，探索屬於自己的成長方向。

Besides the drama, Mei-he also showed students how to weave toys from plants, a pastime from earlier generations. Working with their own hands, the children felt the charm of natural materials and the cleverness of traditional skills — learning by doing, discovering valuable everyday wisdom, and seeing why passing on traditional culture matters.

除了戲劇欣賞，美合學姊也親自指導學生體驗早期童玩的植物編織手作，讓孩子們透過動手實作，感受天然素材的魅力與傳統技藝的巧思，在做中學、學中做的過程中，認識珍貴的生活智慧，體會傳統文化的傳承價值。

Weaving toys from plants植物編織童玩
Drama on stage精彩的戲劇演出

To close, Mei-he encouraged the younger students to keep their love of learning, chase their dreams bravely, face challenges without fear and work hard to write their own wonderful life stories. We also thank the director of the Erlin Public Library for attending on behalf of the township mayor, the county government for its funding, and 故事開門事務所 for its generous sponsorship — together they made this a day of learning that was inspiring, rooted in culture and full of hands-on experience.

活動最後，美合學姊勉勵學弟妹們保持學習熱忱，勇敢追逐夢想、不畏挑戰，努力寫下屬於自己的精彩人生。同時，感謝二林圖書館館長代表鎮長蒞臨欣賞，並感謝縣府經費支持與挹注，以及故事開門事務所的熱情贊助，讓活動得以順利推展，為學生帶來兼具生命啟發、文化傳承與實作體驗的多元學習機會。
