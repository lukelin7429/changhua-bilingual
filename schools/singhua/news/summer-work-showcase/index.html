Summer Work Showcase: Hard Work and Bright Ideas on Display

暑假作品成果展　展現學習成果與創意巧思
←Back to News回到新聞列表

SingHua held a showcase of summer work, displaying what our students learned and created over the summer holidays. From carefully finished summer homework to original creative projects, students recorded their growth through effort and persistence — and by giving each other feedback, they learned to appreciate one another’s strengths.

舉辦暑假作品成果展，展現孩子們暑假期間的學習成果與創意巧思。從認真完成暑假作業到創作特色作品，學生們以努力與堅持記錄成長，並透過師生互評，學習欣賞彼此的優點。

Students who did outstanding work on their summer assignments were also honored at the event. Every bit of care and effort deserves recognition, and we hope our students will carry this confidence and sense of achievement with them as they keep shining on their learning journey!

活動中特別表揚暑假作業表現優異的學生，肯定每一份用心與付出，期盼孩子們帶著自信與成就感，持續在學習路上發光發熱！
