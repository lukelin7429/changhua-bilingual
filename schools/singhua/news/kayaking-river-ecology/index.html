Field Study Day: Kayaking and River Ecology Open New Outdoor Horizons

特色遊學日　輕艇體驗結合溪流生態探索，開啟戶外學習新視野
←Back to News回到新聞列表

Class 6A set out on a special field-study day. In the morning they visited Macuo Elementary School to try kayaking. Starting with the equipment and safety rules, students learned step by step how to paddle, turn and bring their kayaks in to dock, then took on a speed race and an underwater treasure hunt — pushing past their limits, building confidence and working as a team, with plenty of laughter along the way.

六年甲班展開特色遊學活動，上午前往媽厝國小體驗水上輕艇。學生從認識裝備與安全守則開始，逐步學習划行、轉彎及停靠技巧，並透過競速挑戰與水中尋寶活動，在歡笑中突破自我、培養自信與團隊合作精神。

In the afternoon, the class moved on to the Dongluo River Ecological Education Park. Guides introduced this important river of the Changhua plain and the life along its banks, and through hands-on habitat restoration and crafts, students came to see why protecting nature and the land matters — and turned their love for the environment into action.

下午，師生轉往東螺溪生態教育園區，在導覽人員帶領下認識彰化平原的重要河川與流域生態，並透過生態復育實作及手作體驗，深入了解自然環境與土地保育的重要性，將愛護環境的理念落實於行動。

Learning about the river with a guide跟著導覽認識流域生態
Class 6A at the Dongluo River park六甲在東螺溪生態園區

From challenges on the water to quiet observation by the river, the children gained so much from a day of both action and reflection. SingHua thanks Macuo Elementary School and the Dongluo River ecology team for their careful planning and expert guidance, which let our students step outside the classroom, get close to nature, and learn and grow through real experience.

從水上挑戰到溪畔生態探索，孩子們在動態體驗與靜態觀察中收穫滿滿。興華國小感謝媽厝國小與東螺溪生態團隊的用心安排與專業引導，讓學生走出教室、親近自然，在探索中學習，在體驗中成長。
