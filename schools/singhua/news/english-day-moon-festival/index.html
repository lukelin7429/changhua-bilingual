English Day Meets the Moon Festival and Teachers’ Day

英語日融合中秋與教師節　學子勇敢開口說英語，傳遞感恩與祝福
←Back to News回到新聞列表

This English Day brought together the Mid-Autumn Festival and Teachers’ Day, filling the campus with laughter and a warm festive spirit. In a relaxed, cheerful setting, students bravely spoke up in English — growing more confident, learning about culture through the festivals and sharing good wishes — so that learning English blended naturally with everyday life.

英語日結合中秋節與教師節的多元活動，校園中洋溢著歡笑與溫馨的節慶氣氛。學生們在輕鬆愉快的學習情境中勇敢開口說英語，不僅提升語言表達的自信，也藉由節慶主題認識文化、分享祝福，讓英語學習與生活經驗自然結合。

The warmest moment of all came when the children called out, “Thank you, teachers!” Their sincere words turned gratitude for their teachers’ hard work into heartfelt wishes, adding even more warmth to this year’s Teachers’ Day and showing the beauty of real, person-to-person communication in language learning.

活動中最溫暖的時刻，莫過於孩子們大聲說出「Thank you, teachers!」，將對師長的感謝化為真摯的祝福。一句句誠懇的話語，不僅傳遞了學生對老師辛勤付出的感恩，也讓今年的教師節增添更多感動與溫度，展現語言學習中人與人之間真誠交流的美好。

Festival English words中秋節英語單字
Two celebrations in one day中秋與教師節一起慶祝

With the Mid-Autumn Festival here, the school also sent warm wishes to all students, teachers and parents: “Happy Teachers’ Day!” and “Happy Mid-Autumn Festival!” By practicing these greetings, the children used English in a real situation and felt what thankfulness, sharing and family reunion mean at festival time.

適逢中秋佳節，學校也向全體師生與家長送上誠摯祝福，祝大家「Happy Teachers’ Day!」教師節快樂，以及「Happy Mid-Autumn Festival!」中秋佳節愉快！透過英語祝賀語的練習，孩子們將語言應用於真實情境，也在節慶交流中感受感恩、分享與團圓的意義。

We will keep offering rich and varied activities so that children can learn English through fun, get to know cultures through experience, and practice thankfulness and care in daily life. Happy Teachers’ Day to all our hardworking, lovable teachers — and may everyone enjoy a safe, happy long weekend and a full-moon reunion with family!

本校期盼持續透過豐富多元的活動，讓孩子在歡樂中學習英語、在體驗中認識文化，並在生活中實踐感恩與關懷。祝福所有辛苦又可愛的老師教師節快樂，也祝大家連假平安愉快、月圓人團圓，在幸福與溫暖中共度美好佳節！
