Yuanta Happiness Day: A Joyful Trip and Gifts That Warm the Heart

元大文教基金會送愛興華國小　十鼓文創園區歡樂行，幸福大禮包溫暖童心
←Back to News回到新聞列表

On October 3, the Yuanta Foundation (元大文教基金會) invited SingHua’s children to the Ten Drum creative park in Tainan for a day full of laughter and happiness. Besides a joyful day out together, the foundation prepared a special “happiness gift pack” for every child — sneakers, a jacket and long pants — so the children could feel the warmth and kindness of the wider community.

10 月 3 日，元大文教基金會邀請興華國小的孩子們前往臺南十鼓文創園區，展開一場充滿歡笑與幸福的戶外體驗之旅。除了共享歡樂時光，基金會更特別準備「幸福大禮包」，為每位孩子送上球鞋、外套與長褲，讓孩子們在滿滿的關懷與祝福中，感受社會的溫暖與愛心。

The trip left the children with wonderful memories, and it was also a heartwarming lesson in gratitude. To thank the foundation’s friendly volunteers for their company, the children performed a lively dance, returning all that love and good wishes with big smiles and plenty of energy — filling the place with joy.

這趟旅程不僅讓孩子們留下美好的回憶，更是一堂充滿溫度的感恩教育課。面對元大文教基金會志工們熱情親切的陪伴，孩子們也特別獻上一段活力十足的舞蹈，以燦爛笑容與熱情演出，回饋這份珍貴的愛與祝福，讓現場洋溢著歡樂與感動。

Happiness gift packs幸福大禮包
Drumming at the Ten Drum park十鼓園區打鼓體驗

As they received these good wishes, the children also learned to be content, to treasure what they have and to be grateful for every good thing in life. As the day’s message put it: “Being content is happiness; with a grateful heart, you can see even more of life’s good things.” We hope the children will turn this warmth into strength for the road ahead, and one day pass love and kindness on to the people around them.

透過這次活動，孩子們在接受祝福的同時，也學習知足惜福、心懷感恩，珍惜生活中每一份得來不易的美好。正如活動所傳遞的理念：「懂得知足，便是幸福；心懷感恩，便能看見更多美好。」期盼孩子們將這份溫暖化為前進的力量，並在未來把愛與善意傳遞給身邊的人。

SingHua sincerely thanks the Yuanta Foundation for its care and generosity, every volunteer who gave their time so warmly, and our school staff who helped lead the children. With the support of so many kind people, our children’s childhood is more colorful and happy, and seeds of love and gratitude are quietly sprouting in their hearts.

興華國小衷心感謝元大文教基金會的用心與愛心，以及每一位熱情付出的志工叔叔、阿姨、哥哥與姐姐，也感謝學校同仁協助帶領孩子參與活動。因為有各界善心人士的陪伴與支持，孩子們的童年更加繽紛幸福，也讓愛與感恩的種子在心中悄悄萌芽。
