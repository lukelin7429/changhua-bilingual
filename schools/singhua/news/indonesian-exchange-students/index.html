Indonesian Exchange Students Visit SingHua and Open a Window on the World

印尼交換學生走進校園　開啟學子國際視野
←Back to News回到新聞列表

From October 12 to 13, exchange students from Indonesia visited SingHua for a cultural exchange with our middle and upper graders that was full of surprises. The visitors shared Indonesia’s culture, daily life and customs, and through lively interaction they helped our children discover the similarities and differences between Indonesia and Taiwan, widening their view of the world’s many cultures.

印尼交換學生於 10 月 12 日至 13 日蒞臨本校，與中高年級學生展開一場充滿驚喜與收穫的國際文化交流。活動中，交換學生分享印尼的文化特色、日常生活與風土民情，並透過互動交流，引導孩子們認識印尼與臺灣之間的文化異同，拓展多元文化視野。

This rare experience took learning beyond the textbook and brought the world onto our campus. The children not only learned more about Indonesian culture, but also, through sharing and conversation, learned to respect differences and appreciate diversity — growing an open, welcoming global outlook and planting the seeds of international education in their hearts.

這次難得的交流經驗，讓學習走出課本、讓世界走進校園。孩子們不僅增進對印尼文化的認識，也在分享與互動中學習尊重差異、欣賞多元，培養開放與包容的國際素養，讓國際教育的種子在心中悄悄萌芽。

Sharing life in Indonesia分享印尼的生活
A window on another culture打開認識世界的窗

Special thanks to President Lin Cheng-lin (林政霖會長) for kindly making the introduction that made this valuable exchange possible, giving our children the chance to meet, learn from and grow with friends from a different cultural background. We look forward to more cross-cultural exchanges that help our students see a wider world, explore bravely and embrace diversity!

特別感謝林政霖會長的熱心引薦，促成這次珍貴的國際交流機會，讓孩子們得以與不同文化背景的朋友相遇、學習與成長。期待未來持續拓展跨文化交流，帶領學子看見更寬廣的世界，勇敢探索、擁抱多元！
