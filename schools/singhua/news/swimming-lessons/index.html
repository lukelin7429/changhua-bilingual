Swimming Lessons Are Back: Building Skills Step by Step, and Water Safety for Life

游泳課熱力登場　循序學習游泳技能，扎根防溺自救觀念
←Back to News回到新聞列表

Swimming lessons for SingHua’s upper graders are underway! This semester the new Class 5A joins in too. The course begins with getting comfortable in the water, and each student then works through swimming levels 2 to 5 at their own pace — building skills and confidence in a safe environment.

興華國小高年級游泳課正式啟航！本學期加入五年甲班新生，課程從親水體驗與水性適應開始，依學生能力循序挑戰第 2 至第 5 級游泳指標，讓孩子在安全的環境中逐步提升技能，建立自信。

The course puts special weight on drowning prevention and self-rescue. Students learn the ten tips for preventing drowning and memorize the five-step rescue rule: Shout, Call, Reach, Throw, Row. By practicing the jellyfish float, the back float and treading water, they learn to stay calm and buy time if they are ever in danger — becoming safer in and around water.

課程特別重視防溺與自救教育，帶領學生認識防溺十招，並牢記救援五字訣「叫、叫、伸、拋、划」。透過水母漂、仰漂、立泳（踩水）等練習，學習在遇險時保持冷靜、爭取時間，提升水域安全與自我保護能力。

Warming up first下水前先暖身
Step by step in the water循序漸進學游泳

From holding their breath and rhythmic breathing to freestyle, backstroke and endurance challenges, the children will build up their swimming strength step by step, working toward swimming 50 meters and treading water for one minute. SingHua hopes this course will help every child not only learn to swim, but also understand water safety and self-rescue — so they can learn and grow happily, and safely, in the water.

從水中閉氣、韻律呼吸到捷泳、仰泳與耐力挑戰，孩子們將逐步累積游泳實力，朝 50 公尺游泳及立泳 1 分鐘的能力目標邁進。興華國小期盼透過循序漸進的課程，讓孩子不只學會游泳，更能具備正確的防溺觀念與自救能力，在水中安心學習、快樂成長！
