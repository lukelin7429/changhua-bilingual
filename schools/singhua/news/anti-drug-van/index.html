Anti-Drug Van Visits SingHua: Learning to Spot, Understand and Refuse Drugs

反毒行動車入校巡迴　提升學童識毒、知毒、拒毒能力
←Back to News回到新聞列表

Today the Anti-Drug Mobile Van rolled onto campus. Through hands-on, interactive exhibits, our middle graders learned how new kinds of drugs can be disguised and how they harm the body, building the right ideas for staying drug-free.

今天安排「反毒行動車」入校巡迴，透過沉浸式互動體驗，帶領中年級學生認識新興毒品的偽裝方式與危害，建立正確的防毒觀念。

The visit focused on three goals: to spot drugs, to understand them and to refuse them. Students looked at real displays of disguised drugs, learned how drugs damage health, and used role-play questions and multimedia activities to practice saying no firmly and asking for help.

活動以「識毒、知毒、拒毒」為主軸，透過實體展示認識偽裝毒品、了解毒品對身體的危害，並藉由情境問答與多媒體互動，練習勇敢拒絕及主動求助的方法。

Hands-on exhibits互動展示體驗
Watching and learning together一起看、一起學

By starting anti-drug education early, we hope to sharpen children’s awareness and their ability to protect themselves — keeping every student healthy and safe, and building a friendly, drug-free campus together.

期盼透過從小扎根的反毒教育，提升孩子的警覺性與自我保護能力，守護每位學童的健康與安全，共同營造安心、友善的無毒校園。
