Fun English Summer Camp Kicks Off

Fun English Summer Camp 開跑囉！

Dayuan's summer English camp is in full swing! This year, the whole school is divided into two English camps, and first up are highlights from the camp for middle- and upper-grade students.

The course was co-taught by our foreign teacher and Dayuan's own teachers, using lively games, group interaction, situational dialogue, and a variety of activities to help children bravely open their mouths and speak English in a relaxed, happy setting — building their listening and speaking skills, and broadening their view of the world.

Summer isn't just for relaxing — it's also a great time for meaningful learning. Watching the children speak up with confidence and join in with joy, every smile was the most beautiful result of learning! We look forward to more exciting lessons ahead, and invite everyone to keep following along for highlights from the junior camp too!

💙 Happy Learning, Happy Summer!

中文原文 · Chinese

大園國小暑期英語營熱鬧登場！今年全校分為兩個英語營隊，首先為大家帶來的是中高年級英語營精彩花絮。

課程由我們外師搭配本校教師共同授課，透過活潑有趣的遊戲、分組互動、情境對話及多元活動，讓孩子在輕鬆愉快的氛圍中勇敢開口說英語，提升聽、說能力，也培養國際視野。

暑假不只是放鬆，更是充實學習的好時光。看著孩子們自信開口、開心參與，每一張笑容都是學習最美的成果！期待接下來更多精彩課程，也請大家持續關注低年級英語營的精彩分享！

💙 Happy Learning, Happy Summer! 多元展能・自信飛揚，在大園．我很幸福！
