The Feeling Lab

感覺研究所：動畫創作營圓滿落幕

"What does a feeling look like? Can you see it? Touch it? Or can you only feel it in your heart?"

This week, Dayuan's students joined "Big Hands Little Feet," a team who traveled all the way from Germany, for a cross-border learning journey full of creativity and imagination. Through the Feeling Lab stop-motion animation camp, children began with their own senses — using materials, space, sound, movement, and interaction — to slowly turn abstract feelings into lively, playful animated films.

From the very first question — "What does a feeling feel like?" — to designing characters, building sets, filming, and recording sound, every step of the camp brought its own surprises, sparking the children's creativity and their confidence in expressing themselves. Through teamwork and constant discussion, they didn't just finish a set of finished films — they also learned to listen, share, and respect each other's ideas.

On the final day, the children held a showcase for their families. Parents watched their children present their work with confidence and pride, and the room filled with applause and emotion. Two coordinators from the Lu Le Platform, who had helped arrange the program, also visited to interview the students and see a week of hard work pay off.

Special thanks to the Lu Le Platform for matching Dayuan with this rare opportunity, giving children in a rural school the chance to connect with an international team and enjoy a truly cross-cultural, cross-disciplinary learning experience. And heartfelt thanks to Germany's Big Hands Little Feet team for planning every day of the camp with such care, guiding the children to discover feelings in everyday life and grow their skills as young artists — leaving behind one of the most beautiful memories of this international learning journey.

The week of classes may be over, but the children's journey of exploring the world has only just begun.
一週的課程雖然結束了，但孩子們探索世界、勇於創新的腳步才正要開始。
中文原文 · Chinese

「感覺，是什麼呢？」看得見嗎？摸得到嗎？還是只能用心去感受？

這一週，大園國小的孩子們跟著遠從德國而來的「大手小腳」團隊，展開一場充滿創意與想像力的跨國學習旅程，透過「感覺研究所」動畫創作營，從感官探索出發，運用材料、空間、聲音、動作與互動，一步步將抽象的感受化為生動有趣的停格動畫作品。

孩子們在課程中勇於發想、動手創作，從一開始探索「感覺像什麼？」，到設計角色、搭建場景、拍攝動畫、錄製聲音，每一個環節都充滿驚喜，也激發了無限的創造力與表達能力。透過團隊合作與反覆討論，不僅完成了一部部精彩的作品，更學會了傾聽、分享與尊重彼此的想法。

活動最後一天舉辦成果發表會，家長們蒞臨欣賞孩子們的創作成果，看見孩子自信分享、驕傲展示作品的模樣，現場充滿掌聲與感動。鹿樂平臺兩位承辦姐姐也特地到校採訪與觀摩，見證孩子們一週努力學習的豐碩成果。

特別感謝鹿樂平臺媒合這次難得的課程，讓偏鄉孩子有機會與國際團隊交流，享受跨文化、跨領域的學習體驗，也衷心感謝德國大手小腳團隊用心規劃每一天的課程，引導孩子發現生活中的感覺、培養藝術創作能力，讓這趟國際學習之旅留下最美好的回憶。

一週的課程雖然結束了，但孩子們探索世界、勇於創新的腳步才正要開始。相信每一份感受、每一段創作，都將成為成長路上最珍貴的養分。
