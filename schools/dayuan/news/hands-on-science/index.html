Hands-On Science, Week One

夏日樂學．手作中學科學

Dayuan's "Summer Fun Learning" program kicked off with excitement! This two-week, ten-afternoon course centers on "learning science by doing, by making, and by playing," building students' scientific literacy and problem-solving skills through hands-on exploration.

For the first three days, the program was led personally by Director Chang-Ching, our school's own director, who brought a rich and engaging lineup of natural science activities and hands-on projects, guiding the children to observe, think, and explore.

For the next seven days, professional science teachers will take over, bringing even more exciting experiments and inquiry-based lessons, keeping the children discovering the wonders of science throughout the summer.

Learning science by doing, by making, and by playing.
動手做中學科學、做中學、玩中學。

Thank you to every teacher who carefully planned and taught this program — we hope the children keep exploring and growing happily through Summer Fun Learning!

中文原文 · Chinese

大園國小「夏日樂學」熱鬧展開！大園國小「夏日樂學~暑期科學營」熱鬧展開！為期二週、十個下午的課程，以「動手做中學科學、做中學、玩中學」為主軸，讓孩子在動手操作與實驗探索中，培養科學素養與解決問題的能力。

第一天至第三天由本校長慶主任親自帶領課程，內容豐富精彩，透過各式自然科學活動與實作，引導孩子觀察、思考、探索，讓每位學生都收穫滿滿。看看照片，就知道這三天孩子們體驗了哪些有趣的自然科學活動！

接下來的七天，將由專業自然科學教師接棒，帶來更多精彩的實驗與探究課程，陪伴孩子持續發現科學的奧妙，讓這個暑假充滿學習的驚喜與樂趣！

感謝所有用心規劃與授課的老師，期待孩子們在夏日樂學中，持續探索、快樂成長！
