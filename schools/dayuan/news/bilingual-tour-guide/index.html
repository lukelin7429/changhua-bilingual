Little Bilingual Tour Guides Set Off

小小雙語導覽員出發！用雙語認識家鄉，用行動走向國際

Dayuan Elementary took on Changhua County's "International Education Center Little Bilingual Tour Guide Training Program," leading children out of the classroom and into the community. Through a mix of bilingual learning, local culture, and real, on-site guiding practice, the program is helping children build the confidence to introduce their hometown in English — starting from knowing their own land, and walking, step by step, toward the wider world.

Stop 1 — Fengze Community, the Shi-Er Regeneration Park

The children stepped into the story-filled Shi-Er Regeneration Park to see how a community can bring an unused space back to life. Through the park's signature piece — an old pig path turned into public art — they learned about local history and the creativity behind community regeneration. From that one old path, the children saw a community's effort to preserve its history while giving it new life, and practiced telling their hometown's story in simple English along the way.

Stop 2 — Xihu Sugar Refinery

Next, the children explored the nostalgic Xihu Sugar Refinery, discovering a hundred years of sugar-industry heritage — old refinery buildings, railway culture, and the history of the "five-cent train" — and even got to ride the refinery's own vintage narrow-gauge train! Free to enter, the park is home to a beloved old locomotive for train fans, historic sugar-processing buildings, and green space and sweet treats perfect for families. As the children explored, they practiced turning the English they'd learned in class into real conversations about the world around them.

Learn English. Know our hometown. Share our story.
學習英語、認識家鄉、分享家鄉的故事！

Through repeated hands-on visits and bilingual practice, the children aren't just "learning English" — they're learning to observe, express themselves, cooperate, and share. We can't wait to see these brave little tour guides introduce Changhua — and their hometown — to more people, with a confident smile and simple, clear English.

大園國小承辦「彰化縣國際教育中心小小雙語導覽員培訓計畫」，帶領孩子走出教室、深入社區，透過雙語學習 × 在地文化 × 實地導覽，培養孩子用英語介紹家鄉的能力，讓孩子從認識自己的土地開始，勇敢走向國際。

📍 第一站｜豐澤社區・石二創生園區。孩子們走進充滿故事的石二創生園區，認識社區如何將閒置空間重新活化，並透過「舊豬道變身裝置藝術」的特色，了解地方歷史與社區創生的巧思。從舊豬道，看見社區保存歷史、創造新生命的努力，也讓孩子學習用簡單的雙語向大家介紹家鄉的故事。

📍 第二站｜溪湖糖廠。來到充滿懷舊風情的溪湖糖廠，孩子們探索珍貴的百年糖業文化資產，認識製糖老廠房、鐵道文化與五分車歷史，還體驗了充滿特色的懷舊五分車！園區免門票，除了有鐵道迷喜愛的珍貴火車頭、歷史悠久的製糖建築，也有適合親子休閒的綠地與人氣甜點冰品。孩子們一邊探索、一邊學習，將課堂上的英語真正運用在生活中！

Learn English. Know our hometown. Share our story. 學習英語、認識家鄉、分享家鄉的故事！

透過一次次的實地走讀與雙語練習，孩子們不只是「學英語」，更學會觀察、表達、合作與分享。期待這群勇敢的小小雙語導覽員，未來能用自信的笑容與簡單的英語，向更多人介紹彰化、介紹家鄉！
