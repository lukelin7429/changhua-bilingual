Fun English! Junior Camp

Fun English！暑假低年級英語營快樂開課

Dayuan's summer English camp for younger students kicked off with excitement! This year's course was carefully designed by our English specialist teachers, blending everyday English with games, interactive conversations, and situational activities to help children naturally open their mouths and speak English — making learning lively and fun.

The course also included cartoon drawing and dessert-soup cooking, letting children create with their hands while learning English vocabulary and everyday conversation. Learning by doing and learning through play helped strengthen their listening and speaking skills, while also building creativity and life skills — filling learning with joy and a sense of achievement.

Opening an English-learning journey with smiles.
孩子們用笑容開啟英語學習之旅。

This summer, the children opened their English-learning journey with smiles, gaining confidence and a wider view of the world in a relaxed, happy atmosphere — leaving behind wonderful summer memories.

中文原文 · Chinese

大園國小暑假低年級英語營熱鬧展開！本次營隊由英語專長教師精心規劃課程，結合生活化英語教學，透過遊戲、互動對話與情境活動，引導孩子自然開口說英語，讓學習更加生動有趣。

課程更融入卡通繪畫創作與甜湯料理體驗，孩子們一邊動手創作、一邊學習英語單字與生活會話，在做中學、玩中學，提升英語聽說能力，也培養創意與生活技能，讓學習充滿歡樂與成就感。

這個暑假，孩子們用笑容開啟英語學習之旅，在輕鬆愉快的氛圍中累積自信、拓展視野，留下美好的暑假回憶！
