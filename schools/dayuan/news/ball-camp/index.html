Game On! Ball Sports Camp

球類營熱血開打．快樂運動學習趣

Dayuan's summer ball sports camp kicked off with excitement! This year's program features a variety of sports, including basketball, soccer, pickleball, and kickball. With careful guidance from their teachers, students started with the basics and gradually built their skills, teamwork, and confidence.

Even when trying a new sport for the very first time, the children picked it up quickly, their faces lit with joy as they experienced the achievement and fun that sports bring.

Through this summer camp, the children didn't just build physical fitness — they also learned to follow the rules and encourage one another, creating rich and unforgettable summer memories through laughter and sweat.

❤️ Healthy growth, happy learning.
陪伴孩子健康成長、快樂學習！

Special thanks to funding from the Child Welfare League Foundation, which gave the children rich, diverse, and high-quality opportunities to learn through sport.

中文原文 · Chinese

大園國小暑期球類營熱鬧展開！今年規劃籃球、足球、匹克球、樂樂棒球等多元課程，在老師細心指導下，孩子們從基本動作開始練習，逐步培養運動技巧、團隊合作與自信心。即使是第一次接觸不同球類，也能快速上手，臉上洋溢著開心的笑容，享受運動帶來的成就感與樂趣。

透過暑期營隊，孩子們不僅鍛鍊體能，更學會遵守規則、互相鼓勵，在歡笑與汗水中留下充實又難忘的暑假回憶。

❤️ 特別感謝兒童福利聯盟經費補助，讓孩子們擁有豐富、多元且優質的運動學習機會，陪伴孩子健康成長、快樂學習！
