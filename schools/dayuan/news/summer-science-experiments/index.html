Science Gets Even More Exciting

夏日樂學～自然科學實驗營精彩不停！

The Summer Science Camp moved into its most exciting stretch yet, with hands-on experiments that were even more fun than before. Children got to try out spinning eggs, rain sticks, a hydraulic excavator arm, a simple generator, a slide whistle, and a wobbling pencil doll — and after finishing each one, they couldn't stop saying: "Science is actually this amazing — this much fun!"

With teachers guiding them step by step and letting them build and test everything with their own hands, the children didn't just enjoy the fun of "doing it themselves" — they came to understand the science behind each experiment, building their observation skills, their ability to think things through, and their confidence in solving problems. Learning became more than just facts to remember; it turned into a journey of exploring and discovering.

Thank you to every teacher who planned such a rich set of activities, giving the children a summer full of learning, laughter, and growth. This unforgettable stretch of scientific exploration will surely become one of the best memories of their summer vacation.

We're already looking forward to next year's Summer Science Camp!
期待明年夏日樂學再推出更多新奇、有趣的科學實驗！
中文原文 · Chinese

夏日樂學自然科學實驗營進入更精彩、更好玩的實驗課程！孩子們親手體驗玩蛋、雨聲筒、挖土機、發電機、伸縮喇叭、娃娃搖鉛筆實驗等多項科學活動，每完成一個實驗，都驚呼連連：「原來科學這麼神奇、這麼有趣！」

透過老師生動的引導與實作體驗，孩子們不僅享受動手做的樂趣，更從操作中理解自然科學的原理，培養觀察力、思考力與解決問題的能力，讓學習不只是知識，更是充滿探索與發現的旅程。

感謝所有師長用心規劃豐富的課程，讓孩子們在暑假收穫滿滿，在歡笑中學習，在實驗中成長。相信這段難忘的科學探索，將成為孩子們暑假最美好的回憶。

期待明年夏日樂學再推出更多新奇、有趣的科學實驗，陪伴孩子持續探索科學的無限魅力！
