Dancing with Confidence

舞動可愛．舞出自信

The summer dance camp danced into action with full energy! First- through third-graders followed their teacher's expert guidance, moving step by step from rhythm and beats to full dance routines. With cute footwork and bright smiles, the children filled the hot summer days with youthful energy and joy.

In a relaxed and cheerful setting, the children built their sense of rhythm and body coordination, while also learning to express themselves bravely and enjoy the simple fun of dance. Every step, every smile, is one of summer's best footprints of growth.

Special thanks to funding from the Child Welfare League Foundation, which allowed the school to plan such a rich variety of summer camps, giving children more chances to learn and explore — and making this summer even more colorful.

Every step, every smile, is one of summer's best footprints of growth.
每一個舞步、每一個笑容，都是暑假最美好的成長足跡！
中文原文 · Chinese

暑假舞蹈營熱情展開！一至三年級的小朋友跟著老師的專業指導，從律動、節奏到舞步，一步步舞動身軀、展現活力。孩子們用可愛的步伐和燦爛的笑容，為炎炎夏日注入滿滿青春與歡樂。

在輕鬆愉快的課程中，孩子們不僅培養節奏感與肢體協調，更勇敢展現自我、享受舞蹈的樂趣。每一個舞步、每一個笑容，都是暑假最美好的成長足跡！

特別感謝兒童福利聯盟經費補助，讓學校能規劃多元豐富的暑假營隊活動，提供孩子更多學習與探索的機會，讓這個暑假更加多采多姿！

舞蹈營精彩落幕後，接下來又將迎來哪一個令人期待的營隊呢？
