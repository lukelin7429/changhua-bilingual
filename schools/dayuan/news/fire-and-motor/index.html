Fire, Friction & a Spinning Motor

夏日樂學科學營｜越來越有趣！

Each day of the Summer Science Camp has been more exciting than the last! Over these two days, the children became junior scientists, taking on two amazing hands-on science challenges.

Making Fire by Friction
The children tried the age-old method of creating heat through friction, feeling for themselves the wisdom behind how ancient people made fire. Through trial and error, they built up patience and problem-solving skills.

The Mendocino Motor
By combining solar power, magnetism, and electric current, the children made a small motor spin as if by magic — learning about energy conversion and the basics of electromagnetism, and feeling the wonder of science in action.

Science is really all around them in everyday life.
孩子們在動手實作、觀察與討論中，發現科學其實就在生活裡。

Through hands-on building, careful observation, and discussion with each other, the children discovered that science is really all around them in everyday life. Every bit of exploring brought its own surprise, and every success brought its own sense of achievement.

中文原文 · Chinese

夏日樂學科學營的課程一天比一天精彩，越來越有趣！這二天孩子們化身小小科學家，挑戰兩項充滿驚奇的科學實作。

鑽木取火：親自體驗摩擦生熱的原理，感受古人取火的智慧，在不斷嘗試中培養耐心與解決問題的能力。

門多西諾馬達：透過太陽能、磁力與電流的結合，讓馬達神奇地轉動，學習能源轉換與電磁原理，感受科學的魅力。

孩子們在動手實作、觀察與討論中，發現科學其實就在生活裡，每一次探索都充滿驚喜，每一次成功都充滿成就感！
