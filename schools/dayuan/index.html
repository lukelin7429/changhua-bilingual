I.

Welcome to Dayuan

歡迎來到大園國小

A small and beautiful school where students are honest, diligent, and humble in learning.

小而美的校園，學生謹守「誠正勤樸」校訓，克守本分且虛心向學。

Located in Puyan Township, Dayuan Elementary School is a small campus with an outstanding environment, recognized by the Ministry of Education as a sustainable campus. Rainwater collection and permeable pavement designs allow recycled rainwater to irrigate the campus's flowers and trees, keeping the grounds lush and green year-round.

大園國小位於彰化縣埔鹽鄉，雖然學校規模小，但校園環境優美，是教育部認證的永續校園執行學校。校園設有雨撲滿與透水鋪面的環境教育設計，讓回收的雨水澆灌校內花草樹木，使校園終年綠意盎然、草木扶疏。

Dayuan values student development through five main axes: cultivating virtue and nurturing talent, in-depth reading, diverse talents, an English-language foundation, and connecting with the international community — supported by a strong teaching team and a rich, ecological campus.

大園國小重視學生發展，以「培德育才、深耕閱讀、多元展能、英語奠基、接軌國際」五大主軸，讓學生享有最高品質的教育內容，並擁有堅強的教學團隊與生態豐富的校園。

Since the 110th academic year, Dayuan has implemented partial bilingual teaching, building a bilingual environment for students to learn and progress gradually. In the 111th academic year, a Belizean foreign teacher joined the school, creating a more interactive classroom and a livelier language environment.

大園國小自 110 學年度起執行部分領域雙語教學，為孩子營造逐步學習與進步的雙語環境。111 學年度，本校貝里斯籍外師到校，透過中外師教學與互動，創造更生活化、更具國際觀的語言環境。

Since the 112th academic year, Dayuan has served as Changhua County's International Education Resource Center (IERC), responsible for organizing countywide international exchange and education travel programs — a gateway for teachers and students across Changhua to the world.

大園國小於 112 學年度起接下彰化縣國際教育資源中心（IERC）的任務，專責辦理全縣性國際交流與國際教育旅行計畫，為彰化縣的親師生打開一道通往世界的門。

"Sincerity, Rectitude, Diligence, Simplicity"
誠正勤樸 — 大園國小校訓
International · Innovative · Diverse · Virtuous
國際、創新、多元、品德——大園的四大教育目標

Dayuan's school-based curriculum, "Savor Dayuan, Grow Forward," unfolds around four goals — internationalization, innovation, diversity, and moral character — a spirit visible in every corner of campus life, from orchid-lined corridors to colourful classroom courtyards.

大園國小校訂課程「品味大園‧勇往植前」，以國際、創新、多元、品德四大目標開展，這份精神也體現在校園的每個角落——從蘭花點綴的走廊，到繽紛的教室庭園。
