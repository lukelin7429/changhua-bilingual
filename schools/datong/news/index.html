Campus · 校園建設

A Brand-New Building新校舍仁愛樓落成

2024–2025 · 2024–2025 年

Datong's old Ren-Ai Building was taken down and rebuilt. The bright new building gives students safe, modern classrooms — a fresh start that makes Datong feel brand new.

大同的老舊仁愛樓拆除重建。明亮的新校舍為學生帶來安全、現代的教室，讓大同煥然一新、「大不同」。

Sport · 田徑

Strong Day at the County Track Meet縣運田徑佳績

Apr 2024 · 2024 年（113 縣運）

Fourteen Datong athletes competed at the Changhua county track-and-field meet. One runner won first place in the girls' high jump and went on to represent Changhua at the national games.

大同十四位選手參加彰化縣中小學聯合運動會。其中一位在小女乙組跳高奪得第一名，並代表彰化縣晉級全國小學運動會。

Reading · 閱讀

A Trip to the Taipei Book Exhibition台北國際書展親子遊

Feb 2025 · 2025 年 2 月

Families and teachers took a two-day trip to the Taipei International Book Exhibition, the National Palace Museum, and Shilin Residence — opening young eyes to books, art, and the wider world.

親師生展開兩天一夜的旅程，走訪台北國際書展、故宮博物院與士林官邸——為孩子打開書香、藝術與國際視野。

Life Education · 生命教育

Anti-Drug Roadshow Visits Datong反毒行動巡迴車到校

Apr 2025 · 2025 年 4 月

The anti-drug roadshow bus came to campus. With hands-on activities and friendly volunteers, students learned about the dangers of drugs and how to keep themselves safe.

反毒行動巡迴車開進大同校園。透過互動活動與志工老師的解說，學生認識藥物濫用的危害，並學習正確的防範知識。
