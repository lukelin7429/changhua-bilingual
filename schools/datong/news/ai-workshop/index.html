Professional Development · 教師增能

AI in the Classroom —
A Workshop for Educators

AI 代理人教育應用增能研習
July 1, 2025  ·  2025 年 7 月 1 日（民國 114 年）

On July 1, 2025, Datong Elementary School hosted a three-hour professional development workshop on artificial intelligence in education. The principal, directors, and classroom teachers all joined in — exploring how AI agents can support the work that educators do every day.

2025 年 7 月 1 日，大同國小舉辦了一場為期三小時的 AI 教育增能研習，校長、主任及全體老師共同參與，一起探索 AI 代理人如何支援教師的日常教學工作。

What We Explored · 研習主題

The session covered three practical areas where AI can make a real difference in a school setting:

本次研習聚焦三大實務主題，讓每位參與者都能立即應用於教學現場：

  • Designing lesson plans and worksheets — using AI to save time and spark new ideas for curriculum
  • Creating assessments and quizzes — generating test questions aligned to learning objectives
  • Building a personal website — helping teachers establish a professional online presence

一、設計教案與學習單——運用 AI 節省備課時間、激發教學靈感；二、出考卷與評量設計——快速生成對應學習目標的題目；三、經營個人網站——協助教師建立專業的線上形象。

Looking Ahead · 展望未來

The workshop is part of Datong's commitment to keeping teachers well-equipped for a changing educational landscape. As AI tools become part of everyday life, having educators who understand and can use them thoughtfully is more important than ever.

這場研習是大同國小持續推動教師專業成長的具體行動。隨著 AI 工具逐漸融入日常生活，讓教師具備正確認識並善用 AI 的能力，比以往任何時候都更加重要。
