Principal Li Shih-hsun

I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Li Shih-hsun grew up in Tianwei, Changhua, surrounded by rice fields — and a lifelong love of nature. He earned a doctorate in educational information, measurement and statistics, and has spent his career bringing science and technology into the classroom.

李世勳校長在彰化田尾的稻田間長大，從小熱愛自然。他擁有教育資訊與測驗統計博士學位，多年來致力於把科學與科技帶進教室。

Before coming to Datong, he served eight years as principal of Dongshan Elementary in Yuanlin, where he nurtured VEX IQ robotics, a youth baseball team, and a traditional beiguan music ensemble — proof that one school can shine in both technology and culture.

來到大同之前，他在員林市東山國小擔任校長八年，培育出 VEX IQ 機器人、少棒隊與北管樂團——證明一所學校可以同時在科技與文化上發光。

His belief is simple: every activity should return to the heart of education — helping each child gain real knowledge and the confidence to grow. He hopes to make Datong a warm, caring place where teachers and students learn and grow together.

他的信念很簡單：一切教育活動都應回歸教育本質——幫助每個孩子獲得真實的知識與成長的動能。他期盼把大同打造成一個溫暖、友善的園地，讓親師生一起學習、一起成長。

"Treat others with sincerity; do your work with substance."
待人以誠，做事以實。
