Principal Wang Sheng-hsien

王聖賢校長
I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Wang Sheng-hsien has spent more than two decades serving Changhua County's schools — from Hanbao Elementary in Fangyuan to Heren Elementary in Hemei, Caogang Elementary in Lukang, and Xingang Elementary in Shengang, then as the county's Curriculum Supervisor before becoming principal of Tuku Elementary in Zhutang. In August 2026, he was appointed the new principal of Datong Elementary.

王聖賢校長二十餘年的教育生涯走過彰化縣多所學校——從芳苑鄉漢寶國小、和美鎮和仁國小、鹿港鎮草港國小，到伸港鄉新港國小，並曾擔任彰化縣政府課程督學，其後出任竹塘鄉土庫國小校長。115學年度（2026年8月）調任大同國小校長。

Principal Wang brings his SMART philosophy to everything he does: with sincere hands, diligent feet, a genuine heart, and words of praise, he works to build a joyful, quality campus where every child's diverse talents are seen and their value is realized.

王校長將「SMART」核心理念帶入校園：用「務實」的雙手、「勤奮」的雙腳、「真誠」的內心及「讚美」的口吻，打造樂學優質「心」校園，讓每位孩子的多元智慧都被看見，成就每一位孩子的價值與未來。

"Sincere hands, diligent feet, a genuine heart, and words of praise."
務實的雙手、勤奮的雙腳、真誠的內心、讚美的口吻。
