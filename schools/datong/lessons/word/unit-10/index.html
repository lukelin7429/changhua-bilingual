🎓 School Events & Ceremonies

Word of the Day — School Events & Ceremonies. Watch each clip, tap 🔊 to hear it, and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 校園活動與典禮。看影片、點 🔊 聽發音，再把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
graduation (n)
畢業典禮
The graduation is in June.
畢業典禮在六月舉行。
Parents come to the graduation.
家長來參加畢業典禮。
Word 02
graduate (n)
畢業生
Every graduate gets a diploma.
每位畢業生都拿到畢業證書。
The graduates smile for a photo.
畢業生們笑著拍照。
Word 03
hair donation (n)
捐髮
She gives a hair donation to help sick children.
她捐髮幫助生病的孩子。
A hair donation can become a wig.
捐出的頭髮可以做成假髮。
Word 04
workshop (n)
研討會
Teachers join a workshop after school.
老師放學後參加研討會。
We learn new ideas at the workshop.
我們在研討會學到新點子。
Word 05
lecture about law (n)
法律講座
A police officer gives a lecture about law.
一位警察來上法律講座。
The lecture about law teaches us our rights.
法律講座教我們自己的權利。
Word 06
press conference (n)
記者會
The school holds a press conference.
學校舉辦一場記者會。
Reporters ask questions at the press conference.
記者在記者會上提問。
Word 07
first grader (n)
一年級生
Every first grader gets a new book bag.
每位一年級生都拿到新書包。
The first graders line up by the door.
一年級生在門邊排隊。
Word 08
dragon boat (n)
龍舟
We watch a dragon boat race in summer.
夏天我們看龍舟比賽。
The dragon boat moves fast on the river.
龍舟在河上快速前進。
Word 09
traditional Chinese instrument (n)
傳統中國樂器
She plays a traditional Chinese instrument on stage.
她在舞台上演奏傳統中國樂器。
A traditional Chinese instrument makes a beautiful sound.
傳統中國樂器發出優美的聲音。
