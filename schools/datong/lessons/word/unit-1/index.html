🧹 Cleaning & Chores

每日一字 · 打掃與整潔
← All Word of the Day

Word of the Day — Cleaning & Chores. Watch each clip, tap 🔊 to hear it, and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 打掃與整潔。看影片、點 🔊 聽發音，再把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
clean (v)
清潔
Students clean the classroom every day.
學生每天打掃教室。
We clean our desks after lunch.
午餐後我們清潔書桌。
Word 02
sweep (v)
掃地
He sweeps the floor with a broom.
他用掃把掃地。
We sweep the classroom together.
我們一起掃教室。
Word 03
mop (v)
拖地
She mops the floor until it shines.
她把地板拖得發亮。
We mop the floor after we sweep.
掃完地後我們拖地。
Word 04
mop (n)
拖把
The mop is in the corner.
拖把放在角落。
He picks up the mop to clean.
他拿起拖把打掃。
Word 05
wash (v)
洗
We wash our hands before lunch.
午餐前我們洗手。
She washes the cups in the sink.
她在水槽洗杯子。
Word 06
brush (v)
刷牙
I brush my teeth after lunch.
午餐後我刷牙。
Don't forget to brush every day.
別忘了每天刷牙。
Word 07
boil (v)
煮
We boil water to make soup.
我們煮水來做湯。
The teacher boils the dumplings.
老師煮水餃。
Word 08
eat (v)
吃
We eat lunch at noon.
中午我們吃午餐。
The students eat their snacks.
學生吃點心。
Word 09
scoop up (v)
舀
She scoops up the rice with a spoon.
她用湯匙舀飯。
We scoop up the soup carefully.
我們小心地舀湯。
