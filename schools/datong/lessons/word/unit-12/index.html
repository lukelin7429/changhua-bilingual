✏️ Classroom & Stationery

Word of the Day — Classroom & Stationery. Watch each clip, tap 🔊 to hear it, and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 教室與文具。看影片、點 🔊 聽發音，再把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
learn (v)
學習
We learn new words every day.
我們每天學新單字。
I learn English at school.
我在學校學英文。
Word 02
taking attendance (v)
點名
The teacher is taking attendance now.
老師正在點名。
After taking attendance, we start class.
點名後我們開始上課。
Word 03
workbook (n)
習作簿
Open your workbook to page ten.
把習作簿翻到第十頁。
I finish my workbook at home.
我在家寫完習作簿。
Word 04
uniform (n)
制服
We wear a school uniform every day.
我們每天穿學校制服。
Her uniform is clean and neat.
她的制服又乾淨又整齊。
Word 05
tablet (n)
平板
We use a tablet to read e-books.
我們用平板讀電子書。
The tablet helps us learn English.
平板幫我們學英文。
Word 06
glue (n)
膠水
Use glue to stick the paper.
用膠水把紙黏起來。
The glue is on the art table.
膠水在美術桌上。
Word 07
scissors (n)
剪刀
Cut the paper with scissors.
用剪刀剪紙。
Hold the scissors carefully.
小心拿剪刀。
Word 08
doodle (v)
塗鴉
He likes to doodle in his notebook.
他喜歡在筆記本上塗鴉。
Don't doodle on the desk.
別在桌子上塗鴉。
