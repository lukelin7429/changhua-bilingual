Word of the Day — Around Campus. Watch each clip, tap 🔊 to hear it, and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 校園角落。看影片、點 🔊 聽發音，再把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
recycling (n)
回收
We put bottles in the recycling bin.
我們把瓶子放進回收桶。
Recycling keeps our school clean.
回收讓我們的學校乾淨。
Word 02
water dispenser (n)
飲水機
Fill your bottle at the water dispenser.
在飲水機裝滿你的水壺。
The water dispenser is in the hallway.
飲水機在走廊上。
Word 03
hallway (n)
走廊
Walk slowly in the hallway.
在走廊上要慢慢走。
The classrooms are along the hallway.
教室在走廊兩旁。
Word 04
train (n)
火車
We take a train on our school trip.
校外教學我們搭火車。
The train stops at the station.
火車在車站停下。
Word 05
utensil (n)
餐具
We wash our utensils after lunch.
午餐後我們清洗餐具。
A spoon is a kind of utensil.
湯匙是一種餐具。
