Word of the Day — Daily Routine. Watch each clip and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 作息與時間。看影片，把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
recess (n)
下課時間
We play games during recess.
下課時間我們玩遊戲。
Recess is everyone's favorite time.
下課時間是大家最喜歡的時刻。
Word 02
class dismissal (n)
放學
Class dismissal is at four o'clock.
四點放學。
We go home after class dismissal.
放學後我們回家。
Word 03
morning assembly (n)
朝會
The school holds a morning assembly.
學校舉行朝會。
We stand in lines for morning assembly.
朝會時我們排隊站好。
Word 04
after-school program (n)
課後輔導
She stays for the after-school program.
她留下來參加課後輔導。
The after-school program helps us study.
課後輔導幫助我們學習。
Word 05
leave (v)
離開
We leave school at four.
我們四點離開學校。
Please don't leave your trash.
請不要留下你的垃圾。
Word 06
check out (phr)
借書
You can check out books at the library.
你可以在圖書館借書。
She checks out two storybooks.
她借了兩本故事書。
Word 07
stairs (n)
樓梯
We walk up the stairs slowly.
我們慢慢走上樓梯。
The classroom is up the stairs.
教室在樓梯上面。
Word 08
restroom (n)
廁所
The restroom is down the hall.
廁所在走廊那頭。
Wash your hands in the restroom.
在廁所要洗手。
