Word of the Day · 運動與體育

🏀 Sports & PE

每日一字 · 運動與體育
← All Word of the Day

Word of the Day — Sports & PE. Watch each clip, tap 🔊 to hear it, and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 運動與體育。看影片、點 🔊 聽發音，再把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
tee-ball (n)
樂樂棒球
First graders play tee-ball in PE class.
一年級生在體育課打樂樂棒球。
In tee-ball, you hit the ball off a stand.
打樂樂棒球時，球放在球座上打。
Word 02
baseball (n)
棒球
Our school has a baseball team.
我們學校有一支棒球隊。
He throws the baseball to first base.
他把棒球傳到一壘。
Word 03
baseball (n)
棒球
We watch a baseball game after school.
放學後我們看棒球比賽。
She catches the baseball with her glove.
她用手套接住棒球。
Word 04
shoot hoops (v)
投籃
We shoot hoops on the playground.
我們在操場投籃。
He likes to shoot hoops after lunch.
他喜歡午餐後投籃。
Word 05
dribble (v)
運球
She can dribble the ball very fast.
她可以運球運得很快。
First dribble, then pass to a friend.
先運球，再傳給同學。
Word 06
agility ladder (n)
繩梯
We run through the agility ladder in PE.
體育課我們跑繩梯。
The agility ladder makes our feet quick.
繩梯讓我們的腳步變敏捷。
Word 07
bottle rocket (n)
水火箭
We make a bottle rocket in science class.
我們在自然課做水火箭。
The bottle rocket flies high into the sky.
水火箭飛得很高。
