Word of the Day — Around School. Watch each clip, tap 🔊 to hear it, and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 校園點滴。看影片、點 🔊 聽發音，再把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
paint (v)
畫
We paint pictures in art class.
美術課我們畫畫。
She paints a blue sky.
她畫藍色的天空。
Word 02
plant (n)
植物
We water the plant every day.
我們每天澆植物。
The plant grows by the window.
植物在窗邊長大。
Word 03
snowman (n)
雪人
We build a snowman in winter.
冬天我們堆雪人。
The snowman has a carrot nose.
雪人有紅蘿蔔鼻子。
Word 04
nap (v)
小睡
We take a nap after lunch.
午餐後我們小睡。
A short nap feels good.
小睡一下很舒服。
Word 05
Easter (n)
復活節
We hunt for eggs at Easter.
復活節我們找彩蛋。
Easter is in the spring.
復活節在春天。
Word 06
rainy day (n)
下雨天
We stay inside on a rainy day.
下雨天我們待在室內。
Take an umbrella on a rainy day.
下雨天要帶雨傘。
Word 07
refrigerator magnet (n)
冰箱貼
We make a refrigerator magnet in class.
課堂上我們做冰箱貼。
The refrigerator magnet holds my drawing.
冰箱貼夾住我的畫。
