Word of the Day · 遊戲與運動

🎮 Play, Toys & Sports

每日一字 · 遊戲與運動
← All Word of the Day

Word of the Day — Play, Toys & Sports. Watch each clip, tap 🔊 to hear it, and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 遊戲與運動。看影片、點 🔊 聽發音，再把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
hoop rolling (n)
滾鐵圈
Hoop rolling is an old-time game.
滾鐵圈是古早遊戲。
We try hoop rolling in PE.
體育課我們玩滾鐵圈。
Word 02
kendama (n)
劍玉
Kendama is a wooden toy.
劍玉是木頭玩具。
She catches the ball on the kendama.
她把球接到劍玉上。
Word 03
pinball (n)
彈珠台
We play pinball at the fair.
園遊會我們玩彈珠台。
Pinball is fun and fast.
彈珠台又好玩又快。
Word 04
sand bag (n)
沙包
We toss the sand bag back and forth.
我們把沙包丟來丟去。
A sand bag is easy to make.
沙包很容易做。
Word 05
video game (n)
電玩遊戲
He likes to play video games.
他喜歡玩電玩遊戲。
We play a video game after homework.
寫完作業我們玩電玩。
Word 06
toy (n)
玩具
The baby plays with a toy.
寶寶玩玩具。
I share my toys with friends.
我和朋友分享玩具。
Word 07
playground (n)
遊戲場
We run on the playground.
我們在遊戲場奔跑。
The playground has a slide.
遊戲場有溜滑梯。
Word 08
basketball practice (n)
籃球練習
We have basketball practice after school.
放學後我們有籃球練習。
Basketball practice makes us better.
籃球練習讓我們進步。
Word 09
jog (v)
慢跑
We jog around the track.
我們繞著跑道慢跑。
Jogging keeps us healthy.
慢跑讓我們健康。
