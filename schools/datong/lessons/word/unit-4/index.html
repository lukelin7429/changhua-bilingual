Word of the Day — Classroom & Lessons. Watch each clip and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 課堂與學科。看影片，把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
warm-up (n)
暖身
We do a warm-up before PE.
體育課前我們做暖身。
A warm-up gets your body ready.
暖身讓身體準備好。
Word 02
workbook (n)
習作
Open your workbook to page ten.
習作翻到第十頁。
We finish the workbook in class.
我們在課堂完成習作。
Word 03
worksheet (n)
學習單
The teacher hands out a worksheet.
老師發學習單。
I fill in the worksheet.
我填寫學習單。
Word 04
computer class (n)
電腦課
We have computer class on Monday.
星期一我們有電腦課。
In computer class we learn to type.
電腦課我們學打字。
Word 05
music class (n)
音樂課
We sing songs in music class.
音樂課我們唱歌。
Music class is fun.
音樂課很有趣。
Word 06
story (n)
故事
The teacher reads us a story.
老師念故事給我們聽。
I love a good story.
我喜歡好故事。
Word 07
self assessment (n)
自我評量
We do a self assessment after the unit.
單元結束後我們做自我評量。
A self assessment shows what I learned.
自我評量看出我學到什麼。
Word 08
final exam (n)
期末考
The final exam is next week.
下週是期末考。
We study hard for the final exam.
我們努力準備期末考。
Word 09
test (n)
考試
We have a math test today.
今天我們有數學考試。
I do my best on the test.
考試我盡力而為。
