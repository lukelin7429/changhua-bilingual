Word of the Day — Classroom & Library. Watch each clip, tap 🔊 to hear it, and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 教室與圖書館。看影片、點 🔊 聽發音，再把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
write (v)
寫
We write our names on the paper.
我們在紙上寫名字。
She writes a story in English.
她用英文寫故事。
Word 02
technology (n)
科技
We use technology to learn new things.
我們用科技學新東西。
Technology makes our class more fun.
科技讓我們的課更有趣。
Word 03
study hall (n)
自修時間
We read books during study hall.
自修時間我們看書。
Study hall is a quiet time for homework.
自修時間是安靜寫功課的時間。
Word 04
ruler (n)
尺
I draw a straight line with my ruler.
我用尺畫一條直線。
Put your ruler in the pencil case.
把尺放進鉛筆盒。
Word 05
quiet (adj)
安靜的
Please be quiet in the library.
在圖書館請保持安靜。
The classroom is quiet during the test.
考試時教室很安靜。
Word 06
library (n)
圖書館
We borrow books from the library.
我們在圖書館借書。
The library is full of good stories.
圖書館裡有很多好故事。
Word 07
English (n)
英語
We speak English in class.
上課時我們說英語。
I like to learn English songs.
我喜歡學英語歌。
Word 08
bulletin board (n)
佈告欄
The news is on the bulletin board.
消息貼在佈告欄上。
We put our drawings on the bulletin board.
我們把畫貼在佈告欄上。
