Word of the Day · 午餐與食物

🍱 Lunch & Food

每日一字 · 午餐與食物
← All Word of the Day

Word of the Day — Lunch & Food. Watch each clip and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 午餐與食物。看影片，把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
school lunch (n)
營養午餐
We eat school lunch in the classroom.
我們在教室吃營養午餐。
School lunch is healthy and warm.
營養午餐又健康又溫熱。
Word 02
cart (n)
餐車
The lunch cart brings food to class.
餐車把食物送到教室。
Two students push the cart.
兩位學生推餐車。
Word 03
sticky rice ball (n)
湯圓
We eat sticky rice balls in winter.
冬天我們吃湯圓。
Sticky rice balls are sweet and round.
湯圓又甜又圓。
Word 04
puffed rice (n)
米香
Puffed rice is a crunchy snack.
米香是酥脆的點心。
Grandma makes puffed rice for us.
奶奶做米香給我們吃。
Word 05
taro balls (n)
芋圓
Taro balls are soft and chewy.
芋圓又軟又有嚼勁。
We like taro balls with ice.
我們喜歡芋圓加冰。
Word 06
cotton candy (n)
棉花糖
Cotton candy is pink and fluffy.
棉花糖是粉紅色又蓬鬆。
The fair sells cotton candy.
園遊會賣棉花糖。
Word 07
cabbage (n)
高麗菜
We grow cabbage in the garden.
我們在菜園種高麗菜。
Cabbage is good for you.
高麗菜對身體很好。
Word 08
white cherry (n)
白櫻桃
White cherries are sweet fruit.
白櫻桃是甜甜的水果。
She tastes a white cherry.
她嚐了一顆白櫻桃。
