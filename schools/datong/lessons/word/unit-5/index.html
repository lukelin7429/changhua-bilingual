🎒 School Events & Honors

Word of the Day — School Events & Honors. Watch each clip and read the two example sentences aloud.

每日一字 · 校園活動與榮譽。看影片，把兩句例句大聲唸出來。

Word 01
office of academic affairs (n)
教務處
The office of academic affairs plans our classes.
教務處安排我們的課程。
Teachers work in the office of academic affairs.
老師在教務處工作。
Word 02
professional development (n)
專業進修
Teachers join professional development days.
老師參加專業進修。
Professional development helps teachers grow.
專業進修幫助老師成長。
Word 03
book fair (n)
書展
Our school holds a book fair.
學校舉辦書展。
I buy a new book at the book fair.
我在書展買新書。
Word 04
mobile library (n)
行動圖書館
The mobile library visits our school.
行動圖書館來我們學校。
We borrow books from the mobile library.
我們在行動圖書館借書。
Word 05
prize (n)
獎品
The winner gets a prize.
優勝者得到獎品。
I won a prize in the contest.
我在比賽得到獎品。
Word 06
recruit (n)
招生
Our school will recruit new students.
學校要招生。
The first graders are new recruits.
一年級是新招的學生。
Word 07
Chinese language competition (n)
語文競賽
She joins the Chinese language competition.
她參加語文競賽。
The Chinese language competition is in the library.
語文競賽在圖書館舉行。
Word 08
school band (n)
學校樂隊
The school band plays at the ceremony.
學校樂隊在典禮上演奏。
I play the drum in the school band.
我在學校樂隊打鼓。
Word 09
pop-up event (n)
快閃才藝表演
Students give a pop-up event at recess.
下課時學生快閃表演才藝。
The pop-up event surprised everyone.
快閃表演讓大家驚喜。
Word 10
art camp (n)
藝術營
We make crafts at art camp.
在藝術營我們做手工。
Art camp is in the summer.
藝術營在暑假舉行。
