Going Further進階閱讀

Basketball is a team sport with five players on each side. You dribble the ball, pass to your teammates, and shoot it into the hoop. Datong’s basketball team has practiced for more than twenty years. The coach teaches both skills and teamwork. In the 2023 city league, our sixth-grade girls won first place and the boys came second. Hard work and team spirit help the team win — and have fun.

籃球是一項團隊運動，每隊有五名球員。你運球、傳球給隊友，再把球投進籃框。大同籃球隊已經練習超過二十年。教練教的不只是技術，還有團隊合作。在 112 學年度的縣聯賽中，我們六年級女生拿下第一名，男生拿下第二名。努力與團隊精神，幫助球隊獲勝，也帶來歡樂。
