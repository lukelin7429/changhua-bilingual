🎉 Sports Day

校慶運動會
← Photo Lessons

Every year, the whole school celebrates its birthday with a lively Sports Day.

每年全校都以熱鬧的運動會慶祝校慶。

01

The whole school gathers by class to start Sports Day.

全校按班級集合，運動會開始了。

02

Each class walks in together for the opening.

各班一起進場，揭開序幕。

03

Students cheer in their bright class colors.

學生穿著鮮豔的班服為彼此加油。

04

A runner races down the track in her number bib.

一位選手穿著號碼衣在跑道上奔馳。

05

Classes set up their own cheering corners.

各班布置自己的加油區。

06

Runners give their best in the sprint.

選手們在短跑中全力以赴。

07

She pumps her arms and runs as fast as she can.

她擺動手臂，盡全力向前衝。

08

Runners wait on the track for the start.

選手在跑道上等待起跑。

09

The race is close all the way to the finish line.

比賽一路激烈，直到終點線。

10

Teams work together in the field games.

各隊在趣味競賽中同心協力。

11

Parents and friends watch from the side.

家長與朋友在場邊觀賽。

12

Each class marches together in matching shirts.

各班穿著同款班服一起前進。

13

The whole field fills up for the big day.

整個操場擠滿了人，迎接這個大日子。

14

Everyone joins the fun on Sports Day.

大家一起享受校慶的歡樂。

Going Further進階閱讀

Datong Elementary is more than seventy years old. Every year, the whole school celebrates with a big Sports Day. Students run races, play team games, and cheer for their classmates. Each class wears the same color, makes a cheering corner, and tries its best. Sports Day is not only about winning — it is about exercise, teamwork, and being proud of our school.

大同國小已經有超過七十年的歷史。每年全校都用盛大的運動會來慶祝。學生賽跑、玩團體遊戲、為同學加油。各班穿上同樣的顏色、布置加油區、全力以赴。校慶不只是為了贏——更是關於運動、團隊合作，以及以我們的學校為榮。
