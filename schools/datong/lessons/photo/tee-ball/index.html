Going Further進階閱讀

Tee-ball is a fun and easy way to learn baseball. Instead of a pitcher, you hit the ball off a tee with a bat. Then you run around the bases. To score a point, the runner has to reach home plate. Datong’s Happy Dolphins team has trained for many years, with about twenty-four players. They practice at lunchtime and in PE class. Tee-ball teaches us teamwork — everyone has a job, and we all cheer for each other.

樂樂棒球是學習棒球既有趣又簡單的方式。沒有投手，你用球棒把球從打擊座上擊出，然後繞著壘包跑。要得分，跑者必須回到本壘。大同的「快樂海豚」球隊已經練習多年，大約有二十四位隊員。他們利用午休與體育課練習。樂樂棒球教我們團隊合作——每個人都有任務，大家也彼此加油。
