I.

Welcome

歡迎來到大同國小

A coastal school where the river meets the sea.

一所坐落於海岸邊、河海交會處的學校。

Datong Elementary sits in the northwest corner of Changhua, in Shengang, where the Dadu River flows into the Taiwan Strait. Our neighbourhood of tidal flats, wetland birds, wind turbines, and oyster farms is a living classroom for caring about nature.

大同國小位於彰化西北角的伸港，大肚溪在這裡注入台灣海峽。潮間帶、濕地候鳥、海岸風機與蚵田，就是我們認識自然、關懷土地的活教室。

Under Principal Wang Sheng-hsien, Datong weaves English into everyday school life — through a bilingual website, Word of the Day videos, photo lessons, festivals, and classroom English.

在王聖賢校長的帶領下，大同把英語融入每天的校園生活——透過雙語網站、每日一字影片、看圖學英文、節慶英語與課室英語。
