New Semester, Here We Come!開學前溫馨提醒．新學期準備出發

Dear families and students — summer vacation is almost over, and Tuku Elementary is getting ready to welcome everyone back! Here's a friendly back-to-school checklist to help us all ease back into the rhythm of school life together.

Fri, Aug 28 — School Return Day

08:00–10:00, for students in grades 2–6. Please remember to bring your completed summer homework back to school for your teacher!

Sat, Aug 29 — Parent Talk & Class Meeting

09:00–09:15 check-in at the school corridor; 09:15–10:15 class meetings in each homeroom; 10:15–12:00 an exciting parent talk.

Mon, Aug 31 — First Day of the New Semester

Please arrive safely by 07:30; school ends for everyone at 16:00.

Pack your energy and get ready for a wonderful new semester — see you at school!

中文原文 · Chinese

親愛的家長與同學們，美好的暑假即將結束，校園準備好再次迎接大家囉！請收下這份「開學前備忘錄」，跟著我們的步調一起收心：

8/28（五）全校返校日　時間：08:00–10:00，對象：2–6 年級學生。提醒：當天請記得將寫好的暑假作業帶回學校交給老師喔！

8/29（六）親子講座暨班親會活動　09:00–09:15 學校中廊報到；09:15–10:15 各班教室班親會；10:15–12:00 精彩親子講座。

8/31（一）正式開學日　時間：請於 07:30 前順利到校；當天 16:00 統一全校放學。

準備好滿滿的活力，迎接美好的新學期吧！我們學校見！
