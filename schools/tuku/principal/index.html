Empowering Every Person, A Happy Future 成就每個人，共創幸福未來

Education is the work of illuminating lives; school is where hope becomes real. Teaching is not only about passing on knowledge — more importantly, it is about seeing every child, and helping every person succeed.

Principal Chen Mao-chin looks forward to working hand in hand with the teachers, students, parents, and community partners of Tuku Elementary to build a happy school — one where students learn joyfully, teachers teach with peace of mind, parents entrust their children with confidence, and the community takes pride in its school.

教育，是點亮生命的工程；學校，是實現希望的地方。教育不只是傳授知識，更重要的是看見每一個孩子、成就每一個人。

期許與土庫國小全體師生、家長及社區夥伴攜手合作，打造一所學生快樂學習、教師安心教學，家長放心託付、社區引以為榮的幸福學校。

F

Centering every decision on student learning — teaching to each child's strengths.
