The Fourteenth Hand at the Gate 第十四任校長，守護土庫村的農村小學

When Principal Wang Sheng-hsien arrived at Tuku Elementary, he stepped into a school that is small in number but wide in heart — 46 students, 13 teachers, and a whole community learning together. Nestled in Tuku Village at the center of Zhutang Township, the school is surrounded by the rice paddies and wetlands beside the Zhuoshui River.

Principal Wang brings the SMART philosophy to everything he does: with sincere hands, diligent feet, a genuine heart, and words of praise, he is building a joyful, quality campus where diverse talent can bloom and every child's value is realized.

王聖賢校長來到土庫國小，接掌的是一所人數雖少、心卻很寬的學校——46 位學生、13 位老師，全校共學、全員參與。學校坐落於竹塘鄉中心的土庫村，四周是濁水溪沖積的稻田與高灘濕地。

王校長將「SMART」核心理念帶入校園，用「務實」的雙手、「勤奮」的雙腳、「真誠」的內心及「讚美」的口吻，打造一所樂學優質「心」校園，讓每位土庫囝仔的多元智慧都被看見，成就每一位孩子的價值與未來。

The SMART Vision SMART 辦學核心理念

Five letters. One direction. Principal Wang's SMART framework guides every decision — from classroom to community.

五個英文字母，一個方向。王校長的 SMART 核心理念，引導從教室到社區的每一個決策。

S

Special創新教育作為

Innovative thinking to inspire talent — helping every child create a creative, meaningful future.
展現辦學興才的創新思維，讓孩子開創創意人生。
M

More多元育才理念

Building stages for diverse talents to shine — making learning richer and more enjoyable.
搭建多元潛能展現舞台，讓學習更豐富、更有趣。
A

Ability基本學力奠基

Strengthening teaching quality to ensure every student builds solid foundational skills.
增進課程教學品質，確保每位學生的基本學力提升。
R

Relation良好人際建立

Cultivating good character and building mutual trust in every relationship at school.
發揚良善品格，建立相互信任的互動模式。
T

Teams團隊合作共贏

Parents and teachers working as one team — together realizing the school's shared vision.
親師共同合作，以團隊實踐邁向學校共同願景。
