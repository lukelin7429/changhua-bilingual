About Our School

A Village School by the River

濁水溪畔，土庫村的小學
Tuku Village
Our campus, surrounded by green rice fields of Zhutang Township 四面稻田環繞的土庫國小校園
The warm, welcoming campus where every child is seen 溫暖熱情的校園，讓每位孩子都被看見

A small school with a big heart in Changhua's farming village

Tuku Village sits at the heart of Zhutang Township — a place shaped by the Zhuoshui River, home to thriving wetlands, migratory birds, and generations of Hakka families. Our school, Tuku Elementary, stands amid green rice paddies, serving 46 students with 13 dedicated teachers.

The name Tuku traces back to early settlers — some say from the earthen storehouses built by the first villagers; others from the low, muddy farmland that clung to workers' clothes. Whatever its origin, Tuku is a community rooted in the land — and so is our school.

土庫村位於竹塘鄉的中心地帶，緊鄰濁水溪，擁有豐富的高灘濕地與候鳥棲息地，也是彰化客家文化的重要聚落。土庫國小坐落在綠油油的農田之間，目前有 46 位學生、13 位老師及 3 位職工人員，是一所純樸熱情的農村小學。

「土庫」地名的由來，有一說是早期居民的土造倉庫，另一說是地勢低窪泥濘、農作時泥土常沾滿褲子而得名。無論如何，土庫是一個紮根於土地的社區——我們的學校也是。

How We Grow

Read · Explore · Connect

喜閱・探索・共學 — 土庫囝仔的成長力

A small school, a whole world. At Tuku, children grow through reading, hands-on discovery, and learning together as one community.

📚

Read & Create 喜閱藝術

Tuku children love to read and love to make things. Reading opens worlds; art gives those worlds a shape. From picture books to hands-on projects, every child finds a way to express what matters most to them.

土庫的孩子愛閱讀也愛創作。讀書打開世界，藝術給世界一個形狀。從繪本到手作，每個孩子都找到了屬於自己的表達方式。

🌾

Grow & Discover 科學食農

Surrounded by farmland, Tuku children learn science where it begins — in the soil. Food and farming education connects the classroom to the rice paddies right outside our gate, turning curiosity into real understanding.

四周都是農田，土庫的孩子在最接地氣的地方學科學。食農教育把教室和校門外的稻田連在一起，把好奇心變成真實的理解。

🌏

Live & Learn Together 生命樂活・人文社會

At Tuku, the whole school learns together — across grades, with families, and with the community. Weekend family learning sessions, cultural education, and outdoor experiences make learning a living thing shared by all.

土庫全校一起學習——跨年級、邀家長、連社區。假日親子共學、文化教育與戶外體驗，讓學習成為全體共享的生命體驗，即使資源少，也能雨露均霑。

Principal Wang Sheng-hsien leads Tuku Elementary as the school's 14th principal, guided by the SMART philosophy — Special, More, Ability, Relation, Teams. With sincere hands and a genuine heart, he is dedicated to building a joyful, quality campus where every child shines.

王聖賢校長是土庫國小第十四任校長，以「SMART」辦學核心理念——創意創新、多元育才、基本學力、良好人際、團隊合作——用務實的雙手與真誠的內心，打造樂學優質的校園，成就每位土庫囝仔。

Come Visit 歡迎蒞臨土庫

We welcome educators, parents, and friends to visit our small school in Zhutang Township — surrounded by the rice paddies of Changhua's farming heartland, where children explore with curiosity and grow with confidence, all in two languages.

歡迎來到竹塘鄉土庫國小，這裡是彰化農村的純樸角落，也是孩子多元探索、自信展能的雙語校園。無論您是教育工作者、家長，或路過的朋友，土庫的門永遠為您開著。
