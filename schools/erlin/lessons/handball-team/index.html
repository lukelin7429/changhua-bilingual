Handball Team

二林國小手球隊 · 15年隊史

I.

Watch & Learn

觀看影片，一起學英文

Winter training, warm teamwork.

冬天的寒訓，熱血的團隊合作。

←Back to Lessons回到課程總覽

The Erlin Elementary School handball team is practicing hard this winter for more than 2 weeks. The team has been around for 15 years and is really good. They work on running fast, throwing and catching balls, shooting goals, and playing as a team. Their coach is very helpful and teaches them to keep trying. Even when it's cold, they don't stop practicing because they want to be better. They learn to work together and support each other. Let's cheer for the Erlin handball team for their hard work and for playing so well together!

二林國小手球隊這個寒假正在努力練習，練習期超過2週。這個隊伍已經成立了15年，表現非常出色。他們練習快速跑動、投擲和接球、射門，以及團隊合作。他們的教練努力幫助他們，教導他們持續練習才能進步。即使天氣寒冷，他們也不停止練習，因為他們想要提升實力。他們學習團結合作，相互支持。讓我們為二林國小手球隊的努力和出色的團隊合作喝彩！
