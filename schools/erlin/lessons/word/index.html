Campus & Classroom Life · 11 words
校園生活
WORD 01
professional development
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都有教師專業訓練課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
在專業訓練課程中，老師可以學到新的教學技巧與策略。
WORD 02
recess
下課時間
After the bell rings for recess, most students go outside to play.
下課鈴聲響起後，大多數學生都在外面玩。
Recess is when kids can play outside, run around, and have fun.
下課時間是孩子們可以在外面玩耍、跑來跑去、玩得開心的時候。
WORD 03
cross
穿越
Watch out for students crossing the road.
當心正在過馬路的學生。
Crossing guards stop traffic to let students cross the street safely.
交通導護阻擋車輛讓學生安全過馬路。
WORD 04
English class
英文課
The teacher talks about the first day of school in English class.
老師在英文課談論上學的第一天。
In English class, the students answer questions about the first day of school.
在英文課當中，學生回答有關上學第一天的問題。
WORD 05
whiteboard
白板
The students use a whiteboard to solve problems.
同學們使用白板來解決問題。
After talking to the teacher, they use the whiteboard to work on their problems.
在跟老師談論之後，他們用白板來解決問題。
WORD 06
exchange
交換
The students exchange gifts for Christmas.
同學們交換聖誕禮物。
They laugh and talk while they exchange gifts.
他們在交換禮物時邊笑邊談話。
WORD 07
return
歸還
The students return books to the library.
同學們將書歸還給圖說館。
The librarian and the students organize the books being returned.
圖書館員與學生整理歸還的書籍。
WORD 08
mobile library
行動圖書館
The mobile library is a truck that's been converted into a small library.
行動圖書館是由卡車改裝的小圖書館。
The mobile library can bring lots of books to students at different schools!
行動圖書館可以將書帶給不同學校的學生閱讀。
WORD 09
homework
家庭作業
The students are quietly doing their homework in the classroom.
學生們正在教室裡安靜地做作業。
The homework helps them review what they have learned in class.
作業幫助他們複習在課堂上學到的知識。
WORD 10
tour guide
導覽員
A tour guide is giving the students a tour and introducing them to the history of rice in Taiwan.
導遊帶領學生參觀，並向他們介紹台灣的水稻歷史。
The students are learning from the tour guide about rice production and its cultural importance.
學生們正在嚮導遊學習水稻生產及其文化意義。
WORD 11

同學們在吃完午餐後刷牙。

WORD 12
handball
手球
The students are practicing important handball skills, focusing on jumping and throwing.
學生們正在練習重要的手球技能，重點是跳躍和投擲。
The students jump off a small inclined platform and throw the ball to improve their jump height and throwing accuracy, both of which are important in handball.
學生們從一個小傾斜平台上跳下並投球，以提高他們的跳躍高度和投球準確性，這兩者對於手球運動都很重要。
