Bringing Legal Awareness to Campus

法規觀念入校園，吉官分享最齊全
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On today's back-to-school registration day — also a teacher prep day — Erlin's Academic Affairs Office invited Chi Ching-ju, Chief Investigation and Protection Officer at the Keelung District Court, to give a special talk to the faculty. Officer Chi has spent years working in juvenile case handling and campus legal education — not only a frontline protection officer for at-risk youth, but also a trusted resource for teachers and parents alike.

今日返校日兼教師備課日，本校教務處特別邀請基隆地方法院主任調查保護官——吉靜如，蒞臨演講！吉官深耕兒少年事件處理與校園法治教育多年，不僅是第一線陪伴迷途兒少的保護官，更是深受教師與家長信賴的推手。

Drawing on real cases, Officer Chi walked through the range of issues young people face on campus today. In her direct, plain-spoken style, she reminded everyone that "the law isn't meant to punish — it's meant to protect and guide." Listening and empathy, she said, are the key to reaching a child's heart; teachers, too, need to stay calm and gentle when solving problems, so they can offer the strongest support at the moments that matter most.

吉官透過豐富的實務案例，深刻剖析現今校園兒少各種問題。她直白的語言，點出「法律不是為了懲罰，而是為了保護與導正」。吉官提醒大家，傾聽與同理是走入孩子內心的關鍵鑰匙，老師也要理性和溫和地面對問題、解決問題，才能在關鍵時刻給予孩子最強大的支持。

Thank you, Officer Chi, for a talk that gave our teachers real energy and a legal mindset to carry forward! And a special thanks to every Erlin teacher for staying focused and engaged even on such a busy registration day — and to our principal, for attending the whole session in support of the team.

感謝吉官精采的分享，為老師們帶來強大的能量與法治思維！感恩二小老師們團結一心，在忙碌的返校日還認真研習，由衷佩服與感謝認真的團隊。更感謝校長全程參與支持辛苦的老師們。
