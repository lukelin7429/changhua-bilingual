Summer English Camp Wraps Up

大專生暑期英語營，惜別會圓滿落幕
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

This week marked the final week of the college students' summer English camp! To make the most of these last days together, each class held a simple, joyful class party — with food, games, and laughter — the perfect ending to an amazing summer camp.

這週我們渡過了大專生營隊的最後一週！為了給這段時光留下最美好的回憶，各班不只上課，還特別舉辦了歡樂的簡單同樂會，有吃有玩又有笑聲，為我們超棒的暑期營隊畫下最完美的句點！

Time flies — the summer work-study camp has come to a wonderful close! To thank our hardworking college student teachers, the school held a simple but heartfelt farewell party, with the principal attending in person. Thank you again to all our teachers — we look forward to seeing you next year!

時光飛逝，暑假工讀營隊圓滿落幕了！學校為了感謝這些辛苦認真的大專生老師們，辦理了簡單隆重的惜別會，校長特別蒞臨指導。再次感謝所有老師們，期待明年與各位再相會！
