Creative Science Magic Camp

創意科學魔術夏令營精彩回顧
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

The three-day Science Magic Camp put on a lively show at Erlin Elementary! From the first day, when kids stared curiously at the props, to the final day, when they could confidently perform floating tricks, disappearing tricks, and card magic — the transformation was amazing.

為期三天的科學魔術營在二林國小熱鬧展演！孩子們從第一天好奇盯著道具，到最後能流利地展示漂浮術、消失術與牌卡魔法，轉變令人驚豔！

The camp brought more than just fun — through hands-on practice, kids unlocked the math and science behind the magic. Along the way, they sharpened hand-eye coordination and fine motor skills, and built stronger logical thinking, speaking confidence, and creativity.

這次課程不只帶來歡樂，孩子們更在操作中解鎖了數學與科學原理！不僅鍛鍊了手眼協調與細部動作，更大幅提升了邏輯思考、口語表達自信與創造力！

Thank you to the parents for their support, and to every child for diving right in — we can't wait to see this sense of wonder and confidence keep shining.

感謝家長們的支持與寶貝們的投入，期待這份驚奇與自信能繼續發光發熱。
