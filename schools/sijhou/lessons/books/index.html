Sijhou Elementary · 溪州國小

Picture Books 繪本閱讀室

Two original bilingual picture books, written at Sijhou · 溪州自製雙語繪本，就在這裡讀

Book 01

123 Wooden Bird 一二三木頭鳥

A bilingual picture book co-created by Sijhou teachers and students. Like the schoolyard game "123 wooden person" (木頭人), the Wooden Bird story is full of small pauses — moments where a child looks again, listens again, and chooses what to do next. A favorite for grade one and two reading sessions.
溪州師生共同創作的雙語繪本，就像那個喊「123木頭人」的校園遊戲，《123木頭鳥》故事裡充滿安靜的小停頓——孩子停下來、再看一次、再聽一次、決定下一步。一二年級閱讀時段常駐書目。

Book 02

Sijhou's Running Game 溪州拋拋走

A Sijhou-original bilingual picture book about the joy of running and playing on the school grounds — told through the eyes of Sijhou children chasing each other across the campus, past the Teacher-Student Tree, through the corridors, and out to the fields. A celebration of everyday childhood at Sijhou.
溪州自製雙語繪本，用孩子的眼睛記錄校園奔跑的樂趣——穿過走廊、繞過師生樹、衝向操場。一本屬於溪州孩子的日常大冒險。
