Where Does Our Food Come From? An SDG 12 Book Fair

SDG12 責任消費與生產 主題書展開鑼

November 18, 2026 · 115/11/18

The library's newest themed book fair is open! 🥳 From November 18 to 25, Sijhou's library, Yangguang Forest (羊光森林), is putting the spotlight on SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production — one of the United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals, which asks all of us to use the Earth's resources wisely and waste less.

On opening day, students were invited to take on two challenges. In the Food Journey Matching Challenge, they crowded around large, colorful game boards — pointing, talking it over and placing their answers together.

In the Sustainable Food and Farming Challenge, students explored on tablets and filled in worksheets as they went, learning more about where our food comes from and how it reaches our table.

Through games, tablets and books, the challenges helped Sijhou's children understand SDG 12 a little better — and think about how to value food, reduce waste and make sustainable choices. As the book fair poster puts it: "Cherish food, waste less — let sustainability start with reading."

Thank you to our library reading-promotion teacher and the school's Instruction Section for planning and running the book fair.
