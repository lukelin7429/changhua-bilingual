Thanksgiving: More Than Saying Thank You

感恩不只是說謝謝，更是學會珍惜！感恩節國際教育宣導

November 24, 2026 · 115/11/24

Do you know how Thanksgiving Day began in the United States? Why do people eat turkey and pumpkin pie, and what other special customs go with the holiday? 🦃

At Sijhou's international education assembly on November 24, foreign teacher Ana and English teacher Kao Pao-kuei made Thanksgiving the theme. With colorful posters and an introduction in English, they showed the whole school where the holiday came from, the foods families share and the traditions that make the day special. 🇺🇸

From turkey and pumpkin pie to the harvest, students picked up useful English words. By looking at how another country celebrates, they also thought about what it means to give thanks, to share and to appreciate what we have.

Through a lively talk and a round of questions and answers, the children discovered something important: people around the world say thank you in different ways, but the feeling behind it — valuing our lives and being grateful to others — is the same everywhere. 🧡

中文原文 · Chinese

🦃 感恩不只是說謝謝，更是學會珍惜！感恩節國際教育宣導

你知道美國的 Thanksgiving Day（感恩節）是怎麼來的嗎？為什麼感恩節要吃火雞、南瓜派？又有哪些特別的文化與傳統呢？

這次溪州國小的國際教育宣導，由外師 Ana 與英專教師高寶貴老師以 Thanksgiving Day 感恩節為主題，透過生動的海報與英語介紹，帶領孩子們認識美國感恩節的由來、傳統食物與節慶習俗。🇺🇸

從 Turkey（火雞）、Pumpkin Pie（南瓜派）到 Harvest（豐收），孩子們不僅學習實用的英語詞彙，也從不同國家的節慶文化中，理解感謝、分享與珍惜的意義。

透過輕鬆活潑的介紹與互動，讓孩子們發現，雖然各國表達感謝的方式不同，但珍惜生活、感謝他人的心意卻是相通的。

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

Thanksgiving/ˌθæŋksˈɡɪvɪŋ/感恩節

Americans celebrate Thanksgiving in November.美國人在十一月慶祝感恩節。

turkey/ˈtɜːrki/火雞

Many families eat roast turkey on Thanksgiving.許多家庭在感恩節吃烤火雞。

pumpkin pie/ˈpʌmpkɪn paɪ/南瓜派

Pumpkin pie is a favorite Thanksgiving dessert.南瓜派是很受歡迎的感恩節甜點。

harvest/ˈhɑːrvɪst/豐收、收成

Thanksgiving celebrates the autumn harvest.感恩節慶祝秋天的豐收。

grateful/ˈɡreɪtfəl/感激的、感恩的

I am grateful for my family and friends.我很感謝我的家人和朋友。

tradition/trəˈdɪʃən/傳統

Eating a big meal together is a Thanksgiving tradition.一起吃大餐是感恩節的傳統。

share/ʃer/分享

Families share food and stories at the table.家人在餐桌上分享食物和故事。

appreciate/əˈpriːʃieɪt/珍惜、感激

We should appreciate what we have.我們應該珍惜自己所擁有的。
