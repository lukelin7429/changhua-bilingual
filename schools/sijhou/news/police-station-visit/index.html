Little Visitors at the Police Station

幼兒園與陽光班參觀溪州警察分隊

November 11, 2026 · 115/11/11

On November 11, as part of their theme unit, children from Sijhou's kindergarten and the Sunshine Class took a field trip to the police station in Sijhou.

The police officers took great care to explain what each piece of their equipment is for. The children sat in neat rows on the steps, listening closely with eyes wide with curiosity.

Then came the best part of the day — the officers made the children's dream come true by letting them sit inside a real police car! 🚓

Thank you to the officers for teaching our youngest learners with such care. A visit like this helps children get to know the people who work every day to keep our community safe.

2026.11.11 配合主題活動——幼兒園與陽光班幼兒一起參觀溪州警察分隊，感謝警員們用心為幼兒解說各種配備的用途，並滿足幼兒體驗坐警車的夢想。
