I.

Welcome

歡迎來到陸豐國小

A century-old school in the heart of the flower garden town.

一所坐落於花鄉田尾、走過百年的學校。

Lufeng Elementary sits in Tianwei, Changhua — the town famous across Taiwan for its flower nurseries and the Highway Garden. Founded in 1918 as Haifenglun School, Lufeng has grown alongside the farming villages of Lufeng, Haifeng, Liufeng, Renli, and Xincuo for more than a hundred years.

陸豐國小位於彰化田尾鄉——以花卉苗圃與公路花園聞名全台的花鄉。學校創立於 1918 年，原名海豐崙學校，百餘年來與陸豐、海豐、柳鳳、仁里、新厝等農村聚落一同成長。

Under Principal Chen Po-hua, Lufeng puts technology and English at the heart of learning — including a weekly live video-call English class with a teacher in the United States, alongside photo lessons, festivals, and classroom English.

在陳柏華校長的帶領下，陸豐把科技與英語放在學習的核心——包括每週與美國老師連線的英語視訊課，以及看圖學英文、節慶英語與課室英語。

III.

Thursdays with Teacher Melissa

每週四 · 與密西根連線的英語視訊課

Technology brings the world into our classroom.

科技，把世界帶進陸豐的教室。

Lufeng puts technology and English at the heart of learning. Every Thursday at 8 a.m., our upper-grade students sit down for a live video call with Teacher Melissa in Michigan, USA — talking, listening, and learning English across all kinds of topics with a real teacher on the other side of the world.

陸豐把科技與英語放在學習的核心。每週四上午八點，高年級學生與美國密西根州的 Teacher Melissa 即時視訊——和地球另一端的老師用英語對話、聆聽，學習各式各樣的主題。
