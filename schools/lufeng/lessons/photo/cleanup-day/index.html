Going Further進階閱讀

Lufeng students do not just learn inside the classroom. On Community Clean-Up Day, they put on vests, pick up brooms, and tidy the streets around the school. They sweep paths, collect litter, and fill the bins together. Keeping our neighborhood clean is one way Lufeng children show they care about their community.

陸豐的學生不只在教室裡學習。在社區打掃日，他們穿上背心、拿起掃把，把學校周圍的街道整理乾淨。他們掃地、撿垃圾，一起把桶子裝滿。把社區打掃乾淨，就是陸豐孩子表達「關心自己社區」的方式。
