🎃 Halloween Party

萬聖節活動
← Back

Going Further進階閱讀

Every October, Lufeng celebrates Halloween. Students wear costumes, decorate the hall with orange pumpkins, and play games at fun booths. They learn English words like "pumpkin," "ghost," and "trick-or-treat." Halloween is not scary at Lufeng — it is a happy day to dress up, play together, and try new English.

每年十月，陸豐都會慶祝萬聖節。學生穿上裝扮、用橘色南瓜布置活動中心，在好玩的關卡玩遊戲。他們學會 pumpkin、ghost、trick-or-treat 這些英文單字。在陸豐，萬聖節一點都不可怕——而是一個可以變裝、一起玩、練英文的快樂日子。
