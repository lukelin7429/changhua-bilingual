Going Further進階閱讀

Once a year, the whole Lufeng family celebrates Sports Day. The day begins with drums and an opening ceremony. Then students run races on the bright track, cheer for their teams, and visit fun booths. Sports Day is about more than winning — it is about exercise, teamwork, and being proud of our school.

陸豐每年都會舉辦一次校慶運動會。這一天從擊鼓和開幕典禮開始，接著學生在鮮豔的跑道上賽跑、為自己的隊伍加油，還能逛好玩的攤位。運動會不只是比輸贏——更是關於運動、團隊合作，以及以學校為榮。
