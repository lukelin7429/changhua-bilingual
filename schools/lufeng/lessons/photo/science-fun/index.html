Going Further進階閱讀

At Lufeng's Summer Science Camp, learning is hands-on. Students pour, mix, and observe at different challenge stations. They hold up their cups, watch what happens, and ask "why?" Science is everywhere — and at Lufeng, every curious question is the start of a discovery.

在陸豐的夏日樂學科學營，學習是動手做的。學生在不同的闖關站倒、混合、觀察。他們舉起杯子、看會發生什麼，並問「為什麼？」科學無所不在——在陸豐，每一個好奇的問題，都是發現的開始。
