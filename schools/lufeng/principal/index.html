Principal’s Welcome

校長室 · 陳柏華 校長
I.

A Word of Welcome

校長的話

"Give every child room to explore, and they will find their own way to shine."

「給孩子探索的空間，他們就能以自己的方式發光發熱。」

Welcome to Lufeng Elementary. I'm Chen Bo-hua, and I've been honored to serve as principal here in Tianwei's garden town since August 2025. My work here builds on years as a classroom teacher, a school director, and a curriculum supervisor for the Changhua County Government — experience that continues to shape how I think about what a school can be for every child.

歡迎來到陸豐國小。我是陳柏華，很榮幸自 114 年 8 月起在花鄉田尾擔任校長。在此之前，我曾擔任教師、主任，以及彰化縣政府課程督學，這些歷練持續形塑我對「學校能為每個孩子做些什麼」的想法。

III.

Vision for the School

辦學理念

Multiple pathways, so every child can shine in their own way.

多元發展，適性揚才。

🌏
Rooting Bilingual Education
扎根雙語教育

Principal Chen has led Lufeng in launching its bilingual education initiative — a ship setting sail so students can explore a bilingual world and grow into confident, globally-minded learners.

💻
Technology & Innovation
科技與創新教學

He actively encourages teachers and students to enter technology and civic-simulation competitions, and co-authored a book on AI and education — championing digital literacy for the AI era.

🎉
A Vibrant Campus
活力校園營造

Principal Chen values a lively, engaged school community, building strong foundations for every child's growth — working hand in hand with teachers and families to create shared success.
