With Love & Talent — Mother's Day才藝感恩，愛的母親節慶典

Jioushe Elementary rang with music, laughter, and heartfelt gratitude during the annual Talent Show & Mother's Day Appreciation Event. Township Mayor 蕭浚二 joined the celebration, presenting carnations to every parent in attendance — a small flower carrying an enormous amount of love.

Every child on that stage performed for the most important person in their world.

With the warm support of the Parent Committee Chair 蕭安棋, the 舊社老人關懷協會 President 黃秀如, and community partners, the afternoon came alive with performances that showed just how hard Jioushe students work:

🥁 太鼓 Taiko 🥁 爵士鼓 Jazz Drums 🎵 直笛 Recorder 💃 舞蹈 Dance 🎹 鋼琴 Piano 🎸 烏克麗麗 Ukulele 🎭 話劇 Drama

Each child also presented a beautiful handmade card — poured full of the kind of love that only a child can express so purely and completely.

The afternoon glowed with warmth, pride, and the kind of love that fills a room without needing any words. Every performance was given with full hearts — and every mother in that audience felt it deeply.

Happy Mother's Day to every mother — may you always know how loved you are.

中文原文 · Chinese

今天舊社國小舉辦「才藝表演暨母親節感恩活動」，慶祝一年一度溫馨而偉大的母親節。感謝家長們熱情踴躍參與，也感謝鄉公所蕭浚二鄉長致贈康乃馨給每一位家長！

感謝家委會蕭安棋會長、舊社老人關懷協會黃秀如理事長，以及各位委員、常委與副會長的支持與蒞臨。

小朋友們以實際行動表達對媽媽的感謝與愛，帶來精彩多元的才藝表演，包括太鼓、爵士鼓、直笛、舞蹈、鋼琴、烏克麗麗及話劇等，展現平日學習的成果與自信。此外，每位孩子也親手製作精美卡片，滿懷心意獻給最親愛、最偉大的媽媽，讓現場洋溢著溫暖與感動。

所有上台演出的孩子們，用心投入、真情流露，以最純真的方式向媽媽表達感恩與祝福，令人動容。在這充滿感恩的節日裡，誠摯祝福天下所有的媽媽們：母親節快樂，身體健康，幸福平安！
