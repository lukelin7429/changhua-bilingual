A warm community school in the heart of Sheetou

Founded in 1999, Jioushe Elementary School has grown into one of Sheetou Township's most vibrant learning communities. Set against the green hills of Changhua County, the school holds deep roots in the local neighbourhood and a spirit of genuine care for every student.

The school crest — a heart shape woven from books and community — captures the school's guiding belief: that education is an act of love, and that every child deserves a place to grow, explore, and shine.

舊社國小創立於 1999 年，是彰化縣社頭鄉充滿溫度的社區型學校。學校環境優美，師生關係融洽，秉持「全人教育」的理念，致力於讓每位學生都能發揮潛能，快樂學習、健康成長。

愛心形的校徽，是舊社最好的象徵——教育是一種愛，而這裡，是每個孩子安心發光的地方。

What We Stand For

Play · Create · Connect

運動・科技・雙語 — 舊社孩子的三種光芒

At Jioushe, every child finds their strength — on the court, at the keyboard, or in a conversation with the world.

🏀

Play Basketball & Sport

The basketball team is the pride of Jioushe. Trained hard and playing with heart, our student athletes have earned strong results in township, county, and national competitions. Sport here builds more than skill — it builds teamwork, resilience, and a deep confidence that carries into every corner of life.

籃球隊是舊社最閃亮的招牌。學生訓練有素、用心拼搏，在鄉級、縣級乃至全國賽事中屢有亮眼表現。運動在這裡不只是技術，更是培養團隊合作、韌性與自信的最好舞台。

💻

Create Technology & Innovation

Jioushe is equipped with modern technology facilities where students learn coding, explore robotics, and dive into maker activities. Digital literacy and creative thinking go hand in hand here — children don't just use technology, they understand and shape it.

舊社配備現代化科技設備，推動程式設計、機器人課程與創客活動，全面提升學生的數位素養與創新能力。孩子在這裡不只是科技的使用者，更是科技的創造者。

🌏

Connect Bilingual & Global

English is woven naturally into daily school life at Jioushe. Through bilingual themed lessons, our students build real language ability alongside an international perspective — learning to speak to the world while staying rooted in who they are and where they come from.

英語自然地融入舊社的日常校園生活。透過雙語主題課程，學生在真實情境中培養語言能力與國際視野，既能與世界對話，又不忘自己的根。
