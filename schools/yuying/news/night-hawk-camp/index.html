The Night Hawk Overnight Camp

育英夜鷹行動宿營體驗活動圓滿成功
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On 7–8 March, the parents' association ran a two-day, one-night camp for grades 5 and 6. Led by instructors from a professional activity team, the children learned by doing — how to pitch a tent, how to manage outdoor living, and how to take care of themselves away from home.

115 年 3 月 7～8 日，由家長會辦理五、六年級兩天一夜的宿營活動。在專業活動團隊教官的帶領下，孩子們從做中學——練習搭帳棚、打理戶外生活，提升離開家的自理能力。

On the first night, the principal, the association chair, the directors, the teachers and the parents all joined the children to sing and dance together. Family, teachers and students made the fun together — and made some of the best memories of the year.

第一天的晚會，校長、會長、主任、師長與家長們一起陪著孩子唱歌、跳舞。親師生共同創造歡樂，也留下了最美好的回憶。

Day two brought station activities and a big field game, where the children learned to divide up the work and move as one team — building the everyday skills that help them adapt and cope on their own.

第二天的分站活動與大地遊戲，讓孩子們從遊戲中學習分工合作與團隊默契，一點一滴提升適應生活的各項能力。
