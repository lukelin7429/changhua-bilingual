Little Architects, Big Discoveries

小小建築師，創意大發現
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Class 5C travelled to the Yuandou Career Exploration Center for a hands-on course called “A Day at an Architecture Firm.” The students met the tools of the trade — blueprints, show-home models, and the scale that shrinks a whole building onto a single sheet of paper.

五年丙班來到原斗職探中心，體驗一堂名為「建築事務所的一天」的職探課程。孩子們認識了建築的專業工具——藍圖、樣品屋，以及能把一整棟建築縮進一張紙裡的比例尺。

Using site models, they followed a building from the ground up, step by step, and saw the planning and care hidden behind every design. Then they picked up their own materials and built a model of the Eiffel Tower — learning about space and structure by making something real.

課程中，大家透過工地模型觀察建築的施工流程，了解每一個設計背後的巧思與規劃；接著親手 DIY 製作巴黎鐵塔模型，在實作中學習空間概念與結構原理。

Watching the students think hard and build with focus, it was clear they had gathered more than knowledge — they had opened up new ideas about what their own futures might hold.

看著孩子們專注思考、認真創作的模樣，不僅收穫滿滿知識，也為未來的職涯開啟了更多想像與可能。
