An Afternoon Tea for Honor Roll Students

榮耀時刻——與校長、會長的幸福下午茶
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Every honor certificate is a footprint of hard work. This school year, a group of Yuying students each collected ten honor certificates — for their effort in learning, in character, and in serving others.

每一張榮譽狀，都是努力的足跡。114 學年度，育英有一群同學各自集滿了十張榮譽狀——在學習上、品格上、服務上的努力，都被看見了。

To celebrate, the school arranged a special afternoon tea for them with the principal and the chair of the parents' association. Over cake and warm conversation, the students shared the small moments of their growth this year.

為了肯定孩子們的努力，學校特別安排這群同學與校長及家長會長共進一場溫馨的下午茶。在點心與談笑之間，同學們分享著這一年成長的點點滴滴。

Every choice to keep going deserves applause. We hope these students keep shining, and keep writing new chapters in their own learning journeys.

每一次堅持，都值得喝采。期待同學們持續發光發熱，在自己的學習旅程上，寫下更多精彩。
