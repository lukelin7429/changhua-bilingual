Ways to Learn

從校園生活學英文

Real school, real words.

每支影片都取材自育英真實的校園日常。

42 Videos · 7 Themes
Course 01
Word of the Day
每日一字

42 bilingual videos filmed around the Yuying campus and sorted into seven themes — sports and movement, art and making, the classroom, the school day, food and festivals, the garden, and health and safety. Each word is embedded straight from YouTube with two example sentences taken from the video description.42 支雙語影片，全部在育英校園實地拍攝，依七大主題分類——運動律動、美勞創作、課堂學習、校園日常、美食節慶、田園戶外、健康安全。每個單字直接嵌入影片，並附兩句取自影片說明欄的例句。

30 Episodes · For Teachers
Course 02
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers, taught by Sarah Thomas across 10 topics and 30 episodes — from greetings and roll call to exam instructions.給老師使用的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學，共 10 大主題、30 支影片——從問候點名到考試指令。

13 Episodes · By Office
Course 03
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

"Broadcast English" — thirteen short episodes in which each office and section introduces, in English, the announcements it makes over the school PA system, from the academic affairs office to the counselling room.「廣播英語」——13 支短片，由各處室、各組用英語介紹平時透過校園廣播宣布的內容，從教務處到輔導室。
