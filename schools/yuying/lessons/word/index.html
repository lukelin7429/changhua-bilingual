In the Classroom
課堂學習 · 8 字
WORD 17
professional development
專業進修
In Taiwan, all elementary schools have professional development on Wednesday afternoons.
台灣所有小學老師在週三下午有專業進修。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development.
老師在專業進修時學習新技能與教學策略。

Look at the students: they’re all reading quietly.
看看這群學生，他們閱讀時多麼安靜啊！
Reading often can help you improve your vocabulary and learn many new things.
閱讀經常可以讓你增加字彙並且學到很多新知。

A good storyteller teaches a lesson and entertains you at the same time.
一個擅長講說故事的人可以教導大家道理，同時又兼具娛樂大眾。
The storytellers can use real or fictional information to tell a story.
說故事的人可能用真實或者是虛構的情節來說故事。

The School Day
校園日常 · 7 字
WORD 25
recess (n)
下課時間
After the bell rings for recess, most students go outside to play.
下課鈴聲響起後，大多數學生都在外面玩。
Recess is when kids can play outside, run around, and have fun.
下課時間是孩子們可以在外面玩耍、跑來跑去、玩得開心的時候。
WORD 26
dismissal (n)
放學
At dismissal the students wait outside for their parents to pick them up from school.
放學時間，學生在外面等待父母接他們回家。
You should have all your belongings with you at dismissal time.
放學時，你應該將所有個人物品都帶到。
WORD 27
sweep (v)
掃地
Every day, students sweep away fallen leaves on school grounds.
每天學生打掃校園的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.
台灣的學生打掃校園，保持校園的乾淨整齊。
WORD 28
wash your hands
洗手
It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
確定在吃東西之前要用香皂和水來洗手。
WORD 29

消毒
The worker disinfects the school.
工人正在幫學校消毒。
He sprays chemicals to disinfect the school.
他噴化學藥劑幫學校消毒。
WORD 30
school lunch (n)
學校午餐
School lunch is eaten in the classroom in Taiwan.
台灣的學校午餐是在教室裡吃的。
School lunch is designed to be healthy.
學校午餐主旨在健康。
WORD 31
serve (v)
打菜
The students serve lunch to their classmates.
學生為同學打菜。
The task of serving changes every week.
打菜的工作每週輪替一次。

Health & Safety
健康與安全 · 2 字
WORD 41
scrape (n)

The nurse attends to the scrape on the student's knee.
護理師處理學生膝蓋上的擦傷。

WORD 42
CPR
心肺復甦術
CPR is a procedure that can save someone's life if their heart stops beating.
心肺復甦術是種醫療處理，當有人心臟停止跳動時可以用它來救人一命。
CPR isn't only useful for adults to learn; kids can learn and perform CPR, too.
心肺復甦術不僅對大人很有用，小孩子也可以學習做 CPR。
