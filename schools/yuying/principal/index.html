I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Yuying's crest is an eagle — EAGLE. To put into practice the spirit of the 12-year national curriculum — taking the initiative, engaging with others, and seeking the common good — and to meet an age of education that is at once global, rooted in its own place, and attentive to each individual, we have built the school's vision from the letters of that word.

育英的校徽是一隻鷹（EAGLE）。為落實十二年國教「自發、互動、共好」的理念，並回應教育全球化、本土化、個別化的趨勢，我們以 EAGLE 的字首，建立學校願景。

Each letter names something we want for every child who passes through our gate: the confidence to express and cooperate, the drive to learn on their own, the horizon of a global citizen, a lasting love of reading, and the energy to act and solve problems. That is what it means, here, to soar like an eagle.

每一個字母，都是我們對每一個育英孩子的期望：勇於表達與合作、能夠自主學習、具備國際視野、養成閱讀習慣，並擁有行動與解決問題的能量。這，就是育英所說的「展翅飛翔」。

III.

Guiding Beliefs

辦學理念
全球化 —— 讓孩子的視野接軌世界；
本土化 —— 讓學習扎根土地；
個別化 —— 讓每個孩子被看見。
Global in outlook, rooted in place, attentive to each child — the three trends Principal Lin sees shaping education today. · 全球化、本土化、個別化，是林校長眼中形塑當代教育的三個趨勢。

A ninety-year-old school does not stand still. Principal Lin's aim for Yuying is to keep its long tradition of excellence alive while opening the classroom outward — a bilingual, internationally minded education that still knows exactly where it comes from, so that graduates leave as modern citizens with a passion for lifelong learning, a sense of social responsibility, and a wide view of the world.

一所九十年的學校，不會停在原地。林校長帶領育英的方向，是讓悠久的卓越傳統持續發光，同時把課堂向外打開——一種面向國際、雙語並行，卻清楚自己從何而來的教育，讓每一位畢業生都成為具備終身學習熱情、社會關懷心與國際視野的現代優質國民。
