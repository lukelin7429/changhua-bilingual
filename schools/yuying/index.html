I.

Welcome

育英國民小學歡迎您

Let young minds soar like eagles.

讓年輕的心靈如鷹般高飛。

Yuying Elementary opened its doors in April 1933 as a girls' school in the heart of Yuanlin. Over ninety years it has changed its name four times, opened its classrooms to boys and girls alike, and grown into one of the town's best-loved schools — a place where a long history sits comfortably beside a forward-looking, bilingual classroom.

育英國小於民國 22 年（1933）4 月創校，最初名為「員林女子公學校」，專收女童。九十餘年來四度更名、男女共學，一路成長為員林最受地方喜愛的學校之一——悠久的歷史，與面向未來的雙語課堂並肩而立。

Today Yuying is a vibrant community of learners across 37 classes — 32 general classes, 4 artistic-talent dance classes, and a resource class — so that every child can find their own path to growth and success.

如今育英是一個充滿活力的學習社區，共有 37 個班級：32 個普通班、4 個藝術才能舞蹈班，以及 1 個資源班，讓每一位學生都能找到屬於自己的成長與成功之路。

A beacon of learning in Yuanlin, for more than ninety years.
卓越教育的悠久傳統——員林學習的燈塔。
II.

A Campus That Greets You

每天迎接師生的校景

Palms at the gate, a painted sky in the arcade.

校門前的椰影，川堂上方的一片藍天彩繪。

The Front Gate & the Sky Arcade
校門與彩繪川堂

A row of tall palms lines the drive up to the main building, where the school's name has welcomed pupils through the same doorway for generations. Step into the covered arcade and the ceiling opens into a painted blue sky — a small piece of imagination on the way to class.

通往主校舍的道路兩旁，是一整排高大的椰子樹；「育英國民小學」的門楣，已在同一個入口迎接了幾個世代的孩子。走進川堂，抬頭是一片藍天白雲的彩繪——上課途中的一點想像。

III.

The Eagle Vision

鷹的願景：EAGLE 核心理念

Our crest is a soaring eagle — and its name spells out what we hope for every child.

校徽是一隻展翅高飛的鷹，牠的名字（EAGLE）正說明了我們對每個孩子的期望。

E — Expression
表達力 · 溝通互動、團隊合作

Fostering communication, teamwork and meaningful interaction — children who can say what they think and listen well. 培養溝通、團隊合作與有意義的互動。

A — Autonomy
自主力 · 自發學習、創新應變

Encouraging self-directed learning and inventive thinking, so pupils take charge of their own progress. 鼓勵自主學習與創新思維。

G — Globality
國際力 · 國際接軌、開拓視野

Preparing students for a global society with broadened horizons — the aim of our bilingual programme. 為學生準備進入廣闊的全球社會，開拓視野。

L — Literacy
閱讀力 · 閱讀探究、終身學習

Making reading and lifelong learning a fundamental skill and a daily habit. 將閱讀探究與終身學習作為基本能力。

E — Energy
行動力 · 實踐力行、解決問題

Empowering students to act, solve problems and stay curious — with a healthy body and a feel for the arts. 賦予學生行動、解決問題與積極主動的能量。

IV.

Self-Directed, Healthy, Full of Life

自主學習・健康成長・活力育英・展翅飛翔

Cultivating tomorrow's global citizens.

培養明天的全球公民。

Our mission is to develop well-rounded individuals with a passion for lifelong learning, a sense of social responsibility and an international perspective — modern citizens ready to take their place in the world.

育英的使命，是培養具有終身學習熱情、社會關懷心與國際視野的現代優質國民，引導學生走向光明而成功的未來。
