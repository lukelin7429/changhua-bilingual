Five Years Running — A "Three Good" Model Campus榮耀時刻，芙朝國小連續五年榮獲三好校園肯定

Fuchao Elementary has once again been honored by the Master Hsing Yun Education Fund's Public Trust — marking our fifth consecutive year winning this recognition! ✨

Education isn't only about grades. What matters just as much is raising children with good character — living out the "Three Goods":

  • 🌱 Do good deeds
  • 🌱 Speak good words
  • 🌱 Keep a good heart

From a simple "thank you" to lending a hand without being asked, to learning respect, care, and responsibility — our students build good character one small habit at a time, turning the "Three Goods" into something they truly live, not just recite.

Five years of recognition, in a row.

Being recognized five years running wouldn't be possible without every teacher, parent, and community partner who has poured their heart into raising these children — and especially the Fuchao kids themselves, who study hard and live with sincerity every day. ❤️

Looking ahead, Fuchao Elementary will keep walking alongside our students — turning kindness into habit, and character into strength, so every child can become the best version of themselves. 🌈

中文原文 · Chinese

🏆【榮耀時刻｜芙朝國小連續五年榮獲三好校園肯定】🏆

芙朝國小再次榮獲公益信託星雲大師教育基金肯定，這也是我們連續第五年獲獎！✨ 教育不只是學業成績，更重要的是培養孩子具有良好的品格行為🌱做好事🌱說好話🌱存好心

從日常生活中的一句謝謝、一次主動幫忙，到懂得尊重、關懷與負責任，孩子們在校園中一點一滴累積好品格，也讓「三好」真正落實在生活裡。

能夠連續五年獲得肯定，要感謝每一位用心陪伴孩子成長的老師、家長與社區夥伴，更要謝謝每一位努力學習、認真生活的芙朝孩子們 ❤️

未來，芙朝國小也會繼續陪伴孩子，把善良變成習慣、把品格化為力量，讓每一位孩子都能成為更好的自己 🌈✨
