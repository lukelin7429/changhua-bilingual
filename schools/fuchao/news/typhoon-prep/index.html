Before Typhoon Bawi Arrives, Team Fuchao Takes to the Rooftop巴威颱風前，芙朝團隊上天台清理排水

With Typhoon Bawi approaching Taiwan behind a full, well-formed storm system, Fuchao Elementary wasn't taking any chances. We never let our guard down before a typhoon — so this time, our very own "Black Cat Principal" 🐈‍⬛ climbed to the highest point on campus and led the whole team up to the rooftop, sweeping every water pipe and drain hole spotless. Cleanest it's ever been!

One shovel, one bag at a time.

Bag by bag, shovel by shovel, the team cleared away leaves, dirt, and debris from every drain — making sure no clogged pipe would let rainwater seep into the school building. It was hot, sweaty work under the summer sun, but everyone pitched in without complaint.

Thank you to every teacher and staff member who carried the load before the storm. Because of your hard work, Fuchao's campus can rest easy through the wind and rain.

中文原文 · Chinese

巴威風王來臨前，芙朝團隊上天台，
清理水孔清垃圾，確保校舍防滲水，
ㄧ鏟ㄧ袋清乾淨，做好防颱真安心。

每一次的颱風來臨前，我們都不敢輕忽及懈怠，巴威颱風帶著完整的暴風圈靠近台灣～為了守護芙朝國小🐈‍⬛校長帶領著團隊夥伴，登上芙朝制高點，把所有的水管及排水孔都清掃得一塵不染，史上最乾淨！

感謝各位夥伴的負重前行，換來芙朝校園的歲月靜好！

typhoon/taɪˈfun/颱風

A typhoon can bring strong wind and heavy rain.颱風會帶來強風和大雨。

rooftop/ˈruftɑp/屋頂、天台

Our team cleaned the rooftop before the storm.我們的團隊在颱風前清理了屋頂。

drain/dren/排水孔

We cleaned every drain so water won't leak in.我們清理了每個排水孔，水才不會滲進來。

clean/klin/清理、打掃

We clean the school before a typhoon.颱風前我們會清理學校。

shovel/ˈʃʌvəl/鏟子

We used a shovel, bag by bag, to clear the roof.我們用鏟子一鏟一袋地清乾淨屋頂。

team/tim/團隊

Our team worked together on the rooftop.我們的團隊一起在屋頂上合作。

prepare/prɪˈpɛr/準備

We prepare our school before every typhoon.每次颱風來之前我們都會做好準備。

safe/sef/安全的

A clean drain keeps our school safe and dry.乾淨的排水孔讓學校既安全又乾燥。
