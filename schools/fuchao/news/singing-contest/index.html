The First Niuchouzi Cup Singing Contest芙朝國小第一屆牛稠仔盃歌唱比賽

Fuchao Elementary just wrapped up an electrifying, surprise-filled event: the first-ever Niuchouzi Cup Singing Contest — named after Niuchouzi, the original hamlet where our school began a century ago. We didn't know it until we tried it: our Fuchao kids absolutely love to sing! 😍

Facing a packed audience, not one performer showed a hint of stage fright. The moment a microphone touched their hands, every contestant transformed into a full-fledged little superstar, radiating confidence and charisma under the lights.

We can't wait to see everyone bring that same confidence and courage into a joyful summer.

Thank you to every singer who gave us a show-stopping performance, and to the judges and organizing teachers whose hard work made it all possible. This contest was the perfect way to close out the semester — see you next semester! ❤️

中文原文 · Chinese

【芙朝國小第一屆牛稠仔盃歌唱比賽】

一場讓人熱血沸騰、驚喜連連的「芙朝歌唱比賽」！不辦不知道，一辦嚇一跳！原來我們芙朝的孩子們竟然這麼熱愛唱歌！😍

面對台下滿滿的觀眾，孩子們一點也不怯場。一拿起麥克風，每個人都變身為架勢十足的小巨星！在舞台上展現出無比的自信與魅力！

感謝所有參賽歌手帶來的精彩演出，也謝謝評審老師與籌備師長們的辛勞。這場歌唱比賽為這學期畫下了最完美的句點！期待大家帶著這份自信與勇敢，迎接充實又快樂的假期！我們下學期見囉！❤️
