Warmth in Bloom — Growing Up Happy at Fuchao溫馨祝福月，在芙朝，一起幸福成長

May through July is Fuchao's "Season of Warm Blessings" — and the campus is once again full of children's excited laughter and the sweet smell of birthday cake! 🎂

Thank you to our Parent Association Chair, who came to school in person to bring delicious cakes and warm wishes for every child celebrating a birthday — turning each celebration into a joyful, memorable moment. 💛

At Fuchao, growing up isn't only about getting taller.

We believe growing up means slowly learning, day by day, to:

  • 🌱 care for others on your own initiative
  • 🌱 feel and show gratitude
  • 🌱 be brave and responsible
  • 🌱 respect and cooperate with others

So alongside birthdays, we also keep a running "Character Celebration Journal," inviting each child to write down a small moment they're proud of. Some wrote about tidying the classroom without being asked. Some wrote about bravely speaking in front of the class for the first time. One child simply wrote: "Today, I listened patiently while my classmate was talking."

These small changes are Fuchao's children's most precious footprints of growth. In this warm campus, we learn together, grow together, and turn every celebration into one of the best memories a child could have. 🌈

中文原文 · Chinese

🎈【溫馨祝福月｜在芙朝，一起幸福成長】🎈

芙朝國小5~7月的「幸福祝福時光」熱鬧登場啦～ 🎂✨ 校園裡再次充滿孩子們期待的笑聲與甜甜的蛋糕香！感謝會長暖心到校，為壽星孩子們準備美味蛋糕與滿滿祝福，陪伴孩子度過充滿儀式感的快樂時刻 💛

在芙朝，我們相信：成長不只是長高、長大，更是在每天的校園生活中，慢慢學會🌱主動關心🌱懂得感恩🌱勇敢負責🌱尊重與合作

因此，除了慶祝生日，我們也持續透過「品格慶賀小紀錄」，邀請孩子們寫下最近讓自己感到驕傲的小進步 ✨ 有的孩子分享自己主動幫忙整理教室，有的孩子記錄了第一次勇敢上台發表，也有孩子寫下：「今天我有耐心聽同學說話。」

這些看似小小的改變，其實都是芙朝孩子最珍貴的成長足跡 ❤️ 在溫暖的校園裡，我們一起學習、一起陪伴、一起變得更好，讓每一次慶祝，都成為孩子心中最美好的回憶 🌈✨
