Where the fields once decided who went to school

Before 1924, children in this corner of Pitou Township had to walk all the way to Xizhou or Pitou proper just to attend class — and the community longed for a school of its own. In April 1924, the wish came true: local benefactor Huang Ching-lien donated the land, villager Liu Shui-shu and seven neighbours lent more, and a branch classroom of Pitou Public School opened under its first teacher, Su Cu-pi. The school became fully independent in 1930.

Today the campus still sits where it began — at the southern edge of Pitou Township, ringed by rice paddies, bordering Beidou to the east and Xizhou to the south. Most students come from farming families, known for their kindness and diligence. The world has changed around it, but Fuchao's roots in the fields have not.

民國十三年（1924）以前，這一帶的孩子得長途跋涉到溪洲或埤頭才能上學，地方建校的心願日益殷切。1924 年 4 月，願望終於實現：地方仕紳黃清廉先生慷慨捐地，芙朝村劉水樹先生等八位鄉親也出借土地，埤頭公學校牛稠子分教場於焉成立，首任教師是蘇粗皮先生。1930 年，學校正式獨立設校。

如今校園仍座落於當年的起點——埤頭鄉南邊，四周稻田環繞，東鄰北斗鎮、南接溪州鄉。學生多為農家子弟，秉性善良勤樸。時代不斷向前，芙朝紮根稻田的初心，始終未曾改變。

A Message from Our School

Welcome to Fuchao Elementary School

歡迎來到芙朝國小！

At Fuchao Elementary School, we believe in fostering a well-rounded education that empowers our students to reach their full potential. Our community, comprised of 87 wonderful students, is dedicated to embodying our six key principles.

在芙朝國小，我們堅信透過全方位的教育，能讓我們的學生發揮他們的最大潛力。我們學校由 87 位優秀的學生組成，致力於實踐我們的六大核心價值。

Our Mission 我們的使命

Our goal is to implement these principles in order to provide a holistic educational environment for our students. We strive to cultivate an atmosphere of vitality, joy in learning, character, and exploration, where every student feels nurtured and encouraged to grow.

我們的目標是落實這些核心價值，為學生打造全人教育的環境。我們致力於營造充滿活力、樂於學習、重視品格與勇於探索的氛圍，讓每個孩子都感受到關懷，並被鼓勵成長茁壯。

Our Strategy 我們的策略

We are committed to promoting the professional development of our teachers and the effective operation of our curriculum. We believe that by providing the right tools and resources, we can ensure our students have every opportunity to showcase their learning abilities and talents.

我們致力於促進教師的專業成長，並確保課程有效地運作。我們相信，透過提供合適的工具與資源，就能確保每個孩子都有機會展現自己的學習能力與天賦。

Our Environment 我們的環境

Fuchao Elementary School is not just an institution, but a family. By connecting parents, teachers, students, and various resources, we've built a friendly and warm learning environment where everyone feels welcomed.

芙朝國小不只是一所學校，更是一個大家庭。我們串連家長、老師、學生與各方資源，打造出一個友善溫馨的學習環境，讓每一個人都感受到歸屬與歡迎。

Our Students 我們的學生

Our students are at the heart of everything we do. We are proud to educate well-rounded children who are growing in balanced development across five areas of education. We celebrate their achievements and look forward to their continued success.

學生是我們一切努力的核心。我們以培育德智體群美五育均衡發展的孩子為榮，一起慶祝他們的每個成就，並期待他們持續發光發熱。
