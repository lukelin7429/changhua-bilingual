Words on Campus

芙朝校園拍攝的單字影片
WORD 001
professional developmentn.
專業成長
Professional development helps teachers learn new skills.
專業成長能幫助老師學習新技能。
Our teachers take part in professional development every semester.
我們的老師每學期都會參加專業成長課程。
WORD 002
wash your handsphr.
洗手
Wash your hands before you eat.
吃東西前要洗手。
We wash our hands to stay healthy.
我們洗手保持健康。
WORD 003
readv.
閱讀
We read quietly in the library every week.
我們每週都在圖書館安靜閱讀。
Reading a good book opens up a new world.
閱讀一本好書，就打開了一個新世界。
WORD 004
explainv.
說明
Our teacher explains new words with pictures.
老師會用圖片說明新單字。
Please explain your answer to the class.
請向全班說明你的答案。
WORD 005
workbookn.
習作本
A workbook is where we practice what we learn.
習作本是我們練習所學內容的地方。
We write our answers in the workbook every day.
我們每天都在習作本上寫答案。
WORD 006
scarecrown.
稻草人
A scarecrow stands in the rice field to scare away birds.
稻草人站在稻田裡，嚇跑鳥兒。
Farmers near our school make scarecrows for their fields.
學校附近的農夫會在田裡做稻草人。
