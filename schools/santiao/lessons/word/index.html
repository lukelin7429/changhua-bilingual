Music & Clubs
音樂與社團 · 4 字
WORD 01
harmonica (n)
口琴
The students practice the harmonica in class.
學生在課堂上練習口琴。
They play the harmonica in unison.
他們齊聲吹口琴。
WORD 02
ocarina (n)
陶笛
The students play ocarinas in class.
同學們在課堂上吹陶笛。
They read music while they play their ocarinas.
他們邊吹陶笛邊看樂譜。
WORD 03
Taiko drum
太鼓
The students practice the Taiko drum.
學生們練習打太鼓。
They play the Taiko drum in unison.
他們打太鼓時動作整齊劃一。
WORD 04
Wisdom Ball
巧智球
The teacher introduces the Wisdom Ball to the students, encouraging them to interact with its dials and sliders.
老師向學生介紹智慧球，鼓勵他們與球盤和滑塊互動。
Using the Wisdom Ball helps students improve their logical thinking, focus, and patience.
使用智慧球可以幫助學生提升邏輯思考、專注力和耐心。

Sports & Movement
運動與律動 · 4 字
WORD 05
Taekwondo (n)
跆拳道
The students are learning Taekwondo in PE.
學生們在體育課上學習跆拳道。
You learn to kick high in Taekwondo.
你學會在跆拳道踢得很高。
WORD 06
jump rope
跳繩
Jumping rope is a great form of cardio.
跳繩是一種很好的有氧運動。
Once you get the hang of it, you can jump rope really fast!
一旦掌握了竅門，你就能跳得很快！
WORD 07
stretch (v)
伸展
The students stretch to improve their flexibility.
學生透過伸展運動來提高靈活性。
They stretch before they start their dance class.
他們在開始舞蹈課之前做伸展運動。
WORD 08
swimming class
游泳課
The students learn how to swim in swimming class.
同學們在游泳課時學習如何游泳。
They learn the basics of swimming in swimming class.
他們在游泳課時學習游泳的基本動作。

A Healthy, Tidy School
健康與整潔 · 4 字
WORD 09
sweep (v)
掃
Every day, students sweep away fallen leaves on school grounds.
每天學生打掃校園的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.
台灣的學生打掃校園，保持校園的乾淨整齊。
WORD 10
wash your hands
洗手
It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
確定在吃東西之前要用香皂和水來洗手。
WORD 11
physical examination
健康檢查
Students wait in line for a physical examination.
同學們排隊準備接受健康檢查。
They get their throats and ears checked during their physical examination.
在健康檢查的時候，醫護檢視他們的喉嚨與耳朵。
WORD 12
CPR
心肺復甦術
CPR is a procedure that can save someone's life if their heart stops beating.
心肺復甦術是種醫療處理，當有人心臟停止跳動時可以用它來救人一命。
CPR isn't only useful for adults to learn; kids can learn and perform CPR, too.
心肺復甦術不僅對大人很有用，小孩子也可以學習做 CPR。

Explore & Discover
探索與發現 · 3 字
WORD 13
bee (n)
蜜蜂
The students learned about bees and their ecology and behavior.
學生們了解了蜜蜂及其生態和行為。
The farm owner explained how honey is made, helping students see why bees are important.
農場主人解釋了蜂蜜的製作方法，幫助學生了解蜜蜂的重要性。
WORD 14
science (n)
科學
The National Museum of Natural Science brought hands-on science activities to the school.
國家自然科學博物館將科普實作活動帶到學校。
Students explored the wonders of science without leaving school grounds.
學生無需離開校園即可探索科學的奇蹟。
WORD 15
activity (n)
活動
Two American English teachers visit the school to help improve students' English by designing interesting activities.
兩位美國英語老師到訪學校，透過設計有趣的活動幫助學生提升英語能力。
These activities, such as drawing and doing origami, make the English lessons fun for the students.
繪畫和摺紙等活動讓學生的英語課變得有趣。

Events & Festivals
活動與節慶 · 3 字
WORD 16

比賽
The students comptete in a speaking contest at their school.
同學們參與學校辦的演說比賽。
Each student goes up and makes a speech for the contest.
比賽中，每位同學上台演說。

celebrate (v)
慶祝
The students celebrate Christmas at their school.
同學們在學校慶祝聖誕節。
Students wear Santa hats to clebrate Christmas.
同學們戴聖誕老人的帽子來慶祝聖誕節。
WORD 18
Halloween (n)
萬聖節
The teacher explained the origins of Halloween to the students.
老師向學生們解釋了萬聖節的由來。
The students had a lot of fun playing Halloween games at school.
學生們在學校玩萬聖節遊戲，玩得很開心。

校長正在接待一位來自美國的客人。

Some principals gather for professional development to enhance their knowledge and skills.
一些校長聚集在一起進行專業發展，以提高他們的知識和技能。
