I.

Welcome to Santiao

歡迎來到三條國小
Welcome to Santiao Elementary School · 三條國小簡介影片

A harmonica in one hand, a taekwondo kick in the other, and a book in between.

一手拿著口琴，一腳踢著跆拳，中間擺著一本書——這就是三條孩子忙碌又快樂的一天。

Santiao Elementary is a small, happy school beside the Zhuoshui River in Sijhou Township, Changhua, with around 75 students. Here children do not only study — they play the harmonica, practise taekwondo and take the stage to dance, learning to keep a balance between hard work and joy.

三條國小是一所位在濁水溪畔的幸福小學，全校目前約有 75 位學生。我們的孩子不只是讀書——他們手拿口琴、腳踢跆拳，站上舞台跳舞，在忙碌的學習生活中，學會平衡努力與快樂。

Alongside a strong academic programme, Santiao offers seven free after-school clubs, all-day care until 5:30 every weekday, summer and winter camps and learning support — so that every child is looked after, and every child has somewhere to find what they are good at.

除了扎實的學科課程，三條還開辦七大免費社團，週一至週五課後照顧到下午 5：30，並辦理寒暑假營隊與學習扶助——讓每個孩子都被照顧，也讓每個孩子都能找到自己的舞台。

We are proud to build a bilingual learning environment, with teaching in both Mandarin and English, and an outward-looking programme of English certification, themed lessons, festival activities, visiting foreign English teachers and the NTU International Companions exchange.

我們也很自豪能推動雙語學習環境，同時重視華語與英語教學，並透過英語學習認證、主題式教學、節慶活動、外師到校走讀課程與台大國際學伴計畫，培養孩子的國際觀與雙語能力。

One child, one skill — skills a child can carry for life.
我們致力培養孩子「一人一技藝」，讓每個孩子都具備「帶得走」的能力。
II.

The Rainbow Track & the Banyan

校園雙景 · 彩虹跑道與百年老榕

Two things everyone remembers about Santiao.

來過三條的人，都記得這兩樣東西。

A Full-Grass Field, Ringed in Rainbow
全草地大操場 · 全縣唯一的彩虹跑道

Santiao's playing field is all grass, wrapped by a 200-metre standard PU running track painted in rainbow colours — the only one of its kind in the whole county. Rising from the middle of it all is a hundred-year banyan so wide and so cared for that it is spoken of as one of the most beautiful school trees in Taiwan. Children run their laps in its shade.

三條的操場是全草地大操場，外圈環繞著一條200 公尺標準 PU 彩虹跑道——這是全縣唯一的一條。操場中央，是一棵樹冠寬闊、照顧得極好的百年老榕，被譽為「全國最美校樹」；孩子們就在它的樹蔭下一圈一圈地跑。

III.

What Santiao Is Known For

四個我們最用心經營的方向

A small school with a lot going on.

學校雖小，能做的事情很多。

Seven Clubs, All Free
七大免費社團 · 一人一技藝

Every Santiao child can join science blocks, harmonica, painting, ocarina, dance, taekwondo and gateball — seven after-school clubs that cost families nothing. The harmonica club places in the top three at the county music contest almost every year, and took second place in 2023–24. 每位三條孩子都能參加科學積木、口琴、繪畫、陶笛、舞蹈、跆拳道與巧智球等七大免費社團。口琴社幾乎年年在彰化縣音樂比賽名列前三，112 學年度更獲得第二名。

Teams That Win
體育競技 · 跆拳道與跳繩

Santiao's taekwondo team is only a few years old but has already brought home a stack of county medals — gold, silver and bronze — along with sparring titles. The school's rope-skipping team took first place overall at the Sijhou township contest. 三條的跆拳道隊成軍才幾年，已在彰化縣賽拿下大量金、銀、銅牌與對打冠亞軍；跳繩隊在福樟盃（溪州鄉）比賽榮獲總成績第一名。

A Teaching Team to Trust
優質教育團隊 · 逾九成二具碩士學歷

More than 92% of Santiao's teachers hold a master's degree or higher. The team runs professional-growth study and open observation classes every year, so the people in front of your child are both qualified and genuinely keen. 三條國小教師團隊具碩士以上學歷者佔 92% 以上，每年辦理教師成長進修與公開觀課，兼具專業與熱情。

English, Looking Outward
雙語與國際教育

Alongside daily bilingual teaching, Santiao runs English learning certification, themed and festival lessons, campus English situations, visiting foreign English teacher (FET) walk-and-read classes and the NTU International Companions video-exchange programme. 除了日常雙語教學，三條推動英語學習認證、主題式與節慶教學、校園英語情境學習、外師（FET）到校走讀課程，以及台大國際學伴計畫。

V.

Three Goods, One Good School

三條三好 · 成就共好

Good thoughts, good words, good deeds.

存好心、說好話、做好事。

A Three Goods Practice School
三好校園實踐學校

Santiao is not only named a Three Goods Practice School — we live it. In the holidays our children perform for the residents of a centre for people with intellectual disabilities, and the whole school visits the Happy Mount Colony, so that what they see and do plants a small seed of kindness. 三條不只是三好校園實踐學校，我們更帶著孩子身體力行：利用假期到啟智中心表演，帶全校孩子走訪喜樂保育院，讓孩子從所見所聞中，在心裡種下良善的種子。
