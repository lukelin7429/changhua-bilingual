A Happy Start to the New Semester

新學期，從快樂上學開始！
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Dear Santiao children and families — the long summer vacation is over! The school's hundred-year banyan tree has opened its arms wide, ready to welcome everyone back to our warm campus.

親愛的三條寶貝與家長們，漫長的暑假結束囉！學校的百年大榕樹已經張開雙臂，準備迎接大家回到溫暖的校園！

Arrival time: please arrive at school between 7:10 and 7:30 in the morning, so your child has plenty of quiet morning time to settle in.

上學時間：請於早上 7:10～7:30 到校，讓寶貝有充裕的晨光時間。

Lunch: full-day classes begin on the very first day, so please remember to bring a personal set of tableware (the school provides a free set for new students) and a water bottle.

午餐準備：開學日即正式上全天課，請記得攜帶個人餐具（新生餐具學校免費提供）與水壺。

School supplies: please have ready a schoolbag, a pencil case and stationery, a musical instrument, personal tooth-brushing items, and a handkerchief and tissues.

學用品檢查：請備妥書包、鉛筆盒文具、樂器、個人潔牙用品與手帕衛生紙。

Let's begin a brand-new season of learning together, in Santiao's beautiful campus. Go!

讓我們一起在三條美麗的校園裡，展開全新一季的學習旅程吧！Go！
