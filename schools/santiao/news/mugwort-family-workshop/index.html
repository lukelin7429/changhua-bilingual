Mugwort in the Air, Families Side by Side

艾草飄香・親師同行
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Thank you to every parent who came along today — Santiao's parent–teacher gathering for the new school year went beautifully.

感謝各位家長今日熱情參與！三條國小 115 學年度班親會已圓滿辦理完畢。

A special thank-you to teacher Chan Wen-wen of the Yikou Muzi Workshop, who guided parents, children and teachers through making a soothing mugwort massage stick — a simple craft that warms tired shoulders and helps the whole body relax.

特別感謝「一口木子工作坊」詹雯雯老師蒞臨指導，帶領親師生一同手作「放鬆療癒艾草棒」。

In the gentle scent of mugwort, big hands and small hands worked side by side — filling, wrapping, tying. The room was warm and unhurried, and every family went home with something they had made together.

在淡淡艾草清香中，大手牽小手同樂，畫面溫馨又療癒！

After the craft came the class meetings, where parents sat down with the homeroom teachers to hear how their children are learning and living at school — a close, warm connection between home and classroom.

手作時光後的班親會時間，家長們與導師深入交流，用心了解孩子在校的學習與生活點滴，建立緊密而溫暖的親師連結。

Education is not a one-way lesson — it is teachers and parents walking beside the child, together.
「教育不是單向的教導，而是親師雙向的陪伴。」

Parents are the school's most important education partners. Thank you for your support; let us keep walking together, so that every Santiao child grows up happy, confident and strong.

家長是學校最重要的教育合夥人！感謝每位家長的用心參與與支持，讓我們繼續攜手合作，成為孩子最堅強的後盾，陪伴三條寶貝們快樂自信地成長茁壯。

The teachers of Santiao will keep watching over every child. Starting from the heart, we are building a kind and beautiful campus together.

三條師長用心守護，讓我們從心出發，共創溫暖友善的美好校園！
