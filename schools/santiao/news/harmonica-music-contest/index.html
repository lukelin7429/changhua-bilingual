Double Honors for the Harmonica Team

雙喜臨門！口琴隊再創佳績
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Double good news! Santiao's harmonica team entered the 2026–27 Changhua County Student Music Competition and, in a field of strong performers, showed real musical skill and teamwork — and came home with two awards.

雙喜臨門！賀本校口琴隊參加「115學年度彰化縣學生音樂比賽」，在激烈的競賽中展現優異的音樂實力與團隊默契，勇奪雙料佳績！

🏆 Third place, Elementary Harmonica Ensemble
🏆 Third place, Elementary Harmonica Quartet

🏆 國小口琴合奏組第三名
🏆 國小口琴四重奏組第三名

Awards like these are never easy to win. Every moving note and every smooth melody grew out of hours and hours of practice, patience and effort. Thank you to the coaching teachers, who stayed beside the team, guided them with patience and care, and helped every player push past their limits and find new confidence. And thank you to each young musician, who kept practicing and never backed away from a challenge — little by little, you wrote the most beautiful music.

這份榮耀得來不易，每一個動人的音符、每一段和諧的旋律，都是孩子們無數次練習、堅持與努力的成果。感謝指導老師一路以來的用心陪伴、耐心指導與辛勤付出，帶領孩子們突破自我、展現自信；也感謝每一位口琴隊孩子認真練習、不畏挑戰，用點點滴滴的努力譜出最美的樂章！

We are also grateful to all the teachers who cheered the team on, and to every family who cares about our school and supports our children. Because you stand behind them, the children can chase their dreams and step onto the stage with courage.

同時，感謝全體師長的支持與鼓勵，以及所有關心學校、支持孩子成長的家長們，因為有大家一路相挺，讓孩子們能安心追逐夢想、勇敢站上舞台！

Every practice is growth, every note is hard work, and every honor belongs to the whole team.
「每一次練習都是成長，每一個音符都是努力，每一份榮耀都是團隊共同的成果！」

Santiao is so proud of you! Keep playing with passion and confidence, and fill our campus with even more wonderful music.

三條國小以你們為榮！願孩子們繼續帶著熱情與自信，吹奏出更多精彩樂章，讓音樂的美好在校園中持續飄揚！
