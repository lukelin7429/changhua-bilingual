Thank You, Teachers! Teachers' Day at Santiao

桃李芬芳，感恩有您：教師節慶祝活動
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

September is a season of thanks. To show our hard-working teachers how grateful we are, Santiao held a series of lively, heartwarming Teachers' Day activities.

九月是感恩的季節，為了向辛勤耕耘的老師們表達最真摯的謝意，學校舉辦了一系列熱鬧又溫馨的教師節慶祝活動！

It all started last week with the Teacher Appreciation Bingo Challenge. All over campus, children found thoughtful ways to say thank you — giving teachers a shoulder massage, pouring a cup of warm tea, showing off a talent, or giving a big hug full of love. Today, as everyone waited eagerly, the principal drew the lucky numbers. Congratulations to all the children who finished their tasks and got a bingo line!

從上週熱烈開跑的「敬師賓果闖關」，校園隨處可見孩子們貼心的小身影——幫老師搥背按摩、倒杯暖心茶水、獻上拿手才藝、給老師一個大大的擁抱與愛心！今天在期待聲中由校長抽出幸運號碼，恭喜完成任務並順利連線的寶貝們！

Next came the big highlight: “Guess Which Teacher!” One by one, precious childhood photos of our teachers appeared on the big screen. The children gasped, shouted out their guesses, and laughed nonstop.

緊接著掀起全場最高潮的「猜猜我師誰」，大螢幕上一張張珍貴的「師長童年照」，引得孩子們驚喜連連、熱烈搶答，現場笑聲不斷！

At the end, all the students gave a deep, heartfelt bow to the teachers who guide them every day. Warmth filled every heart.

活動尾聲，全體學生以最真誠的心，向平日悉心引導的師長們深深一鞠躬，溫暖的氛圍流淌在每個人心中。

Thank you, teachers! Happy Teachers' Day!
「老師，謝謝您，教師節快樂！」

Special thanks to the Alumni Association for lovely Teachers' Day gifts for every teacher, and to the Parents' Association for delicious Mid-Autumn mooncakes — filling our hard-working teachers with energy and warm wishes!

特別感謝校友會熱心致贈全校師長精美教師節禮物，以及家長會貼心致贈應景美味的中秋月餅，為辛苦的師長們補充滿滿元氣與溫暖祝福！

Wishing every teacher at Santiao a happy Mid-Autumn Festival, a happy Teachers' Day, and all the best!

預祝三條國小全體師長：中秋佳節愉快、教師節快樂、順心如意！
