A Summer Camp Full of Heart

Fu 暑假夏令營：童心共識營
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

This camp's programme was rich and varied, weaving together character education, life education, arts and crafts, international education, PE games, environmental education, classic-text reading and lessons in getting along with others. Every child came home with their hands full.

這次營隊的活動課程豐富又多元，結合品德教育、生命教育、藝文手作、國際教育、體能遊戲、環境教育、讀經活動與人際互動等課程，孩子們收穫滿滿！

Thank you to our long-time partner, the Chinese non-profit organisation that planned the camp with such care; to the volunteer group leaders who came from all over; and to the kitchen volunteers behind the scenes who cooked the delicious lunches.

感謝長期合作的主辦單位中華非營利組織用心籌備，感謝來自各地的志工隊輔，也感謝幕後協助的廚房志工烹調美味午餐。

Every day the children share what they had learned. Thank you all for giving them so much love — we are grateful, and we will see you again next year!

每天孩子們都分享當日的收穫。謝謝您們給孩子們滿滿的愛，我們心存感謝，大家相約明年再見！
