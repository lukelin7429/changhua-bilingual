Seeing the World Through Music

用耳朵看見世界：走進音樂精靈的生命故事
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Today Santiao was honored to welcome Hou Lo-li, a two-time winner of the Presidential Education Award, and her mother, teacher Hou Pei-ying. Together they shared a deeply moving life story with all of our students and teachers.

今天學校非常榮幸邀請到兩度榮獲總統教育獎的侯絡瓈姊姊與媽媽侯沛瀠老師蒞臨校園，為全校師生帶來一場動人心弦的生命故事分享！

When Lo-li was three months old, doctors found that she was completely blind, and from then on she had to feel her way through a world of darkness. But with her mother's endless love, company and encouragement, Lo-li was never defeated by fate. Instead, she discovered an amazing gift — and a bright light — in the world of music.

絡瓈姊姊在出生三個月時，便被診斷出視障全盲，從此只能在黑暗的世界中摸索前行。然而，在媽媽無微不至的愛、陪伴與鼓勵下，絡瓈姊姊不僅沒有被命運擊倒，更在音樂的世界裡發掘出驚人的天賦與光彩！

With great determination, she has won the Presidential Education Award twice (in 2015 and 2023), was invited to Japan for a music exchange, and often performs for charity groups — giving back to society and spreading hope with her warm music.

她憑藉著堅毅不拔的毅力，2度榮獲總統教育獎（2015年、2023年），也曾獲邀至日本進行音樂交流，熱心投入眾多公益團體演出，用溫暖琴音回饋社會、傳遞希望！

During the talk, Lo-li played several pieces on the piano and sang “Luxurious Dream,” a song she composed herself. As the melody filled the hall, Santiao's children clapped along to the beat and sang together — a warm and joyful scene.

講座中，絡瓈姊姊現場帶來了多首精采的音樂演奏，更演唱自己的自創曲——〈奢華夢想〉。當悠揚的旋律響起，台下的三條寶貝們跟著節奏拍手、一同齊聲合唱，畫面溫馨融洽、熱鬧非凡！

Even when your eyes cannot see the light, your heart can become the sun that lights up your dreams.
「即使眼前看不見光，心也能成為照亮夢想的太陽。」

Thank you, Lo-li and Ms. Hou, for such a deep and powerful lesson about life. Every student and teacher at Santiao was truly moved and inspired.

感謝絡瓈姊姊與沛瀠老師帶來這麼深刻且充滿力量的生命教育，讓三條國小的每一位師生都深受感動與鼓舞！
