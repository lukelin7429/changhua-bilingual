A Summer of Harmonicas

逢甲・中科・北市大・彰師大聯合口琴營
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

This summer, we learned the harmonica together! The harmonica summer camp has come to a wonderful close.

今年暑假，我們一起學口琴囉！口琴夏令營圓滿結束。

As well as the harmonica team's older students continuing to sharpen their skills, it was a delight to see so many beginners sprouting and growing strong — after just a few days they could already play a simple tune.

除了琴藝持續精進的口琴隊大孩子，很驚豔有很多初學的小種子正在萌芽茁壯——在短短幾天，就能吹出簡單的樂曲！

At the closing showcase, every child in the beginner, intermediate and advanced classes worked hard to show what they could do. Education is learning and growing, drop by drop.

成果發表會上，初級班、進階班與高級班的每個孩子都努力展現無限潛能。教育，就是點點滴滴的學習與成長。

Our warmest thanks to the student volunteers from Feng Chia University, National Taichung University of Science and Technology, University of Taipei and National Changhua University of Education. Thank you for giving the children a wonderful memory of this summer. See you again next year!

感謝逢甲大學、台中科大、台北市大與彰化師大的志工夥伴！謝謝您們為孩子們今年的夏天留下美好的回憶，相約明年再見！
