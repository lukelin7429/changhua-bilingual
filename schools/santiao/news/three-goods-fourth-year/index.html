Four Years Running: A National Three Goods Practice School

連續第四年，全國三好校園實踐學校
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Congratulations to Santiao Elementary! For the fourth year in a row, the school has been named a Three Goods Practice School in the 16th round of the national programme.

狂賀三條國小連續第四年榮獲第十六屆全國三好校園實踐學校！

We will hold fast to the spirit of the Three Goods and keep building a friendly campus that is beautiful, warm and full of harmony for our children.

我們將秉持三好的理念，持續為孩子們營造美麗、溫馨、和諧的友善校園。

The Three Goods are simple to say and take a lifetime to live: do good deeds, speak good words, think good thoughts.

三好很好說，卻要用一輩子去做：身做好事、口說好話、心存好念。

Do good deeds — help others and serve the community. Speak good words — praise others and use warm language. Think good thoughts — stay kind, keep a right mind and think positively.

做好事：多幫助他人並服務社會；說好話：多讚美別人、說溫暖的話語；存好心：良善正念，保持正向思維。

The school also held a keynote talk, “Three Goods, Growing Good Character in Children,” to share the idea with teachers and families.

學校並辦理「三好，成就孩子好素養」專題演講，與教師和家長分享三好理念。
