A Halloween Adventure at Santiao

三條國小 Halloween 萬聖節大冒險
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Today Santiao was full of laughter and surprises! To celebrate Halloween, the school planned four fun game stations, and the students split into eight teams to set off on a thrilling, playful Halloween adventure.

今天，三條國小充滿了歡笑與驚喜！為了迎接萬聖節，學校精心規劃了四個趣味闖關活動，讓學生們分成八個小隊，一起展開刺激又好玩的萬聖節大冒險！

Children came dressed in homemade costumes — paper hats, capes, and even a few floating green ghosts. At one station, teams scanned a QR code and matched Halloween words to their pictures: bat, spider, vampire, pumpkin, witch and mummy.

孩子們穿上自製的萬聖節裝扮——紙帽子、披風，還有幾個飄來飄去的綠色小幽靈。其中一關要掃描 QR code，把萬聖節英文單字和圖片配對：蝙蝠、蜘蛛、吸血鬼、南瓜、女巫、木乃伊。

Older students stepped up as team leaders, guiding the younger ones hand in hand through every challenge. Everyone gave it their all and showed real teamwork — and children of different grades grew closer along the way.

活動中，高年級的哥哥姊姊們化身小隊長，帶領學弟學妹們攜手合作、勇闖關卡。每位同學都全心投入、認真完成挑戰，展現了團隊合作的精神，也讓不同年級的孩子們在互動中增進情誼。

The moment everyone was waiting for came at the end: after clearing every station, each child received a small gift from the principal — a happy surprise for every hard-working adventurer!

最令人期待的時刻，就是完成所有關卡後，由校長親自送上小禮物，為每位努力闖關的孩子帶來滿滿的驚喜與喜悅！

Learning through laughter, growing through teamwork — Happy Halloween from Santiao!
「在歡笑中學習，在合作中成長！三條國小祝大家 Happy Halloween！」
