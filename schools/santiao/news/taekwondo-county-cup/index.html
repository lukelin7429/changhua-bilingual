Eight Golds and a Team Title in Taekwondo

縣長盃跆拳道：8 金 10 銀 7 銅
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Congratulations to Santiao's young taekwondo athletes! At the Changhua County Magistrate's Cup Taekwondo Championships, their hard training and never-give-up spirit paid off with 8 gold, 10 silver and 7 bronze medals — and the girls' sparring team was crowned team champion!

賀三條國小跆拳道小將們參加彰化縣縣長盃跆拳道錦標賽，憑藉平日辛勤訓練與堅持不懈的精神，勇奪 8 金、10 銀、7 銅的亮眼成績，更一舉拿下對練女子組團體第一名！

Every medal is the fruit of the children's hard work. It also shows the careful guidance of their coaches and the power of teamwork. Congratulations to every winner, and thank you to the coaches and teachers for all their effort.

每一面獎牌，都是孩子們努力付出的成果，也展現了教練用心指導及團隊合作的力量。恭喜所有獲獎選手，也感謝教練與師長們的辛勞付出！

Santiao kids, you are amazing!

三條國小的孩子們，你們真的太棒了！

Keep your courage and determination, take on the next challenge, and reach even higher!
「繼續帶著勇氣與毅力，迎接下一個挑戰，再創高峰！」
