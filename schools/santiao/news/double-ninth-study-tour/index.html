Double Ninth Day: Learning Side by Side with Grandparents

潭心好日，墘香時光：重陽九九，幸福久久
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

For the Double Ninth Festival, a day for honoring our elders, Santiao's fourth- to sixth-graders joined “Warm Hearts, Good Times at Tanqian,” a special course run by the Changhua County Fun Study Center. It was a learning journey full of both knowledge and warmth.

配合重陽節敬老主題，本校四至六年級學生參加彰化縣特色遊學中心辦理的「潭心好日，墘香時光」特色課程，展開一場既有知識又有溫度的學習之旅！

The day began with the children and local grandmas and grandpas wiggling and dancing together to the Taiwanese folk song “The Mountain Girl Is Getting Married.” Laughter filled the air as the children learned, in the happiest way, to respect, love and thank their elders.

活動一開始，學生與阿公阿嬤一起歡樂扭一扭，隨著〈內山姑娘要出嫁〉的旋律開心舞動，現場笑聲不斷，讓孩子在歡樂中學習敬老、愛老與感恩。

Next, Ms. Hui of the Healthy Fresh-Pick Farm taught the students how Hang chrysanthemums grow and why the farm grows them in a nature-friendly way. The children turned young chrysanthemum buds into their own tea bags and shared the fragrant tea with the seniors. Learning across generations, they saw why it matters to treasure food in season and to support friendly farming.

在健康鮮採農園卉姐的帶領下，學生認識杭菊的生長知識與友善栽種理念，親手將胎杭菊製作菊花茶包，並與長輩分享菊花茶的清香。透過青銀共學，孩子體會珍惜當季食材、支持友善農業的意義。

In the afternoon, the group visited a local farm in Dacheng Township to see how technology helps raise chickens, and how eggs are washed and packed. Everyone tried boxing eggs by hand and felt the care and hard work behind every single egg. Finally, at the Dacheng Farmers' Association, they made Taiwanese batter egg pancakes with their own hands — and enjoyed every bite!

下午前往大城在地農場，了解科技化雞隻飼養、雞蛋清洗與包裝的完整流程，並親手體驗裝盒雞蛋，感受每一顆雞蛋背後的用心與辛勞。最後，在大城農會大家動手製作粉漿蛋餅，享受自己動手做的樂趣！

Learning stepped out of the classroom — into the community and the countryside.
「讓學習走出教室，走進人群與農村。」

After a full day, the children came home with new knowledge and real-life experience — and with deeper respect for elders, for food, and for the land. Learning stepped out of the classroom, into the community and the countryside, passing on culture through laughter and building happy memories along the way.

一整天的課程，孩子不僅收穫滿滿的知識與生活體驗，更學會尊敬長輩、珍惜食物、關懷土地。讓學習走出教室，走進人群與農村，在歡笑中傳承文化，在體驗中累積成長的美好回憶！
