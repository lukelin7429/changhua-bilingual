Torches and Taiko at the Hakka Roots Festival

三山國王客底文化節：火把遶境・太鼓熱舞
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Santiao's students, teachers and parents took part in the 2026 Changhua Three Mountain Kings Hakka Roots Festival. On the warm evening of October 24, everyone carried electric torches and joined the torchlight parade through the neighborhood. Points of light sparkled across the night sky — passing on the area's Hakka-rooted culture and drawing the school and the community closer than ever.

三條國小師生熱情參與「2026彰化縣三山國王客底文化節」盛會！10月24日溫馨夜晚，全體師生家長齊心手持電子火把參與「火把遶境」文化踩街，點點燈火光閃耀夜空，不只傳承在地客底文化，更緊密凝聚了學校與社區的濃厚情感。

Drums for the harvest, dances of the Hakka village. At the festival's opening ceremony on October 25, Santiao students took the main stage. Their powerful taiko drum performance shook the whole venue, and the energetic dance that followed was bursting with confidence. Neighbors and guests were amazed and answered with loud applause and cheers.

鼓鳴慶豐收，舞動客庄情。10月25日的文化節開幕典禮上，三條學子們震撼登上大舞台！孩子們帶來氣勢磅礡的太鼓表演，響亮鼓聲震天、撼動全場；隨後登場的熱舞表演展現出無限朝氣與自信，活力四射的舞姿讓現場鄉親與貴賓都驚豔不已，紛紛報以掌聲與滿堂喝采！

Thank you to all our students and teachers for their hard work and many rehearsals, and to our neighbors for their warm support.

感謝全體師生的用心付出與練團辛勞，也謝謝鄉親們最熱烈的支持。

Rooted in our hometown, learning in many ways — helping every child move with confidence and shine.
「持續扎根在地、多元學習，陪伴孩子們自信躍動、閃耀光芒！」

Santiao Elementary in Changhua welcomes you!

彰化縣三條國小歡迎您！
