Building a Kind, Safe Campus Together

大手牽小手，共築友善安全校園
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

A new semester has begun, and the whole Santiao teaching team is working to make the campus a place where every child feels safe, warm and respected. Across a week of talks, the goal was simple: help every child learn to protect themselves and respect others.

新學期開始，三條國小全體師長齊心為孩子營造安心、溫馨且尊重的學習環境！透過多元議題宣導，深化孩子的自我保護與尊重意識。

🚫 Saying no to new drugs. The principal warned the children about a new danger — "zombie vape oil" (etomidate) — and taught an easy rule to remember: don't rely on it, don't try it, don't hide it, and do ask for help. If an unmarked vape or a tempting offer comes your way, refuse it.

🚫 拒絕新興毒品：校長特別提醒孩子們遠離新興毒品「喪屍煙油（依托咪酯）」的危害！牢記具體行動「三不一要」——不依托、不嘗試、不隱瞞、要求助，堅決對不明電子煙及社交誘惑說不。

🛡️ Standing up to bullying. "Use words to warm others, and hearts come closer." Don't forward mean messages, don't call names, and listen with an open heart. If a bully hurts you or a friend, speak up — the national anti-bullying helpline is 1953.

🛡️ 落實友善反霸凌：引導孩子們「用語言溫暖他人，拉近心的距離」、「不轉傳、不謾罵、傾聽同理他人」；遇到霸凌或困難，勇敢發聲求助（反霸凌專線 1953）。

🌸 My body belongs to me. "I am the owner of my body." Children learned that every touch needs permission, and that respecting each other's boundaries keeps everyone safe — online as well as off. The talk also covered digital gender-based violence, and how to stay clear of traps on the internet.

🌸 性平與身體自主：「我是身體的主人」，讓孩子懂得尊重彼此的界線，任何碰觸都需獲得同意；同時建立數位性別暴力防制觀念，謹守防護原則，遠離網路誘惑與傷害。

💪 Looking after body and mind. With the "mood thermometer" and the five SMILE steps — sleep well, move often, talk more, care for others, eat healthily — children practiced noticing and naming each emotion, so they can grow up happy inside as well as out.

💪 身心照顧與關懷：善用「情緒溫度計」與「微笑 SMILE 5 步驟」（睡得好、常動動、多交流、常關心、健康吃），學習覺察與調適情緒，身心健康快樂成長。

🚴 Safe on the road. One last reminder for children and parents alike: in traffic, cars give way to people. When you turn on a bicycle, stay alert and check behind you first. Leave home safely, come home happily.

🚴 交通安全相伴：提醒孩子與家長落實「車輛停讓行人」，騎乘單車轉彎更要提高警覺、留意後方來車，平平安安出門，快快樂樂回家。
