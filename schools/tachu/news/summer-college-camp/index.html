A Joyful Summer

暑期大專生營隊｜歡樂一夏
⌄

After five joyful weeks, Tachu's Summer College Mentor Camp has come to a happy close!

Lessons & Laughter

Throughout the camp, students explored a wide range of engaging lessons, team-building games, and sports activities, all guided by college student mentors. Laughter filled the classrooms as the children built wonderful summer memories together.

One Last Surprise

On the final day, everyone looked forward to the lucky draw — cheering to see who would take home a prize! Amid laughter and surprises, the five-week camp came to its happy conclusion.

A heartfelt thank-you to every college mentor for the care and creativity you brought to this summer.

Thank you for giving Tachu's children a summer full of joy and growth!

中文原文 · Chinese

暑期大專生營隊｜歡樂一夏 · 2026.08.10

為期五週的暑期大專生營隊歡樂落幕啦！

這段時間，孩子們在大專生老師的帶領下，參與多元有趣的課程、團康遊戲及體育活動，在歡笑中留下許多美好的暑假回憶。✨

營隊最後一天還有大家期待的抽獎活動，看看幸運兒是誰！在一片歡笑與驚喜中，為五週的營隊畫下開心的句點。

感謝每一位大專生老師的用心規劃與陪伴，謝謝你們為孩子們帶來一個充實又歡樂的暑假！
