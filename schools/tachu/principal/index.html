I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話
黃黃馭寰 校長

Welcome to Tachu Elementary School. Since 2020, it has been my honor to lead a school with more than a hundred years of history — a school that has shaped generations of children here at the foot of Bagua Mountain.

歡迎來到大竹國小。自民國 109 年（2020）起，我有幸帶領這所擁有百年歷史的學校——在八卦山下，它形塑了一代又一代孩子的成長。

A century-old school carries something precious: roots. Tachu began in 1916 and went on to open the branch campuses that became several of the schools around us today. That history reminds us that a small school can give rise to big things — and that every child who walks through our gate is part of a story far larger than a single year.

百年老校，最珍貴的是「根」。大竹始於 1916 年，後來設立的分校，長成了今日我們身邊的好幾所學校。這段歷史提醒我們：一所小學，也能孕育出大格局；每一個走進校門的孩子，都是一個遠比一個學年更長的故事的一部分。

At Tachu we believe learning should reach beyond the textbook. Our students plant and harvest rice in the school garden, care for the environment, read widely, and perform with pride in the flower-drum baton team. And through our partnership with My Culture Connect, English is becoming part of everyday school life — not just a subject, but a living language our children hear, speak, and enjoy.

在大竹，我們相信學習應該超越課本。孩子們在校園菜園裡種稻、割稻，愛護環境、廣泛閱讀，也在花鼓棒隊裡自信演出。透過與人師教育協會的合作，英語正逐漸成為校園日常的一部分——不只是一門科目，而是孩子們聽得到、說得出、也樂在其中的活語言。

My hope is simple: that every Tachu child grows up kind, curious, and confident — and proud to belong to this hundred-year school.

我的期盼很單純：願每一位大竹的孩子，都能善良、好奇、有自信地長大，也以身為這所百年學校的一員為榮。

"From a hundred-year root, new bamboo grows tall."
百年老根，新竹節節高。 — 與大竹全體師生共勉
