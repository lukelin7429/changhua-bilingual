I.

Watch & Learn

觀看影片，一起學英文！

One word. One video. Two ways to use it.

每次一個字，每支影片都在大竹校園實地拍攝。每個字都附兩句例句，馬上學會怎麼用！

II.

Art & Making

美術與手作

禪繞畫、藍曬、色鉛筆——大竹的美術課充滿創意。

WORD 001
Zentanglen.
禪繞畫
A Zentangle is a small drawing made of simple, repeated patterns.
Zentangle（禪繞畫）是用簡單重複的圖案畫成的小幅畫作。
Tachu students relax by drawing a Zentangle in art class.
大竹的學生在美術課畫禪繞畫來放鬆心情。
WORD 002
cyanotypen.
藍曬
A cyanotype is a picture made by sunlight turning special paper blue.
Cyanotype（藍曬）是利用陽光讓特殊紙張變藍所做成的圖畫。
Tachu students place leaves on paper to make a cyanotype outdoors.
大竹的學生把葉子放在紙上，在戶外製作藍曬作品。
WORD 003
colored penciln.
色鉛筆
A colored pencil is a pencil that draws in color instead of gray.
Colored pencil（色鉛筆）是畫出彩色線條而非灰色的鉛筆。
Tachu students use colored pencils to finish their Zentangle drawings.
大竹的學生用色鉛筆完成他們的禪繞畫作品。
WORD 004
coastern.
杯墊
A coaster is a small mat you put under a cup to protect the table.
Coaster（杯墊）是放在杯子下面、用來保護桌面的小墊子。
Tachu students weave their own coaster out of bamboo strips in art class.
大竹的學生在美術課用竹片編織自己的杯墊。
WORD 005
shark hatn.
鯊魚帽
A shark hat is a hat shaped like a shark that you can wear for fun.
Shark hat（鯊魚帽）是造型像鯊魚、可以戴著玩的帽子。
A Tachu student made a shark hat out of paper in art class.
大竹有位學生在美術課用紙做了一頂鯊魚帽。
III.

Sports & PE

運動與體育

球場上、操場邊，大竹的孩子用英文說運動。

WORD 006
sports equipmentn.
體育器材
Sports equipment is the gear you use to play a game or exercise.
Sports equipment（體育器材）是用來運動或比賽的器具。
Tachu students line up to borrow sports equipment before PE class.
體育課前，大竹的學生會排隊借體育器材。
WORD 007
shootv.
投籃
To shoot means to throw a ball toward the basket, trying to score.
Shoot（投籃）是指把球投向籃框，試著得分。
Tachu students practice how to shoot on the basketball court at recess.
大竹的學生下課時在籃球場練習投籃。
WORD 008
ping pongn.
桌球
Ping pong is a game where two players hit a small ball across a table.
Ping pong（桌球）是兩人隔著球桌互相擊球的運動。
Tachu students play ping pong in the activity center after lunch.
大竹的學生午餐後會在活動中心打桌球。
IV.

School Life

校園生活

校外教學、行動圖書館、慶祝活動——大竹的日常就是最好的教材。

WORD 009
field tripn.
校外教學
A field trip is a class visit to a place outside school to learn something new.
Field trip（校外教學）是班級到校外某個地方學習新知識。
Tachu students visit a temple on their field trip about local history.
大竹的學生在認識在地歷史的校外教學中參觀廟宇。
WORD 010
mobile libraryn.
行動圖書館
A mobile library is a library that travels on wheels to bring books to people.
Mobile library（行動圖書館）是會移動的圖書館，把書帶到大家面前。
The mobile library visits Tachu once a month with new books to borrow.
行動圖書館每個月都會到大竹一次，帶來新書供借閱。
WORD 011
newspapern.
報紙
A newspaper is printed sheets of paper with news and stories.
Newspaper（報紙）是印著新聞與報導的紙張。
Tachu students read the newspaper together during morning reading time.
大竹的學生在晨讀時間一起閱讀報紙。
WORD 012
celebratev.
慶祝
To celebrate means to do something special and happy for an important event.
Celebrate（慶祝）是指為重要的日子做一些特別開心的事。
Tachu students celebrate the school's 100th anniversary with a big banner.
大竹的學生用大布條慶祝學校創校百年。
V.

Culture & Traditions

文化與傳統

書法、花鼓棒隊——大竹的傳統藝文也是英文課。

WORD 013
calligraphyn.
書法
Calligraphy is the art of writing characters beautifully with a brush.
Calligraphy（書法）是用毛筆把字寫得美觀的藝術。
Tachu's Calligraphy Club meets every week to practice calligraphy.
大竹的書法社每週聚會練習書法。
WORD 014
flower-drum baton teamn.
花鼓棒隊
A flower-drum baton team is a group that performs by beating drums and spinning batons together.
Flower-drum baton team（花鼓棒隊）是一起打鼓、轉彩棒表演的團隊。
Tachu's flower-drum baton team performs at every school celebration.
大竹的花鼓棒隊在每次校慶都會上場表演。
VI.

Numbers & Measure

數與量

數學課裡的英文單字。

WORD 015
square metern.
平方公尺
A square meter is a unit used to measure the size of a flat area.
Square meter（平方公尺）是用來測量平面面積大小的單位。
Tachu students calculate the square meters of the classroom in math class.
大竹的學生在數學課計算教室的平方公尺數。
