Every lesson here was filmed by Tachu teachers — in our classrooms and on our field trips. Each video is under a minute, with six words, three sentences, and a matching game.每一堂課都是大竹老師在教室和校外教學實地拍攝的。影片不到一分鐘，搭配六個單字、三個句子和一個配對小遊戲。

🦁▶
Lesson 01
Field Trip to Lukang
鹿港遊學

A day in old Lukang — a very narrow alley, Longshan Temple, an old carved window, and colorful lion heads.到古城鹿港遊學一天：好窄的小巷、龍山寺、古老的雕花窗，還有色彩繽紛的獅頭。

Lesson 02
Mother's Day Fans
母親節扇子

Cut, glue, and make a fan for Mom — then hold it up and say "I love you!"剪一剪、黏一黏，做一把扇子送給媽媽，再舉起來大聲說「我愛妳」！
