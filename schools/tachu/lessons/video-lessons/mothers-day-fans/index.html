Mother's Day Fans

母親節扇子
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

For Mother's Day, our younger students made a picture of a mom and her children, cut it out, and glued it onto a fan. Many also made a card. Then the whole class held up their fans and cards and shouted, "Happy Mother's Day, Mom! I love you!"母親節到了，低年級的小朋友做了一張媽媽和孩子的圖，剪下來黏在扇子上，很多人還做了卡片。最後全班舉起扇子和卡片，一起大聲說：「媽媽，母親節快樂！我愛妳！」

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

cut/kʌt/剪

Cut along the line with your scissors.用剪刀沿著線剪。

glue/ɡluː/黏；膠水

Glue the picture onto the fan.把圖片黏到扇子上。

fan/fæn/扇子

I made a fan for my mom.我做了一把扇子送給媽媽。

card/kɑrd/卡片

My card has pink flowers on it.我的卡片上有粉紅色的花。

heart/hɑrt/愛心

There are many pink hearts on the card.卡片上有好多粉紅色的愛心。

hold up/hoʊld ʌp/舉起

Hold up your fan and smile!舉起你的扇子，笑一個！
