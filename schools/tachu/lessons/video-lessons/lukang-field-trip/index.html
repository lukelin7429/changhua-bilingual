Field Trip to Lukang

鹿港遊學
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Our fifth graders spent a day in Lukang. Lukang Elementary School welcomed us, we walked through a very narrow alley, a guide showed us around old temples, and in the afternoon we painted little lion heads.五年級到鹿港遊學一天。鹿港國小熱情歡迎我們，我們走過一條好窄的小巷，跟著導覽員參觀古老的廟宇，下午還彩繪了自己的小獅頭。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

welcome/ˈwɛlkəm/歡迎

The big screen says "Welcome" to our school.大螢幕上寫著「歡迎」我們學校。

narrow/ˈnæroʊ/窄的

The alley is so narrow that we walk in a line.這條小巷好窄，我們要排成一列走。

temple/ˈtɛmpəl/廟

Lukang Longshan Temple is old and beautiful.鹿港龍山寺古老又美麗。

window/ˈwɪndoʊ/窗戶

This old window has beautiful carvings.這扇古老的窗戶有漂亮的雕刻。

lion/ˈlaɪən/獅子

This lion head has big eyes and a big mouth.這顆獅頭有大大的眼睛和大大的嘴巴。

paint/peɪnt/彩繪；上色

We paint a little lion head with a tiny brush.我們用細細的畫筆彩繪小獅頭。
