June 2023

Three Students Score Perfect 45 — First Time in 20 Years
睽違 20 年！北斗國中 3 名學生獲國中教育會考滿級分
For the first time in two decades, three students from Beidou JHS — Tseng Yu-cheng, Yang Cheng-hung and Chang Yuan-chun — achieved a perfect score of 45 on the national junior high assessment, each with a Grade 5 in writing. They credited consistent effort, less screen time, and working through every question they got wrong.睽違 20 年，曾宥誠、楊丞閎、張蒝畯三名學生在國中教育會考中獲得 45 分滿級分，寫作測驗均為 5 級分。三人共同表示，減少使用 3C 產品、持之以恆地刷題、徹底理解每一道不會的題目，是成功的關鍵。
🎓 Academic Honor
