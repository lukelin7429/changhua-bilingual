Dom Jones Visits Beidou JHS

Dom Jones 到訪北斗國中 · 校園新聞採訪
September 15, 2026 · 115/09/15
🎬 Campus news video校園新聞影片

Dom Jones of My Culture Connect visited Beidou Junior High's campus to film a video news segment, as part of her school assembly tour across Changhua County. Beidou JHS is the home base for My Culture Connect's work in the county, which made it a fitting stop at the start of the new school year.

My Culture Connect（人師教育協會）的 Dom Jones 到訪北斗國中，為她在彰化縣各校的晨會巡迴錄製校園新聞影片。北斗國中是人師教育協會在彰化推動工作的基地，新學年由這裡出發，格外有意義。

During the same visit, Dom also sat down with Principal Wu Bing-lien for an episode of her interview series Voices in Education — on why English matters, and why the future is the age of space.

同一次到訪，Dom 也與吳炳連校長進行了《教育之聲 Voices in Education》訪談，聊英語為什麼重要，以及為什麼未來是太空的時代。
