About This Model關於這個模型

Principal Wu Bing-lien built this miniature of Beidou Junior High with the help of Claude, an AI assistant. The ground and rooftops are real satellite photos from Google Earth, so you can watch twenty years of change on campus, from the earliest color image to today.

這個北斗國中微縮模型由吳炳連校長運用 AI 助理 Claude 製作。地面與屋頂貼的是 Google Earth 的真實衛星影像，可以看到校園二十年來的變化，從最早的彩色影像一路到今天。

Shrine Road and Mazu神社路與媽祖遶境

The road along the east side of campus is called Shrine Road (神社路). During the Japanese colonial era it was the approach to Beidou Shrine, so the model marks it with a torii gate. The Mazu procession of Beidou Dian'an Temple (奠安宮) turns onto this road from the street in front of the school.

校園東側的神社路，是日治時期北斗神社的參道，模型以鳥居示意。北斗奠安宮的媽祖遶境隊伍，會從校門前的道路轉進神社路。

How It Was Made製作方法

Each year's Google Earth Pro image (664 m across, about 0.2 m per pixel) was cropped to the 292 × 326 m model area and laid over the ground and rooftops. Building outlines were measured from the 2025 image, and heights were estimated from the number of floors. The assembly stage and red-clay track follow a December 2025 aerial panorama. People, cars and the procession are illustrations, and time runs fast.

每年的 Google Earth Pro 正射影像（擷取範圍 664 m、約 0.2 m／像素）裁成 292 × 326 m 的模型範圍，貼在地面與屋頂上。建物輪廓量自 2025 年影像，高度依樓層推估；司令台造型與紅土跑道參考 2025 年 12 月空拍環景照。人、車與遶境隊伍為示意，時間加速。

A Campus About to Change即將改變的校園

On November 3, 2026, the school broke ground on rebuilding its aging classroom buildings, Hongdao Hall among them. This model keeps a record of the campus as it looked before the rebuild.

北斗國中在 2026 年 11 月 3 日為老舊校舍拆除重建工程動土，弘道樓即是其中之一。這個模型也為重建前的校園留下紀錄。
