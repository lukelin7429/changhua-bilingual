Lesson 03 · Drone English · Science Program
How Does It Fly?
無人機怎麼飛 · 北斗國中無人機社團
I.

Today's Goals

學習目標 · 今天要學會三件事
🆙
Explain
說明
Explain how lift makes a drone go up.說明「升力」如何讓無人機上升。
🧭
Direction
方向
Use up, down, forward, back, left, and right.用上下、前後、左右描述方向。
⚖️
Balance
平衡
Talk about staying steady and balanced.談無人機如何保持平穩、平衡。
II.

Essential Vocabulary

必備 8 單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
⬆️
Lift
/lɪft/ · n.
升力
The force that pushes the drone up.
"Fast propellers make more lift."
🌬️
Air
/er/ · n.
空氣
What the propellers push to fly.
"Propellers push the air down."
🔄
Spin
/spɪn/ · v.
旋轉
To turn around quickly.
"The propellers spin very fast."
⚖️
Balance
/ˈbæl.əns/ · n.
平衡
Staying steady and level.
"Sensors help the drone keep its balance."
↗️
Forward
/ˈfɔːr.wərd/ · adv.
向前
Toward the front.
"Push the stick to fly forward."
↘️
Backward
/ˈbæk.wərd/ · adv.
向後
Toward the back.
"Pull the stick to fly backward."
🔃
Turn
/tɜːrn/ · v.
轉向
To change direction.
"The drone can turn left or right."
🎚️
Stick
/stɪk/ · n.
搖桿
The small lever on the remote.
"Move the stick to control the drone."
III.

Key Sentences

重點句型 · 三個句型，套用今天的單字
Cause · 原因
When ___, the drone ___.
當 ___，無人機就 ___。
"When the propellers spin fast, the drone goes up."
"When you push the stick, it flies forward."
Direction · 方向
It can go ___.
它可以往 ___。
"It can go up and down."
"It can go left, right, forward, and back."
Ability · 能力
It can ___ in the air.
它可以在空中 ___。
"It can stay steady in the air."
"It can turn around in the air."
IV.

Role Play

角色對話 · 跟著開口說
🗣️
Why Does It Go Up?
A & B  ·  點每句 🔊 跟讀，或按 ▶ 整段播放
A
How does a drone go up into the air?
無人機怎麼飛上去的？
B
The propellers spin and push the air down.
螺旋槳旋轉，把空氣往下推。
A
So pushing air down makes it go up?
所以把空氣往下推，它就上升？
B
Yes! That force is called lift.
對！那個力叫做「升力」。
A
How do you make it go forward?
那怎麼讓它往前飛？
B
Push the stick forward on the remote.
把遙控器上的搖桿往前推。
A
And how does it stay so steady?
它怎麼可以這麼穩？
B
The sensors help it keep its balance.
感應器幫它保持平衡。
A
That's so smart!
太聰明了！
