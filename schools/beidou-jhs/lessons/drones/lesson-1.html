Section 1

Vocabulary

單字與例句 · Words 01–08 · 點 🔊 聽發音
Word 01
drone (n.)
無人機
A drone is a small flying machine with no pilot inside.
無人機是一種裡面沒有駕駛員的小型飛行器。
Our club has two drones for students to practice.
我們社團有兩台無人機給學生練習。
Word 02
propeller (n.)
螺旋槳
A drone has four propellers that spin very fast.
無人機有四個轉得很快的螺旋槳。
Never touch the propellers when the drone is on.
無人機開著的時候，絕對不要碰螺旋槳。
Word 03
remote control (n.)
遙控器
We use a remote control to fly the drone.
我們用遙控器來操控無人機。
Hold the remote control with both hands.
用雙手拿好遙控器。
Word 04
camera (n.)
相機／鏡頭
The camera is under the drone. It can take photos from the sky.
相機在無人機下面，可以從空中拍照。
This drone's camera can also make videos.
這台無人機的相機也可以錄影。
Word 05
battery (n.)
電池
The drone can fly for about 20 minutes on one battery.
一顆電池可以讓無人機飛大約 20 分鐘。
Please charge the battery before class.
上課前請先把電池充電。
Word 06
fly (v.)
飛行
Can you fly the drone in a straight line?
你能讓無人機直線飛行嗎？
We can only fly here when the wind is light.
只有風小的時候我們才能在這裡飛。
Word 07
take off (v.)
起飛
Press this button and the drone will take off slowly.
按這個按鈕，無人機就會慢慢起飛。
Make sure the area is clear before you take off.
起飛前先確認周圍淨空。
Word 08
land (v.)
降落
When the battery is low, land the drone right away.
電池快沒電時，馬上讓無人機降落。
Try to land the drone gently on the mat.
試著讓無人機輕輕降落在墊子上。
Section 2

Role Play

角色對話 · 兩位同學第一次看到社團的無人機
🗣️

At the Drone Club

A = Amy   B = Ben  ·  點每句 🔊 跟讀，或按 ▶ 整段播放

A
Wow, look at this drone! It's so cool.
哇，你看這台無人機！好酷喔。
B
Yes! It has four propellers. Can it really fly?
對啊！它有四個螺旋槳。它真的能飛嗎？
A
Of course. We use the remote control to fly it.
當然。我們用遙控器來操控它。
B
Where is the camera?
相機在哪裡？
A
It's under the drone. It can take photos from the sky.
在無人機下面，它可以從空中拍照。
B
Cool! How long can it fly?
太酷了！它可以飛多久？
A
About 20 minutes. Then we change the battery.
大約 20 分鐘，然後我們要換電池。
B
Let's try it! How do we start?
我們來試試看！要怎麼開始？
A
First we take off slowly. Then we land it carefully. Stay safe and have fun!
先慢慢起飛，再小心降落。注意安全，玩得開心！
