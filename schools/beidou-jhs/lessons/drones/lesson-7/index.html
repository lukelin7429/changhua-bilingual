Lesson 07 · Drone English · Science Program
Drone Safety Rules
飛行安全規則 · 北斗國中無人機社團
I.

Today's Goals

學習目標 · 今天要學會三件事
📜
Rules
規則
Know where you can and can't fly.知道哪裡可以飛、哪裡不行。
🙅
Refuse
婉拒
Say "please don't" and "stop" politely.禮貌地說「請不要」和「停」。
🔒
Privacy
隱私
Respect other people's privacy.尊重別人的隱私。
II.

Essential Vocabulary

✅

OK to do.

🚫
Forbidden
/fərˈbɪd.ən/ · adj.
禁止的
Not allowed at all.
"Flying near the airport is forbidden."
🧍
People

III.

Key Sentences

重點句型 · 三個句型，套用今天的單字
Permission · 許可
You can / can't fly ___.
你可以／不可以在 ___ 飛。
"You can fly in the open field."
"You can't fly near the airport."
Rule · 規則
Don't ___.
不要 ___。
"Don't fly over people."
"Don't fly in bad weather."
Polite · 禮貌
Please ___.
請 ___。
"Please don't fly here."
"Please land your drone."
IV.

Role Play

角色對話 · 跟著開口說
🗣️
Is It Safe to Fly Here?
A & B  ·  點每句 🔊 跟讀，或按 ▶ 整段播放
A
Can we fly the drone here?
我們可以在這裡飛無人機嗎？
B
Let's check the rules first.
先看一下規則。
A
There are a lot of people around.
附近有很多人。
B
Right. Don't fly over people.
對。不要飛過人群。
A
What about the weather?
天氣呢？
B
It's too windy today.
今天風太大了。
A
So we shouldn't fly now?
所以現在不該飛？
B
Right. And remember people's privacy.
對。也要尊重別人的隱私。
A
OK. Let's find a safe, open field.
好。我們去找一個安全、空曠的場地。

準備好驗收今天的學習成果了嗎？
