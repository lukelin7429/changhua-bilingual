Lesson 08 · Drone English · Science Program
Drones That Help People
無人機的應用 · 北斗國中無人機社團
I.

Today's Goals

學習目標 · 今天要學會三件事
🌍
Jobs
應用
Name real jobs that drones do.說出無人機實際的工作。
🔗
Connect
連結
Match a drone to how it helps.把無人機和它的幫助配對。
💬
Opinion
看法
Say which job you think is most useful.說出你覺得哪個用途最有用。
II.

Essential Vocabulary

必備 8 單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
🌾
Farm
/fɑːrm/ · n.
農場
Land where people grow food.
"Drones help farmers check their fields."
📦
Deliver
/dɪˈlɪv.ər/ · v.
送貨
To bring something to someone.
"Some drones deliver packages."
🚑
Rescue
/ˈres.kjuː/ · v.
救援
To save someone in danger.
"Drones can help rescue lost hikers."
🗺️
Map
/mæp/ · v.
測繪
To make a map of a place.
"Drones can map a whole mountain."
🔥
Fire
/ˈfaɪər/ · n.
火災
Something burning out of control.
"Drones watch forest fires from above."
🌊
Flood
/flʌd/ · n.
水災
Too much water on the land.
"Drones check towns after a flood."
🩺
Medicine
/ˈmed.ɪ.sɪn/ · n.
藥品
Things that help sick people.
"Drones can carry medicine to villages."
🔍
Find
/faɪnd/ · v.
尋找
To look for and discover.
"Drones help find missing people."
III.

Key Sentences

重點句型 · 三個句型，套用今天的單字
Use · 用途
Drones can ___.
無人機可以 ___。
"Drones can deliver packages."
"Drones can map a mountain."
Help · 幫助
A drone helps ___ ___.
無人機幫助 ___ ___。
"A drone helps farmers check fields."
"A drone helps doctors send medicine."
Opinion · 看法
I think ___ is the most useful.
我覺得 ___ 最有用。
"I think rescue is the most useful."
"I think delivery is the most useful."
IV.

Role Play

角色對話 · 跟著開口說
🗣️
Drones at Work
A & B  ·  點每句 🔊 跟讀，或按 ▶ 整段播放
A
Drones aren't just toys, right?
無人機不只是玩具，對吧？
B
Right! They do real jobs.
對！它們會做真正的工作。
A
Like what?
例如？
B
Farmers use them to check big fields.
農夫用它們檢查大片農田。
A
That's helpful. Anything else?
很有用。還有呢？
B
Drones can deliver medicine to faraway villages.
無人機可以送藥到偏遠的村子。
A
Wow. Can they help in emergencies?
哇。它們能幫忙緊急狀況嗎？
B
Yes, they help rescue people after a flood.
可以，水災後幫忙救援。
A
I think rescue is the most useful job.
我覺得救援是最有用的工作。
