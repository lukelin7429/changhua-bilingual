Lesson 06 · Drone English · Science Program
Flying with a Camera
空拍攝影 · 北斗國中無人機社團
I.

Today's Goals

學習目標 · 今天要學會三件事
📷
Capture
拍攝
Take photos and videos from the sky.從空中拍照片和影片。
🖼️
Frame
構圖
Talk about angle, zoom, and the frame.談角度、拉近與畫面構圖。
🗣️
Direct
引導
Tell a partner where to point the camera.告訴夥伴鏡頭要對準哪裡。
II.

Essential Vocabulary

必備 8 單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
📷
Photo
/ˈfoʊ.toʊ/ · n.
照片
A picture from the camera.
"Let's take a photo of the field."
🎥
Video
/ˈvɪd.i.oʊ/ · n.
影片
A moving picture.
"Record a short video."
🔴
Record
/rɪˈkɔːrd/ · v.
錄影
To make a video.
"Press here to record."
🔎
Zoom
/zuːm/ · v.
拉近
To make something look closer.
"Zoom in on the building."
📐
Angle
/ˈæŋ.ɡəl/ · n.
角度
The direction the camera points.
"Try a higher angle."
🖼️
Frame
/freɪm/ · n.
畫面
Everything you can see in the photo.
"Keep the tree in the frame."
☀️
Bright
/braɪt/ · adj.
明亮的
With a lot of light.
"The photo is bright and clear."
🌫️
Blurry
/ˈblɜːr.i/ · adj.
模糊的
Not clear.
"Hold it steady or the photo is blurry."
III.

Key Sentences

重點句型 · 三個句型，套用今天的單字
Action · 動作
Let's ___ the ___.
我們來 ___ ___。
"Let's take a photo of the field."
"Let's record a video of the school."
Camera · 鏡頭
___ in / out on the ___.
把鏡頭 ___ ___。
"Zoom in on the flag."
"Zoom out to see everything."
Quality · 品質
The photo is ___.
這張照片很 ___。
"The photo is bright and clear."
"The photo is a little blurry."
IV.

Role Play

角色對話 · 跟著開口說
🗣️
Filming from the Sky
A & B  ·  點每句 🔊 跟讀，或按 ▶ 整段播放
A
Let's take photos from the sky!
我們來空拍照片！
B
Cool! What should we shoot?
好酷！要拍什麼？
A
Let's record a video of the playground.
我們錄操場的影片。
B
Should I zoom in?
要拉近嗎？
A
Yes, zoom in on the basketball court.
對，把籃球場拉近。
B
The frame looks great now.
現在畫面看起來很棒。
A
Hold it steady, or the video will be blurry.
拿穩一點，不然影片會模糊。
B
OK. Now I'm recording.
好，我開始錄了。
A
Perfect. The light is nice and bright.
完美。光線又亮又好。
