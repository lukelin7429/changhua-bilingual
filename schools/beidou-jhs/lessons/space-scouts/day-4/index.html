Day 4 · Part 1

Today We Will Say

今天的句型 · In space, you can't just… You have to…
🚿
For Example: a Shower
First the Earth way, then the space way.
先說地球上的做法，再說太空中的做法。
EarthIn space, you can't just shower.
在太空中，不能直接淋浴。
SpaceYou have to use a wet towel.
你必須用濕毛巾。
How It Works · 怎麼用
  • In space, you can't just + the Earth way
    「在太空中，不能直接……」＋我們在地球上習慣的做法
  • You have to + the space way
    「你必須……」＋太空中真正的做法
💡 can't just = 不能「直接、就這樣」做；have to = 一定要、必須。
Say can't clearly, and finish the t!

Day 4 · Part 3

Life on the Space Station

太空站生活 · 五件事，先猜猜看再按「How?」
Life in Space
Astronauts live and work here. It is about 400 km above Earth. Everything floats!太空人在這裡生活和工作。它在地球上空大約 400 公里。所有東西都會飄起來！
Space Station · Job 1

Wash Your Hair洗頭

How do astronauts wash their hair?
太空人怎麼洗頭？
In space, you can't just use water.
You have to use a wet towel.
在太空中，不能直接用水洗。你必須用濕毛巾。
1 · First
First, put on special shampoo.
首先，抹上特殊的洗髮精。
2 · Next
Next, rub your hair.
接著，搓揉頭髮。
3 · Finally
Finally, dry it with a towel.
最後，用毛巾擦乾。
🔭 Astronauts use no-rinse shampoo, so no water floats away.太空人用免沖洗的洗髮精，水才不會到處亂飄。
Space Station · Job 2

Eat Rice吃飯

Can you eat rice from a bowl in space?
在太空中可以用碗吃飯嗎？
In space, you can't just use a bowl.
You have to eat from a bag.
在太空中，不能直接用碗。你必須從袋子裡吃。
1 · First
First, add hot water to the bag.
首先，把熱水加進袋子裡。
2 · Next
Next, wait a few minutes.
接著，等幾分鐘。
3 · Finally
Finally, open it and eat with a spoon.
最後，打開袋子，用湯匙吃。
🔭 Rice would float out of a bowl! Space food is dried and packed in bags.米飯會從碗裡飄出來！太空食物是乾燥後裝在袋子裡的。
Space Station · Job 3

Drink Water喝水

Can you drink from a cup in space?
在太空中可以用杯子喝水嗎？
In space, you can't just use a cup.
You have to use a drink bag.
在太空中，不能直接用杯子。你必須用飲水袋。
1 · First
First, fill the bag with water.
首先，把袋子裝滿水。
2 · Next
Next, open the straw.
接著，打開吸管。
3 · Finally
Finally, drink slowly.
最後，慢慢喝。
🔭 In space, water floats out of a cup in wobbly balls.在太空中，水會從杯子裡飄出來，變成一顆顆晃動的水球。
Space Station · Job 4

Brush Your Teeth刷牙

Where does the toothpaste go?
牙膏要吐到哪裡？
In space, you can't just spit in the sink.
You have to swallow it.
在太空中，不能直接吐在洗手台。你必須把它吞下去。
1 · First
First, wet your toothbrush.
首先，把牙刷沾濕。
2 · Next
Next, brush your teeth.
接著，刷牙。
3 · Finally
Finally, swallow or spit into a towel.
最後，把牙膏吞下去，或吐在毛巾上。
🔭 There is no sink with running water on the space station.太空站上沒有會流水的洗手台。
Space Station · Job 5

Go to Sleep睡覺

Do astronauts sleep in a bed?
太空人睡在床上嗎？
In space, you can't just lie in bed.
You have to sleep in a bag.
在太空中，不能直接躺在床上。你必須睡在睡袋裡。
1 · First
First, find your bag on the wall.
首先，找到掛在牆上的睡袋。
2 · Next
Next, get inside.
接著，鑽進去。
3 · Finally
Finally, close your eyes!
最後，閉上眼睛！
🔭 The sleeping bags are tied to the wall, so astronauts don't float around at night.睡袋綁在牆上，太空人晚上才不會飄來飄去。
Deeper Dive · Challenge
Because and So
進階挑戰 · 用 because 和 so 說出原因和結果
Water floats, so astronauts use a towel.水會飄起來，所以太空人用毛巾。
Astronauts sleep in bags because they float.太空人睡在睡袋裡，因為他們會飄起來。
so + the result · 所以（後面接結果）because + the reason · 因為（後面接原因）
Try it: How do you sneeze in space?試試看：在太空中要怎麼打噴嚏？
You have to use a tissue, because a sneeze would float around!你必須用面紙，因為打噴嚏的飛沫會到處飄！First, take a tissue. Next, sneeze into it. Finally, throw it away.
首先，拿一張面紙。接著，對著面紙打噴嚏。最後，把它丟掉。

2Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出正確答案，點 🔊 可以聽題目
Q1.In space, you can't just use a cup. You have to use a drink ______.
在太空中，不能直接用杯子。你必須用飲水______。
bag 袋子 ✓ — You have to use a drink bag. 水會飄起來，所以要用有吸管的飲水袋。
Q2.How do astronauts wash their hair? They use a wet ______.
太空人怎麼洗頭？他們用濕的______。
towel 毛巾 ✓ — In space, you can't just use water. You have to use a wet towel. 太空人用濕毛巾和免沖洗洗髮精。
Q3.First, add hot water. ______, wait three minutes. Finally, eat!
首先，加入熱水。______，等三分鐘。最後，開動！
Next 接著 ✓ — First → Next → Finally. 順序是：首先 → 接著 → 最後。
Q4.Astronauts sleep in bags ______ they float.
太空人睡在睡袋裡，______他們會飄起來。
because 因為 ✓ — because + the reason. because 後面接原因：他們會飄起來。
Q5.In space, can you drop a pencil on the floor?
在太空中，鉛筆會掉到地板上嗎？
No, it floats away! 不會，它會飄走！✓ — In space, everything floats. 在太空中，所有東西都會飄起來。
