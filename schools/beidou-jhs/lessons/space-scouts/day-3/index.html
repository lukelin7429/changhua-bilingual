Day 3 · Part 1

Today We Will Say

今天的句型 · You might think… But actually…
☀️
For Example: the Sun
First the myth, then the fact.
先說迷思，再說事實。
MythYou might think the Sun is yellow.
你可能以為太陽是黃色的。
FactBut actually, the Sun is white.
但其實，太陽是白色的。
How It Works · 怎麼用
  • You might think + the myth
    「你可能以為……」＋大家常有的錯誤想法
  • But actually, + the fact
    「但其實……」＋正確的事實
💡 Make a small pause at the comma after actually. Say actually as AK-chu-lee, and finish the t in might.
actually 後面有逗號，要停一下；actually 念成 AK-chu-lee，might 結尾的 t 要念出來。
Warm-up · Last week's pattern 複習上週句型
This is Earth. It's our home planet. It is blue and green.這是地球。它是我們的家園。它是藍色和綠色的。

Day 3 · Part 3

Seven Space Myths

七個太空迷思 · 先投票猜猜看，再看答案
In class we voted with thumbs up or thumbs down. Here, tap your guess to see the answer!
課堂上我們用比讚、倒讚投票；這裡點一下你的答案，就會揭曉！
Myth 1Space · 太空
Can you hear sounds in space?
在太空中聽得到聲音嗎？ 先猜猜看！
You might think space is noisy.
But actually, space is silent.
你可能以為太空很吵。但其實，太空一片寂靜。
Why? Sound needs air. Space has no air.
為什麼？聲音需要空氣才能傳播，而太空沒有空氣。
🔭 So how do astronauts talk? By radio!那太空人怎麼說話？靠無線電！
Myth 2Space Object · Star 恆星
What color is the Sun?
太陽是什麼顏色？ 先猜猜看！
You might think the Sun is yellow.
But actually, the Sun is white.
你可能以為太陽是黃色的。但其實，太陽是白色的。
Why? Earth's air makes it look yellow.
為什麼？是地球的大氣讓它看起來黃黃的。
🔭 From space, astronauts see a white Sun. Never look straight at the Sun!在太空中，太空人看到的是白色的太陽。千萬不要直視太陽！
Myth 3Moon of Earth · 地球的衛星
Does the Moon make its own light?
月球會自己發光嗎？ 先猜猜看！
You might think the Moon makes light.
But actually, the Sun lights the Moon.
你可能以為月球會發光。但其實，是太陽照亮了月球。
Why? Moonlight is sunlight bouncing off rock.
為什麼？月光其實是陽光照到月球岩石後反射回來的光。
🔭 That's why the Moon seems to change shape: we see different amounts of its sunny side.所以月亮看起來會改變形狀（月相）：我們看到它被太陽照亮的部分有多有少。
Myth 4Planet · 行星
Mars is red. Is it hot?
火星是紅色的，它很熱嗎？ 先猜猜看！
You might think Mars is hot.
But actually, Mars is very cold.
你可能以為火星很熱。但其實，火星非常冷。
Why? It is far from the Sun. Its air is thin.
為什麼？它離太陽很遠，大氣又很稀薄。
🔭 Mars is red because of rust in its dust. On average it is about −60°C!火星是紅色的，是因為塵土裡有鐵鏽；它的平均溫度大約是攝氏零下 60 度！
Myth 5Planet · 行星
Which planet is the hottest?
哪一顆行星最熱？ 先猜猜看！
You might think Mercury is the hottest.
But actually, Venus is the hottest planet.
你可能以為水星最熱。但其實，金星才是最熱的行星。
Why? Thick clouds trap the heat.
為什麼？厚厚的雲層把熱鎖住了。
🔭 Venus is about 465°C — hot enough to melt lead, even though Mercury is closer to the Sun.金星大約攝氏 465 度，熱到可以把鉛熔化，雖然水星離太陽更近。
Myth 6Planet · 行星
Is Saturn the only planet with rings?
只有土星有光環嗎？ 先猜猜看！
You might think only Saturn has rings.
But actually, four planets have rings.
你可能以為只有土星有光環。但其實，有四顆行星有光環。
Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.
木星、土星、天王星和海王星。
🔭 Saturn's rings are big and bright. The other rings are thin and dark, so they are hard to see.土星的光環又大又亮；其他三顆的光環又細又暗，很難看見。
Myth 7Space Object · 其他天體
Is Pluto a planet?
冥王星是行星嗎？ 先猜猜看！
You might think Pluto is a planet.
But actually, Pluto is a dwarf planet.
你可能以為冥王星是行星。但其實，冥王星是一顆矮行星。
Why? Scientists changed its group in 2006.
為什麼？科學家在 2006 年把它改分到另一類。
🔭 Pluto was called a planet from 1930 to 2006. Science can change when we learn more!冥王星從 1930 到 2006 年都被叫做行星。當我們知道得更多，科學也會跟著改變！
Deeper Dive · Challenge
Busting a Myth
進階挑戰 · 更多破解迷思的說法
That's a common myth!那是很常見的迷思！
In fact, ________.事實上，……
Scientists know that ________.科學家知道……
Try it: Is the North Star the brightest star?試試看：北極星是最亮的星星嗎？
That's a common myth! In fact, Sirius is the brightest star in our night sky.那是很常見的迷思！事實上，天狼星才是夜空中最亮的恆星。The North Star is easy to find, but it is not very bright — only about the 48th brightest star.
北極星很好找，但其實不太亮，大約只排第 48 名。

Fix the Myth

Fix the Myth · 1 of 3

But actually, ________.

But actually, the Sun is a star!其實太陽是一顆恆星！The Sun is a star — a giant ball of hot gas.
太陽是恆星，一顆巨大的高溫氣體球。
Fix the Myth · 2 of 3
You might think stars are small.
But actually, ________.
你可能以為星星很小。
But actually, stars are very big!其實星星非常大！Stars look tiny because they are very, very far away. Our Sun is a star, and it is about 109 times wider than Earth.
星星看起來很小，是因為它們非常非常遙遠。太陽就是一顆恆星，寬度大約是地球的 109 倍。
Fix the Myth · 3 of 3

還記得第 2 堂嗎？它是一顆岩石衛星，灰灰的、布滿塵土。
