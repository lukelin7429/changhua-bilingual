The Sixteenth Hand at the Gate 第十六任校長，接下長安的教育棒子

When Principal Hsieh Bo-Yu stepped through Changan's gate in August 2025, he became the sixteenth person to lead the school since Taiwan's postwar restoration in 1945. He holds a master's degree from the Graduate Institute of Educational Administration and Policy Development at National Chiayi University, and arrived not at a city school, but at a small farming-village school in Zhutang Township, on the western plain of Changhua County.

The school's crest tells its own story: a bat that once roosted in the betel palms, wings spread wide, eyes sharp and far-seeing. Principal Hsieh's guiding belief — "Focusing entirely on the children, bringing every child to success" — echoes that same spirit: every child deserves the chance to shine.

2025 年 8 月，謝博宇校長踏入長安國小校門，成為自 1945 年台灣光復以來，這所學校的第十六任校長。他擁有國立嘉義大學教育行政與政策發展研究所碩士學位，接下的不是城市裡的大型學校，而是彰化縣竹塘鄉田野間的一所農村小學。

校徽說著自己的故事：一隻曾棲息在蒲葵樹上的蝙蝠，展翅高飛，睿眼深謀。謝校長的辦學信念——「心心念念為孩子，把每個孩子帶上來」——正呼應著同一種精神：堅信每個孩子都有發光的機會。

Six Educational Directives 六大教育方針

Six commitments guide every decision at Changan — from the classroom to the community.

Six Directives · 六大教育方針
  • 🧒
    Student-Centered Priority
    學生主體為先
    以同理與耐心啟發學習動力，培養學生的自主學習與終身學習能力。
  • 📘
    Optimized Curriculum
    優化課程架構
    根據學生背景與在地特色，發展具意義與關聯性的學習內容，使學生學會思辨。
  • 🎨
    Diversity & Creativity
    體現多元創意
    設計多元、有趣的教學活動，融合探究與跨域統整，激發創造力與問題解決力。
  • 👩‍🏫
    Teacher Professionalism
    增進教師專業
    透過持續進修與專業社群，強化教師教學力與課程領導力，成就良師典範。
  • 🤲
    Empathetic Listening
    同理傾聽理解
    用心傾聽學生與同仁的聲音，營造安心、尊重、有歸屬感的友善校園。
  • 🤝
    Partnering with Parents
    攜手家長偕行
    將家長視為教育事業的合夥人，建立信任與支持，共同打造穩定的成長環境。

A Word from Principal Hsieh 謝校長的辦學理念

Educational Philosophy · 辦學理念
Focusing Entirely on the Children 心心念念為孩子，把每個孩子帶上來

Principal Hsieh Bo-Yu firmly believes every child deserves a chance to shine. He is committed to creating a friendly, innovative, life-long learning environment by prioritizing students, fostering professional development, and promoting partnership. 謝博宇校長堅信每個孩子都有發光的機會，致力於打造一個友善、創新且能啟發終身學習的教育場域，以學生為中心，透過專業發展與夥伴互助共同實現。

Philosophy of Teaching 教學理念

Four directions, twelve concrete steps — how Principal Hsieh turns the Changan vision into daily practice.

四大方向、十二項具體作法——謝校長把長安的願景化為日常實踐的方式。

01

Broadening Students' Horizons and Enhancing Learning Motivation 擴展學生視野，提昇學習旨趣

  • Apply for international education programs to strengthen students' global competence.申請國際教育計畫，提昇學生知能。
  • Integrate digital technology and AI innovation to lead intelligent learning.投入數位科技融入，數位 AI 領航。
  • Develop physical education and training programs to build athletic foundations and promote sportsmanship.發展健體活動訓練，扎根體育競技。
02

Strengthening Teacher Professionalism and Activating Teaching Innovation 強化教師專業，促進教學活化

  • Implement professional learning communities to foster focused professional dialogue.落實教師專業社群，聚焦專業對話。
  • Encourage teachers' professional growth and expertise to establish teaching highlights.激勵教師專長學習，奠基教學亮點。
  • Introduce external professional resources to enrich curriculum innovation and instructional quality.引入外部專業資源，創發課教精華。
03

Fostering Parent–Teacher Communication and Advancing Education Together 促發親師溝通，攜手教育前行

  • Strengthen communication channels between parents and teachers to embrace collaborative input.著重親師溝通渠道，融入合夥意見。
  • Engage parent volunteers to enhance the school's collective strength and vision.引入家長志工資源，強大學校願力。
  • Partner with community and industry experts to deepen and diversify curriculum development.共構社區產業業師，昇華課程面向。
04

Integrating Community Culture and Showcasing Local Values 融入社區文化，彰顯在地價值

  • Explore and reflect upon community cultural assets to find pathways for meaningful integration.凝視社區文化內容，思考融合路徑。
  • Extend the essence of local industries to create platforms for cultural and creative expression.延伸社區產業精髓，打造文創舞台。
  • Amplify positive community engagement to build momentum and attract new students.聚集社區正向聲量，營造招生契機。
