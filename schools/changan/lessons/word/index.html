Words on Campus

長安校園拍攝的單字影片
WORD 001
sweepv.
掃
To sweep means to clean the floor with a broom.
掃是指用掃把清潔地板。
We sweep the classroom every morning to keep it tidy.
我們每天早上打掃教室，讓教室保持整潔。
WORD 002
tomaton.
蕃茄
A tomato is a round, red fruit that grows on a vine.
蕃茄是長在藤蔓上的圓形紅色果實。
We grow tomatoes in our school garden.
我們在校園菜園裡種蕃茄。
WORD 003
hairstylistn.
髮型師
A hairstylist is someone who cuts and styles hair.
髮型師是幫人剪髮、做造型的人。
The hairstylist gave her a beautiful new haircut.
髮型師幫她剪了一個漂亮的新髮型。
WORD 004
Taiko drumn.
太鼓
A Taiko drum is a large drum played with two sticks.
太鼓是用兩根鼓棒敲打的大型鼓。
Students perform with the Taiko drum at school events.
學生在學校活動中表演太鼓。
WORD 005
Christmas treen.
聖誕樹
A Christmas tree is a tree decorated with lights and ornaments.
聖誕樹是掛滿燈飾與吊飾的樹。
We decorate a Christmas tree in the classroom every December.
我們每年十二月都會在教室佈置聖誕樹。
WORD 006
martial artsn.
武術
Martial arts are skills used for self-defense and exercise.
武術是用來自我防衛與鍛鍊身體的技巧。
Our school teaches martial arts to build strength and discipline.
我們學校教武術來鍛鍊體力與紀律。
WORD 007
martial artsn.
武術
Martial arts combine movement, focus, and respect.
武術結合了動作、專注與尊重。
Students practice martial arts together every week.
學生們每週一起練習武術。
WORD 008
tabletn.
平板
A tablet is a small computer you can carry and touch.
平板是可以隨身攜帶、用手觸控的小型電腦。
We use a tablet to look up new English words.
我們用平板查詢新的英文單字。
WORD 009
sciencen.
科學
Science is the study of how the world works.
科學是研究世界如何運作的學問。
We do fun experiments in science class.
我們在科學課做有趣的實驗。
WORD 010
flun.
流感
The flu is a sickness that makes you feel tired and feverish.
流感是一種讓人疲倦、發燒的疾病。
Wash your hands often to avoid catching the flu.
常洗手可以避免感染流感。
WORD 011
math classn.
數學課
Math class is where we learn numbers and problem-solving.
數學課是我們學習數字與解題的課。
We solve tricky puzzles together in math class.
我們在數學課一起解有趣的難題。
WORD 012
runv.
跑步
To run means to move quickly using your legs.
跑步是指用雙腳快速移動。
We run laps around the track in P.E. class.
我們在體育課繞著跑道跑步。
WORD 013
ocarinan.
陶笛
An ocarina is a small wind instrument shaped like an egg.
陶笛是一種蛋形的小型吹奏樂器。
Students learn to play sweet songs on the ocarina.
學生們學習用陶笛吹奏悅耳的歌曲。
WORD 014
Mid Autumn Festivaln.
中秋節
The Mid Autumn Festival is a holiday when families gather to watch the full moon.
中秋節是家人團聚賞月的節日。
We eat mooncakes together during the Mid Autumn Festival.
中秋節我們一起吃月餅。
WORD 015
jump ropen.
跳繩
A jump rope is a rope you swing and jump over.
跳繩是用來甩動並跳過的繩子。
We jump rope together during recess.
我們下課時間一起跳繩。
WORD 016
first gradern.
新生
A first grader is a student in the very first year of elementary school.
新生是小學一年級的學生。
The first graders are excited for their first day of school.
一年級新生對開學的第一天感到興奮。
WORD 017
chocolaten.
巧克力
Chocolate is a sweet brown treat made from cacao beans.
巧克力是用可可豆製成的甜美棕色食物。
We share chocolate with our friends on special days.
我們在特別的日子和朋友分享巧克力。
WORD 018
virtual realityn.
虛擬實境
Virtual reality lets you see a make-believe world through a headset.
虛擬實境讓你透過頭戴裝置看見虛構的世界。
We tried virtual reality in our technology class.
我們在科技課體驗了虛擬實境。
WORD 019
swimming classn.
游泳課
Swimming class is where we learn to move safely in water.
游泳課是我們學習在水中安全活動的課。
We practice different strokes in swimming class.
我們在游泳課練習不同的泳姿。
WORD 020
recordern.
直笛
A recorder is a simple wind instrument played by blowing and covering holes.
直笛是靠吹氣與按孔演奏的簡單吹奏樂器。
Every student learns to play a song on the recorder.
每個學生都要學會用直笛吹一首歌。
WORD 021
badmintonn.
羽球
Badminton is a sport played by hitting a shuttlecock over a net.
羽球是隔著球網擊打羽毛球的運動。
We play badminton together after school.
我們放學後一起打羽球。
WORD 022
airn.
空氣
Air is the invisible gas all around us that we breathe.
空氣是圍繞在我們四周、供我們呼吸的無形氣體。
Clean air keeps our bodies healthy.
乾淨的空氣讓我們的身體保持健康。
WORD 023
violinn.
小提琴
A violin is a wooden instrument played with a bow.
小提琴是用弓拉奏的木製樂器。
She practices the violin every afternoon.
她每天下午練習小提琴。
WORD 024
obstaclen.
障礙物
An obstacle is something that blocks your way.
障礙物是擋住去路的東西。
We jump over obstacles during the relay race.
我們在接力賽中跳過障礙物。
WORD 025
decoratev.
裝飾
To decorate means to make something look pretty.
裝飾是指把東西弄得漂亮。
We decorate our classroom for the holidays.
我們在節日時裝飾教室。
WORD 026
Southern Fistn.
南拳
Southern Fist is a style of martial arts known for its strong stances.
南拳是一種以穩健步法著稱的武術流派。
Students perform Southern Fist moves at the school show.
學生們在學校表演中展示南拳招式。
