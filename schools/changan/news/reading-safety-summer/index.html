Reading Builds Wisdom, Safety Welcomes Summer閱讀啟智慧・安全迎盛夏

During today's closing ceremony, Ms. Meng-Shan introduced two inspiring picture books to help students learn important life lessons through reading.

Miriam the Shy encourages children to speak with kindness, care about others' feelings, and understand that thoughtful words can make a positive difference. Students were reminded to respect others and avoid hurting people through careless speech.

The Spider and the Fly teaches children to stay alert and recognize that sweet words can sometimes hide danger. Through this story, students learned the importance of protecting themselves, making wise decisions, and staying safe in everyday situations.

A safe, healthy, and joyful summer for every child.

As the summer vacation begins, the principal and teachers reminded every student to practice safety, maintain healthy daily routines, continue reading and learning, cherish family time, and show kindness and respect to others and the environment. We hope every child enjoys a safe, meaningful, and joyful summer filled with growth.

From July 1 to July 28, our school will also offer a variety of engaging summer enrichment programs. We warmly encourage all students to participate, continue exploring new interests, develop their abilities, and discover the joy of lifelong learning. Let's make this summer a season of learning, happiness, and unforgettable memories!

中文原文 · Chinese

今日結業式活動中，本校孟珊老師透過兩本精彩繪本，引領孩子從閱讀中學習品格與安全知能。

《害羞的蜜莉安》提醒孩子學會同理他人的感受，以善意溝通、口說好話，尊重每一位身邊的人，避免因言語而造成他人的困擾與傷害。

《蜘蛛和蒼蠅》則透過故事情節，引導學生提高警覺，辨識甜言蜜語背後可能隱藏的危險，建立自我保護觀念，學會遠離危險情境，守護自身安全。

平安、健康、快樂，讓每個孩子都能好好度過一「夏」。

暑假即將展開，校長與全體師長也共同叮嚀孩子：落實安全生活、維持正常作息、持續閱讀與學習、珍惜親子相處時光，並以友善、感恩的態度對待家人、同學與生活環境，讓每一天都充滿收穫與成長，度過一個平安、健康、充實又快樂的暑假。

此外，學校自7月1日至7月28日精心規劃多元且豐富的暑期課程與增能活動，鼓勵孩子踴躍參與，在學習中探索興趣、培養能力，讓暑假「學習不停歇、成長不打烊」，共同開心過一「夏」！
