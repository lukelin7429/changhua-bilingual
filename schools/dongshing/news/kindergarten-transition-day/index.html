Penguin Class Visits First Grade

企鵝班幼小銜接體驗課程

"Is it true first graders can't have snack time?" "Are there only squat toilets?" "Will there be tests?" With curious minds full of questions, Dongshing Kindergarten's Penguin Class walked into a real elementary classroom for their long-awaited kindergarten-to-first-grade experience day.

Thank you to second-grade teacher Ms. Hui-Chen and her students for warmly hosting the little visitors — showing them how elementary classes work and patiently answering every question. Through this hands-on exchange, the kindergarteners left with a clearer, more excited picture of what school life has in store.

This kind of transition experience isn't just about getting a head start on lessons. It's about building the courage to face change, the confidence to be more independent, and the willingness to try new things. We know the Penguin Class is ready to step into their next big learning adventure — with confidence to spare!

Curiosity today, confidence tomorrow.

中文原文 · Chinese

「一年級真的不能吃點心嗎？」「只有蹲式馬桶嗎？」「要考試嗎？」帶著滿滿的好奇，大班寶貝走進小學教室，展開期待已久的幼小銜接體驗。

感謝二年級慧貞老師與哥哥、姐姐們的熱情陪伴，帶領寶貝們體驗國小上課方式、耐心回答孩子們的各種疑問，透過互動與分享，孩子們對即將到來的小學生活有更多認識與期待。

幼小銜接，不只是提前學習課程，更重要的是培養孩子面對改變的勇氣、獨立生活的能力，以及願意嘗試、勇敢學習的態度。相信東興附幼的大班寶貝，都將帶著滿滿的自信，迎接人生新的學習旅程！

帶著滿滿的好奇心，迎向自信的明天。
