2026 Summer Sports & Fitness Camp

暑期趣味運動健身營隊

Summer means sports at Dongshing! This year's Fun Sports & Fitness Camp filled the campus with laughter from morning to afternoon. Across the week, students rotated through camps for inline skating, emerging sports, and Chinese yo-yo, trying basketball, tee-ball, dodgeball, waveboarding, hockey, freestyle yo-yo, frisbee, flag football, pickleball, Finnish skittles (Mölkky), and more.

With such a wide mix of sports and friendly competitions, every student came away healthier and stronger.

Along the way, they learned to work as a team — and to win without pride, and lose without discouragement.

Summer means sports — and a whole lot of laughter.

中文原文 · Chinese

暑假就是要一起動起來！2026暑期趣味運動健身營隊讓校園從早到下午都充滿了此起彼落的歡笑聲。整整一週，孩子們輪流參加直排輪營、新興運動競賽營與扯鈴營，體驗籃球、樂樂棒球、躲避球、蛇板、曲棍球、花式扯鈴、飛盤、腰旗美式足球、匹克球、芬蘭木旗（Mölkky）、健球等多元豐富的運動項目與競賽。

豐富多樣的運動內容，讓孩子們贏得了健康的體魄；在過程中，他們也發揮了團隊合作精神，學會了勝不驕、敗不餒。

暑假就從運動開始，歡笑聲此起彼落。
