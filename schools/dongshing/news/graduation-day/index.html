Graduation Day at Dongshing

東興國小畢業典禮

Congratulations, Dongshing graduates! Dongshing Elementary School celebrated its 70th elementary graduation and the 39th graduation for its kindergarten, the Penguin Class, in a ceremony full of flowers, balloons, and proud smiles.

Every lesson, every friendship, and every small victory along the way becomes a memory that lasts forever. As the graduates crossed the stage, Principal Lin Ya-Hui and their teachers sent them off with flowers and warm wishes — ready to spread their wings and soar toward a bright future.

The school is grateful to every guest who came to bless the graduates, and to the honorary association presidents and retired principals who generously donated graduation gifts, including new bicycles, to send this year's class off in style.

Congratulations, Dongshing stars — go soar.

中文原文 · Chinese

畢業快樂，東興的孩子們！東興國小舉辦了第70屆畢業典禮，以及附設幼兒園企鵝班第39屆畢業典禮，現場充滿了鮮花、氣球與滿滿的笑容。

每一堂課、每一段友誼、每一次小小的成就，都將成為永遠的畫面。畢業生一一走上舞台，林雅慧校長與師長們獻上鮮花與祝福，期待他們展翅高飛，迎向燦爛的未來。

特別感謝今天蒞臨、給予孩子祝福的每一位貴賓，也感謝致贈畢業禮品（包括腳踏車）的各級榮譽會長與退休校長們的滿滿心意，讓畢業生們風光地邁向人生新旅程。

祝福畢業生展翅高飛，鵬程萬里。
