I.

Welcome

歡迎來到東興國小

A cozy Lugang campus where folk tradition and bilingual discovery grow side by side.

座落鹿港的溫馨校園，讓民俗傳統與雙語探索一起成長茁壯。

Dongshing Elementary School is a wonderful and cozy place to learn, right here in Lugang Township. Our school has a fun and friendly atmosphere, and we love to find new and exciting ways to learn. We focus on helping every student find their best way to learn and discover all about the world.

東興國小座落於彰化縣鹿港鎮，是一個溫馨又充滿活力的學習園地。校園氣氛活潑友善，我們喜歡不斷嘗試新鮮有趣的學習方式，致力協助每一位孩子找到最適合自己的學習方法，進而認識這個世界。

We're really proud of being great at traditional folk sports, like the Chinese yo-yo. We've been enjoying this fun activity for over 20 years! But there's more to our school than just sports. We have special classes where we learn in English and Chinese, read lots of interesting books, and explore cool subjects like science and farming. We also have different clubs and outdoor activities, including learning about the ocean, to make sure our days are filled with fun and varied learning experiences.

我們特別以傳統民俗體育——扯鈴——為傲，這項活動我們已經樂在其中超過20年！但東興不只有體育。我們開設中英雙語特色課程，鼓勵孩子閱讀大量有趣的書籍，並探索科學與農業等有趣的主題。我們也設有多元社團與戶外活動，包括海洋教育，讓每一天都充滿豐富多樣的學習體驗。

Dongshing Elementary School is like a big, happy family. Here, everyone gets to be important and everyone gets to learn in their own special way. We all work together to be smart, creative, and kind, getting ready to be good friends and helpers in the world.

東興國小就像一個溫暖而緊密的大家庭。在這裡，每一個孩子都很重要，也都能用屬於自己的方式學習成長。我們一起努力，成為聰明、有創意又善良的人，準備好成為世界上的好朋友與好幫手。

We love having visitors! Come and see all the fun and learning happening at Dongshing Elementary School. We can't wait to show you around our school and share our exciting activities with you.

我們很歡迎大家來訪！歡迎親自來看看東興國小豐富有趣的學習日常，我們很期待帶您參觀校園，分享我們精彩的活動。
