sweep/swip/掃

Every day, students sweep away fallen leaves on school grounds.每天學生打掃校園的落葉。

Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.台灣的學生打掃校園，保持校園的乾淨整齊。

run/rʌn/跑步

The students are running at the track.學生們在跑道上跑步。

Running is good exercise and students do it every day.跑步是很好的運動，學生們每天都在跑步。

physical activity/ˈfɪzɪkəl ækˈtɪvɪti/身體活動

The students partake in various physical activities at school.同學們在學校參與體能活動。

It is necessary for them to be careful during these physical activities.在做這些體能活動時注意安全是必要的。

riddle/ˈrɪdəl/謎語

The school displays riddles on the outdoor stage.學校在戶外舞台上展示謎語。

The students try to solve the riddles.學生嘗試解開謎語。

jump rope/dʒʌmp roʊp/跳繩

Jumping rope is a great form of cardio.跳繩是一種很好的有氧運動。

Once you get the hang of it, you can jump rope really fast.一旦掌握了竅門，你就能跳得很快。
