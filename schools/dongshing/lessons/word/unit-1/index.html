professional development/prəˈfɛʃənəl dɪˈvɛləpmənt/專業進修

In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.在台灣，所有學校都有教師專業訓練課程。

Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.在專業訓練課程中，老師可以學到新的教學技巧與策略。

recorder/rɪˈkɔrdər/直笛

The recorder is the first musical instrument that many students learn.直笛是很多學生學的第一項樂器。

You can play many songs on a recorder.你可以用直笛吹奏很多歌曲。

dodgeball/ˈdɑdʒbɔl/躲避球

The students are playing dodgeball.學生們正在玩躲避球。

Dodgeball is a popular school game.躲避球是一種流行的學校遊戲。

Chinese yo-yo/tʃaɪˈniz ˈjoʊjoʊ/扯鈴

The Chinese yo-yo is a popular toy many children enjoy.扯鈴是很受小孩子喜歡的玩具。

The students perform tricks with Chinese yo-yos.同學們用扯鈴表演特技。

band/bænd/樂隊

The band members practice every week.樂隊成員每週固定練習。

Students in the band play different instruments.樂隊中的學生演奏不同的樂器。

wash your hands/wɑʃ jɔr hændz/洗手

It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。

Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.確定在吃東西之前要用香皂和水來洗手。

color in/ˈkʌlər ɪn/著色

The students use pencils to draw animals, and then they color them.同學們用鉛筆畫動物，然後再著色。

They use markers to color in their drawings.他們用彩色筆幫他們畫的圖案著色。

observe/əbˈzɜrv/觀察

The kindergarteners go around and observe different plants.幼稚園小朋友在校園走動觀察植物。

They enjoy observing all the plants.他們喜歡觀察各式各樣的植物。

kindergarteners/ˈkɪndərˌgɑrtnərz/幼稚園生

The kindergarteners sing and dance to a song.這些幼稚園小朋友隨著歌曲又唱又跳。

The teacher leads the kindergarteners.老師引導這一群幼稚園小朋友唱跳。
