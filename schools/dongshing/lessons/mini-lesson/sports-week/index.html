Dongshing's Fun Sports Week

運動週
← All Mini Lessons · 回主題課程
I.

Watch & Learn

觀看影片

Welcome to a super fun week at Dongshing Elementary! Our kids got to play lots of sports. They hit balls in tee-ball, skated around, dodged frisbees, kicked soccer balls, rode skateboards, played hockey, and shot basketballs.

歡迎來到東興國小超級有趣的一週！我們的孩子們參加了很多運動。他們在樂樂棒比賽中擊球、在直排輪上滑行、躲避飛盤、踢足球、溜滑板、打曲棍球和投籃球。

Every day, they laughed and worked together. They learned how to play better and made lots of friends. At Dongshing Elementary, we love playing and learning together. We can't wait to do it all again next year!

每一天，他們都在笑聲和團隊合作中度過。他們學會了如何一起玩，並且交了很多朋友。在東興國小，我們喜歡一起玩耍和學習。我們迫不及待想在明年再次參加這些活動！

II.

Words 實用單字

11個關鍵字
super/ˈsupər/超級的

We had a super fun week of sports.我們度過了超級有趣的運動週。

tee-ball/ˈtiˌbɔl/樂樂棒

In tee-ball, students hit the ball off a tee.在樂樂棒球中，學生把球從球座上打出去。

skate/skeɪt/滑行

The students skate around the court.學生們在場地上滑行。

dodge/dɑdʒ/閃躲

Players dodge the flying frisbee.選手們躲避飛來的飛盤。

frisbee/ˈfrɪzbi/飛盤

They throw the frisbee back and forth.他們來回丟飛盤。

kick/kɪk/踢

The students kick the soccer ball into the goal.學生們把足球踢進球門。

soccer/ˈsɑkər/足球

Soccer is one of the sports at Sports Week.足球是運動週的其中一項運動。

ride/raɪd/騎乘

The students ride skateboards during Sports Week.學生們在運動週騎滑板。

skateboard/ˈskeɪtbɔrd/滑板

Riding a skateboard takes balance and practice.溜滑板需要平衡感和練習。

hockey/ˈhɑki/曲棍球

Students play hockey with sticks and a ball.學生們用球棍和球打曲棍球。

shoot/ʃut/投籃

They take turns to shoot the basketball.他們輪流投籃。
