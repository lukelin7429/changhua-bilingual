I.

Watch & Learn

觀看影片

Welcome to Dongshing Elementary School in Lugang Township! Our school is a cozy place where learning is fun. We love playing the Chinese yo-yo and have been enjoying it for 20 years! But we do more than just sports. We learn in English and Chinese, read cool books, and explore exciting things like science and farming. We also have clubs and outdoor activities, like learning about the ocean, to make our days fun and interesting.

歡迎來到鹿港鎮的東興國小！我們的學校是一個溫馨的學習地方，學習變得很有趣。我們喜歡玩扯鈴，有20年的歷史了！但我們不只是做運動。我們用英文和中文做雙語學習、閱讀有趣的書籍、探索科學和農業等令人興奮的事物。我們還有社團和戶外活動，像是學習海洋知識，讓我們的日子充滿樂趣。

At Dongshing, every student is special. We learn in our own way, work together, and are kind to each other. We're getting ready to be great friends and helpers in the world. Come visit us! We can't wait to show you all the fun and learning at Dongshing Elementary School.

在東興，每位學生都是特別的。我們以自己的方式學習、互相合作、彼此友善。我們正準備成為世界的好朋友和幫手。來訪問我們吧！我們迫不及待想向你展示東興國小所有的樂趣和學習內容。

Science class lets students ask big questions.科學課讓學生提出各種大哉問。
