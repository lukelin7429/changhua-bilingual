Word 01
Christmas
聖誕節
The students listen to a story about Chrismas.
同學們正在聽有關聖誕節的故事。
The school organizes several events for students to get ready for Christmas.
學校為學生舉辦了好幾個活動來迎接聖誕節的到來。
Word 02
anniversary (n.)
周年紀念
The elementary school is celebrating their school anniversary.
這間小學正在慶祝他們的校慶。
They celebrate their school anniversary by having fun games and races.
他們舉辦趣味競賽與賽跑來慶祝校慶。
Word 03
school anniversary
校慶
Parents and students attend the school anniversary.
父母和學生參加校慶。
Everyone is celebrating the school anniversary.
每個人都在慶祝校慶。
Word 04
award presentation
頒獎
The winners of the running races get a prize at the award presentation.
在頒獎典禮中，賽跑得勝者獲得獎品。
The award presentation is held on the school's sports day.
頒獎典禮在學校運動會當天進行。
Word 05
field trip
校外教學
The students go on a field trip.
學生們參加校外教學。
They take a bus on their field trip.
他們搭巴士參加校外教學。
Word 06
gingerbread house
薑餅屋
The students make gingerbread houses in class.
學生們在課堂上製作薑餅屋。
They use gingerbread cookies and different decorations to make their gingerbread houses.
他們用薑餅餅乾和不同的裝飾品來製作薑餅屋。
Word 07
sticky rice ball
湯圓
The students prepare to make sticky rice balls.
同學們準備要搓湯圓。
The sticky rice balls are white and pink.
湯圓的顏色是白色與粉紅色的。
Word 08
Christmas celebration
聖誕節慶祝會
The students go outside for the Christmas celebration.
同學到戶外慶祝聖誕節。
They sing songs on stage for the Christmas celebration.
他們在台上唱歌慶祝聖誕節。
Word 09
field trip
校外教學
The students leave school to go on a field trip.
同學們離開學校進行校外教學。
The students take a field trip to Changhua City.
他們到彰化市做校外教學。
Word 10
field trip (n.)
校外教學
The students go on a field trip.
學生們去校外教學。
They observed many new things during their field trip.
他們在校外教學期間觀察到許多新事物。
Word 11
Mother's Day (n.)
母親節
The students are making Mother’s Day cards.
學生們正在製作母親節卡片。
They are drawing pictures and writing messages to show their love for Mom on Mother’s Day.
他們在母親節畫畫、寫信表達對媽媽的愛。
Word 12
pastry (n.)
糕點
The students learn about different traditional pastries.
After learning about different pastries, the students each design their own pastry.
