Word of the Day · Unit 03

🏃 Sports Day & PE

運動場上的英文

Word 01
swing (v.)
盪鞦韆
During recess, students like to swing.
下課時，學生們喜歡玩盪鞦韆。
You kick your legs up and down to swing.
盪鞦韆時，你的腳要上下踢動。
Word 02
kick (v.)
打水
The students sit at the edge of the pool and practice kicking in the water.
同學們坐在泳游池旁邊練習打水。
You can practice kicking in the water before you learn how to swim.
在你還沒學會游泳前可以先練習打水。
Word 03
swimming class
游泳課
The students are stretching for swimming class.
學生們正在為游泳課做伸展運動。
The students are practicing swimming in swimming class.
學生們正在游泳課上練習游泳。
Word 04
relay race
接力賽
In the relay race, students pass their batons to the next runners.
在接力賽中，同學們把接力棒遞給下一棒。
The relay race is a very exciting event.
接力賽是一項非常刺激的比賽。
Word 05
climb (v.)
爬
The students climb the climbing wall.
同學們正在爬攀爬牆。
They take turns climbing the indoor rock climbing wall.
他們輪流在室內的攀岩牆練習攀爬。
Word 06
harness (n.)
安全帶
The students use a harness to be lowered to the ground floor.
同學們使用安全帶垂降到一樓。
The harness allows the students to be lowered.
安全帶讓同學可以被垂降下去。
Word 07
zipline (v.)
滑降
The students zipline from the second floor of the school.
同學從學校的二樓滑降下去。
The profesionals instruct the students on how to zipline properly.
專業人士指導學生如何正確地滑降。
Word 08
endurance (n.)
耐力
The students test their endurance by pulling heavy weights.
同學們拉重物測試耐力。
They work on their endurance by doing various exercises.
他們藉由做不同的運動來提升耐力。
Word 09
tee-ball (n.)
樂樂棒球
The students play tee-ball on the field.
同學們在操場上玩樂樂棒球。
They take turns hitting the ball during tee-ball.
他們在玩樂樂棒球的時候輪流上場擊球。
Word 10
enter the field
入場
On sports day, the students enter the field with their classmates and teacher.
運動會當天，學生們和他們的同學與老師進場。
Lining up and holding signs, the students enter the field on sports day.
運動會當天，他們排隊並且舉牌進場。
Word 11
perform a dance
舞蹈表演
The third grade students perform a dance for sports day.
三年級同學在運動會當天表演舞蹈。
The students work hard to learn and perform a dance.
同學們很認真學習表演舞蹈。
Word 12
sports day
運動會
Every student must participate in sports day by playing games or running.
每個學生都要參與運動會的活動像是趣味競賽或賽跑。
Many students, teachers and family attend the sports day together.
很多同學、老師與家人一起參加運動會。
Word 13
agility training
敏捷訓練
The students do agility training outside.
學生們在戶外敏捷訓練。
They do different exercises for agility training.
他們的敏捷訓練有好幾種不同的方式。
Word 14
arm strength
臂力
The students do arm strength exercises.
學生們做臂力的訓練活動。
They use weights and bands for arm strength exercises.
他們用重物和彈力帶來做臂力訓練。
Word 15
core strength
核心肌力
The students do core strength training.
同學們做核心肌力的訓練。
They do different exercises for core strength.
他們做不同的運動來增加核心肌力。
Word 16
softball throw
壘球擲遠
The students practice for the softball throw event.
同學們在練習壘球擲遠的項目。
They use different methods to train for the softball throw event.
他們用不同的方式來訓練壘球擲遠的項目。
Word 17
starting blocks
起跑架
The students use the starting blocks to start a race.
同學使用起跑架輔助比賽開時的起跑。
They brace their feet against the starting blocks.
他們將腳固定在起跑架上。
Word 18
shot put
推鉛球
In the shot put, athletes push a heavy metal ball as far as they can.
推鉛球比賽中，選手要把沉重的鉛球盡量推遠。
She practices the shot put every week for the school sports day.
她每週練習推鉛球，為校運動會做準備。
Word 19
swim team
游泳隊
The swim team practices the butterfly stroke.
游泳隊練習蝶泳。
The swim team practices the front crawl and breaststroke.
游泳隊練習自由式和蛙式。
Word 20
dodgebee (n.)
躲避盤
The students are playing dodgebee.
學生們正在玩躲飛盤遊戲。
Dodgebee is like dodgeball, except a soft frisbee is used instead of a ball.
躲避飛盤類似於躲避球，只不過用的是軟飛盤而不是球。
