Word 01
wash your hands
洗手
It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
確定在吃東西之前要用香皂和水來洗手。
Word 02
check out
借書
The students check out books from the library at recess.
同學們利用下課到圖書室借書。
After the librarian checks out the students' books, they can take them home to read.
向圖書館員辦理借閱手續後，同學們可以將書帶回家看。
Word 03
professional development
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都有教師專業訓練課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
在專業訓練課程中，老師可以學到新的教學技巧與策略。
Word 04
storyteller (n.)
講故事的人
A good storyteller teaches a lesson and entertains you at the same time.
一個擅長講說故事的人可以教導大家道理，同時又兼具娛樂大眾。
The storytellers can use real or fictional information to tell a story.
說故事的人可能用真實或者是虛構的情節來說故事。
Word 05
pin (n.)
徽章
The students use colored pencils to decorate their pins.
同學們用色鉛筆來裝飾他們的徽章。
The teacher presses the pins for the students to wear.
老師壓徽章好讓他們可以穿戴。
Word 06
playdough (n.)
黏土
The students make a Santa Claus out of playdough.
學生們用黏土製作了聖誕老人。
They shape the playdough into a Santa Claus.
他們將黏土塑造成聖誕老人的樣子。
Word 07
fun game
趣味競賽
The students play a fun game with a ball and a hoop.
同學們用球與呼拉圈進行趣味競賽。
When the students win the fun game, they feel happy.
當他們贏得趣味競賽時，他們感到非常高興。
Word 08
bubble (n.)
泡泡
The students learn how to make bubbles outside.
同學們學習在室外拉泡泡。
They use different bubble wands to make different-sized bubbles.
他們使用泡泡棒拉出不同大小的泡泡。
Word 09
tablet (n.)
平板
The students are using tablets with keyboards in class.
同學們在課堂上使用加上鍵盤的平板。
The students are using their tablets to learn new things in class.
同學們在課堂上使用他們的平板來學習新的事物。
Word 10
sweep (v.)
掃
Every day, students sweep away fallen leaves on school grounds.
每天學生打掃校園的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.
台灣的學生打掃校園，保持校園的乾淨整齊。
Word 11
board game
桌遊
The students form groups to play a board game.
同學們分組玩桌遊。
They enjoy playing the board game in class.
他們很喜歡在課堂上玩桌遊。
Word 12
clean (v.)
清潔/打掃
The students clean the school every day.
學生們每天打掃學校。
The students wipe windows, sweep, and mop the floor to clean the school.
同學們擦窗戶、掃地與拖地來打掃學校。
Word 13
volunteer (n.)
志工
The traffic volunteers make sure the students are safe while commuting to school.
交通導護志工確保學生上學時的安全。
The traffic volunteers wear neon safety vests to be easily spotted.
交通導護志工穿著反光的安全背心容易讓人看見。
Word 14
toy (n.)
玩具
The students play with a toy butterfly made of paper.
同學們玩用紙製作的蝴蝶玩具。
They run with their toy butterfly.
他們拿著蝴蝶玩具跑步。
Word 15
farm (n.)
農場
The students learn about different insects at the farm.
同學們在農場學習昆蟲的相關知識。
They also feed animals at the farm.
他們在農場也餵食動物。
Word 16
flag ceremony
升旗典禮
The students are outside for the flag ceremony.
同學們到外面參加升旗典禮。
They line up for the flag ceremony.
他們排隊進行升旗典禮。
