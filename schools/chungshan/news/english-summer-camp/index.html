Let the Games Begin!

菲趣BOOK英語營・奧運主題五日冒險
⌄

The long-awaited five-day English camp has officially begun! This year's camp was designed by our foreign English teachers together with Chungshan's own English team, built around an energetic "Chungshan Olympics" theme — full-English interactive lessons, teamwork, and hands-on experiences that turn the whole campus into an immersive English-learning world.

Day 1 · Opening Ceremony

The opening ceremony was lit — literally — by the principal himself, who carried in the torch symbolizing hope and challenge and announced: "Let the Games Begin!" 🔥

"Let the Games Begin!"

Transformed into their own national teams, the children set off on this English journey with excitement and anticipation. Besides learning about the Olympic spirit and joining playful competitions on day one, students also worked in small groups, interacting naturally with the foreign teachers — bravely speaking up in English, building confidence through play, and building teamwork through cooperation.

Day 2 · Learning Beyond the Classroom

On the second day, students stepped out of the classroom for a journey that blended real-life situations with bilingual learning. The first stop was the Taichung MRT, where children rode the metro themselves and even visited the rarely-opened Operation Control Center behind the scenes. Through bilingual guiding, they learned how the whole system runs, along with plenty of everyday English words about transportation.

Next came Taichung Airport. The children didn't actually fly anywhere, but their teachers had prepared something just as exciting: personal boarding passes. From check-in to security to boarding, students role-played the entire flying process and practiced real boarding-gate English conversations, while learning what all that information printed on a boarding pass actually means.

The last stop was the Taiwan Yogurt Cookie College bakery, where children rolled up their sleeves and made their very own egg tarts. In the middle of all that happy baking, students naturally reached for the English phrases they had just practiced — proof that language sticks best when it's part of something fun.

From the MRT, to the airport, to the bakery — every stop became a living English lesson. Warm thanks to our foreign teachers, the English team, and every staff member who made the day possible. Through immersive bilingual learning, the children didn't just study English — they spoke up bravely, explored happily, and gathered a whole day's worth of real, hands-on learning. 🌐✨

Highlights · The Camp on Film

The camp's highlight video is now online! Our foreign teachers put real care into filming these memories — take a look at everything the children learned and all the smiles they shared this week. 🎥

Day 1 · 2026.07.06

🏅English Summer Camp 菲趣BOOK英語營開跑！點燃英語學習熱情🔥　期待已久的五天英語營隊正式揭開序幕！今年的營隊由外籍英語教師攜手本校英語教學團隊精心自主規劃，以充滿活力的「Olympics 奧運」為主題，透過全英語互動課程、團隊合作及多元體驗，打造沉浸式的英語學習環境。

開幕式由校長親自點燃象徵希望與挑戰的奧運聖火，並宣布：「Let the Games Begin!」🔥孩子們化身各國代表隊，帶著興奮與期待，正式展開這場充滿歡笑與挑戰的英語旅程。第一天除了認識奧運精神、參與趣味競賽，也透過分組活動與外籍老師自然互動，勇敢開口說英語，在遊戲中建立自信、在合作中培養默契。

接下來幾天，還有✨校外教學、文化體驗、創意手作、團隊競賽及更多精彩課程等著孩子們，相信每位小朋友都能在豐富有趣的活動中，享受英語、愛上英語，留下難忘的夏日回憶！Learning English through fun, friendship, and adventure!讓我們一起期待孩子們接下來精彩的英語冒險！🌍💙

Day 2 · 2026.07.07

【菲趣BOOK英語營 Day 2 ✈️🚅 沉浸式雙語體驗，Learning Beyond the Classroom!】英語夏令營第二天，孩子們走出教室，展開一場結合生活情境與雙語學習的精彩旅程！

第一站來到台中捷運，實際體驗搭乘捷運，並參訪平時鮮少對外開放的捷運後端行車控制中心。透過雙語導覽，孩子們認識捷運系統的運作方式，也學習許多與交通相關的英語單字與生活用語。

接著前往台中機場，雖然沒有真正搭上飛機出國，但老師們精心設計了專屬「登機證」，讓孩子們化身小小旅客，從報到、安檢到登機，模擬完整的搭機流程，並練習登機情境英語對話，同時認識登機證上的各項資訊及機場出入境流程，讓英語真正融入生活情境。

最後來到台灣優格餅乾學院，孩子們親手挑戰製作香噴噴的蛋塔，在歡樂的烘焙體驗中，也自然運用課程中學到的英語，讓學習變得更加有趣又難忘！從捷運、機場到烘焙體驗，每一站都是一堂生動的英語課。感謝外籍教師、英語教學團隊及行政同仁精心安排與一整天的陪伴！透過沉浸式雙語學習，孩子們不只是學英語，更勇敢開口、快樂探索，在真實情境中累積寶貴的學習經驗，收穫滿滿的一天！🌍✨
