Bilingual Art Master Camp Ends on a Colorful Note

雙語藝術大師培訓營・圓滿落幕
⌄

After four inspiring days, the Bilingual Art Master Camp came to a joyful close today. Under the personal guidance of art master Bai Feng-chung, Chungshan's young artists journeyed through a world of color and creativity.

From brush to blade, every medium tells a different story.

Students explored three very different media — traditional ink painting, watercolor, and acrylic — discovering how each one changes the feeling of a picture. Inspired by themes like insects and lotus flowers, children were encouraged to observe the world closely and turn their imagination into one-of-a-kind artwork. Every stroke of color and every careful choice showed just how much creativity these children carry inside them.

Learning directly from a master like Teacher Bai is a rare and precious opportunity. He generously shared not only technique, but also how to observe with care and express freely — turning art into something bigger than skill: a way of thinking, and a way of truly seeing beauty.

Four days of color, a lifetime of confidence.

Heartfelt thanks to Teacher Bai Feng-chung for four days of patient guidance and generous sharing, helping every child build confidence and find joy in creating. Beyond artistic taste, these four days — carried out in a bilingual environment — also strengthened students' expression and thinking skills, leaving behind a rich and unforgettable summer memory.

中文原文 · Chinese

雙語藝術大師培訓營 圓滿落幕！ ✨🎨

為期四天的雙語藝術大師培訓營，今天正式畫下精彩句點！在藝術大師 白丰中 老師的親自指導下，孩子們展開一場充滿創意與美感的藝術探索之旅。從🖌️水墨畫、🎨多元媒材到🖼️壓克力油畫，透過不同媒材的體驗，感受藝術創作的多元魅力。

課程以昆蟲、荷花等主題為靈感，鼓勵孩子細心觀察生活、勇敢揮灑創意，將腦海中的想像化為一幅幅獨一無二的作品。每一筆色彩、每一份巧思，都展現出孩子們豐富的創造力與自信。

能夠近距離接受白丰中老師的指導，是孩子們難得且珍貴的學習機會。老師不僅傾囊相授創作技巧，更引導孩子用心觀察、自在表達，讓藝術不只是技法，更成為啟發思考與培養美感的力量。

衷心感謝 白丰中老師 四天來悉心陪伴與無私分享，讓每位孩子都能在創作中找到自信、享受藝術，也收穫滿滿的感動與成長。四天的學習，不僅培養了藝術美感，更在雙語環境中提升表達與思考能力，留下充實又難忘的夏日回憶。💖
