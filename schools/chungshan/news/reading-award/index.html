Leading the Way in Reading

領航閱讀・楊校長帶隊親征　中山之光閃耀縣府
⌄

Today was a day full of honor and warmth. Principal Yang Shih-kai set aside time from his own schedule to personally lead one of Chungshan's reading champions, student Hu, to the Changhua County Government for a very special lunch — the "County Magistrate's Guests" recognition banquet.

"Reading is a window to the world."

Hu earned this seat at the table by breaking through the demanding 5,000-point TOP tier in the "Reading Steps Changhua · Flying Read Cloud" certification system — a goal that called for real persistence, page after page, day after day.

Meeting County Magistrate Wang Hui-mei face to face, Hu had the chance to share the simple joy of a good book. On the spot, Principal Yang encouraged her with words the whole school can carry forward: "Reading is a window to the world, and I am honored to personally witness the moment a child opens that window."

Watching Hu step forward to receive her honor at the County Hall wasn't just a personal achievement — it's the clearest proof of what Chungshan's staff and teachers have been quietly building: a school where the love of reading is planted early and given room to grow.

Family and school, growing honor together.

Special thanks go to Hu's guiding teacher for steady, patient encouragement, and to her parents for their quiet support behind the scenes. The seed of reading, planted here at Chungshan, is now blooming — and we cheer loudly for Hu and for Principal Yang, who led the way. 👏

中文原文 · Chinese

【領航閱讀｜楊校長帶隊親征！中山之光閃耀縣府 ✨】

今天是一個充滿榮耀與溫馨的日子！本校 楊式愷校長 特別撥冗，親自帶領著本校的閱讀小尖兵 —— 胡〇禎 同學，前往彰化縣政府參加「縣長小客人」表揚午宴。

能與王縣長面對面分享閱讀的喜悅，是因為胡同學在「讀步彰化・飛閱雲端」認證系統中，以驚人的毅力突破了 5,000分 的 TOP 級門檻！🏆

楊校長在現場勉勵孩子：「閱讀是通往世界的門窗，校長很榮幸能親自見證你開啟這扇窗的精彩瞬間。」看著孩子從校長手中接過鼓勵，在縣府留下光榮的身影，這不僅是孩子個人的成就，更是中山國小行政團隊與教師們深耕閱讀、播種智慧的最好證明。

🤝 親師合作，共造榮耀。特別感謝指導老師平時的辛勤帶領，以及家長們在背後的全力支持。閱讀的種子在中山茁壯，在孩子心中開花。讓我們再次為胡同學與帶領團隊的楊校長大聲喝采！👏
