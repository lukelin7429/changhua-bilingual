The Seed of Art Begins with Taking Part

藝術的種子，從參與開始！
⌄

Today, Chungshan's teachers and students were invited to a press conference hosted by the Changhua County Cultural Affairs Bureau — helping to raise the curtain on the 2026 Changhua Children's Arts Festival, a grand celebration of the arts running from July 9 to August 9 at the Yuanlin Performance Hall.

Opening the five senses, sparking the imagination.

Themed "Yuan-Flow · Let Movement Happen," the festival brings together top performing-arts groups from around Taiwan, with wonderful shows, parent-child workshops, themed exhibitions, and opening events. Through drama, music, puppetry, and interactive experiences, it invites children to open their five senses, spark their imagination, and feel the endless charm of the arts.

We were so happy to see our children step outside the school and take part in one of the county's most important arts events — not only broadening their horizons, but also learning to appreciate, explore, and express themselves within an artistic atmosphere. We look forward to a summer where even more families walk into the theater together, and leave with beautiful memories made of laughter and wonder!

Heartfelt thanks to Teacher Fu Yu-shan and Teacher Zeng Yun-pei for leading the group and sharing the joy of art with the children.

中文原文 · Chinese

藝術的種子，從參與開始！✨🎭

今天，本校師生受邀參加彰化縣文化局主辦的「2026彰化兒童藝術節」宣傳記者會，一同為即將於7月9日至8月9日在員林演藝廳盛大登場的兒童藝術盛會揭開序幕！

活動以「員流．讓流動發生」為主題，集結國內優質表演藝術團隊，規劃精彩演出、親子工作坊、主題特展及開幕活動，透過戲劇、音樂、偶戲與互動體驗，引領孩子打開五感、激發想像力，感受藝術的無限魅力。

很開心看到孩子們走出校園，參與縣內重要藝文活動，不僅拓展視野，也在藝術氛圍中學習欣賞、探索與表達。期待今年暑假，更多親子一起走進劇場，在歡笑與感動中留下美好的藝文回憶！

感謝傅于珊老師、曾允姵老師帶隊，陪孩子一起感受藝術！
