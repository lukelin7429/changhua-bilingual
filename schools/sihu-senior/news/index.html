2026.10.28Sports · 體育
Sihu baseball takes the field at the Black Panther Flag for the tenth time溪高棒球隊第 10 度挑戰黑豹旗，青春無憾

On October 28 the Sihu baseball team took the field at the CTBC Cup Black Panther Flag national high school tournament for the tenth time. Looking back over the years, the team has fought its way into the round of 64 and on into the second round — the best result in the team's history.

10 月 28 日，溪高棒球隊第 10 度征戰黑豹旗！回顧溪高歷年戰績，曾一路拚戰挺進 64 強、闖進第二輪，寫下隊史最佳紀錄。

The trip may have shown the players just how big the world is. Seeing the gap in strength is not a reason to retreat — it shows where to put the effort, so that every drop of sweat becomes nourishment for growth.

這趟旅程，孩子們或許更深刻體會到：世界真的很大！看見實力的差距，不是為了退縮，而是為了找到努力的方向，讓每一次流血流汗，都成為成長的養分。

The school's deepest thanks go to Coach Tsai and Coach Chiang, who for more than a decade have given up club time and holidays to train the players and chase the dream with them, and to the head of physical-education affairs, who has led the team from tournament to tournament. They are the pillars of Sihu's baseball dream.

最感謝的是蔡教練、江教練十多年來的堅持，利用社團時間、犧牲假期，陪著孩子們特訓、築夢；也別忘了一直帶領棒球隊四處征戰的體育組長。他們都是溪高棒球夢最重要的推手與貴人，這份付出，值得我們深深致敬。

For a player, the Black Panther Flag is like stepping onto a major-league field once in a lifetime — or like the wind band taking the stage at Yuanlin Performing Arts Center for its annual concert: a ritual worth treasuring in youth. Even through sweat and soreness, you grit your teeth and finish what you set out to do — no regrets in high school. May the team carry on for generations, and one day bring the championship flag home as the school's treasure, and may this glory stay with the players as they meet every challenge in life.

對孩子而言，參加黑豹旗就像一生能踏上大聯盟球場，或管樂隊登上員林演藝廳完成成果發表，是青春中值得珍藏的儀式感！即使流血流汗，也要咬牙完成心願——高中無憾！祝福溪高棒球隊永續傳承，未來有一天，真能把黑豹旗冠軍旗贏回溪高，作為鎮校之寶；更希望這份拚搏的榮耀，永遠留在心中，陪伴孩子們勇敢迎向人生的每一場挑戰。

2026.10.28Science · 科學
Playing with chemistry: cyanotype prints and soap-making化學玩起來！藍晒 × 皂化反應，實驗超有趣

On October 28, Professor Wu An-tai, Chair of the Department of Chemistry at National Changhua University of Education, brought his team to Sihu for a chemistry adventure with second-year students in the natural-science track.

10 月 28 日，很榮幸邀請到彰師大化學系吳安台教授兼系主任團隊蒞臨溪高，帶領高二學術自然組同學，展開一場充滿驚喜的化學探索之旅！

Cyanotype: using leaves and designs of their own, students made one-of-a-kind prints — each one a piece of science-as-art. Saponification: from the principle to making soap by hand, chemistry from the textbook became something they could see and touch.

藍晒實驗：發揮創意，利用樹葉與自製圖案，打造獨一無二的藍晒作品，每一張都是專屬的科學藝術品！皂化反應：從原理解說到親手製作肥皂，讓課本裡的化學知識變成看得見、摸得到的實作體驗！

Chemistry, it turns out, is more than formulas and equations — it can be this much fun and this creative. Thanks to Professor Wu's team for the excellent guidance, and to Sihu's chemistry teachers for their help, so that students could explore science by doing.

原來化學不只有公式與反應式，還能這麼好玩又有創意！感謝吳安台教授團隊的精彩指導，以及本校化學科教師團隊的協助，讓同學們在動手實驗中探索科學、玩出學習力！

2026.10.22Sports · 體育
No sports day this year, but Sihu's sports spirit keeps running運動會調整，溪高運動精神不打烊

The all-school sports meet planned for November 6 will not be held this year: the basketball and volleyball courts are being fitted with solar-roofed canopies, a project meant to cool the scorching summer courts and give students a more comfortable place to play for years to come.

原定 11 月 6 日登場的全校運動大會，因校內籃球場、排球場正進行「光電球場建置工程」，為了改善夏日戶外球場酷熱的環境，打造更舒適的運動空間，今年全校運動大會將取消辦理。

The sports spirit, though, does not shut down. At Principal Li Ming-chao's instruction, the preliminary rounds of every event are going ahead as planned. Students are training in PE classes and on weekends, setting their own targets and chasing a personal best: run faster, jump farther.

不過，運動會可以調整，溪高人的運動魂絕對不能熄火！在李明昭校長的指示下，各項競賽預賽仍然照常舉行，同學們也利用體育課與假日時間積極練習、加強鍛鍊，為自己設定目標，努力朝著 PB（個人最佳紀錄）邁進——跑得更快、跳得更遠！

In the relay, what students learn is not only speed but trust, mutual support and all-out effort for the team; in winning or losing, they learn to be gracious in victory and resilient in defeat, like true sportsmen. A stronger body brings more energy to the books, too. Walking back to the office, the school was reminded of Jonathan Lee's song “我們都是和自己賽跑的人” (“We Are All Racing Against Ourselves”): the real race is always with yourself.

在接力賽中，我們學習的不只是速度，更是彼此信任、互相扶持、為團隊全力以赴的精神；面對勝負，也要記得「勝不驕、敗不餒」，展現真正的運動家風範！身體練強壯，讀書更有勁！回辦公室的路上，耳邊響起李宗盛〈我們都是和自己賽跑的人〉的歌聲——真正的對手，永遠是自己。

2026.10.17Field Trip · 校外教學
Out of the classroom: a day at Academia Sinica's Open House走出教室，世界就是課堂：中研院開放日

To widen students' horizons, the Office of Academic Affairs took first- and second-year students to the 2026 Academia Sinica Open House on October 17, led by a physics teacher and a civics teacher.

為拓展學生多元學習視野，本校教務處於 10 月 17 日帶領高一、高二同學參加「2026 中研院開放日」，由物理科與公民科老師帶隊北上，走進學術殿堂，展開一場充滿知識與驚喜的探索之旅！

They toured the Hu Shih Memorial Hall and felt the thought and humanism of a great scholar; watched microorganisms under the microscope and ran science experiments with their own hands; learned how economics works through interactive games; and caught an academic lecture, feeling the pull of research and knowledge.

從參觀胡適紀念館、感受大師的思想與人文精神，到透過顯微鏡觀察微生物、親手操作科學實驗，同學們不僅探索科學的奧妙，也在互動遊戲中認識經濟學的有趣知識，更把握難得的機會聆聽學術講座，感受研究與知識的魅力！

After the exams, learning does not have to stop at the classroom door. Through site visits and first-hand experience, Sihu students built a spirit of scientific inquiry, deepened their humanistic concern, and practised independent thinking through observation, reflection and exchange — turning every experience into nourishment for growth.

段考後不只放鬆，更要讓學習走出教室！溪高學子透過實地參訪與親身體驗，培養科學探究精神、深化人文關懷，並在觀察、思考與交流中實踐獨立思辨，讓每一次學習都成為成長的養分！

2026.09.22Careers · 職涯
Stepping into the workplace: seniors visit local employers走進職場，遇見不一樣的未來

On September 22, seniors from the Applied English and Catering Service programmes set out for a hands-on look at the working world in Changhua, as part of the 2026 Youth Workplace Experience Programme — guided by the Ministry of Labor's Workforce Development Agency and hosted by Changhua County Government.

9 月 22 日，高三應用英語與餐飲服務學程同學帶著期待出發，走進彰化在地企業，參加「115 年青年職場體驗計畫」（指導單位：勞動部勞動力發展署，主辦單位：彰化縣政府），來一場「看得見、摸得到」的職場體驗！

The morning began at Taisun Enterprise (泰山企業), with a company introduction and a tour of the production line — food safety, product development and ESG sustainability — and a first-hand look at how the drinks and eight-treasure porridge they know so well are made.

上午來到泰山企業，從精彩的企業介紹到實際產線參觀，認識食品安全、產品開發與 ESG 永續，也親眼看看每天熟悉的飲料與八寶粥是怎麼製作出來的，驚喜滿滿！

In the afternoon they visited the traditional pastry maker Bao Zhen Xiang (寶珍香) and felt the warmth of a family business. The owner personally shared the story of a hundred years of cake-making, treated the students to mung-bean soup to cool off, and sent everyone home with longan cake.

下午走訪寶珍香食品，更感受到滿滿的人情味——老闆親自分享百年製餅的故事，還貼心準備綠豆沙為同學消暑，最後送上桂圓蛋糕，讓大家帶著甜甜的滋味回家。

Thanks to Changhua County Government for building the bridge between campus and industry. The students brought home more than souvenirs: a picture of the workplace, and a clearer sense of where their careers might lead.

感謝彰化縣政府搭起校園與產業之間的橋樑！這一天，同學帶回的不只是伴手禮，更是對職場的想像、對未來職涯方向之探索！

2026.08.24Academic · 學術交流
Sihu signs an academic partnership with National Chung Hsing University溪高與國立中興大學簽訂學術交流協議書

On August 24, Principal Li Ming-chao signed an Academic Partnership Agreement on Transitional Justice and Human Rights Education on behalf of Sihu with National Chung Hsing University. The two schools will draw on local history, community expertise and museum collections, sharing resources between university and high school through coursework, teaching materials, walking tours, exhibitions, lectures and forums. More than a dozen elementary, junior high and senior high schools from the Changhua–Taichung–Nantou area signed on the same day.

8 月 24 日，溪湖高中李明昭校長代表學校與國立中興大學簽訂「轉型正義與人權教育合作協議書」，共同推動轉型正義與人權教育。雙方將結合地方文史、民間實務及館藏展示等專業，透過課程、教材、走讀、展覽、演講及座談會等方式，促進大學與高中教育資源共享。本次交流計畫，中彰投地區約十幾所國小、國中及高中一同與興大簽約。

Meeting a top university, the Principal said, is an important chance for Sihu's teachers and students to grow. Through the school's quality-improvement programme, teachers in Civics and Social Studies have already led students on visits and walking tours to the National Human Rights Museum and related memorial sites, building a genuine respect for human rights that carries into daily life.

李明昭校長表示，與頂尖大學交流是溪高師生自我提升的重要契機。溪高公民與社會科教師已透過優質化計畫，帶領學生參訪國家人權博物館、相關紀念館並進行走讀，建立尊重人權的正確認知，進而落實於日常生活。

The platform, Principal Li added, is very good news for high schools across central Taiwan: he hopes National Chung Hsing University can rally a community of teachers to co-develop lesson plans, sharpen professional skills, and in time open electives that guide students toward self-directed learning and independent research.

李校長指出，此次合作平台對中部高中是一大福音，期待透過中興大學號召，串聯中部教師共備社群，共同發展教案、提升教師專業，進一步開設選修課程及指導學生自主學習與專題研究。

2026.07.01 – 07.05Leadership · 營隊
Hosting the New Century Leadership Camp承辦國教署 115 年度新世紀領導人才培育營

Sihu hosted the K-12 Education Administration's annual New Century Leadership Camp — the 25th cohort, foundation level, Central Region — under the theme "Youth Reading Changhua, Growing a Sustainable Vision." Running July 1–5, it gathered 94 campers, 20 mentor teachers and 3 staff. A Lukang city escape game, four themed walking routes through Sihu and a self-media production project pushed participants to explore local culture and practise teamwork. The Office of Academic Affairs applied to the Changhua County Cultural Affairs Bureau to host an outstanding company from the county's theatre festival, and the Chen Li-hsiang Taiwanese Opera Troupe — Changhua's outstanding performing group for 2026 — answered with its new production.
