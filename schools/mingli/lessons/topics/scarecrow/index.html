Scarecrow

稻草人

Mingli students built real scarecrows from bamboo poles and dry grass — a hands-on farm lesson in keeping the birds away from the crops.明禮的孩子用竹竿和乾草親手做出真正的稻草人——一堂「保護農作物」的食農英語課。

Word 01

crop (n.)
農作物
Scarecrows keep birds away from crops.

Word 03
tie (v.)
綁
The students tie the hay to make the scarecrow's arms and legs.
學生們綁稻草來做稻草人的手和腳。
Word 04
hay (n.)
乾草
The students tie the hay to make the scarecrow's arms and legs.
學生們綁稻草來做稻草人的手和腳。
Word 05
stuff (v.)
塞
The students stuff the hay in the scarecrow's clothes.
學生們將稻草塞入稻草人的衣服。
Word 06
pole (n.)
竿
The teacher sticks a pole in the ground.
老師把竹竿插在地上。
Poles are used to make a scarecrow's body.
竹竿被用來當成稻草人的身體。
Word 07
instruction (n.)
指示說明
The teacher gives instructions on making scarecrows.
老師指導製作稻草人的方法。
The students listen to the instructions.
學生們聆聽說明。
