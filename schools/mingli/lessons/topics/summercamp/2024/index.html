Community Food Exploration

明禮E食代社區探索

In 2024, Mingli students became community explorers — visiting Little Field Coffee beside the rice paddies, ordering food in English, buying tickets at Tianzhong HSR station, interviewing local farmers, and learning Tianzhong pottery at the kiln.2024 年，明禮的孩子成為社區探索者——走訪稻田旁的小田咖啡、用英語點餐、在田中高鐵站購票、採訪在地農夫，並到田中窯學陶藝。

What We Explored

這一年，我們走進社區做了什麼

The whole town became the classroom. Students stepped out of the school gate to use real English in real places — a coffee shop, a train station, a farm, and a pottery studio — and came back as young reporters telling the story in English.

整個小鎮都是教室。孩子們走出校門，在真實場景裡用真實的英語——咖啡館、火車站、農田、陶藝工坊——再化身小記者，用英語把故事說出來。
