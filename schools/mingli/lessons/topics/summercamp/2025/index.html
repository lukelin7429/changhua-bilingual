Three Camp Themes

三大課程主題 · 從食農、科學到社區走讀
Theme 1
Awesome E-Farmers & Digital Detectives
三好E農 · 數位閱讀
  • Smart Gardens & Yummy Tables 智慧田園～健康餐桌Read stories about where our food comes from, become a junior chef, and explore how future farms use cool technology.
  • Be an Earth Hero with Digital Tools 三好E農～環境數位閱讀Use tablets to read e-books about our planet and team up on smart solutions to protect the Earth.
  • English Happy Go! From Farm to Table 英文 Happy Go!An immersive English zone — plant seeds, watch them grow, and cook, all in English.
Theme 2
Magical Farm & Science Explorers
E農藝境 · 魔法探索
  • Amazing Food Scientists! 美味智多星Do tasty experiments, learn the science behind food, and stay safe and clean in the kitchen.
  • Little Weather Makers & Tech Wizards 氣象小創客Use Qblock kits to build a weather station, learn simple coding, and design a smart heat alarm.
  • Happy Little Farmers 小小農夫～幸福田園Plant your own cherry tomato or mint, keep a "Farmer's Diary," and learn natural ways to keep pests away.
Theme 3
Our Town: Art & Adventure Field Trips
社區創生與美學探索
  • Pottery Party at Tianzhong Kiln 藝起樂陶陶Visit a real pottery studio, see how the giant kiln works, and make your own clay masterpiece.
  • Sweet Pretzel Twists & Storytelling 麻花蜜語Learn the story of Mahua, make your own, and create an English picture story to share online.
  • Weather Detectives: HSR Adventure 氣象行者～背包探旅Visit the Changhua HSR Weather Station and see the radar and satellites that keep fast trains safe.

Sweet Twist · 蜜麻花
田中蜜麻花總店參觀 · 向外國旅客介紹家鄉點心
A Dialogue · 購買對話
Shopkeeper 店員
Hello! Welcome to Tianzhong! May I help you?
你好！歡迎來到田中！我能幫您嗎？
Visitor 旅客
Hello! Yes, please. I'm looking for the famous Sweet Twist.
你好！是的，謝謝。我在找有名的蜜麻花。
Shopkeeper 店員
Ah, Sweet Twist! You found it! This is our famous snack.
啊，蜜麻花！您找到了！這是我們有名的點心。
Visitor 旅客
It looks interesting! What does it taste like?
它看起來很有趣！嚐起來怎麼樣？
Shopkeeper 店員
It is crispy and sweet. Do you want to try a flavor? We have original, peanut, sesame, and scallion.
它很酥脆，而且很甜。要不要試試口味？我們有原味、花生、芝麻和香蔥。
Visitor 旅客
Peanut flavor sounds great! Mmm, this is delicious! I would like to buy one bag, please.
花生口味聽起來很棒！嗯，這很美味！我想要買一包，謝謝。
Shopkeeper 店員
Okay! One bag of peanut Sweet Twist. That will be 100 dollars.
好的！一包花生蜜麻花。總共是 100 元。
Visitor 旅客
Here you go. Thank you very much!
給您。非常感謝！
Useful Sentences · 實用造句
Sweet Twist is a famous snack from Tianzhong.
蜜麻花是田中著名的點心。
The sweetness comes from malt sugar.
這個甜味來自麥芽糖。
It has a beautiful golden color.
它有著美麗的金黃色。
I like the crunchy texture of Sweet Twist.
我喜歡蜜麻花鬆脆的口感。
This Sweet Twist is not sticky at all.
這個蜜麻花一點也不黏牙。
The scallion flavor is both salty and sweet.
香蔥口味是鹹甜的。
Sweet Twist is handmade by local people.
蜜麻花是由當地人手工製作的。
This is a great souvenir from Tianzhong.
這是田中很棒的伴手禮。

Weather Station · 氣象站
田中氣象站之旅 · 認識氣象工具，當個小小氣象播報員
A Dialogue · 氣象站之旅
Teacher 老師
Welcome to the weather station! Look at this. What is it?
歡迎來到氣象站！看看這是什麼？
Xiao Ming 小明
It's a thermometer! It tells us how hot or cold it is.
這是一個溫度計！它告訴我們有多熱或多冷。
Teacher 老師
Great job! And see that tall thing? It shows wind direction.
很棒！看到那個高高的東西了嗎？它顯示風向。
Xiao Hua 小華
Oh, is that a wind vane?
喔，那是風向計嗎？
Teacher 老師
Yes! And those spinning cups measure wind speed. This big tube collects rain — it's a rain gauge.
對！那些旋轉的杯子測量風速。這個大管子收集雨水——它是雨量筒。
Xiao Hua 小華
Wow! So many tools to check the weather!
哇！好多工具可以測量天氣！
Teacher 老師
Yes! This weather station helps us know the weather forecast. Let's explore more!
對！這個氣象站幫助我們了解天氣預報。我們繼續探索吧！
Useful Sentences · 氣象行者實用句
It's a sunny day.
今天是個晴朗的日子。
The sky is cloudy.
天空多雲。
It's very windy today.
今天風很大。
Look! A beautiful rainbow!
看！一道美麗的彩虹！
We use a thermometer to check the temperature.
我們用溫度計來量溫度。
The wind vane shows wind direction.
風向計顯示風的方向。
The weather station helps us know the weather.
氣象站幫助我們了解天氣。
Let's check the weather before we go out.
在我們出去之前，先看看天氣吧！

High-Speed Rail · 高鐵站
田中高鐵站導覽 · 從售票口到月台的英語
A Dialogue · 高鐵對話
Xiao-Ming 小明
Wow, Mom! Look! The high-speed train is here!
哇！媽媽，你看！高鐵來了耶！
Mama 媽媽
Yes, Xiao-Ming, this is the High-Speed Rail we're taking today.
對啊，小明，這就是我們今天要搭的高鐵。
Xiao-Ming 小明
Does it go super fast?
它跑得會很快嗎？
Mama 媽媽
It sure does! The HSR goes very, very fast, much faster than driving.
會喔！高鐵跑得非常快，比開車還要快很多。
Xiao-Ming 小明
That's so cool! What's it like inside? Are there toilets?
好酷喔！那高鐵裡面是什麼樣子？有廁所嗎？
Mama 媽媽
Yes, the HSR is very clean inside. Every car has seats, and there are toilets too.
有啊，高鐵裡面很乾淨，每個車廂都有座位，也有廁所。
Xiao-Ming 小明
Awesome! I'm so excited to ride the HSR!
太棒了！我好期待搭高鐵喔！
Useful Sentences · 高鐵實用句
High-speed rail is very fast! We call it HSR for short.
高鐵跑得很快！我們簡稱它為 HSR。
We are at the HSR station. It is very big and clean.
我們在高鐵站。車站又大又乾淨。
Look at the ticket counter. We need to buy a ticket.
看！那是售票口。我們需要買票。
Let's find our car number. This is Car 5.
我們去找車廂號碼。這是第五車廂。
Please find your seat. Put your bag on the rack.
請找到你的座位。把包包放在置物架上。
The train is moving! We are going so fast!
火車在移動了！我們開得好快喔！
There is a toilet on the train.
火車上有廁所。
We are almost there. Let's get off the train.
我們快到了。我們下火車吧。
