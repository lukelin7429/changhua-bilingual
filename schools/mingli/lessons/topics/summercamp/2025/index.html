What We Explored

這一年的社區創生與美學體驗

From rolling sushi and twisting sweet pastry to brewing eco-enzyme and shaping Tianzhong pottery, students learned that beauty and sustainability live right here in their own town — and that English is the tool to share it with the world.

從捲壽司、做甜麻花，到製作環保酵素、捏田中陶藝，孩子們發現美與永續就在自己的家鄉——而英語，正是把它分享給世界的工具。
