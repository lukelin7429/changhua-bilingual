Three Camp Themes

三大課程主題 · 從數位通訊、科學實驗到社區踏查
Theme 1
Digital Comms Station
數位通訊站 · 科技英語力大探索
  • E-Planting & Digital Food Trends E 齊種菜～數位食尚Read picture books about where our food comes from, design a healthy plate, and explore how smart farming feeds a healthy future.
  • Reading E-Paradise: Digital Stories 耕讀 E 樂園～數位故事創作Use e-books and tablets to learn about the environment, then write and share digital stories of your own.
  • English Happy Go! Farm to Table 英文 Happy Go！An immersive English zone — from soil to table, plant, cook and talk, all in English.
Theme 2
Science Lab
科學實驗室 · 餐桌永續與未來探究
  • Climate Change Observers 氣候變遷觀察家～餐桌危機與數據分析Read simple weather charts, use Qblock / Quno sensors to track temperature and humidity, and design a smart heat alarm.
  • Food Science Travelers 食物科學旅行家～從種子到美味料理實作Cook with local Tianzhong rice, watch the science of cooking, and master kitchen-safety rules.
  • Sustainable Mini-Farm 永續農耕小農場～友善環境的實踐哲學Plant your own veggies, test the soil's pH, and build a compost corner for a greener campus.
Theme 3
Local Guide Team
在地嚮導團 · 社區美學與文化踏查
  • Community Artisans Decoded 社區職人解密～在地產業英語導覽Interview local makers in English and build a QR-code guide card for their craft.
  • Old Street House Detectives 街角老屋偵探～田中老街美學印記Hunt for hidden patterns and carvings along Tianzhong's historic old street.
  • Heritage Flavor Map 傳承美味地圖～在地好味道手作體驗Make traditional peanut candy by hand and design a new healthy flavor.
  • Pottery & Cat Village 陶藝與貓村探秘～田中窯美學創作Shape your own clay art at Tianzhong Kiln and explore the cat-painting village.

Local Artisans · 社區職人
社區職人解密 · 用英語訪談在地職人，做一張 QR Code 導覽卡
A Dialogue · 訪談對話
Student 學生
Hello! May I ask you a few questions about your work?
你好！我可以請問您幾個關於工作的問題嗎？
Artisan 職人
Of course! Welcome. What would you like to know?
當然可以！歡迎。你想知道什麼呢？
Student 學生
How long have you been doing this job?
您做這份工作多久了？
Artisan 職人
I have been doing this for over thirty years.
我已經做了超過三十年了。
Student 學生
Wow! What do you love most about your craft?
哇！您最喜歡這門工藝的哪一點？
Artisan 職人
I love making things with my own hands. It takes patience.
我喜歡用自己的雙手做東西，這需要耐心。
Student 學生
Thank you! May I take a photo of your tools?
謝謝您！我可以拍一張您工具的照片嗎？
Artisan 職人
Sure! Please come and see how it works.
當然！請過來看看它怎麼運作。
Useful Sentences · 實用造句
We are interviewing a local artisan today.
我們今天要採訪一位在地職人。
This is a traditional craft in Tianzhong.
這是田中的傳統工藝。
The artisan works with great care.
這位職人工作得非常用心。
Craftsmanship takes many years to learn.
匠人精神需要許多年才能學會。
Can you show me how you make it?
您可以示範您怎麼製作嗎？
We recorded the interview on our tablet.
我們用平板把訪談錄下來。
Let's make a QR code guide card.
我們來做一張 QR Code 導覽卡。
I am proud of the artisans in my hometown.
我以家鄉的職人為榮。

Old Street Houses · 田中老街
街角老屋偵探 · 找出老街建築的歷史圖案與美學印記
A Dialogue · 老街踏查
Teacher 老師
Look up at this old house. What do you see?
抬頭看看這棟老屋。你看到了什麼？
Student 學生
I see beautiful patterns on the wall!
我看到牆上有美麗的圖案！
Teacher 老師
Yes! This is a Japanese-era street house. Those are scrollwork patterns.
對！這是一棟日治時期的街屋，那些是捲草紋。
Student 學生
Why did people make these patterns?
人們為什麼要做這些圖案呢？
Teacher 老師
They showed the family's hopes and good luck.
它們代表了家族的願望與好運。
Student 學生
Can I take a photo with the nine-grid composition?
我可以用九宮格構圖拍一張照片嗎？
Teacher 老師
Great idea! Let's record its location on the map too.
好主意！我們也把它的位置記在地圖上吧。
Student 學生
This old street is like a history book!
這條老街就像一本歷史書！
Useful Sentences · 實用造句
This is an old street house from the Japanese era.
這是一棟日治時期的街屋。
Look at the patterns on the building.
看看建築上的圖案。
The arch above the door is very old.
門上的拱很古老。
We use a map to find the oldest houses.
我們用地圖找最古老的房子。
I like the animal carving on the wall.
我喜歡牆上的動物雕飾。
Let's sketch this beautiful window.
我們來素描這扇美麗的窗戶。
Each pattern tells a story.
每個圖案都有一個故事。
Old buildings keep our town's memory.
老建築保存了我們小鎮的記憶。

Peanut Candy · 花生糖手作
傳承美味地圖 · 親手做花生糖，設計一款新口味
A Dialogue · 手作對話
Teacher 老師
Today we will make peanut candy. Are you ready?
今天我們要做花生糖。準備好了嗎？
Student 學生
Yes! It smells so sweet!
準備好了！聞起來好甜喔！
Teacher 老師
First, we heat the malt syrup until it is golden.
首先，我們把麥芽糖加熱到金黃色。
Student 學生
Then do we add the peanuts?
然後我們加入花生嗎？
Teacher 老師
That's right. Stir them gently and quickly.
沒錯。輕輕地、快快地攪拌。
Student 學生
Now it is getting hard. Can we cut it?
現在它變硬了。我們可以切了嗎？
Teacher 老師
Yes, press it flat and cut it into small pieces.
可以，把它壓平，再切成小塊。
Student 學生
Mmm, my own peanut candy is delicious!
嗯，我自己做的花生糖好好吃！
Useful Sentences · 實用造句
Peanut candy is a traditional snack.
花生糖是傳統的點心。
We heat the malt syrup first.
我們先加熱麥芽糖。
Add the roasted peanuts and stir.
加入烤好的花生並攪拌。
Be careful, the syrup is very hot.
小心，糖漿很燙。
Press the candy flat before it cools.
在它冷卻前把糖壓平。
Cut the candy into small pieces.
把糖切成小塊。
We can design a new healthy flavor.
我們可以設計新的健康口味。
This sweet treat goes well with tea.
這個甜點很適合配茶。

Pottery & Cat Village · 田中窯與貓村
陶藝與貓村探秘 · 在田中窯捏一件陶藝，走讀貓村壁畫
A Dialogue · 陶藝體驗
Guide 導覽員
Welcome to Tianzhong Kiln! This is where we make pottery.
歡迎來到田中窯！這裡是我們做陶藝的地方。
Student 學生
Wow, the kiln is so big! How hot does it get?
哇，這個窯好大！它會燒到多熱呢？
Guide 導覽員
Very hot! The fire makes the clay strong and hard.
非常熱！火讓黏土變得堅固又硬。
Student 學生
Can we make something today?
我們今天可以做東西嗎？
Guide 導覽員
Of course. Let's shape the clay on the pottery wheel.
當然。我們來在拉坯機上塑造黏土吧。
Student 學生
Look, I made a little bowl!
你看，我做了一個小碗！
Guide 導覽員
Well done! Later we can walk to the cat village.
做得好！等一下我們可以走去貓村。
Student 學生
I love the cat paintings on the walls!
我好喜歡牆上的貓咪畫！
Useful Sentences · 實用造句
This is a famous pottery studio in Tianzhong.
這是田中有名的陶藝工坊。
The artist shapes the clay with both hands.
陶藝師用雙手塑造黏土。
The kiln makes the pottery hard and strong.
窯讓陶器變得堅硬。
I am making my own clay bowl.
我正在做自己的陶碗。
The cat village has many wall paintings.
貓村有許多牆上的畫。
We take photos of the cute cat murals.
我們拍下可愛的貓咪壁畫。
Art and our community grow together.
藝術和我們的社區一起成長。
I will give my pottery to my family.
我會把我的陶器送給家人。
