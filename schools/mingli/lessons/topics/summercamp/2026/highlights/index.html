2026 Summer Camp News Report夏日樂學活動新聞報導
Theme 1 · Digital Comms Station
數位通訊站 · 科技英語力大探索
🥬
E-Planting & Digital Food TrendsE 齊種菜～數位食尚
Students rolled up their sleeves in the field, growing their own vegetables and turning the harvest into digital food content.孩子們捲起袖子下田，體驗從種菜到數位食尚拍攝的樂趣。
📖
Reading E-Paradise: Digital Stories耕讀 E 樂園～數位故事創作
Using tablets, students captured their ideas and created their own digital stories.孩子們用平板記錄想法，創作屬於自己的數位故事。
🌾
English Happy Go! Farm to Table英文 Happy Go！
Camp kicked off with icebreaker games and team challenges that got everyone bonding fast.透過破冰活動與分組闖關遊戲，孩子們迅速熟悉彼此，展開夏日樂學的第一步。
Theme 2 · Science Lab
科學實驗室 · 餐桌永續與未來探究
🌦️
Climate Change Observers氣候變遷觀察家～餐桌危機與數據分析
Using data analysis, students explored how climate change is reshaping our meals — and our environment.透過數據分析，孩子們思考氣候變遷如何影響我們的餐桌與環境。
🍳
Food Science Travelers食物科學旅行家～從種子到美味料理實作
From seed to table, students cooked up their own dishes while exploring the science behind their food.孩子們從種子開始，動手實作美味料理，用科學角度看食物的旅程。
🐉
Sustainable Mini-Farm永續農耕小農場～友善環境的實踐哲學
Students explored the philosophy of eco-friendly farming — and turned dragonfruit and mango into their own fruit jellies.孩子們體驗友善環境的耕作哲學，並親手把火龍果與芒果做成好吃的果凍。
Theme 3 · Local Guide Team
在地嚮導團 · 社區美學與文化踏查
🛠️
Community Artisans Decoded社區職人解密～在地產業英語導覽
Students became junior tour guides, using English to interview local craftspeople and learn the stories behind their trades.孩子們化身小小導覽員，用英語向社區職人請益，認識在地產業的故事。
🏘️
Old Street House Detectives街角老屋偵探～田中老街美學印記
Students walked Tianzhong's historic old street, uncovering the aesthetic details hidden in its old buildings.孩子們走讀田中老街，尋找老屋裡的美學印記。
🍬
Heritage Flavor Map傳承美味地圖～在地好味道手作體驗
Students made five-color rice balls and grass jelly by hand, tasting local flavors passed down through generations.孩子們動手做五色湯圓、仙草凍，品嚐在地好味道。
🏺
Pottery & Cat Village陶藝與貓村探秘～田中窯美學創作
At the Tianzhong kiln, students shaped their own ceramic creations.孩子們前進田中窯，展開陶藝與貓村探秘，捏塑專屬作品。
More Camp Days
更多樂學時光 · 科技教育與校外教學
💻
Tech Education Day科技教育
A special session introduced students to digital tools and new skills.一場特別的科技教育課程，讓孩子接觸數位工具與新知。
🎨
Chaya Art Museum Field Trip秀水創價美術館參訪
The class traveled to Xiushui Township to visit the Soka Art Museum, taking in a dose of art and culture.全班前往秀水鄉，參訪創價美術館，感受藝術的薰陶。
