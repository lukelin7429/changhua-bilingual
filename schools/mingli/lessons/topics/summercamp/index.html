Summer Camp

夏日樂學 · 三年的社區探索與農藝美學

Every summer, Mingli students step beyond the classroom — into local coffee shops, rice paddies, high-speed rail stations, and pottery studios — learning English through real experiences in their own community. Pick a year to explore.每年夏天，明禮的孩子走出教室——走進在地咖啡館、稻田、高鐵站與陶藝工坊，在真實的社區場景中學習英語。選一個年份，點進去看看。
