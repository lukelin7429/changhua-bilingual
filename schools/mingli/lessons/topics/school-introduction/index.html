Mingli School Introduction

學校英文介紹

A small, close-knit rural school of 81 students in Tianzhong — proud of its Eco School status, its own farmland, and its agricultural education.位於田中、81 位學生的小而緊密的鄉村小學——以生態學校、自有農田與食農教育為榮。
