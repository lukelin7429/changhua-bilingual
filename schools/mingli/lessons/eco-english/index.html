Green Flag Lessons

明禮生態英語 · 環境保護六課
Six simple lessons to protect our Earth — each with a handout and a quiz.
🌿 Taiwan-US Green Flag · 臺美生態學校綠旗
We earned the Green Flag — now let's learn it in English!
In December 2025, Mingli became a Taiwan-US Green Flag eco-school. These six English lessons turn that big achievement into words our students can use every day.
2025 年 12 月，明禮通過臺美生態學校綠旗認證。這六課把這份榮耀，化成孩子每天都用得上的英文。
Pick a lesson to review or take its quiz.
點選任一課，可進入講義頁複習，或直接做測驗。
