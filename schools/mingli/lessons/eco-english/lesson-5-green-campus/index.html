II.

Essential Vocabulary

必備 5 單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
🌱
Plant
/plɑːnt/ · v. / n.
種植 · 植物
To put a seed in the soil; also a living green thing.
"I plant a small tree today."
🌰
Seed
/siːd/ · n.
種子
A tiny thing that grows into a plant.
"Put the seed in the soil."
🟫
Soil
/sɔɪl/ · n.
土壤
The brown earth that plants grow in.
"Seeds grow in soft soil."
🌿
Grow
/ɡroʊ/ · v.
生長
To get bigger, little by little.
"Plants grow with sun and water."
🌳
Tree
/triː/ · n.
樹
A big tall plant with leaves and branches.
"The tree gives us cool shade."
III.

Say It!

句型練習 · 開口說說看
I plant
Action / 行動
Say what you put in the soil.
用「I plant a ___.」說出你種了什麼。
I plant a + seed / flower / tree
"I plant a seed."
"I plant a flower."
"I plant a tree."
Plants need
Care / 照顧
Say what plants need to grow.
用「Plants need ___.」說出植物需要什麼。
Plants need + sun and water
"Plants need sun."
"Plants need water."
"Plants need good soil."
IV.

What does a plant need?

植物需要什麼？
01
A little green seedling on the windowsill.
窗台上的小綠苗。
It needs sun and water to grow. 需要陽光和水。
02
Dry, hard ground with no water.
乾硬、沒有水的土。
Add water so the seed can grow. 加水，種子才會長。
03
A tiny seed in your hand.
手中的小種子。
Put it in the soil and let it grow. 放進土壤裡讓它長大。
V.

Class Game

課堂遊戲 · 一起動一動
Grow, Grow, Grow! · 長大長大
Seed to Tree · 從種子到大樹
Everyone crouches down like a tiny seed. As the teacher gives sun ☀️ and water 💧, slowly grow up tall into a big tree 🌳. 全班蹲下來當小種子。老師給陽光☀️和水💧，大家慢慢站高，長成一棵大樹🌳。
Example · 範例
Teacher: "Here comes the sun and the rain!"
Class: crouch → stand slowly 🌱→🌿→🌳
Class: "We grow! We are trees!"
