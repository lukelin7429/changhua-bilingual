Word 01
iPad (n.)
iPad
iPad is an example of a tablet.
iPad 是平板的一種。
The students are highlighting facts on their iPads.
學生們用 iPad 在畫重點。
Word 02
tablet (n.)
平板
The students use a tablet to study English.
同學們使用平板來學習英文。
There are many different ways to study English on a tablet.
有很多方式可以在平板上學習英文。
Word 03
desktop computer (n.)
桌上型電腦
There is a desktop computer in every classroom.
每間教室都有一台桌上型電腦。
The teacher is creating her lesson plan on the desktop computer.
這位老師正在桌機上製作她的教學計畫。
Word 04
connect (v.)
連接
Could you help me connect my computer to Wi-Fi?
你可以幫我的電腦連接Wi-Fi嗎？
The teacher connects the computer to a big TV screen.
這位老師將電腦連接到一台電視大螢幕。
Word 05
QR code (n.)
QR 碼
The teacher shows the QR code on the tablet.
老師將平板上的QR code秀給大家掃描。
The students scan the QR code to join the game.
學生們掃QR code加入線上遊戲。
Word 06
addition (n.)
加法
Addition is when you add numbers together.
加法是將數字加在一起。
One example of addition is 2 plus 3 equals 5.
加法的一個例子是2+3=5
Word 07
minus (prep.)
減
Minus means to take away.
減的意思是將數字減去。
Word 08
times (prep.)
乘
Times is repeated addition.
乘是重複的加法。
