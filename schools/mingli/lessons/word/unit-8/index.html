Word 01
take a nap (v.)
午休
After lunch, students take a nap at school.
午餐後，學生睡午覺。
Some students read a book instead of taking a nap after lunch.
有些學生看書而不睡覺。
Word 02
line up (v.)
排隊
The students line up to get a prize.
同學排隊領取獎品。
The students line up to have their heights measured.
同學排隊量身高。
Word 03
brush your teeth (v.)
刷牙
Please brush your teeth after lunch.
午餐後請刷牙。
Brushing your teeth can keep them healthy.
刷牙可以保持牙齒的健康。
Word 04
eye test (n.)
視力檢查
The students are doing an eye test.
學生正在做視力檢查。
During an eye test, you cover one eye and look at the letter "E".
視力檢查時，你蓋住一隻眼睛，然後看字母E的方向。
Word 05
height (n.)
身高
The nurse is measuring the students' heights.
護理師正在量學生的身高。
The school measures students' heights to track their growth.
學校量學生的身高來追蹤他們的成長。
Word 06
nurse (n.)
護士
The nurse is measuring the students' heights.
護理師正在量學生的身高。
The nurse is giving the students an eye test.
護理師正在為學生做視力檢查。
Word 07
upstairs/downstairs (adv.)
上下樓梯
Be careful when you walk upstairs or downstairs.
上下樓梯要小心。
You can hold onto the handrail when you walk upstairs or downstairs.
上下樓梯時可以握著扶手。
Word 08
observe (v.)
觀課
Some teachers are in the back of the classroom observing the class
有些老師在教室後面觀課。
Teachers improve their teaching skills by observing others' classes.
老師藉由觀課來提昇自己的教學技巧。
Word 09
professional development (n.)
教師專業進修
In Taiwan, all elementary schools have professional development on Wednesday afternoons.
台灣所有小學老師在週三下午有專業進修。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development.
老師在專業進修時學習新技能與教學策略。
Word 10
sink (n.)
洗手台
Wash your hands at the sink before you eat lunch.
在你吃午餐前要在洗手台洗手。
The students are washing the dirty cleaning cloths in the sink.
學生在洗手台洗髒抹布。
