Music, Tea & Culture

音樂・茶道・藝文

Word 01
ocarina (n.)
陶笛
These students are playing the ocarina.
這些學生正在吹陶笛。
The ocarina is a musical wind instrument.
陶笛是一種管樂器。
Word 02
Chinese flute (n.)
笛子
The Chinese flute is a woodwind instrument.
笛子是一種木管樂器。
To play the Chinese flute, you need good breath control and the right finger positions.
吹奏笛子需要良好的氣息控制和正確的手指位置。
Word 03
tea ceremony (n.)
茶道
The lady is showing the students how to perform a tea ceremony.
這位女士正在為學生示範茶道。
The students are sipping hot tea at the tea ceremony.
同學們在茶道會場中品嚐熱茶。
Word 04
tea culture (n.)
茶文化
In tea culture, the host prepares tea for the guests.
在茶文化中，主人為客人泡茶。
Tea culture is very important in Taiwan.
茶文化在台灣非常重要。
Word 05
exhibit (n.)
展覽
There is an exhibit in the school's auditorium.
學校禮堂有ㄧ個展覽。
The exhibit features students' artwork and writing.
這個展覽的內容是學生的藝術作品與文章。
