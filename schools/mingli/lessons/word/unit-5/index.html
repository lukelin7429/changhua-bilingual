Celebrations & Visitors

校慶與訪客

Word 01
anniversary (n.)
週年紀念
Mingli Elementary School celebrated its 80th anniversary with running races and games.
明禮國小以賽跑與趣味競賽慶祝八十週年校慶。
To mark the school's anniversary, the principal invited a lot of guests to the school.
為紀念學校校慶，校長邀請很多來賓到校。
Word 02
award presentation (n.)
頒獎
The winners of the running races get a prize at the award presentation.
在頒獎典禮中，賽跑得勝者得到獎品。
The award presentation is held at the end of the school's sports day.
頒獎典禮在學校運動會結束時進行。
Word 03
sign (v.)
簽名
The guests sign their names on the guestbook.
來賓在簽到簿上面簽名。
The volunteers help guests find where to sign their names.
志工幫助來賓尋找簽名的地方。
Word 04
check-in (n.)
檢核
The students say English sentences for their check-in.
學生們說英語句子進行檢核。
They get a stamp at the end of the check-in.
通過檢核，他們會得一個戳印（蓋章）。
Word 05
volunteer (n.)
志工
Every week volunteers come to the school to tell stories.
每週志工都會到學校講故事。
The volunteers teach students about a positive trait each week.
志工每週教導學生一個正向的人格特質。
Word 06
security guard (n.)
警衛
The security guard helps the students cross the street safely.
警衛協助同學們安全過馬路。
The security guard directs traffic in front of the school.
警衛在校門口指揮交通。
Word 07
tour (n.)
參觀
The visitors are given a tour of the school.
訪客參觀這個學校。
The garden is their favorite part of the tour.
菜園是他們參觀中最喜歡的地方。
Word 08
tour bus (n.)
遊覽車
The students are getting on the tour bus.
學生正在上遊覽車。
The students ride the tour bus to a museum.
同學搭乘遊覽車到博物館。
