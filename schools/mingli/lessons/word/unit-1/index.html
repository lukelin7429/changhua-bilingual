Farm, Garden & Scarecrow

食農與稻草人

Word 01
seedling (n.)
秧苗
The tractor prepares the rice paddy to plant rice seedlings.
拖拉機整田之後要播秧苗。
The rice seedlings are planted in the rice paddy.
秧苗種在稻田當中。
Word 02
okra seedling (n.)
秋葵苗
The woman is diligently planting okra seedlings in the soil.
婦女正認真地把秋葵苗種在土裡。
The okra seedlings are properly spaced and thoroughly covered with soil.
秋葵苗間距適當，並用土壤徹底覆蓋。
Word 03
taro (n.)
芋頭
The students mix taro flour and water with their hands.
學生們用手將芋頭粉和水混在一起。
The students work together to roll out many taro balls.
學生們合力做出很多芋圓。
Word 04
vegetable (n.)
蔬菜
There are a lot of vegetables on the school's farm.
學校農場很多的蔬菜。
The principal is harvesting some vegetables.
校長正在採收蔬菜。
Word 05
farm (v.)
種植、耕作
The students learn about farming at school.
學生們在學校學習農業耕作的知識。
The school has land to farm different crops.
學校有土地可以種植不同的作物。
Word 06
weed (v.)
除草
The volunteers are weeding the garden.
志願者們正在花園裡除草。
We need to weed the garden to keep it healthy.
我們需要給花園除草以保持它的健康。
Word 07
mow (v.)
割草
The man is mowing the grass.
這個人正在割草。
You need to mow the grass to keep it short.
您需要修剪草讓草維持短短的長度。
Word 08
repot (v.)
移植/換盆
The students are repotting the flowers.
學生們正在移植這些花。
Repotting is when you move a plant into a new pot.
換盆是指將植物移入新盆中。
Word 09
potted plants (n.)
盆栽
There are a lot of potted plants at this school.
這所學校有很多盆栽。
The student is watering the potted plants at school.
學生正在給學校的盆栽澆水。
Word 10
scarecrow (n.)
稻草人
The teacher shows how to make a scarecrow.
老師示範如何製作稻草人。
The students work together to make scarecrows.
同學們一起努力做稻草人。
Word 11
hay (n.)
乾草
The students tie the hay to make the scarecrow's arms and legs.
學生們綁稻草做成稻草人的手與腳。
The students stuff the hay in the scarecrow's clothes.
學生們將稻草塞進稻草人的衣服。
Word 12
pole (n.)
桿
The teacher sticks a pole in the ground.
老師把竹竿插在地上。
Poles are used to make a scarecrow's body.
竹竿被用來當成稻草人的身體。
Word 13
instruction (n.)
指示說明
The teacher gives instructions on making scarecrows.
老師指導製作稻草人的方法。
The students listen to the instructions.
學生們聆聽說明。
