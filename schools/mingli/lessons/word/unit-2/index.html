Arts, Pottery & Crafts

藝術與陶藝手作

Word 01
aroma stone (n.)
擴香石
The students make aroma stones in class.
學生在課堂上製作芳香石。
They precisely measure everything to make the aroma stones.
他們精確測量製作芳香石的一切。
Word 02
paint pouring (n.)
流體畫
Students and their parents do paint pouring together.
同學們和他們的家長一起創作流體畫。
Paint pouring uses acrylic paint to make a design.
流體畫使用塑膠彩來設計圖形。
Word 03
artist (n.)
藝術家
The artist works with clay.
藝術家使用粘土進行創作。
There are many different things an artist can create.
藝術家可以創造許多不同的東西。
Word 04
balloon (n.)
氣球
The teacher is showing the students how to make balloon art.
這位老師正在為學生示範如何製作氣球藝術。
You can make many different animals with balloosn.
你能用氣球做很多不同的動物。
Word 05
pottery (n.)
陶器
Pottery is a difficult art to learn.
陶藝是一門難學的藝術。
It takes a lot of skills to create pottery.
製作作陶器需要很多技巧。
Word 06
pottery wheel (n.)
陶輪
Pottery wheel is used to create useful objects.
陶輪用於製造有用的物品。
Cups and vases are made using a pottery wheel.
可以用陶輪製作杯子和花瓶。
Word 07
sewing machine (n.)
縫紉機
The teacher helps the students use a sewing machine.
老師幫助學生使用縫紉機。
The student practices using a sewing machine.
學生練習使用縫紉機。
Word 08
mural (n.)
壁畫
A mural is a large painting on a wall.
壁畫是指牆壁上大型的圖畫。
Look at the colorful mural on the wall. It's so pretty.
你看牆上五顏六色的壁畫，真是漂亮啊！
Word 09
bake (v.)
烘焙
The students are baking sweet potatoes.
學生們正在烤蕃薯。
They will eat the sweet potatoes after they're done baking.
他們烤完之後便能享用蕃薯。
