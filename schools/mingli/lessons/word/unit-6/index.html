In the Classroom

教室裡的英文

Word 01
blackboard (n.)
黑板
The students are writing their English names on the blackboard.
學生們正在黑板上寫下他們的英文名字。
The boy is erasing the blackboard.
這位男孩正在擦拭黑板。
Word 02
magnet (n.)
磁鐵
The teacher is attaching a poster to the blackboard with magnets.
老師正在用磁鐵將海報貼到黑板上。
The student is attaching some information to the blackboard using magnets.
學生正在使用磁鐵將一些資訊貼到黑板上。
Word 03
pencil case (n.)
鉛筆盒
A pencil case holds school supplies.
鉛筆盒可以放文具。
The girl is putting her school supplies into her pencil case.
這位小女孩正在把她的文具放進鉛筆盒裡。
Word 04
pencil sharpener (n.)
削鉛筆機
When your pencil becomes dull, you need a pencil sharpener.
當你的鉛筆變鈍時，你需要一台削鉛筆機。
The student is sharpening his pencil with a pencil sharpener.
這位學生正在用削鉛筆機削鉛筆。
Word 05
worksheet (n.)
學習單
The teacher is handing out worksheets to her students.
老師把學習單發給學生。
The students are turning in their worksheets to the teacher.
同學們繳交學習單給老師。
Word 06
workbook (n.)
習作本
The students are writing in their workbooks.
學生們正在寫習作。
The teacher is checking the students' workbooks.
老師正在改學生的習作。
Word 07
stamp (n.)
蓋章、點數
The teacher gives stamps to students for finishing their work.
老師給完成作業的學生蓋章。
The students can use their stamps to get a prize.
學生可以使用他們的印章獲得獎品。
Word 08
raise your hand (v.)
舉手
Raise your hand if you have a question.
如果你有問題可以舉手發問。
Raise your hand if you need to go to the bathroom.
如果你需要上廁所可以舉手。
Word 09
social studies (n.)
社會科
The students are in social studies class.
同學們正在上社會課。
The teacher teaches about religions in social studies class.
老師在社會課教導學生有關宗教的知識。
