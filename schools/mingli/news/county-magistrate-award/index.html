Magistrate's Award & Our Green Showcase114 學年度畢業生縣長獎頒獎典禮 · 章顯綠意，永續相隨

At this year's Magistrate's Award ceremony for graduating students, Mingli Elementary set up one of the most popular booths in the hall. Our theme was "Showcasing Green, Sustainability Follows."在今年的畢業生縣長獎頒獎典禮上，明禮國小設置了全場最受歡迎的展攤之一，主題是「章顯綠意，永續相隨」。

The whole booth was built around the idea of net-zero green living. Visitors could try hands-on activities, look at art made from recycled materials, and learn how a circular economy turns "rubbish" back into something useful.整個展攤以「淨零綠生活」為核心，民眾可以動手體驗、欣賞再生材料做成的作品，並了解「循環經濟」如何把「垃圾」變回有用的東西。

The County Magistrate came to the booth in person and joined our students and their families in the green activities. A graduation is a big milestone in a young person's life — and for this new generation, caring for the environment is one of the most important skills of all.縣長親自蒞臨展攤，與學生和家長一起體驗綠色活動。畢業是孩子人生的重要里程碑，而對這個世代來說，愛護環境正是最重要的能力之一。

From low-carbon crafts made by the students themselves to creative recycling installations, the booth showed that learning does not only happen in the classroom — it happens in every choice we make in daily life. The Magistrate's Award honors not just good grades, but good character and all-round growth.從學生親手做的低碳工藝品，到充滿創意的環保再生裝置，展攤證明了學習不只發生在教室裡，更發生在生活中的每一個選擇。縣長獎肯定的不只是好成績，更是好品德與全面的成長。

As our graduates step into the next stage of their lives, they carry with them a heart full of green hope and responsibility — ready to walk hand in hand with this land toward a greener, more liveable future.當畢業生邁向人生下一個階段，他們帶著滿滿的綠意希望與責任，準備好與這片土地攜手，共創更綠、更宜居的未來。
