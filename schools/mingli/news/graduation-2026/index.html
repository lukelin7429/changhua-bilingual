Words for Our Graduates給畢業生的祝福 · 校長與畢業班導師的話

Today is graduation day at Mingli Elementary. After six years of learning, laughter, and growing up together, our Class of 2026 is ready to take its next step. Before they go, our principal and their homeroom teacher have a few words from the heart. 今天是明禮國小的畢業典禮。經過六年的學習、歡笑與一同成長，2026 屆的畢業生即將邁出下一步。在離開之前，校長與畢業班導師有幾句發自內心的話想對你們說。

🎓
A Message from the Principal
Our Principal校長的祝福

Dear graduates, hello to you all. I am so happy that your six years of learning here at Mingli have brought you to this day — at last, it is time to graduate. I believe that all the little moments Mingli has given you over these six years will stay with you for the rest of your lives.各位畢業生大家好，很開心你們在明禮國小六年的學習走到今天，終於要畢業了。相信這六年來明禮國小帶給你們的點點滴滴，會讓你們銘記一輩子。

The hard work of every teacher here, the teamwork and kindness you have shared with your classmates, and the love that your parents and the whole Mingli family have given you — I am certain you will keep all of it in your heart for a lifetime.這裡每個老師的付出、同學之間的合作幫忙，以及家長和所有明禮國小師生們對您的關愛，我相信您都將一輩子牢記於心。

I hope you will carry everyone's blessings with you, and in the next chapter of your learning, study even harder and give it your whole heart. And when you grow up, I hope you will find the chance to give back — to society, and to the school that raised you.也希望您帶著所有人對您的這一份祝福，在下一個學習旅程中更加認真、更加努力。長大以後有機會可以回饋社會、回饋母校。

To every one of our graduates: may you soar far and high, in good health and good fortune, every step of the way. Thank you.祝福各位畢業生鵬程萬裡，一切健康順利，謝謝。

— With warm wishes, Your Principal明禮國小校長 敬上
🌱
A Message from the Homeroom Teacher
Teacher Hsin-Fang畢業班導師 馨方老師

And now, a blessing from your homeroom teacher. I am Teacher Hsin-Fang, the teacher who has walked beside you for these two years. In our two years together, there have been hard times and happy times, sweat and tears — we lived through it all and grew up together, and I have learned so very much from you.再來是畢業班導師的祝福。我是帶你們兩年的馨方老師。在兩年的過程當中，有苦有笑，有汗水也有淚水，與你們一起共同經歷、成長，學習了很多。

I wish you a future in which you spread your wings and fly high, each of you toward your own bright road ahead. I hope you will discover your own strengths and talents, give everything you have, contribute to the world around you, and grow into someone who truly makes a difference.祝福你們未來展翅高飛，有各自美好的前程。希望你們未來能找到自己的長才優勢，發揮所能、貢獻社會，成為一個社會上有用的人。

Thank you for working so hard together with your teachers over these two years. Tomorrow, at last, you graduate. Blessings to you all!感謝你們這兩年跟老師們一起共同努力，明天終於要畢業了，祝福大家！

— With love, Teacher Hsin-Fang畢業班導師 馨方老師
Congratulations, Class of 2026. Fly far — and remember the way home.
以上就是校長以及畢業班導師的祝福。恭喜 2026 屆畢業生——勇敢飛翔，也別忘了回家的路。🎓
