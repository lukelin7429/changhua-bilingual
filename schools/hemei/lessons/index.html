Ways to Learn

從校園生活學英文

Real school, real words.

每支影片、每份教材，都取材自和美真實的校園日常。

23 Videos · 5 Themes
Course 01
Word of the Day
每日一字

23 bilingual videos filmed around the Hemei campus, grouped into five themes — from the sports field and the music room to a day at school and a trip out of the gate. Each word comes with two example sentences taken from the video.23 支雙語影片，全部在和美校園實地拍攝，分為五大主題——從運動場、音樂教室，到校園的一天與走出校門。每個單字都附兩句取自影片說明欄的例句。

Start Learning →
30 Episodes · For Teachers
Course 02
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers, taught by Sarah Thomas across 10 topics and 30 episodes — from greetings and roll call to exam instructions. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.給老師使用的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學，共 10 大主題、30 支影片——從問候點名到考試指令。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

Start Learning →
13 Episodes · By Office
Course 03
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

"Broadcast English" — thirteen short episodes in which each school office and section introduces, in English, the kind of announcement it makes over the PA system, from the curriculum section to the counselling room.「廣播英語」——13 支短片，由各處室、各組用英語介紹平時透過校園廣播宣布的內容，依教務處（5）、學務處（4）、總務處（1）、輔導室（3）四處室分類。
