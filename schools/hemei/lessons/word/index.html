On the Field & in the Gym
運動場上 · 7 words
WORD 01
table tennis n.
桌球
The students practice playing table tennis.
學生們練習打桌球。
Table tennis is a popular sport in Taiwan.
桌球是台灣流行的運動。
WORD 02
dodgeball n.
躲避球
The students throw balls at each other during the dodgeball game.
在躲避球賽中，同學們用球互丟。
The students get ready to play dodgeball by putting on pads.
同學們穿上護具準備打躲避球。
WORD 03
basketball n.
籃球
Students love to play basketball during recess.
學生們喜歡在課間休息時打籃球。
A pick-up game is an on-the-spot game for basketball.
鬥牛賽是籃球當場臨時組隊的比賽。
WORD 04
roller-blade v.
溜直排輪
You need to wear a helmet when you roller-blade.
當你溜直排輪時需要戴頭盔。
The students practice roller-blading.
學生練習溜直排輪。
WORD 05
climb v.
攀爬
The student climbs up.
學生往上攀爬。
The girl climbs over the ladder.
女孩在攀爬架上爬行。
WORD 06
climb trees v.
爬樹
The students use special equipment to climb trees.
學生們使用特殊設備爬樹。
The instructors teach students how to climb the tree safely.
指導員教學生如何安全地爬樹。
WORD 07
pedal v.
踩踏板、騎（腳踏車）
The students are pedaling.
學生們正在騎腳踏車。
You pedal to go forward.
你踩著踏板向前行進。

Music, Art & Performance
藝術與表演 · 5 words
WORD 08
violin n.
小提琴
The violin is a string instrument.
小提琴是一種弦樂器。
The students are practicing the violin at school.
同學們在學校練習拉小提琴。
WORD 09
calligraphy n.
書法
The boys and girls are learning traditional Chinese calligraphy.
男孩和女孩正在學習中國傳統書法。
To get good at calligraphy, you have to have a lot of patience!
要想練好書法，就得有足夠的耐心！
WORD 10
Chinese yo-yo n.
扯鈴
The Chinese yo-yo is a popular toy many children enjoy.
扯鈴是很受小孩子喜歡的玩具。
The students perform tricks with Chinese yo-yos.
同學們用扯鈴表演特技。
WORD 11
drumstick n.
鼓槌
The performers are using wooden drumsticks to beat the drums.
表演者用木製的鼓槌打鼓。
Each drummer has their own pair of drumsticks.
每個鼓手都有自己的一對鼓槌。
WORD 12
balloon n.
氣球
The artist makes cute characters out of balloons.
藝術家用氣球製作可愛的角色。
The balloons are all different and beautiful colors.
氣球有各種不同又美麗的顏色。

A Day at School
校園的一天 · 6 words
WORD 13
slide n.
溜滑梯
I go down the slide.
我從溜滑梯上滑下去。
I have fun on the slide.
我喜歡玩溜滑梯。
WORD 14
morning assembly n.
朝會
The students gather for the morning assembly.
學生們集合參加朝會。
The students play music at the morning assembly.
學生們在朝會上演奏音樂。
WORD 15
sweep v.
打掃
Every day, students sweep away fallen leaves on school grounds.
每天學生打掃校園的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.
台灣的學生打掃校園，保持校園的乾淨整齊。
WORD 16
type v.
打字
The students type on the computer.
學生們在電腦上打字。
The students type using a keyboard.
學生們用鍵盤打字。
WORD 17
apron n.
圍裙
The teacher helps the student tie her apron.
老師幫學生繫好圍裙。
The girl wears an apron.
這個小女孩穿著圍裙。
WORD 18
professional development n.
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都有給老師的專業進修課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
老師在專業進修課程中學習新技能與教學策略。

Everyday Words & Actions
生活用語與動作 · 3 words
WORD 19
wash your hands phr.
洗手
It’s important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
吃東西之前，一定要用肥皂和水洗手。
WORD 20
table n.
桌子
The students bring food over to the table.
學生們把食物端到餐桌上。
The boy sits at the table.
小男孩坐在桌子前。
WORD 21
pretend v.
假裝
The students pretend to cook food.
學生們假裝做飯。
The boy pretends to eat.
小男孩假裝吃東西。

Out and About
走出校園 · 2 words
WORD 22
flea market n.
跳蚤市場
The kids operate their own flea market at their school.
孩子們在學校經營自己的跳蚤市場。
They sell second-hand goods at the flea market.
他們在跳蚤市場出售二手商品。
WORD 23
crosswalk n.
斑馬線
A crosswalk is where you can safely cross the street.
斑馬線是可以安全過馬路的地方。
Pedestrians have the right of way in a crosswalk.
在斑馬線上，行人有優先通行權。
