A New Semester Begins: Becoming a Better Version of Ourselves

115 學年度上學期始業式｜新學期，一起成為更好的自己！
←Back to News回到新聞總覽
全校學生參加始業式
始業式上的防疫宣導

Today was the opening ceremony for the new semester. Each office shared its key reminders for the term ahead, and the school echoed Friendly Campus Week, asking the children to build a safe, warm place to learn through respect, care and kindness.

今天是 115 學年度上學期的始業式。各處室透過始業式進行新學期重要事項宣導，也響應「友善校園週」，提醒孩子們用尊重、關懷與友善，一起打造安全、溫暖的學習環境。

The principal gave the whole school three things. First: look after yourself. Come to school happy every day, stay safe, follow the rules, protect yourself and respect others.

校長送給全校小朋友三件事。第一件事：把自己照顧好。每天開開心心來上學，也要記得注意安全、遵守規定，保護自己，也尊重別人。

Second: keep your classmates in your heart. A “good morning”, a “thank you”, a “sorry” — simple words that make the campus warmer. Lend a hand when someone needs help, and when you disagree, learn to talk it through and understand each other.

第二件事：把同學放在心上。一句「早安」、一句「謝謝」、一句「對不起」，看似簡單，卻能讓校園更加溫暖。看到同學需要幫忙時，多伸出一隻手；意見不同時，學習好好說話、彼此理解。

Third: be brave enough to become a better you. Whatever your grades or talents are today, don’t underestimate yourself. Improve a little every day — try a little harder than yesterday, and you are already your best self.

第三件事：勇敢成為更好的自己。不管現在的成績如何、才藝如何，都不要小看自己。每天進步一點點，今天比昨天更努力一點，你就是最棒的自己！

School is not only a place to learn knowledge — it is a place where every child is seen, respected and encouraged. This semester, let’s learn hard, treat others kindly, take on challenges bravely, and grow up happy. The principal will be working alongside every child, too.

學校，不只是學習知識的地方，更是一個讓每一個孩子都被看見、被尊重、被鼓勵的地方。新學期，讓我們一起認真學習、友善待人、勇敢挑戰、快樂成長，校長也會和大家一起努力！
