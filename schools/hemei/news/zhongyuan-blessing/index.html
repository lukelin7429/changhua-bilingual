A Prayer for a Safe and Peaceful Campus

祈福納祥．校園平安
←Back to News回到新聞總覽
學校團隊一同上香祈福

On the day of the Zhongyuan Festival, the principal led the school team in the traditional ceremony, offering, with a respectful heart, a prayer to heaven and earth and paying respect to the forces that quietly watch over the campus.

中元節當天，校長帶領學校團隊一同進行中元普渡，以一份虔敬的心，向天地神明祈福，也向校園中默默守護的無形力量表達敬意。

The team prepared a generous table of offerings and lit incense, praying that as the new semester begins, the campus would be safe, staff and students healthy, teaching smooth and all things complete.

準備豐盛供品、虔誠上香，祈願新學期即將展開之際，校園平安、師生健康、教學順利、事事圓滿。

Every bit of the campus’s safety comes from everyone looking after it together — from the principal to every member of staff, working as one to build a happy campus where children can learn with a settled mind and grow up happy.

校園的每一份平安，都來自大家共同的守護；從校長到每一位教職員，大家齊心努力，只為打造一個讓孩子安心學習、快樂成長的幸福校園。

May this blessing turn into good wishes: peace for the Hemei campus, health and joy for every teacher and student, and a smooth, happy new semester for the children.

願這份祝福化作滿滿的祈願：佑護和美校園平安順遂、祝福全校師生健康喜樂、願孩子們天天快樂、平安長大。
