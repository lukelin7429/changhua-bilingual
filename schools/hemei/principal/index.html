I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Hemei Elementary School is led by Principal Ho Ling-ju, who was sworn in on 1 August 2026 as the school’s sixteenth principal. Her career has moved steadily from the classroom to school leadership, and along the way she has come to see three things as the foundation of a school’s progress: curriculum development, teacher professionalism and student learning.

和美國小由何鈴茹校長帶領，於民國 115 年 8 月 1 日就任，是和美國小第十六任校長。她的教育生涯，從教學現場一路走到學校領導；一路走來，她將課程發展、教師專業與學生學習視為學校進步的重要基礎。

Her starting point is a belief about children: give them love and light, and they will carry hope, and the courage to press forward.

她的起點，是一份對孩子的信念：給孩子愛與光芒，讓他擁有希望與奮力向前的勇氣。

Help every child succeed, support every teacher, join hands with the community, and make education whole.
成就孩子、支持老師、攜手社區、圓滿教育。
II.

Educational Philosophy

辦學理念

It begins with a shared belief, and ends in a shared achievement.

始於共同的信念，終於共同的成就。

共同信念 · 幸福同行
A shared belief — and walking the road together in happiness.
Principal Ho sees a school’s management strategy this way: it starts from what everyone believes together, and it is completed in what everyone achieves together. 何校長認為，學校的經營策略始於共同的信念，終於共同的成就。
對孩子 · For Children
Help Them Succeed
成就孩子——看見每個孩子的可能，陪他們一步步走到。
對老師 · For Teachers
Support Them
支持老師——把教師專業成長放在心上，讓老師被理解、被支持。
對社區 · For the Community
Join Hands
攜手社區——重視學校與家庭、社區之間的合作與連結。
對教育 · For Education
Make It Whole
圓滿教育——讓課程、教學與關懷彼此成全，成為完整的一件事。
III.

A Bell, and a Tender Heart

以鈴聲喚醒希望，以茹心灌溉未來

Her name carries the idea: a bell (鈴) and a nurturing heart (茹).

校長的名字裡就藏著這句話——「鈴」是鈴聲，「茹」是灌溉的心。

鈴聲啟智 · Awaken
Wake the Potential
以鈴聲喚醒孩子的潛能，讓學習從心裡被叫醒。
茹心育德 · Nurture
Grow Good Character
以灌溉的心滋養品格，讓好的價值慢慢長出來。
攜手同行 · Together
Flourish as One
攜手同行、共好共榮，一個人走得快，一群人走得遠。
專業領航 · Lead
Steady & Forward-Looking
以專業領航，追求創新與永續，把學校帶向未來。
Set sail with love, and build the future together
用愛啟航，共創未來

Principal Ho often puts her hope for Hemei in a single line: where there is love in education, Hemei has no limits.

何校長常把對和美的期待，濃縮成一句話：教育有愛，和美無限。

IV.

A Life in Education

個人經歷

From the teaching front line, to the county office, to the principal’s chair.

從教學現場，到縣府，到校長室。

From her earliest years in the classroom to school leadership, Principal Ho has kept building practical experience in education — and has kept curriculum, teachers and children at the centre of it.

從教學現場到學校領導，何校長持續累積教育實務經驗，並始終把課程、教師與孩子放在正中央。
