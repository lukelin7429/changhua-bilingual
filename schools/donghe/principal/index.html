I.

Meet Principal Tsai

認識蔡國吉校長

Tsai Kuo-chi is the principal of DongHe Elementary School. He went to school in Shetou Township, Changhua, at Shetou Elementary and Shetou Junior High, then trained as a teacher at National Pingtung Teachers College, where he also studied in the Department of Elementary Education. He later earned a master’s degree from the Graduate Institute of National Education at National Chiayi University.

蔡國吉校長求學於彰化縣社頭鄉的社頭國小、社頭國中，畢業於國立屏東師範學院普通科與初等教育學系，並取得國立嘉義大學國民教育研究所碩士學位。

Before coming to DongHe, he taught at Taihe Elementary School in Changhua City and Chaohsing Elementary School in Shetou, served in many school roles — from accounting to student discipline and general affairs — advised teachers on the county’s social studies teaching advisory team, and led Minjing Elementary School in Zhutang Township as principal.

到任東和之前，蔡校長曾任彰化市泰和國小、社頭鄉朝興國小教師，歷任會計、訓導組長、總務主任等職務，擔任彰化縣九年一貫課程社會領域教學輔導團兼任輔導員，並曾任竹塘鄉民靖國小校長。

Belief & Directions

辦學理念 · 教育信念、辦學方向與經營策略

Every school decision starts with one question: what is best for the child?

辦學理念：教育的一切，都從孩子出發。

“Without education, it is hard to have a future.”
人，沒有教育，難得未來

Education exists for the sake of the child — so education should give every child the best possible environment to become their best self.

教育存在以孩子為目的，教育就應提供孩子最大自我實現的環境。

IV.

My Promise

自我期許 · 教育初衷，念茲學生

Why we teach: always, the students.

自我期許～教育初衷，念茲學生。

“The sun gives warmth because it burns; a life earns respect because it serves.”
太陽因燃燒發熱而給人溫暖，人生因服務奉獻而受人尊重。

With humility and care, professional commitment and a warm heart for service, I carry both the honor and the mission of this role. I will lead our team to work together at their very best, listen closely to what our community needs from its school, and do all I can to meet each family’s hopes for their children’s learning — building an ideal school where everything is for the children, and the children come first in everything. Hand in hand with parents, teachers and students, let us create a bright future together.

我願以謙卑關懷、專業堅持、熱忱服務，肩擔榮譽與使命，帶領同仁發揮教育整體效能，全面瞭解社會對教育的需求，盡可能滿足每個人對教育的想望，積極構築「一切為孩子、為孩子一切」的理想校園，與親師生攜手共創未來美好願景。
