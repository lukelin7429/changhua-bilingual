Summer Fun Learning: All About Herbs

夏日樂學－香草主題課程

For · 對象

This summer, our fourth and fifth graders spent their Summer Fun Learning days in the world of herbs. With Teacher Li Yu-hsiu, they got to know herbs up close — then cooked with them, crafted with them, and even made their own herb field guide.

暑假的夏日樂學香草主題課程，由李玉琇老師帶領 4–5 年級的孩子認識香草，並用香草做料理、做手作，還完成自己的香草圖鑑小書。
