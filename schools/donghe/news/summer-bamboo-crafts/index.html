Summer Fun Learning: Bamboo Crafts

夏日樂學－竹編課程
←Back to News回到新聞列表
Date · 日期
July 30–31, 2026
2026 年 7 月 30–31 日
Teacher · 講師
Teacher Hsieh Ping-cheng
謝秉澄老師
For · 對象
Grades 1–3
1–3 年級

For two days of Summer Fun Learning, our first to third graders worked with bamboo. With Teacher Hsieh Ping-cheng, they got to know the bamboo plant, found out how bamboo is part of everyday life — and then made their own bamboo pinwheels and bamboo fans.

暑假的夏日樂學竹編課程，由謝秉澄老師帶領 1–3 年級的孩子認識竹子、了解竹子與生活，並親手完成竹風車與竹扇。
