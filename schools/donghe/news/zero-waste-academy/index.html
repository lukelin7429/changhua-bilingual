Zero Waste Academy: Building Green Habits for a Sustainable Life

「零廢棄學院」環保宣導活動　培養學童永續生活好習慣
←Back to News回到新聞列表
Date · 日期
October 6, 2026

Speaker · 講師
Teacher Chuang Chu-fang

For · 對象
Grades 1–6
1–6 年級學生

To help students love the environment and value our resources, DongHe held a “Zero Waste Academy” environmental education event. Through lively, fun activities, it showed children why reducing waste, recycling and reusing matter so much — and encouraged them to start with everyday life and become little guardians of the Earth.

為培養學生愛護環境、珍惜資源的觀念，學校舉辦「零廢棄學院」環保宣導活動，透過活潑有趣的宣導方式，帶領孩子認識垃圾減量、資源回收及再利用的重要性，鼓勵學生從日常生活做起，成為守護地球的小小環保尖兵。
