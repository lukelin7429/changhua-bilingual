Summer Fun Learning Field Trip: Plants in the Morning, Animals in the Afternoon

On August 6, our Summer Fun Learning group headed to Nantou for a day of learning outdoors. In the morning they studied plants at Hua Yu Lu Leisure Farm in Nantou City, and in the afternoon they met animals at Jiujiufeng Animal World in Caotun — plants in the morning, animals in the afternoon!

8 月 6 日，夏日樂學走出校園來到南投：上午在南投市花與綠休閒農場學植物，下午到草屯九九峰動物世界看動物——上午學植物、下午看動物！
