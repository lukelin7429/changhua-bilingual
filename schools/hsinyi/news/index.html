Spirit · 九年級加油

Praying for Our Ninth-Graders考生祈福 · 信義為九年級加油

2025 · 114 學年

Every spring, the whole Hsinyi community gathers to cheer on its graduating ninth-graders as they prepare for the national high school entrance exam. Teachers, classmates, and staff line the corridor to offer encouragement — a tradition that reminds every student they are never facing the challenge alone.

每年春天，全校師生聚在一起，為即將參加國中會考的九年級學長姐加油打氣。老師、同學、行政人員一字排開，傳遞鼓勵——這個傳統提醒每個孩子：面對挑戰，你從來不是一個人。

Achievement · 學生榮譽

Students Published in National Magazine學生作品登上智慧果雜誌

Jun 18, 2026 · 115 年 6 月 18 日

Students from Classes 2A, 3B, and 4D earned a remarkable honour: their artwork and written compositions were selected for publication in Smart Fruit Magazine (智慧果雜誌), Issue 166 — a national bilingual education journal. To see your work printed in a nationally distributed magazine is a powerful reminder that what happens in the Hsinyi classroom reaches far beyond its walls.

二甲、三乙、四丁的同學們獲得了難得的殊榮：他們的美術與文字作品入選全國性雙語教育期刊《智慧果雜誌》第 166 期，正式對外發行。能在全國發行的雜誌上看到自己的作品，是一個有力的提醒——信義教室裡發生的事，能夠傳遞到遠方。

Competition · 語文競賽

Language Competition Double Honour語文競賽雙榜

Jun 18, 2026 · 115 年 6 月 18 日

Two Hsinyi students brought home county honours in the same week. Dong Pei-Qin (8B) claimed second place in oral recitation at the language arts competition, delivering a polished and confident performance. Lin Ying-Xin (8A) earned first place in composition writing, with judges praising her clear structure and eloquent expression. Both achievements reflect a school culture that takes language seriously — in Chinese and in English.

同一週內，兩位信義學生在縣級賽場上揚名。八乙董沛親在語文競賽中奪得國語朗讀第二名，表現穩健從容；八甲林映欣則以清晰的結構與流暢的筆法，勇奪作文競賽第一名。兩人的成績，是信義語文文化最好的見證。

Science · 科學展覽

Science Exhibition Sweep科展成績亮眼

Apr 2026 · 115 年 4 月

Hsinyi students swept the 2026 county-level science exhibition, taking top prizes in multiple categories. The team's research in chemistry earned first place, with additional medals in biological and earth sciences. Science at Hsinyi goes beyond textbooks: students design experiments, present findings, and compete at the highest level — all while growing into confident scientific thinkers.

信義學生在 115 年度彰化縣中小學科學展覽中大放異彩，多個科目同時奪獎。化學科摘下第一名，生物科與地球科學也各有佳績。信義的科學教育不只是課本——學生設計實驗、發表成果、走上競賽舞台，在過程中成為有自信的科學思考者。

Milestone · 畢業典禮

The 22nd Graduation Ceremony第 22 屆畢業典禮

Jun 8, 2026 · 115 年 6 月 8 日

Hsinyi celebrated the graduation of its 22nd class — students who began here as six-year-olds in Grade 1 and completed their full nine years on this campus. Few schools can offer that experience. Principal Wang Hsin-Yi sent the graduates forward with a reminder of the motto they have carried for years: "Faithfulness, Unity, Excellence Together." Go carry it out into the world.

信義迎來第 22 屆畢業生的告別時刻——這些孩子從小一踏入這所學校，在這裡完整走完九年。很少有學校能給孩子這樣的體驗。王心怡校長以一句話送行：「信義同心共好——帶著這句話，走進更大的世界。」

Culture · 原住民文化

Indigenous Culture Summer Camp原住民族文化夏令營

Jun 12, 2026 · 115 年 6 月 12 日

Students at Hsinyi took part in an Indigenous Culture Summer Camp, spending several days immersed in the traditions, arts, and stories of Taiwan's indigenous peoples. Through hands-on workshops, music, and storytelling, students discovered that cultural literacy is just as important as bilingual literacy — and that Taiwan's heritage is as rich and complex as its future is promising.

信義學生參加了原住民族文化夏令營，數日間沉浸於臺灣原住民族的傳統、藝術與故事之中。透過手作工坊、音樂與說故事，孩子們體會到：文化素養與語言素養同等重要，而臺灣的文化遺產，與它光明的未來一樣豐富深厚。

Bilingual · 雙語遊學

Online Bilingual Study Tour線上雙語遊學營

2025 · 114 學年

Hsinyi students participated in an online bilingual study tour — a virtual exchange that connected them with English-speaking peers beyond Taiwan's borders. Students prepared introductions, engaged in guided conversations, and discovered that the English they practise every day in the classroom is real, usable, and powerful enough to build a genuine connection across the world. This is what a bilingual campus looks like in action.

信義學生參與了線上雙語遊學營——這場跨越台灣邊界的虛擬交流，讓孩子們與海外英語母語者真實對話。學生事先準備自我介紹、引導式對談，並親身發現：在課堂每天練習的英文，是真實的、可以用的，足以跨越海洋建立真誠的連結。這，就是雙語校園的真正面貌。

Bilingual · 師生自介

Classes 701, 702 & 703 Introduce Themselves in English七年級三班英文自我介紹

2024 · 113 學年

In a landmark bilingual project, all three Grade 7 classes — 701, 702, and 703 — produced group self-introduction videos entirely in English. Standing in front of the camera and introducing yourself and your classmates in a second language is no small thing. These students did it with energy, humour, and unmistakable Hsinyi spirit — and left a record that the whole school can be proud of.

在一個重要的雙語專案中，七年級三個班級——701、702、703——各自拍攝了全程英文的團體自我介紹影片。用第二語言站在鏡頭前介紹自己和同學，絕非易事。這些學生用熱情、幽默與十足的信義精神完成了挑戰，留下了整個學校都引以為傲的紀錄。

Culture · 閱讀推廣

Principal's Reading Chat校長相談室

May 2025 · 114 學年

Every week, Principal Wang Hsin-Yi invites elementary students to her office to talk about the books they've read. She listens closely, asks thoughtful questions, and lets students share their favorite characters and what they've learned — sometimes even reading a page aloud. The chats help students grow more confident, give the principal a closer look at who they are, and make reading something worth talking about.

每週，王心怡校長都會邀請國小部同學到校長室，聊聊他們讀過的書。她仔細聆聽、親切提問，讓同學分享喜歡的角色與心得，有時還會現場朗讀一小段。這段時光讓同學更有自信，也讓校長更了解每個孩子，也讓閱讀變成一件值得分享的事。

Bilingual · 文化分享

Foreign Teacher Shares Filipino Culture外師晨會文化分享

May 2025 · 114 學年

Every week at morning assembly, foreign English teacher Ronica shares a piece of her home country, the Philippines — its food, clothing, and holidays. Students from different classes take turns as interpreters, translating her words into Chinese for the whole school. It's a weekly reminder that learning English also means learning about the world.

每週朝會，外師 Ronica 老師都會分享她的家鄉菲律賓的傳統文化，包括美食、服裝與節日，並由各班同學輪流擔任口譯員，把她的話翻譯成中文給全校聽。這項活動讓同學在學英文的同時，也認識了不同國家的文化。

Curriculum · 信義英閱繪

Discover Taiwan: English Presentations「看見臺灣」學習成果發表

May 2025 · 114 學年

As part of this semester's English curriculum, Hsinyi English Reading & Art, junior high students worked with Ms. Nancy and foreign teacher Ronica to explore the beauty of Taiwan. Students gave short presentations in English about local culture — food, landmarks, and traditions — some even adding their own drawings. The project helped them understand their hometown better while building confidence in English.

本學期英語校訂課程「信義英閱繪」由 Nancy 老師與外師 Ronica 帶領，國中部同學用英文介紹家鄉臺灣的文化特色，例如美食、景點與傳統活動，有些同學還加上自己的插畫。這個活動讓同學更認識家鄉，也練習了用英語表達的能力。

Faculty · 教師增能

Teacher Workshop: International Education教師增能：如何發展學校國際教育

May 2025 · 114 學年

To help teachers bring international education into their classrooms, the Academic Affairs Office invited Ms. Wei, a director from Dacun Elementary School, to share her experience during a Wednesday-afternoon session. Ms. Wei walked through real examples of how her school planned and ran international education programs, giving Hsinyi's teachers practical ideas to bring global topics into daily teaching.

為了推動校內的國際教育，教務處邀請大村國小魏相英主任，利用週三下午的時間分享辦理國際教育的經驗。魏主任以實際案例介紹如何規劃與執行國際教育課程，幫助信義的老師們思考如何把國際議題融入日常教學。
