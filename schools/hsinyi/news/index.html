Spirit · 九年級加油

Praying for Our Ninth-Graders考生祈福 · 信義為九年級加油

2025 · 114 學年

Every spring, the whole Hsinyi community gathers to cheer on its graduating ninth-graders as they prepare for the national high school entrance exam. Teachers, classmates, and staff line the corridor to offer encouragement — a tradition that reminds every student they are never facing the challenge alone.

每年春天，全校師生聚在一起，為即將參加國中會考的九年級學長姐加油打氣。老師、同學、行政人員一字排開，傳遞鼓勵——這個傳統提醒每個孩子：面對挑戰，你從來不是一個人。

Culture · 閱讀推廣

Principal's Reading Chat校長相談室

May 2025 · 114 學年

Every week, Principal Wang Hsin-Yi invites elementary students to her office to talk about the books they've read. She listens closely, asks thoughtful questions, and lets students share their favorite characters and what they've learned — sometimes even reading a page aloud. The chats help students grow more confident, give the principal a closer look at who they are, and make reading something worth talking about.

每週，王心怡校長都會邀請國小部同學到校長室，聊聊他們讀過的書。她仔細聆聽、親切提問，讓同學分享喜歡的角色與心得，有時還會現場朗讀一小段。這段時光讓同學更有自信，也讓校長更了解每個孩子，也讓閱讀變成一件值得分享的事。

Bilingual · 文化分享

Foreign Teacher Shares Filipino Culture外師晨會文化分享

May 2025 · 114 學年

Every week at morning assembly, foreign English teacher Ronica shares a piece of her home country, the Philippines — its food, clothing, and holidays. Students from different classes take turns as interpreters, translating her words into Chinese for the whole school. It's a weekly reminder that learning English also means learning about the world.

每週朝會，外師 Ronica 老師都會分享她的家鄉菲律賓的傳統文化，包括美食、服裝與節日，並由各班同學輪流擔任口譯員，把她的話翻譯成中文給全校聽。這項活動讓同學在學英文的同時，也認識了不同國家的文化。

Curriculum · 信義英閱繪

Discover Taiwan: English Presentations「看見臺灣」學習成果發表

May 2025 · 114 學年

As part of this semester's English curriculum, Hsinyi English Reading & Art, junior high students worked with Ms. Nancy and foreign teacher Ronica to explore the beauty of Taiwan. Students gave short presentations in English about local culture — food, landmarks, and traditions — some even adding their own drawings. The project helped them understand their hometown better while building confidence in English.

本學期英語校訂課程「信義英閱繪」由 Nancy 老師與外師 Ronica 帶領，國中部同學用英文介紹家鄉臺灣的文化特色，例如美食、景點與傳統活動，有些同學還加上自己的插畫。這個活動讓同學更認識家鄉，也練習了用英語表達的能力。

Faculty · 教師增能

Teacher Workshop: International Education教師增能：如何發展學校國際教育

May 2025 · 114 學年

To help teachers bring international education into their classrooms, the Academic Affairs Office invited Ms. Wei, a director from Dacun Elementary School, to share her experience during a Wednesday-afternoon session. Ms. Wei walked through real examples of how her school planned and ran international education programs, giving Hsinyi's teachers practical ideas to bring global topics into daily teaching.

為了推動校內的國際教育，教務處邀請大村國小魏相英主任，利用週三下午的時間分享辦理國際教育的經驗。魏主任以實際案例介紹如何規劃與執行國際教育課程，幫助信義的老師們思考如何把國際議題融入日常教學。
