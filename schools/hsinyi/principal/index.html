王心怡校長
I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Wang Hsin-Yi brings deep experience in junior-high and elementary education to Hsinyi. Taking office in August 2024, she leads a campus that spans the full nine years of compulsory education — a unique responsibility that calls for a unified vision from the very first day of Grade 1 to the final bell of Grade 9.

王心怡校長擁有豐富的國中小教育經歷，於 2024 年 8 月接任信義國中小校長。她所帶領的是一個橫跨完整九年一貫教育的校園——從小學一年級的第一天，到國中三年級的最後一堂課，這份責任需要一以貫之的教育願景。

Principal Wang's educational philosophy centres on the belief that every child should learn how to learn, and learn how to live — with courage, a sense of responsibility, and genuine wisdom. Under her leadership, the school nurtures not just academic achievement but the whole person: resilient, curious, and kind.

王校長的教育哲學核心是：每個孩子都應該學會學習、學會生活——有勇氣、有擔當、有智慧。在她的帶領下，學校不只追求學業表現，更著力培養全人發展：韌性、好奇心，以及對他人的善意。

Guided by the school motto "信義同心共好" — Faithfulness, Unity, Excellence Together — Principal Wang keeps Hsinyi moving forward as a warm, inclusive campus where bilingual education, reading culture, arts, and athletics all have a genuine home.

在校訓「信義同心共好」的精神引導下，王校長帶領信義持續前行，打造一個溫暖、友善的校園——雙語教育、閱讀文化、藝術展演、體育競技，在這裡都有真正的位置。

"Let every child learn to learn, and learn to live — with courage, responsibility, and wisdom."
讓孩子學會學習、學會生活——有勇氣、有擔當、有智慧。 — 王心怡校長
