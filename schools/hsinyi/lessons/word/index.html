II.

Sports & PE

體育活動 · 動起來的英文

球場上、操場邊，信義學生用英文說運動。

WORD 001
shoot hoopsv.
投籃
To shoot hoops means to throw a basketball toward the hoop, trying to score.
Shoot hoops（投籃）是指把籃球投向籃框，試著得分。
Hsinyi students love to shoot hoops together during recess.
信義的學生下課時最愛一起投籃。
WORD 002
soccern.
足球
Soccer is a team sport played by kicking a ball into the other team's goal.
足球是一種團隊運動，靠踢球將球送進對方球門得分。
Hsinyi's soccer team practices on the campus field every week.
信義的足球隊每週都會在校園操場練習。
WORD 003
basketball teamn.
籃球隊
A basketball team is a group of players who train and compete together.
Basketball team（籃球隊）是一群一起訓練、一起比賽的球員組成的隊伍。
Hsinyi's basketball team represents the school in county competitions.
信義的籃球隊代表學校參加縣內比賽。
WORD 004
dance routinen.
舞步 · 舞蹈動作
A dance routine is a set of dance moves performed in a fixed order.
Dance routine（舞步）是指一套按固定順序表演的舞蹈動作。
Students at Hsinyi rehearse a new dance routine for every school festival.
信義的學生每次校慶都會排練一套新的舞步。
WORD 005
cup stackingn.
疊杯
Cup stacking is a fast-paced sport where players stack and unstack cups in set patterns.
Cup stacking（疊杯）是一種依照特定排列快速堆疊、拆疊杯子的競速運動。
Hsinyi students race against the clock in cup stacking during PE class.
信義的學生在體育課會進行疊杯計時競賽。
III.

Campus Life

校園日常 · 每天都用得上

從課後輔導到期中考，信義的日常就是最好的教材。

WORD 006
after-school programn.
課後輔導
An after-school program offers extra learning activities once regular classes end.
After-school program（課後輔導）是指正課結束後提供的額外學習活動。
Many Hsinyi students stay for the after-school program to review their lessons.
許多信義的學生放學後會留下來參加課後輔導複習功課。
WORD 007
midterm examn.
期中考
A midterm exam is a test given partway through the semester to check students' progress.
Midterm exam（期中考）是學期中舉行的考試，用來檢核學生的學習進度。
Hsinyi students spend the week before the midterm exam reviewing every subject.
信義的學生在期中考前一週會複習每個科目。
WORD 008
self assessmentn.
自我評量
A self assessment is when students reflect on and rate their own learning.
Self assessment（自我評量）是指學生自己反思並評估自己的學習狀況。
Teachers at Hsinyi ask students to complete a self assessment after every unit.
信義的老師每個單元結束後都會請學生完成自我評量。
WORD 009
home economics classn.
家政課
A home economics class teaches practical skills like cooking and sewing.
Home economics class（家政課）教導像烹飪和縫紉這樣的生活技能。
Hsinyi students learn to cook a simple dish in home economics class.
信義的學生會在家政課學習烹煮簡單的料理。
WORD 010
correctv.
改正 · 批改
To correct means to fix a mistake and make something right.
Correct（改正）是指修正錯誤、讓事情變得正確。
Hsinyi teachers correct students' homework with patient, encouraging notes.
信義的老師批改作業時總會附上耐心又鼓勵的評語。
IV.

Arts & Music

藝文音樂 · 才藝中的英文

吉他社、說故事時間——信義的藝文生活也是英文課。

WORD 011
musiciann.
音樂家
A musician is someone who plays an instrument or composes music.
Musician（音樂家）是指演奏樂器或創作音樂的人。
Hsinyi invites a guest musician to perform at the annual arts festival.
信義每年藝術節都會邀請音樂家蒞校演出。
WORD 012
guitar clubn.
吉他社
A guitar club is a group of students who meet to practice and play guitar together.
Guitar club（吉他社）是一群學生固定聚會、一起練習彈吉他的社團。
Hsinyi's guitar club performs a mini concert at the end of every semester.
信義的吉他社每學期末都會舉辦一場小型音樂會。
WORD 013
balloonn.
氣球
A balloon is a stretchy bag that can be filled with air and used for decoration.
Balloon（氣球）是一種可以充氣、用來做裝飾的彈性袋子。
Hsinyi's campus is filled with colorful balloons on graduation day.
畢業典禮當天，信義的校園掛滿了五顏六色的氣球。
WORD 014
storyn.
故事
A story is a spoken or written account of events, real or imagined.
Story（故事）是指口述或寫成的事件記述，可能是真實或虛構的。
Hsinyi teachers read a bilingual story to students during morning reading time.
信義的老師在晨讀時間會為學生朗讀雙語故事。
V.

Bilingual Learning

雙語教育 · 學英文也是學態度

老師進修、每天閱讀——信義的雙語精神從師長做起。

WORD 015
bilingual workshopn.
雙語工作坊
A bilingual workshop is a training session conducted in two languages.
Bilingual workshop（雙語工作坊）是指使用兩種語言進行的培訓課程。
Hsinyi teachers regularly attend a bilingual workshop to improve their English teaching.
信義的老師會定期參加雙語工作坊，精進英語教學能力。
WORD 016
professional developmentn.
專業進修
Professional development means training that helps teachers improve their skills.
Professional development（專業進修）是指協助老師提升專業技能的培訓。
Hsinyi teachers take part in professional development sessions every semester.
信義的老師每學期都會參加專業進修課程。
WORD 017
readingn.
閱讀
Reading means looking at and understanding written words.
Reading（閱讀）是指看懂並理解文字的過程。
Reading is at the heart of Hsinyi's culture — the school even won a national reading award.
閱讀是信義校園文化的核心——學校甚至曾獲全國閱讀推手磐石獎的肯定。
