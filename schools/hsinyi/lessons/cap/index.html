I.

Praying for Ninth-Graders

九年級祈福活動

Every May, Hsinyi cheers its ninth-graders toward the biggest test of their school lives.

每年五月，信義為即將面對人生大考的九年級生加油打氣。

←Back to Lessons回到課程總覽

On the afternoon of May 15th, Hsinyi Junior High and Elementary School held a special event to pray for the ninth graders' success in their upcoming exams. The event's theme was "Achieving Dreams." Each ninth grader received some stationery for their exams and a lucky fortune cookie, wishing them the best of luck. In a show of confidence and unity, the students cheered together, loudly encouraging themselves. Each student then took the stage to announce their dream school and declared their goals by striking a gong to the universe. To conclude the event, students and teachers threw paper planes into the sky, symbolizing their dreams taking flight towards their next school. Hsinyi Junior High and Elementary School continues to support its students, helping them feel encouraged and ready for their exams on May 18th and 19th.

5 月 15 日下午，信義國中小舉辦了一場特別活動，為九年級學生即將到來的考試祈福。活動主題為「實現夢想清單」。每位九年級學生都收到了一些考試用的文具和幸運餅乾，祝願他們好運。在展現信心與團結的氛圍中，學生們一起高聲為自己加油打氣。接著，每位學生依次登上舞台，宣布他們心目中的夢想學校，並敲響銅鑼向天地宣告他們的目標。活動的最後，學生和老師們一起將紙飛機拋向天空，象徵他們的夢想飛向下一個學校。信義國中小繼續支持其學生，幫助他們感到鼓舞並準備好迎接 5 月 18 日和 19 日的考試。

II.

What Is the CAP Exam?

什麼是國中教育會考？

One test, five subjects, and a whole country of ninth-graders.

一場考試、五個科目，全國九年級生都要面對的關卡。

The Comprehensive Assessment Program for Junior High School Students (CAP) is an exam in Taiwan for ninth graders, usually held in mid-May by the Ministry of Education. It helps students, teachers, schools, and parents understand the students' learning quality before they go to high school or vocational school. The CAP tests five subjects: Chinese Language, English, Mathematics, Natural Science, and Social Studies.

國中教育會考（CAP）是台灣針對九年級學生的考試，通常由教育部在五月中旬舉行。這項考試有助於學生、老師、學校和家長了解學生的學習品質，為升高中或職業學校做準備。會考包括五個科目：國文、英文、數學、自然科學和社會科學。
