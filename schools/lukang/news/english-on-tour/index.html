English on Tour Comes to Lukang

英語巡迴課程走進鹿港國小，開啟國際視野
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

English is more than words in a textbook — it is a bridge to the wider world and to other cultures. This month, My Culture Connect and the Changhua Renshi Education Association brought their touring English program to Lukang Elementary. Taught entirely in English and built around real-life themes, the lessons invited children to learn English from everyday situations and to grow as global citizens.

讓英語不只是課本上的文字，更是認識世界、理解文化的重要橋梁！感謝 My Culture Connect 與人師教育協會蒞臨鹿港國小，帶來精彩的英語巡迴課程，透過全英語教學與多元主題，引導孩子從生活中學習英語，培養國際理解與全球公民素養。

Grade 5, Class A — Character Education: Kindness. Starting from situations the children meet every day, the class practised being kind to others and showing respect. They talked about how a small act of kindness can make a real difference, and how good character lives in ordinary moments.

五甲｜Character Education: Kindness（品格教育：友善）　從生活情境出發，學習友善待人、尊重他人，讓孩子理解善意的力量，將品格教育落實於日常生活。

Grade 5, Class D — SDG 1: No Poverty. Guided by one of the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals, the class looked at how people live in different parts of the world — some without enough food, clean water or a safe home. The lesson helped children care for others, respect differences and pay attention to global issues such as poverty.

五丁｜SDGs 1: No Poverty（永續發展目標 1：消除貧窮）　透過國際議題的引導，認識世界各地不同的生活處境，培養關懷他人、尊重差異及關注全球議題（例如貧窮）的能力。

Special thanks go to Dom Jones and Mr. Lin Chi-hsiang for their lively, hands-on lessons, and to Mr. Yu Jen-yang, chairman of the Changhua Renshi Education Association, whose support made the visit possible. Thanks to them, Lukang's children had a chance to meet a different culture and look a little further out into the world.

特別感謝 Dom Jones 老師、林吉祥老師帶來生動豐富的課程，以及彰化縣人師教育協會理事長游人仰的支持與促成，讓鹿港國小的孩子有機會接觸多元文化，拓展國際視野。

From learning English to using it, and from knowing about the world to caring for it — every lesson like this plants a small seed of international understanding.

從學習英語到運用英語，從認識世界到關懷世界，讓孩子在每一次的學習體驗中，種下國際理解的種子！
