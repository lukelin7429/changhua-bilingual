A New Term Begins

新學期開始 · 返校日與開學日
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

The long summer holiday is nearly over, and the new term is almost here. Over the last days of the break, it helps to sort out stationery with your child, get everything ready for the first day, and let the routine settle back in — earlier nights, earlier mornings, and a mind that is ready to learn again.

漫長的暑假即將結束，新學期的腳步也近了。趁著假期的最後幾天，和孩子一起整理文具、準備開學用品，也讓作息慢慢收回來——早點睡、早點起，把心情調整好，準備迎接新的學習旅程。

Back-to-school day is Friday 28 August. The morning runs from tidying the classrooms (7:30–7:50) to the staff morning meeting (8:00–8:35) and homeroom time (8:40–10:00), with dismissal at 10:00. The first day of term is Monday 31 August, following the normal schedule: Grade 1 finishes at 12:00, Grade 2 at 12:40, and Grade 3 and above at 15:55.

8 月 28 日（五）是返校日：早上從環境整理（7:30–7:50）、教師晨會（8:00–8:35）到導師時間（8:40–10:00），10:00 放學。8 月 31 日（一）開學日依課表正常上課：一年級 12:00 放學、二年級 12:40 放學、三年級以上 15:55 放學。

The first day is also a welcome for our new first-graders. Parents walk their children in, and at the gate the teachers and the principal are waiting with a smile and a high-five, so that "going to school" begins as something to look forward to. New students wear the school's short-sleeved blue-and-white checked uniform; on that first day Grade 1 finishes at noon, and families collect their children from the Grade 1 classrooms.

開學第一天，也是給小一新生的迎新。爸爸媽媽陪著孩子走進校園，校門口有親切的老師與校長，帶著微笑和擊掌迎接，讓「上學」成為孩子期待的美好開始。新生請穿著本校短袖藍白格制服；當天一年級中午 12 點放學，請家長直接到一年級各班接回孩子。

Welcome, new first-graders — welcome to the Lukang Elementary family. We hope you learn happily here, explore bravely, and make many good friends.

親愛的小一新生，歡迎加入鹿港國小大家庭！願你在這裡快樂學習、勇敢探索，交到好多好朋友。
