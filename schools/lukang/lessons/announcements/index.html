Bilingual Announcements

I.

Broadcast English

廣播英語 · 各處室的英語宣導

The announcements you hear over the PA — in English.

校園廣播裡聽到的宣導，這次用英語說一遍。

←Back to Lessons回到課程總覽

Thirteen short "Broadcast English" episodes, one for each office and section of a school. In each clip, that team gives — in English — the kind of announcement it would make over the public-address system: a registration reminder from the academic affairs office, a hygiene notice from the health section, a message from the counselling room. This is a shared resource used across Changhua's bilingual schools.

13 支「廣播英語」短片，學校的每個處室、每個組各一支。每支片裡，該團隊用英語示範平時會透過校園廣播宣布的內容——教務處的註冊提醒、衛生組的環境宣導、輔導室的一段話。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。
