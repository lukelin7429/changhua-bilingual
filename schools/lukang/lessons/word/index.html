In the Classroom
課堂裡 · 3 words
WORD 01
learn (v)
學習
Students listen carefully to the teacher's lecture while taking notes to learn better.
學生一邊認真聽老師講課，一邊做筆記，以便更好地學習。
Interactive games make learning more fun and effective.
互動遊戲使學習變得更有趣、更有效。
WORD 02
life education (n)
生命教育
Students do a paper-cutting activity in the life education class.
學生們在生命教育課上進行剪紙活動。
Through this life education class, they learn to appreciate differences: no two shapes are the same.
透過這堂生命教育課，他們學會欣賞差異：沒有兩個形狀是完全相同的。
WORD 03
magnet (n)
磁鐵
The students are making their own fridge magnets.
學生們正在製作自己的冰箱貼。
They use glue to stick the parts together and complete the magnet.
他們用膠水將各個部分黏在一起，從而完成磁鐵。
🚌
Out on a Field Trip
校外教學 · 2 words
WORD 04
guide (n)
導遊
The students listen to a guide during the field trip.
學生在校外教學期間聽導遊講解。
The guide explains why bees are important to nature.
導遊解釋了蜜蜂對大自然的重要性。
WORD 05
zipline (n)
滑索
The students take turns riding the zipline during the field trip.
學生們在校外教學活動中輪流乘坐高空滑索。
Everyone enjoys the thrilling experience of the zipline ride.
每個人都享受滑索帶來的刺激體驗。

Around Campus
校園裡 · 1 word
WORD 06
basketball court (n)
籃球場
The kids are playing on the basketball court.
小朋友在籃球場上打籃球。
Most basketball courts have a hoop on each side.
大部分的籃球場兩邊各有一個籃框。
