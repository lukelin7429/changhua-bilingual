Ways to Learn

從校園生活學英文

Real school, real words.

每支影片、每份教材，都取材自鹿港真實的校園日常。

6 Videos · 3 Themes
Course 01
Word of the Day
每日一字

Six bilingual videos filmed around Lukang Elementary, grouped into three themes — a lesson in progress, a field trip, and a corner of the playground. Each word comes with two example sentences taken from the video.6 支雙語影片，全部在鹿港國小校園實地拍攝，分為三大主題——課堂進行中、校外教學，以及操場的一角。每個單字都附兩句取自影片說明欄的例句。

30 Episodes · For Teachers
Course 02
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers, taught by Sarah Thomas across 10 topics and 30 episodes — from greetings and roll call to exam instructions. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.給老師使用的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學，共 10 大主題、30 支影片——從問候點名到考試指令。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

Start Learning →
13 Episodes · By Office
Course 03
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

"Broadcast English" — thirteen short episodes in which each office and section introduces, in English, the announcements it makes over the school PA system, sorted by the Academic Affairs, Student Affairs, General Affairs and Counselling offices.「廣播英語」——13 支短片，由各處室、各組用英語介紹平時透過校園廣播宣布的內容，依教務處、學務處、總務處、輔導室四處室分類。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。
