Two Awards at the County Science Fair

科展雙獎：從芭樂葉裡看見的顏色
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Congratulations to two of our students, whose project — "Revealing the Colours Hidden in Guava" (彰顯「織」色，「芭」不得你現形) — won two awards at the Changhua County Primary and Secondary School Science Fair: an Honourable Mention and the Local Teaching-Materials Award.

恭喜本校兩位同學，作品《彰顯「織」色，「芭」不得你現形》在「彰化縣中小學科學展覽會」表現亮眼，一舉榮獲雙獎——佳作與鄉土教材獎。

Starting from everyday life, the children combined scientific inquiry with creative hands-on work. They explored the natural dyes that can be drawn from guava leaves — a fruit grown all around Shetou — and showed not only solid experimental skills but also real teaching and practical value.

孩子們從生活出發，結合科學探究與創意實作，研究社頭一帶盛產的芭樂，探索芭樂葉可以萃取出的天然染色。作品不僅展現紮實的實驗能力，更具備教學與應用價值，實屬難能可貴。

Thank you to teacher Li Kuo-chih for his dedicated guidance and companionship, and well done to the students for their effort and their results. Nanya Elementary is proud of you.

感謝李國誌老師的用心指導陪伴，也為同學們的努力與成果喝采。湳雅國小以你們為榮。
