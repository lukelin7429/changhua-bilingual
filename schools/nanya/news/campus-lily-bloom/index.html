Lilies Quietly Bloom on Campus

校園百合花悄悄綻放：食農、環境與美感的一堂課
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

In a quiet corner of the campus, we planted lily flowers together with the children. From loosening the soil and watering to waiting for the buds to open, the children learned care, patience and respect for life.

在校園一角，我們和孩子一起種下百合花。從鬆土、澆水到等待開花，孩子學會照顧、耐心與尊重生命。

This was not just planting flowers. It was a living lesson that brought together food-and-farming education, environmental education and aesthetic education all at once.

這不只是種花，更是一堂結合食農教育、環境教育與美感教育的生活課。

When the lilies bloomed at last, the children's smiles were the most beautiful result of all.

當百合盛開的那一刻，孩子的笑容，就是最美的成果。

lily/ˈlɪli/百合花

The children planted lilies in a corner of the campus.孩子們在校園一角種下百合花。

bloom/bluːm/開花、綻放

Everyone waited for the flowers to bloom.大家一起等待花朵綻放。

plant/plænt/種植

They learned how to plant and water the bulbs.他們學會怎麼種球莖、怎麼澆水。

soil/sɔɪl/土壤

First they loosened the soil with their hands.他們先用手把土壤鬆開。

patience/ˈpeɪʃəns/耐心

Waiting for a bud to open takes patience.等一朵花苞打開，需要耐心。

respect/rɪˈspekt/尊重

Caring for a plant teaches respect for life.照顧植物，讓孩子學會尊重生命。

aesthetic/esˈθetɪk/美感的

It was also an aesthetic education about beauty.這也是一堂關於美的美感教育。

result/rɪˈzʌlt/成果

The children's smiles were the best result.孩子的笑容，就是最好的成果。
