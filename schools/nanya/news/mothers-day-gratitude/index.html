A May of Gratitude and Warm Feeling

馨懷感恩、溫情五月：母親節感恩活動
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On 1 May, we welcomed Mother's Day with hearts full of gratitude and love.

5 月 1 日，我們用滿滿的感恩與愛迎接母親節。

Special thanks to the Shetou Township Mayor, who came to school to present carnations; to a representative from Tsun-hsiu, who warmly handed out drinks; and to the head of the Parents' Association, who led a sing-along and gave out sweet cake — filling the day with warmth and joy.

特別感謝社頭鄉長蒞校致贈康乃馨，存修代表熱情贈送飲品，以及家長會長帶動唱並贈送香甜蛋糕，讓活動充滿溫馨與喜悅。

The children expressed their deep thanks to their mothers and other important family members with a wonderful talent show — including a drum performance — a tea-serving ceremony, and a group photo of gratitude. Every moment overflowed with happiness.

孩子們也以精采的才藝表演（包括一段戰鼓演出）、奉茶儀式及感恩合照，表達對媽媽與重要家人的深深謝意。每一刻都洋溢著幸福的氛圍。
