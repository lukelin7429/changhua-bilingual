A Warmer Winter, Because Love Walks With Us

寒冬送暖：國泰基金會連續第七年到湳雅
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Through the Cathay Charity Foundation's "Winter Warmth" programme, volunteers came to Nanya Elementary for the seventh year running.

國泰基金會「寒冬送暖」活動，今年已連續第七年到湳雅國小。

Each child received a carefully prepared Christmas gift, and the head of the Parents' Association gave every student a cup cake.

一份份精心準備的聖誕禮物送到每個孩子手上；家長會長也贈送每位學生杯子蛋糕。

The visit brought the children plenty of surprises and smiles — and a reminder that even in the coldest weeks of the year, they are not alone.

為孩子們帶來滿滿驚喜與笑容，也讓大家知道：在一年最冷的時候，我們並不孤單。
