Scallion Pancakes

蔥油餅 DIY
⌄
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Our local field trip also took us to Shetou Junior High, where a teacher showed us how to cook scallion pancakes on an electric stove. Set the power, heat the oil, put in the pancake, and flip it when it turns golden brown. Then it's time to eat!鄉土踏查也來到社頭國中，老師教我們用電磁爐煎蔥油餅：設定火力、把油加熱、放入餅皮，煎到金黃色就翻面，然後就可以開動囉！

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

pancake/ˈpænˌkeɪk/餅

A scallion pancake is round and flat.蔥油餅圓圓扁扁的。

pan/pæn/平底鍋

Put the pancake in the pan.把餅放進平底鍋。

stove/stoʊv/爐子

Be careful — the stove is hot!小心，爐子很燙！

oil/ɔɪl/油

Add a little oil to the pan.在鍋裡加一點油。

flip/flɪp/翻面

Flip the pancake with a spatula.用鍋鏟把餅翻面。

eat/it/吃

Let's eat it while it's hot!趁熱吃吧！
