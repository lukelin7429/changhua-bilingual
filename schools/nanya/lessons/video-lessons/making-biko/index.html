Making Biko

菲律賓甜米糕 Biko
⌄
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽
🎬
Video coming soon影片上傳中，敬請期待

On a field trip to Wende Elementary School, we learned to make biko, a sweet rice cake from the Philippines. We cooked sticky rice with coconut milk and sugar, kept stirring, and then pressed it into a tray. It tasted sweet — just like our own sweet rice cake!我們到文德國小校外教學，學做菲律賓甜米糕 Biko。把糯米、椰奶和糖一起煮，一直攪拌，最後壓進鋁盤裡。吃起來香香甜甜的，就像我們這邊的甜米糕！

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

coconut milk/ˈkoʊkəˌnʌt mɪlk/椰奶

Coconut milk comes from coconuts.椰奶是從椰子來的。

sticky rice/ˈstɪki raɪs/糯米

Sticky rice is soft and chewy.糯米軟軟的、QQ的。

stir/stɝ/攪拌

Stir the rice so it doesn't burn.攪拌米飯，才不會燒焦。

wok/wɑk/炒鍋

We cook the rice in a big wok.我們用大炒鍋煮米。

tray/treɪ/鋁盤

Press the biko into the tray.把 Biko 壓進鋁盤裡。

piece/pis/一塊

Can I have a piece of biko?我可以吃一塊 Biko 嗎？
