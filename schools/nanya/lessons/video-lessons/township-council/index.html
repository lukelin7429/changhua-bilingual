Visiting the Township Council

參觀社頭鄉民代表會
⌄

On a Shetou field trip, our sixth graders visited the Township Council. The council chairman showed us around, and then we took the seats ourselves — one classmate became the chairman, and the others stood up to ask questions, just like real council members.鄉土踏查這天，六年級來到社頭鄉民代表會。代表會主席為大家介紹，接著我們親自坐上座位——一位同學當主席，其他同學站起來提問，體驗代表質詢的過程。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

welcome/ˈwɛlkəm/歡迎

The sign says, "Welcome, Nanya!"看板上寫著「歡迎湳雅國小」。

council/ˈkaʊnsəl/代表會

The council helps make decisions for our township.代表會幫忙為鄉裡做決定。

chairman/ˈtʃɛrmən/主席

The chairman leads the meeting.主席主持會議。

seat/sit/座位

Each seat has a name sign.每個座位都有名牌。

stand up/stænd ʌp/站起來

Stand up when you speak.發言的時候要站起來。

meeting/ˈmitɪŋ/會議；開會

We had a meeting in the council hall.我們在議事廳開會。
