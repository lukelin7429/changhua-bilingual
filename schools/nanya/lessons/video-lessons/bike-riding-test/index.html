The Bike Riding Test

六年級腳踏車考照
⌄
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Many sixth graders will ride their bikes to junior high. After their final exams, they took a bike riding test on campus. The director checked every helmet and bike, and at the crossing, "pedestrians" could step out at any time — so riders had to stay alert!很多六年級同學上國中後要騎腳踏車上學。畢業考後，學校在校園裡舉辦腳踏車考照。主任一一檢查安全帽和腳踏車，路口關卡隨時會有「行人」穿越，騎車一定要專心！

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

bike/baɪk/腳踏車

I ride my bike to school.我騎腳踏車上學。

helmet/ˈhɛlmɪt/安全帽

A helmet keeps your head safe.安全帽保護你的頭。

check/tʃɛk/檢查

Let me check your helmet.我來檢查你的安全帽。

ride/raɪd/騎

Ride on the right side of the road.騎在道路的右側。

pedestrian/pəˈdɛstriən/行人

A pedestrian is a person walking on the street.行人就是在路上走路的人。

traffic light/ˈtræfɪk laɪt/紅綠燈

Stop when the traffic light is red.紅燈亮時要停下來。
