Video Lessons

主題式影片課程 · 湳雅實拍

← Lessons 回到課程總覽

Every lesson here was filmed at Nanya or on our field trips around Shetou — from the school hallway and the hall stage to the township council and a cooking classroom. Each video is under a minute, with six words, three sentences, and a matching game.每一堂課都在湳雅校園或社頭鄉土踏查時實地拍攝——從走廊、禮堂舞台，到鄉民代表會和烹飪教室。影片不到一分鐘，搭配六個單字、三個句子和一個配對小遊戲。

Watering the Plants
謝謝劉伯伯澆花

Uncle Liu takes care of the flowers and trees at our school and waters them every day.劉伯伯照顧學校的花草樹木，每天幫忙澆花。
