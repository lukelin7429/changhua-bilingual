Taiwanese Storytelling Contest

閩南語講古比賽
⌄
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

At our Taiwanese storytelling contest, students took turns at the microphone to tell stories in Taiwanese. Sixth graders hosted the show, teachers served as judges, and the best storytellers in each grade were chosen.閩南語講古比賽中，小朋友輪流站到麥克風前，用閩南語說故事。六年級的學長姐擔任主持人，老師們擔任評審，選出各年級表現最優秀的參賽者。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

contest/ˈkɑntɛst/比賽

Who will win the contest?誰會贏得這場比賽？

story/ˈstɔri/故事

Tell a story in Taiwanese.用閩南語說一個故事。

microphone/ˈmaɪkrəˌfoʊn/麥克風

Speak into the microphone.對著麥克風說話。

judge/dʒʌdʒ/評審

The judges give each storyteller a score.評審幫每位說故事的人打分數。

audience/ˈɔdiəns/觀眾

The audience listens quietly.觀眾安靜地聽。

number/ˈnʌmbɚ/號碼

Her number is three.她的號碼是三號。
