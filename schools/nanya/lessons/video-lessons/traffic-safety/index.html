Traffic Safety

交通安全教育

Before graduation, our sixth graders had a traffic safety lesson. With big road signs, we learned what to do: slow down at a curve, stop at a yellow light, and let people on the crosswalk go first.畢業前，六年級上了一堂交通安全課。看著一張張交通標誌，我們學到：彎道要放慢、黃燈要停下來、斑馬線上要讓行人先走。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

safety/ˈseɪfti/安全

Safety comes first on the road.路上安全第一。

sign/saɪn/標誌

This sign means "slow down."這個標誌的意思是「慢行」。

slow/sloʊ/慢

Slow down when you see this sign.看到這個標誌要放慢速度。

curve/kɝv/彎道

The road has a sharp curve here.這裡的路有一個急彎。

crosswalk/ˈkrɔsˌwɔk/斑馬線

Cross the street on the crosswalk.過馬路要走斑馬線。

listen/ˈlɪsən/聽

Listen carefully to the safety rules.仔細聽安全規則。
