Making Mugwort Hammers

艾草捶 DIY
⌄
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

On our field trip to the Shetou Farmers' Association, teachers from the 4-H Club showed us how to make mugwort hammers. Mugwort goes inside a ball of pretty cloth, and then we wrapped string around the handle — slowly and tightly, from the top down.到社頭農會參觀時，四健會的老師教我們做艾草捶。把艾草包進漂亮的花布裡，再用繩子從上往下，慢慢地、密密地纏繞出握柄。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

teacher/ˈtitʃɚ/老師

Our teacher shows us each step.老師示範每一個步驟。

hammer/ˈhæmɚ/捶子

This hammer has a soft, round head.這個捶子有軟軟圓圓的頭。

cloth/klɔθ/布

The cloth has pretty patterns on it.這塊布上有漂亮的花紋。

string/strɪŋ/繩子

Hold the string tight.把繩子拉緊。

wrap/ræp/纏繞

Wrap the string around and around.把繩子一圈一圈纏上去。

handle/ˈhændəl/握柄

Hold the hammer by the handle.握住捶子的握柄。
