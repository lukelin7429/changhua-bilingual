Watering the Plants

謝謝劉伯伯澆花
⌄
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Uncle Liu takes care of the flowers, plants, and trees around Nanya. Every day, he pulls out a long hose and waters the hanging pots along the hallway. Thank you, Uncle Liu!劉伯伯照顧湳雅校園裡的花草樹木，每天拉著長長的水管，幫走廊上的吊盆和花草澆水。謝謝劉伯伯！

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

water/ˈwɔtɚ/澆水

Please water the flowers every day.請每天幫花澆水。

hose/hoʊz/水管

Water comes out of the long hose.水從長長的水管流出來。

flower/ˈflaʊɚ/花

The red flowers are very pretty.紅色的花很漂亮。

pot/pɑt/花盆

This pot hangs from the roof.這個花盆掛在屋簷下。

plant/plænt/植物

This plant has big green leaves.這株植物有大大的綠葉子。

leaf/lif/葉子

Each leaf is wet after watering.澆完水，每一片葉子都濕濕的。
