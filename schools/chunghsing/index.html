Where the rice fields of Erlin learn to drum, read, and dream — in two languages.

Chung Hsing Elementary became an independent school in 1956, on land once borrowed from a sugar-factory cane field in Erlin Township, Changhua. Its name — "Chung Hsing," meaning revival and flourishing — was chosen when two neighbouring villages set aside their rivalry to build one school together.

Today it is a warm country campus wrapped in green: a wide running track, a rainbow playground, and century-old heritage trees. Here children learn through the Taiko drum club, Cool English self-learning, hands-on food & farming, and reading — small classes, big care.

中興國小於 民國 45 年（1956）獨立設校，校地最初借自二林溪湖糖廠的甘蔗田。「中興」之名，是當年兩個村莊放下校名之爭、合力建校的共識——取「振興、興盛」之意。如今這是一所被綠意環抱的鄉間校園：寬闊操場、彩虹遊戲場與百年經典樹。孩子在太鼓社團、Cool English 自主學習、食農教育與閱讀中成長——班級雖小，照顧滿滿。

School Features

What Makes Chung Hsing, Chung Hsing中興國小的三大校園風景

🥁

Taiko & Talent太鼓與才藝

The Chung Hsing Taiko drum club is the school's signature — children rehearse on their own and shine on stage, performing at the Erlin district talent showcase.

中興太鼓社團是學校的招牌，孩子主動練習、自信登台，在二林區學生才藝展演綻放光芒。

🗣️

Cool English英語自主學習

With a dedicated English teacher and the national Cool English platform, more than half of our learners passed the Listening Champion challenge, and self-learners earn the Initiative Award.

在專任英語師資與全國 Cool English 平台支持下，英聽王通過人數過半，自主學習者更獲頒「初心獎」。

🌳

Heritage Trees & Nature經典樹與自然

As we approach our 70th year, century-old camphor and chinaberry trees are cared for as living "heritage trees" — alongside food-and-farming lessons and mobile-VR adventures.

迎接 70 週年，校園百年樟樹與苦楝樹被認養為活的「經典樹」；搭配食農教育與行動 VR 課程，自然就是教室。

🗣️ English at Chung Hsing · 英語亮點

Small School, Confident Voices小校園，大聲說英語

English is woven into the everyday — Word of the Day, classroom English, and the Cool English platform let country children read, listen, and check their own progress. Persistence pays off: most of our learners cleared the Listening Champion challenge.英語融入日常——每日一詞、課室英語與 Cool English 平台，讓鄉間孩子讀、聽、自評。努力有回報：多數孩子通過「英聽王」挑戰，自主學習者獲頒初心獎。
