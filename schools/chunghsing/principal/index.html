Principal's Vision

From the Principal校長的話・辦學理念

Shaping a shared vision, and walking it together — child, teacher, family, and community.共構願景，一起前行——孩子、老師、家庭與社區

13th Principal · since 2025

Chen Shih-chieh陳世杰 校長

Principal Chen took office in August 2025 as the 13th leader of Chung Hsing Elementary. As the school approaches its 70th anniversary, his question is a simple one a graduate once asked him — "Everything has changed except the playground; how do we leave children a lasting green memory?"陳世杰校長於 2025 年 8 月接任中興國小第十三任校長。面對即將到來的 70 週年，他常記得一位校友的話——「除了操場，其餘都變了」，並以此自問：如何為孩子留下長久的綠色回憶？

"A school should grow children who are confident, kind, and rooted in their own place — yet brave enough to walk into the world."「教育要讓孩子有自信、有品格、紮根在地，又勇敢走向世界。」
