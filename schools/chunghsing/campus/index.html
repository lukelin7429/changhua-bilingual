🌳 Heritage Trees · 校園經典樹

Trees That Remember Us記得我們的老樹

As Chung Hsing approaches its 70th year, the camphor behind the classrooms and the chinaberry by the slide are cared for as living "heritage trees" — pruned gently by professional climbers so they grow open and healthy, a green memory handed from one generation to the next.迎接 70 週年，校舍後方的樟樹與溜滑梯旁的苦楝樹被認養為活的「經典樹」，由專業攀樹團隊友善修剪，讓老樹開闊而健康——把綠色記憶，一代一代傳下去。
