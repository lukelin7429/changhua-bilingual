I.

Welcome to Shuiwei

歡迎來到水尾國小

Learning with joy, living with happiness, and shining bright.

在地樂活、在地樂學、光耀水尾。

Shuiwei Elementary School sits among the rice fields of Sijhou Township, Changhua. It is a small but closely-knit school, where students and teachers look after one another like family, and where every child is helped to discover what they are good at.

水尾國小坐落在彰化縣溪州鄉的田野之間，是一所小而溫馨、充滿凝聚力的學校。在這裡，師生像家人一樣互相關心，也用心陪伴每一位孩子找到自己的長處。

Our work grows out of one idea — “live well here, learn well here, let Shuiwei shine.” From it we have built four key learning areas, three signature programmes of our own, and a school spirit we call YOUNG: our hope that every Shuiwei child becomes a young green gem in the middle of the fields.

學校的一切，都從一個想法開始——「在地樂活、在地樂學、光耀水尾」。在這個理念下，我們發展出四大特色課程、三項校本課程，以及一種名為 YOUNG 的校風：期待每個水尾的孩子，都能成為田埂中的 YOUNG 綠寶石。

Love the purple scallion, carry on the Three Treasures, and let Shuiwei shine with excellence.
愛戀紫蘿葱．傳承三寶情．卓越耀水尾。

III.

Four Key Learning Areas

四大特色課程

To help students grow, we focus on four things.

為了幫助孩子全面發展，我們規劃了四大學習方向。

Caring for Nature
生態永續

Learning about the plants and animals around us, and how to protect the environment we share — from the old trees on campus to the fields and waterways of Sijhou. 認識身邊的植物與動物，學習如何愛護我們共同的環境——從校園裡的老樹，到溪州的田園與水圳。

Healthy Living
健康樂活

Staying active, eating well and building a positive mind — on the running track, the sports field and the playground that ring our campus. 養成運動習慣、吃得健康、保持正向的心——在環繞校園的跑道、操場與遊戲場上。

Global Vision
全球視野

Understanding different cultures and widening the way we see the world, so a small village school opens onto a much bigger place. 認識不同的文化，拓展看世界的方式，讓一所鄉間小校，通往更寬廣的天地。

Creativity & Talent
創意展能

Expressing ideas through art, music and hands-on projects — the murals along our corridors are painted by the children themselves. 透過藝術、音樂與實作課程表達自己——走廊上的壁畫，就是孩子們親手畫的。

IV.

Three Signature Programmes

三項校本課程

Learning designed to be local, and to be fun.

立足在地、樂在其中的學習設計。

Purple Scallions · Love for Arts · Three Treasures of Shuiwei
紫蘿蔥 · 愛玩藝 · 三寶 YOUNG 水尾

Purple Scallions takes students out to explore the local plants of Sijhou and the culture that has grown up around them. Love for Arts nurtures creativity through music, dance and craft. The Three Treasures of Shuiwei introduces children to the school’s own traditions and the story of the place they live — so that learning here always keeps its feet on Shuiwei ground.

紫蘿蔥帶孩子走出教室，認識溪州的在地植物與其中的文化。愛玩藝透過音樂、舞蹈與手作，培養創造力。三寶 YOUNG 水尾則讓孩子認識學校的特色傳統與家鄉的故事——讓水尾的學習，永遠踏在水尾的土地上。

V.

The YOUNG Spirit & SWES Values

YOUNG 精神與 SWES 核心價值

Small in size, and growing strong together.

學校雖然不大，卻一起穩健地成長。

Youthful · Outstanding · Undertake · Notable · Grope
活力 · 卓越 · 創新 · 耀眼 · 探索

At Shuiwei, students grow with a YOUNG spirit — and we hope every child leaves us able to sense, observe, cooperate, express, explore and practise: six abilities to carry with them for life.
在水尾，孩子帶著 YOUNG 的精神長大；我們期待每個孩子畢業時，都帶著感知力、觀察力、合作力、表達力、探索力、實踐力——六大帶得走的能力。
