Campaign Day

政見發表會
←Back to Video Lessons回到影片課總覽

Hand-drawn posters, a microphone, and a corridor full of listeners. The promises are all about the same thing — keeping Shuiwei clean and green.手繪海報、一支麥克風，走廊上擠滿了聽眾。政見講的都是同一件事——讓水尾更乾淨、更環保。

Words to Know · 單字

Six words from the video
影片裡的六個單字

candidate/ˈkændɪdeɪt/候選人

Every candidate gets a turn to speak.每位候選人都有上台的機會。

poster/ˈpoʊstər/海報

They draw the poster by hand.海報是他們自己手繪的。

speech/spiːtʃ/演說

The speech is short but clear.演說很短，但講得很清楚。

promise/ˈprɑːmɪs/政見

One promise is to recycle every day.其中一個政見是每天做好資源回收。

recycle/ˌriːˈsaɪkl/回收

We recycle paper, cans and bottles.我們回收紙、鐵鋁罐和瓶子。

vote/voʊt/投票

Please vote for me!請投我一票！
