Welcome Our Guests

歡迎來賓
←Back to Video Lessons回到影片課總覽

In the courtyard, the guests say hello over the microphone. Then the children, lined up in blue, perform their welcome piece and dance together.在中庭，來賓拿著麥克風向大家問好。接著穿藍衣的孩子排好隊，表演歡迎的節目，大家一起跳舞。

Words to Know · 單字

Six words from the video
影片裡的六個單字

guest/ɡest/來賓

Our guests come from the parents' association.我們的來賓來自家長協會。

welcome/ˈwelkəm/歡迎

We welcome them in the courtyard.我們在中庭歡迎他們。

microphone/ˈmaɪkrəfoʊn/麥克風

She says hello into the microphone.她對著麥克風向大家問好。

perform/pərˈfɔːrm/表演

We perform for our guests.我們為來賓表演。

dance/dæns/跳舞

The whole class dances in rows.全班排成一排跳舞。

together/təˈɡeðər/一起

We do it all together.我們全部一起做。
