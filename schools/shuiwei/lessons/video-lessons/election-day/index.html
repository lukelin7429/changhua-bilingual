Election Day

投票日
←Back to Video Lessons回到影片課總覽

Students line up at the desk, receive one ballot, step behind the booth, stamp their choice, and drop it in the box. Then everyone watches the count.同學在領票處排隊、領到一張選票，走進圈票處蓋章，再投進票匭。接著大家一起看開票。

Words to Know · 單字

Six words from the video
影片裡的六個單字

election/ɪˈlekʃn/選舉

The school holds a real election.學校舉辦了一場真正的選舉。

ballot/ˈbælət/選票

You get one ballot at the desk.在領票處領到一張選票。

booth/buːθ/圈票處

Step behind the booth so no one sees.走進圈票處，這樣沒有人看得到。

stamp/stæmp/蓋章

Stamp the name you choose.在你選的名字上蓋章。

box/bɑːks/票匭

Put your ballot in the box.把選票投進票匭。

count/kaʊnt/開票

We count the votes together.我們一起開票計數。
