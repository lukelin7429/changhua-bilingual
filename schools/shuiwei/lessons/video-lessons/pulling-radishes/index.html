Words to Know · 單字

Six words from the video
影片裡的六個單字

radish/ˈrædɪʃ/白蘿蔔

We pull a big white radish out of the ground.我們從土裡拔出一條又大又白的蘿蔔。

pull/pʊl/拔

Hold the leaves and pull hard.抓住葉子，用力往上拔。

root/ruːt/根

The long white part is the root.白白長長的那一段就是根。

garden/ˈɡɑːrdn/菜園

Our class grows vegetables in the school garden.我們班在學校的菜園裡種菜。

sink/sɪŋk/水槽

Carry the radishes over to the sink.把蘿蔔拿到水槽邊。

green/ɡriːn/綠色的

The leaves are big and green.葉子又大又綠。
