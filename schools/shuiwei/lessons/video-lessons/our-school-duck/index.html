Our School Duck

我們的校園小鴨
←Back to Video Lessons回到影片課總覽

On the way to school, a girl found a duckling in a ditch. Now the whole school takes care of it — feeding it at break time, watching it swim, and walking with it around the track.一位同學在上學路上，從水溝裡撿到一隻小鴨。現在全校一起照顧牠：下課餵牠、看牠游泳、陪牠在操場散步。

Words to Know · 單字

Six words from the video
影片裡的六個單字

duck/dʌk/鴨子

Our school duck is small and yellow.我們學校的小鴨又小又黃。

water/ˈwɔːtər/水

We put clean water in a big bowl.我們在大盆子裡裝乾淨的水。

swim/swɪm/游泳

The duck likes to swim in the bowl.小鴨喜歡在盆子裡游泳。

feed/fiːd/餵

At break time we feed the duck.下課時間我們餵小鴨。

walk/wɔːk/走路

The duckling can walk all by itself.小鴨可以自己走路。

pet/pet/寵物

The duck is our school pet now.小鴨現在是我們學校的寵物。
