I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Shuiwei Elementary School is led by Principal Tai Feng-tian, who was sworn in on 31 July 2026. He came to the role from a long path through Changhua’s schools — special education, classroom teaching, school administration and the county education office — and holds a master’s degree in elementary education from National Taichung Teachers College and a bachelor’s in special education from National Tainan Teachers College.

水尾國小由戴豐田校長帶領，於民國 115 年 7 月 31 日宣誓就職。他一路走過彰化的校園——特殊教育、班級教學、學校行政，到縣府教育處——並取得國立臺中師範學院國教所碩士，以及國立臺南師範學院特殊教育學系學士。

That range shapes how he leads. He knows the county’s policy work from the inside, and he also knows what a single classroom, a school building project or a difficult parent conversation asks of a teacher. His aim for Shuiwei is simple: a school where teachers are supported and children believe in themselves.

這樣的歷練，形塑了他帶領學校的方式。他熟悉縣府政策的宏觀視野，也清楚一間教室、一項校園工程、一次不容易的親師對話，對老師意味著什麼。他對水尾的期待很單純：一所老師被支持、孩子相信自己的學校。

Lift teachers with love and professionalism; shape children’s bright futures with confidence and a sense of achievement.
用愛和專業來提升教師教學品質，以自信與成就型塑孩子美好未來。

IV.

Educational Philosophy

辦學理念

Two commitments — one to teachers, one to children.

兩個承諾——一個給老師，一個給孩子。

用愛和專業，提升教師教學品質
Lift the quality of teaching with love and with professionalism.
The heart of Principal Tai’s philosophy — and the reason the rest of it holds together. 戴校長辦學理念的核心，也是其餘各項得以成立的原因。
對教師端 · For Teachers
Love & Professionalism
用「愛與專業」凝聚教學團隊，提升教師的教學品質，給偏鄉孩子最優質的陪伴。
對學生端 · For Students
Confidence & Achievement
引導孩子建立「自信」，在學習中獲得「成就感」，用溫暖與耐心型塑孩子美好的未來。
A school with time for every child
一所有時間陪伴每個孩子的學校

Principal Tai often says that every child is different — and that a small school like Shuiwei is exactly the place to give each of them the attention they deserve.

戴校長常說，每個孩子都不一樣；而水尾這樣的小校，正是能夠好好照顧每一個孩子的地方。

With love and professionalism for our teachers, and confidence and achievement for our children — that is how Shuiwei shines.
對老師，用愛與專業；對孩子，給自信與成就——這就是水尾發光的方式。
