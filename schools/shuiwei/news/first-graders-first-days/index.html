The First-Graders' First Days

小一新生的開學日
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

This week our youngest students started school. On the first day, each new first-grader wrote a "wish card" and hung it on the Wish Tree in the classroom — a small wish that showed just how big a Shuiwei child's imagination can be. Step by step, the children will chase those dreams and build a future.

本週，水尾最小的孩子們開學了。開學第一天，每位小一新生都寫下一張「祈願卡」，掛上教室裡的祈願樹——小小的願望，看見水尾孩子無限的想像。踏實逐夢，一步一步築夢未來。

Principal Tai read the picture book "A Well-Mannered Wolf" to the class. Through the big wolf's change of heart and behaviour, the story passes on a message the school holds dear: knowledge shapes character, and reading widens the way we see the world.

豐田校長為小一新生講故事，今天分享的是《一隻有教養的狼》這本繪本。透過大野狼心境與行為的轉變，傳達了學校重視的核心價值——知識改變氣質，閱讀拓展視野。

The first-graders also enjoyed their very first school lunch — Vermont-style curry chicken, tuna "clever" egg, a Japanese-style stew, organic cabbage, winter-melon and pork-rib soup, and dragon fruit. School is a place for learning, the principal told them; take it to heart, learn all you can, and the you of the future will become a child that people are glad to know.

小一新生也享用了人生第一頓學校營養午餐——佛蒙特咖哩雞、鮪魚聰明蛋、日式築間煮、有機白菜、冬瓜排骨湯，還有火龍果。校長告訴孩子：學校是學習的場域，請用心體會、盡情學習，未來的你會成為一位人見人愛的好孩子。
