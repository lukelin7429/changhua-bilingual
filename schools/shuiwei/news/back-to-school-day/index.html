Back-to-School Day

返校日：精靈回到水尾
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On 28 August, the campus came back to life. Shuiwei children walked back through the gate with happy steps — the "black rice, radish and scallion sprites," as we fondly call them, home again.

8 月 28 日返校日，水尾校園開始熱鬧起來。孩子們踩著愉快的步伐回到熟悉的校園——我們口中的「黑米、蘿蔔、青蔥精靈」，回來了。

They swept the campus, tidied their classrooms, chatted with friends and talked with their teachers. A clean campus and busy, cheerful children — it really is the best view a school can have.

他們打掃校園、整理教室，同儕閒聊、師生對話。乾淨的校園、忙碌又開心的孩子——這真的是學校最美的風景。

Back-to-school day opens the curtain on the new term. Everyone is already looking forward to the sparks of learning that the new semester will bring.

返校日為開學拉開序幕。大家都開始期待新學期激盪出的學習火花。
