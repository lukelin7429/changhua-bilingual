After-School Study Time

學童課後學習活動
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

After the last bell, the classroom lights stay on. Students go back to their familiar seats, open their books and workbooks, and write carefully, stroke by stroke. The room fills with a quiet, focused mood for learning.

放學後的教室依然燈火通明！同學們回到熟悉的座位上，認真攤開課本、作業簿，一筆一劃專心寫著，教室裡瀰漫著濃濃的學習氛圍。

Teachers move around the room, patient and unhurried, answering questions one by one so that every child can keep up. Whether it is quiet self-study, small-group discussion, or following extra material on the board, the students show real concentration. Some happily choose a new book and enjoy a little reading time.

老師們在一旁耐心巡視、個別指導，隨時解答同學的疑問，讓每個孩子都能穩穩跟上學習腳步。不管是安靜自習、小組討論，還是跟著老師看黑板上的補充教材，孩子們都展現滿滿的專注力；也有孩子開心地選書、換書，享受閱讀的美好時光。

After-school study is not only about finishing homework. It is a precious time when teachers and students build knowledge together and grow the habit of learning on your own. Thank you to every teacher who stays behind to walk alongside the children, and to every student for a serious, careful attitude to learning.

課後學習活動不只是完成作業，更是老師和同學共同累積知識、培養自主學習習慣的珍貴時刻。感謝每位老師課後留下來用心陪伴孩子；也謝謝同學認真投入、不馬虎的學習態度。
