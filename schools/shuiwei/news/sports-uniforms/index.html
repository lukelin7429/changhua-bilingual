New Sports Uniforms for Every Class

全校運動服發放囉！
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Good news for the new term! This week the school handed out its new sports uniform to every class, from Grade 1 to Grade 6. The set is a soft blue T-shirt with the school's water-drop crest, worn with navy sports shorts, so children can move freely and feel fresh in class, in PE and at play.

開學好消息！本週學校已將新的運動服發送到一到六年級每個班級。這套服裝是印有水滴校徽的柔軟藍色 T 恤，搭配深藍運動短褲，讓孩子們上課、運動、玩耍都能自在活動，穿起來清爽有精神。

The uniforms were donated by a local supporter, Mr. Zhong Jin-xing. Thanks to his gift, every Shuiwei child can start the school year in clothes that fit well and feel comfortable. Whether it is the blue shirt, the yellow shirt or the shorts, each piece carries the community's warm care for the school.

這批運動服由地方善心人士鐘進興委員愛心捐贈。因為這份心意，水尾的每個孩子都能穿上舒適合身的衣物，開心迎接新學年。不管是藍 T、黃 T 還是運動短褲，每一件都是社會大眾對水尾國小滿滿的關愛與支持。

Teachers hope the children will wear the uniform with a grateful heart — keeping it clean and looking after it, so this warm blessing lasts a long time. Families are welcome to talk with their children about saying thank you; learning to count your blessings is an important part of character education.

老師們希望同學收到運動服時心存感恩，好好珍惜——記得愛惜衣物、保持整潔，讓這份溫暖的祝福傳遞得更長久。也歡迎家長和孩子聊聊感謝的心情，讓孩子從小學會惜福、感恩，這也是品格教育很重要的一環。
