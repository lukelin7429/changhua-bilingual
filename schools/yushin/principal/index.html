I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Lin Yuan-ping leads Yushin with a single guiding line: "rooted at home, eyes on the world, learners for life." Her teaching philosophy is just as clear — "to pour fresh water into every child's life, and create moments that lift them to their peak."

林媛萍校長以一句話帶領育新：「在地扎根、放眼世界、終身學習」。她的教育理念同樣清晰——「為孩子的生命注入活水，創造高峰經驗」。

She holds a master's degree in Special Education from National Changhua University of Education, and completed the national training programs for both elementary principals and directors. Before Yushin, she served Changhua's Education Department as a curriculum supervisor, as a mentor on the county's integrative-studies guidance team, and as a seed instructor for the 108 Curriculum and for local-language education.

她擁有國立彰化師範大學特殊教育學系碩士，並完成國民小學校長及主任儲訓。來到育新之前，她曾任彰化縣教育處課程督學、綜合領域教學輔導團兼任輔導員，以及 108 課綱與本土語文教育的種子講師。

Her care for students has been recognized again and again: Changhua Outstanding Special-Education Teacher (2007), the Ministry of Education's Outstanding Digital-Companion Teacher (2016), and the 8th "Educator of Great Love" Distinguished Teacher Award (2019).

她對學生的用心一再獲得肯定：2007 年彰化縣特殊優良教師、2016 年教育部數位學伴計畫傑出帶班老師，以及108 年第八屆教育大愛「菁師獎」。

II.

Four Guiding Ideas

四大經營理念

Every child seen, every difference valued.

看見每個孩子的差異，點燃他們學習的動機與熱情。

🌏
Bilingual & Globally Connected
雙語國際 · 全球鏈結

Bilingual ability grows from the everyday feel of campus life. Through cross-cultural exchange and global issues, students build real international awareness.

💻
Technology for the Future
科技教育 · 翻轉未來

Cloud resources and mobile devices open up creative, hands-on learning — and grow students who solve problems and apply technology with confidence.

📖
Character First, Reading at Heart
品德為首 · 閱讀為重

Character education and a deep reading culture anchor everything — laying the foundation for lifelong learning and a kinder community.

🤝
Local Roots, Shared Good
校本課程 · 在地共好

A school-based curriculum that draws on community resources, lets education flow into the village, and roots local culture in a shared, thriving future.
