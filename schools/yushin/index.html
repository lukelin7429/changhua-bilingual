I.

Welcome

歡迎來到育新國小

A small country school beside the wetland, with the whole world in view.

一所緊鄰溼地的小校，卻把整個世界放進孩子的眼界裡。

Yushin Elementary sits in Ergang Village, Fuxing Township, right beside the Fubao Wetland on the Changhua coast — a place known as a "paradise for water birds" and the "home of dairy cows." Egrets wade in the mudflats, milk trucks pass the rice fields, and our children grow up with the land and the tide as their first teachers.

育新國小坐落於福興鄉二港村，緊鄰彰化海岸的福寶溼地——這裡素有「水鳥天堂」與「乳牛故鄉」之稱。白鷺鷥在潮間帶覓食，運乳車駛過稻田，育新的孩子以這片土地與潮汐為第一位老師。

Founded in 1960, our school was built in the spirit the community still remembers as "a school raised on a peck of rice" (斗米建校) — teachers, families, and neighbors who turned a patch of wasteland into a learning home with whatever they could give. That same spirit lives on today as the school's three values: gratitude, thankfulness, and kindness.

學校創立於民國 49 年（1960），秉持地方至今仍津津樂道的「斗米建校」精神——師生、家長與鄉親有錢出錢、有力出力，把一片荒地建設成學習的家園。這份精神化為今日育新的三大價值：惜福、感恩、友善。

Under Principal Lin Yuan-ping, Yushin follows one guiding line — "rooted at home, eyes on the world, learners for life." Bilingual learning, environmental education, and a proud sporting tradition all grow from it.

在林媛萍校長的帶領下，育新以一句話為辦學方針——「在地扎根、放眼世界、終身學習」。雙語學習、環境教育與引以為傲的運動傳統，都從這裡生長。

II.

What Makes Yushin

育新的特色
🦆
Beside the Wetland
福寶溼地畔

Our campus borders the Fubao Wetland — a haven for egrets and migratory birds, and the dairy country of the Changhua coast. Nature is the first classroom at Yushin.

🌱
A US–Taiwan Eco-School
臺美生態學校

Through our Green Fence Project and a US–Taiwan Eco-School partnership, students learn to care for the land — and to talk about it in two languages.

🚲
Champions on Wheels & Track
自行車與田徑常勝

From slow-cycling balance races to the national shot-put final, Yushin's small team keeps bringing home medals — and even our teachers compete.

🌏
Bilingual & Global
雙語國際 · 全球鏈結

"Bilingual and globally connected" is the first of our principal's guiding ideas. Everyday English flows through campus life, from morning assembly to the lunch cart.

Where the egrets fly, the children dream
白鷺飛處 · 孩子放眼世界

From a wetland village on the Changhua coast, Yushin's children learn that a small school by the sea can reach the whole wide world — in two languages.
