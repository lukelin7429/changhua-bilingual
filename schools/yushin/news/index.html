A Visit from Teacher Dom

Dom Jones 老師到校採訪
A familiar face from the Changhua bilingual family stops by Yushin.
彰化雙語大家庭的熟面孔，來到了育新校園。

On April 7, 2026, Teacher Dom Jones — a partner teacher across the Changhua Bilingual Hub — visited Yushin Elementary to report on campus life and meet our students. Walking the corridors with a camera and a friendly hello, he turned an ordinary school day into a chance for our children to use real English with a real guest.

2026 年 4 月 7 日，Dom Jones 老師——彰化雙語資源網的夥伴教師——來到育新國小，採訪校園生活、認識我們的學生。他帶著相機、一句親切的問候走過校園走廊，把平凡的上學日，變成孩子們與真實訪客開口說英語的好機會。

Athletics · 全國賽事

A National Shot-Put Finalist全國田徑錦標賽 鉛球第七名

Jun 3, 2026 · 2026 年 6 月 3 日

Huang Cheng-hong, a sixth grader at Yushin, threw his way to seventh place in the boys' shot put at the 2026 National Junior High and Elementary School Track and Field Championship. For a tiny six-class country school to place at a national meet is a point of real pride — and proof that talent can come from anywhere, even a wetland village by the sea.

育新六年級的黃丞鴻在「2026 年全國中小學田徑錦標賽」中，勇奪國小男生組鉛球第七名。一所只有六班的海邊小校能在全國賽事中名列前茅，是真正令人驕傲的成就——也證明了天分可以來自任何地方，即使是海邊的溼地小村。

Cycling · 自行車

Champions on Two Wheels — Even the Teacher慢速車競賽 全隊奪冠

May 4, 2026 · 2026 年 5 月 4 日

At the Director's Cup slow-cycling competition — where the rider who balances the longest, not the fastest, wins — Yushin swept the team championship. Fifth grader Huang Yu-qing took fifth in the upper-grade class and fourth grader Huang Yi-chen seventh in the middle grades. Even teacher Chen Xin-hui joined in, riding to second place in the adult division.

在訓育組長盃慢速車競賽中——比的是誰能平衡最久、而非最快——育新一舉拿下團體冠軍。五年級黃昱晴獲高年級組第五名、四年級黃翊宸獲中年級組第七名。連陳心慧老師都下場參賽，騎出成人組第二名的佳績。

Eco-School · 生態學校

The Green Fence Project育新國小綠籬專案

Environmental Education · 環境教育

As a US–Taiwan Eco-School partner, Yushin lives its environmental promise close to home. Through the Green Fence Project, students plant and tend a living hedge around the campus — learning, hands in the soil, how a school can be greener, cooler, and kinder to the birds of the nearby Fubao Wetland.

作為臺美生態學校夥伴，育新把環境承諾落實在身邊。透過綠籬專案，學生親手種植、照顧校園四周的綠籬——雙手沾著泥土，學習一所學校如何變得更綠、更涼爽，也對鄰近福寶溼地的水鳥更友善。

Wetland · 福寶溼地

Growing Up Beside a Bird Paradise在水鳥天堂旁長大

School Identity · 校園特色

Yushin's classrooms sit a short walk from the Fubao Wetland, one of the Changhua coast's great havens for egrets and migratory birds, in a district known as the "home of dairy cows." Wetland, farmland, and seashore make a living science lab — and a powerful reason to learn the English words for the nature on our doorstep.

育新的教室距離福寶溼地只有幾步之遙——這裡是彰化海岸白鷺鷥與候鳥的重要棲地，也是聞名的「乳牛故鄉」。溼地、農田與海岸構成一座活的自然教室，也是孩子們學習身邊自然英語的最佳理由。

Athletics · 接力

Relay Teams Run at the County Meet縣運接力 雙項第五名

Feb 2, 2026 · 2026 年 2 月 2 日

Yushin's relay squad — Chen Qian-yu, Nian Rong-xuan, Huang Yue-xuan, and Huang Yi — sprinted to fifth place in both the 400m and 800m relays at the Changhua County Combined Sports Meet. Earlier in the spring, Yushin athletes also placed at the New Taipei Youth Cup and the Taipei Spring National Championship.

育新接力隊——陳芊妤、粘容萱、黃悅瑄、黃毅——在彰化縣全縣運動會中，於 400 公尺與 800 公尺接力雙雙拿下第五名。春季稍早，育新選手也在新北青年盃與台北春季全國賽中名列前茅。

Bilingual · 雙語校園

English in Every Corner of the Day校園日常處處是英文

Lessons · 雙語課程

From the morning assembly to the lunch cart, Yushin turns everyday routines into English with its Word of the Day video library — 27 campus words across four themes — plus Classroom English and Bilingual Announcements. It's the principal's first guiding idea in action: bilingual, and globally connected.

從朝會到午餐餐車，育新用每日一字影片庫把日常變成英文——27 個校園單字、四大主題——再加上課室英語與英語廣播。這正是校長第一條經營理念的實踐：雙語國際、全球鏈結。
