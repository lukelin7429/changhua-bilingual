II.

Classroom English

課室英語 · 給老師的口袋句庫（影片版）
The phrases teachers say between the lessons — taught on video.
老師在課堂裡每天會用上的英文句子——這次有人示範給你看。

A practical video library of the everyday English a Taiwanese teacher actually uses: greetings and roll call, pre-class set-up, instructions, praise and correction, classroom management, lesson activities, assigning homework, wrapping up, and exam directions. Each video isolates one situation so the phrases stick on first hearing.

這個影片教材庫涵蓋臺灣老師每天會用到的課堂英語——問候點名、課前準備、講解、回饋讚美糾正、教室管理、課堂活動、分派作業、課堂收尾、考試指令。每支影片只處理一個情境，第一次聽就能記住。

III.

Bilingual Announcements

英語廣播 · 校園的英語之聲
The morning intercom in two languages — turning daily announcements into bilingual exposure.
早上的校園廣播，雙語版本——把每天的廣播時間，變成全校學生的英語浸潤。

A bilingual announcement series that reframes the school's daily public-address routine as real-world English. The same notices children hear every morning — events, reminders, and office news — modeled in clear, friendly English alongside Chinese, so the language of school life becomes the language of learning.

這是一個雙語校園廣播系列，把學校每天的廣播例行公事，變成真實情境的英語學習。孩子們每天早上聽到的活動、提醒與處室訊息，都以清楚、親切的英文搭配中文呈現——讓校園生活的語言，成為學習的語言。
