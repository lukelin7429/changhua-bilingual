Unit 01 · A School Day at Yushin

校園日常的英文 · 9 個字
Word 01sweep(v.)
掃
Every day, students sweep away fallen leaves on school grounds.每天學生打掃校園的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.台灣的學生打掃校園，保持校園的乾淨整齊。
Word 02clean the floor(v.)
擦地板
The boys are working together to clean the floor.小男孩一起在擦地板。
They use mops to clean the floor, removing dust and debris.他們使用拖把擦地板，將灰塵與垃圾去除。
Word 03morning assembly(n.)
朝會
Morning assembly is a meeting that administrators, teachers, and students attend every morning at school.朝會是行政人員、老師和學生每天早上要參加的聚會。
Morning assembly is a great time for staff to share important announcements with the student body.朝會是教職人員向學生宣布重要事情的好時機。
Word 04serve(v.)
打菜
The students serve lunch to their classmates.學生為同學打菜。
The task of serving changes every week.打菜的工作每週輪替一次。
Word 05food cart(n.)
餐車
At lunch time, a food cart loaded with various dishes is brought to the classroom.午餐時間，裝滿不同菜色的餐車被推到教室。
Some students are responsible for serving their classmates food from the food cart.一些學生負責將餐車上的食物分配給同學。
Word 06event(n.)
活動事件
The school held a special event to celebrate Children's Day.學校舉辦了一場特別的活動來慶祝兒童節。
At the event, performers sang, played instruments, and danced.在活動中，表演者唱歌、演奏樂器和跳舞。
Word 07math class(n.)
數學課
The students are in math class.學生們正在上數學課。
The teacher teaches math in math class.老師在數學課教數學。
Word 08kindergarten(n.)
幼兒園
Children play different games in kindergarten.小朋友在幼兒園玩不同的遊戲。
In kindergarten, students learn basic skills.學生在幼兒園學習基本的技能。
Word 09professional development(n.)
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.台灣所有學校都有老師專業進修的課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.老師在專業進修課程中學習新技能與教學策略。

Unit 02 · Sports Day

運動場上的英文 · 7 個字
Word 01long jump(n.)
跳遠
Are you good at the long jump?你擅長跳遠嗎？
The students are practicing the long jump.學生們正在練習跳遠。
Word 02high jump(n.)
跳高
The high jump takes a lot of practice.跳高需要很多練習。
The high jump is a fun event to watch.跳高是很好看的賽事。
Word 03relay race(n.)
接力賽跑
The students are practicing for their relay race.學生們正在為他們的接力賽做練習。
A baton is a stick passed in a relay race.接力棒是在接力賽中傳遞的棍子。
Word 04run(v.)
跑步
The students are running at the track.學生們在跑道上跑步。
Running is good exercise and students do it every day.跑步是很好的運動，學生們每天都在跑步。
Word 05Taekwondo(n.)
跆拳道
The students are learning Taekwondo in PE.學生們在體育課學習跆拳道。
You learn to kick high in Taekwondo.在跆拳道中，你學習踢得很高。
Word 06swing(v.)
盪鞦韆
During recess, students like to swing.下課休息時，學生們喜歡盪鞦韆。
You kick your legs up and down to swing.你上下踢腿擺動來盪鞦韆。
Word 07dance routine(n.)
舞步
The students work very hard, rehearsing their dance routines.學生很認真排練他們的舞步。
Once they know the moves by heart, they perform their dance routines on the stage.一旦他們將舞步練到很熟之後，就在舞台上表演。

Unit 03 · Arts & Discovery

書法、詩與探索 · 4 個字
Word 01calligraphy(n.)
書法
Students practice calligraphy at school.學生們在學校練習書法。
Calligraphy is a beautiful way to write.書法是一種美麗的書寫方式。
Word 02poem(n.)
詩
The students practice reciting a poem.學生們練習背誦一首詩。
Poems can express a feeling or idea.詩歌可以表達情感或想法。
Word 03puzzle(n.)
拼圖
The students are putting the puzzle together.學生們正在拼圖。
They are organizing the puzzle pieces to put it together.他們正在將拼圖片組在一起。
Word 04planisphere(n.)
星座盤
These students learn how to use a planisphere.這些學生學習如何使用星座盤。
They use a planisphere outside to learn more about constellations.他們使用室外的星座盤來了解更多關於星座的資訊。

Unit 04 · Garden & Health

田園、食物與健康 · 7 個字
Word 01carrot(n.)
紅蘿蔔
The teacher shows how to pick a carrot.老師示範如何挑選胡蘿蔔。
The student pulls the carrot out of the ground.學生從地裡拔出胡蘿蔔。
Word 02tomato(n.)
番茄
The teacher explains when to pick tomatoes.老師跟學生解釋何時是採摘番茄的最佳時機。
The students look for ripe tomatoes.學生們尋找成熟的番茄。
Word 03plum(n.)
梅子
The kids are picking plums.孩子們正在摘梅子。
Plums are a tasty treat.梅子很美味好吃。
Word 04bread(n.)
麵包
The teacher is cutting up the bread.老師正在切麵包。
The students are eating the bread.學生們正在吃麵包。
Word 05stone(n.)
石頭
Children are picking up stones.孩子們正在撿石頭。
Stones are not fun to fall on.若跌倒在石頭上面可不好玩。
Word 06molar teeth(n.)
臼齒
Molar teeth are the teeth in the back of your mouth, right in front of your wisdom teeth.臼齒是嘴巴後方的牙齒，就在智齒之前。
The dentist performs a procedure to protect the kids' molar teeth from cavities.牙醫師進行醫療程序，保護小朋友的臼齒免於蛀牙。
Word 07healthy lifestyle(n.)
健康的生活方式
The teacher is teaching students how to maintain a healthy lifestyle.老師正在教學生如何保持健康的生活方式。
To keep a healthy lifestyle, get enough sleep, exercise regularly, eat well, and care for your mental health.要保持健康的生活方式，應有充足睡眠、規律運動、健康飲食，並照顧心理健康。
