Summer Reading Trip: A Day at the Taiwanese Culture Park

夏日樂學走讀趣｜臺語文創意園區文化體驗
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Our Summer Reading children left campus today for the Taiwanese Language & Culture Creative Park. With a park guide, they learned about the history and character of the Taiwanese language, and took on a Taiwanese fruit-name challenge through interactive games.

本校參加夏日樂學的孩子今日走出校園，來到臺語文創意園區。在園區老師的導覽下，認識臺語文的發展與特色，透過互動遊戲挑戰臺語水果名稱。

Then they stepped into the traditional-opera classroom, looking closely at the glove puppets and the ornate puppet stage, and even picked up a puppet themselves to try the fun of working it.

接著走進傳統戲曲教室，近距離欣賞布袋戲偶與華麗戲臺，還親自拿起戲偶體驗操偶的樂趣。

The afternoon was bath-bomb DIY. From getting to know the materials and choosing a scent, to colouring, mixing and shaping, the children did every step themselves. Bright powders slowly became dreamy, sweet-smelling bath bombs in their hands, and everyone left with a real sense of achievement.

下午是「泡澡球 DIY」！孩子們從認識材料、聞香選擇，到調色、攪拌、塑形，每一步都自己動手完成。繽紛的粉末在一雙雙小手中，慢慢變成色彩夢幻、香氣十足的泡澡球，大家臉上滿是成就感！
