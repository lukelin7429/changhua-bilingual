Let's Throw a Banquet! Learning Taiwanese Through a Board Game

夏日樂學｜做伙來辦桌
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Pick up your ingredient cards and put on your chef's hat — today I'm the banquet chef! There was no stove in the Summer Reading class today, but the room still turned into a cooking contest, thanks to the fun board game Let's Throw a Banquet.

食材卡拿起來、廚師帽戴起來！今仔日我是「總舖師」！夏日樂學課程今天不開火，卻在教室裡展開「料理爭霸戰」——靠的是趣味桌遊《做伙來辦桌》。

The game is built around Taiwan's banquet culture and its dishes. Familiar everyday ingredients become cards, and each card has a clear picture and Chinese characters, plus zhuyin and Taiwanese romanisation — so children naturally learn the name of each ingredient and how to say it in Taiwanese.

《做伙來辦桌》以臺灣「辦桌文化」與料理為主題，把生活中熟悉的食材做成一張張食材卡；卡片上除了圖案與國字，還搭配注音及台語拼音，讓孩子在遊戲中自然認識食材名稱與台語說法。

「I need scallions!」 「I've got garlic — do you have soy sauce?」 To finish a dish, children have to look closely, think, and trade and collect ingredients with their friends. From 「market shopping」 to 「ingredients expired」 and 「market closed」, the cooking dice bring a surprise every round.

「我需要蔥仔！」「我有蒜頭，你有豆油無？」為了完成料理任務，孩子們必須仔細觀察、動動腦，也要和同伴互動、交換與蒐集食材。從「市場採買」、「徵收食材」到「食材過期」、「市場公休」，充滿變化的烹飪骰，每一回合都充滿驚喜！

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

banquet/ˈbæŋkwɪt/辦桌、宴席

Banquet food is cooked for a big table.辦桌是為一大桌人做的菜。

ingredient/ɪnˈɡriːdiənt/食材

Each card shows one ingredient.每張卡片是一種食材。

board game/bɔːrd ɡeɪm/桌遊

They learn Taiwanese with a board game.他們用桌遊學台語。

Taiwanese/ˌtaɪwɑːˈniːz/台語

The cards teach the Taiwanese name too.卡片也教台語怎麼說。

market/ˈmɑːrkɪt/市場

You buy ingredients at the market.你在市場買食材。

trade/treɪd/交換

Children trade cards with their friends.孩子們和朋友交換卡片。

garlic/ˈɡɑːrlɪk/蒜頭

「Do you have garlic?」「你有蒜頭嗎？」

scallion/ˈskæljən/蔥

「I need scallions for this dish!」「這道菜我需要蔥！」
