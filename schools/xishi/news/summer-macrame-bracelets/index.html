One Cord, One Knot: Braiding a Summer Memory

夏日樂學｜一繩一結，編出專屬的夏日回憶
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

In today's Summer Reading class the children became little weaving experts, turning strands of colourful cotton cord into double-square-knot wrist bands.

今天的夏日樂學課程，孩子們化身小小編織達人，用一條條繽紛的棉繩，製作「雙平結手掛繩」！

The teacher first showed the braiding method and the tricks. The children gathered around the table, watching closely, then chose their favourite colours and began to create. A simple-looking knot actually needs you to alternate left and right, control the tension and keep the order right — real patience and focus.

課程一開始，老師示範編織的方法與技巧，孩子們圍在桌旁專注觀察，接著挑選自己喜愛的顏色開始創作。看似簡單的繩結，其實需要掌握左右交替、拉繩力道與排列順序，展現十足的耐心與專注力。

For the children who finished first, the teacher tied on a hand-fired glass bead, and they went on to braid a cute little flower charm. From loose strands of cord to a finished piece of their own, the children held up their work for a happy photo — the best reward of the day.

先完成的同學，老師幫忙繫上她手工燒製的琉璃珠，再繼續編織可愛的「小花朵吊飾」！從一條條棉繩到完成屬於自己的作品，孩子們拿著成果開心合影，就是今天最棒的收穫！
