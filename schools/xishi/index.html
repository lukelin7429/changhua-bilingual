I.

Welcome to Xishi

歡迎來到西勢國小

A wonderful school for all kids.

在西勢，每個孩子都能被培養、被看見。

Hello from Xishi Elementary School · 彰化縣西勢國小校園導覽

Nestled in Fuxing Township, Changhua County, Xishi Elementary School is a thriving educational community where every child's potential is nurtured. Surrounded by rich cultural heritage and natural beauty, our school is a beacon of learning and growth.

西勢國小位處彰化縣福興鄉中央北端，北鄰福鹿溪、遠眺八卦山脈，遙望台灣海峽，坐落在員鹿路與東西向快速道路的交會處，是出入鹿港小鎮的要道。校園環繞著豐富的文化遺產與自然美景，是學習與成長的燈塔。

Academic and athletic excellence. Our students are known for their diligence and balanced development in both academics and sports. We foster an environment where every child can excel and explore their full potential.

文武兼備、學術與運動並重。西勢的學生質樸勤奮，以在學業與運動上的均衡發展著稱。我們用心營造環境，讓每個孩子都能發光，探索自己的全部潛能。

Community and culture. The heart of our school lies in the strong support of our Parent–Teacher Association and dedicated volunteers. Recognised as a national model community, Xishi is rich in cultural resources, bringing community and school together in harmony.

社區與文化。學校的核心，在於家長會與志工隊的熱心參與，社區對學校也十分認同。西勢社區是全國模範社區，文化資源豐富，學校與社區共榮共享，一同為莘莘學子創造美好前景。

Dynamic and holistic education. We create a campus filled with joy, diversity and comprehensive development — integrating bilingual education into everyday life, strengthening arts and aesthetic learning, and embracing technology-assisted self-directed learning. Xishi is a pioneer school for tablet-based learning, constantly evolving to meet the needs of a modern era.

動態、全人的教育。我們積極營造悅讀、樂學、多元、全人的校園：把雙語課程落實在生活中，強化藝文美感教育，導入科技輔助自主學習計畫，是生生用平板前導學校。近年各界資源挹注，軟硬體更加充實豐富。

Where dreams are nurtured, talents are discovered, and futures are bright.
在這裡，夢想被培養、才能被發現、未來光明璀璨——期待下一個 70，繼續為夢想奔馳！
II.

Where We Stand

福鹿溪畔，鹿港之路上

River behind, mountains ahead, the sea in view.

北鄰福鹿溪，遠眺八卦山脈，遙望台灣海峽。

A Gateway School
員鹿路上、通往鹿港的要道

Xishi sits on the northern edge of Fuxing Township, where Yuan-Lu Road meets the east–west expressway — the road travellers take in and out of the historic town of Lukang. Behind the school runs the Fulu River; on a clear day the Bagua Mountain range stands to the east and the Taiwan Strait glints to the west.

西勢國小位在福興鄉中央北端，員鹿路與東西向快速道路的交會處，正是往返鹿港小鎮的必經之路。校園北邊是福鹿溪，晴天時東可望八卦山脈、西可眺台灣海峽。西勢社區為全國模範社區，文化資源豐富，學校志工積極參與校內各項活動。

III.

What Xishi Is Known For

西勢的四個亮點

Four things we put our hearts into.

四個我們最用心經營的方向。

Academic & Athletic Excellence
文武兼備 · 質樸勤奮的西勢學子

Diligent students, balanced in study and sport, with a standard track and a school-wide culture of physical activity. 學生質樸勤奮、文武兼備，標準操場與運動風氣是西勢的日常。

A Model Community
社區共榮 · 全國模範社區裡的學校

Xishi Community is a national model community; parents and volunteers take an active part in school life, and school and community share their resources. 西勢社區為全國模範社區，家長會與志工熱心參與，學校社區共榮共享。

Bilingual Life & the Arts
雙語生活 · 藝文美感教育

Bilingual learning woven into everyday school life, alongside a strong programme of arts and aesthetic education. 把雙語課程落實在生活中，並持續強化藝文與美感教育。

A Tablet-Pioneer School
科技輔助自主學習 · 生生用平板前導學校

A pioneer school for tablet-based learning, using technology to support students' self-directed learning. 導入科技輔助自主學習計畫，是「生生用平板」前導學校。

IV.

Read, Learn, Grow

悅讀、樂學、多元、全人
A Campus Built for Joyful Learning
悅讀樂學的校園

We work to build a campus that is joyful, diverse and whole — from the ocean-themed "Book Sea" library to shaded flowering walkways. As we look to our next chapter, Xishi stays committed to racing towards dreams and building a future of limitless possibilities for our students.

我們持續營造一座悅讀、樂學、多元、全人的校園——從海洋主題的「書勢海洋」圖書館，到綠蔭花架下的步道。展望下一章節，西勢國小仍致力於追逐夢想，為學生打造無限可能的未來。加入西勢國小：在充滿無限機會的世界裡，一起探索、學習、成長！
