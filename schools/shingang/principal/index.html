Principal Shih Shun-Hsin

施舜馨 校長
I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

There is no perfect individual — only a perfect team. I have been fortunate to work alongside a group of passionate colleagues, sharing many moments of pride and gratitude along the way.

沒有完美的個人，只有完美的團隊。舜馨極其有幸，總是能和一群有熱情的夥伴共同打拼，走過許多令人感動及驕傲的時刻。

Without passion, it is difficult to achieve anything meaningful in any field — and that passion begins with a sense of responsibility. Since coming to Shingang, I have deeply felt this education team's devotion to the school and their responsibility toward every child. Their passion and quiet sacrifices are repaid, again and again, in the growth of our students.

一個人若是沒有熱情，很難在各個領域有所成就，而熱情的出發點正是責任心。舜馨來到新港國小，充份感受到新港教育團隊對學校的認同感及對教育的責任心，其中的熱情和犧牲，都在孩子的成長中得到最棒的回饋。

"It takes a village to raise a child" is an old saying, but a wise one. I believe in it wholeheartedly. Everything of real value in this world is built through the combined effort and wisdom of many — and the involvement and support of every parent is the greatest driving force behind our school's progress. Once again, welcome to the Shingang family.

It takes a village to raise a child. 這是一句非常古老卻饒富智慧的話。舜馨十分認同：「教育一個孩子需要一個村莊的力量」。世界上真正有價值的事物，都需要眾人的力量和智慧，而所有家長的參與與支持是學校進步最大的動力，再一次歡迎您加入新港團隊。

"It takes a village to raise a child."
教育一個孩子，需要一整個村莊的力量。
