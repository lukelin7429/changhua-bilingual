Buzzing with Brilliance

「蜂」芒畢露 · 五年級拼字大賽

It's that time again — the end-of-term Spelling Bee is back! Ding! One buzz of the bell, one word on the line.

As Teacher Chris read each English word and sentence clearly and steadily, students raced to write it down before flipping their answer boards. One small slip, and a speller was out — the tension in the room was electric.

133 competitors battled all the way down to a final 10 standing on stage.

Congratulations to the champion who made it through dozens of rounds, and to everyone who earned a spot in the top ten! For the students who were eliminated along the way, the tears in their eyes said it all — proof of how much they cared. But here's the good news: this year introduced a brand-new "Second Chance" award, picking a few of the most accurate spellers from among those eliminated for special recognition. Who will it go to? We can't wait to find out!

中文原文 · Chinese

又到了期末 Spelling Bee 大賽的時間！叮咚！一聲按鈴，一題考驗。

聽著 Chris 老師字正腔圓、緩緩唸出英文單字和句子，孩子們馬上振筆疾書，接著翻牌公佈答案。稍有不慎就成了遺珠，氣氛真的是緊張又刺激。

五年級參賽人數從 133 人，一路激戰到最後 10 人站上舞台。

恭喜歷經數十題考驗、脫穎而出的冠軍，也恭喜每一位站上舞台的前十名！不小心落馬的孩子，眼眶裡的淚水，是在乎、是用心的證明。但沒關係，今年特別增加了「敗部復活」獎，再從落入挑戰區的孩子中，挑出正確度最高的幾位孩子給予獎勵。獎落誰家呢？期待中！
