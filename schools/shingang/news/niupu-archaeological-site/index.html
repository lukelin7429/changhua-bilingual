A Day Trip a Thousand Years Back

考古彰化 · 一日穿越千年

Thanks to a special program from the Changhua County Cultural Affairs Bureau, and to Teacher Kaiwen for applying for the field-trip grant, our students got to walk straight out of their history textbooks and into the real thing — a hands-on visit to the Niupu archaeological site and its companion museum.

Face to face with replicas of prehistoric humans, ancient pottery shards embedded in an excavation wall, and exhibits on early rice farming, students traded their classroom desks for a museum floor and an actual dig site. It was history you could reach out and touch.

Museum plus dig site turned out to be the perfect learning pairing.

Stepping outside the classroom, students met the past up close — a day none of them will forget.

中文原文 · Chinese

謝謝彰化縣文化局舉辦的考古遺址文資場域參訪體驗計畫，也謝謝凱雯老師申請計畫，把「牛埔遺址校外教學」帶給孩子們，讓他們有機會走進課本裡的歷史，與史前時代人類近距離接觸。

從教室出走，博物館搭配遺址現場，是最完美的學習場域。

讓歷史不再只是課本裡的文字，而是能親眼看見、親手觸摸的真實體驗。

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

archaeology/ˌɑːrkiˈɒlədʒi/考古學

Archaeology helps us understand how people lived thousands of years ago.考古學幫助我們了解幾千年前人類的生活。

ancient/ˈeɪnʃənt/古老的

The museum displays ancient tools found at the Niupu site.博物館展示著在牛埔遺址發現的古老工具。

excavate/ˈekskəveɪt/挖掘

Archaeologists excavate the ground carefully to find hidden treasures.考古學家小心翼翼地挖掘地面，尋找隱藏的寶物。

site/saɪt/遺址；地點

Niupu is a famous archaeological site in Changhua.牛埔是彰化知名的考古遺址。

replica/ˈreplɪkə/複製品

Students saw a replica of an ancient human's skull.學生們看到了古代人類頭骨的複製品。

prehistoric/ˌpriːhɪˈstɔːrɪk/史前的

Prehistoric people lived long before writing was invented.史前人類生活在文字發明之前很久。

artifact/ˈɑːrtɪfækt/文物；手工藝品

Every artifact in the museum tells a story from the past.博物館裡的每件文物都訴說著過去的故事。

fossil/ˈfɒsəl/化石

The animal fossil skeleton amazed all the students.動物化石骨骼讓所有學生都驚嘆不已。
