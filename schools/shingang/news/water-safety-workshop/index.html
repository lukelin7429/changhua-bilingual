Save Yourself First: A Water Safety Lesson

水域安全宣導 · 自救先自保

Right before graduation, Grade 6 headed into the auditorium for something a little different: a hands-on water safety workshop led by professional lifeguards from the Changhua County Water Lifesaving Association.

Students tried on life jackets, watched safety cartoons, and learned the "Five Steps of Rescue" — shout, call, reach, throw, row — a simple reminder that helping others safely always starts with keeping yourself safe first. They also picked up the "Five Do's of Self-Rescue" and the "Five Don'ts of Drowning Prevention," practical rules for real life near water.

Knowing how to stay safe is what keeps you free from danger.

Huge thanks to Coach Tsai, Coach Lai, Coach Chen, and Coach Tseng for their dedication. Watching footage of toddlers in the Netherlands already training in the water was a good reminder: there's still so much we can teach our children about staying safe.

中文原文 · Chinese

趕在畢業前，六年級的孩子們來到活動中心，上了一堂不一樣的課——由彰化縣水上救生協會的專業救生員帶來的校園水域安全宣導及體驗活動。

孩子們實際試穿救生衣、觀看安全宣導動畫，也學會了「救溺五步：叫、叫、伸、拋、划」，記住救人之前，要先確保自己的安全。同時也學到「自救五要」與「防溺五不」，都是貼近生活的實用觀念。

有安全意識的人，才能讓自己遠離危險。

感謝蔡教練、賴教練、陳教練及曾教練的用心。看到影片中荷蘭的幼兒已經開始水中訓練，才發現要教給孩子的，還有好多好多。

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

safety/ˈseɪfti/安全

Safety is something every student should learn about the water.水域安全是每個學生都該學習的課題。

lifeguard/ˈlaɪfɡɑːrd/救生員

The lifeguards showed us how to use a life jacket.救生員教我們怎麼使用救生衣。

rescue/ˈreskjuː/救援

The five steps of rescue start with calling for help, not jumping in.救援五步驟第一步是呼救，而不是直接跳下水。

float/floʊt/漂浮

A life jacket helps your body float in the water.救生衣能幫助你的身體在水中漂浮。

drown/draʊn/溺水

Knowing the five don'ts can help prevent someone from drowning.知道防溺五不能幫助預防溺水。

vest/vest/背心；救生衣

Every student practiced putting on a life vest.每位學生都練習穿上救生衣。

aware/əˈwer/有意識的；警覺的

Being aware of danger keeps you safe near water.對危險有警覺心才能在水邊保持安全。

protect/prəˈtekt/保護

These safety steps protect both you and others.這些安全步驟能保護你自己和其他人。
