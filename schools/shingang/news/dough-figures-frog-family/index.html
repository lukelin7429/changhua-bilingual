A Frog Family, Sculpted by Hand

巧手捏出青蛙家族

A frog in love. A frog in a hat. A frog with a beard. A frog fresh from its tadpole days. A frog catching flies. A frog in a red cape. A frog with long hair… and more — a whole frog family came to life at Shingang, one pinch of dough at a time.

We're grateful to a local councilwoman for helping bring the Wu Family Dough Figure Studio's cultural workshop to our campus. She even became a one-day helper herself, cheering students on to let their creativity run wild.

Under Curator Wu's guidance, there was no limit to what the students could imagine.

What makes Curator Wu's own story even more inspiring: after years of working with his dominant right hand, he retrained his left hand from scratch until it became just as skillful. A true lesson in perseverance for every student watching.

中文原文 · Chinese

談戀愛的青蛙、戴帽子的青蛙、留鬍子的青蛙、從蝌蚪變成的青蛙、捕捉蒼蠅的青蛙、紅披風的青蛙、長頭髮的青蛙……還有還有，青蛙家族一同現身新港！

感謝美女議員大力協助，把巫家捏麵館的捏麵文化推廣活動帶進新港，議員還化身一日小助手，鼓勵孩子們勇於發揮創意。

在巫館長的指導下，孩子們的創意真的不能再更多了！

更勵志的是，巫館長從慣用右手，重新學習把左手也練成了一雙巧手，這份毅力真的太值得孩子們學習了。

sculpt/skʌlpt/捏塑；雕塑

Curator Wu taught the students how to sculpt a tiny frog out of dough.巫館長教學生怎麼用麵團捏塑出一隻小青蛙。

dough/doʊ/麵團

Each frog began as a small piece of colorful dough.每隻青蛙一開始都只是一小塊彩色麵團。

creativity/ˌkriːeɪˈtɪvəti/創意

The students' creativity turned every frog into something new.孩子們的創意讓每隻青蛙都變得獨一無二。

figure/ˈfɪɡjər/人偶；塑像

A dough figure can look like a frog, a warrior, or anything you imagine.捏麵人偶可以是青蛙、武將，或任何你想像得到的樣子。

tradition/trəˈdɪʃən/傳統

Dough figure art is an old Taiwanese tradition.捏麵人是一項古老的台灣傳統技藝。

volunteer/ˌvɒlənˈtɪr/志工；擔任志工

The councilwoman became a one-day volunteer to help the students.議員化身一日志工，協助孩子們創作。

skillful/ˈskɪlfəl/靈巧的

After years of practice, Curator Wu's left hand became just as skillful as his right.經過多年練習，巫館長的左手變得跟右手一樣靈巧。

inspire/ɪnˈspaɪər/激勵；啟發

Curator Wu's story continues to inspire everyone who hears it.巫館長的故事持續激勵著每一個聽過的人。
