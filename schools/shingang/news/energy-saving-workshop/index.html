Shingang's Little Energy-Saving Experts

新港節能小尖兵

A huge thank you to our special guests — the energy-saving team from Taipower's Changhua District Office! They brought a suitcase full of expert knowledge and delivered an electrifying, super-engaging "Energy Conservation" talk for our fourth graders.

What exactly is one kilowatt-hour of electricity? What's the smartest way to run the air conditioner? And how do you keep a sneaky "standby monster" from quietly eating up power at home?

Every answer was inside the presentation.

From global warming to everyday habits at home, the talk covered it all — and by the end, every student had transformed into a smart little "Energy-Saving Genius."

中文原文 · Chinese

感謝特別的貴賓——台電彰化區處的夥伴！帶著滿滿的能源專業知識，為我們四年級的孩子們帶來一場超級精彩、電力十足的「節能宣導」講座！

什麼是一度電？冷氣該如何聰明吹？如何避免養出吃掉電力的「待機怪獸」？

答案通通在講座中。從地球暖化聊到日常生活，經過講座的洗禮，孩子們個個化身為聰明的「節能智慧王」！

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

energy/ˈenərdʒi/能源

Saving energy helps protect our planet.節約能源有助於保護我們的地球。

electricity/ɪˌlekˈtrɪsəti/電力

Taipower taught us how electricity is measured.台電教我們怎麼測量電力。

conserve/kənˈsɜːrv/節約；保存

We can all learn to conserve electricity at home.我們都能學習在家節約用電。

standby/ˈstændbaɪ/待機

A standby monster wastes power even when the TV is off.待機怪獸就算電視關了也在偷偷耗電。

efficient/ɪˈfɪʃənt/有效率的

An efficient air conditioner saves both energy and money.有效率的冷氣機能同時省電又省錢。

habit/ˈhæbɪt/習慣

Turning off lights is a great energy-saving habit.隨手關燈是很棒的節能習慣。

global warming/ˈɡloʊbəl ˈwɔːrmɪŋ/全球暖化

Saving energy can help slow down global warming.節約能源有助於減緩全球暖化。

smart/smɑːrt/聰明的；智慧型的

Every student became a smart energy-saving expert.每位學生都變成了聰明的節能專家。
