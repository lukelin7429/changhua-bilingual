Dancing Into Courage and Opportunity

舞出勇氣與機會

Applause for every one of the more than fifty students brave enough to challenge themselves! Congratulations to all the new faces who earned their spot on the stage.

Inspired by the dazzling performances of their upperclassmen, this year's street dance team tryouts drew an incredible turnout — over 50 students eager to give it a shot. The audition floor buzzed with energy and nerves, every dancer's excitement and self-belief impossible to miss.

Simply being willing to stand up there already makes you a winner.

To see so many students pick up brand-new choreography on the spot and dance it out? Truly impressive. Heartfelt thanks to Instructor Shang-Yi and Instructor Hsiao-He for their expert guidance, and to the Parents' Support Association for their warmest encouragement behind the scenes. Congratulations to the street dance team — here's to dancing out Shingang's confidence and energy together!

中文原文 · Chinese

掌聲獻給勇於挑戰自己的五十多位孩子，恭喜每一位為自己贏得站上舞台機會的新血！

因著學姊們的亮眼表現，這次街舞隊新隊員甄選吸引了超過 50 位孩子熱烈報名。看到甄選現場擠滿了躍躍欲試的身影，那份朝氣與自信，讓人有小小的感動。

光是願意站出來，你就是最勇敢的贏家！

能夠現學現跳，孩子們真的太厲害了。衷心感謝街舞班尚億老師和小賀老師的專業帶領，也感謝家長後援會最溫暖的陪伴與支持。恭喜街舞隊！期待新血的加入，讓我們繼續跳出屬於新港的自信與活力！
