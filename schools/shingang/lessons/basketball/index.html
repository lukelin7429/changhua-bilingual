I.

Our Team Story

校隊故事

Formed in 2023, ranked No. 2 in Changhua County.

2023 年成軍，彰化縣排名第二。

Watch Teacher Chris interview Coach Yang Rongjie and the players of the Shingang basketball team — hard work, teamwork, and school pride, straight from the court.

看 Chris 老師採訪楊榮傑教練與新港籃球隊球員——努力、團隊合作，還有屬於新港的驕傲，都在球場上。

Narration
Welcome to Shingang Elementary, where our basketball team shines under Coach Yang Rongjie's leadership. Formed in 2023, we've quickly made our mark with strong performances and three tournament medals! Every practice with Coach Yang teaches us more than just basketball; it's about hard work and teamwork.
歡迎來到新港國小，這裡的籃球隊在楊榮傑教練的帶領下表現出色。該隊成立於 2023 年，很快就在比賽中嶄露頭角，獲得了三個錦標賽獎牌！每次與楊教練的練習不僅僅是學習籃球技巧，更多的是學習努力和團隊合作。
Teacher Chris
Welcome to our special interview with one of our top performing elementary basketball teams in Changhua County — Shingang Elementary School. Let's hear from the coach and some of the players.
歡迎來到我們的特別採訪，我們將聚焦於彰化縣表現優異的小學籃球隊——新港國小籃球隊。讓我們來聽聽教練和一些球員的分享。
Coach Yang
Hello everyone. I'm Coach Yang. I'm also a teacher at Shengang Junior High School. I love basketball and enjoy teaching our young players. Go Team Shingang.
大家好。我是楊教練。我也是伸港國中的老師。我愛籃球，喜歡教導我們的年輕球員。加油，新港籃球隊。
Teacher Chris
There are currently 35 players competing to be on the basketball team and their numbers are growing all the time.
目前有 35 名球員正在爭取進入籃球隊比賽名單，而且這個數字還在持續增長。
Jim · Center
I'm Jim. I'm playing center. Playing basketball is a lot of fun.
我是 Jim，我打中鋒。打籃球非常有趣。
Player
So far, the team has competed in three tournaments and won ten matches. They are ranked number 2 in Changhua County.
到目前為止，球隊已經參加了三次錦標賽並贏得了十場比賽。他們在彰化縣的排名是第二。
Player
The players and coaches work very hard and we practice four times a week.
球員和教練都非常努力，我們一週練習四次。
Ann · Forward
Hi, I'm Ann. I play forward. We practice every day to be the best.
嗨，我是 Ann，我打前鋒。我們每天練習，目的是成為最好的。
Tom · Forward
Hello, I'm Tom. I play forward. We practice every day to be the best.
你好，我是 Tom，我打前鋒。我們每天練習，目的是成為最好的。
Teacher Chris
Thank you, Coach Yang. Keep working hard and making us proud.
謝謝楊教練。繼續努力，讓我們感到驕傲。
Narration
Cheer for us as we aim for even greater achievements. Thank you, Coach Yang, and everyone who supports us.
在我們爭取更高成就的同時，請為我們加油。謝謝楊教練和所有支持我們的人。

II.

Basketball Fundamentals

籃球基本動作

Four moves every player needs to know.

每個球員都要會的四個基本動作。

🏀
Dribble
運球

To bounce the ball while moving.

🤝
Pass
傳球

To throw the ball to a teammate.

🙌
Rebound
籃板球

To grab the ball after a missed shot.

🥅
Shoot
投籃

To try to score by throwing the ball into the basket.

III.

Player Positions

球員位置

Every player has a unique role on the court.

場上的每位球員都有自己獨特的角色。

🧭
Point Guard
控球後衛

The team's leader — organizes plays and calls the shots.

🎯
Shooting Guard
得分後衛

The team's best shooter and defender.

🤹
Small Forward
小前鋒

Versatile — can do a bit of everything.

💪
Power Forward
大前鋒

Plays close to the basket and is strong.

🗼
Center
中鋒

Usually the tallest player on the team.

"All players work together to score points, defend, and win the game."
所有球員一起合作來得分、防守並贏得比賽。
