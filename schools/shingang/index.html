I.

Welcome

歡迎來到新港國小

A thriving community of 723 students at the heart of Shengang Township — where every child is empowered with knowledge, skills, and the confidence to succeed.

我們位於伸港鄉的中心，是一個充滿活力的學習社群，共有 723 名學生，致力於激發學生學習的熱情，並賦予他們知識、技能與自信，讓他們取得成功。

Shengang Township sits at Changhua's northwestern edge — historically known as a place of strong winds and receding tides, where daily life demanded resilience. That same resilience shaped generations of Shingang students: steady, hardworking, and always moving forward.

位於彰化西北端的伸港鄉，自古即有「風頭水尾」之稱，生活環境較為刻苦，卻也因此砥礪出學生刻苦耐勞、勇往向前的秉性。

Located at the heart of the township's town center, Shingang Elementary carries a long history. Founded in 1917 as a branch school of Homei Line Public School, it was renamed Shingang National School of Changhua County in 1950, and took its current name in 1959.

新港國小位於較為熱鬧的鄉鎮中心生活圈，校史悠長。設立於 1917 年，初始校名為和美線公學校埤仔墘分校，1950 年改名為彰化縣新港鄉新港國民學校，至 1959 年改為現今校名伸港鄉新港國民小學。

II.

What Makes Shingang

新港的特色
🗣️
English Immersion
英語沉浸式教學

Resident foreign teachers teach all students in English, building a solid vocabulary foundation with a target of 200 new words every semester.

🤖
Innovative & AI Learning
創新與 AI 教育

AI education is integrated into the curriculum, paving the way for a technologically advanced learning environment.

📚
Reading Culture
閱讀推廣

The school's own Joyful Reading Volunteer Team helped Shingang earn the Ministry of Education's 111th-year Reading Promoter Group Award.

🤝
Community & Care
社區與關懷

Educational savings accounts, community meal partnerships, and a student self-governance association support every child's wellbeing.

Health · Gratitude · Excellence
健康 · 感恩 · 卓越

Guided by this vision, Shingang's school-based curriculum weaves international education through three strands — communication, cultural understanding, and global issues — preparing every student to be a confident, globally competitive citizen.

III.

Around Our Campus

校園巡禮 · 用照片學英文

A quick tour of Shingang, one landmark — and one new word — at a time.

用五個校園角落，順便學五個英文單字。

School Gate
校門
Every morning, students walk through this colorful gate to begin their day. 每天早上，孩子們穿過這座繽紛的校門，展開新的一天。
Bronze Statue
銅像
This bronze statue honors the teachers who have shaped generations of Shingang students. 這座銅像紀念著教導世世代代新港學子的師長們。
Stage Pavilion
司令台
Painted like an ocean scene, the pavilion hosts morning assembly and school events. 彩繪成海洋場景的司令台，是每天升旗與各項活動的舞台。
Ocean Mural
海洋壁畫
Dolphins and fish swim across the pavilion ceiling, a nod to Shingang's coastal home. 海豚與魚群悠遊在司令台的天花板上，呼應新港臨海的家鄉。
Basketball Court
籃球場
Students shoot hoops and play games on the court between classes. 下課時間，孩子們在籃球場上投籃、玩耍。
