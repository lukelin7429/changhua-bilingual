I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Lin Tsung-jen graduated from the Graduate Institute of Education, College of Social Sciences, Tunghai University. Before coming to Sijhou, he served as acting principal of Changhua County Minglun Junior High School. He now leads Sijhou Junior High School.

林宗仁校長畢業於東海大學社會科學院教育研究所。到溪州國中之前，曾任彰化縣立明倫國中代理校長，現任彰化縣立溪州國中校長。

Philosophy I · 理念一

Philosophy III · 理念三
Teachers, parents, and students growing well together.

"See your own responsibility in what your students need — people-first, leading by example, so that teachers, parents, and students grow well together."
從學生的需要上看見自己的責任；以人為本、以身作則；親師生共好。 — 林宗仁校長的辦學理念
