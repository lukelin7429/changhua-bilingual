III.

Script

講稿與中文翻譯
A Trip to Kaohsiung
Morgan 老師的高雄之旅

  1. I am having such a fun time in Taiwan, and I am visiting so many new places.

    我在台灣玩得很開心，去了好多新地方。

  2. For example, last weekend, my roommates and I went to Kaohsiung.

    舉例來說，上週末我和室友們去了高雄。

  3. How did we get there?

    我們是怎麼去的呢？

  4. First, we took the HSR to Zuoying.

    首先，我們搭高鐵到左營。

  5. Then, we rode the MRT to our hotel.

    接著，我們搭捷運到飯店。

  6. After arriving at the hotel, we were really hungry.

    到了飯店之後，我們真的餓了。

  7. We looked for some place to eat lunch.

    我們找地方吃午餐。

  8. We found a very yummy beef noodle soup shop.

    我們找到一家很好吃的牛肉麵店。

  9. After lunch, we went to Cijin Beach.

    午餐後，我們去了旗津海灘。

  10. To get to the beach, we took a ferry.

    要去海灘，我們搭了渡輪。

  11. It was very hot and sunny at the beach.

    海灘上非常熱，陽光很強。

  12. My roommates and I swam in the ocean for a very long time.

    我和室友們在海裡游了很久的泳。

  13. We had so much fun!

    我們玩得超開心！

  14. Then, we laid on the sand and listened to music.

    然後我們躺在沙灘上聽音樂。

  15. It was very relaxing!

    非常放鬆！

  16. Later, we went to the night market to get dinner.

    後來我們去夜市吃晚餐。

  17. For dinner, I had fried chicken.

    晚餐我吃了炸雞。

  18. I then got ice cream for dessert.

    甜點我吃了冰淇淋。

  19. I am so happy I got the opportunity to visit Kaohsiung and will definitely visit again!

    我很開心有機會去高雄，下次一定還要再去！

IV.

Words to Learn

單字與例句 · 點喇叭聽發音

Eight words from the recording — one for every question.

八個取自這次錄音的單字，五道題目都有對應的字。單字和例句都可以點來聽，每個字的第一句就是錄音裡的原句。

roommate[ˈrumˌmet]n.室友
Q1

Last weekend, my roommates and I went to Kaohsiung.上週末，我和室友們去了高雄。

Our teachers from America share an apartment, so they are roommates.來自美國的老師們住同一間公寓，所以他們是室友。

MRT[ˌɛm ɑr ˈti]n.捷運
Q2

We took the HSR to Zuoying, then rode the MRT to our hotel.我們搭高鐵到左營，再搭捷運到飯店。

Changhua has no MRT yet, so we take the train or a bus.彰化還沒有捷運，所以我們搭火車或公車。

ferry[ˈfɛri]n.渡輪
Q3

To get to the beach, we took a ferry.要到海灘，我們搭了渡輪。

A ferry carries people across the water to Cijin Island.渡輪載著人們渡海到旗津。

beach[bitʃ]n.海灘
Q3

After lunch, we went to the beach and swam in the ocean.午餐後，我們去海灘，還在海裡游泳。

Sijhou has no beach, but the Zhuoshui River is close by.溪州沒有海灘，但濁水溪就在附近。

sunny[ˈsʌni]adj.晴朗的
Q4

It was very hot and sunny at the beach.海灘上非常熱，陽光很強。

On a sunny morning, we have the flag ceremony outside.天氣晴朗的早上，我們在戶外舉行升旗典禮。

relaxing[rɪˈlæksɪŋ]adj.令人放鬆的

We laid on the sand and listened to music. It was very relaxing!我們躺在沙灘上聽音樂，非常放鬆！

Reading in the school library is relaxing.在學校圖書館看書很放鬆。

night market[naɪt ˈmɑrkɪt]n.夜市
Q5

Later, we went to the night market to get dinner.後來我們去夜市吃晚餐。

My classmates and I eat fried chicken at the night market.我和同學們在夜市吃炸雞。

dessert[dɪˈzɝt]n.甜點
Q5

I had fried chicken for dinner, then ice cream for dessert.我晚餐吃炸雞，甜點吃冰淇淋。

Pineapple cake is a popular Taiwanese dessert.鳳梨酥是很受歡迎的台灣甜點。
