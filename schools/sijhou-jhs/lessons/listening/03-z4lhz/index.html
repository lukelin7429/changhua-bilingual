III.

Script

講稿與中文翻譯
All About Thanksgiving
Morgan 老師談感恩節

  1. It is almost Thanksgiving!

    感恩節快到了！

  2. In America, Thanksgiving is a very popular holiday.

    在美國，感恩節是個非常受歡迎的節日。

  3. We celebrate Thanksgiving every last week of November.

    我們每年都在十一月的最後一週慶祝感恩節。

  4. Thanksgiving is a time for the whole family to come together and say what they are thankful for.

    感恩節是全家人團聚、說出自己感謝什麼的時候。

  5. Grandparents, aunts and uncles, and cousins will all eat a big dinner together.

    祖父母、叔叔伯伯姑姑阿姨，還有堂表兄弟姊妹，大家一起吃一頓豐盛的晚餐。

  6. For Thanksgiving, my family usually makes a bunch of yummy food!

    感恩節的時候，我家通常會做一大堆好吃的食物！

  7. For example, we eat turkey, cranberry sauce, stuffing, and mac n’ cheese.

    例如火雞、蔓越莓醬、填料麵包，還有焗烤起司通心粉。

  8. My favorite food to eat during Thanksgiving, however, is pumpkin pie because I love sweet desserts.

    不過我感恩節最喜歡吃的是南瓜派，因為我很愛甜的點心。

  9. After dinner, my family and I usually watch football on the TV.

    晚餐之後，我和家人通常會看電視上的美式足球比賽。

  10. It is a tradition in America to watch football during Thanksgiving.

    感恩節看美式足球是美國的傳統。

  11. Some families even play football outside in their backyards, though the weather is usually pretty windy and cold in November.

    有些家庭甚至會在自家後院玩美式足球，雖然十一月的天氣通常又風大又冷。

  12. This year, I will celebrate Thanksgiving in Taiwan with all my new friends!

    今年我要在台灣，和我所有的新朋友一起過感恩節！

IV.

Words to Learn

單字與例句 · 點喇叭聽發音

Eight words from the recording — one for every question.

八個取自這次錄音的單字，五道題目都有對應的字。單字和例句都可以點來聽，每個字的第一句就是錄音裡的原句。

Thanksgiving[ˈθæŋksˌɡɪvɪŋ]n.感恩節
Q1

In America, Thanksgiving is a very popular holiday.在美國，感恩節是個非常受歡迎的節日。

Our American teachers tell us about Thanksgiving every November.每年十一月，美國老師都會跟我們介紹感恩節。

November[noˈvɛmbɚ]n.十一月
Q2

We celebrate Thanksgiving every last week of November.我們每年都在十一月的最後一週慶祝感恩節。

Our school sports day is in November.我們學校的運動會在十一月。

celebrate[ˈsɛləˌbret]v.慶祝
—

This year, I will celebrate Thanksgiving in Taiwan with all my new friends.今年我要在台灣，和我所有的新朋友一起過感恩節。

At Sijhou Junior High, we celebrate the Moon Festival with mooncakes.在溪州國中，我們吃月餅慶祝中秋節。

thankful[ˈθæŋkfəl]adj.感謝的、感恩的
—

Thanksgiving is a time for the whole family to say what they are thankful for.感恩節是全家人說出自己感謝什麼的時候。

I am thankful for my teachers and my classmates.我很感謝我的老師和同學。

pumpkin pie[ˈpʌmpkɪn paɪ]n.南瓜派
Q3

My favorite food to eat during Thanksgiving is pumpkin pie.我感恩節最喜歡吃的食物是南瓜派。

Pumpkin pie is sweet, so many students like it.南瓜派很甜，所以很多學生都喜歡。

football[ˈfʊtˌbɔl]n.（美式）足球
Q4

After dinner, my family and I usually watch football on the TV.晚餐之後，我和家人通常會看電視上的美式足球。

American football is not the same sport as soccer.美式足球和足球不是同一種運動。

tradition[trəˈdɪʃən]n.傳統
—

It is a tradition in America to watch football during Thanksgiving.感恩節看美式足球是美國的傳統。

Eating zongzi at Dragon Boat Festival is a Taiwanese tradition.端午節吃粽子是台灣的傳統。

windy[ˈwɪndi]adj.風大的
Q5

The weather is usually pretty windy and cold in November.十一月的天氣通常又風大又冷。

On windy mornings, we have the flag ceremony inside.風大的早上，我們在室內舉行升旗典禮。
