II.

Music & Performing Arts

音樂與表演藝術

笛子、二胡、琵琶、管樂社——溪州國中的音樂課教室裡藏著好多樂器。

WORD 001
art clubn.
美術社
The students make rainbow scratch paper in the art club.
學生們在美術社製作彩虹刮畫紙。
They draw many images on their rainbow scratch paper in the art club.
他們在美術社的彩虹刮畫紙上畫了許多圖案。
WORD 002
Chinese fluten.
笛子
The Chinese flute is a woodwind instrument.
笛子是一種木管樂器。
To play the Chinese flute, you need good breath control and the right finger positions.
吹奏笛子需要良好的氣息控制和正確的手指位置。
WORD 003
band clubn.
管樂社
The students practice their instruments in the band club.
學生在管樂社練習樂器。
The teacher instructs the students in the band club.
老師在管樂社指導學生。
WORD 004
double bassn.
低音大提琴
The double bass is the largest string instrument in the orchestra.
低音大提琴是管弦樂團中最大的弦樂器。
The teacher shows the student how to play the double bass.
老師向學生示範如何演奏低音大提琴。
WORD 005
paintv.
畫（用顏料）
The students paint canvases with numbered sections.
學生根據畫布上編號的部分上顏色。
The numbered sections on the canvases are painted to create images.
畫布上的編號部分上色後就會產生圖案。
WORD 006
musiciann.
音樂家
A professional musician introduces the Chinese flute to the students.
一位職業音樂家為學生們介紹中國笛。
The professional musician plays the flute for the students.
這位職業音樂家為同學們演奏笛子。
WORD 007
pipan.
琵琶
The musician teaches the students about the pipa.
這位音樂家教導學生琵琶的知識。
The students learn about an instrument called the pipa.
學生們學習琵琶這項樂器的知識。
WORD 008
music teachern.
音樂老師
The music teacher is teaching his students how to play a traditional Chinese instrument called the erhu.
這位音樂老師正在指導學生演奏一種名為「二胡」的傳統中國樂器。
Two foreign English teachers are also participating in the music teacher's class.
有兩位外國英文老師也在這堂音樂課當中一起學習。
WORD 009
erhun.
二胡
The erhu is a string instrument with two strings.
二胡是一種有兩根弦的弦樂器。
The students are learning to play the erhu.
學生們正在學習拉二胡。
III.

Health & Safety

健康與安全

從心肺復甦術到哈姆立克急救法，這些是每個溪州學生都該學會的救命技能。

WORD 010
CPRn.
心肺復甦術
The teachers are learning about CPR.
老師們在學習做CPR。
They take turns practicing CPR on a CPR manikin.
他們輪流在人體模型上練習做CPR。
WORD 011
Heimlich maneuvern.
哈姆立克急救法
The students practice performing the Heimlich maneuver.
同學們練習做哈姆立克急救法。
The Heimlich maneuver is used to help someone choking.
哈姆立克急救法用來幫助噎到的人。
IV.

Sports & Activities

運動與活動

跳繩、爬樹、體適能社——溪州學生下課時間動個不停。

WORD 012
matn.
墊子
The students do various exercises on a mat.
同學們在墊子上面做各式各樣的運動。
The students use a mat to do different exercises.
同學們用墊子做不同的運動。
WORD 013
jump ropev.
跳繩
Many students jump rope during recess to stay healthy.
在下課時，許多學生會跳繩來保持健康。
It is not easy to jump rope well, but the students practice hard.
雖然跳繩要跳得好很不容易，但是學生們都很認真練習。
WORD 014
climbing treesn.
爬樹
The students use special equipment to climb trees.
學生們使用特殊設備爬樹。
The instructors teach students how to climb the tree safely.
指導員教學生如何安全地爬樹。
WORD 015
fitness clubn.
體適能社團
The school has a fitness club.
學校有體適能社團。
The fitness club has exercise equipment that students can use.
體適能社團有運動器材可供學生使用。
V.

Classroom Essentials

教室裡的日常

黑板、平板、學習單——教室裡每一件小事都能用英文說。

WORD 016
blackboardn.
黑板
The science teacher is writing on the blackboard.
自然老師正在黑板上寫字。
The students are erasing the blackboard.
學生們正在擦拭黑板。
WORD 017
blackboard eraser cleanern.
板擦機
A blackboard eraser cleaner is a device used to remove chalk dust from erasers.
板擦機是一種用於清理板擦上粉筆灰的設備。
The student is cleaning the eraser with a blackboard eraser cleaner.
這位學生正在使用板擦機清潔板擦。
WORD 018
worksheetn.
學習單
The teacher is handing back students' worksheets to them.
老師將學習單發回給學生。
The students are turning in their worksheets to their teacher.
學生將學習單交給老師。
WORD 019
raise your handv.
舉手
Raise your hand if you have a question.
如果你有問題可以舉手發問。
Raise your hand if you need to go to the bathroom.
如果你需要上廁所可以舉手。
WORD 020
tabletn.
平板
The teacher is showing his students how to use a tablet.
老師正在為學生示範如何使用平板。
Sometimes teachers have their students use tablets in class.
有時候老師在課堂上會請學生使用平板。
WORD 021
TV screenn.
電視螢幕
Some teachers use a big TV screen to present materials to their students in the classroom.
有些老師在教室用大的電視螢幕呈現教材給學生看。
There is a big TV screen behind the blackboard.
黑板後面有一個大型的電視螢幕。
WORD 022
virtualadj.
線上（的）
The class is having a virtual lesson with a teacher from the US.
這個班級正在上一位美國老師的線上課。
In the class, the students are playing a game with the virtual teacher.
在課堂上，學生正在和視訊老師一起玩遊戲。
WORD 023
card gamen.
紙牌遊戲
The students play a card game to practice their English.
學生玩紙牌遊戲練習講英文。
You need to pay attention to win the card game.
你必須要專心，玩紙牌遊戲才能獲勝。
WORD 024
take a napv.
睡午覺
After lunch, students take a nap at school.
午餐後，學生睡午覺。
Some students read a book instead of taking a nap after lunch.
有些學生看書而不睡午覺。
VI.

Campus & Everyday Life

校園與日常生活

從洗手台到羊圈——溪州校園裡的日常角落也是英文教室。

WORD 025
goatn.
羊
The people are feeding the goats.
人們正在餵羊。
The goats come to the fence to get food.
羊群到圍籬邊吃東西。
WORD 026
rice burgern.
米漢堡
The students are cooking rice burgers.
學生們在做米漢堡。
A rice burger is a healthy dinner.
米漢堡是很健康的晚餐。
WORD 027
sinkn.
水槽
Wash your hands at the sink before you eat lunch.
在你吃午餐前要在洗手台洗手。
The students are washing the dirty cleaning cloths in the sink.
學生在洗手台洗髒抹布。
WORD 028
boxn.
箱
The students unload boxes of books from the truck.
學生們將一箱一箱的書從卡車搬下來。
The students work together to get all the boxes of books to the school library.
學生們合力將這些裝書的箱子搬到學校圖書館。
WORD 029
upstairs / downstairsadv.
上／下樓梯
Be careful when you walk upstairs or downstairs.
上下樓梯要小心。
You can hold onto the handrail when you walk upstairs or downstairs.
上下樓梯時可以握著扶手。
WORD 030
crossv.
過（馬路）
Watch out for students crossing the road.
當心正在過馬路的學生。
Crossing guards stop traffic to let students cross the street safely.
交通導護阻擋車輛，讓學生安全過馬路。
VII.

School Community

校園人物與活動

頒獎典禮、觀課、實習老師——溪州校園裡的人和事。

WORD 031
award ceremonyn.
頒獎典禮
The students attend the award ceremony.
同學們參加頒獎典禮。
The award ceremony is held at a temple.
頒獎典禮是在一間廟宇舉行。
WORD 032
observev.
觀課
Some teachers are in the back of the classroom observing the class.
有些老師在教室後面觀課。
Teachers improve their teaching skills by observing others' classes.
老師藉由觀課來提昇自己的教學技巧。
WORD 033
internn.
實習生
The intern is teaching a class at Sijhou Junior High School.
這位實習老師正在溪州國中上課。
Someone learning a new skill or a new job is an intern.
學習新技能或新工作的人叫做實習生。

同學們為美國老師介紹台灣的食物。

當有外國朋友來訪時，你可以為他們介紹當地的食物與文化。

professional developmentn.
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
台灣所有學校都有老師專業進修的課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
老師在專業進修課程中學習新技能與教學策略。

同學們為考試祈福。

有時祈福時要燒香。
