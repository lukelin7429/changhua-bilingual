Suit Up, Summer Starts

暑假第一天，熱血開訓：劍道社

Summer break isn't just rest time — it's a training ground full of energy!

暑假不只是休息時間，更是充滿活力的修練場！

Today, the first day of the holiday, Sijhou Junior High's Kendo Club members suited up in full gear and gathered right on time at the dojo. Everyone sweated through focused bamboo-sword strikes and sparring practice, carefully tracking the results of every single move.

今天假期第一天，溪州國中劍道社的夥伴們就全副武裝、準時在道場集合。大家揮灑著汗水，專注地進行木刀揮擊與對抗練習，並認真紀錄下每一次的動作成果。

Opening the break with the disciplined spirit of kendo — this summer, we're destined to be different!

用精進的劍道精神開啟假期，這個暑假，我們注定與眾不同！
