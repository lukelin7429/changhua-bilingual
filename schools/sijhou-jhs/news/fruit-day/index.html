Have You Had Your Fruit Today?

天天五蔬果，健康常陪我

Have you had your fruit today? Five servings of fruits and vegetables a day keeps health right by your side.

天天五蔬果，健康常陪我。

Today, foreign teacher Rayon helped cut down bananas 🍌 from the school's food-and-agriculture garden to share with teachers and students.

今天外師 RAYON 幫忙把校園食農農場的香蕉鋸下來，與學校師生分享。

The harvest also brought smiles to neighbors nearby, with banners cheering on a healthy weight, healthy eating, and happy movement for the whole community.

收成的喜悅也感染了附近的鄰居，看板上寫著「健康體位、健康吃、快樂動」，一起為社區的健康加油。

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

fruit/fruːt/水果

Bananas are a sweet and healthy fruit.香蕉是一種香甜又健康的水果。

vegetable/ˈvedʒtəbl/蔬菜

Eat five servings of fruits and vegetables a day.每天要吃五份蔬菜和水果。

harvest/ˈhɑːrvɪst/採收

Teachers harvest bananas from the garden.老師們從農園裡採收香蕉。

share/ʃer/分享

We share the fresh fruit with everyone.我們把新鮮的水果分享給大家。

neighbor/ˈneɪbər/鄰居

The school cares about every neighbor nearby.學校關心每一位附近的鄰居。

fresh/freʃ/新鮮的

These bananas are fresh from the garden.這些香蕉是剛從農園採收的新鮮香蕉。
