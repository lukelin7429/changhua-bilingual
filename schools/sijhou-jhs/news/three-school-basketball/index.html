A Summer Full of Energy

暑期精彩不斷：三校籃球交流賽

Summer keeps the momentum going at Sijhou. Just one day after the Kendo Club's fired-up first practice of the break, Sijhou Junior High welcomed a Three-School Basketball Exchange today.

溪州國中昨日劍道社熱血開訓後，今日緊接著迎來「三校籃球交流賽」！

The school actively promotes wholesome recreational activities, letting students sweat it out and sharpen their skills together on the court — and stay well away from cigarettes, betel nut, and other unhealthy influences.

學校積極推動正當休閒活動，讓孩子們在球場上揮灑汗水、切磋球技，攜手遠離菸檳等不良環境。

Together, with health and energy, let's kick off a sunny summer full of positive energy!

讓我們一起用健康與活力，開啟充滿正能量的陽光暑假！
