Caring for Every Child

全縣校護齊聚溪州，深度解析慢性病照護實務

Led by Professor Chen Hsiao-yu and Central Advisory Committee member Liu Hsiu-chih, school nurses from across Changhua County gathered at Sijhou Junior High for an in-depth look at chronic illness care.

陳筱瑀教授、劉秀枝委員引領，學校護理師齊聚溪州國中，深度解析慢性病照護實務。

Two back-to-back case-discussion sessions unfolded at Sijhou — rigorous in spirit, and full of dedication. Facing increasingly complex chronic-care needs on campus, school nurses from across the county showed remarkable professional commitment.

連續兩場次的個案討論會議在溪州國中熱烈展開，氣氛既嚴謹又充滿熱誠！面對日益複雜的校園慢性病照護，全縣的校園護理師夥伴展現了極高的專業積極度。

This wasn't just a meeting — it was a deep exchange of real, hands-on experience.

這不只是一場會議，更是一場「實戰經驗」的深度交流！

Nurses shared a wide range of student cases — from diabetes and asthma to epilepsy and metabolic syndrome — sparking lively discussion as everyone raised real challenges from the field, seeking the best solutions under the guidance of two expert mentors: Professor Chen Hsiao-yu, drawing on deep nursing expertise to refine the precision of individual care plans, and Liu Hsiu-chih, offering warm, concrete advice on counseling and connecting resources.

現場藉由分享多樣化的慢性病學童個案，從糖尿病、氣喘到癲癇、代謝症候群等的照顧與應變，引發校護夥伴們的熱烈討論與共鳴。大家紛紛拋出實務遇到的挑戰，並在兩位大師級導師的帶領下尋求最佳解方：陳筱瑀教授以深厚的護理專業，解析個案護理計畫的精準度；中央輔導委員劉秀枝老師則從輔導與資源連結角度，給予溫暖且具體的建議。

Just as importantly, Su Hsiao-lan, a nutritionist from the Education Department's Health & PE Section, also joined — bringing policy-level support and a nutrition expert's perspective that helped complete the picture of chronic-illness care.

更重要的是，教育處體健科蘇筱嵐科員（營養師）的蒞臨，讓現場夥伴感受到政策面的強大後盾，也從營養專業角度切入，讓慢性病照護的拼圖更加完整。

Behind all that lively discussion is a shared commitment to every student living with a chronic illness. School nurses are a campus's most professional medical backbone — and with policy support and expert guidance, every child with medical needs can be gently caught, and grow up healthy, right here on campus.

看著校護夥伴們互動熱烈、交流不間斷的背後，是我們對每一位慢病學童的重視與承諾。校護是學校最專業的醫護支柱，有了政策支持與專家領航，我們要讓每一位有醫療需求的孩子，都能在校園裡被溫柔地接住、健康地成長！
