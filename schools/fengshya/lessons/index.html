Our School's Signature Programmes

鳳霞特色課程

Rooted in Pushin's land, seasons, and community — these programmes are uniquely Fengshya's. 這些課程根植於埔心的土地、四季與社區，是鳳霞獨有的學習記憶。

🥭 Local Heritage · 在地文化

Mango Heritage Education嶄露鋒「芒」——芒果特色課程

From Grade 1 to Grade 6, every Fengshya student follows a spiral mango curriculum — starting with the shape and colour of a mango in first grade, building toward the ecology, history, and sustainable farming practices of Pushin's golden Jinmi mango by sixth. The mango tree in our schoolyard is not just a tree — it is our school's living textbook.

從一年級到六年級，每位鳳霞學生都走過一段螺旋式的芒果學習旅程——從認識芒果的外觀與種類，到了解埔心在地特產「金蜜芒果」的生態、歷史與友善農耕理念。校園裡的土芒果樹，是我們最具體的活教材。

Grades 1 – 6 · 一至六年級
🌱 Food & Agriculture · 食農教育

Seeds, Soil & Table農情食課——從產地到餐桌

Lower grades discover the art hidden inside nature — seeds, pods, and leaves become stunning artworks on the studio table. Upper grades step into the kitchen: using Pushin guava and school-garden vegetables, students design and cook an original recipe, the Guava Fresh Veggie Burger. Science, art, and food in one lesson.

低年級學生運用大自然的元素——種子、果莢、葉片——化腐巧為神奇，創作食農藝術。高年級學生走進廚房，用埔心在地芭樂和學校菜園蔬菜，完成創意料理「芭樂鮮蔬堡」。一堂課裡，有科學、有藝術、有美食。

Grades 1 – 4 (Art) · Grades 5 – 6 (Cooking)
🤝 Cross-Grade Learning · 跨年級合作

Phoenix Family Relay大手牽小手合作闖關

Every school anniversary, upper-grade students strap on numbered bibs and reach back for a younger hand. Together they navigate eight relay stations — reading, English, health, board games, physical education, environmental care, and more. No prizes, no rankings. Just a school that looks after its own.

每年校慶，高年段學生穿上號碼背心，帶著低年段的弟弟妹妹一起闖過八個關卡，包括閱讀、英語、健康促進、益智桌遊、體育、環境教育等。沒有冠軍，只有互助——這就是鳳霞大家庭的日常。

All Grades · 全校
🎓 School Events · 節慶活動

Teacher's Day Bingo Quest謝師賞果——教師節賓果任務

On Teacher's Day, students receive a bingo mission card packed with gratitude challenges — find a teacher, say something kind, and stamp the card. The school fills with laughter and genuine appreciation. A small tradition that reminds everyone: teaching is a gift worth celebrating.

教師節當天，學生拿著賓果任務卡四處尋找老師、說出感謝的話、完成任務蓋章。整個校園充滿笑聲與真誠的謝意。一個小小的傳統，提醒所有人：教育，值得被用心慶祝。

All Grades · 全校
Part B · 乙

Festival English Courses

節慶英語主題課程

Holidays become classrooms. Each season brings English vocabulary, storytelling, games, and a whole lot of fun. 每個節日都是一間教室。隨著四季更迭，學生在英語詞彙、故事、遊戲中迎接每個節慶。

🎃

Halloween English Quest萬聖節英語闖關

Ghost hunts in the library, spooky missions in the hallways — students earn stamps by speaking English and reading Halloween-themed books. Trick or Treat becomes their most-practised sentence of October.

「圖書館找鬼趣」與「假鬼假怪任務」——學生用英語闖關蓋章，閱讀鬼怪主題書籍，在遊戲中自然說出英語。十月裡練習最多的句子，就是 Trick or Treat！

🎄

Christmas English聖誕節英語活動

Cards, carols, and classroom vocabulary — Christmas turns our school into an English-speaking world for a week. Teachers in festive sweaters, students practising holiday greetings, and English word walls decorating every classroom.

卡片、歌曲與英語詞彙——聖誕節讓校園變成一週的英語世界。老師穿著節慶毛衣，學生練習節日問候語，每間教室的英文單字牆也換上了聖誕主題。

🐣
Coming in Spring
🌸

Spring & Valentine's English春季英語節慶課程

Valentine's Day cards written in English, Easter egg hunts with clues in English — spring term brings lighter lessons that weave warmth and language together naturally.

用英語寫情人節卡片、用英語讀復活節彩蛋的線索——春季課程讓語言學習變得溫暖輕盈，在節慶氛圍中自然融入英語。

🍂
Autumn Term
🦃

Thanksgiving & Year-End English感恩節與年終英語課程

Gratitude in two languages — students write thank-you notes in English and share what they are grateful for. A calm end to the year that ties language learning to life's most important lesson.

用兩種語言表達感謝——學生用英語寫感謝小卡，分享自己感恩的事。在年末的寧靜中，語言學習與人生最重要的課題相遇。

Network

These festival courses are part of the Changhua Bilingual Schools Festival English Series — a shared cross-school programme that connects students from over 89 schools across Changhua County through the same seasonal English themes. 以上節慶課程是「彰化縣雙語學校節慶英語系列」的一部分——這是一個跨校共用的課程平台，讓彰化縣 89 所以上的學校學生，透過相同的節慶主題學習英語、相互連結。

Classroom English

課室英語 · 給老師的口袋句庫（影片版）
The phrases teachers say between the lessons — taught on video.
老師在課堂裡每天會用上的英文句子——這次有人示範給你看。

A practical video library covering the everyday English a Taiwanese teacher actually uses in the classroom: greetings and roll call, pre-class set-up, instructions, praise and correction, classroom management, lesson activities, assigning homework, wrapping up, and exam directions. Each video isolates one situation so the phrases stick on first hearing.

這個影片教材庫涵蓋臺灣老師每天會用到的課堂英語——問候點名、課前準備、講解、回饋讚美、糾正、教室管理、課堂活動、分派作業、課堂收尾、考試指令。每支影片只處理一個情境，第一次聽就能記住。

Bilingual Announcements

雙語廣播 · 校園的英語之聲
The morning intercom in two languages — produced by four offices that keep school life running.
早上的校園廣播，雙語版本——由教務、學務、總務、輔導四個處室共同製作。

A 13-episode bilingual announcement series, organised by the four school offices: Academic Affairs (教務處), Student Affairs (學務處), General Affairs (總務處), and Counselling (輔導室). Each office introduces what it does — in both English and Chinese — turning the school's daily PA routine into real-world bilingual exposure for every student in the corridors.

這是一個 13 集的雙語校園廣播系列，依四個處室分組：教務處、學務處、總務處、輔導室，由各組親自介紹自己的工作，雙語對照。把學校每天的廣播時間，變成全校學生在走廊上就能接收的英語浸潤。
