Educational Philosophy

Nine Principles of Our Principal

校長九大辦學理念
I

Sustaining the Vision

Uphold the principle of sustainable development and carry forward the school's long-standing vision.

秉持永續發展原則，傳承學校願景。

II

Character First

Prioritise moral education and give every student diverse opportunities to grow and shine.

重視品德教育，提供學生多元發展機會。

III

Honoring Teachers

Respect educational professionalism and inspire every teacher's passion for teaching.

尊重教育專業，激發教師教學熱忱。

IV

A Warm Campus

Foster a quality campus culture and design a warm, welcoming learning environment for all.

營造優質校園文化，規劃溫馨學習環境。

V

Parents as Partners

Build genuine "educational partnership" relationships with families — growing alongside our children.

發展家長「教育合夥人」夥伴關係，與孩子共同成長。

VI

All-In, Together

Everyone shows up, everyone commits — creating peak experiences and a strong, warm sense of belonging.

全員到齊、全心投入，創造高峰經驗，凝聚堅強、溫馨向心力。

VII

Rooted in Community

Embrace local character, promote our school to the world, and build harmonious public relations with the community.

融合社區特色，行銷學校，營造社區和諧公共關係。

VIII

Creative Teaching

Innovate how we teach, make learning joyful, and give school life deeper meaning every day.

創新教學，寓教於樂，讓學校教育活動更具意義。

IX

A Reading School

Create a culture of reading that enriches every child's inner world — nurturing calm, thoughtful, and self-directed learners.

創造閱讀氛圍，豐富學生心靈，培養靜心平氣、沉穩理性思考心性，養成自主學習能力。
