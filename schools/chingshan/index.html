I.

About Chingshan

關於青山 · 百果山麓的生態校園
Chingshan Elementary is located at the foot of Baiguo Mountain in Yuanlin City — surrounded by nature, and perfect for students who love to explore the outdoors.
青山國小位於員林市百果山麓，校園四周被大自然環繞，是喜歡探索戶外的孩子最好的地方。

Our school is surrounded by nature, with lots of trees, plants, and animals all around. We even made a special map to help everyone learn about the different plants on our campus!

校園裡有許多樹木、植物和小動物，對於喜歡探索戶外的學生來說，這裡是個完美的地方。我們還製作了一張特別的校園植物地圖，幫助大家認識校園裡不同的植物。

At Chingshan, there are many exciting clubs for students to enjoy — a recorder team, a track team, and a Tai Chi fan group. Our recorder team and track team have won many awards, and the Tai Chi fan group gets invited to perform at special events. We also teach students computer programming, which helps them learn how to be creative with technology.

在青山，學生可以參加許多有趣的社團——直笛隊、田徑隊和太極扇隊。直笛隊及田徑隊在歷年彰化縣各項音樂比賽及中小學聯運皆榮獲佳績，太極扇隊常獲邀參與各項慶典活動表演，深獲各界好評。本校近年也與高雄師範大學合作推動資訊教育程式設計，在彰化縣國中小學生 SCRATCH 應用競賽國小遊戲組榮獲優等。

Students at Chingshan can also try calligraphy, where they practice beautiful writing, or Taekwondo. And of course, we love reading — our school has a great selection of books that help students grow their imagination and learn new things.

學生在青山勤習書畫、練跆拳道，動靜合宜；博覽童書、啟迪文思，校園內的藝文創作琳瑯滿目。

At Chingshan, we believe in working hard, being kind, and staying humble. Our teachers, staff, and parents work together to support each and every student, so they can do their best and reach their goals.

「青雲有路勤直上，山斗無峰謙自高」，是青山師生恪守自勵的良言，也呼應校訓「勤樸謙恭、青山崢嶸」八字。青山有專業的教師團隊、熱忱的行政團隊、熱心的義工團隊、堅強的家長會，期望每位學子都能品格出眾、出人頭地。

"Chingshan Elementary is a place where students can learn, have fun, and become the best version of themselves!"
青雲有路勤直上，山斗無峰謙自高。
—— 青山國小師生自勵格言
青山國小簡介 · Welcome to Chingshan Elementary School!
II.

Around Our Campus

校園巡禮 · 百果山麓下的生態小學

A campus alive with murals, gardens, and play — every corner shaped for curious kids.

校園裡充滿壁畫、花園與遊戲空間，每個角落都是為了讓孩子盡情探索而設計。

Mosaic Mural Building
藝術壁畫大樓
A tiled mosaic mural rises across the main teaching building — colour and art greeting students every morning.
Flower Arch Walkway
花架玄關
A bougainvillea-covered arch leads students from the gate toward the main hall — a blaze of pink and green every spring.
Courtyard Fountain
中庭噴水池
Palm trees and a quiet fountain sit at the heart of the campus, framed by classroom buildings on every side.
Rainbow Playground
彩色遊戲場
Slides, climbing nets, and swings sit beneath old banyan trees — a favourite spot for recess.
Colorful Slide
彩虹溜滑梯
A bright blue slide and monkey bars in the school's own park corner — built for climbing, sliding, and imagination.
Student Art Corridor
童畫遊廊
A breezeway lined with display cases and student paintings — the walk between classes doubles as a gallery visit.
Motto Monument
校訓石碑
A stone monument carries the school's self-encouragement verse — "青雲有路勤直上，山斗無峰謙自高" — facing the main plaza.
School Badge
學校校徽
A green mountain rising under blue sky, ringed in the school's name — the emblem every Chingshan student wears with pride.

Recorder Team

Students learn the recorder and compete in Changhua County music competitions year after year, bringing home strong results.
直笛隊歷年參加彰化縣音樂比賽表現優異，是青山孩子共享的音樂傳統。

🏃
Track & Field Team
田徑隊 · 中小學聯運佳績
Our track team trains and competes at inter-school athletics meets, consistently placing among the top results in the county.
田徑隊歷年參加中小學聯運皆榮獲佳績，是青山最自豪的運動社團之一。
🏆 Award-winning
🎭
Tai Chi Fan Group
太極扇隊 · 慶典表演常客
A graceful performance group that blends martial-arts movement with fan choreography — often invited to perform at community festivals and ceremonies.
太極扇隊常獲邀參與各項慶典活動表演，深獲各界好評，是青山的招牌演出團隊。
💻
Computer Programming
程式設計 · 與高雄師大合作
Working with National Kaohsiung Normal University, Chingshan teaches students computer programming through Scratch — helping them turn creativity into code.
近年與高雄師範大學合作推動資訊教育程式設計，在彰化縣國中小學生 SCRATCH 應用競賽國小遊戲組榮獲優等。
🏆 SCRATCH 競賽優等

Chinese Calligraphy
書法課程 · 靜心練字

學生在青山勤習書畫，動靜合宜，也是校園藝文創作的重要來源。
🥋
Taekwondo
跆拳道 · 體能與紀律
A martial-arts elective that builds discipline, fitness, and confidence — another way Chingshan students stay active and focused.
跆拳道課程幫助學生鍛鍊體能，培養紀律與自信心。
📚
Reading Program
閱讀教育 · 博覽童書啟迪文思
A well-stocked library and school-wide reading culture help students grow their imagination and discover new ideas.
學校有豐富的圖書館藏，鼓勵學生博覽童書，啟迪文思，養成終身閱讀的習慣。
"We want all our students to do their best, be proud of their efforts, and reach their goals."
「勤樸謙恭、青山崢嶸」—— 青山國小校訓
IV.

Our Story

校史時間軸 · 從 1923 至今的青山
1923
Founded as a branch school of Yuanlin Public School
民國 12 年 3 月 30 日創立，名為「員林公學校柴頭井分教場」。
1968
Renamed Chingshan National Elementary School
民國 57 年 8 月，改校名為「青山國民小學」，沿用至今。
2026
SCRATCH programming competition — county-level honors
與高雄師範大學合作推動程式設計教育，於彰化縣國中小學生 SCRATCH 應用競賽國小遊戲組榮獲優等。
2026
Principal Huang Hung-tien takes office
民國 115 年 8 月，黃宏田校長接任青山國小校長。
