A New Look for the Computer Lab!

電腦教室冷氣汰換工程完工｜節能又環保

On April 2, 2025, Chingshan Elementary School completed the project to replace the old air conditioners in our computer lab. The new, energy-efficient Hitachi units will not only improve air circulation but also help us save energy and protect the environment.

A special thanks to the Changhua County Government for providing the funding. This upgrade is a big step towards creating an even better and more comfortable learning space for all our students.

中文原文 · Chinese

青山國小電腦教室已於2025年4月2日完成冷氣設備的汰換工程。本次特別選用高效能、節能的日立冷氣，不僅能有效提高空氣流通與降溫效果，更能降低能源消耗，達到環保節能的目標。

這次的設備升級特別感謝彰化縣政府的經費補助，讓學校得以持續為學生們創造更優質、舒適的學習環境！
