Summer Camp Fun at Chingshan Elementary School

暑期營隊｜大專哥哥姐姐返鄉陪伴一二年級學童
青山國小暑期營隊活動紀錄影片

From July 8 to August 9, Chingshan Elementary School held a fun and exciting summer camp for first and second graders. Many college students who grew up in Changhua came back to help teach and play with the kids.

Each week had a different theme, like English, science, sports, reading, and more. The children learned new things, made new friends, and had a great time every day.

Thanks to the kind college students and teachers, everyone enjoyed a happy and special summer full of learning and fun!

中文原文 · Chinese

今年7月8日至8月9日，青山國小為一、二年級學童舉辦了精彩有趣的暑期營隊。許多來自彰化、目前就讀大專院校的學生回到家鄉，協助帶領活動，陪伴孩子們一起學習與成長。

營隊每週安排不同主題課程，包括英語、科學、體育、閱讀等多元活動，孩子們每天都開心學習、快樂交朋友。

感謝所有參與的大專哥哥姐姐與老師們，讓這個暑假變得特別又充實，留下許多美好回憶！
