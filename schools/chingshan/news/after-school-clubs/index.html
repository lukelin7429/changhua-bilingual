A Diverse Range of Clubs Helps Students Soar

給孩子一雙翅膀｜青山國小多元課後社團

Chingshan Elementary School believes in giving every child a pair of wings to help them fly higher and farther. We have invited professional coaches and teachers to lead a variety of after-school clubs, helping our students develop diverse skills and build strong bodies.

Our school offers many clubs, including track and field, badminton, art, calligraphy, table tennis, basketball, dance, rollerblading, and a "Golden Brain" Lego club. Our dedicated teachers, along with a great school environment and affordable fees, make our clubs a fantastic choice for every student!

中文原文 · Chinese

青山國小深信，給孩子們一雙翅膀，他們便能飛得更高更遠！學校積極發展學生的多元智慧，並邀請專業教練與老師蒞校指導課後社團才藝活動，讓孩子們培養豐富技能、鍛鍊強健體魄。

目前學校設有田徑隊、羽球校隊，以及美術社、書法社、桌球社、羽球社、籃球社、舞蹈基礎、直排輪社與金頭腦積木社等多元社團。在老師的認真教學、優質的校園環境與實惠的收費下，青山國小歡迎大家一同加入，提升自我能力，以因應未來多變的世界！
