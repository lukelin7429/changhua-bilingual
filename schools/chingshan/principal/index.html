III.

Principal Huang's Vision

校長辦學理念
Groundedness, growth, and gratitude — a leadership style shaped by five postings across Changhua County.
踏實、成長與感恩——歷經五所學校歷練後，黃宏田校長帶著這樣的辦學態度來到青山。

Principal Huang Hung-tien took office at Chingshan Elementary in August 2026, following postings as a teacher and director at Puxin Elementary, and as principal at Tuku, Puxin, and Tsuenshang Elementary Schools. He holds a master's degree in Adult and Continuing Education from National Chung Cheng University.

黃宏田校長於民國 115 年 8 月接任青山國小校長，歷任埔心國小教師、主任，以及土庫國小、埔心國小、村上國小校長，並取得國立中正大學成人及繼續教育學系碩士學位。

PILLAR 01
Groundedness & Direction
踏實方向 · 知己知彼
Working with sincerity and leading by example, while first understanding the school's context and the needs of teachers, students, and parents before setting educational goals.
  • 做事踏實認真，以身作則，樂觀進取，把握正確方向。
  • 事先了解學校狀況，師生、家長需求，確立教育目標。
PILLAR 02
Teaching & Growth
教學相長 · 因材施教
Investing in facilities and continuous teacher development, while attending closely to individual student differences to draw out every child's potential.
  • 充實各項教學設備，提供師生更佳的學習環境，增進學習效果。
  • 鼓勵教職員隨時進修，充實專業知能，教學相長，創新教學。
  • 注重學生個別差異勤加輔導，發揮教育熱愛，激發學生潛能。
PILLAR 03
A Whole-Person Education
全人教育 · 多元學習
Planning a wide range of school activities and grounding students in local culture, life education, and civic habits like morality, law-abidingness, and safety.
  • 妥善規畫學校各項活動，提供更寬廣、多元的學習空間與機會。
  • 注重鄉土教育、生命教育，培養學生對鄉土、生命的認識與關懷。
  • 加強生活教育——道德、法治、環境、交通安全等教育，培養法治觀念。
PILLAR 04
Campus, Community & the Wider World
校園社區 · 追求卓越
Beautifying and greening the campus year-round, strengthening ties with the community, and staying attentive to education policy in pursuit of excellence and quality.
  • 校園整體規畫，做好美化、綠化工作，營造四季如春綠意盎然的校園。
  • 加強社區聯繫，善用社會資源，學校教育社區化，擴大社教功能。
  • 隨時注意教育政策與時代脈動，落實「追求卓越、提高品質」的教育理念。
"Groundedness, direction, and a heart for every child's potential."
「踏實認真，把握正確方向，激發每個孩子的潛能。」
—— 黃宏田校長談辦學理念
