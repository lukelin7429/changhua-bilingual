I.

Meet Principal Lai

認識賴姵伶校長

Lai Pei-ling became principal of Hsiushui Junior High School on August 1, 2026. A mathematics educator by training, she studied mathematics at National Taiwan Normal University, earned a master’s degree in science education (mathematics) at National Changhua University of Education, and is now a Ph.D. candidate there in industrial education and technology.

賴姵伶校長於民國 115 年 8 月 1 日接任秀水國中校長。校長是數學教育專業出身：畢業於國立台灣師範大學數學系，取得國立彰化師範大學科學教育研究所數學組碩士，目前為該校工業教育與技術研究所博士候選人。

Before coming to Hsiushui, she taught and served in school administration at Changsing Junior High School, advised mathematics teachers for Changhua County and the Ministry of Education, and worked as a curriculum supervisor in the Changhua County Government’s Education Department.

到任秀水之前，校長曾在彰興國中擔任教師與行政工作，並擔任彰化縣及教育部數學領域輔導團隊的輔導員與諮詢教師，也曾任彰化縣政府教育處課程督學。

Her Core Belief · 教育核心精神
「教育是成人之美的志業，
用愛與真誠幫助每個人實現夢想～」
“Education is a calling to help others become their best — using love and sincerity to help every person make their dreams come true.”
II.

Educational Philosophy

辦學理念

A blueprint for a life’s work in education.

教育志業藍圖：願景、使命與核心價值。

Vision · 願景
World-Ready Talent, Responsible Citizens

To develop outstanding young people who can compete and cooperate on the world stage, and to raise future citizens with a strong sense of social responsibility.

打造具備國際競合力的優質人才，培育懷抱社會責任感的未來公民。
Mission · 使命
Every Child, Every Talent

Help each child’s talents grow in the way that suits them, give them many ways to shine, and bring out the best in every child.

適性揚才，多元展能，成就每一個孩子。
Core Values · 核心價值
Love & Sincerity · Thriving Together

Love and sincerity; and a future in which we all thrive together, sustainably.

愛與真誠、永續共榮。
Help every talent grow, give every child a stage — and bring out the best in each one.
適性揚才，多元展能，成就每一個孩子。
III.

Built on the Strongest Shape

圖意：最堅固的三角形

In mathematics, the triangle is the strongest shape.

在數學裡，三角形是最堅固的形狀。

Principal Lai’s vision is pictured with the triangle — the strongest shape in mathematics — wrapped around the school, with three axes circling it. Together they stand for a learning atmosphere that is firm, steady and safe.

以數學中最堅固的三角形包覆與三軸環繞為意象，象徵塑造堅定穩固安全的學習氛圍。

She is committed to building a campus that is safe and full of love, where every student is cared for as if they were our own children (視生如子), so that children can learn with peace of mind and joy.

全力構築「視生如子」、安全有愛的校園，陪伴孩子安心快樂學習。

The goal is for parents, teachers and students alike to grow five strengths:

孕育親師生擁有以下五力：

創造力
Creativity
感知力
Perception
恆毅力
Grit
學習力
Learning
合作力
Collaboration
Growing in mind, heart and spirit — and becoming happy people.
成為知性、感性、靈性兼具的幸福之人。
