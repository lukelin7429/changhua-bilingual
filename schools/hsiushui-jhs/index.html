I.

Welcome to Hsiushui

歡迎來到秀水國中

Learning, growing and dreaming big in Xiushui since 1967.

自 1967 年起，在秀水這片土地上學習、成長、築夢。

Hsiushui Junior High School sits in Xiushui Township, in the heart of Changhua County. Step onto our campus and you will see tall royal palms lined up along a bright red running track, and big blue-and-green shade canopies stretched over the courts, so students can play and practice out of the hot sun.

秀水國中位於彰化縣秀水鄉。走進校園，高大的大王椰子沿著紅色跑道一字排開，球場上方搭起藍綠相間的遮陽棚，讓學生不怕烈日、盡情運動。

With the care of our principal and the hard work of every teacher and student, Hsiushui has a fine school spirit and keeps moving forward, earning the support of education leaders and our local community. Over the years the school has built several strengths of its own: information technology education, science education, our track and field team, and the arts.

在校長的用心經營及全體師生的辛勤耕耘下，秀中校風優良、校務蒸蒸日上，獲得上級長官及社區賢達的愛戴。本校並創造多項學校特色，包含資訊教育、科學教育、田徑隊與才藝教育。

Respect every student’s differences — and bring every child along.
尊重學生個別差異，帶好每一個孩子。
II.

Our Story

秀水國中簡史

From a single class in 1963 to the school we know today.

從 1963 年的一個班級，一路走到今天的秀水國中。

Hsiushui Junior High’s story begins in 1962. On October 23, 1967, the school was officially named Changhua County Hsiushui Junior Middle School, and that date is still remembered every year as our Founding Day. When Taiwan introduced nine-year compulsory education, it became Changhua County Hsiushui Junior High School, and in 1978 it opened a supplementary school so that adults in the community could keep learning too.

本校於民國五十一年成立，至民國五十六年十月二十三日，正式命名為「彰化縣立秀水初級中學」，為紀念此一特殊日期，特定此日為本校創校紀念日。施行九年國民教育後，改制為「彰化縣立秀水國民中學」；民國六十七年增辦附設補習學校，提供民眾進修機會。

  1. 1962民國 51 年
    Planning begins for a new county junior high school in Xiushui.
    開始籌設，縣府先後派陳阿聰、謝瑞章先生為籌備主任。
  2. 1963民國 52 年
    In July, the first class of new students is enrolled at the county-run Xiushui Agricultural School, as a branch class of Lukang Middle School.
    七月，縣立秀水農校以縣立鹿港中學分班名義，招收新生一班。
  3. 1964民國 53 年
    The class officially becomes the junior high section of Xiushui Agricultural School.
    三月，正式附設於秀水農校，定名「秀水農校附設初中」。
  4. 1967民國 56 年
    On August 1 the junior high section becomes independent, and on October 23 it is officially founded as Changhua County Hsiushui Junior Middle School — the day we still celebrate as our Founding Day. In December, Tsai Chin-chung becomes the first principal.
    八月一日脫離農校獨立；十月二十三日正式奉准獨立設校，正名「彰化縣立秀水初級中學」，此日即為創校紀念日。十二月，蔡錦忠先生為首任校長。
  5. 1968民國 57 年
    With the start of nine-year compulsory education in Taiwan, the school becomes Changhua County Hsiushui Junior High School.
    施行九年國民教育，改制為「彰化縣立秀水國民中學」。
  6. 1978民國 67 年
    A supplementary school is added, giving adults in the community a chance to continue their studies.
    增辦附設補習學校，提供民眾進修機會。
  7. 2026民國 115 年
    A new activity center is approved for construction, and on August 1 Principal Lai Pei-ling takes office.
    核定興建活動中心；八月一日，賴姵伶校長接任本校校長。
III.

Our Vision

學校願景

Four hopes for every Hsiushui student.

多元的學習、健康的成長、全人的教育、豪氣的人生——我們對每位秀中學子的期許。

Diverse Learning
多元的學習
  • Respect each student’s differences, and bring every child along.尊重學生個別差異，帶好每一個孩子。
  • Enrich learning with varied teaching and assessment, and a wide range of clubs and activities.運用多元化的教學、評量與多樣化的社團活動，充實學生學習內涵。
  • Unlock each student’s potential, spark a love of learning, and support healthy character growth.開發學習潛能，提升學習興趣，促進學生人格健全的發展。
Healthy Growth
健康的成長
  • Care for a healthy body and a healthy mind.注重外在身體的健康，內在心理的健全。
  • Create a quality learning environment where students grow up safe and healthy.創造優質的學習環境，讓學生安全、健康的成長。
  • Help students grow through learning, and use what they learn in the future.透過教學活動，讓學生在學習中成長，並將知識應用於未來。
Whole-Person Education
全人的教育
  • Raise young people of the digital age who grow in knowledge, feeling, reason, spirit and wisdom.培養兼具知性、感性、理性、靈性、慧性的 E 世代青少年。
  • Value, respect and appreciate many cultures.認同、尊重、欣賞多元文化。
  • Let every student’s bright spots shine, with a warm, humane spirit.發揮亮點與人文精神。
A Bold, Confident Life
豪氣的人生
  • Know yourself, value yourself, believe in yourself, and become your best self.自我瞭解、自我欣賞、自我肯定、自我實現。
  • Build wisdom and a positive, go-getting attitude toward life.創造智慧，培養積極進取的人生。
  • Show confidence, and take your future into your own hands.展現自信，一手掌握自己的未來。

V.

Learning by Doing

技藝教育與職業探索

Real skills, real tools, real futures.

真實的技能、真實的工具，探索真實的未來。

國中是探索的階段。透過技藝教育與職業探索課程，秀中學生走進專業廚房與工作坊——烹飪、烘焙、配線、修機車、站上收銀台——在動手做中發現自己的興趣與長處。

VI.

Arts on Stage

美感教育：舞台上的秀中

Traditional music, recorders and dance — many ways to perform.

傳統音樂、直笛與舞蹈——站上舞台的方式有很多種。

From Nanbeiguan to the dance floor
從南北管到舞台上的熱舞

Hsiushui students keep traditional Taiwanese music alive: our Nanbeiguan (南北管) ensemble has taken the stage at the National Student Nanbeiguan Music Competition in both the 2024–25 and 2025–26 school years. Our recorder ensemble performs in the concert hall, and at Xiushui’s 2025 Christmas Carnival our dancers lit up the stage.

秀中學子傳承傳統音樂：南北管樂團連續站上 113、114 學年度全國學生南北管音樂比賽的舞台；直笛團在音樂廳裡演奏，舞蹈隊更在 2025 秀水聖誕嘉年華的舞台上熱力演出。

Embracing the World
國際教育見學

On the court, at the microphone and out in the wider world — Hsiushui students find their own stage. 在球場上、在麥克風前、在更寬廣的世界裡，秀中學子都能找到自己的舞台：籃球隊捧回獎盃，學生在縣賽中以本土語言展現口說藝術，也有學生參加彰化縣國中師生國際教育見學計畫，擁抱世界。
