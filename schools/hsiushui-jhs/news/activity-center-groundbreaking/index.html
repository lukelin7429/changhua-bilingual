Nearly 60 Years in the Making: Groundbreaking for Our Activity Center

等待近 60 年！秀水國中活動中心正式動土
⌄
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On November 18, 2026, the activity center that Hsiushui Junior High has waited for for nearly sixty years officially broke ground! Thank you to everyone for your care and support, and to the news media for their coverage.

2026 年 11 月 18 日，秀水國中等待近 60 年的活動中心終於正式動土！感謝各界關心與支持，也感謝新聞媒體的採訪與報導。

🏗️ Groundbreaking · 動土
Nov. 18, 2026
2026 年 11 月 18 日
🏟️ New Building · 新建設
Activity Center
秀水國中活動中心
One construction project carries nearly sixty years of hope; one new hall opens a bright future for Hsiushui.
一份建設，承載近 60 年的期待；一座場館，開啟秀中嶄新的未來！

Once again, we thank Changhua County Magistrate Wang Hui-mei (王惠美) and the county government team, our elected representatives, local leaders, former principals, teachers, alumni and every friend who cares about Hsiushui Junior High.

再次感謝王惠美縣長、縣府團隊、各級民意代表、地方人士、歷任校長、師長、校友及所有關心秀水國中的朋友。

We also thank all our friends in the news media for their interviews and reports, which help share this joy and excitement with even more people!

也感謝所有新聞媒體朋友的採訪與報導，讓這份喜悅與感動持續傳遞！
