Dom Jones × Hsiushui, Part 2: From Our Hometown to the SDGs — Then Students Take the Mic

Dom Jones × 秀中 Part 2｜從家鄉聊永續，換秀中同學上場！
⌄
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

After dancing together at the flag ceremony, the fun moved into the AV room. On the morning of November 17, Dom Jones and Hsiushui students talked about their hometown and sustainability — and then she handed the microphone to the students so they could introduce their clubs in English!

升旗時在操場一起跳舞，走進視聽教室，精彩繼續！11 月 17 日上午，Dom Jones 和秀中同學從家鄉聊到永續，再把麥克風交給同學，聽他們用英語介紹自己的社團！🎤

🌱 08:15–09:00 | Seeing Xiushui from the sky. When the first slide lit up, it was our own school! Using an aerial photo of Hsiushui Junior High, Dom walked everyone through the campus, homes, farmland and roads to explain SDG 11, “Sustainable Cities and Communities.” The places we pass on the way to school every day can be a starting point for thinking about the future. How can we make our hometown safer and friendlier? Start right here in Xiushui!

🌱 08:15–09:00｜從空中看秀水，從身邊聊永續。投影片一亮，竟然是我們熟悉的秀中！Dom 用秀水國中的空拍照，帶大家從校園、住家、農田和道路，認識 SDG 11「永續城市與社區」。每天上學經過的地方，也能成為思考永續的起點。怎麼讓自己的家鄉更安全、更友善？就從我們生活的秀水想起！🏫🌾

🎤 10:15–11:00 | The mic goes to Hsiushui students! This time the students were the presenters, while Dom sat in the audience and asked questions. Basketball and volleyball, recorder, Nanguan and Beiguan music, plus the Reading, Vietnamese and Balloon Art clubs — eight clubs took turns telling a friend from far away, in English, what they do at Hsiushui.

🎤 10:15–11:00｜麥克風交給秀中同學！這一場換同學當主講人，Dom 坐在台下聽，也開口提問。🏀🏐 籃球社、排球社，🎶 直笛社、南管社、北管社，還有 📚 閱讀社、🗣️ 越語社和 🎈 氣球社，八個社團輪番登場，把平常在秀中做的事，用英語說給遠方的朋友聽！

🌸 The Balloon Art Club had a little surprise: they twisted balloons into a pink flower right there and gave it to Dom. When the talk turned to music, Dom sang a song; when it turned to sports, she got everyone up and moving!

🌸 氣球社同學還準備了小驚喜，現場把氣球扭成一朵粉紅色的花，送給美麗的 Dom。聊到音樂，Dom 現場唱了一首歌。🎵 聊到運動，Dom 帶著大家一起動起來，知行合一！💪🙌

Getting to know the world — and introducing our hometown and our school to the world.
一邊認識世界，一邊把自己的家鄉和校園介紹給世界。

💛 Thank you to the Taiwan Ren-Shi Education Association for an exchange full of songs, laughter and students speaking up. This is how international education happens at Hsiushui.

💛 感謝人師教育協會的用心安排，讓這場知識性的對話有歌聲、有笑聲，也有同學大方開口的聲音。秀中的國際教育，就在這樣的你來我往中發生。
