Step by Step: The Recorder Ensemble’s Parent Meeting

成就，是一步一步努力而來的！木笛隊班親會
⌄
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Achievement comes one step at a time! Today the Hsiushui recorder ensemble held a parent meeting. Through the students’ music and conversations between teachers and parents, we saw again how hard the team has worked and how much it has grown.

成就，是一步一步努力而來的！今天秀水國中木笛隊舉辦班親會，透過孩子們的音樂分享與師長、家長間的交流，讓我們再次看見木笛隊一路走來的努力與成長。

Many students joined without being able to read music or any musical background. Since then they have played on the stage of Weiwuying in Kaohsiung and even won gold at international contests in Japan. It is a journey that makes us proud.

回顧木笛隊的歷程，從孩子們加入時不會看樂譜、沒有任何音樂基礎，到能站上高雄衛武營的音樂舞台，甚至走向日本國際賽事，獲得金賞獎，這一路的成長與蛻變，真的令人感動與驕傲！

Thank you to our coaches, Ms. Chang Chia-ying (張嘉瑩), Mr. Chen Yu-ching (陳宥境) and Ms. Yeh Yi-hsuan (葉怡萱). Beyond regular rehearsals, they gave many hours of their own time — asking students to record their practice, then listening, coaching and correcting each one, note by note and part by part.

感謝指導老師張嘉瑩老師、陳宥境老師、葉怡萱老師！除了固定的團練時間，老師們還投入大量額外的私人時間，請學生錄製練習音檔，逐一聆聽、指導與修正。從每個音符、每個聲部到整體合奏，都是一次又一次耐心陪伴與專業指導的成果。

Thank you to Hsiu Yu Enterprise for its long-term sponsorship. The school also provides professional instruments for every part, and performance costumes, so students can learn and perform with quality resources.

感謝秀育企業長期持續贊助木笛隊！學校也用心準備不同聲部、不同種類的專業樂器及演出服裝，提供孩子優質的學習與展演資源。

And thank you to every parent! Parents’ Association President Liang Tang-ying (梁棠瑛) gave the team a bonus for the county music contest, and her husband, Mr. Chiang Wei-han (江威漢), came to the meeting to cheer the students on. Thank you to every family that helps with practice, drives students to rehearsals and supports music education.

更要感謝每一位支持孩子的家長！特別感謝家長會長梁棠瑛會長致贈木笛隊「彰化縣學生音樂比賽加菜金」，今天也由會長的先生江威漢先生代表出席，給予孩子們溫暖的鼓勵與支持。謝謝每一位願意陪伴孩子練習、接送孩子、鼓勵孩子，並支持學校音樂教育的家長。

What we hope students take away is more than prizes: resilience when facing setbacks, teamwork and listening, the ability to manage their time and set high standards, and a love of music that lasts a lifetime.

我們希望孩子從木笛隊帶走的，不只是比賽得獎的榮耀，更是：面對挫折時願意堅持的韌性；面對團隊時懂得合作與傾聽；面對學習時懂得安排時間與自我要求；面對人生時，擁有一份受用一生的音樂素養。

One note may be small, but when a group of children work together, they can write the most moving music.
一個音符或許微小，但當一群孩子一起努力，就能譜出最動人的樂章！

Education is never one person’s work — teachers, parents, the school and the community raise children together. Thank you to everyone who supports the Hsiushui recorder ensemble. Know new, build dreams!

教育從來不是一個人的努力，而是老師、家長、學校與社會共同成就孩子。感謝每一位支持秀水國中木笛隊的老師、家長、家長會及企業夥伴，因為有你們，孩子才能安心學習、勇敢追夢。知新築夢，豪氣秀中！
