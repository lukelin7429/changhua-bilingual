First Place Again! Our Recorder Ensemble Wins the County Music Contest

狂賀！本校木笛合奏團勇奪 115 學年度彰化縣學生音樂比賽直笛合奏國中組第一名
⌄
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

At the 2026–27 Changhua County Student Music Contest, our recorder ensemble stood out with a high score of 90.42, winning first place (Superior) in the junior high recorder ensemble division. They kept their title as champions and earned a place in the national finals!

本校木笛合奏團在 115 學年度彰化縣學生音樂比賽中，以 90.42 分的高分脫穎而出，勇奪直笛合奏國中組第一名（特優），再次蟬聯霸主寶座，並順利取得全國決賽代表權！🏆🎶

🏆 1st Place · 第一名
Score 90.42 · Superior
直笛合奏國中組 特優
🎟️ Next Stage · 下一站
National Finals
取得全國決賽代表權

This honor comes from every member’s steady persistence and hard work since the start of the semester. They rehearsed every week and used morning study time, lunch breaks and weekends to polish their pieces — and on stage they showed wonderful harmony and skill.

這份榮耀源自於全體隊員自開學以來不間斷的堅持與汗水，同學們每週認真團練，善用早修、午休及假日時段反覆琢磨，最終在舞台上展現出絕佳默契與精湛琴藝。

Special thanks to coaches Ms. Chang Chia-ying (張嘉瑩), Mr. Chen Yu-ching (陳宥境) and Ms. Yeh Yi-hsuan (葉怡萱); to the principal, the directors, Chairman Wu Jen-ho (吳仁和) and Honorary President Li Chin-yi (李進義) for coming to cheer the students on; to Mr. Shih Szu-pin (施泗濱) for leading the team to the contest; and to all homeroom teachers and parents for their support!

特別感謝張嘉瑩老師、陳宥境老師及葉怡萱老師的悉心指導；感謝校長、主任與吳仁和理事長和李進義榮譽會長親臨現場給予學生鼓勵；感謝施泗濱老師協助帶隊前往；更感謝各班導師與家長們的全力支持！

Congratulations to every member — keep shining at the national finals. Hsiushui is proud of you!
恭喜木笛合奏團全體同學，期待大家在全國決賽舞台上繼續發光發熱，秀中以你們為榮！
