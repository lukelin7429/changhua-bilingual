Read, Think, Win! Science Reading Issue 1 Winners

跨域科普閱讀第一期抽獎揭曉！
⌄
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

The results of the first issue of our Cross-Disciplinary Science Reading series are in! Ten lucky eighth- and ninth-graders were drawn as winners. Congratulations — and thank you to everyone who took part and read so carefully!

跨域科普閱讀第一期抽獎揭曉！恭喜 10 位幸運的八、九年級同學中獎，也謝謝大家熱情參與、認真閱讀！

Issue 1 came out on September 14 with the title “An Adventure with Up” — the animated film in which a house floats away on thousands of balloons. Starting from the story, students explored density, gases, how heat changes matter, and buoyancy, the force that helps things float — all the way to how a hot-air balloon rises into the sky. Then they wrote their answers on answer cards and dropped them into the reading answer box.

第一期於 9 月 14 日出刊，主題是「跟著《天外奇蹟》去冒險！」——從電影裡被成千上萬顆氣球帶上天的房子出發，探討密度、氣體、熱對物質的影響與浮力，一路談到熱氣球升空的原理。同學們讀完文章，把答案寫在答案卡上，投進答案箱。

Special thanks to the Alumni Association, whose funding made the prizes possible — a little extra encouragement for every young reader, and one more reason to keep going.

特別感謝校友會提供經費支持，讓孩子們在閱讀中多一份鼓勵，也多一份前進的動力。

And Issue 2 is already out! Let’s keep reading, thinking and discussing together — and discover even more fascinating science along the way.

跨域科普閱讀第二期也已出刊！歡迎大家繼續一起讀、一起想、一起討論，在閱讀中發現更多有趣的知識。
