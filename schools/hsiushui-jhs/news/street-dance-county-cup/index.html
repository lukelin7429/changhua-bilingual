Fourth Place at the County Cup Street Dance Competition

115學年度縣長盃熱舞大賽｜國中組第四名
⌄
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Congratulations! At the 2026–27 Changhua County Magistrate’s Cup Street Dance Competition, the Hsiushui dance team showed outstanding skill and great teamwork. Standing out from many strong rivals, they won fourth place in the junior high division!

熱烈祝賀！秀水國中舞蹈隊在 115 學年度縣長盃熱舞大賽中展現超凡舞藝與團隊默契，從眾多強敵中脫穎而出，勇奪國中組第四名的佳績！

🏆 4th Place · 第四名
County Magistrate’s Cup Street Dance
115學年度縣長盃熱舞大賽 國中組

A special thank-you to teachers Chun-ling (純玲), Hsing-yuan (幸圓), Pei-yi (霈宜) and Cheng-han (證涵) for their careful coaching and training, which helped the dancers shine on stage and dance with confidence. All of Hsiushui sends its congratulations!

特別感謝純玲、幸圓、霈宜、證涵各位老師的辛勤指導與悉心培訓，讓同學們能在舞台上耀眼綻放、跳出自信。全體師生同表賀忱！

Every drop of sweat is a mark of growth; every move is proof of youth.
每一份汗水都是成長的印記，每一次躍動都是青春的證明！
