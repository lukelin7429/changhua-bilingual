Three Teams, Three Podium Finishes at the County Cup

115學年度縣長盃籃球錦標賽｜三軍皆奪季軍
⌄
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

The four-day 2026–27 Changhua County Magistrate’s Cup Basketball Tournament has come to a close! With strong teams from sports classes all across the county, the competition was fierce — which made reaching the podium all the harder. Congratulations to Hsiushui Junior High: all three of our teams won third place!

為期四天的 115 學年度縣長盃籃球錦標賽終於畫下句點！彰化縣體育班強敵環伺，競爭格外激烈，能夠脫穎而出、站上頒獎台更加不易！恭喜秀水國中籃球隊三軍出擊、全數站上頒獎台，榮獲季軍！

🥉 Third Place · 季軍
Junior High Boys, Division A
國男甲組
🥉 Third Place · 季軍
Junior High Boys, Division B
國男乙組
🥉 Third Place · 季軍
Junior High Girls
國女組

These results are more than an honor — they come from every player’s hard practice, teamwork, effort and refusal to give up.

三組皆獲得季軍，這份成績不只是榮耀，更是每一位選手努力練習、團隊合作與永不放棄的成果！

Thank you to our coaches, whose patient guidance, game plans and support helped the players push past their limits again and again.

感謝陳祥瑞教練、林家誼教練、施泗濱助教，一路上的耐心指導、戰術安排與陪伴，帶著孩子們在球場上一次次突破自己。

Principal Lai came to the gym to cheer the players on — and treated the whole team to drinks. The basketball parents’ support group was there at every game, too, with water, sports drinks and after-game snacks; one family even treated everyone to fried chicken!

感謝賴姵伶校長特地到場為選手們加油打氣，更貼心地請選手們喝飲料！也大大感謝籃球後援會，從飲水後援、運動飲料到賽後點心補充，默默守護著每一位選手，還有家長請孩子們吃雞排！場場不缺席的加油聲，是孩子們在球場上最強大的力量。

When the good news arrived, Parents’ Association President Wang Ming-chieh (王明傑) praised the players and donated NT$10,000 to the team’s development fund — supporting the team with action.

捷報傳來，家長會王明傑會長肯定孩子們的表現，同時挹注籃球隊發展基金 1 萬元，用行動支持孩子們發光發亮。

The tournament is over, but the Hsiushui basketball story goes on. Keep working, keep growing, keep playing for Hsiushui — go, team!

四天的比賽告一段落，但屬於秀水籃球隊的故事還在繼續。繼續努力、繼續成長、繼續為秀水而戰！秀水國中籃球隊，加油！
