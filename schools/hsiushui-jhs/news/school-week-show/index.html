Flash Dances and Warm Interviews Kick Off Anniversary Week

校慶週特別節目熱力登場！快閃演出展活力，溫馨專訪傳承秀中情
⌄
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

To welcome our school anniversary, Hsiushui Junior High held a special program on the Monday and Tuesday of anniversary week.

迎接校慶盛事，本校特別於週一、週二推出了精彩的校慶週系列活動！

🎉 Flash dances at break time: the Indigenous Dance Club, the K-pop Dance Club and a group of ninth-grade girls took over the track. In seconds the whole campus was buzzing with their youthful energy.

🎉 下課快閃舞蹈：邀請原舞社、韓舞社及三年級學姐帶來動感演出，瞬間點燃校園氣氛，展現秀中學子滿滿的青春朝氣！

🎙 Lunchtime special interviews: the basketball team’s captain and vice-captain, Li O-fan and Lin O-de, shared stories of training and glory — and invited younger students to join the team.

🎙️ 午間特別專訪：籃球隊隊長李Ｏ範與副隊長林Ｏ德，熱情分享球隊訓練的點滴與榮耀，號召熱血學弟妹一同加入！

Mr. Tsai Po-kun (蔡博昆), who has served Hsiushui for twenty years, won over the audience with his humor. Behind the laughter was a moving story of dedication — the warm spirit that Hsiushui passes on.

服務滿 20 年的蔡博昆老師：以幽默風趣的風格風靡全場，笑聲中更帶著深情奉獻的感動，傳遞秀中溫暖的傳承底蘊。

Full of laughter and full of heart — the perfect way to celebrate our school.
特別節目在歡笑與感動中，為本次校慶劃下最完美的句點。
