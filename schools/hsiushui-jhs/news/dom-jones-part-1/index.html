Dom Jones × Hsiushui, Part 1: A Big Good Morning at the Flag Ceremony

Dom Jones × 秀中 Part 1｜升旗場上的熱情早安！
⌄
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Early on the morning of November 17, Hsiushui Junior High welcomed Dom Jones, a young leader from California. She chairs the Advocacy Committee of the Taiwan Ren-Shi Education Association and is an ambassador for the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Her very first stop at Hsiushui: the whole-school flag ceremony!

11 月 17 日一早，秀水國中迎來美國加州的青年領袖 Dom Jones（多姆·瓊斯）。她是人師教育協會倡議委員會主席，也是聯合國永續發展目標（SDGs）推廣大使。來到秀中的第一站，就是全校升旗典禮！

🎤 Dom walked right up to the students with a microphone. They answered her confidently — and one even started dancing with her on the spot. They had only just met, and already they were in sync!

🎤 Dom 拿著麥克風來到大家身邊，同學大方接話，還當場和她跳起舞來。才剛見面，就這麼有默契！

The fun carried on along the track. Dom walked between two lines of students, greeting them, shaking hands and chatting as she went. Just look at all the smiling faces — that “good morning” was a warm one!

熱鬧一路延伸到跑道。Dom 從兩排同學中間走過，邊走邊打招呼，和同學握手、聊幾句。看看照片裡那一張張笑臉，就知道這聲「早安」有多熱情！

💛 Thank you to the Taiwan Ren-Shi Education Association for bringing Dom Jones to Hsiushui, and for giving students the chance to speak English and interact with ease.

💛 感謝人師教育協會的用心安排，讓 Dom Jones 走進秀中，也讓同學有機會大方開口說英語、自在互動。

That day, international education at Hsiushui began on the playground — with smiles, conversation and dance steps, we made a new friend from far away.
這天，秀中的國際教育就在操場上展開，同學用笑容、對話和舞步，交了一位來自遠方的新朋友。
