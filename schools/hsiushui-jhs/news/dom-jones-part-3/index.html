Dom Jones × Hsiushui, Part 3: From the Playground to the Principal’s Office

Dom Jones × 秀中 Part 3｜從操場聊到校長室，秀中孩子愈來愈敢說英語了！
⌄
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

🏃 After a quick lunch, Dom went for a few laps around the track. Students who spotted her ran over to say hello — some exercised with her, some showed off their best tricks. Everyone had their own way of welcoming a friend from around the world!

🏃‍♀️ 簡單吃過午餐，Dom 到操場上跑了幾圈。遇見她的同學開心地上前打招呼，有人跟著她一起運動，有人秀出自己的拿手本領，用各自的方法歡迎來自世界的朋友！🙌

💬 Back in the principal’s office, more and more students gathered, all wanting a few words with Dom. The office felt like a fan meeting! As they talked, students grew bolder and more willing to speak English. From a quick hello on the playground to walking into the principal’s office to chat — speaking English really isn’t as hard as they thought!

💬 回到校長室，愈來愈多同學聚了過來，都想和 Dom 說上幾句，校長室頓時熱鬧得像簽唱會現場！🎤 聊著聊著，同學們愈來愈大方，也愈來愈敢開口說英語。從操場上打聲招呼，到主動走進校長室找 Dom 聊天，原來，開口說英語真的沒有想像中那麼難！😊

Don’t be shy. Open your heart and believe in yourself — if you never give up, you can achieve anything.
不用害羞，敞開心胸，相信自己；只要不放棄，就有可能成就任何想做的事。

💛 Dom also shared what she believes in. She encouraged everyone not to be shy, to open up and believe in themselves. Watching the students step forward and say what they wanted to say in English, we could see that courage right in front of us.

💛 談話中，Dom 也和同學分享了她深信的理念。她鼓勵大家不用害羞，敞開心胸，相信自己。看著孩子們主動走上前，用英語說出自己想說的話，那份相信自己的勇氣，就在眼前。🌱

🍍 At farewell, the principal presented Dom with pineapple cakes from “Wangli Town,” a local specialty of Xiushui. In the group photo, Dom is still holding the pink balloon flower made by the Balloon Art Club. A flower and a sweet taste of home — both from the heart of Hsiushui.

🍍 道別時，校長特別送上秀水在地名產「旺梨小鎮」鳳梨酥。合照裡，Dom 手上還拿著氣球社同學親手做的粉紅色氣球花！一朵花、一份家鄉的甜，都是秀中的心意。🌸

Dom, thank you for coming to Xiushui, for talking with us and getting us moving! We hope to see you again soon — next time, Hsiushui students will have even more to say to you!

Dom，謝謝妳來秀水，陪我們聊了這麼多，也帶著我們一起動起來！期待很快再相見，下一次，秀中的孩子還有好多話想跟妳說！👋💕
