Thanksgiving

感恩節
← All Activities · 回英語活動
I.

Why We Celebrate

為什麼我們慶祝感恩節

Thanksgiving teaches us to say thank you to the people we love. We share food and kind words with our family and friends. It's a time to be grateful for what we have.

感恩節教導我們向所愛的人說謝謝。我們與家人和朋友分享食物與溫暖的話語。這是一個感謝擁有的一切的時刻。

Students practiced sharing and gratitude through class activities, learning to say what they are thankful for in English.

學生透過班級活動練習分享與感恩，學習用英語說出自己感謝的事物。

II.

Magic Sentences

用溫暖的句子與家人朋友分享！
Let's eat dinner together.
讓我們一起吃晚餐吧。
I share my food.
我分享我的食物。
I am thankful for you.
我很感謝有你。
Happy Thanksgiving!
感恩節快樂！
Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

turkey/ˈtɜrki/火雞

Many families eat turkey on Thanksgiving.許多家庭在感恩節吃火雞。

family/ˈfæməli/家人

We spend time with family at Thanksgiving.我們在感恩節與家人共度時光。

share/ʃɛr/分享

We share food with our friends.我們與朋友分享食物。

thankful/ˈθæŋkfəl/感恩的

I am thankful for my friends.我很感恩擁有朋友。

dinner/ˈdɪnər/晚餐

Let's eat dinner together.讓我們一起吃晚餐吧。

grateful/ˈɡreɪtfəl/感激的

We feel grateful for what we have.我們對擁有的一切心懷感激。
