Halloween Fun

萬聖節
← All Activities · 回英語活動
I.

Why We Celebrate

為什麼我們慶祝萬聖節

Halloween is a fun time to learn about Western culture. We want to help students feel brave and happy when speaking English. Through songs and games, children can enjoy learning in a creative way.

萬聖節是了解西方文化的趣味時刻。我們希望透過這個節日，幫助學生在說英語時感到勇敢與快樂。透過歌曲與遊戲，孩子們能在充滿創意的方式中享受學習。

Students dressed up in costumes and joined games and songs around campus. It was a spooky, silly, and very English-filled day.

學生們穿上服裝，一起參加校園裡的遊戲與歌唱活動，度過了嚇人又有趣、滿滿英語的一天。

II.

Magic Sentences

萬聖節的魔法語句
Trick or treat!
不給糖就搗蛋！
I like your costume.
我喜歡你的服裝。
This candy is sweet.
這個糖果很甜。
Happy Halloween!
萬聖節快樂！
Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

pumpkin/ˈpʌmpkɪn/南瓜

We carved a pumpkin for Halloween.我們刻了一顆南瓜燈迎接萬聖節。

costume/ˈkɑstum/服裝

I wore a fun costume to school.我穿了有趣的服裝到學校。

candy/ˈkændi/糖果

We shared candy with our classmates.我們和同學分享糖果。

ghost/ɡoʊst/鬼

My friend dressed up as a ghost.我朋友打扮成一隻鬼。

spooky/ˈspuki/嚇人的

The classroom looked spooky and fun.教室看起來既嚇人又有趣。

brave/breɪv/勇敢的

Halloween helps us feel brave speaking English.萬聖節幫助我們勇敢地說英語。
