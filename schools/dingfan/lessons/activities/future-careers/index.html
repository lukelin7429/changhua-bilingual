Future Careers

職業探索
← All Activities · 回英語活動
I.

Why We Celebrate

為什麼我們慶祝職業探索日

Future Careers Day helps students imagine their dreams. We talk about different jobs in English and build the confidence to say what we want to become.

未來職業探索日幫助學生想像自己的夢想。我們用英語聊聊不同的職業，並培養說出自己想成為什麼的自信。

Students visited career posters around campus and practiced asking and answering "What do you want to be?" in English.

學生走訪校園裡的職業海報，練習用英語問答「你想成為什麼？」。
