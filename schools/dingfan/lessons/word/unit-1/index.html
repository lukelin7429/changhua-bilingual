Table tennis is a popular sport in Taiwan.桌球是台灣很受歡迎的運動。

They choose books to read from the mobile library.他們從行動圖書館挑選書來讀。

recess/rɪˈsɛs/下課時間

After the bell rings for recess, most students go outside to play.下課鐘響後，大部分學生會到外面玩。
