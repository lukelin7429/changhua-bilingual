I.

Watch & Learn

觀看影片

Welcome to Dingfan Elementary School, where we're all part of a big family. Our school started back in 1917 and is located in Lukang Township. We're close to farms and places where they make faucets. We learn by doing things like fixing faucets and checking water quality. We also learn in two languages, so we can talk to people from all over the world. Our school has fun clubs too, like music, table tennis, and lion dance.

歡迎來到頂番國小，我們都是一個大家庭的一部分。我們的學校始建於 1917 年，位於鹿港鎮。我們靠近農田和製造水龍頭的地方。我們透過動手做來學習，比如學習修理水龍頭和檢查水質。我們也學習兩種語言，這樣就能和來自世界各地的朋友交談。我們的學校也有有趣的社團，比如國樂、桌球和舞獅。

II.

Words 實用單字

8個關鍵字，各附例句
be located in/bi ˈloʊkeɪtɪd ɪn/位於

Dingfan is located in Lukang Township.頂番位於鹿港鎮。

township/ˈtaʊnʃɪp/鄉鎮

Lukang is a township in Changhua County.鹿港是彰化縣的一個鄉鎮。

close to/kloʊs tu/靠近

The school is close to farms and faucet factories.學校靠近農田和水龍頭工廠。

faucet/ˈfɔsɪt/水龍頭

We learn how to fix a faucet.我們學習如何修理水龍頭。

fix/fɪks/修理

Students learn to fix faucets.學生學習修理水龍頭。

check/tʃɛk/檢查

We check the water quality.我們檢查水質。

language/ˈlæŋɡwɪdʒ/語言

We learn in two languages.我們學習兩種語言。

club/klʌb/社團

Our school has fun clubs, like music and lion dance.我們學校有有趣的社團，像是國樂和舞獅。
