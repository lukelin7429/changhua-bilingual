Our Community is Our Classroom

走讀社區

I.

Watch & Learn

觀看影片

At Dingfan Elementary School, we believe that learning is all around us, not just in our classrooms! We enjoy exploring our town and visiting places like the faucet factories to see how things are made. Students work in teams to explore the local park, library, and market, and even visit local farms. Along the way, we also learn about the manufacturing of water hardware and test water quality in nearby streams.

在頂番國小，我們相信學習無處不在，不僅僅只有在教室裡！我們喜歡探索我們的鎮，參觀像水龍頭工廠這樣的地方，看看事物是如何製造的。學生會分組合作，探索當地公園、圖書館與市場，甚至參觀當地農場。沿途，我們也會學習水五金的製造，並在附近的溪流測試水質。

II.

Words 實用單字

6個關鍵字，各附例句
community/kəˈmjunəti/社區

Our community is our classroom.我們的社區就是我們的教室。

explore/ɪkˈsplɔr/探索

We explore our town to learn about its history.我們探索我們的鎮以了解它的歷史。

teamwork/ˈtimˌwɜrk/團隊合作

You'll work in teams to explore the community.你將與團隊合作探索社區。

water testing/ˈwɔtər ˈtɛstɪŋ/水質測試

We do water testing in nearby streams.我們在附近的溪流做水質測試。

tablet/ˈtæblɪt/平板

We use a tablet to take notes on our walk.我們用平板在走讀時做筆記。

take photos/teɪk ˈfoʊtoʊz/拍照

Students take photos to record what they learn.學生拍照記錄他們學到的事物。
