Water Hardware

水五金
← All Mini Lessons · 回主題課程
I.

Watch & Learn

觀看影片

Water hardware refers to metal parts used with water in houses, like taps (where water comes out), valves (to control the water flow), and pipes (tubes that carry water). Have you ever wondered how water gets to your house? Water hardware makes it all possible.

「水五金」指的是在房子裡用於水的金屬零件，比如水龍頭（水從哪裡出來）、閥門（用來控制水流）、以及管子（運送水的管道）。你有沒有想過水是如何到達你的家中的？水五金讓一切成為可能。

II.

Words 實用單字

8個關鍵字，各附例句
hardware/ˈhɑrdwɛr/五金

Hardware stores sell tools and metal parts.五金行販賣工具與金屬零件。

metal/ˈmɛtəl/金屬

Water hardware is made of metal.水五金是由金屬製成的。

tap/tæp/水龍頭

Water comes out of the tap.水從水龍頭流出來。

valve/vælv/閥門

A valve controls the water flow.閥門用來控制水流。

flow/floʊ/水流

The valve controls the water's flow.閥門控制水的流動。

pipe/paɪp/管子

Water travels through a pipe to your house.水透過管子送到你家。

tube/tuːb/管道

A pipe is a kind of tube that carries water.水管是一種運送水的管道。

bathe/beɪð/沐浴

We use water hardware to bathe every day.我們每天都用水五金來沐浴。
