I.

Welcome

歡迎來到頂番國小

A century-old school between rice fields and faucet factories, where local craft becomes a bilingual classroom.

一所座落於稻田與水龍頭工廠之間的百年老校，讓在地產業成為雙語課堂的教材。

Dingfan Elementary School, established in 1917, is a thriving community located in Lukang Township, Changhua. We're more than just a school — we're a big, vibrant family, celebrating over a century of educational excellence.

頂番國小創立於西元 1917 年，是位於彰化縣鹿港鎮的一所充滿活力的學校。我們不只是一所學校，更是一個溫暖而有活力的大家庭，走過百年，持續在教育路上耕耘。

Between rice fields and faucet factories, Dingfan has grown a curriculum all its own — one that turns the everyday industry around us into hands-on, bilingual learning for every child.

在稻田與水龍頭工廠之間，頂番發展出屬於自己的校本課程——把周遭的產業，轉化為孩子動手實作、雙語學習的教材。

Home of Faucets

Bilingual Pathways
