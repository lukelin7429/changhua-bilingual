I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Wang Shun-Yi took the helm at Dingfan in August 2025 — returning to a school where the story actually began years earlier as a classroom teacher here from 2001 to 2017. In between, Principal Wang served as a curriculum supervisor at the Changhua County Education Department and led Dacheng Elementary School as principal from 2018 to 2025, building experience across teaching, curriculum, and school administration.

王舜儀校長於 2025 年 8 月接任頂番國小校長，回到這所自己曾任教多年的學校。2001 年至 2017 年，王校長曾在頂番擔任教師；其後歷任彰化縣政府教育處課程督學（2017–2018），並於 2018 年至 2025 年擔任大城國小校長，累積了教學、課程與學校行政的完整歷練。

Principal Wang holds a degree in Language Education from National Hualien Teachers College, and a Master's in PE Teaching from the Graduate Institute of Elementary Education at National Hualien University of Education — a background spanning language, physical education, and curriculum leadership.

王校長畢業於國立花蓮師範學院語文教育學系，並取得國立花蓮教育大學國民教育研究所體育教學碩士，學經歷橫跨語文、體育與課程領導。

The vision for Dingfan traces back to words from Malala Yousafzai's UN address, which Principal Wang often returns to when talking about the school's direction. Built on that idea, the school's guiding vision is simple: Fulfilling Every Child.

頂番的辦學願景，源自馬拉拉在聯合國演說中的一段話，也是王校長常提起、作為辦學方向的起點。以此為本，學校的核心願景很簡單：成就每一個孩子。

"One child, one teacher, one book, and one pen can change the world."
馬拉拉・優薩福扎伊（2014 年諾貝爾和平獎得主）於聯合國演說中的話。

III.

Vision for Dingfan

頂番願景：四大主軸

Fulfilling Every Child, built on four pillars.

以「成就每一個孩子」為核心，構築未來發展的四大主軸。

🎨
Diverse
多元

Developing diverse activities to inspire multiple intelligences.

💡
Innovative

Principal Wang often points to Steve Jobs' words about needing real passion from the very start to stay the course — and describes a simple hope for Dingfan: to let every student's talents run free, and every one of the school's strengths shine.

王校長也常提起賈伯斯談「起步時若沒有足夠熱情，就難以堅持下去」的這段話，並期許自己：讓學生天賦自由發展，讓學校的特色盡情發揮。
