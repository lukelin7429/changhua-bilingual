Dr. Metrology Visits Dingfan

連假收心操．度量衡博士到校

Back from the long weekend! Guess what fun things happened at school today? When you hear about the Bureau of Standards, Metrology and Inspection (BSMI), do you picture a rigid, serious government agency?

Today, "Dr. Metrology," Mr. Gao, and Mr. Tseng completely broke that stereotype! They visited our school with fun board games and hands-on teaching tools, guiding our middle and upper graders through exciting challenges.

During the event, the instructors shared how down-to-earth their work actually is — right before peak shopping seasons like Tomb Sweeping Day, the Mid-Autumn Festival, and Lunar New Year, they personally visit traditional markets to check the accuracy of vendors' scales, helping protect everyone's wallets. These vivid examples instantly helped the kids realize that weights and measures are all around us!

Through hands-on activities, the kids learned happily and remembered it well — and what a coincidence, our middle graders had just covered a related math unit, with a quiz the very next day! They got to review while playing board games, absorbing the key points straight into their brains.

Every participating student went home with a small gift, and BSMI staff praised our students for their excellent classroom discipline and sharp responses. Thank you for such a wonderful and practical event!

【連假收心操！猜猜今天學校發生了什麼趣事？】說到「經濟部標準檢驗局」，大家是不是覺得是個「硬梆梆」的嚴肅機構呢？

今天「度量衡博士」、「小高哥哥」與「曾叔叔」帶著超好玩的桌遊與實際教具來到學校，帶著中高年級的孩子闖關，徹底打破了這個刻板印象！

活動中講師分享，原來他們的工作超接地氣！像是在清明節、中秋節、農曆過年等採買旺季前，他們都會親自到菜市場確認攤商磅秤是否準確，幫大家的荷包把關。生動的舉例讓孩子們瞬間秒懂，原來「度量衡」就在我們身邊！

透過動手操作，孩子們不僅學得快樂、知識也記得牢！更巧的是，參加的中年級班級最近剛好上到相關數學單元，明天還要平時考！大家邊玩桌遊邊複習，直接把考試重點吸收進腦袋裡，真的太棒啦！

活動尾聲，每位參加的孩子都開心拿到了參加小禮物！標準檢驗局的人員離開前，更是大讚我們學校的孩子們上課秩序好、反應超級佳！讓我們再次感謝他們帶來這麼精彩又實用的活動！
