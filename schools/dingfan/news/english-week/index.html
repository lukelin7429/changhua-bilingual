Unlocking Future Dreams

五月英語週．解鎖未來夢想指南

Attention everyone! Our May English Week was a huge success! This year's theme focused on "Future Careers," where students from every grade practiced their English while exploring their big dreams.

Grades 1 & 2 · Vocabulary Fun — Our youngest learners challenged themselves to read aloud career vocabulary words. Their adorable confidence melted everyone's hearts!

Grades 3 & 4 · Sentence Challenge — Students in the middle grades took it a step further, practicing complete conversations: "What do you want to be? I want to be a teacher." We were so proud to hear their fluent, confident voices!

Grades 5 & 6 · Dream Career Worksheet — Our senior students combined creativity and English skills to complete a mini-worksheet, sketching and writing about their ultimate dream jobs!

Check out the wonderful photo highlights below to see our talented students in action!

中文原文 · Chinese

各位大朋友、小朋友看過來！我們學校五月份的「英語週」活動圓滿落幕囉！這次的主題圍繞著每個人心中最期待的「未來職業」，快來看看各年級的小小夢想家們如何用英文大方秀自己：

一、二年級【單字大聲唸】 低年級的小寶貝們挑戰大聲唸出各種職業單字，認真發音的模樣簡直萌翻全場！

三、四年級【句型大挑戰】 中年級的小朋友進階挑戰完整對話！大家自信地練習著：「What do you want to be? I want to be a teacher.」看著台下有模有樣的對答，老師們都為你們感到驕傲！

五、六年級【夢想藍圖學習單】 高年級的大哥哥大姊姊則是發揮無限創意，完成專屬的「小小學習單」，用文字寫下並用畫筆勾勒出自己未來最想從事的夢幻職業！
