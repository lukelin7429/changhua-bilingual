Chinese Orchestra Meets the Violin

國樂遇上小提琴．頂番、新營國小音樂交流

Music knows no borders — and it may be the best bridge between schools. Dingfan Elementary in Lukang welcomed a special guest all the way from Tainan: the string orchestra of Sinying Elementary School, for a joint concert unlike any other. On one side, the traditional sound of Chinese silk-and-bamboo instruments; on the other, the classical elegance of Western strings.

Each school brought its own musical color to the stage, with Eastern and Western instruments performing side by side. The afternoon built to a joyful grand finale, with both orchestras playing together — a performance that left the whole audience spellbound.

A conversation in music across counties, where silk-and-bamboo meets the strings.

中文原文 · Chinese

音樂不分國界，更是校際交流最好的橋梁！鹿港鎮頂番國小非常熱鬧，因為迎來了遠從台南來的新營國小弦樂團，兩校共同舉辦了一場別開生面的音樂饗宴，一邊是傳統的絲竹國樂，一邊是西洋的古典弦樂。

兩間學校各自發揮音樂特色，中西樂器同台切磋，最後更帶來精彩的大合奏，優美的樂音讓全場聽眾如痴如醉。

跨越縣市的音樂對話，絲竹與弦樂的浪漫相遇。
