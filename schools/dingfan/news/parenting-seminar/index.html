Parents Without Anger?

親職教育．談正向教養與親子互動

Our school hosted a parenting workshop featuring counselor Wu Tzu-Hsien, who spoke on "Parents Without Anger?! Positive Discipline and Parent-Child Interaction." Her talk left parents with new ways of thinking — and new strength.

Parents often get angry out of love and worry, but when emotions run high, the distance between parent and child often grows too. Children don't necessarily change because they're scolded — but repeated criticism can cost them their confidence and their willingness to communicate.

When parents can steady their own emotions first, children slowly learn to manage their own. When a home is full of understanding and respect, children can grow up secure, responsible, and ready to face the world — rooted in love.

May every family find more understanding, warmth, and laughter.

中文原文 · Chinese

辦理親職教育，邀請巫姿嫺心理師主講「不生氣的父母？！談正向教養與親子互動」。今天講師的分享，帶給家長一些新的思考與力量。

很多時候家長生氣是因為愛與擔心，但我們也會發現：當情緒升高時，親子之間的距離，往往也跟著拉遠了，孩子不一定因為責罵而改變，卻可能因為一次次的否定，而失去自信與願意溝通的心。

當父母願意先穩定自己的情緒，孩子能慢慢學會管理自己的情緒；當家庭充滿理解與尊重，孩子能在愛中長出自信、責任感與面對世界的力量。

願每一個家庭，都能有更多理解、溫暖與笑聲。
