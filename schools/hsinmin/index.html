A school where the rice barn of Changhua learns to sing — in two languages.

Hsinmin Elementary was formally established in 2003 in Tianzhong Township, the historic "rice barn" of Changhua, nourished by the 300-year-old Bagua Canal. From its String Ensemble (2010) and Flute Ensemble (2011) to its bilingual and international education programs, Hsinmin grows learners who are creative, independent, and kind.

新民國小於 2003 年正式立校，坐落於有「田中米倉」之稱的彰化縣田中鎮，受三百年八堡圳灌溉文化滋養。從弦樂團（2010）、長笛合奏團（2011）到雙語與國際教育，新民培養具創意、自主與責任感的孩子，以「永續、多元、優質、卓越」為辦學精神。

What Makes Hsinmin, Hsinmin新民國小的三大辦學特色

🎻

Music & Performance音樂與展演

Hsinmin's String Ensemble and Flute Ensemble are the school's pride — performing from the Changhua music contests all the way to the plaza of Taipei Main Station.

弦樂團與長笛合奏團是新民最自豪的特色，從彰化縣音樂比賽一路演奏到台北車站站前廣場。

🌐

Bilingual & International雙語與國際教育

With Fulbright teachers and a seven-unit bilingual curriculum, Hsinmin runs an IE-GC Global Competence program that opens windows to the wider world.

透過傅爾布萊特外師與七大單元雙語課程，新民開設 IE-GC 國際教育全球素養班，為孩子打開看見世界的窗。

Sustainability & Creativity永續與創意校園
