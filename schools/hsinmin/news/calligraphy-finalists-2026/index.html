Writing Their Own Brilliance 🖌️🌟藝文之光｜書寫出自己的精彩

彰化縣115學年度學生美術比賽 · 書法類決賽入選

Each stroke of the brush writes more than words — it writes a child's focus, patience, and persistence.

Congratulations to Chou Yin-chieh (503) and Yang Chang-hung (602), who were selected as finalists in the calligraphy category of the Changhua County SY115 Student Art Competition! 🌟

From practice after practice to finished works now on display, the two of them used focus and hard work to write a brilliant chapter of their own.

Every sweep of the brush is skill with ink; every stroke is persistence built up.一撇一捺，是筆墨的功夫；一筆一畫，是堅持的累積。

Congratulations to both students, and thank you to Teacher Yeh Hsin-mei (葉馨鎂老師) for her dedicated coaching and support! May they keep carrying this focus and passion, and keep shining on the stage of the arts. Let's all give them a big cheer! 👏

brush/brʌʃ/毛筆

Hold the brush steady and write slowly.穩穩拿好毛筆，慢慢地寫。

ink/ɪŋk/墨

Black ink brings each character to life.黑色的墨讓每個字活了起來。

Every stroke takes focus and patience.每一筆畫都需要專注和耐心。
