Congratulations, Principal Tai! 🎉新民之光，榮耀啟程｜恭賀戴豐田校長榮任水尾國小校長

Every school community is shaped by the people who pour their heart into it. At Hsinmin Elementary, few have done so as quietly — and as beautifully — as Director Tai Feng-tian (戴豐田). And now, after two years of dedicated service, he is embarking on a remarkable new chapter: he has been appointed as the Principal of Shuiwei Elementary School in Xizou Township.

During his time at Hsinmin, Director Tai served as General Affairs Director and Parent Association Secretary — a role that rarely receives the spotlight, yet touches every corner of school life. From campus environment improvements and engineering projects to administrative coordination and parent association affairs, he approached every task with meticulous care, a proactive spirit, and a warm, genuine smile.

"A person who serves with their whole heart doesn't just complete tasks — they quietly make the whole world around them better."一個全心付出的人，不只是完成工作，而是默默讓他周遭的整個世界變得更好。

To his colleagues, he was a partner they could always count on. To our parents, a sincere and caring administrator. To our children, a kind, patient grown-up who always had a smile ready. His two years at Hsinmin were not just years of service — they were years of genuine care, steady dedication, and quietly making the school a better, more beautiful, and more welcoming place.

Now, as he steps into the role of principal, the Hsinmin family sends him off with full hearts and the warmest congratulations. A wonderful director will make a wonderful principal — because leadership, at its best, is just what he has always practiced: care, dedication, and the quiet willingness to show up every single day.

🌸 May your school thrive and your campus flourish.
🌸 May your leadership inspire and unite a wonderful team of educators.
🌸 May every student at Shuiwei feel the same warmth Hsinmin has always known.
🌸 May great blessings — in friendship, in fortune, and in purpose — find you always.

Hsinmin will always be your home. We are so proud of you, Principal Tai — and we cannot wait to hear the great stories Shuiwei Elementary will tell under your leadership.

❤ Congratulations from the entire Hsinmin family!

這兩年的付出，不只是完成一件件工作，更是用真心陪伴新民一路成長，也因此深深贏得全體親師生的尊敬與肯定。

Words About Achievement & Farewells英語學習角 · 和成就與道別有關的小單字

Six key words — tap 🔊 to listen, then try the conversation and quiz.
六個關鍵字，點 🔊 聽發音，再試試對話和小考。

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
congratulate(v.)
恭賀；道喜
We congratulate Director Tai on becoming the new principal of Shuiwei Elementary.
我們恭賀戴主任成為水尾國小的新校長。
2
principal(n.)
校長
A principal is the head of a school who leads all the teachers, students, and staff.
校長是一所學校的負責人，領導所有的老師、學生和工作人員。
3
dedicated(adj.)
全心投入的；盡職的
A dedicated worker always gives their best effort, no matter how big or small the task.
一個全心投入的工作者，無論任務大小，總是全力以赴。
4
milestone(n.)
里程碑；重要時刻
Becoming a school principal is an important milestone in an educator's career.
成為校長是一位教育者職業生涯中的重要里程碑。
5
farewell(n.)
告別；道別；送別
We say farewell to Director Tai with pride and warm wishes for his new adventure.
我們懷著驕傲和美好祝願，向戴主任道別，迎接他的新旅程。
6
flourish(v.)
蓬勃發展；欣欣向榮
We hope Shuiwei Elementary will flourish under the leadership of Principal Tai.
我們希望水尾國小在戴校長的帶領下欣欣向榮。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🎉 Scene · Two students talk about their school director leaving to become a principal.
情境：兩位同學談到他們的主任離開去擔任校長。
A
Did you hear? Director Tai is leaving to become a principal at another school!
你聽說了嗎？戴主任要離開，去另一所學校擔任校長！
B
I know! It's a big milestone for him. We should congratulate him!
我知道！這對他來說是個重要里程碑。我們應該恭賀他！
A
He's always been so dedicated. He fixed so many things around school and always smiled at us.
他一直都那麼全心投入。他修繕了學校好多地方，也總是對我們微笑。
B
I'll miss him. But a good farewell means someone is moving on to something even better!
我會想念他。但好的告別意味著有人正在邁向更美好的事物！
A
I hope his new school will flourish and that all the students there will love him too.
我希望他的新學校能欣欣向榮，那裡的學生也都能喜愛他。
B
They will! Principal Tai is amazing. Let's write him a card to congratulate him before he leaves!
他們會的！戴校長太棒了。讓我們在他離開前寫張卡片恭賀他吧！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.When someone achieves something wonderful, you can ______ them by saying well done.
當有人達成了很棒的事，你可以說「做得好」來______他們。
congratulate 恭賀 ✓ — To congratulate means to tell someone you are happy about their success or good fortune. 恭賀意指告訴某人你為他們的成功或好運感到高興。
Q2.The leader of a school who is in charge of teachers and students is called a ______.
負責管理老師和學生、擔任學校領導者的人稱為______。
principal 校長 ✓ — The principal is the head administrator of a school. They make important decisions and lead the entire school community. 校長是學校的行政首長，負責做出重要決策並領導整個學校社群。
Q3.An important event or achievement that marks a turning point in someone's life is called a ______.
標誌著人生轉折點的重要事件或成就，稱為______。
milestone 里程碑 ✓ — A milestone is a significant event or achievement that marks progress or a new stage in life. 里程碑是標誌進步或人生新階段的重要事件或成就。
Q4.When you say goodbye to someone who is moving on to a new chapter, that moment is called a ______.
當你向某個即將踏上新旅程的人道別，那個時刻叫做______。
farewell 告別 ✓ — A farewell is the act of saying goodbye, often with warm wishes when someone is leaving or moving on. 告別是道別的行為，通常在有人離開或邁向新旅程時，帶著美好祝願。
Q5.When a school is growing, thriving, and doing very well, we say it is beginning to ______.
當一所學校正在成長、蓬勃，且表現非常好，我們說它開始______。
flourish 欣欣向榮 ✓ — To flourish means to grow or develop in a healthy and successful way. Plants, people, and schools can all flourish! 欣欣向榮意指以健康且成功的方式成長或發展。植物、人和學校都可以欣欣向榮！
