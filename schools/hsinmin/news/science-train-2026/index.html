All Aboard the 2026 Taiwan Science Train! 🚆🔬恭喜新民國小錄取．2026 臺灣科普環島列車

Great news! Hsinmin Elementary has been selected to take part in the 2026 Taiwan Science Train! 🎉

This year the science train links up many science stops across Taiwan, bringing science education onto the train and into cities — so children can feel that science is right there in everyday life, through real hands-on experience and exploration.

2026 臺灣科普環島列車 · 打開科學．連結未來
The Trip · 行程資訊
日期 Date
2026年10月20日（星期二）
路線 Route
田中站 → 斗南站
Tianzhong Station → Dounan Station
參加班級
六年 2 班師生 · Class 6-2

On the day, Hsinmin's children will set off from our most familiar station — Tianzhong Station — and climb aboard a train full of surprises, taking part in science activities in the moving carriages. One train ride becomes a science class on wheels!

From campus to carriage, from textbook to real life — we hope the children set off with curiosity, and discover even more of the fun of science through observing, making, and exploring.

❤️ Thank you to the students and teachers of Class 6-2 for taking part so enthusiastically, and thank you to Director Yi-Liang for helping with the application — keeping up the work of winning richer, more varied learning chances for Hsinmin's children.

中文原文 · Chinese

恭喜新民國小錄取！2026 臺灣科普環島列車

好消息！新民國小成功錄取「2026 臺灣科普環島列車」活動！

今年科普列車串聯全臺多個科普活動站點，將科學教育搬進列車、帶進城市，讓孩子透過實際體驗與探索，感受科學就在生活之中。

📅 活動日期｜10/20（二）
🚉 搭乘路線｜田中站 → 斗南站

當天，新民的孩子將從我們最熟悉的「田中站」出發，搭上充滿驚喜的科普列車，在行進的車廂中參與科學體驗活動，讓一趟火車旅程，變成一堂會移動的科學課！

從校園走向列車，從課本走進生活，期待孩子帶著好奇心出發，在觀察、實作與探索中，發現更多科學的樂趣。

❤️ 感謝本次六年 2 班師生熱情參與，也感謝益樑主任協助活動申請，持續為新民孩子爭取更多元、豐富的學習機會！
