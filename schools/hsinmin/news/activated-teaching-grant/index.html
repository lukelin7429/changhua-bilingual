Hsinmin Wins an Activated Teaching Grant! 🌱✨活化教學．多元學習再向前一步

Congratulations to Hsinmin Elementary for being approved for the SY115 Activated Teaching and Diverse Learning Program for elementary and junior high schools! 🎉

The program comes with NT$100,000 in funding to keep fueling teaching innovation and diverse learning — creating richer, more engaging, and more real-life learning experiences for Hsinmin's children.

115學年度活化教學與多元學習計畫 · 在新民，遇見更好的自己
NT$100,000115學年度國民小學及國民中學活化教學與多元學習計畫

Hsinmin keeps weaving technology, cross-disciplinary exploration, and real-life context into its lessons — encouraging children to explore actively, think together, and build problem-solving skills through hands-on learning.

Education means seeing a bigger world together with the children. Thank you to Hsinmin's excellent teaching team for their continued dedication to curriculum and instruction, and special thanks to Director Yi-Liang for carefully planning and writing the proposal, actively securing educational resources so that more support truly reaches the classroom to accompany every child's learning and growth. ❤️

恭喜新民國小｜活化教學・多元學習再向前一步🌿

本次計畫獲核定補助✨10萬元✨持續挹注教學創新與多元學習，為孩子創造更豐富、更有趣，也更貼近生活的學習體驗。

🌱 讓教學更活化，讓學習更多元！🌱
新民持續將科技、跨域探索與生活情境融入課程，鼓勵孩子主動探索、合作思考，在一次次學習體驗中培養解決問題的能力，讓校園成為充滿可能的學習場域。

❤️ 感謝新民優質教育團隊持續投入課程與教學，也特別感謝益樑主任用心規劃與撰寫計畫，積極爭取教育資源，讓更多資源真正走進教室、陪伴孩子學習與成長！
