A Reunion Meal, A Warmth from Afar 🌕💕一份團圓菜，一份來自遠方而恆溫的愛心

Every year as the Mid-Autumn Festival comes around, Hsinmin Elementary receives a very special blessing — a truckload of care from the Xifu sisters (喜福姊姊). 🌸

They travel from one end of Taiwan to the other, just to place a carefully prepared reunion meal into the children's own hands. It is more than a good meal. It is a message that says: I see you, I care about you, and you are worth being loved.

一箱箱團圓菜，從遠方一路送到孩子手上

Thank you to the Xifu sisters for their long-running care for children and their commitment to charity, supporting families and children in need with real action — so that at a festival about reunion, our children can also feel loved, remembered, and valued.

And we want to pass that love on into the children's hands. Dear children: when you receive love, remember to be thankful; when you feel warmth, remember to pass it along. Maybe it's a word of encouragement, a smile, an offer to help — or simply being willing to ask one more question: How are you today? That is love in its simplest form.

被愛，是一種幸福；
懂得感恩，是一種成長；
學會付出，則能讓愛走得更遠。

孩子們親手寫下的感謝卡 · Thank-you cards written by the children

May our children, through being loved again and again, slowly learn to express love, to share it, and to believe they have the power to light up someone else. 🌟

Our hope for Hsinmin's children: may you carry love in your heart and light in your eyes — become your own light first, and then a warm strength in someone else's life. Thank you, Xifu sisters, for making this year's Mid-Autumn not only a reunion, but a relay of love.

中文原文 · Chinese

一份團圓菜，一份來自遠方而恆溫的愛心

🌼 每年到了中秋時節，新民國小總會收到一份特別的祝福－－來自喜福姊姊滿滿的愛。

🚛 他們從台灣頭到台灣尾，只為了把精心準備的中秋團圓菜，親手送到孩子們手中。這不只是一份佳餚，更是一份「我看見你、我關心你、你值得被愛」的溫暖心意。

❤️ 謝謝喜福姊姊長期關懷孩子、投入公益，用實際行動支持需要的家庭與孩子，讓孩子在團圓佳節裡，也能感受到被愛、被惦記、被重視。

而我們也想把這份愛，交到孩子們手裡。親愛的孩子❤️ 當你收到一份愛，請記得心懷感恩；當你感受到一份溫暖，請記得分享出去。或許是一句鼓勵、一個微笑、一次主動幫忙，甚至只是願意多說一句－－你，今天好嗎？那都是愛最簡單的模樣。

#被愛，是一種幸福；
#懂得感恩，是一種成長；
#學會付出，則能讓愛走得更遠。

願孩子們在一次次被愛的經驗裡，慢慢學會表達愛、分享愛，也 #相信自己擁有照亮別人的力量。

🌟 期許新民的孩子們，#願你們心中有愛、#眼裡有光，#先成為自己的光，#也成為別人生命裡溫暖的力量。

謝謝喜福姊姊，讓今年的中秋，不只是團圓，更是一場愛的接力。
