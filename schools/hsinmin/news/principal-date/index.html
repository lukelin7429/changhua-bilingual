A Date with the Principal校長有約｜一期一會的珍貴約會

This term's “A Date with the Principal” for fifth- and sixth-graders opened at a lively Friday lunchtime. For the sixth-graders, this was not just a chat, but their last warm gathering with the principal before graduation.

The children listened quietly to a story — and to the lessons hidden within it. It told of a down-on-his-luck young man who meets a deaf-mute elder; from strangers to gradual friendship, the young man slowly changes his heart. When he finally chooses to become someone willing to give, someone with warmth inside, his once-heavy steps grow light. The principal encouraged the children: change your mindset, change the way you speak and treat others, and everyone can slowly become a better person.

The sixth-graders will soon graduate and leave campus. May they all treasure their days with teachers and classmates, and every encounter and word of guidance — for years later, these elementary memories may quietly become the strength that helped them grow up. May we all think good thoughts, speak kind words, and do good deeds — and keep meeting a better version of ourselves along the way.

中文原文 · Chinese

本學期五、六年級的「與校長有約」於週五午間熱鬧揭開，對六年級的孩子而言，這一次的「校長有約」，不只是一次聊天，更是國小階段最後一次與校長的溫暖相聚。孩子們靜靜聽著故事，也聽著那些藏在故事裡的人生叮嚀。

一位落魄青年與一位聾啞老人相遇的故事，從陌生到逐漸熟悉的過程，年輕人慢慢改變自己的心；當他最後選擇成為一個願意付出、心裡有溫度的人，原本沉重的步伐，也逐漸變得輕盈。校長也勉勵孩子們：改變心態，改變說話與待人的方式，每個人都能在生活中，慢慢成為更好的人。

六年級的孩子們即將畢業離開校園。願大家都能珍惜這段在校期間與師長同學間的日子，珍惜每一段相遇、每一句師長的叮嚀。或許多年後回頭看，這段國小的回憶，早已悄悄成為陪伴自己長大的力量。願我們都能存好心、說好話、做好事，並在人生的路上，持續遇見更好的自己。
