Chasing the Light, Walking Toward the Sun夢想逐光・向陽而行｜新民國小第19屆畢業典禮

The flame trees are in full bloom, and the graduation song rings out. With hearts full of love and good wishes, the graduates of Hsinmin Elementary’s 19th class step forward to a brand-new beginning in their lives. 🎓✨

This year’s graduation ceremony was a little different. With a deep sense of ceremony, the school created one-of-a-kind, heart-warming memories for every child:

🎨 One-of-a-kind colorful memories. The graduates made their own corsages by hand — and every flower bloomed in its own irreplaceable, dazzling color.

🎖️ A special honor, uniquely Hsinmin. Our thanks go to the chair of the Parents’ Association and his wife, who specially presented each child with a fine acrylic pin — a beautiful reflection of the rich results of Hsinmin’s signature courses.

🕯️ Chasing the light, brightening the future. The school gave each graduate a crystal candle, echoing the ceremony’s theme, “Chasing the Light — Candlelight.” May this warm glow light up the journey ahead as you bravely follow your dreams.

🌻 Toward the sun, heads held high. Our thanks to Advisor Shen Jia-wei, who lovingly gave every graduate a sunflower, with the wish that the children carry a bright, sunlit, positive spirit as they walk toward the sun.

Thank you to all our teachers, the Parents’ Association, and our advisors. Because of you, this ceremony overflowed with love and warmth.

To all the graduates of the 19th class — happy graduation! Chase the light bravely, shine with all your light, and go create the brilliant future that is waiting for you. 🚀

Precious memories from the 19th graduation ceremony — handmade corsages, “Hsinmin” acrylic pins, crystal candles, and sunflowers · 第19屆畢業典禮的珍貴回憶：手作胸花、新民壓克力胸針、水晶蠟燭與太陽花

中文原文 · Chinese

【夢想逐光✨向陽而行】新民國小第19屆畢業典禮，給孩子最與眾不同的祝福❤️

鳳凰花開，驪歌響起，新民國小第19屆的畢業生們，帶著滿滿的愛與祝福，邁向人生的下一個新起點！🎓✨

今年的畢業典禮很不一樣，我們用滿滿的儀式感，為孩子們打造了獨一無二的溫馨回憶：

🎨【獨一無二的彩色回憶】畢業生們親手製作專屬胸花，每一朵都綻放著屬於自己、無可取代的耀眼色彩！

🎖️【新民特色專屬榮耀】感謝家長會長賢伉儷特別致贈高級壓克力胸針，精緻展現新民特色課程的豐碩成果。

🕯️【夢想逐光．點亮未來】學校特贈畢業生水晶蠟燭，呼應「夢想逐光-燭光」主題，願這溫暖的光芒，照亮你們勇敢追夢的旅程。

🌻【心向陽光．昂首闊步】感謝沈嘉偉顧問精心致贈每位畢業生一朵太陽花，祝福孩子們帶著陽光般的正向力量，向陽而行！

謝謝所有師長、家長會與顧問的疼愛，因為有您們，讓這場畢業典禮充滿了愛與溫度。

祝福第19屆全體畢業生：畢業快樂！勇敢逐光，綻放光芒！輝煌的未來正等著你們去創造！🚀

3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.The children made their own ______ for the ceremony.
孩子們親手為典禮製作了自己的______。
corsages 胸花 ✓ — Each child made a corsage in their own color. 每個孩子做了一朵自己顏色的胸花。
Q2.The school gave each graduate a crystal ______.
學校送給每位畢業生一個水晶______。
candle 蠟燭 ✓ — The candle echoes the theme “Chasing the Light.” 蠟燭呼應「夢想逐光」主題。
Q3.Advisor Shen gave every graduate a ______.
沈顧問送給每位畢業生一朵______。
sunflower 太陽花 ✓ — A sunflower turns toward the sun, just like the graduates. 太陽花朝向太陽，就像畢業生一樣。
Q4.“Walking toward the sun” means we keep our heads ______.
「向陽而行」代表我們抬頭______。
held high 昂首 ✓ — We walk forward bravely, heads held high. 我們昂首闊步，勇敢向前。
Q5.A ______ future is waiting for the graduates to create.
一個______的未來，正等著畢業生去創造。
bright 明亮的 ✓ — Chase the light, and a bright future will shine. 勇敢逐光，明亮的未來就會發光。
