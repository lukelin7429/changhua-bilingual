Let the World Walk In!🌏 讓世界走進新民，讓孩子勇敢走向世界

The 2026 Nehemiah Character English Camp officially launched at Hsinmin Elementary today — and it is everything we hoped for! Over five days, this camp is not just about learning English. It is a journey filled with love, character-building, and real international exchange. 🌏🇺🇸🇹🇼

Chinese-American youth volunteers from the ADVENT team brought their warmth and enthusiasm straight into the classroom — through drama, games, singing, hands-on activities, and heartfelt sharing. They created a fully immersive English environment where every child is encouraged to open their mouth, speak bravely, and express themselves with confidence. In laughter and connection, the children are building their global outlook and practicing the values of a good character, one day at a time.

Education is not just about accumulating knowledge — it is about lives influencing lives. A heartfelt thank-you to all the partner organizations who made this camp possible:

  • Nehemiah International Volunteer Mission
  • Changhua County Nehemiah Character Education Association
  • Gloria American English Center volunteer teachers
  • Ailing Association Tianzhong Care Center

Thank you for helping this cross-border love continue to shine at Hsinmin. 😊

Words About Language & the World英語學習角 · 和語言與世界有關的小單字

Six key words from this story, each with an example sentence — then a five-question quiz. Tap 🔊 to listen.
六個關鍵字，每個字配一個例句，最後有五題小考。點 🔊 聽發音。

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
immersive(adj.)
沉浸式的；全投入的
The camp created an immersive English environment where children spoke only English all day.
營隊打造了一個沉浸式的英語環境，孩子整天都只說英語。
2
character(n.)
品格；性格；人格
Good character means being kind, honest, and responsible in everyday life.
良好的品格意味著在日常生活中待人仁慈、誠實、負責任。
3
exchange(n.)
交流；交換；互動
Cultural exchange helps us understand each other and see the world from new angles.
文化交流幫助我們相互理解，並以新的視角看見這個世界。
4
volunteer(n.)
志工；自願者
The volunteers from America flew all the way to Taiwan to share their love with the children.
來自美國的志工們遠道而來，只為了將他們的愛帶給這裡的孩子。
5
global(adj.)
全球的；國際性的
Learning English helps children develop a global outlook and connect with the world.
學習英語幫助孩子建立國際視野，與世界產生連結。
6
enthusiastic(adj.)
充滿熱忱的；熱情的
The enthusiastic volunteers made every activity exciting and full of energy.
充滿熱忱的志工們讓每個活動都充滿活力與趣味。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組，A 和 B 輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🎭 Scene · Two students chat about the Nehemiah English Camp on the first day.
情境：兩位同學在聊尼希米英文營第一天的感受。
A
Wow, the English camp was so much fun! The volunteers were so enthusiastic!
哇，英文營真的超好玩！志工們好熱情！
B
I know! The drama activity was my favorite. I had to speak English the whole time!
我知道！話劇活動是我最喜歡的。我全程都要說英語！
A
It felt like such an immersive experience. I forgot I was even learning!
感覺真的很沉浸式。我完全忘記自己是在學習！
B
The volunteers came all the way from America just for us. That's real character — giving without expecting anything back.
志工們從美國遠道而來只為了我們。這才是真正的品格——付出而不求回報。
A
I want to have a global outlook too. I'm going to practice English every day from now on!
我也想擁有國際視野。從現在開始我要每天練習英語！
B
Me too! This camp is the best exchange experience we've ever had at school.
我也是！這個營隊是我們在學校有過的最棒的交流體驗。
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.A learning environment where you use the target language all the time is called ______.
一種讓你全程使用目標語言的學習環境叫做______環境。
immersive 沉浸式的 ✓ — An immersive environment surrounds you completely in the language, so you learn naturally by using it all the time. 沉浸式環境讓你完全置身於語言之中，透過全程使用它來自然學習。
Q2.The values and ways of behaving that make someone a good person are called their ______.
讓一個人成為好人的價值觀與行為方式叫做______。
character 品格 ✓ — Character refers to a person's moral qualities — honesty, kindness, courage, and responsibility. 品格是指一個人的道德特質——誠實、仁慈、勇氣與責任感。
Q3.Which organization sent youth volunteers to run the English camp?
哪個組織派遣青年志工來舉辦英文營？
ADVENT ✓ — Chinese-American youth volunteers from the ADVENT team came from the United States to run the camp. 來自美國 ADVENT 志工團隊的華裔青年遠道前來舉辦此次英文營。
Q4.Relating to the whole world, not just one country, is described as ______.
與整個世界相關，而非只限於一個國家的，叫做______。
global 全球的 ✓ — Global means relating to the whole world. A global outlook means being aware of issues and cultures across different countries. 全球的是指與整個世界相關。全球視野意味著對不同國家的議題與文化有所認識。
Q5.How many days does the Nehemiah English Camp run at Hsinmin?
尼希米英文營在新民國小舉辦幾天？
Five days 五天 ✓ — The Nehemiah Character English Camp runs for five days at Hsinmin Elementary. 尼希米品格英文營在新民國小進行為期五天的課程。
