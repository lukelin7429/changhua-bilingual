Saying No, Loud and Clear 🎭🛡️反毒劇團蒞校宣導．《愛麗絲的奇幻迷藥》

Today the school welcomed the Pingpengcao Children's Theater Troupe (萍蓬草兒童劇團), who brought our children a wonderful play: Alice's Fantastical Potion 《愛麗絲的奇幻迷藥》. Through a lively story and playful characters, the serious subject of drug-prevention education became something vivid and memorable.

Facing all the clever disguises and temptations the drugs took on in the story, our fifth graders were terrific. They stayed focused the whole way through, and at the key moments showed real alertness and good sense — firmly turning temptation down and bravely saying 「No!」 out loud.

劇中角色輪番登場，孩子全程專注、熱烈回應

We hope that through this performance, the children carry that courage and knowledge back into daily life — learning to protect themselves, stay away from harm, and become healthy, safe little anti-drug heroes together. 💪

感謝萍蓬草兒童劇團用戲劇把反毒觀念帶進校園
中文原文 · Chinese

反毒劇團蒞校宣導！

今天學校特別邀請到萍蓬草兒童劇團來到現場，為孩子們帶來精采絕倫的戲劇《愛麗絲的奇幻迷藥》！藉由生動有趣的劇情與角色互動，將嚴肅的「反毒教育」變得活潑又深刻。

面對劇中各種巧妙的毒品偽裝與誘惑，五年級的同學們表現得超級棒！大家不僅全程專注投入，更在關鍵時刻展現出滿滿的警覺性與智慧——面對誘惑堅決拒絕，勇敢大聲說「不」！

希望透過這次的演出，孩子們都能把這份勇氣與知識帶回生活中，學會保護自己、遠離危害，一起成為健康安全的反毒小勇士！

theater/ˈθiːətər/戲劇；劇場

A play can teach big ideas better than a lecture.一齣戲有時比演講更能說明重要的觀念。

actor/ˈæktər/演員

The actors played Alice, the Hatter, and the Queen.演員們扮演愛麗絲、瘋帽客和紅心皇后。

costume/ˈkɒstuːm/戲服

Each costume made a character easy to recognize.每件戲服都讓角色一眼就認得出來。

disguise/dɪsˈɡaɪz/偽裝

Drugs in the story came in a clever disguise.劇中的毒品用了巧妙的偽裝。

brave/breɪv/勇敢的

It takes a brave voice to say no out loud.大聲說不，需要勇敢的聲音。

choice/tʃɔɪs/選擇

Saying no is a choice that protects you.說不，是一個保護自己的選擇。
