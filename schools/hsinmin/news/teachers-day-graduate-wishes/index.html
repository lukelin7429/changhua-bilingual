The Warmest Gift on Teachers' Day 💌❤️畢業孩子捎來一句：「老師，教師節快樂！」

畢業孩子寄回的信與卡片，是老師們最珍貴的教師節禮物

On Teachers' Day, there is no warmer gift than a few simple words from a child who has already graduated: “Happy Teachers' Day, Teacher!” ❤️

Our graduates have moved on to new schools, met new teachers, and begun writing growing-up stories of their own. On this special day, their greetings found their way back to Hsinmin — sharing news of their lives, and bringing back memories of their years here.

Time carries childhood away, but the memories stay.原來，時間帶走的是童年，留下的卻是記憶。

The laughter in class, the running on the playground, a teacher's reminder and a word of encouragement — and all those days of working hard and growing up together. 💛

Thank you for still remembering Hsinmin, and your teachers. Every “thank you, teacher” gives our teachers the strength to keep walking alongside the children in their care.

💓 A special thank-you, too, to the teachers at Tianzhong Junior High School and Wenxing High School, who carry on Hsinmin's heart for education — guiding with patience and caring with love, so that at every stage of life, our children meet teachers who believe in them and stand behind them.

Education is a long and gentle relay. Our children set out from Hsinmin carrying their teachers' love and blessings toward a wider world. And when they look back years from now and still remember the teachers who taught them — that is the most beautiful echo education can have. 🌱

中文原文 · Chinese

教師節，最溫暖的禮物，
莫過於來自畢業孩子的一句：「老師，教師節快樂！」❤️

畢業後的你們，走進不同的校園，遇見不同的老師，也開始書寫屬於自己的成長故事。在這個特別的日子裡，收到你們捎來的問候，分享近況，也勾起那些年在新民的點點滴滴。

💗原來，時間帶走的是童年，留下的卻是記憶。

課堂上的笑聲、操場上的奔跑、老師的一句提醒和一聲鼓勵，還有那些一起努力、一起成長的日子。💛

謝謝你們，畢業了依然記得新民、記得老師。
你們的每一句「謝謝老師」，都是老師們繼續陪伴孩子前行的力量。

💓也要特別感謝一路陪伴孩子成長的田中國中、文興中學師長們，接續新民的教育初心，耐心引導、用心陪伴，讓孩子在人生不同階段，都能遇見願意相信他、支持他的老師。

#教育是一場溫柔而漫長的接力。
從新民出發，帶著老師給予的愛與祝福，走向更寬廣的世界；而當多年後回頭看，還能想起那些曾經教過自己的老師——那就是教育最美好的回聲。🌱

新民永遠是你們成長的起點，老師永遠以你們的成長為榮。
