Sixth-Grade Inter-Class Dodgeball · Graduation-Season Memories六年級班際躲避球賽．畢業季熱血回憶

Graduation season is quietly approaching, and after their final exams the sixth-graders' campus life has been filled with extra energy and warmth by the inter-class dodgeball tournament. As these students prepare to leave their elementary years behind, this graduation-season event is far more than a contest between classes — it is a way to leave a vivid, youthful memory of their time at Hsinmin. The cheers and laughter rising from the playground made these final days on campus all the more precious and unforgettable.

On the court the sixth-graders showed composed, agile, competitive skill. The pace was fast and tactical, every throw and dodge demanding judgement. Some watched their opponents closely and struck at exactly the right moment; others moved nimbly, darting away from incoming balls; and a few defensive aces caught throw after throw, holding their class's line and drawing gasps and applause from the sidelines.

In many ways the match was a miniature of these students' six years of growth. Like the running and dodging on the court, learning takes courage to face challenges and teamwork to go further. Every throw, catch and step embodied the spirit of trying and never giving up — storing up strength for the graduation journey ahead.

Win or lose, the students applauded their opponents, thanking one another for the effort they had put in. This was not just a sports match, but one of the most youthful pages of their elementary lives.

We wish our sixth-graders well: may they carry the courage, focus and teamwork of the dodgeball court into the next stage of their learning. Graduation is not an ending but a new beginning — may they keep shining on the road ahead.

中文原文 · Chinese

【六年級班際躲避球賽．畢業季熱血回憶】🏐🔥

畢業季的腳步悄悄來臨，畢業考後的六年級校園生活，因為班際躲避球賽而增添更多活力與溫暖🌸。在即將離開小學階段的時刻，這場屬於畢業季的運動活動，不只是班級之間的競賽，更像是為國小生活留下青春熱血的回憶。操場上傳來的加油聲與歡笑聲，讓畢業前的校園時光更加珍貴而難忘。

六年級學生在場上展現沉穩又俐落的競賽實力💪，比賽節奏緊湊，球來球往之間充滿策略與判斷。有的同學專注觀察對手動作，抓準時機精準出手🎯；有的同學展現靈活身手，在場上快速移動閃躲攻擊🏃‍♂️；也有守備高手一次次成功接殺來球，守住班級防線，精彩表現讓場邊觀眾忍不住驚呼與鼓掌👏。

這場躲避球賽，也像是六年級同學小學階段成長歷程的縮影✨。六年的學習與生活，就像球場上的奔跑與閃躲，需要勇氣面對挑戰，也需要團隊合作才能走得更遠。每一次投球、接球與移動，都象徵著努力不放棄的精神，也為即將到來的畢業旅程累積力量。

無論勝負如何，同學們都以掌聲向對手致意，感謝彼此在競賽中的努力付出。這不只是一次運動比賽，更是小學生活中最青春的一頁記錄📖。

祝福六年級同學帶著躲避球場上的勇氣、專注與團隊合作精神，迎向下一段學習旅程🌟。畢業不是結束，而是新的開始，願大家在未來的道路上繼續發光發熱，創造更加精彩的人生篇章！🏐🎈
