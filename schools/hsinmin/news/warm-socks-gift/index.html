A Pair of Socks, A Heart Full of Warmth 🧡一雙襪子，傳遞一份溫暖的心意

Hsinmin Elementary received a heartwarming gift this summer! The Changhua County Federation of School Parents' Associations visited our campus to donate a batch of "filial piety socks" — a gift full of love and good wishes.

Gift Delivered · 致贈單位
  • Changhua County Federation of School Parents' Associations｜彰化縣各級學校家長會聯合會
  • Chairman Hsu Yu-Chung｜理事長 許友忠
  • Director Chang Gao-Hua｜張理事 張高華

After the new semester begins, this gift of love will be passed on through the children's own hands — to their beloved grandparents. It's a chance to encourage students to express their affection out loud and show their care in a real, tangible way.

A pair of socks may seem small, but it carries a big lesson: that love is meant to be spoken, and gratitude is meant to be shown. 🧡

中文原文 · Chinese

一雙襪子，傳遞一份溫暖的心意

感謝彰化縣各級學校家長會聯合會，由許友忠理事長、張高華張理事率同仁蒞臨新民國小，致贈「孝親襪」一批，將這份充滿愛與祝福的心意送到我們手中。

我們會將這份愛的禮物，於開學後透過孩子們的小手，轉送給親愛的爺爺、奶奶，外公、外婆，鼓勵孩子們學會把愛說出口、把心意表現出來。

English Learning Corner

Words About Kindness & Family英語學習角 · 和關懷與親情有關的小單字

Six key words — tap 🔊 to listen, then try the conversation and quiz.
六個關鍵字，點 🔊 聽發音，再試試對話和小考。

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
donate(v.)
捐贈
To donate means to give something to help others, without expecting anything back. The parents' association donated warm socks to Hsinmin's students.
捐贈是指給予某物來幫助他人，不求回報。家長會聯合會捐贈了溫暖的襪子給新民的學生。
2
gratitude(n.)
感激；感恩
Gratitude means feeling thankful for something kind that someone has done for you.
感恩是指對別人為你做的善事感到感激。
3
generous(adj.)
慷慨的
A generous person is happy to share and give to others. It was very generous of the association to bring gifts for every student.
慷慨的人樂於與他人分享和給予。家長會聯合會為每位學生準備禮物，真的非常慷慨。
4
express(v.)
表達
To express your feelings means to show or say them clearly. Giving a gift is one way to express love.
表達你的感受就是清楚地展現或說出來。送禮物是表達愛的一種方式。
5
affection(n.)
關愛；情感
Affection is a warm, loving feeling for someone, like the love children have for their grandparents.
關愛是對某人溫暖、疼愛的感覺，就像孩子對祖父母的愛一樣。
6
cherish(v.)
珍惜
To cherish someone means to care for and value them deeply. We should always cherish time with our grandparents.
珍惜某人是指深深地關心和重視他們。我們應該永遠珍惜與祖父母相處的時光。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🧡 Scene · Two students talk about the socks they will give to their grandparents.
情境：兩位同學聊起要送給祖父母的襪子。
A
Did you hear? The parents' association donated warm socks for us to give to our grandparents!
你聽說了嗎？家長會捐贈了溫暖的襪子，讓我們送給祖父母！
B
That's so generous of them! I can't wait to express my love to my grandma.
他們真的太慷慨了！我等不及要向奶奶表達我的愛了。
A
Me too! I want to show my affection and say thank you for everything she does.
我也是！我想展現我的關愛，謝謝她為我做的一切。
B
We should always cherish the time we have with our grandparents.
我們應該永遠珍惜和祖父母相處的時光。
A
Let's feel gratitude and give them the warmest hug too!
讓我們心懷感恩，也給他們一個最溫暖的擁抱吧！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.To give something to help others, without expecting anything back, is to ______.
給予某物來幫助他人、不求回報，叫做______。
donate 捐贈 ✓ — The parents' association donated socks out of pure kindness, expecting nothing in return. 家長會聯合會純粹出於善意捐贈襪子，不求任何回報。
Q2.Feeling thankful for a kind thing someone did for you is called ______.
對別人為你做的善事感到感激，叫做______。
gratitude 感恩 ✓ — The students feel gratitude toward the parents' association for their thoughtful gift. 孩子們對家長會聯合會用心準備的禮物心懷感恩。
Q3.A person who is happy to share and give to others is ______.
樂於與他人分享和給予的人是______的。
generous 慷慨的 ✓ — Donating gifts for every student at Hsinmin was a truly generous act. 為新民每一位學生準備禮物，是非常慷慨的舉動。
Q4.To show or say your feelings clearly is to ______ them.
清楚地展現或說出你的感受，叫做______它們。
express 表達 ✓ — Giving grandparents a pair of socks is one sweet way for children to express their love. 送祖父母一雙襪子，是孩子表達愛的一種貼心方式。
Q5.To care for and value someone deeply is to ______ them.
深深地關心和重視某人，叫做______他們。
cherish 珍惜 ✓ — This gift reminds every child to cherish the time they have with their grandparents. 這份禮物提醒每個孩子珍惜與祖父母相處的時光。
