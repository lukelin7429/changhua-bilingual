PIRLS 2026 · Reading That Connects to the World與世界接軌的閱讀力｜新民國小 PIRLS 2026 任務完成

Reading that connects to the world — Hsinmin Elementary completes its PIRLS 2026 mission. Yesterday, Hsinmin's Class 403 was honored to be selected as one of the schools representing Taiwan in the PIRLS 2026 international reading-literacy assessment. This was not just a test, but a learning journey in step with the world.

Reading has never been merely an exam subject; it is the foundational ability for all learning. At Hsinmin we have always believed: only by being able to read can we understand the world, and only by understanding can we truly learn how to learn.

Through internationalised question formats and a digital platform, this assessment placed the children in a global perspective, exposing them to the front line of reading-literacy challenges and prompting us once more to deepen our daily practice of reading education.

From advance preparation to the day's procedures — instructions, a two-stage reading test and a student questionnaire — the whole process went smoothly. The children were focused and engaged, showing fine learning attitudes and reading ability, which was truly gratifying.

Special thanks to Teachers Yi-jun and Yi-yin for their careful guidance, and to Director Yi-liang for coordinating, so that the assessment ran smoothly to a successful close. Thanks too to the parents for their support — your help with the upcoming parent questionnaire will make this international study more complete and meaningful. At Hsinmin we value not only academic performance, but even more our children's ability to understand the world.
