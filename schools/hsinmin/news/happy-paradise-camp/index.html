Happy Paradise Summer Camp快樂天堂夏令營・免費三日圓夢之旅

Every child deserves a summer full of adventure, friendship, and wonderful memories. The Happy Paradise Foundation is making that dream come true — completely free of charge!

This summer, the Happy Paradise Foundation is inviting students from low-income households in Grades 4 through 6 (including students moving up from Grade 4 to 5, and from Grade 5 to 6) to join a three-day summer camp from August 1 to 3. The camp will take place at the Changhua County Council, and all meals, activities, and accommodation costs — estimated at NT$5,700 per person — are fully covered. There are 120 spots available.

Please note that registration must be done in person at the Happy Paradise Foundation office, located at No. 534, Section 2, Yuanshui Road, Yuanlin City. Phone and online registration are not accepted. If your family qualifies and you'd like your child to have this experience, please visit in person to sign up!

🏕️ Camp At a Glance · 夏令營重點整理

  • Dates: August 1–3, 2026 · 2026年8月1日至3日（三天兩夜）
  • Who: Low-income households, Grades 4→5 and 5→6 · 中低收入戶，升小五、升小六學生
  • Spots: 120 students · 名額 120 名
  • Venue: Changhua County Council · 彰化縣議會
  • Cost: Completely FREE (estimated value NT$5,700 per person) · 全額補助，估計每人費用約 5,700 元
  • Register: In person only at the Happy Paradise Foundation · 限現場報名，電話及網路均不受理
  • Foundation address: No. 534, Sec. 2, Yuanshui Road, Yuanlin City · 員林市員水路二段534號（快樂天堂基金會）

⚠️ 重要提醒：報名限現場辦理，不接受電話及網路報名。請有意願的家長攜帶相關資料親至快樂天堂基金會（員林市員水路二段534號）辦理報名。名額有限，請把握機會！

中文原文 · Chinese

🌟【快樂天堂夏令營・免費圓夢之旅】🌟

親愛的家長們，快樂天堂基金會將於8月1日至3日舉辦三天兩夜的夏令營活動，地點在彰化縣議會，名額共120名。

📌 活動對象：中低收入戶升小五、升小六學生
📌 活動地點：彰化縣議會
📌 活動費用：全額補助（估計每人費用約 5,700 元）
📌 報名地點：快樂天堂基金會（員林市員水路二段534號）
⚠️ 報名方式：限現場報名，不接受電話及網路報名

🎉 讓孩子擁有一個充實而快樂的暑假，歡迎符合資格的家庭踴躍前往報名！名額有限，請把握機會！

2Try the Conversation

🎬 Scene · A student tells a classmate about the free summer camp.
情境：一位同學告訴同班同學這個免費夏令營的消息。
A
My mom saw a poster about a free summer camp. Do you know about it?
我媽媽看到一張免費夏令營的海報。你知道這件事嗎？
B
Yes! It is organized by the Happy Paradise Foundation. Three days and two nights!
對！是由快樂天堂基金會主辦的。三天兩夜！
A
Is it really free? That sounds like a great adventure.
真的是免費的嗎？聽起來是個很棒的冒險。
B
Yes, everything is subsidized. But your family needs to qualify — it is for low-income households.
是的，全額補助。但你的家庭需要符合資格——是為低收入戶準備的。
A
How do we register? Can we do it online?
我們怎麼報名？可以網路報名嗎？
B
No. You must register in person at the foundation office. Hurry — only 120 spots!
不行。你必須親自到基金會辦公室報名。快去——只有120個名額！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.An organization that helps people in need is often called a ______.
一個幫助有需要的人的組織，通常叫做______。
foundation 基金會 ✓ — A foundation is an organization set up to support charitable or educational goals. 基金會是為慈善或教育目標而設立的組織。
Q2.When the government pays for part or all of your cost, it ______ you.
當政府支付你部分或全部費用時，它______你。
subsidizes 補助 ✓ — To subsidize means to pay part or all of someone's costs. Subsidize 的意思是支付某人部分或全部費用。
Q3.How many students can join the Happy Paradise summer camp?
有多少學生可以參加快樂天堂夏令營？
120 ✓ — There are 120 spots available for this summer camp. 這次夏令營共有120個名額。
Q4.To "qualify" for something means you ______ the requirements.
「qualify」某件事的意思是你______條件。
meet 符合 ✓ — To qualify means to meet the requirements or conditions for something. Qualify 的意思是符合某件事的要求或條件。
Q5.For this camp, registration must be done ______.
這個夏令營的報名必須______辦理。
in person 親自 ✓ — Registration is in-person only at the Happy Paradise Foundation office. 報名限親自到快樂天堂基金會辦公室辦理。
