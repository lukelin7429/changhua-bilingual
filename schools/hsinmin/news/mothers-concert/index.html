Mother's Day Thanksgiving Concert & Charity Fair愛在花開，樂音傳愛｜母親節感恩音樂會暨公益義賣

Love blossoms, music spreads affection — a Mother's Day Thanksgiving Concert and Charity Fair. On this beautiful day full of flowers and music, the Hsinmin String-and-Flute Ensemble came to the Chen-Luo Honeybee Hall to take part in the event, jointly organised by the Tianzhong Women's Charity Association and Chiao-Da Enterprise.

The flowing melodies drifting through the air were more than a stacking of notes — they were heartfelt gifts offered to every mother and family member who quietly gives so much for their family.

A mother's love is always silent yet profound, hidden in day-after-day reminders, in the tiniest acts of care, and in every moment of our growing up. Thank you for upholding the warmth of a home with your gentleness and resilience. We also want to thank every hardworking family member — because of your protection and companionship, we can chase our dreams in peace and move forward with courage.

In this month of gratitude, let music carry our thanks and let love blossom in one another's hearts. Blessings to all the mothers and family members who care for us — happy Mother's Day, and may you have peace, health and lasting happiness.

愛在花開，樂音傳愛🌹--母親節感恩音樂會暨公益義賣活動🎼

在這個充滿花香與樂音的美好日子裡，新民弦笛團來到陳羅蜜蜂館，參與由田中婦女愛心會與喬大企業聯合辦理的「愛在花開，樂音傳愛」母親節感恩暨義賣活動。

🫶樂音悠揚的旋律在空氣中流轉，不只是音符的堆疊，更是一份份真摯的心意，獻給每一位為家庭默默付出的母親與家人。

💗母親的愛，總是無聲卻深刻，藏在日復一日的叮嚀裡、在細微入微的照顧中，也在我們成長的每一個片刻。謝謝您用溫柔與堅韌，撐起一個家的溫度，也想對每一位辛苦付出的家人說聲感謝，因為有您們的守護與陪伴，我們才能安心追夢、勇敢前行💞。

💖在這感恩的月份中，讓音樂傳遞感謝，讓愛在彼此心中綻放。

🥰祝福全天下的母親與照顧我們的家人們～～
