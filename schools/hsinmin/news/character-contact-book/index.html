Character Contact Books, Every Day 🌱💜品格聯絡簿．把品格教育帶進每一天

A contact book is more than a bridge between parents and teachers — it's also an important tool for helping children learn and practice good character every single day.

Gift Delivered · 致贈單位
  • Zhenguo Moral Education Association｜中華鎮國文教道德協進會 — Chairman Sung Kung-Chao｜理事長 宋功超；Secretary-General Chang Yung-Sheng｜秘書長 張永昇
  • Zhenguo Temple, Guangxiu Hall｜鎮國宮廣修堂 — Hall Master Chang Han-Chuan｜堂主 張漢川

Out of pure goodwill, these community groups generously donated free "character contact books" to every student at Hsinmin, and personally delivered them to school so this gift of love and blessing could reach the children's hands directly.

A small contact book carries something much bigger — support for education, and hope for every child's growth in character. Thank you for showing your support through action, and for growing together with our students!

A special thank-you also to Director Kuan-Yu, Section Chief Nai-Dan, and Teacher Min-Chi for their warm hosting and help in making this gift possible. ✨

Character grows out of everyday life, and education becomes more beautiful with every act of kindness! 🌱

中文原文 · Chinese

品格聯絡簿｜把品格教育帶進每一天

一本聯絡簿，不只是親師溝通的橋樑，更是陪伴孩子學習品格、實踐好品德的重要媒介。

特別感謝中華鎮國文教道德協進會（理事長宋功超、秘書長張永昇）與鎮國宮廣修堂（堂主張漢川），熱心公益，免費贈送全校學生「品格聯絡簿」，並專程將聯絡簿送到學校，讓這份愛與祝福直接送到孩子手中。

一份小小的聯絡簿，承載的是對教育的支持，更是對孩子品格成長的期盼。感謝您們用行動支持學校、陪伴孩子一起成長！

也特別感謝冠妤主任、乃丹組長、閔棋老師熱心接待與協助，讓這份溫暖順利傳遞。

品格從生活中培養，教育因每一份善意而更美好！
