English in Action: Ordering at McDonald’s! 🍔✨English in Action！IE 國際雙語班麥當勞英語實境校外教學

IE 國際雙語班在田中麥當勞前大合照（2026.11.18）

🍔 Learning English is more than knowing words and practicing sentence patterns — what matters most is speaking up with confidence in real life!

On Wednesday, November 18, students from the Butterfly 🦋 and Gecko 🦎 classes of Hsinmin’s IE International Bilingual Program walked out of school to the McDonald’s in Tianzhong for a fun and challenging English Ordering Challenge!

From Classroom to Real Life
從課堂練習到真實情境
  • 📋
    Before the trip — teachers helped students read an English menu, practice ordering conversations, choose meals, check prices, and plan a budget活動前：認識英文菜單、練習點餐對話、確認價格與預算
  • 🍔
    At McDonald’s — the children became little English doers, bravely telling the staff what they wanted in English and ordering and paying all by themselves. From a simple “I’d like...” to a real conversation, every time they spoke up was a big step toward confidence 💪到店：勇敢用英文點餐、親自付款
  • 🧠
    Think, too! — Teachers Simon and Kevin designed challenging English worksheets, so children of different ages could observe, think, discuss, and solve problems at their own level — learning independence and responsibility along the waySimon 與 Kevin 老師設計的英文學習單

自己點餐、自己付款，開心享用成果

❤️ Special thanks to the parents who joined us and helped guide traffic and keep students safe along the way, and to the teachers for their careful planning — turning an ordinary trip to McDonald’s into an unforgettable English learning experience!

🌍 Learning English Beyond the Classroom! From daring to speak to being able to express themselves; from classroom learning to real life.讓孩子從敢開口，到能表達；從課堂學習，到生活實踐。

At Hsinmin’s IE program, we believe language shouldn’t stay inside textbooks — it’s an important tool for children to understand the world, connect with people, and explore life. May every real experience help our children grow in confidence!

中文原文 · Chinese

English in Action！讓英文走出教室，走進生活！ 🍔✨
📍 新民國小 IE 國際雙語班｜麥當勞英語實境校外教學
學英文，不只是認識單字、練習句型，更重要的是能夠在真實生活中，自信地開口表達！

11月18日（三），新民國小 IE 國際雙語班的 Butterfly 🦋 與 Gecko 🦎 兩班學生，一起走出校園，步行前往田中麥當勞，展開一場充滿樂趣與挑戰的 《English Ordering Challenge》

🌟 從課堂練習到真實情境，讓學習真正發生！
活動前，老師們帶領學生認識英文菜單、練習點餐對話，並學習如何選擇餐點、確認價格與準備預算。到了麥當勞，孩子們化身小小英語實踐家，勇敢地用英文向店員表達需求，親自完成點餐與付款！
從一句簡單的 "I'd like..." 到實際互動，每一次開口，都是孩子累積自信的重要一步。💪

🧠 不只動口，更要動腦！
除了真實的點餐體驗，Simon 和 Kevin老師也特別設計了富有挑戰性的英文學習單，讓孩子們透過觀察、思考與討論，將語言學習延伸到生活情境之中。
不同年齡的孩子，在適合自己的學習任務中練習溝通、解決問題，也學習獨立與負責任的態度。

❤️ 感謝每一份溫暖的陪伴
本次活動特別感謝熱心參與的家長們，沿途協助交通導護與維護學生安全，讓孩子們能夠安心探索、快樂學習。也謝謝老師們用心規劃，讓一次看似平凡的麥當勞之旅，成為孩子們難忘的英語學習經驗！

🌍 Learning English Beyond the Classroom!
在新民國小 IE 國際雙語班，我們相信，語言不應只停留在課本裡，而是孩子認識世界、與人交流、探索生活的重要工具。
讓孩子從敢開口，到能表達；從課堂學習，到生活實踐。
讓每一次真實的體驗，都成為孩子自信成長的養分！
