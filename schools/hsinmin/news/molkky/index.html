Mölkky · A Finnish Skittles Challenge芬蘭撞柱・趣味與挑戰的完美結合

A perfect blend of fun and challenge — recently our fifth-graders took part in a lively 'Mölkky' (Finnish skittles) experience in the auditorium.

In the spacious hall the children competed in groups, focusing on their targets and carefully judging their force; every throw drew gasps and warm applause. From their first attempts and adjustments to gradually mastering the technique and hitting their mark precisely, the activity built not only hand-eye coordination and concentration but also strategic thinking.

Throughout, the children encouraged and reminded one another — some keeping score, some offering advice — showing fine teamwork. Even the occasional mistake was met with a laugh and another try, keeping the whole activity positive and full of energy.

Thanks to 'Brother Zheng-xi' from Nan-yi for this experiential learning in the auditorium. The children learned amid laughter and grew through challenge, leaving behind an unforgettable and wonderful memory!

中文原文 · Chinese

【🎯芬蘭撞柱・趣味與挑戰的完美結合】

日前，本校五年級同學在集會堂進行了一場充滿趣味與挑戰的「芬蘭撞柱」體驗活動！

在寬敞的空間中，孩子們分組競賽，專注瞄準目標、細心拿捏力道，每一次投擲都引來現場的驚呼與熱烈掌聲。從最初的嘗試與調整，到逐漸掌握技巧、精準命中，過程中不僅培養了手眼協調與專注力，也提升了策略思考能力。

活動中，孩子們彼此鼓勵、互相提醒，有人負責計分，有人提供建議，展現出良好的團隊合作精神。即使偶有失誤，也能一笑置之、再接再厲，讓整個活動充滿正向與活力。

感謝南一證溪哥哥，透過這次在集會堂的體驗式學習，孩子們在歡笑中學習，在挑戰中成長，留下了一段難忘又精彩的美好回憶！✨
