Xin Ming Concert · Final Movement馨鳴樂章第四樂章｜最終章，以愛謝幕

The fourth movement of the "Xin Ming Concert" — the grand finale of the school year — has arrived! This special concert series has been a highlight of the academic year at Hsinmin Elementary, bringing students, teachers, and families together through the power of music.

The fourth and final movement brought together performances that showcased the hard work, passion, and beautiful growth of every young musician across the school year. From the first note to the last, every performance was filled with heart and talent that filled the hall with warmth and joy.

Music has a way of connecting people and expressing what words cannot. As this chapter of the Xin Ming Concert closes, we carry with us the melody of every moment shared together. Thank you to every student, teacher, and family member who made this musical journey so unforgettable. Until next year! 🎵💜

🎵 馨鳴樂章第四樂章｜最終章，以愛謝幕 🎵

馨鳴樂章第四樂章，華麗登場！這場音樂系列演出是新民國小年度的年度重頭戲，透過音樂的力量，將學生、老師與家庭凝聚在一起，共同感受美好的時光。

最終章匯集了整個學年的努力、熱情與成長，從第一個音符到最後一聲謝幕，每一個演出都充滿了真心與才華，讓整個音樂廳洋溢著溫暖與喜悅。

音樂有一種奇妙的力量，能夠連結彼此，表達語言無法訴說的情感。隨著這一章節落幕，我們帶走的是每一個共同分享的旋律與回憶。感謝每一位同學、老師和家長，讓這段音樂旅程如此難忘。明年見！🎵💜

1Key Words

最終章；壓軸
The grand finale brought everyone on stage for one last bow.

3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.A section or part of a longer musical work is called a ______.
一首較長音樂作品的一個部分或段落叫做______。
movement 樂章 ✓ — In music, a movement is a self-contained section of a longer composition. 在音樂中，樂章是較長作品中一個獨立的段落。
Q2.The last and most exciting part of a concert or show is the ______.
音樂會或演出的最後也是最精彩的部分叫做______。
finale 最終章 ✓ — A finale is the last and most impressive section of a performance. 最終章是演出最後也是最令人印象深刻的部分。
Q3.A tune or sequence of musical notes that is easy to remember is a ______.
一段容易記住的曲調或音符序列叫做______。
melody 旋律 ✓ — A melody is a series of musical notes arranged in a pleasing order. 旋律是一系列按照悅耳順序排列的音符。
Q4.Something so special that you will never forget it is ______.
如此特別以至於你永遠不會忘記的事情是______的。
unforgettable 難忘的 ✓ — Something unforgettable is so remarkable that it stays in your memory forever. 難忘的事物如此非凡，永遠留在你的記憶中。
Q5.Which movement of the Xin Ming Concert did this article describe?
這篇文章描述的是馨鳴樂章的第幾樂章？
Fourth 第四 ✓ — This article is about the fourth and final movement of the Xin Ming Concert. 這篇文章是關於馨鳴樂章的第四樂章，也是最終章。
