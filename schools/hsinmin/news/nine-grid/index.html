Fifth-Grade Nine-Grid Throwing Contest五年級九宮格投擲賽　瞄準目標、投出精彩

Cheers and laughter filled the air as the fifth-grade Nine-Grid Throwing Contest got under way! Players focused intently, eyeing the numbers on the nine-grid board, taking careful aim before throwing with all their might. Every throw tested hand-eye coordination, concentration, and strategy — a real challenge of accuracy and skill.

“Bang!” — the moment a ball hit its target, the crowd erupted in applause and cheers, and students who knocked down high-scoring squares beamed with delight. Classmates on the sidelines became the best cheering squad, showing wonderful team spirit and sportsmanship.

Through the contest, students trained their physical coordination and learned to stay calm and challenge themselves. Whatever the score, every student gave their all, showing the positive, go-getting spirit of Hsinmin's children. May this memory of fighting and growing together become a precious chapter of their fifth-grade year!

中文原文 · Chinese

歡笑聲與加油聲此起彼落，五年級九宮格投擲賽熱鬧登場！🥳 比賽中，同學們個個全神貫注，緊盯著九宮格上的數字，仔細瞄準目標後奮力投擲。每一次出手都考驗著手眼協調能力、專注力與策略判斷，是一場準度與技巧的挑戰！

🎯「碰！」球擊中目標的瞬間，現場立刻響起熱烈掌聲與歡呼聲；成功擊落高分格的同學更是興奮地露出燦爛笑容。場邊觀賽的同學也化身最佳啦啦隊，不斷為參賽選手加油打氣，展現滿滿的團隊精神與運動家風度。

透過九宮格投擲賽，同學們不僅鍛鍊身體協調能力，也學習在比賽中保持冷靜、勇於挑戰自我。無論成績高低，每位同學都認真投入、全力以赴，展現了新民孩子積極進取的精神！相信這份一起奮戰、共同成長的回憶，將成為五年級同學珍貴的校園篇章！🌟
