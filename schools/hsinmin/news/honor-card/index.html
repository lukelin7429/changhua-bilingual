Honor Card Champions!🏆 榮譽卡前六名表揚｜遇見更好的自己

Congratulations to all the Top Six Honor Card recipients across grades 1 to 5 this semester! Behind every Honor Card is a story of persistent effort and quiet growth — in their studies, in their conduct, and in the kindness they show to the people around them. These students have shown what it means to be the best version of yourself, one small step at a time. 🏆

Growth is never instant. It happens in the small moments — each correction, each reflection, each choice to try again. We are so proud of every student who kept pushing forward and chose to become better, day by day. An Honor Card is not just a recognition; it is an encouragement to keep going.

We hope every student continues to carry forward Hsinmin's Three-Good Spirit: think good thoughts, speak kind words, and do good deeds. May you keep shining on your learning journey ahead! 🌟

🌼 Special Education Volunteers

A warm and heartfelt thank-you also goes to our wonderful special-education volunteer students — the caring helpers who quietly support their classmates every single day. Whether it is helping a friend finish an assignment, tidying up school supplies, or simply being there with a kind and patient presence, these students embody the true spirit of compassion and friendship.

Your kindness does not go unnoticed. Every act of care — big or small — makes our campus a warmer, more inclusive, and more beautiful place. Thank you for turning good intentions into real actions, and for making Hsinmin a school where every student feels seen, supported, and valued. 🌼💛

🏆【一到五年級 榮譽卡前六名表揚｜遇見更好的自己】🏆

🥰恭喜本學期一至五年級榮譽卡前六名的同學！每一張榮譽卡的背後，都代表著孩子們持續不斷的努力與成長。從課業學習的認真投入、行為的自我要求，到人際互動中的尊重與關懷，孩子們用實際行動累積點滴進步，展現出新民學子的優秀風範。

成長從來不是一蹴可幾，而是在每一次的修正與反思中，讓自己變得更好。感謝孩子們願意為自己努力，一步一步朝向更好的自己前進。

榮譽卡不僅是一份肯定，更是一份鼓勵。期盼每位孩子都能保持積極向上的態度，繼續秉持「存好心、說好話、做好事」的三好精神，在未來的學習旅程中持續發光發熱。🌟

🌟【感謝特教小志工，讓善意在校園發光】🌟

🏵️在新民校園裡，一群默默付出的特教小志工，平時在班級中協助同學完成作業、整理學用品、陪伴學習與生活照顧，用實際行動展現關懷與友愛。

🫶感謝小志工們熱心服務、樂於助人，付出時間與心力，成為同學最溫暖的支持力量。你們將善意化為行動，抱持著「存好心、說好話、做好事」的三好精神，讓校園充滿更多理解、包容與溫暖。

❤️謝謝每一位特教小志工，因為有你們，校園變得更加友善而美好！🌱✨

Words About Growth & Kindness英語學習角 · 和成長與善良有關的小單字

Six key words from this story, each with an example sentence — then a five-question quiz. Tap 🔊 to listen.
六個關鍵字，每個字配一個例句，最後有五題小考。點 🔊 聽發音。

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
honor(n.)
榮譽；光榮
Receiving an Honor Card is a sign that you have worked hard and behaved well.
獲得榮譽卡代表你努力向上、品行良好。
2
perseverance(n.)
毅力；堅持不懈
Perseverance means continuing to try even when things are difficult.
毅力就是即使遇到困難也繼續嘗試的精神。
3
growth(n.)
成長；進步
Real growth happens little by little, through daily effort and reflection.
真正的成長是一點一滴累積的，來自每天的努力與反思。
4
volunteer(n./v.)
志工；自願幫忙
The student volunteers helped their classmates every day without being asked.
小志工們每天主動幫助同學，不需要別人要求。
5
compassion(n.)
同情心；慈悲；關懷
Showing compassion means caring about others and wanting to help them.
展現同情心就是關心他人並想要幫助他們。
6
inclusive(adj.)
包容的；友善所有人的
An inclusive school makes every student feel welcome, safe, and valued.
一所包容的學校讓每位學生都感到受歡迎、安全且被重視。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組，A 和 B 輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🎭 Scene · Two friends talk about the Honor Card ceremony and school volunteers.
情境：兩位同學聊到榮譽卡頒獎典禮和小志工的事。
A
Did you see the Honor Card ceremony today? Some of our classmates received awards!
你有看到今天的榮譽卡頒獎典禮嗎？我們班有些同學獲獎了！
B
Yes! It takes real perseverance to earn an Honor Card. They worked so hard all semester.
是啊！獲得榮譽卡需要真正的毅力。他們這整個學期都非常努力。
A
I also heard that the special education volunteers were recognized today. Their compassion is amazing.
我也聽說特教小志工今天也受到表揚。他們的同情心真的很棒。
B
They help others every day — that kind of growth in kindness is just as important as studying.
他們每天都在幫助別人——這種善良的成長和學業一樣重要。
A
It makes our school more inclusive. Everyone feels welcome and cared for.
這讓我們的學校更加包容。每個人都感到受歡迎和被關心。
B
That's the Three-Good Spirit in action — good thoughts, good words, and good deeds!
這就是三好精神的實踐——存好心、說好話、做好事！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.The feeling of pride and respect for doing something right is called ______.
因為做對的事而感到驕傲和受到尊重的感覺叫做______。
honor 榮譽 ✓ — Honor means great respect or pride gained by doing something right or admirable. 榮譽是指因做對或值得讚賞的事而獲得的高度尊重或驕傲。
Q2.Continuing to try hard even when things are difficult is called ______.
即使遇到困難也繼續努力嘗試，叫做______。
perseverance 毅力 ✓ — Perseverance is the quality of continuing to work hard and not giving up. 毅力是指持續努力、不輕易放棄的特質。
Q3.What does the Three-Good Spirit include?
三好精神包含哪些內容？
Good thoughts, kind words, and good deeds 存好心、說好話、做好事 ✓ — The Three-Good Spirit means thinking, speaking, and acting with goodness and kindness. 三好精神就是用善念、善語、善行來對待自己和周遭的人。
Q4.Someone who freely gives their time to help others without being paid is called a ______.
自願付出時間幫助別人而不求報酬的人叫做______。
volunteer 志工 ✓ — A volunteer is someone who chooses to help others without expecting payment. 志工是自願幫助他人而不期望報酬的人。
Q5.A place where everyone is welcomed and treated with fairness is called ______.
一個每個人都受到歡迎並被公平對待的地方，可以說是______的地方。
inclusive 包容的 ✓ — Inclusive means open to everyone, without leaving anyone out. 包容的意思是對所有人開放，不讓任何人被排除在外。
