Strings, Dreams, and a Beautiful Send-Off弦樂傳承・夢想啟航｜送別四位畢業學姊

1 / 3

Some moments in music stay with you forever — and this was one of them.

With warm hearts and a touch of happy tears, Hsinmin's string orchestra gathered to bid farewell to four graduating members who are moving on to junior high school. From early rehearsals to polished performances, from local stages to competition podiums, these four dedicated musicians poured their hearts into every note. They brought home honors for the school, and even more importantly, they showed every younger member what it means to commit, to grow, and to shine.

Music has given them so much: focus, perseverance, teamwork, and the quiet confidence that comes from standing on a stage and knowing you have earned your place there. As they step into a wider world, they carry all of that with them — and the melodies of Hsinmin will always echo in their hearts.

To our four graduating seniors: the orchestra's chapter may be closing, but your own brilliant life symphony is just beginning. Fly high, dream boldly, and keep making music wherever life takes you. We are so proud of you! 🎻✨

中文原文 · Chinese

🎻【弦樂傳承・夢想啟航】🎻

💜 這一天，懷著祝福與不捨的心情，我們歡送四位即將從新民弦樂團畢業的學姊。

🫶 感謝你們一路以來在音樂道路上的堅持與付出，從一次次練習、一場場演出，到各項比賽中的精彩表現，你們用努力譜寫屬於自己的樂章🎼，也為學校爭取無數榮譽🏆，留下珍貴而美好的回憶。

音樂教會了我們專注、毅力與團隊合作，讓我們在舞台上勇敢發光。如今即將邁向國中新旅程，願你們帶著在弦樂團累積的自信💖與熱情💗，持續追逐夢想，在更寬廣的舞台上綻放光彩。弦樂的旋律或許暫時告一段落，但屬於你們的精彩人生樂章才正要開始。

🌟 祝福四位畢業生鵬程萬里、夢想起飛，未來的每一段旋律都能悠揚動人，遇見更好的自己！🎶✨

2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組，A 和 B 輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🎬 Scene · A younger orchestra member talks to a graduating senior before the farewell party.
情境：送別會前，一位低年級的弦樂團員和即將畢業的學姊說話。
A
I will really miss you. You have been in the orchestra for so long!
我真的會很想你。你在樂團待了好久！
B
I will miss everyone too. The rehearsals, the performances, all of it.
我也會想念大家的。排練、演出，全部都是。
A
What is the most important thing music has taught you?
音樂教給你最重要的事情是什麼？
B
Perseverance. When something is hard, you keep going. That is the only way to get better.
毅力。當事情很難時，你繼續前進。那是進步的唯一方式。
A
I will remember that. Thank you for being such a great role model for us.
我會記住的。謝謝你成為我們這麼棒的榜樣。
B
Keep practicing and enjoy every moment on stage. Farewell, and good luck!
繼續練習，享受每一個在台上的時刻。再見，加油！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.A large group of musicians playing together is called an ______.
一大群音樂家一起演奏，稱為______。
orchestra 管弦樂團 ✓ — An orchestra is a large group of musicians who play different instruments together. 管弦樂團是一群演奏不同樂器的音樂家一起表演。
Q2.Practice sessions before a performance are called ______.
演出前的練習場次稱為______。
rehearsals 排練 ✓ — A rehearsal is a practice session to prepare for a performance. 排練是為演出做準備的練習場次。
Q3."Perseverance" means you keep trying even when things are ______.
「perseverance」的意思是即使事情______，你仍然繼續努力。
difficult 困難 ✓ — Perseverance means continuing to try hard even when something is difficult. 毅力是指即使事情困難，也繼續努力嘗試。
Q4.When you win a prize for your school, you bring the school ______.
當你為學校贏得獎項，你為學校帶來了______。
honor 榮譽 ✓ — Winning a prize for your school brings honor and pride to everyone. 為學校贏得獎項，為大家帶來榮譽和驕傲。
Q5.A "farewell" party is held when someone is ______.
「farewell」送別會是在某人______時舉辦的。
leaving or moving on 離開或繼續前行 ✓ — A farewell is a goodbye, usually when someone is leaving for a new chapter in life. 告別是說再見，通常是某人即將邁向人生新篇章時。
