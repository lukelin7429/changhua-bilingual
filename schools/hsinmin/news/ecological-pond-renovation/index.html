A New Beginning for Our Eco-Pond! 🌿生態池地坪整建工程｜開工祈福

A new chapter begins at Hsinmin Elementary! On July 28th, 2026, Principal Huang Huai-hsuan joined hands with Mr. Hung Hsiao-yen of Chang-Cheng Construction Co. and the project team for a groundbreaking ceremony — the official start of the Ecological Pond Renovation Project.

Together, they offered incense, prayed for a safe construction process, and wished for the project to be completed on time. It was a quiet, meaningful moment — a traditional ritual that honors the earth and marks the beginning of something new.

The ecological pond at Hsinmin is far more than a decorative feature. It is a living classroom — a place where students observe pond life, connect with the natural environment, and develop a sense of wonder about the world around them. Through this renovation, the ground area around the pond will be repaired and upgraded, creating a safer, more comfortable, and more educational space for the whole school community.

We are grateful to Chang-Cheng Construction and all partners involved in making this project possible. We look forward to the day when our renewed eco-pond welcomes students back to explore, discover, and learn in a more beautiful natural setting — carrying Hsinmin's commitment to sustainable campus development ever forward. 🌿

中文原文 · Chinese

【生態池整建工程 開工祈福】🌿

今天，新民國小生態池地坪整建工程舉行開工祈福儀式，由黃懷萱校長偕同長成營造有限公司洪曉彥老闆及施工團隊，共同虔誠敬拜，祈求工程平安順利、施工圓滿、如期完工。

生態池不僅是校園的一隅景觀，更是孩子們認識自然、生態探索的重要學習場域。透過此次整建，將改善設施品質，打造更安全、更舒適、更具教育意義的生態環境，讓校園永續發展再向前邁進。

感謝所有參與工程的夥伴，期待工程順利完成，為新民國小的師生帶來更優質的學習空間，也祝福施工期間一切平安順心。

Words About Nature & Renewal英語學習角 · 自然與整建相關的小單字

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
ecology(n.)
生態；生態學
Ecology is the study of how living things — plants, animals, insects — interact with each other and with their environment.
生態學研究生物如何與彼此及環境互動，包括植物、動物、昆蟲等。
2
ceremony(n.)
典禮；儀式
A ceremony is a formal event held to celebrate or mark something important — like a groundbreaking ceremony for a new construction project.
典禮是為了慶祝或紀念重要事情而舉行的正式活動，例如新工程的開工祈福典禮。
3
renovate(v.)
整建；翻新
To renovate means to repair and improve a space to make it safer, more comfortable, or more beautiful — just like Hsinmin's eco-pond!
整建就是修繕並改善空間，讓它更安全、更舒適或更美觀，就像新民的生態池一樣！
4
environment(n.)
環境
The environment is the natural world around us — the air, water, soil, plants, and animals that make up the living world we depend on.
環境是指我們周圍的自然世界，包括空氣、水、土壤、植物和動物，也就是我們賴以生存的生命世界。
5
sustainable(adj.)
永續的
Something sustainable can be kept going for a long time without harming the earth — a well-designed eco-pond is a sustainable learning space for future students!
永續的事物可以長期維持而不傷害地球。一個設計良好的生態池，對未來的學生而言是一個永續的學習空間！
6
groundbreaking(n./adj.)
開工典禮；突破性的
A groundbreaking ceremony marks the official start of a construction project. The word also means something new and pioneering that no one has done before!
開工典禮標誌著建設工程的正式開始。這個字也有「突破性」的意思，指前所未有的全新事物！
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🌿 Scene · A student learns about the eco-pond renovation project at school.
情境：一個學生得知學校生態池整建工程的消息。
A
Did you hear? Our school's ecological pond is getting renovated!
你聽說了嗎？我們學校的生態池要整建了！
B
Really? What does "renovate" mean exactly?
真的嗎？「renovate」是什麼意思？
A
It means to repair and improve the space — they're fixing the ground around the pond to make it safer and better for our environment.
就是修繕並改善空間。他們要修整生態池周圍的地坪，讓環境更安全、更好。
B
That sounds great! Was there a ceremony to start the project?
聽起來很棒！有舉行典禮來開始這個工程嗎？
A
Yes! Principal Huang held a groundbreaking ceremony today with the construction team. They prayed for a safe project.
有！黃校長今天和施工團隊舉行了開工典禮，祈求工程平安順利。
B
I hope it finishes soon. The eco-pond is such a great place to learn about ecology and nature. It's so sustainable for our school!
希望快點完工。生態池是學習生態和自然的好地方，對我們學校的永續發展非常重要！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.The study of how living things interact with each other and their environment is called ______.
研究生物如何與彼此及環境互動的學科叫做______。
ecology 生態學 ✓ — Ecology studies the relationships between living things and their environment. An ecological pond is a perfect place to observe these relationships in action! 生態學研究生物與環境之間的關係。生態池是觀察這些關係實際運作的好地方！
Q2.A formal event or ritual to mark the start of something important is called a ______.
為了標誌重要事情開始而舉行的正式活動或儀式叫做______。
ceremony 典禮 ✓ — A ceremony is a formal event that marks an important occasion. Groundbreaking ceremonies, graduation ceremonies, and award ceremonies are all examples. 典禮是標誌重要場合的正式活動。開工典禮、畢業典禮和頒獎典禮都是例子。
Q3.When you repair and improve a building or space to make it better, you are ______ it.
當你修繕並改善一個建築或空間讓它變得更好，你正在______它。
renovating 整建 ✓ — To renovate means to restore and improve a space. After renovation, Hsinmin's eco-pond will be safer, more beautiful, and ready for many more years of learning! 整建就是恢復並改善空間。整建完成後，新民的生態池將更安全、更美麗，準備好迎接更多年的學習！
Q4.The natural world around us — including air, water, plants, and animals — is called the ______.
我們周圍的自然世界，包括空氣、水、植物和動物，叫做______。
environment 環境 ✓ — The environment includes everything in the natural world around us. Protecting and improving our school's environment — like caring for the eco-pond — is something everyone can help with! 環境包括我們周圍自然世界中的一切。保護並改善學校環境，例如照顧生態池，是每個人都能參與的事！
Q5.Something that can be maintained for a long time without harming the earth is called ______.
能夠長期維持而不傷害地球的事物叫做______。
sustainable 永續的 ✓ — Sustainable means able to last for a long time without using up or harming what we need for the future. A sustainable school environment benefits students today and for generations to come! 永續的意思是能夠長期持續而不耗盡或傷害我們未來需要的資源。永續的校園環境讓今天的學生和未來世代都能受益！
