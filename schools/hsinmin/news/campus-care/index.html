Caring for Our Campus用心整理環境，打造更優質的學習空間

A clean and safe school campus doesn't happen by itself — it takes quiet, dedicated care. Hsinmin Elementary is grateful to Mr. Chen Xiping, the guardian of our school grounds, who recently gave up his personal time to fill in the road holes left by a fallen tree on Gongguan Road and to tidy up the school lawn.

A tidy and comfortable campus is the foundation of happy, productive learning for every child. Mr. Chen works steadily behind the scenes, paying close attention to every corner of the school grounds, so that students always have a safe, comfortable place to study and grow.

Every seemingly ordinary task is an indispensable force that protects our campus. Thank you, Mr. Chen Xiping, for ensuring that Hsinmin Elementary always looks its very best. 💚

中文原文 · Chinese

🌿 用心整理環境，打造更優質的學習空間 🌿

感謝新民國小環境的守護神——陳锡平大哥，利用時間，完成了公館路倒樹留下的坐洞填補，並同步整理校園草皮，讓校園環境更加整潔、安全。

整潔舰適的校園，是孩子快樂學習的重要基礎。感謝陳锡平大哥默默付出、細心維護校園的每一個角落，用心整理環境，只為提供孩子們更安全、更優質、更舰適的學習環境。

每一份看似平凡的工作，都是守護校園不可或缺的力量。謝謝您，讓新民國小的校園始終保持最美好的樣貌。💚

Words About Campus & Care英語學習角 · 和校園與維護有關的小單字

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喊叭聽整句
1
maintain(v.)
維護；保持
It takes a lot of effort to maintain a clean and safe school.
維護一所整潔安全的學校需要很多心劉。
2
tidy(adj./v.)
整潔的；整理
He tidied the school lawn so the children have a nice place to play.
他整理了校園草皮，讓孩子們有一個美好的地方玩耀。
3
dedicated(adj.)
劤屈屈的；全心投入的
Mr. Chen is a dedicated worker who cares deeply about the school.
陳先生是一位專心劤屈屈的工作者，對學校有深厚的情感。
4
environment(n.)
環境；環境条件
A clean environment helps students focus and learn better.
整潔的環境有助於學生專注，學得更好。
5
indispensable(adj.)
不可或缺的；必不可少的
Groundskeepers are indispensable to a well-run school.
校園維護人員對一所運作良好的學校是不可或缺的。
6
foundation(n.)
基礎；根本
A safe campus is the foundation of a good education.
安全的校園是優質教育的基礎。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組，A 和 B 輪流唷；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🎭 Scene · Two students notice the freshly tidied school grounds.
情境：兩位同學發現校園坊場被整理得很乾淨。
A
Wow, the school grounds look so tidy today. Did someone clean them up?
哇，校園今天看起來好整潔啊。有人清理過嗎？
B
Yes! Mr. Chen came and filled the holes in the road and tidied the lawn too.
是啊！陳大哥來填補路上的坐洞，還整理了草皮。
A
He is so dedicated. He does all of this in his free time?
他真的很劤屈岈啊。這些他都是利用空閑時間做的？
B
Yes, he volunteers his time to maintain the environment for us.
是的，他自愿利用時間為我們維護環境。
A
A clean environment is the foundation of our learning. We are so lucky!
整潔的環境是我們學習的基礎。我們真的很幸運！
B
We should thank him. His work is truly indispensable to our school.
我們應該感謝他。他的工作對我們學校真的不可或缺。
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.To keep something in good condition over time is to ______ it.
長期保持某物處於良好狀態，就是對它進行______。
maintain 維護 ✓ — To maintain something means to keep it in good working or clean condition over time. Maintain 是指長期保持某物正常運作或整潔的狀態。
Q2.Something that is ______ is neat and well-organized.
______的就是整齊、整理得很好的。
tidy 整潔 ✓ — Tidy means neat and in order. 整潔 (tidy) 是指整齊、有条不紮的。
Q3.What did Mr. Chen fill in on Gongguan Road?
陳锡平大哥填補了公館路上的什麼？
Holes left by a fallen tree 倒樹留下的坐洞 ✓ — Mr. Chen filled the holes in the road left by a fallen tree. 陳大哥填補了倒樹在公館路上留下的坐洞。
Q4.Something that is absolutely necessary and cannot be removed is ______.
絕對必要、不能缺少的东西，就是______。
indispensable 不可或缺的 ✓ — Indispensable means absolutely necessary and impossible to do without. 不可或缺 (indispensable) 是指絕對必要、缺少不得的。
Q5.According to the article, a tidy campus is the ______ of happy learning.
根據文章，整潔的校園是快樂學習的______。
foundation 基礎 ✓ — The article says a tidy and comfortable campus is the foundation of happy, productive learning. 文章說整潔舰適的校園是快樂學習的重要基礎。
