A Golden Moment for Hsinmin! 🏅新民之光，再傳捷報｜徐承範同學榮獲跆拳道金牌

🏅
Gold Medal — Men's Color Belt C12 · 58–68 kg
南投市全民運動會跆拳道錦標賽
Nantou City Taekwondo Championship
男子色帶 C12 組 58–68 公斤級 冠軍

Hsinmin Elementary is bursting with pride! Congratulations to Hsu Cheng-fan (徐承範), a 5th grader from Class 501, who brought home the Gold Medal in the Men's Color Belt C12 division, 58–68 kg, at the Nantou City Taekwondo Championship!

On competition day, Cheng-fan stepped onto the mat with courage and gave everything he had. Every kick and every stance reflected months of dedicated training — and on that day, all that hard work shone through in the most golden way possible.

This achievement belongs not only to Cheng-fan, but also to Coach Hung Jung-chih (洪榮志教練), whose patient guidance, careful training, and steadfast support made this victory possible. Thank you, Coach Hung, for your dedication and care!

Cheng-fan, you inspire every student at Hsinmin to dream big, train hard, and never give up. We are so proud of you — this is just the beginning! 🏅

【新民之光・再傳捷報】

恭喜本校501徐承範同學參加「南投市全民運動會跆拳道錦標賽」，榮獲男子色帶C12組58–68公斤級金牌！

承範在賽場上全力以赴、勇敢迎戰，展現平日刻苦訓練的成果與堅強鬥志。

恭喜徐承範同學！

也感謝洪榮志教練的悉心指導與辛勤付出！

English Learning Corner

Words About Sports & Achievement英語學習角 · 和運動與成就有關的小單字

Six key words — tap 🔊 to listen, then try the conversation and quiz.
六個關鍵字，點 🔊 聽發音，再試試對話和小考。

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
champion(n.)
冠軍；優勝者
A champion is a person who wins a competition or is the best at something — like a taekwondo champion!
冠軍是在比賽中獲勝或在某方面最出色的人，就像跆拳道冠軍！
2
compete(v.)
競賽；比賽
To compete means to take part in a contest or game and try your best to win against others.
競賽是指參加比賽或遊戲，並盡全力打敗對手。
3
courage(n.)
勇氣；膽量
Courage is the strength to face challenges and keep going even when things feel difficult or scary.
勇氣是在面對挑戰時，即使感到困難或害怕，仍能繼續前進的力量。
4
dedicated(adj.)
全心投入的；刻苦的
A dedicated athlete trains hard every day and never gives up, even when practice is tough.
一個全心投入的運動員每天刻苦訓練，即使練習很辛苦也絕不放棄。
5
achievement(n.)
成就；成果
An achievement is something great you have done through effort and hard work — winning a gold medal is a wonderful achievement!
成就是你透過努力和辛勤付出所完成的偉大事情，贏得金牌就是一項很棒的成就！
6
inspire(v.)
激勵；啟發
When someone inspires you, they make you want to try harder and believe that you can achieve great things too.
當某人激勵你，他們讓你想要更努力，並相信你也能成就偉大的事情。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🏅 Scene · Two students talk about their classmate winning a gold medal at a taekwondo competition.
情境：兩個同學聊起班上同學在跆拳道比賽中奪金牌的事。
A
Did you hear? Cheng-fan won a gold medal at the taekwondo championship!
你聽說了嗎？承範在跆拳道錦標賽上贏得金牌！
B
Wow! He's a real champion now. He must have trained so hard to achieve that.
哇！他現在是真正的冠軍了。他一定非常刻苦訓練才能達成這個成就。
A
Yes! He competes in the 58 to 68 kilogram category. It takes a lot of courage to go up against opponents at a big competition like that.
對！他參加的是58到68公斤的組別。在這樣大型的比賽中面對對手，需要很大的勇氣。
B
I heard his coach is really dedicated too. A great student and a great coach — that's how champions are made!
我聽說他的教練也非常全心投入。一位出色的學生加上一位出色的教練，這就是冠軍誕生的方式！
A
He really inspires me to try harder at what I love. If he can do it, maybe I can too!
他真的激勵我在自己熱愛的事上更加努力。如果他能做到，也許我也可以！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.A person who wins first place in a competition is called a ______.
在比賽中贏得第一名的人叫做______。
champion 冠軍 ✓ — A champion is the winner — the person who comes in first place and proves they are the best. 冠軍是贏家，也就是獲得第一名、證明自己最出色的人。
Q2.When athletes enter a contest and try to beat each other, they ______.
當運動員參加比賽並努力打敗對手時，他們在______。
compete 競賽 ✓ — To compete means to take part in a contest where you try to do better than others. Athletes compete to test their skills and strength. 競賽是指參加比賽，努力比別人做得更好。運動員通過競賽來測試自己的技能和實力。
Q3.The strength to face scary or difficult situations without giving up is called ______.
面對可怕或困難的情況而不放棄的力量叫做______。
courage 勇氣 ✓ — Courage doesn't mean you are not scared — it means you face the challenge anyway, even when your heart is beating fast! 勇氣不代表你不害怕，而是即使心跳加速，你仍然勇敢面對挑戰！
Q4.Something great you have done through hard work and effort is called an ______.
你透過努力和辛勤付出所完成的偉大事情叫做______。
achievement 成就 ✓ — An achievement is a result that came from your own effort and determination. Winning a gold medal is a great achievement! 成就是由你自己的努力和決心所帶來的結果。贏得金牌是一項很棒的成就！
Q5.When someone makes you want to work harder and believe in yourself, they ______ you.
當某人讓你想要更加努力並相信自己，他們______了你。
inspire 激勵 ✓ — To inspire someone means to fill them with the desire and confidence to do something great. A true champion inspires everyone around them! 激勵某人是指讓他們充滿渴望和信心去做偉大的事情。真正的冠軍能激勵周圍的每一個人！
