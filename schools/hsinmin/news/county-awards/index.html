Magistrate's Award Ceremony · A Moment of Glory縣長獎頒獎典禮．榮耀時刻

Congratulations to three outstanding Hsinmin graduates honoured with the Changhua County Magistrate's Award — Chen Yun-zhi (Class 601), Hsu Shu-hsuan (Class 602) and Liu Zhe-xu (Class 603)!

The ceremony was held today at the Changhua County Stadium, where Magistrate Wang Hui-mei presented the awards in person, writing a shining page in the children's elementary-school journey.

Families came to witness the moment of glory, accompanying their children onto the stage. From holding a small hand on the first day of school to receiving one of the highest honours today, behind every smile lie countless moments of a parent's company, encouragement and support.

Our thanks go to the parents who have worked hand in hand with the school over the years, supporting their children through learning challenges and activities and giving them so much love and strength. Today's brilliance is not the result of one child's effort alone, but a beautiful harvest cultivated together by family and school.

We also thank the teachers who have accompanied these children's growth — from careful guidance in class to patient encouragement in activities and warm reminders in daily life. Every step of progress and every honour carries their quiet dedication.

Thanks to Director Yi-liang and Teacher Hsuan-chi for leading and caring for the group throughout the day; to Section Chief Feng-tian for his warm words of encouragement; and to Principal Huai-hsuan for thoughtfully preparing a celebratory lunch to share in the children's joy.

The Magistrate's Award is both a recognition and an expectation. May these children hold fast to their dreams and bravely chase the light, and keep striving and shining on the journeys ahead. At Hsinmin, meet a better version of yourself — and build an even brighter future!
