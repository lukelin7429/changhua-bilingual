Love Starts with Respect: A Talk on Healthy Relationships 💖🌱非預期懷孕教育宣導講座—勵馨基金會

勵馨基金會的情境提問與演練

💗 Liking someone is a beautiful thing. But what does “liking” really mean? Is it always wanting to know where the other person is? Feeling uneasy when they don’t reply? Or is it understanding and respecting each other, and growing together?

💡 In today’s talk by the Garden of Hope Foundation (勵馨基金會), the children started from “liking someone” and learned what a healthy relationship looks like. Through real-life questions, Sister Chen asked them to think: when we say no to a partner’s request, when we don’t reply to a message right away, when we meet an online friend for the first time, or when someone asks for private photos of your body to “prove” your love — are these really love, or are they turning into control?

What We Learned
講座重點
  • 💞
    Love should never force you — you never need physical closeness to prove love, and you should never give up the right to decide about your own body just to please someone. Real liking means respecting each other — knowing how to say no, and respecting someone else’s no.愛，不應該成為勉強自己的理由
  • 📌
    Raising a child is a 24-hour job — Sister Chen explained how an unplanned pregnancy can affect the body, the mind, and a whole life. From pregnancy and birth to feeding, changing diapers, and getting a baby to sleep, raising a child is a responsibility with no days off — far more than just saying “I’ll take responsibility.”育兒是一份全年無休的責任
  • 🛡️
    Protecting yourself — basic legal knowledge, an awareness video from the Ministry of Health and Welfare, and knowledge about preventing pregnancy helped the children build the right awareness to protect themselves.建立正確的自我保護意識
保護自己・尊重生命｜勵馨基金會宣導海報

Protecting yourself isn’t distrusting love — it’s knowing how to cherish yourself. Respecting others is a mature kind of love, too.保護自己不是不相信愛，而是懂得珍惜自己；尊重別人，也是一種成熟的愛。

🌱 A heartfelt reminder: young people can be brave in love, but don’t let a moment’s excitement make you overlook consequences that could change your life. Don’t give up on yourself just to keep someone, and don’t let a sudden impulse put you in a situation you can’t handle.

🎀 May every child, while learning to love others, first learn to love themselves. ❤️ When you like someone, remember: think a little more before your heart races, be a little more careful before you act — love sincerely, and be responsible.

非預期懷孕教育宣導講座--✨勵馨基金會
💗喜歡一個人，是一件美好的事。但喜歡，究竟是什麼？是時時刻刻想知道對方在哪裡？是沒有回訊息就感到不安？還是願意理解、尊重彼此，陪伴對方一起成長？

💡今天透過勵馨基金會的講座，帶領孩子 從「喜歡」出發，重新認識健康的親密關係。陳姊姊透過情境提問，讓孩子思考：當我們拒絕伴侶的要求時；當沒有立即回覆對方訊息，抑或初次和網友的會面，甚至要求你拍下裸照來證明愛或不愛，這些聽起來愛的方式，究竟是一種真切的愛?還是逐漸變成控制？

💞重要的是，愛，不應該成為勉強自己的理由。 不需要用身體的親密來證明愛，也不應該為了討好對方、維繫感情，而放棄自己的身體自主權。#真正的喜歡，是 #彼此尊重，是懂得說不，也 #懂得尊重對方的拒絕。

📌陳姐姐也帶領孩子認識非預期懷孕可能帶來的身體、心理與人生影響。從懷孕、生產，到餵奶、換尿布、哄睡，每一天24小時，育兒是一份全年無休的責任。孩子不只是生命的延續，更需要長時間的照顧、陪伴與教育。這些不是一句「我會負責」就能輕易承擔的。
透過 法律觀念、衛福部宣導影片及避孕知識，提醒孩子建立正確的自我保護意識。

🌱 想給孩子一個深深的叮嚀：
青春可以勇敢去愛，但不要因為一時心動，就忽略了可能改變人生的後果。也不要為了留住一個人而委屈自己，不要因為一時衝動而讓自己陷入無法承擔的處境。#保護自己不是不相信愛，而是 #懂得珍惜自己；#尊重別人，#也是一種成熟的愛。

🎀願每個孩子都能在學習愛人的同時，先學會愛自己。
❤️喜歡一個人，請記得：心動之前多一分思考，行動之前多一分謹慎，愛得真誠，更要懂得負責。
