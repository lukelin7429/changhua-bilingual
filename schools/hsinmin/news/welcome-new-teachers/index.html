Welcome, New Teachers! 🎉新進教師，歡迎加入新民大家庭！

A new chapter begins at Hsinmin Elementary! As the new school year approaches, we are thrilled to welcome four outstanding new teachers to our campus — each bringing their own unique passion, expertise, and heart to serve our students.

We are delighted to introduce the newest members of the Hsinmin family:

徐民欣 老師Teacher Hsu Min-hsin
資源班 · Special Education
阮靖雯 老師Teacher Juan Jing-wen
英文科 · English
許家容 老師Teacher Hsu Chia-jung
音樂科 · Music
蕭慧茹 老師Teacher Hsiao Hui-ju
自然科 · Natural Science

From the resource room where every child's learning needs are met with care, to the English classroom where bilingual confidence grows word by word; from the music room filled with melody and creativity, to the science corner where curiosity leads to discovery — these four teachers bring the diverse and passionate spirit that makes Hsinmin a truly special place to learn.

"A great teacher doesn't just share knowledge — they light a spark that keeps students curious, brave, and eager to learn."好老師不只是傳遞知識，更是點燃那份讓孩子好奇、勇敢、渴望學習的火花。

On behalf of all our students, parents, and staff, we extend the warmest welcome to our four new colleagues. We are so glad you chose Hsinmin — and we look forward to growing together.

❤ Welcome to the Hsinmin family!

English Learning Corner

Words About Teachers & Learning英語學習角 · 和老師與學習有關的小單字

六個關鍵字，點 🔊 聽發音，再試試對話和小考。

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
welcome(v./n.)
歡迎；迎接
We welcome new teachers to our school and hope they feel happy here.
我們歡迎新老師來到學校，希望他們在這裡感到快樂。
2
bilingual(adj.)
雙語的；使用兩種語言的
A bilingual person can speak two languages and switch between them easily.
雙語者能說兩種語言，並能在它們之間輕鬆切換。
3
inspire(v.)
啟發；激勵
A great music teacher can inspire students to love music for their whole lives.
一位優秀的音樂老師能夠啟發學生一輩子熱愛音樂。
4
passionate(adj.)
充滿熱情的；熱衷的
Our new science teacher is passionate about helping students explore the natural world.
我們的新自然老師對幫助學生探索自然世界充滿熱情。
5
diverse(adj.)
多元的；多樣的
Our school has diverse subjects so every student can find something they love.
我們學校科目多元，讓每位學生都能找到自己喜愛的事物。
6
contribute(v.)
貢獻；付出
Every teacher contributes something special to the school and helps make it a better place.
每位老師都為學校貢獻一些特別的事物，讓學校變成更好的地方。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🎉 Scene · Two students talk about the new teachers joining their school.
情境：兩位同學討論學校來了哪些新老師。
A
Did you hear? Our school has four new teachers this year! I'm so excited to meet them.
你聽說了嗎？我們學校今年來了四位新老師！我好期待見到他們。
B
Yes! I heard one is a bilingual English teacher. That sounds amazing for our school!
對！我聽說其中一位是雙語英文老師。對我們學校來說太棒了！
A
There's also a new music teacher. I hope she'll inspire us to sing and play instruments more!
還有一位新的音樂老師。希望她能啟發我們多唱歌、多玩樂器！
B
And a passionate science teacher! I love learning about nature. I can't wait!
還有一位充滿熱情的自然老師！我很喜歡學關於大自然的事。我等不及了！
A
It's wonderful that our school is so diverse. Every teacher contributes something different and special.
我們學校如此多元真的很棒。每位老師都貢獻了不同且特別的事物。
B
I agree! Let's all welcome them warmly. I hope they love being here as much as we do!
我同意！讓我們熱情歡迎他們。希望他們和我們一樣喜歡這裡！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.When someone arrives at a new place, it is kind to ______ them.
當有人來到新地方，友善地______他們是很好的行為。
welcome 歡迎 ✓ — To welcome someone means to greet them in a warm and friendly way. 歡迎某人就是以溫暖且友善的方式迎接他們。
Q2.A school where students learn in both Chinese and English is a ______ school.
學生同時以中文和英文學習的學校叫做______學校。
bilingual 雙語的 ✓ — Bilingual means using or able to use two languages. Many schools in Taiwan are working to become bilingual. 雙語意指使用或能夠使用兩種語言。台灣許多學校正在努力成為雙語學校。
Q3.To give someone the feeling or idea to do something great is to ______ them.
給某人一種啟發或想法去做偉大的事，叫做______他們。
inspire 啟發 ✓ — To inspire means to fill someone with enthusiasm and the desire to do something. 啟發意指讓某人充滿熱情並渴望去做某事。
Q4.Which word describes someone who really loves and cares deeply about what they do?
哪個詞描述一個真心熱愛且深深在乎自己所做事情的人？
passionate 充滿熱情的 ✓ — Passionate describes someone with strong feelings of love or enthusiasm for something. 充滿熱情的描述對某事有強烈熱愛或熱忱的人。
Q5.When each person adds something different and helpful to a group, they all ______ to its success.
當每個人為團體增添不同且有益的東西時，他們都______了它的成功。
contribute 貢獻 ✓ — To contribute means to give or add something — your time, skills, or effort — to help a shared goal. 貢獻意指給予或增添某事物——你的時間、技能或努力——以幫助共同的目標。
