Welcome, Teacher Gao! 🎉歡迎高靖雅老師加入新民大家庭

Hsinmin Elementary is delighted to welcome another wonderful new member to our family — Ms. Gao Jing-Ya (高靖雅老師)! With her solid educational background and a passion for innovative teaching, Ms. Gao is ready to bring fresh energy, creativity, and warmth to our classrooms.

About Ms. Gao 高靖雅老師

🎓Graduated from National Taitung University, Department of Education
台東大學教育學系
📋Completed student teaching at Liren Elementary School, Taichung City
台中市立人國小教育實習
🤝Skilled in cooperative learning — guiding students to think, discuss, and share ideas together
擅長合作學習，引導孩子思考與分享
💻Combines digital technology with teaching to improve learning efficiency and keep classes lively and interactive
結合數位科技提升學習效率，讓課堂充滿互動與活力

We are so excited to have Ms. Gao on our team. Her enthusiasm for education, paired with her modern teaching approach, will create a classroom environment where every child loves to learn. Welcome to Hsinmin, Ms. Gao!

💚💛💜 Welcome! 💜💛💚

Words About Learning & Teaching英語學習角 · 和學習與教學有關的小單字

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
cooperative(adj.)
合作的；共同參與的
Cooperative learning helps students build teamwork skills and learn from each other.
合作學習幫助學生建立團隊合作能力，並互相學習。
2
interactive(adj.)
互動的；有雙向交流的
An interactive class is more fun because everyone gets to participate and share ideas.
互動式課堂更有趣，因為每個人都能參與並分享想法。
3
digital(adj.)
數位的；電子的
Using digital tools in class makes it easier to look up information and create cool projects.
在課堂中使用數位工具讓查資料和做專題更加方便。
4
efficient(adj.)
有效率的；事半功倍的
An efficient study method helps you learn more in less time without feeling tired.
有效率的學習方法讓你花更少時間學到更多，而且不會覺得累。
5
guide(v.)
引導；指引
A great teacher guides students to find answers on their own instead of just giving them the answer.
好老師會引導學生自己找到答案，而不是直接告訴他們答案。
6
enthusiasm(n.)
熱忱；熱情
Ms. Gao's enthusiasm for teaching makes every student excited to come to school.
高老師對教學的熱忱讓每位學生都對上學充滿期待。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組，A 和 B 輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🏫 Scene · Two students talk about their new teacher on the first day of class.
情境：兩位同學在第一堂課後聊起新來的高老師。
A
I really liked Ms. Gao's class! She made us work in groups the whole time.
我好喜歡高老師的課！她讓我們一直都是分組學習。
B
Yes! That's cooperative learning. It's so much better than just listening to a teacher talk.
對！那就是合作學習。比光坐著聽老師講課好多了。
A
She also used a lot of digital tools. We watched a video and then answered questions on a tablet!
她也用了很多數位工具。我們看了影片，然後用平板回答問題！
B
That sounds so efficient! I finished my homework faster because the lesson was so clear.
聽起來好有效率！因為課講得很清楚，我作業也做得更快了。
A
Ms. Gao's enthusiasm is contagious! I actually want to study now.
高老師的熱忱真的會傳染！我現在竟然想讀書了。
B
Me too! She doesn't just teach — she guides us to think and discover things ourselves.
我也是！她不只是教書——她引導我們自己去思考和發現。
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.When students work together in groups to learn, that is called ______ learning.
當學生分組合作學習時，這叫做______學習。
cooperative 合作的 ✓ — Cooperative learning means students work together to reach a shared goal. 合作學習是指學生共同努力達成共同目標。
Q2.Which university did Ms. Gao graduate from?
高老師畢業於哪所大學？
National Taitung University 台東大學 ✓ — Ms. Gao graduated from National Taitung University, Department of Education. 高老師畢業於台東大學教育學系。
Q3.Technology that uses computers, tablets, and electronic devices is called ______ technology.
使用電腦、平板和電子設備的科技叫做______科技。
digital 數位的 ✓ — Digital technology refers to electronic tools and systems that process information as numbers. 數位科技是指以數字方式處理資訊的電子工具和系統。
Q4.A method that helps you get good results without wasting time is ______.
一種幫助你不浪費時間就能得到好結果的方法是______。
efficient 有效率的 ✓ — Efficient means achieving good results with the least amount of time or effort. 有效率的意思是用最少的時間或力氣達到好的結果。
Q5.Where did Ms. Gao complete her student teaching?
高老師在哪裡完成教育實習？
Liren Elementary, Taichung 台中市立人國小 ✓ — Ms. Gao completed her student teaching at Liren Elementary School in Taichung City. 高老師在台中市立人國小完成教育實習。
