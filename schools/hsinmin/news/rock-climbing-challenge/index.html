Reach Higher, Fear Nothing! 🧗挑戰自我，勇敢跨越｜攀岩體驗活動

On July 28th, Hsinmin Elementary students took on a challenge that tested more than just their muscles — it tested their courage. Visiting the rock climbing wall at Erh-shui Junior High School, guided by the school's principal and a team of professional coaches, every child geared up, looked up, and found a way to take that first step off the ground.

Before the climb, coaches carefully helped students into their harnesses, checking every strap with patience and care. Then came the warm-up — arms stretched high, bodies ready, spirits rising. For many, this was their very first time standing at the base of a real climbing wall. The nerves were real. So was the excitement.

One by one, the children reached for the next hold, placed their feet carefully, and kept climbing. Some paused halfway — and then kept going. Some looked down, took a breath, and then looked up again. When a climber struggled, the crowd didn't stay quiet — classmates, parents, and coaches all cheered louder. The wall was high, but the support was higher.

What these children achieved today isn't measured in meters. It's the courage to try something new, the will to overcome fear, and the quiet confidence that grows every time you reach one hold higher. Thank you to the principal and coaches of Erh-shui Junior High for their patient guidance, and to every parent who came to cheer — your voices carried all the way to the top. 🧗

中文原文 · Chinese

挑戰自我，勇敢跨越！新民的孩子們太棒了！

感謝二水國中校長與專業教練群的用心帶領與細心指導，帶領新民的孩子們踏上這場精彩又刺激的極限挑戰！面對高聳的攀岩牆，孩子們從一開始的緊張、猶豫，到彼此加油打氣、相互支持，最終勇敢跨出那一步，成功戰勝心中的恐懼，完成了一次次突破自我的壯舉！看到他們在攀登過程中展現出的堅毅眼神與自信笑容，真的讓人感到無比驕傲與感動。

特別感謝一路相伴、給予孩子們無限支持與鼓勵的各位家長們！有你們當最強大的後盾，孩子們才能無後顧之憂地勇往直前、挑戰極限。這不只是體能的鍛鍊，更是一場寶貴的勇氣試煉。相信這段難忘的冒險回憶，會在孩子們心中種下勇敢追夢的種子！

Words About Courage & Challenge英語學習角 · 勇氣與挑戰相關的小單字

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
climb(v.)
攀爬；攀登
To climb means to go up something using your hands and feet — like climbing a rock wall, a tree, or a mountain!
攀爬就是用手和腳往上爬，像爬岩牆、爬樹或爬山！
2
courage(n.)
勇氣
Courage is the strength to do something even when you are scared — like standing at the bottom of a tall climbing wall and deciding to go up anyway!
勇氣是即使害怕也能去做的力量，就像站在高大的攀岩牆下，決定無論如何都要爬上去！
3
harness(n.)
安全吊帶
A harness is a set of straps worn around your body to keep you safe during activities like rock climbing. It holds you if you slip!
安全吊帶是一組穿戴在身上的帶子，在攀岩等活動中保護你的安全。如果你滑落，它會抓住你！
4
overcome(v.)
克服；戰勝
To overcome means to successfully deal with a fear or challenge — every child who climbed that wall overcame something real today!
克服就是成功面對恐懼或挑戰。今天每一個爬上那面牆的孩子，都真正克服了心中的恐懼！
5
support(v./n.)
支持；鼓勵
To support someone means to cheer them on and help them feel strong — parents and classmates cheering from below gave every climber real support!
支持就是為別人加油，讓他們感到有力量。家長和同學在下方的歡呼聲，給了每位攀岩者真實的支持！
6
achieve(v.)
達成；成就
To achieve means to successfully reach a goal through effort — what these students achieved today isn't just climbing a wall; it's growing their courage!
達成就是透過努力成功實現目標。孩子們今天達成的不只是爬上一面牆，更是讓自己的勇氣成長！
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🧗 Scene · A student tells a parent about the rock climbing experience at Erh-shui Junior High.
情境：一個學生向家長講述在二水國中的攀岩體驗。
A
I went rock climbing today at Erh-shui Junior High! It was so exciting!
我今天去二水國中攀岩了！超刺激的！
B
Wow! How was it? Were you nervous when you first saw the wall?
哇！怎麼樣？你第一次看到攀岩牆時緊張嗎？
A
A little! But the coaches helped us put on our harnesses and explained everything. Then we did a warm-up and I felt better.
有一點！但教練幫我們穿上安全吊帶，把一切都解釋清楚。做完暖身後我就覺得好多了。
B
Did you manage to climb high? Did you reach the top?
你爬得高嗎？有到頂了嗎？
A
I climbed really high! It took courage, but my friends and parents were cheering — their support gave me strength.
我爬得很高！需要很大的勇氣，但我的朋友和家長都在歡呼，他們的支持給了我力量。
B
I'm so proud of you! You really overcame your fear and achieved something amazing today.
我好以你為榮！你真的克服了恐懼，今天達成了一件了不起的事。
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.The set of straps worn around your body for safety during rock climbing is called a ______.
攀岩時穿戴在身上、保護安全的那套帶子叫做______。
harness 安全吊帶 ✓ — A harness is the safety equipment that wraps around your body and keeps you from falling during activities like rock climbing. Always wear one before you climb! 安全吊帶是包覆身體的安全設備，在攀岩等活動中防止你墜落。攀爬前一定要穿戴好！
Q2.To go up something using your hands and feet is to ______.
用手和腳往上爬叫做______。
climb 攀爬 ✓ — When you climb, you use both your hands and feet to move upward. Rock climbing, tree climbing, and mountain hiking all involve climbing! 攀爬是同時用雙手和雙腳向上移動。攀岩、爬樹和登山都需要攀爬！
Q3.When you face a difficult challenge and succeed despite your fear, you ______ it.
當你面對困難的挑戰，克服恐懼而成功，你就______了它。
overcome 克服 ✓ — To overcome a challenge means you faced it directly and succeeded, even when it was hard or scary. Every child who climbed that wall overcame something real! 克服挑戰就是直接面對它並成功，即使過程困難或令人害怕。每個爬上那面牆的孩子都真正地克服了一件事！
Q4.When your friends and parents cheer for you and help you feel strong, they are giving you ______.
當你的朋友和家長為你加油，讓你感到有力量，他們正在給予你______。
support 支持 ✓ — Support means encouragement and help from the people around you. When someone supports you, they believe in you and cheer you on — that kind of support can make you feel unstoppable! 支持是來自周圍的人的鼓勵和幫助。當有人支持你，他們相信你、為你加油，這樣的支持能讓你感到無所不能！
Q5.When you reach a goal through effort and determination, you ______ it.
當你透過努力和決心達到一個目標，你就______了它。
achieve 達成 ✓ — When you achieve something, you have successfully reached your goal through hard work and determination. Today, these students achieved something that will stay with them for a long time! 當你達成某件事，代表你透過努力和決心成功實現了目標。今天，這些孩子達成了一件將長久留在心中的成就！
