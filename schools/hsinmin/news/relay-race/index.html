Anniversary Relay Race · One United Hsinmin Family校慶大隊接力．團結的新民大家庭

On Saturday, December 14, 2024, our school was full of energy as we held our anniversary relay race! Our fourth, fifth and sixth graders all lined up, ready to sprint and pass the baton as fast as they could. It was wonderful to see each class working together and cheering loudly for their teammates. The whole school turned out, cheering at the top of their voices and showing incredible team spirit!

This fun competition was all about teamwork and class unity. Everyone gave their best — not only to win the race, but to support their friends and make their class proud. It was a beautiful day that showed just how united and powerful our Hsinmin family is!

中文原文 · Chinese

在2024年12月14日星期六，我們學校充滿了活力，因為我們舉行了校慶大隊接力比賽！

我們四年級、五年級和六年級的同學們都排好隊，準備盡快衝刺並傳遞接力棒。看到每個班級一起努力，大聲為他們的隊友加油，真是太棒了！整個學校都到場了，大聲地鼓勵著，展現出不可思議的團隊精神！

這場有趣的比賽，重點就是團隊合作和班級向心力。每個人都盡了最大的努力，不僅是為了贏得比賽，更是為了支持他們的朋友，讓自己的班級感到驕傲。這真是美好的一天，展現了我們新民國小這個大家庭是多麼的團結和有力量！
