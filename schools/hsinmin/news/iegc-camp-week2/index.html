IE-GC Summer Camp: Week 2! 🌸IE-GC暑假夏令營｜第二週精彩回顧

Week 2 of IE-GC Summer Camp was full of colour, creativity, and discovery! Building on the animal and adjective foundation from Week 1, children this week explored a fascinating new question: what are baby animals called? They read, wrote sentences, created art, and launched paper airplanes — all in English.

One of the highlights was discovering that many animal babies have their own special names. A baby owl is called an owlet. A baby elephant — just like a baby cow — is called a calf. And a baby lion or panda is called a cub. Students wrote these facts into their own workbooks, turning vocabulary into real English sentences they could understand and remember.

Here's a look at the week's highlights:

🦉
Animals & Their Babies
認識動物寶寶的英文名稱：owl→owlet、cow&elephant→calf、lion&panda→cub
✏
Sentence Writing Practice
把單字寫成句子：A calf becomes an elephant. / A cub becomes a lion.（配上自己畫的插圖！）
🇹
Sailboat Mosaic Art with Kevin 老師
和外籍老師 Kevin 一起用撕紙拼貼技法，完成色彩繽紛的帆船馬賽克畫
✈
Paper Airplane Challenge
摺好紙飛機，走廊大賽開始！每個孩子都是小小工程師，調整、嘗試、再試一次
📚
Story Time with Gwen 老師
Gwen 老師說故事說到欲罷不能，孩子們一本接著一本，在英文故事中自然理解語言

In the art room, Kevin guided students in creating beautiful sailboat mosaic paintings — tearing coloured paper into tiny pieces and arranging them into scenes of sea, sky, and sunshine. Every painting was unique, and every child felt the pride of finishing something beautiful with their own hands.

Then came the paper airplane challenge. Students folded, adjusted, aimed — and launched. Each flight was a tiny experiment. They adjusted the wings, tried a different angle, and cheered each other on. Story time with Gwen was the perfect close to each day: one more book, then one more, then one more. That's how English grows — one story, one sentence, one confident word at a time. 🌸

IE-GC Summer Camp 第二週，我們延續第一週的動物與形容詞主題，帶孩子們進一步透過閱讀、書寫、遊戲與創作，慢慢把英文從單字變成可以理解、可以使用的句子。

本週孩子們開始閱讀更多動物相關故事，也學習動物與牠們孩子的英文說法：🦉 owl → owlet、🐄 cow → calf、🦁 lion → cub、🐘 elephant → calf。孩子們不只是認識單字，也嘗試將學到的內容寫成句子，練習用英文描述動物成長與變化。

藝術課中，孩子們和 Kevin 老師一起完成美麗的帆船撕貼馬賽克畫。透過色彩、形狀與拼貼，孩子們在創作中展現自己的想像力，也讓英語主題學習多了更多動手操作的樂趣。本週也進行了紙飛機大賽，孩子們在比賽中觀察、調整、再嘗試，看著自己的紙飛機飛出去的那一刻，每個小小工程師都超投入！而在 Gwen 老師的說故事時間，孩子們更是一本接著一本，聽到欲罷不能，不斷期待下一個故事。

English Learning Corner

Words About Animals, Art & Confidence英語學習角 · 動物、創作與自信相關的小單字

Six key words — tap 🔊 to listen, then try the conversation and quiz.
六個關鍵字，點 🔊 聽發音，再試試對話和小考。

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
owlet(n.)
貓頭鷹寶寶
An owlet is a baby owl — small, fluffy, and just beginning to learn how to see in the dark!
貓頭鷹寶寶叫 owlet，小小的、毛茸茸的，正開始學習在黑暗中看東西！
2
calf(n.)
幼崽（小牛、小象等）
A calf is a baby cow or a baby elephant — two very different animals, but the same word for their babies!
小牛和小象都叫 calf，兩種很不同的動物，卻用同一個字來稱呼牠們的寶寶！
3
describe(v.)
描述；形容
To describe means to use words to tell what something or someone is like — just like writing "a calf becomes an elephant!"
描述就是用文字說出某樣東西或某人是什麼樣子，就像寫「小象寶寶長大後變成大象」！
4
create(v.)
創作；創造
To create means to make something new using your imagination — like a mosaic sailboat painting that only you could make!
創作就是運用想像力做出新的東西，就像一幅只有你才能做出來的帆船馬賽克畫！
5
launch(v.)
發射；投擲出去
To launch something means to send it into the air or into motion — like launching a paper airplane down the hallway!
Launch 就是把東西送入空中或讓它動起來，就像把紙飛機投射出去，看它飛過走廊！
6
confident(adj.)
自信的
When you feel confident, you believe in yourself and aren't afraid to try — every week at camp, the children grow a little more confident!
當你感到自信，你相信自己，不害怕嘗試。在夏令營的每一週，孩子們都變得更有自信了！

3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.A baby owl is called an ______.
貓頭鷹的寶寶叫做______。
owlet 貓頭鷹寶寶 ✓ — An owlet is the special name for a baby owl. Many animals have their own special word for their babies — did you know a baby panda or lion is also called a cub? 貓頭鷹寶寶有自己專屬的名稱叫 owlet。許多動物都有自己寶寶的專屬稱呼，你知道熊貓寶寶和獅子寶寶也都叫 cub 嗎？
Q2.Which two animals share the same word for their babies — "calf"?
哪兩種動物的寶寶都叫「calf」？
cow & elephant 牛和大象 ✓ — Both a baby cow and a baby elephant are called a calf! English can be surprising — two very different animals, but the same word for their young. 小牛和小象的寶寶都叫 calf！英文有時很有趣，兩種截然不同的動物，寶寶卻用同一個字稱呼。
Q3.When you use words to tell what something or someone is like, you are ______ it.
當你用文字說出某樣東西或某人是什麼樣子時，你正在______它。
describing 描述 ✓ — To describe means to use words to paint a picture of something. When you write "A calf becomes an elephant," you are describing the animal's growth! 描述就是用文字呈現某樣東西的樣子。當你寫下「小象寶寶長大後變成大象」時，你就是在描述這個動物的成長！
Q4.To ______ means to make something new using your imagination — like a painting or a story.
______的意思是運用想像力創造新事物，例如一幅畫或一個故事。
create 創作 ✓ — When you create, you bring something new into the world from your own imagination. Every mosaic painting the students made was unique — a one-of-a-kind creation! 當你創作時，你從自己的想像中帶出一個全新的東西。每個孩子做的馬賽克畫都是獨一無二的創作！
Q5.When you believe in yourself and are not afraid to try new things, you feel ______.
當你相信自己、不怕嘗試新事物時，你感到______。
confident 自信的 ✓ — Confidence grows when you keep trying and learning. Every week at camp, every sentence written, every paper airplane launched — these small steps build big confidence! 自信隨著不斷嘗試和學習而成長。夏令營的每一週、每一個寫下的句子、每一架投出去的紙飛機，這些小小的步驟都在累積大大的自信！
