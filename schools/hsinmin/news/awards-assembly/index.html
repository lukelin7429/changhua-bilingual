Morning Assembly Awards · Roll of Honor頒獎頒獎強強滾｜兒童朝會獲獎榮譽榜

At today's children's morning assembly in the auditorium, wave after wave of applause rang out. Let's look back at the children's shining moments!

Among the honors: the mid-term exam merit list for Grades 4–6; the list of contributors to issue 165 of the school magazine; and the winners of the 2025 read-the-news creativity contest.

Congratulations to Chou Yin-chieh (Class 401), awarded honorable mentions in both the 20th Magistrate's Cup and the Rulin calligraphy competitions; and to Cheng Liang-yu (Class 602) and Huang Ruo-yan (Class 603), awarded distinction in the upper-grade Taiwanese group of the 2025 mother-tongue picture-book contest.

In the Tianzhong JCI 'Little Orators' speech and youth traffic-safety contest, Hsieh Ming-yan (Class 601) took first place and Hung Yu-chin (Class 503) was a winner. In karate, archery and taekwondo, our students also brought home county and city honors — including Hsu Cheng-fan (Class 501), who won the championship at the 2026 Taichung City Cup Formosa International Open.

Congratulations again to all our award-winning students — we look forward to even more wonderful things from you! The full honor roll is listed in Chinese below.
