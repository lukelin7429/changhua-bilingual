Every Effort Brings Us Closer! 🌿第16屆三好校園實踐學校複審

On July 30, 2026, Hsinmin Elementary School took a meaningful step in its journey of character education — participating in the 16th Three Virtues Campus (三好校園) Review.

Principal Huang Huai-Hsuan and Director Guan-Yu traveled to the Fo Guang Shan Taipei Branch (佛光山北道場) for the professional review session. Before the trip, Director Cai-Jun dedicated enormous care to preparing the school's presentation — gathering evidence of Hsinmin's commitment to the Three Virtues: doing good deeds, speaking good words, and keeping a good heart (做好事、說好話、存好心).

The review panel listened carefully and responded with high praise and encouragement. Their kind words reminded everyone that each quiet moment of effort — a teacher's preparation, a student's act of kindness, a parent's support — truly adds up.

This is the second consecutive year Hsinmin has presented for the Three Virtues review, and the warm recognition from the committee fills our whole school community with pride and renewed dedication. A school of goodness begins with each one of us. 🌿🌿🌿

【第16屆三好校園實踐學校複審】🌱🌱🌱

每一份努力，都讓新民更靠近理想的教育。

2026年7月30日，黃懷萱校長偕同冠妤主任，遠赴佛光山北道場，出席「第16屆三好校園實踐學校複審」專業對話。

在這之前，采筠主任花費大量心力備妥學校的簡報資料，將新民國小推動「做好事、說好話、存好心」三好精神的豐富成果，一一呈現於委員面前。

複審過程中，委員們給予高度肯定，讓參與的每一位都備受鼓舞。這是新民連續第二年接受三好校園複審，全體師生家長共同努力的成果，在佛光山道場的這一天，得到了最美的回應。🌱

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
virtue(n.)
美德；品德
A virtue is a good quality or habit that makes a person kind, honest, and caring. The Three Virtues are doing good deeds, speaking good words, and keeping a good heart.
美德是讓一個人善良、誠實、有愛心的好品質或好習慣。三好的美德是：做好事、說好話、存好心。
2
review(n./v.)
審查；複審；回顧
A review is when experts look carefully at something to check its quality. The Three Virtues review invited experts to see how well Hsinmin practices good values every day.
複審是專家仔細審視某件事情品質的過程。三好複審邀請專家來了解新民每天如何實踐三好精神。
3
dedication(n.)
投入；奉獻；盡心盡力
Dedication means working hard and giving your full effort to something you care about. Director Cai-Jun's dedication in preparing the presentation showed everyone how much Hsinmin cares.
投入是指努力工作，全力以赴地做你在乎的事。采筠主任在準備簡報上的投入，讓大家看到新民有多用心。
4
commend(v.)
讚許；稱讚；推薦
To commend someone means to praise them for doing something well. The review committee commended Hsinmin for its wonderful efforts in building a school of goodness.
讚許某人就是稱讚他們某件事做得很好。複審委員讚許新民在建立三好校園上的卓越努力。
5
present(v.)
呈現；報告；發表
To present means to show or explain something to an audience. The school presented its Three Virtues work to the review panel, sharing stories of good deeds from every corner of campus.
呈現就是向聽眾展示或說明某件事。學校向複審委員呈現三好成果，分享校園各個角落的好人好事。
6
character(n.)
品格；品德；個性
Character is the combination of values and habits that shape who a person truly is. Building good character means practicing kindness, honesty, and care every single day.
品格是塑造一個人真正樣貌的價值觀與習慣的總和。培養好品格，就是每天練習善良、誠實和關懷。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🌿 Scene · Two students talk about their school's Three Virtues review.
情境：兩個學生聊到學校的三好校園複審。
A
I heard our school passed the Three Virtues review! What does that mean exactly?
我聽說我們學校通過三好校園複審了！這是什麼意思？
B
It means experts came to check if our school really practices good values every day — like doing good deeds and speaking kind words.
就是說有專家來確認我們學校是不是真的每天實踐三好，像是做好事、說好話。
A
That's amazing! Did the review committee commend our school?
好棒！複審委員有讚許我們學校嗎？
B
Yes! They gave us very high praise. It shows that our school's character education is really working.
有！他們給予高度肯定，這說明我們學校的品格教育確實有成效。
A
I want to help too! What can I do to practice the Three Virtues?
我也想參與！我可以怎麼實踐三好呢？
B
Just show kindness every day — help a friend, say something encouraging, and keep a good heart. That's all it takes!
每天展現善意就好了——幫助朋友、說鼓勵的話、存好心。就這麼簡單！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.A good quality that makes a person kind, honest, and caring is called a ______.
讓一個人善良、誠實、有愛心的好品質叫做______。
virtue 美德 ✓ — A virtue is a positive quality that shapes how we treat others. The Three Virtues — good deeds, good words, good heart — are the core values Hsinmin works to build every day. 美德是塑造我們對待他人方式的正面品質。三好——做好事、說好話、存好心——是新民每天努力培養的核心價值。
Q2.When experts carefully examine something to check its quality, it is called a ______.
當專家仔細審視某件事的品質時，這個過程叫做______。
review 複審 ✓ — A review involves careful examination and evaluation. In the Three Virtues review, the committee checked how deeply Hsinmin's school community lives out these good values every day. 複審涉及仔細的審視與評估。在三好複審中，委員們了解新民校園師生每天實踐這些美德的深度。
Q3.Working hard and giving your full effort to something you care about is called ______.
努力工作、全力以赴地做你在乎的事叫做______。
dedication 投入、奉獻 ✓ — Dedication means putting your whole heart into what you do. Director Cai-Jun's dedication in preparing the school's presentation is a perfect example of what the Three Virtues look like in real life. 投入是指全心全意去做你所做的事。采筠主任在準備學校簡報上的奉獻，正是三好精神在現實生活中的最佳體現。
Q4.To praise someone formally for doing something well is to ______ them.
正式讚許某人某件事做得很好，叫做______他們。
commend 讚許 ✓ — To commend is to offer praise or approval. Receiving words of commendation from the review committee is a wonderful recognition of everyone's hard work at Hsinmin. 讚許就是給予稱讚或肯定。得到複審委員的讚許，是對新民全體師生辛勤付出最美好的肯定。
Q5.The combination of values and habits that makes a person who they truly are is called ______.
塑造一個人真正樣貌的價值觀與習慣的總和叫做______。
character 品格 ✓ — Character is built through daily choices and habits. At Hsinmin, every kind word, every helpful action, and every honest moment is a small brick in building a school of great character. 品格是透過每天的選擇和習慣培養出來的。在新民，每一句好話、每一個助人的行動、每一個誠實的時刻，都是建立卓越品格學校的一塊小磚。
