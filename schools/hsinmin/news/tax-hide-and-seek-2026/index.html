Tax Hide-and-Seek: Play Online and Win a Toy Model! 🎁🐱稅務躲貓貓大冒險！闖關就送限量玩具模型

彰化縣地方稅務局「稅務躲貓貓」活動海報

Are you ready, kids? The Changhua County Local Tax Bureau (彰化縣地方稅務局) has launched “Tax Hide-and-Seek” (稅務躲貓貓), an online interactive game! 🐱🔍

Play the game and learn fun facts about taxes at the same time. The first 600 students who finish the challenge and fill in the form will get a cool tax toy model! Prizes are limited, so invite your parents and classmates to play together!

Event Info
活動資訊
  • 📅
    Dates — from now until December 15, 2026即日起至 115 年 12 月 15 日止
  • 🔗
    Website — changtax-hidenseek.twevent.live活動網址
  • 👥
    Who can join — Student Group: elementary and junior high students in Changhua County; Public Group: everyone學生組：彰化縣國中小學生；社會組：一般民眾
  • 🏆
    Prizes — Student Group: a tax toy model (first 600); Public Group: an NT$100 gift voucher (first 800)學生組：稅務玩具模型（前600名）；社會組：100元商品禮券（前800名）
  • 📢
    Winners announced — December 29, 2026, on the Tax Bureau’s website and Facebook page115 年 12 月 29 日於稅務局官網及 FB 公告
