Welcome, Teacher Yang! 💙歡迎加入新民國小，一起遇見更好的自己

英語增置教師 楊湘琪老師 · 在新民，遇見更好的自己

A new semester brings new partners — and a fresh burst of energy for English learning at Hsinmin! 💪🌟 Please join us in warmly welcoming Ms. Hsiang Chi Yang (楊湘琪), our newly added English teacher, to the Hsinmin family! 🎉👏

👩‍🏫 Teacher Yang brings rich experience teaching English at the elementary level. For years she has planned English activities, co-taught with foreign teachers, and coached students for English contests — and she is especially good at using lively, playful approaches that get children speaking English naturally in everyday life. 🗣️💬

  • 👥

🌍 Our hope is that English won't live only inside textbooks 📚, but will step into children's daily lives. From one brave moment of speaking up, to one fun activity, to every chance to connect with the world, children will steadily build their confidence and widen their view of the world.

More confident English · Wider horizons · A brighter future更自信的英語力．更寬廣的視野．更精彩的未來
