New Voices Join the Choir!美妙歌聲續航｜合唱培訓團新血注入

The music journey at Hsinmin Elementary never stops! Although the sixth-grade choir members have graduated and moved on, a new chapter is beginning. This year, the school successfully applied for the national "Arts and Aesthetics — Choral Education Promotion Plan," and a brand-new Choir Training Team will be formed at the start of the next school year.

The Training Team will meet every Tuesday morning during the school's morning activity time, filling the campus with beautiful music. Auditions for this new team were held on June 29, with a total of 13 enthusiastic students taking part — including students from Grades 2 to 4, and even two first-graders who couldn't wait to try. Their courage and passion for music was truly moving!

The teachers were so proud to see every student step up with confidence and give their very best on the spot. We look forward to seeing these young singers grow and shine on stage in the new school year. Keep working hard — we believe you will amaze everyone! 🎵🌟

中文原文 · Chinese

🎶 美妙歌聲續航！新民合唱培訓團新血注入 🎶

114學年結束，合唱團的六年級學長姐畢業了，但我們的音樂旅程從不停歇！🎒✨今年學校成功申請了「藝術與美感深耕-合唱教育推動計畫」，新的學年度除了高年級團員將全力備戰彰化縣學生音樂比賽，我們更將於新學期成立——【新民合唱培訓團】！未來在每週二的晨光時間，用歌聲喚醒美好的校園早晨。☀️🎵

這次的培訓團甄選，總共有13位同學熱情參與。現場除了2~4年級的同學，還有兩位1年級的小學弟妹也迫不及待前來挑戰，勇敢追尋音樂夢想的態度讓人超級感動！❤️老師肯定大家在台上努力表現自我的自信模樣。🌟

期待開學後，孩子們也能認真投入每一堂課，只要努力學習，相信你們一定會看到驚人的改變！希望很快的，就能在舞台上聽到你們展現自我、散發光芒的美麗歌聲！🎙️💖

2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組，A 和 B 輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🎭 Scene · Two students talk about the choir training team audition.
情境：兩位同學聊到合唱培訓團的甄選。
A
Did you go to the choir training team audition yesterday?
你昨天去參加合唱培訓團的甄選了嗎？
B
Yes! I was so nervous but I tried my best. Thirteen of us auditioned!
有啊！我很緊張，但我盡力表現。我們共有十三位同學參加甄選！
A
I heard even some first-graders came. That takes a lot of courage!
我聽說連一年級的同學也來了。那需要很大的勇氣！
B
Yes! Their passion for music was amazing. The teacher said everyone was enthusiastic.
對啊！他們對音樂的熱情令人讚嘆。老師說大家都非常熱情。
A
I hope you get in! If you practice hard, I know you will improve a lot.
希望你能入選！如果你努力練習，我相信你會進步很多。
B
Thank you! I will work hard and show everyone my confidence on stage one day.
謝謝你！我會努力練習，有一天在舞台上向大家展示我的自信。
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.A tryout or test for a performance group is called an ______.
為了加入表演團體而進行的試演或測試，叫做______。
audition 甄選 ✓ — An audition is a tryout where you perform to show your skills for a role or group. 甄選是你表演以展示自己技能的試演活動。
Q2.How many students participated in the choir training team audition?
有多少位同學參加了合唱培訓團的甄選？
13 ✓ — A total of 13 enthusiastic students participated in the audition. 共有13位熱情的同學參加了甄選。
Q3.Strong belief in your own ability to succeed is called ______.
對自己成功能力的強烈信念叫做______。
confidence 自信 ✓ — Confidence is a strong feeling of trust in your own abilities. 自信是對自己能力的強烈信任感。
Q4.When will the Choir Training Team meet each week?
合唱培訓團每週什麼時候練習？
Tuesday morning 週二早晨 ✓ — The team will meet every Tuesday morning during the school morning activity time. 培訓團在每週二早晨的晨光時間練習。
Q5.To get better at something through effort and practice is to ______ it.
透過努力和練習讓某件事越做越好，就是______。
improve 進步 ✓ — To improve means to get better at something through practice and effort. 進步 (improve) 是指透過練習和努力讓自己越做越好。
