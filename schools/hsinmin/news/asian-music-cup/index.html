Strings of Excellence! 🎻🏆新民弦樂團．亞洲盃國際音樂大賽 榮獲佳績

Every bow stroke tells a story of practice and passion — and this summer, that story turned into a spotlight moment! Congratulations to the young musicians of the Hsinmin Strings Ensemble for their outstanding results at the Asian Cup International Music Competition! 🏆

Yunlin–Chiayi Region · 雲嘉地區

  • Violin, Senior Group — 3rd Place｜小提琴高年級組 第三名

Taichung Region · 台中地區

  • Violin, Senior Group — 1st Place｜小提琴高年級組 第一名
  • Violin, Intermediate Group — 3rd Place｜小提琴中年級組 第三名
  • Viola, Intermediate Group — 1st Place｜中提琴中年級組 第一名

Behind every certificate and every trophy lies countless hours of rehearsal. While other children were relaxing and playing, these young musicians picked up their bows again and again, sharpening their technique and building their skills, all so they could keep improving.

It takes courage to step onto the stage, and it takes even more persistence to keep going. These students turned their everyday dedication into beautiful melodies, proving that steady effort always pays off — and that every bit of hard work becomes the nourishment for a dream.

Thank you to every brave young dreamer, and to the teachers and parents who supported them along the way. May you keep your love of music close, grow stronger with every performance, and shine with confidence on every stage ahead! 🎼

中文原文 · Chinese

努力，終將化作舞台上最耀眼的光芒。

恭喜新民國小弦樂團的孩子們，在亞洲盃國際音樂大賽中榮獲佳績！

雲嘉地區：小提琴高年級組 第三名

台中地區：小提琴高年級組 第一名；小提琴中年級組 第三名；中提琴中年級組 第一名

每一張獎狀、每一座獎盃的背後，都藏著無數次反覆練習的汗水。當同學們享受休息、玩樂的時光時，他們選擇拿起琴弓，一遍又一遍地磨練技巧，累積實力，只為了讓自己更進步。

站上舞台需要勇氣，持續堅持更需要毅力。孩子們用專注與努力，將平日的付出化為悠揚動人的旋律，也用行動證明：只要願意踏實耕耘，每一步都會成為夢想的養分，每一次努力都將迎來美好的果實。

謝謝每一位勇敢追夢的孩子，也感謝一路陪伴與支持的老師及家長。願你們繼續帶著對音樂的熱愛，在每一次演奏中累積能力，在每一個舞台上自信發光，奏出屬於自己的精彩人生！

English Learning Corner

Words About Music & Practice英語學習角 · 和音樂與練習有關的小單字

Six key words — tap 🔊 to listen, then try the conversation and quiz.
六個關鍵字，點 🔊 聽發音，再試試對話和小考。

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
rehearse(v.)
排練；反覆練習
To rehearse means to practice something again and again before a real performance. The strings players rehearsed for months before the competition!
排練就是在正式演出前反覆練習。弦樂團的孩子們在比賽前排練了好幾個月！
2
melody(n.)
旋律；曲調
A melody is a series of musical notes that sound pleasing together. Every violin and viola on stage played a beautiful melody.
旋律是一連串聽起來悅耳動人的音符。舞台上每一把小提琴和中提琴都演奏出美麗的旋律。
3
technique(n.)
技巧；技術
Technique is the skillful way you use your hands and bow to play an instrument well. Good technique takes years of patient practice to build.
技巧是熟練運用雙手與琴弓演奏樂器的方式。好的技巧需要多年耐心練習才能養成。
4
dedication(n.)
專注投入；奉獻精神
Dedication means giving your time and effort fully to something you care about. It takes real dedication to practice every single day.
專注投入就是把時間和努力全心放在自己在乎的事情上。每天不間斷地練習需要真正的專注投入。
5
perform(v.)
演奏；表演
To perform means to play music or act in front of an audience. Standing on stage to perform takes both skill and courage.
表演就是在觀眾面前演奏音樂或演出。站上舞台表演，需要技巧，也需要勇氣。
6
achievement(n.)
成就；成果
An achievement is something great you accomplish through hard work. Winning first place at an international competition is a wonderful achievement!
成就是透過努力所完成的了不起的事情。在國際大賽中獲得第一名是一項很棒的成就！

3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.To practice something again and again before a real show is to ______.
在正式演出前反覆練習，叫做______。
rehearse 排練 ✓ — To rehearse means to keep practicing until every note feels natural. The strings ensemble rehearsed countless times before their award-winning performance. 排練就是不斷練習，直到每個音符都變得自然。弦樂團在獲獎演出前排練了無數次。
Q2.A series of musical notes that sound pleasing together is called a ______.
一連串聽起來悅耳動人的音符叫做______。
melody 旋律 ✓ — A melody is the tune you remember and hum after a song ends. Every violin on stage helped create one unforgettable melody. 旋律是歌曲結束後你還記得、還能哼唱的曲調。舞台上每一把小提琴都共同創造出一段令人難忘的旋律。
Q3.The skillful way you use your hands to play an instrument well is your ______.
熟練運用雙手演奏樂器的方式叫做你的______。
technique 技巧 ✓ — Technique is built through years of careful, patient practice. Strong technique is exactly what helped these young musicians shine on the international stage. 技巧是經過多年細心耐心的練習所養成的。正是紮實的技巧，讓這些年輕音樂家在國際舞台上發光發熱。
Q4.Giving your time and effort fully to something you care about shows ______.
把時間和努力全心投入在自己在乎的事情上，展現出______。
dedication 專注投入 ✓ — Dedication means choosing to practice even when it would be easier to rest and play. That daily dedication is what turned into golden trophies. 專注投入就是選擇練習，即使休息玩樂會更輕鬆。正是這份日復一日的投入，最終化為了金色的獎盃。
Q5.To play music in front of an audience is to ______.
在觀眾面前演奏音樂，叫做______。
perform 演奏 ✓ — To perform takes both skill and courage — standing on stage and sharing your music with everyone watching. Every award winner had to perform their very best on competition day. 演奏需要技巧，也需要勇氣——站上舞台，在眾人面前分享自己的音樂。每位得獎者在比賽當天都必須拿出最好的演出。
