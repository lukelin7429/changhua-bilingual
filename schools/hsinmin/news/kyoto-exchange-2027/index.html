116年日本京都國際教育交流暨走讀學習計畫 · 走出教室，走進世界

Learning doesn't only happen in the classroom — it can begin in every corner of the world! 🌍

Hsinmin Elementary is teaming up with Zhongxiao Elementary (忠孝國小) on the 2027 Kyoto, Japan International Education Exchange and Study Tour. Students will step out of Taiwan and into Kyoto, learning through on-site exploration, cultural experiences, and international exchange — the start of a very different kind of learning journey.
