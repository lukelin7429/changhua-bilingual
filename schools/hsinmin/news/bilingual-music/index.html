Bilingual Music Showcase Concert雙語成果音樂發表會｜溫馨五月、感恩樂音

A warm May, with grateful music drifting through the air — Hsinmin's Bilingual Music Showcase Concert came to a wonderful close. This was a long-prepared feast: teachers began selecting the repertoire and guiding rehearsals last semester, and through the hard work of every fourth- and fifth-grader, the most brilliant results finally bloomed today.

At Hsinmin, children take part in music performances from the second grade. Today's fourth- and fifth-graders had each stepped on stage at least three times before, yet seeing their beloved families in the audience, a flicker of nervousness still showed in their eyes — because they wanted to show their very best to the people they love most.

It is precisely this step-by-step accumulation that let our young hosts perform with such poise and steady stage presence; in their every gesture shone the confidence and grace of Hsinmin students.

Today's programme was a small musical journey around the world: American and Korean folk songs, classic Japanese cartoon themes, gentle Mandarin and Taiwanese numbers, fluent English songs, and powerful Indigenous-language pieces. Compared with last week's rehearsal, the children were livelier and more dynamic, brimming with rhythm.

The most moving picture of all was the gaze of those watching. In the final mass chorus, the parents, grandparents and homeroom teachers in the audience almost all raised their phones, afraid to miss a single shining moment of their treasures. That focus and love was the most touching scene in the hall.

Thank you to every child who worked so hard. Dear fourth- and fifth-graders — thank you for your perseverance and effort. Together we completed a perfect expression of ourselves. Hsinmin is proud of you!

溫馨五月，感恩樂音飄揚 🎶

✨ 新民國小「雙語成果音樂發表會」圓滿落幕 ✨

這是一場籌備已久的音樂盛宴！為了今天的演出，老師們從上學期就開始精心挑選曲目、督促練習；經過四、五年級每位同學的努力，我們終於在今日綻放最燦爛的成果！💪✨

🌿 從青澀到自信的成長之路

在新民國小，孩子們從二年級起就會參與音樂發表。今天上場的四、五年級同學，雖然都已有至少三次的登台經驗，但看到心愛的家人坐在台下，眼神中仍透著些許緊張——那是因為「想把最好的一面，展現給最愛的家人看」。❤️

但也正是因為這一步一腳印的累積，我們看到小主持人們表現得落落大方、台風穩健，孩子們舉手投足間充滿了新民學子的自信與風采！🎤🌟

相較於上週彩排，孩子們今天的表現更加活潑、充滿動感，律動感十足！💃🕺

📸 最動人的畫面，是您注視的目光

真心感謝今日到場為孩子加油的家長們。在最後大合唱的時刻，看到觀眾席上的爸爸、媽媽、阿公、阿嬤，連同各班導師，大家幾乎都舉起了手機，深怕錯過寶貝們任何一個發光的瞬間。那份專注與愛，是現場最令人動容的風景。🥰📱

💖 謝謝每一位努力的孩子

親愛的四五年級同學們，謝謝你們的堅持與努力，我們共同完成了一場完美的自我展現。新民以你們為榮！👏🌈
