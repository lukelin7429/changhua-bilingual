Words About Teaching & Growth英語學習角 · 和教學與成長有關的小單字

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
experience(n.)
經驗；經歷
Experience means the knowledge you gain from doing something for a long time. Teacher Chang has eight years of teaching experience!
經驗是指長時間做某件事所累積的知識。凱棉老師擁有八年的教學經驗！
2
continue(v.)
繼續；持續
To continue means to keep doing something without stopping. Teacher Chang continues to study even while she teaches.
繼續是指不停止地做某件事。凱棉老師即使在教書，也持續進修。
3
bilingual(adj.)
雙語的；能說兩種語言的
A bilingual teacher can explain things in both Chinese and English, making it easier for students to understand.
雙語老師能用中文和英文解釋，讓學生更容易理解。
4
dedication(n.)
專注投入；奉獻精神
Dedication means giving your full effort to something you care about, like teaching students well.
專注投入就是把全部心力放在自己在乎的事情上，例如把學生教好。
5
welcome(v./n.)
歡迎
To welcome someone means to greet them warmly when they join a new place. All the students welcome Teacher Chang!
歡迎某人是指在他們加入新地方時熱情地迎接他們。全校同學都歡迎凱棉老師！
6
inspire(v.)
啟發；激勵
A great teacher can inspire students to love learning and believe in their own abilities.
好老師能啟發學生愛上學習，並相信自己的能力。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
✨ Scene · Two students talk about their new English teacher.
情境：兩位同學聊起新來的英語老師。
A
Have you met our new English teacher, Teacher Chang?
你見過我們新來的英語老師張老師了嗎？
B
Yes! She has a lot of experience, and she's still studying to become an even better teacher.
有！她很有經驗，而且她還在繼續進修，想成為更好的老師。
A
Wow, that's real dedication. What did she teach before coming to Hsinmin?
哇，這真的很有奉獻精神。她來新民之前教過什麼呢？
B
She taught English at Tianjhong Elementary and at a cram school, so she really understands bilingual teaching.
她在田中國小和補習班都教過英語，所以她很懂雙語教學。
A
I bet she'll inspire us to enjoy learning English even more!
我猜她一定會激勵我們更喜歡學英語！
B
Let's welcome her to Hsinmin!
我們一起歡迎她加入新民吧！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.The knowledge you gain from doing something for a long time is called ______.
長時間做某件事所累積的知識叫做______。
experience 經驗 ✓ — Experience is built up over years of practice. Teacher Chang's years in the classroom gave her rich teaching experience. 經驗是經過多年實踐累積而來的。凱棉老師多年的教學生涯讓她累積了豐富的教學經驗。
Q2.To keep doing something without stopping is to ______.
不停止地做某件事，叫做______。
continue 繼續 ✓ — Teacher Chang continues her own studies even while teaching full-time — that's real commitment! 凱棉老師即使全職教書，也持續進修——這是真正的用心！
Q3.A teacher who can explain things in Chinese and English is a ______ teacher.
能用中文和英文解釋的老師是______老師。
bilingual 雙語的 ✓ — Bilingual teachers help students connect ideas in two languages. Teacher Chang is deepening her bilingual teaching skills at graduate school. 雙語老師幫助學生用兩種語言連結概念。凱棉老師正在研究所深化她的雙語教學能力。
Q4.Giving your full effort to something you care about shows ______.
把全部心力放在在乎的事情上，展現出______。
dedication 專注投入 ✓ — Dedication means choosing to keep growing and improving, just like Teacher Chang balancing teaching and studying. 專注投入就是選擇不斷成長與進步，就像凱棉老師一邊教書一邊進修一樣。
Q5.When students greet a new teacher warmly, they ______ her.
當學生熱情迎接新老師時，他們在______她。
welcome 歡迎 ✓ — The whole school welcomes Teacher Chang with open arms! 全校同學張開雙臂歡迎凱棉老師！
