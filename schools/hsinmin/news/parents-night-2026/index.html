Meeting Under the Starlight 🌟星光為伴，親師相遇．新民國小班親會

Tonight, as dusk fell and the stars came out, every classroom at Hsinmin Elementary was already prepared. From children's learning and daily life to how each class runs, teachers had organized and planned with care — all so that this evening could bring a little more understanding, a little more exchange with families, and help everyone find the direction that fits each child best.

A class parents' night is not only teachers meeting families. It is the beginning of teachers and parents walking forward together.

What Tonight Was For · 今晚，我們一起做的事
  • 💬Two views of the same child. Teachers described what a child is like at school; parents shared what that child is like at home. Listening to and understanding one another, they talked over how to walk beside children through every small challenge in learning, friendships, and growing up.老師說說孩子在學校的模樣，家長分享孩子在家中的樣子；彼此傾聽與理解，也一起討論如何陪伴孩子面對學習、人際與成長中的每一個小小挑戰。
  • 🤝The start of trust. A parents' night is not just one gathering — it is the beginning of trust, built around a shared goal: to make a class that feels safe, warm, and full of growth.班親會，不只是一次相聚，更是一段信任的開始，這個共同的目標，是我們一起為孩子打造一個安心、溫暖、充滿成長的班級。

行政團隊逐班拜訪，導師與家長面對面交流

Tonight, PTA President Chen Chih-chiang led the way, bringing the directors of all four offices and our ETA teacher Judy to visit every classroom — greeting each family in person, and warmly inviting parents to join the Parents' Association. The association does more than help the school run events: it hopes to be a warm bridge between school and home, listening to parents' voices from different angles and supporting the school's work, so that children can learn with peace of mind and grow up happily surrounded by more people who care. 🌱

星光為伴，親師相遇－－新民國小班親會

💗 今晚夜幕低垂、星月陪伴，新民國小每一間教室裡，老師們早已準備就緒，從孩子的學習、生活到班級經營，細心整理、用心規劃，只為了今晚能與家長多一份認識、多一點交流，也一起為孩子的成長找出最適合的方向。班親會，不只是老師與家長的相見，更是親師攜手的開始。

💞 老師說說孩子在學校的模樣，家長分享孩子在家中的樣子；彼此傾聽與理解，也一起討論如何陪伴孩子面對學習、人際與成長中的每一個小小挑戰。班親會，不只是一次相聚，更是一段信任的開始，這個共同的目標，是我們一起為孩子打造一個安心、溫暖、充滿成長的班級。

📌 今晚由陳志強會長領軍，帶著四處室主任與 ETA 老師 Judy 到每一個班級拜訪，希望藉由這次機會，向每一位家長問候，也誠摯邀請家長加入家長會的行列。家長會不只是協助學校辦理活動，更希望成為學校與家庭之間溫暖的橋樑，從不同角度傾聽家長的聲音、支持學校的教育工作，讓孩子在更多人的關心與陪伴下安心學習、快樂成長。🌱
