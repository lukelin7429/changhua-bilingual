A Sock Full of Love, A Heart Full of Thanks 💖一雙孝親襪．一份感恩

A grandmother's smile, a grandfather's quiet nod, a child kneeling down to help slip a sock onto a loved one's foot — at home, Hsinmin's children turned a small filial-piety sock into a big, warm act of gratitude.

One photo at a time, one gentle moment at a time — these are the quiet, everyday ways grandparents care for their grandchildren, and the small ways children learn to say thank you back. A pair of socks on a loved one's feet, and a heart full of love.

與你相處的時光，是生命中最溫暖、最純粹的記憶。
謝謝你陪我長大。

Thank you to every grandparent who quietly gives and cares each day — because of you, children grow up feeling safe, and families grow stronger. May this love keep flowing through every home, and may every elder be treated with tenderness. 🌈

中文原文 · Chinese

一雙孝親襪，一份感恩

💗 一張張祖孫合照、一段段溫暖影片，看見的，是孩子的感恩，也是長輩無聲的愛。

謝謝爺爺奶奶、外公外婆，總在日常裡默默付出、用心守護。因為有你們的陪伴，孩子安心長大；因為有你們的守護，我們的家更加穩固。

願這份愛，常在家中流轉；願每一位長輩，都被溫柔以待。
