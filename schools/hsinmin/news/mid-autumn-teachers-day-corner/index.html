A Moon, a Barbecue, and Confucius 🌕👨‍🏫有月亮、有烤肉，還有一位不能缺席的「孔夫子」

The Mid-Autumn Festival is almost here! And quietly, a corner of the Hsinmin campus has turned into a scene bursting with holiday flavor. 🌕

A big bright moon hangs with the words 「中秋節快樂」. On the table sits a feast of barbecue — steak, shrimp, sausages, beef skewers — and ice-cold soda. Just looking at it, you can almost smell the grill! 😋

從月亮、月兔搗藥到烤肉桌，每個細節都藏著心意

But look closely — there's an important guest hiding in this scene: Confucius! 👨‍🏫 Because this year's Mid-Autumn holiday happens to fall together with Teachers' Day. So besides admiring the moon, gathering with family, and eating mooncakes, let's not forget this special day either — and say to the teachers who walk beside our children every day: Thank you, teacher! ❤️

Special thanks to volunteer mom Huang Yi-tzu and teacher Chen Chiu-yen for this creative Mid-Autumn corner — for their ideas and their handiwork. From the moon, the barbecue, and the drinks, to the adorable moon rabbit pounding its medicine, every small detail carries a lot of heart.

And Confucius, seated calmly among it all, seems to remind us: Mid-Autumn is a festival of reunion; Teachers' Day is a day of gratitude. And education is a group of people, carrying love, growing up alongside the children.

🌕 月圓人團圓
🥩 烤肉香滿園
👨‍🏫 感恩老師的每一天
❤️ 新民的校園，因為有大家而溫暖

Wishing everyone at Hsinmin, big and small, a very happy Mid-Autumn Festival — and to all our teachers, a happy Teachers' Day!

中文原文 · Chinese

有月亮、有烤肉，還有一位不能缺席的「孔夫子」😂

中秋節要到了！新民校園裡，也悄悄出現了一個超有「節味」的主題區。

一輪大大的明月，掛著「中秋節快樂」；桌上擺著滿滿的烤肉、蝦子、香腸、牛肉串，還有冰冰涼涼的汽水——光看照片，彷彿都聞得到烤肉香了！😋

但仔細看看，這個場景裡還藏著一位很重要的人物——👨‍🏫 孔夫子！因為今年的中秋連假，剛好也遇上了教師節。所以，除了賞月、團圓、吃月餅，我們也別忘了這個特別的日子——向每天陪伴孩子成長、用心教導孩子的老師們，說一聲：老師，謝謝您！❤️

這麼有創意的中秋主題區，要特別謝謝 #黃奕慈志工媽咪、#陳秋燕老師 的巧思與巧手。從月亮、烤肉、飲料，到可愛的月兔搗藥，一個個小細節，都藏著滿滿的心意。

而那位安坐其中的孔夫子，也彷彿在提醒我們：中秋，是團圓的節日；教師節，是感恩的日子。而教育，就是一群人帶著愛，陪著孩子一起長大。

🌕 月圓人團圓
🥩 烤肉香滿園
👨‍🏫 感恩老師的每一天
❤️ 新民的校園，因為有大家而溫暖。

祝福新民大小朋友：中秋佳節快樂，也祝所有老師——教師節快樂！
