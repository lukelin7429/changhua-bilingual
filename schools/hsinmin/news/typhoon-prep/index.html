Storm-Ready Before the Rain 🌧️風雨未至，守護先行｜新民國小防颱整備中

When a typhoon is on its way, Hsinmin Elementary is already in action. Long before the wind and rain arrive, our dedicated school grounds staff — led by the hardworking Brother Xi-Ping (錫平大哥) of the general affairs office — has been quietly and carefully preparing the campus to keep every student safe.

Since early morning, Brother Xi-Ping has been moving through every corner of the campus with his grass cutter. From overgrown weeds along the fences and slopes, to fallen leaves and debris blocking the drainage channels — every spot is tended with care, making sure water can flow freely and the environment stays tidy and safe.

"Disaster prevention doesn't start when the storm arrives — it starts every day, in every small, careful act."防災不是等到風雨來了才開始，而是平時就把每一個細節做到最好。

These may look like ordinary tasks. But every mowed patch of grass, every cleared drain, and every careful inspection can prevent flooding, falling branches, and mosquito breeding grounds. At Hsinmin, environmental education is not just a subject in the classroom — it is practiced every single day by the people who care for our campus.

Environmental safety is also an act of love: love for our students, love for our teachers, love for the community that gathers here each day.

❤ A heartfelt thank-you to our general affairs team.
❤ Special appreciation to Brother Xi-Ping, who faces the blazing sun every day — quietly, steadily, and with complete dedication — so that our children's school is a safer, more beautiful, and more peaceful place.

中文原文 · Chinese

🌿【風雨未至，守護先行｜新民國小防颱整備中】🌧️

颱風將至，新民國小的守護行動已經悄悄展開。校園裡，總務處的錫平大哥一早便揹起割草機，穿梭在校園每一個角落。從圍籬旁的雜草、邊坡植栽，到排水溝周邊的落葉與雜物，一步一步細心整理，只為了讓排水更順暢、環境更整潔，也讓孩子們擁有更安全的學習空間。

這些看似平凡的工作，其實都是守護校園的重要力量。因為每一次除草、每一次巡檢、每一次清理，都可能降低豪雨積水、樹枝掉落或病媒蚊孳生的風險。

在新民國小，防災不是等到風雨來了才開始，而是平時就把每一個細節做到最好；環境教育，也不只是課堂上的知識，更是每天身體力行的實踐。

❤ 感謝總務處團隊的用心付出。
❤ 特別謝謝錫平大哥不畏烈日、默默守護校園，因為有您們，孩子們的校園才能更加安全、美麗又安心。

Words About Safety & Preparation英語學習角 · 和安全與準備有關的小單字

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
typhoon(n.)
颱風
A typhoon is a powerful storm with strong winds and heavy rain that forms over the Pacific Ocean.
颱風是在太平洋上形成的強烈風暴，帶有強風和豪雨。
2
drainage(n.)
排水；排水系統
Good drainage helps rainwater flow away quickly so it does not flood the streets or the school.
良好的排水系統能讓雨水迅速排走，避免街道或學校淹水。
3
maintenance(n.)
維護；保養；維修
Regular maintenance of the school grounds keeps everyone safe and the campus looking beautiful.
定期的校園維護讓每個人都更安全，也讓校園保持美觀。
4
dedicate(v.)
奉獻；全心投入
Brother Xi-Ping dedicates himself to keeping the campus clean and safe for all the students and teachers.
錫平大哥全心奉獻，為所有學生和老師維護乾淨安全的校園。
5
prevent(v.)
預防；防止
Clearing leaves and debris from drains helps prevent flooding during a typhoon.
清除排水溝的落葉與雜物，有助於颱風期間預防淹水。
6
inspect(v.)
巡視；檢查
Before a typhoon, it is important to inspect the campus to find and fix any safety problems.
颱風來臨前，巡視校園以發現並修復安全問題是很重要的。

喔！如果排水被堵住，颱風時學校可能會淹水。

對。他真的全心奉獻，讓我們安全。我們應該謝謝他！

對！我不知道在問題發生前預防這麼重要。

對——這就是為什麼就算颱風很大，我們也能保持安全。有很棒的人在守護我們。
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.A powerful storm with strong winds and heavy rain is called a ______.
帶有強風和豪雨的強烈風暴叫做______。
typhoon 颱風 ✓ — A typhoon is a tropical cyclone that forms over the Pacific Ocean, common in Taiwan during summer. 颱風是在太平洋上形成的熱帶氣旋，台灣夏季常見。
Q2.Clearing the drains before a typhoon helps the ______ so the campus does not flood.
颱風前清理排水溝有助於______，讓校園不會淹水。
drainage 排水 ✓ — Good drainage channels carry rainwater away safely. Blocked drains lead to flooding. 良好的排水渠道能安全地將雨水排走。排水溝堵塞會導致淹水。
Q3.To look carefully at something to check if it is safe or working correctly is to ______ it.
仔細查看某物以確認其是否安全或正常運作，叫做______。
inspect 巡視；檢查 ✓ — To inspect is to examine something carefully, especially to check for problems. 巡視就是仔細檢查某物，尤其是為了找出問題。
Q4.The regular care and upkeep of buildings and grounds is called ______.
定期照顧和維護建築物與校園環境叫做______。
maintenance 維護 ✓ — Maintenance means taking care of something regularly so it stays in good condition. 維護是指定期照顧某物，使其保持良好狀態。
Q5.Which word best describes Brother Xi-Ping's attitude toward his work?
哪個詞最能描述錫平大哥對工作的態度？
dedicated 全心投入的 ✓ — Brother Xi-Ping shows great dedication — he works hard every day, even under the blazing sun, to protect the school. 錫平大哥表現出高度的奉獻精神——他每天努力工作，即使在烈日下，也要保護校園。
