Gold Again for Hsinmin! 🥇🥊金牌榮耀，再創佳績｜徐承範同學勇奪跆拳道金牌

🥇
Gold Medal · Sparring
115年彰化縣理事長盃全國跆拳道錦標賽
SY2026 Changhua County Chairman's Cup National Taekwondo Championship
國小高年級色帶男子組 58–68 kg · 第一名

Hsinmin Elementary is bursting with pride once more! Congratulations to Hsu Cheng-fan (601, 徐承範), who fought his way to Gold in the sparring event at the SY2026 Changhua County Chairman's Cup National Taekwondo Championship — competing in the Upper-Elementary Color-Belt Boys' 58–68 kg division.

On the mat he attacked bravely and stayed calm under pressure, meeting every opponent with real strength and a spirit that would not back down. Every strike and every step was built on months of steady, hard training.

徐承範同學與金牌、獎狀合影 · Cheng-fan with his gold medal and certificate

Cheng-fan showed the true athlete's spirit — persevere, be brave, and go beyond your own best. This honor is for himself, and for Hsinmin Elementary.

🎉 Congratulations, Cheng-fan! Water your dreams with sweat, and build your glory with effort. Every teacher and student at Hsinmin is cheering you on — this is just the beginning! 💪⭐

中文原文 · Chinese

新民國小賀喜！金牌榮耀，再創佳績！

恭喜本校 601 徐承範同學參加「115年彰化縣理事長盃全國跆拳道錦標賽」，在跆拳道對打比賽中奮勇出擊、沉著應戰，以堅強的實力與不屈不撓的精神，勇奪金牌！

每一次揮拳、每一個步伐，都是平日辛勤練習的累積；每一場比賽，都是突破自我的挑戰。承範用行動展現「堅持、勇敢、超越自己」的運動家精神，為自己也為新民國小爭取最高榮耀！

🎉 恭喜徐承範同學勇奪金牌！
💪 以汗水灌溉夢想，以努力成就榮耀！
🌟 新民國小全體師生與有榮焉，繼續為你加油喝采！
