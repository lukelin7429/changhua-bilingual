Hardworking kids shine the brightest, and every effort deserves recognition! 🌟

Today, Hsinmin’s campus was full of joy and applause. Our children worked hard in many different areas, bravely took on challenges, and turned their everyday effort into great results. For every honor, let’s say it loud: “You’re amazing!” 👏👏

每一份努力，都值得最熱烈的掌聲

From schoolwork and art to reading and English, our children showed their talents and hard work in many different ways. Their happy smiles and hard-earned honors are our school’s most precious reward! (Read our stories on the art competition, calligraphy finals, and reading report awards.)

👏 Congratulations again to all our winners! ❤️ Thank you to our teachers for their careful guidance, and to our parents for their support all along the way.
