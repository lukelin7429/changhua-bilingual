Setting Sail: Hsinmin Earns Its Tech-Education Certification ⛵✨揚帆啟航．新民再獲肯定｜通過彰化縣科技教育／揚帆學校認證

Congratulations to Hsinmin Elementary for passing Changhua County's Curriculum & Instruction — Technology Education / "Setting Sail" School certification! ✨

This certification is recognition of Hsinmin's long-term work in technology education, curriculum innovation, and classroom practice. From bringing digital tools into everyday lessons, to steadily building a varied and creative learning environment, our goal is that children don't just use technology — they use it to explore, think, and create.

Changhua County · Curriculum & Instruction
Technology Education / "Setting Sail" School

Setting sail is both an affirmation and the start of the next voyage. The Hsinmin team will keep refining, keep reaching for excellence, and let technology become a real support for children's learning — carrying them toward a wider future. 🌊

中文原文 · Chinese

揚帆啟航．新民再獲肯定

恭喜新民國小通過彰化縣課程教學－科技教育／揚帆學校認證 ✨

這份認證，是對新民長期深耕科技教育、課程創新與教學實踐的肯定。從數位科技融入課堂，到持續打造多元、創新的學習環境，我們希望讓孩子不只是「使用科技」，更能運用科技探索、思考與創造。

揚帆，是肯定，也是下一段航程的開始。新民團隊將持續精進、追求卓越，讓科技成為孩子學習的助力，帶著孩子航向更寬廣的未來。
