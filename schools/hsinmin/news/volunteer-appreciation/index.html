Thank You for Being There感恩有您｜新民小太陽志工期末感恩餐敘

1 / 2

A semester quietly draws to a close — and what a beautiful one it has been.

Hsinmin's Little Sun Volunteers gathered together for an end-of-semester appreciation dinner, sharing warmth, laughter, and the joy of a semester well spent. These dedicated volunteers give something truly precious: their time. Week after week, they step into the campus, bringing patience, care, and encouragement to children who need it most. A warm greeting, a reassuring look, a quiet moment of listening — each of these small gestures plants a seed of hope and confidence in a child's heart.

Through their companionship, volunteers come to understand more deeply just how much dedication teaching requires, and how much love teachers pour into every single child, every single day. It is this shared belief — that every child deserves to be seen, supported, and celebrated — that makes the bond between school, family, and volunteer community so strong and so meaningful.

To every volunteer who walked through our gates this semester: thank you. Your kindness has made Hsinmin a warmer, brighter place. May this warmth keep flowing, lighting up the futures of more and more children — and may we keep walking this beautiful path together.

中文原文 · Chinese

🥰 感恩有您，讓陪伴成為孩子成長路上最溫暖的力量 🌻

🎀 一個學期的時光，在不知不覺中畫下圓滿句點。新民小太陽志工們也在期末感恩餐敘中齊聚一堂，共同分享這段充滿愛與陪伴的旅程。

🫶 感謝每一位志工夥伴，在忙碌的生活中撥出時間走進校園，用耐心、關懷與鼓勵陪伴孩子成長。或許是一句溫暖的問候、一個肯定的眼神，或是一段靜靜傾聽的時光，雖然每個孩子的成長步伐不同，但在志工們一步一腳印的陪伴下，我們可以慢慢感受孩子的改變與進步，而這些都會在孩子心中種下希望與自信的種子 🌱。

💝 透過陪伴孩子，志工們也更能體會教育工作的不易，更理解老師們日日耕耘、用心守護每一位孩子的辛勞。而正是因為有這份共同的信念與愛，讓學校、家庭與志工形成最堅實的支持力量，陪伴孩子在人生道路上穩健前行。

💓 願這份溫暖持續流動，照亮更多孩子的未來，讓我們攜手同行，在新民校園中遇見更好的自己。

3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.A ______ gives their time freely to help others without being paid.
______免費付出自己的時間幫助他人，不收取報酬。
volunteer 志工 ✓ — A volunteer freely gives time and energy to help others. 志工是自由奉獻時間和精力幫助他人的人。
Q2.______ means being with someone so they do not feel alone.
______的意思是陪在某人身邊，讓他們不感到孤單。
companionship 陪伴 ✓ — Companionship means being present with someone, so they feel supported and less alone. 陪伴是指陪在某人身邊，讓他們感到被支持、不孤單。
Q3.To "encourage" someone means to ______.
「encourage」某人的意思是______。
give them support and confidence 給予支持與自信 ✓ — To encourage means to give someone support, hope, and the belief that they can do it. 鼓勵的意思是給予某人支持、希望，以及相信他們做得到的信念。
Q4.If you feel "grateful," you feel full of ______.
如果你感到「grateful」，你心裡充滿了______。
gratitude 感謝 ✓ — Grateful and gratitude come from the same root. When you are grateful, you feel gratitude. grateful 和 gratitude 來自同一個字根。當你 grateful，你心中充滿了 gratitude。
Q5.What does "patience" help you do when working with children?
在幫助孩子時，「patience」（耐心）能讓你做到什麼？
wait calmly and keep trying 冷靜等待並持續努力 ✓ — Patience means staying calm even when things are slow or difficult. 耐心是指即使事情進展緩慢或困難，也能保持冷靜。
