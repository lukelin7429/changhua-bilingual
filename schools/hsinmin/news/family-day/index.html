Parents' Association Family Day家長會114學年度文康暨家庭日活動

Although our Parents' Association Recreation & Family Day on May 31 has come to a close, the laughter still echoes in our ears, and every photo keeps telling the story of that wonderful day. Thank you to all the parent volunteers and families — we spent a full and heart-warming day at the Magic Mushroom Tribe leisure farm. This was not just a social gathering, but a journey of family companionship, friendship, and shared memories.

Under gentle sunshine and a soft breeze, everyone set aside their daily busyness and enjoyed precious family time. The day's activities included planting rose-mushroom grow-bags (hands-on food-and-farming education), handmade cookie DIY, a guided tour of the high-tech smart-mushroom production plant, a mushroom-cooking show with health and nutrition tips, and even mushroom beauty-product experiences — opening up new horizons of learning.

The farm's wide green lawns became the children's happiest playground — running, chasing, and laughing, with parents alongside rediscovering their own childlike joy. Sunshine, grass, and laughter wove together into the most beautiful picture.

Thank you to our Parents' Association for their long-standing support and dedication. Because of everyone's teamwork, the bond between teachers and families grows closer, and our children enjoy a better environment to learn and grow. The event may be over, but the joy lingers on — we look forward to the next gathering and many more warm, unforgettable memories!

🍄「菇」意相挺　幸福同行 🍄 雖然 5 月 31 日的文康暨家庭日活動已圓滿落幕，但歡笑聲彷彿仍迴盪在耳邊，一張張照片也持續訴說著當天的美好時光。感謝所有家長會夥伴及家人的熱情參與，讓我們在魔菇部落休閒農場度過充實而溫馨的一天。這不只是一場聯誼活動，更是一段親子陪伴、好友相聚、共同創造回憶的美好旅程。

當天陽光和煦、微風輕拂，大家暫時放下平日的忙碌，在輕鬆愉快的氛圍中享受難得的親子時光。🍄 玫瑰菇太空包種植體驗：親手種下希望與期待，體驗食農教育的樂趣。🍪 手工餅乾 DIY 製作：大手牽小手，一同完成專屬的美味點心。🏭 智慧蕈菇生產廠區導覽：認識智慧農業如何結合科技與環保。👨‍🍳 蕈菇料理秀與保健知識分享，✨ 蕈菇美妍產品體驗，收穫滿滿實用的生活知識。

🌿 園區內寬闊的綠地成了孩子們最開心的天地，孩子們盡情奔跑、追逐嬉戲，笑聲此起彼落；家長們則陪伴在側，享受難得的親子互動時光。陽光、草地與歡笑交織成最美的畫面，也讓大人們找回久違的童心。

感謝家長會夥伴長期以來對學校的支持與付出，因為有大家的攜手合作，讓親師關係更加緊密，也讓孩子們擁有更優質的學習與成長環境。活動雖已結束，但歡樂與感動仍持續發酵，期待下一次相聚，再次攜手創造更多溫馨而難忘的回憶！
