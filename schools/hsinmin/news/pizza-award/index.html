Effort Deserves a Reward!🍕 努力值得被看見｜達美樂披薩暖心獻新民

At Hsinmin Elementary, we believe that hard work should always be celebrated — and the community agrees! Manager Pei-ying of Domino's Pizza Tianzhong Yuanji Branch generously donated free personal pizza vouchers to reward students who excelled in their exams. It was a heartwarming gesture from a local business that truly cares about the children in the neighborhood.

Principal Huai-hsuan personally visited the Domino's branch to present a certificate of appreciation to Manager Pei-ying on behalf of the school. This beautiful act of community support reminds our students that their effort and dedication do not go unnoticed — and that the wider community is always cheering them on.

Partnerships like this strengthen the bond between school and community, and inspire students to keep pushing toward their goals. A big thank you to Manager Pei-ying and the entire team at Domino's Tianzhong Yuanji Branch for this wonderful and delicious encouragement! 🍕❤️

中文原文 · Chinese

🍕【努力值得被看見｜達美樂披薩暖心獻新民】🍕

在新民國小，努力從來都不會被忽視！達美樂田中員集店沛盈店長，特別為在考試中表現優異的同學贈送個人披薩兌換券，以美味的披薩獎勵孩子們平日的認真與付出。這份來自社區商家的暖心支持，讓孩子們感受到：努力的成果，是值得被看見、被肯定的。🎉

懷萱校長特地親赴達美樂田中員集店，代表學校致贈感謝狀給沛盈店長，感謝她對新民學子的關心與鼓勵。校長與老師們衷心感謝這份社區的溫暖，讓孩子在努力學習的路上，不只有老師與家長的陪伴，更有社區夥伴的加油！💪❤️

學校與社區攜手合作，共同為孩子的成長喝采。期待未來能有更多這樣的夥伴關係，讓新民的孩子們在愛與鼓勵中，一步步走向更好的自己。謝謝沛盈店長與達美樂田中員集店全體同仁！🍕🌟

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
reward(n./v.)
獎勵；報答；回報
A pizza voucher was given as a reward for excellent exam results.
披薩兌換券是作為優秀考試成績的獎勵而發放的。
2
encourage(v.)
鼓勵；激勵
The manager's kind gift helped encourage students to keep working hard.
店長的善心禮物幫助鼓勵學生繼續努力。
3
partnership(n.)
夥伴關係；合作關係
The partnership between the school and the local pizza shop shows how a community cares.
學校與當地披薩店之間的夥伴關係展示了社區如何相互關心。
4
generous(adj.)
慷慨的；大方的
Manager Pei-ying was very generous in donating free pizza vouchers to the students.
沛盈店長非常慷慨地捐贈免費披薩兌換券給學生。
5
appreciation(n.)
感謝；感激；欣賞
The principal presented a certificate of appreciation to thank the store manager.
校長致贈感謝狀以感謝店長的善舉。
6
community(n.)
社區；群體；社群
A strong community supports its school and celebrates students' success.
一個強大的社區會支持自己的學校並為學生的成功歡呼慶賀。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組，A 和 B 輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🎭 Scene · Two students are talking about the pizza reward from the local store.
情境：兩位同學在聊達美樂店長贈送披薩兌換券的事。
A
Did you hear? A pizza store manager donated free pizza vouchers to students who did well on their exams!
你有聽說嗎？一位披薩店店長捐贈免費披薩兌換券給考試成績優秀的同學！
B
Wow, that's so generous! What a great way to encourage students.
哇，那真的很慷慨！這真是激勵學生的好方法。
A
And our principal personally went to the store to give them a certificate of appreciation!
而且我們的校長還親自去到店裡致贈感謝狀！
B
That shows how important community partnership is. The whole town is cheering for us!
那顯示了社區夥伴關係有多重要。整個鎮都在為我們加油！
A
It makes me want to study harder too. I want to earn a reward like that!
這也讓我更想認真讀書了。我也想獲得這樣的獎勵！
B
Me too! Let's work hard together. Thank you to the pizza shop for their appreciation of our efforts!
我也是！讓我們一起努力。感謝披薩店對我們努力的肯定與支持！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.Something given to recognize and celebrate someone's good work is called a ______.
為了表彰並慶賀某人的努力而給予的東西，叫做______。
reward 獎勵 ✓ — A reward is something you receive for doing good work or behaving well. 獎勵是因為表現良好而得到的回報。
Q2.To give someone confidence and make them want to do more is to ______ them.
給某人信心並讓他們想要更加努力，就是在______他們。
encourage 鼓勵 ✓ — To encourage someone means to give them support, hope, or confidence. 鼓勵某人就是給予他們支持、希望或信心。
Q3.Who donated the free pizza vouchers to reward the students?
誰捐贈了免費披薩兌換券來獎勵同學？
Manager Pei-ying of Domino's 達美樂田中員集店沛盈店長 ✓ — Manager Pei-ying donated the pizza vouchers to reward students for their excellent exam results. 沛盈店長捐贈披薩兌換券獎勵在考試中表現優異的學生。
Q4.Willingness to give more than expected, especially to help others, is called being ______.
願意付出超乎預期，尤其是幫助他人，叫做______。
generous 慷慨的 ✓ — Generous means willing to give more than is necessary, usually to help or please others. 慷慨意指願意給予超過所需，通常是為了幫助或讓他人高興。
Q5.What did Principal Huai-hsuan give to the store manager as a thank-you?
懷萱校長送給店長什麼作為感謝？
A certificate of appreciation 感謝狀 ✓ — Principal Huai-hsuan personally visited the store and presented a certificate of appreciation. 懷萱校長親自前往店裡致贈感謝狀。
