Mixing Learning into the Batter: Sixth Graders Bake Egg Cakes 🐣🥚把學習拌進麵糊，讓校園生活多一點香甜

小小烘焙師上工：把麵糊倒進模具

Recently, our sixth graders rolled up their sleeves and became little bakers, making fragrant Taiwanese egg cakes (雞蛋糕) with their own hands! 🤩

From cracking eggs and measuring the egg-cake mix and soy milk to stirring the batter and pouring it into the molds, everyone got to try every step. It looks like a simple snack, but once you make it yourself, you learn that the amounts matter, stirring takes care, and waiting for the cakes to bake takes a little patience! 👩‍🍳👨‍🍳

Baking Step by Step
一步一步做雞蛋糕
  • 🥚
    Crack — crack the eggs into the bowl打蛋
  • ⚖️
    Measure — measure the egg-cake mix and soy milk carefully — the amounts matter!量取雞蛋糕粉與豆漿
  • 🥣
    Stir — mix the batter well until it’s smooth攪拌麵糊
  • 💧
    Pour — fill each little mold with batter倒入模具
  • ⏳
    Wait — be patient while the egg cakes turn golden耐心等待出爐

動手做、開心吃，還有「我也會做了！」的成就感

When the batter turned into golden egg cakes, every bite was more than delicious — it came with a proud feeling: “I can make this too!”吃著自己參與製作的點心，這一口，除了美味，還多了一份「我也會做了！」的成就感

Special thanks to the Kang Hsuan team — our “uncle” and “big sister” from Kang Hsuan (康軒叔叔、康軒姐姐) — and the sixth-grade homeroom teachers for guiding the children step by step, turning an everyday snack into a hands-on lesson full of flavor! 🥳

At Hsinmin, learning is something you can try with your hands — and savor slowly. This sweet treat, made by their own hands, has left a lovely memory of school life for our sixth graders.

中文原文 · Chinese

把學習拌進麵糊，讓校園生活多一點香甜🐣🥚
近日，六年級的孩子們捲起袖子，當起小小烘焙師，親手製作香噴噴的雞蛋糕🤩

從打蛋、量取雞蛋糕粉與豆漿，到攪拌麵糊、倒入模具，每個步驟都得自己試一試。看似簡單的小點心，動手做才知道，份量要留意、攪拌要仔細，等待出爐更需要一點耐心👩‍🍳👨‍🍳

當麵糊變成金黃的雞蛋糕，香氣也讓大家更加期待！吃著自己參與製作的點心，這一口，除了美味，還多了一份「我也會做了！」的成就感😋

特別感謝康軒叔叔、康軒姐姐，以及六年級導師，用心帶著孩子一步步完成，讓生活中的小點心，成為一堂有滋有味的實作課🥳

在新民，學習可以動手試，也可以細細品嚐。這份親手完成的香甜，為六年級的校園生活，留下一段美好的回憶
