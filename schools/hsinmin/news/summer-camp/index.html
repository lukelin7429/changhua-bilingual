The Super-Power Summer Adventure Sets Sail超能力暑假探險號啟航｜優質新民・最佳選擇

Summer is not just a break — it is golden time for children to explore their interests, build their abilities, and gather energy for growth. 🚀

This year Hsinmin Elementary has carefully designed a whole fleet of summer camps, each one building a different kind of “super-power”: learning, creativity, international awareness, the arts, character, and sports. From English and character building, to world cultures, to music and art, to science, traditional crafts and athletic training — every course holds a little surprise, and a chance for children to grow.

✨ Some children find their confidence on a stage.
✨ Some discover a hidden talent in the sound of an instrument.
✨ Some learn responsibility through teamwork.
✨ And some meet a better version of themselves through exploring and rising to a challenge.

This summer, let Hsinmin walk beside your child on an adventure full of laughter and discovery!

English Learning Corner

Summer Camp · Words to Learn英語學習角 · 暑假營隊的小單字

Eight key words from the summer-camp adventure, each with an example sentence — then a five-question quiz. Tap 🔊 to listen.
八個暑假營隊關鍵字，每個字配一個例句，最後有五題小考。點 🔊 聽發音。

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
summer(n.)
夏天；暑假
Summer is a great time to learn new things.
暑假是學習新事物的好時光。
2
camp(n.)
營隊
I will join a music camp this summer.
今年暑假我要參加音樂營隊。
3
adventure(n.)
冒險
Summer camp is a big adventure for kids.
暑假營隊是孩子的大冒險。
4
explore(v.)
探索
Children explore their interests at camp.
孩子在營隊裡探索自己的興趣。
5
talent(n.)
天賦
She found her talent in the choir.
她在合唱團裡發現了天賦。
6
confidence(n.)
自信
He gained confidence on the stage.
他在舞台上獲得了自信。
7
challenge(n.)
挑戰
Archery is a fun challenge to try.
射箭是值得一試的有趣挑戰。
8
grow(v.)
成長
Children grow a lot during the summer.
孩子在暑假裡成長很多。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組，A 和 B 輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🎬 Scene · Two classmates talk about the Hsinmin summer camps.
情境：兩位同學聊起新民的暑假營隊。
A
Which summer camp will you join?
你要參加哪一個暑假營隊？
B
I want to join the string ensemble. I love music!
我想參加弦樂團，我超愛音樂！
A
That sounds fun. I will try the archery camp.
聽起來很好玩。我要試試射箭營。
B
Cool! Archery is a great challenge.
酷！射箭是很棒的挑戰。
A
This summer will be a big adventure!
這個暑假會是一場大冒險！
B
Yes! Let’s explore and grow together.
對！我們一起探索、一起成長。
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.This summer, Hsinmin opened many fun ______ for children.
這個暑假，新民為孩子開了許多好玩的______。
camps 營隊 ✓ — Hsinmin planned many summer camps. 新民規劃了許多暑假營隊。
Q2.At camp, children ______ their own interests.
在營隊裡，孩子們______自己的興趣。
explore 探索 ✓ — Children explore their interests at camp. 孩子在營隊探索興趣。
Q3.Standing on the stage helped her gain ______.
站上舞台幫助她獲得了______。
confidence 自信 ✓ — Some children find confidence on a stage. 有些孩子在舞台上找到自信。
Q4.Trying archery is a fun ______ for the children.
嘗試射箭對孩子來說是有趣的______。
challenge 挑戰 ✓ — Children meet a better self through a challenge. 孩子在挑戰中看見更好的自己。
Q5.During the summer, children learn and ______ a lot.
在暑假期間，孩子們學習並______了許多。
grow 成長 ✓ — Children grow a lot during the summer. 孩子在暑假裡成長很多。
