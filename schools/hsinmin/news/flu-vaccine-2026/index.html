Flu Shots at School Tomorrow! 💉✨【明天登場！】115年度學童流感疫苗入校施打

流感疫苗施打前的溫馨提醒 · 新民國小關心您的健康

❤️ Tomorrow (Wed., Oct. 21) is flu vaccine day at school! To help everything go smoothly, please remind your child of these 5 key points tonight. 🎒👇

5 Things to Remember
今晚請提醒孩子 5 大重點
  • 🎒
    Pack the NHI card — put your National Health Insurance card in your school bag tonight健保卡今晚就放入書包！
  • 👕
    Wear a loose top — choose a loose, comfortable shirt穿著寬鬆舒適的上衣！
  • 🥪
    Eat breakfast — always eat breakfast before school上學前一定要吃早餐！
  • 🌡️
    Fever check — no shot if body temperature is over 38°C that day當日體溫 ＞38℃ 不予注射！
  • 🏡
    Rest afterward — avoid hard exercise after the shot and get plenty of rest施打後避免劇烈運動，多多休息！

After school, please read the “After Your Vaccination” notice carefully, and don’t let your child do hard exercise 🏃‍♂️🚫. If your child needs to see a doctor because they feel unwell, be sure to tell the doctor that they received a flu vaccine on Oct. 21. 🩺💬

The poster also reminds families: get a good night’s sleep the night before, don’t come on an empty stomach, and keep to a normal daily routine.

Catch-Up Shot Reminder
補接種小叮嚀
  • 🩺
    Checked by a doctor — on vaccine day, a doctor will check every student before the shot施打當天每位學童都會由專業醫師親自評估
  • 😴
    Missed it? No worries — if your child was unwell, not cleared by the doctor, or absent that day若孩子當天因身體不適、經醫師評估未接種，或是剛好請假缺席也不用擔心
  • 📄
    Get the form — once your child is well, ask the school health center for a catch-up vaccination form恢復健康後，請向學校健康中心索取「補接種單」
  • 🏥
    Book a clinic — after Oct. 28 (7 days after the school vaccine day), book a visit at a contracted clinic or hospital10/28 後（校園施打日 7 日後），家長即可自行預約合約醫療院所補施打

Thank you, families, for your help — together, let’s keep our campus healthy and safe!感謝各位家長的熱情配合與協助，讓我們攜手打造健康安心的校園環境！守護寶貝健康！

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

vaccine/vækˈsiːn/疫苗

A vaccine helps protect your body from getting sick.疫苗幫助保護身體不生病。

flu/fluː/流感

The flu can make you feel very tired.流感會讓你覺得很累。

fever/ˈfiːvər/發燒

With a fever over 38°C, you can’t get the shot.發燒超過38度就不能打針。

temperature/ˈtemprətʃər/體溫

The nurse will check your temperature.護理師會幫你量體溫。

breakfast/ˈbrekfəst/早餐

Eat breakfast before you come to school.上學前要吃早餐。

rest/rest/休息

Get plenty of rest after the shot.打完針要多多休息。
