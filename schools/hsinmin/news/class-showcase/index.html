IE-GC Class Showcase & Open HouseIE-GC班成果發表會暨開放參觀日

A school year has passed, and the children have quietly grown so much. From barely daring to speak English at first, to now singing, acting, and joining class activities in English; from needing constant reminders, to gradually learning to complete tasks independently. Day by day the changes may seem small, but looking back, they have added up into wonderful growth.

We sincerely invite you to the IE-GC class showcase, to see the children's hard work and confident selves this term.

📅 Date: Saturday, June 13 · 🕙 Time: 10:00–11:00 · 📍 Venue: Hsinmin Elementary Conference Room. The program includes a semester review video, the English drama “The Enormous Turnip,” a music performance, and a certificate ceremony. We are also opening our doors to families interested in the IE-GC class.

If you are preparing to send your child to elementary school, and looking for a learning environment that balances English ability, life education, and the joy of learning, come and see the children's most authentic learning. Some things are most convincing when seen with your own eyes. Online sign-up: tally.so/r/WO1WWk

中文原文 · Chinese

一個學年過去了，孩子們也悄悄長大了許多。從一開始不太敢開口說英文，到現在能用英文唱歌、演戲、參與課堂活動；從需要老師時時提醒，到慢慢學習獨立完成任務。這些改變，也許每天看起來只有一點點，但回頭看時，卻已經累積成令人驚喜的成長。

誠摯邀請大家一起來參加 IE-GC 班本學期的成果發表會，看見孩子們這一學期努力的成果與自信的模樣。

📅 日期｜06/13（六）🕙 時間｜10:00－11:00　📍 地點｜新民國小會議室。當天除了有：✨ 學期回顧影片、✨ 英文戲劇演出《The Enormous Turnip》、✨ 音樂成果表演、✨ 學期授證典禮，也特別開放對 IE-GC 班有興趣的家庭一同參觀。

如果您正準備讓孩子進入小學，如果您正在尋找一個兼顧英文能力、生活教育與學習樂趣的學習環境，歡迎來看看孩子們最真實的學習樣貌。有些事情，親眼看見最有說服力。期待與您相見，一起見證孩子們的成長與蛻變 ❤️　🔗 線上報名：https://tally.so/r/WO1WWk
