Pippi Spooky House: Welcome Home, Alumni! 🎃💙新民萬聖節皮皮怪怪屋－校友場溫馨登場

畢業了，還是想回來的地方

✨ Even after graduation, it’s still the place they want to come back to.

Today (Oct. 21) at 5 p.m., the Pippi Spooky House welcomed some very special guests — Hsinmin graduates! ❤️ Right after their own school day ended, they hurried back to their familiar campus to say hello to their old teachers, catch up with old friends, and take on the surprise-filled spooky house together! 😆 (See the alumni night announcement.)

👻 A familiar campus — with a few extra scares. 💕 The kids have grown up, but their smiles are just as familiar.

和昔日的老師打招呼、與老朋友相聚

For our teachers, the happiest part isn’t how lively the event was — it’s seeing graduates still choose to come back to Hsinmin, visit their teachers, and share the little stories of growing up.

🌟 Special thanks to the team members who stayed late to welcome our alumni. ❤️ Thank you for staying after a long, busy day so the kids coming home could feel Hsinmin’s warmth once again!

The scares in a haunted house last only a few minutes — but the bond between teachers and students lasts for years. Graduating isn’t leaving; it’s having one more place you can always come back to.鬼屋的驚嚇只有短短幾分鐘，但師生之間的情誼，卻能延續好多年。畢業不是離開，而是多了一個隨時可以回來的地方。

Hsinmin will always welcome you home! 🏡

新民萬聖節皮皮怪怪屋－校友場溫馨登場🎃👻
✨ 畢業了，還是想回來的地方✨
今天（10/21）下午5點，皮皮怪怪屋迎來一群特別的客人——新民畢業的學長姐們❤️
才剛放學，就迫不及待回到熟悉的校園，和昔日的老師打招呼、與老朋友相聚，再一起挑戰充滿驚喜的怪怪屋！😆

👻 熟悉的校園，多了幾分驚嚇
💕 長大了的孩子，依然有著熟悉的笑容。
對老師而言，最開心的不是活動有多熱鬧，而是看見畢業的孩子，依然願意回到新民走走、看看老師，分享長大後的點點滴滴。

🌟 特別感謝今晚留下來陪伴校友的工作夥伴！
❤️ 謝謝大家在忙碌了一整天後，仍願意留下來，讓回家的孩子們再次感受到新民的溫暖！

🎃 鬼屋的驚嚇只有短短幾分鐘，但師生之間的情誼，卻能延續好多年。
❤️ 畢業不是離開，而是多了一個隨時可以回來的地方。
新民，永遠歡迎你們回家！
