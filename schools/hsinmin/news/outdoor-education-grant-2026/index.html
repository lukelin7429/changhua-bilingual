Hsinmin Wins Funding for Outdoor Education! 🎉🌿成功爭取115學年度戶外教育與海洋教育計畫經費

115學年度戶外教育子計畫2 · 總經費七萬五千元

🌿 Step out of the classroom and explore the world! 🌊 Learning shouldn’t stay only in textbooks — hands-on experiences help children discover endless possibilities in everyday life!

Hsinmin Elementary has won funding from the SY115 Outdoor and Marine Education Program (Outdoor Education Sub-program 2) for two projects, totaling NT$75,000! 🎉

Funded Projects
本次獲補助項目
  • 🌳
    Sub-program 2-1: Outdoor education at school — NT$30,000子計畫2-1｜學校實施戶外教育　補助 30,000 元
  • 🧭
    Sub-program 2-3: Self-directed learning routes — NT$45,000子計畫2-3｜自主學習路線　補助 45,000 元
  • 💰
    Total funding — NT$75,000!補助總經費 75,000 元

Through outdoor education and self-directed learning routes, we’ll take children beyond the campus to get close to nature and learn about the ocean — observing, exploring, experiencing, and thinking in real-life settings, building independent learning skills and widening their view of the world.

Every trip out is a new lesson, and every exploration becomes precious nourishment for growing up!每一次出發，都是一次新的學習；每一段探索，都將成為孩子成長的珍貴養分！

❤️ Thank you to everyone who supported and recognized us. We look forward to Hsinmin’s children setting out with curiosity and passion toward a wider world of learning!

At Hsinmin, learning steps out of the classroom — and the world becomes our children’s classroom! 🌎
