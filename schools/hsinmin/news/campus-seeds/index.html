The Incredible World of Seeds 🌿種子不可思議｜校園植物探索

Every seed holds a secret — a tiny miracle wrapped in a shell, waiting for the right moment to burst into life. At Hsinmin Elementary, our students had the chance to step outside, slow down, and discover just how incredible the world of seeds truly is.

During a special campus nature exploration activity, students walked through the school grounds with curious eyes and open hands — collecting seeds, touching them, smelling them, and asking questions. From papery wings that float on the breeze, to hard round shells that protect tiny green life inside; from seeds no bigger than a grain of sand, to ones as large as a marble — the variety was stunning.

"When you really stop and look, you see that a seed is not just the beginning of a plant — it is a tiny, perfect story about life, patience, and hope."當你真的停下來仔細看，你會發現種子不只是植物的開始，更是一個關於生命、耐心與希望的小小完整故事。

Students learned that seeds travel in remarkable ways — some fly with the wind, some hitch a ride on an animal's fur, some float on water, and some simply drop and roll. Every seed has its own clever strategy for finding a new home. And every one of them, no matter how tiny, carries the instructions for an entire plant inside.

Being curious about nature is one of the most important skills a learner can have. When we observe the world around us carefully, we begin to see the extraordinary in the ordinary — and that is where science, wonder, and a lifelong love of learning all begin.

🌿 Thank you to our teachers and students for bringing the campus alive with curiosity!

中文原文 · Chinese

🌱【種子不可思議｜校園植物探索】

每一顆種子，都藏著一個小小的奇蹟。在新民國小，孩子們走出教室，踏上校園的每一個角落，用眼睛、用雙手，去發現那個隱藏在草叢間、樹下和泥土裡的不可思議世界。

孩子們蹲下來，仔細觀察各種形態的種子：有輕薄如紙、可以隨風飄飛的翅果；有外殼堅硬、保護著嫩芽的豆莢；有細如沙粒的微小種子，也有大如彈珠的圓滾種子。每一顆都有自己的故事，每一顆都有自己旅行的方式。

透過這次探索，孩子們不只認識了植物的多樣性，更學會了用好奇的眼光去看待身邊的自然。那份「哇！原來如此！」的驚喜感，正是科學探究精神最美好的起點。

🌿 感謝帶領孩子探索自然的老師們，也謝謝每一位帶著好奇心走入校園的孩子。種子不只種在土壤裡，也種在每一顆渴望學習的心裡。

Words About Seeds & Nature英語學習角 · 和種子與自然有關的小單字

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
seed(n.)
種子
A seed is the part of a plant from which a new plant can grow.
種子是植物能長出新植株的部分。
2
germinate(v.)
發芽；萌芽
Seeds need water, warmth, and soil to germinate and start growing into a plant.
種子需要水分、溫暖和土壤才能發芽，開始長成植物。
3
sprout(n./v.)
嫩芽；（使）萌芽
After a few days in the soil, a tiny sprout appeared above the ground — life had begun!
在土壤中幾天後，一顆小嫩芽冒出地面——生命開始了！
4
observe(v.)
觀察；注意
Scientists observe plants carefully to understand how they grow and survive.
科學家仔細觀察植物，以了解它們如何生長和存活。
5
curious(adj.)
好奇的；求知欲強的
A curious mind is a learning mind — asking questions helps us discover new and amazing things.
好奇的心就是學習的心——提問幫助我們發現新奇而驚人的事物。
6
nature(n.)
大自然；自然界
Nature includes all the plants, animals, and environments that exist outside of what humans make.
大自然包括所有存在於人類所造之外的植物、動物和環境。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🌿 Scene · Two students explore seeds they found on the school campus.
情境：兩位同學在校園裡探索他們找到的種子。
A
Look at this! I found a seed under the big tree. It has little wings on it!
你看！我在大樹下找到一顆種子。它有小翅膀！
B
Wow! That's a winged seed. Nature made it that way so it can fly through the air and find a new place to grow.
哇！那是翅果。大自然讓它長成這樣，是為了讓它能飛過空中，找到新的地方生長。
A
That's so cool! I'm curious — what does this seed need to germinate?
太酷了！我很好奇——這顆種子需要什麼才能發芽？
B
It needs water, sunlight, and good soil. Then a little sprout will push up through the ground!
它需要水、陽光和好土壤。然後一根小嫩芽就會從地裡冒出來！
A
I want to observe it every day and see how it grows. Science is so amazing!
我想每天觀察它，看它怎麼長大。科學真是太神奇了！
B
Yes! Every seed in nature has its own special story. Let's collect more and learn about them!
對！大自然中的每顆種子都有自己的特別故事。讓我們多收集一些，深入了解它們吧！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.The tiny object inside a plant that can grow into a new plant is called a ______.
植物內部能長成新植株的小小物體叫做______。
seed 種子 ✓ — A seed contains everything a new plant needs to begin growing — food, instructions, and protection. 種子包含新植物開始生長所需的一切——養分、生長指令和保護。
Q2.When a seed absorbs water and begins to grow, we say it starts to ______.
當種子吸收水分並開始生長，我們說它開始______。
germinate 發芽 ✓ — Germination is the process by which a seed begins to grow. It needs the right conditions: moisture, warmth, and oxygen. 發芽是種子開始生長的過程，需要適當條件：水分、溫暖和氧氣。
Q3.The first small green growth that appears when a seed begins to grow is called a ______.
種子開始生長時最先冒出的小小綠色芽稱為______。
sprout 嫩芽 ✓ — A sprout is the tiny first shoot of a new plant, just beginning to emerge from the soil. 嫩芽是新植物剛開始從土壤中冒出的細小嫩苗。
Q4.To watch something carefully and take note of what you see is to ______ it.
仔細觀看某物並記錄所見，叫做______它。
observe 觀察 ✓ — To observe is to watch carefully and notice details. Scientists observe the world to learn how it works. 觀察是仔細觀看並注意細節。科學家透過觀察世界來了解它的運作方式。
Q5.Which word best describes a student who loves to ask questions and discover new things?
哪個詞最能描述一個喜歡提問並發現新事物的學生？
curious 好奇的 ✓ — A curious person always wants to know more, asks questions, and explores the world around them. 好奇的人總是想知道更多，會提問並探索周遭的世界。
