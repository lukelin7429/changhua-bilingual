Painting Tianjhong · Children's Art Contest彩繪田中・兒童繪畫比賽開跑！

Pick up your brushes and let your imagination run wild! The Tianjhong Junior Chamber of Commerce is organizing the "Painting Tianjhong — Children's Art Contest" on Saturday, June 27, and all students are warmly welcome to join!

The event will take place at Fuyù Garden Hotel (Fuyù Huayuan Jiudian) — the address is No. 100, Section 2, Zhanqu Road, Longtan Village, Tianjhong Township, Changhua County. Contest paper will be provided on the day, but please remember to bring your own art supplies. Awards will be presented for first, second, and third place.

This is a wonderful chance to show off your creativity and meet fellow young artists from around the community. Register now by scanning the QR code on the poster — and get ready to paint your masterpiece! 🎨✨

🎨 Contest At a Glance · 比賽重點整理

  • Date: Saturday, June 27, 2026 · 2026年6月27日（六）
  • Venue: Fuyù Garden Hotel · 馥御花園酒店（彰化縣田中鎮龍潭里站區路二段100號）
  • Organizer: Tianjhong Junior Chamber of Commerce · 田中國際青年商會
  • Prizes: 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place awards · 設有第一、二、三名獎項
  • What to bring: Your own art supplies (paper provided) · 請自備繪畫工具，現場提供畫紙
  • Register: Scan the QR code on the poster · 掃描海報上的 QR code 報名

Words About Art & Creativity英語學習角 · 和美術與創意有關的小單字

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
contest(n.)
比賽；競賽
She entered a drawing contest and won second place.
她參加了一個繪畫比賽，獲得了第二名。
2
creativity(n.)
創意；創造力
Art is a great way to express your creativity.
美術是表達你創意的好方式。
3
award(n.)
獎項；獎品
Three awards will be given to the top three artists.
前三名的藝術家將各獲得一個獎項。
4
masterpiece(n.)
傑作；代表作
Every child has the ability to create a masterpiece.
每個孩子都有能力創作出一件傑作。
5
supplies(n.)
用品；材料
Please bring your own art supplies to the contest.
請自備美術用品來參加比賽。
6
imagination(n.)
想像力
Good art always starts with a strong imagination.
好的藝術作品總是從豐富的想像力開始。

3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.A ______ is a competition where you try to win a prize.
______是一個你努力贏得獎項的競賽。
contest 比賽 ✓ — A contest is a competition where participants try to win a prize or honor. 比賽是參賽者努力爭取獎項或榮譽的競賽。
Q2.The ability to think of new and original ideas is called ______.
想出新的、獨創想法的能力叫做______。
creativity 創意 ✓ — Creativity is the ability to use your imagination to come up with new ideas. 創意是運用想像力產生新想法的能力。
Q3.What should students bring to the art contest?
同學應該帶什麼去參加繪畫比賽？
Their own art supplies 自備美術用品 ✓ — Paper is provided, but students must bring their own art supplies. 現場提供畫紙，但同學需要自備美術用品。
Q4.A very famous or excellent work of art is called a ______.
一件非常著名或出色的藝術作品叫做______。
masterpiece 傑作 ✓ — A masterpiece is an outstanding or excellent piece of work. 傑作是一件卓越出色的作品。
Q5.Where will the art contest take place?
繪畫比賽將在哪裡舉辦？
Fuyù Garden Hotel 馥御花園酒店 ✓ — The contest is held at Fuyù Garden Hotel, Tianjhong Township, Changhua County. 比賽在彰化縣田中鎮的馥御花園酒店舉辦。
