IE-GC Summer Camp: Week 1 Highlights! 🌎IE-GC暑假夏令營｜第一週精彩回顧

Summer break isn't just for resting — it's the perfect time for children to open their eyes wider, grow their confidence, and discover the world through new experiences. That's exactly what happened during the first week of IE-GC Summer Camp at Hsinmin Elementary!

Week 1 centered on two exciting themes: Animal Adjectives and Taiwan. Rather than memorizing words from a list, students encountered English through videos, reading, games, physical activities, art, and science experiments — letting language grow naturally in real, meaningful situations.

Every morning, students also began practicing a Daily Journal — writing one English sentence to record how they felt and what was on their mind. After writing, each student shared their entry one-on-one with the teacher. English wasn't just words on paper — it was something to say out loud, with confidence.

暑假不只是休息，也是孩子打開視野、累積自信的好時機。IE-GC 暑假夏令營第一週，我們以動物Adjectives 形容詞以及TAIWAN 作為主要學習核心，搭配主題素材，透過影片、閱讀、遊戲、體育、美術與科學實驗，引導孩子在情境中自然接觸英文、練習表達，也用不同方式探索世界。

English Learning Corner

Words About Language Learning & Expression英語學習角 · 和語言學習與表達有關的小單字

Six key words — tap 🔊 to listen, then try the conversation and quiz.
六個關鍵字，點 🔊 聽發音，再試試對話和小考。

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
adjective(n.)
形容詞
An adjective is a word that describes a noun — like "fast," "small," or "curious." It tells us more about what something is like.
形容詞是描述名詞的詞，例如「快的」、「小的」或「好奇的」。它告訴我們某樣東西是什麼樣子的。
2
describe(v.)
描述；形容
To describe means to say or write what something or someone is like, using words that paint a picture.
描述是指用言語或文字說出某樣東西或某個人是什麼樣子，就像用文字畫出一幅畫。
3
explore(v.)
探索；探查
To explore means to go somewhere new or try something unknown in order to learn and discover more about it.
探索是指前往新地方或嘗試未知事物，以便了解和發現更多關於它的事情。
4
curious(adj.)
好奇的；求知欲強的
When you are curious, you want to learn more and find answers — curious learners always ask "Why?" and "How?"
當你好奇時，你會想要學習更多並找到答案。好奇的學習者總是問「為什麼？」和「怎麼做？」
5
journal(n.)
日記；日誌
A journal is a personal notebook where you write about your thoughts, feelings, and experiences each day.
日記是一本個人筆記本，你每天在裡面寫下自己的想法、感受和經歷。
6
teamwork(n.)
團隊合作
Teamwork is when a group of people work together and help each other to achieve a shared goal. Together, everyone achieves more!
團隊合作是一群人一起努力並互相幫助，以達成共同目標。一起，大家能完成更多！
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🌎 Scene · Two students talk about what they learned at English summer camp.
情境：兩個同學聊起他們在英文夏令營學到的事。
A
What did you learn at camp this week? I heard you studied adjectives.
你這週在夏令營學了什麼？我聽說你們學了形容詞。
B
Yes! An adjective is a word that describes things. Like a cheetah is fast, and an elephant is strong.
對！形容詞是描述事物的詞。比如獵豹很快，而大象很強壯。
A
That sounds fun! Were you curious about anything you discovered?
聽起來很有趣！有沒有什麼讓你感到好奇的發現？
B
Yes! In the science experiment, we made a hot air balloon. I was so curious about how it floats up in the air.
有！在科學實驗中，我們做了一個熱氣球。我對它怎麼飄上天感到好奇。
A
And you also write a daily journal? What do you write about?
你們也要寫每日日記嗎？你都寫些什麼？
B
Just one sentence in English — like how I feel or what I did. Then I share it with the teacher. It really helps me explore English through teamwork and talking!
只要一句英文，寫我的感受或我做了什麼。然後和老師分享。這真的幫助我透過合作和說話來探索英文！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.A word that describes a noun — like "big," "fast," or "strong" — is called an ______.
描述名詞的詞，例如「大的」、「快的」或「強壯的」，叫做______。
adjective 形容詞 ✓ — Adjectives describe nouns — they tell us what something or someone looks like, feels like, or is like. Big, small, fast, slow, strong, curious are all adjectives! 形容詞描述名詞，告訴我們某樣東西或某人是什麼樣子的。大、小、快、慢、強壯、好奇，這些都是形容詞！
Q2.When you use words to tell someone what something looks like or what it is like, you are ______ it.
當你用文字告訴別人某樣東西是什麼樣子時，你正在______它。
describing 描述 ✓ — To describe is to use words to paint a clear picture of what something or someone is like. Good descriptions use adjectives! 描述是用文字清楚呈現某樣東西或某人是什麼樣子。好的描述會用上形容詞！
Q3.A person who always wants to know more, asks lots of questions, and loves learning new things is ______.
一個總是想知道更多、問很多問題、喜歡學習新事物的人是______的。
curious 好奇的 ✓ — A curious person is eager to learn and discover new things. Being curious is one of the best qualities a learner can have! 好奇的人渴望學習和發現新事物。擁有好奇心是學習者最棒的特質之一！
Q4.A personal notebook where you write your thoughts, feelings, or experiences each day is called a ______.
每天用來記錄自己想法、感受或經歷的個人筆記本叫做______。
journal 日記 ✓ — A journal is a book for personal writing — you can write about your day, your feelings, your dreams, or anything you want to remember. 日記是用於個人書寫的本子，你可以寫下你的一天、你的感受、你的夢想，或任何你想記住的事情。
Q5.When everyone in a group helps each other and works toward the same goal, that is called ______.
當一個群體中的每個人都互相幫助、朝著同一個目標努力，那就叫做______。
teamwork 團隊合作 ✓ — Teamwork means everyone contributes and helps one another to reach a shared goal. Great things happen when people work as a team! 團隊合作是指每個人都出力並互相幫助，以達成共同目標。當人們以團隊形式合作時，偉大的事情就會發生！
