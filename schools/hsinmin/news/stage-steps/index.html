Safety Meets Beauty · New Stage Steps美學與安全階梯｜感謝家長會江柏樟顧問的貼心好禮

1 / 5

At first glance, it looks like just a staircase. Look closer, and you will find care built into every step.

Hsinmin Elementary's assembly hall has a beautiful new set of stage steps — custom-designed and generously donated by Parent Association Advisor Jiang Bo-zhang, our former vice president of the Parents' Association. Simple as it may look, every detail was thoughtfully chosen: a sturdy aluminum frame provides lasting strength, while a wooden board on each step brings a warm, gentle feel underfoot. The real star of the design, however, is the non-slip pad — a thoughtful touch that keeps every step safe, whether a student is bounding up to perform, a singer is stepping into the spotlight, or a guest is descending after a ceremony.

Since its arrival, the new staircase has made school life noticeably smoother and safer for students, teachers and families alike. And it hasn't taken long to earn an unofficial new title: the most popular photo spot on campus! From beaming school guests and teachers gathering for a warm group shot, to the choir lining up in full voice for a concert, everyone has been drawn to the steps as the perfect place to capture a shining moment together.

On behalf of all students, teachers and families at Hsinmin, we extend our warmest, most heartfelt thanks to Advisor Jiang for this generous and wonderfully thoughtful gift. Your care has made our school a safer, more beautiful, and more joyful place to learn, grow, and shine together. 🙏❤️

🎉【校園美事】多一份貼心，增一份安全！

新民國小集會堂舞台「美學與安全階梯」正式登場 🏫❤️

這是一座專為新民國小集會堂舞台量身打造的全新階梯！看似簡單，背後卻藏著滿滿的細節與貼心：鋁製的骨架堅固耐用，上方特別加了一層木板，為上下舞台的步伐帶來柔軟與彈性；最讓人感動的，是那細緻的止滑墊設計，讓每一次的登台與下台，都多了一份安心與安全的守護。

這座階梯不僅大幅提升了全校親師生在集會與活動時的使用方便性與效率，更化身為校園裡最熱門的「拍照新景點」與大合照的完美站位！無論是貴賓與師長們的溫馨合影，或是孩子們在音樂交流演出、合唱團上台時的精采展現，這座階梯都默默地托起每一個閃耀的瞬間。

新民國小全體親師生，特別在此向「家長會前副會長——江柏樟顧問」致上最誠摯的謝意！感謝江顧問的用心付出與巧妙設計，您的這份貼心，讓新民的校園活動空間變得更安全、更進步、更美好。
