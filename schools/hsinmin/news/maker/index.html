Maker Course · My Youth Seal六年級創客課程：我的青春印記-手做印章

On the eve of graduation, our sixth-graders completed a very cool maker challenge — designing and crafting their own one-of-a-kind personal seal (stamp) from scratch. More than just a class, it was the perfect graduation gift to themselves.

First, the students used CANVA to design their stamps digitally — adding chibi illustrations, personalised fonts, and their own emblems. CANVA's rich asset library and intuitive interface let everyone bring their creativity to life and produce professional-level stamp drafts with ease.

Then came the main event: using the beamo laser engraver, students watched their digital designs come to life, precisely carved by laser into a real object — the true Maker spirit of turning a virtual draft into a physical work.

This stamp is more than a craft project; it carries the little moments of campus life. With this warm, meaningful keepsake in hand, our graduates can bravely step into their next journey and stamp out a wonderful future of their own. Special thanks to the Tianwei Technology Center for transferring a laser engraver to Hsinmin so our children could enjoy this course.

中文原文 · Chinese

畢業前夕，我們六年級的準畢業生們完成了一項超級酷炫的創客任務！從無到有，大家透過數位工具與實作體驗，親手打造了世上獨一無二的專屬印章。這不僅是一堂課，更是送給自己最棒的畢業禮物！🎁

🎨 CANVA 揮灑創意，人人都是設計師！揮別傳統手繪，同學們這次使用 CANVA 進行數位排版與圖案設計。不管是加上 Q 版插畫、個性化字體還是專屬圖騰，CANVA 豐富的素材庫與直覺的操作介面，讓大家的創意完美落地，輕鬆完成專業級的印章草圖！

🛠️ beamo 雷雕實作，科技素養大爆發！設計圖完成後，重頭戲登場！同學們實際參與 beamo 雷射雕刻機的運作過程。看著自己的數位設計圖透過雷射光束，精準且俐落地雕刻成型，完美展現了將虛擬圖稿轉化為實體作品的 Maker 精神！

🎓 這顆印章，不只是普通的創客作品，更刻印著在校園裡的點點滴滴。帶著這份飽含溫度與成就感的專屬紀念品，相信畢業生們都能勇敢迎向新旅程，蓋出屬於自己的精彩未來！🙏 感謝田尾科技中心移撥給新民一台雷射雕刻機，讓新民的孩子能夠順利進行課程。
