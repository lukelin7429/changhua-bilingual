Welcome, Teacher Wu! 🎉歡迎孟姿老師加入新民大家庭

Hsinmin Elementary is thrilled to welcome a new member to our family this summer — Ms. Wu Meng-Zi (吳孟姿老師)! With her warm smile, enthusiastic teaching style, and nearly a decade of classroom experience, we know she is going to bring tremendous energy and love to our students.

About Ms. Wu 吳孟姿老師

🎓Graduated from National Taichung University of Education, Department of English
臺中教育大學英語學系
📚9 years of experience as a lower-grade homeroom teacher, nurturing young learners at Santsun Elementary (臺南三村國小) and Waipou Elementary (臺中外埔國小)
🏆Guided a student to 3rd place in the Taichung City Language Arts Competition (Writing)
指導學生榮獲臺中市語文競賽作文組第三名
✨Known for her warm smile and active, hands-on teaching style that keeps students engaged and inspired

Ms. Wu brings with her not just academic knowledge, but also a genuine love for children and a passion for helping young learners find their voice. We are so excited to have her energy, creativity, and warmth at Hsinmin. Please join us in giving her the warmest welcome!

💛💚💜 Welcome to Hsinmin, Ms. Wu! 💜💚💛

中文原文 · Chinese

新民國小今天迎來了一位全新的家庭成員——吳孟姿老師💛 孟姿老師畢業於臺中教育大學英語學系，擁有近九年的低年級導師資歷，曾服務於臺南三村國小及臺中外埔國小，並成功指導學生榮獲臺中市語文競賽作文組第三名。

孟姿老師以溫暖的笑容😊 和活潑的教學風格深受孩子喜愛，相信她的到來一定能為新民帶來滿滿的活力與愛！

新民大家庭全體歡迎孟姿老師！🎉🎉🎉

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
enthusiastic(adj.)
充滿熱忱的；充滿熱情的
An enthusiastic teacher makes every lesson feel exciting and worth looking forward to.
一位充滿熱忱的老師能讓每堂課都令人期待且充滿樂趣。
2
nurture(v.)
培育；關心呵護
Good teachers nurture students' confidence so they are not afraid to try new things.
優秀的老師會培育學生的自信心，讓他們不怕嘗試新事物。
3
experience(n.)
經驗；資歷
Ms. Wu brings nine years of classroom experience to Hsinmin, which means she has lots of great ideas.
吳老師帶著九年的教學資歷來到新民，這代表她有滿滿的好點子。
4
inspire(v.)
激勵；啟發
She inspires students to do their best by always believing in them.
她透過始終相信學生來激勵他們發揮最好的自己。
5
passionate(adj.)
充滿熱情的；熱愛的
Ms. Wu is passionate about helping young children find their voice in English.
吳老師熱衷於幫助小朋友用英語找到自己的聲音。
6
welcome(v./n.)
歡迎
We welcome Ms. Wu with open arms and look forward to learning and growing with her.
我們張開雙臂歡迎吳老師，期待與她一起學習和成長。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組，A 和 B 輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🎉 Scene · Two students talk about their new teacher on the first day of school.
情境：兩位同學在新學期第一天談論新來的老師。
A
Did you meet our new teacher, Ms. Wu? She seems so nice!
你有見到我們的新老師吳老師嗎？她看起來好親切！
B
Yes! She was so enthusiastic when she introduced herself. I could tell she really loves teaching.
有！她自我介紹的時候好熱情。我看得出來她真的很熱愛教學。
A
She has nine years of experience! That means she knows so many ways to help us learn.
她有九年的教學資歷！這代表她知道好多幫助我們學習的方法。
B
I hope she will inspire us to be better writers. I heard she coached a student to win third place in a competition!
我希望她能激勵我們把文章寫得更好。我聽說她曾指導學生在比賽中得到第三名！
A
Let's give her a big welcome and work hard in her class!
我們給她一個熱烈的歡迎，然後在她的課上認真學習吧！
B
Agreed! I think this is going to be an amazing year with Ms. Wu!
同意！我覺得和吳老師在一起的這一年一定會很精彩！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.Where did Ms. Wu Meng-Zi graduate from?
吳孟姿老師畢業於哪所大學？
NTCU 臺中教育大學 ✓ — Ms. Wu graduated from National Taichung University of Education, Department of English. 吳老師畢業於臺中教育大學英語學系。
Q2.A teacher who makes you feel excited and motivated is ______.
一位讓你感到興奮且充滿動力的老師是______。
enthusiastic 充滿熱忱的 ✓ — An enthusiastic person shows a lot of excitement and interest. An enthusiastic teacher makes you want to learn more. 充滿熱忱的人表現出很大的熱情與興趣。一位熱忱的老師讓你想學更多。
Q3.How many years of teaching experience does Ms. Wu have?
吳老師有幾年的教學資歷？
Nine years 九年 ✓ — Ms. Wu has nine years of experience as a lower-grade homeroom teacher. 吳老師擁有九年的低年級導師資歷。
Q4.To encourage someone to grow and feel confident is to ______ them.
鼓勵某人成長並感到自信，是在______他們。
nurture 培育 ✓ — To nurture someone means to care for them and help them develop their potential. 培育某人意味著關心他們並幫助他們發展潛力。
Q5.What competition did a student win under Ms. Wu's coaching?
在吳老師的指導下，學生贏得了哪個比賽？
Taichung Writing Competition 臺中市語文競賽作文組 ✓ — Ms. Wu guided a student to 3rd place in the Taichung City Language Arts Competition, Writing category. 吳老師指導學生榮獲臺中市語文競賽作文組第三名。
