Disaster-Prevention Education Takes Root防災教育向下扎根｜新民國小精彩課程紀錄

Disaster-prevention education takes root at Hsinmin. We were honoured to invite the Chelungpu Fault Preservation Park: Dr. Chung Ling-ho led a professional team into school to bring Class 403 two rich and lively disaster-prevention lessons.

Lesson one — decoding the emergency kit. Starting from everyday life, the team introduced the importance of an emergency kit. Through an interactive board game, students discussed in groups and thought hard about 'if it were you, what would you bring?' — learning practical knowledge and building their ability to respond, all in a joyful atmosphere.

Lesson two — the secrets of earthquakes and strata. With clear, easy-to-understand explanations, students learned the causes of earthquakes and the principle of plate compression, and were introduced to Taiwan's common faults — especially the Changhua Fault — so they could understand the geological conditions and potential risks near where they live. Using a sandbox experiment, they observed first-hand how strata deposit and, under compression, form folds and faults, turning abstract science into something concrete.

Both lessons combined knowledge with fun. Students not only gained a great deal but also, almost imperceptibly, built up correct disaster-prevention concepts — from understanding to practice, bringing this education truly into daily life. Thank you to Dr. Chung's team for their dedicated guidance; Hsinmin looks forward to promoting ever more diverse disaster-prevention education.

Did you know? The Changhua Fault lies in the eastern part of Changhua County, running from Dajia in Taichung in the north, southward along the western foot of the Bagua Tableland toward Nantou and Yunlin — about 82.2 km in total.

【防災教育向下扎根｜新民國小精彩課程紀錄】🌍

新民國小很榮幸邀請 #車籠埔斷層保存園區，由 #鍾令和 博士帶領專業團隊入校，為四年三班學生帶來兩節內容豐富又生動有趣的防災教育課程！

🔹第一節課｜防災包大解密

從日常生活出發，介紹防災包的重要性，透過互動式桌遊活動，讓學生分組討論、動腦思考「如果是你，會帶哪些物品？」在歡樂的氛圍中學習實用的防災知識，培養應變能力。

🔹第二節課｜地震與地層的秘密

透過淺顯易懂的講解，認識地震的成因與板塊擠壓的原理，並介紹臺灣常見的斷層分布，特別是 #彰化斷層 的特性，讓學生了解自己生活環境附近的地質條件與潛在風險。課程中也利用 #沙箱實驗，實際觀察地層沉積、擠壓後產生的摺皺與斷層現象，讓抽象的科學概念變得具體又有感！

✨兩節課程兼具知識性與趣味性，學生不僅收穫滿滿，也在潛移默化中建立正確的防災觀念。從理解到實作，讓防災教育真正落實於生活之中。

感謝鍾博士帶領的專業團隊用心指導，新民國小也期待未來持續推動更多元的防災教育，讓孩子們在安全與知識中穩健成長與茁壯。💪

彰化斷層位於彰化縣東部，北起台中大甲，沿八卦台地西側山腳向南延伸至南投、雲林一帶，全長約82.2公里。
