Exploring Lukang's Ancient Treasures! 🏙🐉恭喜．本校爭取彰化縣特色遊學課程「古剎尋龍 與神同行」

Congratulations! Hsinmin Elementary has successfully secured a spot in the Changhua County Special Field-Study Program — Lukang Elementary's "Seeking the Dragon in the Ancient Temple, Walking with the Gods" special course.

This November, the students of Class 6-1 will explore Lukang on foot, discovering the beauty of its historic architecture and time-honored heritage.

Thank you to the Changhua County Special Field-Study Center for giving children the chance to explore career paths, engage in diverse learning, and take part in cultural exchange through hands-on experience — opening up a brand-new horizon for our students. ✨

中文原文 · Chinese

恭喜！本校成功爭取到彰化縣特色遊學課程～鹿港國小：【古剎尋龍 與神同行】特色課程。

今年11月，6年1班的同學即將用小腳行鹿港，探索鹿港古色古香的建築之美。

感謝彰化縣特色遊學中心，讓孩子從體驗過程中進行職涯探索、多元學習、文化交流等，開拓學生嶄新視野。
