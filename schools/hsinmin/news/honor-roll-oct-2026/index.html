Every Effort Deserves to Be Seen 🌻👏朝會榮譽榜｜每一份努力，都值得被看見

朝會榮譽榜：為在不同領域努力的同學喝采

A new week begins with applause! 👏 At today's morning assembly, we cheered for students who worked hard, challenged themselves, and earned great results in many different areas — some through careful schoolwork, some with creative artwork, some by taking on English reading, and some by pushing their limits in sports!

This Week's Honor Roll
朝會榮譽榜
  • 🥇
    Taekwondo champion — one of our sixth graders won first place in the elementary boys' color-belt 58–68 kg division at the 44th Nantou City Sports Games Taekwondo Championship! (Office of Student Affairs)學務處獎項｜六年級同學參加「南投市第44屆全民運動會跆拳道錦標賽」，勇奪國小男子色帶組 58–68KG 冠軍
  • 📖
    Cool English Reading King — outstanding results in the SY115 contest — see the list (Office of Academic Affairs)教務處獎項｜115年度 Cool English 英閱王比賽，成績卓越
  • 📚
    Excellent summer homework — grades 4–6, semester 115-1 — see the list115-1 四、五、六年級暑假作業優秀名單
  • 🎨
    Published in Wisdom Fruit No. 167 — our students' calligraphy and paintings appeared in the county magazine — see the list本校學生優秀書畫作品刊登於第167期《智慧菓》
跆拳道錦標賽國小男子色帶組 58–68KG 冠軍

🥋 Session after session of training and drop after drop of sweat built the confidence and strength our taekwondo champion showed in competition — a true example of persistence, courage, and never giving up, earning honor for himself and for the school.

Behind every honor is a child's hard work — and teachers who guided, encouraged, and walked alongside them. ❤️ Thank you to all the teachers who coached our students!

🌱 There is no single answer in learning, and there's more than one stage. Whether it's schoolwork, the arts, English reading, or sports, anyone who is willing to work hard and take on a challenge can find their own light.

May every child shine on their own stage, and become a better self every day at Hsinmin!願每一位孩子，都能在自己的舞台上發光發熱，在新民的每一天，都成為更好的自己！
