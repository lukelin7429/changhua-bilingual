Five Days. A Lifetime of Memories. ❤️2026尼希米英語品格營 圓滿落幕

Five days ago, a group of enthusiastic young volunteers from the United States arrived at Hsinmin — full of smiles, energy, and a heart full of love for the children of Taiwan. Today, as the 2026 Nehemiah English Character Camp comes to a beautiful close, we look back with gratitude, joy, and hearts deeply moved by what we shared together.

Over five unforgettable days, our students found the courage to speak English, the confidence to connect with the world, and the warmth of friendship that crosses every border. Through games, drama, singing, and heartfelt conversations, they didn't just learn language — they learned to respect, care, cooperate, and share. English became a bridge to the world; character became a lifelong companion.

"Life influences life — these five days showed us that love truly has no borders."生命影響生命，五天的相處讓孩子感受到跨越國界的愛與付出。

What moved us most deeply is this: these American volunteers — to return to Taiwan and serve — personally fundraised for their own airfare and travel expenses. They gave up their summer break, put down their other work, and spent five precious days walking alongside our children. They brought not just English, but life itself speaking to life.

At the closing ceremony, Hsinmin Elementary presented each volunteer and partner organization with a heartfelt bilingual certificate of appreciation, a beautiful calligraphy fan personally brushed by Teacher Ye Hsin-Mei, and a special Hsinmin folder. In return, the Nehemiah team surprised us with handwritten thank-you cards and the iconic 2026 commemorative T-shirt — a keepsake that makes this friendship even more precious.

With deepest thanks to · 誠摯感謝

  • Nehemiah Foundation · 尼希米基金會
  • Gloria English Center · 葛洛麗亞美語中心
  • Tianzhong Bread of Life Christian Church · 基督教田中靈糧堂
  • All volunteers from the United States and Taiwan · 所有來自美國與臺灣的志工夥伴

The most beautiful view in education is not just what happens inside the classroom — it is a group of people coming together because of a shared belief, and through willingness to give, helping more children see the world and discover a better version of themselves.

Five days may be short,
but the friendship will never end.
The camp is over,
but the dream is only just beginning.
We look forward to next summer — see you again at Hsinmin. 🅍💙

🌏❤️五天的相遇，一輩子的感動❤️🌏

✨2026尼希米英語品格營 圓滿落幕✨

五天前，來自美國的 ADVENT 志工團隊帶著熱情與笑容來到新民；五天後，孩子們帶著更大的自信、更開闊的視野，以及滿滿的回憶，為今年的尼希米英語品格營畫下最美的句點。

這五天，我們看見孩子勇敢開口說英文、主動與外國老師互動，在遊戲、戲劇、歌曲與課程中，不只學習語言，更學會尊重、關懷、合作與分享。英語成為溝通世界的橋梁，品格則成為陪伴孩子一生的力量。

最令人感動的是，這群來自美國的青年志工，為了回到臺灣服務，自行籌措機票與旅費，放下暑假與工作，用五天的時間陪伴孩子成長。他們帶來的不只是英語，更用生命影響生命，讓孩子感受到跨越國界的愛與付出。

結業式中，新民國小特別致贈中、英文感謝狀、由書法老師葉馨鎂老師親筆揮毫的書法扇，以及新民國小專屬資料夾，向每一位默默付出的單位與志工表達最誠摯的謝意；同時，也收到尼希米團隊精心準備的感謝卡片與2026紀念T-shirt，讓這份珍貴的情誼更加深刻。

誠摯感謝 尼希米基金會、葛洛麗亞美語中心、基督教田中靈糧堂，以及所有來自美國與臺灣的志工夥伴，因為有你們，孩子們擁有了一個充滿歡笑、溫暖與收穫的夏天。

五天雖然短暫，友誼不會停留在今天
營隊雖然結束，夢想才正要啟程
期待明年夏天，我們再次相聚新民

English Learning Corner

Words About Gratitude & Connection英語學習角 · 和感謝與連結有關的小單字

Six key words — tap 🔊 to listen, then try the conversation and quiz.
六個關鍵字，點 🔊 聽發音，再試試對話和小考。

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
closing(adj./n.)
結業；結束的；閉幕的
The closing ceremony is a special event that marks the end of a program or camp.
結業典禮是標誌著一個活動或營隊結束的特別儀式。
2
gratitude(n.)
感謝；感恩
Gratitude is the feeling of being thankful — it is one of the most important things we can share with others.
感謝是心懷感恩的感受——這是我們與他人分享最重要的事之一。
3
volunteer(n.)
志工；義工；自願者
A volunteer is someone who freely gives their time and energy to help others without being paid.
志工是指自願付出時間和精力幫助他人，而不收取報酬的人。
4
calligraphy(n.)
書法；書寫藝術
Calligraphy is the art of beautiful handwriting — in Taiwan, it is often done with ink and a brush.
書法是美麗書寫的藝術——在台灣，通常用墨水和毛筆來書寫。
5
souvenir(n.)
紀念品；紀念物
A souvenir is a small object that you keep to remember a special person, place, or event.
紀念品是你保存下來，用來記住某個特別的人、地方或事件的小物品。
6
impress(v.)
使感動；使印象深刻
The volunteers' kindness and dedication truly impressed everyone at the closing ceremony.
志工們的善良與奉獻精神真的讓結業式上的每個人都深受感動。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🏫 Scene · Two students talk about the Nehemiah Camp closing ceremony.
情境：兩位同學聊起尼希米品格營的結業式。
A
The closing ceremony was so moving! I almost cried.
結業式好感人！我差點哭出來。
B
Me too. I didn't know the volunteers paid for their own tickets just to come here!
我也是。我不知道志工們是自己出機票錢來這裡的！
A
That's incredible. I really want to show my gratitude to them.
真的難以置信。我真的很想向他們表達感謝。
B
Our school gave them a beautiful calligraphy fan as a souvenir. That was a great idea!
學校送給他們一把書法扇作為紀念品。這個主意真好！
A
I hope they come back next year. They really impressed me with their kindness.
我希望他們明年還回來。他們的善心真的讓我印象深刻。
B
Same! Being a volunteer is such a wonderful thing. Maybe one day we can be volunteers too.
我也是！當志工是很棒的事。也許有一天我們也能當志工。
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.The event at the end of the camp where everyone says goodbye is called the ______ ceremony.
在營隊結束時大家道別的活動叫做______典禮。
closing 結業 ✓ — A closing ceremony marks the end of an event or program. 結業典禮標誌著一個活動或課程的結束。
Q2.Someone who gives their free time to help others without pay is a ______.
自願付出時間幫助他人、不收報酬的人是______。
volunteer 志工 ✓ — Volunteers choose to give their time freely to help others. 志工選擇自由地付出時間幫助他人。
Q3.What special gift did Hsinmin give the volunteers as a keepsake?
新民送給志工什麼特別的紀念品？
A calligraphy fan 書法扇 ✓ — Hsinmin presented a calligraphy fan brushed by Teacher Ye Hsin-Mei as a souvenir. 新民致贈由葉馨鎂老師揮毫的書法扇作為紀念品。
Q4.The feeling of being thankful for what others have done is called ______.
對他人所做之事心懷感激的感受叫做______。
gratitude 感謝；感恩 ✓ — Gratitude is a warm feeling of appreciation for kindness you have received. 感謝是對你所獲得的善意感到溫暖的感受。
Q5.A small object kept to remember a special event is a ______.
用來記住特別事件而保存的小物品是______。
souvenir 紀念品 ✓ — A souvenir helps you remember and treasure a special memory. 紀念品幫助你記住並珍藏一段特別的回憶。
