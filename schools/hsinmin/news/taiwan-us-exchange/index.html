Taiwan–US Student Exchange · Music Without Borders台美學生交流｜國際領航・在地共好

The moment the music began, language, nationality and distance all seemed to melt away. Over two days, students and parents from Taipei American School visited three of our partner schools — Hsinmin Elementary, Wenxing High School and Ershui Junior High — for a Taiwan–US student exchange filled with music, culture and friendship. Through performing, making and sharing together, the children came to know one another, and discovered the same passion and dreams shining across very different cultural backgrounds.

We warmly welcomed the visiting students and families from Taipei American School: Morgan Emmie Lin, Tracey Chen, Athena Chang, Evangeline Chang, Emma DeMelo, Apryl Song, Jianni Song, David Lo and Emily Fisher.

On the morning of June 12, the exchange opened here at Hsinmin Elementary. With warm, professional hosting from Director Cai-yun, students, teachers and parents from different cultures quickly settled into the spirit of the day. Our Dance Club opened with “Golden,” bursting with confidence and energy; the String Ensemble played “Contentment (知足),” a gentle melody carrying gratitude; and the Choir sang “Sing for Peace,” lifting voices for peace and hope.

The most moving moment came with a full ensemble of “A Million Dreams” and the Taiwanese classic “Hot Rice Dumplings (燒肉粽).” Though it was the children's first time performing together — with almost no rehearsal — they built an instant rapport through the music and completed this rare cross-cultural performance side by side. Director Yi-liang joined the players and Director Guan-yu accompanied on piano, building a bridge of friendship note by note.

Hands-on culture followed the music. In the Maker class, led by Teacher Yi-jun, visitors crafted their own personalised seals using laser engraving — and, touchingly, several of our sixth-grade graduates returned to campus to act as little teachers, guiding the guests through each step. In the calligraphy class led by Teacher Xin-mei, the children took up the brush themselves and stamped their finished works with their own hand-carved seals, completing a one-of-a-kind cultural keepsake. Thanks too to Teacher Xuan-qi for handling photography, sound and translation throughout.

In the afternoon the group moved on to Wenxing High School, warmly received by Principal Huang Shu-mei and her administrative team. The Wenxing String Ensemble and flute performed “I Only Care About You (我只在乎你)” with elegant, refined musicianship — and many of the performers were in fact Hsinmin graduates, now studying there. Once again the combined “A Million Dreams” and “Hot Rice Dumplings” rang out, connecting children from different schools and cultures through a shared song.

The students also tried a corn-husk doll handicraft, showing real creativity and care in their making, and toured Wenxing's school history room to learn about the school's founding story and its educational ideals.

That evening, teachers and students stayed at Ershui Junior High, beginning another leg of the journey full of surprises. Principal Wu Jian-zhong thoughtfully arranged accommodation and dinner, so the visiting students and parents felt warmly cared for after their long day.

A highlight of the evening was a stargazing lesson. Principal Wu Bing-lian of Beidou Junior High personally brought professional astronomy equipment to Ershui to teach, leading the children to explore the vast night sky through telescopes and sharing a wealth of astronomical knowledge and a spirit of discovery. Under the starlight the children learned not only science, but also witnessed the warmth and generosity of educators collaborating across schools.

On the morning of June 13, the exchange continued at Ershui Junior High. The children handcrafted “bamboo-frame night lamps (籠仔篙小夜燈),” a craft rich in local character, and learned about the history of the Bage Canal — the wisdom of the early settlers who opened the land and built its waterways — feeling the depth of Changhua's cultural heritage.

Hsinmin once again offered “Contentment,” and the combined “Hot Rice Dumplings” with the American School students rang out one more time — the signature shared melody of the whole journey. The Ershui Junior High wind band then brought lively favourites including the “Doraemon” theme and “Until the End of the World” from Slam Dunk, drawing enthusiastic applause.

Musical exchange has no full stop. The children have already promised to play strings and winds together again in the future, keeping alive the friendship that music has begun. This precious journey grew from a wonderful connection brought about by Principal Huai-xuan — and what we saw was far more than a single visit; it was a learning stage built by a community of educators working together for the children.

Music knows no borders, yet in a single second it can connect heart to heart. Education can cross national lines — letting the world walk into our campus, and letting our children walk out into the world. We look forward to continuing this partnership, so that more children can see the world through exchange, and so that the world can see Taiwan.

音樂響起的那一刻，語言、國籍與距離，彷彿都消失了。感謝台北美國學校學生與家長蒞臨新民國小、文興高中、二水國中，展開一場充滿音樂、文化與友誼的台美學生交流之旅。

兩天的交流活動中，孩子們透過音樂、藝術與文化體驗彼此認識，也看見了不同文化背景下共同的熱情與夢想。

感謝台北美國學校（Taipei American School）學生與家長蒞臨交流：Morgan Emmie Lin、Tracey Chen、Athena Chang、Evangeline Chang、Emma DeMelo、Apryl Song、Jianni Song、David Lo、Emily Fisher。

☀️【6/12 上午｜新民國小】交流活動由新民國小揭開序幕！感謝采筠主任擔任活動主持人，以溫暖且專業的引導串起整場交流活動，讓來自不同文化背景的師生與家長迅速融入交流氛圍。

💃 新民舞蹈社帶來《Golden》，展現孩子們的自信與活力。🎻 弦樂團演奏《知足》，以悠揚旋律傳遞感恩與珍惜。🎤 合唱團獻唱《Sing for Peace》，唱出和平與希望。

🎶 最令人感動的時刻，莫過於《A Million Dreams》與《燒肉粽》大合奏。雖然是第一次合作演出，幾乎沒有彩排，但孩子們很快便透過音樂建立默契，共同完成這場難得的跨國交流演出。益樑主任也加入演奏行列，冠妤主任鋼琴伴奏，與孩子們一起用音樂架起友誼的橋梁。

📸 感謝宣綺老師擔任攝影、音控及翻譯工作，讓交流活動順利進行。🛠️ 創客課程由怡君老師帶領印章製作，運用鐳雕技術打造專屬印章。更特別的是，多位六年級畢業生特地返校擔任小老師，陪伴來訪學生一起完成作品。

✍️ 書法課程由馨鎂老師帶領，孩子們親手揮毫，並將自己創作的書法作品蓋上專屬印章，完成獨一無二的文化紀念。

🏫【6/12 下午｜文興高中】下午來到文興高中進行交流活動，感謝黃淑玫校長及行政團隊（洪英慈主任、施志勳主任、圖書館張主任）熱情接待，讓交流旅程持續精彩展開。

🎻 文興弦樂團與長笛演奏《我只在乎你》，展現優雅細膩的音樂實力，許多一同演出的都是新民畢業的學長姐。🎶 隨後再次進行《A Million Dreams》與《燒肉粽》聯合演出，讓不同學校、不同文化背景的孩子們透過音樂彼此連結，感謝伊君老師指揮，畢業生子芸爸爸協助攝影。

🌽 孩子們也體驗玉米娃娃手作課程，在動手創作中展現創意與巧思。🏛️ 參觀文興高中校史室時，透過創校歷史與校史介紹，深入認識文興高中一路走來的教育精神與辦學理念。

🌙【6/12 晚間｜二水國中】晚間師生入住二水國中，展開另一段充滿驚喜的學習旅程，感謝吳建中校長悉心安排住宿與晚餐，讓遠道而來的學生與家長感受到滿滿的溫暖與照顧。

🔭 晚間觀星課程更是一大亮點。北斗國中吳炳連校長特別攜帶專業觀星設備親自前往二水國中授課，帶領孩子們認識浩瀚星空，透過望遠鏡觀察夜空中的奧秘，分享豐富的天文知識與探索精神。在星光陪伴下，孩子們不僅學習科學知識，也感受到教育夥伴跨校合作、無私分享的熱情與用心。感謝采筠主任及麗棋組長全程陪同與協助，讓交流活動更加圓滿順利。

🌅【6/13 上午｜二水國中】第二天的交流活動持續在二水國中進行！💡 孩子們親手製作充滿地方特色的「籠仔篙小夜燈」，並透過課程認識八堡圳歷史，了解先民開墾與水利建設的智慧，感受彰化豐厚的人文底蘊。

🎻 新民國小再次帶來《知足》演奏，🎶 與美國學校合奏的《燒肉粽》再度響起，成為這趟交流旅程最具代表性的共同旋律。🎺 二水國中管樂隊則帶來《哆啦A夢》及《灌籃高手－直到世界盡頭》等精彩曲目，獲得現場熱烈掌聲。

💙 感謝采筠主任協助樂器運送及活動聯繫工作，並全程陪伴交流團隊，讓兩天的活動得以順利進行。也感謝賴昱興老師、王伊君老師、音樂總監韻綾、芬珍、羿情活動前的練習與準備。

音樂交流沒有句點🎵 孩子們已經相約未來再次進行弦樂與管樂合奏，讓這段因音樂而結下的友誼持續延續下去。

💖 這段珍貴的交流旅程，起於懷萱校長牽起的美好緣分。我們看見的不只是一次參訪活動，更是一群教育夥伴共同為孩子打造的學習舞台。

音樂可以不分區域，卻能在一秒鐘連結彼此的心。教育可以跨越國界，讓世界走進校園，也讓孩子走向世界。期待未來持續攜手合作，讓更多孩子透過交流看見世界，也讓世界看見臺灣🌍✨
