English Learning Corner

Words About Learning & Success英語學習角 · 和學習與成功有關的小單字

Six key words from this story, each with an example sentence — then a five-question quiz. Tap 🔊 to listen.
六個關鍵字，每個字配一個例句，最後有五題小考。點 🔊 聽發音。

1Key Words
關鍵字 · 點 🔊 聽單字，再點小喇叭聽整句
1
vocabulary(n.)
詞彙量；單字
Building a strong vocabulary is the key to reading and speaking well in English.
建立豐富的詞彙量是讀好英語、說好英語的關鍵。
2
certification(n.)
認證；合格證書
Passing the certification proves that you have learned and remembered the words.
通過認證證明了你已學會並記住了那些單字。
3
perseverance(n.)
毅力；堅持不懈
Memorising vocabulary takes perseverance — a little practice every single day.
背單字需要毅力——每天都要練習一點點。
4
milestone(n.)
里程碑；重要時刻
Passing the English vocabulary certification is an important milestone for every student.
通過英語單字認證對每位同學來說都是一個重要的里程碑。
5
accumulate(v.)
累積；積累
If you accumulate ten new words every day, you will have a huge vocabulary in one year.
如果你每天累積十個新單字，一年後你將擁有龐大的詞彙量。
6
encourage(v.)
鼓勵；激勵
Thank you to all the parents who encouraged their children to keep learning every day.
感謝所有每天鼓勵孩子持續學習的家長。
2Try the Conversation
對話練習 · 兩個人一組，A 和 B 輪流唸；點 ▶ 可整段播放
🎭 Scene · Two students talk after receiving their vocabulary certification results.
情境：兩位同學在拿到英語單字認證成績後聊天。
A
Congratulations! I heard you passed the English vocabulary certification with a high score!
恭喜你！我聽說你以高分通過了英語單字認證！
B
Thank you! It was not easy, but I accumulated a little vocabulary every day.
謝謝你！這並不容易，但我每天都累積一點詞彙量。
A
That shows great perseverance! Vocabulary really is the foundation of learning English.
這展現了很好的毅力！詞彙量真的是學英語的基礎。
B
Yes! This certification feels like such an important milestone for me.
是啊！這張認證對我來說感覺像是一個重要的里程碑。
A
Did your parents encourage you a lot? I think family support makes such a big difference.
你的父母有鼓勵你很多嗎？我覺得家人的支持非常重要。
B
Absolutely! Let's keep going — maybe next time I can aim for the gold medal!
當然！讓我們繼續努力——也許下次我可以目標金牌！
3Quick Quiz
五題小考 · 選出最合適的英文單字，點 🔊 可聽題目
Q1.All the words that someone knows or uses in a language is their ______.
一個人在某種語言中知道或使用的所有單字，就是他們的______。
vocabulary 詞彙量 ✓ — Vocabulary refers to all the words a person knows and can use in a language. 詞彙量是指一個人在某種語言中知道並能使用的所有單字。
Q2.An official document that proves you have passed a test or completed a course is a ______.
證明你通過測試或完成課程的正式文件叫做______。
certification 認證 ✓ — A certification is an official document showing you have completed a standard or qualification. 認證是一份顯示你已達到某一標準或資格的官方文件。
Q3.To slowly build up more and more of something over time is to ______ it.
隨著時間慢慢建立越來越多的某樣東西，就是______它。
accumulate 累積 ✓ — To accumulate means to gradually collect or increase something over time. 累積是指隨著時間逐漸收集或增加某樣東西。
Q4.An important stage or event that marks progress in a journey is a ______.
標誌著旅程中進展的重要階段或事件叫做______。
milestone 里程碑 ✓ — A milestone is a significant event or stage that shows important progress. 里程碑是一個顯示重要進展的重要事件或階段。
Q5.How many students in Grade 1 received a Bronze medal?
有幾位一年級同學獲得了銅牌？
6 ✓ — Six Grade 1 students received Bronze medals: 梁允嘉, 李宇安, 黃崇棠, 林妍歆, 劉厚賢, and 王芃彌. 共有六位一年級同學獲得銅牌。
