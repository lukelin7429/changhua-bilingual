Huang Huai-Hsuan黃懷萱 校長

"Let every child grow up sustainable, diverse, and excellent — and find their own voice along the way."

Welcome to Hsinmin Elementary School. Nestled in Tianzhong — the rice barn of Changhua, watered by the three-hundred-year-old Bagua Canal — our campus has, since 2003, grown into a place where music and language give children wings. Our String Ensemble and Flute Ensemble carry the sound of Hsinmin from the county stage to the heart of Taipei; our bilingual and international education opens windows to the wider world.

We believe an education built on sustainability, diversity, quality, and excellence nurtures learners who are creative, independent, and kind. Here, reading, ecology, the arts, and technology grow side by side — and every child is encouraged to discover, and to share, their own voice.

歡迎來到新民國小。我們坐落於有「田中米倉」之稱的彰化縣田中鎮，受三百年八堡圳灌溉文化滋養。自 2003 年立校以來，新民以音樂與語言為孩子插上翅膀——弦樂團與長笛合奏團，把新民的樂聲從彰化縣的舞台一路帶到台北車站；雙語與國際教育，則為孩子打開看見世界的窗。

我們深信，以永續、多元、優質、卓越為精神的教育，能培養具創意、自主與責任感的孩子。在這裡，閱讀、生態、藝術與科技並肩成長，我們鼓勵每一個孩子發現自己的聲音，也勇敢地與世界分享。
