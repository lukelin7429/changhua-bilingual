A note from Mr. Dave · 新民的好朋友

“Hello, hello, Hsinmin! It's your Mr. Dave here. Hope you all had a great Lunar New Year!”

哈囉新民！我是 Mr. Dave。希望大家都有個愉快的農曆新年。我和家人共度了美好的時光，也吃到了我最愛的台式炸雞！今天，我要跟大家分享農曆新年後的另一個重要慶典——元宵節 (Lantern Festival)，這裡有五個單字想跟大家分享。

Five Festival Words五個元宵單字

Rice Ball
湯圓 / 元宵
“I like to eat rice balls with my family.”
Lantern
燈籠
“We carry colorful lanterns at night.”
Riddle
謎語 / 燈謎
“We play riddle games with our friends.”
Temple
寺廟
“We pray for good luck at the temple.”
Full Moon
滿月
“The full moon is big and bright tonight.”
祝新民全體師生元宵節快樂！
“I hope you guys have a great day!” — Mr. Dave
