🎻 String & Flute Ensemble

新民弦笛樂團
← All Lessons

At Hsinmin Elementary, we have some really special ensembles — our String Ensemble and Flute Ensemble. Imagine kids holding violins, cellos, and flutes, and playing amazing music together. They practice during ensemble times and perform in lots of places, like train stations, temples, and school events — it's like having a mini-concert! We've won many awards in competitions, which is super exciting. Joining the String Ensemble or Flute Ensemble at Hsinmin means you'll play amazing music, make new friends, and have a lot of fun. It's like being part of a musical adventure at Hsinmin Elementary.

在新民國小，我們有一些非常特別的樂團——我們的弦樂樂團和長笛樂團。想像孩子們拿著小提琴、大提琴、長笛，一起演奏很好聽的音樂。他們在樂團時間練習，在許多地方表演，像是火車站、宮廟和學校活動，這就像是舉辦一場迷你音樂會！我們在比賽中贏得了許多獎項，這真是令人興奮。加入新民的弦樂樂團或長笛樂團，意味著你將演奏動聽的音樂、結交新朋友，並且擁有很多樂趣——這就像參與新民國小的音樂冒險！

Meet the String Ensemble認識弦樂團

Have you ever heard of a string ensemble? It's a group of musicians who play string instruments like violins, violas, and cellos. When they play together, it creates beautiful music that can make you feel happy, sad, or excited. String ensembles can play different types of music, from classical to pop. They can perform in big concert halls or even in small groups at weddings or parties. Playing in a string ensemble is a great way to learn teamwork and communication skills — and a fun way to make music and express yourself.

你曾經聽說過弦樂團嗎？它是一群演奏如小提琴、中提琴和大提琴等弦樂器的音樂家。當他們一起演奏時，會創造出美妙的音樂，這種音樂可以讓你感到快樂、悲傷或興奮。弦樂團可以演奏不同類型的音樂，從古典到流行。他們可以在大型音樂廳表演，或者甚至在婚禮或派對上以小組形式演出。參與弦樂團是學習團隊合作與溝通技巧的絕佳方式，也是一種有趣地製作音樂、表達自己的方式。

Meet the Flute Ensemble認識長笛樂團

Do you know what a flute ensemble is? It's a group of people who play flutes together. There can be different types of flutes in a flute ensemble; some common ones are the piccolo, alto, and bass flutes. Flute ensembles can play different types of music, like classical or folk music. Flute ensembles are important because they make beautiful music together. So next time you hear a group of flutes playing together, you'll know it's a flute ensemble.

你知道什麼是長笛樂團嗎？那是一群一起演奏長笛的人。一個長笛樂團中可以有不同類型的長笛；一些常見的長笛包括短笛、中音長笛和低音長笛。長笛樂團能演奏不同類型的音樂，像是古典音樂或民間音樂。長笛樂團之所以重要，是因為它們能夠一起創造美妙的音樂。所以，下次當你聽到一群長笛一起演奏時，你就會知道那是一個長笛樂團。
