Temple

奉天宮 · 剪黏課

03 / 05

For their Jiannian (Cut-and-Paste Ceramic) Art Class, the students visited Fengtian Temple. They looked for mythical creatures in the temple. They used tablets to take pictures and share with everyone. The temple's decorations and carvings were beautiful and lifelike. The teachers and masters taught them many new things.

在剪黏課程的最後，學生們參觀了奉天宮。他們在廟宇裡尋找神獸。他們使用平板電腦拍照並與大家分享。廟宇裡的裝飾和雕刻既精美又栩栩如生。老師和師傅的講解讓他們收穫良多。
