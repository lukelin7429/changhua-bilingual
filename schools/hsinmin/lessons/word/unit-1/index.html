Word 01
sweep (v.)
掃
Every day, students sweep away fallen leaves on school grounds.
每天學生打掃校園的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.
台灣的學生打掃校園，保持校園的乾淨整齊。
Word 02
cross (v.)
穿越
Watch out for students crossing the road.
當心正在過馬路的學生。
Crossing guards stop traffic to let students cross the street safely.
交通導護阻擋車輛讓學生安全過馬路。
Word 03
Chinese class
國語課
The students are in Chinese class.
同學們正在上國語課。
They learn Chinese idioms in Chinese class.
他們在國語課學習中文的成語。
Word 04
math class
數學課
The students are in math class.
學生們正在上數學課。
The teacher teaches math in math class.
老師在數學課教數學。
Word 05
music class (n.)
音樂課
Each student plays the recorder in music class.
在音樂課時，每一位學生都在吹直笛。
They also learn how to read music in music class.
他們在音樂課也學習如何看樂譜。
Word 06
calligraphy (n.)
書法
The students are quietly practicing Chinese calligraphy.
同學們安靜地練習寫書法。
The calligraphy teacher is patiently instructing her students.
書法老師很有耐心地指導她的學生。
Word 07
check out
借書
The students check out books from the library at recess.
同學們利用下課到圖書室借書。
After the librarian checks out the students' books, they can take them home to read.
向圖書館員辦理借閱手續後，同學們可以將書帶回家看。
Word 08
recess (n.)
下課時間
After the bell rings for recess, most students go outside to play.
下課鈴聲響起後，大多數學生都在外面玩。
Recess is when kids can play outside, run around, and have fun.
下課時間是孩子們可以在外面玩耍、跑來跑去、玩得開心的時候。
Word 09
stretch (v.)
伸展
The students stretch in class.
學生在課堂上做伸展律動。
They follow the instructions of the teacher to stretch in class.
他們在課堂上照老師的指示做伸展運動。
Word 10
nature (n.)
自然
The students take a test on nature.
學生們在考自然。
The students have been studying nature in class.
學生在上自然課。
Word 11
eye test (n.)
視力檢查
The students take an eye test in the nurse's office.
學生在護士辦公室接受視力檢查。
They take an eye test to check their vision.
他們進行視力測試來檢查他們的視力。
Word 12
nurse's office (n.)
保健室
The students get their height measured in the nurse's office.
學生在護士辦公室測量身高。
The students take an eye test in the nurse's office.
學生在護士辦公室接受視力檢查。
Word 13
row (n.)
排
The students in the first row wear colorful hats and make hand gestures to the music.
第一排的學生戴著五顏六色的帽子，隨著音樂做手勢。
The students in the back row sing and dance with their hands.
後排的學生們唱著歌並且手舞足蹈。
Word 14
restroom (n.)
洗手間
The student guides introduce a restroom on campus designed with a Mondrian style.
學生導遊介紹校園內一間採用蒙德里安風格設計的衛浴。
The restroom has red, yellow, blue, and black lines, making it a fun and artistic space.
衛浴採用了紅、黃、藍、黑色的線條，營造出充滿趣味和藝術感的空間。
