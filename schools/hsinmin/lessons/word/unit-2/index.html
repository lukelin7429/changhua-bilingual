🚒 Safety & Emergency

安全與應變

From fire drills to CPR — the English every Hsinmin student needs to stay safe and help others.

Word 01
professional development
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都有教師專業訓練課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
在專業訓練課程中，老師可以學到新的教學技巧與策略。
Word 02
evacuation drill
逃生演習
The students gather for an evacuation drill.
學生們聚集在一起進行逃生演習。
The evacuation drill teaches students how to safely get off a tour bus in an emergency.
逃生演練教導學生如何在緊急情況下安全下車。
Word 03
fire extinguisher (n.)
滅火器
The students practice using fire extinguishers.
學生練習使用滅火器。
They learn how to use fire extinguishers in case of an emergency.
他們學習如何在緊急情況下使用滅火器。
Word 04
fire station (n.)
消防局
Students visit the fire station to learn about staying safe on the water.
學生們參觀消防局，了解水上安全知識。
They explore life rafts, life jackets, and lifebuoys at the fire station.
他們在消防局探索救生筏、救生衣和救生圈。
Word 05
CPR
心肺復甦術
Students are learning how to perform CPR.
學生們正在學習如何做CPR。
They use CPR manikins to practice CPR.
他們使用人體模型來練習做CPR。
Word 06
Heimlich maneuver (n.)
哈姆立克急救法
Medical workers are instructing the students on how to perform the Heimlich maneuver.
醫護人員正在指導學生如何進行哈姆立克急救法。
The Heimlich maneuver can save people from choking.
哈姆立克急救法可以避免人們窒息。
Word 07
first aid skills (n.)
急救技能
The students are learning how to use an AED machine for their first aid skills training.
學生們正在學習如何使用AED機器進行急救技能訓練。
Medical workers instruct them on developing their first aid skills.
醫護人員指導他們演示急救技能。
Word 08
drill (n.)
演習
Schools sometimes hold drills to teach students how to act quickly and safely during a disaster.
學校有時會舉行演習，教導學生如何在災難中快速、安全地採取行動。
During the drills, students learn life-saving skills, such as using a fire hose and performing CPR.
在演習期間，學生學習救生技能，例如使用消防水帶和進行心肺復甦術。
