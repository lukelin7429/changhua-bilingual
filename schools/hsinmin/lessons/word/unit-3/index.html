Word 01
jump rope competition
跳繩比賽
The students participate in a jump rope competition.
學生們參加跳繩比賽。
Many students participate in the jump rope competition.
許多學生參加跳繩比賽。
Word 02
talent show
才藝表演
A talent show is when students perform their special skills for others to enjoy.
才藝表演是學生表演特殊才藝給別人欣賞的機會。
During the talent show, students play different musical instruments.
在這場才藝表演中，同學們演奏不同的樂器。
Word 03
flash mob performance (n.)
快閃表演
When they do the flash mob performance, passersby enjoy their music.
當他們進行快閃表演時，路人都會欣賞他們的音樂。
The students perform at Taipei Main Station in a flash mob performance.
學生們在台北車站進行快閃表演。
Word 04
string instrument (n.)
弦樂器
The students perform string instruments on stage.
學生們在舞台上演奏弦樂器。
The students have been practicing their string instruments for a long time.
學生們已經練習弦樂器很久了。
Word 05
flute (n.)
長笛
The students play flutes on stage.
學生們在舞台上吹奏長笛。
They play their flutes in front of an audience.
他們在觀眾面前吹奏長笛。
Word 06
perform (v.)
表演
While performing, the young musicians dance to the music.
表演時，年輕的音樂家隨著音樂跳舞。
There is a recorder ensemble performing with various types of recorders.
有一個直笛合奏團，用各種類型的直笛進行表演。
Word 07
cup dance (n.)
杯子舞
A group of students are performing a cup dance.
一群學生正在表演杯舞。
They use cups as props in their cup dance, creating sounds to match the rhythm of the music.
他們在杯舞中使用杯子作為道具，創造出與音樂節奏相匹配的聲音。
Word 08
guzheng (n.)
古箏
The guzheng is a traditional Chinese musical instrument.
古箏是中國傳統樂器。
In the guzheng club, everyone practices playing the guzheng.
在古箏社裡，大家都在練習演奏古箏。
Word 09
dance routine (n.)
舞蹈動作
Their dance routine is full of energy and creativity.
他們的舞蹈充滿活力和創造力。
The students are performing a dance routine in their student activity center.
學生們正在學生活動中心表演舞蹈。
Word 10
competition (n.)
比賽
The school hosts a basketball shooting competition.
學校舉辦籃球投籃比賽。
Students compete in the competition to see who can make the most baskets within a set time.
學生們參加比賽，看誰能在規定時間內投進最多的籃子。
Word 11
ballroom dance (n.)
國標舞
The student is performing a ballroom dance solo with graceful movements.
學生們正在以優美的動作表演交際舞獨奏。
His hands are up as if he were dancing with a partner in a ballroom dance.
他的手向上，彷彿正與舞伴一起跳交誼舞。
Word 12
flute (n.)
長笛
The school flute team performed a surprise music flash mob at the school gate.
校笛隊在校門口表演了一場驚喜的音樂快閃。
The students played their flutes beautifully, bringing joy to everyone passing by.
學生們優美的笛聲為路過的人帶來歡樂。
Word 13
guzheng (n.)
古箏
The students perform gracefully on the guzheng.
學生們優雅地演奏古箏。
The guzheng is a string instrument that produces a soft and peaceful sound.
古箏是一種能發出柔和、平和聲音的弦樂器。
