Word 01
archery (n.)
射箭
The students practice archery, using bows to shoot arrows at targets.
學生們練習射箭，用弓把箭射向目標。
The goal of archery is to hit your target with an arrow.
射箭的目標是用箭射中目標
Word 02
cooking class (n.)
烹飪課
The students make candied haws in cooking class.
烹飪課上，學生製作糖葫蘆。
They learn how to make candied haws in cooking class.
他們在烹飪課上學習如何製作糖葫蘆。
Word 03
reward (n.)
獎勵
The students receive a reward on Children's Day for being good role models.
學生們在兒童節會因為表現良好而獲得獎勵。
They receive their reward on stage.
他們在舞台上獲得獎勵。
Word 04
birthday (n.)
生日
The man celebrates his 107th birthday.
該男子慶祝了自己的 107 歲生日。
The students wish the man a happy birthday.
學生們祝福這個男人生日快樂。
Word 05
raffle draw (n.)
抽獎
The school runs a raffle draw.
學校舉辦抽獎活動。
The teachers draw a raffle number during the raffle draw.
抽獎時老師們抽出抽獎號碼。
Word 06
canoe (v.)
划獨木舟
The students learn how to canoe.
學生學習如何劃獨木舟。
They practice canoeing in a swimming pool.
他們在游泳池裡練習劃獨木舟。
Word 07
bake (v.)
烘焙
The students are learning how to bake.
學生們正在學習如何烘烤。
You can make sweet treats by baking.
你可以通過烘烤來製作甜點
Word 08
archery (n.)
射箭
The students learn how to do archery at school.
學生在學校學習如何射箭。
They try to shoot the balloons when they do archery.
他們射箭時嘗試射氣球。
Word 09
tour (n.)
參觀
The foreign visitor is given a tour of the school by both teachers and the principal.
老師和校長都帶領外國訪客參觀了學校。
During the tour, they show the visitor interesting sights around the school.
在參觀過程中，他們向遊客展示了學校周圍有趣的景點。
Word 10
drone (n.)
四軸飛行器
The students assemble drones in class.
學生們在課堂上組裝四軸飛行器。
They fly their drones in class and outside.
他們在課堂上和課外操作四軸飛行器。
Word 11
museum (n.)
博物館
Students from Hsinmin Elementary School visited a museum to learn about traditional Nanguan and Beiguan performing arts.
新民國小學生參觀博物館，了解南關、北關傳統表演藝術。
During the fun trip, they explored the museum and learned to play various traditional instruments.
在這次有趣的旅行中，他們參觀了博物館並學習演奏各種傳統樂器。
Word 12
temple (n.)
寺廟
The students from Hsinmin Elementary School visit a local temple to learn about its history.
新民國小的學生參觀當地寺廟，了解寺廟的歷史。
They also explore the beautiful artwork within the temple.
他們也探索寺廟內美麗的藝術品。
