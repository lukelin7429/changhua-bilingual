🎎 Culture & Festivals

文化傳統節慶

Red rice cakes, lion dances, glove puppetry — Taiwan's living culture explained in bilingual words.

Word 01
red rice cake (n.)
紅龜粿
The students make red rice cakes.
學生們製作紅龜粿。
They put the red rice cake into circular molds.
他們將紅龜粿放入圓形模具中。
Word 02
nine square grid (n.)
九宮格
The students have fun with the nine-square grid.
學生們對九個方格很感興趣。
They throw a ball at the nine-square grid in front of them.
他們向面前的九個方格丟了一個球。
Word 03
mini egg cake (n.)
雞蛋糕
The school makes mini egg cakes.
學校製作雞蛋糕。
The students enjoy eating the mini egg cakes.
學生們很喜歡吃雞蛋糕。
Word 04
Children's Day (n.)
兒童節
The students celebrate Children's Day at school.
學生們在學校慶祝兒童節。
They receive popcorn for Children's Day.
他們在兒童節收到爆米花。
Word 05
mochi (n.)
麻糬
Teachers and students pound mochi.
老師和學生一起搗麻糬。
They pound the mochi until it has the right consistency.
他們搗麻糬直到達到理想的黏稠度。
Word 06
glove puppetry
布袋戲
Budaixi, also known as glove puppetry, is a traditional form of puppetry in Taiwan.
布袋戲，又稱布袋戲，是台灣傳統的木偶戲形式。
Glove puppetry usually features elaborate sets and dramatic music.
布袋戲通常以精緻的佈景和戲劇性的音樂為特色。
Word 07
lion dance (n.)
舞獅
Some students perform the lion dance, while other students play percussion instruments.
有些學生表演舞獅，有些學生則演奏打擊樂器。
The lion dance is a traditional dance performed during the Lunar NewYear.
舞獅是農曆新年期間表演的傳統舞蹈。
Word 08
Christmas (n.)
聖誕節
The students performed Christmas carols on stage, sharing the joy of the season with everyone.
學生們在舞台上表演聖誕頌歌，與大家分享節日的喜悅。
They sang and played instruments beautifully to celebrate Christmas together.
他們唱歌、彈奏美妙的樂器，一起慶祝聖誕節。
Word 09
storyteller
說故事的人
A good storyteller teaches a lesson and entertains you at the same time.
一個擅長講說故事的人可以教導大家道理，同時又兼具娛樂大眾。
The storytellers can use real or fictional information to tell a story.
說故事的人可能用真實或者是虛構的情節來說故事。
