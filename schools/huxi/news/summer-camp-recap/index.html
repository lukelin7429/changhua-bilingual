A Look Back at Summer Camp

湖西國小暑期營隊回顧
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Here is our final look back at the Huxi summer camp! The days stayed colourful right to the end.

湖西國小暑期營隊最後回顧！活動依舊豐富多彩。

There was art that was both creative and fun; lively, healthy P.E. activities; rich, sweet hands-on food-and-farming lessons; and, of course, plenty of English, maths and everyday-knowledge learning. The children even went out beyond the school gate to watch a ball game.

兼具創意和樂趣的美術創作，活力滿點的健康體育活動，豐盛甜蜜的食農教學手工製作，當然還有英語、數學和生活知識的學習，更有機會踏出校園去欣賞球賽。

A summer of fun and a summer of learning — neither one at a discount.

暑假的樂趣和學習，都不打折。
