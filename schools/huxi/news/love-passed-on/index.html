Huxi's Care, Passed On

湖西用心，愛在傳承
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Our thanks to Director Yue-zu, who carries on the spirit of Huxi's life education programme and leads the volunteer team in walking alongside our children, so that this warmth and care are passed on.

感謝月足主任延續學校生命教育的理念與精神，帶領志工團隊持續陪伴孩子，讓這份愛與溫暖得以傳承。

With new classes added this year, the teaching team planned ahead — sharing out the work early and preparing every story with care. That is how Huxi's children get to learn through stories, grow through companionship, and feel the goodness of life through kindness.

面對新增班級，教育團隊超前部署、及早規劃，完成分工、用心備課。湖西的孩子才能在故事中學習、在陪伴中成長、在關懷中感受生命的美好。

Huxi's children are lucky to have you watching over them. When the school puts its heart in, children grow with confidence and parents can feel at ease.

湖西的孩子，有您們的守護，真的很幸福。湖西用心，成長安心，家長放心。
