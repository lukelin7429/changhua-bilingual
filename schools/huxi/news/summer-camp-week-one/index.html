Summer Camp, Week One

暑期營隊第一週小回顧
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

A quick look back at week one of the Huxi summer camp.

湖西國小暑期營隊第一週小回顧。

The days ran from an energetic morning activity into teamwork games that helped everyone make friends, then creative art and design, and lessons full of nature, English and everyday knowledge.

從早上的活力晨間活動，到交流感情的團康遊戲，特別重視團隊合作，讓大家交到新朋友；接著是創意的美術與設計，以及充滿自然、英語和生活知識的課程。

On the college-run camp, the children balanced learning and fun, and spent a full, happy stretch of the summer.

大專營的孩子們學習和樂趣兼顧，度過充實又快樂的暑假。
