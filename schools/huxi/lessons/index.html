Ways to Learn

從校園生活學英文

Real school, real words.

每支影片、每份教材，都取材自湖西真實的校園日常。

38 Videos · 6 Themes
Course 01
Word of the Day
每日一字

Thirty-eight bilingual videos filmed around the Huxi campus and grouped into six themes — the school day, PE and play, arts and making things, field trips and places, food and celebrations, and health and helping out. Each word is embedded straight from YouTube with two example sentences taken from the video description.38 支雙語影片，全部在湖西校園實地拍攝，依六大主題分類——校園的一天、運動與遊戲、藝術與手作、校外教學、美食與節慶，以及健康與服務。每個單字直接嵌入影片，並附兩句取自影片說明欄的例句。

Start Learning →
30 Episodes · For Teachers
Course 02
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers, taught by Sarah Thomas across 10 topics and 30 numbered episodes — from greetings and roll call to exam instructions. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.給老師使用的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學，共 10 大主題、30 支編號影片——從問候點名到考試指令。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

Start Learning →
13 Episodes · By Office
Course 03
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

"Broadcast English" — thirteen short episodes in which each school office and section introduces, in English, the announcements it makes over the public-address system, from the curriculum section to the counselling room. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.「廣播英語」——13 支短片，由各處室、各組用英語介紹平時透過校園廣播宣布的內容，從教務處教學組到輔導室。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

Start Learning →
Foreign English Teacher
Course 04
Meet Our Foreign English Teacher
認識我們的外師

Huxi's foreign English teacher, Hannah Mae Lopez Tolosa, keeps a class website of songs, stories, games and lesson materials for students and families. It opens in a new tab.湖西的外籍英語教師 Hannah Mae Lopez Tolosa 老師，經營一個班級網站，放了歌曲、故事、遊戲與課堂教材，供學生與家長使用。點擊後在新分頁開啟。
