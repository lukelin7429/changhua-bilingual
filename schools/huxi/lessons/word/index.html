Around the School Day
校園的一天 · 9 字
WORD 01
recess (n)
下課時間
After the bell rings for recess, most students go outside to play.
下課鈴聲響起後，大多數學生都在外面玩。
Recess is when kids can play outside, run around, and have fun.
下課時間是孩子們可以在外面玩耍、跑來跑去、玩得開心的時候。
WORD 02
sweep (v)
掃地
Every day, students sweep away fallen leaves on school grounds.
每天學生打掃校園的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.
台灣的學生打掃校園，保持校園的乾淨整齊。
WORD 03
workbook (n)
習作本
The students are working on their workbooks.
學生們正在寫習作。
The teacher is checking the students' work.
老師正在改學生的習作。
WORD 04
worksheet (n)
學習單
The students do a worksheet.
同學們在填寫學習單。
They use crayons to do the worksheet.
他們用蠟筆來填寫學習單。
WORD 05
write (v)
寫字
The students write in their workbooks.
同學們在習作本上寫字。
They are focused on writing neatly.
他們很專注把字寫工整。
WORD 06
quiz (n)
小考
The students are taking a quiz in Chinese class.
同學們正在小考。
They listen to questions and write down their answers during the quiz.
在小考的時候，他們先聽題目然後寫下答案。
WORD 07
club (n)
社團
The school has an after-school English club.
學校在放學後有個英文社團。
The foreign English teacher is teaching kids about Christmas in the club.
外國老師在社團中指導學生有關聖誕節的知識。
WORD 08
tablet (n)
平板
The students use a tablet to study English.
同學們使用平板來學習英文。
There are many different ways to study English on a tablet.
有很多方式可以在平板上學習英文。
WORD 09
professional development
教師專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都有教師專業進修課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
在專業進修課程中，老師可以學到新的教學技巧與策略。

WORD 10
run (v)
跑步
The students run on the track.
同學們在跑道上跑步。
They run on the track for exercise.
他們在跑道上跑步運動。
WORD 11
jump rope
跳繩
The kids jump rope to get some exercise.
孩子們跳繩做運動。
When the weather is nice, you can go outside to jump rope!
天氣好的時候，你可以到外面去跳繩。
WORD 12

The students dance to the music.
學生們跟隨著音樂節奏跳舞。
The students dance together in class.
學生們在課堂上一起跳舞。
WORD 13
seesaw (n)
翹翹板
To play on the seesaw, two people sit on opposite ends and take turns going up and down.
蹺蹺板的玩法是兩個人坐在板子的兩端輪流上下擺動。
The kids are having fun playing on the seesaw.
小朋友在蹺蹺板上玩得很快樂。
WORD 14
soccer (n)
足球
Soccer is a very popular sport.
足球是很受歡迎的運動。
The students are having a lot of fun playing soccer.
同學們正享受踢足球的樂趣。
WORD 15
basketball (n)
籃球
The students play basketball outside.
同學們在外面打籃球。
They shoot the ball when playing basketball.
他們在打籃球的時候投籃。
WORD 16
swimming class
游泳課
The students learn how to swim in swimming class.
同學們在游泳課時學習如何游泳。
They learn the basics of swimming in swimming class.
他們在游泳課時學習游泳的基本動作。

Arts & Making Things
藝術與手作 · 7 字
WORD 17
origami (n)
摺紙
Origami is the art of folding paper to create art.
摺紙是將紙摺成作品的藝術。
Origami artists use paper to create flowers, stars, and even animals such as cranes and fish.
摺紙藝術家使用紙張創造花朵、星星，甚至像鶴與魚的動物。
WORD 18
calligraphy (n)
書法
The students write calligraphy for the upcoming lunar new year.
同學們寫書法準備即將到來的農曆新年。
They write calligraphy for lunar new year decorations.
他們寫書法作為農曆新年的裝飾品。
WORD 19
dye (v)
染布
The students dye cloths with tumeric.
學生們用薑黃染布。
Dying the cloth with tumeric makes it bright yellow.
用薑黃染布會讓布料顏色呈現鮮亮的黃色。
WORD 20
punch needle embroidery
俄羅斯刺繡
The students do punch needle embroidery in class.
同學們在課堂上做俄羅斯刺繡。
They use yarn for punch needle embroidery.
他們用毛線來做俄羅斯刺繡。
WORD 21
magic trick
魔術
The students go to watch magic tricks.
同學們去看魔術表演。
The magician performs magic tricks in front of the students.
魔術師在學生前面表演魔術。
WORD 22
memory game
記憶遊戲
Memory games are a fun way to learn the names and shapes of some musical instruments.
記憶遊戲是學習樂器的名稱和認識不同樂器形狀的有趣方式。
In this memory game, students will choose pairs of cards to flip over and win by revealing all the matching pairs.
在這個記憶遊戲中，學生將選擇成對的卡片翻開，當卡片配對他將就獲勝。
WORD 23
string quartet (n)
弦樂四重奏
A string quartet performs for the students in the schoolyard.
弦樂四重奏在校園裡為學生表演。
The students listen attentively to the string quartet's performance.
學生們聚精會神地聆聽弦樂四重奏的演奏。

Field Trips & Places
校外教學 · 6 字
WORD 24
field trip
校外教學
The students go on a field trip to a historical site.
學生們的校外教學活動是到一處古蹟參觀。
They learn many new things on their field trip.
他們在校外教學時學習到很多新的事物。
WORD 25
tour (n)
參觀導覽
The students take a tour of the museum.
同學們參觀博物館。
They wear headsets while taking the tour.
他們在參觀時戴著耳機。
WORD 26
museum (n)
博物館
Huxi Elementary School took its students on a field trip to a museum dedicated to Nanguan and Beiguan music.
湖西小學帶學生參觀南管北管音樂博物館。
During the fun trip, they explored the museum and learned about various traditional costumes and instruments.
在愉快的旅程中，他們參觀了博物館，並了解了各種傳統服飾和樂器。
WORD 27
theater (n)
劇院
Visiting the National Taichung Theater was a great experience for the students of Huxi Elementary.
參觀台中國家歌劇院對於湖西國小的學生來說是個很棒的經驗。
They were able to explore the unique architecture and learn about different performing arts in the theater.
他們能夠探索獨特的建築並了解不同的表演藝術。
WORD 28
sugar refinery
糖廠
Three students visit their local sugar refinery.
三位學生參觀當地的糖廠。
During their visit to the Xihu Sugar Refinery, they learn about different types of sugars.
在參觀西湖糖廠期間，他們了解了不同類型的糖。
WORD 29
loach (n)
泥鰍
The students catch loaches with shallow buckets.
學生們用很淺的水桶來抓泥鰍。
They catch these loaches as part of their field trip.
抓泥鰍是他們校外教學的活動之一。
