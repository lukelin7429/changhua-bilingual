I.

Welcome to Huxi

歡迎來到湖西國小
Welcome to Huxi Elementary School · 湖西國小簡介影片

A place where education and nature keep company, and every child has a stage of their own.

在這裡，教育與大自然為伴，每個孩子都有屬於自己的舞台。

Huxi Elementary School stands in the quiet west of Xihu Township, Changhua. The campus is a green, growing thing — a small ecological oasis where black-crowned night herons, grey-capped woodpeckers and ring-necked doves build nests among the trees, and where, in May and June, children crowd around the wax trees to find rhinoceros beetles. A pond called Xie-Shi Garden is the class outdoor laboratory for water plants and animals; along the courtyard grow banana, waxberry, papaya and longan, free for the picking at break time.

湖西國小位在彰化縣溪湖鎮西隅，校園綠意扶疏、生態豐富。常見黑冠麻鷺、小啄木鳥、環頸斑鳩在校園築巢；每年 5、6 月，光蠟樹上還會出現獨角仙，讓孩子驚喜不已。「謝施園」是學生觀察水生動植物的園地，中庭兩側種著香蕉、楊梅、木瓜、龍眼，是下課採摘的幸福樂園。

It is a small school — around 200 students and about 30 staff — and it is built to look after each of them closely. A 200-metre PU running track and a level grass field give students and neighbours a wide, even space to run; after-school care runs to 5:30 p.m.; and the whole staff works towards one simple promise: parents at ease, children glad to be here.

這是一所小校——約 200 位學生、教職員工約 30 位——而學校用心把每個孩子帶好。200 公尺 PU 跑道與平整無高低差的操場草皮，是師生與社區揮灑汗水的地方；課後照顧辦到下午 5:30；全體教職員朝著同一個心願努力：家長放心、學生開心。

Discover a place where education harmonizes with nature, and every child’s potential is nurtured and celebrated.
在這裡，教育與大自然和諧共處，每個孩子的潛能都被珍視、被看見。來湖西探索、學習、成長吧！
II.

A Forest School in the Middle of Town

溪湖鎮上的森林小學

Come in through the gate and the noise of the street falls away.

從校門走進來，街上的喧囂就安靜了下來。

Xie-Shi Garden, the Orchard Courtyard, the Old Trees
謝施園 · 中庭果樹區 · 老樹與綠廊

The campus is planted like a garden and used like a laboratory. Xie-Shi Garden is a small pond where classes watch water plants and pond life through the year; the courtyard orchard puts fruit within a child’s reach; and shaded brick walkways wind between old trees where the birds nest. It is, quite deliberately, a beautiful place to be a child — and the reason Huxi is known around Xihu as “the forest school in the middle of town.”

校園像花園一樣被細心經營，也像實驗室一樣被使用。謝施園是一方小水池，四季都能觀察水生動植物；中庭果樹區把果實放在孩子伸手可及之處；紅磚綠廊在老樹之間蜿蜒，樹上就是鳥兒築巢的地方。這是我們刻意經營、讓孩子在其中長大的一方天地——也是溪湖人口中「鎮上那所森林小學」的由來。

III.

What Huxi Is Known For

四個我們最用心經營的方向

A small school with a lot going on.

學校雖小，能做的事情很多。

A Reading School, in Two Languages
中英文閱讀特色學校

Reading is Huxi’s school-based curriculum. The school built a new library, stocked it with graded Chinese and English books, wrote its own ten-volume idiom textbook (200 idioms a term, grades 2–6) and runs regular literacy checks. In 2020 Huxi was funded under the county’s “Books of Love” English reading programme and received 24 boxes of English picture books. 湖西以閱讀為校本課程：新建圖書館、購置中英文分級圖書、自編十冊成語教材（二～六年級，每學期 200 個成語），並落實識字量檢測。109 年通過彰化縣「英文愛的書庫」補助計畫，獲贈 24 箱英文繪本。

Everyone a Swimmer
游泳特色學校，人人都是水中蛟龍

Taiwan is an island, and Huxi has taught swimming for seventeen years. With Ministry of Education support, grades 3–6 swim free of charge, twelve lessons a term, working step by step through the school’s own water-safety and swimming indicators. By graduation 85% of students can swim 25 metres — well above the national target. 台灣四面環海，湖西開辦游泳教學已十七年。積極爭取教育部補助，讓三～六年級學生免費學游泳，每學期 12 堂課，循序漸進學會水中自救與游泳技能。畢業前能游 25 公尺者達 85%，遠高於教育部規定的比率。

Clubs That Draw Out Potential
多元學習社團，激發生命潛能

Every child’s gifts are different lengths, like fingers on a hand. To give them room to explore, Huxi runs sports clubs (table tennis, fitness, inline skating), arts clubs (string orchestra, dance), science clubs (Gigo blocks, tchoukball) and a Summer Fun learning programme. 每個孩子的潛能，就像五根手指頭，長短不一、各有所長。為了讓孩子有更多探索與展能的機會，學校開辦運動性社團（桌球、體適能、直排輪）、藝文性社團（弦樂團、舞蹈社）、科學性社團（智高積木、巧智球），以及夏日樂學暑期學藝課程。

A Kitchen of Its Own, and Care for Every Child
自辦營養午餐，帶好每位學生

Huxi cooks its own school lunch and has been rated Outstanding in the county lunch review — for hygiene, sourcing, menu design, nutrition education and student satisfaction alike. Rice steam drifts through the classrooms at noon, the food still warm. Alongside it: after-school care to 5:30 p.m., the Ministry’s learning-support programme, clubs and holiday activities. 湖西自辦營養午餐，在縣府午餐考核中獲得「特優」——環境衛生、食材管理、菜單設計、營養教育、學生滿意度皆名列前茅。中午飯菜還溫熱，教室飄著米食香氣。此外並辦理課後照顧到下午 5:30、教育部學習扶助計畫、多元社團與寒暑假學藝活動。

V.

Parents at Ease, Children Glad to Be Here

家長放心，學生開心

One promise, in six words.

一句話，是我們對每個湖西孩子與家庭的承諾。

Read well · Swim strong · Explore widely · Be well looked after
我們希望每個湖西的孩子，六年後帶著這些走出校門

A strong habit of reading in both Chinese and English; the confidence of a swimmer; a club or a talent that is truly their own; and the steadiness that comes from a warm meal, a caring teacher and a school that has time for them. Huxi is small on purpose — so that no child is a stranger.
扎實的中英文閱讀習慣、游泳的自信、一項真正屬於自己的社團或才藝，以及一頓溫熱的午餐、一位用心的老師、一所有時間陪伴他們的學校所帶來的安定。湖西刻意維持小校規模——好讓沒有一個孩子是陌生人。
