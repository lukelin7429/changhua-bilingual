Meet Our Principal

認識校長 · 游美雯 Yu Mei-wen
An educator from Changhua, devoted to arts, the environment, and bilingual learning.
一位來自彰化的教育工作者，深耕藝術、環境與雙語教育。

Principal Yu Mei-wen is a native of Changhua County. Across a long career as a teacher, director, and principal in Changhua schools, she has been recognized for excellence in teaching, environmental education, and the arts — and now leads Beidou Elementary with a vision of cultivating beauty and knowledge in every child.

游美雯校長是彰化縣人。在彰化多所學校歷任教師、主任與校長的漫長生涯中，她在教學、環境教育與藝術領域屢獲肯定，如今帶領北斗國小，秉持「在每個孩子身上培養美感與學識」的願景。

北斗藝揚好美力，藏修游息品書香
"Cultivating Beauty and Knowledge in Every Student."
