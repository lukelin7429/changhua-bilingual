01

A Century by the Old Town

學校簡介 · About Our School
A school woven into the heart of Beidou for over a century.
一所深耕北斗、與小鎮共同成長超過百年的學校。

Beidou Elementary School is located in Beidou Township, southeast of Changhua County. Our school is an important part of the community, situated near Beidou Junior High School and the National Beidou Senior Home Economics & Commercial Vocational High School. Beidou, once a key transportation hub for southern Changhua, continues to play an important role in local politics and business.

北斗國小位於彰化縣東南方的北斗鎮，是社區的重要組成部分，毗鄰北斗國中與國立北斗高級家事商業職業學校。北斗過去是南彰化的重要交通中心，至今仍在地方政治與商業中扮演重要角色。

The school is surrounded by historical sites, public offices, and businesses, creating a rich environment for learning. With strong support from parents and the community, Beidou Elementary is known for academic success and helping students develop a wide range of skills.

學校周圍有歷史遺址、公家機關與商業設施，為學習創造了豐富的環境。在家長與社區的強力支持下，北斗國小以學術成就與培養學生多元能力而聞名。

We enhance education through arts, aesthetics, reading, digital literacy, and the exploration of local and diverse cultures — developing students with many skills, preparing them for the future, and making learning a meaningful part of daily life.

我們透過藝術、美感、閱讀、數位素養，以及在地與多元文化的探索提升教育品質，培養具備多元能力的學生，幫助他們迎向未來，讓學習成為生活中有意義的一部分。

02

Taiwan-American Eco-School

臺美生態學校 · A Model for Sustainability

Beidou Elementary promotes sustainability through its Taiwan-American Eco-School Initiative, focusing on sustainable food practices and waste reduction. By integrating the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) into everyday learning, the school is shaping a greener future and inspiring young minds to become responsible global citizens.

北斗國小透過臺美生態學校計畫推動永續，聚焦永續食物與廢棄物減量兩大主題，將聯合國永續發展目標（SDGs）融入日常學習，打造更綠色的未來，培養學生成為負責任的全球公民。

Sustainable Food: Local Ingredients永續食物：在地好食材（SDG 2 & 12）

Students and teachers grow coriander, strawberries, bok choy, and cherry tomatoes, gaining hands-on experience in food production. The school also promotes food appreciation and waste reduction — encouraging students to avoid picky eating and to take home leftover lunch food.

師生共同種植香菜、草莓、小白菜與小番茄，從做中學了解食物來源。學校也提倡珍惜食物與減少浪費，鼓勵學生不偏食，並將午餐剩食打包帶回家。

SDG 2 消除飢餓 Zero HungerSDG 12 負責任消費 Responsible Consumption
Waste Reduction & Recycling廢棄物減量與回收：小行動，大影響（SDG 11 & 13）

The school organizes waste sorting and recycling and promotes a plastic-free lifestyle. To deepen environmental education, Beidou partners with local groups — the Tzu Chi Foundation teaches students to reuse packing straps, and the Maple Eco-School offers lessons on making eco-friendly soap.

學校定期舉辦垃圾分類與資源回收，並推廣減塑生活。為深化環境教育，北斗與在地團體合作——慈濟基金會教導學生重複利用打包帶，楓樹環保小學堂教授製作環保皂。

SDG 11 永續城鄉SDG 13 氣候行動
Silver Certification榮獲生態學校銀牌認證

Thanks to the dedication of teachers, students, parents, and community volunteers, Beidou earned Silver Certification in the Taiwan-American Eco-School Initiative in 2024. Here, sustainability is more than a concept — it is a way of life.

在老師、學生、家長與社區志工的共同努力下，北斗國小於 2024 年獲得臺美生態學校銀牌認證。在北斗，永續不只是理念，而是一種生活方式。

03

Learning Safety Through Play

防災教育 · Disaster Preparedness
🔥 Fire Safety火災安全

Fire safety is specialized knowledge, yet fire scenarios are diverse and variable. Through a fun, gamified approach, students improve their fire-escape knowledge and learn to choose the right response.

火災安全是一項專業知識，但火災情境多樣多變。我們透過遊戲化的方式，讓學生在輕鬆有趣中增進火災逃生知識，學會正確應變。

⛈️ Thunderstorm Safety雷雨安全

Thunderstorms develop quickly and are hard to predict. Using realistic simulations, students learn how to assess risks and make the safest decisions when a storm strikes.

雷雨發生迅速且難以預測。我們透過模擬真實情境的遊戲，讓學生學會判斷風險，在雷雨來襲時做出最安全的應變。

🌏 Earthquake Safety地震安全

Earthquakes cannot be precisely predicted, but an early-warning system buys precious seconds. Through gamified scenarios, students learn the correct actions to take the moment a warning sounds.

地震無法精準預測，但地震預警系統能爭取寶貴的幾秒鐘。我們透過遊戲化情境，讓學生學會在預警響起時做出最正確的反應。

04

Winter Camp with Teacher Anna

英語冬令營 · Cooking & Eco-Vocabulary

In our English Winter Camp, a foreign teacher leads students through hands-on activities — including cooking the Filipino dessert bilo-bilo — while picking up everyday English and eco-vocabulary along the way.

在英語冬令營中，外師帶領學生動手做菲律賓甜點 bilo-bilo，一邊烹飪一邊學習生活英語與環保詞彙。

🌱 Eco-Vocabulary環保詞彙小教室

Recycle — things like bottles, paper, or cans that you would normally throw away, turned into new things that can be used again.

回收 Recycle——瓶子、紙張或罐頭之類本來會丟掉的東西，把它們變成可以再次使用的新東西。

Garbage / Trash — things you don't want anymore and throw away; not useful anymore.

垃圾 Garbage——不再想要、丟掉的東西，不再有用了。

06

A Market Full of Love

愛心義賣市集 · Second-Hand Charity Fair

With a loud gong, Beidou Elementary opened its second-hand charity fair. Students ran booths filled with toys, school supplies, and daily items — and a special highlight was the English-speaking booths run by foreign teachers, where every transaction was conducted in English. The Principal, parents, and local leaders struck the "love gong" to open the fair, which began with a string-music performance.

隨著一聲響亮的鑼聲，北斗國小的二手愛心義賣市集熱鬧開幕。學生們經營著擺滿玩具、文具與日常用品的攤位，特別的亮點是外師負責的英語攤位，所有交易都以英語完成。校長、家長與地方領袖一同敲響「愛的鑼聲」揭幕，活動以弦樂表演揭開序幕。

The proceeds were donated to World Vision to support children in need, and the Parent Association sold gift boxes to raise funds for disadvantaged students. The fair taught students to value resources, care for others, and make thoughtful spending decisions.

義賣所得捐給世界展望會，幫助需要幫助的孩子；家長會也販售禮盒，為校內弱勢學生籌款。這場市集教導學生珍惜資源、關懷他人，並培養明智的消費觀念。
