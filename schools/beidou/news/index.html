Campus · 校舍重建·3 Nov 2026

A New Chapter for Building DD 棟教室拆除重建 · 動土典禮

The county government held the groundbreaking ceremony for the reconstruction of Building D at Beidou Elementary, presided over by County Magistrate Wang Hui-mei. Building D opened in 1975 with four classrooms and grew through eight separate extensions into the block we know today; now, to keep students and staff safe and to raise the quality of teaching, the county has committed NT$206.66 million to replace it. The new four-storey building will hold more than twenty rooms, including flexible teaching spaces and a library classroom, with the surrounding grounds redesigned to feel more open and welcoming. Completion is expected by the end of 2028. During construction the county is also funding temporary classrooms, utility relocation and temporary restrooms so that teaching can carry on as normal. The Magistrate noted that education is a hundred-year undertaking, and that giving children a safe, well-equipped place to learn is a responsibility the county does not take lightly.

彰化縣政府於北斗國小舉行「D 棟教室拆除重建工程」動土典禮，由縣長王惠美親自主持，宣告校園建設邁入新的里程碑。D 棟教室自民國 64 年四間教室啟用，歷經八次擴建才形成目前的方直外觀，承載了最多師生的回憶；為確保師生安全及提升教學品質，縣府編列 2 億 666 萬餘元縣款推動拆除重建，預計 117 年底完工。重建後將提供四層樓、含多元教室與圖書教室等合計 20 餘間教室的全新校舍，周邊並做整體規劃，使教學環境更為開闊與友善。重建期間，縣府同步補助安置教室、水電遷移與臨時廁所經費，讓師生維持正常良好的教學環境。王縣長表示，教育是百年大計，為孩子打造安全、完善的學習環境，是縣府責無旁貸的責任。

Award · 全國賽·18 Oct 2026

National Champions in Tee-Ball樂樂棒球隊勇奪全國冠亞軍

At the national Youth Cup tee-ball championship, Beidou's players met the best teams in the country and came home with the national title in the fifth-grade division and runner-up in the sixth. Behind a trophy stand a great many people: the three teachers who travelled with the squad, the parents who rode along to look after the players, who brought bananas to keep energy up and sorted out lunches, and everyone who stood and cheered. The children trained through winter cold and summer heat, diving for balls on the grass after school, swinging again and again — ordinary days that quietly added up to this.

在 115 年度青年盃全國樂樂棒球錦標賽中，北斗國小樂樂棒球隊憑藉平日刻苦的訓練與永不放棄的精神，勇敢迎戰全國各地好手，締造輝煌戰績：五年級組全國冠軍、六年級組全國亞軍！一座獎盃的背後，是無數人的默默付出——感謝綉紋老師、華軒老師、育瑋老師一路隨隊指導；感謝隨車協助、細心照護每一位小選手的家長；感謝貼心準備香蕉為孩子補充體力、熱心張羅午餐的家長；更感謝所有到場加油吶喊的家長。還記得凜冽寒風中的寒訓與烈日當頭的暑訓嗎？孩子們從不輕言放棄，下課後在草皮上一次次奮力撲接，在揮汗如雨中反覆練習打擊——那些看似平凡的日常，其實都在為今天一點一滴累積力量。

The moment everyone will remember came in the final. In the semi-final one player had thrown himself after ball after ball until both arms were raw and bleeding. Patched up, he insisted on starting the final anyway; the dressing kept peeling off in the red dirt until he simply tore it away, handed it to his coach and said, “Coach, don't bother — I don't need it.” In the bottom of the third his team trailed 7–13. The batters ahead of him clawed back three runs to make it 10–13, and with the bases loaded he was the last man up. He looked at the sky and swung. The ball shot past the fielders and kept rolling; the throw came in to second, but he never slowed, never looked back, and ran on to third — the throw came again, the third baseman could not hold it, and he sprinted for home. 14–13. The whole ground erupted. Named MVP, he walked over to his coach and said: “Coach, did you know — when you're really happy you cry. I cried coming home.” Congratulations to every one of our players. Winning is worth cheering, but more precious still is not flinching in the face of difficulty, not giving up after a setback, and going forward together.

最令人動容的，是冠軍戰那個永不放棄的身影。四強賽中，有一位孩子一次又一次奮不顧身地撲接，雙臂早已破皮流血；簡單包紮後，他仍堅持在冠軍戰上場，身上沾滿紅土，手臂上的敷料掉了又貼、貼了又掉，最後他索性將敷料撕下交到教練手中，堅定地說：「教練，不用貼了！」冠軍戰三局下半，北斗後攻，比數 7：13 落後 6 分。前八棒積極進攻追回 3 分，追至 10：13，此時壘上滿壘，最後一棒上場的，正是那位雙手流血仍堅持奮戰的孩子——黎Ｏ佑。他拿起球棒，眼睛望向天空，大棒一揮！強勁的飛球劃破球場，落地後持續向外野滾去，外野手全力追球、回傳二壘，他卻沒有停下腳步，頭也不回地衝向三壘；球再傳三壘，三壘手漏接，他繼續往前衝，奮力衝回本壘——14：13，逆轉勝！全場沸騰。當大會為他掛上 MVP 獎牌時，他走向教練說：「教練，你知道嗎？當一個人很高興的時候會哭，回到本壘我哭了。」恭喜北斗國小樂樂棒球隊！願孩子們永遠記得：勝利固然值得喝采，但比勝利更珍貴的，是面對困難時不退縮、面對挫折時不放棄，以及與夥伴攜手向前的勇氣。

Nutrition · 營養教育·10 Oct 2026

Serving by the 3-2-1 Rule午餐打菜比例 321 · 均衡吃得飽

Over two lunchtimes this week we invited the dietitian from the Beidou Junior High central kitchen into our third-grade classrooms to teach “serving 3-2-1”. The idea is simple enough to remember at the serving counter and precise enough to matter: fill the plate from the six food groups in proportion — three parts grains and starches, two parts vegetables, one part beans, fish, egg or meat. Serve it that way and you eat well, you eat in balance, and you still finish full.

10 月 6 日、10 月 8 日兩天中午，邀請北斗國中央廚慧薰營養師，進到三年級班級做「打菜比例 321」的營養教育宣導。「打菜比例 321」指的是：將六大類食物依比例打菜——全穀雜糧類 3 份、蔬菜類 2 份、豆魚蛋肉類 1 份。吃得精巧，吃得均衡，也吃得飽。

Health Education · 反毒宣導·24 Sep 2026

The Anti-Drug Truck Rolls In反毒教育行動車到校

Our thanks to the county government, whose anti-drug education truck visited the school and gave our fifth-graders a genuinely eye-opening session. The programme had three parts. Films: real cases and animation made the true face of illegal drugs immediately clear. Lifelike models: replica samples showed how new synthetic drugs are disguised as snacks and drinks, so children learn to look twice at anything handed to them. Interactive games: playing along, students practised the actual skill of saying no. Thank you to the county for the careful planning and to the volunteer instructors who ran it. Together — school, teachers and families — we are weaving a protective net around our children, and teaching them to say “no” with confidence.

感謝縣府的反毒教育行動車來到學校，為五年級的孩子們帶來一場超精彩、超震撼的反毒宣導活動！這次的內容非常豐富——吸睛影片觀賞：用真實案例與動畫，讓學生秒懂毒品的真面目；擬真模型解說：透過仿真模型，揭開新興毒品偽裝成零食、飲料的糖衣，建立敏銳的防範心；趣味互動遊戲：在邊玩邊學的互動中，加深「拒絕毒品」的技巧與正確觀念。再次感謝縣府的用心規劃與志工老師們的推廣，讓我們親師生攜手，共同織起緊密的校園防護網，陪伴孩子們健康成長，一起向毒品說「不」！

Soap From Recycled Cooking Oil回收廚餘油製環保皂
