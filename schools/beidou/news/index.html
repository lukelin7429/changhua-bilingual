English Camp · 英語營·15 Jul 2026

A Six-Day English Summer Camp六天英語夏令營

Our most popular foreign teacher, Ms. Anna, wrapped up a joyful six-day English Summer Camp last Friday. For six days the campus was filled with children's laughter and confident English conversation. Rather than memorizing vocabulary at their desks, students learned English through hands-on, cross-curricular activities: they became little scientists — shaping clay volcanoes and following a chemical "eruption" entirely in English — and even made Pastillas de Leche, a traditional Filipino milk candy, mixing and kneading along to English steps on the screen. Thank you, Ms. Anna, and every enthusiastic camper — keep this curiosity and love of English alive!

上週五，本校最受歡迎的外師 Anna 老師帶領的「六天英語夏令營」圓滿落幕！這六天裡，校園總是充滿孩子們的歡笑聲與自信的英文對話。Anna 老師精心設計了跨領域素養課程，把語言學習融入動手做的體驗：孩子們化身小小科學家，用黏土捏出火山模型，並用全英文理解化學反應；還挑戰製作菲律賓傳統甜點 Pastillas de Leche（牛奶糖），一邊看著投影幕上的英文步驟，一邊親手揉出甜蜜的滋味。謝謝 Anna 老師的用心，也謝謝每一位熱情參與的孩子——在英文學習的路上，繼續保持這份好奇心與熱情！

College Youth Summer Camp大專青年暑期工讀營隊

The much-anticipated College Youth Summer Camp opened last week! It was heart-warming to hear the campus fill with children's laughter once again. Graduation · 畢業典禮·12 Jun 2026

Brave Adventures, No Regrets勇敢冒險 青春不留白 · 畢業典禮

Under the theme "Brave Adventures, a Youth Without Regrets," Beidou Elementary and its affiliated kindergarten held a warm and joyful graduation ceremony. From the presentation of diplomas and scholarships to blessings from younger students, heartfelt speeches, and the singing of the school song, every moment was moving — and the string, dance, and music performances showed the confidence and many talents of Beidou's children. Thank you to county officials, the local community, the Parents' Association, and every family who attended. May our graduates move forward with gratitude, chase their dreams bravely, and soar on the journey ahead.

北斗國民小學暨附設幼兒園「勇敢冒險 青春不留白」畢業典禮，在溫馨與歡樂中圓滿落幕。從頒發畢業證書、各項獎學金，到在校生祝福、畢業生感恩致詞與校歌傳唱，每個環節都充滿感動；穿插的弦樂、舞蹈與音樂表演，更展現北斗孩子多元學習的成果與自信風采。感謝縣府長官、地方各界、家長會與所有到場的家長——願孩子們懷抱感恩、勇敢逐夢，在未來的旅程中展翅高飛。

Donation · 愛心捐贈·4 Jun 2026

A Gift to the Library校友捐贈多媒體設備

Our heartfelt thanks to alumnus Mr. Yang Feng-lu for his generous donation, which helped the school add multimedia equipment and books to the library's presentation space. Beyond reading, storytelling, and multimedia teaching, the space now doubles as an indoor venue for P.E., movement, and group activities on rainy days — and, with a large display, for video lessons, online talks, and digital learning. One act of kindness has not only enriched our reading environment but given our children a more complete and flexible space to learn. Thank you, Mr. Yang, for your care and support for your alma mater.

感謝本校校友楊豐祿先生慷慨捐贈經費，協助學校增置圖書室展演空間的多媒體設備與圖書資源。這處空間除了供學生閱讀、說故事及多媒體教學使用，遇天候不佳時還可作為室內體育、律動與團體活動空間；結合大型顯示設備後，更能辦理視訊教學、線上講座與數位學習課程。一份善意，不只豐富了閱讀環境，更讓孩子擁有更完善、更具彈性的學習空間。感謝楊豐祿先生對母校的關懷與支持，嘉惠學子。

Award · 榮譽·2 Jun 2026

County Champions: Radio Drama交通安全廣播劇 · 全縣冠軍

Congratulations to our sixth-grade team, whose outstanding performance won First Place in the county at the Changhua Traffic-Safety Radio Drama competition! After weeks of practice, the children used lively voices and wonderful teamwork to turn road-safety knowledge into a fun radio story — revealing the hidden dangers of a large vehicle's blind spots and turning path. Thank you to their teacher for the dedication, and cheers to our little "radio stars"! Their recording later aired on Kuo Sheng Radio, inviting everyone to learn traffic safety and get home safe.

恭喜本校六年級團隊參加「彰化縣交通安全教育廣播劇」比賽，以出色的表現榮獲全縣冠軍！孩子們在準備期間反覆練習，用生動的聲音表情與絕佳默契，把交通安全知識化為精彩有趣的廣播故事，破解大型車「內輪差」與「視野死角」的隱形危機。感謝老師的用心付出，也讓我們一起為這群「廣播小達人」按讚喝采！錄音作品後續也在國聲廣播電台播出，邀請大家一起開心學交安、出門保平安。
