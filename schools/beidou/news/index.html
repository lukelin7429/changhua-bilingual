Seeing Taiwan from Above小小齊柏林・大大看見臺灣

Look up and you see blue sky and clouds — but what do you see when you look down on the land we live on? On 19 November the “Seeing Taiwan” campus screening programme flew into Beidou Elementary. A lecturer from the Chi Po-lin Foundation guided our students through the late director's aerial footage in four parts — Mountains, Rivers, Coasts and Cities — a journey across Taiwan's skies without ever boarding a plane. This was more than a film session: the lecturer kept the children observing, asking questions and sharing ideas, so that from one breathtaking shot to the next they saw both the beauty of Taiwan and the environmental challenges it faces. We hope the day planted a seed of care for the land in every child. Our thanks to the Foundation and its lecturers, to teacher 翁友梅, to Ruitai Construction, and to classes 3A, 4D, 5B and 5E.

當我們抬頭仰望，看見的是藍天白雲；但如果換個角度從天空俯瞰我們生活的土地，又會看見什麼呢？11 月 19 日，「小小齊柏林－看見台灣校園公播計畫」飛進北斗國小！齊柏林基金會的專業講師，帶著孩子們跟著齊柏林導演的空拍鏡頭，透過《見山》《映河》《逐岸》《覓城》四段影像，展開一趟不用搭飛機也能翱翔臺灣天際的旅程。這不只是看影片，更是一堂會思考的環境教育課：講師引導孩子觀察、提問、討論與分享，從一幕幕震撼的空拍畫面中，孩子們看見臺灣的美，也開始關心這片土地面臨的環境問題。希望這趟空中之旅，能在北斗孩子心中種下愛護土地的種子。感謝齊柏林基金會與講師團隊、翁友梅老師、睿泰建設，以及三甲、四丁、五乙、五戊的參與。

Parents · 親職教育·17 Nov 2026

Parenting with the Brain in Mind親職講座：從本能反應到策略型父母

Thank you to every parent who came to our parenting talk, “Starting from Brain Science: From Instinctive Reactions to Strategic Parenting.” Using everyday scenarios, the speaker helped us see our children afresh — and hear ourselves again. Three ideas stood out. First, say “you can” more than “don't”: the brain struggles with negatives, so “don't climb” plants the word “climb”; “run on the grass, walk in the corridor” works far better. Second, behind a child's question is a real need — “What's this?” while staring at sweets usually means “Can I have one?” Hear the need first, then agree on the rules together. Third, setting boundaries helps children build a sense of self: disappointment and anger are normal, and accepting those feelings builds a steady character and resilience. As the speaker reminded us, we cannot be by our children's side forever; our real task is to help them thrive when we are not there.

親職教育講座「腦科學出發——從本能反應到策略型父母」圓滿落幕！感謝各位爸爸媽媽撥冗參與，講師用生活化的情境題，帶我們重新認識孩子，也重新聽見自己。現場最有感的三個重點：一、少說「不要」，多說「可以」——大腦聽不懂否定詞，說「不要爬」，孩子腦中先浮現的是「爬」；改成「在草地上可以用跑的，在走廊上用走的」，孩子更容易做到。二、孩子的問題背後有真正的需求——孩子盯著糖果問「這是什麼？」其實是想問「我可以吃嗎？」先聽懂需求，再一起討論規則。三、設界線，是在幫孩子建立自我認同——失望、生氣都是正常的，接納情緒、學習面對失望，正是在培養穩定的人格與心理韌性。講師也提醒：家長無法陪伴孩子一生，我們真正要做的，是讓孩子在我們不在身邊時，依然能好好發展。教養這條路，我們一起同行。

Campus · 校園設施·15 Nov 2026

A Friendlier Restroom友善廁所的新篇章

After renovating every restroom on campus in 2023 and adding one for the kindergarten's two-year-old class, we have kept reviewing and improving. With NT$1,038,000 in combined funding from the Ministry of Education and the Changhua County Government, the east restroom on the second floor of Building B — first built in 1994 — has now been fully rebuilt. The work fixed long-standing problems: no grab bars, poor flooring and drainage, broken fittings, incomplete emergency call buttons, shared facilities for boys and girls, and old T8 lighting. The new design adds thoughtful touches: private cubicles for boys too, bidet sprayers for easier cleaning, roomier stalls, bright white tiles, LED lighting on rewired circuits, a painted screen against the afternoon sun, a layout with no hidden corners, ceiling fans to keep the floor dry, and washbasins placed outside to separate wet and dry areas. 上週五，本校最受歡迎的外師 Anna 老師帶領的「六天英語夏令營」圓滿落幕！這六天裡，校園總是充滿孩子們的歡笑聲與自信的英文對話。Anna 老師精心設計了跨領域素養課程，把語言學習融入動手做的體驗：孩子們化身小小科學家，用黏土捏出火山模型，並用全英文理解化學反應；還挑戰製作菲律賓傳統甜點 Pastillas de Leche（牛奶糖），一邊看著投影幕上的英文步驟，一邊親手揉出甜蜜的滋味。謝謝 Anna 老師的用心，也謝謝每一位熱情參與的孩子——在英文學習的路上，繼續保持這份好奇心與熱情！
