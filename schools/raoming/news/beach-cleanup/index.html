Wanggong Beach Cleanup王功環境教育與淨灘

Our sixth-graders visited Wanggong for environmental education and service learning. They explored the tidal flats (catching crabs and shellfish), cleaned the beach, discovered the fishing harbour and coastal plants, and made oyster-shell crafts — learning first-hand why protecting our coast matters. Thank you to the Wanggong Oyster Art & Culture Association.六年級的孩子前往王功進行環境教育與服務學習。當天進行潮間帶巡禮（抓螃蟹、蚵螺）、淨灘服務、探索漁港與濱海植物生態，並體驗蚵殼創作，親身認識保護海岸的重要。感謝王功蚵藝文化協會。
