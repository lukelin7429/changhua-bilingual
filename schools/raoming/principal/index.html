A Warm Welcome

校長的歡迎詞
"Our principal leads us with kindness, and teaches us to shine in our own ways."
「我們的校長以愛心領導我們，教導我們以自己的方式發光。」

Welcome to Raoming Elementary School in Yuanlin City, Changhua County. Ours is a school where children love to learn and grow — where good values, joyful reading, and a curiosity about languages and the wider world go hand in hand.

歡迎來到位於彰化縣員林市的饒明國小。在這裡，孩子們熱愛學習與成長——良好的品格、享受閱讀，以及對語言和世界的好奇心，相伴而行。

Our teachers don't just teach well; they truly care about every child, making Raoming a friendly and joyful place to learn. Together with My Culture Connect, we invite our students to step onto a bigger stage and share who they are — in English, with confidence.

我們的老師不只教學出色，更真心關懷每一個孩子，讓饒明成為友善而快樂的學習園地。我們也與人師教育協會攜手，邀請孩子站上更大的舞台，用英文、有自信地分享自己。

"Come and join our school family — where learning is an adventure!"
「來加入我們的學校大家庭吧——在這裡，學習就像一場冒險！」
Jiang Shu-fang
江淑芳 校長 · 饒明國小
