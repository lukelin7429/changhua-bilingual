I.

About Raoming

關於饒明 · 員林市一所熱愛學習的小學
A special place where we love to learn and grow.
一個我們熱愛學習與成長的特別地方。

Welcome to Raoming Elementary School in Yuanlin City, Changhua County. Our school is a special place where we love to learn and grow. We learn about good values, enjoy reading, explore different languages, and learn about the world around us.

歡迎來到位於彰化縣員林市的饒明國小。我們的學校是一個特別的地方，在這裡我們熱愛學習與成長。我們學習良好的價值觀、享受閱讀、探索不同的語言，並認識周圍的世界。

Our teachers are not just great at teaching — they really care about each of us, making Raoming a friendly and fun place to learn. Our classmates are energetic and kind, making every day exciting. With tall trees and wide green fields, our campus looks like a beautiful picture: a peaceful place where we love to play and learn.

我們的老師不僅教學出色，更真正關心我們每一個人，讓饒明成為友善又有趣的學習園地。我們的同學充滿活力又善良，讓每一天都充滿朝氣。有著高聳的樹木與寬廣的綠地，我們的校園就像一幅美麗的畫——一個我們喜愛玩耍與學習的寧靜之地。

"Come and join our school family, where learning is an adventure!"
「來加入我們的學校大家庭吧，在這裡學習就像一場冒險！」

International Exchange · 國際交流
A Friendship Across the Pacific
跨越太平洋的友誼
Raoming has become exchange partners with Windship Middle School in California, USA. The two schools swapped mascots — our hand-sewn lion dancer and their pirate banana slug — and take them travelling to share their hometowns.饒明與美國加州 Windship Middle School 結為交流夥伴，兩校互換吉祥物——我們手縫的舞獅與他們的海盜香蕉蛞蝓——並帶著對方旅行，分享彼此的家鄉。
