Find Your Spark

多元社團 · 盡情揮灑創意與活力

Raoming offers many after-school clubs so every child can be creative, active, and confident — from our award-winning Lion Dance Team to blocks, dance, English, basketball and board games. Each club comes with some basic English to learn too! 饒明國小積極推動多元社團，讓孩子在校園裡就能盡情揮灑創意與活力——從屢獲佳績的舞獅隊，到積木、舞蹈、英語、籃球與桌遊。每個社團還附上一點基礎英語，邊玩邊學！

🦁🥁
Lion Dance Team
舞獅隊 · 饒明的驕傲
Raoming's proudest tradition. Our team are county champions — first place in the four-lion event — and they keep this folk art alive at temple festivals across the region.饒明最自豪的傳統。舞獅隊曾勇奪彰化縣縣長盃四獅冠軍，並到各地廟會節慶演出，讓傳統技藝向下紮根。歡迎對舞獅有興趣的孩子加入！

English Club
英語社
A fun, immersive space to practise speaking English. We break free from fixed topics, focus on talking more, and keep learning across subjects — a window to the wider world.提供孩子更多接觸英文的環境，打破主題限制、加強口語表達，不斷更新與跨領域學習，成為孩子們的世界之窗。

Smart Builder Blocks Club
金頭腦積木社
Kids build robots, vehicles, dinosaurs and more from blocks of every shape — boosting imagination, spatial thinking, problem-solving and teamwork.孩子用各種形狀的積木發揮想像力，拼出機器人、工程車、恐龍、房子，全方位激發創造力、空間能力、社交與解決問題的能力。

Board Game Club
桌遊社
Following the spirit of the 108 Curriculum, we use over thirty board games to build autonomous action, communication and social participation — learning strategy and teamwork through play.呼應 108 課綱核心素養，運用三十多款桌遊培養「自主行動」「溝通互動」「社會參與」，在遊戲中學策略與合作，體驗不為考試而教學的樂趣。

Basketball Club
