Smart Builder Blocks Club
金頭腦積木社
← School Clubs 社團活動
Build robots and dinosaurs — and talk about it in English!
In the Smart Builder Blocks Club, kids build robots, cars and dinosaurs from blocks of every shape. While we build, we use simple English to ask for what we need and show off our creations.在金頭腦積木社，孩子用各種形狀的積木組出機器人、車子與恐龍。一邊組裝，一邊用簡單英文表達需求、分享自己的作品。

Talk Together

對話練習 · 兩人一組，點 🔊 跟著唸
Two builders work together at the table. 兩位小小工程師一起組裝。
A
What are you building?
你在組什麼？
B
I am building a dinosaur!
我在組一隻恐龍！
A

哇，好大喔！
B
Can you help me?

A

B
