Our lion dance, now in English!
The lion dance is a proud Raoming tradition. These six lessons turn our drums, our moves, and our good-luck wishes into simple English our students can say out loud.
舞獅是饒明引以為傲的特色。這六堂課，把我們的鑼鼓、動作與祝福，變成孩子們開口就能說的英文。
