Lesson 02 · Lion Dance · 舞獅英語
👀 The Lion's Body
獅頭與獅身 · 饒明舞獅英語 · 六堂課
← Previous 📚 All 6 Lessons · 課程目錄 Next →
I.

Today's Goals

學習目標 · 今天要學會三件事
🦁
Learn
學會
Name the parts of the lion.說出獅子的身體部位。
💬
Say
開口說
Use "The lion has a…".用「The lion has a…」描述獅子。
🤝
Know
知道
Two friends make one lion together.知道兩個人合作變成一隻獅子。
II.

Words to Know

必學 5 單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
🦁
Head
/hed/ · n.
頭（獅頭）
"One friend holds the lion head."
👀
Eyes
/aɪz/ · n.
眼睛
"The lion blinks its big eyes."
👄
Mouth
/maʊθ/ · n.
嘴巴
"The lion opens its mouth wide."
🌀
Tail
/teɪl/ · n.
尾巴（獅尾）
"The other friend is the lion tail."
🧍
Body
/ˈbɒd.i/ · n.
身體
"Two people, one lion body!"
III.

Say It!

句型練習 · 開口說說看
The lion has a…
Describe / 描述
Tell about the lion's parts.
用「The lion has a…」描述獅子的部位。
The lion has a + big / small + part
"The lion has a big head."
"The lion has bright eyes."
"The lion has a long tail."
Two friends, one lion.
Teamwork / 合作
One is the head, one is the tail.
一個當獅頭，一個當獅尾。
I am the + head / tail
"I am the head."
"You are the tail."
"We are one lion!"
IV.

Talk Together

對話練習 · 兩人一組，點 🔊 跟著唸
Two children pick who will be the head and the tail. 兩個小朋友決定誰當獅頭、誰當獅尾。
A
Look! The lion has a big head.
你看！獅子有一顆大大的頭。
B
And bright eyes that blink!
還有會眨的明亮眼睛！
A
I want to be the head.
我想當獅頭。
B
OK! I am the tail.
好啊！我當獅尾。
A
Two friends, one lion. Let's go!
兩個人，一隻獅子。我們上吧！
V.

Class Game

課堂遊戲 · 一起動一動
Touch It! · 指出來！
Lion Parts · 獅子部位
The teacher calls a part. Touch that part fast! Head, eyes, mouth, tail, body.老師喊一個部位，大家快點指出來！頭、眼睛、嘴巴、尾巴、身體。
How to play · 怎麼玩
Teacher: "Eyes!" → Touch your eyes 👀
Teacher: "Mouth!" → Touch your mouth 👄
Teacher: "Tail!" → Wiggle behind you 🌀
