Word of the Day · Unit 06

Nature & Seasons

自然與節令
📺 9 videos · 9 個影片
← All Units · 回 Lessons

Seedlings, weeds, wind and ice — the words for the garden and the changing weather around campus.幼苗、雜草、風與冰——校園裡的花圃與四季天氣用的英文。

Word 01
prune (v)
修剪
The students prune the tomato plants.
同學們修剪番茄。
They prune the tomato plants to maintain the plant's health.
她們修剪番茄來保持植物的健康。
Word 02
weed (v)
除草
The students weed the vegetable garden.
同學們在菜園除草。
They weed the vegetable garden to help the vegetables grow.
他們在菜園除草讓幫助蔬菜成長。
Word 03
seedling (n)
幼苗
The students work with seedlings during agricultural education class.
在食農課當中，同學們學習栽種幼苗。
They use tweezers to handle the seedlings.
他們用鑷子來夾幼苗。
Word 04
wind (n)
風
The students run with a toy to play with the wind.
學生們帶著玩具跑來和風一起玩耍。
The toy helps them play with the wind.
玩具幫助他們與風一起玩耍。
Word 05
air (n)
空氣
The students learn about air in class.
學生在課堂上學習有關空氣的知識。
They use a toy to demonstrate the movement of air.
他們用玩具來示範空氣的運動。
Word 06
tea (n)
茶
Tea is prepared for everyone to drink.
有人泡茶給大家喝。
The teachers try different kinds of tea.
老師們品嘗不同的茶。
Word 07
ice (n)
冰
Kindergarteners learn about ice in class.
幼稚園學生在課堂上學習冰的相關知識。
They use salt to learn more about ice.
他們使用鹽巴來學習更多冰的相關知識。
Word 08
pinwheel
風車
The students are having a great time playing with pinwheels outside.
學生們在戶外玩風車玩得很開心。
They are running on the grass to make their pinwheels spin in the wind.
他們在草地上奔跑，讓風車在風中旋轉。
Word 09
wood shredder
碎木機
The students go outside to observe how to use a wood shredder.
同學們到室外觀看如何操作碎木機。
After observing, the students use the wood shredder.
在觀摩學習後，他們操作碎木機。
