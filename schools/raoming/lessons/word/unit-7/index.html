Word of the Day · Unit 07

Festivals

節慶
📺 8 videos · 8 個影片
← All Units · 回 Lessons

Christmas crafts, a lion dance, winter-solstice rice balls — the festival words that fill the Raoming calendar.聖誕手作、舞獅、冬至湯圓——填滿饒明行事曆的節慶英文。

Word 01
Christmas tree
聖誕樹
The students set up a Christmas tree for the holiday season.
學生們為即將到來的聖誕假期搭了一棵聖誕樹。
They take turns putting the Christmas tree together.
他們輪流將聖誕樹組裝起來。
Word 02
Christmas carol
聖誕頌歌
The students learn a dance and sing a Christmas carol to celebrate Christmas.
同學們學習跳舞並且唱聖誕頌歌來慶祝聖誕節。
The Christmas carol is named We Wish You a Merry Christmas.
這首聖誕頌歌的名字叫做「我們祝你聖誕快樂」。
Word 03
Christmas stocking
聖誕襪
The students color Christmas stockings in class.
學生們在課堂上為他們的聖誕襪著色。
They show off their colorful Christmas stockings.
他們展現色彩繽紛的聖誕襪作品。
Word 04
Christmas gifts
聖誕禮物
The students receive Christmas gifts from their teacher for Christmas.
同學們從他們的老師手中得到聖誕節的禮物。
Christmas gifts can be dolls, toys, books or candy.
聖誕禮物有可能是洋娃娃、玩具、書或是糖果。
Word 05
Santa Claus (n)
聖誕老人
The students fold a Santa Claus out of paper.
同學們用紙摺成聖誕老人。
They draw a face on their Santa Claus.
他們在聖誕老人的紙上畫出他的臉。
Word 06
snowman (n)
雪人
The students cut circles to make snowmen out of paper.
同學們將紙剪成圓圈來製作雪人。
They draw smiley faces on their snowmen.
他們在雪人臉上畫微笑的表情。
Word 07
sticky rice ball
湯圓
The students prepare to make sticky rice balls.
同學們準備要搓湯圓。
The sticky rice balls are white and pink.
湯圓的顏色是白色與粉紅色的。
Word 08
lion dance
舞獅
The students practice the lion dance.
學生們在練習舞獅。
They dress up in a traditional costume to do the lion dance.
舞獅時他們穿著傳統服飾。
