Word of the Day · Unit 05

A School Day

校園的一天
📺 12 videos · 12 個影片
← All Units · 回 Lessons

From the school gate to the lunch tray, the computer lab to the after-school program — a day at Raoming, word by word.從校門口到營養午餐、電腦教室到課後輔導——饒明的一天，一字一句。

Word 01
go to school
上學
The students go to school in the morning.
同學們早上上學去。
They greet the teacher and enter their classroom when they go to school.
他們上學時，會跟老師問候然後進到教室去。
Word 02
school gate
校門口
Santa Claus greets the students at the school gate.
聖誕老人在校門口迎接學生們。
The students receive treats for Christams at the school gate.
學生們在校們口得到聖誕的點心。
Word 03
school lunch
營養午餐
The students each rice, meat and vegetables for school lunch .
同學們營養午餐吃飯、肉還有蔬菜。
The students eat school lunch at their desks.
同學們在自己的桌子吃營養午餐。
Word 04
after school program
課後輔導
The students go to their after-school program when the last class is finished.
在最後一節課結束後，同學們上課後輔導課程。
They study and do homework in the after-school program.
在課後輔導課程中，他們讀書並且寫功課。
Word 05
school club
社團
The students participate in various school clubs.
同學們參與各式各樣的學校社團。
School clubs include different arts and sports.
學校社團包含不同的藝術與體育類型。
Word 06
computer lab
電腦教室
A computer lab is a room for students to use computers.
電腦教室是一個可以讓學生使用電腦的教室。
In the computer lab, students are using the computers to study English.
在電腦教室裡，同學們正在使用電腦學習英文。
Word 07
field trip (n)
校外教學
The students go on a field trip.
學生們去校外教學。
They observed many new things during their field trip.
他們在校外教學觀察到許多新事物。
Word 08
playground (n)
遊樂場
The students play different games at the school playground.
學生們在學校遊樂場上玩著不同的遊戲。
The playground has monkey bars for students to hang on.
遊樂場有攀爬架讓學生懸吊。
Word 09
clock (n)
時鐘
The teacher teaches how to read a clock in math class.
老師在數學課指導如何看時鐘。
The students use learning clocks to learn how to read a clock.
學生們使用時鐘教具來學習如何看時鐘。
Word 10
tablet (n)
平板
The teacher is showing his students how to use a tablet.
這位老師正在為學生示範如何使用平板。
Sometimes teachers have their students use tablets in class.
有時候老師會讓學生在上課中使用平板。
Word 11
sweep (v)
掃
Every day, students sweep away fallen leaves on school grounds.
每天學生打掃校園的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.
台灣的學生打掃校園，保持校園的乾淨整齊。
Word 12
professional development
專業進修
In Taiwan, all elementary schools have professional development on Wednesday afternoons.
台灣所有小學老師在週三下午有專業進修。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development.
老師在專業進修時學習新技能與教學策略。
