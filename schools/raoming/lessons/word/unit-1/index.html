Word of the Day · Unit 01

Stories, Reading & Words

故事、閱讀與文字

From the library to the storytelling stage — the words Raoming kids use to read, write, and share a good story.從圖書館到說故事舞台——饒明孩子閱讀、書寫、分享故事時用得到的英文。

Word 01
story (n)
故事
The students write a story in class.
學生在課堂上寫一個故事。
They draw a picture for their story.
他們為自己的故事畫了一幅圖畫。
Word 02
storytelling
講故事
The students practice storytelling in class.
學生們在課堂上練習講故事。
Each student memorizes a story for storytelling.
每一位同學把故事背起來說給大家聽。
Word 03
storyteller (n)
講故事的人
A good storyteller teaches a lesson and entertains you at the same time.
一個擅長講說故事的人可以教導大家道理，同時又兼具娛樂大眾。
The storytellers can use real or fictional information to tell a story.
說故事的人可能用真實或者是虛構的情節來說故事。
Word 04
storytelling contest
說故事比賽
The students participate in a storytelling contest.
學生們參加說故事比賽。
They each tell a story for the storytelling contest.
他們每人為說故事比賽講一個故事。
Word 05
tells a story
講故事
The girl tells a story to her classmates.
這位小女孩對她的同學講一個故事。
The girl has a pink costume and holds a magic wand while she tells a story.
她講故事時，穿著粉紅色的服裝，手中還拿著一 支魔法棒。
Word 06
reading
閱讀
Look at the students. They’re all reading quietly.
看看這群學生，他們閱讀時多麼安靜啊！
Reading often can help you improve your vocabulary and learn many new things.
閱讀經常可以讓你增加字彙並且學到很多新知。
Word 07
library (n)
圖書館
The students check out books at the library.
同學們在圖書館借書。
They check out books from the library to study at home.
他們從圖書館借書回家讀。
Word 08
mobile library
行動圖書館
The students go to the mobile library.
同學們造訪行動圖書館。
They choose books to read from the mobile library.
他們從行動圖書館內挑書來閱讀。
Word 09
newspaper (n)
報紙
The students are reading a newspaper to learn about current events.
同學們正在讀報瞭解時事。
They also improve their reading abilities by reading newspapers.
他們可以藉由讀報來提升閱讀能力。
Word 10
Chinese characters
國字
The teacher teaches Chinese characters in class.
老師在課堂上教導國字。
The students read the Chinese characters out loud.
學生們大聲讀出國字。
Word 11
calligraphy (n)
書法
The boys and girls are learning traditional Chinese calligraphy.
這群男孩和女孩正在學習中國傳統的書法。
To get good at calligraphy, you have to have a lot of patience!
書法要寫得好必須要很有耐心。
