Word of the Day · Unit 04

Sports & Games

運動與遊戲
📺 14 videos · 14 個影片
← All Units · 回 Lessons

Jump rope, dodgeball, kickball, the nine-square grid — every move on the Raoming playground, in English.跳繩、躲避球、足壘球、九宮格——饒明操場上的每個動作，用英文說。

Word 01
race (n)
賽跑
The students run in a relay race outside.
學生們在外面進行接力賽。
They have to go through a hula hoop and tag their teammate in the race.
他們必須穿過呼拉圈並在比賽中標記他們的隊友。
Word 02
jump rope
跳繩
The teacher shows the students how to jump rope.
老師向學生展示如何跳繩。
The students try to jump rope.
學生們嘗試跳繩。
Word 03
jump rope competition
跳繩比賽
The students participate in a jump rope competition.
學生們參加跳繩比賽。
Many students participate in the jump rope competition.
許多學生參加跳繩比賽。
Word 04
hula hoop (n)
呼啦圈
The students go outside to learn how to use a hula hoop.
學生們到室外學習如何使用呼拉圈。
Each student practices playing with a hula hoop.
每個學生練習使用呼拉圈。
Word 05
spin (v)
旋轉
The students blow on the paper to make it spin.
同學們吹紙讓它旋轉。
They make the paper spin on their desks and on the floor.
他們在桌上和地板上讓紙張旋轉。
Word 06
dodgeball
躲避球
Dodgeball is a fast-paced game where the goal is to eliminate all the players from the opposing team.
躲避球是一種節奏快速的遊戲，目標是將對手隊伍的所有選手淘汰出局。
In dodgeball, you eliminate an opposing player by hitting them with the ball.
在躲避球比賽中，你用球擊中對方的選手讓他們出局。
Word 07
kickball
足壘球
The students play kickball on the outdoor court.
學生們在戶外球場上踢足壘球。
Kickball has similar rules to baseball, but a ball is kicked instead of being struck with a bat.
足壘球的規則與棒球類似，但球是用腳踢的，而不是用球棒擊打的。
Word 08
fitness test
體適能測驗
The students are practicing for their fitness test.
學生們正在為體能測驗進行練習。
A fitness test may include sit-ups and stretches.
體能測試可能包括仰臥起坐和伸展運動。
Word 09
nine square grid
九宫格
The students have fun with the nine-square grid.
學生們對九個方格很感興趣。
They throw a ball at the nine-square grid in front of them.
他們向面前的九個方格丟了一個球。
Word 10
throw
丟、扔
The students are practicing throwing the ball far away.
學生們正在練習把球丟遠。
They take turns throwing the ball on the running track.
他們在跑道上輪流丟球。
Word 11
rollerblade
溜直排輪
These students are practicing to rollerblade.
這些學生正在練習溜直排輪。
Rollerblading can be for fun and also for exercise.
溜直排輪既可提供娛樂，又可作為運動。
Word 12
game (n)
遊戲
The students play games to learn addition.
學生們透過玩遊戲來學習加法。
They play games in pairs.
他們兩人一組玩遊戲。
Word 13
board game
桌遊
The students form groups to play a board game.
同學們分組玩桌遊。
They enjoy playing the board game in class.
他們很喜歡在課堂上玩桌遊。
Word 14
walk (v)
走
The students go to the track to walk.
同學們到操場走路。
They walk for exercise and to improve their health.
他們走路是為了運動，讓身體更健康。
