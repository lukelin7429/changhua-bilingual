Spread Your Wings第68屆畢業典禮圓滿落幕

Today, we gathered together to witness Desing Elementary's 68th graduating class step into the next chapter of their lives.

A ceremony full of tradition and gratitude.

Younger students carefully pinned flowers on their graduating "senpai," passing along their best wishes — and the graduates pinned flowers on their teachers in return, thanking them for years of patient guidance.

During the traditional campus walk, the principal and the PTA president led the graduates past all their favourite childhood spots — the slide where they used to play, and the Kexing Garden that has slowly grown into a bountiful harvest.

The graduates played the ukulele together with their schoolmates, a musical farewell full of both nostalgia for the campus and hope for the future.

In a moving tribute, graduates presented flowers to their homeroom teachers from every grade, thanking them for years of care — a scene that touched everyone in the room.

Finally, the graduates rode off on bicycles, carrying their teachers' words and a heart full of memories, ready to pedal toward a wider world.

Congratulations, graduates — may your dreams keep blooming on every new stage ahead!

中文原文 · Chinese

🎓 展翅高飛，夢想啟航——德興國小第 68 屆畢業典禮圓滿落幕

今日，我們齊聚一堂，共同見證德興國小第 68 屆畢業生邁向人生下一個階段的重要時刻。

典禮中，充滿了傳承與感恩的溫暖：

🌟 【傳承與祝福】 學弟妹們細心為學長姐別上胸花，傳遞滿滿的祝福；畢業生們也懷著感恩的心，親手為師長別上胸花，感謝這幾年來的諄諄教誨。

🚲 【回憶的路途】 隨著傳統的校園巡禮，校長及會長帶領畢業生們一一回憶這六年來的角落，那溜滑梯上的遊戲，那逐漸豐收的可興園……

🎶 【青春的樂章】 畢業生們用精湛的烏克麗麗和學弟妹一起演奏，訴說對校園的不捨與對未來的期待。

💐 【濃濃師生情】 在致謝環節中，畢業生們將手中的鮮花獻給陪伴成長的低、中、高年級導師，句句感謝凝聚了多年的情感，現場氛圍令人動容。

🚲 【勇敢的啟程】 畢業生們騎上單車離開校園，帶著師長的叮嚀與滿滿回憶，準備奔向更廣闊的未來。

時光飛逝，總讓人措手不及，但德興國小的教育與回憶將會成為你們最堅強的後盾。祝福畢業生們：畢業快樂，鵬程萬里，讓夢想在新的舞台持續綻放！
