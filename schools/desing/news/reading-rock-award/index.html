Desing Shines on the National Stage德興在全國頂尖舞台榮耀綻放！

Desing Elementary stood on the nation's highest stage of honor, receiving this heavy, gleaming trophy from the Deputy Minister of Education and the Director-General of the K-12 Education Administration.

That moment of applause and camera flashes belonged to every teacher, parent, and student at Desing — the proudest symphony of them all.

The Reading Rock Award: the nation's highest honor for reading promotion.

It represents countless hours of teachers planning lessons together, and it captures how our students grew through their "Cacao 2.0" inquiry project — shedding their shyness to become confident local advocates.

With love and professionalism, we broke down walls and lit up a community reading "heart" that reaches across townships, in a place that is neither big city nor deep countryside.

Behind this honor is deep gratitude — to every official, parent, and community partner who walked this road together with Desing. Because you believed in us, our children could connect with the world through the love of books.

Holding this trophy, our steps will be even more determined. Through reading, the Desing team helps every child become their very best!

中文原文 · Chinese

🏆德興在全國頂尖舞台榮耀綻放！✨

走上全國至高的榮譽殿堂，從教育部張廖萬堅次長、國教署彭富源署長手中接過這座沉甸甸的獎座。那一刻的掌聲與閃光燈，是屬於全體德興親師生最榮耀的交響樂。

這座象徵閱讀最高肯定的磐石獎座，凝聚了德興師長們無數個共備的時刻，更見證了孩子們在「可可2.0」的探究中，褪去青澀，翻轉為有力量的「地方倡議者」。我們用愛與專業打破圍牆，在非山非市的土地上，點亮了跨鄉鎮的社區閱讀心臟。

榮耀背後，是深深的感謝。謝謝一路上與德興並肩同行的長官、家長與社區夥伴，因為有您們的相信，德興的孩子得以在書香中與世界接軌。

捧著這座獎，我們的步履會更加堅定。

德興團隊透過閱讀，讓每個孩子成為頂尖！❤️
