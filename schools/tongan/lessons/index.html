Word of the Day

每日一字 · 同安自製雙語影片

Real English, filmed in our own classrooms and out on our own hillside.

真實的英文，全部在同安自己的教室與山坡上拍攝。

Every word below comes with a short bilingual video made right here at Tongan, plus two example sentences in English and Chinese. Watch, listen, and read along — then try saying it out loud. The words are grouped into three themes that show what learning at our school really looks like.

下面每個單字都搭配一支同安自製的雙語短片，以及兩句中英對照例句。看影片、聽發音、跟著讀——然後大聲說出來。這些單字依三大主題分組，正是同安學習生活的真實樣貌。

健康

讓學生從小養成熱愛運動和學習健身知識，對於增強骨骼和鍛鍊肌肉是極其重要的。

Our Tongan Life
同安特色 · 咖啡・土地・美學
3 words · 3 字
Word 08
coffee (n)
咖啡
The students learned how to make pour-over coffee stands by cutting, drilling, and sanding wood during the coffee class.
學生們在咖啡課上學習如何透過切割、鑽孔和打磨木材來製作手沖咖啡架。
They also learned how to roast and grind coffee beans, then packed the coffee powder into drip coffee bags.
他們還學習如何烘焙和研磨咖啡豆，然後將咖啡粉放入滴濾咖啡袋。
Word 09
pineapple (n)
鳳梨
The wonderful young children from Tongan Elementary were excited to assist local farmers with the process of planting pineapples firsthand.
來自同安國小的孩子們很高興能夠親手幫助當地農民種植鳳梨。
This field trip taught the importance and joy of farming, as well as an understanding of how pineapples grow.
這趟校外教學教導大家農業的重要性與樂趣，以及了解鳳梨如何生長。
Word 10
violin (n)
小提琴
The students practice the violin in class.
學生們在課堂上練習小提琴。
They follow the teacher's instructions to play the violin.
他們按照老師的指示拉小提琴。
III.

Classroom English

課室英語 · 給老師的口袋句庫（影片版）
The phrases teachers say between the lessons — taught on video.
老師在課堂裡每天會用上的英文句子——這次有人示範給你看。

A practical video library of the everyday English a Taiwanese teacher actually uses: greetings and roll call, pre-class set-up, instructions, praise and correction, classroom management, lesson activities, assigning homework, wrapping up, and exam directions. Each video isolates one situation so the phrases stick on first hearing.

這個影片教材庫涵蓋臺灣老師每天會用到的課堂英語——問候點名、課前準備、講解、回饋讚美糾正、教室管理、課堂活動、分派作業、課堂收尾、考試指令。每支影片只處理一個情境，第一次聽就能記住。

IV.

Bilingual Announcements

英語廣播 · 校園的英語之聲
The morning intercom in two languages — written and recorded by the four school offices.
早上的校園廣播，雙語版本——由教務、學務、總務、輔導四個處室共同寫稿、共同上鏡。

A bilingual announcement series, organized by the four school offices: Academic Affairs (教務處), Student Affairs (學務處), General Affairs (總務處), and Counseling (輔導室). Each office introduces what it does in both languages — turning the daily public-address routine into real-world bilingual exposure.

這是一個雙語校園廣播系列，依四個處室分組：教務處、學務處、總務處、輔導室，各組親自介紹自己的工作，雙語對照——把每天的廣播時間，變成全校學生在走廊上就能接收的英語浸潤。
