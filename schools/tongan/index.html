I.

Welcome

歡迎來到同安國小

A tiny school on the ridge, with a great big idea of what learning can be.

一所八卦台地上的迷你小學，懷抱著對「學習」最寬廣的想像。

Tongan Elementary sits high on the Bagua tableland of Fenyuan, Changhua — a quiet place of coffee groves, pineapple fields, and beehives. Founded in 1922, our school has watched over the children of Zhonglun and Tongan villages for more than a century.

同安國小坐落在彰化縣芬園鄉的八卦台地上——這裡有咖啡園、鳳梨田與蜂箱。學校創立於民國 11 年（1922），逾百年來守護著中崙、同安兩村的孩子。

We are small — fewer than seventy students — and we turn that into our greatest strength. With so few children, every single one is known, seen, and given room to shine. A decade ago this school nearly closed; today it is a nationally recognized "Top 100 Future-Education School," proof that a country school need never settle for less.

我們很小——全校不到七十人——而我們把「小」變成最大的優勢。因為孩子少，每一個孩子都被看見、被認識、都有發光的舞台。十年前這所學校幾乎要被裁併；今天它是全國「未來教育臺灣 100」特色學校，證明偏鄉小校絕不必妥協於「弱勢」。

Our promise is captured in three words our children live by — "Learning by Doing" and "Just do it!" Here, every child learns to be kind, capable, and curious — ready for Taiwan and ready for the world, in two languages.

我們的信念濃縮成孩子們天天實踐的一句話——「做中學」、「Just do it!」在這裡，每個孩子都學著成為「好品德、有能力、愛學習」的『好有愛』孩子——以雙語，迎向臺灣、迎向世界。

II.

The New Six Arts

新六藝 · 四大學習主軸

One small school, four big ways to grow.

一所小學校，四條成長的路。

🔬
Science & Makers
科普創客

Building blocks, coding, woodworking, and science fairs. Tongan has placed first in the county science fair five years running — and second in the whole nation.

🌍
International & Bilingual
國際雙語

Spoken English, a foreign-teacher classroom, bilingual P.E., and gold-medal CyberFair projects connect our children to the wider world.

🎻
Arts & Beauty
藝術美學

Every child at Tongan learns the violin. Add dance, drama, and keyboard, and art becomes part of everyday school life.

🌱
Sustainable Explorers
永續探索

Rock climbing, tree climbing, archery, and cycling — plus our own coffee, pineapples, and bees. The whole hillside is our classroom.

III.

Our Signature: Coffee

招牌校本課程：咖啡
From seed, to cup, to "Coffee Master"
從一顆種子，到一杯手沖，成為「咖啡達人」

Working with local coffee farmers, Tongan students follow the whole journey — planting the trees, roasting and grinding the beans, even building wooden pour-over stands by sawing, drilling, and sanding. Coffee is how our children learn about their land, their community, and the joy of doing things with their own hands.
