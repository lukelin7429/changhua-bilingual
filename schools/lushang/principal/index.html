陳薇淳

A Vision Held With Care 用心傾聽、用愛教育、用專業陪伴

Principal Chen Wei-Chun's core belief is to "listen whole-heartedly, educate with love, and accompany with professionalism," in service of one vision: achieving success for every child, so that every potential can be seen. She is committed to opening up infinite possibilities through education, and to building a learning environment full of hope and growth.

陳薇淳校長的核心信念是「用心傾聽、用愛教育、用專業陪伴」，並以「成就每一個孩子，讓每一份潛能都能被看見」為願景目標。她致力於透過教育開創無限可能，營造充滿希望與成長的學習環境。

Three Ways of Being Present 辦學理念 — 三個「用」字

👂

Listen Whole-heartedly用心傾聽

Genuinely listening to the voices of teachers and students as the solid foundation for educational advancement.

真誠傾聽師生的心聲，作為教育推動的穩固基礎。

💗

Educate with Love用愛教育

Starting with love and care, accompanying children to discover their inner brilliance, and growing together with teachers.

以愛與關懷為出發點，陪伴孩子發掘並綻放亮點；同時與教師攜手共學成長。

🤝

Accompany with Professionalism用專業陪伴

Supporting students' breakthroughs and teachers' growth through professional knowledge, innovative teaching, and continuous learning.

透過專業知識、創新教學與持續進修，支持師生不斷突破，打造具備專業力與人文關懷的幸福園地。

Four Management Strategies 經營策略

🌟

Cultivate Diverse Potential培養學生多元潛能

Respecting individual differences and inspiring multiple intelligences and multi-faceted learning abilities.

尊重個別差異，啟發多元智慧與多面向的學習能力。

📚

Strengthen Teacher Professionalism強化教師專業實力

Continuously refining teaching skills to demonstrate professional integrity and educational image.

持續精進教學技巧，展現專業素養與教育專業形象。

🏡

Create a Warm, High-Quality Campus營造溫暖優質校園

Building a humanistic campus atmosphere based on care, listening, and effective communication.

以關懷、傾聽與良好溝通為基礎，打造具人文精神的校園氛圍。

🚀

Advance Sustainability & Excellence推進學校永續卓越

Constructing a high-quality educational environment to guide the school toward excellence and sustainable development.

建構優質教育環境，引領學校朝向卓越與永續發展。

Come Visit 歡迎蒞校交流

Educators, parents, and friends are warmly welcome to visit Lushang Elementary — a village school at the southern tip of Fangyuan Township where children learn to explore, make, read, and live smart, in two languages.

歡迎教育工作者、家長與朋友蒞校交流。路上國小位於芳苑鄉最南端，孩子在這裡用雙語學習探索、手作、閱讀與智慧生活。
