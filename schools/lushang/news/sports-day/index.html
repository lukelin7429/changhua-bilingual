A Day of Energy, Laughter, and Team Spirit路上國小103週年校慶運動會

On April 2nd, Lushang Elementary School celebrated its 103rd anniversary with a Sports Day. Students, teachers, and parents all came together to join the fun.

The day started with special performances that showed just how much the students had practiced.

First, students played the ocarina. Then, others danced with a dragon. The audience was impressed by each show — you could see how much the students had practiced. After the performances, the races began. The 60-meter and 100-meter dash were exciting and full of energy.

There were also fun games like the Stick & Ball Relay and the Ball Rolling Race. Students from different grades worked as a team to run, cheer, and play. Some parents even joined in the games and had fun playing with their children.

Students cheered loudly during the relay race.

The tug-of-war was just as exciting — everyone pulled the rope with all their strength, and teams cheered loudly and worked together!

During the Sports Day, everyone enjoyed tasty treats from the food truck. Two cute frog mascots gave out small gifts, and students happily took photos with them!

This year's Sports Day was full of energy, laughter, and team spirit. It was a wonderful day for everyone at Lushang Elementary School!

中文原文 · Chinese

4月2日，路上國小舉辦了103週年校慶運動會。學生、老師、家長和社區朋友們都一起來參加，熱鬧又開心！

活動一開始是精彩的表演。學生們先演奏了美麗的陶笛音樂，接著，其他同學帶來了舞蹈表演。觀眾們看得目不轉睛，大家都能感受到學生們努力練習的成果。

表演結束後，緊接著是刺激的賽跑比賽。60公尺和100公尺短跑比賽充滿速度和活力，大家在旁邊大聲加油。

運動會上還有很多好玩的活動，像是球棒接力賽和滾球比賽。不同年級的同學們一起合作、一起奔跑，現場充滿了笑聲。還有一些爸爸媽媽也一起下場玩，和孩子們一起享受比賽的樂趣。

接力賽中，學生們大聲為隊友加油。拔河比賽也一樣精彩，大家都用盡全力，臉上掛著開心的笑容。

活動中，還有美食餐車進駐，大家吃著美味的小點心，心情超級好！現場還出現了兩隻可愛的青蛙吉祥物，發送小禮物，學生們開心地排隊合照！

今年的運動會充滿了活力、笑聲和團隊精神，為大家留下了美好的回憶。

這真是路上國小一個快樂又精彩的一天！
