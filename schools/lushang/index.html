A village named for the road between two towns

The name Lushang — literally "on the road" — comes from its location along the old route linking Fanzaiwa to Erlin Street by way of Xinwu. Before 1920 the settlement was known as Lushangcuo; it later divided into the villages of Lushang, Furong, Sancheng, and Luping. Lushang itself began in 1760, the 25th year of the Qianlong era, as a single-surname village founded by settlers of the Xie family.

The campus sits at the southernmost tip of Fangyuan Township, bordered by homes to the east and farmland on the other three sides — flat, open, and free of tall buildings nearby. Past the front gate and courtyard stands a two-storey school building, with classrooms and offices on the first floor and the principal's office and school history room above. The Huai-en Pavilion greets visitors just inside the gate, with the student dining hall behind it, while a tree-lined boulevard and basketball court open up to the southwest.

路上地名起源於此處位於昔日自番仔挖經新厝抵二林街的路上，因此得名。大正9年（1920）以前名路上厝，聚落分屬於路上、福榮、三成、路平等四村。路上厝為清乾隆25年（1760）由謝姓進入拓墾而成的聚落，係由謝姓所組成的同姓村。學校位置在芳苑鄉的最南端，校園四周除東邊有住家外，另外三邊均為農田，校園地勢平坦，四周亦無高聳之建築物。

校內空間規劃簡單明瞭，一進入校門，過了中庭即是校舍，校舍為兩層樓建築。第一樓層主要為辦公室及班級教室，第二層樓主要為校長室及校史室，左右兩側分別為專科教室和社團教室。進入校門後的右手邊有懷恩亭，懷恩亭後即是學生午餐廚房及餐廳，由學校東邊側門進入的話，可以看到教師機汽車車棚及學生腳踏車車棚；校舍後方為操場及遊戲器材區，學生活動空間大，校園西南側有樹蔭大道及籃球場。

Our Curriculum

Explore · Create · Read · Live Smart

樂探索・易手作・愛悅讀・新智活 — 路上的校本課程四主題

Four themes run through everyday learning at Lushang, pairing hands-on creativity with digital-age readiness — helping children take their next step, wherever the road leads.

Joyful Exploration 樂探索

Curiosity leads the way — children explore the world around them through hands-on discovery, observation, and inquiry, in and beyond the classroom.

好奇心帶路——孩子在教室內外，透過動手觀察與探索，認識周遭的世界。

Easy Handicraft 易手作

Learning by making — students build, craft, and create, turning ideas into things they can hold, and knowledge into skills they can use.

做中學——孩子動手創作，把想法變成作品，把知識變成能力。

Love Reading 愛悅讀

A love of books starts right at the gate — Lushang's own Community Reading Center welcomes students and neighbours alike into a shared reading life.

閱讀從校門口開始——路上國小的「社區閱讀中心」，讓學生與社區鄰里共享閱讀時光。

New Smart Life 新智活

AR-assisted bilingual practice, smart robot programming, and point-and-read pronunciation pens fill Lushang's digital bilingual classroom — funded in part by a NT$570,000 gift from Mr. Xie Shih-ying, chair of the Lushang Daitianfu temple committee.

AR擴充實境雙語學習、智慧機器人程式語言學習、智慧點讀筆口說練習——路上代天府主委謝世英先生贊助57萬元設立的數位雙語教室，就在這裡。
