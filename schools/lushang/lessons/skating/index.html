Gear Up!

護具單字：上場前先穿好

Safety first! Before every class, we sit down and put on our gear. Tap 🔊 to hear each word. 安全第一！每次上課前，大家都先坐下來把護具穿好。點 🔊 聽發音。

helmet/ˈhɛlmɪt/安全帽

A helmet goes on your head. It keeps your head safe if you fall.安全帽戴在頭上，跌倒時可以保護頭部。

knee pads/ˈni ˌpædz/護膝

Knee pads cover your knees. They take the bump when you fall forward.護膝包住膝蓋，往前跌倒時由它來承受撞擊。

elbow pads/ˈɛlˌboʊ ˌpædz/護肘

Elbow pads go on your elbows. They keep your arms from getting scraped.護肘套在手肘上，讓手臂不會擦傷。

wrist guards/ˈrɪst ˌɡɑrdz/護腕

Wrist guards protect your hands and wrists. They help when you land on your hands.護腕保護手掌和手腕，用手撐地時特別重要。

inline skates/ˈɪnˌlaɪn ˌskeɪts/直排輪鞋

Inline skates have wheels in one straight line. They let you roll and glide.直排輪鞋的輪子排成一直線，讓你可以滾動、滑行。

buckle/ˈbʌkəl/扣環

A buckle holds your skate tight. Close every buckle before you stand up.扣環把鞋子固定緊。站起來之前，每個扣環都要扣好。

Skating Moves

動作單字：我們練習的招式

These are the moves we practice in class, one step at a time. 這些是社團課一步一步練習的動作。

ready position/ˈrɛdi pəˌzɪʃən/預備姿勢

The ready position is how we stand before we skate. Bend your knees and lean forward a little.預備姿勢是出發前的站法：膝蓋彎曲，身體稍微往前傾。

glide/ɡlaɪd/滑行

To glide is to roll smoothly without stepping. Push, then glide!滑行就是不踏步、順順地往前滾。推一下，然後滑！

swizzle/ˈswɪzəl/開合（葫蘆步）

In a swizzle, your feet go out and then back in. Out, in — and you roll forward!開合時，兩腳先往外開、再往內合。開、合，就會往前滑！

one-foot glide/ˈwʌn ˌfʊt ˌɡlaɪd/單腳滑行

A one-foot glide means rolling on just one skate. It helps you find your balance.單腳滑行就是只用一隻腳滑，可以練習平衡感。

skate backward/ˈskeɪt ˈbækwərd/倒退滑

To skate backward, you roll behind you, not in front. Turn your toes in and push out.倒退滑是往後方滑。腳尖往內收，再把腳往外推。

stop/stɑp/停下來（煞車）

To stop is to end your glide safely. Every skater must learn how to stop.煞車就是安全地停下來。每個溜冰的人都一定要學會煞車。

🛑

Coach's Tips: How to Stop

教練的煞車三口訣

First, our coach shows everyone how to stop. Then each group takes a turn. Before every group goes, the coach says the three tips again. 教練先示範一次怎麼煞車，再請每一組輪流上場練習。每一組上場前，教練都會把口訣再講一次。

1Glide Straight 第 1 步・溜直線

Roll forward in a straight line. Keep your knees bent and look ahead. 先直直地往前滑，膝蓋微彎，眼睛看前方。

2Feet Apart 第 2 步・腳打開

Slide your feet out, a little wider than your shoulders. 兩腳慢慢往外打開，比肩膀再寬一點。

3Toes In 第 3 步・前輪收

Turn your front wheels in, like a slice of pizza. Your skates slow down, and you stop! 把前輪往內收，兩隻鞋就像一片披薩的形狀，速度慢下來，就停住了！

Say It in English

實用句子：點 🔊 跟著唸

Use these sentences at practice — with your friends, your coach, and your family. 練習時就用得到的句子——對同學、對教練、對家人都能說。

1
Safety first! I always wear my helmet.
安全第一！我一定會戴安全帽。
2
Can you help me close my buckle?
你可以幫我扣上扣環嗎？
3
Bend your knees and look ahead.
膝蓋彎曲，眼睛看前方。
4
Push and glide, push and glide!
推、滑，推、滑！
5
I can skate backward now!
我現在會倒退滑了！
6
Glide straight, feet apart, toes in — stop!
溜直線、腳打開、前輪收——停！
7
I fell down, but I got back up.
我跌倒了，但我又站起來了。
8
We practice every Friday morning.
我們每個星期五早上練習。
