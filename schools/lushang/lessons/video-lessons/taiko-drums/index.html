Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

drum/drʌm/鼓

Each student stands behind a big drum.每位同學站在一面大鼓後面。

drumstick/ˈdrʌmstɪk/鼓棒

Hold one drumstick in each hand.兩隻手各拿一支鼓棒。

hit/hɪt/敲

Hit the middle of the drum.敲鼓面的正中間。

teacher/ˈtiːtʃər/老師

Our teacher shows us each move.老師示範每一個動作。

row/roʊ/一排

The drums stand in a long row.鼓排成長長的一排。

together/təˈɡɛðər/一起

We play the same beat together.我們一起打同一個節奏。
