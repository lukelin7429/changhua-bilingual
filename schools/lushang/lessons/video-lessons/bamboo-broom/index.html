The Bamboo Broom

竹掃把
🧹
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Students use bamboo brooms to clean up the fallen leaves on the campus paths. A bamboo broom sweeps up leaves better than a regular broom.學生使用竹掃把清理校園步道上的落葉。竹掃把比一般掃把更容易把落葉掃起來。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

broom/bruːm/掃把

Hold the broom with both hands.用雙手握住掃把。

bamboo/bæmˈbuː/竹子

This broom is made of bamboo.這支掃把是用竹子做的。

sweep/swiːp/掃

Sweep the leaves off the path.把步道上的落葉掃掉。

pile/paɪl/一堆

The leaves are in a big pile.落葉堆成一大堆。

path/pæθ/步道

The path goes across the lawn.步道穿過草地。

clean/kliːn/打掃

We clean the garden together.我們一起打掃花園。
