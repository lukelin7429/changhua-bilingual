Fan Dance: The Show

扇子舞・演出篇
🌊
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

A fan dance is more than one person spinning a fan. The whole group practices formations: kneeling in long rows, hiding behind the fans, sitting in a circle, and lifting the fans up high, row by row, like a wave. On our 104th anniversary Sports Day, we performed the fan dance outdoors for everyone.扇子舞不只是一個人轉扇子。全體同學一起練習隊形：排成長長的一排跪下、躲在扇子後面、坐成一個大圓圈，再一排一排把扇子高高舉起，像一道海浪。104週年校慶運動會上，我們在戶外為大家表演了扇子舞。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

kneel/niːl/跪下

Kneel down on the floor in a row.在地板上排成一排跪下。

hide/haɪd/躲

We hide behind our big fans.我們躲在大扇子後面。

lift/lɪft/舉起

Lift your fan over your head.把扇子舉過頭頂。

high/haɪ/高高地

The back row holds the fans up high.後排把扇子舉得高高的。

circle/ˈsɝːkəl/圓圈

We sit in a big circle.我們坐成一個大圓圈。

perform/pərˈfɔːrm/表演

We perform for our families and friends.我們表演給家人和朋友看。
