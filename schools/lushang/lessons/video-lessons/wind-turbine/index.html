Making a Wind Turbine

動手做風力發電機
🌬️
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

The science museum brought a hands-on class to Lushang. After learning that wind can make electricity, students cut, connect, and fit blades onto a small motor on top of a bottle.科博館來到路上國小帶大家動手做。先認識風可以用來發電，再剪一剪、接一接，把葉片裝到寶特瓶上的小馬達。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

scissors/ˈsɪzərz/剪刀

Be careful when you use scissors.用剪刀的時候要小心。

pinwheel/ˈpɪnwiːl/風車

The green pinwheel spins in the wind.綠色風車在風中轉動。

blade/bleɪd/葉片

Each blade catches the wind.每一片葉片都能接住風。

motor/ˈmoʊtər/馬達

The motor sits on top of the bottle.馬達裝在寶特瓶的上面。

build/bɪld/組裝

We build it step by step.我們一步一步把它組裝起來。

help/hɛlp/幫忙

The teacher comes to help us.老師過來幫我們的忙。
