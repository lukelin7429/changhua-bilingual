A Day at the Museum

參訪故宮南院
🏛️

We visited the Southern Branch of the National Palace Museum. A guide met us in the bright lobby, we pointed at treasures on the long art timeline, and in the dark rooms, boats and mountains moved all around us.我們參觀了故宮南院。導覽員在明亮的大廳迎接我們，大家在長長的藝術年表前指著一件件寶物；走進暗暗的展廳，船隻和山景就在四周的牆上動了起來。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

museum/mjuˈziːəm/博物館

A museum keeps old and special things.博物館收藏古老又特別的東西。

listen/ˈlɪsn/聽

Sit down and listen to the guide.坐下來聽導覽員說明。

point/pɔɪnt/指

Point to the one you like best.指出你最喜歡的那一件。

painting/ˈpeɪntɪŋ/畫

We look at an old painting on the wall.我們欣賞牆上的一幅古畫。

art/ɑːrt/藝術

The museum has art from all over Asia.博物館裡有來自亞洲各地的藝術品。

boat/boʊt/船

Little boats sail across the wall.小船在牆上航行。
