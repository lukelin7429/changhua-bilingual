Little Visitors

幼稚園小朋友來訪
🧒
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

A yellow school bus pulls in, and a long line of kindergarten children walks through the gate. The big kids clap, wave, and then play a ball game with their little visitors.黃色的幼兒園校車開進校園，小朋友排成長長一排走進校門。大哥哥大姊姊拍手、揮手歡迎，還陪他們一起玩球。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

welcome/ˈwɛlkəm/歡迎

The sign says welcome to Lushang.看板上寫著歡迎來到路上。

line/laɪn/一排

The children walk in a long line.小朋友排成長長一排走進來。

clap/klæp/拍手

We clap our hands to say hello.我們拍手打招呼。

run/rʌn/跑

The little kids run across the court.小朋友跑過球場。

bus/bʌs/校車

The kindergarten kids come by bus.幼稚園小朋友坐校車來。

wave/weɪv/揮手

We wave goodbye to the bus.我們向校車揮手說再見。
