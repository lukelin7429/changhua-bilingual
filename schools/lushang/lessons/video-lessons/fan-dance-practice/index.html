Fan Dance: Practice

扇子舞・練習篇
🎀
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Before we dance, we tie ribbon bows on our fans. Then we practice the basic move: step in place, raise one arm straight, and spin the fan with the other hand. We learn where our balance and strength come from, so the fan turns at an even speed. Our dance coach shows us each move and helps us fix our mistakes.跳舞前，我們先在扇子上綁好緞帶蝴蝶結。接著練習基本動作：原地踏步、一手舉直、另一手轉扇子。我們要了解身體的施力點和重心，扇子才能轉得穩、速度一致。舞蹈教練會親自示範，並個別指導、糾正我們的動作。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

ribbon/ˈrɪbən/緞帶

Each fan has a white ribbon.每把扇子上都有一條白色緞帶。

tie/taɪ/綁

Tie the ribbon into a bow.把緞帶綁成蝴蝶結。

fan/fæn/扇子

Our dance fan is big and round.我們的舞蹈扇子又大又圓。

spin/spɪn/旋轉

Spin the fan, and the cloth flies up.把扇子轉起來，扇上的布就飛起來了。

step/stɛp/踏步

Keep your whole foot down when you step.踏步時整個腳掌要踩穩，不要用腳尖。

copy/ˈkɑːpi/模仿

Watch the coach and copy her moves.看著教練，模仿她的動作。
