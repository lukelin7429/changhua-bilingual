主題式影片課程

Every lesson here was filmed at Lushang or on our class trips. Each video is under a minute, with six words, three sentences, and a matching game.每一堂課都在路上校園或校外教學實地拍攝。影片不到一分鐘，搭配六個單字、三個句子和一個配對小遊戲。

🥁▶
Lesson 01
Taiko Drums
太鼓課

Rows of big drums, a stick in each hand, and one loud beat all together.一排排大鼓，雙手握著鼓棒，全班一起打出響亮的節奏。

Start Learning →
🌸▶
Lesson 02
Paper Flowers
宣紙花 DIY

Fold a strip of soft paper, open it petal by petal, and a flower appears.把薄薄的宣紙摺起來，一片片拉開花瓣，一朵花就出現了。

Start Learning →
🌿▶
Lesson 03
Making Mosquito Spray
左手香防蚊液

Leaves of Indian borage soak in a big jar and become our own mosquito spray.把左手香的葉子泡在大罐子裡，做成我們自己的防蚊液。

Start Learning →
🤸▶
Lesson 04
Aerobics Contest
健身操比賽

Purple uniforms, big moves, and judges watching every step.穿著紫色運動服，動作做好做滿，評審在一旁仔細看。

Start Learning →
🏮▶
Lesson 05
The Ghost Festival
中元普渡

In the seventh lunar month, we fold joss paper and burn it as an offering.農曆七月，我們一起摺金紙、燒金紙，完成普渡的祭拜。

Start Learning →
🎺▶
Lesson 06
At the Brass Factory
參訪銅管樂器工廠

Old trumpets behind glass, and tube horns we get to blow ourselves.玻璃櫃裡的老小號，還有我們自己動手吹的管子號角。

Start Learning →
🏛️▶
Lesson 07
A Day at the Museum
參訪故宮南院

A visit to the Southern Branch of the National Palace Museum — old art, and walls that come alive.全班參觀故宮南院，欣賞古老的藝術品，也走進會動的光影展廳。

Start Learning →
🧒▶
Lesson 08
Little Visitors
幼稚園小朋友來訪

Kindergarten friends come to visit, and the big kids line up to welcome them.幼稚園的小朋友來參觀，路上的大哥哥大姊姊排好隊歡迎他們。

Start Learning →
🏃▶
Lesson 09
Let's Go Jogging
慢跑

Jogging keeps us healthy — and at school, we only jog on the track.慢跑讓身體更健康——在學校，我們只在操場跑道上慢跑。

Start Learning →
🏐▶
Lesson 10
Volleyball Practice
排球練習

Toss, catch, and pass — practicing volleyball with a partner under a big blue sky.拋球、接球、傳球——在藍天下和夥伴一起練排球。

Start Learning →
🎩▶
Lesson 11
A Magic Show
魔術表演

A magician comes to school with ropes, scarves, flags, and a very surprising hat.魔術師帶著繩子、絲巾、旗子和一頂神奇的帽子來到學校。

Start Learning →
🍮▶
Lesson 12
Making Aiyu Jelly
搓愛玉

Put on gloves, rub the aiyu seeds in water, and watch it turn into jelly.戴上手套，把愛玉籽放在水裡搓一搓，看著水慢慢變成愛玉凍。

Start Learning →
🍰▶
Lesson 13
Teachers Bake a Pound Cake
老師做水果磅蛋糕

This time the teachers are the students — mixing butter and filling pans for a fruit pound cake.這次換老師當學生——攪拌奶油、把麵糊裝進模具，一起做水果磅蛋糕。

Start Learning →
🌬️▶
Lesson 14
Making a Wind Turbine
動手做風力發電機

A science museum visits our school, and we build little wind turbines with our own hands.科博館到校服務，我們親手組裝小小的風力發電機。

Start Learning →
🪨▶
Lesson 15
Rocks and Minerals
觀察岩石與礦物

Open the box, pick up a rock, and compare it with the pictures in the guidebook.打開標本盒，拿起一塊石頭，和圖鑑上的照片比一比。

Start Learning →
⚖️▶
Lesson 16
Learning About Levers
認識槓桿

Hang weights on each side and see if you can make the lever balance.在兩邊掛上砝碼，看看能不能讓槓桿平衡。
