主題式影片課程

Every lesson here was filmed at Lushang or on our class trips. Each video is under a minute, with six words, three sentences, and a matching game.每一堂課都在路上校園或校外教學實地拍攝。影片不到一分鐘，搭配六個單字、三個句子和一個配對小遊戲。

🥁▶
Lesson 01
Taiko Drums
太鼓課

Rows of big drums, a stick in each hand, and one loud beat all together.一排排大鼓，雙手握著鼓棒，全班一起打出響亮的節奏。

Start Learning →
🌸▶
Lesson 02
Paper Flowers
宣紙花 DIY

Fold a strip of soft paper, open it petal by petal, and a flower appears.把薄薄的宣紙摺起來，一片片拉開花瓣，一朵花就出現了。

Start Learning →
🌿▶
Lesson 03
Making Mosquito Spray
左手香防蚊液

Leaves of Indian borage soak in a big jar and become our own mosquito spray.把左手香的葉子泡在大罐子裡，做成我們自己的防蚊液。

Start Learning →
🤸▶
Lesson 04
Aerobics Contest
健身操比賽

Purple uniforms, big moves, and judges watching every step.穿著紫色運動服，動作做好做滿，評審在一旁仔細看。

Start Learning →
🏮▶
Lesson 05
The Ghost Festival
中元普渡

In the seventh lunar month, we fold joss paper and burn it as an offering.農曆七月，我們一起摺金紙、燒金紙，完成普渡的祭拜。

Start Learning →
🎺▶
Lesson 06
At the Brass Factory
參訪銅管樂器工廠

Old trumpets behind glass, and tube horns we get to blow ourselves.玻璃櫃裡的老小號，還有我們自己動手吹的管子號角。

Start Learning →
🏛️▶
Lesson 07
A Day at the Museum
參訪故宮南院

A visit to the Southern Branch of the National Palace Museum — old art, and walls that come alive.全班參觀故宮南院，欣賞古老的藝術品，也走進會動的光影展廳。
