Painting Lanterns

繪畫燈籠
🎨
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

In art class, each student gets a round, white paper lantern. Holding it steady with one hand, they paint it slowly and carefully with a paintbrush.美勞課上，每個人都拿到一顆圓圓的白色紙燈籠。一手扶著燈籠，一手拿畫筆，慢慢地、仔細地上色。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

lantern/ˈlæntərn/燈籠

My lantern is made of paper.我的燈籠是紙做的。

paint/peɪnt/上色

I paint my lantern purple.我把燈籠塗成紫色。

paintbrush/ˈpeɪntbrʌʃ/畫筆

Dip your paintbrush in the paint.把畫筆沾一沾顏料。

blue/bluː/藍色的

The top of the lantern is blue.燈籠的上半部是藍色的。

white/waɪt/白色的

This lantern is still white.這顆燈籠還是白色的。

round/raʊnd/圓圓的

The lantern is big and round.燈籠又大又圓。
