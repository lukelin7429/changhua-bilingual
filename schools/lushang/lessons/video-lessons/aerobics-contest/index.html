Aerobics Contest

健身操比賽
🤸
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

It's contest day in the activity hall. Each class takes the floor to jump, stretch, and dance to the music, while the judges take notes and the whole school watches.今天是健身操比賽，各班輪流上場，跟著音樂跳躍、伸展、舞動，評審認真記分，全校同學在旁邊觀看。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

contest/ˈkɑːntɛst/比賽

Every class joins the contest.每一班都參加比賽。

dance/dæns/跳舞

We dance to the music.我們跟著音樂跳舞。

jump/dʒʌmp/跳

Jump up high on the beat.跟著節拍跳高高。

arms/ɑːrmz/手臂

Swing your arms up and down.把手臂上下擺動。

judge/dʒʌdʒ/評審

The judges write down our scores.評審寫下我們的分數。

watch/wɑːtʃ/觀看

The other classes sit and watch.其他班級坐著觀看。
