The Ghost Festival

中元普渡
🏮
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽
🎬
Video coming soon影片上傳中，敬請期待

The Ghost Festival comes in the seventh month of the lunar calendar. Students fold joss paper into little shapes, then drop it into the fire one piece at a time while the teachers keep everyone safe.中元普渡在農曆七月。同學們把金紙摺成小小的形狀，再一張一張丟進金爐裡，老師在旁邊注意大家的安全。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

joss paper/dʒɑːs ˈpeɪpər/金紙

We burn joss paper during the Ghost Festival.中元普渡時我們燒金紙。

gold/ɡoʊld/金色的

Each piece of joss paper has a shiny gold square.每張金紙上都有一塊亮亮的金色方塊。

fire/ˈfaɪər/火

The fire in the burner is very hot.金爐裡的火非常燙。

burn/bɜːrn/燒

The paper burns very fast.紙很快就燒起來了。

throw/θroʊ/丟

Throw the paper gently into the fire.輕輕把紙丟進火裡。

box/bɑːks/箱子

The joss paper comes in a big box.金紙裝在一個大箱子裡。
