At the Brass Factory

參訪銅管樂器工廠
🎺
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

On our field trip to a brass instrument factory, we saw shiny trumpets from many years ago, listened to the guide, and then blew into horns made from plastic tubes.校外教學來到卡羅爾銅管樂器觀光工廠，我們看到許多年代久遠、閃閃發亮的小號，聽導覽員解說，還用塑膠管做的號角吹出聲音。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

trumpet/ˈtrʌmpɪt/小號

A trumpet has three buttons on top.小號上面有三個按鍵。

shiny/ˈʃaɪni/閃亮的

The old trumpets are gold and shiny.這些老小號金黃又閃亮。

horn/hɔːrn/號角

My horn makes a loud sound.我的號角可以發出很大的聲音。

tube/tuːb/管子

The tube goes around in a big circle.管子繞成一個大圓圈。

blow/bloʊ/吹

Blow hard to make a sound.用力吹才會發出聲音。

guide/ɡaɪd/導覽員

The guide tells us about each trumpet.導覽員為我們介紹每一支小號。
