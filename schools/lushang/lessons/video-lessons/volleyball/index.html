Volleyball Practice

排球練習
🏐
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

On a sunny day, the class spreads out on the track with volleyballs. The coach shows how to keep your arms straight, then everyone practices tossing and passing with a partner.大晴天，全班帶著排球到跑道上練習。教練先示範手臂要伸直併攏，再讓大家和夥伴一起練習拋球和傳球。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

volleyball/ˈvɑːlibɔːl/排球

This volleyball is red, white, and blue.這顆排球是紅、白、藍三色的。

coach/koʊtʃ/教練

The coach shows us how to hold our arms.教練示範手臂該怎麼擺。

toss/tɔːs/拋

Toss the ball gently to your friend.把球輕輕拋給你的朋友。

catch/kætʃ/接

Watch the ball and catch it with both hands.看著球，用雙手接住。

pass/pæs/傳球

Pass the ball with your forearms.用前臂把球傳出去。

partner/ˈpɑːrtnər/夥伴

Find a partner and practice together.找一個夥伴一起練習。
