Tea Time

泡茶
🍵
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Making tea is an old and graceful tradition in Chinese culture. Hot water goes into a small teapot, the tea is poured into little cups, and everyone sits down to talk about school.泡茶是中華文化中古老而優雅的傳統。熱水倒進小茶壺，再把茶倒進一個個小茶杯，大家坐下來一起討論學校的事情。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

tea/tiː/茶

This green tea smells nice.這杯綠茶聞起來很香。

teapot/ˈtiːpɑːt/茶壺

The teapot has blue flowers on it.茶壺上有藍色的花。

kettle/ˈkɛtl/水壺

The water in the kettle is boiling.水壺裡的水煮開了。

teacup/ˈtiːkʌp/茶杯

Each teacup is small and round.每個茶杯都小小圓圓的。

hot/hɑːt/熱的

Be careful. The tea is hot!小心，茶很燙！

chat/tʃæt/聊天

The teachers chat over tea.老師們一邊喝茶一邊聊天。
