Rocks and Minerals

觀察岩石與礦物

In science class, each student gets a box of rock and mineral samples. They pick up each one, look closely, and compare it with the guidebook while the teacher explains.自然課上，每個人都拿到一盒岩石礦物標本。大家一塊一塊拿起來仔細觀察，一邊聽老師說明，一邊和圖鑑對照。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

rock/rɑːk/岩石

This rock is dark and heavy.這塊岩石又黑又重。

mineral/ˈmɪnərəl/礦物

A mineral can be shiny or dull.礦物有的亮亮的，有的暗暗的。

tray/treɪ/分格盒

Each sample has its own spot in the tray.每個標本在分格盒裡都有自己的位置。

yellow/ˈjɛloʊ/黃色的

This mineral is bright yellow.這個礦物是亮黃色的。

compare/kəmˈpɛr/比較

Compare the two rocks side by side.把兩塊石頭放在一起比較看看。

guidebook/ˈɡaɪdbʊk/圖鑑

Find the rock in your guidebook.在圖鑑裡找到這塊石頭。
