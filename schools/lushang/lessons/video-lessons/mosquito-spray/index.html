Making Mosquito Spray

左手香防蚊液
🌿
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

The leaves of Indian borage have been soaking in a big jar. Now it's time to pour the liquid into cups, pass it through a funnel, and fill our own little spray bottles.左手香的葉子已經在大罐子裡泡了一段時間。現在把泡好的液體倒進杯子，用漏斗裝進小小的噴瓶裡，完成自己的防蚊液。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

leaf/liːf/葉子

A leaf from this plant has a strong smell.這種植物的葉子味道很濃。

jar/dʒɑːr/罐子

The leaves soak in a big glass jar.葉子泡在一個大玻璃罐裡。

pour/pɔːr/倒

Pour slowly so you don't spill.慢慢倒，才不會灑出來。

cup/kʌp/杯子

Hold the cup steady.把杯子拿穩。

funnel/ˈfʌnl/漏斗

A funnel helps the liquid go into the bottle.漏斗讓液體順利流進瓶子裡。

bottle/ˈbɑːtl/瓶子

Close the bottle tightly.把瓶子蓋緊。

Say It · 用句子說說看

Tap 🔊 and Say It with Me點 🔊 跟著唸

1
Pour the liquid slowly into the cup.
把液體慢慢倒進杯子裡。
2
Smell it! The smell is very strong!
聞聞看！味道好濃喔！
3
This spray keeps mosquitoes away.
這瓶噴霧可以讓蚊子不靠近。
