Let's Go Jogging

慢跑
🏃
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Everyone heads out to the colorful track for a jog. Running is good for our bodies, but school has one rule: jog on the track only, never in the hallway or the classroom.大家到彩色跑道上慢跑。跑步對身體很好，但學校有一個規定：只能在操場跑道上慢跑，不可以在走廊或教室裡奔跑。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

jog/dʒɑːɡ/慢跑

We jog around the track every morning.我們每天早上繞著跑道慢跑。

track/træk/跑道

Our school track is red, blue, and green.我們學校的跑道有紅、藍、綠三種顏色。

lane/leɪn/跑道的一道

Stay in your own lane.待在自己的那一道跑。

curve/kɜːrv/彎道

Slow down a little at the curve.跑到彎道時稍微放慢速度。

grass/ɡræs/草地

The grass is next to the track.草地就在跑道旁邊。

healthy/ˈhɛlθi/健康的

Jogging keeps our bodies healthy.慢跑讓我們的身體保持健康。
