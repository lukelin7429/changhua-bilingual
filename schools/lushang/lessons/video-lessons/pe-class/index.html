P.E. class takes place on the outdoor basketball courts. Students dribble, pass, and jump up to shoot at the hoop — and have lots of fun running around.體育課在戶外籃球場上課。學生們運球、傳球、跳起來投籃，跑來跑去玩得好開心。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

basketball/ˈbæskɪtbɔːl/籃球

He holds the basketball with both hands.他用雙手拿著籃球。

hoop/huːp/籃框

The ball goes toward the hoop.球朝著籃框飛過去。

shoot/ʃuːt/投籃

Jump up and shoot!跳起來投籃！

court/kɔːrt/球場

The basketball court is green and red.籃球場是綠色和紅色的。

dribble/ˈdrɪbl/運球

Dribble the ball down the court.運球穿過球場。

fun/fʌn/好玩的

P.E. class is so much fun!體育課好好玩！
