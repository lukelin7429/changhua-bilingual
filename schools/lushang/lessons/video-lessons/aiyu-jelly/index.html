Making Aiyu Jelly

搓愛玉
🍮
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Aiyu jelly is a Taiwanese treat. Big and small students share bowls of water, put on plastic gloves, and gently rub and squeeze the seeds until the water turns thick and wobbly.愛玉是台灣特有的點心。大小朋友圍著一碗碗清水，戴上塑膠手套，把愛玉籽輕輕搓、用力擠，直到水變得又稠又Q。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

gloves/ɡlʌvz/手套

Put on clean plastic gloves.戴上乾淨的塑膠手套。

bowl/boʊl/碗

Our group shares one big bowl.我們這組一起用一個大碗。

rub/rʌb/搓

Rub the seeds between your hands.用雙手搓一搓愛玉籽。

squeeze/skwiːz/擠壓

Squeeze hard to get the jelly out.用力擠，把愛玉擠出來。

water/ˈwɔːtər/水

The seeds go into cold water.愛玉籽放進冷水裡。

brown/braʊn/咖啡色的

The water turns light brown.水變成淡淡的咖啡色。

Say It · 用句子說說看

Tap 🔊 and Say It with Me點 🔊 跟著唸

1
Put on your plastic gloves first.
先戴上塑膠手套。
2
Rub the seeds gently in the water.
把愛玉籽放在水裡輕輕搓。
3
Soon the water turns into jelly!
很快地，水就會變成愛玉凍！
