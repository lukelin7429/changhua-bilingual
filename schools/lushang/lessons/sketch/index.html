Three Steps to a 3D Cube

三步驟，畫出立體正方體

Let's Sketch Together! is a three-lesson course that our art teacher, Mr. Chu, designed from his many years of teaching children to sketch. It is simple, easy to understand, and easy to try — and each lesson builds on the one before. 「藝起來學素描」是美術老師朱博揚從多年教兒童素描的經驗發展出來的自編課程，共三節課。課程以「簡單、易懂、好操作」為原則，三個單元環環相扣。

🌈
Step 1 · Rainbow Python 第一步・彩虹大蟒蛇

First, get to know your pencil: H is hard and light, B is soft and dark. Students learn to sharpen a pencil by hand, hold it the right way, and loosen up the wrist with a “rubber pencil” trick. Then they shade a “Rainbow Python” — pencil tones that go from light to dark. 先認識鉛筆：H 比較硬、顏色淡；B 比較軟、顏色深。學生學習自己削鉛筆、正確握筆，再用「鉛筆變軟了」的視覺魔術放鬆手腕，然後畫出由淺到深的「彩虹大蟒蛇」。

🛣️
Step 2 · The Beauty of Perspective 第二步・透視之美

Lushang means “on the road” — and a road is a great way to learn perspective! Just like a road going far away, the lines meet at one point. Students draw a square, mark a point at 45 degrees, and connect three lines to turn it into a 3D cube. 路上國小的「路上」，就是 on the road——而一條路正好最適合學透視！就像伸向遠方的道路，線條會交會在同一點。學生先畫一個正方形，在 45 度角點一點、連上三條線，平面的正方形就變成立體的正方體。

💡
Step 3 · Magic Cube: Light & Shadow 第三步・魔術方塊（光與影）

When light shines on a cube, you see three sides — a light side, a dark side, and gray in between. Students shade each side differently, and their flat drawing pops off the page as a 3D cube. 光線照在正方體上，會出現三個面——亮面、暗面，還有中間的灰面。學生把三個面畫出不同的深淺，平面的畫就變成立體的方塊。

Meet the Teacher

講師介紹
Art Teacher · 美術老師
Mr. Chu 朱博揚 老師
Mr. Chu turned his years of teaching children to sketch into three simple lessons. In class, he sits with students one by one so every drawing gets a little better. 朱老師把多年教孩子素描的經驗，設計成三堂簡單好上手的課。上課時，他會坐下來一個一個指導，讓每一張畫都更進步。
