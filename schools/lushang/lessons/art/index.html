Art, Made Bilingual

美勞課，用雙語上

Welcome to Lushang Elementary School's bilingual program! At Lushang, we mix arts and crafts with learning English. Our teachers use both English and Chinese, creating a fun and bilingual environment. In arts and crafts classes, students work with different materials, learn about colors, patterns, and shapes, and practice English at the same time. We also have a bilingual art club where students can further develop their skills and practice English in a creative setting. 歡迎來到路上國小的雙語課程！在路上，我們將美勞結合英語學習。我們的老師使用英語和中文，創造了一個有趣的雙語環境。在美勞課上，學生使用不同的材料，學習顏色、圖案和形狀，同時練習英語。我們還有一個雙語藝術社團，讓學生可以在創意的環境中進一步發展技能並練習英語。

Why Arts & Crafts?

為什麼要上美勞課？
✂️
Creativity, Skill, and New Friends 發揮創意、學習技能、結交新朋友

Looking for a fun way to be creative and make new friends? In arts and crafts class, you'll use materials like paper, glue, paint, and clay to create art like drawings, paintings, and sculptures — and useful things like pots, baskets, and decorations. Teachers show you how to make things, and you can also use your imagination to create your own projects. Studies show that arts and crafts can help with stress relief and improve hand-eye coordination. 想找一個有趣的方法來發揮創意並結交新朋友嗎？在美勞課中，你會使用像紙張、膠水、顏料和黏土等材料，創作出繪畫、油畫和雕塑，還會製作花盆、籃子和裝飾品等實用物品。老師會教你如何製作東西，你也可以發揮自己的想像力來創作。研究表明，美勞可以幫助減輕壓力並改善手眼協調能力。

Cool & Warm Colors

認識冷暖色系

Cool colors are blues, greens, and purples — they remind us of the sky, water, and nature, and can make us feel calm and relaxed. Warm colors are reds, oranges, and yellows — they remind us of the sun, fire, and autumn leaves, and can make us feel excited and energized. 冷色包括藍色、綠色和紫色，讓我們想起天空、水和大自然，能讓人感到平靜和放鬆。暖色包括紅色、橙色和黃色，讓我們想起太陽、火焰和秋天的樹葉，能讓人感到興奮和充滿活力。

Cool Colors冷色

Cool colors make us feel calm and relaxed. 冷色讓我們感覺平靜和放鬆。

Blue — like the sky and the ocean, blue makes us feel peaceful.藍色：像天空和海洋一樣，讓我們感覺和平。
Indigo — like the deeper parts of the ocean, indigo feels peaceful.靛色：像深海一樣，讓我們感覺平靜。
Purple — calming, especially lighter shades like lavender.紫色：讓人平靜，特別是像薰衣草一樣的淺紫色。
Warm Colors暖色

Warm colors make us feel happy and excited. 暖色讓我們感覺開心和興奮。

Red — strong, and can make us feel excited.紅色：很強烈，能讓我們感覺興奮。
Orange — bright, and makes us feel energetic.橙色：很明亮，讓我們感覺有活力。
Yellow — cheerful, and makes us feel happy.黃色：很開心，讓我們感覺快樂。

Blue & White Porcelain

認識青花瓷
🏺
A Tradition in Clay and Blue 黏土與藍色顏料的傳統工藝

Have you ever heard of blue and white porcelain? It's a type of pottery with blue designs painted on white clay. The designs can be flowers, animals, or cool patterns like waves and clouds. Artists shape the clay into cups, plates, or vases and paint blue designs using a special blue color. The pottery is baked in a very hot oven to make it shiny and hard. You can see blue and white porcelain in museums, art galleries, and sometimes in people's homes. 你聽說過青花瓷嗎？這是一種在白色黏土上繪有藍色圖案的陶瓷。這些圖案可以是花、動物或像波浪和雲朵這樣的酷炫圖案。藝術家將黏土塑造成杯子、盤子或花瓶，並使用特別的藍色顏料繪製藍色圖案。這些陶瓷在非常高溫的烤爐中烘烤，使其光亮且堅硬。你可以在博物館、美術館，有時甚至在家裡看到青花瓷。
