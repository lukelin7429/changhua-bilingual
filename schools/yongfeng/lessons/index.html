Ways to Learn

從校園生活學英文

Real school, real words.

每支影片、每份教材，都取材自永豐真實的校園日常。

53 Videos · 6 Themes
Course 01
Word of the Day
每日一字

53 bilingual videos filmed around the Yongfeng campus, grouped into six themes — from Chinese class and recorder practice to carp streamers, loquats and the martial-arts club. Each word comes with two example sentences taken from the video.53 支雙語影片，全部在永豐校園實地拍攝，分為六大主題——從國語課、直笛練習，到鯉魚旗、枇杷與武術社。每個單字都附兩句取自影片的例句。

Course 02
Teacher Lana's Classroom
外師 Lana Wong 資源站

The resource site of Lana Wong — Yongfeng's foreign English teacher. Teaching materials, picture books and classroom activities, gathered in one place for students and families to explore.永豐外師 Lana Wong 老師的個人資源站，收錄教學教材、繪本與課堂活動，方便學生與家長一站瀏覽。

30 Episodes · For Teachers
Course 03
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers, taught by Sarah Thomas across 10 topics and 30 episodes — from greetings and roll call to exam instructions. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.給老師使用的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學，共 10 大主題、30 支影片——從問候點名到考試指令。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

13 Episodes · By Office
Course 04
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

"Broadcast English" — thirteen short episodes in which each office and section introduces, in English, the announcements it makes over the school PA system, from the curriculum section to the counselling room. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.「廣播英語」——13 支短片，由各處室、各組用英語介紹平時透過校園廣播宣布的內容，從教務處教學組到輔導室。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。
