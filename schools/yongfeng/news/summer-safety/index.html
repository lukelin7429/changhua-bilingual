A Safe, Happy Summer: Water and Sports Safety

快樂夏日、平安戲水：暑期水域與運動安全宣導
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

As summer arrives, more young people head to the water. Families are asked to keep to lawful swimming places with a lifeguard on duty, and to stay away from unwatched streams and coastline, or anywhere that carries a warning sign forbidding swimming.

入夏後，青少年戲水活動增加。請大家選擇有救生員駐守的合法戲水場域，勿前往無人看管的溪流、海邊，或設有警告標示、禁止戲水的區域。

Before getting in, always warm up to help prevent a cramp, and keep three ideas in mind if trouble comes: stay calm, call for help, float on your back. A safe, happy summer means playing hard without taking risks.

下水前一定要熱身，預防抽筋；萬一發生狀況，記得三個觀念：冷靜、呼救、仰漂。安排合適又安全的暑假活動，開心玩耍不冒險。

The school also reminds everyone about summer heat. Watch out for heatstroke and heat exhaustion: warm up before exercise, hydrate in good time, wear breathable clothes, avoid the midday heat, and choose sports venues that are well equipped and up to safety standards.

學校也提醒夏季高溫的熱傷害。注意中暑與熱衰竭並加以防範：運動前充分熱身、適時補充水分、穿著透氣衣物、避開中午高溫時段，並選擇設施完善、安全達標的運動場館。

Have a healthy, peaceful summer — and come back to school in good shape.

健康平安過夏天，開學再見。

safety/ˈseɪfti/安全

Water safety matters in summer.夏天戲水安全很重要。

drown/draʊn/溺水

Learn to swim so you do not drown.學會游泳，才不會溺水。

lifeguard/ˈlaɪfɡɑːrd/救生員

Only swim where a lifeguard is on duty.只在有救生員駐守的地方戲水。

warning sign/ˈwɔːrnɪŋ saɪn/警告標示

Never pass a warning sign.絕不越過警告標示。

warm up/wɔːrm ʌp/熱身

Always warm up before you swim.下水前一定要熱身。

cramp/kræmp/抽筋

Warming up helps prevent a cramp.熱身可以預防抽筋。

hydrate/ˈhaɪdreɪt/補充水分

Hydrate often when it is hot.天氣熱時要常常補充水分。

heatstroke/ˈhiːtstroʊk/中暑

Rest in the shade to avoid heatstroke.在陰涼處休息，避免中暑。
