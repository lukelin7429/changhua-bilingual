Celebrating a Year of Effort: End-of-Term Awards

表揚一學期的努力：期末頒獎
←Back to News回到新聞總覽
得獎同學手持獎狀合影
期末頒獎典禮上的得獎同學

To celebrate a term of hard work and fine results, the school gave out a set of end-of-term awards. There were prizes for the top three in the final exams, and a Progress Award for students who kept trying, pushed past their own limits and lifted their marks.

為表揚同學們一學期來的努力與優異表現，學期末頒發多項獎項。有期末考試前三名的獎項，也有進步獎，肯定持續努力、突破自我、成績進步的同學。

A Top-Five Award recognised the year’s strongest overall results. The Reading Star Award and the reading-certificate prizes went to children who love to read and who completed their reading challenges. The Huiguang arts competition prize celebrated creativity and outstanding work.

「114 學年前五名」表揚學業成績名列前五名的同學。「閱讀之星」與閱讀認證獎勵，頒給熱愛閱讀、積極完成閱讀認證的同學。「惠光藝文競賽」則表揚在藝文競賽中展現創意與優秀表現的同學。

There were scholarships for students who study hard and do well, prizes for pieces chosen in the school magazine, and awards for everyone who joined in the quiz activities — every kind of achievement had its moment.

另有獎助金，鼓勵努力向學、表現優良的同學；「智慧菓投稿得獎」表揚踴躍投稿、作品獲選的同學；各項有獎徵答活動也頒發獎項給獲獎的同學，各種成就都被看見。

Congratulations to every winner. Keep that eager attitude to learning, be brave in taking on a challenge, and keep shining on the road ahead.

恭喜所有獲獎同學！希望大家持續保持積極學習的態度，勇於挑戰自我，在未來的學習道路上持續發光發熱，再創佳績。
