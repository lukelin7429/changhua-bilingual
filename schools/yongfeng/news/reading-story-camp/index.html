Reading, Takes Flight: The Summer Story Camp

閱讀翩翩起舞：115 年暑期閱讀故事營
←Back to News回到新聞總覽
說故事志工分享繪本
孩子玩故事配對遊戲

This summer, Yongfeng ran a story camp with the Changhua Story & Reading Association. Their volunteers led the children into an interesting world of stories, and through lively storytelling, sparked the children’s interest in reading and their imagination.

今年暑假，永豐與彰化縣故事閱讀協會合作辦理閱讀故事營。協會的志工帶領學生走進有趣的故事世界，透過精彩的故事分享，引發孩子的閱讀興趣與想像力。

The camp was more than listening. Children made their own crafts by hand, and used tablets to make short films — thinking up the story, filming it, and acting it out — building creativity and teamwork along the way.

營隊不只是聽故事。學生動手完成有趣的手作作品，還運用平板進行微電影創作，從故事發想、拍攝到演出，培養創意與團隊合作能力。

Finally, each child made a little book, turning what they had read into a work of their own. Through “listen to a story, make a craft, film a movie, create a book”, reading reached out from the page into life and into making things.

最後，孩子透過製作小書，將閱讀內容轉化為自己的作品。在「聽故事、做手作、拍影片、創作小書」的過程中，享受閱讀的樂趣，也讓閱讀從書本延伸到生活與創作。
