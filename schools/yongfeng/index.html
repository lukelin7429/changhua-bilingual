I.

Welcome to Yongfeng

歡迎來到永豐國小

A small school with big dreams.

一所小而美、小而精緻的偏鄉優質小校。

Nestled in the charming rural landscape of Fuxing Township, Yongfeng Elementary School is a gem of education, dedicated to providing a nurturing and quality learning environment for every child. Our small size is our strength — it makes room for a close-knit, supportive community and a personalised approach to learning.

永豐國小坐落於福興鄉迷人的鄉村風光中，是一所小而美、小而精緻、地處偏鄉的優質小校。在一群擁有熱忱與愛心的教師團隊與志工夥伴耕耘下，為孩子們創造出溫馨優質的學習樂園。學校的小型規模正是它的優勢，讓師生成為一個緊密、彼此支持的社群，也讓教育能貼近每一個孩子。

At Yongfeng we take pride in a curriculum that balances academics and physical development. From English and reading to ocarina and violin, from tennis and martial arts to building-block robotics, our programmes are built to grow well-rounded children. With the support of dedicated teachers, volunteers, parents and local enterprises, our students shine across a wide range of fields.

永豐積極推動多元課程，讓孩子能文（英語、閱讀、陶笛、小提琴、積木機器人）也能武（網球、武術、扯鈴、游泳）。在家長、志工團隊的支持及各界企業的資源挹注下，永豐的孩子展現多元成長的新風貌。

Understanding the challenges of rural education, we focus not only on academic excellence but on cultural richness and community involvement. Each year the whole school takes part in at least one outdoor-education trip, so that children experience different cultures, widen their horizons and build real-world life experience.

許多偏鄉學子的家庭背景普遍弱勢，家長多以工作為重心，較無暇給孩子額外的學習機會，因此學校教育更是責無旁貸。除了主題課程，每年至少辦理一場全校性戶外教育，體驗不同族群文化，增加學習視野與生活經驗，讓永豐學子健康、快樂、有效的學習。

A journey where learning is joyful, effective and whole — a golden childhood, with Yongfeng alongside.
快樂、有效、全面的學習之旅 — 金色童年，永豐相隨。

V.

Able in the Arts, Able in Sport

能文能武

Three signature courses carry the whole school.

三大主題課程，貫串整所學校。

Yongfeng develops its children along its own strengths. Three thematic courses lead the way: the Diabolo Club, which keeps a Taiwanese folk art alive in young hands; a tennis programme the school calls “Yongfeng Net, Straight Ahead”; and the Everyday English Learning Camp, which connects the classroom to the wider world. Around them sit reading and writing, painting, and a hands-on farm-to-table course rooted in the local community.

永豐結合並延續學校發展特色，以「發揚民俗技藝—扯鈴社團」、「體育特色—永網直前」與「英語教學國際接軌—生活英語學習營」為三大主題課程。自 109 學年度起，再增加「悅讀慶豐收—閱讀寫作」、「永豐美樂地—繪畫的想像天地」與「食農教育—在地扎根踏查體驗」三個主題課程，並邀請社區共同推動，讓課程更有意義。

A good place to read and grow
讀冊和成長的好所在

Learning at Yongfeng reaches past the classroom — onto the courts, into the gardens, out on the annual outdoor-education trip. The aim is simple: children who are healthy, happy and genuinely learning.

永豐的學習不只待在教室裡——它在球場上、在花園裡、在每年的戶外教育裡。目標很單純：讓孩子健康、快樂，而且真的學到東西。

VI.

What Makes Yongfeng, Yongfeng

今日永豐的學校特色

Four things the school is known for.

四項讓永豐被記住的事。

A Small, Finely Run School
小班小校 · 精緻教學

Small classes and a small school make room for a personalised approach — every child known, every child seen. 小班小校讓教學能貼近每個孩子，看見每一位學生的需要。

“Yongfeng Net, Straight Ahead”
體育特色 · 永網直前

Tennis is the school’s signature sport, played on a half-covered, rain-sheltered court so training carries on whatever the weather. 網球是永豐的體育特色，半戶外遮雨球場讓訓練風雨無阻。

Folk Arts & Everyday English
扯鈴社團 · 生活英語學習營

The Diabolo Club keeps a folk art spinning in young hands, while the Everyday English Learning Camp connects the classroom to the world. 扯鈴社團傳承民俗技藝，生活英語學習營讓英語與國際接軌。

Reading, Writing & the Land
閱讀寫作 · 繪畫 · 食農教育

Newer thematic courses in reading and writing, painting, and farm-to-table education root learning in the local community. 悅讀慶豐收、永豐美樂地與食農教育三個主題課程，讓學習在社區裡扎根。

在家長、志工團隊的支持及各界企業的資源挹注下，永豐持續發展學生多元試探活動，使孩子展現多元成長的新風貌。學校也年年辦理全校性戶外教育，帶孩子把學習的觸角延伸到校外。
