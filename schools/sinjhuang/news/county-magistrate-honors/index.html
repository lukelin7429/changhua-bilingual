Sinjhuang Shines Nationwide

新庄之光，耀眼全國！王惠美縣長送紅榜祝賀
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

This afternoon, Sinjhuang Elementary was filled with pride and joy! On behalf of County Magistrate Wang Hui-mei, Education Supervisor Huang Jun-qi, Secretary Zhuang Jun-cheng, and Secretary Zhang Jia-lin visited the school on Thursday afternoon to deliver a warm congratulatory red banner.

今天下午，新庄國小迎來滿滿的榮耀與喜悅！縣府黃俊錡督學、莊峻承秘書及張嘉麟秘書，特地於週四下午代表縣長王惠美蒞校，送上熱騰騰的祝賀紅榜。

The banner congratulated a sixth-grade student from Class 6-Ding on winning the gold medal in the boys' elementary shot put at the 2026 Taichung Cup National Junior and Elementary Track and Field Championships.

恭喜本校六丁陳同學勇奪「115年臺中盃全國田徑公開賽－國小男子鉛球金牌」！

Beyond encouraging the young champion to keep pushing his limits, the visiting officials praised Sinjhuang's years of dedication to athletics — from past standout alumni to today's rising stars, the school has become a true cradle for top track and field talent.

黃督學與2位縣府秘書除了親自勉勵陳同學再接再厲、持續突破自我外，更大力肯定新庄國小近年在體育領域的深耕與傑出成果。從過去的傑出校友，到如今閃耀全國的陳同學，新庄國小已然成為培育頂尖田徑選手的搖籃！

Thank you to the county magistrate and her team for this recognition — this honor belongs to every teacher, student, and parent at Sinjhuang. We'll keep working to give our children a stage where they can shine. Go, Sinjhuang track and field!

感謝縣長與縣府團隊的關懷與肯定，這份榮耀屬於全體新庄師生與家長。我們會繼續努力，提供孩子們發光發熱的舞台！新庄田徑，一級棒！
