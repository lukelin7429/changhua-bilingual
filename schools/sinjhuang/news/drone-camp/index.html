Summer Drone Camp

新庄國小暑期營隊～無人機高空飛行營
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Want to lift your child's view to a whole new height? Join professional Teacher Yang and explore the technology behind drones — also known as quadcopters!

想讓孩子的視野躍升新高度嗎？跟著專業楊老師，一探無人機的科技奧秘！

Starting from the basics, students build solid foundational knowledge to prepare them to design their own drones in the future. The course also covers the latest flight regulations — such as no registration needed under 250 grams, and the option to earn a flight license and learn about safe flying zones from age 14 — while opening children's eyes to real-world applications like pesticide spraying, disaster relief and firefighting, search and rescue, police work, and delivery services.

從基礎觀念啟航，孩子將建立扎實知識，奠定未來自主設計無人機的實力。課程不僅涵蓋最新飛行法規（如：250g 以下免註冊、14歲即可考取學習執照與安全飛行區域），更開闊孩子眼界，探索無人機於農藥噴灑、救災滅火、搜救、警政及物流外送等前瞻商業應用！

Unlock a tech-forward view of the world and a future career path — this diverse club broadens every child's horizons. Come and enroll at Sinjhuang!

開啟科技眼界，解鎖未來職涯！多元社團開拓您的視野～歡迎踴躍就讀新庄。
