Passing It On

傑出校友返校指導學弟鉛球技巧
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Outstanding alumnus Lin, now an eighth-grader at Heqiun Junior High School who already placed 8th at the National Junior High Games, came back to help coach a Sinjhuang teammate's shot put technique.

傑出校友和群國中林同學（國二已勇奪全中錦第8名）協助指導陳同學鉛球技巧。

Three years ago, Lin could only put the shot 9.8 meters. Small and slight in build, he relied on technique instead of size — and went on to throw over 12 meters at the national championships, bringing honor to the school.

3年前林同學推9.8米～身材瘦小靠技巧，當年參加全國賽推12米多為校爭光！

During county games training on July 24, Lin passed on his shot put technique to his junior teammate — one senior guiding one junior, the warmth between them plain to see.

1150724縣運集訓時林同學指導陳同學推鉛球技巧，學長帶學弟，學長對學弟的厚愛之情，自然流露。

On Thursday, July 23, the junior teammate threw 13.35 meters during training at the stadium, setting a new personal record. Sinjhuang's shot put results have been outstanding in county-wide competition these past few years — everyone is welcome to join the track and field team and help bring more honor to our school!

陳同學1150723週四在體育場集訓推出13.35公尺再創紀錄。新庄這幾年鉛球全縣競賽表現可圈可點。歡迎大家踴躍參加田徑隊，一起來為校爭光喔！
