Diabolo Team Dazzles

扯鈴隊參加代天宮慈善嘉年華晚會表演
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On July 26, our diabolo team performed at the Daitianggong Charity Carnival evening show. Thank you to Principal Wu Wen-hui, Secretary-General Chen and his wife, Vice Chairman Huang and her husband, Honorary Chairman Wang, Volunteer Team Captain Yu, and all the parents who took time to come cheer the children on and guide them.

1150726扯鈴隊參加代天宮慈善嘉年華晚會表演～謝謝吳文輝校長、陳文霆秘書長賢伉儷、黃惠意副會長賢伉儷、王寅權榮譽會長、志工隊俞廷隊長及家長們特別撥冗前來幫孩子們加油打氣與指導。

No wonder the children performed with such polish tonight — their formations were sharp and their energy was outstanding!

難怪今天孩子們的表現特別整齊，表現得特別棒！

Thank you to Coach Zhang for leading the diabolo team through tonight's dazzling performance — it was community outreach at its best, and a wonderful advertisement for Sinjhuang Elementary. Everyone is welcome to enroll at Sinjhuang, and even more welcome to join the diabolo team!

謝謝張教練指導扯鈴隊帶來今天精彩的表演，可以說是幫新庄國小做了社區最佳行銷代言，歡迎大家踴躍就讀新庄國小，更歡迎踴躍加入扯鈴隊。

Finally, thank you to Discipline Section Chief Yi-hua for handling all the paperwork and helping the team complete this year's performance mission.

最後謝謝訓育宜樺組長處理行政事務，完成今年度表演任務。
