National Shot Put Gold

狂賀！鉛球全國金牌～新庄之光
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On the afternoon of July 28, our track and field team's student from Class 5-Ding won the gold medal in the boys' elementary shot put at the Taichung Cup National Track and Field Championships — bringing honor to Changhua County.

本校田徑隊陳同學1150728下午勇奪台中盃全國田徑錦標賽國小男生鉛球全國金牌～為彰化縣爭光。

Thank you to Principal Wu Wen-hui and Chairman Li Ming-cheng for fully supporting the team's expenses, and to Student Affairs Director Li Shih-hung for his coaching.

謝謝吳文輝校長及李銘承會長全力支持經費。指導老師：學務李世宏主任。

Everyone is warmly invited to enroll at Sinjhuang — the number-one elementary school — and to join our track and field team!

歡迎就讀第一名的新庄國小，更歡迎大家踴躍參加田徑隊！新庄國小全體師生暨家長委員會仝賀。
