Summer Soccer Camp

暑期足球營～熱血奔跑，足夢踏實！
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

From July 1 to August 28, our students traded screens for the soccer field, learning solid ball-handling skills under the professional guidance of Coach Lai. Camp days were full of sweat and running, building strong, healthy bodies along the way.

115/7/1-115/8/28 在賴教練的專業指導下，孩子們不僅掌握了扎實的足下競技技巧，更在汗水與奔跑中鍛鍊出強健體魄。

The camp gave children a reason to put down their phones and tablets and step onto the field instead — passing and attacking together, they learned firsthand what teamwork really means.

這場營隊讓孩子放下 3C 螢幕、走入球場，在傳球與進攻之間切身領悟團隊合作的精神。

A summer made brighter by soccer — our students came away with real skills, new friendships, and a portable kind of confidence and persistence they'll carry with them.

充實的暑假因足球而精采，孩子們不僅收穫了滿滿的專業技能與同儕情誼，更帶回了帶著走的堅持與自信！
