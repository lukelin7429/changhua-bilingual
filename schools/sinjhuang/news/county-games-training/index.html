Sweat and Determination

縣運選手集訓花絮
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Sweating it out, chasing another win — the Sinjhuang track and field team is training hard for the 2026 Changhua County Games! At the Hemei Township Sports Meet, our team won 4 overall championship titles, and top-three finishers will represent Hemei Township at the county games from August 5th to 7th.

【揮汗備戰・再創佳績】新庄田徑隊全力迎戰115年縣民運動大會！新庄國小田徑隊在「115年和美鎮民運動大會」展現驚人實力，一舉勇奪 4 項總錦標冠軍！其中榮獲前三名的優秀選手，更將於 8 月 5 日至 7 日代表和美鎮出征縣運會。

This success is thanks to the dedicated coaching of PE Section Chief Bi-qi and Student Affairs Director Shih-hung. With the Changhua County Games approaching, our young athletes are training hard under the blazing summer sun.

這份榮耀的背後，得益於體育組必麒組長與學務世宏主任的專業指導與無私付出。面對即將到來的「115年彰化縣縣民運動大會」，田徑小將們無懼炎炎夏日，頂著豔陽在操場上積極揮汗練習。

With careful guidance from their teachers and their own hard work, we believe they'll perform at their best and break through their limits — bringing more honor to our school and community. Let's cheer on these brave young athletes chasing their dreams!

相信在師長的悉心帶領與選手們的勤勉苦練下，大家定能發揮最佳實力、突破自我，再次為學校與地方爭光！讓我們共同為這群勇敢追夢的體育小將們加油打氣！
