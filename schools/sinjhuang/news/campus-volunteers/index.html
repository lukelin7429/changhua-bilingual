Summer Guardians

暑假幕後英雄～環境志工協助校園整理
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Even in summer vacation, Sinjhuang's campus stays beautiful — thanks to the unsung heroes working behind the scenes. On July 21, Principal Wu Wen-hui led Ms. Shu-fen and environmental volunteers Mr. and Mrs. Lin Shu-long, Mr. and Mrs. Wang Guo-hsing, volunteer Ms. Lin Hsueh-mei, and security guard Mr. Tang in mowing the grass around the field, basketball court, and long jump area, and cleaning up the campus under the blazing heat.

1150721暑假新庄校園依舊美麗～因為有幕後英雄~環境志工群協助校園割草與打掃。感謝吳文輝校長率領淑芬小姐及環保志工林樹龍大哥賢伉儷、王國興大哥賢伉儷、志工林雪梅大姐、保全湯大哥等人頂著豔陽高溫天氣幫忙操場、籃球場、跳遠周邊草地割草，維護校園環境整潔打掃工作。

Thanks to their selfless dedication, Sinjhuang stays a clean and beautiful campus for our students and the whole community. Having heroes like you behind the scenes is truly Sinjhuang's good fortune — thank you again!

提供一個乾淨美麗的新庄校園，感謝您們無私的奉獻～嘉惠新庄學子及社區民眾！有您們這些幕後英雄真是新庄之福！再次感恩。
