Hello, little champions!

哈囉，小小冠軍們！

←Back to Lessons回到課程總覽

Today, we're talking about our superstars — the Sinjhuang Elementary School track and field team! This team is amazing. They run fast, jump high, and throw far. They are a group of students just like you, who love sports and teamwork.

今天，我們來談談我們的超級明星——新庄小學的田徑隊！這個隊伍非常棒。他們跑得快、跳得高、投得遠。他們就像你們一樣，是一群熱愛運動和團隊合作的學生。

Even when it's cold outside during winter break, they still practice really hard. They want to be the best they can be. And guess what? Their hard work pays off!

即使寒假外面天氣寒冷，他們仍然努力訓練。他們想要盡力發揮。而你知道嗎？他們的努力得到了回報！

For many years, our team has done great things. They have won races, jumped over the highest bars, and thrown balls really far. It's a tradition here, a special part of our school that makes us all proud.

多年來，我們的隊伍做出了偉大的成就。他們贏得了賽跑，跳過了最高的橫桿，投擲得非常遠。這是我們學校的一個傳統，一個讓我們所有人都感到特別驕傲的地方。

This wouldn't be possible without our wonderful coach and teachers. They teach our team how to be strong, fast, and smart on the field. They show them how to work together and support each other. Because of them, our students shine bright in track and field.

如果沒有我們優秀的教練和老師，這一切都不可能實現。他們教導我們的隊伍如何在田徑場上變得更強、更快、更聰明。他們展示了如何團結合作，互相支持。因為他們，我們的學生在田徑運動中綻放光芒。

We are so proud of our team. They show us what it means to work hard and never give up. Go, Sinjhuang stars! Keep running, jumping, and throwing. We are cheering for you!

我們為我們的隊伍感到非常自豪。他們向我們展示了什麼是努力和永不放棄的意義。加油，新庄之星！繼續跑步、跳躍和投擲。我們為你們加油！
