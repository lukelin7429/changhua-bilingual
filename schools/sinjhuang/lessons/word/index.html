WORD 01
professional development
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都會為老師安排專業進修課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
老師們在專業進修課程中學習新的技巧與教學策略。
WORD 02
Chinese yo-yo (n)
扯鈴
The Chinese yo-yo is a popular toy many children enjoy.
扯鈴是許多孩子都喜愛的一項童玩。
The students perform tricks with Chinese yo-yos.
學生們用扯鈴表演各種花式技巧。
WORD 03
wash your hands
洗手
It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手非常重要。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
吃飯前一定要用肥皂和水把手洗乾淨。
WORD 04
weekly English
每週英語
The school has a weekly English program for students.
學校為學生安排了每週英語課程。
The students have to learn a new sentence in English every week.
學生每週都要學一句新的英文句子。
WORD 05
jump rope (n)
跳繩
Jumping rope is a great form of cardio.
跳繩是很棒的有氧運動。
Once you get the hang of it, you can jump rope really fast.
一旦抓到訣竅，你就可以跳得非常快。
WORD 06
class dismissal
放學
The students line up outside for class dismissal.
放學時，學生們在外面排隊。
They go home after class dismissal.
放學後，他們就回家了。
WORD 07
soccer (n)
足球
Soccer is a very popular sport.
足球是一項非常受歡迎的運動。
The students are having a lot of fun playing soccer.
學生們踢足球踢得很開心。
WORD 08
dodgeball (n)
躲避球
The students are playing dodgeball.
學生們正在玩躲避球。
Dodgeball is a popular school game.
躲避球是校園裡很受歡迎的遊戲。
WORD 09
playground (n)
遊樂場
The students play different games at the school playground.
學生們在學校的遊樂場玩各種遊戲。
The playground has monkey bars for students to hang on.
遊樂場裡有單槓，讓學生可以吊掛玩耍。
WORD 10
serve (v)
打菜、盛菜
During school lunch, students are served food or serve themselves.
學校午餐時間，學生有人被打菜，也有人自己盛菜。
School lunch is always served fresh and healthy.
學校午餐總是新鮮又健康。
WORD 11
soccer practice (n)
足球訓練
The students dribble soccer balls in soccer practice.
學生在足球訓練中練習運球。
They have to dribble around the cones in soccer practice.
他們在足球訓練中要繞過標誌桶運球。
WORD 12
basketball practice (n)
籃球訓練
The students dribble basketballs in basketball practice.
學生在籃球訓練中練習運球。
They shoot and pass the ball in basketball practice.
他們在籃球訓練中練習投籃與傳球。
WORD 13
calligraphy (n)
書法
The students are quietly practicing Chinese calligraphy.
學生們安靜地練習書法。
The calligraphy teacher is patiently instructing his students.
書法老師耐心地指導學生。
WORD 14
explore (v)
探索
The students go out to explore their community.
學生們走出校園，探索他們的社區。
They explore different parts of their community.
他們探索社區裡的不同角落。
WORD 15
event (n)
活動
The school holds a disability awareness event.
學校舉辦了一場身心障礙意識宣導活動。
This event aims to foster empathy and understanding among students.
這個活動希望培養學生的同理心與理解力。
WORD 16
assemble (v)
組裝
The students assemble their robots.
學生們組裝自己的機器人。
They follow the instructions to assemble their robots.
他們按照說明組裝機器人。
WORD 17
chess club (n)
象棋社
The students play Chinese chess in the chess club.
學生們在象棋社下中國象棋。
The chess club meets after school to play Chinese chess.
象棋社在放學後聚會，一起下象棋。
WORD 18
ping pong championship
乒乓球錦標賽
The students participate in the ping pong championship.
學生們參加乒乓球錦標賽。
They worked very hard to get to the ping pong championship.
他們非常努力才打進乒乓球錦標賽。
WORD 19
graduation trip
畢業旅行
The students go on a graduation trip to a recreational farm.
學生們到休閒農場進行畢業旅行。
The students play and enjoy their time together during their graduation trip.
學生們在畢業旅行中一起玩樂、共度美好時光。
WORD 20
Taekwondo (n)
跆拳道
The students are learning Taekwondo in PE.
學生們在體育課學習跆拳道。
You learn to kick high in Taekwondo.
在跆拳道中，你會學到把腳踢得很高。
WORD 21
kick (v)
踢
The students sit at the edge of the pool and practice kicking in the water.
學生們坐在泳池邊，練習在水中踢腿。
You can practice kicking in the water before you learn how to swim.
在學會游泳之前，你可以先練習水中踢腿。
WORD 22
observe (v)
觀課
Some teachers are in the back of the classroom, observing the class.
有些老師會在教室後方觀課。
Teachers improve their teaching skills by observing others' classes.
老師們透過觀摩別人的課堂來精進教學技巧。
WORD 23
Taiwanese (n)
台語
The teacher is teaching Taiwanese in the Taiwanese class.
老師在台語課上教台語。
The students learn Taiwanese from a storybook.
學生們透過故事書學習台語。
WORD 24
computer lab (n)
電腦教室
A computer lab is a room for students to use computers.
電腦教室是提供學生使用電腦的教室。
In the computer lab, students can make fun pictures and play learning games.
在電腦教室裡，學生可以創作有趣的圖畫，也能玩學習遊戲。
WORD 25
farm
耕作
The students learn about farming at school.
學生們在學校學習耕作。
The school has land to farm different crops.
學校有田地可以種植不同的農作物。
