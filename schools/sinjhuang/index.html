I.

Welcome

歡迎來到新庄國小

A century of shade, a century of growth.

百年樟蔭，百年樹人。

Established in 1923, Sinjhuang Elementary School stands as a beacon of educational excellence, proudly framed by two majestic, century-old camphor trees. Our tradition of excellence has been nurturing young minds for generations.

本校從民國 12 年 5 月 21 日創校至今已滿百歲，校園內矗立著兩棵公告列管的珍貴老樟樹，樹齡超過百年依舊翠綠茁壯，陪伴了無數新庄學童的成長與學習。

We are honored to have been awarded the 2014 Silver Award for Teaching Excellence from the Ministry of Education, and our commitment to holistic education was recognized again in 2017, when we were named a Featured Life Education School. Guided by our principal and every teacher, Sinjhuang has grown a bilingual, technology-driven vision that carries the value of "a hundred years to grow a person" into every classroom.

新庄國小擁有優良傳統（103 年教育部教學卓越銀質獎、106 年度教育部生命教育特色學校），在校長及全體教師努力營造「以雙語、科技為導向」的理念下，發展出新庄的課程願景及學生圖像，透過多元的課程，體現百年樹人的價值。

Sinjhuang Elementary is more than an institution — it is a community shaping a brighter future for every child who walks beneath our ancient camphor trees. We invite you to discover our programs and join our journey.

新庄國小不僅是一所學校，更是一個攜手打造孩子美好未來的大家庭。歡迎您走進新庄，親身感受這片老樟樹下的學習旅程。

Healthy living, diverse innovation, joyful learning — raising every child to be a citizen of the Earth.
課程願景：健康樂活、多元創新、快樂學習，成就孩子成為地球公民。
Rooted for a Century
老樟樹，百年的陪伴

Two officially registered camphor trees, each more than a hundred years old, still stand tall and green on our campus — living witnesses to generation after generation of Sinjhuang children growing up in their shade.

校園內矗立著兩棵公告列管的珍貴老樟樹，樹齡超過百年依舊翠綠茁壯，陪伴了無數新庄學童的成長與學習。

II.

Life at Sinjhuang

新庄的課程願景

Bilingual, tech-driven, and rooted in every child's potential.

雙語科技為導向，開展每個孩子的潛能。

Bilingual & Tech-Driven
雙語與科技導向

Guided by a vision that blends bilingual and technology education, Sinjhuang's curriculum connects with the core competencies of the 108 Curriculum, helping children build a global outlook and digital literacy through self-directed, hands-on learning.

Personalized Growth
適性揚才

The new curriculum values adaptive education that sparks every child's unique potential. Sinjhuang actively plans diverse opportunities for students to explore and shine, so each child's talents can grow in their own way.

Global Citizens
地球公民

Health, happiness, and innovation run through everything we teach — raising children who are joyful learners today and thoughtful citizens of the world tomorrow.

A Century of Excellence
百年優良傳統

Since 1923, Sinjhuang has built a tradition of excellence — recognized with the Ministry of Education's Silver Award for Teaching Excellence in 2014, and named a Featured Life Education School in 2017.

World View & Tech Literacy
新庄國小的課程願景，與 12 年國教課綱強調的核心素養及自主學習充分接軌，期透過「課程設計與教學引導」打造孩子的世界觀與科技素養，透過自主行動學習，引領學生走向世界。
Vision · 課程願景 II
Adaptive, Multi-Talent Learning
12 年國教課程重視適性教育，激發每個孩子不同的潛能，本校也積極策畫多元展能機會，開展每個學生的不同潛質，使其潛能能得到適性發展。
Vision · 學生圖像
Healthy · Innovative · Joyful
健康樂活、多元創新、快樂學習——新庄國小以此為課程願景，成就孩子成為地球公民。
III.

Our Crest

校徽的故事

Our school crest is built around a heart — a symbol of Sinjhuang's whole-hearted devotion to education. Two embracing arms echo a parent's care, while a book unfolds in yellow, red, and black to represent a rich and diverse learning culture, with a bird in flight standing for children soaring toward their dreams. The lush leaves above represent our century-old campus trees and long history.

以心型為 Logo 基底，代表新庄國小對於教育全心全意、全力以赴，並融入擁抱之姿的人形意象，象徵師長對於學童的愛護與關懷，諄諄教誨引導學子們走向入德之門。透過黃、紅、黑展開的書本意象來代表多元發展的學習風氣，同時置入飛鳥型態來象徵展翅高飛。厚實的茂盛樹葉代表百年校樹、悠久的創校歷史。
