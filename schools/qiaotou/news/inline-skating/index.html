Fast on Eight Wheels

115 學年度縣長盃競速直排輪溜冰錦標賽　捷報
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Speed skating is a strange sport to watch, because the effort does not look like effort. The skater barely seems to be trying, and then the clock tells you what actually happened. At the Changhua County Magistrate’s Cup Speed Inline Skating Championship, two Qiaotou skaters went round the track against the rest of the county — and came home with three certificates.

競速直排輪是一項看起來不太費力的運動——選手好像沒有很用力，然後碼錶會告訴你剛才到底發生了什麼事。在彰化縣 115 學年度縣長盃競速直排輪溜冰錦標賽中，橋頭國小兩位選手與全縣好手同場競技，一共抱回三張獎狀。

In the girls’ Group A for grades five and six, a sixth grader from Class 6A took second place over 400 meters in 50.399 seconds, and fourth over 600 meters in 1 minute 21.085 seconds. Those numbers are worth saying out loud: 400 meters in fifty seconds is roughly 29 kilometers an hour, which is faster than most people ride a bicycle through town.

在國小五、六年級女子甲組，六年甲班的選手以 50 秒 399 拿下 400 公尺計時賽第二名，並以 1 分 21 秒 085 拿下 600 公尺計時賽第四名。這兩個數字值得唸出來：400 公尺跑 50 秒，大約是時速 29 公里——比大多數人在市區騎腳踏車還快。

In the boys’ Group B for grade three, a third grader from Class 3A finished sixth over 200 meters in 30.349 seconds — a county-level final, in the third grade, at a shade under 24 kilometers an hour.

在國小三年級男子乙組，三年甲班的選手以 30 秒 349 拿下 200 公尺計時賽第六名——三年級就站上縣級賽道，時速將近 24 公里。

Nobody skates like this by accident. Behind every one of those times are mornings on the track, a lot of falling over in the early years, and the patience to shave off hundredths of a second that nobody else would notice. Congratulations to both skaters — and to everyone at home and at school who got them to the start line. 🏅

沒有人會不小心溜得這麼快。這些成績的背後，是一個又一個在跑道上的早晨、剛學時摔過的無數次，以及為了削掉零點零幾秒而反覆練習的耐心。恭喜兩位選手，也恭喜把他們送到起跑線上的家人與師長！🏅

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

skate/skeɪt/溜冰

They skate on four wheels in a line.他們踩著一直線的四個輪子溜冰。

speed/spiːd/速度

Speed is distance divided by time.速度就是距離除以時間。

race/reɪs/競賽

A 400-meter race is over in under a minute.400 公尺的競賽，不到一分鐘就結束了。

track/træk/賽道

Every lap of the track looks the same.賽道上的每一圈看起來都一樣。

meter/ˈmiːtər/公尺

One meter is about one long step.一公尺大約是一大步。

fast/fæst/快的

Twenty-nine kilometers an hour is fast.時速 29 公里，真的很快。

finish/ˈfɪnɪʃ/終點

Keep going all the way to the finish.一路撐到終點為止。

place/pleɪs/名次

Second place in the whole county is no small thing.全縣第二名次，可不是小事。
