Hummingbirds on the Restroom Wall

全新「性別友善廁所 All Gender Restroom」正式亮相
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

A school restroom is not usually the room anybody wants to show visitors. Qiaotou has just changed that. After careful planning and construction, the school’s new All Gender Restroom opened on November 27 — and it was built to break the stereotype of what a school restroom is, by putting art, a touch of the forest and genuinely thoughtful fittings all into the same space.

學校的廁所，通常不會是你想帶客人去看的那一間。橋頭國小剛剛改變了這件事。歷經細心規劃與施工，全新的「性別友善廁所（All Gender Restroom）」於 11 月 27 日正式亮相。這次校方打破了傳統廁所的刻板印象，把美感教育、自然童趣與貼心機能通通搬進了這裡。

Push the door and you walk into what the school calls its forest gallery. A hummingbird is in flight across the curved wall, a branch of green leaves runs along beside it, and squirrels sit at either end. On the white brick wall next door hang framed paintings made to be looked at: children reading on the grass under a big tree, and children drumming in the shade. Coming in here feels less like a trip to the restroom and more like walking through an exhibition.

推開門，就走進校方所說的「森林系美學藝廊」。弧形牆面上有蜂鳥展翅飛舞，旁邊一根長滿綠葉的樹枝橫過牆面，兩頭各站著一隻可愛的小松鼠。一旁的白色磚牆則掛上了專門要讓人駐足的藝術畫作：孩子們在大樹下的草地上閱讀，以及孩子們在樹蔭裡開心擊鼓。每一次走進來，都像逛展覽一樣心情愉悅。

The room is also simply a nicer place to be. High windows pull daylight all the way in, a black raised ceiling lifts the space, and warm wood-grain partitions soften it. It is airy and bright — a decisive goodbye to dark and damp.

這個空間本身也舒服得多。高窗引入滿滿的自然陽光，黑色挑高頂棚把空間拉高，再搭配溫潤的木紋隔間，通風又明亮，徹底告別陰暗潮濕。

And then the details, which are the part that really matters:

再來是真正貼心的細節，也是最關鍵的部分：

🏡 Respect for everyone. At the entrance there is a clear floor plan and friendly signage, so that teachers, students, and a parent bringing a child to the restroom can all use it with confidence.
💧 A sloped washbasin. The basin is set at an angle on purpose, so that when little hands are washing, the water does not splash straight back out at them.
🔒 Self-contained cubicles. Some of the cubicles hold a seated toilet (with a very cute frog on it), a urinal, their own small washbasin and an emergency call button, all in one — full marks for both privacy and convenience.

🏡 尊重多元：入口設有清楚的平面導覽與友善標示，無論是師生，或是陪伴孩子如廁的家長，都能安心使用。
💧 傾斜的洗手台：洗手台特別做了傾斜設計，讓小朋友洗手時水花不易濺出來。
🔒 獨立多功能空間：部分隔間內同時整合了坐式馬桶（還有超可愛的青蛙圖案）、小便斗、專屬洗手台與緊急求助鈴，隱私與便利性滿分。

A campus this nice takes everybody to keep it that way. Students are asked to treat the fittings kindly and leave the room tidy for the next person — and then to enjoy it, because it belongs to all of them. ✨

美好的校園環境需要大家一起守護！歡迎同學們在使用時愛惜設備、保持整潔，一起享受這個既舒適又充滿美感的校園新空間吧！✨
