Eight Voices, One Sound

彰化縣 115 年度國民小學英語團體朗讀比賽　優勝
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Group reading is not one person reading eight times over. It is eight people arriving at the same word at the same moment, for several minutes, without once looking up to check. On November 10, eight students from Class 6A did exactly that for Qiaotou at the Changhua County English Group Reading Contest for Elementary Schools — and brought home an Excellence award.

團體朗讀不是一個人讀八次，而是八個人在同一個瞬間抵達同一個字，連續好幾分鐘，中間還不能抬頭確認。11 月 10 日，橋頭國小六年甲班八位學生代表學校參加彰化縣 115 年度國民小學英語團體朗讀比賽，榮獲優勝佳績！

In their group, D1, twenty-four teams were placed: three took the top grade, seven were awarded Excellence, and fourteen took honorable mentions. Qiaotou was one of the seven — and went up second in the running order, which means they had to set the standard before most of the room had heard anybody read.

他們所在的 D1 組共有 24 隊獲獎：特優 3 隊、優勝 7 隊、佳作 14 隊，橋頭就是優勝的那七隊之一。而且他們的出場順序是第 2 號——在全場大多數人都還沒聽過任何一隊朗讀之前，就得先把標準立在那裡。

None of it happened in the week before. They started in the summer holiday and kept going right up to the eve of the contest, in break times and in the hours after school — the same lines, again, and then again, until the eight of them breathed in the same places. That is what a judge is actually listening for, and it is the part nobody in the audience sees.

這些都不是比賽前一週才開始的。他們從暑假就開始練，一路練到比賽前夕，利用下課時間與課餘時間，同樣的句子一次又一次，練到八個人連換氣都在同一個地方。評審真正在聽的就是這個，而這一段，台下的人是看不到的。

Thank you to the team behind the team: Teacher Hsiu-chi, who coached them in English; Teacher Ya-hui, their class teacher, who kept everything else running while they practiced; and the parents’ support squad, who were there the whole way. Hard, honest work, and a sweet result at the end of it. 🏆

感謝這支超強團隊——秀琪英語老師的指導、雅蕙導師的協助，以及家長後援隊一路上的支援。札實的努力，終獲甜美的果實，為校爭光！🏆

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

read/riːd/朗讀

Eight of them read as if they were one.八個人朗讀起來像同一個人。

voice/vɔɪs/聲音

Every voice matters, even in a group.就算是團體，每一個聲音都算數。

team/tiːm/團隊

A team wins together or not at all.團隊是一起贏，不然就一起沒有。

practice/ˈpræktɪs/練習

They began practice back in the summer.他們從暑假就開始練習了。

rhythm/ˈrɪðəm/節奏

Reading together needs one shared rhythm.一起朗讀，要有共同的節奏。

stage/steɪdʒ/舞台

The stage is quiet before the first word.第一個字出口前，舞台上是安靜的。

contest/ˈkɑːntest/比賽

Twenty-four teams were placed in this contest.這場比賽裡有 24 隊獲獎。

proud/praʊd/驕傲

The whole school is proud of these eight.全校都以這八個人為驕傲。
