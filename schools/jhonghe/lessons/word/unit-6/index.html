Word 01
parking area (n.)
停車區
The school has a parking area for bikes and scooters.
學校有一個自行車和小型摩托車的停車區。
The parking area let you park your bike or scooter in the shade.
停車區可讓您將自行車或踏板車停在陰涼處。
Word 02
air quality (n.)
空氣品質
The school shows a flag every morning to tell students about the air quality.
學校每天早上會掛旗子告訴學生當天空氣品質。
The teacher is taking out a yellow flag to show that the air quality is okay.
這位老師拿出黃旗表示空氣品質普通。
Word 03
seesaw (n.)
蹺蹺板
- To play on the seesaw, two people sit on opposite ends and take turns going up and down.
蹺蹺板的玩法是兩個人坐在板子的兩端輪流上下擺動。
- The kids are having fun playing on the seesaw.
小朋友在蹺蹺板上玩得很快樂。
Word 04
potted plant (n.)
盆栽
We like to care for my potted plants and watch them grow.
我們喜歡照顧盆栽並且看著它們成長。
Arranging potted plants is a relaxing activity.
盆栽造型設計是一個可以令人放鬆的活動。
Word 05
scouting (n.)
童軍活動
Scouting is an activity for young people.
童軍運動是一項適合年輕人的活動。
Scouting teaches you to work together.
童軍運動會教導你團隊合作。
Word 06
contest (n.)
比賽
The school holds many different contests for students to participate in.
學校舉辦很多不同的比賽讓學生參加。
The students compete in a calligraphy contest.
同學們參加書法比賽。
Word 07
award presentation (n.)
頒獎
At the award presentation, the school's principal is giving out certificates of recognition to students.
在頒獎典禮中，校長將獎狀發給學生。
The students get a prize at the award presentation.
同學們在頒獎典禮中得到獎品。
Word 08
Christmas (n.)
聖誕節
Our school  has a Christmas party where we play games.
我們學校有聖誕慶祝活動讓學生玩遊戲。
The students dance together to celebrate Christmas.
同學們跳舞慶祝聖誕節。
