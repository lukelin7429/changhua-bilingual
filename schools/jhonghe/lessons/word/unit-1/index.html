Track Team English

田徑隊的英文

Word 01
track team (n.)
田徑隊
The track team practices every morning.
田徑隊每天早上練習。
The track team's coach trains and prepares the students for track meets.
田徑隊教練訓練學生，為他們的比賽作好準備。
Word 02
coach (n.)
教練
The coach gives regular training sessions to the school's track team.
教練定期給學校的田徑隊訓練課。
The coach is teaching the students how to do a high jump.
教練正在教學生如何跳高。
Word 03
high jump (n.)
跳高
- The high jump takes a lot of practice.
跳高需要很多練習。
- The high jump is a fun event to watch.
跳高是很好看的賽事。
Word 04
long jump (n.)
跳遠
The long jump is a popular field event.
跳遠是田賽很普遍的項目。
The students are practicing the long jump at school.
同學們正在學校練習跳遠。
Word 05
shot put (n.)
鉛球
Shot put is a sport where people throw a heavy ball.
推鉛球是一種運動，選手必須將很重的球投擲出去。
The students are practicing shot put.
同學們正在練習推鉛球。
Word 06
marathon (n.)
馬拉松
A marathon is a very long race that takes a lot of time to run.
馬拉松是耗時很長的一種賽跑。
The students are training for an upcoming marathon.
同學們正在為即將到來的馬拉松比賽做訓練。
Word 07
agility ladder (n.)
敏捷梯
The agility ladder helps with your speed.
繩梯幫助你提升速度。
The students move through the agility ladder quickly.
同學們快速移動通過繩梯。
Word 08
cleats (n.)
釘鞋
Cleats are a shoe with spikes on the bottom.
釘鞋是底部有鞋釘的鞋子。
The students are taking off their shoes and putting on their cleats.
同學們正在脫鞋子並且穿上釘鞋。
