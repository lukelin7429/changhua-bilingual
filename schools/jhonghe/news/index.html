2019.11 🌏 In Loving Memory

Grandpa Mike Came to Visit

麥克爺爺來訪中和

In 2019, American volunteer educator Grandpa Mike (Michael Dishnow, 1943–2025) came to Jhonghe to teach English. He flew to Taiwan again and again at his own expense, school by school across Changhua. The school shares his visit film in loving memory. 2019 年，美國志工老師麥克爺爺（Michael Dishnow，1943–2025）來到中和教英語。他一次次自費飛來台灣，一校一校走訪彰化。謹以這段訪問影片懷念他。

June 4, 2026 ⭐ Sports Flagship

115 County Games · Pitou Qualifier Hosted at Jhonghe

115 縣民運動大會埤頭鄉國小組選拔賽

On June 3, athletes from every elementary school in Pitou Township gathered at the Jhonghe track for the 115-year County Games qualifier — a tribute to how seriously this small school takes its athletics program. 6 月 3 日埤頭鄉各國小選手齊聚中和國小田徑場，舉行 115 年縣民運動大會埤頭鄉國小組選拔賽。

Highlights
  • Pitou Township Mayor Du Yi-cai attended in person to cheer on the athletes 埤頭鄉長杜懿彩女士親臨會場
  • Pitou Township Office sponsored sports drinks & pencil cases for every athlete
  • Qualifiers will represent Pitou at the County Games stage
June 2, 2026 ⭐ Music Flagship

Recorder Ensemble · Flash Performance at the Palace Museum South

直笛隊快閃故宮南院

Invited by the National Palace Museum Southern Branch's "Art at the Palace · Fun Maneuvers" program, the Recorder Ensemble gave a flash performance in the museum grounds — two pieces, polished and confident, to enthusiastic applause from visitors. 直笛隊受邀至國立故宮博物院南部院區「藝起來·趣宮略」活動，於園區內進行兩首樂曲的快閃演出，獲現場遊客熱烈掌聲。

Highlights
  • Children also joined the Children's Creativity Center "Fun Maneuvers" escape-room challenge
  • Combined music + culture + escape-room learning in a single field trip
  • Thank-you to the conductor and to all the supporting parents
May 21, 2026 Health Education

Dental Hygiene Champions at the County Trial

潔牙小尖兵 · 全國潔牙觀摩彰化縣預賽

Ten fifth-graders represented Jhonghe at the 2026 Changhua County dental hygiene trial — three rounds of pineapple-cake tasting, flossing, and dentist-led evaluation. 十位五年級小朋友代表中和參加 2026 全國國小學童潔牙觀摩彰化縣地區預賽，分三階段：知識組（吃鳳梨酥答題）、潔牙組（用牙線清潔殘渣）、乾淨組（牙醫師評估）。

Winners
  • Chen Yu-ru (5A) · Top 10 individual in the toothbrush group 五年甲班陳鈺儒同學牙刷組個人前十名
  • Zhang Yu-ting (5B) · Team 1st 五年乙班張語庭全隊第一強
  • Zhang Xin-yun (5B) · Team 2nd 五年乙班張馨云全隊第二強
May 14, 2026 🌏 MCC Partnership

Dom Jones Visits Jhonghe · The World Steps Onto Campus

國際教育交流 · Dom Jones 蒞校

US educator and social-movement leader Dom Jones (now based in Taiwan) visited Jhonghe as part of her My Culture Connect campus tour. Children welcomed her with dance, recorder performances, a bilingual Dragon Boat play, and English conversation. 來自美國加州的國際教育家 Dom Jones 透過人師教育協會合作的全台巡迴校園計畫蒞臨中和。孩子們以舞蹈、直笛演奏、雙語端午話劇與英語接待，展現雙語學習成果。

Why this matters
  • Dom partners with My Culture Connect (人師教育協會) on a nationwide school tour since Dec 2025
  • Topics: global citizenship, empathy, UN Sustainable Development Goals
  • Direct confirmation of Jhonghe's bilingual program in real action — not coursework on paper
April 23, 2026 Tech / VR · AR

Into the Metaverse · 4A Visits the County's First VR/AR Classroom

走進未來教室 · 元宇宙智慧館

Grade 4A visited Changhua Arts High School's Metaverse Smart Hall — the county's first dedicated VR/AR learning space. Students explored Changhua's historic sites in 3D and stretched their reflexes with Switch interactive games. 四甲學生前往彰化藝術高中設置的全彰化縣首座「元宇宙智慧館」，透過 VR/AR 沉浸式探索彰化古蹟，並透過 Switch 遊戲互動提升反應力。

April 17, 2026 Career Education

6A's Career Day · Space Makeup Artists & Woodworking

職業探索 · 空間化妝師 (木作工程)

Grade 6A spent the day learning what a "space makeup artist" really does — touring the woodworking studio, watching career-path videos, building with their hands, and reflecting on what they discovered. 六甲展開職業探索，主題「空間化妝師－木作工程」：中心影片欣賞、教室導覽、主題課程動手體驗、綜合回饋分享。

March 31, 2026 ⭐ Centennial Heritage

Gratitude Across Generations · Teacher Zhang Kaolai Memorial Scholarship

感恩傳承・愛在中和 — 張考來老師紀念獎學金

Chairman Zhang Shanming of Kinmen Metals — a Jhonghe alumnus — donated another NT$100,000 to the scholarship he established in memory of his father Teacher Zhang Kaolai (who both studied and taught at Jhonghe). Total scholarship fund now NT$1.3 million, plus NT$10,000 for the upcoming centennial. 榮譽校友張山明董事長感念父親張考來老師曾在中和國小就讀並任教，設立紀念獎學金；今再捐 10 萬，累計達 130 萬元，另捐 1 萬支持明年（2027）即將到來的百週年校慶。

Scholarship rules
  • Recipients: 1 per class per grade · academic 1st + conduct grade B or above
  • Awarded: at the graduation ceremony every year
  • The line that captured it: "考 (test) first, 來 (come) collect the 考來 scholarship!" — Chairman Zhang's signature pun
March 24, 2026 Children's Day

Star-Rated Children's Day · The Hall Becomes a Steakhouse

兒童節牛排大餐 + 西餐禮儀

The school activity center was transformed into a "star-rated Western restaurant" for Children's Day. The whole school learned to use knife and fork — and the PTA Chair stepped in as the day's head server, ladling soup and serving steaks to every child. 活動中心搖身一變成「星級西餐廳」，全校學牛排與西餐禮儀；家長會長親自擔任當日服務生，為孩子盛湯、端牛排。

February 24, 2026 Spring Field Trip

Spring Opening · Into the Flower City

走進花城時旅 · 開學第二天到溪州公園

On the second day of the new term, the school took the children to Xizhou Park for the "2026 Flowers in Changhua · Flower City Time-Travel" expo — five themed pavilions tracing Changhua's path from traditional flower country to sustainable city. 開學第二天，全校至溪州公園參與「2026 花在彰化·花城時旅」春遊賞花海活動，透過五大展區看彰化從傳統花鄉蛻變為現代永續城市。

January 26 – 30, 2026 Native Language Education

Small Hands, Big Culture · Winter-Break Dough-Craft Class

寒假捏麵文化課程 · 12 生肖創作

For one week of the winter break, instructors from the Wu Family Dough-Craft Studio in Xihu taught children traditional dough sculpture — kneading 12 zodiac animals while naturally switching into Taiwanese to talk about each one. 由溪湖巫家捏麵館老師帶領孩子認識傳統捏麵技藝與在地文化，以 12 生肖為主題創作，過程中自然開口使用本土語。

Why this matters
  • Part of Jhonghe's 114-year Native Language Promotion Plan
  • Sub-plan 5-2: local industry experts invited to teach on campus
  • Language learning through hands-on cultural making
