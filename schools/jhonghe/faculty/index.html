01

A Small Team, A Whole School

一個小團隊，一所完整的學校

Jhonghe is organized into three offices — Academic Affairs & Student Affairs (教導處), General Affairs (總務處), and Guidance & Counselling (輔導室). Six publicly listed staff plus the foreign English teacher carry the daily work of a centennial rural school. 中和國小依小校編制設教導處、總務處、輔導室三處。公開列名的教職員六位加上外師，承擔起百年偏鄉校的日常運作。
