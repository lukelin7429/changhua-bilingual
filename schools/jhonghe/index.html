01

A Hundred-Year Story

百年小校的故事

From a single classroom on Lukouchu land to a centennial rural school.

從一間路口厝的教室，到一所即將迎接百年的偏鄉小校。

In the spring of 1921, four local elders — Zhang Qingfeng, Zhang Si, Cai Youcai, and Fu Zhongzhou — pooled funds to build a school on land donated by Zhang Qingfeng himself: 2.1128 jiǎ of paddy field, two classrooms, one outhouse. On April 1, 1926, the branch class became an independent school named Lukouchu Public School — Lukouchu, "the homestead at the crossroads," is still the old Taiwanese name for this place. 民國 10 年春，地方仕紳張清風、張賜、蔡有財、傅仲周四位先生發起捐資興學，由張清風先生捐出 2.1128 甲校地，募日圓 1700 圓建教室 2 間、廁所 1 棟。民國 15 年 4 月 1 日正式獨立為「路口厝公學校」——「路口厝」是這片土地的台語舊地名，意為「路口的聚落」。

Today the school sits at the southern edge of Pitou, looking across the Zhuoshui River toward Xiluo. The land is still farmland, and the families are still mostly farmers — but the children play the recorder, run the track, and meet international visitors in English. 學校位於埤頭鄉南端，與西螺隔濁水溪相望。學區家長以農為主，民風純樸——但孩子吹直笛、跑田徑、用英語接待國際嘉賓。

A Lineage of Three Schools

合興 · 中和 · 大湖 — 三所學校的母女關係

Pitou's small-school network has Jhonghe sitting right in the middle — born from one school, and mother to another. 中和在埤頭的學校網絡裡承上啟下：自合興分出、又生出大湖。

Centennial · 2027
100 Years
Lukouchu, 1921 → Jhonghe, 2027
路口厝公學校，到中和國小

Counting from the founding of Lukouchu Public School, Jhonghe Elementary will mark its 100th anniversary in 2027 — a centennial owed to four founders, four villages, and a hundred years of farming families who never stopped sending their children to school.
2027 年是中和創校一百週年——感念四位創校仕紳、四個學區村莊，以及百年來持續送孩子上學的農村家庭。

05

Where We Are

學校位置與學區

Jhonghe sits at the southern tip of Pitou Township, looking across the Zhuoshui River toward Xiluo in Yunlin. To the east lies Xizhou, to the west Zhutang, to the north Xihu — four townships meeting at this corner of Changhua's flat farming heartland. 中和國小位於埤頭鄉南端，與雲林西螺隔濁水溪相望；東鄰溪州，西接竹塘，北通溪湖——四個鄉鎮在彰南平原的這一角交會。

The school's catchment covers four farming villages — neighbours, cousins, friends. The same families have been sending their children to this school for a hundred years. 學區涵蓋四個農村——彼此是鄰居、是親戚、是朋友。同一批家族把孩子送到中和國小，已經一百年。
