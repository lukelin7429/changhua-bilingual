I.

Welcome to Tianjhong

歡迎來到田中國小

A happy place to learn, and a bright place to set sail.

「幸福田中」學習，向未來「快樂啟航」。

Tianjhong Elementary School started in 1914, on the street of Tianzhong Town in the south-east corner of Changhua County. For more than a hundred years — through the work of generation after generation of teachers — it has been a place where children learn, grow and become their best selves, and its graduates go on to do well in every walk of life.

本校創立於民國三年（1914），位於彰化縣東南隅小鎮——田中鎮街上，至今已百餘年。在歷任師長的努力下，為社會、國家培育出許多傑出的人才，校友在各行各業都有傑出的表現。

Families here care deeply about their children's education, and the school and its teachers work hard to give every student a many-sided place to learn. Our hope is simple: that every child learns happily at "Happy Tianjhong", and sets sail brightly towards the future.

本校學生家長對於孩子的教育，大都相當用心與關心；學校及教師也提供學生多元的學習環境，期待每一位學子都能在「幸福田中」學習，並向未來「快樂啟航」。

A whole-person education that nurtures the talents of the future.
學校願景：涵養全人教育 · 培育未來人才——打造「自主學習、社會互動、團隊共好」的優質學校。
II.

A Century on Main Street

田中鎮上的百年老校

In the home of Changhua's rice.

田中是稻米的故鄉，我們的校園就在小鎮的心臟地帶。

Rooted in the Town
與小鎮一同長大

Tianjhong sits right on Jhongjhou Road, in the middle of Tianzhong Town — a rice-farming township on the south-eastern edge of Changhua. The campus has grown alongside the town for over a century, and generations of local families have walked through its gate.

田中國小就在田中鎮中州路上，坐落於這座稻米之鄉的中心。百餘年來，校園與小鎮一起成長，一代又一代的田中子弟從這道校門走進校園、走向世界。

III.

What Tianjhong Is Known For

涵養全人教育 · 培育未來人才

Four directions we put our hearts into.

四個我們最用心經營的方向。

Multi-Faceted Exploration
多元探索 · 雙語國際與閱讀自學

A bilingual school with a foreign English teacher and international education linked to our new-resident families, opening children's eyes to the world. School-wide reading, dual-language reading and SDGs discussion build strong reading and self-directed learning. 申請雙語教學與外師協同教學，結合新住民子女教育推動國際教育；由閱讀推動教師規劃全校閱讀，導入雙閱讀教學與 SDGs 議題，厚植孩子的閱讀力與自學力。

Technology & Innovation
科技創新 · 機器人與生生用平板

Information education with a robotics exploration course keeps children in step with AI. Through the "a tablet for every student" programme and online learning platforms, teaching is matched to each child's needs and self-directed learning is built up. 發展國小資訊教育，以機器人探索課程熟悉 AI 科技的脈動；導入「生生用平板」推展數位學習，運用線上學習平台依學生個別需求進行適性教學，培養自主學習能力。

Humanities & Care
人文關懷 · 鄉土探索

Tianzhong is the home of rice. Our school-designed "Hometown Exploration" curriculum is built around the food, clothing, homes and travel of Tianzhong life, so children come to know the land they live on and grow a strong sense of who they are. 田中是稻米的故鄉，以田中的食衣住行為主軸規劃校定課程「鄉土探索」，讓孩子更認識生活的這片土地，培養自我認同。

Health & Active Living
健康樂活 · 田中馬拉松 GO！

A campus marathon, a badminton team, a baseball team and a rollerblading club — plus our "Tianjhong Marathon GO!" curriculum, tied to the town's community marathon, to build a lifelong habit of moving. Whole-person education of head, heart and will, with competency-based teaching and good character. 辦理校園馬拉松、羽球隊、棒球隊及直排輪社，並發展「田中馬拉松 GO！」校定課程，結合社區馬拉松比賽，培養孩子運動的好習慣；強調知、情、意，導入素養導向教學，健全學生良好品格。

IV.

Learn Together, Help Each Other

一起學習，互相幫助
A Campus Built for Joyful Learning
悅讀樂學的校園

We want every student to learn about lots of things, know how to use technology, care about others, and stay healthy and happy. At Tianjhong we believe in learning together and helping each other — so that every child can be their best self and set sail with confidence.

我們希望每位學生都能學習多樣的知識、懂得使用科技、關心他人，並保持健康快樂。在田中，我們相信一起學習、互相幫助，讓每個孩子都能成為最好的自己，帶著自信快樂啟航。你準備好加入田中國小這個快樂又聰明的大家庭了嗎？我們迫不及待想與你一起學習和成長！
