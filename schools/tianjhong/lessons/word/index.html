Sports & Movement
運動律動 · 5 字
WORD 01
tee-ball
樂樂棒球
In tee-ball, you hit the ball with a bat.
在玩樂樂棒時，您使用球棒擊球。
To score a point in tee-ball, the runner has to get to the home plate.
要在樂樂棒的比賽中得分，跑者必須到達本壘。
WORD 02
baseball practice (n)
棒球訓練
The students go to baseball practice.
學生們去棒球練習。
They play catch during baseball practice.
他們在棒球練習時玩接球遊戲。
WORD 03
skateboard (n)
滑板
The students are practicing riding their skateboards.
學生們正在練習滑板。
The students ride their skateboards together.
學生們一起滑板。
WORD 04
inline skating
直排輪
The students learn some basic inline skating movements.
學生學習一些基本的直排輪滑動作。
They practice inline skating with balance and coordination to play inline hockey.
他們練習溜直排輪，掌握平衡和協調技能，以便打直排輪曲棍球。
WORD 05
dance (v)
跳舞
The students dance to the music.
學生們跟隨著音樂節奏跳舞。
The students dance together in class.
學生們在課堂上一起舞蹈。

Arts & Culture
藝文與文化 · 6 字
WORD 06
tea ceremony (n)
茶道
The students practice performing a tea ceremony.
同學們在練習茶道。
You have to pay attention to details during a tea ceremony.
進行茶道時，你必須注意細節。
WORD 07
calligraphy (n)
書法
The boys and girls are learning traditional Chinese calligraphy.
男孩和女孩正在學習中國傳統書法。
To get good at calligraphy, you have to have a lot of patience!
要練好書法，必須有足夠的耐心！
WORD 08
Go
圍棋
The students play Go in class.
學生們在課堂上下圍棋。
They take turns while they play Go.
他們輪流下圍棋。
WORD 09
modelling clay
造型黏土
You can use modelling clay to make your very own artwork.
您可以使用造型黏土來製作自己的藝術品。
Modelling clay is easy and fun to use.
黏土模型使用起來既簡單又有趣。
WORD 10
building blocks
積木
The students are playing with building blocks.
學生們正在玩積木。
Students are following instructions for the building blocks.
學生們正在按照積木的說明進行操作。
WORD 11
read music
讀樂譜
The students learn how to read music in music class.
學生在音樂課上學習如何讀樂譜。
They read music to play different instruments.
他們閱讀音樂來演奏不同的樂器。

他們在國語課學習中文的成語。

同學們在課堂上學習國字。

老師改同學的作業。

他們使用紅筆來改學生的作業。

Healthy Habits
健康好習慣 · 5 字
WORD 18
wash your hands
洗手
It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
確定在吃東西之前要用香皂和水來洗手。
WORD 19
eye test (n)
視力檢查
The students take an eye test.
學生們進行視力檢查。
They take an eye test in the nurse's office.
他們在護士辦公室進行視力檢查。
WORD 20
recycling (n)
回收
Recycling is a way to reuse materials like paper and plastic.
資源回收是將紙類和塑膠之類的材質再次回收使用。
Recycling can help take care of the planet.
資源回收有助於照顧我們的地球。
WORD 21
sweep (v)
掃
Every day, students sweep away fallen leaves on school grounds.
每天學生打掃校園的落葉。
Students in Taiwan sweep the school grounds, keeping them clean and neat.
台灣的學生打掃校園，保持校園的乾淨整齊。
WORD 22
mop (v)
拖地
Every day, the students mop the floor.
同學們每天拖地。
Mopping the floor helps to keep the school clean.
拖地可以讓學校保持乾淨。

Around School
校園生活 · 3 字
WORD 23
recess (n)
下課時間
After the bell rings for recess, most students go outside to play.
下課鈴聲響起後，大多數學生都在外面玩。
Recess is when kids can play outside, run around, and have fun.
下課時間是孩子們可以在外面玩耍、跑來跑去、玩得開心的時候。
WORD 24
cross (v)
穿越
Watch out for students crossing the road.
當心正在過馬路的學生。
Crossing guards stop traffic to let students cross the street safely.
交通導護阻擋車輛讓學生安全過馬路。
WORD 25
professional development
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都有教師專業訓練課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
在專業訓練課程中，老師可以學到新的教學技巧與策略。
