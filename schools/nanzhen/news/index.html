Festival · 節慶·24 Sep 2026

Thank You, Teachers — and Happy Moon Festival教師節快樂 · 中秋節快樂

Teachers’ Day and the Moon Festival arrived in the same week, so Nanzhen celebrated both at once. Students went from classroom to office with handmade signs — purple flowers, pink hearts and bright stars that read “Thank you, teacher,” “Great” and “Awesome” — and handed each teacher a small gift. Every teacher got a photo, a smile and a big thank-you. Happy Teachers’ Day, and a happy Moon Festival to every family!

教師節與中秋節在同一週到來，南鎮國小一起慶祝！孩子們舉著親手做的字牌——紫色的花、粉紅的愛心、亮晶晶的星星，上面寫著「謝謝老師」「讚」「棒」——一間一間走進教室和辦公室，送上小禮物，和每位老師合影留念。祝福所有老師教師節快樂，也祝福每個家庭中秋節快樂！

Award · 榮譽·24 Sep 2026

Twenty-Three New Reading Kings酷英網「英閱王」認證 · 23 位學生通過

Reading in English, one book at a time, adds up. In the 2026 Cool English Reading King challenge, 23 Nanzhen students earned their certificates by reading English stories on the Cool English website. They lined up in front of the school building with their certificates held high. Let’s give them a big round of applause!

一本一本地讀，英文閱讀的力量就這樣累積起來。在 2026 年教育部酷英網「英閱王」認證中，南鎮國小共有 23 名學生通過認證——他們在酷英網上讀完一本本英文故事。孩子們拿著獎狀在校舍前合影，請大家給他們熱烈的掌聲！

Culture · 多元文化·22 Sep 2026

The Mobile Museum Brings the World to Our Library移動博物館再度來到南鎮

The Mobile Museum is back at Nanzhen! This time the exhibition introduces cultures from many countries, and the favorite part is the traditional clothing. To open the show, fifth graders dressed up and held a fashion walk on campus — Korean hanbok, Japanese kimono and bright, colorful costumes from many other cultures. The exhibition stays in the school library for two weeks, so come and visit! Thank you to Teacher Snoopy for bringing it to our school.

精彩的移動博物館又來到南鎮國小了！這次展出各國多元特色文化，其中最吸睛的，就是各國、各民族的傳統服飾。五年級小朋友換上韓服、和服與各國色彩繽紛的傳統服飾，在校園裡來一場服裝走秀。展覽在圖書館展出兩週，小朋友快利用時間去參觀喔！感謝 Snoopy 老師申請，讓世界走進我們的校園。

Award · 榮譽·1 Jul 2026

A Third Plaque Goes Up on the Wall第三面獎牌榮耀上架

Nanzhen now has three plaques on its wall of honor. Next to the British Council’s International School Award and the Ministry of Education Reading Excellence Award from 2018, the school has hung its newest honor: the 2026 Reading Excellence Award. Each plaque stands for years of work by Nanzhen’s teaching team. Many thanks to our guidance council president, Mr. Chan, and his wife, who helped put the new plaque in its place.

南鎮國小的榮譽牆上，現在有三面獎牌了！在英國文化協會 ISA 國際學校獎、107 年教育部閱讀磐石學校獎旁邊，掛上了最新的榮耀——115 年教育部閱讀磐石學校獎。每一面獎牌，都是南鎮教學團隊努力得來的成果。感謝最親切的詹登富輔導會長及夫人，協助我們將這份榮耀上架！
