I.

Meet Principal Wang

認識王錦鴻校長

Wang Chin-hung became principal of Nanzhen Elementary School on August 1, 2024, taking over from Principal Chiu Hsien-chang, who had led Nanzhen for eight years. Before coming to Nanzhen he was principal of Tianwei Elementary School, just down the road in the same township. He holds a master’s degree from the Graduate Institute of Education at National Chung Cheng University.

王錦鴻校長於 113 年 8 月 1 日接任南鎮國小校長，接棒領導南鎮八年的邱顯場校長。王校長先前擔任同鄉田尾國小校長，畢業於國立中正大學教育研究所碩士。

Principal Wang’s way of leading a school has won strong support from teachers, parents and students alike. He is warm and easy to talk to, and good at bringing people together — families, teachers and community volunteers. At Nanzhen he has led the teaching team in carrying on the school’s fine traditions while bringing in new ideas from the wider world of education.

王錦鴻校長的辦學風格深受親師生支持，作風親和，擅長凝聚地方與社區志工的力量。接任南鎮國小校長後，他帶領教學團隊延續學校的優良傳統，並積極融合全新的教育趨勢。

A Wish for Nanzhen · 南鎮前景
南鎮國小前景「錦」繡，有如飛「鴻」展翅
A future as bright as brocade (錦), rising like a wild goose in flight (鴻).
The two characters of the principal’s given name, woven into one hope for the school. 把校長名字裡的「錦」與「鴻」，織成對學校的一句祝福。

IV.

Educational Philosophy

辦學理念

Three beliefs that shape how Principal Wang leads a school.

王校長辦學的三個核心信念。

Peak Experiences & a Global View · 「高峰經驗」與國際視野

Principal Wang sets great store by drawing out children’s potential. He believes that when primary-school children get the chance to take part in big — even international — competitions, they not only see more of the world but also taste a “peak experience” of success. In his earlier post he backed school sports and arts teams, such as tchoukball and competitive sport stacking, through funding difficulties all the way to competing abroad for Taiwan — and winning world titles.

王校長相當重視激發學生的潛能。他認為在小學階段若能讓孩子有機會參與大型甚至國際性賽事，除了增加見識，更能獲得成功的「高峰經驗」。在其過往任內，曾大力支持並帶領學校體育與藝能團隊（如巧固球隊、競技疊杯隊）突破經費困難，出國為台爭光、勇奪世界冠軍。

Reading & Many Paths · 深耕閱讀與多元適性

He works to give schools a comfortable place to read, in both spaces and resources — in a previous post, for example, he joined hands with the parents’ association to rebuild the library with a forest theme. And he encourages children to find their own strengths through many kinds of activities and cross-disciplinary learning.

王校長積極為學校打造舒適的軟硬體閱讀環境（例如過往任內與家長會聯手整建具備森林風的特色圖書館），並鼓勵孩子透過多元活動、跨域學習找到自己的長處。

Rooted in Home, Growing Together · 在地情感與親師共好

Friendly and approachable, Principal Wang is good at gathering the strength of the local community and its volunteers. He brings the character of the area — Tianwei’s flowers and gardening, and its local temple culture — into school life, which livens up the ties between parents, teachers and students and deepens children’s sense of belonging to their hometown.

王校長作風親和，擅長凝聚地方與社區志工的力量。他將地方產業特色（如田尾的花卉園藝、在地宮廟文化）融入校園，在活絡親師生互動的同時，也深化學童對家鄉的認同感。

V.

Three Directions for Nanzhen

南鎮的三大發展主軸

Light up yourself, love our home, embrace the world — in practice.

把「點亮自己、愛我家園、擁抱世界」落實在每一天的課程裡。

Green Fields, Fresh Tracks
校本彈性課程「綠野鮮蹤」

Nanzhen sits in Tianwei, the “home of flowers,” surrounded by gardens and farmland. Children explore the fields with all five senses and learn about local rice and flowers such as chrysanthemums and carnations — even making traditional puffed-rice treats (米香). Lessons also head out to spots around the school district, such as the 波波草 flower fields and the 旅人小屋 (Traveler’s Hut), for real-world, cross-disciplinary project learning.

南鎮位於「花的故鄉」田尾鄉，學區周邊圍繞著豐富的園藝栽種與農田景觀。課程引導孩子用五感探索田園之美，將稻米、花卉（如菊花、康乃馨）等在地產業納入課程，還能動手製作傳統米食（如米香），涵育愛鄉愛土的情懷；並結合學區內的特色景點（如波波草、旅人小屋等賞花秘境），在真實情境中進行跨領域與專題探究學習。

The More Local, the More Global
越在地越國際

Through the national “a tablet for every student” program, Principal Wang has led the whole staff in digital training, weaving the UN Sustainable Development Goals, bilingual learning and technology into the school’s own courses — so that children in the countryside can think globally too. Building on the ISA International School Award, the school keeps growing cross-cultural communication and reading.

結合政府「生生用平板」計畫，王校長帶領全校教師深化數位研習，將 SDGs（聯合國永續發展目標）、雙語、科技力融入日常的校本特色課程，讓鄉村的孩子也能具備與國際接軌的思維；並延續 ISA 國際學校獎認證的基礎，持續推動跨文化溝通與閱讀教育，提升學童的閱讀力、科技力與溝通力。

Many Stages, Proud Traditions
多元舞台與傳統強項

Principal Wang firmly believes in giving children a peak experience of success, and he fully supports Nanzhen’s long-standing strengths: a deep tradition in sport, with the tchoukball and track teams giving children a place to shine and grow in confidence; and in music, a record of eight straight years as county champions in both recorder ensemble categories.

王校長堅信要給孩子「成功的高峰經驗」，全力支持並擦亮南鎮既有的金字招牌：擁有深厚體育基底的巧固球隊與田徑隊，持續提供孩子展現天賦與建立自信的舞台；音樂方面，學校曾創下連續八年榮獲彰化縣直笛合奏與重奏雙冠軍的輝煌紀錄。

Give every child a peak experience of success.
給每個孩子一次「成功的高峰經驗」。
