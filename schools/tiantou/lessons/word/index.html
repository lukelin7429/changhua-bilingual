These bilingual videos were filmed right here at Tiantou — watch each one and learn an English word from our own campus. Every word comes with two example sentences so you can use it yourself! 這些雙語影片都在田頭國小校園實地拍攝。每支影片學一個英文單字，還搭配兩個例句，讓你馬上學會怎麼用！

Words on Campus

田頭校園拍攝的單字影片
WORD 001
tee-balln.
樂樂棒球
We play tee-ball on the field after school.
我們放學後在操場打樂樂棒球。
In tee-ball, you hit the ball off a stand instead of a pitch.
樂樂棒球是把球放在架子上打，不用人投球。
WORD 002
tchoukballn.
巧固球
Tchoukball is a team sport played with a bouncy net.
巧固球是用彈網來玩的團隊運動。
We bounce the ball on the net when we play tchoukball.
我們打巧固球時，把球彈到網子上。
WORD 003
unicyclen.
獨輪車
A unicycle has only one wheel.
獨輪車只有一個輪子。
It takes good balance to ride a unicycle.
騎獨輪車需要很好的平衡感。
WORD 004
rollerbladev.
溜直排輪
We rollerblade around the playground.
我們在操場上溜直排輪。
Always wear a helmet when you rollerblade.
溜直排輪時一定要戴安全帽。
WORD 005
stretchv.
伸展
We stretch our arms and legs before exercise.
運動前我們會伸展手腳。
A good stretch keeps your body from getting hurt.
好好伸展可以避免身體受傷。
WORD 006
paintv.
彩繪
We paint colorful pictures in art class.
我們在美術課彩繪繽紛的圖畫。
The students paint the wall with a pretty mural.
學生們把牆面彩繪成漂亮的壁畫。
WORD 007
dancev.
跳舞
We dance together on the stage.
我們一起在舞台上跳舞。
Everyone loves to dance at the school party.
大家都喜歡在學校同樂會跳舞。
WORD 008
drumn.
鼓
The drum makes a loud, strong beat.
鼓發出響亮有力的節拍。
He plays the drum in the school band.
他在學校樂隊打鼓。
WORD 009
recordern.
直笛
Every student learns to play the recorder.
每個學生都學吹直笛。
She played a beautiful song on her recorder.
她用直笛吹了一首好聽的歌。
WORD 010
woodworkingn.
木工
In woodworking, we make things out of wood.
木工課我們用木頭做東西。
We made a small box in woodworking class.
我們在木工課做了一個小盒子。
WORD 011
kilnn.
窯
A kiln is a very hot oven for clay.
窯是燒陶土的高溫爐子。
We bake our clay bowls in the kiln.
我們把陶碗放進窯裡燒。
WORD 012
brickn.
磚
A brick is a hard block for building.
磚是蓋房子用的硬塊。
The old wall is made of red bricks.
那道舊牆是用紅磚砌成的。
WORD 013
rice dumplingn.
粽子
We eat rice dumplings during the Dragon Boat Festival.
端午節我們吃粽子。
A rice dumpling is wrapped in bamboo leaves.
粽子是用竹葉包起來的。
WORD 014
meatballn.
貢丸
A meatball is a small, bouncy ball of meat.
貢丸是一顆顆有彈性的肉丸。
We had meatball soup for lunch.
我們午餐喝貢丸湯。
WORD 015
learningn.
學習
Learning new things is fun every day.
每天學習新事物很有趣。
Learning English helps us talk to the world.
學習英文讓我們和世界對話。
WORD 016
board gamen.
桌遊
A board game is played on a table with friends.
桌遊是和朋友在桌上一起玩的遊戲。
We play a board game on rainy days.
下雨天我們玩桌遊。
WORD 017
mobile libraryn.
行動圖書館
A mobile library is a library on wheels.
行動圖書館是會移動的圖書館。
The mobile library brings books to our school.
行動圖書館把書帶到我們學校。
WORD 018
Chinese classn.
國語課
In Chinese class, we read and write Chinese.
國語課我們讀寫中文。
We learned a new poem in Chinese class.
我們在國語課學了一首新詩。
WORD 019
mathn.
數學
In math, we learn numbers and shapes.
數學課我們學數字和形狀。
I solved every problem in math today.
我今天把數學題目都解開了。
WORD 020
self-assessmentn.
自我評量
A self-assessment is when you check your own learning.
自我評量就是檢查自己的學習。
We do a self-assessment at the end of each unit.
每個單元結束我們會做自我評量。
WORD 021
Taiwanesen.
台語
Taiwanese is a language many families speak at home.
台語是許多家庭在家說的語言。
We sing a song in Taiwanese.
我們用台語唱一首歌。
WORD 022
QR coden.
QR code
A QR code is a square you scan with a phone.
QR code 是用手機掃描的方格圖案。
Scan the QR code to open the website.
掃描 QR code 就能打開網站。
WORD 023
wash your handsphr.
洗手
Wash your hands before you eat.
吃東西前要洗手。
We wash our hands to stay healthy.
我們洗手保持健康。
WORD 024
sweepv.
掃
We sweep the classroom every day.
我們每天打掃教室。
Please sweep the floor after lunch.
午餐後請把地板掃乾淨。
WORD 025
rakev.
耙
We use a rake to gather the fallen leaves.
我們用耙子把落葉聚在一起。
They rake the garden in the autumn.
秋天他們在花園耙落葉。
