The Campus Escape — A Mystery Adventure校園實境解謎體驗

As graduation day drew near, Tiantou's teachers did something wonderfully creative: they designed a unique campus mystery game — a real-life escape experience played across the school grounds. On June 2, 2026, the graduating class set off to explore corners of the campus they had walked past every day but never truly seen.

The campus holds infinite knowledge — if only you slow down and look.

Armed with tablets and clue sheets, students raced through the grounds in teams, scanning familiar spots with fresh eyes. The sundial in the courtyard, the quiet garden by the pond, the green lawns near the zebra sculpture — each hiding place held a puzzle, a question, a piece of a story.

The teachers wove creativity and wisdom into every challenge, turning six years of shared knowledge into a game of discovery. It was, in every way, one last love letter to a place they were about to leave.

Look around one more time — this campus you call home has more stories to tell than you know.

Every clue cracked, every mystery solved was a small celebration — of what they had learned, who they had become, and the teachers who quietly shaped them. By the end of the day, they didn't just finish a game. They rediscovered Tiantou.

中文原文 · Chinese

隨著畢業的日子漸近，田頭國小的老師精心設計了一款獨特的解謎遊戲。在這場充滿挑戰的實境解謎活動中，畢業生們探索那些在校園生活中常看卻又容易忽略的角落。

校園裡蘊藏著無限的知識，而我們的老師們運用創意和智慧，透過多樣的教學方式，把這些知識轉化為成長的養分。

讓我們一起在解謎中再一次發現這片熟悉的土地吧！
