Big Hands, Little Hands — A Warm Community Lunch大手牽小手，溫馨共餐時光！

This grateful month of May brought something extra special to Tiantou. On May 27, 2026, our lower-grade students transformed into little angels for a day — visiting the neighbourhood community dining station to share a warm meal with the grandparents of the community.

The smiles of the grandparents shone just as bright as the sunshine that day.

The moment our little ones walked in, the room lit up. To show their appreciation for such a warm welcome, the children performed their very best — dancing to "Mama, I Love You" and "Little Flower" with so much energy and joy that the grandparents couldn't stop smiling, clapping, and laughing along.

祖孫同樂的畫面
就是最美的風景
A grandparent and grandchild sharing joy together — that is the most beautiful sight of all.

Then came the most eagerly awaited part of the day — lunch! The grandmothers of the community dining station served up their famous pumpkin congee, warm and nourishing and utterly delicious. Watching the children eat with such delight, faces glowing with satisfaction, several of them shouting "Grandma, I want another bowl!" — it was simply the most adorable thing.

Today, both our hearts and our stomachs were filled to the brim with warm, love-filled goodness. Thank you, Tiantou Community Dining Station, for giving our little ones such a beautiful memory to carry with them always.

中文原文 · Chinese

這個感恩的五月，田頭國小的校園裡多了一份特別的溫暖～今天低年級的小寶貝們，化身為快樂的小天使，來到社區共餐站作客囉！

一走進共餐站，阿公阿嬤們的笑容就和今天的陽光一樣燦爛，熱烈地歡迎這群小客人的到來。為了報答阿公阿嬤的熱情，孩子們使出渾身解數，現場大跳『媽媽我愛你』和『小花朵』舞蹈！

看著孩子們活力滿滿、搖擺著可愛的身軀，阿公阿嬤們笑得合不攏嘴，整個共餐站都充滿了幸福的笑聲與滿滿的愛～

接下來最期待的午餐時間到啦！不得不說，社區共餐站的阿嬤們手藝真的太讚了！金瓜鹹粥營養又美味，看著小寶貝們吃得津津有味、小臉蛋上全都是滿足的笑容，好幾個小傢伙還忍不住大喊：「阿嬤，我還要再一碗！」實在是太可愛了啦！

祖孫同樂的畫面，就是最美的風景。

謝謝社區共餐站給孩子們這麼棒的回憶，今天的心跟胃，都被熱騰騰的愛給填飽了呢！
