Breathing Easy — Air Quality Monitoring每季空氣品質監測，守護田頭學習環境

On June 12, 2026, two large air-quality monitoring vehicles arrived at Tiantou Elementary — and they made quite a sight in the school's compact front courtyard! One is our quarterly monitor, which collects data over a full 24 hours. The other is an annual mobile monitoring unit that will be stationed here for nearly five days to carry out a thorough, week-long measurement.

All of this — for one simple reason: clean air for our children to learn in.

Both vehicles happened to be parked at the same time today, their large frames standing out rather boldly in the limited space. It was a gentle reminder for our students: please keep a safe distance from the monitoring equipment while it is in operation.

Monitoring the air our children breathe is not just a scientific task — it is an act of care. We are proud that Tiantou takes this responsibility seriously, season by season and year by year.

中文原文 · Chinese

每一季一次的空氣品質監測（大約需要一整天24小時收集數據），今天開始擺放進行，和全年度的空品監測車，週一開始擺放，需要將近五天的時間來進行監測，都是為了替小朋友學習環境的空氣品質把關。

但兩輛大車今天到明天停放時間重疊，在校園前庭不大空間裡，顯得特別突兀，得提醒小朋友不可以靠近。
