Little Hands Paint Dragon Boats小手畫龍舟，創意慶端午！

The Dragon Boat Festival has arrived, and Tiantou Elementary is overflowing with festive spirit! Our lower-grade after-school painting class has been wonderfully lively. Under the careful guidance of Teacher Yong-xi, our little ones let their imaginations soar — and stroke by stroke, each child created their very own, one-of-a-kind dragon boat. Looking at their focused eyes and the bursts of bright colour, we are sure they are all marvellous little artists.

At Tiantou, every child is one of a kind.

We have always believed that teaching children according to their needs is what unlocks their greatest potential. At Tiantou Elementary, we plan learning that fits each grade — content matched to every child's age and stage of growth.

Even better, our teachers are quietly full of remarkable talents! Through their professional skills and child-centred, adaptive teaching, they don't just guide children to learn with joy — they help every child "level up" in both learning attitude and ability.

Thank you, Teacher Yong-xi, for leading our younger children to soar through the world of art. Watching boat after playful dragon boat leap to life on paper, this Dragon Boat Festival has become especially meaningful.

中文原文 · Chinese

端午節到來，田頭國小到處都洋溢著濃濃的節慶氣氛！最近我們低年級的「課照繪畫班」好熱鬧呀～在永錫老師的細心指導下，小寶貝們發揮無限想像力，一筆一畫，都創作出了屬於自己、獨一無二的「端午龍舟」！看著孩子們專注的眼神和繽紛的色彩，真的覺得他們都是最棒的小藝術家。

在田頭，每個孩子都是獨一無二的寶貝。

我們一直深信，因材施教才能激發孩子最大的潛能。在田頭國小，我們針對不同的年級，規畫最適合他們年紀的學習內容。

更棒的是，我們學校的老師們各個身懷絕技、深藏不露！透過校內老師的專業技能與適性教學，不僅能引導孩子快樂學習，更讓孩子們在學習態度與能力上都「加倍升級」！

謝謝永錫老師帶領低年級孩子們在藝術的世界裡翱翔，看著一艘艘充滿童趣的龍舟躍然紙上，這個端午節變得特別有意義呢！
