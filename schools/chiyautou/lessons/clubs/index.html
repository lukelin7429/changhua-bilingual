Six Clubs, Six Vocabularies

六個社團，六套英語

Pick a club. Learn the words, the commands and the sentences that belong to it.

選一個社團，學會屬於那個社團的單字、口令與句子。

←Back to Lessons回到課程總覽

A bilingual school is not made by adding an English lesson. It is made when the English turns up inside the thing the child was going to do anyway — and at Chiyau Tou, that thing is club time. So each of our six clubs gets its own page here, built the same way: the words for the gear and the actions, the commands the coach actually calls out, and the sentences a child needs in order to ask a question, answer one, or say look at this. Every line has a speaker button, and every page ends with a matching game.

雙語學校不是靠「多加一堂英語課」做出來的，而是當英語出現在孩子本來就要做的那件事裡面的時候，才真的成立——在橋頭，那件事就是社團時間。所以這裡的六個社團各有一頁，做法都一樣：器材與動作的單字、老師真的會喊出口的口令，以及孩子提問、回答、或說一句「你看這個」時用得到的句子。每一句都有喇叭可以點，每一頁的最後都有一個配對遊戲。

They are written for both sides of the club. A teacher can take the command list straight into the session and use it as it stands; a child can read the sentence list the night before and turn up able to join in. Nobody has to be fluent for this to work — eight words and eight commands is enough to run a whole practice in English.

這些內容是寫給社團裡的兩邊看的。老師可以把口令表直接帶進課堂照著用；孩子可以前一晚讀過句子表，隔天就講得出來。這件事不需要誰很流利——八個單字加八句口令，就足以用英語把一整次練習跑完。

Inline Skating Club
四輪傳動　直排輪社 · Speed, With the Wind
