Dance Club

舞姿曼妙　舞蹈社 · Graceful in Motion
⌄
I.

Move to the Beat

舞蹈社

Move freely with the music and let people see who you are. Dance trains coordination and stage presence — and it is where a lot of energy goes.

隨音樂節奏自由律動，展現個人魅力！鍛鍊肢體協調與展現力，跳出滿滿活力。

←All Six Clubs回到社團總覽

Music and movement are the medium here, and what the club explores is the beauty of a body in rhythm. Basic steps, changes of formation and full routines build coordination and an eye for the art of it — and the unspoken understanding a group needs before it can move as one.

舞蹈社團以音樂與律動為媒介，引導學生探索身體動作與節奏之美。透過基本舞步、隊形變化及舞碼練習，培養肢體協調、藝術美感與團隊默契。孩子們自信地舞動身影，在歡樂學習中展現青春活力與多元才華。

Dance is counting before it is anything else. Almost every instruction a dance teacher gives is attached to a number — five, six, seven, eight — because a group can only move as one if everybody is counting the same thing at the same time. Learn the counting words in English and you can follow a class anywhere in the world.

跳舞在成為別的東西之前，先是「數拍子」。舞蹈老師幾乎每一句指令都黏著一個數字——五、六、七、八——因為一群人要動成一個人，前提是大家在同一個時間數同一件事。把數拍子的英文學會，你到世界上任何一堂舞蹈課都跟得上。

II.

Words for the Club

社團基本單字 · 8 個

Tap the speaker to hear each word.

點喇叭就可以聽發音。

beat/biːt/節拍

Clap on every beat.每一拍都拍一下手。

rhythm/ˈrɪðəm/節奏

Find the rhythm before you move.先抓到節奏再動。

step/stɛp/舞步

That step goes to the left.那個舞步是往左邊。

stretch/strɛtʃ/伸展

We always stretch before we dance.跳舞前一定要伸展。

spin/spɪn/旋轉

Keep your eyes on one spot when you spin.旋轉的時候眼睛盯著一個點。

pose/poʊz/定格姿勢

Hold the last pose for three counts.最後的姿勢定三拍。

stage/steɪdʒ/舞台

We practice on the stage before the show.表演前我們會在舞台上排練。

together/təˈɡɛðər/一起

The last eight counts must be together.最後八拍一定要整齊一起。

老師上課的口令

每次上課都會聽到的那幾句。

Find your spot.

Stretch first.

IV.

What You Can Say

學生必學的句子

Ask, answer, and join in — in English.

提問、回答、加入——用英語說一次。

Can we hear the music again?
可以再放一次音樂嗎？
I forgot the next step.
我忘記下一個動作了。
Which foot goes first?
先出哪一隻腳？
Can you show me slowly?
可以慢慢示範一次嗎？
We’re ready!
我們準備好了！
That was fun!
好好玩！
