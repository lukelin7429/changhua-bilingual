Unicycle Club

騎乘單輪　獨輪車社 · Riding on One Wheel
⌄
I.

Balance, Taken to the Limit

獨輪車社

Balance, pushed as far as it will go. You start out wobbling and end up in charge — and along the way you build the focus and the stubbornness that unlock some genuinely surprising tricks.

挑戰極致平衡！從搖搖晃晃到自由駕馭，培養專注力與堅毅意志，解鎖驚艷特技。

←All Six Clubs回到社團總覽

This one puts balance, technique and sheer persistence in the same activity. Children begin holding on to a wall and end up riding on their own, getting past one difficulty at a time. Keeping at it builds core strength and coordination — and, less visibly, patience, courage, and a refusal to give up on something hard.

獨輪車社團是一項結合平衡、技巧與毅力的特色活動。學生從扶牆練習到獨立騎乘，逐步挑戰自我、克服困難。透過持續練習，不僅鍛鍊核心肌力與身體協調能力，更培養耐心、勇氣與永不放棄的學習精神。

A unicycle gives you one wheel and nothing to hold, which means the only thing keeping you up is you. That is why this club is really a course in concentration: the distance you can ride goes up almost exactly as fast as your willingness to keep getting back on. Most riders remember the day they first crossed the whole court without touching the wall.

獨輪車給你一個輪子，而且沒有東西可以扶，所以撐住你的只有你自己。這也是為什麼這個社團其實是一門「專注」的課：你能騎的距離，增加的速度幾乎剛好等於你願意再爬上去的次數。多數人都記得自己第一次沒扶牆騎完整個場地的那一天。

II.

Words for the Club

社團基本單字 · 8 個

Tap the speaker to hear each word.

點喇叭就可以聽發音。

unicycle/ˈjuːnɪsaɪkl/獨輪車

A unicycle has one wheel and no handlebars.獨輪車只有一個輪子，也沒有把手。

saddle/ˈsædl/座墊

Sit right on top of the saddle.坐在座墊的正上方。

pedal/ˈpɛdl/踏板

Keep both feet on the pedals.兩腳都踩在踏板上。

wheel/wiːl/輪子

Point the wheel where you want to go.輪子朝著你要去的方向。

mount/maʊnt/上車

Hold the wall to mount the first few times.前幾次上車先扶著牆。

wobble/ˈwɑːbl/搖晃

You wobble less once you look up.眼睛抬起來，就比較不會搖晃。

focus/ˈfoʊkəs/專注

You need focus more than you need strength.這件事需要專注，比需要力氣多。

practice/ˈpræktɪs/練習

Ten minutes of practice every day is enough.每天練習十分鐘就夠了。

III.

What the Coach Says

老師上課的口令

The instructions you will hear in every session.

每次上課都會聽到的那幾句。

Hold the wall first.
先扶著牆。
Sit up straight.
坐直。
Keep pedaling.
繼續踩。
Don’t look down.
不要看地上。
One more meter!
再一公尺！
Line up your wheel.
把輪子對正。
Take a rest.
休息一下。
You’ve got it!
你抓到訣竅了！
IV.

What You Can Say

學生必學的句子

Ask, answer, and join in — in English.

提問、回答、加入——用英語說一次。

I can ride five meters.
我可以騎五公尺。
Can you hold my hand?
可以牽我一下嗎？
My unicycle is too tall.
我的獨輪車太高了。
I keep wobbling.
我一直搖晃。
Watch me!
看我！
Let’s try again.
我們再試一次。
