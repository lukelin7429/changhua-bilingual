I.

An Old Skill, New Tricks

扯鈴社

A classic folk art, kept going and then pushed somewhere new. The diabolo trains hand-eye coordination and reaction speed — and looks fantastic once you can throw it.

傳承經典民俗技藝，融入創意花式招式！訓練手眼協調與反應力，玩出酷炫新高度。

←All Six Clubs回到社團總覽

The diabolo is one of the oldest toys anyone in Taiwan still plays with, and it works on a single idea: a spinning thing stays upright. Everything else — the throws, the wraps, the tricks with names — is built on keeping that spin going. This is also the club where a child gets to explain a piece of their own culture to a visitor, which is a good reason to have the English for it.

扯鈴是台灣人到今天還在玩的最古老玩具之一，而它只靠一個原理運作：轉起來的東西不會倒。其餘的一切——拋接、纏繞、那些有名字的花式——都蓋在「讓它繼續轉」這件事上面。這也是最有機會讓孩子親口向外國訪客介紹自己文化的社團，所以把英文學起來很值得。

II.

Words for the Club

社團基本單字 · 8 個

Tap the speaker to hear each word.

點喇叭就可以聽發音。

diabolo/diˈæbəloʊ/扯鈴

A diabolo spins on a string between two sticks.扯鈴在兩根鈴棒之間的繩子上旋轉。

string/strɪŋ/鈴繩

Keep the string tight.鈴繩要拉緊。

stick/stɪk/鈴棒

Hold one stick in each hand.兩隻手各拿一根鈴棒。

spin/spɪn/旋轉

The faster it spins, the steadier it is.轉得越快，就越穩。

throw/θroʊ/拋

Throw it straight up, not forward.要往正上方拋，不是往前拋。

catch/kætʃ/接

Catch it on the string, not with your hand.用繩子接，不要用手接。

trick/trɪk/花式

I learned a new trick today.我今天學會一個新花式。

folk art/foʊk ɑːrt/民俗技藝

The diabolo is a Taiwanese folk art.扯鈴是台灣的民俗技藝。

III.

What the Coach Says

老師上課的口令

The instructions you will hear in every session.

每次上課都會聽到的那幾句。

Wind up the string.
把繩子繞好。
Start it spinning.
先把鈴轉起來。
Pull with your right hand.
右手往上拉。
Keep it level.
保持水平。
Throw it up high.
往上拋高。
Catch it on the string.
用繩子接住。
Try that trick again.
那個花式再試一次。
Well done!
做得好！
