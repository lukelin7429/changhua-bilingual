I.

Ways to Learn

從校園生活學英文

English for the things that already happen here every week.

這裡每週本來就在發生的事，用英語再說一次。

Three courses, and all three are built on the same idea: the English a school needs most is the English attached to what it is already doing. A club session, a lesson in progress, a message over the loudspeaker. Learn those and the language stops being a subject and starts being the way the day runs.

三門課，而三門課蓋在同一個想法上：一所學校最需要的英語，是黏在它本來就在做的事上面的英語——一次社團活動、一堂正在進行的課、一段從擴音器裡放出來的廣播。把這些學起來，語言就不再是一個科目，而是一天運作的方式。

6 Clubs · For Students
Course 01
English for Our Clubs
我們的社團

All six Chiyau Tou clubs — inline skating, unicycle, tennis, dance, diabolo and paper clay — each with its own page: the words for the gear, the commands the coach calls out, the sentences a child needs to join in, and a matching game at the end.橋頭六個社團——直排輪、獨輪車、網球、舞蹈、扯鈴、紙黏土——每個社團一頁：器材單字、老師的口令、學生必學的句子，最後還有配對遊戲。

Start Learning →
30 Episodes · For Teachers
Course 02
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers, taught by Sarah Thomas across 10 topics and 30 episodes — from greetings and roll call to exam instructions. A shared resource across Changhua’s bilingual schools.給老師使用的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學，共 10 大主題、30 支影片——從問候點名到考試指令。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

Start Learning →
13 Episodes · By Office
Course 03
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

“Broadcast English” — thirteen short episodes in which each office and section introduces, in English, the announcements it makes over a school’s public-address system, from the curriculum section to the counseling room.「廣播英語」——13 支短片，由各處室、各組用英語介紹平時透過校園廣播宣布的內容，從教務處教學組到輔導室。
