First Day: Wish Cards, Slam Dunks and a Grandparents’ Week

115 學年度開學迎新
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Monday, August 31 was the first day of the 2026–27 school year, and Chiyau Tou made a morning of it.

8 月 31 日（一）是 115 學年度的開學日，橋頭把這個早上辦成了一場熱鬧的迎新活動。

The new children drew their own wish cards — the ones now hanging on the little tree by the registration desk — and had their photo taken with the principal and the chair of the parents’ association. Then came the games: a slam-dunk station and a leaping-carp station, which is the kind of obstacle course where you are through it before you have had time to be nervous about a new school.

新生畫了自己的「心之祈願卡」——就是現在掛在報到處那棵小樹上的那些——也跟校長、家長會長拍照留念。接著是闖關：超好玩的灌籃高手與魚躍龍門，那種還來不及對新學校緊張，就已經闖完的關卡。

The same morning carried a Grandparents’ Week celebration, a book given to every new child, a parents’ meeting and a health talk. It is a lot to fit into one day, and that is deliberate: a first day should feel like a place worth coming back to on the second.

同一個早上還有溫馨的祖孫週感恩活動、贈送新生書籍禮物、家長座談會及健康宣導。一天塞進這麼多事情是刻意的：開學日應該讓人覺得，這個地方明天還值得再來一次。

Thank you to Chairman Liu Ming-chin and his wife for coming to welcome the new children, and to the whole teaching team for putting the morning together. Let us walk alongside these children while they grow.

感謝劉明進會長及夫人到校迎接新生，感謝教學團隊協同辦理。讓我們攜手陪伴孩子快樂成長！
