A Buffet to Welcome Principal Chen

俊文校長到職 Buffet
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On 4 August 2026 Chiyau Tou held a buffet to welcome Principal Chen Chun-wen on taking up his post. The principal, the whole staff, parents and invited guests gathered in one room; the school’s new chapter began over a warm and cheerful lunch.

115 年 8 月 4 日，橋頭國小舉辦歡迎俊文校長到職 Buffet，校長與全校教職員、家長及來賓歡聚一堂，在溫馨愉快的交流中展開嶄新的校務篇章。

A meal is a practical way to start. It puts everybody in the same room without an agenda, which is exactly what a school needs on the day a new principal arrives — the teaching team, the parents’ association, the volunteers and the guests all meeting each other rather than being introduced from a stage.

用一頓飯開始，是一個很務實的做法。它讓所有人在沒有議程的情況下同處一室，而這正是一所學校在新校長到任那天最需要的——教師團隊、家長會、志工與來賓彼此見面，而不是站在台上被一一介紹。

The gathering drew people closer together, and it stands for something: that everyone here intends to work side by side and to look after the children’s learning and growing. Chiyau Tou goes on innovating and moving steadily forward, building a happy, high-quality campus to learn in.

餐會凝聚彼此情誼，也象徵大家攜手合作、共同守護孩子學習成長的美好願景。橋頭國小持續創新、穩健前行，打造幸福、優質的學習校園。
