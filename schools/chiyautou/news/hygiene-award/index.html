Top Award in the County Campus Disease-Prevention Contest

校園傳染病防治競賽特優
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Chiyau Tou has been awarded Excellence — the top grade — in the 2025 Changhua County campus infectious-disease prevention contest. It is a county-level award, and it is not given for a single event.

本校榮獲 114 年度彰化縣校園傳染病防治競賽特優！這是縣級的獎項，而且它不是因為辦了一場活動而拿到的。

A healthy campus is something everybody has to look after together. Health education in ordinary lessons, health messages at assembly, keeping the place clean, and then the disease-prevention work itself — we have done all of it one step at a time, until prevention is simply part of how a school day runs, and the children have somewhere safe and healthy to learn in.

校園健康需要每一個人的共同守護。從平日的衛生教育、健康宣導、環境清潔，到傳染病防治工作的落實，我們一步一腳印，將防疫融入校園生活，為孩子打造安心、健康的學習環境。

Thank you to the staff, the students, the parents and every partner who has taken part in this work. Because you were careful and because you worked together, the idea of a healthy campus keeps putting out new shoots and growing stronger.

感謝全校師生、家長及所有一起投入校園防疫工作的夥伴，因為大家的用心與合作，讓健康校園的理念持續發芽、茁壯！

Keep it up, and let us look after our health together. The honor belongs to every single one of us.

防疫不鬆懈，健康一起守！榮耀屬於全校每一位共同努力的夥伴！
