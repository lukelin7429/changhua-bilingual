Six stories from the start of the school year — and every one of them comes with English you can take away.

開學前後的六則報導——而且每一則後面，都有可以帶走的英文。

Each story below has its own page, and each page is built the same way: photographs of what actually happened, the report in English and Chinese side by side, then eight vocabulary cards taken from that story with a speaker button on every one, and a matching game to finish. Read the news, and you have learned eight words without setting out to.

下面每一則新聞都有自己的一頁，每一頁的做法都一樣：照片、中英對照的報導，接著是從這則新聞裡取出的八張單字卡（每一張都可以點來聽發音），最後是一個配對遊戲。看完新聞，你會發現自己順手學了八個單字。
