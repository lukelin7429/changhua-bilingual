Da-An's Words

大安的英文單字

These words come from Da-An's own story — from our unicycle champions to our moon-gate garden to the mountain we once climbed together. 這些英文單字來自大安的故事——從我們的獨輪車冠軍隊、詩藝園月洞門，到我們一起爬上的那座山。

🦄
Unicyclen.
獨輪車
I ride my unicycle every morning before school!
我每天早上上學前都騎獨輪車！
Our unicycle team won four gold medals at the County Cup.
我們的獨輪車隊在縣長盃贏了四面金牌。
🏹
Archeryn.
射箭
We practice archery after school.
我們放學後練習射箭。
Aim carefully — archery takes lots of focus.
仔細瞄準——射箭需要很強的專注力。
🎺
Brass Bandn.
管樂團
Our brass band played music on top of the mountain!
我們的管樂團在山頂演奏！
I play the flute in our school's brass band.
我在學校管樂團吹長笛。
🌿
Gardenn.
花園
Walk through the moon gate and into our school garden.
走過月洞門，進入我們的學校花園。
We read poems in the garden on sunny days.
晴天的時候，我們在花園裡讀詩。
🏆
Championn.
冠軍
We are the champions of the County Unicycle Cup!
我們是縣長盃獨輪車錦標賽的冠軍！
Every champion practices hard every single day.
每個冠軍都每天努力練習。
⛰️
Mountainn.
山
We climbed Hehuan Mountain — it's 3,417 meters high!
我們爬了合歡山——它有 3,417 公尺高！
A mountain always looks taller from the bottom.
從山腳往上看，山總是更高。
