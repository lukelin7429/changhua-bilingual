All 17 Words

十七個英文單字 · 大安校園拍攝
WORD 001
sweepv.
掃
The students sweep the classroom every afternoon.
學生們每天下午打掃教室。
Can you help me sweep the hallway?
你能幫我掃走廊嗎？
WORD 002
professional developmentphr.
專業進修
Teachers attend professional development workshops every semester.
老師們每學期參加專業進修研習。
Professional development helps our teachers learn new ideas.
專業進修幫助老師們學習新知識。
WORD 003
basketballn.
籃球
We love playing basketball during recess.
下課時我們很喜歡打籃球。
The basketball team practices hard every day after school.
籃球隊每天放學後認真練習。
WORD 004
testn.
考試
We have an English test every Friday.
我們每個星期五有英文考試。
I studied hard for the math test last night.
我昨晚為了數學考試認真讀書。
WORD 005
jump ropephr.
跳繩
Jump rope is our favorite game at recess.
跳繩是我們下課最喜歡的遊戲。
She can jump rope for five minutes without stopping!
她可以跳繩五分鐘不停下來！
WORD 006
recordern.
直笛
Every student at Da-An learns to play the recorder.
大安國小每個學生都學吹直笛。
She played a beautiful song on the recorder at the concert.
她在音樂會上用直笛演奏了一首美麗的歌。
WORD 007
warm-upn.
暖身
We always do a warm-up before PE class.
我們在體育課前總是先做暖身運動。
A good warm-up gets your body ready to move!
做好暖身運動，讓身體準備好動起來！
WORD 008
wind instrumentphr.
吹管樂器
The flute and recorder are both wind instruments.
長笛和直笛都是吹管樂器。
Da-An has an amazing wind instrument ensemble.
大安國小有一個很棒的吹管樂團。
WORD 009
dance performancephr.
舞蹈表演
Our class put on a dance performance at the school assembly.
我們班在朝會表演了舞蹈。
Everyone cheered during the dance performance on Sports Day.
運動會的舞蹈表演時，大家都歡呼喝采。
WORD 010
correctv.
批改
Our teacher uses a red pen to correct our homework.
我們的老師用紅筆批改我們的作業。
She corrects all the tests and gives them back the next day.
她批改完所有考卷，隔天還給我們。
WORD 011
washv.
洗
We always wash our hands before lunch.
我們午餐前總是洗手。
Please wash the paintbrushes after art class.
美術課後請把畫筆洗乾淨。
WORD 012
constellationn.
星座
We learned about the twelve constellations in science class.
我們在自然課學習了十二星座。
Can you spot the Orion constellation in the night sky?
你能在夜空中找到獵戶座嗎？
WORD 013
fluten.
長笛
She plays the flute in the school wind ensemble.
她在學校吹管樂團演奏長笛。
The flute makes a beautiful, bright sound.
長笛發出美麗明亮的聲音。
WORD 014
pottery wheelphr.
陶輪
We used a pottery wheel to make clay bowls in art class.
我們在美術課用陶輪製作陶碗。
It takes a lot of practice to shape clay on a pottery wheel.
在陶輪上塑造陶土需要很多練習。
WORD 015
archeryn.
射箭
Da-An Elementary has a great archery team.
大安國小有一支很棒的射箭隊。
She hit the bullseye on her very first try in archery!
她在射箭時第一次就射中靶心！
WORD 016
martial artsn.
武術
The martial arts club meets every Tuesday after school.
武術社每個星期二放學後集合。
Learning martial arts teaches us discipline and respect.
學習武術教導我們紀律和尊重。
WORD 017
contraptionn.
裝置
The students built a clever contraption for the science fair.
學生們為科學展製作了一個聰明的裝置。
What does this funny contraption do? Let's find out!
這個奇妙的裝置有什麼用？讓我們來找出答案！
