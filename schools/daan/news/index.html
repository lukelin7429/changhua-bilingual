Three Golds on One Wheel

獨輪逐金！大安勇奪教育盃獨輪車錦標賽 3 金 4 銀 1 銅

At the 2025–26 Education Cup Unicycle Championship, Da-An Elementary's riders turned in one of the school's strongest performances ever — pedaling home with 3 gold, 4 silver, and 1 bronze medals, plus a long string of fourth- through eighth-place finishes across many events.

Third-grader Li An-lun was unstoppable, winning gold in both the 200-meter speed race and the IUF obstacle course. Sixth-grader Li Mu-jie took gold in the girls' IUF obstacle course and silver in the 200-meter speed race, while Lai Liang-zhu earned silver in the 100-meter sprint.

As a team, Da-An's middle-grade boys finished 2nd overall and the upper-grade girls placed 3rd — proof of just how deep this little school's unicycle program runs.

在 114 學年度教育盃獨輪車錦標賽中，大安國小選手繳出全校歷來最亮眼的成績之一：3 金 4 銀 1 銅，外加多項第四到第八名。三年級李安倫獨拿 200 公尺競速與 IUF 迴旋障礙賽雙金；六年級李牧潔奪女子 IUF 障礙賽金牌、200 公尺競速銀牌；賴亮茿獲 100 公尺競速銀牌。團體賽方面，中年級男生組第 2 名、高年級女生組第 3 名——展現偏鄉小校扎實的訓練底蘊。

  • 🥇 李牧潔 · 女子 IUF 障礙
  • 🥈 賴亮茿 · 100m 競速
  • 🏆 中男團體第 2
  • 🏆 高女團體第 3

A Second-Grader's Poem Goes to Print

二年級小詩人！藩峻義作品獲《智慧菓》165 期刊登

Great talent can come in the smallest packages. Second-grade student Pan Jun-yi had an original poem selected for publication in Issue 165 (March 2026) of Smart Fruit (智慧菓), a children's literary magazine that showcases standout young writers from across Taiwan.

Seeing your own words in a printed magazine is a milestone many writers wait years to reach — and Pan reached it in the second grade. It's a bright reminder that Da-An's small campus grows not only athletes, but storytellers and poets too.

了不起的才華，也可以裝在最小的身體裡。二年級的藩峻義同學創作的詩稿，獲選刊登於兒童文學刊物《智慧菓》第 165 期（2026 年 3 月號）。能在正式出版的刊物上看見自己的文字，是許多作家等待多年的里程碑，而藩峻義在二年級就做到了——大安的小校園，不只培養運動健將，也孕育說故事的人與小詩人。

  • 📖 《智慧菓》第 165 期
  • 🗓️ 2026 年 3 月號刊登

Steady Aim, Strong Finish

穩定出箭！大安射箭隊教育盃榮獲團體第 2 名

Da-An's young archers showed nerves of steel at the 2025–26 Education Cup Archery Championship, where both the boys' and girls' recurve teams climbed onto the podium with a 2nd-place team finish.

In the girls' 10-meter recurve, Li Mu-jie placed 2nd, Lin Pin-yu 3rd, and Lai Liang-zhu 8th. In the boys' division, Li An-lun finished 6th and Zheng Yue-an 8th.

Archery rewards focus, breath control, and patience — quiet qualities these students carry straight back into the classroom every day.

在 114 學年度教育盃射箭錦標賽中，大安國小小射手沉著穩定，男、女反曲弓團體雙雙站上頒獎台，奪得團體第 2 名。女子組 10 公尺反曲弓，李牧潔第 2 名、林品妤第 3 名、賴亮茿第 8 名；男子組李安倫第 6 名、鄭躍安第 8 名。射箭講究專注、調息與耐心，而這些特質，正是孩子們每天帶回教室的禮物。

  • 🥉 林品妤 · 女子個人第 3

Four Golds at the Magistrate's Cup

縣長盃獨輪車錦標賽 大安再奪 4 金

At the 2025 County Magistrate's Cup Unicycle Championship, Da-An's riders captured 4 gold, 1 silver, and 2 bronze medals, plus a long run of fifth- through eighth-place finishes — one of the biggest medal hauls in the whole field.

Li An-lun doubled up with golds in the 200-meter speed race and the IUF obstacle course; Zhuo Xuan-ye won the 100-meter sprint; and Li Mu-jie took gold in the girls' IUF obstacle course. The middle-grade boys also earned silver in the team freestyle routine.

For a small rural school, lining up at the county championships and riding home with golds is no small feat — it's the reward for hours of practice on the playground.

在 114 學年度縣長盃獨輪車錦標賽中，大安國小奪下 4 金 1 銀 2 銅，再加上一連串第五到第八名，是全場最豐收的隊伍之一。李安倫獨拿 200 公尺競速與 IUF 障礙賽雙金；卓炫燁勇奪 100 公尺競速金牌；李牧潔摘下女子 IUF 障礙賽金牌；中年級男生團體花式也榮獲銀牌。對一所偏鄉小校而言，站上縣級舞台還能抱回金牌，是操場上無數練習換來的果實。

Reading Pays Off: The Monthly Library Quiz

讀出樂趣！大安圖書室每月有獎徵答

Every month, the Da-An school library runs a prize quiz (有獎徵答) that turns reading into a treasure hunt. Students dig into the month's featured books, search for the answers, and submit their entries for a chance to win small prizes.

The November 2025 round wrapped up with the answer key posted so everyone could check their work. It's a simple idea with a big payoff: month after month, the quiz keeps children turning pages, building vocabulary, and discovering that the library is one of the best rooms in the whole school.

大安國小圖書室每個月都會舉辦「有獎徵答」，把閱讀變成一場尋寶遊戲：孩子們鑽進當月的主題書籍裡，找出答案、投件，就有機會抽中小獎品。2025 年 11 月這一回合，隨後公布了解答卷讓大家對照。這個點子簡單，效果卻很大——月復一月，徵答活動讓孩子持續翻書、累積詞彙，也讓他們發現：圖書室，是全校最棒的房間之一。

  • 📖 鼓勵主題閱讀
  • 🎁 答對抽小獎

Girls' Recurve Team Takes the Top Spot

縣長盃射箭 女子反曲弓團體勇奪第一

Da-An's girls' recurve team struck gold at the 2025 County Magistrate's Cup Archery Championship, claiming 1st place in the team event for the 10-meter recurve.

Individually, Mu-jie placed 2nd, Liang-zhu 3rd, and Pin-yu 4th — a near-clean sweep of the top of the leaderboard. In the boys' division, Li An-lun added an 8th-place finish.

A team title at the county level is the kind of result that takes a whole season of early-morning practice — and these girls delivered.

Eleven Young Innovators Advance in the AI & STEAM Challenge

11 位小發明家！通過彰化縣 AI 暨 STEAM 實作創意競賽

Da-An proved it's not just a sports powerhouse: eleven students qualified in the Basic Group A category of the 2025 Changhua County AI & STEAM Practical Creativity Competition — an event that asks young makers to solve real problems with technology, design, and teamwork.

The team spanned three grade levels: sixth-graders Zhang Jun-yao, Xie Bing-hao, Lin Xin-you, and Zhang Sheng-feng; fifth-graders Zhuo Qing-yun, Zeng Yi-de, Huang Wei-jun, and Li Mu-jie; and third-graders Zheng Yue-an, Zheng Yue-le, and Li An-lun.

From coding to hands-on building, competitions like this give Da-An's students an early taste of the science and engineering skills that will shape their futures.

大安不只是運動強校：在 2025 彰化縣 AI 暨 STEAM 實作創意競賽中，全校共 11 位學生通過基礎 A 組——這項競賽要求小小創客運用科技、設計與團隊合作，解決真實世界的問題。團隊橫跨三個年級：六年級張鈞耀、謝秉澔、林欣佑、張勝烽；五年級卓青澐、曾易得、黃煒竣、李牧潔；三年級鄭躍安、鄭躍樂、李安倫。從寫程式到動手實作，這樣的競賽讓大安的孩子提早嚐到，未來將形塑他們人生的科學與工程能力。

Da-An Takes On the Nation's Best

全國教育盃獨輪車錦標賽 大安選手躍上全國舞台

Four Da-An riders qualified for the 2025 National Education Cup Unicycle Championship, testing themselves against the country's finest in the IUF obstacle race.

Third-grader Li An-lun placed 3rd in the middle-grade boys' IUF obstacle event; second-grader Zhou Hong-yan took 4th in the lower-grade boys' division; Zheng Le-an finished 5th; and sixth-grader Li Mu-jie earned 8th in the upper-grade girls' obstacle race, plus 7th in the 100-meter speed event.

Reaching a national final is a milestone all by itself — and these four riders carried Da-An's name onto the biggest stage of all.

大安國小四位選手通過層層關卡，挺進 2025 年全國教育盃獨輪車錦標賽，與全國好手在 IUF 障礙賽中一較高下。三年級李安倫獲中年級男子組 IUF 障礙賽個人第 3 名；二年級周泓諺奪低年級男子組第 4 名；鄭樂安第 5 名；六年級李牧潔則在高年級女子組障礙賽拿下第 8 名，並在 100 公尺前進競速獲第 7 名。能站上全國決賽本身就是一座里程碑——而這四位選手，把大安的名字帶上了最大的舞台。

Six Students Crowned "Listening Kings"

聽力王者！六位大安學生榮獲 Cool English 英聽王獎

Six Da-An students earned the "Listening King" award in the 2025 Cool English Listening Competition — a national online program that challenges children to sharpen their English listening skills, one clip at a time.

The winners were fifth-graders Huang Wei-jun, Zhuo Qing-yun, Liu Xin-wei, Zeng Yi-de, and Li Mu-jie, together with fourth-grader Zhou Yu-cheng. Each of them logged hours of practice on the Cool English platform to reach the top tier.

For a small rural school, raising a roomful of confident English listeners is exactly the kind of bilingual progress Da-An is built on.

六位大安學生在 2025 年「Cool English 英聽王比賽」中榮獲「英聽王獎」——這是一項全國線上競賽，鼓勵孩子一段一段地磨利自己的英語聽力。得獎者為五年級黃煒竣、卓清雲、柳昕惟、曾易得、李牧潔，以及四年級周堉程。他們都在 Cool English 平台上累積了大量練習，才登上頂尖之列。對偏鄉小校來說，培養出一群自信的英語聽者，正是大安立校的雙語初心。

Brothers on Wheels Light Up the Tainan Cup

輪轉台南！鄭躍安、鄭躍樂兄弟滑輪溜冰奪佳績

Third-grade brothers Zheng Yue-an and Zheng Yue-le laced up for the 2025 Tainan Mayor's Cup Roller Skating Championship and skated their way onto the podium.

Zheng Yue-le swept his Group B division — 1st in the forward double-foot S-curve, 1st in the forward cross-over, and 1st overall in the individual all-around. Zheng Yue-an added a 5th in the forward double-foot S-curve and two 6th-place finishes in Group A.

Whether on one wheel or eight, Da-An's students just keep finding new ways to fly.

三年級的鄭躍安、鄭躍樂兩兄弟，穿上溜冰鞋出征 2025 年台南市主委盃滑輪溜冰錦標賽，雙雙滑上頒獎台。鄭躍樂在 B 組橫掃全場：前溜雙足 S 形第 1 名、前溜交叉形第 1 名、個人綜合成績第 1 名；鄭躍安則在 A 組拿下前溜雙足 S 形第 5 名與兩項第 6 名。不論是一個輪子還是八個輪子，大安的孩子總能找到新的方式飛翔。

  • 🥇 鄭躍樂 · B 組綜合第 1
  • 🥇 鄭躍樂 · 雙足 S 形 + 交叉形
  • 🛼 鄭躍安 · A 組第 5、第 6
