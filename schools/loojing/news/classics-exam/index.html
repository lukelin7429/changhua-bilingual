The National Classics Examination

洛津國小．全國經典會考

Reading widely matters less than reading deeply — understanding what you read is what carries you far.

Reading isn't just about gathering knowledge. It's about building a child's power to think, understand, and express. Loojing Elementary encourages every student to set out from reading and take on the National Classics Examination, broadening their horizons and building deeper roots through classic works — laying solid ground for the learning still to come.

Let reading become a habit, and let the classics become nourishment. Starting today, sit down with your child and read a good book — one more measure of confidence for the future.

中文原文 · Chinese

【 洛津國小－全國經典會考 】

讀得多，不如讀得深；讀得懂，才能走得遠！

閱讀，不只是累積知識，更是在培養孩子思考、理解與表達的力量。洛津國小鼓勵孩子從閱讀出發，迎接「全國經典會考」，在一本本經典作品中拓展視野、累積底蘊，也為未來的學習打下扎實基礎。

讓閱讀成為習慣，讓經典成為養分。從今天開始，陪孩子讀一本好書，為未來多一份自信！
