Strings Take Flight

弦音飛揚．佳績奏響．洛津弦樂團舞台綻放耀眼光芒

A bow sweeps across the strings, carrying a dream forward; each note writes down a moment of honor.

Over summer break, while many enjoyed a slower pace, the students of Loojing Elementary's String Ensemble kept practicing hard. Carrying the fruits of their daily effort, they competed in the 2026 Mayor's Cup String and Flute Ensemble Competition — showing what they've built through beautiful music, and bringing wonderful news back to Loojing!

The results were outstanding — congratulations to all our winners!

  • Loojing Ensemble 110 — 2nd Place
  • Loojing Ensemble 111 — Excellent
  • Loojing Ensemble 114 — Excellent
  • Loojing Strings A Elite Group — 3rd Place

Every award is proof of the children's hard practice; every beautiful note is the result of focus and persistence built up over time.

String music isn't only about performing — it's a lesson in teamwork. Through ensemble playing again and again, students learn to listen; through coordinating with one another, they learn to collaborate; and on stage, they learn to show themselves with courage.

Special thanks to the teachers who devoted themselves to coaching the ensemble — from daily practice to competition preparation, guiding with care and patience, and walking with the children step by step so every melody could shine even brighter. Thanks too to the parents for their support and companionship along the way — because school, teachers, and parents together built the warmest support behind these children, they could chase their musical dreams without worry.

Effort never misses its moment; brilliance will always find its voice. Congratulations to all our little award-winning musicians — you are truly amazing!

中文原文 · Chinese

【弦音飛揚・佳績奏響！洛津弦樂團舞台綻放耀眼光芒】

琴弓揮動夢想，音符寫下榮耀！

暑假裡，當許多人享受悠閒時光，洛津國小弦樂團的孩子們，依然認真投入練習。孩子們帶著平日累積的努力，參加 2026市長盃絃笛樂團音樂比賽，用悠揚琴聲展現實力，也為洛津帶回滿滿的好消息！

比賽成績亮眼，恭喜得獎！

  • 洛津110團－第二名
  • 洛津111團－優等
  • 洛津114團－優等
  • 洛津弦A菁英組－第三名

每一座獎項，都是孩子們辛勤練習的見證；每一個優美音符，都是專注與堅持累積而成的成果。

弦樂，不只是演奏，更是一場團隊的學習。孩子們在一次次合奏中學會傾聽，在彼此配合中學會合作，也在舞台上學會勇敢展現自己。

特別感謝辛苦指導弦樂團的老師們，從日常練習到比賽準備，細心指導、耐心陪伴，帶著孩子們一步一步成長，讓每一段旋律都更加動人。也感謝家長們一路以來的支持與陪伴，因為有學校、老師與家長共同為孩子築起最溫暖的後盾，孩子們才能無後顧之憂地追逐音樂夢想！

今天的獎項，是對努力的肯定；明天的旋律，將繼續寫下更多精彩！願洛津弦樂團的孩子們，繼續以琴弓拉出自信，以音符傳遞熱情，在音樂的道路上，一路發光、一路成長！

洛津弦樂，弦動精彩！琴聲悠揚，榮耀綻放！努力不會缺席，精彩終將發聲！大家一起為洛津國小弦樂團喝采、按讚、拍拍手！恭喜所有得獎的小小音樂家們！你們真的太棒了！
