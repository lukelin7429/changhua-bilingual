All Aboard the Fun-Learning Express

樂學列車即將到站

Summer will eventually come to an end. But growth is only just beginning.

Along the way, we opened a book with our children — and opened their imagination too. We folded a paper airplane — and let a dream take flight. We completed a science experiment — and lit up their curiosity. We picked up a tablet to learn — and caught a glimpse of the world. We chased kites across the field — and chased after courage.

It turns out the most beautiful harvest isn't how many activities we finished, but one more spark in a child's eyes, one more measure of confidence in their heart.

The Fun-Learning Express is about to pull into the station — what it carries away is laughter; what it leaves behind is growth.

Thank you to every child who filled this summer with smiles, and to every parent and partner who walked this road together.

May this summer become a memory from childhood that brings a smile every time it's remembered.

中文原文 · Chinese

夏天，終究會結束。但成長，才正要開始。

這一路，我們陪著孩子翻開一本書，也翻開了想像；摺起一架紙飛機，也放飛了夢想；完成一場科學探索，也點亮了好奇；拿起平板學習，也看見了世界；追著風箏奔跑，也追逐著勇敢。

原來，最美的收穫，不是完成了多少活動，而是孩子眼裡多了一道光，心中多了一份自信。

樂學列車即將到站，帶走的是歡笑，留下的是成長。

謝謝每一位孩子用笑容豐富了這個夏天；也謝謝每一位家長與夥伴一路同行。

願今年夏天，成為孩子童年裡，一段想起來就會微笑的回憶。
