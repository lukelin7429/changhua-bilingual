I.

A Place to Learn and Grow

一所學習與成長的學校

Loojing Elementary School, located in Lugang Township, Changhua, is a school full of energy and culture.

洛津國小位於彰化縣鹿港鎮，是一所充滿活力與人文氣息的學校。

We encourage students to love learning, enjoy the arts, and be thankful. Our school vision, "A Land of Learning, A Melody of Growth," guides us in creating a well-rounded education for every child.

我們鼓勵學生熱愛學習、享受藝術、心懷感恩。學校願景「學習的沃土，成長的樂章」，引領我們為每個孩子打造全人教育。

II.

Learning with Joy

快樂學習

We offer many learning programs, including after-school care, tutoring, English practice classes, and art lessons. To help students improve their English, we have a foreign teacher, Richard, teaching upper-grade students. Our kindergarten program has also won an award for its outstanding teaching.

我們提供多元學習方案，包括課後照顧、課業輔導、英語練習課與藝術課程。為提升學生英語能力，特聘外籍教師 Richard 為高年級授課；附設幼兒園也曾以優異教學表現獲獎。

Learning with Joy
快樂學習

After-school care, tutoring, English practice, and art lessons — with foreign teacher Richard and an award-winning kindergarten.

Music for the Heart
音樂洗滌人心

A string ensemble, recorder group, ocarina class, and violin club — with professional teachers helping students enjoy music and develop their talents.

Building Good Character
品格教育扎根

Students read classics like Di Zi Gui and Bai Xiao Jing to learn respect, kindness, and responsibility, with a special Friday night reading class.

Good Habits for Life
生活好習慣

We teach the importance of respect, good manners, and being on time — habits that help students grow into kind and responsible individuals.

Staying Active and Healthy
動起來，更健康

Through the SH150 program and clubs like soccer, roller skating, taekwondo, track and field, and martial arts, students build strength and teamwork.

A Fun, Caring, Well-Rounded Campus
有趣、關懷、全人發展的校園

At Loojing Elementary School, our goal is to help every child grow with knowledge, creativity, and strong values.
