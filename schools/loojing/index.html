I.

A Place to Learn and Grow

一所學習與成長的學校

Loojing Elementary School, located in Lugang Township, Changhua, is a school full of energy and culture.

洛津國小位於彰化縣鹿港鎮，是一所充滿活力與人文氣息的學校。

We encourage students to love learning, love the arts, and live thankfully. Our school vision grew from the school itself: in 2001, teachers across three generations looked back on Loojing's history and chose three ideas to guide us — love learning, love the arts, live thankfully. As Taiwan moved to its twelve-year basic education curriculum, we deepened that vision, and in 2023 shaped it into a new couplet.

我們鼓勵學生熱愛學習、享受藝術、心懷感恩。洛津的校本願景，是全校老師一起長出來的：90 學年度，老中青三代教師齊聚一堂，從洛津的歷史出發，共同議定「愛學習、愛藝術、愛感恩」；隨著十二年國教推動，學校持續深化願景，112 學年度起凝鍊為新的對句。

"A Land of Learning, A Melody of Growth."
「洛地文風盛，津月琴音揚」— 洛津國小 112 學年度校本願景。
II.

Rooted in Lugang

扎根鹿港

A school in an old town

古鎮裡的學校

Loojing sits in Lugang — a town centuries old, rich in history, craft, and local flavour. We turn that heritage into lessons, so children come to know their own place first-hand.

洛津國小坐落於文風鼎盛、歷史悠久的鹿港古鎮。在濃厚的人文與地方物產孕育下，學校擁有得天獨厚的在地文化資源，並將它轉化為課程，讓孩子親身認識自己的家鄉。

軸線 01
A Taste of Lugang
食在鹿港 — 從在地小吃與物產認識家鄉。
軸線 02
Stories of Lugang
戲說鹿港 — 從老街傳說與信仰認識歷史。
軸線 03
Walking Lugang
鹿港行腳 — 走讀古蹟巷弄，用腳步認識土地。
III.

Five Ways to Grow

五個成長的方向

Diligent study for wisdom, music for the heart, classics for character, daily life for attitude, sport for a strong body.

勤學增智慧、音樂美心靈、經典鑄品格、生活養態度、體育健體魄。

Learning with Joy
快樂學習

Homework guidance, remedial classes, an English practice class, and art — and foreign teacher Richard teaches English to the upper grades. Our kindergarten's theme-based teaching won the Changhua County Teaching Excellence Award.課業指導、補救教學、英語勤學班與美術；外師 Richard 為高年級授課，附設幼兒園以主題教學榮獲彰化縣教學卓越獎。

Music for the Heart
音樂洗滌人心

A string ensemble, recorder group, ocarina class, and violin club — led by a team of professional visiting teachers who help students enjoy music and grow their talents.弦樂團、直笛團、陶笛、小提琴等音樂社團，由專業外聘教師群帶領。

Building Good Character
品格教育扎根

Loojing is a classics-reading model school. Students work through more than a hundred classic texts — the Di Zi Gui, the Bai Xiao Jing and more — and the Tiannan reading class meets on Friday evenings.洛津是讀經典範學校；學生研讀弟子規、百孝經等一百多項教材，天南讀經班每週五晚上開課。

Good Habits for Life
生活好習慣

We put everyday life education first — courtesy, respect for teachers, and punctuality, practised until they become second nature.重視日常生活教育，將禮貌、尊師重道、守時等落實於生活，內化為態度。

Staying Active and Healthy
動起來，更健康

The SH150 programme is built into the school day, with outdoor exercise to refresh body and mind. Sports clubs include soccer, inline skating, taekwondo, track and field, and martial arts.SH150 融入每日作息，戶外運動調劑身心；設足球、直排輪、跆拳道、田徑、武術等運動社團。

IV.

The CHAMP Framework

CHAMP 全人素養

Loojing's humanities raise the whole child; five strengths open the future.

洛蘊人文育全人，津揚五力展未來 —— 以在地人文培育全人，以五種能力迎向未來。

C
Character
品格力

Classics reading and life education, so respect and gratitude are lived every day.深化品格教育，透過經典閱讀與生活教育，落實尊重與感恩。

H
Health
健康力

Sport and health education — SH150 and clubs building strong bodies and a lasting attitude to life.結合體育活動與健康教育，促進全人發展與永續生命態度。

A
Aesthetics
美感力

Art and many instruments — strings, recorder, ocarina, violin — nurturing a feel for beauty.涵育藝術素養，結合多元藝術與器樂學習，提升生命美感。

M
Mindset
態度行動力

Life education that leads students to take part, work as a team, and try new things.強化實踐參與，引導學生主動參與、團隊合作與積極創新。

P
Perseverance
學習力

Inquiry across subjects and disciplines, building the habit of lifelong learning.精進探究學習，培養學生主動探究與終身學習力。

"Loojing's humanities raise the whole child — to be a CHAMP."
「洛蘊人文育全人，津揚五力展未來」～ To be a CHAMP
A Fun, Caring, Well-Rounded Campus
有趣、關懷、全人發展的校園

At Loojing Elementary School, our goal is to help every child grow with knowledge, creativity, and strong values.
