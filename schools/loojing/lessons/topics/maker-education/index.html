Maker Education

自造教育
← Lessons · 回課程列表

Creating, Exploring, and Innovating!

At Loojing Elementary School, we believe that learning happens best when students create with their own hands. Our Maker Education program lets students design, build, and test their own projects — turning curiosity into real, working creations.

Learning by Doing

In Maker Education, students design, build, and test their own projects using coding, 3D printing, and engineering tools, turning ideas into real creations that boost motivation and a sense of achievement.

Connecting Different Subjects

Our program combines science, technology, engineering, art, and math (STEAM), so students can explore multiple fields of knowledge while working with their hands, and build understanding through real, hands-on projects.

Collaboration with Other Schools

To give students even more opportunities, Loojing Elementary works with other schools and maker centers so students can access more advanced technology and hands-on courses:

  • Lukang Senior High School Maker Center — students develop innovative thinking and problem-solving skills, getting an early taste of cross-disciplinary learning and applied technology education.
  • Changhua Arts High School Metaverse Lab — through AR and VR, students explore virtual worlds, making learning more immersive and interactive.
  • Fuxing Junior High Maker Center — hands-on projects integrate nature, science, and technology, helping students learn through creating and boosting their interest and results.
  • Heren Elementary Augmented Reality English Center — students use robots, AI recognition, and interactive apps to explore digital learning and artificial intelligence, discovering how technology can transform education.

With Maker Education, learning becomes an adventure! At Loojing Elementary, we encourage every student to become an innovator and inventor, ready for a future shaped by technology.

中文原文 · Chinese

創造、探索、發明！

在洛津國小，我們相信動手做與探索新點子能讓學習變得更有趣！因此，我們推動自造教育，讓學生透過實作學習，發展解決問題、創意思考與團隊合作能力。

從實作中學習

在自造教育課程中，學生透過設計、組裝與測試來完成自己的作品，運用編程、3D列印與工程工具，將想法變為實際成品，提升學習興趣與成就感。

跨領域整合，提升學習力

我們的課程融合科學（Science）、技術（Technology）、工程（Engineering）、藝術（Art）、數學（Mathematics）（STEAM），讓學生在動手做的過程中學習多元知識，理解力也隨之提升。

攜手多所學校，共享資源

為了提供學生更豐富的學習機會，洛津國小與多所學校及創客中心合作，讓學生接觸更多先進科技與實作課程：

  • 🔹 與鹿港高中創客中心合作——培養學生創意思維與解決問題能力，提前接觸跨學科學習，並體驗科技教育的應用。
  • 🔹 與彰化藝術高中元宇宙智慧館合作——學生透過AR與VR體驗，進入數位創意世界，讓學習更沉浸式、互動化。
  • 🔹 與福興國中自造教育中心合作——透過動手實作，將自然、科學與科技融合，讓學生在創作中學習，提升興趣與學習成效。
  • 🔹 與和仁國小擴增實境英語科技學習中心合作——透過機器人、AI辨識與互動APP，學生體驗數位學習與人工智慧技術，發掘科技如何提升教育。

在洛津國小，我們讓學習變成一場冒險！透過自造教育，每位學生都能成為創新者、發明家，迎向科技未來！

create/kriˈeɪt/創造
