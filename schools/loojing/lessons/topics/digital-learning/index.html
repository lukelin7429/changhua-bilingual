Digital Learning

數位學習
← Lessons · 回課程列表

Learning Smarter, Growing Stronger

At Loojing Elementary School, we believe digital tools can make learning more fun and exciting! Our teachers keep building their digital teaching skills, so students learn through technology — not just by reading, but by exploring, discovering, and creating.

Bringing Digital Learning to Everyone

We don't just use tablets in class — we teach students how to use digital tools wisely. This helps them learn better and prepares them for the future. We also encourage parents and teachers to work together, so every child gets the best learning experience both at school and at home.

Connecting Different Subjects with Digital Learning

We use digital tools to make different subjects more interesting. Our program includes:

  • Reading Challenges — students complete online reading tasks to improve their skills.
  • Environmental Education — learning about nature and sustainability through digital activities.
  • English Learning — using digital platforms to practice English in fun ways.
  • Life Education — understanding values and life skills through online lessons.
  • Student Surveys — answering questions to express opinions and learn more about different topics.

By combining technology with these subjects, students gain knowledge in a creative and meaningful way.

Teachers Keep Learning Too!

To give students the best education, our teachers also attend digital training programs. They learn how to use new technology to design lessons that are both fun and effective. Through digital learning, the teachers and students of Loojing Elementary grow together!

聰明學習，快樂成長

在洛津國小，我們相信數位工具可以讓學習變得更有趣、更精彩！老師們不斷提升數位教學技能，讓學生透過科技學習，不只是讀書，而是探索、發現、創造！

數位學習帶給大家新體驗

我們不只是使用平板上課，而是教會學生如何善用數位工具來學習。這不僅能幫助他們學得更好，也能為未來做好準備。此外，我們也鼓勵家長和老師共同努力，讓孩子在學校和家裡都能有最好的學習體驗。

數位學習融入不同領域

我們運用數位科技，讓不同學科變得更有趣，包括：

  • 閱讀挑戰 — 學生透過線上閱讀活動提升閱讀能力。
  • 環境教育 — 透過數位課程學習環保與永續發展。
  • 英語學習 — 運用數位平台，讓英語學習變得生動有趣。
  • 生命教育 — 透過線上課程，培養學生的品格與生活技能。
  • 學生意見調查 — 讓學生透過問卷發表意見，學習不同主題知識。

透過這些數位課程，學生不僅能學習新知，還能用創意方式吸收與應用。

老師們也持續進修！

為了提供學生最好的學習體驗，我們的老師也積極參加數位教學培訓。他們學習如何運用新科技，設計有趣又有效的課程。透過數位學習，洛津國小的師生們一起成長！
