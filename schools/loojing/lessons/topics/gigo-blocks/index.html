Lessons · Course 02

Gigo Blocks

智高積木
← Lessons · 回課程列表

Hands-on Learning for Creative Thinkers!

At Loojing Elementary School, learning isn't just about books — it's about exploring, creating, and building! Our Gigo Blocks program helps students develop logical thinking, creativity, and hands-on skills. Through building with blocks, students learn basic mechanical engineering, physics, and problem-solving, all while strengthening teamwork.

Hands-on Learning for Stronger Skills

Through building with Gigo Blocks, students improve their spatial awareness, concentration, and problem-solving skills. Whether it's a simple gear or a complex moving mechanism, every attempt strengthens logical thinking and creativity. Working in teams, students also learn to communicate, collaborate, and search for the best solution together.

From Building to Coding — Exploring Smart Technology

Our program doesn't stop at construction — students also learn basic coding and automation! By using simple code, students can bring their block creations to life and experience how machines run automatically. This builds a real sense of achievement and helps students develop computational thinking for future tech learning.

A Strong STEAM Foundation

The Gigo Blocks Program integrates Science, Technology, Engineering, Art, and Mathematics (STEAM), encouraging students to build creativity and problem-solving skills through hands-on work — laying a strong foundation for future challenges.

At Loojing Elementary, we believe that learning should be fun, and creativity should be limitless! Through Gigo Blocks, students aren't just playing — they're building their own future.

中文原文 · Chinese

動手學習，培養創意思維！

在洛津國小，學習不僅限於書本，更是一場探索、創造與建構的旅程！我們的智高積木課程幫助學生培養邏輯思維、創意發想與動手實作能力，透過積木組裝學習機械工程、基礎物理與問題解決，同時鍛鍊團隊合作精神。

動手操作，提升多元能力

透過智高積木課程，學生能夠提升空間概念、專注力與解決問題的能力。無論是簡單的齒輪組合或是複雜的動力機械，每一次的嘗試都能強化邏輯思維與創造力。此外，在團隊合作中，學生學會溝通、協作與分享，一起尋找最好的解決方案。

從搭建到編程——探索智慧科技

課程不僅限於組裝機械，還結合基礎程式設計與自動化控制！透過簡單的編碼，學生可以讓自己的積木作品動起來，體驗如何讓機械自動運作。這不僅增加學習成就感，也幫助學生建立運算思維，為未來的科技學習奠定基礎。

奠定STEAM基礎，迎向未來挑戰

智高積木課程涵蓋科學（Science）、技術（Technology）、工程（Engineering）、藝術（Art）、數學（Mathematics），讓學生在動手實作中培養創造力與解決問題的能力，為未來挑戰做好準備。

在洛津國小，我們相信學習可以是有趣的，創造可以是無限的！透過智高積木課程，學生不只是玩樂，更是在搭建屬於自己的未來！
