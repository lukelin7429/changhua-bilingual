Enhancing English Skills Through Conversations with Japanese Speakers

At Loojing Elementary School, we encourage students to improve their English by engaging in real conversations. Talking with Japanese speakers helps students strengthen their listening and speaking skills, and put English to practical, everyday use.

Natural and Fluent Communication

Through daily conversations, students become familiar with natural expressions, pronunciation, and intonation, building a stronger feel for the language and more fluent spoken English.

Cultural Exchange and Global Understanding

By communicating with Japanese speakers, students also gain insights into different ways of speaking English. This helps them understand cultural differences and build stronger cross-cultural communication skills.

Building Confidence in Speaking English

The more students practice, the better they become at forming sentences, using vocabulary correctly, and responding quickly, making conversations more fluent. Taking part in real, authentic exchanges helps students overcome the fear of speaking English and gradually build confidence.

With consistent practice and long-term effort, this learning method can greatly improve English communication skills and broaden students' global outlook — a valuable skill for both future careers and everyday life.

中文原文 · Chinese

與日本人對話，提升英語溝通能力

在洛津國小，我們鼓勵學生透過真實對話來提升英語能力。與日本人進行英語交流，能幫助學生增強聽力與口說能力，並提升在日常生活中的實際運用。

自然流暢的溝通

透過日常對話，學生能熟悉自然的語言表達、發音與語調，培養語感，使口語表達更加流暢自然。

跨文化交流與國際理解

與日本人對話，學生能了解不同的英語表達方式，進一步認識文化差異，提升跨文化溝通能力。

建立英語口說自信

透過不斷練習，學生能加強句型運用與詞彙掌握，提高反應速度，使對話更加流利。參與真實情境的交流，幫助學生克服開口說英語的恐懼，逐步建立自信。

持續練習並長期努力，這種學習方式將有效提升英語溝通能力，拓展國際視野，無論是在未來的職場或日常生活中，都將是一項寶貴的技能！
