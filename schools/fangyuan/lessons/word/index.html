Ocean & Tidal Flat

海洋與潮間帶 · 6 個單字
WORD 001
beach (n)
海灘
Fangyuan's beach is famous for its wide tidal flat.
芳苑的海灘以廣闊的潮間帶聞名。
Many families visit the beach to look for oysters and shells.
許多家庭會到海灘尋找牡蠣與貝殼。
WORD 002
intertidal zone (n)
潮間帶
The intertidal zone is covered by water at high tide.
漲潮時，潮間帶會被海水淹沒。
Students explore the intertidal zone to learn about sea-ox culture.
學生走進潮間帶，認識海牛文化。
WORD 003
mangrove (n)
紅樹林
A mangrove is a tree that grows in salty coastal water.
紅樹林是生長在鹹水海岸邊的樹木。
Mangroves protect the coastline and give small fish a safe home.
紅樹林保護海岸線，也是小魚安全的家。
WORD 004
oyster (n)
牡蠣
An oyster grows inside a hard, bumpy shell.
牡蠣生長在堅硬、凹凸不平的殼裡。
Fangyuan's tidal flat is famous for its delicious oysters.
芳苑的潮間帶以美味的牡蠣聞名。
WORD 005
leaf (n)
葉子
A leaf uses sunlight to make food for the plant.
葉子利用陽光為植物製造養分。
We collect fallen leaves for our art class.
我們蒐集掉落的葉子來上美勞課。
WORD 006
star (n)
星星
A star shines brightly in the night sky.
星星在夜空中閃閃發光。
We can see many stars from our seaside campus at night.
晚上從我們的海邊校園可以看到很多星星。

Sports & Playground

體育與遊戲場 · 3 個單字
WORD 007
dodgeball (n)
躲避球
Dodgeball is a game where players throw a ball to tag each other.
躲避球是一種互相丟球的遊戲。
We play dodgeball together during PE class.
我們在體育課一起玩躲避球。
WORD 008
basketball court (n)
籃球場
The basketball court is where students shoot hoops after class.
籃球場是同學們下課後投籃的地方。
Our basketball court has lines painted in bright colors.
我們的籃球場畫著色彩鮮豔的線條。
WORD 009
relay race
接力賽
In a relay race, each runner passes the baton to a teammate.
接力賽中，每位跑者都要把接力棒交給隊友。
Our class worked together to win the relay race.
我們班同心協力贏得了接力賽。

Classroom & Study

教室與學習 · 7 個單字
WORD 010
presentation (n)
報告
A presentation is when you share information in front of the class.
報告是在全班面前分享資訊。
She gave a presentation about Fangyuan's sea-ox culture.
她做了一份關於芳苑海牛文化的報告。
WORD 011
social studies
社會科
Social studies teaches us about people, places, and history.
社會科教我們認識人、地方與歷史。
In social studies, we learned why Fangyuan is called an oyster town.
在社會課，我們學到芳苑為什麼被稱為蚵鄉。
WORD 012
Chinese characters
國字
Chinese characters are the written symbols we use in Mandarin.
國字是我們書寫中文時使用的文字符號。
We practice writing Chinese characters every morning.
我們每天早上都會練習寫國字。
WORD 013
correct (v)
改正
To correct a mistake means to make it right.
改正錯誤就是把它變正確。
The teacher helped us correct our spelling.
老師幫我們改正拼字的錯誤。
WORD 014
test (n)
考試
A test checks how much you have learned.
考試用來檢查你學會了多少。
We studied together before the English test.
我們在英語考試前一起讀書。
WORD 015
reading activity
閱讀活動
A reading activity helps students enjoy books together.
閱讀活動幫助學生一起享受閱讀。
Our library holds a reading activity every week.
我們的圖書館每週都有閱讀活動。
WORD 016
history (n)
歷史
History tells the story of what happened in the past.
歷史訴說著過去發生的故事。
Fangyuan's sea-ox culture is an important part of our local history.
芳苑的海牛文化是我們在地歷史的重要一部分。

Campus Life

校園生活 · 11 個單字
WORD 017
walkway (n)
步道
A walkway is a path for people to walk safely.
步道是讓人安全行走的通道。
The covered walkway connects our classrooms to the playground.
有頂棚的步道連接教室與操場。
WORD 018
visitor (n)
訪客
A visitor is someone who comes to see our school.
訪客是來參觀我們學校的人。
We welcome every visitor with a friendly smile.
我們用友善的微笑歡迎每一位訪客。
WORD 019
tour guide (n)
導遊
A tour guide leads visitors around a place and explains what they see.
導遊帶領訪客參觀，並解說所見的事物。
Our students act as tour guides to introduce Fangyuan's campus.
我們的學生擔任導遊，介紹芳苑的校園。
WORD 020
professional development
專業進修
Professional development helps teachers learn new skills.
專業進修幫助老師學習新技能。
Our teachers join professional development workshops every semester.
我們的老師每學期都會參加專業進修工作坊。
WORD 021
game (n)
遊戲
A game is a fun activity with rules to follow.
遊戲是一種有規則可循的有趣活動。
We play English games to practice new words.
我們玩英語遊戲來練習新單字。
WORD 022
award (n)
獎
An award is a prize given for doing something well.
獎是因為表現優異而獲得的獎勵。
Our school won an award for its creative teaching.
我們學校因創意教學而獲獎。
WORD 023
treasure hunt
尋寶遊戲
In a treasure hunt, players follow clues to find a hidden prize.
尋寶遊戲中，玩家跟著線索找到藏起來的獎品。
We had a treasure hunt around campus during English class.
我們在英語課到校園裡玩尋寶遊戲。
WORD 024
charity event
慈善活動
A charity event raises money or goods to help others.
慈善活動募集金錢或物資來幫助他人。
Our school held a charity event for families in need.
我們學校為有需要的家庭舉辦了慈善活動。
WORD 025
weekly English
每週英語
Weekly English is a short lesson we have every week.
每週英語是我們每週一堂的小課程。
Weekly English helps us practice speaking a little bit at a time.
每週英語幫助我們一點一點練習開口說。
WORD 026
work together
合作
To work together means to help each other finish a task.
合作就是互相幫忙完成一件事。
Our class worked together to clean up the beach.
我們班同心協力一起淨灘。
WORD 027
weekly English
每週英語
Every week, we learn something new in Weekly English.
每週英語裡，我們每週都學到新東西。
Weekly English keeps our classroom full of English words.
每週英語讓我們的教室充滿英文單字。

Arts & Culture

藝術與文化 · 12 個單字
WORD 028
paint (v)
彩繪
To paint means to add color to something with a brush.
彩繪就是用畫筆為東西上色。
Students paint colorful murals on the walls of our school.
學生們在學校牆上彩繪出繽紛的壁畫。
WORD 029
harmonica (n)
口琴
A harmonica is a small instrument you play by blowing and breathing.
口琴是用吹氣與吸氣演奏的小型樂器。
Our Harmonica Club practices every week after school.
我們的口琴社每週放學後都會練習。
WORD 030
harmonica (n)
口琴
You hold a harmonica with both hands and move it side to side.
演奏口琴時要用雙手握住，左右移動。
Many Fangyuan students learn to play the harmonica.
許多芳苑的學生都在學吹口琴。
WORD 031
harmonica (n)
口琴
A harmonica can play both happy and slow, gentle songs.
口琴可以吹奏歡樂的曲子，也可以吹奏緩慢溫柔的曲子。
Our Harmonica Club performs at school events.
我們的口琴社會在學校活動中表演。
WORD 032
dance (v)
跳舞
To dance means to move your body to music.
跳舞就是隨著音樂擺動身體。
Students dance together during our festival celebrations.
學生們在節慶慶祝活動中一起跳舞。
WORD 033
dance (v)
跳舞
Dancing helps us feel the rhythm of the music.
跳舞讓我們感受音樂的節奏。
Our class learned a new dance for the school anniversary.
我們班為校慶學了一支新舞蹈。
WORD 034
tea ceremony
茶道
A tea ceremony is a traditional way of preparing and serving tea.
茶道是一種傳統的泡茶與奉茶方式。
Students learn patience and manners through the tea ceremony.
學生透過茶道學習耐心與禮儀。
WORD 035
hometown (n)
家鄉
Your hometown is the place where you grew up.
家鄉是你成長的地方。
Fangyuan is our hometown, right beside the sea.
芳苑是我們的家鄉，就在海的旁邊。
WORD 036
origami
摺紙
Origami is the art of folding paper into shapes.
摺紙是把紙折成各種形狀的藝術。
We made origami boats to celebrate our seaside school.
我們摺了紙船來慶祝我們的海邊學校。
WORD 037
weekly Taiwanese sentence
閩南語每週一句
Our weekly Taiwanese sentence teaches one local phrase at a time.
閩南語每週一句每次教我們一句在地話。
Learning a weekly Taiwanese sentence keeps our home language alive.
學習閩南語每週一句讓我們的母語持續傳承。
WORD 038
Taiwanese sesame noodles
台灣麻醬麵
Taiwanese sesame noodles are noodles mixed with a rich sesame sauce.
台灣麻醬麵是拌著濃郁芝麻醬的麵條。
Our cooking class made Taiwanese sesame noodles together.
我們的烹飪課一起做了台灣麻醬麵。
WORD 039
Mother's Day
母親節
Mother's Day is a special day to thank our mothers.
母親節是感謝媽媽的特別節日。
We made cards for Mother's Day to show our love.
我們為母親節做卡片，表達我們的愛。

Everyday Objects & Science

生活物品與科學 · 7 個單字
WORD 040
bake (v)
烘烤
To bake means to cook food using dry heat in an oven.
烘烤就是用烤箱的乾熱烹煮食物。
Students bake cookies together in our cooking class.
學生們在烹飪課一起烘烤餅乾。
WORD 041
battery (n)
電池
A battery stores energy to power small machines.
電池儲存能量，用來驅動小型機器。
Our science project needed a battery to make the light turn on.
我們的科學作業需要電池才能讓燈亮起來。
WORD 042
wrap (v)
包
To wrap means to cover something completely with paper or cloth.
包就是用紙或布把東西完全覆蓋起來。
We wrap gifts for our classmates during the holiday party.
我們在節慶派對上為同學包裝禮物。
WORD 043
drone
無人機/空拍機
A drone is a small flying machine controlled by remote control.
無人機是用遙控器操控的小型飛行器。
We used a drone to take photos of our whole campus.
我們用空拍機拍下整個校園的照片。
WORD 044
assemble (v)
組裝
To assemble means to put the parts of something together.
組裝就是把零件組合在一起。
We assemble our model rockets carefully, piece by piece.
我們一片一片小心地組裝模型火箭。
WORD 045
cooking class
烹飪課
In cooking class, students learn to prepare simple dishes.
在烹飪課，學生學習準備簡單的菜餚。
Our cooking class made a local Fangyuan snack together.
我們的烹飪課一起做了芳苑在地小吃。
WORD 046
bottle rocket
水火箭
A bottle rocket is a plastic bottle filled with water and air that shoots into the sky.
水火箭是裝滿水和空氣、能射向天空的塑膠瓶。
We launched bottle rockets together during our science day.
我們在科學日一起發射水火箭。
