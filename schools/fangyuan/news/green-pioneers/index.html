Green Pioneers: Environmental Education Comes to Campus綠色小尖兵，環保從校園做起

On June 30, Fangyuan Elementary hosted a “Green Pioneers” environmental education event, welcoming staff from Yung-Yi Green Energy Materials Co. to campus to teach students about recycling.

Protecting the earth starts on campus.

Through lively explanations and hands-on activities, students learned about sorting waste, recycling, and reusing resources, and how to put environmental awareness into practice in daily life. In a joyful learning atmosphere, the children built a genuine sense of environmental responsibility.

Protecting the earth is more than a slogan — it begins with small actions in everyday life. We believe every student can become a “Green Pioneer” who guards the environment, starting with themselves and their own families, working together toward a cleaner, more sustainable home.

Thank you for helping environmental education take root.

Thank you to Yung-Yi Green Energy Materials Co. for sharing their expertise with our students, helping environmental education take root and planting the seeds of sustainability in children's hearts.

中文原文 · Chinese

6月30日，芳苑國小辦理「綠色小尖兵」環保教育活動，由永溢綠能材料股份有限公司蒞校，帶領孩子認識資源回收的重要觀念。

環保從校園做起。

透過生動有趣的解說與互動體驗，學生了解垃圾分類、資源回收及循環再利用的知識，學習如何將環保行動落實在日常生活中。孩子們在歡樂的學習氛圍中，建立正確的環保觀念。

愛護地球，不只是口號，而是從生活中的每一個小動作開始。相信每位孩子都能成為守護環境的「綠色小尖兵」，從自己做起、從家庭做起，攜手打造更乾淨、更永續的美好家園。

感謝讓環境教育向下扎根。

感謝永溢綠能材料股份有限公司到校分享專業知識，讓環境教育向下扎根，也讓永續發展的種子在孩子心中萌芽。
