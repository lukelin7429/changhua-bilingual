Kindergarten's End-of-Term Adventures幼兒園期末活動分享

A rainy walk to give thanks.

On June 24, our kindergarten's little ones walked to Putian Temple to offer their thanks. Light rain began to fall just before they set out — but it also softened the sun's heat, so the walk stayed cool and comfortable. We're grateful for Mazu's blessing.

At the temple, the children quietly offered their wishes, thanking Mazu for keeping them healthy and strong, and hoping the new school year ahead would bring continued safety, health, and happiness every day.

Splashing away the summer heat.

Just a few days later, on a rare sunny day after a long stretch of rain, our kindergarten children made the most of the weather with a joyful water play day. The impressive water guns in their hands were a gift from a classmate's mother — and with them, the children also gave the dusty walls and windows a good rinse, turning playtime into a small act of cleaning, without wasting a drop of water.

Every ending is a new beginning.

From a quiet moment of gratitude at the temple to a burst of laughter and water on a summer afternoon, our kindergarten's end-of-term days were full of warmth. We look forward to seeing these little ones grow, healthy and happy, into the new school year.

中文原文 · Chinese

細雨中的感恩之行。

6月24日，幼兒園的寶貝們步行到普天宮祈願，出門前天空飄起毛毛細雨，因為遮住部分陽光，因此也顯得不是那麼熱，真的謝謝媽祖的保佑。

寶貝們在普天宮靜心地祈願，感謝媽祖保佑我們健康茁壯，希望新的學年開始，能繼續保佑我們平安健康，天天都開心。

玩水消暑。

幾天後，連續多日的雨天終於放晴，幼兒園寶貝們利用這個難得的好天氣一起玩水消暑，大家手上拿著的厲害水槍，是柚子媽媽送給大家的禮物，我們也順手利用水槍把髒髒的牆壁和窗戶清潔乾淨，真的是一舉兩得，沒有浪費水資源。

每個結束，都是新的開始。

從普天宮裡安靜的感恩，到夏日午後歡笑玩水的時光，幼兒園的期末生活充滿溫馨。期待這群小寶貝，健康快樂地邁向新的學年！
