I.

Welcome to Santan

歡迎來到三潭國小
Welcome to Santan Elementary School · 三潭國小歡迎影片

A campus among the rice fields, wide enough for every talent.

坐落在稻田之間的校園，寬敞得足以容納每一種才能。

Welcome to Santan Elementary School! We are in the beautiful town of Tianzhong, Changhua County, and we are home to around 200 students. Nestled among lush rice fields, our campus has spacious, well-kept grounds — perfect for the many activities our children love, from soccer to drumming class.

歡迎來到三潭國小！本校位於美麗的彰化縣田中鎮，現有約200 名學生。我們的校園坐落在鬱鬱蔥蔥的稻田之中，擁有寬敞且維護良好的場地，非常適合孩子們喜愛的各種活動——從足球到太鼓班。

At Santan we are committed to a well-rounded education. Alongside a strong academic programme, we offer a wide range of after-school clubs to help every child grow and discover their talents. We are also proud to build a bilingual learning environment, with teaching in both Mandarin and English.

在三潭國小，我們致力於為學生提供全面的教育。除了扎實的學科課程外，還提供各式各樣的課外活動，幫助每個孩子成長、發掘自己的才能。我們也很自豪能夠推動雙語學習環境，同時重視華語與英語教學。

Vitality · Joy · Health · Learning
活力 · 快樂 · 健康 · 學習——寫在司令台上的八個字，也是三潭孩子每一天的樣子。
II.

Where the Three Ponds Meet

三潭之名 · 學區與歷史

A school named for the villages it serves.

校名，來自我們服務的三個村里。

Independent Since 1956
民國四十五年獨立設校

Santan stands in Beilu Village, Tianzhong Township, with the historic Bapu Canal and Wenxing High School just to the north. Our catchment covers the three "pond" villages the school is named after — Zhongtan, Longtan and Dingtan — together with part of Beilu. What began as the Santan branch of Tianzhong Public School became a school in its own right in 1956, and in 2024 it marked its 68th anniversary.

本校位於彰化縣田中鎮北路里，北邊隔著八堡圳與文興中學相鄰；學區範圍包含田中鎮的中潭里、龍潭里、頂潭里以及部分的北路里——「三潭」之名即由此而來。學校自民國四十五年由田中國民學校三潭分校正式獨立為三潭國民小學，至民國 113 年已滿六十八週年。

III.

What Santan Is Known For

四個我們最用心經營的方向

Room to run, and room to try everything.

有奔跑的空間，也有嘗試各種可能的空間。

Clubs for Every Talent
多元社團 · 拓展學習的觸角

To widen what children can reach for, Santan has opened club after club over the years — dance, table tennis, taiko drumming, ocarina, inline skating, ukulele, archery, diabolo and taekwondo among them — so that every student has somewhere to find what they are good at. 為拓展學生學習的觸角，陸續開辦舞蹈、桌球、太鼓、陶笛、直排輪、烏克麗麗、射箭、扯鈴、跆拳道……等各類型社團，提供更加多元的學習環境。

The Marathon Curriculum
校訂課程 · 以馬拉松為主軸

Our school-designed curriculum takes the marathon as its spine and runs three themed strands off it — a History & Culture marathon, a Sports marathon and a Food marathon — each one going deeper and wider as children move up the school. 本校校訂課程以馬拉松為主軸，延伸文史馬、運動馬及美食馬三大主題課程，逐年以加深、加廣的方式延展學習課程。

Teams That Go the Distance
運動校隊 · 足球、田徑與射箭

Santan fields a soccer team, a track and field team and an archery team, and our young athletes have done well in competition across every one of these sports. The rainbow sports wall along the track was painted for them. 本校另成立足球隊、田徑隊及射箭隊，在各項體育項目皆有優異成績；操場邊的彩虹運動牆，正是為這群孩子而畫。

One Growing Family
校園大家庭 · 從幼兒園到六年級

Santan has ten general classes and two itinerant resource classes, so support reaches the children who need it. Since 2021 the campus has also been home to the Santan Non-Profit Preschool, publicly commissioned and privately run — which means our youngest learners grow up on the same grounds. 學校設有普通班 10 班與不分類巡迴班 2 班；自民國 110 年起附設公辦民營的三潭非營利幼兒園開始招生，讓最小的孩子也在同一片校園中長大。
