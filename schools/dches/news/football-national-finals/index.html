Eighth in Taiwan, and the Shirt Passed On114 學年度小學生足球賽全國賽 · 榮耀落幕，夢想啟航

On July 5, at the Chaoma Football Field in Taichung, the whistle went for the last time on the 2025–26 National Elementary School Football Tournament. Dacheng Elementary School, carrying the colors of Changhua County into the school division, finished eighth in Taiwan.

Getting there took most of the school year. 773 teams entered this season; after nearly three months of county qualifying rounds, 198 came through to the national finals — nine days of football at a single stadium. To walk off that pitch inside the top eight is no small thing for a city primary school.

Every run, every tackle, every shot

This result belongs to every player who left everything on the pitch, to the coaching team who trained them through the year, to the teachers and parents who traveled to every match, and to everyone who has ever cheered this team on.

The children watered a dream with sweat and met each challenge with stubbornness. Every run, every tackle, every shot at goal carried the same refusal to give up — and showed what years of patient football teaching at Dacheng have grown.

Handing on the shirt

For the sixth-graders, this was not only the end of a national tournament. It was the most precious memory of their elementary football lives. Thank you for writing a chapter that is entirely your own; thank you for handing the courage, unity and persistence this team is known for down to every younger teammate.

Now the shirt, the responsibility and the honor pass formally to the fifth-graders. What is handed on is not only skill, but an attitude that does not step back from a challenge. What is taken up is not only a place on the pitch, but a promise to the team.

The next generation of Dacheng footballers will build on the ground their seniors laid — and spread their wings on the pitches ahead.

中文 · Chinese

114 學年度小學生足球賽學校組全國賽圓滿落幕。

恭喜本校足球隊，代表彰化縣征戰全國，在激烈競爭中展現堅強實力與團隊精神，最終榮獲全國第八名！這份榮耀，屬於每一位全力以赴的球員、辛勤指導的教練團、一路陪伴的師長與家長，以及所有支持大成鵟鷹足球隊的夥伴。

一路走來，孩子們用汗水灌溉夢想，用堅持迎接挑戰。每一次奔跑、每一次攔截、每一次奮力射門，都展現了永不放棄的精神，也讓大家看見大成國小深耕足球教育的豐碩成果。

對六年級的小將們而言，這不僅是全國賽的終點，更是國小足球生涯最珍貴的回憶。謝謝你們用努力與熱情，寫下屬於自己的精彩篇章；謝謝你們以身作則，將大成鵟鷹勇敢、團結、堅持到底的精神，傳承給每一位學弟妹。

如今，你們將球衣、責任與榮耀，正式交棒給五年級的學弟妹。傳承的不只是球技，更是面對挑戰永不退縮的態度；接下的不只是位置，更是一份對團隊的承諾與使命。

相信新一代的大成鵟鷹，將承襲學長們努力奠定的基礎，持續精進、勇敢追夢，在未來的賽場上展翅高飛，繼續為大成國小締造更多感動與榮耀。

賽事資訊：114 學年度小學生足球賽全國決賽於 2026 年 6 月 27 日至 7 月 5 日在臺中朝馬足球場舉行，全國 773 隊報名、198 隊進入決賽（資料來源：運動部全民運動署）。
