「只有在愛的溫度裡，教育才能成功，才能真正影響人的內心。」

Welcome to Dacheng. Before arriving here in August 2025, I spent more than thirty years in Changhua and Taoyuan classrooms and principals' offices — at Minsheng and Pinghe Elementary in Changhua City, and as principal of Luoyang Elementary. Across every one of those schools, I have held to one belief: that every child carries unlimited possibility, and that our task is to open it.

歡迎來到大成。在 2025 年 8 月來到這裡之前，我在彰化與桃園的教室與校長室裡待了三十多年——彰化市民生國小、平和國小，以及螺陽國小校長任內。在每一所學校，我都守著同一個信念：每個孩子都有無限的可能，而我們的責任，就是把它打開。

I believe education is a calling in which one life moves another. That is why we treat innovation, mother tongue and English, reading, the arts, technology and whole-person growth not as extras, but as the everyday substance of a Dacheng childhood. The six principles below shape how we run this school, day to day.

我相信，教育是一種以生命感動生命的志業。這也是為什麼，在大成，創新、母語與英語、閱讀、藝文、科技與全人發展，從來不是附加品，而是孩子每一天學習的本體。以下六大方向，是大成每天怎麼運作的底色。

II.

Six Guiding Principles

辦學理念六大方向
PRINCIPLE 01
🌟
Innovation for Every Child
創新教育作為 · 搭建每個學生的成功經驗
Connecting the 12-year curriculum to children's real lives — individualised, adaptive, multi-pathway learning that builds confidence and the competencies they will need for the future.
PRINCIPLE 02
🤲
Patience, Sincerity, Professionalism
勇於承擔 · 以耐心誠意專業邁向卓越
Everything we do begins with the child's learning. In conflict, we listen — to every side — with patience and sincerity, and work toward concrete, effective solutions.
PRINCIPLE 03
💗
One Life Moves Another
人本關懷 · 教育是生命感動生命的志業
"Only in the warmth of love can education succeed." When teacher and child are truly together, the power of care shines through every interaction — and shapes the heart.
PRINCIPLE 04
🌈
Unlimited Possibility
相信教育有無限可能 · 開啟多元智能
"Everyone is born with a gift to give." We respect difference, teach without exclusion, adapt to each learner, and open up the many intelligences inside every child.
PRINCIPLE 05
🧠
Five-Sense, Five-Power Learning
五官六感的五力學習 · 內化為核心能力
In an age where knowledge is everywhere, what children need is the ability to use it — knowledge management, creativity, and competencies that come alive in real situations.
PRINCIPLE 06
🪜
A Mentor to Teachers & Students
校長應成為師生的貴人 · 讓師生敢於追夢
Teachers are the soul of a school. By nurturing their professional growth — collaborative, situated, outcome-focused — we build a shared vision and lead the school from the ordinary toward the excellent.
