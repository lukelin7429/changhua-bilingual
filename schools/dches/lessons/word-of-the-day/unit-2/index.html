Unit 02 · 4 Words

Read & Write

閱讀語文 · Word of the Day

Reading, writing, and words about words. 閱讀、寫作，以及關於語言的字

← Back to Lessons
Word 01
read (v.)
閱讀
I love to read story books.
我喜歡閱讀故事書。
We read together in the library.
我們在圖書館一起閱讀。
Word 02
write (v.)
寫作
I write in my notebook every day.
我每天在筆記本上寫字。
Let's write a short story together.
我們一起寫一篇短篇故事吧。
Word 03
idiom (n.)
俚語
"It's raining cats and dogs" is an idiom.
「下著貓和狗」是一句俚語。
An idiom has a special, hidden meaning.
俚語有一個特別的、隱藏的意思。
Word 04
check out (v.)
借書
I want to check out this book.
我想借這本書。
You can check out three books at a time.
你一次可以借三本書。
