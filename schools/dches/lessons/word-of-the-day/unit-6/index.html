Unit 06 · 10 Words

Out in the World

校外活動 · Word of the Day

Trips, the zoo, and the world beyond the school gate. 校外教學、動物園，與校門外的世界

← Back to Lessons
Word 01
graduation trip (n.)
畢業旅行
Our graduation trip was so much fun.
我們的畢業旅行非常好玩。
On the graduation trip we made memories.
畢業旅行讓我們留下回憶。
Word 02
outdoor education (n.)
戶外教育
Outdoor education takes us out of the classroom.
戶外教育帶我們走出教室。
We learn by doing in outdoor education.
戶外教育讓我們動手學習。
Word 03
outdoor (adj.)
戶外的
We love outdoor activities.
我們喜歡戶外活動。
The outdoor air is fresh and cool.
戶外的空氣清新又涼爽。
Word 04
tour guide (n.)
導遊
The tour guide showed us the way.
導遊為我們帶路。
A tour guide tells us about each place.
導遊向我們介紹每個地方。
Word 05
Earth Day (n.)
地球日
On Earth Day we care for our planet.
地球日這天我們愛護地球。
We plant trees on Earth Day.
地球日我們種樹。
Word 06
HoPlay (n.)
夜市爭霸
HoPlay is like a fun night-market game.
HoPlay 像是好玩的夜市爭霸遊戲。
We learned English while playing HoPlay.
我們在玩 HoPlay 的時候學英語。
Word 07
Wanpi World Safari Zoo (n.)
頑皮世界野生動物園
We saw many animals at Wanpi World Safari Zoo.
我們在頑皮世界看到很多動物。
Wanpi World Safari Zoo is a wild-animal park.
頑皮世界是一座野生動物園。
Word 08
role play activity (n.)
角色扮演活動
In the role play activity I was a shopkeeper.
角色扮演活動中我當店長。
A role play activity helps us speak English.
角色扮演活動幫我們開口說英語。
Word 09
thank you card (n.)
感謝卡
I made a thank you card for my teacher.
我為老師做了一張感謝卡。
A thank you card says "thank you" from the heart.
感謝卡用真心說一聲謝謝。
Word 10
jump rope (v.)
跳繩
We jump rope on the playground.
我們在操場上跳繩。
Can you jump rope twenty times?
你可以跳繩二十下嗎？
