Unit 01 · 9 Words

Around Campus

校園生活 · Word of the Day

The everyday words of a Dacheng school day. 大成校園裡的日常英語

← Back to Lessons
Word 01
restroom (n.)
洗手間
May I go to the restroom, please?
我可以去洗手間嗎？
The restroom is at the end of the hall.
洗手間在走廊的盡頭。
Word 02
upstairs / downstairs (adv.)
上下樓梯
Our classroom is upstairs.
我們的教室在樓上。
Walk slowly when you go downstairs.
下樓梯的時候要慢慢走。
Word 03
wash your hands (v.)
洗手
Please wash your hands before lunch.
午餐前請先洗手。
We wash our hands with soap and water.
我們用肥皂和水洗手。
Word 04
mouthwash (n.)
漱口水
We use mouthwash after lunch.
我們午餐後使用漱口水。
Mouthwash helps keep our teeth clean.
漱口水幫助保持牙齒乾淨。
Word 05
nap (n.)
小睡
We take a short nap after lunch.
午餐後我們小睡一下。
A nap helps me feel awake again.
小睡一下讓我又有精神。
Word 06
serve (v.)
打菜
Today I serve the rice to my class.
今天輪到我幫班上打飯。
The helpers serve lunch to everyone.
值日生幫大家打菜。
Word 07
kindergarten (n.)
幼稚園
My little sister is in kindergarten.
我妹妹在念幼稚園。
The kindergarten is next to our school.
幼兒園就在我們學校旁邊。
Word 08
body (n.)
身體
I can move my whole body.
我可以動我整個身體。
Exercise keeps my body strong.
運動讓我的身體強壯。
Word 09
traffic safety (n.)
交通安全
We learn about traffic safety at school.
我們在學校學交通安全。
Traffic safety keeps us safe on the road.
交通安全讓我們在路上平安。
