Unit 05 · 8 Words

Food & Nature

食農自然 · Word of the Day

From the garden to the estuary — food and the living world. 從菜園到河口——食物與大自然

← Back to Lessons
Word 01
grow vegetables (v.)
種植蔬菜
We grow vegetables in the school garden.
我們在學校菜園種植蔬菜。
It is fun to grow vegetables ourselves.
自己種菜很有趣。
Word 02
agriculture class (n.)
食農課程
In agriculture class we learn about food.
食農課我們認識食物。
Agriculture class takes us into the garden.
食農課帶我們到菜園。
Word 03
plant (v.)
種植
Let's plant a seed in the soil.
我們把種子種進泥土裡吧。
We plant flowers in the spring.
春天我們種花。
Word 04
insect (n.)
昆蟲
A butterfly is an insect.
蝴蝶是一種昆蟲。
We found an insect on the leaf.
我們在葉子上發現一隻昆蟲。
Word 05
orange (n.)
柳橙
An orange is sweet and juicy.
柳橙又甜又多汁。
I eat an orange after lunch.
午餐後我吃一顆柳橙。
Word 06
hard-boiled egg (n.)
水煮蛋
I had a hard-boiled egg for breakfast.
早餐我吃了一顆水煮蛋。
A hard-boiled egg is cooked in hot water.
水煮蛋是在熱水裡煮熟的。
Word 07
kimbap (n.)
紫菜飯卷
Kimbap is rice rolled in seaweed.
紫菜飯卷是用海苔包起來的飯。
We tried making kimbap in class.
我們在課堂上試做紫菜飯卷。
Word 08
estuary ecosystem (n.)
河口生態
Many birds live in the estuary ecosystem.
許多鳥類住在河口生態裡。
We study the estuary ecosystem near the sea.
我們研究海邊的河口生態。
