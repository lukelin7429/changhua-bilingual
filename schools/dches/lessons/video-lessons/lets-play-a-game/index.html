Let's Play a Game

我們來玩桌遊 · 下課十分鐘

When class ends, the desks turn into game boards — marbles, blocks, and beads come out. 下課鐘一響，書桌就變成遊戲桌——彈珠、積木和串珠通通拿出來

← Back to Video Lessons
Recess at Dacheng — classmates pull out board games and play together at their desks until the bell rings. Watch first, then learn the key words below.
大成的下課時間——同學拿出桌遊，在座位上一起玩到上課鐘響。先看影片，再學下面的關鍵單字。
A.

Words to Know

遊戲時間的單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
game (n.)
遊戲
We choose a game from the shelf.
我們從架子上挑一個遊戲。
play (v.)
玩
We play at our desks after class.
下課後我們在座位上玩。
board game (n.)
桌遊
Chinese checkers is my favorite board game.
跳棋是我最喜歡的桌遊。
marble (n.)
彈珠
I move one marble across the star board.
我把一顆彈珠移過星形棋盤。
stack (v.)
疊
We stack the blocks until the tower wobbles.
我們把積木疊到塔開始搖晃為止。
friend (n.)
朋友
I always play this game with my friend.
我總是跟我的朋友玩這個遊戲。
turn (n.)
輪到
I wait for my turn and then move a piece.
我等輪到我，然後移動一顆棋子。
block (n.)
積木
One more block and the tower falls.
再一塊積木，塔就要倒了。
win (v.)
贏
It is fun to win, and it is fine to lose.
贏很開心，輸了也沒關係。
B.

Say It

用句子說說看 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
Let's play a game!
我們來玩遊戲吧！
It is my turn now.
現在輪到我了。
I move a marble across the board.
我把一顆彈珠移過棋盤。
Let's stack the blocks higher.
我們把積木疊得更高。
I play with my friend every day.
我每天都跟朋友一起玩。
You win! Good game.
你贏了！好棒的一局。
