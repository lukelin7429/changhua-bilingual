A Charity Sale

愛心義賣 · 走廊上的攤位

A table of snacks, a long line of classmates, and a small way to help someone far away.一張擺滿點心的桌子、一列排得長長的同學，還有一份送給遠方的心意

The Genesis Social Welfare Foundation brings its yellow banner to our breezeway, and the whole school lines up. Students buy a snack, and the money goes to people who need care. Watch first, then learn the key words below.
創世基金會把黃色的旗幟帶到我們的走廊，全校排隊來買。同學買下一份點心，這些錢會幫助需要照顧的人。先看影片，再學下面的關鍵單字。
A.

Words to Know

愛心義賣的單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
sale (n.)
義賣
Our school holds a charity sale in the breezeway.
我們學校在走廊辦了一場愛心義賣。
buy (v.)
買
I buy one bag of cookies at the table.
我在攤位上買了一包餅乾。
money (n.)
錢
I bring a little money from home.
我從家裡帶了一點錢來。
wait (v.)
等候
We wait in a long line.
我們排在長長的隊伍裡等。
volunteer (n.)
志工
The volunteers wear bright yellow vests.
志工們穿著鮮黃色的背心。
snack (n.)
點心
Every snack on the table is for a good cause.
桌上每一份點心都是為了做好事。
donate (v.)
捐助
We donate our pocket money to help.
我們把零用錢捐出來幫忙。
help (v.)
幫助
One small coin can help someone far away.
一個小銅板就能幫助遠方的人。
kind (adj.)
善良的
It feels good to be kind.
當個善良的人，感覺很好。
B.

Say It

用句子說說看 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
Today is our charity sale.
今天是我們的愛心義賣。
The volunteers wear yellow vests.
志工們穿著黃色背心。
We wait in line to buy a snack.
我們排隊買點心。
How much money do you need?
你需要多少錢？
The money goes to people who need help.
這些錢會給需要幫助的人。
It feels good to be kind.
當個善良的人，感覺很好。
