Making a Paper Eagle

鷹揚八卦 · 剪一隻紙老鷹

Colored paper, scissors, and glue become a flock of eagles — the birds that cross Bagua Mountain each autumn. 色紙、剪刀和膠水，變成一群老鷹——每年秋天飛過八卦山的那種鳥

← Back to Video Lessons
Dacheng students cut and glue colored paper into eagles, then hold their finished birds up high. Watch first, then learn the key words below.
大成的同學用色紙剪剪貼貼做出老鷹，完成後把自己的鳥高高舉起。先看影片，再學下面的關鍵單字。
A.

Words to Know

紙藝創作的單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
paper (n.)
紙
We pick red, pink, and purple paper for our eagle.
我們挑了紅色、粉紅色和紫色的紙做老鷹。
scissors (n.)
剪刀
I hold the scissors carefully and cut slowly.
我小心拿著剪刀，慢慢地剪。
glue (n.)
膠水
A little glue keeps every piece in place.
一點點膠水就能讓每個部分固定住。
cut (v.)
剪
I cut along the line to make a wing.
我沿著線剪，做出一片翅膀。
eagle (n.)
老鷹
Every autumn, we watch the eagle fly over Bagua Mountain.
每年秋天，我們看著老鷹飛過八卦山。
wings (n.)
翅膀
I spread the paper wings wide before I hold it up.
我把紙做的翅膀張開，再把它舉起來。
beak (n.)
鳥嘴
I glue an orange beak onto my eagle's face.
我把橘色的鳥嘴黏在老鷹臉上。
make (v.)
做
We make an eagle out of paper and glue.
我們用紙和膠水做出一隻老鷹。
color (n.)
顏色
I choose a bright color so my eagle stands out.
我選了鮮豔的顏色，讓我的老鷹更醒目。
B.

Say It

用句子說說看 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
We make a paper eagle today.
今天我們做一隻紙老鷹。
Please pass me the scissors.
請把剪刀遞給我。
I cut along the line.
我沿著線剪。
A little glue holds the wings.
一點膠水就能固定翅膀。
Look at the big wings!
看這對大翅膀！
Eagles fly over Bagua Mountain.
老鷹飛過八卦山。
