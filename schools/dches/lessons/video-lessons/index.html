New video lessons are on the way

We're filming and editing themed lessons from real moments at Dacheng — campus life, festivals, science, the arts and more. Each one will live on its own page with a video, key words, useful sentences, and a short quiz. 我們正在剪輯一系列以大成真實校園拍攝的主題影片課——校園生活、節慶、科學、藝術等等。每一課都會獨立成一頁，含影片、關鍵單字、實用句型，以及一個小考。
