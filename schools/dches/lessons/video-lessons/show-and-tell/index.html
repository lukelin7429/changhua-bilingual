Show and Tell

上台分享 · 帶一樣寶貝來介紹

Dacheng students bring a favorite toy to school, hold it up, and tell the class all about it. 大成的小朋友帶一樣最喜歡的玩具到學校，舉起來，向全班介紹它

← Back to Video Lessons
A first-grade show and tell at Dacheng — one classmate at a time stands up front, holds up a favorite toy, and tells everyone about it. Watch first, then learn the key words below.
大成一年級的上台分享——同學一個一個站到台前，舉起自己最喜歡的玩具，向大家介紹。先看影片，再學下面的關鍵單字。
A.

Words to Know

上台分享的單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
show (v.)
展示
I show my toy to the whole class.
我把玩具展示給全班看。
hold up (v.)
舉起來
I hold up my toy with both hands.
我用雙手把玩具舉起來。
tell (v.)
說
I tell my classmates why I like this toy.
我告訴同學我為什麼喜歡這個玩具。
listen (v.)
聽
We listen quietly when a friend is speaking.
同學說話的時候我們安靜地聽。
share (v.)
分享
We share our toys and our stories.
我們分享玩具，也分享故事。
bring (v.)
帶來
I bring my toy to school in my bag.
我把玩具放在書包裡帶到學校。
toy (n.)
玩具
My toy is small enough to hold in one hand.
我的玩具小到一隻手就拿得住。
favorite (adj.)
最喜歡的
I chose my favorite toy for show and tell.
我挑了我最喜歡的玩具來上台分享。
clap (v.)
拍手
We clap after every classmate finishes.
每位同學講完，我們都拍手。
B.

Say It

用句子說說看 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
It is show and tell time.
上台分享的時間到了。
This is my favorite toy.
這是我最喜歡的玩具。
I hold it up so you can see.
我把它舉起來讓你們看得到。
Let me tell you about it.
讓我來介紹它。
Please listen carefully.
請仔細聽。
Thank you! Let's clap.
謝謝你！我們拍手。
