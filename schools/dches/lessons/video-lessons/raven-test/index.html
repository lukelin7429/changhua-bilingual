The Raven's Test

瑞文氏測驗 · 大成五年級

Patterns, shapes, and quiet thinking — watch Dacheng fifth graders tackle the Raven's Progressive Matrices, in simple English. 圖樣、形狀、靜靜思考——看大成五年級同學做瑞文氏測驗，用最簡單的英語一起學

← Back to Video Lessons
Dacheng fifth graders take the Raven's Test — a pattern puzzle that shows how we think and reason. Watch first, then learn the key words below.
大成五年級同學進行瑞文氏測驗——一套透過圖形規律了解思考能力的測驗。先看影片，再學下面的關鍵單字。
A.

Words to Know

瑞文氏測驗會用到的單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
test (n.)
測驗
We take a test today.
我們今天進行測驗。
pattern (n.)
圖樣；規律
Find the rule in the pattern.
找出圖樣中的規律。
shape (n.)
形狀
What shape comes next?
下一個是什麼形狀？
answer (n.)
答案
Pick the right answer.
選出正確的答案。
think (v.)
思考
Think carefully before you choose.
選擇之前仔細思考。
look (v.)
看；觀察
Look at the picture carefully.
仔細看這張圖。
choose (v.)
選擇
Choose the one that fits.
選出最合適的那個。
quiet (adj.)
安靜的
The classroom is very quiet.
教室裡非常安靜。
right (adj.)
正確的
Is this the right answer?
這是正確答案嗎？
check (v.)
檢查
Check your answers when you finish.
做完後檢查你的答案。
B.

選出正確的答案。

教室裡非常安靜。
Check your answers when you finish.
做完後檢查你的答案。
