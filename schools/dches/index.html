I.

About Dacheng

關於大成 · 彰化市區雙語大校
A large, modern elementary school in the heart of Changhua City — bilingual, digital, and warm at heart.
坐落彰化市中心的現代化大校。我們把雙語、科技與一顆溫暖的心，放進每一個孩子的學習日常。

Welcome to Dacheng Elementary School. As a school, we are young — we opened our doors in 2004 and are now in our 23rd school year. But our roots reach far deeper: our affiliated kindergarten traces its history all the way back to 1917, and celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2017. New school, old heart.

歡迎來到大成國小。論校齡，我們很年輕——2004 年（民國 93 年）創校，如今邁入第 23 個學年；但若論根，我們其實很深：附設幼兒園的歷史可一路追溯到 1917 年，並在 2017 年舉辦了創園百年慶。新學校，老靈魂。

Today Dacheng is one of Changhua City's larger elementary schools, on Ziqiang Road, with specialist rooms for science, computing, art, baking and sensory learning. Our crest says it best: two children holding up a single heart — education as one life moving another.

如今的大成，是彰化市規模較大的國小之一，坐落自強路上，設有自然、電腦、美勞、烘焙與感覺統合等專科教室。我們的校徽說得最好：兩個孩子，一起捧起一顆心——教育，是一個生命感動另一個生命。

Dacheng was built for a new century, and set out to be several things at once: a happy and safe hall of learning; a stronghold of education that passes on what lasts; a green, living environment the neighborhood is proud of; and a cultural landmark handed down through generations.

大成國小設校後於 93 學年度開始招生，是一所邁向新世紀優質的好學校：是快樂安全的學校殿堂；是永續傳承的教育堡壘；是社區引以為傲的有機環境；是世代相傳的文化標誌。

We hold to breakthrough and invention — so that children wear bright smiles as they learn, teachers shine with wisdom as they teach, parents look on with boundless hope, and the children of Dacheng see their dreams come true.

大成國小秉持突破與創新，讓孩子在學習中洋溢著璀璨的笑容，讓師長在教學中綻放出智慧的光芒，讓家長在眼神中展現出無限的希望，讓大成國小的孩子們美夢成真。

"Education is a calling in which one life moves another."
「教育是一種以生命感動生命的志業。」
—— 校長 張鴻章 Hung-Chang Chang
II.

Our Vision

學校願景 · 大成要把孩子帶到哪裡
1
Strengthen character education, and awaken every kind of intelligence.
強化品德教育、啟發多元智慧。
2
Respect many cultures, and shape a campus where everyone is treated kindly.
尊重多元文化、型塑友善校園。
3
Build digital know-how, and widen the view all the way out to the world.
建構數位資訊、拓展全球視野。
4
Raise healthy, lively, inventive and excellent children for the 21st century.
培養健康、活潑、創新、卓越的二十一世紀未來兒童。
5
Grow a school that is varied, finely made and forward-looking — modern and technological, rooted in its own place, and humane.
營造多元、精緻、前瞻、現代的科技化本土化人文學校。
III.

The Dacheng Learner

課程地圖與學生圖像 · 我們希望孩子長成的樣子

Every subject at Dacheng is mapped back to four pictures of the child we hope to send on to junior high — four words that tell teachers what a lesson is really for.

大成的課程地圖，最後都回到四幅「學生圖像」——四個詞，說明一堂課究竟是為了什麼。

Portrait 01
Innovation & Technology
創新科技
Learn under your own steam, put technology to work, and be the kind of person who can solve a problem. 自主學習、運用科技、具備解決問題的能力。
Portrait 02
Health & Self-Reliance
健康自主
Eat well, move often, and grow up confident and happy. 均衡飲食、規律運動、自信快樂。
Portrait 03
Wisdom & Humanities
智慧人文
Care about the place you come from, know your own culture — and from there, grow the respect for other cultures that international understanding asks for. 在地關懷、文化認同，並涵養尊重多元文化、國際理解的素養。
Portrait 04
Character & Kindness
品德友善
Be thankful, put yourself in others' shoes, make room for difference, and share what you have. 感恩惜福、關懷同理、尊重包容、互助分享。
IV.

Bilingual & Beyond

雙語與更多 · 大成的學習風景

Dacheng runs its own Dacheng Bilingual Network for English learning, alongside a tablet for every student, AI and digital literacy, a farm-to-table agriculture class, and a school-wide reading culture. Four corners of a Dacheng day:

大成自建「大成雙語網」推動英語學習，再加上生生用平板、AI 與數位素養、食農教育，以及全校閱讀風氣。以下是大成日常的四個角落：

FEATURE 01
🌐
Dacheng Bilingual Network
大成雙語網 · 把英語織進每一天
The school's own bilingual learning hub — Word of the Day videos, classroom English, and themed lessons. Our students have already taken the stage at the county English Reader's Theater contest.
FEATURE 02
💻
A Tablet for Every Student
生生用平板 · 科技走進課堂
Through the national "a-tablet-for-every-student" program, devices and Chromebooks are woven into everyday lessons — from Wordwall games to space science.
FEATURE 03
🌱
Farm-to-Table Class
食農教育 · 從種菜到餐桌
Children grow vegetables, learn where food comes from, and bring agriculture into the classroom — hands in the soil, English on the tongue.
FEATURE 04
📖
A Reading School
大成愛閱人 · 用閱讀打底
A campus-wide reading platform and library culture — because every bilingual learner is first, and always, a reader.
V.

Around Campus

校園巡禮 · 大成的一草一木

A city school with room to breathe: a full running track, an old banyan, mosaic art on the lamp posts and even on the washbasins — and a sky-bridge that says our name in English.

市區裡難得寬敞的校園：一圈完整的跑道、一棵老榕樹、燈柱和洗手台都是馬賽克藝術——還有一座用英文寫著校名的空橋。
