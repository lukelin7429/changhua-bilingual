I.

About Dacheng

關於大成 · 彰化市區雙語大校
A large, modern elementary school in the heart of Changhua City — bilingual, digital, and warm at heart.
坐落彰化市中心的現代化大校。我們把雙語、科技與一顆溫暖的心，放進每一個孩子的學習日常。

Welcome to Dacheng Elementary School. As a school, we are young — we opened our doors in 2004 and are now in our 22nd year. But our roots reach far deeper: our affiliated kindergarten traces its history all the way back to 1917, and celebrated its 100th anniversary in 2017. New school, old heart.

歡迎來到大成國小。論校齡，我們很年輕——2004 年（民國 93 年）創校，如今邁入第 22 個年頭；但若論根，我們其實很深：附設幼兒園的歷史可一路追溯到 1917 年，並在 2017 年舉辦了創園百年慶。新學校，老靈魂。

Today Dacheng is one of Changhua City's larger elementary schools, on Ziqiang Road, with specialist rooms for science, computing, art, baking and sensory learning. Our crest says it best: two children holding up a single heart — education as one life moving another.

如今的大成，是彰化市規模較大的國小之一，坐落自強路上，設有自然、電腦、美勞、烘焙與感覺統合等專科教室。我們的校徽說得最好：兩個孩子，一起捧起一顆心——教育，是一個生命感動另一個生命。

"Education is a calling in which one life moves another."
「教育是一種以生命感動生命的志業。」
—— 校長 張鴻章 Hung-Chang Chang
II.

Bilingual & Beyond

雙語與更多 · 大成的學習風景

Dacheng runs its own Dacheng Bilingual Network for English learning, alongside a tablet for every student, AI and digital literacy, a farm-to-table agriculture class, and a school-wide reading culture. Four corners of a Dacheng day:

大成自建「大成雙語網」推動英語學習，再加上生生用平板、AI 與數位素養、食農教育，以及全校閱讀風氣。以下是大成日常的四個角落：

FEATURE 01
🌐
Dacheng Bilingual Network
大成雙語網 · 把英語織進每一天
The school's own bilingual learning hub — Word of the Day videos, classroom English, and themed lessons. Our students have already taken the stage at the county English Reader's Theater contest.
FEATURE 02
💻
A Tablet for Every Student
生生用平板 · 科技走進課堂
Through the national "a-tablet-for-every-student" program, devices and Chromebooks are woven into everyday lessons — from Wordwall games to space science.
FEATURE 03
🌱
Farm-to-Table Class
食農教育 · 從種菜到餐桌
Children grow vegetables, learn where food comes from, and bring agriculture into the classroom — hands in the soil, English on the tongue.
FEATURE 04
📖
A Reading School
大成愛閱人 · 用閱讀打底
A campus-wide reading platform and library culture — because every bilingual learner is first, and always, a reader.
