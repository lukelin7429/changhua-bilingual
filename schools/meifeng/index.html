I.

Welcome

歡迎來到美豐國小

A small school at the southwest tip of Changhua — where every child is known by name.

位於彰化縣最西南端、大城鄉的偏鄉小校——每一位孩子都被叫得出名字。

Mei Feng Elementary sits in Sanfeng Village, Dacheng Township — on the coastal plain between the Zhuoshui River and the Taiwan Strait. Founded in April 1952 as a branch of Sigang Public School and established as an independent school in 1964, we serve the two villages of Fengmei and Sanfeng — the villages whose names give us our own: Mei + Feng.

美豐國小位於彰化縣大城鄉三豐村，地處濁水溪與台灣海峽之間的沿海平原。1952 年 4 月創校為「西港國民學校美豐分校」，1964 年獨立設校，學區涵蓋「豐美村」與「三豐村」——「美豐」校名即取自兩村之名。

Designated by the Ministry of Education as a remote-area school in 1984, Mei Feng today serves 32 children across 6 classes — one class per grade, with 13 teachers who know every child personally. Small in number, full in care.

美豐自 1984 年起經教育部核定為偏遠地區學校，目前全校 6 班共 32 位學生（一至六年級各一班），13 位教師——人數雖少，照顧卻深。
