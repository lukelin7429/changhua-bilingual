I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

"Cultivating Virtue, Cherishing Art — Turning lives forward."

「美德豐藝，開創未來，翻轉人生。」

I hope Mei Feng becomes a warm ecological school — a place where every child can grow into someone with virtue (美德) and many gifts (豐藝), open new doors, and turn the course of their own life forward.

我期許美豐國小成為一所溫暖的生態小學——希望每一位孩子都能成為「美德豐藝」之人，開創未來、翻轉人生。

Our school-based curriculum, F.A.M.I.L.Y., weaves six threads through every grade: Farming, Art, Manners, International (English & IT), Literature, and Young energy. Small school, full curriculum — every child finds their own stage.

我們的校本課程是「F.A.M.I.L.Y.」六字頭：農事體驗、藝術涵養、禮貌品格、英文與資訊、閱讀與詩詞創作、青春活力——校雖小、志卻豐。
