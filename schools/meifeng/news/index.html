Milestone · 60 週年校慶

Mei Feng Turns 60美豐 60 週年校慶

Apr 20, 2024 · 民國 113 年 4 月 20 日

Sixty years since the school stood up on its own. Alumni, families, and county leaders gathered for a day of taiko drumming, dance, and craft games — celebrated under the theme "A Cycle Complete, Climbing Higher." The day also marked the opening of a freshly renovated campus.

獨立設校滿 60 年。校友、家長與縣府代表齊聚一堂，在「甲子美豐・再創高峰」主題下，以太鼓、熱舞與童年闖關遊戲歡慶——這天同時也是校園全面整修啟用的日子。

Champion · 2024 鼓藝錦標賽

Drum-Arts Team — Anping Cup Champions龍獅鼓藝隊 · 安平聖母盃團體組第一名

2024 · 民國 113 年

Our lion-dance drum team took 1st place in the team category at the 2024 Anping Holy Mother Cup Lion-Dance & Drum-Arts Championship — bringing championship rhythm home to a school of just 32 children.

美豐龍獅鼓藝隊榮獲 2024 安平開台聖母盃龍獅鼓藝錦標賽打擊鼓藝團體組第一名——把這所只有 32 人的小校的鼓聲，打到全國的舞台。

Campus · 教育環境啟用

A Renovated Campus Reopens教育環境優化啟用

Apr 20, 2024 · 民國 113 年

Together with the 60th-anniversary celebrations, Mei Feng unveiled a renovated front courtyard, a "gu-ting-pen" (granary-themed) central corridor, refreshed multi-purpose rooms, new touch-screen displays, a reading book-cart, and an upgraded lunch kitchen — funded by NT$2.98M from the MOE and NT$2.14M from the county.

配合 60 週年校慶，美豐同步啟用：前庭整修、古亭畚意象中廊、多功能教室、數位觸控電視、閱讀書車、午餐廚房——教育部補助 298 萬、縣府挹注 214 萬。

Reading · PaGamO 種子

A PaGamO Reading-Literacy Seed SchoolPaGamO 閱讀素養種子學校

2025 · 113 學年度

Selected as a Reading Literacy Seed School by the PaGamO platform — a recognition shared by only a handful of schools in Changhua. In a 32-student campus, every reader counts.

榮獲 113 學年度 PaGamO 閱讀素養種子學校——全彰化縣僅少數學校入選。在 32 人的小校裡，每一位讀者都不被忽略。

Health · 健康促進

A Health-Promoting Supportive Environment健康促進學校 · 支持環境特色獎

2025 · 114 年度

The Ministry of Health and Welfare awarded Mei Feng the Supportive Environment Feature Prize at the 2025 Health-Promoting Schools competition — recognition of how the campus turns daily routines into healthy habits.

衛福部國健署 114 年健康促進學校特色獎勵競賽榮獲支持環境特色獎——肯定美豐把日常作息變成健康習慣的努力。

Arts · 藝文深耕

Two Years of Excellent in Arts & Humanities藝術與人文教學深耕 · 連續優等

2024 · 2025 · 113–114 學年

Changhua County rated Mei Feng's arts-and-humanities curriculum Excellent two years running — across general arts visits and a separate visual-arts deep-cultivation review.

連續兩年獲彰化縣藝術與人文教學深耕訪視「優等」（113、114 學年度），並獲視覺藝術期末訪視「優等」——「豐藝」不是口號。
