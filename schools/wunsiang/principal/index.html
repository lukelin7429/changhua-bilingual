Principal Yang Su-ling

I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Yang Su-ling holds a doctorate and a master's degree from National Changhua University of Education, and a bachelor's degree from National Pingtung Teachers College. Before leading Wunsiang Elementary, she built deep administrative experience across nearly every office in school leadership.

楊素綾校長擁有國立彰化師範大學博士、碩士學位，以及國立屏東師範學院學士學位。在接掌文祥國小之前，她已歷練過學校行政幾乎每一個崗位。

She has served as Director of Academic Affairs, Director of Student Affairs, Director of General Affairs, and Director of Guidance, as well as Cashier Section Chief and Teaching & Registration Section Chief — and, at the county level, as a Curriculum Supervisor for the Changhua County Government's Department of Education.

她曾擔任教務主任、教導主任、總務主任、輔導主任，也曾任出納組長、教學註冊組長；在縣府層級，她更擔任過彰化縣政府教育處課程督學。

From classroom registration to county-level curriculum supervision — every seat at the table, before the principal's seat.
從教學註冊組長到縣府課程督學——在坐上校長這個位置之前，她幾乎坐遍了每一個位置。
