I.

Welcome

歡迎來到文祥國小

A Huatan Township campus where six curriculum visions grow one child at a time.

花壇鄉的一所小學，用六大課程願景，陪伴孩子一步步成長。

Wunsiang Elementary School sits in Liucuo Village, Huatan Township, in Changhua County. Around its school crest — an open book beneath a temple roofline — the school has built a whole-child curriculum framework spanning School Vision, Student Portrait, and Curriculum Map.

文祥國小位在彰化縣花壇鄉劉厝村。以校徽——屋簷下的一本翻開的書——為核心，學校發展出「學校課程願景、學生圖像、課程地圖」三大軸線的完整課程架構。
