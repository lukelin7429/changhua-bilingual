II.

Word of the Day

每日一字 · 文祥自製雙語影片

Six words, six short videos — straight from Wunsiang's own campus life.

六個單字，六支短片——都來自文祥自己的校園生活。

Every word below comes with a short bilingual video, plus two example sentences in English and Chinese. Watch, listen, and read along — then try saying it out loud.

下面每個單字都搭配一支雙語短片，以及兩句中英對照例句。看影片、聽發音、跟著讀——然後大聲說出來。

Word 01
mobile library (n. phr.)
行動圖書館
A mobile library brings books to places that don't have one nearby.
行動圖書館把書本帶到附近沒有圖書館的地方。
A mobile library visits Wunsiang so every child can borrow a book.
行動圖書館會到文祥來，讓每個孩子都能借書。
Word 02
tea culture (n.)
茶文化
Tea culture is the tradition of growing, brewing, and sharing tea.
茶文化是種植、沖泡與分享茶的傳統。
Learning about tea culture helps Wunsiang students understand local traditions.
認識茶文化，能幫助文祥的孩子了解在地傳統。
Word 03
bilingual (adj.)
雙語的
Being bilingual means speaking two languages well.
雙語的意思是能流利使用兩種語言。
Wunsiang is proud to be a bilingual elementary school.
文祥國小以身為一所雙語小學為榮。
Word 04
concert (n.)
音樂會
A concert is a performance of music for an audience.
音樂會是表演給觀眾聽的音樂演出。
Wunsiang students perform in a concert every year.
文祥的學生每年都會參加音樂會演出。
Word 05
activity (n.)
活動
An activity is something people do for learning or fun.
活動是人們用來學習或娛樂的事情。
Every activity at Wunsiang helps children grow.
文祥的每一項活動都幫助孩子成長。
Word 06
tea ceremony (n. phr.)
茶道
A tea ceremony is a traditional way of preparing and serving tea.
茶道是一種傳統的泡茶與奉茶方式。
Students at Wunsiang learned the steps of a tea ceremony.
文祥的學生學習了茶道的步驟。
"Six words, one habit — English starts to feel like something you can carry in your pocket."
「六支短片，一個習慣——英文開始像隨手帶在口袋裡的東西。」
III.

English Through Pictures

看圖學英文

A photo from campus life, a story in two languages.

一張校園生活的照片，一段雙語的故事。

A special bookmobile has come to our school! It's like a library on wheels, full of exciting books. Students are sitting outside, enjoying stories and learning new things. It's so much fun when the mobile library visits, bringing adventures right to us!

一輛特別的書車來到我們學校了！它就像一間裝滿了精彩書籍的移動圖書館。學生們正坐在外面，開心地閱讀故事並學習新事物。當這輛移動圖書館來訪時，真是太有趣了，它把冒險直接帶到我們身邊！

Swimming is lots of fun! In these students' swimming lessons, students are learning how to float using a kickboard. Swimming also helps you stay cool on a hot day and makes your body strong. It's really good to learn how to swim to be safe in the water.

游泳很有趣！在這些學生的游泳課中，他們正在學習如何使用浮板來漂浮。游泳也能讓你炎熱時保持涼爽，並讓你的身體變得強壯。學會游泳對於在水邊保持安全真的很好喔。

This is a picture from an awards ceremony at Wunsiang Elementary School. The school principal is in the middle, posing for a photo with the students after presenting their awards. All the children are holding their special awards, which they earned for their good work in school. It's a happy day where everyone celebrates the students' achievements.

這是一張文祥國民小學頒獎典禮的照片。學校校長站在中間，在頒發獎項給學生們之後，正與他們合影留念。所有的孩子都拿著他們特別的獎項，這些獎項是他們在學校表現良好而獲得的。這是一個大家慶祝學生們成就的快樂日子。

Look at these kids at the World GreenMech Contest! It's a fun game where students build amazing machines and cool inventions. They work together in teams, using their smart ideas to solve puzzles and make things move. In this picture, you can see them busy putting parts together, like real engineers! It's a great way to learn how to build and work with friends.

看看這些參加世界機關王大賽的孩子們！這是一個有趣的比賽，學生們可以在裡面建造很棒的機器和很酷的發明。他們分組合作，運用聰明的點子來解決難題，讓東西動起來。在這張照片裡，你可以看到他們正忙著把零件組裝起來，就像真正的工程師一樣！這是一個學習如何建造東西和與朋友合作的好方法。

Look at these students picking fresh, red tomatoes! They are inside a greenhouse, where the plants can grow big and strong. It looks like a fun day learning where our food comes from. They are getting some healthy snacks right from the vine!

看這些學生正在採摘新鮮的紅番茄！他們在一間溫室裡，植物可以在那裡長得又大又壯。這看起來是個學習食物從哪裡來的好玩日子。他們正在從藤蔓上直接採摘健康的點心呢！

IV.

Classroom English

課室英語 · 給老師的口袋句庫（影片版）
The phrases teachers say between the lessons — taught on video.
老師在課堂裡每天會用上的英文句子——這次有人示範給你看。

A practical video library of the everyday English a Taiwanese teacher actually uses: greetings and roll call, pre-class set-up, instructions, praise and correction, classroom management, lesson activities, assigning homework, wrapping up, and exam directions. Each video isolates one situation so the phrases stick on first hearing.

這個影片教材庫涵蓋臺灣老師每天會用到的課堂英語——問候點名、課前準備、講解、回饋讚美糾正、教室管理、課堂活動、分派作業、課堂收尾、考試指令。每支影片只處理一個情境，第一次聽就能記住。

V.

Bilingual Announcements

英語廣播 · 校園的英語之聲
The morning intercom in two languages — written and recorded by the four school offices.
早上的校園廣播，雙語版本——由教務、學務、總務、輔導四個處室共同寫稿、共同上鏡。

A bilingual announcement series, organized by the four school offices: Academic Affairs (教務處), Student Affairs (學務處), General Affairs (總務處), and Counseling (輔導室). Each office introduces what it does in both languages — turning the daily public-address routine into real-world bilingual exposure.

這是一個雙語校園廣播系列，依四個處室分組：教務處、學務處、總務處、輔導室，各組親自介紹自己的工作，雙語對照——把每天的廣播時間，變成全校學生在走廊上就能接收的英語浸潤。
