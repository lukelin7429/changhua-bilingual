Huatan's Virtuous Fragrance

花壇德馨．品德教育向下扎根

Through the joint efforts of Huatan's "De Sin" character education program and seven township elementary and junior high schools, children learn everyday habits — following the rules, showing courtesy, and expressing gratitude — letting good character take root, one small moment at a time.

Character education isn't a single lesson. It's practiced every day.

On July 21, principals, directors, teachers, and parents from each school accompanied this group of young "Character Ambassadors" on a learning journey to the Feniu Ranch and the Sanyi Wood Sculpture Museum. At Feniu Ranch, children fed ducks by hand and tried milking a cow, learning to respect life through real interaction with animals. At the Sanyi Wood Sculpture Museum, they walked among finely carved wooden artworks, broadening their artistic horizons and their appreciation for craft and culture.

Character education was never meant to be a single day's lesson — it is something practiced in everyday life. We hope these children carry what they've learned back to campus and back home, continuing to treat others with kindness and act with integrity, becoming role models for their classmates and a source of warmth for society.

Congratulations to every selected Character Ambassador, and heartfelt thanks to Huatan's "De Sin" program and the seven participating schools for their dedication — letting seeds of goodness keep sprouting and growing, and guiding children toward an even better future.

中文原文 · Chinese

在花壇德馨與鄉內七所國中小共同努力下，孩子們從日常生活中學習守規矩、懂禮貌、知感恩，讓良好的品行一點一滴扎根，成為成長最珍貴的養分。

7月21日，各校校長、主任、老師及家長們，陪伴著這群品德小尖兵，一同前往飛牛牧場與三義木雕博物館展開學習之旅。

在飛牛牧場，孩子們親手餵鴨、體驗擠牛奶，從與動物互動、尊重生命的過程中，體會生命教育的真諦；走進三義木雕博物館，一件件細膩的木雕作品，不僅開拓了藝術視野，也培養了美感與欣賞文化的能力。

品德教育從來不是一天的課程，而是每天生活中的實踐。期待孩子們把這份學習帶回校園、帶回家庭，持續以良善待人、以誠信處事，成為同學學習的榜樣，也成為社會溫暖的力量。

恭喜所有獲選的品德小尖兵，也感謝花壇德馨及鄉內七所學校攜手耕耘，讓善的種子持續萌芽、茁壯，陪伴孩子走向更美好的未來。
