From Black Sheep to Dancing Bones

從黑綿羊到會跳舞的骨頭：一到五年級的韻文與繪本英語課

At Wunsiang Elementary, English class often begins with a song. This semester, from first grade all the way up to fifth, students have been learning English through nursery rhymes and picture books — singing, coloring, moving and reading along.

Grade 1 · 一年級

Baa Baa Black Sheep唱韻文、畫綿羊

First graders built their lessons around the classic nursery rhyme Baa Baa Black Sheep. Singing along with an animated version on the classroom screen, they listened for the rhyming sounds at the ends of the lines, like wool and full.

Then out came the markers. Each child colored a black sheep and its three bags of wool, then stepped up to the big screen to show off the finished picture.

Grade 2 · 二年級

Itsy Bitsy Spider小蜘蛛爬呀爬

Second graders sang Itsy Bitsy Spider, the song about a little spider who climbs up the water spout, gets washed out by the rain, and climbs right back up once the sun comes out. Along the way they spotted the rhymes: spout and out, rain and again.

Learning through rhymes, their teacher explains, lets children pick up English in a natural way, and helps them grow to enjoy it.

Grade 3 · 三年級

A Fun Day開心的一天

Third graders spent their lesson with A Fun Day, following the story on the big screen with their books open, and joining in with actions and big smiles from their seats.

Grade 5 · 五年級

Dem Bones會跳舞的骨頭

Fifth graders read Dem Bones, a picture book from the BookFlix collection in the reading zone of Cool English, the Ministry of Education's online English platform. The book is all about the human skeleton, walking through the body bone by bone, from the toes up to the head.

The audio book has a strong beat, and rhythm and rhyme help students remember what they read. So the lesson was built around "dancing bones": students got up and moved with the song, then worked on skeleton worksheets.

The lesson also connected with the school library's themed book display on ghost festivals around the world, and with the health center's health-promotion activities. Students came away knowing how the skeleton fits together and how to keep their bodies healthy — the kind of learning that reaches beyond a single subject, just as Taiwan's competency-based curriculum hopes.

When English has a beat, children want to join in.

From a black sheep's three bags of wool to a skeleton that dances, every grade took a different step. The idea behind them is the same: when English comes with rhythm and rhyme, children can't wait to sing along.

中文版 · Chinese

在文祥國小，英語課常常是從一首歌開始的。這學期，從一年級到五年級，孩子們透過英文韻文（Nursery Rhymes）和繪本學英文：一邊唱、一邊畫、一邊動，也一邊讀。

一年級｜Baa Baa Black Sheep

一年級以唱誦韻文〈Baa Baa Black Sheep〉為課程主軸。孩子們跟著大螢幕上的動畫一句句唱，留意句尾押韻的聲音，像是 wool 和 full。接著拿起彩色筆，為自己的黑綿羊和三袋羊毛著色，再一個個站到大螢幕前，展示完成的作品。

二年級｜Itsy Bitsy Spider

二年級唱的是〈Itsy Bitsy Spider〉：小蜘蛛爬上排水管，被大雨沖了下來；太陽出來曬乾雨水，小蜘蛛又再爬上去。孩子們在歌裡找到押韻的字，像是 spout 和 out、rain 和 again。以自然的方式習得英文，也從中培養孩子對英語文的學習興趣。

三年級｜A Fun Day

三年級的孩子這堂課讀的是 A Fun Day。大家翻開書本，跟著大螢幕上的故事走，還在座位上跟著做動作，笑得好開心。

五年級｜Dem Bones

五年級以 Cool English 閱讀專區 BookFlix 裡的繪本 Dem Bones 作為讀本。繪本主軸是介紹人體骨架（skeleton），從腳趾一路介紹到頭部。有聲書的好處是學生可以透過節奏、韻律加強閱讀記憶，所以「會跳舞的骨頭」成為這堂課的設計重點：孩子們站起來跟著歌曲動一動，再完成骨架學習單。

這堂課也和學校圖書館的「世界鬼節」主題書展，以及健康中心的健康促進活動結合。孩子們除了認識骨架構造，也學到健康知識，正符合現今素養導向教學的精神。

從黑綿羊的三袋羊毛，到會跳舞的骨架，每個年級走的是不一樣的一步，道理卻是一樣的：英文有了節奏和韻律，孩子就迫不及待想跟著唱。

Learning Corner · 英語學習角

Sing, Listen & Rhyme

唱一唱、聽一聽、找押韻。點 🔊 聽發音。

1Sing Along
跟著唱：點每一句的 🔊，或按 ▶ 整首播放。紅字是押韻的字。
Baa Baa Black Sheep一年級的韻文
Baa, baa, black sheep, have you any wool?
咩咩黑綿羊，你有羊毛嗎？
Yes sir, yes sir, three bags full.
有的，先生，有的，先生，滿滿三大袋。
One for my master, one for my dame,
一袋給我的主人，一袋給女主人，
And one for the little boy who lives down the lane.
還有一袋，給住在巷子那頭的小男孩。

押韻：wool / full，dame / lane

Itsy Bitsy Spider二年級的韻文
Itsy bitsy spider climbed up the water spout.
小小的蜘蛛，爬上了排水管。
Down came the rain and washed the spider out.
大雨落下來，把蜘蛛沖了出去。
Out came the sun and dried up all the rain.
太陽出來了，把雨水全都曬乾。
And the itsy bitsy spider climbed up the spout again.
小小的蜘蛛，又再爬上了排水管。

押韻：spout / out，rain / again

2Words to Learn
單字學習：點左邊的 🔊 聽單字，點句子旁的 🔊 聽例句。
sheep/ʃiːp/綿羊

The black sheep has three bags of wool.黑綿羊有三袋羊毛。

wool/wʊl/羊毛

A sweater made of wool keeps us warm.羊毛做的毛衣能讓我們保暖。

spider/ˈspaɪdər/蜘蛛

The little spider climbed up the water spout.小蜘蛛爬上了排水管。

rain/reɪn/雨

The rain washed the spider out.大雨把蜘蛛沖了出去。

sun/sʌn/太陽

The sun came out and dried up the rain.太陽出來，把雨水曬乾了。

rhyme/raɪm/押韻；韻文

"Spout" and "out" rhyme because they end with the same sound.spout 和 out 結尾的聲音一樣，所以押韻。

bone/boʊn/骨頭

Drinking milk helps keep your bones strong.喝牛奶能幫助骨頭保持強壯。

skeleton/ˈskelətən/骨架；骨骼

Our skeleton holds up the whole body.骨架撐起我們整個身體。

4Quick Quiz
五題小考：選出最合適的答案，點 🔊 可聽題目。
Q1.The black sheep has three bags of ______.
黑綿羊有三袋______。
wool 羊毛 ✓ — "Yes sir, yes sir, three bags full!" 滿滿三大袋羊毛。
Q2.The itsy bitsy spider climbed up the water ______.
小蜘蛛爬上了______。
water spout 排水管 ✓ — The spider climbed up the water spout. 蜘蛛爬上了排水管。
Q3.Which word rhymes with "rain"?
哪一個字和 rain 押韻？
again ✓ — "Rain" and "again" end with the same sound. rain 和 again 結尾的聲音一樣。
Q4.Out came the ______ and dried up all the rain.
______出來了，把雨水全都曬乾。
sun 太陽 ✓ — The sun dried up all the rain. 太陽把雨水都曬乾了。
Q5.All the bones in our body make up our ______.
我們身體裡所有的骨頭，組成了我們的______。
skeleton 骨架 ✓ — The fifth graders' Dem Bones lesson was all about the skeleton. 五年級的 Dem Bones 課就是在認識骨架。
