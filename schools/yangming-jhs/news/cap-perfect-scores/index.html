II.

A Proud Campus

全校師生同賀

The News from the Exam Hall 放榜的好消息

Every May, Taiwan's ninth-graders sit the country's most important junior-high examination — the test that helps decide which senior high school they will attend next. This spring, Yangming's Class of 2026 took the exam, and when the grades were released, three students had earned a perfect score: Liu Wan-Yu (Class 905), Tu Jie-Fei (Class 909), and Zhuo Pin-Tong (Class 912).

每年五月，全台灣的國三學生都要參加最重要的一場升學考試——這場考試會幫助決定他們下一階段就讀哪一所高中。今年春天，陽明國中 115 級的同學參加了會考，成績放榜時，三位同學拿下滿級分：三年 5 班劉宛俞、三年 9 班涂倢菲、三年 12 班卓品彤。

"A perfect score is not luck. It is three years of quiet, steady work finally showing its shape."

「滿級分不是運氣。那是三年安安靜靜、一步一步的努力，終於長出了形狀。」

At Yangming, the whole campus shares in the celebration — classmates, homeroom teachers, subject teachers, and the families who supported these students through three years of junior high. A perfect score belongs to one student's name, but it grows out of a whole community.

在陽明，這份喜悅是全校一起分享的——同班同學、導師、各科任課老師，還有陪伴這些孩子走過三年國中的家人。滿級分寫的是一個同學的名字，但它是從一整個群體裡長出來的。

📘 English Corner: How to Say 「會考」 會考的英文怎麼說？
國中教育會考（「會考」）
Comprehensive Assessment Program for Junior High School Students
這是教育部採用的正式英文名稱，常縮寫成 CAP。日常對話裡，老師也常直接說 "the junior-high entrance exam"（國中升學考試）或 "the senior-high entrance exam"， 因為它的功能就是銜接高中。每年 5 月舉行，由國三（9th-grade）學生應考。
滿級分
a perfect score / top grade in every subject
會考每一科分成三個等級：精熟 (Proficient)、基礎 (Basic)、 待加強 (Needs Improvement)，其中「精熟」最高還可再細分為 A++、A+、A。 「滿級分」就是每一科都拿到最高等級，英文可說 "a perfect score" 或 "straight top grades / all A++"。
放榜 · 報喜
the results are out · share the good news
「放榜」可以說 "the results are released / the results are out"； 像海報上「捷報」這種報喜，英文常用 "great news!" 或 "we're proud to announce…"。

To Wan-Yu, Jie-Fei, and Pin-Tong — and to every member of the Class of 2026 who studied hard and gave their best — Yangming Junior High says congratulations. The next chapter, senior high school, is waiting.

給宛俞、倢菲、品彤——也給每一位努力讀書、全力以赴的 115 級同學——陽明國中，恭喜你們。下一個篇章，高中，正在前方等著。
