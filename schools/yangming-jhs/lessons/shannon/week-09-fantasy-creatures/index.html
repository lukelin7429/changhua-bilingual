Fantasy Creatures
奇幻生物 · 真實 vs 幻想世界
I.

Reality vs Fantasy

真實 vs 幻想 · 兩個世界的區別
🌍 Reality
  • It exists in the real world.
  • You can detect it with your 5 senses.
  • You can: see it, hear it, touch it, smell it, and taste it.
✨ Fantasy
  • It does not exist in "real life."
  • You cannot detect it with all 5 senses.
  • You can only: see it and hear it — virtual, on screen, video game.

III.

Key Vocabulary

5 個核心詞彙 · 描述奇幻生物
Fantasy
Magic, monsters, and things that are not real. 幻想世界
Creature
A living thing that is not a human. Can be an animal or a monster. 生物
Magical
Having special powers that follow magic rules, not science rules. 魔法的
Qualities
The things that describe how something looks or acts. 特質
Unique
Being the only one of its kind. Special and different from everyone else. 獨特的
