A Message To My Future Self
給未來高中自己的一封信 · want to / hope to / plan to
I.

The Bottle Letter Scenario

漂流瓶情境 · 寫給高中自己的訊息
⛵🍾
Right now you are sailing through junior high school, soon you land in high school.
Imagine you are already in high school, at the beach with your friends.
You see a bottle floating on the water — you open it to find a piece of paper.
It is a message you wrote to yourself when you were still in junior high.
你現在正在「航行」國中生活，即將登陸高中彼岸。
想像你已經升高中了，某天和朋友在海邊，看到一個漂流瓶——打開後發現一張紙條。
那是你還在國中時寫給自己的信。今天，我們就來寫這封信！
II.

What Do I Want to Accomplish?

三大目標類別 · 我在高中想做什麼？
📚
My Studies
學業目標
  • Pass the entrance exam (通過會考/入學考試)"I want to pass the exam and go to a great high school."
  • Study my favorite subject (讀我最喜歡的科目)"I hope to spend more hours studying history."
  • Get excellent grades (取得好成績)"My goal is to get excellent grades in math."
  • Learn a new language (學習新語言)"I plan to learn a new language, like Japanese."
🎭
My Hobbies & Friendships
社團與生活
  • Join a cool school club (加入超酷的社團)Dance 熱舞社 / Animation 漫研社 / Sports 運動社
  • Make new best friends (交到新的摯友)
  • Go to high school festivals (參加學校園遊會/校慶)
🌱
My Personal Growth
個人成長
  • Try a brand new hobby (嘗試全新的興趣)
  • Stay healthy and sleep enough (保持健康與睡眠充足)
  • Be brave when things are hard (面對困難時保持勇敢)
  • Balance study and play (平衡學習與娛樂)
III.

Grammar Anchors · The Intentions

三個意圖句型 · 用英文寫下你的願望
I want to…
渴望 · A deep personal desire
Use for things you really, deeply wish for.
"I want to join the basketball club."
I hope to…
心願 · A future wish or possibility
Use for wishes that may or may not happen — hopeful tone.
"I hope to get a great desk mate."
I plan to…
計畫 · A steady, organized action step
Use for concrete steps you have already decided to take.
"I plan to study science every day."
IV.

Write Your Letter

用三個句型寫你的漂流瓶信
Dear Future "High School" Me,
Right now, I am sailing through junior high, but soon I will arrive at your shores.
When I get to high school, I want to                                (action goal / study dream)
I also hope to                                        (club / friendship wish)
To make sure I reach my goals, I plan to                    (daily habit / action step)
Signed by my hand in 2026,
                               (Your Name)
