I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

My guiding line as an educator is a short one: education is nothing but love and example. Everything else — the plans, the policies, the paperwork — is built on top of those two words. Young people learn what care looks like by being cared for, and they learn what a professional looks like by watching one at work.

我的辦學理念很短：「教育無他、愛與榜樣」。其餘的一切——計畫、制度、公文——都建立在這兩個詞之上。孩子從被關心中學會什麼是關心，也從看著大人認真工作中，學會什麼是專業。

A vocational school carries a particular responsibility. Our students come to us to learn a trade they can live by, so what we owe them is not only a qualification but the habits that go with it: to serve, to keep to a professional standard, to govern themselves, and to keep looking outward at a world that is larger than one workshop.

技職學校有它特別的責任。學生來到這裡，是要學一門足以安身立命的本事；因此我們該給的不只是一紙資格，還有隨之而來的習慣——服務的態度、專業的標準、自律的能力，以及望向比一間工場更大的世界的眼光。

I came to ChungShih on 1 August 2024. It is my privilege to work with our teachers, our families and the industries around us to keep this school a place where every student can find the thing they are good at — and be properly good at it.

我於 2024 年 8 月 1 日（民國 113 年 8 月 1 日）到任崇實。能與本校教師、家長以及周邊產業一起努力，讓每個學生都能找到自己擅長的事、並且真正把它做好，是我的榮幸。

IV.

Love and Example

愛與榜樣

Nothing a school teaches carries further than what the adults in it do.

學校教的所有東西裡，走得最遠的，是大人們怎麼做。

Love and example are not soft words in a vocational school — they are the two things a workshop runs on. A student learns safety because a teacher never cuts the corner. A student learns care because somebody took the trouble to show them again. That is why Principal Chen asks administration to serve, teachers to hold a professional standard, and guidance to build self-discipline: each of the nine principles is, in the end, a way of setting an example worth copying.

在技職學校裡，「愛」與「榜樣」不是軟性的詞——實習工場正是靠這兩件事運轉的。學生學會安全，是因為老師從不便宜行事；學生學會細心，是因為有人願意再示範一次。這就是為什麼陳校長要求行政強調服務、教師重視專業、輔導培養自律：九項理念到最後，都是在立下一個值得被模仿的榜樣。

A campus with room to breathe
一座留白的校園

Wide lawns, long arcades and old trees — a campus built so that students have somewhere to sit down between one workshop and the next.

開闊的草地、長長的拱廊與老樹——這樣的校園，讓學生在兩堂實習課之間，有地方可以坐下來。
