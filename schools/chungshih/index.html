I.

Welcome to ChungShih

歡迎來到國立員林崇實高工

A place where students learn to become experts in what they love.

崇實高工，是一個讓學生學著成為自己所愛之事的專家的地方。

Since 1949 we have grown from a small school into a big family that teaches the job skills that matter — technology, engineering, design and more. We are in Yuanlin City, at the heart of southern Changhua, and everything we do is about helping you get ready for a bright future.

自 1949 年（民國 38 年）以來，我們從一所小學校成長為一個大家庭，教授技術、工程、設計等重要職業技能。學校位於南彰化的中心員林市，我們所做的一切，都是為了幫助你為光明的未來做好準備。

Our teachers are dedicated to teaching. Every department has its own character, and technical subjects and academic subjects are valued alike — which is why ChungShih is the first choice for students from southern Changhua who want a vocational education in industry.

本校教師投入教學，科科有特色，技能學科都重視，是南彰化學子就讀工科技職學校的首選。

School is more than study. We believe in being good at your books and good at life, so alongside the workshops you will find sport, music and clubs — and a library that is a comfortable place to discover new books and new ideas.

學校不只是讀書的地方。我們相信學業與生活同樣重要，所以在實習工場之外，還有運動、音樂與社團——以及一座舒適的圖書館，讓你在這裡發現新的書與新的想法。

We are more than a school. We are a starting point for your dreams.
我們不僅是一所學校；我們是你夢想的起點。

III.

Creativity · Superiority · Vision · Sublime

學校願景：創新・卓越・宏觀・崇高

Our vision is spelled out of our own initials — C, S, V, S.

學校願景，就藏在學校英文校名的縮寫裡——C、S、V、S。

創新 Creativity · 卓越 Superiority
宏觀 Vision · 崇高 Sublime
Four words taken from the initials of ChungShih Vocational School — and four things we ask of every student who walks through our gate. 四個字，取自學校英文校名的縮寫，也是我們對每一位走進校門的學生的期許。
C · 創新
Creativity
希望學生能「創新」專業能力——把學到的技術，用在還沒有人想過的地方。
S · 卓越
Superiority
追求「卓越」精進——把一件事做到好，然後再做得更好。
V · 宏觀
Vision
「宏觀」國際視野——技術沒有國界，眼光也不該有。
S · 崇高
Sublime
「崇高」人文關懷——有本事，也要有溫度。

We want our students to innovate in their professional skills, to strive for excellence, to broaden their international perspective, and to carry a humane concern for the people around them. Those four words are not a slogan on a wall; they are the standard we hold a ChungShih graduate to.

我們希望學生能「創新」專業能力、追求「卓越」精進、「宏觀」國際視野、「崇高」人文關懷。這四個詞不是牆上的口號，而是我們對一位崇實畢業生的標準。

我們提供的學習

Six departments, one sports class, and an evening division — each with its own character.

六個科、一個體育班，還有進修部——科科有特色。

Electrical Engineering
電機科——從配電盤接線到電路檢修，把電這件看不見的事，練成看得見的手藝。
Tech & Engineering · 技術與工程
Electrical & Air Conditioning Engineering
電機空調科——冰箱、冷氣、冷凍空調系統的電路判讀與故障排除。

Computer Science
資訊科——電腦系統、網路架設、程式設計與資訊安全，打好數位時代的技術底子。

Chemical Engineering
化工科——在實驗室裡認識化學品與反應，也認識「安全第一」這四個字的重量。

Interior Space Design
室內空間設計科——把一個空間，從量測、製圖，一路想到人住進去以後。

Furniture Design
家具設計科——從木料、機具到成品，自己設計的椅子，自己做得出來。
Sports · 體育班

體育班——跆拳道、羽球與田徑，讓會跑、會跳、能拚的孩子有專屬的舞台。

Universities & Industry
學校行政與各科課程發展常與科技大學及產業合作，滾動式調整課程規劃、改善教學環境，讓學生學到的是業界真正需要的。

Workshops, not just classrooms
教室之外，還有實習工場

A vocational school is measured by what its students can actually make. Our departments run on machines, benches, panels and drawing boards — and on the habit of doing a thing again until it is right.

一所技職學校，要看學生真正做得出什麼。各科的日常是機具、工作檯、配電盤與製圖桌——以及一次又一次重做到對為止的習慣。

V.

A Winning Spirit

勝利的精神：技藝與技能競賽

We are known for doing well in competition — because our students and teachers put the work in.

我們以在比賽中表現出色而聞名——因為學生和老師都下了功夫。

An 80% award rate in national skills competitions
全國技藝及技能競賽，得獎率高達八成

ChungShih has taken part in the national team technical-creativity training and competition for 18 consecutive years. Every year our teams have reached the final, and every year they have finished in the top three. 本校參加全國技藝及技能競賽得獎率高達八成；連續 18 年參加全國技術型高級中等學校學生團隊技術創造力培訓與競賽，年年皆打入決賽，獲得前三名。

Competition is not the point of a vocational education, but it is a good test of one. Preparing for a national skills competition asks a student to work to a tolerance, to a deadline, and to a standard set by somebody else — which is exactly what a workplace will ask of them later.

比賽不是技職教育的目的，卻是很好的檢驗。準備一場全國技能競賽，學生必須做到規定的精度、時限與別人訂下的標準——那正是日後職場對他們的要求。

VI.

A Place to Read and Grow

閱讀與成長的地方

Our library is a cozy spot to discover new books and new ideas.

圖書館是一個舒適的地方，可以在這裡發現新書與新想法。

We take the humanities and the reading habit seriously. The National Yuanlin Chung Shih Library was built as a place students actually want to sit in — long shared tables, a timber ceiling, shelves sorted by subject, and a wall where the librarian's picks and the newest arrivals face outward so a cover can catch your eye. New books arrive every semester, and the reading schemes that go with them are there to turn a one-off visit into a habit.

我們重視人文及閱讀涵養。崇實圖書館被設計成一個學生真的願意坐下來的地方——長條共讀桌、木質天花板、依類別排列的書架，還有一整面把館長推薦與到館新書「面朝外」陳列的牆，讓書封先抓住你的眼睛。每學期引進新書，搭配鼓勵閱讀的機制，把一次造訪變成一個習慣。

VII.

More Than Just School

不只是一所學校

Sport, scouting and a marching band — room to be more than your timetable.

體育活動、童軍與管樂隊——在課表之外，還有很多可能。

We encourage students to develop in more than one direction. The school puts real weight behind sports activities, scouting and a marching band, which take our students out into the community and to events beyond the school gate. A full running track, ball courts and a covered court mean the weather rarely stops anything.

我們鼓勵學生多元發展，重視體育活動、童軍及設立管樂隊，使學生學習參與社區及校外活動。完整的 PU 跑道、球場與風雨球場，讓天氣很少能擋住任何一件事。

Rain or shine, there is somewhere to play
晴天雨天，都有地方可以動起來

Taekwondo, badminton and track and field are part of the school's sporting life, and the covered court keeps basketball and volleyball going through a Changhua summer.

跆拳道、羽球與田徑是校園運動生活的一部分；有了風雨球場，籃球與排球在彰化的夏天也照常進行。
