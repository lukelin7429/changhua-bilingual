A Fair for Our 75th Year

75 週年校慶愛心感恩園遊會
⌄
←Back to Campus News回到校園新聞

Booths, food, games and club performances — the whole school turned out to mark 75 years.

攤位、美食、遊戲與社團表演——全校一起慶祝創校 75 週年。

ChungShih Vocational High School celebrated its 75th anniversary with an exciting school fair. Students set up a variety of booths offering delicious food and engaging activities. Visitors enjoyed exploring each unique booth and participating in the fun.

In addition to the booths, there were numerous performances by different school clubs, showcasing their talents and entertaining the crowd. Everyone had a fantastic time, creating wonderful memories and celebrating the school’s milestone in a joyous and vibrant atmosphere.

崇實高工慶祝 75 週年校慶，舉辦了一場精彩的園遊會。學生們擺設了各種攤位，提供美味的食物和有趣的活動。來賓們享受著探索每個獨特的攤位，並參與其中的樂趣。

除了攤位之外，還有許多不同社團的精彩表演，展示他們的才藝並娛樂觀眾。大家都玩得很盡興，創造了美好的回憶，在歡樂和充滿活力的氛圍中慶祝學校的重要里程碑。
