II.

CCC Chinese School × ChungShih

紐約首府中文學校 x 崇實高工 · 2025 年 7 月

Three days in July 2025, spent with students from Albany, New York.

2025 年 7 月的三天，與來自美國紐約州 Albany 的同學一起度過。

Students from CCC Chinese School — the Chinese school of the Chinese Community Center of the Capital District of New York — came to ChungShih for a three-day exchange. They met our students, trained in taekwondo, worked their way through game stations, walked the Yuanlin fruit and vegetable market, and spent a morning in the workshop as little carpenters.

來自紐約首府中文學校（CCC Chinese School，隸屬紐約首府華人社區中心）的同學到崇實高工進行為期三天的交流。他們與本校學生相見歡、體驗跆拳道、闖關遊戲，走進員林果菜市場，也在實習工場當了一個上午的小木工。

The eight films below were made during those three days, in the order they happened.
