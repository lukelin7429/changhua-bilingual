Exchange Program with De La Salle

兩校文化交流活動：崇實高工 × 菲律賓德拉薩大學高中部
⌄
←Back to Campus News回到校園新聞

A signing ceremony, performances, calligraphy, DIY classes, and trips out to Asia University and the Wufeng Lin Family Garden — eight short films from the exchange.

簽訂儀式、表演、書法、DIY 課程，還有亞洲大學與霧峰林家花園之行——八支短片記錄這趟交流。

ChungShih welcomed students and teachers from the senior high school division of De La Salle University in the Philippines for a cultural exchange program between the two schools. The visit opened with a signing ceremony and ended with a closing ceremony.

In between, the two schools shared performances, worked side by side in hands-on DIY classes, and tried Chinese calligraphy together. The visitors also went beyond the campus — out into the local community, to Asia University, and to the Wufeng Lin Family Garden.

The eight short films below were filmed during the exchange.

崇實高工接待來自菲律賓德拉薩大學高中部的師生，展開兩校文化交流活動。行程從簽訂儀式揭開序幕，最後以閉幕儀式畫下句點。

期間兩校學生同台表演、一起動手做 DIY 課程，也一同體驗書法。來訪師生還走出校園，深入在地社區，並參訪亞洲大學與霧峰林家花園。
