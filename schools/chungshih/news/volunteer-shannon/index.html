Talking About Our Majors — in English

用英語介紹自己的科系：與國際志工 Shannon 的交流
⌄
←Back to Campus News回到校園新聞

Students from every department presented their major and their career goals in English — to a volunteer in Los Angeles.

各科學生用英語介紹自己的科系與未來志向，對象是遠在洛杉磯的國際志工。

On June 28, 2024, students from different departments at the school introduced their majors and future career goals to international volunteer Shannon. They practiced delivering their presentations in English, leading to a pleasant and engaging discussion.

Shannon, who resides in Los Angeles, California, was thrilled to connect with the students from Taiwan and learn about their areas of expertise.

2024 年 6 月 28 日，來自不同科系的學生向國際志工 Shannon 介紹了他們的主修科目和未來的職業目標。他們用英文進行報告，雙方交流愉快且充滿互動。

住在美國加州洛杉磯的 Shannon，對能夠認識這些台灣學生並了解他們的專業領域感到非常高興。
