Weight Training

重量訓練

Weight training is very important for high school track team members. It helps them get stronger and more powerful, which is needed for different track and field events. In the picture, you can see a student doing a squat with a heavy barbell while a teammate watches. This exercise builds the leg muscles, which are important for running fast, jumping high, and other quick movements.

重量訓練對高中田徑隊的成員非常重要。它能幫助他們變得更強壯、更有力量，這對參加不同的田徑項目來說是必需的。在圖片中，你可以看到一名學生正在做深蹲，舉著一個很重的槓鈴，而一位隊友在旁邊觀察。這個訓練可以鍛鍊腿部肌肉，這對於跑得更快、跳得更高以及其他快速動作非常重要。

Off the Blocks

起跑技術

The track team members are working on their start techniques, mastering the use of starting blocks, fine-tuning their posture, and sharpening their focus so they can explode off the line when the gun goes off. This training is key to boosting their performance in races, enhancing both their reaction time and explosive power. For sprinters, a strong start is critical, as it can make all the difference in how the race plays out.

田徑隊的隊員正在練習起跑技術，熟練使用起跑器，調整姿勢，並集中注意力，以便在槍聲響起時能夠迅速衝出起跑線。這種訓練對於提升他們的比賽表現至關重要，不僅能提高反應速度，還能增強爆發力。對於短跑選手來說，一個強而有力的起跑至關重要，因為它可以對整場比賽的結果產生關鍵影響。

Our track and field team trains every afternoon.我們的田徑隊每天下午訓練。

weight training/weɪt ˈtreɪ.nɪŋ/重量訓練

Weight training helps runners get stronger and more powerful.重量訓練幫助跑者變得更強壯、更有力量。

squat/skwɑːt/深蹲

He does a squat with a heavy barbell.他舉著很重的槓鈴做深蹲。

barbell/ˈbɑːr.bel/槓鈴

A teammate watches while he lifts the barbell.隊友在一旁看著他舉起槓鈴。

starting block/ˈstɑːr.t̬ɪŋ blɑːk/起跑器

Sprinters push off the starting blocks when the gun goes off.槍聲響起時，短跑選手從起跑器上蹬出去。

sprinter/ˈsprɪn.t̬ɚ/短跑選手

For a sprinter, a strong start is critical.對短跑選手來說，強而有力的起跑至關重要。

They fine-tune their posture before the race.比賽前他們仔細調整自己的姿勢。

This training enhances reaction time and explosive power.這種訓練能提高反應速度並增強爆發力。

Idioms & Expressions · 慣用語

What people really say · 母語人士這樣說

hit the ground running
一開始就全力衝刺

To start something quickly and with full energy, right from the first moment.

“She hit the ground running in her first season with the team.”她加入隊上的第一個球季就火力全開。

go the extra mile
多付出一分努力

To do more than what is expected or required of you.

“Our coach asks us to go the extra mile in every practice.”教練要求我們每一次練習都多拚一點。

neck and neck
不相上下

So close in a race or contest that no one can tell who is ahead.

“The two runners were neck and neck at the last bend.”兩名選手在最後一個彎道上不相上下。

a level playing field
公平的競爭環境

A situation in which everyone has the same fair chance.

“Clear rules give every athlete a level playing field.”清楚的規則讓每位選手都站在公平的起跑點上。
