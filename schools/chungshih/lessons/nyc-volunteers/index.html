A Journey of Cultural Exchange and Lasting Friendships

一趟文化交流與長久友誼的旅程

Led by Dr. Liu Dan, nine enthusiastic high school students from New York, USA, arrived in Taiwan in early July 2025 for a meaningful English teaching volunteer trip. Their visit aimed to foster cultural exchange and provide invaluable English language support to Taiwanese students. Although initial plans were slightly altered due to Typhoon Danas, the students embraced the opportunity to spend more quality time with their welcoming host families, deepening their cultural immersion. ChungShih Vocational High School was honored to host these bright young minds, facilitating a vibrant exchange of knowledge and culture.

在劉丹醫師的帶領下，九位來自美國紐約的熱情高中生於 2025 年 7 月初抵達台灣，展開一場意義非凡的英語教學志工之旅。他們此行的目的是促進文化交流，並為台灣學生提供寶貴的英語學習支援。儘管原訂計畫因丹娜絲颱風而略有調整，學生們仍把握機會與熱情好客的寄宿家庭共度更多美好時光，加深了他們的文化沉浸體驗。崇實高工很榮幸能接待這些才華洋溢的年輕學子，共同促進知識與文化的交流。

Hands-On Learning & Fun

親身學習與樂趣

In the afternoons, our American guests immersed themselves in the vibrant life at ChungShih Vocational High School. They engaged directly with our high school students, participating in a variety of hands-on and interactive activities designed to share Taiwanese culture and build camaraderie.

Taekwondo experience. Students actively participated in Taekwondo sessions, learning basic stances and movements, experiencing the discipline and power of this traditional martial art.

Woodworking workshop. Creativity flowed in the woodworking practical courses, where American and Taiwanese students collaborated on projects, showcasing their ingenuity and craftsmanship.

Taste of Taiwan. A highlight was undoubtedly the opportunity to savor authentic Taiwanese delicacies, a delicious way to explore the local culinary heritage and bond over shared meals.

下午時段，我們的美國貴賓們沉浸在崇實高工充滿活力的校園生活中。他們直接與我們的高中生互動，參與各種動手實作和互動式活動，旨在分享台灣文化並建立友誼。

跆拳道體驗。學生們積極參與跆拳道課程，學習基本姿勢和動作，體驗這項傳統武術的紀律與力量。

木工實作坊。創意在木工實作課程中流動，美籍和台籍學生們合作完成專案，展現他們的巧思與工藝。

台灣美食饗宴。無疑，品嚐道地台灣美食是此行的一大亮點，這是探索當地飲食文化並透過共享餐點建立情誼的美味方式。

Exploring the Heart of Changhua

探索彰化之心

Beyond the school grounds, the students also ventured out to gain a deeper understanding of the local community. They visited the bustling Yuanlin Fruit and Vegetable Market, experiencing the vibrant pulse of daily Taiwanese life. Their exploration extended to nearby historical sites and charming unique cafes, allowing them to truly feel the distinctive character of Changhua.

除了校園之外，學生們也走出校門，對當地社區有了更深入的了解。他們參觀了熱鬧的員林果菜市場，體驗台灣日常生活的脈動。他們的探索延伸到附近的古蹟和迷人的特色咖啡館，讓他們真正感受到彰化獨特的風情。這些行程為他們提供了對崇實高工周圍豐富文化底蘊的寶貴見解。

A Journey of Connection and Warmth

一趟連結與溫暖的旅程

Though their volunteer activities spanned just a week, these American students forged deep and meaningful friendships with our Taiwanese students. More profoundly, they experienced the boundless care and warmth extended by their host families, creating unforgettable memories. This journey, filled with love and mutual exchange, left a profound and beautiful impression of Taiwan on them.

ChungShih Vocational High School eagerly anticipates the opportunity to welcome them back to Changhua next year, continuing this precious friendship across the Pacific!

儘管他們的志工活動僅僅為期一週，這些美國學生與我們的台灣學生建立了深厚而有意義的友誼。更重要的是，他們體驗到寄宿家庭無微不至的關懷與溫暖，創造了難忘的回憶。這趟充滿愛與相互交流的旅程，在他們心中留下了對台灣深刻而美好的印象。

崇實高工熱切期待明年能再次歡迎他們回到彰化，延續這份跨越太平洋的珍貴情誼！

Sentence Patterns · 實用句型

Fill in the blank and make it yours
套進去，就是你自己的句子

Have you ever tried ___?
你有沒有試過……？

Have you ever tried stinky tofu?你有沒有試過臭豆腐？

Have you ever tried Taekwondo?你有沒有試過跆拳道？

This is called ___ in Chinese.
這個中文叫做……。

This is called 木工 in Chinese.這個中文叫做「木工」。

This fruit is called 芭樂 in Chinese.這種水果中文叫做「芭樂」。

Would you like to ___ with us?
你要不要跟我們一起……？

Would you like to join us for lunch?你要不要跟我們一起吃午餐？

Would you like to walk to the market with us?你要不要跟我們一起走去市場？

In Taiwan, we usually ___.
在台灣，我們通常……。

In Taiwan, we usually take off our shoes indoors.在台灣，我們進屋通常會脫鞋。

In Taiwan, we usually eat rice with every meal.在台灣，我們幾乎每餐都吃飯。

Thank you for ___.
謝謝你……。

Thank you for teaching us today.謝謝你今天教我們。

Thank you for having me in your home.謝謝你們讓我住在你們家。
