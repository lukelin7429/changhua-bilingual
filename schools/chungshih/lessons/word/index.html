Electrical & Air Conditioning
電機與空調 · 5 字
WORD 01
electrical (adj)
電的
Students are learning how to connect wires and set up electrical panels.
學生們正在學習如何連接電線和安裝配電板。
They practice working with real electrical equipment to get better.
他們練習使用真實的電氣設備來提高水準。
WORD 02
electrical circuit
電路
The teacher is instructing the students on how to inspect the electrical circuits of refrigeration and air conditioning systems.
老師正在指導學生如何檢查冷凍空調系統的電路。
Refrigeration and air conditioning systems, including refrigerators and air conditioners, require thorough troubleshooting.
冷凍空調系統，包括冰箱和空調，需要徹底排除故障。
WORD 03
control panel
控制箱
The students are practicing wiring the control panels.
學生們正在練習控制面板接線。
For instance, they need to learn how to wire for switching between the main power supply and the backup generator.
例如，他們需要學習如何接線以在主電源和備用發電機之間進行切換。
WORD 04
multimeter (n)
三用電錶
The student is practicing using a multimeter to test electrical resistance.
學生正在練習使用萬用電表測試電阻。
A multimeter is a versatile tool that can measure voltage, current, and resistance in electrical circuits.
萬用電表是一種多功能工具，可以測量電路中的電壓、電流和電阻。
WORD 05
measurement (n)
測量
The student is using a tape measurer to make different measurements on the board.
學生正在使用捲尺在黑板上進行不同的測量。
He is practicing taking measurements for an electrical wiring layout.
他正在練習測量電氣線路佈局。

Chemical engineering students are conducting experiments in the lab.
化學工程專業的學生正在實驗室裡進行實驗。

他們必須學習如何使用量筒和其他實驗室儀器。

Furniture & Design
家具與設計 · 1 字
WORD 07
woodworking (n)
木工
The furniture design students are practicing woodworking using various machines.
家具設計專業的學生正在使用各種機器練習木工。
They improve their woodworking skills with different tools to create their own furniture.
他們使用不同的工具來提高木工技能來製作自己的家具。

Sports
體育 · 1 字
WORD 08
Taekwondo (n)
跆拳道
Students are practicing high kicks and punches in Taekwondo, demonstrating precision and strength.
學生在跆拳道中練習高踢和拳擊，展示了精確性和力量。
In Taekwondo, they refine their kicks and defensive moves, applying techniques in a controlled setting.
在跆拳道中，他們精進踢擊和防守動作，在控制環境中應用技巧。

At School
師長與校園 · 2 支影片
WORD 09
professional development
專業進修（教師研習）
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都有教師專業訓練課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
在專業訓練課程中，老師可以學到新的教學技巧與策略。
WORD 10
professional development
專業進修（輔導知能）
High school counselors gather for professional development, attending lectures by a professor.
高中輔導員聚集在一起進行專業發展，參加教授的講座。
The theme of the professional development meeting is how to prevent student suicide or self-harm.
專業發展會議的主題是如何預防學生自殺或自殘。
