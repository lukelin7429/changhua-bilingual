化工科 Chemical Engineering Department — 科系介紹影片

The Chemical Engineering department equips students with knowledge in chemical processes, materials, and environmental technologies. Our practical curriculum prepares graduates for immediate employment. Transform your future with cutting-edge chemical engineering skills and hands-on experience.

What You Study · 主要學習內容
To cultivate fundamental technical personnel for the chemical industry.
  • Analysis, Testing and Control of Chemical Raw Materials and Product Properties化工原料和產品性質的分析、檢驗與管制
  • Usage and Maintenance of Chemical Machinery, Instruments and Analytical Tools各式化工機械儀表和分析儀器的使用與維護

目標：培養化學工業的基層技術人才。

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

laboratory/ˈlæb.rə.tɔːr.i/實驗室

Always wear goggles in the laboratory.在實驗室裡一定要戴護目鏡。

experiment/ɪkˈsper.ə.mənt/實驗

Record every step of the experiment.實驗的每一個步驟都要記錄下來。

analysis/əˈnæl.ə.sɪs/分析

Analysis tells us what is in the sample.分析告訴我們樣品裡有什麼。

raw material/rɑː məˈtɪr.i.əl/原料

We test the raw material before production starts.生產開始之前，我們先檢驗原料。

measuring cylinder/ˈmeʒ.ɚ.ɪŋ ˈsɪl.ɪn.dɚ/量筒

Pour the liquid slowly into the measuring cylinder.把液體慢慢倒進量筒裡。

safety/ˈseɪf.ti/安全

Safety comes first in every chemical experiment.每一次化學實驗，安全都是第一位。

process/ˈprɑː.ses/製程

A process engineer improves how a factory makes things.製程工程師改善工廠的生產方式。

quality/ˈkwɑː.lə.t̬i/品質

Quality assurance checks every batch before it ships.品保在出貨前檢查每一批產品。

Key Sentences · 實用句子

Say these in the workshop · 在實習工場這樣說

Put on your goggles and gloves.

戴上護目鏡和手套。

這兩種藥品不可以混在一起。
