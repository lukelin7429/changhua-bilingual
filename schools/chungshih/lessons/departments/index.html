Every department at ChungShih has its own character, and each page below carries the same four things: a short introduction video made by the department itself, what you actually study, where it takes you after graduation — straight into work or on to a degree — and the certificates you can earn along the way.

崇實科科有特色。以下每一科的頁面都有同樣的四件事：該科自己拍攝的介紹影片、實際的學習內容、畢業後的出路（直接就業或繼續升學），以及在學期間可以考取的證照。

Each page also has an English section built for the department's own technical terms: a word list with pronunciation you can tap and hear, sentences you would really say in the workshop, and a matching game to check what stuck.

每一頁還附上為該科專業術語設計的英語學習區：可以點擊發音的單字表、在實習工場真的用得上的實用句子，以及一個檢查記憶的配對遊戲。
