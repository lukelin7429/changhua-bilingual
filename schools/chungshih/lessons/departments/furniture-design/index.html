家具設計科 Furniture Design Department — 科系介紹影片

The Furniture Design department trains students in creating functional and aesthetic furniture pieces. Our practical curriculum prepares graduates for immediate employment. Shape your future with innovative design skills and hands-on experience.

What You Study · 主要學習內容
To give students the essential skills and knowledge of furniture design.
  • Aesthetic Appreciation in Furniture Design家具設計美學賞析

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

woodworking/ˈwʊdˌwɝː.kɪŋ/木工

Woodworking begins with learning to use the machines safely.木工從安全操作機具開始。

joint/dʒɔɪnt/接合；榫接

A strong joint holds the chair together for years.牢固的榫接讓椅子用上好幾年。

grain/ɡreɪn/木紋

Sand along the grain, not across it.順著木紋磨，不要橫著磨。

prototype/ˈproʊ.t̬ə.taɪp/原型

We build a prototype before making the real chair.做出真正的椅子之前，我們先做一個原型。

model/ˈmɑː.dəl/模型

A scale model shows the design before it is built.縮尺模型讓人在製作前先看見設計。

ergonomic/ˌɝː.ɡəˈnɑː.mɪk/人體工學的

An ergonomic chair supports your back while you work.人體工學椅在工作時支撐你的背。

finish/ˈfɪn.ɪʃ/表面塗裝

The finish protects the wood and brings out its color.表面塗裝保護木頭，也讓木色顯現出來。

craftsmanship/ˈkræfts.mən.ʃɪp/工藝

Good craftsmanship shows in the details.好的工藝，看細節就知道。

Key Sentences · 實用句子

Say these in the workshop · 在實習工場這樣說

Measure twice, cut once.

量兩次，鋸一次。

Sand it until it feels smooth.

磨到摸起來平滑為止。

Is this joint tight enough?

這個接合夠緊嗎？

I designed this chair myself.

這張椅子是我自己設計的。
