Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

refrigeration/rɪˌfrɪdʒ.əˈreɪ.ʃən/冷凍

Refrigeration keeps food cold and safe.冷凍讓食物保持低溫與安全。

air conditioner/ˈer kənˌdɪʃ.ən.ɚ/冷氣機

We learn to install and repair an air conditioner.我們學習安裝與維修冷氣機。

compressor/kəmˈpres.ɚ/壓縮機

If the circuit is fine, check the compressor next.電路沒問題，下一步就檢查壓縮機。

refrigerant/rɪˈfrɪdʒ.ɚ.ənt/冷媒

Handle the refrigerant carefully and never release it.處理冷媒要小心，絕不可任意排放。

thermostat/ˈθɝː.mə.stæt/溫控器

The thermostat tells the system when to stop cooling.溫控器告訴系統何時停止製冷。

duct/dʌkt/風管

Cool air travels through the duct to every room.冷空氣經過風管送到每個房間。

installation/ˌɪn.stəˈleɪ.ʃən/裝修；安裝

The installation technician mounts the outdoor unit.裝修技術員負責安裝室外機。

appliance/əˈplaɪ.əns/家電

A home appliance repair technician fixes fridges and washers.家電維修技術員修理冰箱與洗衣機。
