Ways to Learn

從實習工場學英文

Real workshops, real words.

每支影片、每份教材，都取材自崇實真實的校園與實習現場。

6 Departments · 6 Videos
Course 01
Our Six Departments
六大科系介紹

Electrical engineering, electrical and air conditioning engineering, computer science, chemical engineering, interior space design and furniture design — each with its own introduction video, what you study, where it takes you after graduation, and the certificates you can earn. Every page ends with the department's own technical English: a word list you can tap to hear, sentences you would really say in the workshop, and a matching game.電機科、電機空調科、資訊科、化工科、室內空間設計科、家具設計科——每一科都有自己的介紹影片、學習內容、就業發展與相關證照。每一頁最後都附上該科的專業英語：可點擊發音的單字表、實習工場真的用得上的句子，以及一個配對遊戲。

10 Videos · 5 Themes
Course 02
Word of the Day
每日一字

Ten bilingual videos filmed in the school's own workshops, labs, design rooms and training spaces, grouped into five themes — electrical and air conditioning, chemical engineering, furniture and design, sports, and life at school. Each word is embedded straight from YouTube with two example sentences taken from the video description.10 支雙語影片，全部在崇實的實習工場、實驗室、設計教室與練習場地實地拍攝，依五大主題分類——電機與空調、化工、家具與設計、體育，以及師長與校園。每個單字直接嵌入影片，並附兩句取自影片說明欄的例句。

Start Learning →
30 Episodes · For Teachers
Course 03
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers, taught by Sarah Thomas across 10 topics and 30 numbered episodes — from greetings and roll call to exam instructions. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.給老師使用的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學，共 10 大主題、30 支編號影片——從問候點名到考試指令。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

Start Learning →
13 Episodes · By Office
Course 04
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

"Broadcast English" — thirteen short episodes in which each school office and section introduces, in English, the announcements it makes over the public-address system, from the curriculum section to the counseling room. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.「廣播英語」——13 支短片，由各處室、各組用英語介紹平時透過校園廣播宣布的內容，從教務處教學組到輔導室。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。
