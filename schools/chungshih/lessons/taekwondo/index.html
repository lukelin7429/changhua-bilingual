What is Taekwondo? — 認識跆拳道

What Is Taekwondo?

什麼是跆拳道？

Have you ever heard of Taekwondo? It's a Korean martial art that uses your body as a weapon. You learn how to kick, punch, and move your body in special ways. Taekwondo is not just about fighting; it also teaches respect and discipline. People wear white uniforms and colored belts that show their level. It's a fun activity that helps you stay healthy and make friends. In fact, there are over 70 million people practicing Taekwondo worldwide. It's a great way to learn about Korean culture, too. So, why not give Taekwondo a try?

你有沒有聽過跆拳道？這是一種韓國武術，使用你的身體作為武器。你將學會如何踢、打，以及以特別的方式移動你的身體。跆拳道不僅僅是關於戰鬥；它還教授尊重和紀律。人們穿著白色制服和顯示他們等級的彩色腰帶。這是一項有趣的活動，有助於保持健康並結交朋友。事實上，全世界有超過 7000 萬人在練習跆拳道。這也是學習韓國文化的絕佳方式。那麼，為什麼不嘗試跆拳道呢？

Idioms & Expressions · 慣用語

What people really say · 母語人士這樣說

give it your best shot
全力以赴

To try as hard as you possibly can, whatever the result.

“Whatever the score, give it your best shot.”不管比分如何，都要全力以赴。

keep your chin up
別氣餒

To stay cheerful and confident when things are difficult.

“Keep your chin up — there is always the next match.”別氣餒，永遠還有下一場。

practice makes perfect
熟能生巧

Doing something again and again is what makes you good at it.

“Practice makes perfect, so we train every week.”熟能生巧，所以我們每週都練。

roll with the punches
見招拆招

To adapt calmly when things do not go your way.

“A good athlete learns to roll with the punches.”好的運動員學會見招拆招。
