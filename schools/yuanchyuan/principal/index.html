Meet the Principal · 認識校長

Principal Lin Shu-chen

林淑貞校長 · 彰化縣二水鄉源泉國民小學
I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Dear parents and friends,

各位親愛的家長平安：

I am Lin Shu-chen, and I took up the post of principal at Yuanchyuan Elementary School on 1 August 2024.

我是林淑貞校長，自 113 年 8 月 1 日起接任源泉國小校長。

Over the years, under a succession of dedicated principals and with the strong help of the parents' association and the support of families, the children of Yuanchyuan have done wonderfully. This is a school with a fine culture of its own, and it has raised many people who now serve our country and society.

這幾年，源泉國小在多位前輩校長領導、家長會大力協助及家長支持之下，學生的表現讓人激賞。源泉國小擁有優質的校園文化，培育出眾多國家社會人才。

Our team cares deeply about the children's education, and works hard to bring in and pull together resources from every quarter — all so that our students can learn in a better environment. The children here are fortunate: our teachers' warmth for education guides them toward an all-round development — strong results in sport, a many-sided feel for music, and the steady shaping of good character.

源泉國小的學校團隊對學生的教育很用心，積極爭取並整合各界資源，只希望給予學生更好的教育環境。源泉國小的學生很幸福，因為有學校團隊的教育熱忱，引導著學生得到全人教育的發展：強健的體育績優表現、多才多藝的音樂素養、形塑學生良好品德發展。

I believe that with the school and every family working together, each of our students can enjoy a healthy body and mind, develop their own interests and potential, and paint their own patch of beautiful sky.

我相信，源泉國小在學校及各位家長共同努力之下，每個學生都能擁有健康的身心，發展自己潛能興趣，揮灑出自己一片美麗的天空。

Thank you for all your support of the school. If anything is ever on your mind, you are always welcome to talk it through directly with our teachers and with the school. I wish every family peace and happiness.

在此，感謝各位家長對學校的支持，若有任何問題，歡迎與老師及學校做最直接的溝通與了解。敬祝各位家長事事順心如意、家庭幸福美滿。

Lin Shu-chen · Principal　林淑貞 校長　113.08.01

Education is a handing-on — holding tradition and new ideas together, to shape a school culture worth keeping.
教育是傳承，兼顧傳統與創新，形塑優質學校文化。
II.

Leadership at a Glance

校長經歷摘要

A whole career in Changhua County schools — classroom, administration, and the principal's office.

一輩子深耕彰化縣基層教育，從教學現場、學校行政，到校長室。

Education · 學歷
Elementary Education & School Administration
國立屏東師範專科學校、國立屏東師範學院畢業；國立臺中師範學院國民教育研究所學校行政碩士；國立嘉義師範學院輔導二十學分班。
Service · 服務經歷
Teacher → Director → Principal
彰化縣花壇國小、南鎮國小導師；大湖國小教導主任；永光國小、三條國小、僑義國小校長；113 學年度起任源泉國小校長。
Focus · 辦學重點
Happy Learning, Whole-Child Growth
學生快樂學習、落實全人教育；教師專業自主、家長主動參與、行政支援教學。

Principal Lin began as a homeroom teacher at Huatan and then Nanzhen Elementary, spent eight years as Director of Educational Affairs at Dahu Elementary, and has since led three schools as principal — Yongguang (2008–2011), Santiao (2011–2018) and Qiaoyi Elementary (2018–2024) — before coming to Yuanchyuan in 2024. Every one of those schools is in Changhua County.

林校長從花壇國小、南鎮國小導師起步，於大湖國小擔任教導主任八年，之後歷任永光國小（97–100 學年度）、三條國小（100–107 學年度）與僑義國小（107–113 學年度）校長，113 學年度起接掌源泉國小。這一路走來，服務的都是彰化縣的學校。

III.

Educational Philosophy

辦學理念

Five commitments, one purpose — a good school for every child.

五項堅持，一個目的：讓每個孩子都在一所好學校裡長大。

學生快樂學習 · 教師專業自主 · 家長主動參與
行政支援教學 · 拓展公共關係
Children learn happily — teachers stay professional — parents take part — administration serves teaching — the school reaches out.
Principal Lin keeps these five working together, so that a small school runs on quality, warmth and trust. 林校長讓這五件事彼此支持，使一所小校在品質、溫度與信任之上運作。
其一 · One
Happy Learning, Whole Child
學生快樂學習，落實全人教育——支持孩子發展學藝潛能，涵養優雅氣質與良好品德。
其二 · Two
Professional Teachers
教師專業自主，實踐終身學習；提高教學品質，提升學生自我學習與終身學習的能力。
其三 · Three
Parents Take Part
家長主動參與，發展親師合作；辦理親職教育，發揮家庭教育的功能。
其四 · Four
Administration Serves Teaching
行政支援教學，建立品質卓越；主動服務、溝通協調、走動管理，發掘問題並解決問題。
其五 · Five
A School That Reaches Out
拓展公共關係，進行學校行銷；結合社區資源與文化，營造學校社區化。
IV.

Beyond the Office

校長，也是一位薩克斯風手

The saxophone in the photo is not a prop.

照片裡的薩克斯風，不是道具。

Away from the paperwork, Principal Lin plays the saxophone, paints, reads, and picks up a table-tennis bat when she can. For a school where children learn the ocarina, the taiko drums and more, it helps to have a principal who knows first-hand what it takes to keep at an instrument — and who still finds the joy in it.

放下公文，林校長喜歡吹薩克斯風、畫畫、閱讀，有空也打打桌球。在一所孩子學陶笛、學太鼓的學校裡，有一位親身知道「把一項樂器練成」需要什麼、而且仍樂在其中的校長，是一件很好的事。

Let every child develop their own interests and potential — and paint their own patch of beautiful sky.
讓每個孩子發展自己的潛能與興趣，揮灑出屬於自己的一片美麗天空。
