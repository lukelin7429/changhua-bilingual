Sports & PE
體育課 · 9 words
WORD 01
basketball (n)
籃球
Students love to play basketball during recess.
學生們喜歡在課間休息時打籃球。
A pick-up game is an on-the-spot game for basketball.
鬥牛賽是籃球的當場臨時組合的比賽。
WORD 02
volleyball (n)
排球
The PE teacher is showing the students how to play volleyball.
體育老師正在為學生示範打排球的技巧。
The students are practicing bumping the volleyball.
同學們正在練習低手擊球。
WORD 03
bounce (v)
彈起
In this PE class, each student has to bounce a ball on the ground while he or she runs across the basketball court.
在這班的體育課，每個學生都邊拍球邊在籃球場上來回跑。
The kids have to use the right amount of strength when they bounce the balls so they can control them while they run.
小朋友要用適當的力道拍球以便能在跑步時還能控制球。
WORD 04
run (v)
跑步
The students are running at the track.
學生們在跑道上跑步。
Running is good exercise and students do it every day.
跑步是很好的運動，學生們每天都在跑步。
WORD 05
karate (n)
空手道
Karate is a form of martial arts.
空手道是武術的一種形式。
The coach is teaching the moves in karate.
教練正在教空手道的動作。
WORD 06
unicycle (n)
獨輪車
The students practice riding unicylces.
學生練習騎獨輪車。
They use a handrail to help them balance themselves on the unicycles.
他們使用扶手來幫助他們在獨輪車上保持平衡。
WORD 07
drill (n)
訓練
The students do dribbling and shooting drills.
學生進行運球和投籃訓練。
They take turns doing the drills in class.
他們在課堂上輪流練習。
WORD 08
flip (v)
翻轉
The students learn how to flip their bodies forward and dismount on the pull-up bar.
學生學習如何向前翻轉身體並在單桿上下車。
This exercise helps them develop balance and coordination as they flip.
這項練習可以幫助他們在翻轉時訓練平衡和協調性。
WORD 09
pull up bar
單槓
The students hang from the pull-up bar and must stay up for a certain amount of time.
學生們吊在單桿上，並且必須保持一定的時間。
The purpose of hanging from the pull-up bar is to train the students' arm strength.
吊單槓的目的是訓練學生的臂力。

孩子們正在玩跳格子，他們小心地從一個格子跳到另一個格子。

跳格子是有趣的遊戲，可以幫助學生學習數字和平衡。
WORD 13
