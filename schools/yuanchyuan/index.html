I.

Welcome to Yuanchyuan

歡迎來到源泉國小

A small school, full of enthusiasm and a warm sense of community — where every child is helped to flourish.

一所小而美的學校，充滿熱情與溫暖的社區感，幫助每一位孩子茁壯成長。

Hello and welcome! Set in the beautiful Ershui Township, our school may be small — with around a hundred students — but it is filled with energy and a close, caring community. Here we believe that every student is unique, and our goal is to help each one of them flourish.

位於風景秀麗的二水鄉，源泉國小雖然只有大約一百名學生，卻擁有充滿熱情與溫暖社區感的校園。在這裡，我們相信每位學生都是獨一無二的，我們的目標是幫助他們每一位都能茁壯成長。

At Yuanchyuan we are devoted to nurturing students who are not only sincere and well-grounded but also keen to embrace learning. Our approach is crafted to make learning not just fun but deeply meaningful, so that children come to understand their surroundings and their place in the world.

在源泉，我們致力於培育學生不僅真誠、踏實，而且熱衷於學習。我們的教育方法讓學習不僅有趣，而且深具意義，使孩子全面了解他們的周圍環境，以及自己在世界中的角色。

Our curriculum is carefully designed to ignite a love of learning, sharpen problem-solving, and encourage all-round personal growth — a rich learning experience that stays with our students for life.

我們的課程精心設計，旨在點燃學習的熱愛、增強解決問題的能力，並鼓勵全面的個人成長，創造一個伴隨學生一生的豐富學習體驗。

Every student is unique — and together, we celebrate learning and personal growth.
每位學生都是獨一無二的——在這裡，我們一起慶祝學習與成長。

III.

What We Stand For

學校願景

Four words, shaped together by the whole school.

全校師生一起形塑的四個字：誠樸、踏實、樂觀、進取。

誠樸 · Sincere
Honest & Unpretentious
以真誠待人、以樸實做事，養成表裡如一的品格。
踏實 · Grounded
Steady, Step by Step
一步一腳印，把每一件事做穩、做好，打下扎實的基礎。
樂觀 · Optimistic
Hopeful & Bright
遇到困難不退縮，帶著好心情面對每一天的挑戰。
進取 · Aspiring
Always Moving Forward
保持好奇與衝勁，願意嘗試、樂於學習，追求更好的自己。

These four words sit at the heart of a whole-child education. We want learning at Yuanchyuan to widen every path a child can take — drawing on many subjects, many cultures, and the wisdom of those who came before — so that our students grow healthy characters and the kind of abilities and habits that last a lifetime.

這四個字，是全人教育的核心。我們希望源泉的學習能拓寬孩子的每一條路——融入各科教學、認識多元文化、吸取前人智慧——讓學生培養健康的人格，以及帶得走、用一輩子的能力與習慣。

IV.

A Forest-School Childhood

森林小學的美麗童年

One of the most beautiful campuses in Changhua — and plenty of room to run.

彰化縣最美麗的校園之一，也是孩子盡情奔跑的地方。

Yuanchyuan is a forest school that puts children's health and happy learning first. The campus is green and wide open, backed by the hills of the Baguashan range, with generous space to play, mature old trees, and a rich variety of play and sports equipment. It is a place built for a beautiful childhood.

源泉是一所重視孩子健康、快樂學習的森林小學。校園綠意盎然、腹地開闊，背倚八卦山脈，活動空間寬敞，老樹成蔭，遊樂與運動設施多樣豐富——是一處為美麗童年而生的園地。

Room to grow, under the hills
在山的懷抱裡，慢慢長大

Old trees, wide lawns, a blue running track and the mountains for a backdrop — at a small school like ours, every child has space to move, to breathe, and to be a child.

老樹、大草地、藍色跑道，還有一整片山當背景——在源泉這樣的小校，每個孩子都有奔跑、呼吸、好好當個孩子的空間。

V.

Something for Every Child

精緻多元的免費社團

Free after-school clubs — from folk dance and street dance to soccer, basketball and art.

免費社團，從民族舞蹈、熱舞到足球、籃球與藝術創作，總有一個適合你。

We work hard to fire up a love of learning and to keep our courses lively and varied, so that every child has the chance to open up their own gifts. Our free clubs run the full range — the arts, music and sport — giving students room to discover and grow their talents.

我們努力激發學習熱情、活化多元課程，營造讓孩子開展天賦潛能的優質環境。免費社團橫跨藝術、音樂與運動，讓每一位學生都有機會發現並培養自己的才能。

Find your thing
找到你熱愛的事

Every child is encouraged to pick up an art — one person, one skill — and to keep at it until it becomes their own.

我們鼓勵「一人一藝」，讓每個孩子都能找到、練成一項屬於自己的才藝。
