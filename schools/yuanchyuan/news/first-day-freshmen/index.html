First Day, New Journey: Welcoming Our Little Newcomers

開啟學習新旅程：歡迎源泉的小小新鮮人
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Today is the first day the little newcomers officially joined our warm Yuanchyuan family.

今天是「小小新鮮人」正式加入我們溫馨大家庭的第一天。

Watching the children walk onto campus with their little backpacks and their shy-but-excited smiles was simply lovely. We welcome every one of them.

看著寶貝們背著小書包、帶著害羞又興奮的笑容踏進校園，真的太可愛了。我們歡迎每一位新生。

There was a first morning in the classroom, a group photo by the anniversary sign, and giant bubbles floating under the old tree — gentle ways to explore a new place.

在教室裡度過第一個早晨、在週年看板前拍大合照、在老樹下追著巨大的泡泡——用最溫柔的方式探索新環境。

May you learn happily here, explore bravely, and grow up safe and healthy. Happy first day — let's set off together!

願你們在這裡快樂學習、勇敢探索，平安健康地長大。開學快樂，我們一起出發囉！

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

first/fɜːrst/第一

Today is the first day of school.今天是開學的第一天。

backpack/ˈbækpæk/後背包、書包

Each child carries a little backpack.每個孩子都背著一個小書包。

shy/ʃaɪ/害羞

Some new students feel a little shy.有些新生覺得有點害羞。

excited/ɪkˈsaɪtɪd/興奮的

They are excited to meet new friends.他們很興奮能認識新朋友。

smile/smaɪl/微笑

A warm smile makes school feel friendly.溫暖的微笑讓學校變得友善。

welcome/ˈwɛlkəm/歡迎

We welcome the new first-graders.我們歡迎小一新生。

explore/ɪkˈsplɔːr/探索

Children love to explore the new campus.孩子喜歡探索新校園。

brave/breɪv/勇敢

Be brave and try something new.勇敢一點，試試新的事物。
