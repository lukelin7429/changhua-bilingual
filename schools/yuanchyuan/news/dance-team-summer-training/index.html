Sweat and Smiles: The Dance Team's Summer Training

舞蹈隊暑假集訓：汗水與笑容
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

This summer the dance team didn't rest. In the studio the children put in the work — careful stretches, handstand practice, and every move done with focus and strength.

這個暑假，舞蹈隊的孩子們沒有閒著。在舞蹈教室裡揮灑汗水——認真拉筋、練習倒立，每一個動作都充滿力量與專注。

Training was hard, but the teacher and the children kept encouraging each other and improving together. That team spirit is genuinely moving.

集訓過程很辛苦，但老師與孩子們互相鼓勵、一起進步，這份團隊精神真的讓人感動。

Small bodies, big energy. They put on bright Hakka floral-cloth hats and rehearsed a lively dance — from solid basics and backbends to smooth footwork, balance and jumps. Every drop of sweat is a mark of progress.

小小的身體裡，蘊藏著大大的能量。孩子們戴上繽紛的客家花布斗笠，排練輕快的舞蹈——從紮實的下腰基本功，到流暢的走位、平衡與跳躍，每一滴汗水都是進步的痕跡。

Summer doesn't slow us down, and the passion doesn't fade. We can't wait to see them perform.

暑假不放鬆，熱情不打折！期待孩子們最終的精彩展現。
