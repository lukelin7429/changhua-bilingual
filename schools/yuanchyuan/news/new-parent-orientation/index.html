A New Chapter Together: The First-Grade Parents' Orientation

新生家長座談會：共譜成長新樂章
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

One morning the campus was full of life: our new Grade 1 students and their parents came together for the first-grade meeting.

今天早上，校園裡熱鬧非凡——最可愛的小一新生和家長們，一起參與「新生家長座談會」。

The whole administration team turned out. Led by the principal, the directors and section chiefs of academic affairs, general affairs and counselling introduced the campus, the learning resources and the pastoral care, so parents could picture their child's day.

學校的行政團隊全員出動！由校長帶領教導、總務、輔導等各處室主任與組長，親切地介紹校園環境、學習資源與生活照護，讓家長對孩子的未來充滿期待。

The Grade 1 homeroom teacher, Miss Hsin-ju, met everyone with real warmth. She shared her class-management ideas and explained clearly what to prepare for the new term.

一年級導師信如老師帶著滿滿的熱情與大家相見歡，分享豐富的班級經營理念，也詳盡說明開學後的各項注意事項與準備工作。

Parents listened closely and asked many good questions — warm, lively teacher-parent talk. Education is a marathon of love, and parents, teachers and children run it together.

家長們專注聆聽、積極提問，親師互動熱絡。教育是一場充滿愛的馬拉松，需要親師生共同攜手同行。
