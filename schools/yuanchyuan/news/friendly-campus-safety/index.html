A Kind and Ready Campus: Friendly Campus & Disaster-Prevention Week

友善校園與防災宣導：溫暖又安心的開學第一週
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

This week is the first week of the new school year, and Yuanchyuan held two lively, useful lessons on a friendly campus and on disaster prevention.

本週是開學的第一週，源泉國小進行了兩節充實又實用的友善校園與防災宣導活動。

Arranged by the academic-affairs office and the counselling office, an officer from the Changhua County Police led everyone through the heart of a friendly campus — stopping bullying, respecting people who are different from us, and paying attention to traffic safety — so that we build a warm, secure feeling on campus together.

由教導處與輔導室安排，彰化縣警局的警察姊姊帶領大家認識「友善校園」的核心精神：防制校園霸凌、尊重多元，以及注意交通安全，一起營造溫暖又安心的校園氛圍。

The general-affairs office then explained the disaster-prevention drill. When an earthquake or an emergency comes, remember the three key moves — drop down, take cover, and hold on — stay calm, follow directions, and evacuate in good order.

接著由總務處為大家講解防災演練與宣導。當地震或災害來臨，記得掌握「趴下、掩護、穩住」三大避難要領，保持冷靜、聽從指揮進行疏散。

When safety habits become second nature, they add one more layer of protection for everyone at school.

把防災意識化為本能，就能為校園安全多添一份保障。

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

friendly/ˈfrɛndli/友善的

Our school wants to be a friendly place for everyone.我們的學校希望成為對每個人都友善的地方。

respect/rɪˈspɛkt/尊重

We respect classmates who are different from us.我們尊重和我們不一樣的同學。

safety/ˈseɪfti/安全

Road safety keeps us safe on the way to school.交通安全讓我們上學路上平安。

bully/ˈbʊli/霸凌、欺負

Tell a teacher if someone tries to bully you.如果有人想霸凌你，要告訴老師。

traffic/ˈtræfɪk/交通、車流

Look both ways before you cross the traffic.穿越車流前要看左右兩邊。

earthquake/ˈɜːrθkweɪk/地震

In an earthquake, get under a strong desk.地震時要躲到堅固的桌子底下。

cover/ˈkʌvər/掩護、遮蓋

Drop down and cover your head with your arms.蹲下並用手臂掩護頭部。

calm/kɑːm/冷靜

Stay calm and follow the teacher's directions.保持冷靜，聽從老師的指示。
