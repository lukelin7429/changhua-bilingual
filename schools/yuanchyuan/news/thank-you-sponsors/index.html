Thank You for Your Support: I Love My School

感恩贊助，感謝有您：吾愛吾校
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Warm thanks to Riwei Electric and the Ershui Ande Temple for their generous support, which brought a strong current of warmth into Yuanchyuan's school-development fund.

感謝日偉電機與二水安德宮的熱情贊助，為源泉國小的校務發展基金注入了強大的暖流。

In recognition, the county government presented an “I Love My School” certificate of thanks. The honour affirms these organisations' long-standing care for education, and it encourages our students, too.

縣府特別頒贈「吾愛吾校」感謝狀。這份殊榮，是對熱心單位長期支持教育的肯定，也是對源泉學子最大的鼓勵與期許。

Because of this care and giving, Yuanchyuan's learning environment grows better, and the children can learn and grow with richer, fuller resources.

因為有這份愛與付出，源泉國小的教育環境更加美好，孩子們也能在更豐富、更完善的資源下成長學習。

We offer our most sincere thanks, and hope the whole community will keep walking with us toward a bright future for education at Yuanchyuan.

我們致上最誠摯的謝意，也期盼社區大家繼續攜手，共同成就源泉教育的美好未來。
