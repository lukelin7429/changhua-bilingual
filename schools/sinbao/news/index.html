English · 讀者劇場

Honorable Mention in Reader's Theater英語讀者劇場比賽 榮獲 C 組佳作

2025 · 114 School Year · 114 學年度

Our Sinbao team stepped onto the stage at the county's elementary & junior-high English Reader's Theater competition — and came home with an Honorable Mention in Group C. For weeks the children rehearsed lines, voices, and movement entirely in English, learning that a story really comes alive when you perform it together.

新寶代表隊登上彰化縣國中小英語讀者劇場比賽舞台，榮獲 C 組佳作。孩子們花了好幾週，用全英文反覆練習台詞、聲音與肢體，體會到——當大家一起把故事「演」出來，故事才真正活了起來。

English · Cool English 英聽王

Sixteen "Listening Champions"16 位學生榮獲 Cool English 英聽王

2026 · 115 School Year · 115 年度

Sixteen Sinbao students earned awards in the nationwide Cool English "Listening King" contest. Training their ears on the Ministry of Education's Cool English platform, they listened, answered, and climbed the leaderboard — proof that careful listening is the first step to speaking with confidence.

本校 16 位學生在全國「Cool English 英聽王」比賽中獲獎。他們在教育部 Cool English 平台上練聽力、答題、衝排行榜——印證了「先聽得懂」，正是「敢開口」的第一步。

English · 自主學習

Self-Directed Learners, Month After MonthCool English 自主學習活動獎 連月獲獎

2025–2026 · 114–115 School Year

Month after month, Sinbao students have won Cool English self-directed learning awards — 21 winners in February, 12 in March, 12 in April, and more. On their own time, these children log in, practice English, and keep their streaks alive. Learning English has become a habit, not a homework.

一個月接著一個月，新寶學生屢屢榮獲 Cool English 自主學習活動獎——二月 21 人、三月 12 人、四月 12 人……孩子們利用課餘時間自己登入、自己練英文、自己維持連續紀錄。學英文，已經成為一種習慣，而不只是功課。

Reading · 數位閱讀

Winners at the Digital Reading Contest2026 彰化縣數位閱讀競賽 榮獲優勝

2026 · 115 School Year · 115 學年度

Our students took a Winner's prize at the 2026 Changhua Digital Reading Competition. Reading on screen, they searched, compared, and judged information across several texts — the very skills a reader needs to navigate today's bilingual, digital world.

本校學生在 2026 彰化縣數位閱讀競賽中榮獲優勝。孩子們在螢幕上閱讀，學會搜尋、比較、判斷跨篇章的資訊——這正是當代讀者悠遊雙語數位世界所需的關鍵能力。

English · 團體朗讀

One Voice in English Group Reading國小英語團體朗讀比賽 榮獲優勝

2025 · 114 School Year · 114 年度

At the county's Elementary English Group Reading contest, our young readers won a Winner's prize. Standing shoulder to shoulder, they matched rhythm, pronunciation, and feeling — dozens of voices reading as one. It is one of the bravest things a small school's children can do, and they did it beautifully.

在彰化縣國小英語團體朗讀比賽中，新寶的小讀者們榮獲優勝。他們肩並肩站著，對齊節奏、發音與情感——數十個聲音讀成一個聲音。對一所小學校的孩子來說，這是最需要勇氣的事之一，而他們做得非常漂亮。

Reading · 美感閱讀

Reading the World with "Anne Kids News"參與「安妮新聞」美感智能閱讀計畫

2026 · 115 School Year

Sinbao joined the "Anne Kids News" aesthetic & smart-reading program, bringing a beautifully designed children's newspaper into the classroom. Through its visual stories, students practice reading for meaning, talking about big ideas, and looking at the world with curious, thoughtful eyes.

新寶加入「安妮新聞」美感智能閱讀計畫，把一份設計優美的兒童報紙帶進教室。透過圖文並茂的報導，學生練習讀懂內容、討論大議題，也學會用好奇而細膩的眼睛看世界。
