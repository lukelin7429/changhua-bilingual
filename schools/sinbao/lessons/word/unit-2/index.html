Word of the Day · Unit 02 🎨

Arts, Music & Making

藝術與創作 · 6 個單字 · 6 部影片

Word 01
drumstick (n.)
鼓棒
He holds a drumstick in each hand to play the drum.
他兩手各拿一支鼓棒打鼓。
The drummer taps the beat with his drumsticks.
鼓手用鼓棒敲出節拍。
Word 02
rhythm (n.)
節奏
We clap our hands to keep the rhythm.
我們拍手來保持節奏。
Music has a rhythm you can feel in your body.
音樂有一種你身體能感覺到的節奏。
Word 03
clay (n.)
黏土
Students shape animals out of soft clay.
同學們用軟軟的黏土捏出動物。
We make small bowls with clay in art class.
美術課上我們用黏土做小碗。
Word 04
play (n.)
話劇
Our class puts on a short play for the school.
我們班為全校演出一齣短劇。
Everyone has a part to act in the play.
每個人在話劇裡都有一個角色要演。
Word 05
movie (n.)
電影
We watch an English movie in class on Friday.
星期五我們在課堂上看一部英文電影。
My favorite movie makes me laugh every time.
我最愛的電影每次都讓我笑。
Word 06
chocolate (n.)
巧克力
The children make sweet chocolate in cooking class.
孩子們在烹飪課做香甜的巧克力。
Chocolate melts quickly in your warm hands.
巧克力在你溫暖的手裡很快就融化。
