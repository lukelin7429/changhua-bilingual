Sports & Movement

運動與律動 · 6 個單字 · 6 部影片

Word 01
jump rope (n.)
跳繩
Students jump rope together during recess.
下課時，同學們一起跳繩。
Jumping rope is great exercise for your whole body.
跳繩對全身都是很棒的運動。
Word 02
hurdling (n.)
跨欄賽跑
In hurdling, runners jump over barriers as they race.
跨欄賽跑時，選手一邊跑一邊跳過欄架。
She practices hurdling every day on the track.
她每天在跑道上練習跨欄。
Word 03
hula hoop (n.)
呼拉圈
He can spin a hula hoop around his waist for a long time.
他可以把呼拉圈在腰上轉很久。
Many students play with the hula hoop at recess.
許多同學下課時玩呼拉圈。
Word 04
agility ladder (n.)
繩梯
Students run through the agility ladder to get faster.
同學們穿過繩梯來加快腳步。
The agility ladder helps you train quick feet.
繩梯能幫助你訓練敏捷的步伐。
Word 05
Chinese yo-yo (n.)
扯鈴
The Chinese yo-yo spins and hums on the string.
扯鈴在繩子上旋轉，發出嗡嗡聲。
Our club performs amazing Chinese yo-yo tricks.
我們社團表演精彩的扯鈴特技。
Word 06
activity (n.)
活動
We have a fun outdoor activity this afternoon.
今天下午我們有一個有趣的戶外活動。
Every school activity helps us learn something new.
每一個學校活動都讓我們學到新東西。
