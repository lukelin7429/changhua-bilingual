Learning & Tech at School

Word 01
PaGamO (n.)
線上學習遊戲
We play PaGamO to practice math and reading.
我們玩 PaGamO 來練習數學和閱讀。
On PaGamO, students answer questions to win land.
在 PaGamO 上，學生答題來贏得土地。
Word 02
typing competition (n.)
打字比賽
Our class joins a typing competition every year.
我們班每年參加打字比賽。
In a typing competition, fast and correct hands win.
打字比賽中，又快又準的手會獲勝。
Word 03
point (n.)
點數
You earn a point for every correct answer.
每答對一題就得到一點。
The team with the most points wins the game.
點數最多的隊伍贏得比賽。
Word 04
Chinese characters (n.)
國字
Students practice writing Chinese characters neatly.
同學們練習把國字寫得工整。
Each Chinese character has its own meaning.
每個國字都有自己的意思。
Word 05
clock (n.)
時鐘
The clock on the wall shows nine o'clock.
牆上的時鐘顯示九點。
We learn to tell time by reading a clock.
我們透過看時鐘學會看時間。
Word 06
spring (n.)
彈簧
A spring bounces back when you press it down.
彈簧被壓下去後會彈回來。
We test how a spring stretches in science class.
自然課上我們測試彈簧怎麼伸長。
