Word of the Day · Unit 04 🎉

Nature, Safety & Celebrations

自然 · 安全 · 節慶 · 6 個單字 · 6 部影片
← Unit 3 · 校園學習All Units · 回 LessonsUnit 1 → 運動律動
Word 01
plant (v.)
種植
Students plant young trees on the school grounds.
同學們在校園裡種下小樹。
We plant seeds and water them every morning.
我們種下種子，每天早上澆水。
Word 02
eco-pond (n.)
生態池
Frogs and fish live in our school eco-pond.
青蛙和魚住在我們學校的生態池裡。
The eco-pond teaches us how nature works together.
生態池教我們大自然如何共同運作。
Word 03
firefighter (n.)
消防員
A firefighter teaches us how to stay safe from fire.
消防員教我們如何遠離火災的危險。
Firefighters are brave people who help in danger.
消防員是危難時伸出援手的勇敢的人。
Word 04
earthquake drill (n.)
地震演習
During an earthquake drill, we drop, cover, and hold on.
地震演習時，我們蹲下、掩護、穩住。
An earthquake drill helps us stay calm and safe.
地震演習幫助我們保持冷靜與安全。
Word 05
Chinese New Year (n.)
農曆新年
We celebrate Chinese New Year with our families.
我們和家人一起慶祝農曆新年。
During Chinese New Year, children receive red envelopes.
農曆新年時，孩子們會收到紅包。
Word 06
celebrate (v.)
慶祝
We celebrate our school's birthday with games and songs.
我們用遊戲和歌曲慶祝學校的生日。
Friends celebrate together when good things happen.
好事發生時，朋友們一起慶祝。
