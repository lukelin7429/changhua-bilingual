I.

Welcome

歡迎來到新寶國小

A small coastal school where every child — and every culture — belongs.

一所海邊的小學校，讓每個孩子、每一種文化，都有屬於自己的位置。

Sinbao Elementary sits in Sinbao, a coastal village in Fangyuan Township on the western edge of Changhua — a land of tidal wetlands, oyster farms, and sea breezes. Founded in 1981, our school has grown alongside this seaside community for more than four decades.

新寶國小坐落於彰化西側海岸、芳苑鄉的新寶村——這裡有潮間帶溼地、蚵田與不停吹拂的海風。學校創立於民國 70 年（1981），逾四十年來與這片濱海社區一同成長。

What makes us special is our diversity. Sinbao has long been one of Changhua's most multicultural schools, with children from many of Taiwan's Indigenous peoples — Amis, Bunun, Atayal, Paiwan, Saisiyat, and Tsou — learning side by side. We believe a small school can open onto a very wide world, and that learning English is one more door into it.

我們最珍貴的特色是多元。新寶長年是彰化縣族群最多元的學校之一，阿美、布農、泰雅、排灣、賽夏、鄒等臺灣原住民族的孩子在這裡並肩學習。我們相信，一所小學校也能通向很寬廣的世界——而學英文，正是其中一扇門。

II.

What We Value

新寶的四個核心價值
🌈
Diversity
多元

From Indigenous heritage to art, music, English, soccer, and Chinese yo-yo clubs — every child finds a place to shine at Sinbao.

🏅
Excellence
卓越

With dedicated arts instruction and a strong English program, our students reach high — and win county honors in reading, listening, and reader's theater.

🙏
Gratitude
感恩

We teach children to be thankful — to parents, to teachers, and to the people and the land that care for them.

💛
Appreciation
惜福

To value time, to value what we have, and to value one another — the quiet habits that make a good and kind person.
