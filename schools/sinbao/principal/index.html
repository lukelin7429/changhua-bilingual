I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Welcome to Sinbao Elementary School. Since 2020, it has been my honor to lead this small but remarkable school on the Fangyuan coast — a place where children from many backgrounds and many of Taiwan's Indigenous peoples learn, play, and grow up together.

歡迎來到新寶國小。自民國 109 年（2020）起，我很榮幸帶領這所芳苑海岸上雖小卻不平凡的學校——在這裡，來自不同背景、來自臺灣多個原住民族的孩子，一同學習、一同遊戲、一同長大。

At Sinbao we hold to four simple values: diversity, excellence, gratitude, and appreciation. We want every child not only to learn well, but to grow into a good and kind person — someone who is thankful for what they have, and who treats others with respect.

在新寶，我們堅持四個樸實的價值：多元、卓越、感恩、惜福。我們希望每個孩子不只把書讀好，更能成為一個善良正直的人——懂得感恩所擁有的，也懂得尊重身邊的每一個人。

A small coastal school is no barrier to a wide horizon. Through art, music, sports, and a strong bilingual program, our children discover that the world is open to them — and that English is one more way to step into it. I warmly invite you to look around our campus, watch our students speak English, and share in the life of Sinbao.

海邊的小學校，絕不妨礙孩子擁有寬廣的視野。透過美術、音樂、運動與紮實的雙語課程，孩子們發現世界向他們敞開——而英文，正是走進世界的另一條路。誠摯邀請您走訪我們的校園、看看孩子用英文說話，一同分享新寶的點滴。

"A small school by the sea can still open onto the whole world."
海邊的小學校，也能通向整個世界。 — 方順陽校長
