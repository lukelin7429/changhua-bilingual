A Summer of Discovery — College Mentors Bring New Energy to Wansing萬興國小115年大專生暑期營隊

This summer, Wansing invited two outstanding college mentors — Ms. Pin-Jie and Ms. Hong-Yu — to lead an all-new College Mentor Summer Camp for our students. Together, the two mentors designed a full slate of activities that balanced academics with exploration, so summer stayed full and never boring.

Six weeks, six ways to grow

  • Science Experiments — hands-on labs that turn curiosity into discovery
  • Coding & Digital Skills — learning programming languages and building logical thinking
  • Global Awareness — widening students' view of the world and the issues that shape it
  • Art & Culture — from calligraphy to visual appreciation
  • Active Sports & CoolEnglish — moving bodies and cheering on English, in equal measure
  • Homework Support — steady, warm company for every question along the way

Watching the children's focused, radiant smiles, it's clear this summer was full of both learning and joy.

中文原文 · Chinese

今年暑假，萬興國小特別邀請到兩位優秀的大專生隊輔——品婕老師與泓瑜老師，為孩子們帶來一場豐富多元的「大專生暑期營隊」！兩位老師精心規劃了滿滿的多元課程，兼顧學術與探索，讓孩子的暑假充實不無聊。

課程涵蓋自然科學實驗，動手做實驗、探索科學奧秘；數位與程式設計，學習程式語言、培養邏輯思維；國際視野與議題探索，拓展世界觀、學習關心社會大小事；美感與文化，包含書法藝術與多元鑑賞；活力體育與 CoolEnglish，動靜皆宜、快樂學英文與展現體能；還有課業輔導與陪伴，溫馨陪伴孩子面對課業疑難雜症。

看著孩子們專注又燦爛的笑容，相信這個暑假一定收穫滿滿、樂趣無窮！
