Summer Art Camp — Life Is Art, From the Ground Up暑期美術營

In this summer's Art Camp, Wansing's children turned their hands into tiny kitchens and their brushes into a jungle. With careful fingers, students molded miniature clay dishes — a bowl of curry rice, a lazy afternoon tea set — each one a tiny, edible-looking sculpture.

Brushes met color just as boldly

Bright flamingos and cheerful frogs came to life on paper, alongside figure drawings that captured bodies in both motion and stillness.

Every child finished camp with a piece that was entirely their own — proof that life itself can be art, and that an eye for beauty can be built one summer at a time.

中文原文 · Chinese

在暑期美術營裡，孩子們用雙手巧妙捏塑出好吃的「咖哩飯」與悠閒的「午茶時光」，每一份都像迷你版的美食雕塑。

彩筆同樣揮灑得大膽——紅鶴、青蛙的可愛模樣躍然紙上，也有動靜分明的人物畫作。

孩子們用愉悅的心情，完成屬於自己獨一無二的作品。生活即藝術，藝術向下扎根。
