Summer Dance Camp — Youth in Motion, Confidence in Bloom萬興暑期舞蹈營｜青春揮灑，自信綻放

With the first beat of music, Wansing's Summer Dance Camp burst into rhythm and energy! Guided by a professional instructor, students sweated through every beat — from basic movements to a full choreographed routine, every moment brought full commitment.

From shy first steps to a room full of smiles

As the days went by, students moved from a shy exploration of movement to letting their bodies fully open up, breaking into bright, unstoppable smiles. Every time the class landed a beat together, every time they finished a routine side by side, the room filled with genuine joy and a real sense of achievement.

Every leaping moment was the purest, most sparkling kind of happiness these children could know.

中文原文 · Chinese

音樂一響，萬興暑期舞蹈營立刻充滿了歡快的律動與動感！孩子們跟著專業老師的節奏揮灑汗水，從基礎的律動到完整的舞蹈編排，每一刻都全情投入。

隨著拍子起舞，孩子們從一開始害羞的摸索，逐漸放開肢體，臉上紛紛洋溢著燦爛無比的笑容。每一次合拍的突破、每一段舞蹈的完成，都讓現場充滿了發自內心的喜悅與成就感！

每一個躍動的瞬間，都是孩子們最純粹、最閃耀的快樂時光。

rhythm/ˈrɪðəm/節奏

Dancers move together to the rhythm of the music.舞者跟著音樂的節奏一起動作。

routine/ruːˈtiːn/（舞蹈）編排

The class learned a full dance routine this summer.這個暑假全班學會了一整套舞蹈編排。

stretch/strɛtʃ/伸展

We stretch our legs before every dance class.每次上舞蹈課前我們都會伸展雙腿。

confident/ˈkɒnfɪdənt/有自信的

Dancing on stage makes students feel more confident.在台上跳舞讓學生更有自信。

teamwork/ˈtiːmwɜːrk/團隊合作

A group dance takes real teamwork to shine.團體舞需要真正的團隊合作才能發光發熱。

Finishing the routine together felt like a big achievement.一起完成編排讓大家覺得很有成就感。
