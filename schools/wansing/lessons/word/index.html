Words on Campus

萬興校園拍攝的單字影片
WORD 001
speakingn.
口說
Speaking means using your voice to share ideas with someone else.
Speaking（口說）是指用聲音把想法說給別人聽。
Every week, Wansing students practice speaking a new English sentence out loud.
每週，萬興的孩子都會練習大聲說出一句新的英文句子。
WORD 002
phonicsn.
自然拼讀法
Phonics is a way of learning English by matching letters to their sounds.
Phonics（自然拼讀法）是一種把字母和發音對應起來學英文的方法。
With phonics, Wansing students can sound out new words on their own.
透過自然拼讀法，萬興的孩子能自己拼出新單字的發音。
WORD 003
essayn.
作文
An essay is a short piece of writing that explains an idea or tells a story.
Essay（作文）是一篇說明想法或故事的短文。
Students at Wansing practice writing an essay in both Chinese and English.
萬興的學生會練習用中文和英文寫作文。
WORD 004
art classn.
美勞課
Art class is the lesson where students draw, paint, and make things with their hands.
Art class（美勞課）是讓學生畫畫、上色、動手做東西的課。
Wansing students look forward to art class every week.
萬興的孩子每週都很期待美勞課。
WORD 005
assemblev.
組裝
To assemble means to put separate pieces together to make one whole thing.
Assemble（組裝）是指把分開的零件組合成一個完整的東西。
In art class, students assemble small parts into a finished model.
在美勞課，學生會把小零件組裝成一個完整的作品。
WORD 006
sculpturen.
雕塑品
A sculpture is a piece of art made by shaping materials like clay, stone, or metal.
雕塑品是用黏土、石頭或金屬等材料塑形而成的藝術作品。
Students in art class turn simple clay into their own small sculpture.
美勞課上，學生把簡單的黏土捏成屬於自己的小雕塑品。
WORD 007
parrotn.
鸚鵡
A parrot is a colorful bird known for copying the words it hears.
鸚鵡是一種色彩鮮豔的鳥，以模仿聽到的話聞名。
Watching a parrot repeat a word is a fun way to learn how sounds work.
看鸚鵡重複一個字，是認識語音有趣的方式。
WORD 008
farmingn.
耕作
Farming is the work of growing crops or raising animals for food.
Farming（耕作）是種植作物或飼養動物以取得食物的工作。
Many families near Wansing still depend on farming for their living.
萬興附近許多家庭至今仍靠耕作維生。
WORD 009
attention to detailn.
注意細節
Attention to detail means noticing the small things that others might miss.
Attention to detail（注意細節）是指留意別人可能忽略的小地方。
Good attention to detail helps students catch mistakes before handing in their work.
良好的注意細節能幫助學生在交作業前就抓出錯誤。
WORD 010
snackn.
點心
A snack is a small amount of food eaten between meals.
Snack（點心）是正餐之間吃的小份食物。
Students bring a snack to eat during morning recess.
學生會帶點心在早上下課時間吃。
WORD 011
tacon.
塔可餅（墨西哥捲餅）
A taco is a Mexican dish made of a folded tortilla filled with meat or vegetables.
Taco（塔可餅）是一種墨西哥料理，把餡料包進對折的玉米餅裡。
Trying a taco is a tasty way to learn about food from another culture.
嚐一口塔可餅，是認識異國飲食文化的美味方式。
WORD 012
professional developmentn.
專業進修
Professional development means learning new skills to do a job even better.
Professional development（專業進修）是指學習新技能，把工作做得更好。
Wansing teachers regularly join professional development to bring new ideas back to their students.
萬興的老師定期參加專業進修，把新的教學方法帶回課堂。
