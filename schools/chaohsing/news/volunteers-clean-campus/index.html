Before the First Day: Volunteers Ready the Campus

開學前，志工老師先把校園掃乾淨
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

The new term had not officially started yet, but Chaohsing's volunteer helpers had already reported back to school. Brooms in hand, they swept up the fallen leaves and tidied the campus inside and out — all for the day the children come back.

新學期還沒正式開始，朝興國小的志工老師已經先回到學校報到。拿起掃把、清掃落葉，把校園裡裡外外整理得乾乾淨淨，只為迎接孩子們回來的那一天。

A simple job like sweeping carries a lot of heart. Loving a school does not have to mean doing something grand. Sometimes it just means being willing, before the first day, to walk one more round and sweep one more time — so the children come back to a clean, comfortable campus.

看似簡單的掃地，背後卻藏著滿滿的心意。愛一所學校，不一定要做什麼轟轟烈烈的大事；有時候，就是願意在開學前多走一步、多掃一遍，讓孩子回來時看見一個乾淨、舒服的校園。

What the children see is a tidy campus. What they feel is that someone has been waiting for them to come home. Thank you to every volunteer helper — with you here, the Chaohsing campus always has a little extra warmth.

孩子看見的，是整潔的校園；孩子感受到的，是有人一直在等他回來。謝謝每一位志工老師，有你們一起，朝興的校園總是多一份溫暖。

This busy work before the term begins is a gentle way of saying: “Welcome home, children.” The new term — we are ready.

開學前的這份忙碌，其實是一種溫柔的告白：「孩子，歡迎回家。」新學期，我們準備好了。
