Hands, Napkins and a Little Magic: Decoupage at Summer Camp

手作蝶古巴特：夏日樂學裡的動手做
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

A single paper napkin and a plain everyday object — a tote bag — slowly became a one-of-a-kind work in the children's hands. Choosing a pattern, planning the design, gluing each piece down, waiting for it to dry: every step was a chance to focus, to try, and to create.

一張餐巾紙、一個生活小物，經過孩子的巧手與創意，慢慢變成獨一無二的作品。從挑選圖案、構思設計，到親手黏貼、等待乾燥，每一個步驟，都是孩子專注、嘗試與創造的過程。

It turns out that learning can be close to everyday life, and full of warmth. In the Summer Fun Learning camp the children worked with their hands together, shared with each other, and discovered something together, too.

原來，學習可以很生活，也可以很有溫度。在「夏日樂學」裡，我們一起動手、一起分享，也一起發現。

When a child is willing to make something, imagination takes on a shape. When a child believes in themselves, creativity becomes possible.

當孩子願意動手做，想像就有了形狀；當孩子相信自己，創意就有了可能。

This summer was not only about learning facts — it let each child carry home a piece of “I can do it” confidence.

這個夏天，不只是學習知識，更讓孩子帶走一份「我做得到」的自信。
