One Gear at a Time: The Gadget King Challenge

一個齒輪一步步：夏日樂學「機關王」課程
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

This year's Summer Fun Learning turned a classroom into a creativity lab, where the children took on the “Gadget King” course — a challenge full of thinking and making.

今年夏日樂學，我們將教室變成了創意實驗室，帶著孩子挑戰充滿思考與創造力的「機關王」課程。

From reading a plan and learning the parts, to building a structure and testing how it runs, every stage tested the children's observation, their logic, and their patience.

從閱讀圖面、認識零件，到組裝結構、測試運作，每一個環節都考驗著孩子的觀察力、邏輯力與耐心。

When a machine would not run, they did not rush to give up. They took it apart, fixed it, and built it again — finding answers inside their mistakes, and gathering experience inside the challenge.

當機關無法順利運作時，他們沒有急著放棄，而是一次次拆解、修正、再組裝，在失敗中找答案，在挑戰中累積經驗。

The future needs more than children who can solve problems. It needs learners who dare to explore, who are brave enough to try, and who are good at working together. This summer, every child became the “Gadget King” of their own learning journey.

未來需要的，不只是會解題的孩子，更是敢於探索、勇於嘗試、善於合作的學習者。這個夏天，我們讓每一位孩子都成為自己學習旅程中的「機關王」。

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

gear/ɡɪr/齒輪

A gear turns another gear.一個齒輪帶動另一個齒輪。

part/pɑːrt/零件

First they learn the name of each part.他們先認識每個零件的名字。

build/bɪld/組裝、搭建

They build a machine from the plan.他們照圖面組裝一台機器。

plan/plæn/圖面、設計圖

Read the plan before you start.開始前先看圖面。

test/test/測試

They test if the machine runs.他們測試機器會不會動。

fix/fɪks/修正、修理

If it breaks, they fix it and try again.壞了就修好再試一次。

patience/ˈpeɪʃns/耐心

Building a machine needs patience.組裝機器需要耐心。

together/təˈɡeðər/一起、合作

They solve the problem together.他們一起解決問題。
