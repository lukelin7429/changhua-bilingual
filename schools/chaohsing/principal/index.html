I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Chaohsing Elementary School is led by Principal Kuo Shu-yuan, a long-serving grassroots educator in Changhua County. Before taking up the post at Chaohsing he served as Director of Academic Affairs at Xihu Elementary School, among other key roles, building a broad base of experience in teaching and in leading a school.

朝興國小由郭書源校長帶領。郭校長長期深耕於彰化縣基層教育，在接任朝興國小校長前，曾於彰化縣溪湖國民小學擔任教務主任等重要職務，累積了豐富的行政與教學領導經驗。

His work at Chaohsing is built around three ideas that belong together: learning that is rooted in place, an education rich in aesthetic literacy, and a commitment to the whole child.

郭校長帶領朝興國小，主要圍繞著「在地化、美感素養與全人教育」的核心理念，讓三件事情彼此支持、缺一不可。

Let children make things with their own hands, so that creativity and life become one.
讓孩子動手創作，落實「創作與生活合一」的理念。
Rooted, creative, whole
在地・美感・全人

Principal Kuo puts his hope for Chaohsing in the everyday work of the school: children who know where they come from, who can make something beautiful, and who are growing up as complete people.

郭校長對朝興的期待，就藏在學校每天的日常裡：讓孩子知道自己從哪裡來，能親手做出美的作品，並且成長為一個完整的人。

II.

Educational Philosophy

辦學理念

Three commitments, one purpose.

三項堅持，一個目的。

在地化 · 美感素養 · 全人教育
Rooted in place — rich in aesthetic literacy — committed to the whole child.
Principal Kuo keeps these three together: an education that starts from the local land, grows a real eye for beauty, and never loses sight of the whole child. 郭校長讓這三件事彼此支持：教育從在地的土地出發，培養真實的美感，並且始終不忘照顧完整的孩子。
其一 · One
Rooted in Place
結合社頭在地的產業意象——芭樂、鳳梨、織襪——讓孩子走入社區踏查，從生活中自發性學習。
其二 · Two
Aesthetic Literacy
以「提升學生的美感力與創作力」為主題，推動藝文課程，讓學生動手創作、落實「創作與生活合一」。
其三 · Three
The Whole Child
重視跨領域的素養學習，融入原住民多元文化與多元族群融合的精神，陪伴每一個孩子完整成長。
III.

Aesthetics, the Arts & the Local Land

藝文深耕與在地食農

Two projects the principal has grown at Chaohsing.

校長在朝興深耕的兩件事。

藝文深耕 · The Arts
Making, Not Just Looking
大力推動學校藝文課程，主張讓學生動手創作；曾親自規劃並帶領學生舉辦畢業美展，融入原住民多元文化與族群融合的教育精神。
LIFE 食農 · The Land
A Course Designed Together
結合社頭在地產業，引導師生共同設計以『LIFE』為主題的食農課程，涵蓋「食安、在地、多樣、農業」核心，重視孩子走入社區踏查。
創作與生活 · Everyday
Creativity and Life as One
把美感教育帶進日常——從繪畫、工藝到田野踏查，讓孩子在生活裡自然地培養美感素養。
From the studio to the orchard
從美勞教室到芭樂園

Under Principal Kuo, learning at Chaohsing moves between the art room and the fields — food safety, guava dolls, pineapple custard, a young-farmer field trip — so that a small rural school connects its children to the traditions around them.

在郭校長帶領下，朝興的學習在美勞教室與田地之間移動——食安小學堂、編織芭樂公仔、鳳梨奶烙、少農踏查——讓一所鄉間小校，把孩子與身邊的傳產緊緊連在一起。

IV.

Leading by Being There

以學生為主體，親自陪伴

The principal is in the classroom too.

校長也站上講台。

Principal Kuo puts students at the centre and backs teaching with action. Alongside his leadership work, he takes open classes of his own — teaching a Grade 2 life-curriculum unit on growing vegetables in water, for example — supporting his teachers by standing where they stand and walking alongside the children as they grow.

郭校長強調以學生為主體，並用行動支持教學。除了行政領導，他也親自參與校內的公開授課——例如親自為二年級學生講授「水耕蔬菜」生活課程——站在老師的位置上支持老師，也一路陪伴孩子成長。

Alongside the children
和孩子在一起

On campus by the guava trees, out on a field trip — Principal Kuo is usually somewhere in the picture, right next to the children.

在校園的芭樂樹下、在校外的踏查路上——郭校長常常就在照片裡，站在孩子旁邊。

Do education well, and it blesses the children — a small rural school, with its heart set on one thing.
辦好教育，造福學子——一所鄉間小校，把心思放在同一件事上。
