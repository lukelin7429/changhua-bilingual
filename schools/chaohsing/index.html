I.

Welcome to Chaohsing

歡迎來到朝興國小

Where tradition meets creativity, and every child is empowered to explore, learn and grow.

傳統與創意在此相遇，每一位孩子都能探索、學習、成長。

Celebrating more than a hundred years of history, Chaohsing Elementary School has been a cornerstone in cultivating talents who contribute to our nation and society. As a well-established rural school in Shetou Township, we take pride in a long-standing tradition of academic and creative excellence.

朝興國小是一所百年歷史的學校，歷經一世紀的歲月，孕育栽培了許多國家社會的人才，不管是對國家、社會或教育，長期以來都貢獻良多。本校地處社頭鄉，屬一般地區小學，長年以學術與創作的卓越傳統為傲。

Our school-based curriculum is uniquely designed to enhance students’ aesthetic appreciation and creative ability. Through arts-and-culture themed teaching, children create works of their own, so that creativity and daily life grow together and an eye for beauty comes naturally.

學校校本課程以提升學生的美感力與創作力為主題，致力於學生藝文涵養與創作能力的提升。以藝文深耕主題教學，讓學生創作自己的作品，落實「創作與生活合一」的理念，自然地陶冶生活中的美感素養。

Tradition meets creativity, and every student is empowered to explore, learn and grow.
見證傳統與創意的結合，每位學生都被賦予探索、學習與成長的能力。

IV.

學校願景

Four words on the stage wall, painted under a rainbow.

司令台彩虹下的八個字，是朝興的願景。

Grow up healthy, learn happily
健康成長・快樂學習・樂觀進取・創新卓越

司令台上，山巒與彩虹之下，大大寫著朝興日常奉行的四句話。它們定調了朝興的一切：健康的童年、快樂的學習、樂觀進取的態度，以及創新求變的自信。

V.

The Arts, Creativity & the Local Land

藝文深耕與在地食農

Two threads run through the whole school: making art, and knowing the land.

兩條主線貫串整所學校：動手創作，以及認識這片土地。

Chaohsing’s curriculum is built to grow children’s aesthetic sense and creative power. Through arts-and-culture themed teaching, students make works of their own — putting the idea that creativity and life belong together into daily practice.

朝興的課程以培養孩子的美感力與創作力為核心。透過藝文深耕的主題教學，學生創作屬於自己的作品，把「創作與生活合一」的理念，落實在每一天的學習裡。

Shetou Township has a clear identity of its own, built on socks and guava. A series of hands-on activities lets children make things with their hands and come to understand the beauty of their home — the “town of stockings” and its orchards. The school’s “LIFE” food-and-farming course is designed together by teachers and students, with units such as the Food-Safety School, Knitting Guava Dolls, Pineapple Milk Custard, Getting to Know Guava, and the Foothill Pineapple Young-Farmer Field Trip. Its goals — innovative, local, diverse, agricultural, food-safe — are shaped to fit children of every age, so that learning about local heritage stays joyful.

社頭鄉的地方意象明確，以襪子與芭樂為發想，學校安排系列活動，讓孩子動手做、認識「絲襪故鄉」與芭樂園之美，落實生活美學。朝興的「LIFE」在地食農教育課程由師生共同參與設計，內容包含「食安小學堂」、「編織芭樂公仔」、「美味鳳梨奶烙」、「認識芭樂你我他」與「山腳鳳梨少農踏查趣」等系統課程，展現「創新、在地、多樣、農業、食安」的學習目標，符合不同年齡層學童的需求，讓孩子從課程中快樂認識在地傳產。

Creativity and life, together
創作與生活合一

From painting and craft to a farm-to-table course rooted in the community, learning at Chaohsing reaches past the classroom — into the studio, the orchard and the fields around the school.

從繪畫、工藝到扎根社區的食農課程，朝興的學習不只待在教室裡——它在美勞教室、在芭樂園、在校園周邊的田地。

VI.

What Makes Chaohsing, Chaohsing

今日朝興的學校特色

Four things the school is known for.

四項讓朝興被記住的事。

A Century of History
百年老校 · 人才輩出

More than a hundred years of education in Shetou, and generations of graduates who have gone on to serve their communities and their country. 在社頭辦學超過一百年，一代又一代的畢業生走向社會，貢獻國家與地方。

Aesthetics & Creation
美感深耕 · 動手創作

A school-based curriculum built around aesthetic appreciation and creative skill, with arts-and-culture themes — from painting to Taiwanese opera — running through every year group. 以美感力與創作力為主題的校本課程，藝文深耕（從繪畫到歌仔戲）貫串各年級。

Socks & Guava — Local Heritage
絲襪故鄉 · 芭樂之鄉

Learning tied to the identity of Shetou: hands-on activities that help children know the “town of stockings” and its guava orchards. 課程扣連社頭的地方意象，用動手活動讓孩子認識絲襪故鄉與芭樂園。

“LIFE” Food & Farming
LIFE 在地食農教育

A food-and-farming course designed by teachers and students together — food safety, guava, pineapple, field trips — innovative, local and diverse. 師生共同設計的食農課程——食安、芭樂、鳳梨、田野踏查——創新、在地而多元。
