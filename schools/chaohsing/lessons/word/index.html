Reading & Stories
閱讀與故事 · 5 words
WORD 01
story listening
聽故事
The teacher models story listening to his students.
這位老師為同學示範「聽故事」的教學方法。
Story listening is a strategy to help students comprehend what they hear.
「聽故事」是幫助學生理解英文的一個策略。
WORD 02
read (v)
閱讀
The student chooses a book to read.
學生選擇一本書閱讀。
The student reads a book.
學生們在讀書。
WORD 03
reading
閱讀
Look at the students: they’re all reading quietly.
看看這群學生，他們閱讀時多麼安靜啊！
Reading often can help you improve your vocabulary and learn many new things.
閱讀經常可以讓你增加字彙並且學到很多新知。
WORD 04
storyteller
說故事的人
A good storyteller teaches a lesson and entertains you at the same time.
一個擅長講說故事的人可以教導大家道理，同時又兼具娛樂大眾。
The storytellers can use real or fictional information to tell a story.
說故事的人可能用真實或者是虛構的情節來說故事。
WORD 05
check out
借書
The students check out books from the library at recess.
同學們利用下課到圖書室借書。
After the librarian checks out the students' books, they can take them home to read.
向圖書館員辦理借閱手續後，同學們可以將書帶回家看。

Arts, Music & Opera
藝術、音樂與戲曲 · 4 words
WORD 06
Taiwanese opera
歌仔戲
The students practice for a Taiwanese opera performance.
學生們練習歌仔戲表演。
They have to work hard for the Taiwanese opera performance.
他們要為歌仔戲表演而努力。
WORD 07
opera (n)
戲劇
The students are practicing traditional opera originally from Taiwan.
學生們正在練習來自台灣的傳統戲劇。
This opera is known as Gezai Opera, and it is known for its beautiful music and expressive singing.
該戲劇被稱為歌仔戲，以優美的音樂和富有表現力的唱腔而聞名。
WORD 08
dance (v)
跳舞
The students dance to beautiful melodies for their Gezai Opera.
學生們隨著優美的旋律翩翩起舞，演繹歌仔戲。
They enjoy practicing dancing with their teachers and classmates.
他們喜歡與老師和同學一起練習舞蹈。
WORD 09
modelling clay
造型黏土
You can use modelling clay to make your very own artwork.
您可以使用造型黏土來製作自己的藝術品。
Modelling clay is easy and fun to use.
造型黏土使用起來既簡單又有趣。

Guava Garden & Farming
芭樂園與食農 · 4 words
WORD 10
guava garden
芭樂園
The school has its own guava garden where students can learn how to take care of plants.
學校有自己的芭樂園，學生可以在那裡學習如何照顧植物。
The students get to work outside, pulling weeds and watering the plants to take care of the guava garden.
學生們開始在戶外工作，拔除雜草並澆灌植物以照料芭樂園。
WORD 11
farmer (n)
農民
The students take a day to experience the life of a farmer.
學生們用一天的時間體驗農民的生活。
They work together to do the daily work of farmers.
他們一起工作，做農民的日常工作。
WORD 12
prune (v)
修剪
The students learn how to prune guava trees.
學生學習如何修剪番石榴樹。
The teachers use pruning shears to prune the guava trees.
老師們用修枝剪修剪番石榴樹。
WORD 13
field trip
戶外教學
The students go on a field trip.
學生們正在進行戶外教學。
They do different activities during their field trip.
他們在戶外教學期間從事不同的活動。

Hands-On & Making
動手做 · 3 words
WORD 14
drill (v)
鑽（孔）
The man drills holes to install wheels on the cart.
這位師傅正在鑽孔，以便在推車上安裝輪子。
The teacher helps him drill the holes.
這位老師幫他鑽孔。
WORD 15
activity (n)
活動
The students are joining a woodworking activity to create their own wooden boxes.
學生們正在參加木工活動，製作自己的木箱。
In this activity, they use sandpaper to make the wood smooth and glue to hold the pieces together.
在這項活動中，他們使用砂紙使木材變得光滑，並使用膠水將碎片固定在一起。
WORD 16
organize (v)
整理
The students organize the shelves.
學生整理書架。
The teacher helps the students organize the shelves.
老師幫助學生整理書架。

School Life & Play
校園生活與遊戲 · 7 words
WORD 17
professional development
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都有教師專業訓練課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
在專業訓練課程中，老師可以學到新的教學技巧與策略。
WORD 18
weekly English
每週英語
The school has a weekly English program.
學校有「每週英語」的方案。
For the weekly English program, the students have to learn a new sentence in English.
這個方案是要學生每個禮拜學講一句英語。
WORD 19
after-school program
課後照顧
The students go to their after-school program when the last class is finished.
在最後一節課結束後，同學們去上課後照顧課程。
They study and do homework in the after-school program.
在課後照顧課程中，他們讀書並且寫功課。
WORD 20
Mother Language Day
母語日
The school has a special day for Mother Language Day to teach students about Taiwanese culture.
學校特別設定一天是母語日，讓學生可以學習台灣的文化。
On Mother Language Day, students learn to say common words in Taiwanese.
母語日當天，同學們學習用台語講一些常用的話。
WORD 21
retirement (n)
退休
The school celebrates the custodian's retirement.
學校慶祝校工退休。
They prepare a cake to celebrate his retirement.
他們準備了一個蛋糕來慶祝他的退休。
WORD 22
basketball practice
籃球訓練
The students are outside for basketball practice.
同學們在外面做籃球訓練。
They work hard during basketball practice.
他們非常認真地做籃球訓練。
WORD 23
board game
桌遊
The students form groups to play a board game.
同學們分組玩桌遊。
They enjoy playing the board game in class.
他們很喜歡在課堂上玩桌遊。
