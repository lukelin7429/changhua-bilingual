Campus Life · 校園生活

A Thank-You for Teachers' Day教師節感恩活動

Chenggong and Xipan Branch held a warm Teachers' Day event. Students designed and made their own gifts and cards, writing heartfelt messages to thank their teachers — then took happy photos together. One student wrote: “Thank you, teacher, for teaching me math every day — and your smile is the best.”

成功與西畔分校舉辦溫馨的教師節活動。學生親手設計、製作禮物與卡片，寫下感謝與祝福的話，再與老師開心合影。一位學生寫道：「謝謝老師每天教我數學，您的笑容最美。」

Campus Life · 校園生活

A Joyful First Day for New Students新生開學日

Our new students had a wonderful first day. They took photos with the principal, received a welcome gift and a storybook, played at fun game stations with a foreign teacher, and learned to wash their hands the right way. At the Xipan Branch, new students walked through the “learning gate” and “safe gate,” struck a big drum, and were warmly helped by older students.

新生度過美好的第一天：與校長合影、領到迎新禮物與故事書、和外師一起闖關遊戲、學習正確洗手。西畔分校的新生走過「學習門」與「平安門」、敲響大鼓，並由學長姐熱情協助。
