I.

In the Spotlight

校園焦點 · 學生主播的英文新聞影片

At Chenggong, the news is read by the children themselves. Our students step up to the camera and report real campus events — in English — turning every school happening into a chance to practise speaking to the world. 在成功，新聞由孩子親自播報。學生站上鏡頭，用英文報導校園裡真實發生的事——把每一場校園活動，變成一次面向世界開口說英語的機會。

Campus Life · 校園生活

A Thank-You for Teachers' Day教師節感恩活動

Chenggong and the Xipan Branch held a warm Teachers' Day celebration that filled the whole school with gratitude. Students designed and made their own gifts and cards by hand, carefully writing heartfelt messages to thank their teachers and wish them well. One child wrote simply, “Thank you, teacher, for teaching me math every day — and your smile is the best.” After handing over their gifts, students and teachers took happy photos together, capturing the warm friendship at the heart of our small school.

成功與西畔分校舉辦溫馨的教師節活動，讓整所學校充滿感謝。學生親手設計、製作禮物與卡片，仔細寫下感謝與祝福；一位孩子寫著：「謝謝老師每天教我數學，您的笑容最美。」送禮後，師生開心合影，記錄下這所小學最珍貴的情誼。

Campus Life · 校園生活

A Joyful First Day for New Students新生開學日

Our newest students had a wonderful first day at school. They took photos with the principal, received a welcome gift and a storybook to spark a love of reading and imagination, played at fun game stations with a foreign teacher, and learned to wash their hands the right way to stay healthy. At the Xipan Branch, the new children walked through the “learning gate” and the “safe gate,” struck a big drum for good luck, and were warmly guided by their older schoolmates — a joyful, unforgettable welcome.

新生度過美好的第一天：與校長合影、領到迎新禮物與故事書（點燃閱讀與想像）、和外師一起闖關遊戲、學習正確洗手保健康。西畔分校的新生走過「學習門」與「平安門」、敲響大鼓祈福，並由學長姐熱情帶領——是歡樂又難忘的迎新。

A Charity Sale for the Graduation Trip六年級義賣籌旅費

Culture · 客家文化

A Hakka Sentence Every Tuesday週二朝會學客語

Hakka culture is part of daily life at Chenggong. At the Tuesday morning assembly, the whole school learns a new Hakka sentence and a Hakka proverb together — keeping the local language alive, one week at a time.

客家文化是成功的日常。每週二朝會，全校一起學一句新客語和一句客家諺語——讓在地語言，一週一週地傳承下去。
