WOTD 01 ▶
film crew (n.)
攝影團隊
"The students show their best talents to the film crew."
學生們向攝影團隊展現他們最好的才華。
WOTD 02 ▶
sentence (n.)
句子
"The students chat with the foreign teacher to practice the weekly sentence."
學生們透過與外籍老師交談，來練習「每週一句」。
WOTD 03 ▶
singing contest (n.)
歌唱比賽
"They are brave on the stage in front of the judges during the singing contest."
在歌唱比賽中，他們在評審面前的舞台上表現得非常勇敢。
WOTD 04 ▶
charity sale (n.)
義賣
"The sixth-grade students held a charity sale to raise money for their graduation trip."
六年級的學生們舉辦了一場義賣，為畢業旅行籌集資金。
WOTD 05 ▶
jump rope competition (n.)
跳繩比賽
"Many students participate in the jump rope competition."
許多學生參加跳繩比賽。
WOTD 06 ▶
meeting (n.)
會議
"Teachers and parents attend the Class Parent-Teacher Meeting for the new semester."
老師和家長們參加了新學期的班級親師座談會。
WOTD 07 ▶
SH150 program (n.)
SH150 方案
"The goal of the SH150 program is to promote regular exercise and better fitness for students."
SH150 方案的目標是促進學生的規律運動與更好的體適能。
WOTD 08 ▶
swimming class (n.)
游泳課
"The students learn how to swim in swimming class."
同學們在游泳課時學習如何游泳。
WOTD 09 ▶
game (n.)
遊戲
"The teacher uses a game to make English class fun and engaging."
老師用遊戲讓英語課變得有趣又吸引人。
WOTD 10 ▶
Hakka (n.)
客家語
"The students learn Hakka proverbs and a new sentence at the Tuesday morning assembly."
學生們在每週二的朝會上，學習一句新的客語和客家諺語。
🪕 A Hakka sentence every Tuesday
At Chenggong, Hakka isn't only a word on a screen. Every Tuesday morning assembly, students learn a new Hakka sentence and a Hakka proverb — and the school's “weekly sentence” in English is practised in real conversations with our foreign teacher.
在成功，客語不只是螢幕上的一個字。每週二朝會，學生會學一句新客語和一句客家諺語；而英語「每週一句」則透過與外師的真實對話來練習。
