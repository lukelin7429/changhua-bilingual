Meet Our Principal

校長室 · 林茂興校長
I.

Principal Lin Mao-hsing

林茂興 校長 · 親自介紹成功國小
林茂興校長親自用中英雙語介紹成功國小 · A bilingual welcome from the Principal
Lin Mao-hsing
林茂興 校長
M.A. Children's Literature · 兒童文學碩士 Ph.D. Language Education · 語文教育博士 Changhua Language Education Leader · 縣語文輔導團召集人

“Hello everyone, I'm Lin Mao-hsing, the Principal of Chenggong Elementary School in Changhua County. It's great to talk about our school today!

We are a remote school by the Zhuoshui River, but our teachers are truly amazing. We offer many special programs beyond regular lessons. Our students learn visual arts, music, and Hakka culture with lion dance. We also have active sports clubs for basketball and dance. We work hard to give our students a rich education and help them shine.

We also focus on English and International Education with great teachers. We want our students to understand their local roots while also seeing the bigger world. Thank you for listening. We are committed to making Chenggong Elementary School a wonderful place for all our students to learn and grow.”

「大家好，我是彰化縣成功國小的林茂興校長。我們學校位於濁水溪畔，雖然位置偏遠，但老師們都非常優秀。除了常規課程外，我們提供許多特色課程——視覺藝術、音樂，以及客家文化（例如舞獅），還有籃球、舞蹈等活躍的體育社團。我們也注重英語與國際教育，希望學生在了解本土文化的同時，也能放眼更廣闊的世界。我們致力於把成功國小打造成一個讓所有孩子都能學習、成長的美好家園。」
II.

Educational Philosophy

辦學理念 · 以學生為核心的四個面向
01
🌱
Child at the Centre
學生為核心的教育實踐
Teaching to each child's strengths, respecting individual differences, and nurturing character and whole-person growth — so every child has a chance to succeed in a happy, healthy, and safe campus.
02
💡
Teaching & Curriculum Innovation
教學與課程創新
Encouraging creative teaching that builds critical and original thinking, blending local culture with technology to develop a distinctive school-based curriculum — and using digital platforms to teach to every learner.
03
🤝
Service-Minded Administration
行政與團隊經營
A warm, efficient administrative team that serves rather than manages — respecting teachers' professionalism, building a culture of peer learning, and leading with honesty and transparent decisions.
04
🏡
Home & Community Partnership
親師社區合作
Open communication and a partnership with families and the community — drawing on the parents' association and local resources to grow the school together, side by side.
"Respect every child's difference, and every child will find a way to shine."
「適性揚才——尊重孩子的個別差異，讓每個孩子都有成功的機會。」
—— 校長 林茂興
III.

A Lifetime in Education

學經歷 · 從萬來國小到成功國小
1990
Began teaching at Wanlai Elementary
台東師專畢業，分發北斗鎮萬來國小服務（民國 79 年）。
2003
M.A. in Children's Literature, NTTU
國立台東大學兒童文學研究所碩士畢業；同年起借調語文領域輔導團專任輔導員。
2004
Qualified as a Director; served at Chaoyang Elementary
考取主任，調溪州鄉潮洋國小擔任教導主任及總務主任（民國 93 年）。
2011
Qualified as a Principal
考取校長，借調縣府學管科（民國 100 年）。
2012
Principal of Qingshan Elementary, Yuanlin
遴選至員林鎮青山國小擔任校長（民國 101 年）。
2014
Ph.D. in Language Education, NTCU
國立臺中教育大學語文教育學系博士畢業，取得語文教學博士學位（民國 103 年）。
2018
Principal of Nanzhou Elementary
遴選至南州國小擔任校長（民國 107 年）；長年擔任新住民輔導團召集人。
Today
Principal of Chenggong Elementary
現任成功國小校長，並擔任彰化縣語文領域輔導團召集人、國語文競賽作文組召集人、新住民語文教學支援人員培訓講師。
