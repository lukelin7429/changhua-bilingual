I.

About Chengkung

關於成功 · 濁水溪北岸的偏鄉小校
A remote school can still open a window to the whole world.
偏遠，不代表看不見世界——成功國小要為孩子打開那扇窗。

Welcome to Chengkung Elementary School, a small rural school on the north bank of the Zhuoshui River in Xizhou Township, Changhua. Together with our Xipan Branch Campus, we are officially listed as a remote school — but our teachers are devoted, and our classrooms are full of music, art, and curiosity.

成功國小坐落於彰化縣溪州鄉、濁水溪（古稱螺溪）北岸，與西畔分校同為列冊偏遠學校。校雖小，師資卻用心，教室裡滿是音樂、藝術與好奇心。

We build our school on two simple pillars — a love of international language learning, and a deep pride in Hakka culture. We want every child to understand their own roots while looking out at the wider world.

我們以兩條主軸經營校務——國際語言學習的熱情，與對客家文化的深厚認同。讓每個孩子在了解自己根源的同時，也能放眼更廣闊的世界。

"Local roots, global eyes."
「扎根在地，放眼國際。」—— 成功國小辦學主軸
II.

Two Pillars

雙軸特色課程 · 國際語言 × 客家文化

Everything at Chengkung grows from two school-based curriculum threads — one reaching outward to the world's languages, one reaching down into the soil of local Hakka tradition.

成功的校本課程由兩條主線交織而成——一條向外伸向世界的語言，一條向下扎進客家的土壤。

PILLAR 01
🌐
International Language Learning
國際語言學習趣 · 英語與世界學習
English and World Learning with dedicated teachers and a foreign teacher — plus a Wednesday-afternoon English club, English camps, and the Little Diplomat Camp that lets rural children practise being citizens of the world.
PILLAR 02
🪕
Hakka Culture
客家文化 · 客語、舞蹈與獅藝
The Hakka language, traditional Hakka dance, and lion dance are woven into school life. Every Tuesday assembly, students learn a new Hakka sentence and a Hakka proverb — and our Xipan Branch earned a top award for Hakka-integrated curriculum.

IV.

Featured Programs

特色課程與社團 · 音樂、藝術、體育、閱讀、營隊
🎶
Harmonica Band
口琴隊 · 週五下午
Every Friday afternoon, the school fills with the sound of the harmonica. Music is part of who we are at Chengkung.
每週五下午口琴隊練習，琴音流淌校園——成功國小的音樂招牌。校歌更由彰化在地音樂家、「台灣囝仔歌之父」施福珍作曲。
🎼 校歌作曲：施福珍
🎨
Art for Everyone
藝術協同課程
Through collaborative art classes, every child becomes a “little artist” — free to splash colour and imagine boldly.
藝術協同課程，讓每一個孩子都是揮灑色彩的小小藝術家。
🛼
Sports Clubs
體育社團 · 直排輪、舞蹈、籃球
Inline skating, dance, and basketball clubs give every child a way to move, compete, and build confidence after class.
直排輪、舞蹈、籃球社團，加上週三下午多元社團與課後照顧班，讓孩子放學後也能盡情運動、培養自信。
🏕️
Holiday Camps
寒暑假營隊 · 含小小外交官營
During the holidays, students join reading & writing camps, English camps, Hakka-language camps, harmonica camps, and the international Little Diplomat Camp.
寒暑假開設閱讀寫作營、英語營、客語營、口琴營，以及國際教育「小小外交官營」，讓學習不打烊。
V.

A School That Sings

校歌與客家歌 · 田中央的歌聲
Songs of Chengkung · 成功之聲

"𠊎个學校在田中央" "My School in the Middle of the Fields" — a Hakka song of our own

Our school song opens with the words "Mount Jade stands tall, the Luo River runs long…" — written in 1968 and set to music by Shih Fu-chen, the beloved Changhua composer known as the “father of Taiwanese children's songs.” The Xipan Branch even has a school song of its own. 校歌以「玉山高聳　螺溪長流　學堂莊嚴　歷史悠久」開篇，創作於民國 57 年，由彰化在地、人稱「台灣囝仔歌之父」的施福珍作曲；西畔分校另有專屬校歌。 Most recently, we teamed up with Golden-Melody Hakka singer-songwriter Yen Chih-wen to create an original Hakka song — “𠊎个學校在田中央” (My School in the Middle of the Fields) — celebrating a small school surrounded by rice paddies and Hakka heart. 近期更與客家金曲創作歌手顏志文合作，創作客語歌〈𠊎个學校在田中央〉，唱出田園環抱、客家情濃的成功國小。
