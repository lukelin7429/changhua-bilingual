A Bilingual Camp for Our Youngest

太平國小低年級雙語營圓滿落幕！新生快樂學英語！
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

To help the incoming first-graders get to know the campus, Taiping ran a lively summer bilingual camp on 26 and 27 August. Teacher Gan Yao-hua and foreign English teacher Jovel joined forces to walk the children through their very first steps in bilingual learning — with a lot of laughing along the way. 🎉

為了讓即將入學的一年級寶貝們熟悉校園，學校在 8/26、8/27 舉辦了超熱鬧的暑期雙語營！由甘耀華老師與外師 Jovel 老師強強聯手，帶領孩子們在歡笑中跨出雙語學習的第一步！

The two days were packed:

兩天的精彩課程包羅萬象：

8/26 — Living & Non-living Things. The children listened to a story, played a sorting game, and made their own very cute paper animals. 🐰

8/26 生物與非生物（Living & Non-living Things）：聽故事、玩分類遊戲，還動手做了超可愛的紙動物手作 🐰

8/27 — The Five Senses. They sang and danced a five-senses song, then took on the exciting Mystery Bag adventure and a relay race. 🐯

8/27 五感體驗（Five Senses）：唱唱跳跳五感歌、挑戰刺激的神秘袋大冒險與接力賽 🐯

Through crafts, stories and team games, the children not only found the courage to speak English — they also made new friends and got to know a new place. Looking at all those smiles, we are sure our little ones can hardly wait to start their exciting life as elementary-school students!

透過手作、故事與團隊遊戲，孩子們不只勇敢開口說英文，更認識了新朋友與新環境。看著大家滿滿的笑容，相信寶貝們已經迫不及待要開啟精采的小學生活啦！

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

sense/sens/感官

We have five senses: sight, hearing, smell, taste and touch.我們有五種感官：視、聽、嗅、味、觸。

living/ˈlɪvɪŋ/有生命的

A dog is living; a rock is not.狗是有生命的，石頭不是。

sort/sɔːrt/分類

The children sort the cards into two groups.孩子們把卡片分類成兩組。

craft/kræft/手作、勞作

They made a paper animal in the craft activity.他們在手作活動中做了紙動物。

story/ˈstɔːri/故事

The teacher read a story before the game.老師在遊戲前念了一個故事。

brave/breɪv/勇敢

The children were brave enough to speak English.孩子們勇敢開口說英文。
