Socks of Filial Love

孝親愛心襪，讓愛從生活開始
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Our thanks go to the Changhua County Federation of School Parents’ Associations, whose chair led a team to Taiping Elementary in person to deliver a gift full of love — the “Socks of Filial Love.” Every carefully chosen pair carries the softest care for our children, and plants a seed of love for the filial-piety experience that is about to begin.

感謝彰化縣各級學校家長會聯合會由理事長帶領團隊，親自將滿滿愛心的「孝親愛心襪」送到太平國小！每一雙細緻的襪子，都包裹著對孩子們最柔軟的關懷，也為即將展開的孝親體驗播下了愛的種子。

The school will use this warm gift in character education, in welcome activities and during Grandparents’ Week. We hope that through one small action — bending down to put socks on an elder — the two generations can be drawn a little closer, and children can practise gratitude and filial care in everyday life.

學校將會把這份溫暖的禮物運用於品格教育、迎新活動及祖孫週中。我們期盼透過「彎下腰為長輩穿襪」的小小動作，拉近兩代人的距離，讓孩子在生活中實踐感恩與孝心。

When the feet are warm, the heart is warm too. May these socks of love walk beside our children — and may that love begin in ordinary life.

腳暖了，心就熱了！願這雙愛心襪陪伴孩子們，穿出幸福，讓愛從生活中開始！

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen · 點喇叭聽發音

socks/sɑːks/襪子

The children put warm socks on their grandparents.孩子們為阿公阿嬤穿上溫暖的襪子。

gratitude/ˈɡrætɪtuːd/感恩

We say thank you to show our gratitude.我們說謝謝，表達感恩。

gift/ɡɪft/禮物

The parents’ group brought a gift of love.家長會帶來一份充滿愛的禮物。

warm/wɔːrm/溫暖

When the feet are warm, the heart is warm too.腳暖了，心就熱了。

respect/rɪˈspekt/尊敬、孝敬

Putting on socks for an elder is a way to show respect.為長輩穿襪，是一種孝敬的方式。

elder/ˈeldər/長輩

The children bend down to help an elder.孩子彎下腰幫助長輩。
