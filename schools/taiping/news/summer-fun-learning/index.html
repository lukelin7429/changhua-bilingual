A Summer That Is Not Left Blank

太平國小夏日樂學：讓孩子的暑假不留白
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Taiping is a good school, and a good school makes sure the summer is not left blank.

太平國小是一所優質的學校，優質學校讓孩子暑假不留白。

The Summer Fun Learning programme brought the children close to nature with tree climbing, building the grit to keep pushing upward. A flower-arranging class sharpened their eye for beauty and gave everyday life more taste; a Zentangle art class grew their artistic sense; a street-dance class made childhood a little more colourful; a daily-English class carried them a step towards the wider world; games sparked their creativity and their sense of teamwork; and in the baking class, the dorayaki that came out of the oven had everyone thinking of Doraemon…

夏日樂學活動攀樹讓孩子接近大自然，培養奮發向上的毅力；花藝課程提升美感使生活更有品味；禪繞美學課培養藝術涵養；流行舞蹈使學生童年更精彩；生活英語課使我們邁向國際化；趣味遊戲激發孩子創意與群性；烘焙課裡的銅鑼燒使人想起哆啦 A 夢……

There was also a visit to Dongtian Temple woven together with digital learning, so that children who grow up here do not forget the feeling of their hometown.

還有參觀東天宮融入數位學習，在地孩子不忘鄉土情。

Our thanks to an outstanding team of teachers and a professional administrative team. Taiping children — I just want to make you even better!

感謝卓越的師資群、專業的行政團隊。太平孩子，我就是要讓你們更優質！
