Music & Clubs
音樂與社團 · 5 字
WORD 01
lion dance (n)
舞獅
The coach is teaching moves in the lion dance.
教練正在教舞獅動作。
The lion dance is a very popular and traditional performance.
舞獅是一種非常流行的傳統表演。
WORD 02
ocarina (n)
陶笛
These students are playing the ocarina.
這些學生正在吹陶笛。
The ocarina is a musical wind instrument.
陶笛是一種管樂器。
WORD 03
martial arts (n)
武術
The teacher leads the students in their martial arts lesson.
老師在武術課中帶領學生做動作。
The students practice different martial arts moves.
學生們練習不同武術的招式。
WORD 04
paint (v)
畫
The students paint the lion face on the lantern.
學生們在燈籠上畫獅子的臉。
The students use different colors to paint.
學生們用不同的顏色作畫。
WORD 05
performance (n)
表演
The students put on a performance at a retirement home.
學生們在老人之家做表演。
In the performance, the students dance, sing, and play instruments.
表演中，學生們跳舞、唱歌與演奏樂器。

Sports & Movement
運動與律動 · 9 字
WORD 06
hula hoop (n)
呼拉圈
The students are playing hula hoops.
學生們正在搖呼拉圈。
Hula hoops are round and colorful toys.
呼拉圈是圓圓的、彩色的玩具。
WORD 07
jump rope (v)
跳繩
The kids jump rope to get some exercise.
孩子們跳繩鍛鍊身體。
When the weather is nice, you can go outside to jump rope.
天氣好的時候，還可以出去跳繩呢。
WORD 08
stretch (v)
伸展
The students are doing their stretching exercise.
學生們正在做伸展運動。
Stretching helps muscles and joints stay healthy.
伸展有助於肌肉和關節保持健康。
WORD 09
rollerblade (v)
溜直排輪
These students are practicing to rollerblade.
這些學生正在練習溜直排輪。
Rollerblading can be for fun and also for exercise.
溜直排輪可以純粹是為了好玩，也可以作為運動。
WORD 10
ball control (n)
控球
This student is practicing his ball control.
這位學生正在練習控球。
In a basketball game, ball control is very important.
在籃球賽中，控球是非常重要的。
WORD 11
shoot hoops
投籃
Shooting hoops is slang for playing basketball.
「投籃」是打籃球的俚語。
Shooting hoops is just shooting baskets for fun.
投籃只是為了好玩而投籃。
WORD 12
pickup game (n)
籃球鬥牛賽
The students are playing a pickup game of basketball.
學生們正在打籃球鬥牛賽。
A pickup game is an unofficial game played for fun.
鬥牛賽是非正式、純好玩的一種比賽。
WORD 13
swimming (n)
游泳
The teacher leads a warm-up before swimming.
老師帶學生在游泳前熱身。
The students use a kick board to practice swimming.
同學們用浮板練習游泳。
WORD 14
hiking (n)
健行
Hiking in the mountains is very beautiful.
在山區健行景色很美麗。
Hiking the trails is good exercise.
在步道健行是很好的運動。

A Healthy, Tidy School
健康與整潔 · 5 字
WORD 15
wash your hands
洗手
It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
吃東西之前，記得要用香皂和水洗手。
WORD 16
upstairs / downstairs (adv)
上下樓梯
Be careful when you walk upstairs or downstairs.
上下樓梯要小心。
You can hold onto the handrail when you walk upstairs or downstairs.
上下樓梯時可以握著扶手。
WORD 17
rake (n)
耙子
A rake is a tool used to pick up fallen leaves.
耙子是用來拾起落葉的工具。
The students are using the rakes to clean up the school grounds.
學生們正在用耙子清理校園。
WORD 18
eraser (n)
板擦
The students are cleaning the board with an eraser.
學生們正在用板擦擦黑板。
They then clean the eraser with a cleaning machine.
然後他們用板擦機清潔板擦。
WORD 19
cross (v)
過馬路
Watch out for students crossing the road.
當心正在過馬路的學生。
Crossing guards stop traffic to let students cross the street safely.
交通導護攔下車輛，讓學生安全過馬路。

Watering plants is called gardening.
澆灌植物叫做園藝。

The students are in geometry class.
學生們正在上幾何課。
Geometry is a study of shapes.
幾何學是對形狀的研究。

長度
Length is a unit of measurement.
長度是測量的單位。
The students are learning how to measure length.
學生們正在學習如何測量長度。
WORD 23
insect (n)
昆蟲
The students are studying insects.
這些學生正在研究昆蟲。
The teacher is showing different types of insects.
這位老師展示不同種類的昆蟲。
WORD 24
Earth (n)
地球
Earth is the planet we live on.
地球是我們居住的星球。
On Earth Day, the teacher shows her students ways to take care of the Earth.
在世界地球日當天，老師為學生展示愛護地球的各種方式。
WORD 25
train (n)
火車
The class goes to the train station to buy tickets for the train.
這個班級到火車站買車票搭火車。
Once they have their tickets, they wait on the platform for the train to come.
拿到車票後，他們在月台等待火車到站。

The student chooses a book to read.
學生選擇一本書閱讀。

WORD 27
read-aloud competition
朗讀比賽
The students practice for their read-aloud competition.
學生為朗讀比賽練習。
The teacher helps the students prepare for their read-aloud competition.

storyteller (n)
講故事的人
A good storyteller teaches a lesson and entertains you at the same time.
擅長講故事的人可以教導道理，同時又兼具娛樂效果。
The storyteller can use real or fictional information to tell a story.

Each student performs in a storytelling contest.

The teachers grade students' reading in the storytelling contest.
老師們在說故事比賽中為學生打成績。

Around School
校園生活 · 2 字
WORD 30
playground (n)
遊樂場
These students are enjoying using the playground equipment.
這些學生正在享受使用遊樂場設備的樂趣。
Playground equipment includes swings, slides, monkey bars, and climbing pipes.
遊樂場設備包括鞦韆、滑梯、單槓和攀爬管。
WORD 31
professional development
教師專業進修
In Taiwan, all elementary schools have professional development on Wednesday afternoons.
在台灣，所有小學老師在週三下午有專業進修。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development.
老師在專業進修時學習新技能與教學策略。
