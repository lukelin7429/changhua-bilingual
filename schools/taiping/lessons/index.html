Ways to Learn

從校園生活學英文

Real school, real words.

每支影片、每份教材，都取材自太平真實的校園日常。

31 Videos · 6 Themes
Course 01
Word of the Day
每日一字

Thirty-one bilingual videos filmed around the Taiping campus and grouped into six themes — music and clubs, sports and movement, a healthy and tidy school, explore and discover, reading and storytelling, and school life. Each word is embedded straight from YouTube with two example sentences taken from the video description.31 支雙語影片，全部在太平校園實地拍攝，依六大主題分類——音樂與社團、運動與律動、健康與整潔、探索與發現、閱讀與表達，以及校園生活。每個單字直接嵌入影片，並附兩句取自影片說明欄的例句。

Start Learning →
30 Episodes · For Teachers
Course 02
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers, taught by Sarah Thomas across 10 topics and 30 numbered episodes — from greetings and roll call to exam instructions. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.給老師使用的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學，共 10 大主題、30 支編號影片——從問候點名到考試指令。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

Start Learning →
13 Episodes · By Office
Course 03
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

"Broadcast English" — thirteen short episodes in which each school office and section introduces, in English, the announcements it makes over the public-address system, from the curriculum section to the counselling room. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.「廣播英語」——13 支短片，由各處室、各組用英語介紹平時透過校園廣播宣布的內容，從教務處教學組到輔導室。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

Start Learning →
Foreign English Teacher
Course 04
Meet Our Foreign English Teacher
認識我們的外師

Taiping's foreign English teacher, Jovel Morbos Capitle, keeps a class website of songs, stories, games and lesson materials for students and families. It opens in a new tab.太平的外籍英語教師 Jovel Morbos Capitle 老師，經營一個班級網站，放了歌曲、故事、遊戲與課堂教材，供學生與家長使用。點擊後在新分頁開啟。
