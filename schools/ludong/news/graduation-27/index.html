Farewell, Class of 27

鹿東國小第27屆畢業典禮

Prelude · 前曲

Never forgetting the stars, running toward the future. Before the ceremony officially began, the graduating class gathered once more on the school field for their final student morning assembly, raising the flag together one last time. Younger students offered blessings to every graduate and pinned corsages onto their uniforms, while graduates presented bouquets of thanks to their teachers. Led by the principal, the PTA chair, the guidance-council chair, and the school's directors and teachers, the graduates took one more deep, quiet moment to feel — and say goodbye to — the campus that had been their home for six years.

Highlights · 高光時刻

Thank you to the county government officials representing the County Magistrate, the county councillors, Lukang Township Mayor Hsu, elected representatives at every level, village chiefs, and the principals and directors from nearby schools who came to offer their blessings. Thank you to PTA Chair Yang Hong-kai, Guidance Council Chair Nien Yu-jui, and the entire parent committee for supporting and organizing this important day for our graduates. Thank you to the principal, the directors, and every teacher who walked alongside our sixth graders through this important chapter — and thank you to the families whose encouragement, care, and sacrifice carried each graduate here. This was the graduating class's moment to shine, carried by everyone's most sincere wishes. In this moment of honor, thank you to everyone who presented awards and gave these children their shining light. Ludong's 27th graduation ceremony sparkled like starlight.

Farewell · 珍重再見

Dear graduates of Ludong Elementary's 27th class — carry with you every precious moment of these six years. We wish you a journey of ten thousand miles, wings wide open, ready to soar, shining brightly in the future as the brightest star in the sky. With warm wishes from all the teachers and students of Ludong Elementary.

中文原文 · Chinese

《鹿東國小第27屆畢業典禮，前曲》

恆星不忘，奔向未來。在典禮正式開始前，再站在操場上，我們升旗開始，複習畢業前最後一次的學生朝會。讓學弟學妹們為全體畢業生獻上祝福，戴上畢業生的胸花、為師長獻上感恩的花束，在校長、家長會長、輔導會長及主任師長們的帶領下，再一次對朝夕相處六年的校園，深深的感受與道別。

《鹿東國小第27屆畢業典禮，高光時刻》

恆星不忘，奔向未來。感謝代表縣長為全體畢業生祝福的縣府長官、議員、鹿港鎮大家長許鎮長、各級民意代表、里長、各校校長主任等貴賓的蒞臨祝福！感謝楊洪凱家長會長、粘峪瑞輔導會長及全體家長委員會為畢業生的重要時刻，支援活動的辦理與執行！感謝校長、主任、全體師長們的陪伴與參與畢業生們六年級來重要時光！謝謝畢業生身後鼓勵、照護與付出的家人們！全體畢業生們的高光時刻，有大家最誠摯的祝福。在這榮耀的一刻，感謝所有頒獎人惠賜孩子們閃耀的光芒！鹿東國小第27屆畢業典禮，星光閃耀。

《離情依依，珍重再見》

親愛的鹿東國小第27屆畢業生，帶著這六年來珍貴的點滴，祝福你們～鵬程萬里 展翅翱翔，在未來發光發熱，成為天空中最閃亮的星。～鹿東國小全體師長與在校生 敬祝～
