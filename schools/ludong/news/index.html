Culture Research · 網界博覽會

Platinum Award for a Lukang Culture Project2026 國際學校網界博覽會白金獎

May 6, 2026 · 2026 年 5 月 6 日

Ludong students earned a Platinum Award in the local specialty category of the 2026 International Schools CyberFair. The honor continues a strong tradition of turning Lukang's local culture into student research that can be read by the wider world.

鹿東學生在 2026 國際學校網界博覽會「地方特產特色類」獲白金獎。這份成果延續學校長期以鹿港文化做學生研究、並向國際讀者介紹在地故事的傳統。

Roller Skating · 溜冰

National President's Cup Roller-Skating Success總統盃全國小學溜冰錦標賽

May 6, 2026 · 2026 年 5 月 6 日

At the 23rd President's Cup National Elementary Roller-Skating Championship, Ludong students recorded results across sprint races, relay races, time trials, and elimination events. One student placed first in a 600-meter sprint, while others earned podium and top-eight finishes.

鹿東學生於第 23 屆總統盃全國小學溜冰錦標賽，在爭先賽、接力賽、雙人計時賽與淘汰賽等項目皆有佳績，其中包含 600 公尺爭先賽第 1 名及多項前八名成績。

Dance · 舞蹈

Outstanding Honors in the National Student Dance Competition全國學生舞蹈比賽特優

May 6, 2026 · 2026 年 5 月 6 日

Ludong dancers received outstanding recognition in both modern dance and classical dance in the elementary group of the National Student Dance Competition. The results show disciplined practice, teamwork, and the confidence to perform on a national stage.

鹿東舞蹈團隊在 114 學年度全國學生舞蹈比賽國小組，於現代舞與古典舞皆獲特優肯定，展現長期練習、團隊合作與站上全國舞台的自信。

Art · 美術

Young Artists Recognized Nationally全國學生美術比賽

Mar 19, 2026 · 2026 年 3 月 19 日

The school honor roll also records recognition in the National Student Art Competition. For Ludong, art is more than a contest result: it is one way students learn to observe carefully, express clearly, and make sense of the world around them.

鹿東榮譽榜亦列有全國學生美術比賽成果。對學校而言，美術不只是競賽名次，也是孩子學習觀察、表達與理解世界的重要途徑。

Language · 本土語言

Learning Language Through Taiwanese Taigi全國語文競賽台灣台語字音字形

Mar 19, 2026 · 2026 年 3 月 19 日

Ludong's language-learning work includes recognition in Taiwanese Taigi phonetics and character-form competition. In a town rich with local memory, language learning helps students hear culture more deeply and speak about home with pride.

鹿東在台灣台語字音字形相關競賽中獲肯定。位於文化底蘊深厚的鹿港，語言學習讓孩子更深入聽見文化，也更有能力說出家鄉。

Science · 科學探究

Science Fair Learning Builds Curiosity彰化縣中小學科學展覽會

May 6, 2025 · 2025 年 5 月 6 日

Ludong's county science-fair participation reflects the same habit that drives its culture projects: ask a good question, gather evidence, explain what you found, and share it with others. Science gives students another language for careful thinking.

鹿東參與彰化縣中小學科學展覽會，延續學校重視探究的精神：提出問題、蒐集證據、說明發現，並與他人分享。科學讓孩子用另一種方式練習清楚思考。
