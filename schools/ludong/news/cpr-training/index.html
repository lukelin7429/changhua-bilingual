A Gift of Health

健康禮物．CPR急救訓練

Thank you to the Ludong PTA for funding this program, and to Changhua Christian Hospital for providing medical staff and technical support! Before their graduation ceremony, sixth graders took on one more important lesson: CPR and first-aid training, learning the knowledge and hands-on skills for cardiopulmonary resuscitation. We wish every student success in earning this important certification.

Thank you to nurses Yu-chun and Paige for leading fifth and sixth graders through health topics including menstrual equity and how to use National Health Insurance. Thank you to Director Bo-fu, and team leads Hsin-yun, Li-wen, and Wei-cheng for handling logistics, with every sixth-grade homeroom teacher staying by their students' side throughout.

As the countdown to graduation continues, the children's bags — and hearts — keep filling up.

中文原文 · Chinese

感謝鹿東國小家長會支持經費、彰化基督教醫院醫護技術支援！六年級畢業典禮前重要急救技術學習，CPR急救知識及技術訓練，預祝每一位同學成功取得重要技術的證照。

謝謝鈺純、佩奇護理師引領五六年級的同學們認識月經平權、健保使用等健康知識！謝謝柏富主任、心韻、俐妏、韋程組長庶務工作支援！六年級各班師長全時陪伴指導。

畢業倒數做中，孩子們的行囊充實飽滿。
