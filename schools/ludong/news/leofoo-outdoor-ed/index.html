Making Memories Before Graduation

六年級戶外教學．畢業前美好的回憶

After finishing their graduation exams, the sixth grade set off on one more trip together — this time to an outdoor adventure training ground in the mountains of northern Taiwan, with rock climbing, tightrope walking, orienteering, and more, followed by a high-energy evening program.

Thank you to every sixth-grade teacher for watching over each child from start to finish, and to the principal and directors for joining along the way.

The anticipation before the trip, the teamwork and encouragement during each activity, and the memories built together through play — thank you to everyone who has walked alongside us through this stage of growing up.

In these final countdown days before graduation, more and more happy memories keep being added. Youth is brighter with you in it.

中文原文 · Chinese

六年級選定畢業考後再出發去旅行，北部山區的體能訓練場，有攀岩、有走繩、有定向⋯⋯，還有熱力十足的晚會活動。謝謝六年級師長們看前顧後的照顧著每一位孩子！謝謝校長、主任們的陪伴！

行前的期待、活動中互相合作和支持、遊戲中一起創造美好回憶，謝謝成長過程中每一位陪伴我們的重要他人！

在畢業前倒數的日子裡，不斷的注入充滿幸福的記憶，青春有你，精彩無比。
