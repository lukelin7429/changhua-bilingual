Principal Hsieh Hsiu-hua

謝秀華校長
I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Hsieh Hsiu-hua brings to Ludong a long record of classroom teaching, school leadership, curriculum supervision, and educational innovation in Changhua County. Before leading Ludong Elementary School, she served in elementary schools and in the county education office, building experience across teaching, administration, curriculum, reading, outdoor education, and international education.

謝秀華校長具備豐富的教學、行政、課程督學與教育創新經驗。來到鹿東之前，她曾任國小教師、組長、主任、校長及彰化縣政府教育處課程督學，長期投入課程、閱讀、戶外教育與國際教育。

Her vision begins with children. In a fast-changing world, she believes students need the basic abilities to listen, speak, read, write, and think clearly. Just as important, they need character: courtesy, optimism, effort, curiosity, and the courage to explore what they do not yet know.

她的辦學理念以孩子為中心。面對快速變化的世界，孩子需要聽、說、讀、寫與邏輯思考等基本能力，也需要良好的品格：有禮、樂觀、努力、好奇，並勇於探索未知。

At Ludong, Principal Hsieh works toward a safe, caring, professional campus where teachers, parents, community partners, and outside resources come together. The goal is not only to manage a school well, but to build a strong educational network around every child.

在鹿東，謝校長重視安全友善的校園環境，也重視專業與照顧並行的行政團隊。她期待整合校內外資源，讓教師、家長、社區與外部支持共同成為孩子成長的教育網絡。

"A school is where children spend the longest hours outside their homes. It must be safe, caring, and worthy of their growth."
學校是孩子在家庭以外停留時間最長的地方，因此安全、友善與專業照顧，是教育的基礎。
