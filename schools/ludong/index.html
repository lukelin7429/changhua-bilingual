I.

Welcome

歡迎來到鹿東國小

A Lukang school where local culture becomes a doorway to the wider world.

一所立足鹿港文化、引導孩子走向世界的學校。

Ludong Elementary School is located in Lukang, one of Taiwan's best-known historic towns. Founded in 1998, Ludong grew from a newly established campus into a lively learning community serving families across the Ludong, Dingcuo, Luhe and nearby neighborhoods.

鹿東國小位於臺灣重要歷史文化城鎮鹿港。學校創立於民國 87 年（1998），從新設校園逐步發展成服務鹿東、頂厝、鹿和及周邊社區的重要學習據點。

The school's story is deeply connected to its place. Around Ludong are temples, traditional crafts, foodways, old houses, Nanguan music, and a living community memory. For students, Lukang is not only a hometown; it is also a rich classroom for research, language, art, and civic imagination.

鹿東的故事與土地緊密相連。鹿港有廟宇、傳統工藝、在地飲食、老屋、南管音樂與豐富的社區記憶。對孩子而言，鹿港不只是家鄉，也是研究、語言、藝術與公民想像的真實教室。

II.

What Makes Ludong

鹿東的特色
🏮
Rooted in Lukang
鹿港在地文化

Students learn from the town around them: traditional food, craft, old houses, temples, and community stories.

📚
Reading & Inquiry
閱讀與探究

Reading platforms, library resources, and student research projects help children turn curiosity into deeper learning.

🌿
Campus Nature
校園自然探索

Campus plants, animal identification, tree maps, and outdoor spaces make nature a visible part of daily school life.

🌏
Bilingual Pathways
雙語學習路徑

Classroom English, bilingual announcements, and video-based learning resources give students steady exposure to practical English.

From Lukang traditions to global classrooms
從鹿港傳統，到世界教室

Ludong students have repeatedly turned Lukang culture into international student research: cypress straw hats, turtle-shaped rice cakes, tea, old houses, Nanguan music, paper pavilions, and environmental questions have all become windows for learning in English.
