Butterflies, Rice & the Land
蝴稻螺陽 · 生態與食農 · 4 字
WORD 01
butterfly (n)
蝴蝶
Butterflies are colorful insects.
蝴蝶是五顏六色的昆蟲。
Students enjoy visiting the butterfly garden.
同學們很喜歡參觀蝴蝶園。
WORD 02
rice (n)
稻米
Rice is an important part of our diet.
米是我們飲食重要的一部份。
The students pick weeds in the rice field.
同學們在稻田除草。
WORD 03
rice field
稻田
The students take a field trip to experience life on a rice field.
學生們參加了校外教學，體驗稻田生活。
They wear gloves and use special tools to cut the rice stalks by themselves.
他們戴著手套，用專用工具自己割稻稈。
WORD 04
compost (n)
堆肥
The students add leaves to the compost bin.
學生將落葉放入堆肥槽。
The students use rakes to mix the compost.
學生用耙子翻攪堆肥。
🎻
Music & Performance
音樂與表演 · 6 字
WORD 05
recorder ensemble (n)
直笛團隊
The school's recorder ensemble is playing a song.
學校直笛團正在表演一首歌。
Students in the recorder ensemble play different sized recorders.
直笛團的學生吹奏不同大小的直笛。
WORD 06
violin (n)
小提琴
Several students are playing the violin.
有好幾位學生在演奏小提琴。
The violin is difficult to learn.
小提琴很難學。
WORD 07
conductor (n)
指揮
The conductor's job is to direct the musicians.
指揮的工作是引導音樂表演者。
The conductor stands facing the musicians.
指揮站立的方向是面對音樂表演者。
WORD 08
dancer (n)
舞者
A group of young dancers are dancing on stage.
一群小舞者在舞台上跳舞。
People are amazed by the young dancers' performance.
觀眾對小舞者的表現印象深刻。
WORD 09
dance (n)
舞蹈
The dancers teach students dance moves.
舞者教學生舞蹈動作。
The students try different dance moves.
學生們嘗試不同的舞蹈動作。
WORD 10
skit (n)
短劇
The students perform skits about Renaissance art.
學生表演有關文藝復興藝術品的短劇。
Skits are short and fun performances that can tell stories.
短劇是簡短有趣，又可以述說故事的表演。
🎨
Art & Making
藝術與手作 · 3 字
WORD 11
artwork (n)
藝術作品
The students share facts about the artwork.
學生分享藝術作品的相關資訊。
There are different pieces of artwork in the room.
展示間有很多的藝術作品。
WORD 12
costume (n)
戲服
The students are wearing costumes for their skit.
學生為短劇穿戲服。
Costumes show who the students are in the skit.
戲服可以顯示學生在短劇中扮演的角色。
WORD 13
cookie cutter (n)
餅乾模具
The students cut out cookies with cookie cutters.
學生們用餅乾模具切出餅乾。
Cookie cutters can be different shapes.
餅乾模具可以有不同的形狀。

Sports & Movement
運動與體能 · 5 字
WORD 14
soccer (n)
足球
Soccer is a popular sport around the world.
足球是世界上非常流行的運動。
The students are playing in a soccer game.
學生正在進行一場足球賽。
WORD 15
freestyle football
花式足球
A professional football coach gives a demonstration of freestyle football.
專業足球教練示範花式足球。
He performs various neat ball-tricks for the students to learn using different parts of the body.
他為學生們表演各種巧妙的球技巧，讓他們學習使用身體的不同部位。
WORD 16
jump rope
跳繩
Jump rope is a good way to stay active and healthy.
跳繩是保持活躍與健康的好方式。
The students enjoy playing jump rope at recess.
同學們喜歡在下課時間跳繩。
WORD 17
stretch (v)
伸展
It's important to stretch for good physical health.
伸展對於身體健康很重要。
After the students stretch, they will exercise.
在同學們伸展後，他們就會運動。
WORD 18
coordinate (v)
協調
The students practice coordinating in their PE class.
同學們在體育課練習身體協調性。
The students have to coordinate their feet when moving.
同學們在雙腳移動時必須有協調性。

Campus Life
校園生活 · 5 字
WORD 19
morning assembly (n)
朝會
The teacher goes through the lost and found at the morning assembly.
老師在朝會期間讓同學失物招領。
The principal presents awards at the morning assembly.
校長在朝會期間頒獎。
WORD 20
gather (v)
聚集
In the morning, the students gather in the courtyard.
同學們在早上時在校園內集合。
After they gather, the teacher gives announcements.
在他們集合之後，老師宣布重要訊息。
WORD 21
flag (n)
旗
The national flag is raised in the morning.
國旗在早上升起。
The school band provides music for the flag-raising ceremony.
學校樂隊為升旗儀式演奏音樂。
WORD 22
read (v)
閱讀
The students go to the library to read books.
學生們去圖書館看書。
They quietly read their books at a table.
他們在一張桌子旁靜靜地看書。
WORD 23
professional development (n)
專業進修
In Taiwan, all elementary schools have professional development on Wednesday afternoons.
台灣所有小學老師在週三下午有專業進修。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development.
老師在專業進修時學習新技能與教學策略。

Traditions & Fun
傳統與趣味 · 2 字
WORD 24
lion dance (n)
舞獅
The lion dance is a traditional dance performed during the lunar new year.
舞獅是農曆新年期間表演的傳統舞蹈。
Some students perform the lion dance, while other students play percussion instruments.
一些學生表演舞獅，而另一些學生則配合演奏打擊樂器。
WORD 25
magic trick (n)
魔術
The kids are performing magic tricks for their classmates.
孩子們正在為他們的同學表演魔術。
You can amaze the audience with magic tricks!
你可以用魔術讓觀眾驚嘆不已！
