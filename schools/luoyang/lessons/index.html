Ways to Learn

從校園生活學英文

Real school, real words.

每支影片、每份教材，都取材自螺陽真實的校園日常。

25 Videos · 6 Themes
Course 01
Word of the Day
每日一字

25 bilingual videos filmed around the Luoyang campus and grouped into six themes — butterflies and rice, music and performance, art and making, sports and movement, campus life, and traditions. Each word is embedded straight from YouTube with two example sentences taken from the video description.25 支雙語影片，全部在螺陽校園實地拍攝，依六大主題分類——蝴稻生態、音樂表演、藝術手作、運動體能、校園生活，以及傳統趣味。每個單字直接嵌入影片，並附兩句取自影片說明欄的例句。

Start Learning →
30 Episodes · For Teachers
Course 02
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers, taught by Sarah Thomas across 10 topics and 30 episodes — from greetings and roll call to exam instructions. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.給老師使用的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學，共 10 大主題、30 支影片——從問候點名到考試指令。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

Start Learning →
13 Episodes · By Office
Course 03
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

"Broadcast English" — thirteen short episodes in which each office and section introduces, in English, the announcements it makes over the school PA system, from the curriculum section to the counselling room.「廣播英語」——13 支短片，由各處室、各組用英語介紹平時透過校園廣播宣布的內容，從教務處教學組到輔導室。
