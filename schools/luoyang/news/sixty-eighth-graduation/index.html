Farewell, Class of 68

第68屆畢業典禮
←Back to News回到新聞總覽
畢業生在美勞教室裡，留下最後的合影
畢業典禮現場，全校師生齊聚一堂
「第六十八屆畢業典禮」舞台上的團體合照
校長與畢業生一一握手，道一聲珍重再見

On the evening of the ceremony, Luoyang’s 68th graduating class stepped up onto the stage one final time, surrounded by the blessings of teachers, family and friends. And just like that — they had graduated.

螺陽第68屆的孩子們，今天晚上在大家的祝福中，站上舞台最後一次——就這樣，畢業囉！

What these students carry away from Luoyang was never only exam papers, however many of those there were. It’s every small memory built along the way — the kind that stays with a person long after the details of any test have faded.

孩子們在螺陽的點點滴滴，留在心裡的，從來不只是密密麻麻的考卷，而是一起經歷過的種種回憶——那些會一直留在心底的東西。

Today marks the close of one chapter. But a graduation is never really an ending — it’s a beginning wearing a different name. From here, each of them sets out, bravely, toward whatever comes next.

今天是一個階段的結束，但也是另一階段的開始——啟程囉！往下一段旅程，勇敢地邁進、成長。

graduate/ˈɡrædʒueɪt/畢業（動詞／畢業生）

The whole class will graduate tonight.整個班級今晚就要畢業了。

ceremony/ˈserəmoʊni/典禮

Families came to watch the graduation ceremony.家人們前來觀禮這場畢業典禮。

memory/ˈmeməri/回憶

Every student carries their own memories of Luoyang.每個學生都帶著屬於自己的螺陽回憶。

blessing/ˈblesɪŋ/祝福

The graduates left the stage with everyone’s blessing.畢業生們帶著大家的祝福走下舞台。

stage/steɪdʒ/階段；舞台

Graduation is the end of one stage and the start of another.畢業是一個階段的結束，也是另一個階段的開始。

brave/breɪv/勇敢的

Each graduate stepped forward, brave and ready.每位畢業生都勇敢地向前邁進。

journey/ˈdʒɜːrni/旅程

A whole new journey is waiting for them.一段全新的旅程正在等著他們。

farewell/ˌferˈwel/道別、告別

It was time to say farewell to their elementary school.該是向小學說再見的時候了。
