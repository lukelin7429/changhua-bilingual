Closing Ceremony: A Summer Well Earned

休業式頒獎典禮
←Back to News回到新聞總覽
低年級孩子在老榕樹下，手持獎狀合影
領完獎狀，和老師在樹下合影留念
一大群孩子手持獎狀，笑得燦爛
獎狀在手，比讚慶祝這學期的努力

The closing ceremony, or 偉業式, is Luoyang's end-of-term awards day — the last big gathering before summer truly begins. Under the shade of the campus's old banyan trees, class after class came forward to be recognised.

休業式，就是螺陽的期末頒獎典禮——也是真正放暑假前的最後一次全校集合。在校園裡的老榕樹下，各班孩子一批批走上前，領取屬於自己的肯定。

Across the year, students had shown off what they were good at on all kinds of stages — in competitions, in performances, in everyday effort that finally got its moment. On the very last day of the term, each of them got to hold the proof of it in their own two hands.

這一年裡，孩子們在各式各樣的競賽舞台中，展現了自己的專才。在學期的最後一天，他們終於能親手拿到那份屬於自己努力的獎勵——暑假來囉！

Then it was time to rest — properly. Luoyang’s teachers sent every student off with the same wish: enjoy a full, unhurried summer, recharge completely, and come back after the break as an even better version of yourself.

接下來，就是好好休息的時候了。螺陽的老師們，也把同樣一句祝福送給每一位學生：好好的休息充電，讓自己過個充實的暑假，開學後，成長成更棒的自己！

ceremony/ˈserəmoʊni/典禮

The whole school gathered for the closing ceremony.全校師生一起參加休業典禮。

certificate/sərˈtɪfɪkət/獎狀

Each student held up their own certificate.每個學生都拿著自己的獎狀。

achievement/əˈtʃiːvmənt/成就、成果

The award stood for a whole term’s achievement.這份獎項代表了一整學期的成果。

talent/ˈtælənt/專才、才能

Students showed off their talent on many different stages.學生們在許多不同的舞台上展現專才。

recharge/riˈtʃɑːrdʒ/充電、恢復精力

Summer is the perfect time to recharge.暑假正是充電的好時機。

proud/praʊd/驕傲的

Every family felt proud of their child that day.那天，每個家庭都為孩子感到驕傲。

vacation/veɪˈkeɪʃn/假期

A long, happy summer vacation was just beginning.一個漫長又開心的暑假假期才剛要開始。

Teachers hope every student will grow over the summer.老師們希望每個學生在暑假裡都能成長。
