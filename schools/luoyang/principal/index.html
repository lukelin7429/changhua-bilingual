I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話 · 教育信念
讓教育
循著充滿歡樂與信心的學習道路前進
發展每個孩子的潛能
點燃每位教師的熱情
深體家長的企盼
在這份親師生綿密互動的園地中
孩子們伴著燦爛的笑容
成長… 茁壯…
Let education travel a road full of joy and confidence — drawing out the potential in every child, lighting the passion in every teacher, and taking to heart what every family hopes for. In a garden where children, teachers and parents are closely woven together, children grow up smiling.

Principal Hsu is a scholar of reading. She earned her Ph.D. in Education from National Taiwan Normal University with a dissertation on building a code of teaching ethics for elementary school teachers, and her earlier master's research followed a sixth-grade reading group through reciprocal teaching. For more than twenty years she has been writing, teaching and leading on the same question: how children learn to read, and how teachers learn to teach.

許校長是一位長期深耕閱讀教學的實務研究者。她的國立臺灣師範大學教育學博士論文，研究國民小學教師教學倫理守則的建構；碩士論文則是一份國小六年級閱讀小組實施交互教學的個案研究。二十多年來，她始終圍繞著同一個問題書寫、教學與帶領：孩子如何學會閱讀，老師又如何學會教。

That work has never stayed inside one school. She was the founding convener of Changhua County's Reading Instruction Advisory Group and still serves as its deputy convener; she has taught as an adjunct assistant professor at three universities; and she has published books, journal articles and newspaper columns that carry classroom questions out to a wider public.

這份工作從未只停留在一所學校。她是彰化縣閱讀教學輔導團的創團組長，至今仍擔任副組長；曾於三所大學兼任助理教授；並以專書、學術期刊論文與報章專欄，把教室裡的問題帶到更寬廣的公共視野。

There is the school its principal makes it.
「有怎樣的校長，就有怎樣的學校。」——期勉自己秉持專業的堅持與謙遜的態度，一步一腳印。

教育願景

A cradle where children learn how to learn.

促使學校成為啟發學生「學習如何學習」的搖籃。

立足基點 · 力求創新

Keep step with the times, and keep acting reflectively. Stand on firm ground and still strive to innovate; hold to professionalism, fairness and justice — so that the school becomes a cradle in which children learn how to learn, and grow into lifelong inquirers.

There is the school its principal makes it — so I hold myself to professional conviction and a humble manner, one footprint at a time, and try to run a school that walks with the times and along an ethical path. Here, the fragrance of growth opens — and the laughter of children.
