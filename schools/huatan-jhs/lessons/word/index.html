II.

School Life

校園生活

圖書館、掃地、報告——花壇校園裡最日常的英文詞彙。

WORD 001
libraryn.
圖書館
A library is a place with rows of books where students go to read and study.
圖書館是一個擺滿書本、讓學生閱讀與自習的地方。
Huatan students visit the library every week to borrow new books.
花壇的學生每週都會到圖書館借新書。
WORD 002
check outv.
借書
To check out a book means to borrow it from the library and take it home.
Check out（借書）是指從圖書館把書借出、帶回家的動作。
Students check out books at the front desk before the library closes.
學生在圖書館關門前，會到櫃台借書。
WORD 003
sweepv.
掃地
To sweep means to clean a floor by pushing dirt away with a broom.
Sweep（掃地）是指用掃把把地上的灰塵推走、清潔地板。
Every class sweeps the classroom and hallway during cleaning time.
每個班級在打掃時間都要掃教室和走廊。
WORD 004
essayn.
短文
An essay is a short piece of writing that explains ideas about one topic.
Essay（短文）是一篇針對單一主題、說明想法的短篇寫作。
Students write an essay in English class to practice expressing their thoughts.
學生在英文課寫短文，練習表達自己的想法。
WORD 005
presentv.
上台報告
To present means to stand in front of the class and share what you learned.
Present（上台報告）是指站在全班面前，分享自己所學的內容。
Huatan students present their science projects using slides and clear English.
花壇的學生會用投影片和清楚的英文報告科學專題。
WORD 006
cooking classn.
烹飪課
A cooking class is a lesson where students learn to prepare food step by step.
烹飪課是學生一步步學習準備食物的課程。
In cooking class, Huatan students practice teamwork while making real dishes together.
在烹飪課上，花壇學生一起動手做菜，同時練習團隊合作。
WORD 007
science fairn.
科學展覽
A science fair is an event where students display experiments and share their results.
科學展覽是學生展示實驗、分享成果的活動。
Huatan's science fair lets students show off projects built in the AIoT Center.
花壇的科學展覽讓學生展示在 AIoT 中心完成的作品。
III.

Arts & Music

音樂藝術

唱歌、跳舞、直笛、打鼓——花壇的音樂課堂英語。

WORD 008
singv.
唱歌
To sing means to make music with your voice, following a tune.
Sing（唱歌）是指跟著旋律、用聲音唱出音樂。
Huatan students sing together at the school's music festival every year.
花壇學生每年都會在音樂發表會上一起唱歌。
WORD 009
dancen.
舞蹈
A dance is a series of movements performed to music.
舞蹈是配合音樂節奏所做的一連串動作。
Students learn a new dance for the school's year-end performance.
學生為學校的期末表演學習新的舞蹈。
WORD 010
recordern.
直笛
A recorder is a simple wind instrument that students blow into to play notes.
直笛是一種吹奏樂器，學生用嘴吹氣來吹出音符。
Huatan students practice the recorder in music class every week.
花壇學生每週都在音樂課練習吹直笛。
WORD 011
drumsn.
鼓
Drums are instruments that make sound when you hit them with sticks or hands.
鼓是一種用鼓棒或雙手敲打，就能發出聲音的樂器。
The school band plays the drums to keep the beat during performances.
學校的樂隊靠打鼓來掌握表演時的節奏。
IV.

Science & Nature

自然科學

磁力、種子、氣壓——花壇的自然課實驗英語。

WORD 012
magnetic forcen.
磁力
Magnetic force is the invisible pull or push between magnets and metal objects.
磁力是磁鐵與金屬物體之間看不見的吸引或推開的力量。
Students test magnetic force in science class by moving magnets near paper clips.
學生在自然課用磁鐵靠近迴紋針，測試磁力的作用。
WORD 013
seedn.
種子
A seed is the small part of a plant that can grow into a new plant.
種子是植物的一小部分，能夠長成新的植物。
Students plant a seed in the school garden and watch it grow each week.
學生在校園菜園種下種子，每週觀察它的生長。
WORD 014
air pressuren.
氣壓
Air pressure is the force of air pushing on everything around us.
氣壓是空氣對周遭一切事物施加的力量。
Huatan students use a simple experiment to see how air pressure works.
花壇學生透過簡單的實驗，觀察氣壓如何作用。
V.

Technology

科技數位

瀏覽、iPad、人工智慧——花壇的 AIoT 校園英語。

WORD 015
browsev.
瀏覽（網路）
To browse means to look through information on the internet.
Browse（瀏覽）是指在網路上查看資訊。
Students browse the internet to find facts for their AIoT projects.
學生瀏覽網路，為 AIoT 專題尋找資料。
WORD 016
iPadn.
平板電腦
An iPad is a tablet computer that students use to read, write, and learn.
iPad 是一種平板電腦，學生用它來閱讀、寫作和學習。
Huatan students use an iPad in class to research and complete assignments.
花壇學生上課使用 iPad 查資料、完成作業。
WORD 017
artificial intelligencen.
人工智慧
Artificial intelligence is technology that lets computers learn and make decisions like humans.
人工智慧是讓電腦能像人類一樣學習與做決定的科技。
Huatan's AIoT Center teaches students how artificial intelligence works through hands-on projects.
花壇的 AIoT 中心透過實作專題，教學生了解人工智慧如何運作。
VI.

Culture & Community

文化與社區

粽子、扯鈴、志工服務——花壇的文化與社區英語。

WORD 018
rice dumplingn.
粽子
A rice dumpling is sticky rice wrapped in a bamboo leaf, filled with meat or beans.
粽子是用竹葉包裹糯米，內餡包著肉或豆類的傳統食物。
Families make rice dumplings together to celebrate the Dragon Boat Festival.
家家戶戶會一起包粽子，慶祝端午節。
WORD 019
Chinese yo-yon.
扯鈴
A Chinese yo-yo is a spinning toy that players balance and toss on a string between two sticks.
扯鈴是一種旋轉的傳統玩具，玩家用兩根棍子和一條線讓它保持平衡並拋接。
Huatan students perform Chinese yo-yo tricks at school events to share Taiwan's culture.
花壇學生在學校活動中表演扯鈴特技，分享台灣文化。
WORD 020
volunteerv.
志工服務
To volunteer means to give your time to help others without being paid.
Volunteer（志工服務）是指不求報酬，付出時間幫助他人。
Huatan students volunteer in the community to learn the value of giving back.
花壇學生投入社區志工服務，學習回饋的價值。
