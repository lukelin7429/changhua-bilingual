I.

Welcome

歡迎來到花壇國中

A school that carries sixty years of history — and builds the next sixty with code and curiosity.

六十年的歷史厚度，加上對科技與創新的熱情——這就是花壇國中。

Huatan Junior High sits at the foot of Baguashan in Huatan Township — a place long famous as Taiwan's brick-kiln hometown. The same red clay that built generations of homes now fires a different kind of ambition: a school that blends tradition with technology, community with competition.

花壇國中坐落於八卦山山麓的花壇鄉，這裡是台灣著名的磚窯故鄉。曾經燒製磚瓦、蓋遍家家戶戶的紅土，如今燃起另一種火焰——傳統與科技並行、社區與競技共榮的辦學精神。

Founded in 1961, Huatan JHS has sent more than 20,000 graduates into the world. Today, under Principal Tu Ming-hsiu, the school runs one of the county's few junior-high AIoT Science & Technology Centers, fields a powerhouse track-and-field team with national qualifiers, and has earned the Ministry of Education's Reading Cornerstone Award. Every pillar of the school grows from the same soil: a belief that a small-township school can do big things.

創立於民國 50 年（1961），花壇國中已培育超過 2 萬名畢業生。在涂明秀校長的帶領下，學校設有全縣少數的國中級 AIoT 人工智慧物聯網科技教育中心，田徑隊多人達全中運標準，並榮獲教育部閱讀磐石獎。所有的光芒，都生長於同一片土壤：一所小鄉鎮的學校，可以做到很大的事。

Where brick meets code
從磚土到程式——花壇的故事

The same township that fired bricks for a generation now shapes students who build with algorithms, run national-level races, and speak in two — sometimes three — languages. That is Huatan.

II.

60+ Years of History

一甲子的校史

Eleven principals, one township, six decades of growing up together.

十一任校長，一個鄉鎮，六十多年的共同成長。

1961
Founded
民國 50 年 12 月核准設校，源於縣長呂世明倡議「一鄉鎮一國中」，地方仕紳合力促成，隔年 9 月正式開學。
2008
Morning Light Building
拆除老舊危險教室後，「晨曦樓」完工啟用，同步整建運動場與籃球場。
2019
AIoT Education Center Established
全縣少數的國中級人工智慧物聯網教育中心成立，開設機器學習、物聯網感測、生成式 AI 等實作課程。
2022
First Female Principal
涂明秀校長接任，為創校以來第十一任、也是首位女校長，推動八大特色課程。
Today
20,000+ Graduates and Counting
一甲子有餘的歷史，累計畢業生超過兩萬人，遍及學術、教育、商業、農業等各行各業。
Archival photos · 創校初期的校舍老照片，見證一甲子前的花壇初中。
III.

What Makes Huatan

花壇的四大亮點
AIoT & Innovation
人工智慧物聯網教育中心

Established in 2019, the AIoT Center offers hands-on courses in machine learning, IoT sensors, generative AI, and robotics — open to both students and visiting schools across the county.

Track & Field
田徑強校

With multiple athletes qualifying for the National Junior High Games each year, Huatan's track team is one of Changhua's strongest. Record-breakers, champions, and future Olympians train here.

Technical Skills
技藝教育・職人精神

Since 1988, Huatan has run a technical skills program producing national champions in electrical assembly, painting, and CAD. Graduates go on to top engineering universities — and come back to give.

Reading & Language
閱讀磐石・多元語文

Ministry of Education Reading Cornerstone Award winner. First place in county Minnan-language oral arts. Runner-up in English Reader's Theater. Language lives across the whole campus.
