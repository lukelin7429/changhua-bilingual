A Voice That Lights the Way從逆境走向榮耀，讓世界聽見花壇國中的生命故事

There is a kind of applause that celebrates more than achievement — it honors a journey that never gave up.

At the 2026 Presidential Education Award ceremony, Huatan Junior High student Li Pei-hua received one of the nation’s highest student honors, and stepped onto the stage to speak on behalf of every junior-high award recipient in Taiwan.

Courage, in the end, always finds its own light.

Before an audience watching from across the country, she spoke plainly and from the heart about her own journey, thanking everyone who had walked beside her — and telling every struggling student the same thing: however hard the road, don’t let go of hope, because moving forward with courage always leads somewhere worth arriving.

In that moment, she spoke for more than herself — she spoke for every child who has ever had to fight for their dream. Her words carried no flourish, only sincerity, and they moved everyone in the room to see, once again, what education is truly for: not only passing on knowledge, but walking beside a life as it grows, and keeping hope alive.

Thank you to the family, teachers, and community that walked this road with Pei-hua. Because of that quiet, steady support, she could dream boldly and walk toward a wider world.

Today, we are proud of Li Pei-hua. Tomorrow, Huatan Junior High will keep walking beside every student who needs it — so that every life gets the chance to be seen, to be believed in, and to shine.

中文原文 · Chinese

有一種掌聲，不只是為了成就，更是為了一段永不放棄的生命旅程。

在2026總統教育獎頒獎典禮上，花壇國中學生李佩樺榮獲總統教育獎，更榮幸代表全國國中組獲獎學生登台致詞。站在全國矚目的舞臺上，她以堅定而真摯的話語，分享一路走來的成長歷程，向所有陪伴她的人表達最深的感謝，也鼓勵每一位正在努力的孩子：無論遇到多少困難，都不要放棄希望，因為勇敢前行，終將迎來屬於自己的光芒。

那一刻，她代表的不只是自己，更代表無數努力奮鬥、勇敢追夢的孩子。她的發言沒有華麗的辭藻，卻充滿真誠與力量，深深感動了在場每一位來賓，也讓大家再次看見教育最美的價值——教育，不只是傳授知識，更是陪伴生命成長、點燃希望的力量。

一路走來，佩樺用堅毅、樂觀與永不放棄的精神，克服生命中的挑戰；她用努力證明，只要心中有夢、腳步不停，再遠的目標都能一步一步抵達。

感謝一路支持佩樺的家人、師長、教育夥伴及社會各界，因為有大家無私的陪伴與相信，孩子才能勇敢追夢，走向更寬廣的世界。

今天，我們以李佩樺為榮；未來，花壇國中也將持續以愛為起點，以教育為力量，陪伴更多孩子跨越困境、迎向希望，讓每一個生命都能被看見、被相信，綻放最耀眼的光彩。

恭喜李佩樺榮獲2026總統教育獎！花壇國中，以妳為榮；彰化，以妳為傲；臺灣，因妳而更加溫暖。
