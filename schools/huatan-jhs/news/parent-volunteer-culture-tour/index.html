On the Road Together花壇國中親師志工走讀文化活動

Today, Huatan Junior High’s parent-teacher volunteer culture tour set off amid warm anticipation and even warmer send-offs.

Before departure, Legislator Huang Hsiu-fang, the township mayor, the council chair, and many longtime friends of Huatan came in person to wish the group a safe and rewarding trip — a reminder of just how much the community stands behind this school.

The bus was full of laughter the whole way — songs, shared stories, endless snacks and cold drinks, and thoughtful service that made every volunteer feel cared for.

The first stop was Hualu Leisure Farm, where the group wandered fragrant gardens, admired the scenery, and learned about essential-oil plants and their many uses.

The best part of travel isn’t the scenery — it’s the people beside you.

Next came the Sanyi Wood Sculpture Museum, where finely detailed carvings revealed the depth of Taiwan’s wood-carving tradition and the patience behind every piece.

The day ended at MITSUI OUTLET PARK Taichung Port, where everyone shopped for their favorite brands and headed home with full hands and full hearts.

Warm thanks to Principal Tu Ming-hsiu and PTA Chair Qian-zhan for their support, to the parents’ association for planning the day, and to every committee member, advisor, volunteer teacher, and staff member who came along — turning an ordinary day into one more shared, happy memory for Huatan.

中文原文 · Chinese

今天，花壇國中親師志工走讀文化活動，在大家滿滿的期待中熱鬧展開！

出發前，特別感謝黃秀芳立委、鄉長、代表會主席以及許多一路支持花壇國中的貴人們，親自到場祝福所有夥伴旅途平安、收穫滿滿，也讓大家感受到來自社區最溫暖的支持與鼓勵。

一路上，遊覽車內笑聲不斷，大家分享心情、唱歌同歡，氣氛熱絡又溫馨。更令人感動的是，沿途還有吃不完的點心、喝不完的冰鎮飲料、豐盛可口的餐點，以及貼心又優質的服務，讓每位參與夥伴都感受到滿滿的照顧與幸福。

第一站來到花露農場，漫步在花草芬芳的園區中，不僅欣賞了美麗的景色，更深入認識各種精油植物及其用途，在輕鬆愉快的氛圍中增長知識、放鬆身心。

旅行最美好的，不只是沿途的風景，而是和對的人一起出遊。

接著前往三義木雕博物館，欣賞一件件精湛細膩的木雕藝術作品，感受臺灣木雕工藝的文化底蘊與匠人精神，每一件作品都令人讚嘆不已。

最後抵達 MITSUI OUTLET PARK 台中港（三井 OUTLET），大家盡情選購自己喜愛的品牌商品，滿載而歸，也為這趟旅程畫下最愉快的句點。

衷心感謝花壇國中明秀校長和千展會長的支持、家長會精心規劃活動內容，更謝謝所有家長委員、顧問、志工老師及教職同仁們的參與和互相陪伴，讓活動圓滿順利、賓主盡歡。期待下一次，我們再一起出發，攜手走讀學習、共享美好時光！
