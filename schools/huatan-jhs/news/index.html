Athletics · 縣田徑賽

Track & Field: County Meet 2025114 學年彰化縣田徑錦標賽

Spring 2025 · 114 學年

Huatan swept five national-games berths at the county's 2025 track-and-field championships. The women's team placed second overall, the men's team came in fourth, and five individual athletes punched tickets to the National Junior High Games — proving once again that a small-township school can run with the county's best.

花壇國中在 114 學年彰化縣田徑錦標賽中大放異彩：女子團體第二、男子團體第四，五名選手獲得全中運資格——再次證明一所鄉鎮學校的田徑實力，不輸任何對手。

Alumni · 校友回饋

Gold Medalist Rewires the English Village校友金牌重返母校，翻新英語國際村

Nov 2024 · 113 年 11 月

Li Yu-xiang, a national track-and-field champion who grew up in Huatan Township, returned to his junior-high alma mater carrying gold medals and a gift of craft. He spent three days as a volunteer electrician, replumbing the wiring of Huatan's English Village classroom — a project estimated to have saved the school over NT$1.2 million. The story was reported by Liberty Times. "I wouldn't be where I am without this school," he said. "This is the least I can do."

田徑金牌校友李宇翔回到母校，以三天的義務電工勞動翻新了花壇國中英語國際村的配電系統——估計為學校省下新台幣超過 120 萬元，並獲自由時報報導。「沒有這所學校就沒有我，這只是我能做到最少的事。」

Athletics · 113 年縣運

Triple Gold at County Games 113113 年縣運三金 · 梁睿文、王婕恩、游汶萱

2024 · 民國 113 年

Three Huatan athletes claimed gold at the Changhua County Games in 2024: Liang Rui-wen in the 400-meter hurdles, Wang Jie-en in the 100-meter sprint, and Yu Wen-xuan in the long jump. All three were not just local champions — they set county records and advanced to the national level, placing Huatan's name on the national athletics map.

花壇國中三名選手在 113 年縣運奪金：梁睿文（400 公尺跨欄）、王婕恩（100 公尺短跑）、游汶萱（跳遠），均破縣紀錄並晉升全國賽，讓花壇的名字登上全國田徑版圖。

Technology · AIoT教育

Where Machine Learning Meets Junior HighAIoT 科技教育中心 · 2019 年啟動

Est. 2019 · 108 年起

In 2019, Huatan launched one of the first AIoT Science & Technology Centers at the junior-high level in Changhua County. Students work with IoT sensors, train simple machine-learning models, and explore the world of generative AI — all before they turn fifteen. The center also opens its doors to students and educators from neighboring schools, turning Huatan into a county resource for technology education.

花壇國中於 2019 年設立全縣少數的國中級 AIoT 人工智慧物聯網科技教育中心，提供感測器操作、機器學習入門、生成式 AI 探索等課程，更開放鄰近學校師生共同使用，成為縣內科技教育的重要資源。

Reading · 閱讀磐石

Ministry of Education Reading Cornerstone Award教育部閱讀磐石獎

2022 · 111 年

Huatan Junior High was named a Ministry of Education Reading Cornerstone School (閱讀磐石獎) — one of the country's most prestigious recognitions for sustained, campus-wide reading culture. The award follows years of investment in the school library, a reading-mentor program where older students guide younger ones, and a commitment to pairing every subject with rich bilingual reading materials.

花壇國中榮獲教育部閱讀磐石獎，肯定學校長年深耕閱讀文化：圖書館整頓、高年級閱讀小老師制度、各科雙語閱讀素材融入，讓閱讀不只是語文課的事，而是全校的日常。

Language Arts · 語文

Mother Tongue First: Minnan Language Champion縣語文競賽閩南語組第一名

2024 · 113 學年

A Huatan student placed first in Changhua County in the Minnan-language oral arts competition — the language of the school's own township, where brick kilns once rang with the Taiwanese spoken word. The win is a reminder that at Huatan, bilingual education is not just about English: it is about honoring every language a student carries.

一名花壇學生在彰化縣語文競賽閩南語朗讀組奪得縣第一名。花壇鄉本是傳統閩南語環境，在雙語教育的浪潮下，學校始終珍視母語——多語共榮，才是真正的語文教育。

Technical Skills · 技藝

Skilled for Life: National Skills Competition技藝教育 · 全國技競佳績

Ongoing · 持續

Since launching a technical skills program in 1988, Huatan has sent students to the National Junior High Skills Competition in fields including electrical assembly, drawing, and design. The program has produced multiple national-rank finishers. Many graduates continue on to vocational high schools and engineering universities — and several have returned to join the Huatan team that shaped them.

花壇國中自 1988 年起辦理技藝教育，多次在全國技藝競賽中締造佳績，領域涵蓋電工、繪圖與設計。許多畢業生就讀職業高中或工程大學，甚至回母校擔任指導——技藝的火炬，一代一代傳下去。

English · 英語教育

English Village: A New Home for Language英語國際村正式啟用

2024 · 113 年

Huatan's English Village classroom received a full renovation — thanks to alumni, parent volunteers, and the school team working together. The reimagined space features dedicated role-play stations (immigration checkpoint, hotel, restaurant, weather forecast), giving students a place to use English as they would in the real world: nervously, excitedly, in full sentences, with strangers. It is now one of the school's most-visited rooms.

花壇國中英語國際村完成翻新，設有出入境查驗、飯店、餐廳、氣象播報等情境角落，讓學生在真實脈絡中使用英語。在校友義工和家長的支持下，這個語言場域煥然一新，成為全校最受歡迎的學習空間之一。

Arts · 英語戲劇

Reader's Theater: County Runner-Up英語讀者劇場縣賽亞軍

2024 · 113 學年

Huatan's English Reader's Theater team took second place at the Changhua County competition, performing a bilingual script that blended local folklore with modern dialogue. The team — all eighth graders — rehearsed for three months under the guidance of their English and homeroom teachers. The performance drew praise for its pronunciation, stage presence, and the confidence of students who once hesitated to speak English at all.

花壇英語讀者劇場團隊在彰化縣賽奪得亞軍，以在地民俗為題材演繹雙語劇本。這支由八年級生組成的隊伍歷經三個月排練，憑藉流利的英語發音與自信的台風贏得評審肯定，也讓那些曾經不敢開口說英語的孩子，站上了舞台。

Sport · 武術

Martial Arts: Third Place Team Title武術運動 · 全縣團體第三

2024 · 113 學年

Huatan's martial arts team claimed third place in the team category at the Changhua County Martial Arts Championships. Three individual athletes also advanced to higher-level competitions. Martial arts — alongside track and field — is one of the school's longest-running sports programs, with training open to students from all three grade levels.

花壇武術隊在彰化縣武術錦標賽奪得團體組第三名，並有三名個人選手晉升更高層級競賽。武術與田徑並列為花壇歷史最悠久的體育項目之一，三個年級的學生均可參與訓練。
