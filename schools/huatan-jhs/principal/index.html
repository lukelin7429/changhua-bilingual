I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話
▶Principal Tu — English Self-Introduction涂明秀校長英文自我介紹 · 在 YouTube 觀看↗

Principal Tu Ming-hsiu leads Huatan Junior High with a vision that every student — whatever their path — can find a place to grow and shine. Since taking office in 2022, she has championed the school's dual identity: a community school deeply rooted in Huatan's brick-kiln heritage, and a forward-looking campus where AI, athletics, and the arts sit side by side.

涂明秀校長以「讓每個孩子都能找到自己的舞台」為核心，帶領花壇國中前行。自 2022 年接任以來，她積極推動學校兼顧在地文化底蘊與前瞻教育視野：一方面讓 AIoT 科技教育紮根，另一方面維繫田徑、技藝與語文的多元亮點。

Under her leadership, the school completed the renovation of the English Village classroom with the hands-on help of a proud alumni — and local parents who gave their time and skill to save the school over a million New Taiwan dollars. Principal Tu sees in that story exactly what she hopes to nurture: a school that gives back and keeps giving.

在她任內，學校完成了英語國際村教室的翻新整修，背後是一位優秀校友、熱心家長義務出工出料的心意——這正是涂校長念茲在茲的核心：一所懂得感恩、懂得回饋的學校。

"A small-township school can do big things — when its community believes in it."
只要社區與學校攜手，偏鄉的孩子一樣可以做到大事。 — 涂明秀校長
