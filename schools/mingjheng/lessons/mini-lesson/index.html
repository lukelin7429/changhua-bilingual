LESSON 01
Mingjheng Elementary SchoolMini Lesson
介紹明正國小

Welcome to Mingjheng Elementary School. We're a big school with over a thousand students, right in the middle of beautiful gardens and cool places for learning.

At Mingjheng, we learn about our world, how to care for it, and how to be good friends. Learning here is all about doing great things.

We also have a new student activity center! It's a big, fun space where we can all play and learn.

Our teachers are super nice and make learning fun. Plus, our families and people from around us help make our school awesome.

Join us at Mingjheng Elementary for learning, playing, and growing together. We're not just about books; we're about making dreams come true. See you at Mingjheng!

歡迎來到明正國小。我們是一所有著一千多名學生的大學校，是個周圍有美麗的花園和學習的好地方。

在明正，我們學習我們的世界，如何照顧這個世界，以及如何成為各國友人的好朋友。在這裡學習就是為了做很棒的事情。

我們還有一個全新的學生活動中心！這是一個大而有趣的空間，我們都可以在這裡玩耍和學習。

我們的老師非常好，讓學習變得有趣。另外，我們的家庭和周圍的人幫助使我們的學校變得棒極了。

加入明正國小的大家庭，一起學習、玩耍和成長。我們不僅僅是只有讀書；我們是個讓夢想成真的地方。期待在明正見到你！

LESSON 02
Student Activity CenterMini Lesson
學生活動中心

Hello everyone! Today we have exciting news from Changhua County. Mingjheng Elementary School in Hsiushui Township has a brand-new student activity center.

This center is a special place where students can play and learn indoors. It's much more than just a room; it's a large, welcoming space for all kinds of activities.

Building this center was a big team effort. The Changhua County Government helped a lot. We also need to say a big thank you to County Magistrate Ms. Wang and Principal Lin. Without their hard work, this wouldn't have been possible.

Now, the student activity center is ready for school and community events. It shows how working together can make wonderful things happen.

大家好！今天我們從彰化縣帶來令人興奮的消息。秀水鄉的明正國小有了一個全新的學生活動中心。

這個中心是一個特別的地方，學生可以在室內進行遊戲和學習。它不僅僅是一個教室；它是一個適合各種活動的大而溫馨的空間。

建造這個中心是需要龐大的團隊努力。彰化縣政府提供了很多幫助。我們要向王縣長和林校長說一聲大大的謝謝。沒有他們的辛勤工作，這一切都不可能實現。

現在，學生活動中心已經準備好舉辦學校和社區活動。這顯示了共同努力可以創造出美好的事物。

LESSON 03
Chenguang Natural Ecological FarmMini Lesson
晨光自然生態循環農場

On January 22nd, 2024, the students of Mingjheng Elementary School had an exciting day! They visited the Chenguang Natural Ecological Farm. At the farm, they discovered a special way of growing plants and raising fish together, called an aquaponics system.

They learned how this cool system doesn't use any harmful chemicals or fertilizers. It's all about keeping things natural and safe. The students saw how plants, fish, and tiny creatures all help each other to grow in what's called a "biological chain."

This farm takes good care of our Earth and also teaches people how to enjoy nature. They even learned about the tools and equipment that help the farm work, and how to build places like this.

It was not just a fun trip, but a day full of learning and exploring how we can be friends with the environment. What an adventure at Chenguang Farm!

2024年1月22日，明正國小的學生們度過一個有趣的日子！他們參觀了晨光自然生態循環農場。在農場，他們發現了一種特殊的種植植物和養魚的方法，這叫做魚菜共生系統。

他們了解到這個酷炫的系統不使用任何有害的化學品或肥料。一切都是為了保持自然與安全。學生們看到了植物、魚和微小生物如何在所謂的「生物鏈」中互相幫助成長。

這個農場非常關心我們的地球，也教導人們如何享受自然。他們甚至學到了幫助農場工作的工具和設備，以及如何建造這樣的地方。

這不僅僅是一次有趣的旅行，而是一天充滿了學習和探索的一天，我們學習到如何成為環境的朋友。在晨光農場的冒險多麼精彩啊！
