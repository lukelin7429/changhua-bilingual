Chenguang Natural Ecological Farm

晨光自然生態循環農場
← Mini Lesson回到 Mini Lesson↑ Lessons回到課程總覽

On January 22nd, 2024, the students of Mingjheng Elementary School had an exciting day! They visited the Chenguang Natural Ecological Farm. At the farm, they discovered a special way of growing plants and raising fish together, called an aquaponics system.

They learned how this cool system doesn't use any harmful chemicals or fertilizers. It's all about keeping things natural and safe. The students saw how plants, fish, and tiny creatures all help each other to grow in what's called a "biological chain."

This farm takes good care of our Earth and also teaches people how to enjoy nature. They even learned about the tools and equipment that help the farm work, and how to build places like this.

It was not just a fun trip, but a day full of learning and exploring how we can be friends with the environment. What an adventure at Chenguang Farm!

2024年1月22日，明正國小的學生們度過一個有趣的日子！他們參觀了晨光自然生態循環農場。在農場，他們發現了一種特殊的種植植物和養魚的方法，這叫做魚菜共生系統。

他們了解到這個酷炫的系統不使用任何有害的化學品或肥料。一切都是為了保持自然與安全。學生們看到了植物、魚和微小生物如何在所謂的「生物鏈」中互相幫助成長。

這個農場非常關心我們的地球，也教導人們如何享受自然。他們甚至學到了幫助農場工作的工具和設備，以及如何建造這樣的地方。

這不僅僅是一次有趣的旅行，而是一天充滿了學習和探索的一天，我們學習到如何成為環境的朋友。在晨光農場的冒險多麼精彩啊！
