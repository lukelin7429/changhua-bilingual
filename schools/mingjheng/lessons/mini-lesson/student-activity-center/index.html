Student Activity Center

學生活動中心

Hello everyone! Today we have exciting news from Changhua County. Mingjheng Elementary School in Hsiushui Township has a brand-new student activity center.

This center is a special place where students can play and learn indoors. It's much more than just a room; it's a large, welcoming space for all kinds of activities.

Building this center was a big team effort. The Changhua County Government helped a lot. We also need to say a big thank you to County Magistrate Ms. Wang and Principal Lin. Without their hard work, this wouldn't have been possible.

Now, the student activity center is ready for school and community events. It shows how working together can make wonderful things happen.

大家好！今天我們從彰化縣帶來令人興奮的消息。秀水鄉的明正國小有了一個全新的學生活動中心。

這個中心是一個特別的地方，學生可以在室內進行遊戲和學習。它不僅僅是一個教室；它是一個適合各種活動的大而溫馨的空間。

建造這個中心是需要龐大的團隊努力。彰化縣政府提供了很多幫助。我們要向王縣長和林校長說一聲大大的謝謝。沒有他們的辛勤工作，這一切都不可能實現。

現在，學生活動中心已經準備好舉辦學校和社區活動。這顯示了共同努力可以創造出美好的事物。
