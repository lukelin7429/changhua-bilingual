II.

Campus Life

校園生活

志工、說故事的人、老師的進修時光、洗手習慣——明正國小每天的日常節奏。

WORD 001
volunteern.
志工
Parent volunteers help out at Mingjheng's school gate every morning.
家長志工每天早上都在明正的校門口幫忙。
Being a volunteer means giving your time to help others without being asked.
當志工代表不求回報、主動付出時間幫助他人。
WORD 002
storytellern.
講故事的人
A storyteller uses their voice and expressions to bring a book to life.
說故事的人用聲音和表情把一本書變得生動起來。
Every week, a storyteller visits Mingjheng to read picture books to the students.
每週都有說故事的人來明正，為學生朗讀繪本。
WORD 003
professional developmentn.
專業進修
In Taiwan, every school holds professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，每所學校都會為老師安排專業進修時段。
During professional development, Mingjheng's teachers learn new skills together.
在專業進修時間，明正的老師們一起學習新技能與教學策略。
WORD 004
wash your hands
洗手
"Wash your hands!" the teacher reminds students before lunch.
「去洗手！」老師在午餐前提醒學生。
Washing your hands well helps keep germs away.
好好洗手能幫助預防細菌感染。
III.

Sports & Exercise

運動時光

下課、暖身、籃球、跑步、運動——明正操場上的活力時光。

WORD 005
recessn.
下課時間
When the bell rings for recess, students head outside to play.
下課鐘聲一響，學生們就往外跑去玩耍。
At Mingjheng, recess is a chance to run around and have fun with friends.
在明正，下課時間是和同學一起奔跑、歡笑的時光。
WORD 006
warm up
暖身
Before P.E. class starts, students warm up together on the field.
體育課開始前，學生會一起在操場上暖身。
Warming up gets your muscles ready before you run or play sports.
暖身能讓肌肉在跑步或運動前先做好準備。
WORD 007
basketballn.
籃球
Students shoot the ball at the hoop when they play basketball.
打籃球時，學生會朝籃框投籃。
Mingjheng's basketball court is always busy after school.
明正的籃球場放學後總是很熱鬧。
WORD 008
runv.
跑步
Students run around the track during P.E. class.
體育課時，學生繞著操場跑步。
Running every day helps students stay healthy and strong.
每天跑步能幫助學生保持健康與體力。
WORD 009
exercisev.
運動
Students exercise together on the playground every morning.
學生每天早上一起在操場運動。
Regular exercise keeps your body strong and healthy.
規律運動能讓身體強健、保持健康。
IV.

Playground Fun

童玩遊戲

彈珠台、冰棒棍、猜拳、跳舞——明正孩子下課時間的歡樂時光。

WORD 010
Pachinkon.
彈珠台
Pachinko is an old-fashioned game where you flick a marble around a wooden board.
彈珠台是一種老式遊戲，玩家彈動彈珠讓它在木板上滾動。
Students take turns playing Pachinko during recess.
下課時間，學生輪流玩彈珠台。
WORD 011
popsicle stickn.
冰棒棍
Students use popsicle sticks to build small crafts and models.
學生用冰棒棍製作小手工藝品和模型。
A popsicle stick is a simple material that can become almost anything with a little creativity.
冰棒棍是很簡單的材料，只要發揮創意就能變成各種作品。
WORD 012
rock-paper-scissorsn.
猜拳
Students play rock-paper-scissors to decide who goes first.
學生玩猜拳來決定誰先開始。
Rock-paper-scissors is a quick and fun game for two players.
猜拳是一個簡單又好玩的雙人遊戲。
WORD 013
dancev.
跳舞
Students dance together to music during class.
學生在課堂上隨著音樂一起跳舞。
Dancing helps students express themselves and have fun moving their bodies.
跳舞能幫助學生表達自己，並在動身體的過程中獲得樂趣。
