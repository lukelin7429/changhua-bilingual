I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Dr. Chun-Cheng Lin has served as principal of Mingjheng Elementary School in Shoushui Township, Changhua County, since August 2021. Holding a doctorate in education, he has also taught as an adjunct assistant professor at National Chiayi University and Central Taiwan University of Science and Technology, and has been recognized with the Ministry of Education's Reading Promotion Award and Friendly Campus Award, among other honors.

林俊成校長自民國110年8月起擔任彰化縣秀水鄉明正國小校長。校長擁有教育學博士學位，並曾於國立嘉義大學、中州科技大學兼任助理教授，先後榮獲教育部「動閱讀績優個人獎」、「友善校園績優個人獎」等多項殊榮。

Principal Lin's guiding vision is that "quality is the starting point of value and dignity" — placing students' right to learn at the center, with teachers, students, and parents treating one another with genuine respect, so that the school can build a campus where everyone wins.

林校長的辦學願景是「品質是價值與尊嚴的起點」——以學生的受教權為中心，師生、家長彼此尊重、真誠相待，共同打造一個三贏的校園。

III.

Five Development Priorities

五大辦學措施

How Principal Lin puts the school's vision into practice.

林校長如何將辦學願景落實於校務推展。

⚡
Agile Administration
敏捷有效行政

SWOT-informed, results-focused, humane systems built on a "students first" principle and democratic decision-making.

善用SWOT分析、重視目標達成、建立人性典章制度，秉持「學生第一」理念與民主決策。

👩‍🏫
Empowering Teachers
激勵教師專業

Caring for every staff member, sustaining the love and patience of teaching, and encouraging ongoing professional development.

主動關懷教職員、激發教育愛心與耐心，實施正常化教學並鼓勵專業進修。

🌈
Student Diversity
學生多元發展

Valuing moral education, offering varied learning stages, and advancing bilingual, information, and reading education.

重視生活道德教育、提供多元學習舞台，推動資訊英語教學與各項社團、閱讀背誦。

🏫
A Learning Campus
學習化校園

Designing learning trails throughout campus, echoing the community's character, and shaping elegant gardens and art spaces.

規劃各種學習步道、校舍配合社區特色，打造優雅庭園並善用校園空間藝文佈置。

🤝
Community Resources
社區資源結合

Empowering the parents' association, promoting parenting education, training volunteer teams, and weaving in community development.

發揮家長會功能、推動親職教育，組訓志工團隊並結合社區總體營造。
