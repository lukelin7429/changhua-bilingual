Eye Care with Love

視力保健：VR 任務探索四種眼疾的世界

Thank you to the Taiwan Foundation for the Blind for this rare opportunity to hold an "I Protect My Eyes" health education event at Mingjheng! The Foundation's education team brought an "Eye Exploration VR Mission" to campus, letting students experience first-hand what the world looks like through four different eye conditions.

〔視力保健〕感謝台灣防盲基金會到明正國小辦理〔I護眼衛教活動〕的難得機會！臺灣防盲基金會衛教團隊帶著「眼睛探索VR任務」來到校園，讓同學們親身感受四種眼疾的世界。

Through cardboard VR viewers, students saw the clouded haze of cataracts, the narrowing tunnel vision of glaucoma, the dark, warped blind spot of macular degeneration, and the torn, fractured sight of retinal detachment — a vivid, memorable way to understand why eye care matters.

同學們透過紙製VR眼鏡，親身感受白內障的朦朧霧視、青光眼逐漸縮小的視野、黃斑部病變的黑影變型，以及視網膜剝離的裂痕視覺——用一種印象深刻的方式，理解護眼的重要性。
