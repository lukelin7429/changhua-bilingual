Walking in Their Shoes

敬老宣導及扮老體驗

Thank you to Director Yi-Ting Lin of the Huashan Social Welfare Foundation's Shoushui Love Angel Station, who visited Mingjheng during Family Month of the 114th school year to hold an "Elder Respect Awareness & Aging Simulation" event.

感謝華山基金會秀水愛心天使站林奕廷站長，在明正國小114學年度家庭月蒞校辦理「敬老宣導及扮老體驗」。

Student volunteers put on white wigs, dark glasses, ear mufflers, and walked with a cane — feeling, for a few minutes, what it's like to move through the world with an elder's eyes, ears, and joints. The experience helped students better appreciate and care for their parents, passing on the tradition of filial devotion.

學生志願者戴上白髮假髮、深色眼鏡、耳罩，並拄著拐杖走路——用幾分鐘的時間，親身感受長者的視力、聽力與行動限制。這樣的體驗讓學童更加珍惜對父母愛之關懷表現，有助於優良孝道的傳承。

Thank you to the Counseling Office team for organizing it!

感謝輔導室團隊辦理！

elder/ˈɛldər/長者

We should treat every elder with kindness and patience.我們應該用善意和耐心對待每一位長者。

respect/rɪˈspɛkt/尊重

The event taught students to show respect for older people.這場活動教導學生尊重長輩。

wig/wɪg/假髮

Students wore a white wig to look like an elder.學生戴上白色假髮，扮成長者的樣子。

cane/keɪn/拐杖

Walking with a cane is much slower than students expected.拄著拐杖走路，比學生預期的還要慢得多。
