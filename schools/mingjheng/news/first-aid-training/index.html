First Aid Training for Our Graduating Sixth Graders

急救訓練：畢業前的重要一課

With graduation just around the corner, Mingjheng's sixth-graders recently completed a hands-on first aid course. The Student Affairs Office and homeroom teachers invited a first aid team from Changhua Christian Hospital to teach the entire sixth grade essential lifesaving skills, so that students can help themselves — and others — in an emergency.

「急救訓練」明正國小六年級學生快畢業了，學務處及導師們特別邀請彰基急救團隊到學校教導全體六年級畢業生的急救訓練，讓學生習得相關急救知能，日後碰到緊急事故能自救也能幫助他人！

Students practiced CPR chest compressions on training mannequins, learned how to use an AED, and worked in teams to help a "choking" classmate — turning what could be a scary emergency into a skill they can now use with confidence.

學生們在訓練假人身上練習CPR胸外按壓、學習AED的使用方式，並分組演練協助「噎到」的同學——把原本令人害怕的緊急狀況，變成一項孩子們能夠有自信面對的技能。

Thank you to the Parents' Association for sponsoring this activity!

感謝家長會贊助本次活動相關經費！

Words to Learn · 單字學習

first aid/fɜrst eɪd/急救

Every sixth grader learned first aid skills before graduation.每位六年級學生在畢業前都學會了急救技能。

CPR/ˌsiː piː ˈɑːr/心肺復甦術

Students practiced CPR on training mannequins.學生在訓練假人身上練習心肺復甦術。

AED/ˌeɪ iː ˈdiː/自動體外心臟去顫器

An AED can help restart a stopped heart.自動體外心臟去顫器能幫助心臟恢復跳動。

mannequin/ˈmænɪkɪn/假人

The instructor showed students how to use a mannequin to practice.教官示範如何用假人來練習。

emergency/ɪˈmɜrdʒənsi/緊急狀況

In an emergency, staying calm is the first step.遇到緊急狀況時，保持冷靜是第一步。

rescue/ˈrɛskjuː/救援

Learning first aid means you can rescue a friend in trouble.學會急救，代表你能在朋友遇到危險時伸出援手。
