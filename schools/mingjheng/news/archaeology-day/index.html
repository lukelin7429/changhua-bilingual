Changhua Archaeology: A Day Across a Thousand Years

彰化考古．一日穿越千年

On June 2, 2026, the fifth-grade Class Ji of Mingjheng Elementary took part in "Changhua Archaeology: A Day Across a Thousand Years," a cultural heritage field experience organized by the Changhua County Cultural Affairs Bureau under the guidance of the Ministry of Culture's Bureau of Cultural Heritage — completely free of charge.

彰化縣秀水鄉明正國小五年己班於115年6月2日參與「彰化考古．一日穿越千年」文資場域體驗活動，本活動由文化部文化資產局指導，彰化縣文化局承辦，全程免費。

In the Human Cultures Hall of the National Museum of Natural Science, students watched pottery and cord-marked designs step right out of their textbooks, and the special "Whale Excavation" exhibition revealed that the Hengchun Peninsula isn't just a tourist destination — its very soil holds ancient cultural treasures.

學生在國立自然科學博物館的人類文化廳裡，親眼見證陶器、繩紋從課本裡走出來，在「鯨掘」特展中，驚覺恆春半島不單單只是旅遊勝地，地層裡更蘊含久遠的文化資產。

In the afternoon, at the Niupu Site experience program in Changhua, guides' stories carried everyone a thousand years back in time, as students held stone axes and gently touched pottery shards, tracing a path back to the ancient past!

下午的彰化牛埔遺址體驗課程，在工作人員們娓娓道來的故事裡，大家穿越千年，手握石斧、輕撫陶片，一同追尋千年前！
