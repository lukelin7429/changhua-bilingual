I.

Welcome

歡迎來到明正國小

A beacon of learning and innovation in the serene countryside of Shoushui.

靜謐鄉間裡，一座學習與創新的燈塔。

Situated in the serene Shoushui Township of Changhua County, Mingjheng Elementary School emerges as a beacon of learning and innovation. Surrounded by beautifully landscaped gardens and equipped with impressive facilities, our school provides a stimulating environment for intellectual and personal growth. At Mingjheng, we do more than educate; we cultivate a community where every student has the opportunity to thrive and excel.

明正國小坐落於彰化縣秀水鄉的靜謐鄉間，是學習與創新的燈塔。校園庭園景致優美、設施完善，為孩子的智識與人格成長提供充滿活力的環境。在明正，我們不僅僅是傳授知識，更致力於打造一個讓每個孩子都有機會發光發熱、盡情成長茁壯的學習社群。

Beautifully landscaped, impressively equipped
庭園景致優美，設施一應俱全

A stimulating environment for intellectual and personal growth, where gardens, courts, and open sky give every child room to breathe and grow.

為智識與人格成長打造充滿活力的環境——庭園、球場與開闊天空，讓每個孩子都有自在呼吸、盡情成長的空間。

II.

Our Educational Vision

我們的教育願景

Local roots, global perspective.

立足在地情懷，展望國際視野。

At Mingjheng, we are committed to shaping well-rounded citizens who are attuned to their local roots yet possess a global perspective. Our curriculum is meticulously designed to foster democratic literacy, environmental awareness, and lifelong learning capabilities. We believe in the power of education to transform lives and communities.

明正國小致力於培養兼具在地情懷與國際視野的健全公民。我們的課程精心設計，旨在培養學生的民主素養、環境意識與終身學習的能力。我們深信教育的力量能夠翻轉生命、成就社區。

Key Goals of Our Curriculum

課程四大目標

🌏
Holistic Development
培育全人發展

Nurturing individuals who appreciate their cultural heritage, embrace democratic values, and understand their role in the global community, with a keen environmental consciousness.

珍視文化根基、擁抱民主價值，理解自己在全球社群中的角色，並保有敏銳的環境意識。

🌱
Individual Growth
尊重個別成長

Tailoring our approach to match each student's physical and mental development, respecting personal growth and diverse cultural backgrounds.

依學生身心發展階段調整教學，尊重個人成長歷程與多元文化背景，促進均衡發展。

👩‍🏫
Empowering Teachers
賦能教師成長

Emphasizing autonomy, teamwork, and skill enhancement in creative teaching methods, curriculum integration, and diverse evaluation strategies.

強調自主性與團隊合作，精進創意教學、課程統整與多元評量的能力。

🤝
Community Collaboration
社區攜手合作

Actively involving parents and local resources, inviting community talents to help craft a quality, community-integrated educational experience.

積極邀請家長參與、善用在地資源，攜手打造優質且融入社區特色的教育經驗。

III.

Our Approach to Learning

我們的學習方法

Learner-centered, reading first.

以學生為中心，從閱讀出發。

At the heart of Mingjheng's educational philosophy is a learner-centered approach. We believe in starting with a strong foundation in reading, which opens doors to communication, sharing, and independent thinking. This methodology enables our students to adeptly handle real-life situations, fostering problem-solving skills and igniting the potential for limitless creativity.

以學生為中心，是明正國小教育理念的核心。我們相信從紮實的閱讀基礎出發，能為溝通表達、分享交流與獨立思考開啟大門。這樣的教學方法讓學生能夠靈活應對真實生活情境，培養解決問題的能力，並激發無限的創造潛能。

Discover Mingjheng Elementary School, where education transcends the classroom.
來認識明正國小——一所教育超越教室邊界的學校。
