A Word from the Principal

校長的話 · 謝宗翰校長辦學理念

Eight guiding principles for a warm, bilingual, internationally-minded campus. 八大辦學主軸——溫馨友善、素養導向、本土國際並重、合作學習、藝文閱讀、藝術造校、全人教育、科技創新

I.

Principal Hsieh

校長 · 謝宗翰
18th Principal · 第十八任校長
Tsung-Han Hsieh
謝 宗翰 校長
Took office · 2025-08-01
Tai-He Elementary School, Changhua County
"Local and global at once — through mother tongue, English, and multicultural learning."
「重視母語教學、英語教學、鄉土教學、多元文化教育，透過全球在地化、在地全球化的策略，發展學校特色。」

Welcome to Tai-He. Our school is a hundred and five years old, but every September a new class of first-graders arrives with their own questions, their own hopes, and their own quiet courage. Our job is the same job principals before me have done: to make sure each of those children grows up deeply confident — in themselves, in their words, and in their place in the world.

歡迎來到泰和。我們是百年老校，但每年九月，總有一群帶著自己的問題、自己的期待、自己的勇氣的一年級孩子來報到。我和前面 17 位校長一樣，要做的事都一樣——讓每一位孩子都長成一個對自己有信心、對世界有理解的人。

That is why we believe in holding mother tongue, English, and the world in the same hand. Why every English class has a Foreign English Teacher. Why we treat reading, music, art, and sport as essential, not as extras. The eight principles below shape how we run this school day to day.

這也是為什麼我們強調：母語、英文、世界，三者在同一隻手裡握著；為什麼每一堂英文課都有外師駐校；為什麼閱讀、音樂、美術、體育——我們從不視為附屬，而是每個孩子應有的學習日常。以下八大主軸，是泰和每天怎麼運作的底色。

II.

Eight Guiding Principles

辦學理念八大主軸
PRINCIPLE 01
💗
A Warm, Friendly Campus
營造溫馨友善校園 · 展現人文關懷之美
Character, life, gender equality, human rights, civic and life education — woven into the school day. Caring for vulnerable students, inclusive education, and a barrier-free campus in mind, body, and learning.
PRINCIPLE 02
🌱
Competency-Based Teaching
實施素養導向教學 · 呈現適性發展之美
Self-driven, interactive, mutually supportive — that's how we want students to learn. Small-class teaching that adapts to each child's pace, path, and style.
PRINCIPLE 03
🌐
Local & Global at Once
兼重本土化國際化 · 彰顯多元文化之美
Mother-tongue teaching, English teaching, place-based learning, multicultural education, cross-campus rural-urban exchange. "Glocal" in both directions — going out, and inviting the world in.
PRINCIPLE 04
🤝
Co-Teach & Co-Learn
協同教學合作學習 · 發揚攜手合作之美
Teaching teams that design creative lessons, run curriculum development, and pursue action research together. Group learning, learning communities, English reading contests, science fairs.
PRINCIPLE 05
📚
Reading & the Arts
推動閱讀重視藝文 · 增進生活品味之美
Building a learning organization, book clubs, well-resourced libraries, explicit reading strategy instruction. Activating curriculum, faculty, and clubs to deepen aesthetic experience in music, art, and movement.
PRINCIPLE 06
🌿
School as Museum & Park
藝術造校綠化美化 · 型塑校園空間之美
"School as art museum, school as park." Art-corridor planning, artist-in-residence, sustainable green-campus design — aligned to all nine green-building indicators.
PRINCIPLE 07
🌅
Whole-Person Education
全人教育自我實現 · 弘揚生命圓滿之美
Knowing, feeling, willing, doing; body, mind, hands, brain; intellectual, moral, physical, social, aesthetic; truth, goodness, beauty, the sacred. An integrated guidance system: instruction · discipline · counseling.
PRINCIPLE 08
💡
Tech, Critical Thought, Innovation
善用科技批判反省 · 分享卓越創新之美
Digital learning, knowledge management, knowledge leadership — building real information literacy and information ethics in students and staff. Critically reflecting on official knowledge, curriculum, and pedagogy.
"Every child should grow up deeply confident — in themselves, in their words, and in their place in the world."
「讓每一個孩子，都長成對自己、對自己的話、對自己在世界上的位置，都深具信心的人。」
—— 校長 謝宗翰
