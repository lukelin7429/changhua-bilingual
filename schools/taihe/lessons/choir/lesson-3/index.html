Choir English · Lesson 3 of 6

Singing Techniques

歌唱技巧 · 第三課

Posture, breathing, diction, projection — the techniques your conductor talks about at every rehearsal. 姿勢、呼吸、咬字、音量投射——每次排練指揮都在說的技巧。

All Lessons › Choir English › Lesson 3
A.

Words to Know

本課單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
posture (n.)
姿勢；站姿
Good posture helps you breathe deeply and sing with power.
良好的姿勢幫助你深呼吸，並以更有力量的方式歌唱。
breathe (v.)
呼吸
Breathe from your belly, not just your chest.
用肚子呼吸，不要只用胸腔。
articulate (v.)
咬字清楚；發音清晰
Articulate each word clearly so the audience can understand you.
每個字都要清楚發音，讓觀眾能聽懂你唱什麼。
project (v.)
投射（聲音）；傳送
Project your voice to the back of the hall so everyone can hear.
把你的聲音投射到大廳後方，讓每個人都能聽到。
warm up (phr.v.)
暖嗓；熱身
We always warm up for ten minutes before every rehearsal.
我們每次排練前都會先暖嗓十分鐘。
diction (n.)
發音；咬字
Good diction means the audience understands every word you sing.
咬字清楚，觀眾就能聽懂你唱的每一個字。
B.

Example Sentences

例句練習 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
Stand tall! Good posture opens your lungs and makes your voice stronger.
站直！良好的姿勢能打開你的肺部，讓聲音更有力。
Remember to breathe deeply at every rest in the music.
記得在樂曲的每個休止符時深呼吸。
Please articulate the consonants — every "t" and "d" must be clear.
請咬清楚子音——每個「t」和「d」都要清晰。
Project your voice forward — imagine sending your sound to the last row.
把聲音往前投射——想像你的聲音要送到最後一排。
We warm up with scales and simple exercises to protect our voices.
我們用音階和簡單練習暖嗓，保護我們的聲帶。
Our conductor says good diction is just as important as a good voice.
我們的指揮說，好的咬字跟好的嗓音一樣重要。
C.

Dialogue Practice

對話練習 · 點 🔊 跟著說
A
Why does our conductor always say "project your voice"?
為什麼我們的指揮老是說「投射你的聲音」？
B
It means you should sing loudly enough for the whole audience to hear you clearly.
意思是你要唱得夠響亮，讓全場觀眾都能清楚聽到你。
A
Oh! How do I do that? Do I just sing louder?
原來如此！我怎麼做呢？只是唱更大聲嗎？
B
Not just louder — first fix your posture. Stand straight and open your chest.
不只是更大聲——先修正你的姿勢。站直，打開你的胸腔。
A
And then breathe deeply?
然後深呼吸？
B
Exactly! And don't forget your diction. If people can't understand your words, the song loses its meaning.
對！還有別忘了咬字清楚。如果大家聽不懂你唱什麼，這首歌就失去了意義。
A
I see! Posture, breathing, and diction — I'll focus on all three from now on.
我懂了！姿勢、呼吸、咬字——從現在起我要同時注意這三點。
D.

Short Reading

短篇閱讀 · 點 🔊 聽全文朗讀

Singing in a choir requires much more than a beautiful voice. Technique is everything. The first step is good posture: stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and your back straight. Next, learn to breathe correctly. Choir singers breathe deeply from the diaphragm — the muscle below the lungs — so their voices are supported and powerful. Diction is also essential. Choir singers must articulate every word clearly so the audience understands the lyrics. Finally, singers must learn to project their voices — to direct the sound outward and fill the entire hall. Good technique takes practice, but every rehearsal makes you a stronger, more confident singer.

在合唱團唱歌，需要的不只是美麗的嗓音，技巧才是一切。第一步是良好的姿勢：雙腳與肩同寬站立，背部挺直。接下來，學習正確的呼吸方式。合唱團的歌手從橫隔膜（肺部以下的肌肉）深呼吸，讓聲音更有支撐、更有力量。咬字也非常重要。合唱歌手必須清楚發音每個字，讓觀眾能聽懂歌詞。最後，歌手必須學會「投射」聲音——把聲音向外傳送，填滿整個大廳。好的技巧需要練習，但每一次排練都讓你成為更強大、更有自信的歌手。
