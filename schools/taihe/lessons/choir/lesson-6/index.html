Choir English · Lesson 6 of 6

My Choir Journey

我的合唱故事 · 第六課

Memory, experience, effort, improve, proud, achieve — reflect on what choir has given you, and share your story in English. 回顧合唱帶給你的一切，用英文說出你自己的故事。

All Lessons › Choir English › Lesson 6
A.

Words to Know

本課單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
memory (n.)
記憶；回憶
My favorite memory is singing on stage at the national competition.
我最美好的回憶是在全國比賽舞台上演唱。
experience (n.)
經驗；體驗
The choir gave me an experience I could never get from a textbook.
合唱帶給我的是任何課本都無法給予的體驗。
effort (n.)
努力；付出
All the hard effort during rehearsals made our final concert beautiful.
所有排練時的辛苦付出，讓我們最後的演唱會變得非常美好。
improve (v.)
進步；改善
My voice has improved so much since I joined the choir two years ago.
自從兩年前加入合唱團，我的聲音進步了很多。
proud (adj.)
驕傲的；自豪的
I am so proud of what our team accomplished this year.
我為我們團隊今年所完成的事感到非常自豪。
achieve (v.)
達成；實現
We achieved our goal of performing at the city-wide music festival.
我們實現了在全市音樂節演出的目標。
B.

Example Sentences

例句練習 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
One memory I will never forget is the standing ovation at our concert last year.
我永遠不會忘記的一個回憶，是去年演唱會上觀眾的起立鼓掌。
The experience of singing with a group teaches you things that no classroom can.
和一群人一起唱歌的體驗，教給你的是任何課堂都無法傳授的東西。
It took a lot of effort, but every practice was worth it.
這需要很多努力，但每一次練習都是值得的。
My English has also improved because I have to learn English song lyrics.
我的英文也進步了，因為我必須學英文歌詞。
I am proud to be a member of the Tai-He choir.
我很自豪能夠成為泰和合唱團的一員。
Together we achieved something none of us could have done alone.
我們一起達成了任何一個人單獨都無法完成的事。
C.

Dialogue Practice

對話練習 · 點 🔊 跟著說
A
I heard you're graduating soon. What was your favorite experience in the choir?
我聽說你快要畢業了。你在合唱團最喜歡的體驗是什麼？
B
It's hard to pick just one. But my best memory is performing at the music festival in fifth grade.
很難只選一個。但我最美好的回憶是五年級在音樂節上演出。
A
That sounds amazing! Did it take a lot of effort to get ready?
聽起來很棒！準備那次演出需要很多努力嗎？
B
Yes! We had extra rehearsals every week. But I improved so much during that month.
是的！我們每週都有額外的排練。但是在那個月裡我進步了很多。
A
Are you proud of what you achieved?
你為自己達成的事感到自豪嗎？
B
Absolutely! Choir taught me that with effort and teamwork, you can achieve almost anything. I'll always carry that with me.
當然！合唱教會了我：只要努力和團隊合作，幾乎什麼都能達成。我會永遠把這份精神帶在身邊。
D.

Short Reading

短篇閱讀 · 點 🔊 聽全文朗讀

When you leave the choir, you take more than just memories. You take the skills, the friendships, and the lessons that shaped who you are. Every experience — the long rehearsals, the stage fright, the moment the audience claps — becomes part of your story. The effort you put in did not just improve your singing. It improved your patience, your discipline, and your confidence. You learned that achieving a difficult goal is possible when you work with others. You felt proud not just for yourself, but for every member of your team. A choir teaches you that your voice alone is beautiful — but together, you are extraordinary. That is your choir journey. And now you can share it with the world in English.

當你離開合唱團時，你帶走的不只是回憶，還有那些塑造了你的技能、友誼和人生道理。每一個體驗——漫長的排練、舞台上的怯場、觀眾鼓掌的那一刻——都成為你故事的一部分。你所付出的努力，不只讓你的歌聲進步，也提升了你的耐心、自律和自信。你學到了：當你與他人攜手合作，達成困難的目標是可能的。你感到驕傲，不只是為了自己，而是為了團隊中的每一位成員。合唱告訴你：你一個人的聲音已經很美好——但大家在一起，你們是無與倫比的。這就是你的合唱故事。現在，你可以用英文把它分享給全世界。
