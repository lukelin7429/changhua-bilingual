Choir English · Lesson 5 of 6

On Stage

站上舞台 · 第五課

Stage, audience, bow, nerves, confidence, costume — words for the most exciting day of the year: performance day! 舞台、觀眾、鞠躬、緊張、自信、服裝——說出一年中最令人興奮的那一天。

All Lessons › Choir English › Lesson 5
A.

Words to Know

本課單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
stage (n.)
舞台
Walking onto the stage for the first time made my heart beat fast.
第一次走上舞台讓我的心跳加速。
audience (n.)
觀眾；聽眾
The audience clapped loudly at the end of every song.
每首歌結束後，觀眾都大聲鼓掌。
bow (v.)
鞠躬；謝幕
After the final song, we all stepped forward and bowed together.
最後一首歌結束後，我們一起向前踏出一步，齊聲鞠躬謝幕。
nerves (n.)
緊張；怯場
I had terrible nerves backstage, but once the music started I felt calm.
我在後台緊張得不得了，但音樂一開始我就平靜下來了。
confidence (n.)
自信；信心
Performing gives you confidence that you can use in everyday life.
演出能給你自信，讓你在日常生活中也能更勇敢。
costume (n.)
服裝；演出服
We all wore the same costume so we looked united on stage.
我們都穿著相同的服裝，在舞台上看起來很整齊一致。
B.

Example Sentences

例句練習 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
We practiced for months before finally stepping onto the stage.
我們練習了好幾個月，才終於踏上舞台。
The audience was so large that I could barely see the back of the hall.
觀眾多到我幾乎看不到大廳最後面。
When we bowed, the applause felt like thunder — it was amazing.
當我們鞠躬謝幕時，掌聲如雷——真的太棒了。
It's normal to feel nerves before a performance — even professional singers do.
演出前感到緊張是正常的——就連專業歌手也會這樣。
Each performance builds more confidence, so keep singing!
每一次演出都能培養更多自信，所以繼續唱下去！
Our costumes are purple and white — the colors of our school.
我們的演出服是紫色和白色——我們學校的顏色。
C.

Dialogue Practice

對話練習 · 點 🔊 跟著說
A
The concert is tomorrow! I'm so nervous I can't sleep.
音樂會明天就要舉行了！我緊張到睡不著。
B
I had nerves too last year. But once you step on stage, you'll feel the energy from the audience.
我去年也很緊張。但是一旦你踏上舞台，你就會感受到觀眾的能量。
A
Really? Does that help? And what about the costume — do I put it on before we go in?
真的嗎？那樣有幫助嗎？還有服裝——我在進去之前就要穿上嗎？
B
Yes, we all change together backstage. When we wear the same costume, it feels like we're one team.
是的，我們在後台一起換衣服。穿上相同的服裝，感覺我們就是一個整體。
A
I love that! And when do we bow?
我喜歡這樣！那我們什麼時候鞠躬謝幕？
B
After the last song, the conductor will signal us. We step forward and bow together. The applause will give you incredible confidence!
最後一首歌之後，指揮會給我們信號。我們一起向前踏步、一起鞠躬。那掌聲會帶給你難以置信的自信！
D.

Short Reading

短篇閱讀 · 點 🔊 聽全文朗讀

Performance day is the most exciting and scary day for any choir member. Before the concert, the singers put on their costumes and wait backstage, full of nerves. But the moment the conductor raises the baton and the music begins, something magical happens — the nerves disappear and the training takes over. On stage, the choir faces a sea of faces in the audience. Every eye is watching. Every ear is listening. When the last note fades and the audience begins to clap, the feeling is unforgettable. The choir steps forward and bows together, and in that moment, every singer feels a rush of confidence and pride. Performance day is not just about singing. It is about growing.

演出日是每位合唱團員最令人興奮也最緊張的一天。演唱會開始前，歌手們穿上服裝，懷著滿心的緊張在後台等待。但在指揮高舉指揮棒、音樂響起的那一刻，神奇的事情發生了——緊張消失了，訓練的成果自然流瀉而出。站在舞台上，合唱團面對著台下一片觀眾的臉。每一雙眼睛都在看，每一雙耳朵都在聆聽。當最後一個音符消逝，觀眾開始鼓掌的那一刻，那份感動令人終生難忘。合唱團一起向前踏步、一起鞠躬謝幕，就在那個瞬間，每一位歌手心中湧現自信與驕傲。演出日不只是唱歌，更是成長的一刻。
