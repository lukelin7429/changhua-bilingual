Choir English

合唱英語 · 六課自學課程

Six lessons for Tai-He choir members in Grade 5 and 6 — learn to talk about your choir experience in English. 專為泰和合唱團五、六年級團員設計——學會用英文分享你的合唱故事。

← Back to All Lessons · 回課程首頁

Each lesson has five parts: vocabulary, example sentences, dialogue practice, a short reading, and a quiz. Click 🔊 to hear any word or sentence spoken aloud. Work through all six lessons and you will be able to tell your choir story in English!

每一課包含五個部分：單字、例句、對話練習、短篇閱讀、小考。點 🔊 就能聽英文朗讀。六課學完，你就能用英文跟外國人說你的合唱故事！
