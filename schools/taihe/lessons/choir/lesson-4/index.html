Choir English · Lesson 4 of 6

Teamwork & Harmony

團隊合作 · 第四課

Teamwork, cooperate, listen, balance, support, focus — how singing together teaches us to be better people. 合唱如何教我們成為更好的人——團隊合作的力量。

All Lessons › Choir English › Lesson 4
A.

Words to Know

本課單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
teamwork (n.)
團隊合作
Without teamwork, a choir cannot make beautiful music.
沒有團隊合作，合唱團無法創造美麗的音樂。
cooperate (v.)
合作；配合
We cooperate well because we trust and respect each other.
我們合作得很好，因為我們互相信任和尊重。
listen (v.)
聆聽；傾聽
Listen to the other voice parts while you sing your own.
在唱自己聲部的同時，也要聆聽其他聲部。
balance (n.)
平衡；均衡
Good balance means no one voice part is too loud or too soft.
好的平衡表示沒有任何一個聲部太大聲或太小聲。
support (v.)
支持；幫助
The older choir members support the newer ones during rehearsal.
老團員在排練時會支持和幫助新團員。
focus (v.)
專注；集中注意力
Focus on the conductor at all times during the performance.
演出時要隨時專注在指揮身上。
B.

Example Sentences

例句練習 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
Choir requires a lot of teamwork — every single singer counts.
合唱需要很多團隊合作——每一位歌手都很重要。
We cooperate well, so our rehearsals are always productive.
我們合作得很好，所以每次排練都很有效率。
Always listen to the piano accompaniment to stay in rhythm.
要隨時聆聽鋼琴伴奏，才能保持在正確的節奏上。
The balance between loud and soft sections creates the beauty of the piece.
強弱之間的平衡創造了這首曲子的美感。
We all support each other — that's what makes us a real team.
我們互相支持——這就是讓我們成為真正團隊的原因。
Focus on the music — don't look around during the performance!
專注在音樂上——演出時不要東張西望！
C.

Dialogue Practice

對話練習 · 點 🔊 跟著說
A
I made a mistake during rehearsal today — I sang the wrong notes.
我今天排練的時候出錯了——我唱錯音了。
B
That's okay! Everyone makes mistakes. That's why we have teamwork.
沒關係！每個人都會出錯。這就是為什麼我們需要團隊合作。
A
But I think I ruined the balance of the whole section.
但是我覺得我破壞了整個聲部的平衡。
B
Don't worry. Next time, just listen more carefully to the other parts.
別擔心。下次，只要更仔細地聆聽其他聲部就好。
A
You're right. I should focus more and cooperate better with everyone.
你說得對。我應該更專注，更好地和大家配合。
B
That's the spirit! We support each other here. You'll do great next time.
這才是正確態度！我們在這裡互相支持。你下次一定會表現很好的。
D.

Short Reading

短篇閱讀 · 點 🔊 聽全文朗讀

A choir is not just about individual talent. It is about teamwork. Each singer must listen carefully to the others and adjust their voice so that the whole group is balanced. If one person sings too loudly, it can ruin the harmony for everyone. That is why choir teaches students a very important lesson: your actions affect the people around you. In the choir, we cooperate, we support each other, and we focus together on making something beautiful. The skills you learn in choir — listening, cooperating, and focusing — are useful not only in music, but in every part of life.

合唱不只是個人才華的展現，而是關於團隊合作。每位歌手都必須仔細聆聽他人，並調整自己的聲音，讓整個團體保持平衡。如果有一個人唱得太大聲，可能會破壞所有人的和聲。這就是為什麼合唱教給學生一個非常重要的道理：你的行為會影響你周圍的人。在合唱團裡，我們合作、互相支持，共同專注於創造美好的音樂。你在合唱中學到的技能——聆聽、合作、專注——不只在音樂上有用，在生活的每個層面都很重要。
