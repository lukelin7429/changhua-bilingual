Choir English · Lesson 1 of 6

What Is a Choir?

認識合唱 · 第一課

Learn the six words every choir member needs to know to talk about choir in English. 學會六個關鍵單字，讓你能用英文介紹合唱團。

All Lessons › Choir English › Lesson 1
A.

Words to Know

本課單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
choir (n.)
合唱團
Our school choir wins competitions every year.
我們學校的合唱團每年都贏得比賽。
conductor (n.)
指揮
The conductor raises her baton and everyone becomes quiet.
指揮舉起指揮棒，所有人立刻安靜下來。
rehearsal (n.)
排練
We have rehearsal every Tuesday and Wednesday after school.
我們每週二、三放學後都有排練。
perform (v.)
表演；演唱
We will perform at the city concert hall in December.
我們十二月將在市立音樂廳演出。
harmony (n.)
和聲
The harmony in this piece is really beautiful.
這首曲子的和聲真的很美。
melody (n.)
旋律
Can you hum the melody of our school song?
你能哼出我們校歌的旋律嗎？
B.

Example Sentences

例句練習 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
Our school choir has forty-two members.
我們學校的合唱團共有四十二位成員。
The conductor smiled when we sang the last note perfectly.
當我們把最後那個音唱得完美時，指揮微笑了。
Today's rehearsal will focus on the final verse of the song.
今天的排練將著重在歌曲的最後一段。
We are excited to perform in front of our parents next week.
我們很期待下週在爸媽面前演唱。
The soprano and alto parts create beautiful harmony together.
女高音和女低音聲部一起創造出美麗的和聲。
Everyone knows the melody — it's our school song!
每個人都知道這個旋律——那是我們的校歌！
C.

Dialogue Practice

對話練習 · 點 🔊 跟著說
A
Hey, are you in the school choir?
嘿，你有加入學校合唱團嗎？
B
Yes! I've been in the choir for two years now.
是的！我加入合唱團已經兩年了。
A
What do you do in the choir?
你在合唱團做些什麼？
B
We rehearse twice a week and perform at school events.
我們每週排練兩次，並在學校活動中演唱。
A
What part do you sing?
你唱哪個聲部？
B
I sing harmony — my voice blends with the melody.
我唱和聲——我的聲音和旋律融合在一起。
A
That sounds amazing! How can I join?
聽起來好棒！我要怎麼加入？
B
Talk to our conductor after school tomorrow!
明天放學後去找我們的指揮說！
D.

Short Reading

短篇閱讀 · 點 🔊 聽全文朗讀

The Tai-He Elementary School Choir is one of our school's greatest treasures. Every year, students from Grade 3 to Grade 6 try out, and the best singers are selected to join. The choir rehearses every Tuesday and Wednesday after school. Under the guidance of a skilled conductor, choir members learn how to sing in harmony, breathe correctly, and perform with confidence. The choir performs at the school's annual concert, the city arts festival, and special events across Changhua County. For many students, joining the choir is one of the best memories of their elementary school years.

泰和國小合唱團是我們學校最珍貴的寶藏之一。每年，三年級到六年級的學生都可以試唱，最優秀的歌手會被選入合唱團。合唱團每週二、三放學後排練。在指揮的帶領下，團員學習如何唱和聲、正確呼吸，並充滿自信地上台演唱。合唱團在學校年度音樂會、市立藝術節及彰化縣各地活動登台演出。對許多學生而言，加入合唱團是國小時代最美好的回憶之一。
