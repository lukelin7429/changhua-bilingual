Choir English · Lesson 2 of 6

Our Voices

認識聲部 · 第二課

Learn the words for different voice parts — soprano, alto, pitch, blend, range, and voice part. 學習聲部相關詞彙——女高音、女低音、音高、融合、音域、聲部。

All Lessons › Choir English › Lesson 2
A.

Words to Know

本課單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
soprano (n.)
女高音
The soprano section sings the highest notes.
女高音聲部唱最高的音。
alto (n.)
女低音；中低音
She sings alto — her voice adds depth to the harmony.
她唱女低音——她的聲音讓和聲更有深度。
pitch (n.)
音高
Keep your pitch steady — don't go too high or too low.
保持音高穩定——不要唱太高或太低。
blend (v.)
融合；混合
When our voices blend well, the choir sounds like one instrument.
當我們的聲音融合得好，合唱團聽起來就像一件樂器。
range (n.)
音域
She has a wide range — from very low to very high notes.
她的音域很寬——從很低到很高的音都能唱。
voice part (n.)
聲部
Each singer belongs to a voice part based on their natural range.
每位歌手根據自己的自然音域被分配到一個聲部。
B.

Example Sentences

例句練習 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
The soprano section always enters first in this piece.
在這首曲子裡，女高音聲部總是第一個進來。
I sing alto, so I usually sing lower notes than the sopranos.
我唱女低音，所以我通常比女高音唱更低的音。
Listen carefully to the piano — it will help you find your pitch.
仔細聽鋼琴——它能幫你找到正確的音高。
Our two sections blend beautifully in the chorus.
我們兩個聲部在副歌部分融合得非常美。
Her range is impressive — she can sing both high and low notes.
她的音域令人印象深刻——高音和低音都能唱。
Do you know your voice part for the school concert?
你知道你在學校音樂會的聲部是什麼嗎？
C.

Dialogue Practice

對話練習 · 點 🔊 跟著說
A
What voice part do you sing in the choir?
你在合唱團唱哪個聲部？
B
I sing soprano. My voice is quite high.
我唱女高音。我的聲音很高。
A
Really? I sing alto. My range is a bit lower.
真的嗎？我唱女低音。我的音域低一點。
B
Do you find it hard to keep your pitch right?
你覺得保持音高正確很難嗎？
A
Sometimes! I need to listen carefully to the other voices.
有時候！我需要仔細聆聽其他人的聲音。
B
Exactly. And when we blend well, the music sounds amazing!
對啊。當我們融合得好的時候，音樂聽起來超棒的！
A
That's why I love being in the choir. Every voice part matters.
這就是我喜歡合唱團的原因。每個聲部都很重要。
D.

Short Reading

短篇閱讀 · 點 🔊 聽全文朗讀

In a choir, not everyone sings the same note. Singers are divided into voice parts. The soprano section sings the highest notes, while the alto section sings lower notes that create harmony below. Each voice part has its own important role. A good choir conductor listens carefully to make sure every section sings at the right pitch. When the voice parts blend together, they create a full, rich sound that one singer alone could never make. Finding your voice part is one of the most exciting steps in joining a choir — it tells you exactly where your voice belongs.

在合唱團裡，不是每個人都唱同一個音。歌手被分配到不同的聲部。女高音唱最高的音，而女低音則唱較低的音，在下方構成和聲。每個聲部都有各自重要的角色。好的合唱指揮會仔細聆聽，確保每個聲部都以正確的音高演唱。當各聲部融合在一起時，它們創造出一種豐富飽滿的聲音，是任何一個人單獨無法唱出的。找到你的聲部，是加入合唱團最令人興奮的一步——它告訴你你的聲音屬於哪裡。
