My School Day · Lesson 2

Let's Clean Up!

打掃行動 · 掃、拖、擦、撿、整理！

Action words for morning cleaning — sweep, mop, wipe, and more! 晨光打掃的動作單字：掃地、拖地、擦、撿、整理——用英文說說看！

← Back to My School Day
A.

Words to Know

打掃動作的英文 · 點 🔊 聽發音
sweep (v.)
掃地
Sweep the floor with a broom.
用掃把把地板掃乾淨。
mop (v.)
拖地
Mop the floor until it shines.
拖地拖到閃閃發亮。
wipe (v.)
擦
Wipe the desks and windows clean.
把桌子和窗戶擦乾淨。
pick up (v.)
撿起
Please pick up the trash on the ground.
請把地上的垃圾撿起來。
tidy up (v.)
整理
Tidy up your desk before you leave.
離開前把你的桌子整理好。
outdoor area (n.)
外掃區
My outdoor area is near the playground.
我的外掃區在操場旁邊。
B.

Say It While You Clean

打掃時這樣說 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
Time to clean up! Everyone get your tools.
打掃時間到！大家拿好工具。
Sweep first, then mop.
先掃地，再拖地。
Don't forget to wipe the windows.
別忘了擦窗戶。
Please pick up the trash near the door.
請把門口附近的垃圾撿起來。
My outdoor area is near the gate.
我的外掃區在大門旁邊。
We're done! What a clean classroom!
打掃完了！好乾淨的教室！
