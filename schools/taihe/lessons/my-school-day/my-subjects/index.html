My School Day · Lesson 4

My Subjects

我的學科 · 國語課、數學課、自然課……

Six subjects — learn how to say each class in English! 六個學科的英文說法，從今天起用英文說「我今天有數學課」！

← Back to My School Day
A.

Words to Know

學科名稱的英文 · 點 🔊 聽發音
Chinese class (n.)
國語課
I have Chinese class first today.
我今天第一節是國語課。
math class (n.)
數學課
Math class has lots of numbers.
數學課有好多數字。
science class (n.)
自然課
We do experiments in science class.
我們在自然課做實驗。
social studies (n.)
社會課
Social studies teaches us about our community.
社會課讓我們認識社區。
PE class (n.)
體育課
We run and play games in PE class.
體育課要跑步和玩遊戲。
arts and crafts (n.)
美勞課
I love arts and crafts day!
我最喜歡有美勞課的日子！
B.

Talk About Your Day

用英文說說你的課表 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
What class do you have now?
你現在上什麼課？
I have math class. It's time to count!
我有數學課，算數時間到了！
Science class is so much fun!
自然課好有趣！
PE class is my favorite!
體育課是我最喜歡的！
What class is next?
下一節是什麼課？
Next is arts and crafts. I can't wait!
下一節是美勞課，等不及了！
