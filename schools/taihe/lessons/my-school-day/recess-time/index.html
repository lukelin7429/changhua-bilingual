My School Day · Lesson 5

Recess Time!

下課了！· 跳繩、打球、鬼抓人……

The bell rings — let's go play! Learn playground games in English. 鈴聲響了，下課！跳繩、打球、捉迷藏……用英文說說你最愛的下課活動！

← Back to My School Day
A.

Words to Know

下課活動的英文 · 點 🔊 聽發音
jump rope (v.)
跳繩
Let's jump rope together!
我們一起跳繩吧！
play ball (phr.)
打球
Do you want to play ball with us?
你想跟我們一起打球嗎？
hide-and-seek (n.)
捉迷藏
Hide-and-seek is my favorite game.
捉迷藏是我最喜歡的遊戲。
swing (n.)
鞦韆
I love to play on the swing.
我喜歡盪鞦韆。
tag (n.)
鬼抓人
Let's play tag! You're it!
我們來玩鬼抓人！你當鬼！
playground (n.)
操場
We play on the playground at recess.
我們下課在操場玩。
B.

Say It at Recess

下課時這樣說 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
The bell rings. It's recess time!
鈴聲響了，下課了！
Let's go to the playground!
我們去操場！
I want to play hide-and-seek. Who's it?
我想玩捉迷藏。誰當鬼？
You're it! Count to ten!
你當鬼！數到十！
Push me on the swing, please!
推我一下鞦韆，好嗎！
Time's up! Back to class.
時間到！回教室。
