Shuttlecock Kicking

踢毽子 · 泰和的下課時光

A classic Taiwanese playground game — and the English words to play it in. 台灣傳統童玩「踢毽子」，配上玩這個遊戲會用到的英語

← Back to Lessons
Tai-He students playing jianzi — shuttlecock kicking — on the school grounds. Watch how they kick, balance, and take turns, then learn the words below.
泰和的同學在校園裡踢毽子。看他們怎麼踢、怎麼保持平衡、怎麼輪流玩，再學下面的英語單字。
A.

Words to Know

踢毽子會用到的單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
shuttlecock (n.)
毽子
Kick the shuttlecock up in the air.
把毽子往空中踢。
kick (v.)
踢
I can kick it ten times!
我可以踢十下！
foot (n.)
腳
Kick it with the side of your foot.
用腳的內側踢。
balance (n.)
平衡
Good balance keeps you steady on one foot.
平衡感好，單腳站才穩。
throw (v.)
丟、拋
Throw the shuttlecock to your friend.
把毽子拋給你的朋友。
catch (v.)
接住
Can you catch it with one hand?
你能用一隻手接住嗎？
take turns (phr.)
輪流
Let's take turns with our classmates.
我們跟同學輪流玩。
playground (n.)
操場
We play on the playground after lunch.
午餐後我們在操場上玩。
B.

Say It While You Play

玩的時候這樣說 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
Let's play shuttlecock!
我們來踢毽子吧！
Kick the shuttlecock with your foot.
用你的腳踢毽子。
How many times can you kick it?
你可以踢幾下？
Don't let it fall!
不要讓它掉下來！
Pass it to your friend.
把它傳給你的朋友。
Take turns with your classmates.
跟同學輪流玩。
