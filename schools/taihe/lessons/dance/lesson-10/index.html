Dance Course · Lesson 10

Six Years of Dance

六年的舞蹈

Look back on everything dance has given you — and share it with the world. 泰和舞蹈英語課 · 第 10 課

← Back to Dance Course · All Lessons
A.

Words to Know

本課重點單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
grow v.
成長
I have grown so much through dance.
我透過舞蹈成長了很多。
strong adj.
強壯的
Six years of dance have made me strong.
六年的舞蹈讓我變得強壯。
graceful adj.
優雅的
Our movements are smooth and graceful now.
我們的動作現在流暢又優雅。
share v.
分享
I want to share my love of dance with the world.
我想把我對舞蹈的熱愛分享給全世界。
remember v.
記得
I will always remember my dance teachers.
我永遠都會記得我的舞蹈老師。
B.

Key Sentences

重點例句 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
Six years of dance have made me stronger and more graceful.
六年的舞蹈讓我變得更強壯、更優雅。
I have grown so much — in my body and in my heart.
我成長了很多——在身體上，也在心裡。
Dance taught me to be confident, to work as a team, and to never give up.
舞蹈教會了我要有自信、要團隊合作，以及永不放棄。
I will always remember the music, the stage, and my dance partners.
我永遠都會記得那些音樂、舞台，還有我的舞蹈夥伴們。
Now I can share my story of dance with friends from around the world.
現在，我可以把我的舞蹈故事分享給來自世界各地的朋友。
C.

Dialogue

對話練習 · 點 🔊 跟著說
Foreign Friend
You danced for six years? What did you learn?
Tai-He Student
I learned to move my body, feel the rhythm, and keep my balance.
Foreign Friend
Did it make you stronger?
Tai-He Student
Yes! And more graceful, more confident, and better at working with others.
Foreign Friend
That is amazing. Dance sounds like the best school subject!
D.

Short Reading

短篇閱讀 · 點 🔊 聽整句
We started dance class in first grade, and now we are in sixth grade.
Six years is a long time — and dance has changed us.
Our bodies are stronger and more graceful.
Our minds are more confident and focused.
We have learned to work together, to practice hard, and to never give up.
Most of all, we have learned to love dance.
Wherever we go in life, the lessons of dance will stay with us forever.
