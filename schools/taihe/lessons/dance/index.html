Dance Course

舞蹈英語課 · 泰和六年舞蹈

Ten lessons about dance — body, rhythm, balance, confidence, and performing. 十堂關於舞蹈的英語課——身體、節奏、平衡、自信，以及登台表演

← Back to All Lessons · 所有課程

Tai-He students have danced every week since first grade. These ten lessons teach you the English words and stories behind six years of dance — so you can share what you have learned with the world. 泰和的小朋友從一年級就開始每週上舞蹈課。這十堂課把六年舞蹈的英語單字和故事都整理進來，讓你可以跟全世界分享你學到了什麼。
