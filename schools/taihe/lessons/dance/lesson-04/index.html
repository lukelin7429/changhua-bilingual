Dance Course · Lesson 04

Step by Step

一步一步來

Steps, jumps, turns — learn the moves that build every dance. 泰和舞蹈英語課 · 第 04 課

← Back to Dance Course · All Lessons
A.

Words to Know

本課重點單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
step n.
步伐
Take one big step to the right.
往右邊邁出一大步。
jump v.
跳
Jump up and land softly on both feet.
往上跳，然後輕輕用雙腳落地。
turn v.
轉身
Turn around in a full circle.
原地轉一整圈。
kick v.
踢腿
Kick your leg out to the front.
把腿往前踢出去。
point v.
指、伸直
Point your toes when you stretch your leg.
伸腿的時候，把腳趾用力伸直。
B.

Key Sentences

重點例句 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
First, take three steps to the left.
首先，往左走三步。
Jump up and stretch your arms at the same time.
跳起來的同時，把手臂伸出去。
Turn slowly and keep your eyes on one spot.
慢慢轉身，眼睛盯著一個點。
Kick your leg and then point your toes.
踢出你的腿，然後把腳趾用力伸直。
Every dance is made of small steps put together.
每個舞蹈都是由很多小動作組合而成的。
C.

Dialogue

對話練習 · 點 🔊 跟著說
Teacher Sara
Today we learn a new step. Watch me first!
Annie
It looks hard, Teacher Sara.
Teacher Sara
No worries — we do it step by step. First, step to the right.
Annie
Like this? Step, then jump?
Teacher Sara
Yes! Step, jump, and then turn. You got it!
D.

Short Reading

短篇閱讀 · 點 🔊 聽整句
Every dance is made of many small moves put together.
A "step" is when you move one foot to a new place.
A "jump" is when both feet leave the ground.
A "turn" is when your whole body spins around.
When you kick, you swing your leg out quickly.
When you point your toes, you stretch your foot to look longer.
Learning step by step — one move at a time — is the best way to learn dance!
