Pool Rules & Safety

規則與安全 · 泰和游泳課 第4課

Stay safe and follow the rules — every swimmer's most important lesson.保持安全、遵守規則——每位泳者最重要的一課。

Words to Know

游泳安全英文單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
rule (n.)
規則
Follow the rules to keep everyone safe.
遵守規則讓大家保持安全。
safe (adj.)
安全的
The lifeguard keeps the pool safe for all swimmers.
救生員讓泳池對所有泳者都是安全的。
whistle (n.)

Stop swimming when you hear the whistle.
聽到哨子聲時停止游泳。
emergency (n.)

In an emergency, shout for help and get out of the water.
緊急情況時，大叫求救並離開水中。

Always swim with a buddy — never swim alone.

drown (v.)

Never swim alone to prevent drowning.
絕對不要一個人游泳以防溺水。

如果有人需要幫助，向他們伸出你的手。

C.

Let's Talk

對話練習 · 點 🔊 跟著說
Teacher
Before we swim, let's review the rules. Rule one: never run near the pool. Why?
游泳前，我們來複習規則。第一條：不要在泳池附近奔跑。為什麼？
Student A
Because the floor is wet and we might fall!
因為地板是濕的，我們可能會跌倒！
Teacher
Correct! Rule two: always swim with a buddy. Never swim alone.
正確！第二條：永遠和夥伴一起游泳。不要一個人游。
Student B
What is the buddy system?
「夥伴制度」是什麼？
Teacher
You and your partner watch each other. If something goes wrong, your buddy can get help.
你和你的夥伴互相照看。如果發生什麼事，你的夥伴可以去求援。
Student A
What do we do when we hear the whistle?
我們聽到哨子聲時要做什麼？
Teacher
Stop swimming immediately and look at the lifeguard. A whistle means there is a warning or an emergency.
立刻停止游泳並看向救生員。哨子聲代表有警告或緊急情況。
Student B
Safety first! I will always follow the rules.
安全第一！我一定會遵守規則。
D.

Reading Time

閱讀時間 · 點 🔊 聽全文

Safety is the most important thing at a swimming pool. There are rules everyone must follow. First, never run near the pool — the wet floor is very slippery. Second, always swim with a buddy. The buddy system means no one swims alone, and you can help each other in an emergency. Third, always listen to the lifeguard. When you hear the whistle, stop and pay attention. The whistle is a warning that something needs your attention. If you see someone who needs help, reach out your arm and call for the lifeguard. Following these rules keeps everyone happy and safe!

在游泳池中，安全是最重要的事。每個人都必須遵守規則。第一，不要在泳池附近奔跑——濕地板非常滑。第二，永遠和夥伴一起游泳。夥伴制度代表沒有人單獨游泳，緊急情況時可以互相幫助。第三，永遠聽從救生員的指示。聽到哨子聲時，停下來注意。哨子聲是警告你注意某件事。如果你看到有人需要幫助，伸出你的手並呼叫救生員。遵守這些規則，讓大家都能快樂且安全！
