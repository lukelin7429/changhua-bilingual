Racing & Timing

競賽與計時 · 泰和游泳課 第5課

Ready, set, go! Learn how a swimming race works.預備，開始！學習游泳比賽的英文單字和用語。

← Back to Swimming Lessons · 回游泳課
A.

Words to Know

競賽英文單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音
race (n.)
比賽
Our class had a swimming race today.
我們班今天舉行了游泳比賽。
start (v.)
開始
The race will start when I blow the whistle.
我吹哨子時比賽將開始。
finish (v./n.)
完成／終點
Touch the wall to finish the race.
碰牆代表完成比賽。
timer (n.)
計時器
The timer records how fast you swim.
計時器記錄你游得多快。
lap (n.)
一趟
Swim one lap from one wall to the other.
從一端牆游到另一端算一趟。
record (n.)
紀錄
She broke the school record with a perfect swim!
她用完美的表現打破了學校紀錄！
lane (n.)
水道
Each swimmer stays in their own lane during a race.
比賽中每位泳者待在自己的水道。
champion (n.)
冠軍
The champion swam the fastest of all!
冠軍游得比所有人都快！
B.

Example Sentences

英文例句 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
Get ready for the race — swimmers, take your positions!
準備好參加比賽——泳者，就定位！
Stay in your own lane and swim as fast as you can.
待在自己的水道，盡全力游快。
Touch the wall to finish, then look at the timer.
碰牆完成後，再看計時器。
Two laps makes 50 meters — that's our race distance!
兩趟等於50公尺——那是我們的比賽距離！
The champion smiled and waved after breaking the school record.
冠軍打破學校紀錄後微笑揮手。
C.

Let's Talk

對話練習 · 點 🔊 跟著說
Student A
I'm so nervous! It's my first swimming race!
我好緊張！這是我的第一次游泳比賽！
Student B
Don't be nervous. Just swim your best and stay in your lane!
別緊張。盡力游，並待在自己的水道！
Teacher
Swimmers, take your positions at the wall. When I blow the whistle, the race will start.
泳者，在牆邊就定位。我吹哨子時比賽就開始。
Student A
How many laps is the race?
比賽游幾趟？
Teacher
Two laps — that's 50 meters total. Touch the wall at the far end, turn, and swim back to finish.
兩趟——總共50公尺。碰到遠端的牆，轉身，再游回來完成。
Student B
I want to break the school record!
我想打破學校紀錄！
Teacher
Ready... Set... Go! Great job! The timer says you swam 50 meters in 55 seconds — a personal best!
預備……開始！做得很好！計時器顯示你50公尺游了55秒——個人最佳紀錄！
Student A
I finished my first race! I'm so happy!
我完成了我的第一次比賽！我好開心！
D.

Reading Time

閱讀時間 · 點 🔊 聽全文

Swimming races are exciting! In a race, each swimmer has their own lane. The race starts when the teacher blows the whistle. Swimmers push off the wall and swim as fast as they can. A lap means swimming from one end of the pool to the other. In our school pool, two laps equal 50 meters. A timer records how fast each swimmer goes. When you touch the wall at the end, you have finished! The fastest swimmer becomes the champion. Some champions even break school records! At Tai-He Elementary School, racing helps students improve and build confidence. Everyone can win if they try their best!

游泳比賽非常令人興奮！在比賽中，每位泳者有自己的水道。老師吹哨子時比賽開始。泳者用腳蹬牆，盡全力游快。一趟就是從泳池一端游到另一端。在我們學校的泳池，兩趟等於50公尺。計時器記錄每位泳者游得多快。當你碰到終點的牆，就完成了！最快的泳者成為冠軍。有些冠軍甚至打破學校紀錄！在泰和國小，比賽幫助學生進步並建立自信。只要盡力，每個人都可以是贏家！
