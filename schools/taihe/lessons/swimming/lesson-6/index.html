Let's Talk

對話練習 · 點 🔊 跟著說

角色扮演：Student A 扮演訪客，Student B 扮演泰和學生介紹學校。
Role-play: Student A plays a visitor; Student B is a Tai-He student showing off the school.

Student A
Hello! This is a beautiful school. What makes it special?
你好！這是一所美麗的學校。是什麼讓它這麼特別？
Student B
Welcome to Tai-He Elementary School! We have our very own swimming pool!
歡迎來到泰和國小！我們有自己的游泳池！
Student A
Really? That's impressive! Tell me more.
真的嗎？太令人印象深刻了！告訴我更多。
Student B
In all of Changhua County, only three elementary schools have a swimming pool. Tai-He is one of them. It's our most unique feature!
在整個彰化縣，只有三所國小有游泳池。泰和就是其中之一。這是我們最獨特的特色！
Student A
Wow! What do students learn here?
哇！學生在這裡學什麼？
Student B
We learn freestyle, backstroke, and breaststroke. Some students are champions! We are very proud of our swimming achievements.
我們學習自由式、仰式和蛙式。有些學生是冠軍！我們非常以我們的游泳成就為驕傲。
Student A
That's amazing. I wish my school had a pool too!
太棒了。我希望我的學校也有泳池！
Student B
Please come visit us again. You are always welcome at Tai-He!
請再來參觀我們。你隨時歡迎來泰和！
D.

Reading Time

閱讀時間 · 點 🔊 聽全文

Welcome to Tai-He Elementary School — a truly special and unique school in Changhua City, Taiwan! What makes us stand out? We have our very own swimming pool. In all of Changhua County, only three elementary schools have a swimming pool — and Tai-He is one of them! This is our most famous feature. Our students learn freestyle, backstroke, and breaststroke. They compete in races, break records, and become champions. When international visitors come, our students are proud to introduce our pool and share our swimming achievements. Swimming builds strength, confidence, and pride. We love our school pool, and we love sharing it with the world. You are always welcome at Tai-He!

歡迎來到泰和國小——台灣彰化市一所真正特別且獨一無二的學校！是什麼讓我們與眾不同？我們有自己的游泳池。在整個彰化縣，只有三所國小有游泳池——泰和就是其中之一！這是我們最有名的特色。我們的學生學習自由式、仰式和蛙式。他們參加比賽、打破紀錄、成為冠軍。當國際訪客來訪時，我們的學生很驕傲地介紹我們的泳池並分享游泳成就。游泳培養體力、自信和驕傲感。我們熱愛學校的游泳池，也熱愛與世界分享它。泰和永遠歡迎你！
