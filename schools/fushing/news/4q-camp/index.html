Grateful for You! 4Q Happy Parent-Child Camp

感恩有您！4Q快樂親子成長營圓滿成功

Yesterday (July 18), Fushing Elementary warmly welcomed the Chongde Cultural and Educational Foundation for a joy-filled "4Q Happy Parent-Child Growth Camp"! ✨

昨天（7/18）非常感謝崇德文教基金會來到福興國小，舉辦充滿歡笑與正能量的「4Q快樂親子成長營」！✨

Through a rich lineup of interactive stations and challenges, the camp guided children to learn from the heart — IQ (intelligence), EQ (emotions), HQ (health), and CQ (creativity).

透過豐富的互動與闖關，帶領孩子們從心學習IQ智慧、EQ情緒、HQ健康、CQ創意。

Watching parents and their children join hands to complete challenges together was truly touching.

看到家長與寶貝們大手牽小手、齊心協力完成挑戰的畫面，真的讓人無比感動！

Thank you to every volunteer teacher for their selfless dedication, leaving Fushing's children with unforgettable summer memories. Here's to carrying this love and positive energy forward, together, as our children grow!

感謝所有志工老師的無私奉獻，為福興國小的孩子們留下最難忘的暑期回憶。未來，我們一起帶著這份愛與正能量，陪伴孩子快樂成長！
