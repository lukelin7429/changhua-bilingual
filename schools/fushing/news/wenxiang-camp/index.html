Bridges to the World: Wenxiang Experience Camp

文向體驗營帶著鄉內5校學生展開精彩台北之旅

From July 7–8, the Wenxiang Education Foundation held the "Wenxiang Experience Camp," bringing together students from five elementary schools in our township for an unforgettable trip to Taipei!

7/7~7/8文向教育基金會辦理「文向體驗營」，帶著我們鄉內5所國小的小朋友們展開了精彩的台北之旅！

Most touching of all, the foundation covered every student's food and lodging in full, letting the children learn and explore with total peace of mind.

最讓人感動的是，基金會全額免費提供全程食宿，讓孩子們能無後顧之憂地快樂學習。

A jam-packed itinerary · 豐富行程超精彩

CPC Discovery Center 🛠️ — hands-on interactive technology, exploring the mysteries of energy.

中油探索館🛠️：體驗互動科技，探索能源的奧秘。

National Palace Museum — witnessing historic artifacts up close, feeling the shock of culture.

台北故宮：親眼見證歷史文物，感受文化震撼。

Taipei Zoo — stepping into nature, getting up close with adorable animals.

木柵動物園：走進大自然，與可愛動物近距離接觸。

Taipei Fine Arts Museum — immersed in art, sparking limitless imagination.

市立美術館：接受藝術洗禮，激發無限想像力。

Eight days full of unforgettable moments broadened the children's horizons — memories that will surely shine as some of the brightest of their childhood!

這八天的點點滴滴，開拓了孩子們的視野，絕對會成為大家童年最閃亮的回憶！

Special thanks: to the Wenxiang Education Foundation for their generosity and care, building a bridge to the world for the children of our township. Thank you, Wenxiang — having you means so much.

特別感謝：謝謝文向教育基金會的大愛與用心，為鄉內的孩子們搭起通往世界的橋樑。謝謝文向，有您們真好。
