II.

Sports & PE

體育活動 · 動起來的英文

操場上、球場邊，福興學生用英文說運動。

WORD 001
jumpv.
跳躍
The students practice the long jump.
學生們練習跳遠。
After each jump, the teacher measures the distance.
每次跳遠後，老師都會測量跳多遠的距離。
WORD 002
throwv.
丟
The students compete to see who can throw a softball the farthest.
學生比賽看誰能把壘球丟得最遠。
They enjoy the friendly competition while practicing their throwing skills.
他們一邊享受友善的比賽，一邊練習投擲技巧。
WORD 003
exercisen.
運動
Morning exercise is a good way to start the day.
健康操是開始新一天的好方法。
Students do different moves for about ten minutes for their exercise.
學生做不同的動作約十分鐘來鍛煉。
WORD 004
dodgeballn.
躲避球
The students are playing dodgeball.
學生們正在玩躲避球。
Dodgeball is a popular school game.
躲避球是一種流行的學校遊戲。
WORD 005
rollerbladev.
溜直排輪
These students are practicing to rollerblade.
這些學生正在練習溜直排輪。
Rollerblading can be for fun and also for exercise.
溜直排輪既可提供娛樂，又可作為運動。
WORD 006
badmintonn.
羽球
These students are learning to play badminton.
學生們正在學習打羽毛球。
Badminton can be a single or team sport.
羽毛球可以是單人運動，也可以是團隊運動。
WORD 007
jump ropev.
跳繩
The kids jump rope to get some exercise.
孩子們跳繩鍛鍊身體。
When the weather is nice, you can go outside to jump rope!
天氣好的時候，你可以到外面去跳繩。
WORD 008
kendon.
劍道
The students practice kendo outside.
學生們在戶外練習劍道。
Kendo is a martial art from Japan.
劍道是一項來自日本的武術。
III.

Arts & Music

藝術與音樂 · 才藝中的英文

直笛、水彩、陶笛——福興的藝文課也是英文課。

WORD 009
recordern.
直笛
The recorder is the first musical instrument that many students learn.
直笛是很多學生學的第一項樂器。
You can play many songs on a recorder.
你可以用直笛吹奏很多歌曲。
WORD 010
watercolorn.
水彩
In art class, the students are painting with watercolor.
學生們在美術課用水彩畫畫。
To use watercolor, you need a brush, a palette and water.
用水彩繪畫時，你需要畫筆、調色盤還有水。
WORD 011
dancev.
跳舞
Students are dancing together to music.
同學們隨著音樂節拍跳舞。
Pom-poms are fun to shake while you dance.
跳舞時揮舞彩球很好玩。
WORD 012
drumn.
鼓
The students practice playing their drums.
同學們正在打鼓。
Playing the drum is a popular activity in Taiwan.
在台灣，打鼓是非常普遍的活動。
WORD 013
ocarinan.
陶笛
These students are playing the ocarina.
這些學生正在吹陶笛。
The ocarina is a musical wind instrument.
陶笛是一種管樂器。
WORD 014
outdoor sketchingn.
戶外寫生
The students have an outdoor sketching class on the grass.
學生們在草地上進行一堂戶外寫生的課程。
They enjoy drawing the outdoors in their outdoor sketching class.
他們喜歡在戶外寫生課描繪戶外的景物。
IV.

Campus Life

校園生活 · 每天都用得上

從朝會到營養午餐，福興的日常就是最好的教材。

WORD 015
field tripn.
校外教學
Students go on a field trip to a farm to learn about plants and participate in farm activities.
學生們到農場進行校外教學，了解植物並參與農場活動。
During the field trip, students make tofu pudding and learn about where food comes from.
在校外教學期間，學生們製作豆腐布丁並了解食物的來源。
WORD 016
lawnn.
草坪
A worker is mowing the lawn at the school.
一名工人正在學校修剪草坪。
He drives a four-wheeled lawnmower across the lawn, back and forth.
他駕駛著四輪割草機在草坪上來回移動。
WORD 017
restroomn.
廁所
The students clean the school restroom.
學生打掃學校廁所。
They scrub down the restroom.
他們擦洗洗手間。
WORD 018
school lunchn.
營養午餐
School lunch is eaten in the classroom in Taiwan.
台灣的學校午餐是在教室裡吃的。
School lunch is designed to be healthy.
學校午餐主旨在健康。
WORD 019
morning assemblyn.
朝會
The students gather for the morning assembly.
學生們聚集參加朝會。
The flag is raised during the morning assembly.
在朝會時國旗會升上去。
WORD 020
tabletn.
平板
The students use tablets in class.
同學們上課時使用平板。
The foreign English teacher helps the students with their tablets.
外籍英語老師協助同學使用平板。
WORD 021
pizzan.
披薩
The students roll the dough to make pizza.
學生們滾麵糰準備做披薩。
They put different toppings on the pizza.
他們將配料加在披薩上面。
WORD 022
Taiwanesen.
台語
The students learn Taiwanese in class.
同學們在課堂上學習台語。
They learn Taiwanese by watching a video in class.
他們藉由觀看影片來學習台語。
WORD 023
rehearsaln.
排練
Students had a rehearsal for sports day on the track and field.
學生們在操場上為即將到來的運動會進行排練。
During the rehearsal, they learned to follow commands and move together.
在排練過程中，他們學會了聽從指揮，確保動作協調。
WORD 024
professional developmentn.
專業進修
In Taiwan, all elementary schools have professional development on Wednesday afternoons.
台灣所有小學老師在週三下午有專業進修。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development.
老師在專業進修時學習新技能與教學策略。
