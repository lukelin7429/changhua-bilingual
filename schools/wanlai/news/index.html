Early 2025 · 寒假🌏 International Education · 國際教育
Little Diplomats Winter Camp
小小外交官冬令營 · 探索印尼文化

At Wanlai, learning about the world is part of growing up. In our Little Diplomats Winter Camp, children spent five fun-filled days exploring the culture of Indonesia with our wonderful teacher, Miss Yayati — painting shadow puppets, making their own masks, and cooking Indonesian pineapple tarts called nastar. The adventure continued in Taipei, riding the high-speed train and visiting Taipei 101. 在萬來，認識世界是成長的一部分。在「小小外交官」冬令營，孩子們和優秀的 Yayati 老師一起，用五天快樂地探索印尼文化——彩繪皮影戲偶、製作面具、做印尼鳳梨酥「nastar」；旅程還延伸到台北，搭高鐵、登上台北 101。
