Word of the Day · Unit 12

Health & Safety

健康與防災
📺 9 videos · 9 個影片

Word 01
wash your hands
洗手
It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
確定在吃東西之前要用香皂和水來洗手。
Word 02
fire (n)
火、火災
The students gather to learn what to do if there is a fire.
學生們聚集在一起學習如果發生火災該怎麼辦。
They learn what to do if there is a fire on a bus.
他們學習如果公車上發生火災該怎麼辦。
Word 03
evacuation drill
逃生演練
The students gather for an evacuation drill.
學生們聚集在一起進行逃生演習。
The evacuation drill teaches students how to safely get off a tour bus in an emergency.
逃生演練教導學生如何在緊急情況下安全下車。
Word 04
escape training
逃生訓練
Firefighters visit the school to conduct a fire escape training session.
消防員來到學校進行火災逃生訓練。
Teachers and staff learn how to use the escape ladder safely during the fire escape training session.
教師和工作人員在火災逃生訓練中學習如何安全使用逃生梯。
Word 05
CPR
心肺復甦術
Students are learning how to perform CPR.
學生們正在學習如何做CPR。
They use CPR manikins to practice CPR.
他們使用人體模型來練習做CPR。
Word 06
dengue fever
登革熱
In the summer, dengue fever is a big health risk.
在夏季，登革熱是一個很大的健康風險。
Students watch a video to learn how to prevent getting dengue fever.
學生觀看影片以了解如何預防登革熱。
Word 07
general healthcare
一般衛生保健
School volunteers attend general healthcare training.
學校的志工參加一場有關保健的研習。
They learn about general healthcare at the training.
他們在研習中學習一般醫療保健的常識。
Word 08
drug (n)
毒品
An Anti-Drug Education Vehicle visits the school to promote anti-drug education.
反毒教育車到訪學校宣傳禁毒教育。
Students learn about the harmful effects of drugs by watching videos and playing games.
學生透過觀看影片和玩遊戲來了解毒品的有害影響。
Word 09
bully (v)
霸凌
It's important to learn from an early age the negative effects of bullying.
從小就了解霸凌的負面影響很重要。
The students are given a course on being kind, polite and respectful to their classmates.
學生將學習如何友善、禮貌和尊重同學。
