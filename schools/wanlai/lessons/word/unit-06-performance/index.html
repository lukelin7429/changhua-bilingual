Word of the Day · Unit 06

Performance & Drama

表演與戲劇

Word 01
dance routine
舞蹈動作
The girls work very hard, rehearsing their dance routines with their instructor.
這群女孩子很認真和老師排練他們的舞步。
Once they know the moves like the backs of their hands, the girls perform their dance routines in front of their classmates.
一旦他們將舞步練到很熟之後，他們在同學的面前表演。
Word 02
dance (v)
跳舞
Students are dancing together to music.
同學們隨著音樂節拍跳舞。
Pom-poms are fun to shake while you dance.
跳舞時揮舞彩球很好玩。
Word 03
talent show
才藝表演
A talent show is when students perform their special skills for others to enjoy.
才藝表演是學生表演特殊才藝給別人欣賞的機會。
During the talent show, students play different musical instruments, sing or dance.
在這場才藝表演中，同學們演奏不同的樂器。
Word 04
dance performances
舞蹈表演
The students are participating in dance performances for the whole school.
學生們正在參加全校的舞蹈表演。
Through dance performances, the students gain confidence and spark their interest in creative expression.
透過舞蹈表演，學生們獲得了自信並激發了他們對創意表達的興趣。
Word 05
performance (n)
表演
Students organized a flash mob performance to play the moon lute at the park in Beidou.
學生們在北斗公園舉辦了快閃表演，彈奏月亮琵琶。
Their sudden musical performance was enjoyed by all passersby in the park.
他們突然的音樂表演受到了公園裡所有路人的欣賞。
Word 06
parade (n)
遊行
The school organized a festive parade with students in colorful costumes marching through the streets.
學校組織了一場節日遊行，學生們穿著色彩繽紛的服裝在街上遊行。
Dressed in traditional attire, the students enthusiastically joined a cultural street parade led by their teachers.
學生們穿著傳統服裝，心情愉悅，自豪地參加了由老師帶領的文化街頭遊行。
Word 07
mask (n)
面具
The students make masks in class.
學生們在課堂上製作面具。
The masks are very colorful.
面具的顏色非常鮮豔。
Word 08
shadow puppet
皮影戲偶
The students learn about Indonesian shadow puppets.
學生了解印尼皮影戲。
They make Indonesian shadow puppets in class.
他們在課堂上製作印尼皮影戲偶。
