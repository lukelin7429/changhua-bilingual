Learning & Class

Word 01
prepare (v)
準備
The students prepare a breakfast beverage that they drink every day.
同學們準備每天早餐可以喝的飲料。
They prepare cooked rice, peanuts, sugar, and boiling water to make their beverage.
他們準備煮好的米飯、花生、糖和開水來製作飲料。
Word 02
advanced (adj)
進階的
Advanced players practice the moon lute.
進階班的演奏者在練習彈月琴。
The students have practiced for a long time to be in advanced music class.
同學們已經練習一段期間了，所以能到進階的音樂課中。
Word 03
phrase
短語
The teacher teaches the students Indonesian phrases.
老師教學生印尼語短語。
The students practice saying Indonesian phrases.
學生練習說印尼語短語。
Word 04
worksheet (n)
學習單
Students are working on their worksheets during a reading class.
在閱讀課上，學生們正忙著填寫學習單。
These worksheets help them organize and record what they‘ve read.
學習單幫助他們組織和記錄他們所讀的。
Word 05
board game
桌遊
The students play a board game with a teacher.
學生們和老師一起玩桌遊。
This board game allows students to learn how to budget money.
這個桌遊戲可以讓學生學習如何預算金錢。
Word 06
practice (v)
練習
The students practice for the morning ceremony.
學生們為早上的升旗儀式進行練習。
They take turns practicing being the leader of the morning ceremony.
他們輪流練習擔任升旗儀式的領隊。
Word 07
group discussion
小組討論
The students have a group discussion in class.
學生在課堂上進行小組討論。
They have a group discussion with their partners.
他們與合作夥伴進行小組討論。
Word 08
learn (v)
學習
Playing games and singing songs are great ways to learn English quickly.
玩遊戲和唱歌是快速學習英語的好方法。
Students learn English songs in a fun way by moving along to the rhythm.
學生們跟隨節奏，以有趣的方式學習英語歌曲。
Word 09
science class
自然課
The students are making Tanghulu, popcorn, and chocolate fondue in their science class.
學生在科學課上製作糖葫蘆、爆米花和巧克力火鍋。
Through these hands-on activities, they are exploring scientific principles and applying their knowledge in fun ways during science class.
透過這些實作活動，他們在科學課上探索科學原理並以有趣的方式運用他們的知識。
Word 10
cooking class
烹飪課
Students are learning to make Indonesian-style fried instant noodles during the cooking class.
學生們在烹飪課上學習製作印尼風味的炒方便麵。
Adding fresh vegetables to the noodles is a good way to make it healthier!
在麵中加入新鮮蔬菜是讓這道菜更健康的好方法！
Word 11
computer class
電腦課
The students learn how to use computers in computer class.
同學們在電腦課中學習如何使用電腦。
Each student uses a computer during computer class.
每個學生在電腦課的時候都有一台電腦可以使用。
Word 12
textbook
教科書
The students receive their new textbooks at the start of the semester.
學生們在學期領新教科書。
Each student collects their own textbooks for different subjects one by one.
每位學生領取自己不同科目的教科書。
Word 13
workshop
研討會
The English teachers attend a workshop to learn how to support students who need extra help.
英語老師參加研討會，學習如何為需要額外幫助的學生提供支援。
In the workshop, they share strategies and design lessons to help build students’ confidence.
在研討會上，他們分享策略和設計課程以幫助建立學生的信心。
Word 14
professional development
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，所有學校都有教師專業訓練課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
在專業訓練課程中，老師可以學到新的教學技巧與策略。
Word 15
creativity (n)
創造力
Art class is an ideal setting for developing your creativity.
美術課是發展創造力的理想環境。
These students are displaying their creativity by making beautiful and intricate works of art.
這些同學藉由創作美麗與精緻的藝術作品來展現他們的創造力。
Word 16
video
影片
The students are preparing for an English speaking contest by using video resources.
學生們正在利用影片資源，為一場英語朗讀比賽做準備。
They are practicing their pronunciation and fluency by listening and speaking along with the videos.
他們正在透過聽取影片並跟著說，來練習他們的發音和流暢度。
