Word 01
library (n)
圖書館
The students go and learn about the library.
同學們到圖書館參觀學習。
They learn about all the things they can do at the library.
他們學習在圖書館能夠做的事情。
Word 02
reading
閱讀
Look at the students. They’re all reading quietly.
看看這群學生，他們閱讀時多麼安靜啊！
Reading often can help you improve your vocabulary and learn many new things.
閱讀經常可以讓你增加字彙並且學到很多新知。
Word 03
reading log
閱讀紀錄單
The school showcases students' reading logs for browsing.
學校展示學生的閱讀紀錄單供瀏覽。
These reading logs feature thoughts from their story readings.
這些閱讀紀錄單包含了他們閱讀故事時的想法。
