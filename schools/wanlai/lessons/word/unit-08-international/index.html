International Flavors

國際文化美食

Word 01
Indonesian (adj)
印尼的
The teacher is teaching Indonesian culture.
老師正在教授印尼文化。
Students are learning about Indonesian traditions.
學生們正在學習認識印尼的文化。
Word 02
Indonesian turmeric rice
印尼薑黃飯
The students try Indonesian turmeric rice at school.
學生在學校品嚐印尼薑黃飯。
It is their first time trying Indonesian turmeric rice.
這是他們第一次嘗試印尼薑黃飯。
Word 03
Indonesian dance
印尼舞蹈
Students practice an Indonesian dance with horse props.
學生用馬道具練習印尼舞蹈。
They follow the teacher’s movements and learn the dance steps together.
他們跟著老師的動作，一起學習舞步。
Word 04
Halo halo
菲律賓冰品
The students are making the famous Filipino dessert, Halo-halo.
學生們正在製作著名的菲律賓甜點：Halo-halo。
They are mixing various ingredients, such as fruits and crushed ice, to make their own Halo-halo.
他們正在混合各種配料，像是水果和碎冰，來製作自己的Halo-halo甜點。
Word 05
pho (n)
河粉
The teachers and students eat pho in class.
老師們和同學們在課堂上吃著越南河粉。
Pho is a popular Vietnamese dish.
越南河粉是一道很普遍的越南佳餚。
Word 06
culture (n)
文化
The students learn about Indonesian culture in class.
學生們在課堂上了解印尼文化。
The teacher explains many things about Indonesian culture.
老師解釋了很多關於印尼文化的事情。
Word 07
mix (v)
混合
The students mix the ingredients before baking pineapple cakes.
學生們在烘烤鳳梨酥之前將配料混合。
After mixing the ingredients, they roll out balls for baking.
混合食材後，他們食材搓呈圓形以備烘烤。
Word 08
roll (v)
包、捲
The teachers learn how to roll rice in seaweed with other ingredients.
老師們學習如何用海苔和其他食材包米飯。
They roll the seaweed with many ingredients to make a healthy snack.
他們將海藻與多種配料一起捲製成健康的零食。
Word 09
coconut
椰子
The students are making coconut rice balls by hand in class.
學生們在課堂上手工製作椰粉小丸子。
They shape the dough, boil the balls, and roll them in coconut flakes to finish the snack.
他們將麵團捏成丸子，煮熟，然後裹上椰粉，就完成了這道小吃。
Word 10
feast (n)
盛宴
The school prepares many ingredients for the students to make a feast.
學校準備了很多食材為學生們做一頓大餐。
The students are making the feast to celebrate their upcoming graduation.
學生們正在舉辦盛宴來慶祝即將到來的畢業典禮。
Word 11
pineapple cake
鳳梨酥
The students make pineapple cakes in class.
學生在課堂上製作鳳梨酥。
They bake their pineapple cakes in the oven.
他們在烤箱裡烤鳳梨酥。
Word 12
rice milk
米漿
The students make rice milk.
學生製作米漿。
They drink the rice milk after they make it.
米漿做好後他們就喝。
