Word 01
recess (n)
下課時間
After the bell rings for recess, most students go outside to play.
下課鈴聲響起後，大多數學生都在外面玩。
Recess is when kids can play outside, run around, and have fun.
下課時間是孩子們可以在外面玩耍、跑來跑去、玩得開心的時候。
Word 02
sweep (v)
掃地
The students sweep the floor outside.
學生們在外面掃地。
They sweep the dust into the dustpan.
他們把塵土掃到畚斗。
Word 03
morning assembly
晨會
The students gather for the morning assembly.
學生們聚集參加朝會。
The flag is raised during the morning assembly.
在朝會時國旗會升上去。
Word 04
study hall
自修時間
The students are diligently studying in study hall.
學生們在自修時間認真學習。
In study hall the students have to work independently and quietly.
在自修時間，學生必須獨立、安靜地學習。
Word 05
midterm exams
期中考
Midterm exams are exams given around the midpoint of a grading term, such as a quarter or semester.
期中考是一個期中的考試，有可能學季制或是學期制。
Students spend a lot of time and effort preparing for their midterm exams.
同學們花很多時間與精力準備期中考。
Word 06
weekly English
每週英語
The school has a weekly English program.
學校有每週英語的方案。
For the weekly English program, the students have to learn a new sentence in English.
這個方案是要學生每個禮拜學講一句英語。
Word 07
station
站 (闖關遊戲）
Students visit various stations around the school for a series of fun games
學生們參觀學校周圍的各個車站，進行一系列有趣的遊戲
Each station presents a task for the students to complete.
每個站都會提供學生一個需要完成的任務。
Word 08
station (n)
站 (英語闖關）
Students go to different stations around the school.
學校校園裡設立不同的站讓同學造訪。
Each station has an activity to help students practice speaking English.
每個站都有一個活動幫助學生練習說英語。
Word 09
booth (n)
攤位
On sports day, a booth is set up to promote environmental awareness.
運動會當天學校設了一個攤位來提倡環保意識。
At the booth, the students separate recyclable materials into different categories.
在這個攤位，學生們將回收品放別放入不同的種類中。
Word 10
meeting
會議
Teachers and parents attend the Class Parent-Teacher Meeting for the new semester.
老師和家長們參加了新學期的班級親師座談會。
During the meeting, the parents are learning about the curriculum of the school.
在會議期間，家長們了解學校的課程。
Word 11
introduce (v)
介紹
The teacher introduces her home country, Indonesia, to the students.
老師向學生介紹她的祖國印尼。
The students listen to the teacher introduce her home country.
老師介紹她的祖國後，學生們做一份作業表。
Word 12
presentation
介紹
The teacher makes a presentation on Indonesian turmeric rice.
老師介紹印尼薑黃米。
The students listen to the teacher's presentation.
學生們聆聽老師的簡介說明。
