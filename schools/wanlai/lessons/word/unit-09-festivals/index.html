Word 01
Christmas wreath
聖誕花環
The students make a Christmas wreath out of paper.
同學們用紙製作聖誕花圈。
They fold the paper to make Christmas wreaths.
他們摺紙來製作聖誕花圈。
Word 02
Mother's Day
母親節
The students are making Mother’s Day cards.
學生們正在製作母親節卡片。
They are drawing pictures and writing messages to show their love for Mom on Mother’s Day.
他們在母親節畫畫、寫訊息表達對媽媽的愛。
Word 03
Mother's Day cards
母親節卡片
These students are making Mother's Day cards for their mothers.
這些學生正在為他們的母親製作母親節卡片。
They draw pictures and put a message on their Mother's Day cards.
他們畫畫並在母親節卡片上留言。
Word 04
anniversary (n)
校慶
The elementary school is celebrating their school anniversary.
這間小學正在慶祝他們的校慶。
They celebrate their school anniversary by having fun games and races.
他們舉辦趣味競賽與賽跑來慶祝校慶。
