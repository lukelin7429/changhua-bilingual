Course 01
The Moon Lute
月琴雙語課

Wanlai's signature course in six lessons — meet the instrument, learn to play and sing, guide visitors in English, and share music with the community. Every lesson has a handout and a quiz.萬來的特色課程，共六堂課——認識月琴、學彈唱、用英語當小導覽員、走入社區表演。每一課都有講義與小考。

Course 02
Word of the Day
每日一字

106 short bilingual videos filmed around Wanlai, sorted into 13 themed units — every word comes with two example sentences.106 支在萬來校園拍攝的雙語短片，分成 13 個主題單元，每個字都附上兩句中英例句。

Start Learning →
Course 03
Video Lessons
主題式影片課程

20 short English lessons filmed around Wanlai — painting our classroom, a moon-watching night, Game Day, halo-halo, batik bags, the horse dance, and more. Each has six words, three sentences, and a matching game.20 堂在萬來校園實拍的英語短片課——彩繪教室、觀月之夜、闖關活動、菲律賓剉冰、印尼蠟染、騎馬舞等等，每課六個單字、三個句子和一個配對小遊戲。

Start Learning →
Course 04
Teacher Angel Mae's Resource Site
Angel Mae 老師資源站

English in action at Wanlai — morning assembly English, classroom activities, a practice corner, the English Club, weekly sentences, storytelling, and camps, built by Teacher Angel Mae, Wanlai's foreign English teacher.萬來的英語現場——晨會英語、課堂活動、練習角、英語社團、每週句型、說故事與營隊紀錄，由外師 Angel Mae 老師親自建置。

Visit the Site ↗
Course 05
Festivals English
節慶英語

16 festivals from Taiwan and around the world — each with its story, key words, a short dialogue, and its own quiz.16 個臺灣與世界各地的節慶——每個節慶都有由來故事、關鍵單字、情境對話與專屬小測驗。

Start Learning →
Course 06
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers — 30 episodes across 10 topics, from greetings to exam directions.老師每天會用到的課堂英語——10 大主題、30 支影片，從問候點名到考試指令。

Start Learning →
Course 07
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

Thirteen short episodes in which each school office shares its announcements in English.13 支短片，各處室用英語介紹平時的校園廣播內容。
