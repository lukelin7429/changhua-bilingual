The Moon Lute

月琴雙語課 · 把萬來的特色變成孩子能說的英文

The moon lute is Wanlai's pride — so we turned it into a six-lesson bilingual course. From meeting the instrument to performing for the community, every lesson has a handout (vocabulary with 🔊 audio, sentence patterns, dialog) and its own quiz with instant marking. 月琴是萬來的驕傲，我們把它做成一套六堂的雙語課。從認識樂器到走入社區表演，每一課都有「講義」（單字含 🔊 發音、句型、對話）與專屬「小考」即時批改。

Word of the Day

每日一字 · 校園裡的英文小故事

Over 100 short bilingual videos filmed right here at Wanlai — each one teaches a single English word through a real moment on campus. The words are sorted into 13 themed units; pick a theme, watch the video, and read two example sentences for every word. 超過 100 支在萬來校園實地拍攝的雙語短片，每支用一個校園真實片刻教一個英文單字。單字分成 13 個主題單元——選一個主題、看影片，每個字都附兩句中英對照例句。

Classroom English
