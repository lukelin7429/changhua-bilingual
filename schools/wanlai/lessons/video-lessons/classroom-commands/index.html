Classroom Commands

教室指令
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

At an English teaching workshop in our Global Village classroom, teachers practiced the commands they use every day — each one shown on screen in English and Chinese.在國際村英語教室的研習中，老師們一起練習每天都會用到的課室英語指令，每一句都用中英對照的投影片示範。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

take your seat/teɪk jʊr sit/請坐

Class is starting. Please take your seat.要上課了，請坐。

pay attention/peɪ əˈtɛnʃən/專心

Pay attention when the teacher is talking.老師說話時要專心。

push in your chair/pʊʃ ɪn jʊr tʃɛr/把椅子推回去

Push in your chair before you leave.離開前把椅子推回去。

clean up/klin ʌp/清理

Clean up your desk after art class.美術課後把桌子清理乾淨。

hands off/hændz ɔf/不要碰

Hands off — that is not yours.不要碰，那不是你的。

seatmate/ˈsitˌmeɪt/同桌

My seatmate sits right next to me.我的同桌就坐在我旁邊。
