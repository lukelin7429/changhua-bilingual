Batik Bags

印尼蠟染包包
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

At the Little Diplomats summer camp, students tried batik, a famous art from Indonesia. They dipped a small tool into hot wax and drew patterns on their own cloth bags.在小小外交官暑期營，同學們體驗印尼有名的蠟染藝術：用小工具沾熱熱的蠟，在自己的布包上畫出圖案。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

batik/ˈbætɪk/蠟染

Batik is a way to make patterns on cloth with wax.蠟染是用蠟在布上做出圖案的方法。

wax/wæks/蠟

The wax melts in a small hot pan.蠟在小小的熱鍋裡融化。

dip/dɪp/沾

Dip the tool in the wax, then draw.把工具沾一沾蠟，再畫畫。

stove/stoʊv/爐子

The stove keeps the wax warm.爐子讓蠟保持溫熱。

pattern/ˈpætərn/圖案

She draws a flower pattern with wax.她用蠟畫出花朵圖案。

bag/bæɡ/袋子

I will carry my new bag to school.我會背著新袋子去上學。
