Video Lessons

主題式影片課程 · 萬來校園實拍

Every lesson here was filmed at Wanlai — in our classrooms, on the school yard, and at the school gate. Each video is under a minute, with six words, three sentences, and a matching game.每一堂課都在萬來校園實地拍攝——教室、操場、校門口。影片不到一分鐘，搭配六個單字、三個句子和一個配對小遊戲。

🎨▶
Lesson 01
Painting Our Classroom
彩繪英語教室

Graduates came back with brushes and paint — and koalas and an owl appeared on our walls.畢業生帶著畫筆和顏料回到學校，教室牆上多了無尾熊和貓頭鷹。

The Tadpole Story
大愛媽媽說故事

A volunteer mom tells first graders how a little tadpole becomes a frog.大愛媽媽來到一年級，說一個小蝌蚪變成青蛙的故事。

Phonics Practice
自然發音練習

Letters, sounds, and the blends bl and br — practiced at the big screen.字母、發音，還有 bl、br 子音組合，在大螢幕前一起練習。

Lesson 04
Word Challenge
六年級單字挑戰

Sixth graders test their English words — on laptops, on whiteboards, and on the big screen.六年級挑戰英語單字——用筆電、用小白板，也在大螢幕上答題。

A Night with the Moon

Full moon, half moon, a lunar eclipse — and real telescopes on the school yard at night.滿月、半月、月食——晚上還在操場用真正的望遠鏡看月亮。

Honor Stars' Pizza Lunch
榮譽之星披薩午餐

Students who help others every day get a certificate — and a pizza lunch with the principal.每天樂於助人的同學領到獎狀，還和校長一起吃披薩午餐。

A Little Excavator

Summer Camp

College students lead a summer camp — group work, big posters, and a show for parents.大學生哥哥姊姊帶營隊——分組合作、畫大海報，最後上台發表給家長看。

Cleaning the Air Conditioners

Before the new school year, volunteers climb ladders to clean every air conditioner.新學期開始前，志工們爬上梯子，把每一台冷氣清洗乾淨。

Classroom Commands

Shaved ice, jelly, pudding, and banana — make the Filipino dessert halo-halo.剉冰、果凍、布丁、香蕉——動手做菲律賓甜點 Halo-halo。
