Morning Run

晨跑運動
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽
🎬
Video coming soon影片上傳中，敬請期待

Before class begins, Wanlai students head outside for a morning run. They warm up and stretch together, then run laps around the track and the field.上課前，萬來的學生到戶外晨跑。大家一起暖身、伸展，再沿著跑道和操場跑一圈又一圈。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

morning/ˈmɔrnɪŋ/早上

We run every morning before class.我們每天早上上課前跑步。

warm up/wɔrm ʌp/暖身

Warm up your arms and legs first.先活動一下手和腳暖身。

stretch/strɛtʃ/伸展

Stretch slowly so you don't get hurt.慢慢伸展，才不會受傷。

track/træk/跑道

The track goes around the field.跑道圍繞著操場。

field/fild/操場；草地

The soccer goals are on the field.足球門在草地上。

run/rʌn/跑步

Run with your friends.和朋友一起跑步。
