Game Day: Challenge Stations

闖關活動・挑戰篇
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

In the corridors and the activity center, Game Day had challenges for busy hands: tangram puzzles, a bow and target, and a cup-stacking race against the clock.在走廊和活動中心裡，闖關日準備了考驗手眼協調的關卡：七巧板拼圖、拉弓射靶，還有和時間賽跑的疊杯挑戰。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

tangram/ˈtæŋɡrəm/七巧板

A tangram has seven flat pieces.七巧板有七塊平平的板子。

shape/ʃeɪp/形狀

Use the pieces to make a shape.用這些板子拼出一個形狀。

bow/boʊ/弓

Pull the string of the bow back.把弓弦往後拉。

target/ˈtɑrɡɪt/箭靶

The middle of the target is worth ten points.箭靶正中間是十分。

cup/kʌp/杯子

Each yellow cup is light and easy to stack.每個黃色杯子都很輕，很好疊。

stack/stæk/疊

Stack the cups into a tall tower.把杯子疊成一座高塔。
