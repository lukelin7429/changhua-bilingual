On Game Day, the whole school moved from station to station. Out on the yard, students hopped along the hopscotch path, jumped through hoops, and lined up for the next challenge.歡樂童年闖關日，全校學生一關一關挑戰。在操場上，大家沿著跳房子單腳跳、跳過一個個呼拉圈，再排隊挑戰下一關。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

hopscotch/ˈhɑpˌskɑtʃ/跳房子

Hopscotch is a game of hopping on numbered boxes.跳房子是在數字格子上跳的遊戲。

number/ˈnʌmbər/數字

Each box has a big number on it.每一格都有一個大大的數字。

cone/koʊn/三角錐

Each orange cone shows us the way.橘色的三角錐幫我們標出路線。

hoop/hup/呼拉圈

Put each hoop in a row on the ground.把呼拉圈在地上排成一排。

jump/dʒʌmp/跳

Jump into the next hoop.跳進下一個呼拉圈。

line up/laɪn ʌp/排隊

Please line up for the next station.請排隊挑戰下一關。
