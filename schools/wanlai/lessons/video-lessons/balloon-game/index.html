The Balloon Game

夏日樂學氣球遊戲

In our summer English class, the teacher held up balloons and asked, "What color?" Teams took turns answering before the ten-second timer ran out.夏日樂學英語課，老師舉起氣球問「What color?」，各隊輪流在十秒計時結束前回答。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

balloon/bəˈlun/氣球

Each balloon on the floor is a different color.地上的每顆氣球顏色都不一樣。

timer/ˈtaɪmər/計時器

The timer counts down from ten.計時器從十開始倒數。

team/tim/隊伍

Which team are you on — A, B, C, or D?你是哪一隊？A、B、C 還是 D？

hold/hoʊld/拿著

Hold the balloon with both hands.用兩隻手拿著氣球。

pick up/pɪk ʌp/撿起來

Pick up a balloon from the floor.從地上撿起一顆氣球。

wait/weɪt/等待

Wait for your turn.等輪到你。
