The Healthy Kids Award

畢業健康獎
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽
🎬
Video coming soon影片上傳中，敬請期待

Before graduation, sixth graders who kept healthy habits received the Healthy Kids Award. They lined up on the outdoor stage, in front of a colorful mural, for a group photo.畢業前，保持良好健康習慣的六年級學生獲頒「畢業健康獎」。大家站上戶外舞台，在繽紛的彩繪牆前合照。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

award/əˈwɔrd/獎

The award sign has our school crest on it.獎項看板上有我們的校徽。

healthy/ˈhɛlθi/健康的

Eating vegetables keeps you healthy.吃蔬菜讓你保持健康。

graduate/ˈɡrædʒuət/畢業生

Each graduate will go to junior high next year.每位畢業生明年都要上國中了。

mural/ˈmjʊrəl/壁畫

The mural has a big black bear on it.壁畫上有一隻大黑熊。

stage/steɪdʒ/舞台

The students stand on the stage.學生們站在舞台上。

photo/ˈfoʊtoʊ/照片

Let's take a photo together.我們一起拍張照片吧。
