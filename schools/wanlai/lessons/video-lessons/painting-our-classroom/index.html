Painting Our Classroom

彩繪英語教室
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Our graduates came back to school to paint the English classroom. Step by step, a gray koala, a little owl in a graduation cap, and bright colors filled the white walls.畢業的學長姐回到學校彩繪英語教室。一筆一筆，灰色的無尾熊、戴學士帽的小貓頭鷹和繽紛的色彩，布滿了原本白白的牆。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

paint/peɪnt/畫；油漆

We paint a big cat on the wall.我們在牆上畫一隻大貓。

brush/brʌʃ/畫筆

Use a small brush for the eyes.畫眼睛要用小畫筆。

wall/wɔl/牆壁

The classroom wall was all white before.教室的牆以前全是白色的。

koala/koʊˈɑlə/無尾熊

A koala has round ears and a big nose.無尾熊有圓圓的耳朵和大鼻子。

owl/aʊl/貓頭鷹

The owl is wearing a graduation cap.這隻貓頭鷹戴著學士帽。

wash/wɑʃ/洗

Wash the brushes when you finish.畫完要把畫筆洗乾淨。
