A Night with the Moon

觀月追星奇遇記
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Families came to school for a moon-watching night. First, a talk showed how the moon changes its shape and what a lunar eclipse looks like. Then everyone went outside to look through the telescopes.萬來的親子一起來學校參加賞月活動。先聽講座，認識月亮形狀的變化和月食的樣子；接著到戶外，輪流透過望遠鏡看月亮。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

full moon/fʊl mun/滿月

A full moon is round and bright.滿月又圓又亮。

half moon/hæf mun/半月

Tonight we can see a half moon.今晚可以看到半月。

star/stɑr/星星

A bright star is shining near the moon.一顆亮亮的星星在月亮旁邊閃耀。

lunar eclipse/ˈlunər ɪˈklɪps/月食

During a lunar eclipse, the moon turns red.月食的時候，月亮會變成紅色。

telescope/ˈtɛləˌskoʊp/望遠鏡

A telescope makes the moon look big.望遠鏡讓月亮看起來很大。

look/lʊk/看

Look through the telescope with one eye.用一隻眼睛看望遠鏡。
