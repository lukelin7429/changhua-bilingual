Rujak: Indonesian Fruit Salad

印尼水果沙拉
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Rujak is a popular fruit salad from Indonesia, perfect for hot days. Our teachers cut fresh fruit, ground peanuts and chili in a stone mortar, and mixed the fruit with the sauce.Rujak 是印尼很受歡迎的消暑水果沙拉。老師們切好新鮮水果，用石臼把花生和辣椒搗碎做成醬料，再和水果拌在一起。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

fruit/frut/水果

Rujak has many kinds of fruit.Rujak 裡有好多種水果。

mango/ˈmæŋɡoʊ/芒果

Slice the green mango into thin pieces.把青芒果切成薄片。

cucumber/ˈkjuˌkʌmbər/小黃瓜

A cucumber is cool and crunchy.小黃瓜清涼又脆。

chili/ˈtʃɪli/辣椒

Red chili makes the sauce spicy.紅辣椒讓醬料變辣。

grind/ɡraɪnd/磨碎；搗碎

Grind the peanuts in a stone bowl.在石碗裡把花生搗碎。

sauce/sɔs/醬料

Pour the sauce over the fruit.把醬料淋在水果上。
