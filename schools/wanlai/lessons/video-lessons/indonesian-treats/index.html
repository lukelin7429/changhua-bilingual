Indonesian Treats

印尼點心
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Our Indonesian teachers shared their favorite treats. Students mixed batter, poured it into cups, and baked egg cakes and banana cakes. There was also ketupat — rice cooked in woven leaves — and pandan lemon juice.印尼老師們和我們分享家鄉的點心。同學們攪拌麵糊、倒進紙杯，烤出雞蛋糕和香蕉蛋糕；還有用葉子編成小籃子煮的米粽 Ketupat，以及香蘭檸檬汁。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

mixer/ˈmɪksər/攪拌器

The mixer makes the batter smooth.攪拌器把麵糊打得很滑順。

batter/ˈbætər/麵糊

Batter is made of eggs, flour, and sugar.麵糊是用蛋、麵粉和糖做的。

pour/pɔr/倒

Pour the batter into each cup.把麵糊倒進每個杯子裡。

oven/ˈʌvən/烤箱

Put the cakes in the hot oven.把蛋糕放進熱烤箱。

rice cake/raɪs keɪk/米粽

This rice cake is wrapped in woven leaves.這個米粽用編織的葉子包著。

juice/dʒus/果汁

Pandan lemon juice is green and sweet.香蘭檸檬汁是綠色的，甜甜的。
