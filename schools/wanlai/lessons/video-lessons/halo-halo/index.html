Halo-Halo

菲律賓剉冰 Halo-halo
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Students learned to make halo-halo, a cold dessert from the Philippines. They cooked jelly, made pudding, sliced bananas, and shaved ice — then piled it all into a cup and mixed it up.同學們學做菲律賓的冰涼甜點 Halo-halo：煮果凍、做布丁、切香蕉、剉冰，最後全部裝進杯子裡，攪一攪就能吃了。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

jelly/ˈdʒɛli/果凍

This green jelly is soft and sweet.這種綠色果凍軟軟甜甜的。

pudding/ˈpʊdɪŋ/布丁

The pudding is made with eggs and milk.布丁是用蛋和牛奶做的。

banana/bəˈnænə/香蕉

Cut the banana into small pieces.把香蕉切成小塊。

shaved ice/ʃeɪvd aɪs/剉冰

The machine turns ice into shaved ice.機器把冰塊變成剉冰。

mix/mɪks/攪拌；混合

Mix everything together with a spoon.用湯匙把全部攪拌在一起。

taste/teɪst/品嚐

Taste it — is it sweet?嚐嚐看，甜不甜？

Say It · 用句子說說看

Tap 🔊 and Say It with Me點 🔊 跟著唸

1
Halo-halo is a cold Filipino dessert.
Halo-halo 是菲律賓的冰涼甜點。
2

把果凍和布丁放在剉冰上。
3

Halo-halo 的意思是「混一混」。
