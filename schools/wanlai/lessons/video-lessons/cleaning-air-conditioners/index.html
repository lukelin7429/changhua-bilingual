Cleaning the Air Conditioners

清洗冷氣
← Video Lessons 回到影片課總覽

Every summer, our general affairs office and alumni volunteers clean the classroom air conditioners. They climb ladders, take out the filters, and rinse them clean.每年暑假，總務處和校友志工都會清洗教室的冷氣。他們爬上梯子、取下濾網，再把濾網沖洗乾淨。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

air conditioner/ɛr kənˈdɪʃənər/冷氣機

The air conditioner keeps the classroom cool.冷氣讓教室保持涼爽。

ladder/ˈlædər/梯子

Stand on the ladder carefully.小心地站在梯子上。

climb/klaɪm/爬

He has to climb up high.他得爬到高處。

filter/ˈfɪltər/濾網

A dirty filter makes the air dusty.髒的濾網會讓空氣變得灰塵很多。

rinse/rɪns/沖洗

Rinse the filter with clean water.用清水沖洗濾網。

volunteer/ˌvɑlənˈtɪr/志工

A volunteer helps without being paid.志工是不拿報酬來幫忙的人。
