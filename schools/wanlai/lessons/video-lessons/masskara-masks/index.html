MassKara Festival Masks

菲律賓面具節

MassKara is a festival in Bacolod City in the Philippines, famous for its smiling, colorful masks. After looking at photos of the festival, our students painted masks of their own.MassKara 是菲律賓巴科洛德市的節慶，以色彩繽紛的笑臉面具聞名。看完節慶的照片後，同學們動手彩繪自己的面具。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

festival/ˈfɛstəvəl/節慶

A festival is a big party for a whole city.節慶是一整個城市一起慶祝的大派對。

mask/mæsk/面具

A mask covers your face.面具可以把臉遮起來。

eye/aɪ/眼睛

Paint a black line around each eye.在每隻眼睛周圍畫一圈黑線。

palette/ˈpælɪt/調色盤

Mix your paints on a palette.在調色盤上調顏料。

colorful/ˈkʌlərfəl/色彩繽紛的

MassKara masks are bright and colorful.MassKara 的面具鮮豔又繽紛。

decorate/ˈdɛkəˌreɪt/裝飾

Decorate your mask with dots and lines.用點點和線條裝飾你的面具。
