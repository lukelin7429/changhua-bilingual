The Erhu Club

二胡社團

In the erhu club, students learn to play a traditional Chinese instrument with two strings. Their teacher plays alongside them as they read their sheet music and practice.二胡社團裡，同學們學習演奏只有兩條弦的傳統樂器。老師在旁邊一起拉，大家看著樂譜認真練習。

Words to Know · 單字

Six Words from the Video影片裡的六個單字

erhu/ˈɜrhu/二胡

The erhu is a Chinese instrument.二胡是中國的傳統樂器。

string/strɪŋ/弦

Each string makes a different sound.每條弦發出的聲音都不一樣。

case/keɪs/琴盒

Keep your erhu in its case.把二胡收進琴盒裡。

sheet music/ʃit ˈmjuzɪk/樂譜

Read the sheet music as you play.一邊拉一邊看樂譜。

teacher/ˈtitʃər/老師

Our teacher plays the erhu with us.老師和我們一起拉二胡。

practice/ˈpræktɪs/練習

We practice the same song many times.同一首曲子我們練習很多次。
