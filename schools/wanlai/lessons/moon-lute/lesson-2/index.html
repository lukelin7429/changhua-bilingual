Moon-Lute Course · Lesson 02 / 6

How We Play

我們怎麼彈
PART 1

Read

先讀一段

We hold the moon lute and pluck the two strings with our fingers. The strings make a soft melody. With practice, we can play it well!我們把月琴抱好，用手指撥兩條絃，絃會發出柔和的旋律。多練習，我們就能彈得很好！
