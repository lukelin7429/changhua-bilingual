The Moon Lute
月琴雙語課 · 用英文介紹我們的家鄉樂器
PART 1

Meet the Moon Lute

認識月琴

The moon lute (月琴) is a special Taiwanese instrument. It has a big, round body — round like the full moon — and only two strings. At Wanlai, our moon-lute club learns to play it and sing old Taiwanese folk songs. In this lesson, you will learn to talk about the moon lute in English! 月琴是一種特別的台灣樂器。它有一個又大又圓的琴身——圓得像滿月——而且只有兩條絃。在萬來，我們的月琴社團學習彈奏它，並吟唱古老的台灣歌謠。這堂課，你將學會用英文介紹月琴！

PART 5

Why It Matters

月琴的故事

Long ago, traveling singers in Taiwan used the moon lute to tell stories and sing folk songs. At Wanlai, keeping the moon lute alive means keeping our mother tongue and our tradition alive too. When our club performs for grandparents at care centers, music becomes a bridge between young and old. 很久以前，台灣的走唱藝人用月琴說故事、唱歌謠。在萬來，讓月琴繼續響起，也就是讓我們的母語與傳統繼續活著。當社團為關懷據點的爺爺奶奶演出，音樂就成了老少之間的橋樑。

Your Turn

換你說說看

Be a Moon-Lute Guide! 🎤

假裝有一位客人來參觀，用英文介紹月琴：
  1. Show the moon lute and say: "This is a moon lute."拿出月琴說：「這是月琴。」
  2. Tell one fact: "It has two strings."說一個特點：「它有兩條絃。」
  3. Say what you do: "We sing Taiwanese folk songs."說你們做什麼：「我們唱台灣歌謠。」
  4. Invite them: "Come and listen to our music!"邀請他們：「來聽我們的音樂吧！」
