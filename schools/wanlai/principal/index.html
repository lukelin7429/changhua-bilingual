I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Chen Jing-ting became Wanlai's seventh principal in August 2022. She holds a Ph.D. in Language Education from National Taichung University of Education, and before coming to Wanlai she served as principal of Shuiwei Elementary School and Chengkung Elementary School in Changhua County.

陳靜婷校長於民國 111 年 8 月接任萬來國小第七任校長。校長擁有國立臺中教育大學語文教育學系博士學位，曾任彰化縣水尾國小、成功國小校長。

Her approach is student-centered: to lift up every single child. She hopes to be a keeper of the flame in education — one who lights the lamp of learning, carries it alongside her students, and passes it on.

校長的教育理念以學生為本，「帶好每一位學生」，並期許自己做好「點燈、提燈、傳燈」的教育傳承者。

"To light a lamp, to carry a lamp, to pass the lamp on."
「做好點燈、提燈、傳燈的教育傳承者。」— 陳靜婷 校長
II.

What Guides Us

辦學理念

Three beliefs that guide our school.

萬來的三個信念

Lift Up Every Child
帶好每一位學生

Every child matters here. We get to know each learner and help every one of them move forward at their own pace.

Light · Carry · Pass On
點燈・提燈・傳燈

We light a love of learning, carry it alongside our children, and pass it on — teaching as a quiet act of handing the lamp forward.

Rooted, Yet Open
扎根家鄉・放眼世界

From the moon lute to everyday English, our children grow proud of where they come from while reaching out to the wider world.
