Welcome to Wanlai

歡迎來到萬來
🎓
Principal Chen Jing-ting
陳靜婷 校長 · 萬來國小第 7 任校長
A school where every child is known, valued, and encouraged to shine.
一所看見每個孩子、珍視每個孩子、鼓勵每個孩子發光的學校。

On behalf of our whole school family, welcome to Wanlai Elementary School. We are a small school in Beidou Township, and that is our strength — here, every teacher knows every child by name, and every child has the space to find their own voice.

謹代表全校大家庭，歡迎您來到萬來國小。我們是北斗鎮的一所小學校，而「小」正是我們的優勢——在這裡，每位老師都叫得出每個孩子的名字，每個孩子都有找到自我聲音的空間。

We believe a good education grows from care. Our children read widely, speak English a little more confidently each week, play the moon lute and share it with their community, and welcome friends from many cultures. These are the everyday ways we help them grow into kind, curious, and confident people.

我們相信，好的教育源於關懷。萬來的孩子廣泛閱讀、每週更有自信地開口說英語、學習月琴並把樂音分享給社區、歡迎來自不同文化的朋友——這些日常，正是我們陪伴孩子長成善良、好奇而自信的人的方式。

"At Wanlai, we grow good people first — and everything good grows from there."
「在萬來，我們先培養好的人——一切美好，都從這裡生長。」
— Principal Chen Jing-ting · 陳靜婷 校長

What Guides Us

萬來的三個信念
🌱
Care First
關懷為先
In a small school, no child is a stranger. We know each learner, and we meet them where they are.
🌏
Open to the World
放眼世界
Through English, music, and a multicultural campus, our children learn that the world is wide — and theirs to explore.
🪕
Rooted at Home
扎根家鄉
From the moon lute to Beidou's own stories, we help children love where they come from as they reach outward.
