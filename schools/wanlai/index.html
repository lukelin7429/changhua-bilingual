I.

Welcome

歡迎來到萬來

Learn with a smile, move with health, shine with confidence.

微笑學習・健康舞動・自信展能

Welcome to Wanlai Elementary School in Beidou Township, Changhua County! Wanlai is a friendly public primary school where every child counts — a place to explore our hometown, to move and play, and to discover the talents that help each of us shine.

歡迎來到彰化縣北斗鎮萬來國小！這是一所親切的公立小學，我們相信每個孩子都重要——在這裡探索家鄉、盡情運動，也發掘讓自己發光的才能。

Wanlai opened in 1974 as a branch campus of Beidou Elementary School and became an independent school in 1982. Its name honors Mr. Yang Wanlai, an overseas Chinese benefactor living in Japan who bought the land to build the school — and who later gave a concert piano, half the cost of the music classrooms, and the school gate.

萬來於民國 63 年（1974）設立為北斗國小萬來分校，民國 71 年（1982）獨立設校。校名是為了感念旅日華僑楊萬來先生購地建校；之後他又陸續捐贈演奏型鋼琴、音樂教室（逸志堂）半數經費，以及學校校門。

Today, Wanlai's school-designed curriculum grows out of Beidou's own landscape, culture, and community — from hometown walks and rice-food lessons to coding, logic games, the moon lute, and English woven into everyday school life.

今天的萬來，依據北斗在地的地理環境、人文特色與產業，規劃出自己的校訂課程——從家鄉走讀、米食文化，到程式設計、邏輯遊戲、月琴，以及融入校園日常的英語。

III.

Vision in the Classroom

願景走進課程 · 五大校訂特色

Five signature themes, rooted in Beidou.

依據北斗在地特色，規劃五大校訂特色主題

Wanlai links its three-part vision directly to its school-designed flexible curriculum. Drawing on Beidou's geography, culture, and local economy, the school has planned five signature themes that bring the vision into children's everyday learning.

萬來國小在課程設計上，將「微笑學習、健康舞動、自信展能」三大願景與校訂彈性學習課程緊密結合。學校依據北斗鎮當地的地理環境、人文特色與經濟條件，規劃出五大校訂特色主題，讓願景落實於孩子的日常學習中。

I · Learn with a Smile · 微笑學習

Hometown Walks & Food Education

Learning goes outdoors. Younger grades walk the streets of old Beidou to meet its history and people, while middle and upper grades study the local rice-food culture and get hands-on with food and farming — joyful learning in real settings.

家鄉走讀食農教育（走讀寶斗、米的願望）：帶領學生走出教室，低年級走讀北斗街區與人文，中高年級則深入研究在地「米食文化」與進行食農體驗，在真實情境中快樂學習。
I · Learn with a Smile · 微笑學習

Logic & Reasoning

Fun logic games and brainstorming replace dull rote memorization. As children solve puzzles and think things through, they discover the smile — and the sense of achievement — that comes with real understanding.

邏輯推理課程：在彈性課程中融入趣味邏輯遊戲與腦力激盪，擺脫傳統枯燥的死記硬背，讓孩子在解謎與思考過程中體驗獲得知識的微笑與成就感。
II · Move with Health · 健康舞動

Active Clubs & After-School Activities

Clubs such as hit-song MV dance, badminton, and karate get students moving and working up a sweat on a regular basis — in the morning before class and after school.

多元社團與課後活動：學校開設如「哈燒MV流行熱舞」、「羽球」、「空手道」等動態社團，鼓勵學生在晨間或課後時間規律運動、揮灑汗水。
II · Move with Health · 健康舞動

Healthy Habits in Practice

Balanced eating — saying no to sugary drinks and drinking more water — and regular exercise are built into daily routines, with role-play and real-life scenarios that teach children to look after their own health.

健康促進宣導與實踐：將均衡飲食（如推廣拒糖飲、多喝水）與規律運動習慣融入日常常規，並在課程中加入情景模擬或角色扮演，引導孩子在活潑的氛圍中學會守護自己的健康。
III · Shine with Confidence · 自信展能

Global Education & Technology

Global learning opens children's eyes to the wider world, while technology classes build digital literacy — skills that connect students to the future and grow their self-confidence.

國際教育與資訊科技：透過國際視野的開拓與科技工具（如資訊科技課程）的學習，培養學生與未來世界接軌的技能，從而提升數位素養與自尊自信。
III · Shine with Confidence · 自信展能

Talent Shows & Stages for All

School events, festivals, and the graduation season give every child a stage — like the sixth graders' talent showcase and class dance performances — so students strong in academics, the arts, or sports can all share their work with pride.

才藝展演與舞台搭建：在學校活動、節慶或畢業系列活動中，安排六年級才藝發表等精彩演出（如班級熱舞表演）。學校提供多元萬來的舞台，讓不論是擅長學術、藝術或體育的孩子，都能在眾人面前大方展現成果，建立帶得走的自信心。

IV.

The Moon-Lute Club

月琴社團 · 萬來的驕傲
Songs that travel
月琴・走出校園的傳統樂音

The moon lute (月琴) is a round, two-stringed Taiwanese instrument — the heart of our folk-music club. Children learn to play and sing old Taiwanese songs, building a love for their mother tongue along the way. Our young players don't just perform at school — they bring their music to community care centers, temple fairs, and town festivals across Beidou, sharing warmth with their neighbors.

月琴是一種圓身雙絃的台灣傳統樂器，也是萬來音樂社團的核心。孩子們學習彈奏與吟唱閩南語歌謠，在樂聲中愛上自己的母語。小小演奏家不只在校內表演——他們把樂音帶到社區關懷據點、廟會與北斗的地方節慶，將溫暖分享給鄉里。

VII.

Our Crest

校徽的故事
  • W · A Bird in Flight · 飛鳥
    The letter W, from WAN-LAI, takes the shape of a bird soaring freely — a symbol of Wanlai's open, democratic spirit and wide horizons.
    「W」字母結合飛鳥意象，以自由翱翔的飛鳥，象徵萬來自由民主開放的學風，具有如飛鳥般開闊的視野與格局。
  • L · A Flower of Love · 愛之花
    Three cursive L's — L for Love — form a flower: parents, teachers, and students growing a bright life together on a foundation of love. In Chinese, "three" also means "many," echoing the 萬 (ten thousand) in Wanlai's name.
    以三個草寫「L」組成花形，「L」延伸為「愛」（LOVE），象徵親、師、生在以愛為基礎的教育理念下，共創璀璨的生命花朵；古以「三」表「多」，也呼應校名「萬」字。
  • Colors · 色彩
    White for a pure, innocent place of learning; red for teachers' passion for education and love of life; green for a sustainable, eco-friendly school.
    白底象徵教育園地的純淨無邪；紅色代表教師對教育的熱誠、對生命的熱愛；綠色代表環保、永續的經營理念。
