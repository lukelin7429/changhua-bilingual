Champions of Care

居仁社區兒少保護宣導活動・朗讀繪畫比賽奪佳績

Luoqing had a lot to celebrate this month. Students across every grade level took part in the 2026 Beidou Township "Zero Tolerance for Violence, Happiness in Juren" event, organized by the Juren Community Development Association — a reading and painting competition that turned an important topic into art and story.

Painting Kindness

In the painting categories, entrants from both the lower grades (1st–3rd) and upper grades (4th–6th) placed across every rank — from 3rd place all the way up to Honorable Mention — filling the stage with colorful posters about protecting one another and living without fear.

Reading Courage Aloud

On the recitation stage, Luoqing's readers were just as strong, taking 2nd and 3rd place in the upper-grade round and 3rd place and Honorable Mention in the lower-grade round — every performance a small act of courage in front of a crowd.

Proud of every voice, proud of every brushstroke.

Congratulations to every Luoqing student who stepped on that stage — Luoqing is proud of you!

中文原文 · Chinese

狂賀！居仁社區朗讀及繪畫比賽奪佳績 · 2026.07.09

本校學生參加北斗鎮居仁社區發展協會辦理「115 年度北斗鎮家庭暴力防治朗讀及繪畫比賽」，榮獲佳績！

繪畫組高年級（四、五、六年級）：榮獲第二名、優選佳績。
繪畫組低年級（一、二、三年級）：榮獲第一名、第三名、優選佳績。

朗讀組高年級（四、五、六年級）：榮獲第二名、第三名、優選佳績。
朗讀組低年級（一、二、三年級）：榮獲第三名、優選佳績。
