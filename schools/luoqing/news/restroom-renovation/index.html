A Fresh Bloom in the East Wing

後棟大樓東側廁所整修完工・性別友善與無障礙設施全面升級

After nearly four months of work, the wait is over — and the transformation makes every bit of the inconvenience worth it. The east-side restrooms in Luoqing's back building have reopened, brighter, more thoughtful, and more welcoming than ever.

Privacy, First and Foremost

The new all-gender restroom gives every user real privacy — its own lighting, automatic flushing, and a shelf to set down a bag or books, so nobody has to juggle their things at the sink.

Accessible, and Full of Care

The accessible stall was designed around real needs: a hook that's actually within reach, water-saving flush options, a privacy screen, and an emergency call button for anyone who needs help.

And this is only the beginning.

This is only the first step — the west-side restrooms are next in line for their own makeover.

中文原文 · Chinese

東側廁所整修完工 · 2026.06.22

蛻變後的璀璨亮眼，讓等待期間的不便煙消雲散！後棟大樓東側廁所整修工程在歷經近四個月的打造後，迎來煥然一新的設施。

性別友善廁所讓使用者享有絕對隱私權，獨立照明、自動沖水設施，與置物平台增添使用便利性。

無障礙廁所周延的便利設施，無礙更有愛！便利的掛鉤、環保的沖水選項、防窺板與求救鈴的設置，滿足了多面向的需求。

而這，只是起手式！還有西側廁所等待我們下一步的妝點！
