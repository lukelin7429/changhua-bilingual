Watch & Learn

觀看影片，一起學英文

The first thirteen videos each teach a single word. The last seven are longer topic lessons — basketball, running, calligraphy, jump rope, the playground, morning assembly, and the school library — each with its own set of vocabulary and sentences. 前 13 支影片一支教一個字。最後 7 支是主題課，涵蓋籃球、跑步、書法、跳繩、遊戲區、朝會、圖書室，每支都有自己的一組單字和例句。

WORD 008
professional developmentn.
專業進修

台灣所有小學老師在週三下午有專業進修。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development.
老師在專業進修時學習新技能與教學策略。

最後一排學生請起立，幫忙收你那一排的考卷。
