Music Together

一起吹直笛 · 一起唱旋律

Welcome to our school's Recorder Club, where we come together to learn and enjoy the beautiful melodies of the recorder!

歡迎加入我們學校的直笛社，在這裡，我們聚在一起學習和享受直笛的美妙旋律！

The recorder is a woodwind instrument that belongs to the flute family. It's like a long tube with holes and a mouthpiece. You blow into it and cover and uncover the holes with your fingers to make different sounds. It is easy to learn and fun to play!

直笛是一種屬於長笛家族的木管樂器。它就像一個長管，上面有洞和一個吹嘴。你吹進去，用手指遮住和打開這些洞，就能發出不同的聲音。學起來很容易，而且很好玩！

In our club, we learn how to read musical notes and play songs step by step with the help of our teacher. Whether you're new to the recorder or already know a bit, you're welcome to join us for musical adventures!

在我們的社團裡，我們學習如何閱讀音符，並在老師的幫助下一步步學會演奏歌曲。無論你是剛接觸直笛還是已經有一點基礎，都歡迎加入我們的音樂冒險！

More about Recorder Club

更多練習

Welcome to our school's Recorder Club. Here, we learn and enjoy the beautiful melodies of the recorder. The recorder is a woodwind instrument that belongs to the flute family. It's easy to learn and fun to play. Our club teaches how to read musical notes and play songs step by step. Our teacher helps us along the way, whether you're new or already know a bit. Being part of a group and learning music has many benefits; studies show that learning music can improve memory and cognitive skills. Join us for musical adventures and make new friends.

Welcome to our Recorder Club, where music and fun come together! The recorder is a woodwind instrument that's easy to learn and fun to play. It's like a long tube with holes and a mouthpiece that you blow into. Covering and uncovering the holes with your fingers makes different sounds. In our club, we learn how to read musical notes and play songs step by step. Playing music has been shown to enhance brain development and improve memory. Whether you're new to the recorder or already know a bit, you're welcome to join us!
