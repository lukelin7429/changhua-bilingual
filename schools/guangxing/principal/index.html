From the Principal's Desk校長室 · 校長的話

Principal · 校長

Hsu Shih-yuan徐詩媛 校長

"I Can Do! Be the best version of yourself."「I Can Do！成為最棒的自己。」

Welcome to Guangxing Elementary. We sit in the sugarcane land of southern Erlin, Changhua — and we are quietly devoted to one belief: that every single child can be reached, together building a warm, caring campus we call the "Healthy Living Home."

歡迎來到廣興國小。我們座落在彰化二林這片充滿蔗香的土地上，致力於成就每一位孩子，共築一個溫暖友善的「健康生活家」校園。

Education, to us, is more than passing on knowledge. It is lighting the spark of curiosity in a child's heart — and guiding them to care for their own corner of the world while building the ability to meet it.

教育不只是知識的傳遞，更是點燃孩子心中主動探索的火花，引導他們在關懷在地的情懷中，具備接軌世界的能力。

School Vision

The ICAN DO Action-Taker學校願景 · 培育「行動生活家」

Our core goal is to grow children into action-takers carrying the ICAN DO spirit — so that when the future tests them, they meet it with the wisdom and resilience to solve real problems.

教育的核心，是培育具備 ICAN DO 精神的行動者，讓孩子面對未來挑戰時，擁有解決問題的智慧與韌性。

We hold fast to the founding wish behind everything we do — to bring out the best in every child — and to keep building a campus with warmth, a true "Healthy Living Home."

我們持續秉持「成就每一位孩子」的教育初衷，致力於建構一個充滿溫度的「健康生活家」友善校園。

Tempered by grit, teamwork, planning, technology, diligence and adaptability, may every Guangxing child live out the spirit of "I Can Do! Be the best version of yourself" — and set out from Guangxing as a confident dreamer who takes action and loves a sustainable world.

期待每一位廣興學子，在恆毅、合作、規劃、科技、精進與適應力的淬鍊下，實踐「I Can Do！成為最棒的自己」的精神，從廣興出發，成為「展自信、跨行動、愛永續」的行動夢想家。

And so we keep our door open to families and to our community — because a school that grows close to the people around it gives every child a place to learn everywhere, and explore at any moment.

同時持續推動「學校社區化」，透過與家長及社區的緊密夥伴關係，凝聚對教育的向心力，共同為孩子打造一個處處可學習、時時可探索的樂活成長園地。

With heart, Hsu Shih-yuan徐詩媛 Principal · Guangxing Elementary School 廣興國小校長
