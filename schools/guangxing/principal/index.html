Welcome to Guangxing歡迎來到廣興

Principal · 校長

Welcome to Guangxing Elementary. We are a small country school in southern Erlin Township, Changhua — wrapped in farmland, far from the city, and quietly happy that way.

歡迎來到廣興國小。我們是一所位於彰化縣二林鎮南端的鄉間小校——田園環繞、遠離城市，並且很安靜地以此為樂。

"We don't believe that children here have fewer opportunities. The school takes on more responsibility — so every child knows they are seen." 「我們不認為廣興的孩子機會比較少。學校多承擔一點責任，每個孩子才會知道自己被看見。」

Because we are small, we can be careful. Class sizes are tiny — sometimes only a handful of children per grade — but that means every learner is known by every teacher. Lessons run from 7 in the morning until 7 at night for children who need full-day care; our free after-school clubs teach African drumming, dance, taekwondo, calligraphy, and Go.

因為我們小，所以可以細。一個年級可能只有幾個孩子，但這代表每一位老師都認識每一個學習者。需要的孩子可以從早上 7 點待到晚上 7 點；我們的免費才藝課程包含非洲鼓、舞蹈、跆拳道、書法與圍棋。

We don't teach English only inside the classroom. Word of the Day videos play in our broadcasts, Photo Stories tell campus events in two languages, and our bilingual website is built with the My Culture Connect 人師教育協會 team so any family can join from home.

英文教育不只在教室裡發生。每日一字影片穿插在校園廣播中，Photo Stories 用雙語紀錄校園活動，這個雙語網站由人師教育協會團隊協力建置——讓任何家庭都能從家裡一起學。

Come visit when you can. The school door is open.

有空就來廣興走走。我們的校門，永遠是開的。

With aloha, 廣興國小校長 Principal · Guangxing Elementary School
