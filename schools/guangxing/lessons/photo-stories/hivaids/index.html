HIV & AIDS Assembly愛滋衛教講座

A special presentation was held in the school's assembly hall to educate students on disease transmission. They gained awareness and knowledge about the deadly immune disease, HIV/AIDS.

▶ Listen · 聆聽朗讀

學校在大禮堂舉行了一場特別的講座，向學生宣導疾病傳播的相關知識。他們提高了對致命免疫疾病 HIV／愛滋病的認識與了解。

WOTD · HIV/AIDS · 朗讀者 Edwin Schadewald
Learning Corner
Learn the Words & Talk About It學單字 · 練句子 · 開口說 · 小測驗
1 Keywords 關鍵字
presentation (n.)
講座；簡報
assembly hall (n.)
大禮堂
educate (v.)
教育；宣導
disease (n.)
疾病
transmission (n.)
傳播
awareness (n.)
認識；意識
2 Example Sentences 例句
A special presentation was held in the assembly hall.
大禮堂裡舉行了一場特別的講座。
It educated students about disease transmission.
它向學生宣導疾病的傳播。
The students gained awareness about staying healthy.
學生們提高了保持健康的認識。
3 Talk About It 對話練習
A
Where is everyone going?
大家要去哪裡？
B
To the assembly hall for a special presentation.
去大禮堂聽一場特別的講座。
A
What is it about?
是關於什麼的？
B
It teaches us about disease transmission and how to stay healthy.
它教我們疾病如何傳播，以及如何保持健康。
4 Quick Quiz 小測驗 · 五題
Question 1
Where was the presentation held?
講座在哪裡舉行？
✓ In the school's assembly hall. 在學校大禮堂。
Question 2
What was the presentation about?
講座的主題是？
✓ About disease transmission. 關於疾病傳播。
Question 3
What does "to educate" mean?
educate 是什麼意思？
✓ To educate is to teach and inform. 教育就是教導與宣導。
Question 4
What did the students gain?
學生們獲得了什麼？
✓ They gained awareness and knowledge. 提高了認識與知識。
Question 5
What does "awareness" mean?
awareness 是什麼意思？
✓ Awareness means knowing and understanding. 認識＝知道並理解。
