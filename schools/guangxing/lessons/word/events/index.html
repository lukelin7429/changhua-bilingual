Unit 04 · Word of the Day

Campus Events校園大事件

Graduation, anniversary, holiday celebration, even drills — the big days on the Guangxing calendar.畢業、校慶、節慶、防災演習——廣興年曆上的大日子。

Word 01
graduation (n)
畢業典禮
The sixth graders celebrate their graduation in June.
六年級學生在六月慶祝畢業典禮。
Parents come to school to watch the graduation ceremony.
家長來學校觀看畢業典禮。
Word 02
anniversary (n)
校慶
We celebrate our school anniversary with fun activities and performances.
我們舉辦有趣的活動和表演慶祝校慶。
The school anniversary parade is the most exciting event of the year.
校慶遊行是一年中最令人興奮的活動。
Word 03
earthquake drill
地震演習
We practice an earthquake drill once every semester.
我們每學期練習一次地震演習。
During an earthquake drill, students hide under their desks for protection.
地震演習時，學生躲在桌子底下保護自己。
Word 04
award (n)
獎
She received an award for being the best student in her class.
她因成為班上最優秀的學生而獲獎。
The principal gives out awards to students at the school ceremony.
校長在學校典禮上頒獎給學生。
Word 05
Christmas celebration
慶祝聖誕
Our school has a big Christmas celebration every December.
我們學校每年十二月舉辦盛大的聖誕慶典。
Students sing carols and exchange gifts at the Christmas celebration.
學生在聖誕慶典上唱聖誕歌曲和交換禮物。
