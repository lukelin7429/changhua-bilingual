Unit 05 · Word of the Day

Toys, Crafts & Hobbies玩物與興趣

Spinning tops, building blocks, Go boards, calligraphy brushes — what Guangxing kids do for fun.陀螺、積木、圍棋、書法——廣興孩子的快樂時光。

Word 01
cupcake (n)
杯子蛋糕
Students bake a cupcake for the school event.
學生為學校活動烤杯子蛋糕。
She decorated her cupcake with colorful frosting and sprinkles.
她用彩色糖霜和糖粒裝飾她的杯子蛋糕。
Word 02
top (n)
陀螺
Children learn to spin a top with a piece of string.
孩子們學習用繩子轉動陀螺。
The top spins so fast it looks like it is standing still.
陀螺轉得很快，看起來好像靜止不動。
Word 03
building blocks
積木
The children build a tall tower with building blocks.
孩子們用積木蓋了一座高塔。
Playing with building blocks helps kids develop creativity and problem-solving skills.
玩積木有助於培養孩子的創造力和解決問題的能力。
Word 04
contraption (n)
裝置
The students build a funny-looking contraption from recycled materials.
學生用回收材料做了一個有趣的裝置。
Their contraption uses a rolling ball to knock down a row of blocks.
他們的裝置用一顆滾動的球打倒一排積木。
Word 05
calligraphy (n)
書法
We practice calligraphy with a brush and black ink.
我們用毛筆和黑墨水練習書法。
Beautiful calligraphy takes years of practice to master.
美麗的書法需要多年練習才能精通。
Word 06
Go (n)
圍棋
Students play Go during the afternoon club activity.
學生在下午社團活動中下圍棋。
Learning to play Go sharpens your thinking and strategy skills.
學習下圍棋可以鍛鍊你的思考和策略能力。
