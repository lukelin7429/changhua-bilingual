Unit 02 · Word of the Day

Classroom & Subjects課堂與學科

Inside the classroom — subjects, lessons, tests and the English to talk about them.教室裡的事——學科、課程、考試，與用英文聊它們的方法。

Word 01
online class (n)
線上課程
We have an online class when the teacher is away.
老師不在時我們上線上課程。
The students join their online class on a tablet at home.
學生在家用平板電腦上線上課程。
Word 02
encourage (v)
鼓勵
Teachers always encourage students to do their best.
老師總是鼓勵學生盡力表現。
Our principal encourages everyone to keep learning every day.
校長鼓勵大家每天持續學習。
Word 03
social studies
社會科
In social studies, we learn about communities, history, and maps.
在社會科，我們學習社區、歷史和地圖。
Social studies class helps us understand the world around us.
社會科幫助我們了解周圍的世界。
Word 04
test (n)
考試
We have a spelling test every Friday afternoon.
我們每週五下午有拼字考試。
The students study hard to prepare for the English test.
學生努力讀書準備英文考試。
Word 05
Chinese characters
國字
We practice writing Chinese characters with a brush and ink.
我們用毛筆和墨水練習寫國字。
Learning Chinese characters takes a lot of practice and patience.
學習國字需要大量練習和耐心。
Word 06
color in
著色
The students color in a picture of the school campus.
學生為學校校園的圖片著色。
She loves to color in her drawings with bright crayons.
她喜歡用亮色蠟筆為圖畫著色。
Word 07
computer lab
電腦教室
We go to the computer lab to learn how to type and code.
我們到電腦教室學習打字和程式設計。
The school has a big computer lab with many computers for students.
學校有一間寬敞的電腦教室，有很多電腦供學生使用。
