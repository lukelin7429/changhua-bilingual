Zero Waste Academy零廢棄學院活動

On April 10, 2026, our school hosted a very meaningful event: the "Zero Waste Academy"! We invited expert lecturer Mr. Huang Zhi-ping to teach our students how to live a life that respects our planet by reducing waste.

The Magic of Disappearing Trash. Mr. Huang used fun examples to help students think about a big question: How much trash do we make every day, and can we make it "disappear"? The students listened carefully and shared many great ideas. They learned that by practicing the 3Rs: Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle, everyone can become a hero for the Earth.

A Promise to Our Environment. This class taught us that protecting the Earth is about our daily choices. It's about being responsible for our environment. Let's start today—by using less plastic and recycling more—to enjoy the beauty of a zero-waste lifestyle!

115 年 4 月 10 日，校園裡迎來了一堂守護地球的特別課程——「零廢棄學院活動」！我們榮幸邀請到擁有豐富經驗的黃志平老師，帶領同學們認識如何透過「資源不浪費」，讓地球變得更美麗。

垃圾消失的魔法？課堂上，黃老師用生動的互動問答，引導大家思考：我們每天製造了多少垃圾？有沒有可能讓垃圾「消失」？同學們聽得目不轉睛，熱烈討論著各種環保小撇步。原來，零廢棄並非遙不可及，只要在生活中落實「減量（Reduce）、重複使用（Reuse）、做好回收（Recycle）」，每個人都能變身成守護地球的小英雄。

對環境負責的承諾。這堂課讓孩子們明白，保護環境不只是口號，而是一種「對生命負責」的態度。從減少使用塑膠袋、重複利用文具開始，讓我們一起在校園裡實踐綠色生活，感受零廢棄帶來的純淨與美好！
