Build It, Fly It!無人機一日營 — 從理論到實踐：組裝、懸停、挑戰降落墊

Screwdrivers, motor wires, and one big question: how does a drone hover?　　螺絲起子、馬達線路，還有一個大問題：無人機為什麼能停在空中？

Science is not only words in a textbook — sometimes you can build it with your own hands and watch it fly! At our Drone Day Camp, "From Theory to Practice," students explored the science, put a drone together, and learned to fly it.

The day began with a big question on the screen: Why can a drone hover in the air? The teacher explained how air helps a drone stay up, introduced common kinds of drones, and showed real equipment up close — an FPV racing drone frame, FPV goggles, and a fire-safe bag for storing batteries.

Next, students worked in teams of two to four. With small screwdrivers in hand, they connected the motor wires, attached black and white propellers, and added propeller guards and landing legs. Talking and helping one another, they assembled a pile of parts into a drone of their own.

Then it was time to fly! Holding their controllers, students practiced four beginner moves: unlock, up and down, forward and back, left and right. Indoors, they took off from wooden stools and tried to land right on the round landing pads. Outdoors on the basketball court, they flew their drones high into the open sky.

Back in the classroom, everyone finished a worksheet and a short quiz to review what they had learned. Watching a drone they built themselves rise into the air — that is a feeling every student will remember!

科技不只是課本上的文字，更是能親手打造與飛翔的夢想！本校辦理「無人機一日營：從理論到實踐」，讓孩子們經歷了一場結合科學原理、動手組裝與飛行操控的充實科技之旅！

營隊一開始，老師帶領大家探討「無人機為什麼可以懸停在空中？」的空氣動力學原理，並解析常見的無人機分類與基礎操控觀念。現場還展示了專業的 FPV 穿越機機架、FPV 飛行眼鏡以及 ARRIS LIPO SAFE 防爆電池袋等設備，讓同學們近距離認識無人機技術。

接著孩子們以 2 至 4 人為一組，手持小型螺絲起子專注鎖固機身結構，從連接馬達線路、安裝黑白螺旋槳，到裝上螺旋槳保護罩與防撞腳架，大家互相討論、合作配合，將分散的零件一步步組裝成專屬的無人機！

完成組裝後，隨即進入緊張刺激的飛行實作！同學們拿起遙控器，熟記「解鎖、上升／下降、前進／後退、左移／右移」四大新手關卡。在室內，孩子們列隊操控微型無人機從木凳起飛，挑戰精準降落在標有「AERC 亞洲教育創新機器人競賽」圖樣的圓形降落墊上；移師戶外籃球場後，更享受將無人機升上高空、靈活遠距離操控的飛行樂趣！

經過精采的實飛演練，孩子們回到教室認真填寫學習單與評量卷，將學到的物理原理與操作經驗融會貫通。看著自己親手組裝的無人機成功升空，大家都滿滿的成就感！

drone/droʊn/無人機

A drone can fly with no pilot inside.無人機裡面不用坐飛行員就能飛。

assemble/əˈsembəl/組裝

Let's assemble the drone step by step.我們一步一步把無人機組裝起來吧。

propeller/prəˈpelər/螺旋槳

Each propeller spins very fast.每一片螺旋槳都轉得很快。

screwdriver/ˈskruːdraɪvər/螺絲起子

Turn the screwdriver to tighten the screw.轉動螺絲起子，把螺絲鎖緊。

motor/ˈmoʊtər/馬達

The motor makes the propeller turn.馬達讓螺旋槳轉動。

hover/ˈhʌvər/懸停

A drone can hover in the air like a hummingbird.無人機可以像蜂鳥一樣懸停在空中。

Hold the controller with both hands.用雙手拿好遙控器。

Try to land on the round pad.試著降落在圓形降落墊上。
