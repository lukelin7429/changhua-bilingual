Smart with Your Allowance金融素養宣導 — 從零用錢開始，學習管理自己的小小財富

Questions, answers, and a scam taken apart　　一問一答，把詐騙的手法拆開來看

Every coin in a child's pocket is a chance to learn. In this financial literacy talk, students looked at saving, spending and simple planning — and at how to stay alert when money and the internet meet.

The speaker walked everyone through a three-way scam, step by step. Someone says "I want to buy a phone." Someone else says "Send me the money and I'll ship it to you." In the end, nobody gets the phone. The children followed the arrows on the screen and worked out exactly where the trap was hiding.

We hope our students build a clear idea of money from an early age: knowing the difference between what they need and what they want, living within their means, and valuing what they have worked for.

Education is not only about doing well in school. It is also about building the skills for the life ahead.

Starting from a small allowance, children learn to save, to think, to choose and to take responsibility — one step at a time.

生活中的每一筆零用錢，都是孩子學習理財的好機會。本次金融素養宣導帶領孩子認識儲蓄、消費與理財的重要性，學習如何珍惜金錢、做好簡單的收支規劃，也了解面對金錢與網路交易時，應該保持警覺、保護自己的重要資訊。

講師以「三方詐騙」的流程圖逐步說明：一方說「我要買手機」，另一方說「你匯錢給我，我寄手機給你」，最後誰也沒有拿到手機。孩子跟著螢幕上的箭頭一步一步推，自己找出陷阱藏在哪裡。

我們希望孩子從小建立正確的金錢觀念，懂得「需要」與「想要」的不同，學習量入為出、珍惜每一分努力得來的成果。

教育不只是教孩子把書讀好，也希望陪伴孩子培養面對未來生活的能力。

從小小的零用錢開始，學習儲蓄、思考、選擇與負責，一步一步累積屬於自己的生活智慧。讓我們一起陪孩子學會理財，也學會更安心、更有智慧地面對未來。

Words to Learn · 單字學習

Tap 🔊 to listen
點喇叭聽發音

allowance/əˈlaʊəns/零用錢

Save part of your allowance every week.每個星期都存下一部分零用錢。

spend/spend/花費、花錢

Think twice before you spend your money.花錢之前先想兩次。

scam/skæm/詐騙

A stranger asking you to send money first is a scam.陌生人叫你先匯錢，那就是詐騙。

wise/waɪz/明智的

A wise shopper compares the price first.明智的購物者會先比價。

plan/plæn/計畫

Make a simple plan for the money you get.為你拿到的錢做一個簡單的計畫。

password/ˈpæswɜːrd/密碼

Never share your password with anyone.絕對不要把密碼告訴別人。

online/ˌɑːnˈlaɪn/網路上的

Be careful when you buy something online.在網路上買東西要特別小心。

goal/ɡoʊl/目標

Saving for a goal makes it easier to wait.為了一個目標存錢，等待就變得容易了。
