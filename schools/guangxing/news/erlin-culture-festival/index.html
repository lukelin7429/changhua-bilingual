A Night of Dance and Thanks2026 二林文化季 — 藝術活動日暨優良教師表揚

Ribbons, popsicle cakes, and a well-deserved award　　飄揚的彩帶、可愛的冰棒蛋糕，還有實至名歸的榮耀（頒獎照中間為昭安老師）

On the evening of Saturday, October 3, the square in front of Erlin Township Library filled with lights and music. It was Art Day at the 19th Erlin Culture Festival — a night of performances that also honors the town's outstanding teachers.

Congratulations to one of our own teachers, who was named an Outstanding Teacher of Erlin Township this year. Well deserved!

Schools and community groups took turns on stage, with powerful taiko drums and saxophone music. Then came the Guangxing Dance Team. Coached by our dance teacher, older and younger dancers moved as one, blue ribbons flying above their orange costumes.

Many people made the night shine. Parents cheered and helped from start to finish. Our baking teacher ran a booth with super-cute popsicle pound cakes. A student ambassador read a poem of thanks to teachers, and our director and school nurse helped with hair and makeup.

Thank you to the Erlin Township Office and the Erlin Farmers' Association. We are so grateful for a beautiful night together.

第十九屆二林文化季「藝術活動日暨優良教師表揚」，已於 10 月 3 日（星期六）晚上在二林鎮立圖書館廣場圓滿舉辦。現場結合地方各級學校與社區社團精銳演出，有震撼的太鼓表演、薩克斯風演奏，還有廣興舞蹈隊的精彩呈現。

恭喜昭安老師榮獲二林鎮優良教師，實至名歸！

由依雯老師指導的廣興舞蹈隊，學姊學妹齊心，舞者們穿著亮橘色的表演服、揮舞藍色彩帶，呈現最完美的表演！

感謝孩子們的最佳應援團——暖心的家長們全程陪伴協助；感謝烘焙老師曉茹老師協助設攤，製作超級可愛的冰棒磅蛋糕；感謝親善小天使帶來好聽的感謝師恩詩歌吟誦；也感謝主任和護理師協助梳化妝。

感謝二林鎮公所、二林鎮農會，讓大家共度美好的夜晚，心中滿滿感恩。
