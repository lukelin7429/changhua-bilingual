Seasonal · Qingming Festival

Herbal Rice Cake for Qingming Festival草仔粿製作體驗 — 清明節

To celebrate the traditional Qingming Festival, our school held a special Herbal Rice Cake (Tsao-A-Ke) making activity! Under the guidance of our teachers, students learned to knead the dough and carefully fill the sweet, green rice cakes.

This hands-on experience not only taught the children about Taiwanese food traditions but also gave them the joy of making delicious food for the holiday!

配合慎終追遠的清明節到來，學校舉辦了「草仔粿製作體驗」活動。在老師們的指導下，同學們親手揉捏麵團，學習製作這道充滿傳統節慶風味的米食。

從艾草粉的揉入到內餡的包製，孩子們不僅體驗了傳統習俗，更從動手實作中感受到了節慶的溫馨與親手製作的樂趣。

Words to Learn · 單字學習

點喇叭聽發音

festival/ˈfestɪvl/節日

Qingming is a traditional festival.清明是一個傳統節日。

dough/doʊ/麵團

We learned to knead the green dough.我們學會揉綠色的麵團。

knead/niːd/揉、搓

First we knead it until it is soft.先把它揉到軟軟的。

filling/ˈfɪlɪŋ/內餡

Then we carefully add the filling.然後小心地包進內餡。

herb/ɜːrb/香草、青草

The green color comes from an herb.綠色來自一種青草。

steam/stiːm/蒸

At the end we steam the rice cakes.最後把粿拿去蒸。

tradition/trəˈdɪʃn/傳統

This is a Taiwanese food tradition.這是台灣的飲食傳統。

taste/teɪst/品嚐

We tasted what we made ourselves.我們品嚐自己親手做的成果。
