Awards, Reminders and a Morning Run學生朝會 — 頒獎、宣導暨 SH150 活動

Cheers on the court, footsteps on the track　　球場上的掌聲，跑道上的腳步聲

The week began with a lively morning assembly. Classes that did especially well in tidiness and order were called up front, and the whole school clapped for them.

Each award board stands for more than a name. It shows that the children are willing to work at it together — looking after their own classroom and keeping the campus neat, orderly and pleasant for everyone.

The assembly also made room for two reminders that matter: a friendly campus, where classmates respect and care for one another, and campus safety, where children learn to protect themselves and look out for others.

And then came everyone's favorite part — the SH150 run. Children jogged lap after lap in the morning sunshine, building the habit of daily exercise and filling the day ahead with energy.

We believe learning does not only happen inside a classroom. Every award, every reminder and every lap around the track is a footprint of growing up.

Thank you to our teachers for walking beside the children, and to every family for the support and trust you give the school.

新的一週，從一場充滿鼓勵與活力的學生朝會開始。朝會時光，一起為在整潔與秩序方面表現優良的班級喝采。

一面面獎牌，不只是榮譽，更代表著孩子們願意一起努力、彼此合作，讓校園變得更加整潔、有序與美好。

除了肯定孩子們的好表現，也利用朝會時間進行「友善校園」與「校園安全」宣導，提醒孩子尊重彼此、關心同學，也學習在校園生活中保護自己、照顧他人。

最後，當然少不了充滿活力的「SH150 跑步運動」。讓孩子們動一動、跑一跑，在陽光與汗水中培養運動習慣，也為一天的學習注入滿滿能量。

我們相信，教育不只是在教室裡學習知識，更是在每天的生活中，陪著孩子學會自律、合作、友善、安全與勇敢前進。每一次獲獎、每一次宣導、每一次奔跑，都是孩子成長的足跡。

謝謝老師們用心陪伴，也謝謝每一位家長一直以來對學校的支持與信任。讓我們一起陪著孩子，在廣興這個溫暖的校園裡，快樂學習、健康成長，成為更好的自己。
