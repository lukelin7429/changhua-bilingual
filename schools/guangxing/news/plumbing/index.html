Junior Plumbers at the Vocational Discovery Center小小水電師 — 原斗職探中心體驗

On April 19, 2026, our students visited the "Yuandou Vocational Discovery Center" for a hands-on plumbing and electrical workshop! Guided by expert teacher Mr. Chen, students entered a simulated house to experience the daily work of a technician.

Learning by Doing: My First Home Repair. The "Junior Plumbers" handled every task with focus! They learned how to use "thread seal tape" to stop leaks, install faucets, and adjust water levels in toilet tanks. They even tried wiring sockets and making their own DIY extension cords.

Building Courage and Skills. Through this experience, students didn't just learn skills; they built the courage to solve real-life problems. Seeing the proud smiles on their faces after finishing the tasks was the best part of the day. It turns out that learning can be so real and rewarding when you do it with your own hands!

115 年 4 月 19 日，校園裡的一群小小探險家前往「原斗國中小職探中心」，展開了一場超實用的水電體驗之旅！在二林社區大學陳發財老師的專業引導下，孩子們走進仿真模型屋，挑戰成為頂尖的「小小水電師傅」。

從摸索到精通：居家修繕初體驗。水電工作一點也難不倒他們！從練習纏繞「止洩帶」防止漏水、正確安裝水龍頭，到修理馬桶水箱的水位調整，甚至親手組裝插座與製作 DIY 延長線，每一個關卡都是智慧與技巧的考驗。

做中學：解決問題的勇氣。「原來馬桶水箱是這樣運作的！」孩子們在動手做的過程中，不僅學會了實用的生活技能，更在面對複雜線路與零件時，培養出冷靜解決問題的能力。看到任務成功後孩子們臉上燦爛的笑容，我們看見了科技種子正透過雙手，實踐出最有成就感的學習！
