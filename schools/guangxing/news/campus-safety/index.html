My Body, My Rules友善校園安全宣導 — 防暴力、防霸凌、性侵害與性騷擾防治

Two numbers worth remembering: 110 and 113　　兩個一定要記住的號碼：110、113

A child's safety and dignity matter more than anything. In this campus safety session, students learned about body boundaries, about respect, and about what to do when something does not feel right.

The lesson began with one simple idea: your body belongs to you. Students talked about where their own boundaries are, how to notice an uncomfortable moment, and why it is always okay to say no, leave the situation, and tell a grown-up they trust.

Three sentences stayed on the screen: violence is a chain of wrong learning; refuse to stay silent; speak up and stop violence. Next to them were two numbers every child should remember — 110 and 113.

The other half of the lesson mattered just as much: respecting a classmate's boundaries, and being kind in the way we speak and play. Everyone deserves respect, and everyone has the right to feel safe.

Safety education is not a one-time talk. It grows day by day, through understanding, respect, and the company of the adults around them.

We want every child at Guangxing to know this: if something happens, you do not have to be afraid, and you are not alone. Your teachers are here, and we will work it out together.

孩子的安全與尊嚴，是我們最在乎的事。本次「防暴力、防霸凌、性侵害防治、性騷擾防治」宣導，陪伴孩子認識自己的身體界線與應有的尊重，學習辨識不安全的行為。

課程從一個簡單的觀念開始：你的身體屬於你自己。孩子一起討論自己的界線在哪裡、怎麼察覺讓自己不舒服的時刻，也明白遇到害怕或受到傷害的情況時，可以勇敢說不、離開現場，並尋求信任的師長協助。

螢幕上留下三句話：暴力是一連串錯誤學習、拒絕沉默不做暴力的共犯、勇敢發聲制止暴力。旁邊還有兩個每個孩子都要記住的號碼 —— 110 與 113。

課程的另一半同樣重要：學習尊重他人的界線，用友善的態度與同學相處。每個人都值得被尊重，也都有保護自己的權利。

安全教育不只是一次宣導，而是從日常生活中慢慢建立孩子的保護力與判斷力。願我們一起用理解、尊重與陪伴，為孩子打造一個安全、友善、沒有恐懼的學習環境。

也讓孩子知道：遇到問題不用害怕，你不是一個人。學校與師長會陪著你，一起想辦法、一起守護。
