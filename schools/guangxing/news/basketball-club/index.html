Dribbling into the Sunset課後籃球社 — 夕陽下的運球聲，是校園裡最動聽的節奏

Low dribbles, high shots, and Coach Cui right beside every player.　　低姿態運球、奮力投籃，崔教練一路陪在身邊。

Late afternoon at Guangxing: a soft breeze blows and the sunset turns the sky gold. Classes are over, but the playground is still full of energy — and full of the sound of bouncing balls.

This is our after-school basketball club. Under the careful guidance of Coach Cui, students practice staying low as they dribble, again and again. Then they jump and shoot, sending the ball toward the hoop with all their strength.

They practice until they are covered in sweat, yet their focused faces and bright smiles are truly moving. The best part of sports is not only the joy of scoring. It is also these basic moves, repeated over and over — not always exciting, but they build real skill. Even when they are out of breath, the children keep their eyes on the ball, and nobody gives up.

A special thank-you to Coach Cui, who never tires of showing each move in person and fixing every child's form, always patient as each player grows a little better.

Sports build strong bodies. They also give children a place to work up a sweat, burn off energy, and learn to play as a team.

傍晚的廣興校園，微風徐徐，夕陽下的陽光也變得格外溫柔。當一天的課程結束，我們的操場依然充滿著滿滿的活力！🏀

看看我們課後籃球社的孩子們，在崔教練悉心的指導下，一次次練習著扎實的低姿態運球，一次次奮力投籃，把球送向籃框。

雖然練得滿頭大汗，但那專注的神情和純真的笑容，真的讓人好感動。運動最迷人的地方，不只是投進籃框那一刻的喜悅，更是這些不斷重複、枯燥卻扎實的基本動作練習。看著孩子們雖然氣喘吁吁，卻依然緊緊盯著手中的籃球，沒有人輕言放棄。

更要特別感謝崔教練，總是那麼有耐心，不厭其煩地親自示範、微調每一個孩子的動作，陪伴他們一步步進步。

運動不僅能鍛鍊好體格，也讓孩子們盡情揮灑汗水、放電，並學習團隊精神。
