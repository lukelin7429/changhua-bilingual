Climbing to the Treetops攀樹體驗 — 氣候變遷與淨零綠校園

Getting ready · Climbing up · Reaching the canopy　　準備出發 ‧ 沿繩攀升 ‧ 登上樹冠

Are there little monkeys in the school trees? No — it is our very cool tree-climbing class! Today the fourth and fifth graders put on proper helmets, tree-climbing harnesses and gloves, and challenged themselves in the dappled green shade. Every step upward was full of courage and concentration.

The children learned a special way to go up, called "sit, pull, step, stand." They gripped a friction knot on the rope, put their feet into a foot loop, and used the strength of their legs together with their hands to rise slowly toward the canopy. Down on the ground, a trained instructor watched every climber closely, holding the end of the rope to keep each child safe.

When the children beat their fear of heights and reached the leafy branches, they saw something no textbook can give — the school seen from a tree's point of view. Touching the bark with their own hands and watching the tiny lives around them up close, they built a deeper bond with the land beneath them.

This was more than exercise. It was a real lesson in environmental and life education. By using tree protectors and other proper gear, we learned how to get close to nature while doing as little harm to the tree as possible. Well done to every brave young tree climber who made it to the top!

校園驚現小小猴子？原來是超酷的攀樹課程！今天 4、5 年級的孩子們穿戴起專業的安全帽、攀樹坐帶與手套，在陽光斑駁的綠蔭中挑戰自我，每一步的升起都寫滿了勇氣與專注。

這次的攀樹課程，孩子們體驗了獨特的「坐、拉、踩、站」上升技巧，大家抓握著繩索上的摩擦結，腳踩著踏圈，利用腿部力量踩踏並與手部協調，緩緩向樹冠層挺進。而在地面，專業教練始終全神貫注，雙手緊握繩索末端，為每一位攀爬者提供最穩固的確保與引導。

當孩子們克服對高度的恐懼，攀升至茂密的枝椏間時，那種視角翻轉的驚喜是課本無法給予的。從大樹的角度俯瞰校園，不僅開闊了視野，更讓大家在親手觸摸樹皮、近距離觀察生態的過程中，與土地建立了更深刻的情感連結。

這不只是一次體育鍛煉，更是一堂深刻的環境與生命教育。透過使用樹木保護器等專業裝備，我們學習如何在親近自然的同時，將對樹木的傷害降到最低，在挑戰極限的過程中學會尊重自然環境。為今天每一位勇敢登頂的小小攀樹師喝采！
