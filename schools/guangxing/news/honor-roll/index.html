Recurring Event

Midterm Honor Roll Ceremony月考成績優異頒獎典禮

To recognize and encourage our students' hard work, we held a special awards ceremony after the recent midterm exams!

Our Principal presented beautiful certificates and lovely prizes to the students who performed excellently. This celebration shows we appreciate their effort and encourages them to keep learning!

Seeing the confident and joyful smiles on the students' faces made all the teachers very proud. Let's keep up the great work and aim for excellence in every step of our learning journey!

為了肯定與鼓勵孩子們在學習上的努力，在月考結束後，學校舉行了成績優異頒獎典禮。

校長親自頒發了精美的獎狀和豐富的獎品給表現優異的同學。這不僅是對孩子們付出的肯定，也鼓勵他們持續保持學習的熱情與動力。

看到同學們臉上充滿自信與喜悅的笑容，師長們都深感欣慰。期許所有孩子都能從每次學習中獲得成長，並繼續追求卓越！
