Archaeology Discovery Trip考古小探險 — 牛埔遺址 × 中興莊

On April 29, 2026, our 4th graders joined a special "Archaeology Discovery Trip" to explore our local history from a thousand years ago. It was an amazing day filled with exploration and surprises!

Digging for Secrets: Junior Archaeologists at Work. At the Niupu Site, students turned into mini-archaeologists. They practiced how to dig and observe historical remains. They learned that history is not just in books, but hidden right under our feet! It was an exciting way to learn about protecting our heritage.

A Journey Through Time: Music and Old Villages. The students also visited the "Senior Arts & Opera Hall" to hear traditional music. Later, they explored "Zhongxing Village" to see beautiful old houses. Through these visits, they understood how people lived in the past. This "learning by doing" trip gave our students wonderful memories and a better understanding of their hometown!

115 年 4 月 29 日，四年級的小探險家們參與了「考古彰化．一日穿越千年」體驗計畫，展開了一場跨越時空的文化之旅。透過實地走訪與感官體驗，孩子們對家鄉的歷史與傳統藝術有了更深刻的認識。

小小考古學家，挖掘地底秘密。在牛埔遺址，孩子們化身小小考古學家，親手體驗挖掘與觀察。每一個發現都讓大家興奮不已，不僅學習到歷史保存的重要性，更理解了歷史是如何被科學地發現。

時光迴廊：從傳統戲曲到歷史建築。除了考古實作，孩子們也造訪了「樂齡戲曲館」認識南北管音樂，並漫步於充滿故事的「中興莊」。透過專業導覽，大家從傳統樂音與歷史建築中，看見了過去的生活樣貌。這場「玩中學」的時光旅行，在孩子們心中留下了最珍貴的文化回憶！
