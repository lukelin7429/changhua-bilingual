The Boy with the Pink Shadow性平劇場 — 攜手廣興非營利幼兒園，看見每個人的不一樣

Puppets on stage, hands up in the hall　　台上布偶登場，台下小手舉高高

This time, the invitation came from our friends at the kindergarten. Guangxing Non-profit Kindergarten applied for a gender equality theater program, Little Red Riding Hood Roll Call (小紅帽點點名), and asked Guangxing Elementary to join in. Little ones in purple shirts and big kids from our school filled the hall together.

The show was a puppet play by the Garden of Hope Foundation (勵馨基金會). Its story, The Boy with the Pink Shadow, comes from a picture book about a little boy whose shadow is pink, while everyone else in his family has a blue one. He wonders if being different is a problem.

Between scenes, the actors invited children up to the stage to share what they thought, and hands went up all over the hall. Through a fun and lively show, the children learned about gender equality in a happy, relaxed way.

The message was simple and strong: every person is unique, and boys and girls are equal. When we accept each other just as we are, our school becomes a kinder place for everyone.

Working together with the kindergarten lets us share good learning with even more children. Thank you, Guangxing Non-profit Kindergarten, for inviting us!

感謝廣興非營利幼兒園用心申請勵馨基金會「性別故事屋——小紅帽點點名」性別平等劇場活動，並邀請廣興國小一同參與。穿著紫色上衣的幼兒園小朋友和國小的哥哥姊姊坐滿了禮堂，一起欣賞這場偶戲。

今天的故事是《粉紅色影子的男孩》：家裡每個人的影子都是藍色的，只有小男孩的影子是粉紅色。他擔心自己和別人不一樣，是不是哪裡出了問題？

演出中，演員邀請孩子上台分享自己的想法，台下的小手一隻接一隻舉起來。透過生動有趣的戲劇演出，孩子在歡樂的氛圍中認識性別平等，學習尊重差異、理解彼此，建立友善與包容的態度。

每個人都是獨一無二的，男生女生都是平等的。當我們願意接納彼此原本的樣子，校園就會變得更溫暖。

透過幼兒園與國小的跨園校合作，讓教育資源彼此分享，陪伴孩子從小培養尊重、平等與同理心。教育的美好，在於彼此支持、攜手同行——感謝廣興非營利幼兒園的用心與邀請，讓我們一起為孩子打造更友善、更多元包容的學習環境，一起創造更美好的校園。
