World GreenMech: Building Letters & Numbers世界機關王 — 用積木拼出英文與數字

The World GreenMech course lets students use blocks to build their English names. They also use blocks to make things they see in daily life or in movies and cartoons. This helps them use their imagination and be creative.

Teachers lead students through hands-on learning. They build with their hands and play games while learning. This makes learning fun and easier to understand.

In the World GreenMech course, students feel proud because they make things by themselves. They also learn how things are made and how they work. The course helps them think of new ideas, solve problems, and enjoy learning through play.

We made colorful masks with building blocks! Our favorite part is the eyes—some are big, square, or even rainbow-colored! Each mask shows our fun ideas and big imaginations.

We used colorful blocks to build numbers, then played a fast and fun number game! The bright colors made it tricky to recognize the numbers, and we screamed with excitement! Some of us answered too early because we were too excited, and the classroom was filled with laughter.

We used blocks to build our English names and words—so cool and creative! We used to write with markers, but now we can actually build letters piece by piece. They look 3D and super special.

世界機關王課程讓學生用積木拼出自己的英文名字。他們也會用積木製作生活中或卡通、電影裡看到的東西，發揮想像力，創作有趣的作品。

老師帶著學生動手做中學。他們一邊動手組裝，一邊玩遊戲，讓學習變得更有趣，也更容易理解。

在世界機關王課程中，學生會因為自己動手完成作品而感到驕傲。他們也會學到東西是怎麼做出來的、怎麼運作的。這門課能幫助學生動腦想新點子、解決問題，並在玩樂中享受學習的樂趣。

我們用積木做出彩色的面具！我們最喜歡的地方是眼睛——有的大、有方形，還有彩虹色的呢！每一個面具都展現了我們有趣的點子和豐富的想像力！

我們用彩色積木拼出各種數字，接著玩了一場又快又好玩的數字遊戲！繽紛的顏色有時會讓我們看不清楚數字，大家興奮得大叫！

我們用積木拼出自己的英文名字和各種英文字，真的太特別了！以前我們都用色筆寫字，沒想到竟然可以用積木把字母一塊一塊拼出來，不但有立體感，還超有創意。
