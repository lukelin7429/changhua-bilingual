I.

About Nanhsing

關於南興 · 彰化市最南端、面向世界的小學
A school where reading, languages, and the wider world meet.
一所讓閱讀、語言與世界相遇的學校。

Welcome to Nanhsing Elementary School, a friendly public primary school at the southern edge of Changhua City. Founded in 1953, Nanhsing today is home to around 600 children who love to read, explore languages, and discover the world.

歡迎來到南興國小——一所位於彰化市最南端的友善公立小學。南興創校於 1953 年，如今約有 600 位孩子在這裡熱愛閱讀、探索語言、認識世界。

We are proud to host Changhua County's first English Book Bank (英文愛的書庫), an International Education Center, and a beautiful bookstore-style library. With a spirited taiko drum team and an award-winning track team, there is always something to celebrate at Nanhsing.

我們很自豪擁有彰化縣首座「英文愛的書庫」、國際教育中心，以及一座誠品風格的美麗圖書館。加上活力十足的太鼓隊與屢獲佳績的田徑隊，南興每天都有值得喝采的事。

"At Nanhsing, every child reads, every child belongs, and every child reaches for the world."
「在南興，每個孩子都閱讀、都被接納、都伸手擁抱世界。」
