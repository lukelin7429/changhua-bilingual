A Warm Welcome

校長的話 · 王有煌 校長
Wang Yu-huang
王有煌 校長 · 南興國小

"A principal is the head of the school family, the soul of the school, and a model for both teachers and students." With this belief, Principal Wang Yu-huang leads Nanhsing — a school facing the challenges of a changing world with the courage to learn, adapt, and keep growing.

「校長是學校的大家長，是學校的靈魂，是師生學習的典範。」王有煌校長以此信念帶領南興，面對知識經濟的挑戰與社會變遷，保有知變、應變的能力與理想。

A graduate of National Chi Nan International University (M.A. in Education Policy and Administration) and Tainan University, Principal Wang has served as principal of Shendong and Wende Elementary Schools, and in many leadership roles across Changhua's schools. He brings to Nanhsing a lifelong devotion to education.

王校長畢業於國立暨南國際大學教育政策與行政學系碩士、國立台南大學教育系，曾任彰化縣伸東國小、文德國小校長，並歷任多所學校行政主管，為南興帶來對教育終生的關懷。

"We are the ones we have been waiting for — and the change begins with us."
「未來在等待的人就是我們自己；改變就要開始，而那個開始就是我們自己。」

Seven Guiding Beliefs

七大辦學理念
1
Awaken a passion for teaching
喚醒教育熱忱

Teaching is nothing but love and example. Where teachers hold this in their hearts, education becomes possible.

2
Sharpen classroom practice
精進課堂能力

Every great ideal must finally be realized in the classroom; we grow teachers' expertise so that ideals become reality.

3
Nurture the whole child
培育全人兒童

So that every student learns with joy, grows up healthy, and develops in their own way.

4
A campus of excellence
精緻卓越校園

Shaping a school that is healthy, energetic and full of fresh ideas.

5
A safe and caring place
優質安全環境

Building a warm, harmonious campus where every staff member is a “catcher in the rye” for our children.

6
A humane campus
建立人文校園

Enriching children's lives so they can paint a bright and colorful future.

7
A win-win school
營造全贏學校

Bringing families, school and community together as one community of life.

"A thousand-mile journey begins with a single step; with one heart, we can build a fortress of happiness."
「千里之行始於足下，眾志方能成城，願以至誠心行萬里路。」— 王有煌 校長
