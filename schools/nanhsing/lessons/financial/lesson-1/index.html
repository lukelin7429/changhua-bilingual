🪙 What Is Money?

認識金錢
← Prev All lessons · 課程目錄 Next →
I.

The Big Idea

核心觀念

Money is what we use to trade for the things we need. It comes as coins and bills, and each one has a different value. Money does not just appear — people earn it by working. When we understand what money is, we can start to use it wisely.

金錢是我們用來交換所需物品的東西。它有硬幣和紙鈔，每一種面額都不同。金錢不會憑空出現——人們是靠工作賺來的。當我們了解金錢是什麼，就能開始聰明地使用它。

II.

Words to Know

必學單字 · 點 🔊 聽發音與例句
🪙
money
金錢
We use money to buy things.
我們用金錢買東西。
Save your money carefully.
小心地存好你的錢。
🟡
coin
硬幣
A coin is small and round.
硬幣小小圓圓的。
I have five coins.
我有五個硬幣。
💵
bill
紙鈔
A bill is made of paper.
紙鈔是紙做的。
This bill is one hundred dollars.
這張紙鈔是一百元。
💪
earn
賺取
People earn money by working.
人們靠工作賺錢。
I earn coins by helping at home.
我幫忙做家事賺零用錢。
III.

Let's Talk

開口說 · 對話練習 · 點 🔊 跟著唸
Two students count their savings. 兩位學生數他們的存款。
A
How much money do you have?
你有多少錢？
B
I have three coins and one bill.
我有三個硬幣和一張紙鈔。
A
How did you earn it?
你是怎麼賺到的？
B
I helped my mom at home.
我在家幫媽媽做事。
💡 Try this: Count the coins in your piggy bank and add up the total.
數數你撲滿裡的硬幣，把總額加起來。
