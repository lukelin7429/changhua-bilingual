Culture, Craft, and Wonder走進成美，文化體驗日

As their elementary school days drew to a close, Fude's sixth-graders set off on a very special graduation adventure. Invited by the Ting Hsin Huo-Der Education Foundation and Chengmei Cultural Park, 14 graduates joined Principal Cho Hong-bin, their teachers, and PTA Chair Mr. Chen Rui-rong for the 2026 Graduates' Cultural Experience Day.

Hands in ink, hearts full of tradition.

At Chengmei, students explored the beauty of traditional Taiwanese arts and crafts — painting fans with their own designs and trying indigo dyeing in a large vat. It was a day of creativity, discovery, and connection to the heritage of this land. A perfect send-off before their next chapter begins.

中文原文 · Chinese

在告別國小生活倒數之際，福德六年級畢業生迎來了最特別的青春賀禮！感謝頂新和德文教基金會與成美文化園的誠摯邀請，由卓鴻賓校長帶領14位畢業生，並邀請家長會陳瑞榮會長共同參與「2026年畢業生文化體驗活動」。

雙手染上文化的色彩，心裡留下最美的記憶。

在成美文化園，孩子們親手彩繪扇子、體驗藍染，感受台灣傳統文化的深厚魅力。每一筆、每一抹，都是獨一無二的畢業回憶。帶著這份文化的溫度，福德的孩子們準備迎向人生的下一個篇章！
