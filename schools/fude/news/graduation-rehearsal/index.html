Ready, Set, Graduate!鳳凰花前的最後準備

One day before the big event, Fude's beautifully decorated auditorium came alive with energy and excitement. The joint graduation rehearsal for Fude Elementary and Fude Non-profit Kindergarten got underway — every step carefully practised, from seating to award presentations, showing the deepest respect for this milestone moment.

A teacher's love knows no distance.

A special surprise came through a video call: retired teacher Mr. Lin Dong-xian joined from abroad to send warm words of encouragement, along with a heartfelt gift he had sent across the ocean. Time passes and distance grows, but at Fude, a teacher's care for their students never fades.

中文原文 · Chinese

六月十日，禮堂響起悠揚的彩排樂章，福德國小暨福德非營利幼兒園聯合畢業典禮預演盛大展開。叮嚀的走位、隆重的頒獎演練，每一個細節都凝聚著對成長的最高敬意！

時間與距離，阻不了師長的愛。

退休教師林東顯組長，在螢幕前跨越萬里重洋捎來溫暖的視訊勉勵，跨海致贈的精緻畢業禮物，承載著資深師長對孩子最深切的期盼與疼愛。時間會流逝，距離會拉長，但福德師長守護學子的這份愛，永遠沒有邊界。
