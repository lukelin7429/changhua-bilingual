About this Program · 關於品格教育

Growing Good People 培養品格，從根做起

The name Fude — virtue and blessing — is not just our school's name. It is our promise. At Fude Elementary, character education is not a subject on the timetable: it is the spirit woven into every lesson, every ceremony, and every ordinary school day.

We focus on four core virtues that guide students from their first day to their last: honesty in word and action, respect for every person and living thing, gratitude for all that makes their lives possible, and responsibility for the community they belong to.

「福德」不只是校名，更是我們對每一個孩子的承諾。在福德國小，品格教育不是課表上的一門課，而是融入每一堂課、每一次典禮、每一個平凡校園日子的精神。我們以誠信、尊重、感恩、責任四大核心品德，陪伴每一位學生從入學到畢業。
