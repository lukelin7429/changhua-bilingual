About the Camp · 關於夏日樂學

Summer Is for Learning Something New

暑假，是學新東西最好的季節

Every summer, Fude Elementary opens its doors for a week of joyful, English-filled learning. The 2025 Summer Fun Camp brings together children for English games, creative crafts, outdoor exploration, science activities, and the kind of friendships that only happen when you spend a whole week learning together.

No exams. No pressure. Just curious children, enthusiastic teachers, and the pure fun of discovering that English can feel like play — every single day.

每個暑假，福德國小打開大門，舉辦一週充滿英語的快樂學習。2025 夏日樂學匯聚孩子們，一起玩英語遊戲、做創意手作、進行戶外探索與科學活動，建立只有在一週共同學習中才能誕生的友誼。沒有考試、沒有壓力，只有好奇的孩子、熱情的老師，還有每天發現英文也可以很好玩的純粹快樂。
