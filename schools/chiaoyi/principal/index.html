I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Principal Yu Chih-hsin graduated from the Department of Industrial Education at National Changhua University of Education, and holds a Master's degree in Mathematics Education from National Taichung University of Education. Before coming to Chiaoyi, he served as a homeroom teacher and division head at Heren Elementary School in Homei, as director at Jianxin Elementary School in Fangyuan, and as a curriculum supervisor with the Changhua County Education Department. He has led Chiaoyi Elementary School since August 2024.

游智信校長畢業於國立彰化師範大學工業教育學系，並取得國立臺中教育大學數學教育碩士學位。到僑義之前，曾任和美鎮和仁國小導師與組長、芳苑鄉建新國小主任，以及彰化縣政府教育處課程督學。自民國113年8月起擔任僑義國小校長。

Beyond the classroom, he brings a longstanding interest in educational administration and mathematics education, and is also a certified track-and-field official — a lifelong enthusiasm for sport that shapes how he sees student growth.

課堂之外，校長長期關注教育行政與數學教育，同時也是田徑賽事的合格裁判，對運動抱有濃厚熱忱，這份熱忱也延伸到他對孩子成長的期待。

"Positive leadership starts with putting students first."
正向領導，學生第一。 — 游智信校長的辦學理念
