Learning, Fun & Growth

【學習×樂趣×成長】僑義國小115年夏日英語營・前三天精彩全紀錄

On a scorching summer day, what could make children laugh out loud while confidently speaking English? Our Summer Fun-Learning English Camp, of course! The first three days of rich activities have wrapped up beautifully — watching the children go from shy to speaking up, from hesitant to playing a beautiful melody, filled us with pride. Let's look back on this jam-packed three-day adventure.

炎炎夏日，什麼活動能讓孩子們一邊開懷大笑、一邊自信開口說英文？沒錯，就是我們精彩無比的「夏日樂學英語營」！前三天的豐富課程已經圓滿落幕，看著孩子們從害羞到大聲對話、從摸索到吹奏出美妙旋律，真的讓人滿滿感動與驕傲。讓我們一起回顧這三天的超充實魔法旅程吧。

Day 1 — A First Encounter with Creativity and Music. The day began with an energizing English warm-up. Then came story time with the picture-book classic Bark, George, with children giggling as they imitated all kinds of animals. Next, hands-on clay crafting let each child mold their very own favorite animal friend. Music and movement rounded out the day — learning body parts while dancing along to Rondeau and Mary Had a Little Lamb.

Day 1：創意與音樂的初相遇。用英文開啟活力的一天！接著共讀經典繪本《Bark, George》，孩子們在歡笑聲中模仿各種小動物；再用輕黏土捏出自己最喜歡的專屬動物夥伴；最後透過音樂律動認識身體部位，手腳並用跟著《Rondeau》與《瑪莉有隻小綿羊》快樂起舞！

Day 2 — Colors, Magic, and a Rhythm Party. A whole-body game got children naturally picking up everyday conversation and vocabulary. Then a picture-book deep dive into The Day the Crayons Quit had the crayons running away from home — and the kids writing about it on their worksheets. A playful "musical fruit grid" made learning Do, Re, Mi and Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star feel effortless, and a cup-tapping session — with a bit of rest-note theory mixed in — turned the room into an eye-catching rhythm concert.

Day 2：色彩魔法與節奏派對。透過手腳並用的互動遊戲，自然學會日常對話與單字；導讀繪本《The Day the Crayons Quit》，跟著彩色蠟筆一起離家出走、寫學習單；再透過有趣的「音符九宮格水果遊戲」，輕鬆學會高音 Do、Re、Mi 與《靜夜星空》；最後結合休止符概念，敲擊出超吸睛的杯子節奏音樂！

Day 3 — Real-Life Flavor and a Grand Finale. Kids faced off in a fast-paced letter-matching contest using straws and flashcards. Then came Farm to Toast — future bakers learned the English for toast and toppings, spread their own butter and jam, and tasted the sweet result of their own labor. Finally, a recorder showcase: after practicing alto and soprano fingerings and pitch, the children proudly performed Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star and We Are All in One Family — a perfect finale to three unforgettable days.

Day 3：美味生活實踐與成果驗收。用吸管與字卡進行超刺激的字母配對對抗賽；接著化身小小烘焙師，在「Farm to Toast」活動中學習吐司與配料的英文，親手抹醬、品嚐自己勞動的成果；最後是直笛星光重奏，挑戰 Alto 與 Soprano 的指法與音高練習，成功吹奏出《小星星》與《當我們同在一起》，為三天的營隊畫下完美句點！

"I can play the recorder now, I'm so happy!"
「我最喜歡英語遊戲了，很有趣！」「繪本故事好好聽，我學到好多新單字！」「我會吹直笛了，好開心！」「和同學一起完成任務，超有成就感！」

Special thanks to our dedicated instructors — Teacher Wan-jing, Teacher Jairah, Teacher Mei-ling, and Teacher Kuan-ting — for their care and guidance, and to every parent for their support. These young adventurers built confidence through play and learned teamwork along the way — truly the best gift of the summer. We can't wait to share more highlights and happy memories from the rest of the camp!

感謝授課老師婉菁老師、Jairah 老師、美玲老師、冠廷老師的用心帶領，以及所有家長們的支持。這群小小冒險王在玩樂中建立自信、在團隊中學習合作，這絕對是今年夏天最棒的禮物！讓我們一起期待接下來更多精彩課程與美好回憶吧！

Through playful English games, picture books, hands-on crafts, and speaking practice, children built their English skills in a relaxed, joyful atmosphere — while the recorder sessions, with fingering, rhythm, and ensemble practice, nurtured musicality and teamwork. Learning through play, playing through learning — every day was full, and every day was a happy one this summer.

孩子們透過有趣的英語遊戲、繪本故事、手作創作及口語練習，在輕鬆愉快的氛圍中提升英語表達能力；直笛課程則透過指法、節奏與合奏練習，培養音樂素養與團隊默契。在玩中學、學中玩，讓孩子每天都收穫滿滿，度過充實又快樂的暑假！
