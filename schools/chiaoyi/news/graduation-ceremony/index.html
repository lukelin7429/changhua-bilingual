Congratulations, Class of 66

恭喜畢業！僑義國小第66屆畢業典禮

We're grateful to every guest who joined the ceremony, and to everyone in the community who contributed graduation gifts and heartfelt wishes — filling our graduates with love and encouragement.

感謝蒞臨典禮的各位貴賓，以及各界熱心人士提供畢業禮品與誠摯祝福，讓畢業生感受到滿滿的關愛與鼓勵。

We're equally thankful to the teachers and students across the whole school, who worked together to make every part of the ceremony come together smoothly, leaving the children with precious, beautiful memories.

也感謝全校師生齊心協力、共同投入，順利完成畢業典禮各項任務，為孩子們留下珍貴而美好的回憶。

May our graduates carry their teachers' hopes and their classmates' blessings as they chase their dreams with courage and confidence — stepping forward into a bright and wonderful future!

祝福畢業生帶著師長的期許與同學的祝福，勇敢追夢、自信前行，迎向燦爛美好的未來！
