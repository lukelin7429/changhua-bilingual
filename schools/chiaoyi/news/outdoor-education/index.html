A Wonderful Day of Outdoor Education

僑義國小戶外教育活動圓滿成功

On May 22, teachers and students across the whole school stepped outside the classroom for a full and joyful day of outdoor education. Through visits, hands-on experiences, and activities, children learned while having fun, and grew while exploring.

5月22日，全校師生一同走出校園，展開充實又愉快的戶外教育之旅。孩子們透過參訪、體驗與手作活動，在歡樂中學習，在探索中成長。

Thank you to every teacher for your care and companionship, and to every parent for your support and cooperation, which helped the day come together so smoothly.

感謝所有師長的用心陪伴與照顧，也感謝家長們的支持與配合，讓活動順利圓滿完成。

A happy send-off, a safe return, and a day full of rewards!

快樂出發，平安返校，收穫滿滿！
