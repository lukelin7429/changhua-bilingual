The Warmest Cheer

最暖心的應援

A heartfelt thank-you to Representative Cheng Chin-yi, who once again visited Chiaoyi Elementary to check in on our students — bringing freshly baked, fragrant bread along with saqima and sports drinks to give the children a boost of energy!

感謝鄭進議代表再次蒞臨關心僑義國小學子，特地送來剛出爐、香氣四溢的可口麵包，還有沙其馬及運動飲料，為孩子們補充滿滿活力與能量！

Chiaoyi students have recently been fortunate to receive Representative Cheng's care and encouragement. This kind of local support doesn't just warm children's hearts — it lets everyone feel the deep bond and warmth of our hometown community.

近期僑義學子深受鄭代表照顧與鼓勵，這份來自地方的關懷，不僅溫暖了孩子們的心，也讓大家感受到濃濃的家鄉情誼與人情味。

On the path of education, support and companionship from every corner of society are some of the most precious forces in a child's growth. Thank you, Representative Cheng, for your long-standing care for Sijhou's education and for our students, and for expressing your warmth and blessings through real action.

教育的路上，有社會各界的支持與陪伴，是孩子成長最珍貴的力量。感謝鄭進議代表長期關心溪州教育、愛護學子，用實際行動傳遞溫暖與祝福。

We believe our children will turn this care into motivation — studying hard, growing healthy, and one day giving back to their hometown and community. With sincere thanks to Representative Cheng Chin-yi, and gratitude for your care and support for the students of Sijhou.

相信孩子們都能將這份關愛化為前進的動力，努力學習、健康成長，未來回饋家鄉、服務社會。誠摯感謝鄭進議代表，感謝您對溪州學子的關愛與支持。
