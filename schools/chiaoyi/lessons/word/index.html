II.

PE, Sports & Safety

體育、運動與安全

從運球、跳繩到心肺復甦術——僑義孩子動起來的體育英文。

WORD 001
dribblev.
運球
The teacher is showing how to dribble the ball in PE class.
老師在體育課上演示如何運球。
The students practice dribbling using both their left and right hands.
學生們用左右手練習運球。
WORD 002
jump ropev.
跳繩
Jumping rope is a great form of cardio.
跳繩是一種很好的有氧運動。
Once you get the hang of it, you can jump rope really fast!
一旦掌握了竅門，你就能跳得很快！
WORD 003
swimming classn.
游泳課
The students are stretching for swimming class.
學生們正在為游泳課做伸展運動。
The students are practicing swimming in swimming class.
學生們正在游泳課上練習游泳。
WORD 004
kickv.
打水
Kicking is part of swimming.
打水是游泳的一部份。
The students are practicing kicking.
學生們正在練習打水。
WORD 005
tug of warn.
拔河
We're playing tug-of-war at recess.
我們在下課時間玩拔河。
The two teams are pulling on the rope in the tug-of-war game.
兩個隊伍在拔河時拉繩子。
WORD 006
protective gearn.
護具
The teacher is showing his students how to put on protective gear.
老師正在教他的學生如何穿上防護裝備。
This type of protective gear is used in kendo class.
這種護具用於劍道課程。
WORD 007
CPRn.
心肺復甦術
CPR is a procedure that can save someone's life if their heart stops beating.
心肺復甦術是種醫療處理，當有人心臟停止跳動時可以用它來救人一命。
CPR isn't only useful for adults to learn; kids can learn and perform CPR, too.
心肺復甦術不僅對大人很有用，小孩子也可以學習做CPR。
III.

Arts, Crafts & Cooking

美術、手作與烹飪

畫畫、彩繪燈籠、做棉花糖雪人、煎鬆餅——僑義的美術課教室裡總有新玩意。

WORD 008
drawv.
畫畫
The students must draw a family photo in the art class.
學生們必須在美術課上畫家人的照片。
After drawing, they color their pictures.
畫完後，再給他們的圖上色。
WORD 009
paintv.
畫（彩繪）
The students get ready to paint their lanterns.
同學們準備好要為他們的燈籠彩繪。
They paint different designs on their lanterns.
他們在燈籠上面畫上不同的樣式。
WORD 010
art projectn.
美術作業
The students share their art projects.
同學們分享他們的美術作業。
The art project is on how to take care of the earth.
這項美術作業主題是照顧地球。
WORD 011
marshmallown.
棉花糖
The teacher shows us how to make a marshmallow snowman.
老師為我們示範如何做棉花糖雪人。
The students build snowmen out of marshmallows.
同學們用棉花糖做成雪人。
WORD 012
maskn.
面具
The teacher shows how to make a Santa Claus mask.
老師示範如何製作聖誕老人面具。
The students wear their masks after they are finished.
完成後，學生們戴上他們的面具。
WORD 013
pancaken.
鬆餅
The student uses a pan to cook a pancake.
學生用平底鍋煎鬆餅。
The teacher helps his students make pancakes.
老師幫助他的學生製作鬆餅。
IV.

Reading & Literacy

閱讀與語文

自然發音、劃重點、朗讀比賽——僑義推廣閱讀的日常英文。

WORD 014
phonicsn.
自然發音
The teacher reviews phonics in class.
老師在課堂上複習自然發音。
The students play a game to practice their phonics skills.
同學們玩一個遊戲來練習他們自然發音的技巧。
WORD 015
highlightv.
劃重點
The students are highlighting their books.
學生們正在書上劃重點。
You highlight the important information in your reading.
閱讀時你會將重要的資訊畫重點。
WORD 016
newspapern.
報紙
These students are reading newspapers.
這些學生正在看報紙。
Newspapers give you news, sports, weather, and other information.
報紙為您提供新聞、體育、天氣和其他訊息。
WORD 017
storytellern.
講故事的人
A good storyteller teaches a lesson and entertains you at the same time.
一個擅長講故事的人可以教導大家道理，同時又兼具娛樂大眾。
The storytellers can use real or fictional information to tell a story.
講故事的人可能用真實或者是虛構的情節來說故事。
WORD 018
read aloud competitionn.
朗讀比賽
A read-aloud competition helps students with their speaking skills.
朗讀比賽有助於學生的說話技巧。
The students are preparing for a read-aloud competition.
學生們正在為朗讀比賽做準備。
WORD 019
promotev.
推廣
On April 23, World Book Day, a day to promote books and reading, is celebrated around the world.
4月23日世界閱讀日是全世界推廣書本與閱讀的日子。
On World Book Day, the school organizes an event at the school library to promote reading.
在世界閱讀日當天，學校在圖書館辦了一個活動來推廣閱讀。
V.

Campus Life & Science

校園生活與科學

從擦黑板機到簡單電路——僑義校園裡處處是英文教室。

WORD 020
turn on / turn offphr. v.
打開／關掉
The student cleans the eraser after turning on the eraser cleaning machine.
這位學生打開擦黑板機清理板擦。
After he's done, he will turn it off.
結束後他會把機器關掉。
WORD 021
competitionn.
比賽
The school held a competition to identify plants and animals.
學校舉辦了一場植物和動物鑑定比賽。
The competition was meant for students to get excited about learning the names of plants and animals at school.
比賽的目的是讓學生對在學校學習植物和動物的名稱感到興奮。
WORD 022
harmonican.
口琴
The students practice playing their harmonicas.
學生們練習吹口琴。
The teacher helps her students read the music for harmonica.
老師幫助她的學生閱讀口琴樂譜。
WORD 023
Earthn.
地球
Earth is the planet we live on.
地球是我們居住的星球。
On Earth Day, the teacher shows her students ways to take care of the Earth.
在世界地球日當天，老師為學生展示愛護地球的各種方式。
WORD 024
circuitn.
電路
A circuit is a path for electricity to travel in.
電路是讓電流通行的路徑。
The students have made a simple circuit.
學生們製作了一個簡單電路。
WORD 025
sweepv.
掃
Can you sweep the floor with the broom?
你能用掃帚掃地嗎？
I'm sweeping the dust into a dustpan.
我正在把灰塵掃到畚斗。
