I.

Welcome

歡迎來到僑義國小

Surrounded by rice fields, under wide open skies — a serene campus to learn and grow.

稻田環繞、藍天相伴——一所寧靜美麗、適合學習與成長的校園。

Welcome to Chiaoyi Elementary School, located in the idyllic Sijhou Township of Changhua County, Taiwan. Surrounded by peaceful rice fields, our school is a serene and beautiful place to learn and grow. Our spacious school ground, with its clear blue skies and fluffy white clouds, is the perfect setting for children to learn and play.

歡迎來到僑義國小，學校座落於彰化縣純樸的溪州鄉。四周環繞著恬靜的稻田，是一處寧靜美麗、適合學習與成長的地方。開闊的校園，藍天白雲相伴，是孩子學習與遊戲的最佳場所。

At Chiaoyi School, we are dedicated to providing our students with a top-quality education. We place a strong emphasis on English language learning, and have received numerous certificates of recognition for our efforts in this area. In addition to our language programs, we offer a wide range of extracurricular activities, including a band and a kendo team, to help our students develop their talents and interests.

在僑義國小，我們致力於提供學生優質的教育。學校特別重視英語學習，長期以來在這方面的努力也獲得多項獎項肯定。除了語言課程之外，我們也提供豐富多元的課外活動，包括管樂團與劍道隊，協助學生發展各自的才能與興趣。

Our music education program is a particular point of pride for us. Whether you are a seasoned musician or just starting out, we have something to offer you at Chiaoyi School.

音樂教育是僑義國小特別引以為傲的一環。無論你是經驗豐富的音樂愛好者，或是剛要起步的初學者，僑義國小都能為你提供合適的學習資源。

A campus close to the land, close to childhood
貼近土地，貼近童年的校園

A red-brick archway framed by seasonal blossoms, a courtyard watched over by a family of playful bear sculptures — Chiaoyi is a small rural campus with a big sense of warmth.

紅磚拱門映著時令花樹，校園裡還有一整列俏皮的熊雕塑靜靜陪伴——僑義是一所小而溫暖的鄉間校園。

II.

What Makes Chiaoyi

僑義的特色

Rice fields, English, band, and kendo.

稻田、英語、管樂與劍道——僑義孩子成長的四種養分。

Rice-Field Campus
稻田環繞的校園

Set among the peaceful rice fields of Sijhou Township, with wide-open grounds under clear blue skies — a calm, unhurried place for children to grow.

English Learning Excellence
英語學習成果

A long-standing emphasis on English language learning, recognized with numerous certificates of achievement over the years.

School Band
管樂團

Music education is a particular point of pride — the school band gives students of every level a place to pick up an instrument and perform together.

Kendo Team
劍道隊

Beyond the classroom, the kendo team builds focus, discipline, and resilience — one more way Chiaoyi students discover their talents.

"Whether you are a seasoned musician or just starting out, Chiaoyi has something to offer you."
無論是經驗豐富的音樂愛好者，或是剛要起步的初學者，僑義都能為你提供合適的學習資源。
