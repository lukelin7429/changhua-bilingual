II.

Campus Life

校園生活

下課、放學、老師的進修時光、課堂上的通知——仁豐國小每天的日常節奏。

WORD 001
recessn.
下課時間
When the bell rings for recess, students head outside to play.
下課鐘聲一響，學生們就往外跑去玩耍。
At Renfeng, recess is a chance to run around and have fun with friends.
在仁豐，下課時間是和同學一起奔跑、歡笑的時光。
WORD 002
dismissaln.
放學
At dismissal, students wait outside for their parents to pick them up.
放學時，學生在校外等待家長來接。
Before dismissal, make sure you have all your belongings with you.
放學前，記得帶齊自己所有的東西。
WORD 003
professional developmentn.
專業進修
In Taiwan, every school holds professional development sessions for teachers.
在台灣，每所學校都會為老師安排專業進修時段。
During professional development, Renfeng's teachers learn new skills together.
在專業進修時間，仁豐的老師們一起學習新技能與教學策略。
WORD 004
announcementn.
通知
The teacher makes an important announcement during class.
老師在課堂上發布重要通知。
Students listen carefully whenever the teacher has an announcement to share.
每當老師要宣布事情時，學生都會專心聆聽。
III.

Sports & Playground

運動與遊戲

溜滑梯、盪鞦韆、吹泡泡、投籃、鉛球、木球——仁豐操場上的活力時光。

WORD 005
sliden.
溜滑梯
Going down the slide is one of the most fun parts of recess.
溜下溜滑梯是下課時間最好玩的事情之一。
Renfeng's playground slide always draws a line of excited students.
仁豐操場上的溜滑梯，總是吸引一排興奮排隊的學生。
WORD 006
swingn.
盪鞦韆
To swing higher, you kick your legs back and forth.
盪鞦韆時，要前後擺動雙腳才能盪得更高。
During recess, many Renfeng students head straight for the swings.
下課時間，許多仁豐學生會直接衝向鞦韆。
WORD 007
bubblen.
泡泡
Students use different tools to blow bubbles outside.
學生用不同的工具在戶外吹泡泡。
No matter what tool they use, every bubble comes out round.
不管用什麼工具，吹出來的泡泡都是圓的。
WORD 008
basketballn.
籃球
Students shoot the ball at the hoop when they play basketball.
打籃球時，學生會朝籃框投籃。
Renfeng's basketball court is always busy after school.
仁豐的籃球場放學後總是很熱鬧。
WORD 009
shot putn.
鉛球
In shot put, students take turns throwing a heavy ball as far as they can.
鉛球比賽中，學生輪流把沉重的球盡量丟遠。
Shot put is one of the track and field events Renfeng students practice.
鉛球是仁豐學生練習的田徑項目之一。
WORD 010
woodballn.
木球
Woodball is a sport where players use a mallet to hit a ball through a gate.
木球是一種用球槌把球擊過球門的運動。
Woodball is a lot like golf, and it's a sport Renfeng students get to try.
木球很像高爾夫球，是仁豐學生會接觸到的運動項目。
IV.

Academics & Reading

學術與閱讀

社會課、平板學習、輪流練習發表、行動圖書館送書到校——呼應仁豐「點亮閱讀」的課程願景。

WORD 011
social studiesn.
社會科
In social studies class, students learn about people, places, and society.
在社會課，學生學習關於人、地方與社會的知識。
Renfeng students pay close attention during social studies.
仁豐學生在上社會課時都很專心。
WORD 012
tabletn.
平板
Students use tablets with keyboards to complete activities in class.
學生使用搭配鍵盤的平板電腦完成課堂活動。
Tablets help Renfeng students explore and learn new things hands-on.
平板幫助仁豐學生用動手操作的方式探索新知識。
WORD 013
practicev.
練習
The teacher encourages students to take turns and practice speaking up.
老師鼓勵學生輪流練習發表意見。
Students practice both expressing their own ideas and being good listeners.
學生不只練習表達自己，也練習當一個好的聆聽者。
WORD 014
mobile libraryn.
行動圖書館
A mobile library is a truck that's been turned into a small library on wheels.
行動圖書館是一輛改裝成小型圖書館的卡車。
The mobile library brings books to Renfeng and other schools nearby.
行動圖書館把書送到仁豐和附近的學校。
V.

Arts, Culture & Making

藝術、文化與創作

書法、台語、絲巾舞蹈、宋江陣、積木裝置、種豆子——仁豐孩子動手做、動口說、動身體。

WORD 015
calligraphyn.
書法
In calligraphy class, students practice traditional Chinese brush writing.
在書法課上，學生練習傳統中國毛筆字。
Getting good at calligraphy takes plenty of patience.
要把書法寫好，需要很多的耐心。
WORD 016
Taiwanese
台語
Students learn Taiwanese as part of their language class.
學生在語文課學習台語。
Renfeng students often learn Taiwanese by watching videos together in class.
仁豐學生常常在課堂上一起看影片學台語。
WORD 017
dancev.
跳舞
Students dance to gentle music while waving silk scarves.
學生隨著輕柔的音樂舞動絲巾。
As they dance, Renfeng students feel the rhythm and beauty of the music.
跳舞時，仁豐學生感受到音樂的節奏與美感。
WORD 018
Song Jiang Battle Array
宋江陣
Song Jiang Battle Array is a traditional Taiwanese folk performance that students practice together.
宋江陣是一種台灣傳統民俗表演，學生們一起練習。
Performing Song Jiang Battle Array takes great teamwork and skill.
表演宋江陣需要很好的團隊合作與技巧。
WORD 019
contraptionn.
裝置
Students build all kinds of contraptions out of building blocks.
學生用積木組裝各式各樣的裝置。
They follow instructions carefully as they put their contraptions together.
他們仔細按照步驟，把自己的裝置組裝起來。
WORD 020
seedn.
種子
Students plant bean seeds as part of a science activity.
學生種下豆子的種子，作為科學活動的一部分。
With care and time, the seeds Renfeng students plant will grow into new plants.
只要用心照顧，仁豐學生種下的種子就會長成新的植物。
