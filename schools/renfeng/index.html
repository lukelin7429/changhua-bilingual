I.

Welcome

歡迎來到仁豐國小

Point Alight Reading. Develop Every Talent. Help Every Child Shine.

點亮閱讀，多元展能，成就每一個孩子

At Renfeng Elementary, we believe every student can shine. Led by our caring principal and teachers, we make reading a big part of learning, because it helps students succeed in school and in life — connecting closely with the core competencies and self-directed learning at the heart of Taiwan's 12-year curriculum.

仁豐國小的課程願景是「點亮閱讀，多元展能，成就每一個孩子」，與12年國教課綱強調的核心素養及自主學習充分接軌，期望透過「Reading to Learn（讀以學）」打造閱讀素養，讓學生透過閱讀終身學習。

Renfeng isn't just about reading, though. The new curriculum also values teaching to each child's strengths — so we help every student discover what they're good at. We offer a wide range of classes so every child can find something they love and show off their talents.

12年國教課程同時重視適性教育，發展每個孩子不同的潛能，仁豐國小也極力發展每個學生的不同潛質，期待孩子在多樣化的課程中有機會多元發展，一展長才。

At Renfeng Elementary, we're more than just a school — we're a community where students grow up to be smart, caring, and ready for the future. Come join us and see how we learn and grow together!

在仁豐國小，我們不僅僅是一所學校。我們是一個社區，學生在這裡成長為聰明、關懷他人並為未來做好準備的人。來加入我們，看看我們是如何一起學習和成長的！

在自信中成長，學會展翅飛翔

A colourful campus in the heart of Tianwei Township — open courts, garden corners, and cheerful colour wherever you look, where every child finds room to explore and grow.

位於田尾鄉的繽紛校園——開闊的球場、悠閒的花園角落，處處充滿童趣色彩，讓每個孩子都有探索與成長的空間。

II.

Renfeng's Big Five Skills

仁豐素養五力 Go！

What do we want our students to take with them after six years at Renfeng?

孩子們在仁豐國小就學六年，畢業後到底要帶走什麼能力？

Our whole school team thought hard about this question together, and confirmed the student profile we call "Renfeng's Big Five Skills, Go!" — five abilities every child should carry with them when they graduate.

仁豐國小全校團隊透過多次討論，共同思考這個核心問題，確認了仁豐國小的學生圖像「仁豐素養五力Go！」，是每個孩子畢業時都該帶著走的五項能力。

📖
Reading
閱讀力

Being good at reading and understanding information — understanding text, building meaning, learning independently.

理解文本、建構意義、自主學習。

🔍
Observation
觀察力

Noticing details and remembering what they see — five senses fully open, observing closely, recording carefully.

五感全開、深入觀察、詳細記錄。

💬
Expression
表達力

Sharing thoughts clearly and using technology — expressing fully, using technology, representing ideas in many forms.

完整表達、科技運用、多元表徵。

💎
Creativity
創造力

Coming up with new ideas and making cool things — using imagination, playing with ideas, producing something original.

發揮巧思、舞動想像、創意產出。

💡
Critical Thinking
思辨力

Thinking hard, understanding different views, and explaining their own ideas well — thinking actively, exploring deeply, reasoning with evidence.

積極思考、探此究彼、觀點論證。

"Renfeng's Big Five Skills, Go!" — the compass for every child's six years at Renfeng.
仁豐素養五力Go！——陪伴每個孩子走過六年仁豐歲月的羅盤。
