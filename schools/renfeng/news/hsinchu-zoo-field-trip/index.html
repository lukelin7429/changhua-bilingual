Enriching Experiential Learning

豐富體驗多元學習！仁豐國小高年級展開本學期第二次兩天一夜校外教學

Fifth- and sixth-grade students from Renfeng Elementary School recently took part in their second two-day, one-night field trip of the semester. As part of their outdoor environmental education curriculum, students visited Hsinchu Zoo and the Taoyuan Railway Pavilion.

仁豐國小五、六年級學生日前展開本學期第二次兩天一夜的校外教學活動。配合戶外教育環境探索課程，師生一同造訪了新竹市立動物園及桃園軌道願景館等景點。

Through guided tours, interactive exhibits, and a ride on a miniature train, students learned about ecology, local history, and railway development hands-on — and made unforgettable memories with their classmates along the way.

透過專人解說、互動展覽以及試乘微型小火車，孩子們不僅在實境中學習生態知識與地方軌道歷史，也與同班同學留下豐富而美好的回憶。
