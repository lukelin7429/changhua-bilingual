Overseas Youth Bring Fun English Learning to Renfeng

海外青年齊聚仁豐 英語營帶領學童開心學英文

Renfeng Elementary recently held the 2026 Overseas Youth English Camp, inviting overseas volunteers to lead fun, hands-on English activities. In interactive games like "Can You Find the Shape?" and group poster drawing, students practiced English naturally in a relaxed, happy atmosphere.

仁豐國小近日舉辦「2026海外青年英語營」，邀請海外志工帶來活潑生動的互動教學。透過「Find the Shape」猜圖遊戲與小組繪畫創作，孩子們在輕鬆愉快的氣氛中，自然而然地開口說英文。

Working side by side with volunteers from other countries, Renfeng students grew more confident speaking English — and made new international friends along the way.

和來自不同國家的志工一起活動，仁豐的孩子們不僅更有自信開口說英語，也交到了跨國好朋友。
