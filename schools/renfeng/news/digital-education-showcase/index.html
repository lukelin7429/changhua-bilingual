Embracing the Future of Learning

「擁抱學習的未來」數位教育創新成果發表會 仁豐國小展現學生創新與表達力

Students from Renfeng Elementary School recently took part in the "Embracing the Future of Learning" Digital Education Innovation Showcase. At the event, students presented their learning projects — from AI picture book creation to robot storytelling.

仁豐國小學生日前參加「擁抱學習的未來」數位教育創新成果發表會。現場學生們親自發表AI繪本創作與機器人說故事等數位學習成果。

Confidently demonstrating their work to visitors and guests, students showed off strong digital skills while also building their public speaking and presentation confidence.

孩子們自信地向來賓展示自己的作品，不僅展現了良好的數位素養，也大大提升了口語表達力與自信心。
