Strong Parent Participation in Parenting Education Seminar

親職教育講座迴響熱烈 家長踴躍出席做最佳教育合夥人

Renfeng Elementary School recently held a Parenting Education Seminar on digital-age parenting and hands-on family activities. Parents turned out in strong numbers, asking questions and joining hands-on workshops together with their children.

仁豐國小日前舉辦親職教育講座，主題涵蓋網路世代教養與親子手作體驗。許多家長踴躍出席，不但認真提問，也和孩子一起參與手作活動。

Through this sharing and parent-child interaction, the seminar brought families and the school closer together — showing that parents are truly the school's best partners in education.

透過這次分享與親子互動，講座拉近了家庭與學校的距離，展現家長正是學校最好的教育合夥人。
