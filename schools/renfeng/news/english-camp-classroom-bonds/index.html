Growing Enthusiasm in Class

上課更有精神！仁豐學童與海外青年志工互動熱絡

As the 2026 Overseas Youth English Camp continued, Renfeng students grew more and more excited for class each day, and their conversations with the overseas volunteers became warmer and more natural.

隨著「2026海外青年英語營」的進行，仁豐的孩子們上課越來越有精神，和海外志工的互動也越來越自然、越來越熱絡。

In group worksheets and fun digital lessons — like learning the simple sentence "I like hamburgers" — students joined in eagerly and raised their hands again and again. These close, friendly moments helped students speak English more happily and confidently.

不管是小組共同完成學習單，還是像「I like hamburgers」這種簡單句型的互動數位教學，學生們都踴躍參與、搶著舉手發言。這樣親密的交流，讓孩子們能更快樂、更有自信地用英語表達自己。
