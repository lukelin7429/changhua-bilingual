Little Guests in the Principal's Office

校長室裡的小客人：親手製作 Oreo 冰炫風，消暑又過癮

Renfeng students recently became special guests in the principal's office for a cool, sweet summer treat. Principal Chen welcomed the students himself and hosted a fun DIY Oreo McFlurry activity.

仁豐國小的學生日前成為校長室的特別小客人，體驗了一場清涼又美味的夏日活動。陳志盛校長親自接待，並舉辦了DIY Oreo冰炫風的趣味教學。

Students crushed Oreo cookies and mixed them into vanilla ice cream to create their own personal dessert — a perfect way to beat the summer heat! Everyone enjoyed it so much that they even got to make a second cup, with smiles all around.

學生們動手把Oreo餅乾壓碎，拌入香草冰淇淋，做出屬於自己的特製點心——在炎炎夏日裡格外消暑！大家都吃得非常開心，甚至還能再做一杯，現場笑聲不斷。

Thank you to the school's Counseling Office for planning this thoughtful, refreshing event.

這次活動要特別感謝輔導室的用心規劃。
