I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Chih-Sheng Chen is the principal of Renfeng Elementary School, located in Tianwei, a beautiful township in Changhua County. At Renfeng, we believe every child has the potential to shine. We focus on reading, creativity, and critical thinking to help students grow and succeed. Our goal is to create a caring and inspiring learning environment where students love to learn and explore new ideas.

陳志盛校長現任彰化縣田尾鄉仁豐國小校長。在仁豐，我們相信每個孩子都有發光發熱的潛能。我們著重閱讀、創造力與思辨力，幫助學生成長茁壯、邁向成功。我們的目標是打造一個充滿關懷與啟發的學習環境，讓學生樂於學習、勇於探索新想法。

I'm excited to welcome you to our school, and I look forward to working together to help our students reach their full potential!

我很開心能歡迎您來到我們學校，也期待與大家攜手合作，幫助每一位學生發揮全部的潛能！

Philosophy I · 理念一
Give every student rich, varied opportunities to learn.
提供學生多元學習機會
Philosophy II · 理念二
Let every student experience real success along the way.
讓學生擁有成功的經驗
Philosophy III · 理念三
Help every child spread confident wings and fly freely.
讓每個學生展開自信的翅膀，自在飛翔
"Spread your confident wings, and fly freely."
提供學生多元學習機會與成功的經驗，讓每個學生都能「展開自信的翅膀，自在飛翔」。 — 陳志盛校長的辦學理念
