Digital Reading: Charting a Course Through the Galaxy of Knowledge

暑期營 · 數位閱讀篇 · 航向知識新銀河

Reading isn't just about turning paper pages anymore — in the digital age, it's a key skill for navigating the future. This summer, Tsunshang led students on a learning adventure that spans the universe, planting "dual reading" literacy deep into campus life.

閱讀不只是翻閱紙本，更是在數位浪潮中航向未來的關鍵能力！今年暑假，村上國小帶領孩子們展開一場跨越宇宙的學習探險，將「雙閱讀」素養深深扎根在校園中。

In digital reading class, the young "space cadets" worked step by step through their exploration: setting precise keywords to pinpoint their target in the vast online universe; browsing mountains of information to judge what's true, filter what matters, and build sharp information-monitoring instincts; and confidently turning what they learned about space science into clear, organized ideas to present to the class.

在數位閱讀課堂上，小太空人們循序漸進地展開探索歷程：精準搜尋——學習設定關鍵字，在浩瀚的網路世界中精準定位目標；資料擷取與整合——瀏覽海量資訊，判斷真偽、去蕪存菁，培養敏銳的訊息監控能力；自信發表分享——將吸收的太空科學知識，轉化為條理分明的觀點並登台表達。

The most moving moments in class were the students' bright, focused eyes glued to the screen, deep in discussion and helping one another along the way. From deep reading on paper to digital inquiry, Tsunshang is helping every child fall in love with reading, learn to learn on their own, and build the lifelong learning skills they'll need for the future.

課堂上最令人感動的畫面，莫過於孩子們眼神發亮、專注盯著螢幕，熱烈討論問題與互助合作的模樣。從紙本深讀到數位探究，村上陪伴每個孩子愛上閱讀、學會自主學習，裝備迎向未來的終身學習力！
