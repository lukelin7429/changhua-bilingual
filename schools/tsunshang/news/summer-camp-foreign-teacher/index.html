Sing, Move, Speak: A Week of All-English Fun

暑期營 · 外師全英篇 · 唱唱跳跳說英文

Summer break should be full of joyful, meaningful learning — and Tsunshang's five-week summer English camp kept the fun going all season long!

孩子的暑假，就是要玩得精彩、學得開心！為期五週的暑期營英語課熱鬧展開，幸福村上的孩子們學習不中斷。

This week, lively foreign teacher Anne led an all-English immersion class built around two themes — food-and-agriculture education and space exploration — weaving English naturally into playful lessons.

這次特別聘請活潑熱情的外師 Anne 進行全英教學，圍繞著「食農教育」與「太空探索」兩大主題，把英語自然融入趣味課程中。

There was no rote memorization here. Teacher Anne combined art projects, joyful music, and movement, guiding students to explore the world by making, singing, and dancing. Confident English conversations filled the classroom, with smiles and laughter everywhere.

沒有死記硬背的單字，Anne 老師巧妙結合了藝術創作、歡樂音樂與肢體律動，帶領孩子們在動手做、開口唱、盡情跳的沉浸式環境中探索世界。教室裡處處是自信的英語對話，孩子們的臉龐洋溢著滿滿的笑容與歡樂。

Through this hands-on, experience-based teaching, language became more than a school subject — it turned into a wonderful tool for exploring the universe and everyday life. Tsunshang is helping every child speak up bravely and grow with confidence and joy.

創新的體驗式教學，讓語言不再只是學科，而是探索浩瀚宇宙與生活的美好工具。村上國小陪伴孩子在體驗中勇敢開口、在快樂中自信成長。
