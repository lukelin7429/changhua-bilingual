Building Together: A Week of STEAM Discovery

暑期營 · STEAM篇 · 動手做，一起學

Tsunshang's five-week, full-day summer camp is packed with hands-on learning. In this week's STEAM lessons, students explored their own interests while discovering the world around them.

村上為期五週的整日暑期營課程豐富紮實，在STEAM課程中，孩子學習找尋自己的興趣及探索外在世界的能力。

Piece by piece, students used building blocks to design and construct their own creations, combining hands-on focus with careful thinking. Working in teams, they assembled larger structures together — a scene that was genuinely moving to watch.

每位孩子透過積木組合，手腦並用、專注學習完成作品，更結合團隊的互助合作，組裝機關王組件成就大作品，這樣的學習場景，看了令人感動。

STEAM stands for Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics — five fields considered essential skills for future talent. Cross-disciplinary learning like this helps children build problem-solving abilities, along with creativity and curiosity.

STEAM（科學、科技、工程、藝術、數學，五大領域學科的縮寫）是未來人才必備之核心技能與素養。跨領域的學習，除了可以幫助孩子們培養解決問題的能力外，也培養他們的創造力與好奇心。
