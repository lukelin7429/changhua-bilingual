II.

Music & Performing Arts

音樂與表演藝術

從笛聲、鼓聲到才藝發表會——村上國小的舞台上總是充滿音樂。

WORD 001
Chinese fluten.
笛子
The Chinese flute is made of bamboo.
笛子是由竹子做成的。
The musician performs a melody on his Chinese flute.
這位音樂家用笛子演奏樂曲。
WORD 002
drumn.
鼓
The students practice playing their drums.
同學們正在練習打鼓。
Playing the drum is a popular activity in Taiwan.
在台灣，打鼓是非常普遍的活動。
WORD 003
talent shown.
才藝表演
A talent show is when students perform their special skills for others to enjoy.
才藝表演是學生表演特殊才藝給別人欣賞的機會。
During the talent show, students play different musical instruments.
在這場才藝表演中，同學們演奏不同的樂器。
WORD 004
English songn.
英語歌
Singing English songs is a great way to practice English.
唱英文歌是練習英文很棒的方式。
Students sing English songs while moving their bodies to the rhythm.
同學們唱英文歌時，身體隨著節奏擺動。
WORD 005
performv.
表演
The students are performing on stage and singing songs in English to celebrate Mother's Day.
學生們在舞台上表演，用英語演唱歌曲來慶祝母親節。
Performing music on Mother's Day is a great way to show your mom how much you appreciate her.
在母親節表演音樂是向媽媽表達您對她的感激之情的好方法。
III.

Health & Sports

健康與運動

從洗手到越野賽跑——村上學生的健康與體能，從日常小事做起。

WORD 006
wash your handsv.
洗手
It's important to wash your hands after using the bathroom.
上完廁所後洗手是很重要的。
Make sure to wash your hands with soap and water before eating.
確定在吃東西之前要用香皂和水來洗手。
WORD 007
vaccinen.
疫苗
The students are getting their Covid-19 vaccines.
學生們正在接種 Covid-19 疫苗。
Many healthcare workers came to the school to give students their vaccines.
許多醫護人員來到學校為學生接種疫苗。
WORD 008
warm-upn.
暖身
The students do warm-ups to get ready to run.
學生們做熱身準備跑步。
Warm-ups loosen the muscles and reduce the chance of injuries.
熱身可以放鬆肌肉並減少受傷的機會。
WORD 009
cross country runningn.
越野賽跑
Cross country running is a long distance sport.
越野賽跑是一項長距離的運動。
The students are taking part in a cross country running event.
學生們正在參加越野跑步比賽。
IV.

School Life & Community

校園生活與人物

從老師的專業進修到畢業典禮——村上校園裡的大小事，都能用英文說。

WORD 010
professional developmentn.
專業進修
In Taiwan, all schools have professional development sessions for teachers.
台灣所有學校都有老師專業進修的課程。
Teachers learn new skills and strategies during their professional development sessions.
老師在專業進修課程中學習新技能與教學策略。
WORD 011
report the newsv.
報導新聞
The school has a club where students can practice reporting the news.
學校有一個社團，學生可以在那裡練習報導新聞。
When the students report the news in front of their classmates, they can practice their public speaking skills.
當學生們在同學面前報導新聞時，他們可以練習他們的公眾演講技巧。
WORD 012
gearn.
齒輪
The teacher is explaining how gears work.
這位老師正在講解齒輪運作的道理。
The participants have to assemble parts to create a machine with gears.
參與者必須組合零件做成有齒輪的機器。
WORD 013
sign languagen.
手語
The volunteers are teaching the students Taiwanese sign language.
志工們正在教學生台灣的手語。
The students practice Taiwanese sign language with a song and dance.
同學們用歌曲和舞蹈來練習台灣的手語。
WORD 014
graduationn.
畢業典禮
A graduation is a ceremony for students who have completed the highest grade level at their school.
畢業典禮是學生在學校完成最高年級學業時的一個儀式。
At a graduation, students often receive awards, get their diplomas, and give speeches.
在畢業典禮中，學生通常會得到獎勵、領取畢業證書，並且有畢業生代表致詞。
WORD 015
presentationn.
報告
The school principal is giving a presentation in the auditorium.
校長正在禮堂裡做報告。
The students listen to his presentation carefully.
學生們認真聆聽他的報告內容。
