I.

A Century of Excellence and Innovation

百年卓越與創新

Over a century of tradition, carried forward by ocarina, taiko drums, and a love of reading.

超過一世紀的教育傳統，由陶笛聲、太鼓聲與滿滿的書香延續下去。

Established in 1922, Tsunshang Elementary School in Dacun Township, Changhua County, stands as a beacon of education with a proud history that spans over a century. Today the school is home to approximately 339 eager learners, reflecting a blend of rich tradition and innovative learning methods.

成立於1922年的彰化縣大村鄉村上國小，代表著超過一世紀的優越教育史。目前，我們的學校擁有約339名學生，結合了豐富傳統和創新方法。

Tsunshang Elementary is known for offering diverse, free specialty courses to its students. Our ocarina and taiko drum courses, in particular, have garnered wide attention — the school's top-tier ocarina and taiko drum teams have consistently impressed audiences, earning accolades and performance invitations from many sectors.

村上國小為學生提供多樣化的免費特色課程。特別是我們的陶笛和太鼓課程，受到了廣大的關注。學校擁有卓越的陶笛和太鼓隊伍，持續獲得各界的讚譽和表演邀請。

Beyond music, Tsunshang has made notable strides in academic life. Our commitment to promoting reading and bilingual education has borne fruit — the campus's reading corner has become a well-loved spot, known for its inviting ambiance and rich collection. In recent years the school has scaled greater heights: students and teachers alike were honored with the prestigious Platinum Award at the Cyberfair Competition, and the school was recognized as an outstanding school for promoting reading in Changhua County.

除了音樂，我們在學術領域也取得了顯著的進步。我們致力於推廣閱讀和雙語教育，校園裡的共讀站已成為人們喜愛的閱讀角落，以其宜人氛圍和豐富館藏聞名。近年來，我們的學生和教師的努力在各個平台上都得到了認可——在網博競賽中榮獲白金獎，並獲選為彰化縣推廣閱讀特優學校。

"We believe in nurturing talent, fostering creativity, and building future leaders."
我們相信培養人才、促進創意，並培育未來的領袖 — 邀請您加入村上國小的旅程。
Tradition meets play
傳統與童趣並存

From the century-old lotus garden to the playful "Tsun Shang Airlines" mural along our corridors, Tsunshang blends deep-rooted heritage with everyday delight.

從百年歷史的蓮鄉蓮苑，到走廊上童趣十足的「村上航空」彩繪牆，村上國小把深厚的歷史底蘊與生活中的巧思融合在一起。

II.

Special Courses & Achievements

特色課程與顯著成就

Music, reading, and recognition — the pillars of everyday life at Tsunshang.

音樂、閱讀與榮譽，是村上國小日常生活的三大支柱。

Ocarina Team
陶笛隊

A free specialty course open to every student, nurturing a top-tier ocarina team that has earned performance invitations from many sectors.

免費開放全體學生選修的特色課程，培育出屢獲各界演出邀約的頂尖陶笛隊伍。

Taiko Drum Team
太鼓隊

Alongside ocarina, our taiko drum course and team have impressed audiences again and again, becoming one of the school's signature performing arts.

與陶笛並列的太鼓課程與隊伍，屢次驚豔觀眾，是學校最具代表性的表演藝術之一。

Reading Corner
共讀站

A campus reading corner with an inviting ambiance and a rich collection — a favorite spot where bilingual reading habits take root.

氛圍宜人、館藏豐富的校園共讀站，是培養雙語閱讀習慣的最佳去處。

Award-Winning Achievements
競賽榮譽

Platinum Award at the Cyberfair Competition, and recognition as an outstanding school for promoting reading in Changhua County.

榮獲網博競賽白金獎，並獲選為彰化縣推廣閱讀特優學校。

"Nurturing talent, celebrating achievement, growing together."
培養人才、慶祝成就，與孩子們一起成長。
