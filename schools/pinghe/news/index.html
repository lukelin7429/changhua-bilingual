Bilingual Learning · 雙語活動

Earth Day Becomes a Bilingual Adventure世界地球日雙語闖關活動

Apr 8, 2025 · 2025 年 4 月 8 日

Pinghe celebrated Children's Day with a campus-wide Earth Day challenge. Students moved through club experiences, local-language stations, SDGs tasks, and English 3R missions: Reduce Energy Use, Reuse Water, and Recycle Waste. The activity connected environmental action, bilingual learning, and local culture in one joyful school day.

平和在兒童節舉辦世界地球日雙語闖關，結合社團體驗、本土語、SDGs 永續議題與英語 3R 關卡，讓孩子在遊戲中練習環保行動與雙語表達。

Hockey · 曲棍球

Pinghe Hockey Wins Double Championships全國協會盃男甲、女甲雙料冠軍

Mar 17, 2026 · 2026 年 3 月 17 日

Pinghe's hockey team brought home a remarkable set of results at the national association cup: championships in both the boys' A and girls' A divisions, plus podium finishes in boys' B, girls' B, and mixed-team events. The school credited the athletes' daily morning and after-school practice, the coaches' guidance, and strong support from Principal Wang Li-hui, families, and teachers.

平和曲棍球隊在 115 年全國協會盃曲棍球錦標賽勇奪男甲、女甲冠軍，並於男乙、女甲 B 隊與混合組取得佳績。這份榮耀來自孩子長期練習、教練指導與親師生支持。

Dance · 舞蹈班

Dance Students Shine on the National Stage全國學生舞蹈比賽特優與優等

Mar 4, 2026 · 2026 年 3 月 4 日

Pinghe's dance program earned national recognition in the 114 school-year National Student Dance Competition. Class 608 received the highest distinction in modern dance, while Class 509 earned an excellent rating in Chinese classical dance. The honor reflects patient training, artistic discipline, family support, and the confidence students build through performance.

平和舞蹈班於 114 學年度全國學生舞蹈比賽中，608 班現代舞榮獲特優，509 班中國古典舞榮獲優等，展現長期訓練與藝術教育成果。

Athletics · 田徑

Track Team Brings Home Citywide Honors彰化市運動大會多項佳績

Jun 2, 2026 · 2026 年 6 月 2 日

At the 115 Changhua City Sports Meet, Pinghe students sprinted, jumped, threw, and relayed their way to a long list of awards. The girls' team earned second place overall in elementary-school track and field, and the boys' team placed third overall. The results show more than speed: they show perseverance, teamwork, and daily practice becoming visible on the track.

平和田徑隊參加 115 年彰化市運動大會，在短跑、跳遠、鉛球與接力項目獲得多項佳績，女生組田徑總錦標第二名、男生組第三名。

Music · 音樂教育

Choir and Recorder Fill the Campus with Sound校內合唱與直笛競賽

May 21, 2026 · 2026 年 5 月 21 日

Pinghe's school music competition gave students a chance to practice listening, teamwork, rhythm, and public performance. Third- and fourth-grade classes joined the choir competition, while fifth-grade students performed on recorder. The school thanked music teachers and classroom teachers for guiding students through the process.

平和舉辦 114 學年度校內音樂競賽，中年級合唱與五年級直笛讓校園充滿歌聲與笛聲，也讓孩子在音樂中練習合作與自信。

New Students · 新生入學

New Students Begin with the Five Strengths新生報到結合校本課程與特色校隊

Apr 14, 2026 · 2026 年 4 月 14 日

Pinghe welcomed incoming first graders with a lively registration event built around the school's curriculum and signature teams. Instead of simply completing paperwork, children experienced learning stations, school traditions, and the atmosphere of a campus ready to welcome them into the Pinghe community.

平和 115 學年度新生報到結合校本課程與特色校隊，設計新生學習體驗闖關，讓孩子一入學就感受校園特色與學習期待。
