II.

Video Transcript

影片文字稿

Welcome to Pinghe Elementary School. We're a special school located near the Changhua train station.

歡迎來到平和國小。我們是一所很特別的學校，位於彰化火車站的附近。

At Pinghe, we teach five important things.

在平和，我們教導五件重要的事情。

💻
T · Technology
科技

First, we focus on technology to prepare our students for a world with lots of technology.首先，我們專注於科技(T)，以準備我們的學生進入一個充滿科技的世界。

📚
R · Reading
閱讀

Second, we love reading. Reading helps our students learn and think better.其次，我們熱愛閱讀(R)。閱讀幫助我們的學生提升學習和思考能力。

🎭
A · Art
藝術

Third, we emphasize art; music and dance help our students be creative and enjoy beauty.第三，我們強調藝術(A)；音樂和舞蹈幫助我們的學生發揮創造力並欣賞美的事物。

🌏
I · Internationalism
國際觀

Fourth, we teach internationalism; we want our students to understand people from all over the world.第四，我們教導國際觀(I)；我們希望我們的學生了解世界各地的人民。

🌱
N · Nature
在地自然

Finally, we teach about nature in our town; we want our students to care for the environment and be good citizens.最後，我們教導我們當地的自然生態(N)；我們希望我們的學生關心環境並成為良好的公民。

We also have fun clubs like sports, music, computers, and games. These clubs let students do what they love and learn in fun ways.

我們還有好玩的社團，如體育、音樂、電腦和遊戲等等。這些社團讓學生做他們喜愛的事情並以有趣的方式學習。

Come check out our website to learn more about Pinghe Elementary School and how we help students become smart, kind, and ready for the future.

查看我們的網站，了解更多關於平和國小的訊息，以及我們如何幫助學生變得聰明、善良並為未來做好準備。

"We can't wait to show you why our school is a great place to learn and grow."
我們迫不及待想向您展示我們的學校為什麼是一個學習和成長的好地方。
