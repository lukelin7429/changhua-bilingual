II.

Campus Life

校園生活

翹翹板、導護、打掃——平和最日常的校園時刻。

WORD 001
seesawn.
翹翹板
A seesaw is a long plank that goes up and down when two people sit on opposite ends.
Seesaw（翹翹板）是一塊長板，兩人分坐兩端、一上一下地玩。
Pinghe students take turns riding the seesaw together at recess.
平和的學生下課時輪流一起玩翹翹板。
WORD 002
crossing guardn.
導護
A crossing guard is a person who helps students cross the street safely.
Crossing guard（導護）是幫助學生安全過馬路的人。
Every morning, a crossing guard stands at Pinghe's gate to keep students safe.
每天早上，導護老師都會站在平和的校門口守護學生安全。
WORD 003
shotn.
打疫苗
A shot is an injection that a nurse or doctor gives to keep you healthy.
Shot（打疫苗）是護理師或醫生為了健康而施打的針劑。
Pinghe students line up calmly to get their shot at the school health center.
平和的學生在健康中心排隊，勇敢地打疫苗。
WORD 004
sweepv.
打掃
To sweep means to clean a floor by pushing dirt away with a broom.
Sweep（打掃）是指用掃把把地板上的灰塵推開、清理乾淨。
Pinghe students sweep their classroom together every afternoon.
平和的學生每天下午一起打掃教室。
WORD 005
school lunchn.
學校午餐
School lunch is the meal that students eat together at school.
School lunch（學校午餐）是學生們在學校一起吃的餐點。
Pinghe students enjoy a warm school lunch in the classroom every day.
平和的學生每天都在教室裡享用溫熱的學校午餐。
WORD 006
servev.
打菜、盛菜
To serve means to give food to someone during a meal.
Serve（打菜、盛菜）是指用餐時把食物分給大家。
Pinghe students take turns to serve lunch to their classmates.
平和的學生輪流幫同學打菜。
WORD 007
leaf blowern.
吹葉機
A leaf blower is a machine that blows fallen leaves into a pile.
Leaf blower（吹葉機）是把落葉吹成一堆的機器。
The custodian uses a leaf blower to keep Pinghe's palm-lined walkway tidy.
工友用吹葉機讓平和的椰林步道保持整潔。
III.

Sports & Movement

體育與活動

曲棍球、跳高、飛盤躲避球——平和孩子在球場上學英文。

WORD 008
hockeyn.
曲棍球
Hockey is a team sport where players use a curved stick to hit a ball into a goal.
Hockey（曲棍球）是球員用彎曲球棍把球打進球門的團體運動。
Pinghe's hockey team has won championships at the national level.
平和的曲棍球隊曾在全國賽事中奪冠。
WORD 009
high jumpn.
跳高
The high jump is a track and field event where athletes jump over a raised bar.
High jump（跳高）是田徑項目之一，選手要跳過升高的橫桿。
Pinghe students practice the high jump on the school field before track meets.
平和的學生在運動會前於操場練習跳高。
WORD 010
competev.
競爭
To compete means to try to win against others in a game or contest.
Compete（競爭）是指在比賽中努力想要贏過對手。
Pinghe's athletes compete bravely at the Changhua City Sports Meet every year.
平和的運動選手每年都在彰化市運動大會上勇敢競爭。
WORD 011
frisbee dodgeballn.
飛盤躲避球
Frisbee dodgeball is a game where players throw a flying disc to try to tag their opponents.
Frisbee dodgeball（飛盤躲避球）是玩家互丟飛盤、設法擊中對手的遊戲。
Pinghe students play frisbee dodgeball together during PE class.
平和的學生在體育課一起玩飛盤躲避球。
WORD 012
basketballn.
籃球
Basketball is a sport where two teams try to shoot a ball through a hoop.
Basketball（籃球）是兩隊互相投籃、把球投進籃框的運動。
Pinghe students shoot hoops on the school's basketball court at recess.
平和的學生下課時在校內籃球場投籃。
WORD 013
volleyballn.
排球
Volleyball is a sport where players hit a ball back and forth over a net.
Volleyball（排球）是球員隔著球網互相擊球的運動。
Pinghe students practice serving and setting during volleyball club.
平和的學生在排球社團練習發球與舉球。
WORD 014
gamen.
遊戲
A game is a fun activity played by following a set of rules.
Game（遊戲）是依照規則進行的有趣活動。
Pinghe students invent new games together on the playground.
平和的學生在遊戲場一起發明新遊戲。
IV.

Learning in Class

課堂學習

電腦課、學習單、詞彙競賽——平和的課堂日常。

WORD 015
professional developmentn.
專業進修
Professional development is training that helps teachers improve their skills.
Professional development（專業進修）是幫助老師精進教學能力的訓練。
Pinghe teachers attend professional development workshops to learn new teaching methods.
平和的老師參加專業進修工作坊，學習新的教學方法。
WORD 016
computer classn.
電腦課
Computer class is a lesson where students learn to use computers and technology.
Computer class（電腦課）是學生學習使用電腦與科技的課程。
Pinghe students learn to type and code during computer class.
平和的學生在電腦課學習打字與寫程式。
WORD 017
technologyn.
科技
Technology is the use of tools, machines, and science to solve problems.
Technology（科技）是運用工具、機器與科學來解決問題的方法。
Pinghe students use technology to make presentations and creative projects.
平和的學生運用科技製作簡報與創意作品。
WORD 018
worksheetn.
學習單
A worksheet is a piece of paper with questions or exercises for students to complete.
Worksheet（學習單）是印有題目或練習、供學生完成的紙張。
Pinghe students fill in an English worksheet to practice new vocabulary.
平和的學生填寫英文學習單來練習新單字。
WORD 019
vocabulary contestn.
詞彙競賽
A vocabulary contest is a competition where students test how many words they know.
Vocabulary contest（詞彙競賽）是測驗學生詞彙量的競賽活動。
Pinghe students challenge each other in a lively vocabulary contest.
平和的學生在熱鬧的詞彙競賽中互相挑戰。
WORD 020
ordern.
順序
Order means the correct sequence in which things should happen or be arranged.
Order（順序）是指事情該發生或排列的正確次序。
Pinghe students line up in order before walking to the auditorium.
平和的學生依照順序排隊，走向活動中心。
V.

Arts & Performance

藝術與表演

排練、直笛、音樂課——平和的藝術教育也是英文課。

WORD 021
rehearsev.
排練
To rehearse means to practice a performance before showing it to an audience.
Rehearse（排練）是指在正式演出前先練習。
Pinghe's dance students rehearse every day before their big competition.
平和的舞蹈班學生在大型比賽前每天排練。
WORD 022
recordern.
直笛
A recorder is a simple wind instrument that students blow into and play with their fingers.
Recorder（直笛）是學生用嘴吹、用手指按孔演奏的簡單管樂器。
Pinghe's fifth graders perform on the recorder during the school music competition.
平和的五年級學生在校內音樂競賽中演奏直笛。
WORD 023
music classn.
音樂課
Music class is a lesson where students sing, play instruments, and learn about music.
Music class（音樂課）是學生唱歌、演奏樂器與學習音樂知識的課程。
Pinghe students sing together and learn new songs in music class.
平和的學生在音樂課一起唱歌、學習新歌曲。
WORD 024
presentationn.
簡報
A presentation is a talk that shares information, often with slides or pictures.
Presentation（簡報）是搭配投影片或圖片、向大家分享資訊的報告。
Pinghe students practice public speaking by giving a presentation in class.
平和的學生透過在課堂上做簡報練習公開演說。
VI.

Culture, Events & Trips

文化、活動與旅行

布袋戲、母親節、校外旅行——平和孩子在文化與活動中學英文。

WORD 025
hand puppetn.
布袋戲
A hand puppet is a doll-like character worn on the hand and moved by the fingers.
Hand puppet（布袋戲）是套在手上、用手指操控的偶戲人偶。
Pinghe students learn about Taiwanese culture by watching a traditional hand puppet show.
平和的學生透過觀賞傳統布袋戲認識台灣文化。
WORD 026
Mother's Dayn.
母親節
Mother's Day is a special day when families celebrate and thank their mothers.
Mother's Day（母親節）是家人慶祝、感謝媽媽的特別節日。
Pinghe students make handmade cards to celebrate Mother's Day.
平和的學生親手製作卡片慶祝母親節。
WORD 027
tripn.
旅行、行程
A trip is a journey to a place, often for learning or fun.
Trip（旅行、行程）是前往某個地方的旅程，通常是為了學習或玩樂。
Pinghe students pack their bags for an exciting class trip.
平和的學生為了刺激的班級校外旅行整理行李。
WORD 028
popsicle stickn.
冰棒棍
A popsicle stick is a small flat wooden stick, often reused for craft projects.
Popsicle stick（冰棒棍）是扁平的小木棍，常被重複利用做手工藝。
Pinghe students build a model using colorful popsicle sticks in art class.
平和的學生在美術課用彩色冰棒棍做模型。
WORD 029
check inv.
辦理登機手續
To check in means to officially register for a flight before boarding.
Check in（辦理登機手續）是指登機前正式辦理報到的手續。
Pinghe students practice how to check in at the airport before their trip.
平和的學生在旅行前練習如何在機場辦理登機手續。
WORD 030
luggagen.
行李
Luggage is the bags and suitcases people carry when they travel.
Luggage（行李）是旅行時攜帶的包包與行李箱。
Pinghe students pack their luggage carefully before a school trip.
平和的學生在校外旅行前仔細打包行李。
