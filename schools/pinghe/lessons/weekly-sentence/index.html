I.

Watch & Learn

觀看短劇，一起學一句英文！

Pinghe's own teachers write, act, and star in every episode.

每一集都是平和老師自己編劇、自己演出的短劇，用輕鬆幽默的方式教一句實用英文。每週一句，馬上就能現學現用。

EPISODE 01
Break a leg
祝你好運
Say "Break a leg" to wish someone good luck before a performance or a big moment — it has nothing to do with actual legs!
在表演或重要時刻前，跟朋友說「Break a leg」祝他好運——跟真的腳一點關係都沒有！
EPISODE 02
Long time no see
好久不見
"Long time no see" is a casual greeting for when you run into someone you haven't seen in a while.
巧遇好久沒見的朋友時，用「Long time no see」打招呼，輕鬆又自然。
EPISODE 03
Are you sure?
你確定嗎？
Ask "Are you sure?" when you want to double-check someone's decision or answer.
想跟對方再次確認決定或答案時，就問一句「Are you sure?」。
EPISODE 04
What is that?
那是什麼？
Point and ask "What is that?" when you see something unfamiliar and want to know what it is.
看到不認識的東西時，指著它問一句「What is that?」。
EPISODE 05
I'm the best
我是最棒的
A Children's Day special! Say "I'm the best" with a big confident smile to celebrate yourself.
兒童節特輯！用自信又開心的笑容說一句「I'm the best」，好好為自己加油打氣。
EPISODE 06
We will see
讓我們繼續看下去
Say "We will see" when the outcome isn't certain yet and everyone will just have to wait and find out.
結果還不確定時，說一句「We will see」，表示「讓我們繼續看下去」。
EPISODE 07
I see
我理解了
A short, simple way to show you understand what someone just explained: "I see."
聽懂對方剛剛說的話時，簡短回應一句「I see」就可以了。
EPISODE 08
No pain no gain
沒有痛苦便不會成長
Use "No pain, no gain" to encourage someone — or yourself — that hard work and effort lead to real progress.
用「No pain, no gain」鼓勵自己或別人：辛苦付出，才能真正進步。
EPISODE 09
So far so good
目前為止還行
When someone asks how things are going and everything is fine so far, answer "So far so good."
別人問近況如何，而目前一切都還順利時，就回答一句「So far so good」。
EPISODE 10
Spill the beans
洩漏秘密
Use the idiom "spill the beans" when someone accidentally — or on purpose — reveals a secret.
用「spill the beans」這個慣用語，形容有人不小心（或故意）說出了秘密。
EPISODE 11
It's pouring out
雨下很大欸
Say "It's pouring out" when you look outside and the rain is really coming down hard.
看到窗外下著傾盆大雨時，就可以驚呼一句「It's pouring out」。
