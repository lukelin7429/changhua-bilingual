Around School
校園生活 · 3 words
WORD 01
school band (n)
學校樂隊
The school band provides music for the morning assembly.
學校樂隊為朝會儀式演奏音樂。
Students in the school band play different instruments.
學校樂隊的學生演奏不同的樂器。
WORD 02
award (n)
獎
Each student receives an award.
每個學生都接受頒獎。
The principal gives each student their award.
校長頒發獎項給每位學生。
WORD 03
drill (n)
訓練
The students are practicing passing the ball during a handball drill.
學生們在手球訓練練習傳球。
The drill focuses on footwork and speed.
這項訓練的重點是步法和速度。

Safety & Nature
防災與自然 · 2 words
WORD 04
earthquake drill
地震演習
The students cover their heads during the earthquake drill.
在地震防災演練中，學生們用東西遮蓋頭部。
They go outside to learn more about an earthquake drill.
他們到教室外學習更多地震防災演練的知識。
WORD 05
leaf / leaves (n)
樹葉
Students study each leaf's shape and size to learn more about the plants.
學生研究每片葉子的形狀和大小，以便更多地了解植物。
Students observe and draw the leaves closely to find their parts, like the veins and edges.
學生仔細觀察並畫出葉子，找到它們的部分，如葉脈和邊緣。
