Lessons at Pusin

Ways to Learn

用英語，走進校園的每一天

Real school, real words.

每支影片、每份教材，都取材自埔心真實的校園生活，或彰化雙語學校的共用資源。

5 Videos · 2 Themes
Course 01
Word of the Day
每日一字

Bilingual videos filmed around the Pusin campus — from the school band and prize-giving to a handball drill, an earthquake drill and a leaf study. Each word comes with two example sentences taken straight from the video.在埔心校園實地拍攝的雙語影片——從學校樂隊、頒獎，到手球訓練、地震演練與樹葉觀察。每個單字都附兩句取自影片的例句。

Start Learning →
30 Episodes · For Teachers
Course 02
Classroom English for Teachers
給老師的課室英語

Everyday classroom phrases for teachers, taught by Sarah Thomas across 10 topics and 30 episodes — from greetings and roll call to exam instructions. A shared resource across Changhua's bilingual schools.給老師使用的課堂英語，由 Sarah Thomas 老師示範教學，共 10 大主題、30 支影片——從問候點名到考試指令。這是彰化雙語學校的共用教材。

Start Learning →
13 Episodes · By Office
Course 03
Bilingual Announcements
校園英語廣播

"Broadcast English" — thirteen short episodes in which each office and section at Pusin introduces, in English, the announcements it makes over the school PA system, from the curriculum section to the counselling room.「廣播英語」——13 支短片，由埔心各處室、各組用英語介紹平時透過校園廣播宣布的內容，從教務處教學組到輔導室資料組。
