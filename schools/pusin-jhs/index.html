I.

Welcome to Pusin

歡迎來到埔心國中

A holistic education, in a loving and friendly place — where every child grows in body, mind, spirit and intellect.

在有愛的友善環境裡，推動全人教育，讓每個孩子的身、心、靈、腦都健康成長。

Pusin Junior High School places a strong emphasis on holistic education, aiming to nurture “well-rounded, diligent learners.” Our vision is to cultivate students who excel in character, academics and leadership. Through the Seven Habits and the Jing Si Aphorisms, we promote character education, strengthen reading, and lift academic performance — helping children grow into the leaders of the future.

埔心國中重視「全人教育」，學校願景是培育「敦品勵學埔心人」。透過「七個習慣」和「靜思語」推動品格教育、閱讀能力和學力提升，在有愛的友善環境下，培育孩子身、心、靈、腦健康成長，成為未來的領導人。

We believe that every child, placed in the right position, is a talent. Alongside the core subjects of Chinese, English and Mathematics, we value literacy in the arts and in technology. A dedicated sports class and a wide range of clubs give students many stages on which to shine, and by taking part in county- and national-level competitions they meet new fields of knowledge, learn joyfully, work with others, and build abilities they carry for life.

我們相信「每一個孩子放對位置就是人才」。除了加強國、英、數主科學力，也重視藝術與科技素養；體育班與多元社團提供孩子展能的舞台，透過參加全國性、全縣性的比賽，接觸不同領域知能，快樂學習、與人合作，擁有帶著走的能力，拓展視野、提昇自信心。

Every child, placed in the right position, is a talent.
每一個孩子放對位置就是人才。

III.

What We Stand For

學校願景

Six words at the heart of everything: 敦品勵學埔心人 — well-rounded, diligent Pusin people.

一切的核心，是六個字：敦品勵學埔心人。

全人教育 · Whole Person
Body, Mind, Spirit, Intellect
在有愛的友善環境下，照顧孩子身、心、靈、腦的健康成長，而不只是分數。
品格 · Character
The Seven Habits & Jing Si Aphorisms
以「七個習慣」與「靜思語」推動品格教育，讓好習慣與好態度成為日常。
學力 · Learning
Reading, and the Core Subjects
札實推動閱讀能力與國、英、數主科學力，打好帶得走的學習基礎。
展能 · Talent
A Stage for Every Child
體育班與多元社團，加上全縣、全國性的比賽舞台，讓每個孩子找到發光的位置。

Holistic education means keeping academic rigour and character-building in balance. At Pusin we want learning to widen every path a student can take — through many subjects, the arts, technology, and time spent working alongside others — so that our young people leave us with healthy characters and the kind of abilities and habits that last a lifetime.

全人教育，是讓學術的嚴謹與品格的塑造彼此平衡。在埔心，我們希望學習能拓寬孩子的每一條路——透過各科教學、藝術、科技，以及與人合作的時間——讓學生帶著健康的人格，以及用一輩子的能力與習慣離開這裡。

IV.

Placed in the Right Position

放對位置，就是人才

A dedicated sports class, many clubs, and real competition — room for every kind of talent.

體育班、多元社團，還有真正的比賽舞台——每一種才能都有位置。

We strengthen the core subjects, but we do not stop there. Pusin runs a dedicated sports class and a wide spread of clubs, and our students step out to compete at county and national level. Along the way they meet new fields of knowledge, learn to enjoy the work, and learn to do it together — broadening their horizons and building real confidence.

我們加強主科學力，但不止於此。埔心設有體育班與多元社團，學生走出校園，參加全縣、全國性的比賽。在這個過程裡，他們接觸不同領域的知能，學會樂在其中，也學會與人合作——拓展視野，累積真正的自信。

Learn joyfully, and together
快樂學習，一起前進

A big field, a full track, courts for basketball and soccer — and clubs that run from sport to the arts. There is space here to move, to try, and to find the thing you are good at.

大操場、完整跑道、籃球與足球場，還有從運動到藝術的各式社團。在埔心，有奔跑、嘗試、找到自己專長的空間。

V.

Arts & Technology Literacy

藝術與科技素養

Hands on the tools — from paintbrush to circuit board.

動手用工具——從畫筆，到電路板。

Beyond the core subjects, Pusin students spend real time making things. Our technology centre has students designing and building — wind turbines, trusses, coding a small robot to move — while covered walkways around the campus double as a gallery for student artwork and calligraphy. Arts and technology are not extras here; they are how our students learn to see, to plan, and to solve problems.

除了主科，埔心的學生花時間真正動手做。學校的科技中心裡，孩子設計與製作——風力發電機、結構桁架、寫程式讓小車動起來——校園的風雨走廊則兼作學生美術與書法作品的展場。藝術與科技不是額外的點綴，而是孩子學會觀察、規劃、解決問題的方式。

Make it, test it, fix it
做出來、測試、再修好

In the technology centre a design starts as an idea, becomes a model, and then has to actually work — the same loop that engineers and artists live by.

在科技中心，一個設計從想法開始，變成模型，然後真的要能運作——這正是工程師與藝術家共通的循環。
