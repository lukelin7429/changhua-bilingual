Filial Socks: A Warm Gift from the Parents' Federation

孝親穿襪，溫馨傳愛：家長會聯合會的暖心禮
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

As part of the 2026 “Filial Socks” campaign run by the Changhua County Federation of School Parents' Associations, Chairperson Hsu Yu-chung led the board of directors and supervisors to visit Pusin Junior High in person.

配合彰化縣各級學校家長會聯合會 115 年舉辦的「孝親穿襪活動」，許友忠理事長帶領理監事團隊親臨埔心國中。

The federation generously donated caring activity socks, so that students could pass on their thanks and love through a simple, hands-on act. Our thanks also go to Chen Shih-wen, chairperson of the Changhua County Teachers' Union, for helping guide the event.

聯合會大方致贈愛心活動襪，讓孩子用一個簡單、親手完成的動作，把感謝與愛意傳遞出去。也特別感謝彰化縣教師工會陳時文理事長一同指導。

When a child kneels to pull a sock onto a grandparent's foot, the filial spirit and the habit of gratitude quietly begin to sprout in that child's heart.

當孩子蹲下身、親手為長輩穿上襪子，孝順與感恩的美德，就在孩子心中悄悄萌芽。

A pair of socks is a small thing. The warmth it carries is not.

一雙襪子是小事，它承載的溫暖不是。
