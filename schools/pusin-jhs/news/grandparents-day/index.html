A Cup of Tea, a Pair of Socks: Grandparents' Day at Pusin

一杯茶、一雙襪：埔心國中的祖父母節
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

For the 2026 school year, Pusin Junior High held a warm Grandparents' Day for its seventh-graders — a chance to say “thank you” to the elders in their lives with actions, not just words.

埔心國中在 115 學年度為七年級同學辦理祖父母節活動，讓孩子用行動、而不只是言語，向生命中的長輩說一聲「謝謝您」。

The students served tea, one cup at a time, and handed each grandparent a small gift. A quiet bow, a cup held in two hands — small gestures that carry a child's most sincere gratitude.

同學一杯一杯地奉茶，也為每位阿公阿嬤送上一份小禮物。一個鞠躬、雙手奉上的一杯茶——小小的動作，裝的是孩子最真摯的孝心與感謝。

We are especially grateful to the school's Parents' Association, which prepared warm socks for every seventh-grader to take home. Putting the socks on an elder's feet with your own hands carries the school's blessing all the way back to the family.

特別感謝家長聯合會貼心準備溫暖的襪子，贈送給七年級同學帶回家。親手為長輩穿上襪子，把學校滿滿的祝福與暖意帶回每一個家庭。

One pair of socks, one cup of tea, one small gift — let love set out from the campus and warm every home.

一雙襪子、一杯茶、一份小禮物，讓愛從校園出發，溫暖每一個家庭。
