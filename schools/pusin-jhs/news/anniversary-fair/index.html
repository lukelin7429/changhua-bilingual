Pusin Turns 65: Anniversary Fair and Celebrations

埔中65，允文允武：校慶園遊會暨系列活動
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Pusin Junior High is about to celebrate its 65th anniversary! The school warmly invites parents, alumni, neighbors and guests to come back to campus, see what the students have learned, and share in the school’s growth and change.

埔心國中即將迎來65週年校慶，誠摯邀請所有家長、校友、社區鄉親及各界貴賓蒞臨埔中，分享孩子們的學習成果，共同見證學校的成長與蛻變！

Date: Saturday, 12 December 2026
Time: The celebration begins at 9:10 a.m.
Place: Pusin Junior High School, Puxin Township, Changhua County

活動日期：115年12月12日（星期六）
活動時間：上午9:10正式開始
活動地點：彰化縣埔心鄉埔心國民中學

This year’s theme is “Pusin 65: Strong in Learning, Strong in Sports.” Students will put on dance, school band and clarinet performances, and mothers from the Puxin community will take the stage too. There will also be an exhibition of past yearbooks and a show of student work — plus about 30 fair booths where you can shop, snack and try new things!

今年校慶以「埔中65，允文允武」為主軸，除了孩子們精彩的舞蹈、管樂、單簧管演奏，更有埔心鄉社區媽媽們精彩表演，以及歷屆畢冊展、學生成果作品展等豐富活動。更有多達30攤園遊會攤位歡迎您前往消費、體驗喔！

Sixty-five years is a story of tradition and care passed from one generation to the next, and every child’s growth is the most precious view at Pusin. Through the celebration, the school hopes students can show their confidence, share their joy with their families, and let the whole community see the energy and character of Pusin Junior High.

65年，是一段傳承與陪伴的故事；每一位孩子的成長，都是埔中最珍貴的風景。期待透過校慶活動，讓孩子展現自信、跟家長分享喜悅，也讓社區看見埔心國中的活力與特色。

Come and join us to share the pride and joy of the anniversary! Note: A limited number of 65th anniversary souvenirs will be on sale at the fair — once they’re gone, they’re gone!

誠摯邀請您蒞臨，共享校慶的榮耀與喜悅！備註：當天在園區有限量的65週年紀念小物，數量有限，售完為止，歡迎選購喔！
