Thank You for Being Here: Teacher's Day at Pusin

師恩滿埔中・感謝有您
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

On the eve of Teacher's Day, Pusin Junior High was filled with thanks and good wishes!

教師節前夕，埔心國中校園洋溢著濃濃的感恩與祝福！

The school made a special visit to every elementary school in its district to deliver a very special kind of mail: Teacher's Day cards, handwritten by graduates of those schools who now study at Pusin. Each card carried a student's warmest memories and thanks back to the teachers who first taught them.

學校特別走訪學區各國小，將畢業生親手寫下的敬師卡片送回母校，傳遞最真摯的思念與感謝。

On campus, the Parents' Association and the Alumni Association prepared Teacher's Day cake and coffee for the teachers. The county government and the township office sent Teacher's Day gift vouchers, and the Counseling Office put together small gifts to help relieve stress — so that even on their busiest days, teachers could feel how much they are cared for.

校內家長會、校友會也貼心準備教師節蛋糕與咖啡，為老師們送上暖暖心意。縣府及公所致贈教師節禮券，輔導室更準備紓壓小禮物，讓老師在忙碌之餘感受滿滿的關懷。

From the campus to the whole school district, every good wish brings teachers and students a little closer. At Pusin and the elementary schools around it, one warm message could be heard everywhere: “Thank you, teachers — we appreciate you and we respect you.”

從校園到學區，一份份祝福串起師生情誼，也讓埔心國中及各國小處處充滿「感謝老師、敬愛老師」的溫馨氣氛。
