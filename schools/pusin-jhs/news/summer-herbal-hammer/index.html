Herbs, Mallets and a Shoulder Rub: Summer Fun Learning

夏日樂學：草藥鎚製作與「您辛苦囉」
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

In this session of the Summer Fun Learning camp, students made a herbal mallet — a small cloth pouch on a handle, filled with dried, fragrant plants like mint, mugwort and cinnamon twig.

在這場夏日樂學營裡，同學動手做「草藥鎚」——一支小布槌，裡面裝著薄荷、艾草、桂枝等曬乾的香草。

The teachers were wonderfully professional, and the children worked with real interest and care through every step — cutting, crushing, filling and tying.

老師非常專業，孩子則帶著高度的興趣，認真地完成每一個步驟——剪、搗、裝、綁。

Then came the best part: using the finished mallets to give each other a gentle massage and say, “You've worked so hard — thank you.” A small craft that helps muscles relax, and words that warm the heart.

接著是最棒的部分：用做好的槌子互相輕輕按摩，一起說聲「您辛苦囉」。一個能讓肌肉放鬆的小手作，加上一句暖到心裡的話。

Thank you to the teachers who guided every group with such care. The Summer Fun Learning camp was a hit.

謝謝用心指導每一組的師長們。夏日樂學營，真的很棒！
