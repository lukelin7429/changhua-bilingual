Unlocking Money and Skills: A Day of Discovery

解鎖金融與技能大冒險，完美賦歸！
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Students and teachers from Pusin Junior High and Luocuo Elementary had a full, exciting day of learning. In the morning they headed straight for the CTBC Wen-Hui Hall for a first taste of finance education.

埔心國中與羅厝國小師生度過超充實的一天。上午直奔中國信託文薈館，解鎖金融教育的初體驗。

In the afternoon they flew over to the Nangang Exhibition Center to watch the god-tier skills of the medal winners at the National Skills Competition — young experts who compete at the very top of dozens of trades.

下午秒飛南港展覽館，欣賞全國技能競賽神級選手的超群絕技——這些年輕高手，在數十個職種裡站上競賽的頂點。

They also played their way through the theme halls and tried a hands-on craft class. This trip to explore technology and careers was, in the students' own words, “way too much fun.”

大家也一起瘋玩主題館、體驗手作課程。這趟探索科技與職涯的旅程，用同學自己的話說，就是「太好玩啦！」

The best field trips open a door — and let students walk through to see what might be on the other side.

最好的校外教學會打開一扇門，讓學生走過去，看看另一邊有什麼。
