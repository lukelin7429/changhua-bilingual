Courtside at the BUIAC: An International Game in Kaohsiung

感恩結善緣：佛光盃國際大學籃球邀請賽
←Back to News回到新聞總覽

Our thanks go to the Fo Guang Shan Yuanlin Lecture Hall and to President Chang Li-hsin for a kind connection made on the school's behalf.

感謝佛光山員林講堂、張立馨會長與本校結善緣。

Through them, Pusin students travelled to the Kaohsiung Arena to watch the Buddhist University International Athletic Cup (BUIAC) tournament in person — an invitational that brought international college teams from Taiwan, Canada, the USA, Japan, the Philippines and beyond.

透過他們的安排，埔中學生前往高雄巨蛋，現場觀賞「佛光盃國際大學籃球邀請賽」——這場邀請賽，集結了來自臺灣、加拿大、美國、日本、菲律賓等地的大學隊伍。

The court ran hot, and young players met as friends through the game. For our spectators it was a rare sporting occasion — and a real widening of their horizons.

球場上熱血沸騰，青年以球會友。對我們的孩子來說，這是一場難得的體育盛會，也是一次視野的拓展。

Thank you for bringing our students so much to take home, and so much to feel.

謝謝您們帶給孩子們滿滿的收穫與感動。
