Principal Wu Xi-ming

I.

A Word from the Principal

校長的話

Education, to me, is a noble calling — the work of loving and helping others — and being a principal is a calling within that calling. I hold to one plain idea: to build a high-quality campus where teachers feel secure, students are dedicated, and parents are confident.

教育是一種愛人與助人的尊貴志業，校長更是志業中的志業。我期許自己以一個具體的理念自持：營造老師安心、學生用心、家長放心的優質校園。

“We can count the seeds in an apple, but we cannot know how many apples a seed will bear.” Every child here carries that same unknown, limitless potential. I ask every one of our educators to work at nurturing every child — so that each of them finds a stage on which to shine.

「我們可以數出一顆蘋果有幾粒種子，但我們無法知道一粒種子將來可以結出幾顆蘋果。」每個孩子都懷著這樣未知而無限的可能。我期許每位教育人員努力培育每一個孩子，讓每個孩子找到發光發熱的舞台。

A leader is the dealer of hope. An effective principal draws out a team of teachers who carry educational ideals, and draws out from a community the care and positive hopes that parents hold for their children. Only when education partners are closely joined can a school that is both happy and effective come true. I look forward to working alongside every colleague, family and neighbour of Pusin to make that school real.

校長是學校希望的領航員（A leader is the dealer of hope）。有效能的校長能激發一群有教育理想的教師，也能引領社區家長對子女教育的關懷與正向期許。唯有教育伙伴彼此緊密結合，一所具幸福感又有效能的學校才得以實現。願與埔心的每一位夥伴、家庭與鄰里共同努力。

II.

Leadership at a Glance

校長經歷摘要

A career in Changhua County education — the classroom, school administration, the county Education Department, and the principal's office.

一路深耕彰化縣教育：從教學現場、學校行政、縣府教育處，到校長室。

Education · 學歷
Mathematics & Science Education
國立彰化師範大學數學系學士；國立彰化師範大學科學教育研究所碩士。
Service · 服務經歷
Teacher → Director → Supervisor → Principal
臺北縣鳳鳴國中教師兼導師；彰化縣芳苑國中教師兼導師、教務組長、輔導主任、教導主任、補校主任；鹿鳴國中教務主任；彰興國中總務主任、教務主任、導師；彰化縣政府教育處學生事務與特殊教育科課程督學；和群國中校長。
Focus · 辦學重點
Teachers Secure · Students Dedicated · Parents Confident
以「老師安心、學生用心、家長放心」為核心，重視生活與品格教育、多元自主的學習力，以及學生身心健康的成長。

Principal Wu trained as a mathematician at National Changhua University of Education and went on to a master's in science education there. He began as a homeroom teacher, then held a full range of administrative roles — academic affairs, counselling, guidance and general affairs — across Fangyuan, Luming and Zhangxing Junior High Schools. He served as a curriculum supervisor in the Student Affairs and Special Education Section of the Changhua County Government Education Department, and was principal of Hequn Junior High School before coming to Pusin.

吳校長為國立彰化師範大學數學系出身，並取得該校科學教育研究所碩士。他從導師起步，歷經芳苑、鹿鳴、彰興國中教務、輔導、教導、總務等完整的學校行政歷練，曾任彰化縣政府教育處學生事務與特殊教育科課程督學，並擔任和群國中校長，之後接掌埔心國中。

IV.

A Leader Is the Dealer of Hope

校長是學校希望的領航員

You can count the seeds in an apple. You cannot count the apples in a seed.

你能數出一顆蘋果裡的種子，卻數不出一粒種子裡的蘋果。

It is the line Principal Wu returns to most often. It is a reminder that a school's job is not to predict which children will succeed, but to make sure that every one of them is given the soil, the light and the time to find out. At Pusin that means uniting teachers, families and the wider community around a single, patient task: nurturing each child until they find the stage that is theirs.

這是吳校長最常引用的一句話。它提醒我們，學校的工作不是預測哪個孩子會成功，而是確保每一個孩子都有足夠的土壤、陽光與時間去發現自己。在埔心，這意味著把老師、家庭與社區凝聚在同一件需要耐心的事情上：培育每個孩子，直到他們找到屬於自己的舞台。

Let every educator work to nurture every child — and let every child find a stage on which to shine.
期許每位教育人員努力培育每一個孩子，讓每個孩子找到發光發熱的舞台。
