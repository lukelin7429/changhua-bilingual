September meeting · 九月會議

My School Contact Card

Ten minutes with your co-teacher fills this in. Print it, keep it in your desk drawer, and photograph it for your phone. Nobody can make this list for you — the names differ at every school and change every year, so a county-wide list would be out of date before the first exam week.

請在九月的第一次會議，花十分鐘和協同教師一起填完，列印後放辦公桌抽屜，並拍一張存在手機裡。這份名單每校不同、每年會換人，所以由你自己填最可靠。
