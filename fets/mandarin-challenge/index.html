Mandarin Challenge

Every question for all nine FET meetings, in one place. Work through the bank at your own pace — try each item first, then tap Show answer for the correct option and an explanation. Answers stay hidden until you ask for them.

九次外師會議會用到的題目，全部列在這裡。照自己的步調一題一題想，想完再按「看答案」顯示正解與說明——答案預設是蓋起來的，你不按就不會看到。

Daily Practice · 每日練習

Practise on your phone, five minutes a day

The same questions, turned into short rounds with recorded audio. It remembers what you get wrong and brings it back at the right time. Add it to your home screen and it works offline.

同一份題庫，變成每天五分鐘的小回合，每題都有真人發音。答錯的會自動排到隔天再出現。加到手機主畫面就能離線使用。

Practice quiz · 自學測驗

Questions are drawn at random from the whole level and the options are shuffled, so no two runs are the same. Nothing is recorded — repeat it as often as you like.

從整級題庫隨機抽題、選項也會打散，每次都不一樣。不會登錄成績，可以放心一直重來。
