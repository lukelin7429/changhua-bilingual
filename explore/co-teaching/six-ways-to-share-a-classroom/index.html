Chapter 02 · 第二章

Six Ways to
Share a Classroom

共用一間教室的六種方式

About 11 minutes. Six self-check questions at the end. Nothing on this page is recorded or uploaded.

約十一分鐘讀完，末尾六題自我檢查。本頁不記錄、不上傳任何作答。

Chapter 1 argued that most co-teaching friction comes from accountabilities you cannot see. This chapter is about something you can see, and change, without any difficult conversation at all: what the two of you are physically doing with your bodies for those forty minutes.

第一章談的是你看不見的責任差異。這一章談的是你看得見、而且不必經過任何難堪對話就能改變的東西： 那四十分鐘裡，你們兩個人的身體實際上在做什麼。

Section 01The six models, in a Taiwanese English class

Marilyn Friend and Lynne Cook's six co-teaching models are the standard vocabulary for this worldwide. They were developed for inclusive classrooms rather than for language teaching, so the versions below are translated into what they look like in an elementary or junior high English class in Changhua.

Friend 與 Cook 的六種協同模式是全世界通用的標準語彙。它們原本是為融合教育的課堂發展出來的，不是為語言教學， 所以下面直接翻成彰化國中小英語課的實際樣子。

  1. One Teach, One Observe — one of you runs the lesson; the other is not idle but watching a specific thing and writing it down. Which four students never spoke. How long the instructions took. Who copied instead of answering. 一人教、一人觀察——另一人不是閒著，而是盯著一件說好的事並記下來：哪四個學生從頭到尾沒開口、指令講了幾分鐘、誰是用抄的而不是自己答的。
  2. One Teach, One Assist — one of you leads; the other circulates, helps individuals, translates, keeps order. This is the default almost everywhere. 一人教、一人協助——一人主導，另一人走動、個別協助、翻譯、維持秩序。這幾乎是全世界的預設。
  3. Parallel Teaching — you split the class in half and both teach the same content at the same time, in two halves of the room. Each child is in a group half the size, so each child gets roughly twice the talking time. 平行教學——把班級分成兩半，兩位老師同時各教相同內容。每個孩子所在的組別人數減半，開口的機會大約多一倍。
  4. Station Teaching — the content is split into parts, and students rotate. You run one station, your co-teacher runs another, and a third runs itself (a worksheet, a listening task, a reading corner). 站點教學——把內容拆成幾塊，學生輪流跑站。你顧一站、協同老師顧一站，第三站自己運作（學習單、聽力任務、閱讀角）。
  5. Alternative Teaching — one of you takes the large group while the other works with a small group who need something different: pre-teaching for the students who will otherwise be lost, or an extension for the ones who finished in three minutes. 差異教學——一人帶大組，另一人帶一個需要不同東西的小組：替跟不上的學生先教一遍，或替三分鐘就寫完的學生加深。
  6. Team Teaching — both of you lead the whole class at once, trading turns inside the same activity. It looks effortless and it is the hardest one; it needs a shared plan and a lot of rehearsal. 合作教學——兩人同時帶全班，在同一個活動裡輪流接話。看起來最輕鬆，其實最難：需要共同的計畫和大量默契。

Notice what makes the list a list

These are not six personalities or six philosophies. They are six answers to one very practical question — where is each teacher standing, and who is talking to whom. That is why the vocabulary is useful: it lets you propose a change without saying anything about your co-teacher at all.

這六項不是六種個性，也不是六種理念，而是對同一個很實際的問題的六種答案—— 兩位老師各自站在哪裡、誰在跟誰說話。這正是這套語彙有用的地方： 你可以提出改變，而完全不必評論你的協同老師這個人。

Section 02Why everyone lands on number two

One Teach, One Assist is the model that requires no planning, no agreement, and no conversation. It happens by default the moment two teachers walk into a room without having decided anything. That is exactly why it dominates — and why it is where most of the resentment in these partnerships lives.

「一人教、一人協助」是唯一不需要備課、不需要協議、不需要對話的模式。兩位老師走進教室、什麼都沒說好， 它就自動發生了。這正是它會勝出的原因，也正是這種搭檔關係裡多數怨氣的所在。

Role ambiguity is the single most-discussed problem in the co-teaching literature across Asia, and this model is where it lands. But here is the part foreign teachers usually get wrong, and it matters:

「角色不明」是亞洲各地協同教學研究中被討論最多的問題，而它的落點就是這個模式。 不過下面這件事，外師通常會弄錯，而且很重要：

The complaint you already know

你已經知道的那種抱怨

  • The foreign teacher is used mainly to say words out loud from the textbook.
  • Research on team teaching in Japan gave this its famous name — the human tape recorder — describing teachers whose role was narrowed to reading aloud, with no turn at asking, evaluating, or starting an exchange. 日本的協同教學研究給了它一個有名的稱呼——「人形錄音機」——指角色被壓縮成只負責唸出聲音，沒有提問、評量或開啟互動的份。

The complaint you probably have not heard

你大概沒聽過的那一種

  • The same model, in other classrooms, pushes the local teacher into the supporting role — translating, disciplining, handling the paperwork — while the foreign teacher takes instructional leadership.
  • Research on these partnerships records role ambiguity and power imbalance running in both directions. 研究記錄到的角色不明與權力失衡是雙向的：同一個模式，在別的教室裡把本國老師推到協助的位置——翻譯、管秩序、處理行政——而由外師掌握教學主導權。

So before you decide which of you is being wasted in this arrangement, consider that your co-teacher may be sitting on the same complaint from the opposite side — and that neither of you has said it out loud. One model, two people quietly feeling like furniture.

所以在你斷定這個安排浪費了誰之前，先想一想：你的協同老師很可能從另一邊抱著同一種抱怨， 而你們兩個誰都沒說出口。同一個模式，兩個人各自默默覺得自己是家具。

Section 03The two worth proposing first

If you are going to change one thing, change it to Station or Parallel. The reason is not that they are fashionable. It is structural: they are the two models in which both teachers are actively leading instruction at the same time, which makes the assistant role impossible to fall into. Research on co-teaching consistently points to these two as the strongest of the six for that reason.

如果你只打算改一件事，就改成站點教學或平行教學。理由不是它們比較時髦， 而是結構性的：這兩種是兩位老師同時都在主導教學的模式，讓「助手」這個角色根本無法形成。 協同教學研究一致認為六種當中這兩種最強，原因正在於此。

Parallel, in a real Changhua classroom

Split the class down the middle. You take one half at the front, your co-teacher takes the other half at the back. Both halves do the same speaking activity, at the same time. Nobody has to plan two lessons — you plan one and teach it twice, simultaneously. The noise doubles and the talking time per child roughly doubles with it.

把班級對半分。你在前面帶一半，協同老師在後面帶另一半，兩邊做相同的口說活動、同時進行。 沒有人需要備兩份課——備一份，同時教兩遍。教室會變吵一倍，每個孩子開口的時間也大致多一倍。

Station, in a real Changhua classroom

Three stations, twelve minutes each. Yours is speaking — the thing only you can give them. Your co-teacher's is whatever the exam actually asks for, taught in the way they know works for this class. The third station runs itself: a worksheet, a listening task on a tablet, a reading corner. This one is worth proposing precisely because it hands your co-teacher the exam-facing piece they are accountable for, instead of asking them to give it up.

三站，每站十二分鐘。你這站是口說——只有你給得了的東西。協同老師那站是段考真正要考的內容，用他知道對這個班有效的方式教。 第三站自己運作：學習單、平板上的聽力任務、閱讀角。這個模式特別值得提， 正因為它把「要考的東西」交回給要負責的人，而不是要求他放棄那一塊。

Section 04How to propose one without it sounding like a complaint

The failure mode here is predictable. "I think we should change how we teach together" is heard as I think you are doing it wrong, and it arrives without a plan attached. Four things make the difference:

這裡的失敗方式很好預測。「我覺得我們應該改變協同的方式」聽起來就是「我覺得你做錯了」， 而且後面沒有接任何具體方案。有四件事會決定成敗：

  1. Name the model, not the person. "Could we try station teaching for Unit 5?" is a proposal about furniture. "Could I have more of the lesson?" is a proposal about status. 說模式，不要說人。「第五課我們要不要試試站點教學？」談的是桌椅怎麼擺；「我可以多負責一點課嗎？」談的是誰比較大。
  2. Attach it to their problem, not yours. If they are worried about exam coverage, the station model gives them a protected twelve minutes for exactly that. 扣在他的問題上，不是你的。如果他擔心段考進度，站點模式正好給他十二分鐘專門處理那件事。
  3. Make it one lesson, and make it reversible. "For one class, and if it is worse we go back" is a much smaller thing to agree to than a change of method. 只試一節，而且可以退回去。「試一節，不好就改回來」比「改變教法」是小得多的承諾。
  4. Agree in advance how you will know. One observable thing: how many children spoke a full sentence, whether the worksheet got finished. Otherwise the debrief becomes two opinions. 事先講好怎麼判斷有沒有效。挑一件看得見的事：有幾個孩子講出完整句子、學習單有沒有寫完。否則課後檢討就只是兩種感覺在對撞。

What the research does not say

The six models are a vocabulary, not a ranking, and no study has settled which one works best in a Taiwanese English classroom. The evidence that Station and Parallel are stronger comes largely from co-teaching research in other settings, and the question of how co-teaching style affects student achievement is explicitly named as an under-researched gap.

Treat the models as options to choose between deliberately, matched to the objective and the room — not as a ladder you are supposed to climb.

六種模式是一套語彙，不是排行榜；沒有任何研究確定過哪一種在台灣的英語教室裡最好。 「站點與平行比較強」的證據主要來自其他情境的協同教學研究，而「協同方式如何影響學生成效」被研究者自己明白列為研究缺口。 請把這六種當成可以刻意挑選的選項——依教學目標與當天的班級狀況選——而不是一座你應該往上爬的階梯。

Section 05Three sentences to take into next week

  1. The default is a default, not a decision. One Teach, One Assist happens when nobody chose anything. 預設之所以是預設，是因為沒有人做過選擇。沒人決定任何事的時候，「一人教、一人協助」就自動發生。
  2. Your co-teacher may hold the same complaint from the other side. Role ambiguity runs both ways in the research. 你的協同老師可能從另一邊抱著同一種抱怨。研究記錄到的角色不明是雙向的。
  3. Propose a model, for one lesson, tied to their problem. That is a request about seating, not about status. 提出一個模式、只試一節、扣住他的問題。那是關於座位的請求，不是關於地位的請求。

Chapter 3 handles the harder case: what to do when the disagreement is not about where you stand, but about what should be taught at all.

第三章處理更難的情況：當你們的分歧不是站在哪裡，而是這堂課到底該教什麼。

Check yourself

自我檢查

Six situations. Pick the reading that best matches the chapter. Nothing here is scored, saved, or seen by anyone else.

六個情境，選出最符合本章觀點的解讀。不計分、不存檔、不會有第二個人看到。

Q1.Why does One Teach, One Assist end up being what most pairs do?
為什麼多數搭檔最後都落在「一人教、一人協助」？
B. It is the only model that needs no planning, no agreement, and no conversation — so it is what two teachers fall into by default. That is a description of how it arises, not an argument that it is wrong every time. 它是唯一不需要備課、不需要協議、不需要對話的模式，所以兩位老師會自動掉進去。這是在說它怎麼形成的，不是說它每次都錯。
Q2.You want each child to get more speaking time, and you do not want to plan two different lessons. Which model fits?
你想讓每個孩子多一點開口的時間，又不想備兩份不同的課。哪一種模式適合？
D. Parallel means both halves do the same content at the same time — one plan, taught twice simultaneously, with each child in a group half the size. Alternative teaching (B) does need a different plan for the small group. 平行教學是兩半同時做相同內容——備一份、同時教兩遍，而每個孩子所在的組別人數減半。B 的差異教學則確實需要為小組另備一份。
Q3.Your co-teacher is anxious about exam coverage and resists changing anything. Which proposal is most likely to be accepted?
協同老師很在意段考進度，抗拒任何改變。哪一個提案最可能被接受？
A. Station hands them a protected block for exactly the thing they are accountable for, instead of asking them to give it up. A proposal attached to the other person's problem is far easier to say yes to than one attached to yours. 站點模式把他要負責的那塊還給他一段受保護的時間，而不是要他放棄。扣在對方問題上的提案，比扣在自己問題上的容易答應得多。
Q4.The "human tape recorder" description comes from research on team teaching in which country?
「人形錄音機」這個說法出自哪一個國家的協同教學研究？
C. It comes from the Japanese team-teaching literature, describing foreign teachers narrowed to reading aloud with no turn at asking or evaluating. The underlying problem — role ambiguity — is documented across Asia, but the phrase is Japan's. 出自日本的協同教學文獻，形容外師的角色被壓縮成只負責唸讀，沒有提問或評量的份。底層問題「角色不明」在亞洲各地都有記錄，但這個說法來自日本。
Q5.According to the chapter, what does the research on role ambiguity in these partnerships actually show?
依本章所述，關於這種搭檔關係中「角色不明」的研究實際上顯示了什麼？
D. The same model can leave the foreign teacher reading aloud, or push the local teacher into translating and disciplining while the foreign teacher leads. You cannot assume you know which way it runs in your own room until you ask. 同一個模式，可能讓外師淪為唸讀，也可能把本國老師推去翻譯和管秩序、由外師主導。在你開口問之前，你無法假設自己教室裡是哪一種。
Q6.Which way of proposing a change is most consistent with this chapter?
哪一種提出改變的方式最符合本章的建議？
B. It names a model rather than a person, it is one lesson, and it is reversible. A is about status, C makes your co-teacher wrong, and D is a change of method with no plan attached — the version that reliably goes nowhere. 它說的是模式不是人、只試一節、而且可以退回去。A 談的是地位，C 等於判對方錯，D 是沒有具體方案的「改變教法」——最可靠地毫無下文的那一種。
