Chapter 03 · 第三章

Changhua City
on Foot

步行彰化市

About 11 minutes. Six self-check questions at the end. Nothing on this page is recorded or uploaded.

約十一分鐘讀完，末尾六題自我檢查。本頁不記錄、不上傳任何作答。

You have already been here. Changhua Station is where you change buses for Lukang, and where you pass through on the way to almost anywhere else. What almost nobody realizes is that three of the county's best things are within walking distance of the platform you were standing on.

你已經來過這裡了。彰化車站是你轉車去鹿港的地方，也是你去幾乎任何地方都會經過的地方。 幾乎沒有人意識到的是：全縣最好的三樣東西，就在你站過的那個月台的步行範圍內。

Section 01The roundhouse — plan the day around this one

The Changhua Roundhouse 扇形車庫 is a fan-shaped locomotive depot: twelve stalls radiating from a turntable, with engines rotated into place one at a time. It has been working for about a century, and it is the only surviving fan-shaped roundhouse in Taiwan. It is not a museum reconstruction. It is a functioning railway facility that lets you stand and watch.

扇形車庫是扇形的機車庫：十二股道從轉車盤呈放射狀展開，火車頭一輛一輛被轉進定位。 它已經運作了大約一個世紀，而且是全台僅存的扇形車庫。 它不是博物館的復原品，而是一座還在運轉、並且開放你站著看的鐵路設施。

This is where trips go wrong — read before you set a date

Entry is free, but the opening pattern catches people out: closed on Mondays, weekday afternoons only, and open from the morning on weekends and public holidays. On arrival one person from your group registers names at the office. Guided tours exist but are for groups of ten or more and must be applied for well in advance — as an individual you simply walk in and look.

Confirm the current hours on the county tourism site before you go; opening arrangements at a working railway facility can change.

免費參觀，但開放模式很會坑人：週一不開放、平日只有下午， 假日與國定假日才從上午開始。入園時由一人到管理室登記姓名與人數。 有導覽解說，但限十人以上團體且須提前申請；個人直接進去自由參觀即可。 出發前請到彰化縣旅遊資訊網確認當期開放時間——運轉中的鐵路設施，開放方式是會變的。

Note what this means in practice: the roundhouse and Lukang Old Street are both shut on Mondays. If Monday is your only free day, Changhua County is telling you to stay home and rest.

注意這在實務上的意思：扇形車庫和鹿港老街都是週一休。 如果你只有星期一有空，那是彰化縣在告訴你：待在家裡休息吧。

Section 02Bagua Mountain and the Great Buddha

The Great Buddha 八卦山大佛 sits above the city on Bagua Plateau, completed in 1961 and for a time described as the largest Buddha statue in East Asia. You can walk up from the city; the climb is short by the standards of anywhere else in Taiwan, because Changhua is the lowest-lying county on the island and this is the only real height in it.

八卦山大佛坐在八卦台地上俯瞰市區，1961 年竣工，曾一度被稱為東亞第一大佛。 從市區可以走上去；以台灣其他地方的標準來說這段爬升很短，因為彰化是全島海拔最低的縣，而這是縣內唯一真正的高度。

From the top you can see the whole Changhua plain — and on a clear day, the flat land running west toward the coast you visited in chapter 2. It is the single best way to understand the shape of the county you are working in.

從山上可以看見整片彰化平原——天氣好的時候，還看得到平地一路向西延伸到你在第二章去過的那片海岸。 這是理解你工作所在這個縣的形狀最好的方式。

One piece of history, handled carefully

In 1895, when Taiwan was ceded to Japan, this hill was the site of one of the largest battles fought on the island. Local people know this; there are memorials on the hill. It is worth knowing so that you understand why the place matters to people here.

It is also a subject where a foreign teacher is far better off asking than offering views. Let your colleagues and your students tell you what it means to them — the history of this period is discussed in more than one way in Taiwan, and listening is the professional choice.

1895 年台灣割讓給日本時，這座山是全島規模最大的戰役之一的戰場。在地人知道這件事，山上也有紀念設施。 知道這件事，你才理解這個地方對這裡的人為什麼重要。 但這也是一個外師「提問」遠比「表達看法」合適的題目。讓同事和學生告訴你這對他們的意義—— 這段歷史在台灣有不只一種談法，而傾聽是專業的選擇。

Section 03The Confucian Temple

Changhua Confucian Temple 彰化孔子廟 was founded in 1726, three years after the county itself was established, and it is a designated national historic site. If you have been to the temples in Lukang, come here for the contrast — and the contrast is the point.

彰化孔子廟創建於 1726 年，就在彰化設縣三年之後，是國定古蹟。 如果你去過鹿港的廟，來這裡是為了對比——而對比正是重點。

A folk temple (Lukang Mazu)

民間信仰廟宇（鹿港天后宮）

  • Incense, offerings, divination blocks, noise.
  • People come to ask for something.
  • Statues of deities as the focus.

A Confucian temple

孔廟

  • Quiet, restrained, almost empty by comparison.
  • Historically a school as much as a place of ritual.
  • Tablets with names rather than statues — the focus is a teacher, not a god.

For a teacher, that last line is worth sitting with. The county's name comes from the phrase 建學立師以彰雅化 — "establish schools and teachers, so that refined civilization may be made visible." The place you work was named after the idea that teaching is what makes a civilization visible. That is not a bad thing to remember on a difficult Wednesday.

對一個老師來說，最後那一行值得停下來想一想。彰化的縣名出自 建學立師以彰雅化——「設立學校與老師，讓文明得以彰顯」。 你工作的這個地方，是以「教學讓文明被看見」這個想法命名的。這件事在難熬的星期三，還蠻值得記得的。

Section 04What to eat

  • Changhua meatball 彰化肉圓 — the city's signature dish and genuinely different from the versions elsewhere in Taiwan. A chewy translucent skin around pork, served with a sweet-savory sauce. Try it here before you form an opinion about bawan. 彰化肉圓——本市的招牌，和台灣其他地方的版本確實不同。半透明的Q皮包著豬肉，淋上甜鹹醬汁。在對肉圓下定論之前，先在這裡吃一次。
  • Braised pork rice 爌肉飯 — a thick slab of braised pork belly over rice. Changhua's version is a local institution, and it is what people here eat when they are hungry rather than when they are being tourists. 爌肉飯——厚厚一塊滷五花蓋在飯上。彰化的版本是在地的經典，而且是這裡的人「肚子餓」時吃的東西，不是「當觀光客」時吃的東西。
  • Cat-mouse noodles 貓鼠麵 — no cats and no mice; the shop's founder had a nickname. Old, famous, and a good story to tell your students. 貓鼠麵——沒有貓也沒有老鼠，是創始人的綽號。老字號、有名，而且是個可以講給學生聽的好故事。

wǒ yào dēngjì cānguān, yí ge rén.

Change 一個人 to 三個人 sān ge rén for three people.

Section 06Taking it back to the classroom

  1. The turntable is a physics lesson that turns. Why rotate the engine instead of building a loop? Ask that question before you give the answer; it works at every level. 轉車盤是一堂會轉的物理課。為什麼要轉火車頭，而不是蓋一個環形軌道？先問，再給答案；這題在任何年段都成立。
  2. Use the view from Bagua Mountain as a map. Show a photograph from the top and ask students to point out where their own township is. Geography stops being abstract the moment they can see their own house from a picture. 把八卦山的視野當地圖。秀一張山上拍的照片，請學生指出自己的鄉鎮在哪裡。當他們能在照片裡找到自己家，地理就不再抽象了。
  3. Tell them what the county's name means. Most students have never been told that 彰化 is a sentence about teachers. Coming from the foreign teacher, it lands differently. 告訴他們縣名的意思。多數學生從沒被告知「彰化」是一句關於老師的話。由外師說出來，感受會不一樣。

That completes this pack. Three trips, all reachable, all within an hour of where you teach. The only remaining step is the one no web page can do for you.

這一包到此結束。三趟行程，都到得了，都在你教書的地方一小時之內。 剩下的那一步，沒有任何網頁能替你做。

Check yourself

自我檢查

Six questions. Nothing here is scored, saved, or seen by anyone else.

六題。不計分、不存檔、不會有第二個人看到。

Q1.You have a free Monday and want to see the roundhouse and Lukang Old Street. What does the chapter say?
你星期一有空，想去扇形車庫和鹿港老街。本章怎麼說？
C. The roundhouse is closed Mondays and much of Lukang Old Street is too. If Monday is your only free day, the county is telling you to rest. 扇形車庫週一不開放，鹿港老街也有很多沒開。如果你只有星期一有空，那就是彰化縣在叫你休息。
Q2.You are visiting the roundhouse alone on a Wednesday. What do you need to do?
你星期三一個人去扇形車庫。你需要做什麼？
A. Entry is free; weekdays are afternoons only; one person registers names at the office. The fourteen-day application is for guided tours, which are for groups of ten or more — as an individual you just walk in. 免費入園；平日只有下午；由一人到管理室登記。提前十四天申請的是導覽解說，且限十人以上團體——個人直接進去就好。
Q3.What makes the Changhua Roundhouse worth a special trip?
扇形車庫為什麼值得專程一趟？
D. Twelve stalls radiating from a turntable, about a century old, and not a reconstruction — a functioning railway facility you are allowed to stand and watch. 十二股道從轉車盤放射出去，已有大約一世紀歷史，而且不是復原品——是一座還在運轉、並且開放你站著看的鐵路設施。
Q4.A student asks you what you think about the 1895 fighting on Bagua Mountain. What is the professional response?
學生問你對 1895 年八卦山戰役的看法。專業的回應是什麼？
B. Know the history so you understand why the place matters here. But this period is discussed in more than one way in Taiwan, and a foreign teacher is far better off asking than offering views. C dodges a real question that a student trusted you with; B answers it. 知道這段歷史，才理解這個地方為什麼重要。但這段歷史在台灣有不只一種談法，外師提問遠比表達立場合適。C 是迴避了一個學生信任你才問出口的問題；B 才是回應。
Q5.How does a Confucian temple differ from a folk temple like Lukang Mazu?
孔廟和鹿港天后宮那樣的民間信仰廟宇有什麼不同？
C. Tablets with names rather than statues of deities; people do not come to ask for something. Changhua's own name — 建學立師以彰雅化 — is a sentence about establishing schools and teachers. 是寫著名字的牌位而不是神像；人們不是為了求什麼而來。彰化的縣名「建學立師以彰雅化」本身就是一句關於興學與立師的話。
Q6.Which classroom follow-up does this chapter recommend for the view from Bagua Mountain?
關於八卦山的視野，本章建議的課堂延伸是哪一個？
A. Geography stops being abstract the moment a child can find their own house in a picture. It needs no preparation and no budget — just the photograph you already took. 當孩子能在照片裡找到自己家，地理就不再抽象了。這不必準備、不必經費，用你已經拍好的那張照片就行。
