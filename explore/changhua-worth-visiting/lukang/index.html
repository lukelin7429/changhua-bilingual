Chapter 01 · 第一章

Lukang
in One Day

鹿港一日

About 12 minutes. Six self-check questions at the end. Nothing on this page is recorded or uploaded.

約十二分鐘讀完，末尾六題自我檢查。本頁不記錄、不上傳任何作答。

You almost certainly live within an hour of Lukang, and there is a reasonable chance you have not been. This is not a list of sights. It is a plan for one Saturday, written for someone who has to get there by public transport, wants to know what is actually worth stopping for, and would like to be able to order lunch.

你住的地方離鹿港幾乎一定不到一小時，而你很可能還沒去過。這不是一份景點清單， 而是一個星期六的計畫，寫給要搭大眾運輸過去、想知道哪些真的值得停下來、而且希望自己點得了午餐的人。

Section 01What Lukang actually is

For roughly a century, Lukang was the second most important city on this island. There is an old ranking that Taiwanese people still quote — 一府二鹿三艋舺, "first Tainan, second Lukang, third Bangka" — placing Lukang above what is now Taipei. It was a port, facing China across the strait, and the wealth of that trade is why a town this small has temples this good.

有大約一百年的時間，鹿港是全島第二重要的城市。台灣人到現在還在引用一句老排名—— 一府二鹿三艋舺——鹿港排在今天的台北之前。它是一個港口，隔著海峽面向中國， 而那段貿易累積的財富，正是這麼小的一個鎮為什麼會有這麼好的廟。

Then the harbor silted up. The trade left, the railway went elsewhere, and Lukang stopped growing — which is precisely why it is still standing. The reason the streets bend and narrow the way they do is that nobody had the money or the reason to straighten them.

後來港口淤積了。貿易走了，鐵路也沒有經過，鹿港就停止成長——而這正是它至今還在的原因。 街道之所以那樣彎、那樣窄，是因為沒有人有錢、也沒有理由把它們拉直。

Something to say to your students on Monday

Ask them why an old town has narrow, curved streets. Most will guess "because it is old." The better answer — because it stopped being rich — is the kind of thinking a social studies teacher would love you for, and it works in simple English.

問學生：為什麼老街道又窄又彎？多數會猜「因為它很舊」。更好的答案是「因為它不再有錢了」—— 這種思考方式會讓社會科老師很喜歡你，而且用簡單的英語就講得出來。

Section 02Getting there, and which day to pick

Lukang has no train station. That single fact shapes the whole trip: you take a train to Changhua City and change to a bus.

鹿港沒有火車站。光是這件事就決定了整趟行程的形狀：先搭火車到彰化市，再轉客運。

  1. Take the TRA to Changhua Station 台鐵彰化站. From anywhere on the Changhua plain this is short and frequent. 搭台鐵到彰化站。從彰化平原上任何地方過去都很快、班次也多。
  2. Walk out of the station and turn left. The bus station is right there — you do not need a taxi and you do not need to cross anything major. 出站後往左走，客運站就在旁邊——不需要叫計程車，也不必穿越什麼大馬路。
  3. Take a Lukang-bound bus operated by Changhua Bus 彰化客運. Routes toward Lukang include the 6900 and 6936. The ride is roughly half an hour. 搭往鹿港的彰化客運，6900、6936 等路線都到，車程大約半小時。
  4. Coming by high speed rail? The HSR station called 高鐵彰化站 is not in Changhua City — it is in Tianzhong, in the south of the county, and there are separate buses from there. Tell visiting friends this before they book. 搭高鐵來？高鐵彰化站不在彰化市，在南邊的田中，那裡另有客運。朋友要來之前先告訴他們。

Do not trust a printed timetable — including this one

Route numbers are fairly stable; departure times, frequencies, and fares are not, and weekend service differs from weekday service. Check the live times before you leave, on Changhua Bus's own site or in a transit app. This page deliberately does not print times, because a wrong time is worse than no time.

路線號碼相對穩定，但發車時間、班距與票價都會變，而且假日和平日班表不同。 出發前務必查即時班次——彰化客運官網或大眾運輸 App。本頁刻意不印時刻表，因為錯的時間比沒有時間更糟。

Which day

  • Avoid Monday. A lot of Old Street is shut, and some of what is open opens late. This catches out more first-time visitors than anything else. 避開星期一。老街有不少店沒開，開的也可能晚開。這是最多人第一次去踩到的雷。
  • Weekdays are quiet, weekends are alive. If you want photographs, go on a weekday. If you want the town at full volume — vendors calling, families everywhere — go on a weekend and accept the crowd. 平日安靜，假日熱鬧。想拍照就平日去；想看整個鎮火力全開——攤販吆喝、到處都是家庭——就假日去，並接受人潮。
  • Arrive by mid-morning. Most shops open around nine or ten and start closing from six or seven. An afternoon-only trip loses half of it. 上午中段就到。多數店家九點、十點左右開，六點、七點起陸續收。只排下午等於少掉一半。

Section 03Four things worth your time

1. Lukang Longshan Temple 鹿港龍山寺

If you only do one thing, do this one. It is widely considered one of the finest pieces of traditional temple architecture in Taiwan, and the thing to look up at is the octagonal caisson ceiling 八卦藻井 — a spiralling wooden dome assembled from interlocking brackets, the largest of its kind here. It is quieter and more solemn than the Mazu temple; if you have been told all Taiwanese temples are loud and smoky, this is the one that corrects you.

只做一件事的話，就做這一件。它被廣泛認為是台灣傳統廟宇建築的極品，而該抬頭看的是 八卦藻井——由層層斗栱交錯疊起的螺旋木造穹頂，全台同類中最大的一座。 它比天后宮安靜、肅穆得多；如果有人告訴你台灣的廟都吵、都煙霧瀰漫，這一座會糾正你。

2. Lukang Tianhou Temple 鹿港天后宮

The Mazu temple, and the busiest place in town. Mazu 媽祖 is the sea goddess — which tells you everything about what this town used to do for a living. Come here for the opposite experience to Longshan: incense, noise, people actually praying rather than sightseeing.

媽祖廟，鎮上最熱鬧的地方。媽祖是海神——這件事已經說明了這個鎮從前靠什麼維生。 來這裡是為了跟龍山寺相反的體驗：香火、人聲，以及真的在拜拜而不是在觀光的人。

Temple manners, in four lines

Enter through the right-hand door as you face the temple — the dragon door 龍門 — and leave through the left, the tiger door 虎門. Never use the center door; that one is for the gods. Step over the raised wooden threshold 門檻, never on it. Photographs of the building are normally fine; photographs of people mid-prayer are not.

面對廟宇時從右邊的門進去（龍門），從左邊的門出來（虎門）； 中門絕對不走，那是給神明走的。跨過凸起的門檻，不要踩在上面。 拍建築通常沒問題，拍正在祈禱的人則不行。

3. The Old Street 鹿港老街

Really Putou Street and Yaolin Street 埔頭街、瑤林街. Two things to look for rather than just walking through: the Half-Well 半邊井, a well deliberately built half inside a wall and half outside so that neighbors without one could still draw water; and the doorway plaques naming each family's ancestral place. The half-well is the single best story in Lukang for a classroom — generosity built into architecture.

其實就是埔頭街與瑤林街。有兩樣東西值得特地找，而不只是走過去：半邊井—— 一口刻意一半築在牆內、一半在牆外的井，好讓沒有井的鄰居也能取水；以及門楣上標示各家祖籍的堂號。 半邊井是鹿港最適合帶進教室的一個故事——把慷慨蓋進建築裡。

4. Breast-Touching Lane 摸乳巷

A famously narrow alley — narrow enough that two adults cannot pass without turning sideways, which is where the cheerful name comes from. Worth two minutes and a photograph. Given the name, this is one to mention to your students carefully, or not at all.

一條出了名的窄巷——窄到兩個大人不側身就過不去，那個俏皮的名字就是這麼來的。值得花兩分鐘拍張照。 不過就那個名字而言，要跟學生講就得小心措辭，或者乾脆不提。

Section 04What to eat

Lukang is a food town, and the food is coastal. Four things to try, in rough order of how likely you are to love them:

鹿港是個吃的地方，而且吃的是海線的東西。四樣值得試，大致依你會喜歡的機率排序：

  • Oyster omelette 蚵仔煎 — the national snack, and better here because the oysters come from the coast twenty minutes away. 蚵仔煎——全國性的小吃，在這裡更好吃，因為蚵仔來自二十分鐘車程外的海邊。
  • Thin noodle soup 麵線糊 — thickened, savory, eaten with a spoon rather than chopsticks. Cheap and everywhere. 麵線糊——勾芡、鹹口，用湯匙吃而不是筷子。便宜，而且到處都有。
  • Ox-tongue biscuits 牛舌餅 — no ox involved; they are named for the shape. The standard thing to bring back for your office. 牛舌餅——跟牛沒有關係，是因為形狀得名。帶回辦公室分同事的標準伴手禮。
  • Phoenix-eye cake 鳳眼糕 — a very dry, very sweet pressed cake, an old Lukang specialty. Try one before buying a box. 鳳眼糕——非常乾、非常甜的壓製糕點，鹿港的老字號特產。先試一個再決定要不要買一盒。

Bringing something back for the office is not optional politeness in a Taiwanese school — it is how you say I thought about you while I was away. A box of ox-tongue biscuits on the office table buys more goodwill than it costs.

在台灣的學校，帶伴手禮回辦公室不是可有可無的客套，而是在說「我不在的時候有想到你們」。 辦公桌上一盒牛舌餅換到的善意，遠超過它的價錢。

Section 05The Mandarin you will actually use

Not tourist phrasebook Mandarin — these are the sentences you will genuinely need between the bus station and the last stall. Tap 🔊 to hear each one.

不是觀光手冊上的中文——這幾句是你從客運站到最後一個攤位之間真的會用到的。按 🔊 可以聽發音。

Asking whether this bus goes to Lukang

請問這班車有到鹿港嗎？

qǐngwèn zhè bān chē yǒu dào Lùgǎng ma?

Asking how to get somewhere on foot

請問龍山寺怎麼走？

qǐngwèn Lóngshān Sì zěnme zǒu?

Swap in any place name — this is the sentence frame you will reuse most.

Ordering by pointing, which is completely acceptable

我要這個，一份。

wǒ yào zhège, yí fèn.

"I want this one, one portion." Point at what someone else is eating.

How much is it

多少錢？

duōshǎo qián?

Eat here or take away

內用還是外帶？

nèiyòng háishì wàidài?

外帶，謝謝。

wàidài, xièxie.

You will be asked this, not the other way round. Knowing the question is the point.

Saying you do not eat something

我不吃香菜。

wǒ bù chī xiāngcài.

Cilantro. Swap in 牛肉 niúròu (beef) or 豬肉 zhūròu (pork) as needed.

Asking where the toilet is

請問廁所在哪裡？

qǐngwèn cèsuǒ zài nǎlǐ?

Asking permission before photographing someone or something

我可以拍照嗎？

wǒ kěyǐ pāizhào ma?

Inside a temple, ask first. It takes one second and changes how you are seen.

Section 06Taking it back to the classroom

You are not a tourist; you are a teacher who lives here. The difference is what you do with the trip on Monday. Three things that need no preparation:

你不是觀光客，你是住在這裡的老師。差別在於星期一你拿這趟旅行做什麼。三件不必準備的事：

  1. Show four photographs and let them tell you. Your students know Lukang better than you do. Handing them the expert role in English is worth more than a vocabulary list. 秀四張照片，讓他們告訴你。學生比你懂鹿港。用英語把「專家」的角色交給他們，價值超過一張單字表。
  2. Ask them to teach you one food word. They will correct your tones and enjoy it enormously, and you will actually remember the word. 請他們教你一個食物的詞。他們會糾正你的聲調而且樂在其中，而你真的會記住那個詞。
  3. Use the half-well as a discussion prompt. "Why would someone build half of their well outside their own wall?" That question works at every level from grade three to junior high. 用半邊井當討論題。「為什麼有人要把自己的井蓋一半在牆外？」這個問題從三年級到國中都成立。

And tell your co-teacher you went. It is a small thing, and it is the kind of small thing that makes the conversations in the co-teaching pack easier to start.

還有，跟你的協同老師說你去了。這是一件小事，而正是這種小事讓 協同教學那一包裡的對話比較容易開得了口。

Check yourself

自我檢查

Six questions about planning the trip and not embarrassing yourself when you get there. Nothing here is scored, saved, or seen by anyone else.

六題，關於怎麼規劃這趟行程，以及到了現場不要出糗。不計分、不存檔、不會有第二個人看到。

Q1.You want to reach Lukang by public transport from a school on the Changhua plain. What is the route?
你想從彰化平原上的學校搭大眾運輸到鹿港。路線是什麼？
B. Lukang has no train station at all — that single fact shapes the whole trip. The bus station is immediately to your left as you leave Changhua Station, and the ride is roughly half an hour. 鹿港根本沒有火車站——光這件事就決定了整趟行程的形狀。出彰化車站往左就是客運站，車程大約半小時。
Q2.A friend is flying in and says they will take the high speed rail to "Changhua Station." What do you tell them?
朋友要飛來，說他會搭高鐵到「彰化站」。你要告訴他什麼？
D. 高鐵彰化站 is in Tianzhong, well south of Changhua City, with its own separate bus connections. Telling visitors this before they book saves them an unpleasant surprise. 高鐵彰化站在田中，位於彰化市南邊很遠的地方，有自己另外的接駁客運。在朋友訂票前先講，可以省掉一場不愉快的意外。
Q3.You have one free day and it happens to be a Monday. What does the chapter say?
你只有一天空檔，而且剛好是星期一。本章怎麼說？
A. This catches out more first-time visitors than anything else. If you want quiet, go on a different weekday and arrive by mid-morning — most shops open around nine or ten and start closing from six or seven. 這是最多人第一次去踩到的雷。想要安靜的話，挑別的平日，並在上午中段前抵達——多數店家九點、十點左右開，六、七點起陸續收。
Q4.You are entering Lukang Tianhou Temple. Which is correct?
你正要進鹿港天后宮。哪一個做法正確？
C. Facing the temple, enter by the right-hand dragon door 龍門 and leave by the left-hand tiger door 虎門. The center door is for the gods. Step over the raised wooden threshold 門檻 — standing on it is read as disrespect. 面對廟宇，從右邊的龍門進、左邊的虎門出，中門是給神明走的。跨過凸起的門檻，踩上去會被視為不敬。
Q5.Why does Lukang still have narrow, curving streets and exceptional temples?
為什麼鹿港至今仍有窄而彎的街道和格外精美的廟宇？
A. The trade paid for the temples; losing the trade is why nobody ever had the money or the reason to straighten the streets. "Because it stopped being rich" is a far better answer than "because it is old" — and a good question for your students. 是貿易付錢蓋了那些廟；而失去貿易，正是沒有人有錢也沒有理由把街道拉直的原因。「因為它不再有錢了」比「因為它很舊」是好得多的答案，也是很好的課堂提問。
Q6.You are standing at a food stall and cannot read the menu. What is the most practical move?
你站在小吃攤前，看不懂菜單。最實際的做法是什麼？
D. "wǒ yào zhège, yí fèn" — I want this one, one portion. Pointing is completely acceptable here and it is faster than any app. Be ready for the follow-up question 內用還是外帶？ (eat in or take away) — knowing the question is the real skill. 「我要這個，一份。」用指的在這裡完全可以接受，而且比任何 App 都快。準備好接下一句「內用還是外帶？」——聽得懂那個問題才是真本事。
