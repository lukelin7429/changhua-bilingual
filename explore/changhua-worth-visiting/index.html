Topic Pack 02 · 主題包二

Changhua
Worth Visiting

值得走一趟的彰化

You live here. Your students live here. Most of the places in this pack are less than an hour from your school, and a surprising number of foreign teachers finish a contract without ever going. Each chapter is one day out, planned for someone without a car — which train, which bus, which day to avoid, what to eat, what to say, and what to do with it in class on Monday.

你住在這裡，你的學生也住在這裡。這一包裡的地方多半離你的學校不到一小時， 但相當多外師整個聘期結束都沒去過。每一章是一趟一日行程，寫給沒有車的人—— 搭哪班火車、轉哪班客運、避開哪一天、吃什麼、說什麼，以及星期一回到教室怎麼用。

Times and fares are deliberately missing

Bus route numbers are reasonably stable; departure times, frequencies, and fares are not, and weekend service differs from weekday. These chapters give you the shape of the journey and tell you where to check the live times — a wrong time printed on a web page is worse than no time at all.

客運的路線號碼相對穩定，但發車時間、班距與票價都會變，假日與平日班表也不同。 這些章節給你行程的形狀，並告訴你去哪裡查即時班次——網頁上印一個錯的時間，比不印還糟。

Want the county background first?

The Hub's About Changhua page covers the geography, the industries, and what each part of the county is known for. This pack picks up where that leaves off: how to actually go.

想先看縣的背景？本站的認識彰化介紹地理、產業與各地特色。 這一包從那裡接下去：怎麼真的去成。
