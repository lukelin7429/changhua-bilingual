Topic Pack 04 · 主題包四

Running
the Room

教室現場：分組、指令、秩序

The other three packs are about your co-teacher, the county you live in, and the materials you make. This one is about the forty minutes themselves — twenty-something children, one room, and whatever you decided to do with them. It is the part nobody trained most foreign teachers for, and the part that decides whether the lesson you planned is the lesson that happens.

另外三包談的是你的協同老師、你住的這個縣、你做出來的教材。這一包談的是那四十分鐘本身—— 二十幾個孩子、一間教室，以及你決定要拿他們怎麼辦。 這是多數外師從來沒被訓練過的一塊，也是決定「你備的課」會不會變成「真正發生的課」的一塊。

Why this is its own pack

Grouping, instructions and behavior kept turning up as footnotes in the other packs — a line in the co-teaching chapters about who handles order, a line in the worksheet chapter about the first ninety seconds. They are not footnotes. They are the largest single thing standing between a good plan and a good lesson, so they get their own place.

分組、指令、秩序在其他幾包裡一直只能當註腳出現——協同教學裡提一句誰管秩序、學習單那章提一句最初的九十秒。 它們不是註腳。它們是「好的計畫」和「好的一堂課」之間最大的一道關卡，所以獨立成一包。

Not classroom management in the abstract

There is no shortage of general advice about managing a class. What there is very little of is advice for someone who is not the homeroom teacher, does not share a first language with the children, sees each class once a week, and shares the room with a colleague who has the relationship. That is the situation these chapters are written for.

關於班級經營的一般性建議並不缺。真正稀少的是寫給這種人的建議：不是導師、跟孩子沒有共同母語、 每個班一週只見一次，而且要跟一位「跟孩子有關係」的同事共用教室。這幾章就是為那個處境寫的。
