Explore · 自學平台

Learn whatever you want.
Nobody is keeping score.

你想學什麼就學什麼——這裡沒有人在計分。

The Mandarin Challenge and the School Culture rounds are things we ask you to do. This is not. Every topic pack here is optional, nothing is recorded, nothing is uploaded, and no result reaches anyone but you. Read a chapter, check yourself with a few questions, and close the tab.

「中文挑戰」和「校園文化」是每月會議要交的；這裡不是。所有主題包都是自由選讀， 不記錄、不上傳，答對答錯只有你自己知道。讀一章、做幾題自我檢查，就可以關掉。

How this works

使用說明

Each pack is a small set of chapters. A chapter is one article you can finish in about ten minutes, followed by a handful of questions so you can tell whether you actually took it in. The questions ask you to judge a situation, not to remember a term.

每個主題包由幾章組成。一章是一篇大約十分鐘讀得完的文章，後面附幾道題讓你確認自己是不是真的讀懂了。 題目考的是情境判斷，不是名詞記憶。

Your answers are not saved, not counted, and not sent anywhere — refresh the page and it is gone. If you want to redo a set, press Try again. New packs and chapters are added over time; the ones marked coming soon are being written.

你的作答不會存檔、不會計分、不會傳到任何地方——重新整理就消失了。想重做按「Try again」。 主題包會陸續增加，標示「準備中」的表示還在寫。
