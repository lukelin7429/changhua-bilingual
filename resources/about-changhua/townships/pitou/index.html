What Pitou Township is famous for 特色

Pitou (埤頭, "pond head") is Changhua's guava (芭樂) heartland — sweet, crunchy pearl guava grown in the sandy alluvial soils here is exported nationally. The township also grows sugar apple (釋迦, custard apple) and wax apple (蓮霧). Pitou's agriculture-focused community holds a Guava Festival each autumn.

埤頭鄉是彰化縣的芭樂之鄉——在這片砂質沖積土壤中生長的珍珠芭樂，口感清脆香甜，銷售全台。鄉內除了芭樂，也盛產釋迦與蓮霧。每年秋天，當地農業社區會舉辦熱鬧的芭樂節慶典。

Places to visit 值得一遊

🌿

Guava Orchards芭樂農場

Pitou's guava farms welcome visitors for fruit-picking between October and February, when the pearl guava harvest is at its peak. Many farms offer hands-on experiences such as guava jam-making and guided orchard tours, making them a favorite destination for school trips and family outings.

🏛️

Pitou Township Center埤頭鄉公所

The Pitou Township Center serves as the administrative heart of the community, where local governance and public services come together. The surrounding area reflects the quiet, close-knit character of rural Changhua, with small markets and community spaces nearby.

🏯

Longmen Temple龍門廟

Longmen Temple is one of Pitou's most important places of worship, dedicated to local deities and serving as a center of traditional Taiwanese folk religion. The temple's ornate architecture and vibrant festivals draw visitors throughout the year and offer a window into the spiritual life of the farming community.

🚴

Zhuoshui River Cycling Path濁水溪堤防自行車道

The cycling path along the Zhuoshui River embankment offers scenic rides through Pitou's rural landscape, with views of farmland, fruit orchards, and the wide river plain. The flat, well-maintained route is popular with cyclists of all ages and is especially beautiful in the early morning light.

Did you know? 你知道嗎？

Six facts about Pitou Township — perfect for quizzes and classroom discussions.

六個關於埤頭鄉的趣味知識，適合課堂問答。

🍈

Crunchy Pearl Guava Standard珍珠芭樂品質標竿

Pearl guava (珍珠芭樂) grown in Pitou is firmer and sweeter than tropical varieties — its crunchy texture is considered a quality benchmark in Taiwan's fruit market.

🧑‍🌾

Pick Your Own Guava果園採果體驗

Guava picking season runs October–February; farm-stay programs include guava jam-making, giving visitors a hands-on taste of rural Pitou life.

🛡️

Eco-Friendly Bagging Technique無農藥套袋法

Pitou farmers pioneered bagging techniques (套袋法) for guava to prevent insect damage without pesticides — a smart, eco-friendly innovation that is now widely used across Taiwan.

🌊

Floods that Enrich the Land洪水帶來肥沃土地

The township borders the Zhuoshui River — floods historically brought new alluvial soil, enriching the land for fruit trees and making Pitou one of Changhua's most fertile agricultural areas.

🍬

Especially Sweet Custard Apple甜蜜釋迦的秘密

Custard apple (釋迦) grown in Pitou is notably sweet; local growers attribute this to Zhuoshui River water irrigation, which carries minerals from the mountains upstream.

🎉

Annual Guava Festival Fun一年一度芭樂節

The community's Guava Festival (芭樂節) includes a guava-eating contest and orchard tours, drawing visitors from across Taiwan to celebrate the township's most beloved crop.
