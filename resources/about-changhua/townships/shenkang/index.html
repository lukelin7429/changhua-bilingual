What Shenkang Township is famous for 特色

Shenkang hugs Changhua's northeastern coastline where the Dadu River meets the Taiwan Strait. Its tidal flats support oyster farming, crab catching, and a seasonal birdwatching trail. The township is notable for its fishing villages and coastal leisure farms that open to city visitors on weekends.

伸港鄉緊鄰彰化縣東北側海岸，位於大肚溪注入台灣海峽的出海口。廣闊的潮間帶孕育了牡蠣養殖、抓螃蟹等漁業活動，也設有季節性賞鳥步道。鄉內的漁村風情獨具，多家休閒農場每逢假日開放城市旅客前來體驗。

Places to visit 值得一遊

🦩

Shenkang Coastal Wetlands海岸溼地

These expansive tidal flats along the Taiwan Strait are a haven for migratory shorebirds and a showcase of coastal biodiversity. Visitors can walk a boardwalk trail at low tide to observe crabs, shellfish, and seasonal wading birds up close. The wetlands are especially lively in autumn and spring during the East Asian flyway migration season.

🦪

Fishing Port & Oyster Sheds漁港與蚵棚

Shenkang's small fishing harbor is the heart of the local economy, where colorful boats return each morning with fresh catches. The nearby oyster sheds use traditional hanging-cage techniques passed down through generations of north Changhua coastal families. A stroll through the port in the early morning gives visitors a vivid look at Taiwan's inshore fishing culture.

🐦

Dadu River Estuary Bird Zone大肚溪口候鳥保護區

The estuary where the Dadu River empties into the sea is one of Taiwan's most important stopovers on the East Asian–Australasian Flyway, attracting tens of thousands of migrating birds each year. Black-faced spoonbills, grey herons, and a wide variety of sandpipers and terns can be spotted from designated observation platforms. The zone is managed as a protected nature reserve to safeguard the fragile wetland ecosystem.

🌾

Coastal Leisure Farms海濱休閒農場

Several family-run leisure farms dot the Shenkang coastline, offering weekend visitors hands-on experiences like oyster shucking, crab fishing, and seasonal seafood barbecues. The open-air settings combine sea breezes with rural farmland, creating a relaxing escape from urban life. Many farms also serve fresh locally sourced seafood meals that highlight the flavors of the north Changhua coast.

Did you know? 你知道嗎？

Six facts about Shenkang Township — perfect for quizzes and classroom discussions.

六個關於伸港鄉的趣味知識，適合課堂問答。

🦆

A Wetland Bird Sanctuary候鳥的重要驛站

The Dadu River estuary beside Shenkang is a protected wetland and one of Taiwan's key migratory bird stopover points. Every year, thousands of shorebirds and waterfowl rest and feed here on their long journeys between northern and southern hemispheres.

🦪

Unique Oyster Farming Method獨特的懸吊式養蚵

Shenkang's oyster farms use the traditional hanging-cage method unique to the north Changhua coast. Oyster clusters are suspended in wire cages from bamboo poles, allowing them to filter nutrient-rich tidal water and grow to full size without touching the seabed.

⚓

A Name Meaning "Extending Harbor"「伸港」地名的由來

The township name "Shenkang" (伸港) means "extending harbor," reflecting its historical role as a minor coastal port during the Qing Dynasty era. Goods and settlers once passed through this stretch of shoreline, shaping the community that still lives here today.

🦀

Famous Autumn Crab Season秋冬螃蟹季吸引饕客

Local crab season in autumn and winter draws seafood lovers from Taichung and Changhua City to Shenkang's coastal restaurants and leisure farms. The blue crabs and mud crabs caught in the estuary are prized for their rich roe and sweet meat.

🌆

Gateway to Greater Taichung大台中都會圈的邊緣

Shenkang borders Taichung's Wuqi District to the north, making it part of the greater metropolitan fringe. This unique position means residents enjoy the quiet coastal lifestyle while remaining just a short drive from one of Taiwan's largest urban centers.

💨

Offshore Wind Turbine Views海上風力發電的美景

The coastal path along Shenkang's shoreline offers sweeping views of the offshore wind turbines now dotting the Taiwan Strait. These towering turbines are part of Taiwan's push toward renewable energy, and on clear days they create a striking skyline that blends nature with modern technology.
