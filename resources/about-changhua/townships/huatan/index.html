What Huatan Township is famous for 特色

Huatan ("Flower Platform") is anchored by Hushanyuan (虎山岩), a centuries-old stone-carved Buddhist cave temple carved into the hillside. The township borders the Bagua Plateau and is a gateway for hikers heading up to the Bagua Trail. Jasmine flower farms here once supplied Taiwan's tea-flower blending industry.

花壇鄉以虎山岩為核心地標——這座開鑿於山壁之中、歷史悠久的石刻佛教洞窟廟宇，見證了數百年的信仰傳承。花壇鄉緊鄰八卦台地，是登山客前往八卦山步道的重要入口。這裡的茉莉花農場在過去曾大量供應台灣茶葉窨花產業所需的花材。

Places to visit 值得一遊

⛩️

Hushanyuan Cave Temple虎山岩

One of Changhua's most dramatic religious sites, this Buddhist temple was hewn directly into a basalt hillside starting in the 18th century. Narrow passages lead worshippers deeper into the rock, where the innermost shrine is carved into living stone. The surrounding forested hillside is lined with stone steps and smaller shrines.

🥾

Bagua Trail Trailheads八卦山步道入口

Huatan sits at the foot of the Bagua Plateau, making it a popular starting point for hikers tackling the famous Bagua Trail. The trail offers sweeping views over the Changhua Plain and the Taiwan Strait, especially stunning at sunrise. Fitness walkers and nature lovers of all levels frequent these well-maintained paths.

🏘️

Huatan Old Street花壇老街

This historic street preserves the quiet character of a traditional Taiwanese rural market town. Local vendors sell seasonal produce, handmade snacks, and goods that reflect the agricultural heritage of the township. Walking the old street gives visitors a sense of everyday life in central Taiwan unchanged for generations.

🙏

Wende Temple文德宮

A well-regarded community temple dedicated to local deities, Wende Temple serves as a spiritual and social gathering place for Huatan residents. Its colorful architecture and intricate roof carvings are fine examples of Taiwanese temple craftsmanship. Annual festivals here draw both locals and visitors from neighboring townships.

Did you know? 你知道嗎？

Six facts about Huatan Township — perfect for quizzes and classroom discussions.

六個關於花壇鄉的趣味知識，適合課堂問答。

⛏️

A Temple Carved in Stone石中鑿出的廟宇

Hushanyuan (Tiger Mountain Rock) was first carved by monks in the 18th century; its innermost shrine is cut directly into living stone.

🌅

Best Sunrise Hike最佳日出登山路線

The trail from Huatan up Bagua Mountain is considered one of Changhua's best sunrise hiking routes, drawing early risers from across the county.

🌸

Taiwan's Jasmine Farms台灣的茉莉花農場

Jasmine (茉莉花) cultivation here peaked in the 1970s; some family farms still grow it for specialty teas, keeping this fragrant tradition alive.

🌋

Where Geology Changes Fast地質急速轉變之處

Huatan is where the Changhua Plain meets the Bagua Plateau — geology shifts from alluvial soil to red basalt within just a few hundred meters.

📚

County's Oldest School縣內最古老的學塾

The local Confucian school (義學) dates to 1774, making it one of the oldest community schools in Changhua County — over 250 years of learning.

🌼

Annual Flower Market年度花卉社區市集

The township hosts an annual flower-based community market each spring, celebrating its floral heritage and bringing neighbors together for local crafts and food.
