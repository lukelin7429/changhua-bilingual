What Puyan Township is famous for 特色

Puyan (埔鹽, "Plains Salt") sits in the heart of Changhua's flat agricultural plain. The township is a patchwork of rice paddies, corn fields, and lotus ponds — a classic central Taiwan agricultural landscape. Local aquaculture ponds support tilapia and freshwater eel farming. The annual lotus bloom season draws photographers from across the county.

埔鹽鄉位於彰化縣平原的核心地帶，地名源自昔日將食鹽從沿海運往內陸平埔的歷史記憶。鄉內稻田、玉米田與蓮花池交錯成景，呈現出台灣中部最典型的農業風光。當地的養殖漁業以吳郭魚與淡水鰻魚為主，而每年盛夏的蓮花盛開季節，更吸引縣內各地的攝影愛好者前來記錄這片粉色花海。

Places to visit 值得一遊

🌸

Puyan Lotus Fields埔鹽蓮花田

Every July and August, Puyan's aquaculture ponds burst into bloom with thousands of pink and white lotus flowers. Photographers and families flock to the roadside fields at dawn to catch the blossoms before the summer heat sets in.

🐟

Freshwater Fish Farms淡水養殖漁場

Puyan's low-lying land is dotted with aquaculture ponds where tilapia and freshwater eel are raised. These working farms are a window into Taiwan's inland fishing industry, supplying seafood to restaurants and markets across the island.

🏮

Puyan Mazu Temple埔鹽媽祖廟

This traditional folk religion temple is the spiritual center of community life in Puyan. The temple hosts colorful seasonal festivals and processions that bring together residents from across the township in celebration and worship.

🏡

Traditional Farmland Landscapes農村田園景觀

Wandering the rural lanes of Puyan, visitors encounter classic three-sided courtyard farmhouses (三合院), winding irrigation channels, and open rice paddies that change color with every season — lush green in summer, golden at harvest time.

Did you know? 你知道嗎？

Six facts about Puyan Township — perfect for quizzes and classroom discussions.

六個關於埔鹽鄉的趣味知識，適合課堂問答。

🧂

A Name with Salt History地名藏鹽的歷史

Puyan's name records the historical practice of salt distribution (鹽) from coastal depots to inland plains (埔). The name is a reminder that trade and commerce shaped Taiwan's villages long before modern roads existed.

🪷

Lotus Goes Commercial蓮花變成商機

Lotus farming here began as a decorative crop but expanded when lotus seed (蓮子) and lotus root (蓮藕) became commercially valuable. Today both the flower and its edible parts are important local products.

📸

Pink Ponds in Summer夏日粉紅花海

July and August lotus blooms turn Puyan's pond edges into a photogenic pink landscape. The fleeting season lasts only a few weeks, making every visit feel like a rare and special event worth capturing.

🍣

Eels Go to Japan鰻魚供應日本料理

The freshwater eel (鰻魚) farms here supply eel to Japanese restaurants across Taiwan. Eel farming requires careful water management and is considered one of the more demanding forms of aquaculture in the region.

🏠

Old Courtyard Farmhouses保留三合院古厝

Many Puyan families maintain traditional three-sided courtyard farmhouses (三合院). These U-shaped homes, built around a central courtyard, reflect generations of family life and are increasingly rare in modernizing Taiwan.

📍

Center of the Plain平原地理中心

The township sits at the geographic center of the county plain, equidistant from the coast and the plateau. This central position made Puyan a natural crossroads for agricultural trade across Changhua's historic farming communities.
