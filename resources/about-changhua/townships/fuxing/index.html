What Fuxing Township is famous for 特色

Fuxing is famous for the Fuxing Granary (福興穀倉), a Japanese-era warehouse complex now converted into a cultural arts space. The township's rice paddies are among the most productive in the county. Fukuang Village hosts a well-preserved cluster of traditional red-brick Hokkien farmhouses — a rare surviving example of early 20th-century rural architecture.

福興鄉以福興穀倉聞名——這座日治時期的倉庫群如今已改建為文化藝術空間。鄉內的稻田是全縣生產力最高的農地之一。福光村保存了一批紅磚閩南式農舍，是二十世紀初期農村建築的珍貴遺跡，列為縣定文化資產。

Places to visit 值得一遊

🏛️

Fuxing Granary Cultural Park福興穀倉文創園區

Built in 1914 during the Japanese colonial era, this historic granary complex once served as the main rice storage facility for the entire Changhua region. Today it has been lovingly restored into a vibrant cultural and arts venue hosting markets, exhibitions, and local craft studios.

🧱

Fukuang Hokkien Farmhouses福光村閩南古厝

Fukuang Village is home to a remarkably well-preserved cluster of traditional red-brick farmhouses built by early Hokkien settlers. These century-old structures feature classic southern Fujian architectural details — ornate ridge tiles, courtyard layouts, and earthen walls — and are protected on the county heritage list.

🙏

Fuxing Tianhou Temple福興天后宮

This local Mazu temple is the spiritual heart of the community, dedicated to the sea goddess who protects fishermen and travelers. The temple hosts vibrant annual festivals with traditional music, lantern processions, and elaborate offerings that draw visitors from across the county.

🍚

Rice Museum米文化館

This small but fascinating museum celebrates Fuxing's deep connection to rice cultivation, tracing the history of paddy farming in the region from ancient methods to modern techniques. Visitors can learn about the different varieties of rice grown locally and sample some of the premium 彰化米 that the township is proud to produce.

Did you know? 你知道嗎？

Six facts about Fuxing Township — perfect for quizzes and classroom discussions.

六個關於福興鄉的趣味知識，適合課堂問答。

🏚️

Granary Built in 1914穀倉建於1914年

The Fuxing Granary was built in 1914 and once stored rice for the entire Japanese colonial administration in Changhua. It was one of the most important agricultural infrastructure sites in the region during that era.

💨

Natural Cooling Without Electricity無電力天然通風

The granary's ventilation design keeps rice dry without modern HVAC — a clever pre-electricity cooling system that used airflow channels and raised flooring to regulate temperature and humidity naturally, protecting the rice harvest year-round.

🧱

Heritage Red-Brick Farmhouses縣定文化資產古厝

Fukuang Village's red-brick farmhouses are on the county heritage list, recognizing their outstanding historical and architectural significance. These structures are among the most intact examples of early Hokkien rural settlement architecture still standing in Changhua County.

🌾

Premium "Changhua Rice" Brand彰化米品牌

Fuxing produces a variety of rice labeled 彰化米 sold under premium branding. The fertile paddies and ideal growing conditions of the township contribute to its reputation for high-quality, fragrant rice prized by consumers across Taiwan.

🌊

Wetlands Meet Rice Paddies濕地與稻田交匯

The township straddles both coastal wetland and inland paddy zones, creating a unique dual landscape. This geographic diversity supports a rich variety of bird species and aquatic life alongside the productive agricultural fields that define Fuxing's character.

🛒

Weekend Slow Food Market週末慢食市集

A community-organized "slow food" market at the granary runs on weekend mornings, bringing together local farmers, artisan food producers, and crafters. It celebrates the agricultural heritage of the township and offers visitors a taste of authentic Fuxing flavors and handmade goods.
