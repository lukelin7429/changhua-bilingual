What Tianwei Township is famous for 特色

Tianwei is Taiwan's flower capital — its 14 km² of land contains six kilometers of nurseries and garden centers along the Provincial Highway 1 corridor, creating the Tianwei Highway Garden (田尾公路花園). Orchids, roses, hydrangeas, chrysanthemums, and bonsai trees are sold wholesale to florists across the island.

田尾鄉是台灣的花卉之都。在這片僅14平方公里的土地上，沿著省道台一線綿延六公里的苗圃與花市構成了聞名遐邇的「田尾公路花園」。蘭花、玫瑰、繡球花、菊花與盆景樹在此地大量種植，並批發販售給全台各地的花商。

Places to visit 值得一遊

🛣️

Tianwei Highway Garden田尾公路花園

A breathtaking six-kilometer corridor of nurseries and flower shops stretching along Provincial Highway 1. Visitors can cycle or walk through the entire route, discovering hundreds of plant varieties — from tropical orchids to elegant bonsai — all grown by local farming families.

🏡

Tianwei Taiwan Garden台灣花園

A curated botanical showcase garden that celebrates Taiwan's rich horticultural heritage. The garden features themed planting zones, seasonal floral displays, and landscape installations that highlight the diversity of plants cultivated across the island.

🎋

Bonsai Specialty Farms盆景專業農場

Scattered throughout Tianwei are family-run farms specializing in the ancient art of bonsai. These living sculptures — some shaped over decades — represent the patience and mastery of Tianwei's multigenerational farming culture, and many are available for purchase by collectors.

💐

Tianwei Flower Market田尾花卉市場

The bustling wholesale market at the heart of Tianwei's flower economy. Buyers from florists and wedding planners across Taiwan arrive early each morning to select fresh-cut flowers and potted plants. Weekend visitors can also shop retail at stalls lining the market entrance.

Did you know? 你知道嗎？

Six facts about Tianwei Township — perfect for quizzes and classroom discussions.

六個關於田尾鄉的趣味知識，適合課堂問答。

🚲

Cycle 6 km of Flowers六公里花廊

The Tianwei Public Road Garden (公路花園) is 6 km long — you can cycle the entire nursery corridor without repeating a plant species.

📐

Smallest Rural Township彰化縣最小鄉鎮

Tianwei is the smallest rural township in Changhua County by area (14.1 km²), yet it punches far above its weight in agricultural output and tourism.

👴

Three Generations of Farmers三代傳承

The flower industry here employs three generations of the same families — skills passed from grandparents to grandchildren, preserving traditional horticultural knowledge.

✈️

Orchids Shipped Worldwide蘭花銷往全球

Orchid export from Tianwei was, at its peak, a multi-million-dollar industry shipping to Japan, the US, and Europe — putting this tiny township on the global map.

🌸

Best Time to Visit最佳造訪季節

The best time to visit is spring (March–April) and autumn (October–November) when temperatures are mild and flowers are at peak bloom across the entire corridor.

🚜

Pioneers of Garden Tourism農業旅遊先驅

Tianwei farmers pioneered the concept of "garden tourism" (農業旅遊) in Taiwan in the 1980s, transforming working farms into visitor destinations decades before agritourism became a national trend.
