What Fangyuan Township is famous for 特色

Fangyuan is Changhua's largest township by area and its oyster capital. The Wanggong Fishing Port (王功漁港) — a village within Fangyuan — is the county's most famous coastal attraction, offering ox-cart tidal flat tours, oyster harvesting, and the red-and-white Wanggong Lighthouse. Fangyuan's vast tidal flats are also a key offshore wind energy zone.

芳苑鄉是彰化縣面積最大的鄉鎮，也是縣內最重要的蚵仔產地。位於鄉內的王功漁港是彰化最知名的海岸景點，以牛車蚵田體驗、採蚵活動以及紅白相間的王功燈塔聞名遐邇。芳苑廣闊的潮間帶更是台灣發展離岸風力發電的重點區域之一。

Places to visit 值得一遊

⚓

Wanggong Fishing Port王功漁港

One of the most scenic fishing harbors on Taiwan's west coast, Wanggong Port is famous for its colorful fishing boats and bustling seafood market. At low tide, the vast mudflats stretch for kilometers, creating a dramatic landscape that turns golden at sunset. It is the perfect starting point for exploring Fangyuan's coastal culture.

🚜

Ox-Cart Tidal Flat Tours牛車蚵田體驗

Hop aboard a traditional ox-drawn cart and ride out across the tidal mudflats to visit working oyster beds — an experience found nowhere else in Taiwan. Farmers guide visitors to learn how oysters are cultivated, harvested, and processed using generations-old methods. This living agricultural tradition is one of Fangyuan's most beloved and irreplaceable attractions.

🏮

Wanggong Lighthouse王功燈塔

Built in 1966, the striking red-and-white striped Wanggong Lighthouse is the only lighthouse in Changhua County. Standing at the edge of the tidal flats, it serves both as a navigation beacon for fishing boats and as a popular spot for birdwatching and photography. The view from its base — looking west toward the Taiwan Strait at dusk — is unforgettable.

🦅

Fangyuan Wetland Bird Reserve芳苑濕地鳥類保護區

The expansive intertidal wetlands of Fangyuan are a critical stopover for migratory shorebirds traveling along the East Asian-Australasian Flyway. Rare and endangered species such as the Black-faced Spoonbill and Kentish Plover have been spotted here during peak migration seasons. Birdwatchers from across Taiwan and beyond come to observe hundreds of species in their natural coastal habitat.

Did you know? 你知道嗎？

Six facts about Fangyuan Township — perfect for quizzes and classroom discussions.

六個關於芳苑鄉的趣味知識，適合課堂問答。

📸

Taiwan's Most-Photographed Coast最上鏡的海岸村落

Wanggong (王功) is Changhua's most photographed coastal village, famous for its unique ox-cart rides to harvest oysters in the tidal flats.

📐

Changhua's Biggest Township彰化最大的鄉鎮

Fangyuan is the largest township in Changhua County by area (96.4 km²) — larger than some counties in other parts of Taiwan.

🏮

County's Only Lighthouse彰化唯一的燈塔

The Wanggong Lighthouse (built 1966) is the county's only lighthouse and doubles as a bird-watching lookout overlooking the vast tidal flats.

🦪

Fatter, Sweeter Oysters更肥美的蚵仔

Fangyuan oysters (蚵仔) grow in colder coastal waters, which yields fattier, sweeter oysters than southern Taiwan varieties — a gourmet secret locals love to share.

💨

Offshore Wind Energy Hub離岸風電重鎮

The offshore wind farm visible from Wanggong Beach is part of Taiwan's ambitious renewable energy expansion, harnessing the powerful winds of the Taiwan Strait.

🐂

One-of-a-Kind Ox-Cart Tours台灣唯一牛車農遊

Ox-cart tours are the only agricultural vehicle tours of their kind still operating in Taiwan — a living tradition that connects visitors directly to coastal farming heritage.
