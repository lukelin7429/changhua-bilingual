What Puxin Township is famous for 特色

Puxin is known across Taiwan for its Rose Garden (玫瑰花園) and the Taiwan Sugar Corporation's historic Puxin Farm (埔心牧場), now a popular agri-tourism destination with cattle, pony rides, and a dairy café. Roses grown here supply florists island-wide, making Puxin and Tianwei the county's dual flower township.

埔心鄉以玫瑰花園和台灣糖業公司歷史悠久的埔心牧場聞名全台。埔心牧場現已成為熱門的農業觀光景點，遊客可以欣賞牛群、體驗小馬騎乘，並在乳品咖啡館享用新鮮牧場美食。當地種植的玫瑰花供應全島花店，使埔心與田尾並列彰化縣的花鄉雙璧。

Places to visit 值得一遊

🌹

Puxin Rose Garden玫瑰花園

A fragrant wonderland where rows of colorful roses bloom season after season, drawing visitors and photographers from across Taiwan. The garden supplies cut roses to florists island-wide, and peaks in spring when hundreds of varieties burst into color. It is one of the best places in Changhua County to experience flower farming up close.

🐄

Taiwan Sugar Puxin Farm & Dairy台糖埔心牧場

Originally established by the Taiwan Sugar Corporation in 1950 as a livestock operation, this sprawling farm has transformed into a beloved family destination. Visitors can hand-feed cattle, enjoy pony rides, sample fresh dairy ice cream, and learn how milk is produced in Taiwan. The shaded walking paths and open green fields make it a perfect weekend outing.

🏯

Puxin Guangfu Temple埔心廣福宮

A traditional Taiwanese folk-religion temple at the heart of community life in Puxin, dedicated to the local earth gods and protective deities. The temple's ornate roof carvings and incense-filled halls reflect centuries of local devotion and Taiwanese craftsmanship. During major festivals, the temple square fills with lanterns, music, and religious processions that are a vivid window into Changhua culture.

🏡

Traditional Farmhouses傳統農舍聚落

Scattered across the flat rice paddies of Puxin, old Taiwanese red-brick farmhouses offer a glimpse of rural life as it was lived for generations on the west plain. Some families still practice traditional rice farming and operate small roadside stands selling seasonal produce. Walking or cycling along the township's country lanes past these farmhouses is a peaceful way to experience everyday life in rural Changhua.

Did you know? 你知道嗎？

Six facts about Puxin Township — perfect for quizzes and classroom discussions.

六個關於埔心鄉的趣味知識，適合課堂問答。

🏭

Sugarcane to Dairy Farm蔗糖廠變牧場

Taiwan Sugar Corporation's Puxin Farm (est. 1950) was originally a sugarcane processing station repurposed as a livestock farm.

✈️

Roses Fly to Japan玫瑰花銷日港

The rose farms here export cut roses to Japan and Hong Kong, particularly around Valentine's Day when demand for fresh blooms surges.

🥛

Rare Dairy Cows Here罕見的乳牛鄉

Puxin is one of the few townships in Changhua where you can find dairy cows — a rare sight on Taiwan's west plain where crop farming dominates.

🧵

Textile Corridor Connection紡織走廊相連

The local 社頭-埔心 textile corridor extends from neighboring Shetou through Puxin, linking the two townships in a shared tradition of fabric weaving.

⛰️

Bagua Mountain Gateway八卦山東側入口

Puxin shares the Baguashan Scenic Area eastern boundary, giving residents and visitors access to Bagua Mountain hiking trails and scenic overlooks.

🌸

Spring Rose Market Blooms春季玫瑰市集

A community "Rose Market" (玫瑰市集) pops up each spring, selling rose jams, oils, and fresh bouquets — a festive celebration of Puxin's flower farming heritage.
