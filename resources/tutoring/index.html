Tutoring Lesson
Library.

Ready-to-teach 60-minute kits for one-on-one sessions — open any lesson and teach straight from it. Warm-ups, key phrases you can hear out loud, leveled questions, an activity and tutor tips. No preparation needed.

可立即上手的 60 分鐘一對一教案——打開就能教。暖身、可朗讀的關鍵句、分級問題、活動與老師小提醒，免備課。英文為主、中文輔助。
