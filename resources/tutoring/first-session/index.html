Before you start tutor prep

開始前 · 給老師的準備

This part is for you, not class time. Sixty seconds before the student joins: 這部分是給你的，不是上課時間。學生上線前的六十秒：

  • Test your camera, microphone, and headset. Sit somewhere quiet and well-lit.測試你的鏡頭、麥克風和耳機。坐在安靜、光線充足的地方。
  • Have this page open. You'll teach straight from it, top to bottom.這一頁保持開著，直接照著從上到下上課。
  • Your one goal today: make the student feel comfortable and talking. Everything else is a bonus.今天唯一的目標：讓學生覺得自在、願意開口說話。其他都是加分。
Mindset: In the first lesson, don't correct every mistake — it can make a shy student shut down. Smile, nod, and react warmly to every answer. Let the student talk about 70% of the time, and don't be afraid of a little silence — give them time to think. 心法：第一堂課別糾正每個錯誤——這會讓害羞的學生封閉起來。微笑、點頭，對每個回答都熱情回應。讓學生說大約七成的話，也別怕一點點沉默——給他時間思考。

1 · Warm-up — say hello 5 min

暖身 · 打聲招呼

Start with very easy questions so the student gets an early "win." Ask one, then really listen: 用非常簡單的問題開始，讓學生一開始就有「成功」的感覺。問一個問題，然後真的去聽：

  • Hi! How are you today?嗨！你今天好嗎？
  • Can you see and hear me okay?你看得到、聽得到我嗎？
  • What's the weather like where you are?你那邊的天氣怎麼樣？
  • What did you have for breakfast (or lunch) today?你今天早餐（或午餐）吃了什麼？
Tip: React to every answer — "Oh, nice!", "Me too!", "That sounds good!" Warmth in the first two minutes sets the tone for the whole hour. 小提醒：對每個回答都有反應——「喔，真好！」「我也是！」「聽起來不錯！」前兩分鐘的熱情，會定下整堂課的基調。

2 · Meet your tutor — you go first 8–10 min

認識你的老師 · 你先示範

Introduce yourself first. When the student hears the pattern from you, they'll find it much easier to do their own. Speak slowly and use these simple frames: 先介紹你自己。當學生先聽到你的句型，他要自己說時就會容易許多。放慢速度，用這些簡單的句型：

My name is ___. You can call me ___.

I'm from ___. (your city and country)

In my family, there are ___ people.

In my free time, I like ___.

My favorite food is ___.

I'm really happy to be your tutor!

Then hand it over gently: "Do you have any questions for me?" — this invites the student to speak without pressure. 然後溫和地交棒：「你有什麼想問我的嗎？」——這能在沒有壓力的情況下邀請學生開口。

Tip: Keep it short and friendly (a minute or two). You're modeling, not giving a speech. If you can, share a photo of your hometown or pet — students love that. 小提醒：保持簡短又親切（一兩分鐘）。你是在示範，不是在演講。可以的話，分享一張家鄉或寵物的照片——學生很喜歡。

3 · Key phrases for introducing yourself 8 min

自我介紹的關鍵句 · 點 🔊 帶讀

Practice these together: you say it, the student repeats. Tap 🔊 to hear the pronunciation. 一起練習這些句子：你說，學生跟著重複。點 🔊 聽發音。

  • Nice to meet you.很高興認識你。 Nice to meet you! I'm your tutor.
  • My name is… / You can call me…我的名字是…／你可以叫我… My name is Daniel. You can call me Dan.
  • I'm from…我來自… I'm from Changhua, Taiwan.
  • I'm ___ years old.我＿＿＿歲。 I'm ten years old.
  • I like… / I love…我喜歡…／我很愛… I love basketball and drawing.
  • My favorite ___ is ___.我最喜歡的＿＿＿是＿＿＿。 My favorite color is blue.
  • I have ___ brothers and sisters.我有＿＿＿個兄弟姊妹。 I have one brother and one sister.
  • Can you say that again, please?可以請你再說一次嗎？ Teach this one — it lets the student ask for help politely.
  • How do you say ___ in English?＿＿＿的英文怎麼說？ How do you say 謝謝 in English? — "Thank you!"
Tip: Teaching "Can you say that again?" early gives shy students a safe way to keep going instead of freezing. Praise them when they use it. 小提醒：早一點教「Can you say that again?」，能給害羞的學生一個安全的方式繼續下去，而不是卡住不動。他用到的時候要稱讚他。

4 · All about you — student's turn 12–15 min

關於你 · 換學生說

Now the student introduces themselves, using the same frames. Start with the easy questions; move up only if they're comfortable. 現在換學生用同樣的句型自我介紹。從簡單的問題開始，學生覺得自在時再往上加。

BEGINNER 初級
  • What's your name? How old are you?你叫什麼名字？你幾歲？
  • Where are you from?你來自哪裡？
  • Do you like school? What's your favorite subject?你喜歡學校嗎？你最喜歡的科目是什麼？
INTERMEDIATE 中級
  • Tell me about your family.跟我說說你的家庭。
  • What do you usually do after school?你放學後通常做什麼？
  • What are you good at?你擅長什麼？
ADVANCED 進階
  • What's a normal day like for you?你平常的一天是什麼樣子？
  • What's something you're proud of?有什麼事是你引以為傲的？
  • If you could change one thing about your week, what would it be?如果可以改變你這一週的一件事，你會改什麼？
Tip: This is where you quietly gauge their level (see Tutor notes). Don't rush — give them time to build full sentences, and gently feed a word if they're stuck. 小提醒：這裡就是你默默判斷學生程度的地方（見老師筆記）。別急——給他時間組出完整句子，卡住時溫和地餵一個字。

5 · Let's talk — favorites & free time 10–12 min

換你說 · 喜好與空閒時間

Keep the conversation going around things they enjoy — it's the easiest way to get a student talking. 圍繞著學生喜歡的事情持續對話——這是讓學生開口最容易的方法。

BEGINNER 初級
  • What's your favorite food? Color? Animal?你最喜歡的食物是什麼？顏色呢？動物呢？
  • Do you like music? What kind?你喜歡音樂嗎？哪一種？
INTERMEDIATE 中級
  • What do you like to do on weekends?你週末喜歡做什麼？
  • What's a movie or show you love? Why do you like it?有沒有你很愛的電影或影集？你為什麼喜歡它？
ADVANCED 進階
  • If you had a totally free day, what would you do?如果你有完全自由的一天，你會做什麼？
  • Is there a hobby you'd love to try? What stops you?有沒有一個你很想嘗試的興趣？是什麼讓你卻步？
Tip: Every time they answer, follow up with "Why?" or "Tell me more." That one extra question is where the real speaking practice happens. 小提醒：每次學生回答後，都接著問「為什麼？」或「再多說一點。」真正的口說練習，就發生在這多問的一句裡。

6 · Activity — find 3 things in common 8–10 min

活動 · 找出三個共同點

A fun game to build rapport. The goal: discover three things you and your student both like or both do. 一個建立默契的有趣遊戲。目標：找出你和學生三個都喜歡或都會做的事。

  1. Take turns asking: "Do you like ___?" or "Do you ___?"輪流問：「Do you like ___?」或「Do you ___?」
  2. When you both say "yes," that's one thing in common!當你們兩個都說「yes」，那就是一個共同點！
  3. Keep going until you've found three.繼續下去，直到找出三個為止。

You: "Do you like pizza?" — Student: "Yes!" — You: "Me too! That's one!" 🍕

End with: "We both like ___, ___, and ___. We have a lot in common!"

Tip: Let the student ask you questions too — practicing question forms ("Do you…?") is great speaking practice, and it makes the lesson feel like a real conversation, not a test. 小提醒：也讓學生問你問題——練習問句句型（「Do you…?」）是很好的口說練習，也讓這堂課像真正的對話，而不是考試。

7 · Wrap-up — end on a high note 5 min

收尾 · 在高點結束
  • Ask the student to tell you 2–3 things they remember about you (a gentle, low-pressure review).請學生告訴你關於你他記得的 2～3 件事（一個溫和、低壓力的複習）。
  • Give honest, specific praise: "You spoke in full sentences today — great job!"給予真誠、具體的稱讚：「你今天用完整的句子說話——做得很棒！」
  • Let them choose what's next: "What would you like to talk about next time — food, travel, or your hometown?" When students pick the topic, they show up excited.讓學生選下次的主題：「下次你想聊什麼——食物、旅行，還是你的家鄉？」學生自己選主題時，他會帶著期待出現。
  • Say a warm goodbye and confirm your next session.溫暖地道別，並確認下一次的上課時間。
Tip: The last 30 seconds are what they'll remember. Smile, use their name, and tell them you're looking forward to next time. 小提醒：最後 30 秒是學生會記住的。微笑、叫出他的名字，並告訴他你很期待下一次。

🧭 Tutor notes — reading your student's level

老師筆記 · 判讀學生的程度

Use this first session to figure out their level, so you can plan the next one. Jot down what you notice: 用這第一堂課判斷學生的程度，好規劃下一堂。把你注意到的記下來：

BEGINNER LOOKS LIKE… 初級
  • Short answers, single words; needs lots of repetition and help.回答很短、單字為主；需要大量重複和協助。
  • Next time: more vocabulary, simple yes/no and this-or-that questions, plenty of modeling.下次：更多單字、簡單的 yes/no 與二選一問句、大量示範。
INTERMEDIATE LOOKS LIKE… 中級
  • Full sentences with some hesitation; can answer "why."能講完整句子，偶有遲疑；能回答「為什麼」。
  • Next time: short readings, opinion questions, simple role-plays.下次：短篇閱讀、意見類問題、簡單的角色扮演。
ADVANCED LOOKS LIKE… 進階
  • Speaks freely, asks you questions back, handles abstract topics.能自在地說、會反問你問題、能處理抽象的主題。
  • Next time: discussions, current events, longer conversations and debates.下次：討論、時事、更長的對話與辯論。
Most important: write down their name, interests, and 1–2 topics they enjoyed — and bring those back next time. Nothing builds rapport like a tutor who remembers. 最重要：記下學生的名字、興趣，以及他喜歡的 1～2 個主題——下次再把這些提起來。沒有什麼比「會記得的老師」更能建立默契。
