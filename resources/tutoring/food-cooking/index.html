Food & Cooking

食物與烹飪

Everyone eats, so this is one of the easiest topics to get a shy student talking. Your student shares the food they love, and you share yours too — it's a warm back-and-forth, not a quiz.

⏱ ~60 minutes 🎯 All levels 🔊 Tap to hear any phrase

Before you start tutor prep

開始前 · 給老師的兩分鐘準備

This lesson needs no preparation. Skim it once, keep this page open in your browser, and tap any 🔊 button if you want to hear how a phrase sounds before you say it. Here is your quick checklist: 這堂課不用準備。先快速看過一遍，把這一頁開在瀏覽器裡，想先聽某句話怎麼念就點任何 🔊 按鈕。以下是你的快速檢查清單：

  • Open this page and your Google Meet window side by side.把這一頁和你的 Google Meet 視窗並排打開。
  • Have a glass of water nearby — talking about food makes everyone hungry!手邊放一杯水——聊食物會讓大家都餓起來！
  • Think of one or two dishes you love so you can share when it's your turn.想一兩道你喜歡的菜，輪到你時就能分享。
  • Turn your camera on and smile; a relaxed face helps a nervous student relax too.打開鏡頭並微笑；放鬆的表情也能讓緊張的學生放鬆。
Mindset: Let your student do about 70% of the talking. Don't correct every small mistake — if you understood them, that's a win. Jump in only when a fix really helps. And remember: this is a two-way chat, so share your own favorite foods too. That keeps it friendly and natural, not like an interview. 心法：讓學生說大約七成的話。別糾正每個小錯誤——只要你聽懂了，就是成功。真正有幫助時再介入。還有記得：這是雙向的聊天，所以也分享你自己愛吃的食物。這樣才友善自然，而不像在面試。

1 · Warm-up 5 min

暖身 · 輕鬆開場

Start gently. These are easy questions just to get your student speaking and comfortable with your voice. 輕鬆開始。這些是簡單的問題，只是要讓學生開口、習慣你的聲音。

  • Hi! How are you today?嗨！你今天好嗎？
  • Are you hungry right now, or did you just eat?你現在餓了嗎，還是剛吃飽？
  • What did you eat today? Tell me one thing.你今天吃了什麼？跟我說一樣。
  • Do you like to eat at home, or do you like to eat out?你喜歡在家吃，還是喜歡外食？
Tip: If the student gives a short answer, gently ask "Why?" or "Tell me more." Then share your own quick answer too — "Me? I just had coffee!" — so it feels like a real chat. 小提醒：學生回答太短時，溫和地問「為什麼？」或「再多說一點」。然後也分享你自己的簡短回答——「我嗎？我剛喝了咖啡！」——這樣才像真的聊天。

2 · Key words & phrases 8 min

關鍵單字與句子 · 點 🔊 帶讀

Read each word aloud together. Tap 🔊 to hear it, then have the student repeat and say the example sentence. Don't rush — clear and slow is better than fast. 一起把每個字念出來。點 🔊 聽發音，再請學生跟著念，並說出例句。別急——清楚而慢比快更好。

  • meal一餐 Dinner is my favorite meal of the day.
  • breakfast早餐 I eat breakfast at seven in the morning.
  • lunch午餐 We had rice and chicken for lunch.
  • dinner晚餐 My family eats dinner together every night.
  • recipe食譜 My grandmother gave me this recipe.
  • ingredient食材；原料 Egg is the main ingredient in this dish.
  • to cook烹煮；做菜 I like to cook on the weekend.
  • to fry煎；炒；炸 First, fry the onions in the pan.
  • to bake烘烤 I love to bake bread at home.
  • delicious美味的 This soup is really delicious!
  • spicy / sweet / sour辣的／甜的／酸的 I like spicy food, but not too sour.
  • My favorite dish is ___我最喜歡的一道菜是＿＿＿ My favorite dish is beef noodle soup.
  • I usually eat ___我午餐／平常通常吃＿＿＿ I usually eat noodles for lunch.
Tip: After each word, ask the student to use it in their own sentence. "Can you make a sentence with spicy?" Personal sentences stick much better than repeating yours. 小提醒：每個字之後，請學生用它造自己的句子。「你能用 spicy 造一個句子嗎？」自己造的句子比重複你的句子記得更牢。

3 · Read & talk 8 min

讀與說 · 你念 A，學生念 B，再交換

Read this short conversation together. You take part A, the student takes part B — then switch roles and read it again. Tap 🔊 on any line to hear it first. 一起念這段短對話。你念 A，學生念 B——然後交換角色再念一遍。點任何一句的 🔊 可以先聽。

A

What is your favorite food?

你最喜歡的食物是什麼？
B

My favorite dish is dumplings. I love them!

我最喜歡的一道菜是水餃。我超愛！
A

Nice! Are they hard to make?

不錯耶！它們很難做嗎？
B

Not really. You need flour, pork, and some vegetables.

其實還好。你需要麵粉、豬肉和一些蔬菜。
A

What do you do first?

你會先做什麼？
B

First, you mix the meat and vegetables together.

首先，把肉和蔬菜拌在一起。
A

And then?

然後呢？
B

Then you fold them and boil them in hot water.

然後把它們包起來，放進熱水裡煮。
A

That sounds delicious. I want to try!

聽起來好好吃。我想試試看！
B

You should! They are so good with soy sauce.

你該試試！它們配醬油超讚。
Tip: After reading, close the script and ask the student to tell you the steps from memory. Then tell them about a dish you know how to make, using the same simple words like first and then. 小提醒：念完後，把腳本關掉，請學生憑記憶說出步驟。然後跟他說一道你會做的菜，用一樣簡單的字像 first 和 then。

4 · Let's talk 12 min

換你說 · 依程度選一組，問、聽、回應，也分享自己

Pick the level that fits your student. Ask, listen, and react — and share your own answers too. If one level feels too easy or too hard, just move up or down. 選擇適合學生的程度。問、聽、回應——也分享你自己的答案。如果某個程度太簡單或太難，往上或往下移即可。

BEGINNER 初級
  • What is your favorite food?你最喜歡的食物是什麼？
  • What do you usually eat for breakfast?你早餐通常吃什麼？
  • Do you like spicy food?你喜歡吃辣嗎？
INTERMEDIATE 中級
  • Tell me about a meal you really enjoyed. Where were you?跟我說一頓你真的很享受的餐。你那時在哪裡？
  • What food do you eat when you are happy or sad?你開心或難過時會吃什麼食物？
  • Is there any food you don't like? Why?有沒有你不喜歡的食物？為什麼？
ADVANCED 進階
  • How have your eating habits changed in the last few years?這幾年來你的飲食習慣有什麼改變？
  • Do you think food in your city is getting better or worse? Why?你覺得你所在城市的食物變得更好還是更差？為什麼？
  • If you could only eat one cuisine for the rest of your life, what would you choose, and what would you miss?如果這輩子只能吃一種料理，你會選哪種？又會懷念什麼？
Tip: Whatever the student says, answer the same question yourself in one or two sentences. "For me, breakfast is just toast and coffee — what about you?" This keeps the energy warm and balanced. 小提醒：無論學生說什麼，你也用一兩句回答同一個問題。「對我來說，早餐就是吐司加咖啡——那你呢？」這樣能讓氣氛溫暖又平衡。

5 · Going deeper 10 min

再深入一點 · 鼓勵更長的回答

Now push for a little more detail. Encourage longer answers, and don't be afraid of a short, friendly silence while the student thinks. 現在多挖一點細節。鼓勵更長的回答，學生思考時短短的、友善的沉默不用怕。

BEGINNER 初級
  • Describe one dish you like. What color is it? Is it hot or cold?描述一道你喜歡的菜。它是什麼顏色？是熱的還是冷的？
  • Who cooks at your home?你家是誰做菜？
INTERMEDIATE 中級
  • Walk me through a dish you love — what's in it, and how does it taste?帶我走一遍一道你愛的菜——裡面有什麼，吃起來如何？
  • Do you cook at home, or do you order food more often? Why?你比較常在家做菜，還是叫外送？為什麼？
ADVANCED 進階
  • Where do you draw the line between healthy food and junk food? Is junk food always bad?健康食物和垃圾食物的界線你怎麼劃分？垃圾食物一定是壞的嗎？
  • Some people say cooking at home is a dying skill. Do you agree?有些人說在家煮飯是正在消失的技能。你同意嗎？
Tip: Listen for one useful word the student is missing, give it to them naturally ("Ah, you mean it's creamy"), and then let them keep talking. One small gift at a time. 小提醒：留意一個學生缺的好用單字，自然地給他（「啊，你是說它很 creamy」），然後讓他繼續說。一次給一個小禮物。

6 · Activity 10 min

活動 · 教我做你最愛的簡單料理

"Teach me to make your favorite simple dish." Ask the student to explain, step by step, how to make something easy they enjoy — fried rice, a sandwich, instant noodles, anything. You are the learner, so ask lots of questions! 「教我做你最愛的簡單料理。」請學生一步一步解釋怎麼做一樣他喜歡的簡單食物——炒飯、三明治、泡麵，什麼都行。你是學的人，所以多問問題！

  1. Ask: "What dish will you teach me today?"問：「你今天要教我做哪道菜？」
  2. Ask what ingredients you need.問需要哪些食材。
  3. Ask for the steps in order: "What do you do first? And then?"依序問步驟：「你會先做什麼？然後呢？」
  4. Repeat the steps back to check you understood.把步驟複述一遍，確認你聽懂了。
  5. Now swap — you teach the student a simple dish from your home.現在交換——你教學生一道你家鄉的簡單料理。

To make ___, you need ___ and ___.

First, you ___.

Then, you ___.

Finally, you ___ and eat it!

It tastes ___.

Tip: Use the sentence frames on screen to help a stuck student. Keep playing the curious learner — "Wait, how much salt?" — and the student will speak more without even noticing. 小提醒：用螢幕上的句型架構幫助卡住的學生。持續扮演好奇的學習者——「等等，要放多少鹽？」——學生就會在不知不覺中說得更多。

7 · Wrap-up 5 min

收尾 · 溫暖正向地結束

End on a warm, positive note so the student leaves feeling good about speaking English. 在溫暖、正向的氣氛中結束，讓學生帶著「我會說英文，感覺很好」的心情離開。

  • Recap: "Today we talked about food and cooking. Tell me one new word you learned."回顧：「今天我們聊了食物和烹飪。跟我說一個你今天學到的新單字。」
  • Praise something specific: "You explained the steps really clearly — nice job!"給具體的稱讚：「你把步驟解釋得好清楚——做得很棒！」
  • Let the student choose: "What topic would you like to talk about next time?"讓學生選：「下次你想聊什麼主題？」
  • Warm goodbye: "Thank you for sharing your favorite food with me. See you next time!"溫暖道別：「謝謝你跟我分享你最愛的食物。下次見！」
Tip: Praise effort, not just accuracy. "You kept going even when it was hard — that's exactly how you improve." Confidence is half the lesson. 小提醒：稱讚努力，而不只是正確。「就算很難你也繼續說——這正是進步的方法。」自信就是這堂課的一半。

🧭 Tutor notes

老師筆記 · 各程度的樣貌與下次的點子

A quick look at what each level usually sounds like in this lesson, plus an idea for next time. 快速看一下這堂課裡各程度通常的樣貌，以及下次的一個點子。

BEGINNER 初級
  • Short answers, single words, lots of "yes / no." Give choices ("rice or noodles?") and read the dialogue twice.短答、單字、很多「是／不是」。給選項（「飯還是麵？」），並把對話念兩遍。
  • Next time: a "Hobbies & Free Time" lesson with simple everyday verbs.下次：用簡單日常動詞的「興趣與休閒」課程。
INTERMEDIATE 中級
  • Full sentences with some hesitation. They can describe and explain steps with a little help on vocabulary.能講完整句子，偶有遲疑。在單字上稍加幫忙，他就能描述並解釋步驟。
  • Next time: "Travel & Vacations" — describing a trip they enjoyed.下次：「旅行與假期」——描述一趟他喜歡的旅程。
ADVANCED 進階
  • Comfortable giving opinions and reasons. Push for richer words and follow-up "Why do you think so?" questions.能自在地表達意見和理由。要求更豐富的字彙，並追問「你為什麼這麼想？」
  • Next time: a debate-style angle like "Is fast food ruining home cooking?"下次：辯論式的角度，例如「速食正在毀掉家常菜嗎？」
Most important: The goal isn't perfect grammar — it's a student who finishes the call having spoken a lot and enjoyed it. Stay warm, stay curious, and share your own food stories. That's what keeps them coming back. 最重要：目標不是完美的文法——而是讓學生在通話結束時，覺得自己說了很多、也很享受。保持溫暖、保持好奇，分享你自己的食物故事。這才是讓他一再回來的原因。
