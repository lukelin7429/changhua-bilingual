Night Markets & Street Food

夜市與小吃 · 學生就是最懂的人

Your student practices talking about food, prices, and favorite places while ordering and describing real Taiwanese night-market snacks. Here the student is the expert — be curious, ask questions, and let them teach you about Taiwan.

⏱ ~60 minutes 🎯 All levels 🔊 Tap to hear any phrase

Before you start tutor prep

開始前 · 給老師的兩分鐘準備

Take two minutes before your student joins. A calm, ready tutor helps a nervous student relax. 學生上線前先準備兩分鐘。老師從容，緊張的學生就會放鬆。

  • Test your camera and microphone.先測試你的鏡頭與麥克風。
  • Keep this page open — you will teach straight from it.這一頁保持開著，直接照著上課。
  • Have a glass of water nearby and smile when your student arrives.手邊放杯水，學生進來時先微笑。
  • Tap any 🔊 button to hear a word or phrase out loud.點任何 🔊 按鈕就能聽到該字或句子的發音。
Mindset: Don't over-correct. Let your student do about 70% of the talking. On this topic your student is the expert on Taiwan and you are the curious guest — ask "Really? Tell me more!" far more often than you correct grammar. 心法：別一直糾正。讓學生說大約七成的話。這個主題裡，學生是最懂台灣的人，你是好奇的客人——多問「真的嗎？多說一點！」，少改文法。

1 · Warm-up 5 min

暖身 · 輕鬆開場

Start gently. Ask these out loud and react warmly to every answer. 輕鬆開始。把問題念出來，對每個回答都熱情回應。

  • Do you like night markets? Why or why not?你喜歡夜市嗎？為什麼喜歡或不喜歡？
  • When did you last go to a night market? Who did you go with?你上次去夜市是什麼時候？跟誰去的？
  • Which night market is closest to your home?離你家最近的是哪個夜市？
  • What is one food you always look for at a night market?去夜市你一定會找的一樣食物是什麼？
Tip: If your student gives a short answer, gently echo it back as a question — "Oh, you went with your family? That sounds fun. What did you eat?" This keeps them talking. 小提醒：學生回答太短時，把它當成問題回問——「喔，你跟家人去？聽起來很棒，你們吃了什麼？」這樣能讓他繼續說。

2 · Key words & phrases 8 min

關鍵單字與句子 · 點 🔊 帶讀

Read each word together. Tap 🔊 to model it, then have your student repeat and use the example sentence. 一起念每個字。點 🔊 示範發音，再請學生跟著念，並用例句造句。

  • night market夜市 The night market is busy on Saturday nights.
  • food stall小吃攤 That food stall sells the best dumplings.
  • vendor攤販；老闆 The vendor is cooking noodles right now.
  • fried chicken雞排；炸雞 I always buy a piece of fried chicken first.
  • stinky tofu臭豆腐 Stinky tofu smells strong but tastes great.
  • oyster omelet蚵仔煎 My favorite snack is the oyster omelet.
  • grilled skewer烤串；串燒 She ordered three grilled skewers.
  • crispy酥脆的 The chicken is hot and crispy.
  • spicy辣的 Can you make it a little spicy?
  • How much is it?這個多少錢？ How much is it for one cup?
  • I'd like ___, please / One ___, please我要一份＿＿＿，謝謝 I'd like an oyster omelet, please. One bubble tea, please.
Tip: Ask your student which of these foods they actually like or dislike. Real opinions ("I love stinky tofu, but my friend hates it!") produce far more natural speaking than drills. 小提醒：問學生這些食物他真正喜歡或討厭哪些。真實的意見（「我超愛臭豆腐，但我朋友很討厭！」）比反覆操練更能練出自然的口說。

3 · Read & talk 8 min

讀與說 · A 是客人，B 是老闆

Read this together. A is the customer, B is the vendor. 一起念這段對話。A 是客人，B 是攤販老闆。

A

Hi! What do you have here?

嗨！你這裡有什麼？
B

We have fried chicken, grilled skewers, and oyster omelet.

我們有雞排、烤串和蚵仔煎。
A

That smells great. How much is the fried chicken?

好香喔。雞排多少錢？
B

It's sixty dollars for one piece.

一份六十元。
A

Okay, I'd like one piece, please. Can you make it spicy?

好，我要一份，謝謝。可以做辣的嗎？
B

Sure! A little spicy or very spicy?

沒問題！小辣還是大辣？
A

A little spicy, please. And one bubble tea.

小辣，謝謝。再來一杯珍奶。
B

Great. That's ninety dollars all together.

好的，總共九十元。
A

Here you go. Thank you so much!

錢給你，非常謝謝！
B

Enjoy your food! Please come again.

慢用！歡迎再來。
Tip: Read it through twice, then swap roles so your student practices both ordering and selling. Encourage them to change the food and prices to match a real stall they know. 小提醒：先念兩遍，再交換角色，讓學生練到「點餐」和「賣東西」兩邊。鼓勵他換成自己熟悉的攤位食物和價錢。

4 · Let's talk 12 min

換你說 · 依程度選一組，讓學生主導

Now your student shares their own experiences. Choose the level that fits and let them lead. 換學生分享自己的經驗。選擇適合的程度，讓他主導對話。

BEGINNER 初級
  • What is your favorite night-market food?你最喜歡的夜市食物是什麼？
  • Do you like spicy food or not spicy food?你喜歡辣的還是不辣的？
  • What do you usually drink at the night market?你在夜市通常喝什麼？
INTERMEDIATE 中級
  • Which night market do you like best, and why?你最喜歡哪個夜市？為什麼？
  • What do you usually order when you go? Walk me through it.你去的時候通常點什麼？帶我走一遍。
  • Who do you go to the night market with, and what do you do there?你都跟誰去夜市？在那裡都做些什麼？
ADVANCED 進階
  • How have the night markets in your area changed over the years?這些年來你家附近的夜市有什麼改變？
  • Is there a food that visitors find strange but you really love? Explain it to me.有沒有一種外國人覺得奇怪、你卻很愛的食物？解釋給我聽。
  • If a night market and a restaurant cost the same, which would you choose, and why?如果夜市和餐廳價錢一樣，你會選哪個？為什麼？
Tip: After every answer, ask "Why?" or "Tell me more." You are learning about Taiwan — show real interest and your student will open up. 小提醒：每個回答後都問「為什麼？」或「再多說一點」。你正在認識台灣——展現真心的興趣，學生就會打開話匣子。

5 · Going deeper 10 min

再深入一點 · 描述、解釋、推薦

Push a little further. These questions ask your student to describe, explain, and recommend. 再往前推一點。這些問題要學生去描述、解釋和推薦。

BEGINNER 初級
  • Describe your favorite food. Is it hot, sweet, crispy, or spicy?描述你最愛的食物。它是熱的、甜的、脆的，還是辣的？
  • What color is it? How does it smell?它是什麼顏色？聞起來如何？
INTERMEDIATE 中級
  • Are there any games at your night market, like ring toss or shooting balloons? How do you play?你的夜市有套圈圈、射氣球之類的遊戲嗎？怎麼玩？
  • Describe one dish step by step, as if I have never seen it before.一步一步描述一道菜，當作我從沒看過它。
ADVANCED 進階
  • A foreign friend is visiting Taiwan. What three foods would you tell them to try, and in what order?一個外國朋友來台灣玩，你會叫他依序試哪三樣食物？
  • What is one food you would warn a visitor about before they order it?有哪一樣食物你會在訪客點之前先提醒他？
Tip: When your student describes a dish, repeat it back in clean English ("So it's a soft pancake with oysters and egg, and it's a little sticky — got it!"). This gives gentle modeling without stopping their flow. 小提醒：學生描述一道菜時，用乾淨的英文複述一遍（「所以是一種軟軟的煎餅，加蚵仔和蛋，有點黏——懂了！」）。這樣能溫和示範，又不打斷他的節奏。

6 · Activity — role-play the night market 10 min

活動 · 夜市角色扮演

Time to act it out. Your student becomes the vendor or the guide, and you are the visiting customer. 來演演看。學生當攤販或導覽，你當來訪的客人。

  1. Your student picks a real night-market stall they know and decides what it sells.學生選一個他熟悉的真實攤位，決定它賣什麼。
  2. You walk up as a curious foreign visitor and ask about the food.你以好奇的外國訪客身分走上前，詢問食物。
  3. Your student describes the dishes, gives prices, and takes your order.學生介紹菜色、報價，並接你的點單。
  4. Then switch: you become the vendor and your student is the visitor ordering.然後交換：你當攤販，學生當點餐的訪客。
  5. Finish by having your student recommend one more thing for you to try.最後請學生再推薦一樣讓你試試。

Visitor: "Hi! What do you recommend here?"

Vendor: "You should try the ___. It's really ___."

Visitor: "How much is ___?"

Vendor: "It's ___ dollars. Would you like it spicy?"

Visitor: "Yes, please. I'd like ___, please."

Vendor: "Good choice! And you should also try ___ next time."

Tip: Stay in character and react like a real tourist — surprise, hunger, curiosity. The more fun you make it, the more your student forgets to be nervous. 小提醒：保持角色，像真的觀光客一樣反應——驚訝、肚子餓、好奇。你玩得越開心，學生越會忘記緊張。

7 · Wrap-up 5 min

收尾 · 在高點結束
  • Ask your student to name 2–3 foods they talked about today.請學生說出今天聊到的 2～3 樣食物。
  • Give specific praise — for example, "You described the oyster omelet so clearly, I really want to try it!"給具體的稱讚——例如「你把蚵仔煎描述得好清楚，我真的好想吃！」
  • Let your student pick the topic for next time (festivals, school life, family food, travel).讓學生選下次的主題（節慶、學校生活、家裡的菜、旅行）。
  • Say a warm goodbye and thank them for teaching you about Taiwan.溫暖地道別，謝謝他教你認識台灣。
Tip: End on a high note. The last thing your student feels should be that they did well and that you enjoyed learning from them. 小提醒：在高點結束。學生最後的感受，應該是「我表現得很好，老師也很開心跟我學」。

🧭 Tutor notes

老師筆記 · 判斷程度、規劃下次

Use this to judge the level you saw and to plan next time. 用這個判斷你看到的程度，並規劃下一堂課。

BEGINNER 初級
  • Answered in single words or short phrases ("fried chicken", "very spicy").用單字或短語回答（「雞排」「很辣」）。
  • Next time: pre-teach 5 food words and practice "I'd like ___, please" until it's automatic.下次：先教 5 個食物單字，把「I'd like ___, please」練到自動反應。
INTERMEDIATE 中級
  • Made full sentences and could order, give prices, and describe a favorite dish.能講完整句子，會點餐、報價、描述喜歡的菜。
  • Next time: push longer answers — ask them to compare two night markets or explain a game.下次：要求更長的回答——請他比較兩個夜市，或解釋一個遊戲。
ADVANCED 進階
  • Told stories, gave opinions, and explained foods a visitor might find strange.會說故事、表達意見，並解釋訪客可能覺得奇怪的食物。
  • Next time: add follow-up "why" questions and discussion topics like food culture and change over time.下次：加入追問「為什麼」，並討論飲食文化與其隨時間的變化。
Most important: Write down your student's name, the foods they love, the night market near their home, and one thing they were proud to teach you. Bring it up next lesson — it shows you listened and builds real trust. 最重要：記下學生的名字、他愛的食物、他家附近的夜市，以及他驕傲地教你的一件事。下次上課提起來——這證明你有在聽，能建立真正的信任。
