United Nations Association
of Orange County

聯合國協會 · 橙縣分會（UNA-OC）
"Educate and inspire individuals to support the UN and its goals."

A community chapter of the United Nations Association of the USA (UNA-USA), UNA-OC turns the UN's global goals into local action — championing the Sustainable Development Goals, fostering global citizenship, and connecting young people to the wider world.

UNA-OC 是美國聯合國協會（UNA-USA）的社區分會，把聯合國的全球目標化為在地行動——推動永續發展目標（SDGs）、培養全球公民意識，並讓青年連結更廣大的世界。

Why we are partners · 為何結為夥伴

One world, taught from two shores

A shared mission · 共同的使命

UNA-OC and My Culture Connect share the same belief: that young people learn best when the world feels real and close. UNA-OC brings the UN Sustainable Development Goals and global citizenship into local life; MCC brings living English and a global stage into Taiwan's classrooms. Together they give students a local-to-global horizon.

UNA-OC 與人師（MCC）懷抱相同信念：當世界變得真實而靠近，孩子學得最好。UNA-OC 把聯合國永續發展目標（SDGs）與全球公民意識帶進在地生活；人師把活的英語與國際舞台帶進臺灣課堂。兩者共同為學生開啟「在地連結全球」的視野。

A living bridge · 真實的橋樑

The connection between the two organizations is a person. Dom Jones — Chairwoman of MCC's Advocacy Board and a partner of the hub — serves as UNA-OC's Government Affairs Ambassador, advancing the UN's 2030 Agenda. Through her, the SDGs travel from Orange County straight into Taiwan's schools.

兩個組織之間的連結，是一個人。Dom Jones——人師倡議委員會主席、本資源網的夥伴——同時擔任 UNA-OC 的政府事務大使（Government Affairs Ambassador），推動聯合國 2030 議程。透過她，SDGs 從橙縣一路走進臺灣的校園。

Our Ambassador · 我們的大使

Dom Jones — the bridge between UNA-OC and Taiwan

Government Affairs Ambassador · 政府事務大使

Dom Jones 多姆・瓊斯

UNA-OC · UN SDG Advocate · MCC Advocacy Board

A California educator, advocate and media personality, Dom carries the UN's 2030 Agenda into her work as UNA-OC's Government Affairs Ambassador — and brings the same global-citizenship message to Taiwan's students through her school assembly tour built on English education, global connection, and kindness.

來自加州的教育家、社會運動者與媒體人。Dom 以 UNA-OC 政府事務大使的身分推動聯合國 2030 議程，並把同樣的全球公民訊息，透過以「英語教育、全球連結、善意」為主軸的校園巡迴，帶給臺灣的學生。

What They Do · 主要工作

Turning global goals into local action

🎯

SDG Champions

永續目標倡議者

🤝

Community Partnerships

社區夥伴合作

Working alongside food banks, refugee resettlement and community organizations whose work supports the SDGs.

📣

Advocacy

政策倡議

Advocating for UN-aligned policies and the 2030 Agenda at the local, state and federal level.

🎓

Youth Engagement

青年參與

Drawing young people into UN values through events, mentoring and leadership opportunities.

🏫

Campus & Model UN

校園與模擬聯合國

Campus chapters, Model UN chapters and university chapters that grow the next generation of global citizens.

🌏

Local-to-Global

Connecting everyday community life to the United Nations — and, through MCC, to Taiwan's classrooms.

Their Mission · 他們的使命

Local-to-global engagement

"Educate and inspire individuals to support the UN and its goals" — fostering global citizenship and promoting UN values, from Orange County to the wider world.

「教育並激勵每一個人支持聯合國及其目標」——培養全球公民意識、推廣聯合國價值，從橙縣連結到更廣大的世界。

UNA-OC is a chapter of the United Nations Association of the USA, part of a nationwide network supporting the work of the United Nations and the UN Foundation. UNA-OC 是美國聯合國協會的分會，屬於全美支持聯合國及聯合國基金會工作的網絡之一。
