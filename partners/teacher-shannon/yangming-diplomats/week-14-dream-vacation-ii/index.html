My Dream Vacation
我的夢想旅行 · 用五個問題規劃你的完美假期
0.

Dream It. Plan It. Say It!

做夢、計畫、說出來！
🌍🏖️⛰️🗺️
Everyone has a dream vacation — a perfect place they would love to visit one day!
Today we use the 5 W's — Who, What, Where, When, and Why — to plan and talk about our dream trips in clear, confident English.
每個人心裡都有一個夢想旅行地點！今天我們用五個W問題（誰、什麼、哪裡、何時、為什麼）來規劃並說出你的夢想旅程，說得清晰又有自信。
I.

The 5 W's of Travel Planning

旅遊計畫的五個W關鍵問題
QUESTION 1
WHO
誰去？
"I would like to go with my family."
"I would travel with my best friend."
"I would love to go by myself someday."
QUESTION 2
WHAT
做什麼？
"I want to try local food."
"I hope to visit museums."
"I plan to take lots of photos."
QUESTION 3
WHERE
去哪裡？
"I dream of going to Japan."
"My dream destination is Iceland."
"One day I want to visit New Zealand."
QUESTION 4
WHEN
什麼時候？
"I want to go after I graduate."
"I hope to travel next summer."
"I plan to go when I finish high school."
QUESTION 5
WHY
為什麼？
"I want to go because I love the culture."
"I dream of visiting there because the scenery is breathtaking."
"I want to go because I want to practice my English."
II.

Useful Travel Phrases

旅遊必用句型 · 15 句
My dream destination is…
我的夢想目的地是…
"My dream destination is Australia."
I have always wanted to visit…
我一直很想去…
"I've always wanted to visit Paris."
I would love to travel to…
我非常想去…旅行
"I would love to travel to Canada."
One day I hope to…
有朝一日我希望…
"One day I hope to see the Northern Lights."
I am fascinated by…
我對…非常著迷
"I am fascinated by Japanese street food."
The scenery is breathtaking.
景色令人嘆為觀止
"I heard the scenery in New Zealand is breathtaking."
I would like to try…
我想試試看…
"I would like to try authentic Italian pizza."
It is on my bucket list.
這在我的人生清單上
"Seeing the Great Wall is on my bucket list."
I plan to go there after…
我計畫在…之後去那裡
"I plan to go there after graduating."
The culture there is unique.
那裡的文化很獨特
"The culture there is unique and full of history."
I want to experience…
我想體驗…
"I want to experience local life in Vietnam."
I would travel by…
我會搭…去
"I would travel by train through Europe."
I am saving up for a trip to…
我正在為去…存錢
"I am saving up for a trip to South Korea."
I would spend about…days there.
我大概會在那待…天
"I would spend about ten days there."
Because the food / scenery / culture / people…
因為食物/景色/文化/人…
"Because the food there is amazing and affordable."

✓ Pro Tips for Talking About Your Dream Vacation
Use "would" for dreams and wishes
"I would love to go to Iceland." / "I would stay in a cozy cabin."
Would makes it clear this is a dream — not a definite plan yet.
Give a Reason with "Because"
Always add "because" to explain WHY: "I want to visit Japan because I love the culture and the cherry blossoms are stunning."
Use Adjectives to Make It Vivid!
amazing · breathtaking · peaceful · exciting · unforgettable · unique · vibrant
Don't just say "It's good." Say "The scenery is absolutely breathtaking!"
