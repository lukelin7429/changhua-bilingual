CCC Chinese School comes to Changhua

紐約首府華社中文學校 × 彰化教育文化交流

An ongoing partnership with the CCC Chinese School of Albany, New York. Since 2022 their students have tutored Taiwan's children in English; in 2024 and again in 2025 they crossed the Pacific to learn, teach, and exchange culture in Changhua.

與位於紐約州首府 Albany 的首府華社中文學校長期合作。自 2022 年起,他們的學生線上輔導臺灣孩子英文;2024、2025 連續兩年來到彰化,實地學習、教學、進行文化交流。

The Partnership · 這段合作

A bridge from New York to Changhua

How it began · 緣起

Since 2022, My Culture Connect has partnered with CCC Chinese School in Albany, the capital of New York State. Their students tutor Taiwan's elementary children in English — fostering cultural exchange and real language practice across the Pacific.

自 2022 年起,人師與位於紐約州首府 Albany 的首府華社中文學校合作。他們的學生線上輔導臺灣國小孩童英文,跨越太平洋促成真實的文化交流與語言練習。

On the ground · 來到臺灣

In July 2024, 14 CCC students came to Taiwan for meaningful volunteer work. In 2025 they returned to Changhua — visiting elementary and vocational schools, making tofu, learning calligraphy, dragon dance and Chinese music, and teaching alongside local students.

2024 年 7 月,14 位 CCC 學生來臺進行志工服務;2025 年再度回到彰化——走訪國小與高工、體驗製作豆腐、書法、舞龍與國樂,並與在地學生協同教學。
