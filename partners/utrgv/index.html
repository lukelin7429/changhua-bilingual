🎓 University Partner · 大學夥伴 · 2017

UTRGV comes to Taiwan

德州大學大河谷分校 × 彰化中小學體驗交流

In 2017, a cohort of student teachers from the University of Texas Rio Grande Valley crossed the Pacific to teach in Changhua's classrooms — one of the program's earliest international partnerships, and the start of bringing living English to Taiwan's students.

2017 年,一整梯來自德州大學大河谷分校(UTRGV)的師資生跨越太平洋,走進彰化的中小學課堂——這是本計畫最早的國際合作之一,也是把「活的英文」帶給臺灣學生的起點。

🇺🇸 UTRGV👩‍🏫 21 Student Teachers🏫 Changhua Schools📅 2017
The Partnership · 這段合作

A first global stage, eight years on

What happened · 緣起

Led by Professor Robert Gilbert, more than twenty UTRGV student teachers spent time in Changhua's elementary and middle schools — co-teaching, sharing their cultures, and giving students their first real conversations with teachers from the other side of the world.

在 Robert Gilbert 教授帶領下,二十多位 UTRGV 師資生走進彰化的中小學,協同教學、分享自身文化,讓孩子第一次與來自地球另一端的老師真正對話。

Why it still matters · 為何留存

This was one of the earliest international exchanges behind today's hub. These video reflections — recorded by the student teachers themselves — are a living record of why bringing the world into Taiwan's classrooms works.

這是今日資源網背後最早的國際交流之一。這些由師資生親自錄下的回顧短片,是「把世界帶進臺灣教室」為何有效的真實見證,值得被保存。
